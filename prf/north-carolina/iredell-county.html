PRF insurance in Iredell County, North Carolina: 2027 grids, values and record

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF) is USDA’s rainfall-index insurance for grazing land and hay ground. USDA’s 2027 files offer it in Iredell County on 10 grids: 18497, 18796, 18797, 18798, 19096, 19097, 19098, 19396, 19397, 19398. It pays when precipitation in the grid over two months you choose comes in under a set share of normal.

As of Oct 12, the record from 1951 to 2025 on the middle value of the county’s 10 grids shows October to November came in under 90% of normal in 38 of 75 years. On that same middle, October to November ran 50% of normal in 2025 and November to December ran 38% in 2025. So far in 2026, the middle shows March to April at 39% and June to July at 108%.

USDA’s 2027 county base values, the same on every grid in the county. Protection per acre is the value times your coverage level times a productivity factor of 60 to 150: grazing at 90% and 100 is $62.01 an acre.

Example, not a recommendation: 100 acres of grazing at 90% coverage, productivity factor 100, half in April to May and half in June to July, on grid 19096, the middle of the county’s 10 grids by premium. The full premium is $628: USDA pays $320 and you pay $308, or $3.08 an acre. Run over 1951 to 2025 at 2027 rates, it would have paid in 48 of 75 years, an average of $528 a year, or $5.28 an acre. That average is under the full premium of $628; it comes out above your share only because USDA pays the rest. That is the past record, not a forecast, and it leaves out the administrative fee.

What each pair of months costs in Iredell County

At 90% coverage, across the 10 grids that touch the county: first the middle of the 10 for that pair of months (it can be a different grid in each row), then the lowest and highest. The premium is the full rate per $100 of protection; USDA pays 59% at 70%, 59% at 75%, 55% at 80%, 55% at 85%, 51% at 90%. Normal is the grid’s average precipitation for those months. A year counts, of the 75 from 1951 to 2025, when the grid’s index for those months came in under 90% of normal, which is when a 90% policy pays.

PRF dates for 2027

  • Dec 1, 2026: sales closing. New policies and changes are due at your agent.
  • Dec 1, 2026: acreage report.
  • Sep 1, 2027: premium billed, no earlier than this.

From 2027, grazing acres need livestock you have an interest in, equal to at least 25% of the stocking rate, or no payment is due and the premium is still owed. PRF pays on the grid’s precipitation index, not on your production. How PRF works · Before you sign · PRF in North Carolina

PRF questions in Iredell County

When is the PRF deadline in Iredell County for 2027?

The sales closing date is Dec 1, 2026, the same across the country. The acreage report is due Dec 1, 2026, and the premium is billed no earlier than Sep 1, 2027.

Which PRF grid is Iredell County in?

Grids 18497, 18796, 18797, 18798, 19096, 19097, 19098, 19396, 19397, 19398 touch Iredell County. A grid is a quarter degree of latitude by a quarter degree of longitude, about 17 miles north to south. Your grid follows from a point inside the acres you insure; type a ZIP or coordinates in the calculator to find it.

How much does PRF cost per acre in Iredell County?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and your productivity factor. USDA’s 2027 county base value is $68.90 an acre for grazing, $329.00 for non-irrigated hay. At 90% coverage and a productivity factor of 100, grazing land is protected for $62.01 an acre, and the full premium runs $9.10 to $18.45 per $100 of protection depending on the months, on the middle of the 10 grids. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. In the example above, 100 acres of grazing at 90% with half in April to May and half in June to July, you pay $3.08 an acre on grid 19096. The calculator gives the figure for your acres and months.

How often has PRF paid in Iredell County?

At 90% coverage a pair of months pays when the index comes in under 90% of normal. Over 1951 to 2025, on the middle of the 10 grids, that happened in 27 to 38 of 75 years, depending on the months. Run at 2027 rates, $1 of full premium at 90% would have paid back a middle $0.86 across the county’s grids and months. That is the past record, not a forecast.
