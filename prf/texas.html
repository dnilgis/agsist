PRF insurance in Texas: 2027 county values and rates

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF) is USDA’s rainfall-index insurance for grazing land and hay ground. USDA’s 2027 files offer it in 254 Texas counties, on 1,120 grids. The policy pays when precipitation in the grid over two months you choose comes in under a set share of normal.

PRF dates for 2027

  • Dec 1, 2026: sales closing. New policies and changes are due at your agent.
  • Dec 1, 2026: acreage report, with any lease signed and in hand.
  • Sep 1, 2027: premium billed, no earlier than this. Premium owed comes out of any payment first.

From 2027, grazing acres need livestock you have an interest in, equal to at least 25% of the stocking rate, or no payment is due and the premium is still owed (2027 Crop Provisions, section 4(j)). USDA’s share of the premium needs form AD-1026 on file with the Farm Service Agency. PRF pays on a precipitation index for the grid, not on your production. PRF questions in Texas

When is the PRF deadline in Texas for 2027?

The sales closing date is Dec 1, 2026, the same across the country. The acreage report is due Dec 1, 2026, and the premium is billed no earlier than Sep 1, 2027.

How much does PRF cost per acre in Texas?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and the county’s value. USDA’s county base value for grazing land in Texas runs from $8.50 to $34.60 an acre, and for non-irrigated hay from $67.00 to $224.00. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. The calculator gives the figure for your grid, acres and months.

Is PRF offered in every Texas county?

USDA’s 2027 files list PRF in 254 Texas counties, across 1,120 grids. Each county row in the table above opens the calculator on that county.

Has PRF paid more than it cost in Texas?

On the full premium, about the same as everywhere: across Texas’s grids at 90% coverage, $1 of premium would have paid back a middle $0.88 over 1951 to 2025 at 2027 rates, close to the national middle of $0.87. That average is made of a few big years and many small or empty ones, and none of it says what next year will do.
