PRF insurance in Lamar County, Alabama: 2027 grids, values and record

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF) is USDA’s rainfall-index insurance for grazing land and hay ground. USDA’s 2027 files offer it in Lamar County on 7 grids, each in the table of grids below. It pays when precipitation in the grid over two months you choose comes in under a set share of normal.

What it costs here at 2027 rates (USDA files read Oct 12, 2026): you pay $271 a year, $2.71 an acre, for 100 acres of grazing at 90% coverage, productivity factor 100, half in April to May and half in June to July, on grid 16668, the middle of the county’s 7 grids by premium. USDA pays $283 of the $554 full premium. Run over 1951 to 2025 at 2027 rates, it would have paid in 49 of 75 years, an average of $462 a year, or $4.62 an acre. That average is under the full premium of $554; it comes out above your share only because USDA pays the rest. That is the past record, not a forecast, and it leaves out the administrative fee. Example, not a recommendation.

What each pair of months costs in Lamar County

At 90% coverage, across the 7 grids that touch the county: first the middle of the 7 for that pair of months (it can be a different grid in each row), then the lowest and highest. The premium is the full rate per $100 of protection; USDA pays 59% at 70%, 59% at 75%, 55% at 80%, 55% at 85%, 51% at 90%. Normal is the grid’s average precipitation for those months. A year counts, of the 75 from 1951 to 2025, when the grid’s index for those months came in under 90% of normal, which is when a 90% policy pays.

PRF dates for 2027

  • Dec 1, 2026: sales closing. New policies and changes are due at your agent.
  • Dec 1, 2026: acreage report.
  • Sep 1, 2027: premium billed, no earlier than this.

From 2027, grazing acres need livestock you have an interest in, equal to at least 25% of the stocking rate, or no payment is due and the premium is still owed. PRF pays on the grid’s precipitation index, not on your production. How PRF works · Before you sign · PRF in Alabama

PRF questions in Lamar County

When is the PRF deadline in Lamar County for 2027?

The sales closing date is Dec 1, 2026, the same across the country. The acreage report is due Dec 1, 2026, and the premium is billed no earlier than Sep 1, 2027.

Which PRF grid is Lamar County in?

Grids 16367, 16368, 16369, 16668, 16669, 16968, 16969 touch Lamar County. A grid is a quarter degree of latitude by a quarter degree of longitude, about 17 miles north to south. Your grid follows from a point inside the acres you insure; type a ZIP or coordinates in the calculator to find it.

How much does PRF cost per acre in Lamar County?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and your productivity factor. USDA’s 2027 county base value is $50.60 an acre for grazing, $263.00 for non-irrigated hay. At 90% coverage and a productivity factor of 100, grazing land is protected for $45.54 an acre, and the full premium runs $10.16 to $17.66 per $100 of protection depending on the months, on the middle of the 7 grids. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. In the example above, 100 acres of grazing at 90% with half in April to May and half in June to July, you pay $2.71 an acre on grid 16668. The calculator gives the figure for your acres and months.

How often has PRF paid in Lamar County?

At 90% coverage a pair of months pays when the index comes in under 90% of normal. Over 1951 to 2025, on the middle of the 7 grids, that happened in 30 to 35 of 75 years, depending on the months. Run at 2027 rates, $1 of full premium at 90% would have paid back a middle $0.85 across the county’s grids and months. That is the past record, not a forecast.
