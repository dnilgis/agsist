PRF insurance in San Francisco County, California: 2027 grids, values and record

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF) is USDA’s rainfall-index insurance for grazing land and hay ground. USDA’s 2027 files offer it in San Francisco County on 3 grids: 21031, 21330, 21331. It pays when precipitation in the grid over two months you choose comes in under a set share of normal.

As of Oct 12, the record here runs from 1951 to 2025. On the middle value of the county’s 3 grids, May to June and September to October each came in under 90% of normal in 46 of 75 years. In 2025 on the middle, April to May was 20% of normal and May to June was 18%. All 3 grids had their driest year in 2013, and grid 21031 bottomed out at 22% that year.

What each pair of months costs in San Francisco County

At 90% coverage, across the 3 grids that touch the county: first the middle of the 3 for that pair of months (it can be a different grid in each row), then the lowest and highest. The premium is the full rate per $100 of protection; USDA pays 59% at 70%, 59% at 75%, 55% at 80%, 55% at 85%, 51% at 90%. Normal is the grid’s average precipitation for those months. A year counts, of the 75 from 1951 to 2025, when the grid’s index for those months came in under 90% of normal, which is when a 90% policy pays.

PRF dates for 2027

  • Dec 1, 2026: sales closing. New policies and changes are due at your agent.
  • Dec 1, 2026: acreage report.
  • Sep 1, 2027: premium billed, no earlier than this.

From 2027, grazing acres need livestock you have an interest in, equal to at least 25% of the stocking rate, or no payment is due and the premium is still owed. PRF pays on the grid’s precipitation index, not on your production. How PRF works · Before you sign · PRF in California

PRF questions in San Francisco County

When is the PRF deadline in San Francisco County for 2027?

The sales closing date is Dec 1, 2026, the same across the country. The acreage report is due Dec 1, 2026, and the premium is billed no earlier than Sep 1, 2027.

Which PRF grid is San Francisco County in?

Grids 21031, 21330, 21331 touch San Francisco County. A grid is a quarter degree of latitude by a quarter degree of longitude, about 17 miles north to south. Your grid follows from a point inside the acres you insure; type a ZIP or coordinates in the calculator to find it.

How much does PRF cost per acre in San Francisco County?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and your productivity factor. USDA’s 2027 county base value is $22.30 an acre for grazing. At 90% coverage and a productivity factor of 100, grazing land is protected for $20.07 an acre, and the full premium runs $22.17 to $44.07 per $100 of protection depending on the months, on the middle of the 3 grids. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. The calculator gives the figure for your acres and months.

How often has PRF paid in San Francisco County?

At 90% coverage a pair of months pays when the index comes in under 90% of normal. Over 1951 to 2025, on the middle of the 3 grids, that happened in 33 to 46 of 75 years, depending on the months. Run at 2027 rates, $1 of full premium at 90% would have paid back a middle $0.88 across the county’s grids and months. That is the past record, not a forecast.
