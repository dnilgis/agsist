What each pair of months costs in San Bernardino County

At 90% coverage, across the grids that touch the county that price each pair of months (104 in all; a row priced on fewer says how many): first the middle grid for that pair of months (it can be a different grid in each row), then the lowest and highest. The premium is the full rate per $100 of protection; USDA pays 59% at 70%, 59% at 75%, 55% at 80%, 55% at 85%, 51% at 90%. Normal is the grid’s average precipitation for those months. A year counts, of the 75 from 1951 to 2025, when the grid’s index for those months came in under 90% of normal, which is when a 90% policy pays.

How much does PRF cost per acre in San Bernardino County?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and your productivity factor. USDA’s 2027 county base value is $8.70 an acre for grazing. At 90% coverage and a productivity factor of 100, grazing land is protected for $7.83 an acre, and the full premium runs $31.08 to $50.44 per $100 of protection depending on the months: July to August on the middle of the 16 grids that price those months, May to June on the middle of the 6 grids that price those months. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. The calculator gives the figure for your acres and months.

How often has PRF paid in San Bernardino County?

At 90% coverage a pair of months pays when the index comes in under 90% of normal. Over 1951 to 2025, on the middle of the grids that price each pair of months, that happened in 40 to 46 of 75 years, depending on the months. Run at 2027 rates, $1 of full premium at 90% would have paid back a middle $0.88 across the county’s grids and months. That is the past record, not a forecast.
