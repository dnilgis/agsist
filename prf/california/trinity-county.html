PRF insurance in Trinity County, California: 2027 grids, values and record

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF) is USDA’s rainfall-index insurance for grazing land and hay ground. USDA’s 2027 files offer it in Trinity County on 29 grids: 23726, 23727, 23728, 23729, 24026, 24027, 24028, 24029, 24326, 24327, 24328, 24329, 24626, 24627, 24628, 24629, 24630, 24926, 24927, 24928, 24929, 24930, 25227, 25228, 25229, 25230, 25231, 25530, 25531. It pays when precipitation in the grid over two months you choose comes in under a set share of normal.

What each pair of months costs in Trinity County

At 90% coverage, across the grids that touch the county that price each pair of months (29 in all; a row priced on fewer says how many): first the middle grid for that pair of months (it can be a different grid in each row), then the lowest and highest. The premium is the full rate per $100 of protection; USDA pays 59% at 70%, 59% at 75%, 55% at 80%, 55% at 85%, 51% at 90%. Normal is the grid’s average precipitation for those months. A year counts, of the 75 from 1951 to 2025, when the grid’s index for those months came in under 90% of normal, which is when a 90% policy pays.

PRF dates for 2027

  • Dec 1, 2026: sales closing. New policies and changes are due at your agent.
  • Dec 1, 2026: acreage report.
  • Sep 1, 2027: premium billed, no earlier than this.

From 2027, grazing acres need livestock you have an interest in, equal to at least 25% of the stocking rate, or no payment is due and the premium is still owed. PRF pays on the grid’s precipitation index, not on your production. How PRF works · Before you sign · PRF in California

PRF questions in Trinity County

When is the PRF deadline in Trinity County for 2027?

The sales closing date is Dec 1, 2026, the same across the country. The acreage report is due Dec 1, 2026, and the premium is billed no earlier than Sep 1, 2027.

Which PRF grid is Trinity County in?

Grids 23726, 23727, 23728, 23729, 24026, 24027, 24028, 24029, 24326, 24327, 24328, 24329, 24626, 24627, 24628, 24629, 24630, 24926, 24927, 24928, 24929, 24930, 25227, 25228, 25229, 25230, 25231, 25530, 25531 touch Trinity County. A grid is a quarter degree of latitude by a quarter degree of longitude, about 17 miles north to south. Your grid follows from a point inside the acres you insure; type a ZIP or coordinates in the calculator to find it.

How much does PRF cost per acre in Trinity County?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and your productivity factor. USDA’s 2027 county base value is $32.50 an acre for grazing. At 90% coverage and a productivity factor of 100, grazing land is protected for $29.25 an acre, and the full premium runs $18.72 to $38.94 per $100 of protection depending on the months: January to February on the middle of the 29 grids, August to September on the middle of the 8 grids that price those months. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. The calculator gives the figure for your acres and months.

How often has PRF paid in Trinity County?

At 90% coverage a pair of months pays when the index comes in under 90% of normal. Over 1951 to 2025, on the middle of the grids that price each pair of months, that happened in 33 to 44 of 75 years, depending on the months. Run at 2027 rates, $1 of full premium at 90% would have paid back a middle $0.88 across the county’s grids and months. That is the past record, not a forecast.
