PRF insurance in Grand County, Utah: 2027 grids, values and record

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF) is USDA’s rainfall-index insurance for grazing land and hay ground. USDA’s 2027 files offer it in Grand County on 25 grids: 21980, 21981, 21982, 21983, 21984, 22280, 22281, 22282, 22283, 22284, 22580, 22581, 22582, 22583, 22584, 22880, 22881, 22882, 22883, 22884, 23180, 23181, 23182, 23183, 23184. It pays when precipitation in the grid over two months you choose comes in under a set share of normal.

As of Oct 12, the middle value of the county’s 25 grids came in under 90% of normal in May to June and June to July in 40 of 75 years since 1951. In 2025 the middle ran 30% of normal in January to February and 151% in October to November. Every grid listed had its driest year in 1956, with grid 21980 at 33%. The preliminary estimate for grid 21981 puts August to September 2026 at 71.6.

USDA’s 2027 county base values, the same on every grid in the county. Protection per acre is the value times your coverage level times a productivity factor of 60 to 150: grazing at 90% and 100 is $3.24 an acre.

Example, not a recommendation: 100 acres of grazing at 90% coverage, productivity factor 100, half in April to May and half in June to July, on grid 21981, the middle of the county’s 25 grids by premium. The full premium is $78: USDA pays $40 and you pay $38, or $0.38 an acre. Run over 1951 to 2025 at 2027 rates, it would have paid in 57 of 75 years, an average of $69 a year, or $0.69 an acre. That average is under the full premium of $78; it comes out above your share only because USDA pays the rest. That is the past record, not a forecast, and it leaves out the administrative fee.

What each pair of months costs in Grand County

At 90% coverage, across the 25 grids that touch the county: first the middle of the 25 for that pair of months (it can be a different grid in each row), then the lowest and highest. The premium is the full rate per $100 of protection; USDA pays 59% at 70%, 59% at 75%, 55% at 80%, 55% at 85%, 51% at 90%. Normal is the grid’s average precipitation for those months. A year counts, of the 75 from 1951 to 2025, when the grid’s index for those months came in under 90% of normal, which is when a 90% policy pays.

PRF dates for 2027

  • Dec 1, 2026: sales closing. New policies and changes are due at your agent.
  • Dec 1, 2026: acreage report.
  • Sep 1, 2027: premium billed, no earlier than this.

From 2027, grazing acres need livestock you have an interest in, equal to at least 25% of the stocking rate, or no payment is due and the premium is still owed. PRF pays on the grid’s precipitation index, not on your production. How PRF works · Before you sign · PRF in Utah

PRF questions in Grand County

When is the PRF deadline in Grand County for 2027?

The sales closing date is Dec 1, 2026, the same across the country. The acreage report is due Dec 1, 2026, and the premium is billed no earlier than Sep 1, 2027.

Which PRF grid is Grand County in?

Grids 21980, 21981, 21982, 21983, 21984, 22280, 22281, 22282, 22283, 22284, 22580, 22581, 22582, 22583, 22584, 22880, 22881, 22882, 22883, 22884, 23180, 23181, 23182, 23183, 23184 touch Grand County. A grid is a quarter degree of latitude by a quarter degree of longitude, about 17 miles north to south. Your grid follows from a point inside the acres you insure; type a ZIP or coordinates in the calculator to find it.

How much does PRF cost per acre in Grand County?

It depends on the grid, the months, the coverage level and your productivity factor. USDA’s 2027 county base value is $3.60 an acre for grazing. At 90% coverage and a productivity factor of 100, grazing land is protected for $3.24 an acre, and the full premium runs $17.86 to $25.58 per $100 of protection depending on the months, on the middle of the 25 grids. USDA pays 51% to 59% of the premium. In the example above, 100 acres of grazing at 90% with half in April to May and half in June to July, you pay $0.38 an acre on grid 21981. The calculator gives the figure for your acres and months.

How often has PRF paid in Grand County?

At 90% coverage a pair of months pays when the index comes in under 90% of normal. Over 1951 to 2025, on the middle of the 25 grids, that happened in 32 to 40 of 75 years, depending on the months. Run at 2027 rates, $1 of full premium at 90% would have paid back a middle $0.88 across the county’s grids and months. That is the past record, not a forecast.
