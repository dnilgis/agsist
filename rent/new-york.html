Rent for your ground, not the county average

The county number doesn’t know your ground. This scales the county’s 2026 rent by your own corn yield.

Pick a county and type your yield.

Derived by AGSIST, not a NASS number: the county’s 2026 NASS dryland rent divided by its 2026 corn trend yield (our 14-yr trend, same practice as the rent), times your yield. It assumes rent moves in step with yield, which real leases only roughly do. Use your APH or a typical year, not your best one. 35 of 47 New York counties have both numbers; for the rest the Corn trend column below says why there is none.

Two more ways to figure a fair rent

Share of gross. Your bushels times a price, and the share of that the rent takes. The share starts at the county’s own: its 2026 rent as a share of its trend-yield gross at this price.

Default: New York’s 2025 average price received for corn (USDA NASS, preliminary).

Return on land value. What the land is worth times the return an owner wants on it.

Cost-based. What is left of your gross after your own costs and pay for your work. That needs your budget, so this page doesn’t guess one.

Questions

What is the average cash rent per acre in New York for 2026?

The middle New York county pays $73 per acre for non-irrigated cropland: half of the 47 counties with a published 2026 USDA NASS rate are above it and half below. NASS also puts out its own statewide average, a separate figure that is not shown here. Rents vary widely by county: highest Suffolk ($146), Livingston ($141), Cayuga ($133); lowest Schoharie ($38), Essex ($45), Otsego ($48.50). The county mean is a survey reference point, not a rate card.

How much does pasture rent for per acre in New York?

The median USDA NASS county cash rent for permanent pasture in New York is $22.25 per acre across the 16 counties with a published 2026 pasture rate. Highest Fulton ($127), Herkimer ($40), Cortland ($39.50); lowest Oneida ($13.50), Chenango ($14.50), Steuben ($15). Each county's pasture rate is in its own column in the county table below.

How much did New York cash rent change from 2025 to 2026?

Comparing the same 44 counties published in both years, the median non-irrigated cropland rent went from $69 to $72.75, +5.4% year over year. That is the change in the state median, not in a typical county: each county's own rent moved its own way.

Why is my New York county not listed?

USDA NASS publishes a county rate only where enough Cash Rents Survey responses came back (and the county has at least 20,000 acres of cropland plus pasture). If your county is missing, NASS did not publish a rate for it.

When is county cash rent data released?

USDA NASS releases county cash rent estimates each August for the current crop year. This page rebuilds automatically when new data lands.

When must I give notice to terminate a New York farm lease?

Commonly cited: written notice about 90 days before expiration, certified mail. Confirm the current rule with a New York attorney before relying on it. The AGSIST cash farm lease page has the details and a printable lease.
