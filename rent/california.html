Questions

What is the average cash rent per acre in California for 2026?

The middle California county pays $437 per acre for irrigated cropland: half of the 41 counties with a published 2026 USDA NASS rate are above it and half below. NASS also puts out its own statewide average, a separate figure that is not shown here. Rents vary widely by county: highest Napa ($4,000), Ventura ($3,340), Orange ($2,360); lowest Shasta ($205), Lassen ($225), Glenn ($246). For non-irrigated cropland the middle county paid $34.75 (20 counties). The county mean is a survey reference point, not a rate card.

How much does pasture rent for per acre in California?

The median USDA NASS county cash rent for permanent pasture in California is $17.50 per acre across the 38 counties with a published 2026 pasture rate. Highest Marin ($42), Sutter ($32), Glenn ($31); lowest Ventura ($5.50), Santa Clara ($5.60), Santa Barbara ($9.90). Each county's pasture rate is in its own column in the county table below.

How much does farmland rent for per acre per month in California?

Cash rent is quoted per acre per year, not per month. Divided by 12, the middle California county's 2026 irrigated cropland rent of $437 a year is about $36.42 an acre a month. Pasture: $17.50 a year, about $1.46 a month.

How much did California cash rent change from 2025 to 2026?

Comparing the 31 counties published in both years, the median irrigated cropland rent went from $360 to $410, +13.9% year over year. That median is not the $437 headline, which counts every county published in 2026. That is the change in the state median, not in a typical county: each county's own rent moved its own way.

Why does my California county say not published?

USDA NASS publishes a county rate only where enough Cash Rents Survey responses came back (and the county has at least 20,000 acres of cropland plus pasture). A county marked not published had no 2026 rate of any kind from NASS; 3 California counties are in the table that way. Some counties NASS reports only inside a combined district, and those show as not published too. A rent more than three survey years old shows n/a with the last year it was published.

When is county cash rent data released?

USDA NASS releases county cash rent estimates in August of each survey year for that crop year. There was no survey in 2015 or 2018.

When must I give notice to terminate a California farm lease?

Leases over one year must be written to be enforceable; put the termination deadline in the lease itself (industry guides suggest ~July 1). Confirm the current rule with a California attorney before relying on it. The AGSIST cash farm lease page has the details and a printable lease.

Sources and terms

Source: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026 county estimates, and NASS county yield estimates. Data as of 2026-10-06.

These are the 2026 county rents, the newest NASS has put out (released Aug 21, 2026). NASS puts county rents out in late August of each survey year (it ran no survey in 2015 or 2018); the 2027 date is not on its calendar yet.

What this can’t tell you. These are county means from a voluntary USDA survey. Rents vary widely inside a county, driven by soil, drainage, field size and how badly a neighbor wants the ground. Year-over-year stats compare only counties published in both years.
