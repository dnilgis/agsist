Questions

What is the average cash rent per acre in Nebraska for 2026?

The median USDA NASS county cash rent for non-irrigated cropland in Nebraska is $141 per acre across the 80 counties with a published 2026 rate. Rents vary widely by county: highest Cuming ($289), Thurston ($269), Wayne ($269); lowest Sioux ($23.50), Banner ($30.50), Kimball ($34.50). The county mean is a survey reference point, not a rate card.

How much did Nebraska cash rent change from 2025 to 2026?

Comparing the same 73 counties published in both years, the median non-irrigated cropland rent moved +3.6% year over year.

Why is my Nebraska county not listed?

USDA NASS publishes a county rate only where enough Cash Rents Survey responses came back (and the county has at least 20,000 acres of cropland plus pasture). If your county is missing, NASS did not publish a rate for it, and this page never estimates one.

When is county cash rent data released?

USDA NASS releases county cash rent estimates each August for the current crop year. This page rebuilds automatically when new data lands.

When must I give notice to terminate a Nebraska farm lease?

Nebraska statute sets the deadline at September 1 for oral/holdover leases (Neb. Rev. Stat. section 76-1445). Miss it and the lease typically continues another year on the same terms. See the AGSIST cash farm lease for the details and a printable lease.
