Questions

What is the average cash rent per acre in Minnesota for 2026?

The median USDA NASS county cash rent for non-irrigated cropland in Minnesota is $189 per acre across the 81 counties with a published 2026 rate. Rents vary widely by county: highest Martin ($280), Sibley ($278), Dodge ($277); lowest Aitkin ($18.50), Carlton ($19), St. Louis ($20.50). The county mean is a survey reference point, not a rate card.

How much did Minnesota cash rent change from 2025 to 2026?

Comparing the same 79 counties published in both years, the median non-irrigated cropland rent moved -0.5% year over year.

Why is my Minnesota county not listed?

USDA NASS publishes a county rate only where enough Cash Rents Survey responses came back (and the county has at least 20,000 acres of cropland plus pasture). If your county is missing, NASS did not publish a rate for it — this page never estimates one.

When is county cash rent data released?

USDA NASS releases county cash rent estimates each August for the current crop year. This page rebuilds automatically when new data lands.
