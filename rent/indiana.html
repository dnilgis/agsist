Questions

What is the average cash rent per acre in Indiana for 2026?

The median USDA NASS county cash rent for non-irrigated cropland in Indiana is $217 per acre across the 89 counties with a published 2026 rate. Rents vary widely by county: highest Warren ($303), Benton ($300), Clinton ($298); lowest Perry ($127), Switzerland ($134), Brown ($134). The county mean is a survey reference point, not a rate card.

How much did Indiana cash rent change from 2025 to 2026?

Comparing the same 88 counties published in both years, the median non-irrigated cropland rent moved +1.6% year over year.

Why is my Indiana county not listed?

USDA NASS publishes a county rate only where enough Cash Rents Survey responses came back (and the county has at least 20,000 acres of cropland plus pasture). If your county is missing, NASS did not publish a rate for it — this page never estimates one.

When is county cash rent data released?

USDA NASS releases county cash rent estimates each August for the current crop year. This page rebuilds automatically when new data lands.

When must I give notice to terminate an Indiana farm lease?

Indiana statute sets the deadline at 3 months before the end of the lease year (Ind. Code section 32-31-1-3). Miss it and the lease typically continues another year on the same terms. See the AGSIST cash farm lease for the details and a printable lease.
