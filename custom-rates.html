Custom farm rates by state: what hired combining, planting, spraying and hauling cost

The going rate for custom work, from the surveys universities and state ag departments run of farmers and custom operators. Each number is the survey's own average, with its range, unit and year, and a link to the survey. Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

Questions

What does custom combining cost per acre?

Survey averages for combining corn, per acre: Iowa $46.05 (2026 survey, 119 answers); Indiana $36.97 (2025 survey, 53 answers); Kansas $41.87 (2026 survey, 82 answers); Missouri $47.56 (2025 rates, 2026 survey, 9 answers); North Dakota $43.87 (2024 survey, 38 answers); Nebraska $49.29 (2026 survey, 42 answers); Ohio $34.61 (2024 survey, 60 answers); Wisconsin $38.39 (2024 survey, 46 answers). Soybeans: Iowa $43.05 (2026 survey, 94 answers); Indiana $35.94 (2025 survey, 54 answers); Kansas $38.67 (2026 survey, 79 answers); Missouri $42.33 (2025 rates, 2026 survey, 9 answers); North Dakota $36.02 (2024 survey, 61 answers); Nebraska $50.87 (2026 survey, 38 answers); Ohio $33.53 (2024 survey, 58 answers); Oklahoma $27.11 (2021 survey, 9 answers); Wisconsin $37.13 (2024 survey, 46 answers). Some surveys price combining alone and some include the grain cart and hauling, so check the wording in each state's table. Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom rate for planting corn?

Survey averages for planting row crops without fertilizer, per acre: Iowa $26.50 (2026 survey, 69 answers); Indiana $22.97 (2025 survey, 31 answers); Kansas $22.64 (2026 survey, 48 answers); Missouri $24.65 (2025 rates, 2026 survey, 23 answers); North Dakota $21.02 (2024 survey, 28 answers); Nebraska $29.19 (2026 survey, 8 answers); Ohio $23.51 (2024 survey, 44 answers); Wisconsin $23.36 (2024 survey, 14 answers). Planting with fertilizer and no-till planting run a little higher in most surveys; each state's table has its lines.

What does custom spraying cost?

Ground rig spraying, per acre, chemical not included: Iowa $9.35 (2026 survey, 121 answers); Indiana $8.39 (2025 survey, 40 answers); Kansas $8.57 (2026 survey, 196 answers); Missouri $9.42 (2025 rates, 2026 survey, 20 answers); North Dakota $9.23 (2024 survey, 111 answers); Nebraska $9.85 (2026 survey, 29 answers); Ohio $9.89 (2024 survey, 97 answers); Oklahoma $6.48 (2021 survey, 56 answers); Wisconsin $11.48 (2024 survey, 42 answers).

Who sets these custom rates?

Nobody sets them. Each number is an average of what farmers and custom operators told a university or state survey they charged or paid. AGSIST copies the survey's own numbers and links the survey; it does not set, adjust or update them between surveys. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

Which states are covered?

States with a public survey read for this page: Iowa (2026), Indiana (2025), Kansas (2026), Missouri (2025 rates), North Dakota (2024), Nebraska (2026), Ohio (2024), Oklahoma (2021), Wisconsin (2024). Illinois Extension points readers to the Iowa survey; no Illinois survey of its own was found. Kentucky's guide averages other states' surveys, so it is not a Kentucky survey. Michigan State publishes machinery cost estimates, not a survey of rates charged, so it is not mixed in here. No current University of Minnesota custom rate survey was found (the last one in the university archive is from 1993). SDSU Extension ran a new survey in 2026; its report was not out when this page was built.

Why do the same jobs differ between states?

The surveys were taken in different years, ask about the job in different words (combine only, or combine, cart and haul), and hear from different people. Some print the lowest and highest answer, Iowa leaves out the single lowest and highest, Purdue prints a one standard deviation band and Oklahoma prints no range. Compare the wording before you compare the numbers.

What does custom grain hauling cost per bushel?

Kansas's 2026 survey prices hauling from the field to the farm or the nearest elevator. Corn averaged 23.9 cents a bushel, plus 5.5 cents a bushel a mile past 16 miles. Soybeans averaged 24.2 cents a bushel, plus 5.6 cents a bushel a mile past 18 miles.

Do custom combiners charge extra for a big corn crop?

Some do. In Kansas's 2026 survey, harvesters who charge a base rate averaged $35.27 an acre for corn, plus $0.279 a bushel on yield over 69 bushels an acre. Those who charge one flat rate with no yield charge averaged $41.87 an acre.

Where the numbers come from

Each state's survey, as published. AGSIST typed the averages, ranges and answer counts from the survey itself and links it; it did not set, adjust, inflate or average them. A job a survey left unclear is left out rather than guessed at, and each state page says which. The full data: index.json, one file per state beside it.
