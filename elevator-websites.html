What you get

  • Your bids, on your siteToday’s cash price and basis by crop, delivery month and location, with the time they were set. Your staff change them in one admin page from a phone or a computer. If you already post bids on a board AGSIST reads, the site can take them from there instead.
  • Your own domainThe site runs at your address, like yourcoop.com. No AGSIST branding across the top and no ads.
  • Hours, contacts and directionsDelivery hours for each location, a tap-to-call button, the people to ask for, and a map link to every yard. A notice line for days like “closed for inventory” or “dump open late this week.”
  • Bid alerts for your farmersA sign-up where a farmer picks a crop and a price and gets an email when your bid gets there. They confirm their address and can stop with one click.

Questions

Can the site be on our own domain?
Yes. If you have a domain, Sig points it at the new site. If you don’t, he helps you register one in your elevator’s name, so it stays yours whatever happens.
Who updates the bids?
Your staff, in one admin page that works on a phone. They set cash or basis for each crop, month and location and press save. If your bids are already on a board AGSIST reads, the site can pick them up from there and nobody has to type them twice.
We already have a website. Does this replace it?
It can, or it can sit beside it. Some elevators move the whole site over. Others keep their main site and put the bid board on a page like bids.yourcoop.com, linked from the menu. Sig will look at what you have and tell you which is less work.
Is there a contract?
No. The setup fee is paid once, then the monthly fee is month-to-month. Cancel anytime. Your domain stays in your name.
What do our farmers see?
A fast page on their phone with today’s bids for each location and the time they were set, your hours, a button to call, directions, and any notice you post. They can sign up for an email when your bid reaches their price. No login and no ads.
Will it show futures prices?
No. Republishing live exchange quotes takes a license, so the site shows the cash prices and basis you post. Farmers who want the board can find futures on AGSIST.
