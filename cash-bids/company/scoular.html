Questions about Scoular bids

What is Scoular paying for corn today?

The Scoular corn bids we read run from $3.94 a bushel at Fremont to $5.74 at Jerome, across 17 locations, for the nearest delivery each posts, posted Nov 10. Call the location to confirm before you haul.

What is Scoular paying for soybeans today?

The Scoular soybean bids we read run from $11.38 a bushel at Champion (Lamar) to $11.93 at Covington, across 11 locations, for the nearest delivery each posts, posted Nov 10. Call the location to confirm before you haul.

How many Scoular locations does AGSIST read?

20 Scoular locations in Kansas, Nebraska, Missouri, Florida, Idaho, Iowa, Montana and Ohio. Every one has a current bid on this page.

Is AGSIST part of Scoular?

No. AGSIST is not affiliated with Scoular. These bids are read from Scoular's public bid board (scoularview.com). For a contract, a price later in the day or anything else, call the location.

Sources and terms

Bids come from each Scoular location’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open.
