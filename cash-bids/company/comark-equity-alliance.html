Where these come from. The AGSIST elevator network reads each CoMark Equity Alliance location’s posted bid board directly; no third-party bid feed is used. A bid shows only if the board was read successfully in the last 6 hours and did not fail on the latest pass, the price was posted in the last 72 hours, and its delivery period has not ended. Each price is the location’s nearest delivery month for the grade it posts most; the town page has every month and the futures contract each basis is quoted against.
