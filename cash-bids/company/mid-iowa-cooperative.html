Sources and terms

Bids come from each Mid-Iowa Cooperative location’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open. Each price is the location’s earliest delivery period for its regular grade, or a specialty grade where that is all it posts; the town page has every month and the futures contract each basis is quoted against. A basis far off the elevators around it is left out here. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day; always call the elevator to confirm before you haul.
