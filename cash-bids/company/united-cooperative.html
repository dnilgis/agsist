Questions about United Cooperative bids

What is United Cooperative paying for corn today?

The United Cooperative corn bids we read run from $3.91 a bushel at Denmark (Denmark Grain) to $4.09 at Boscobel (Boscobel Grain), across 16 locations, for the nearest delivery each posts, posted Oct 28. Call the location to confirm before you haul.

What is United Cooperative paying for soybeans today?

How many United Cooperative locations does AGSIST read?

16 United Cooperative locations in Wisconsin. Every one has a current bid on this page.

Is AGSIST part of United Cooperative?

No. AGSIST is not affiliated with United Cooperative. These bids are read from United Cooperative's public bid board (unitedcooperative.com). For a contract, a price later in the day or anything else, call the location.

Where these come from. The AGSIST elevator network reads each United Cooperative location’s posted bid board directly; no third-party bid feed is used. A bid shows only if the board was read successfully in the last 6 hours and did not fail on the latest pass, the price was posted in the last 72 hours, and its delivery period has not ended. Each price is the location’s nearest delivery month for the grade it posts most; the town page has every month and the futures contract each basis is quoted against. A basis far off the elevators around it is left out here and flagged on the town page. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day.
