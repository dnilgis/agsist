Is AGSIST part of New Vision Co-op?

No. AGSIST is not affiliated with New Vision Co-op. These bids are read from New Vision Co-op's public bid board (newvision.coop). For a contract, a price later in the day or anything else, call the location.

Where these come from. The AGSIST elevator network reads each New Vision Co-op location’s posted bid board directly; no third-party bid feed is used. A bid shows only if the board was confirmed on the latest read, the price was posted in the last 72 hours, and its delivery period has not ended. Each price is the location’s nearest delivery month for the grade it posts most; the town page has every month and the futures contract each basis is quoted against. A basis far off the elevators around it is left out here and flagged on the town page. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day.
