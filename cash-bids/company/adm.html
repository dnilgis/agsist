Questions about ADM bids

What is ADM paying for corn today?

The ADM corn bids we read run from $3.81 a bushel at Saint Cloud (St. Cloud) to $5.01 at Vidalia, across 82 locations, for the nearest delivery each posts, posted Oct 22. Call the location to confirm before you haul.

How many ADM locations does AGSIST read?

107 ADM locations in Illinois, Indiana, Nebraska, Kansas, Missouri, Minnesota, Iowa, Kentucky, Michigan, North Dakota, Ohio, Tennessee, Arkansas, Georgia, Louisiana, New York, Oklahoma and Washington. Every one has a current bid on this page.

Is AGSIST part of ADM?

No. AGSIST is not affiliated with ADM. These bids are read from ADM's public bid board (adm.gradable.com). For a contract, a price later in the day or anything else, call the location.

Where these come from. The AGSIST elevator network reads each ADM location’s posted bid board directly; no third-party bid feed is used. A bid shows only if the board was read successfully in the last 6 hours and did not fail on the latest pass, the price was posted in the last 72 hours, and its delivery period has not ended. Each price is the location’s nearest delivery month for the grade it posts most; the town page has every month and the futures contract each basis is quoted against. A basis far off the elevators around it is left out here and flagged on the town page. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day.
