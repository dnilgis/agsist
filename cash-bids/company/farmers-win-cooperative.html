How many Farmers Win Cooperative locations does AGSIST read?

18 Farmers Win Cooperative locations in Iowa and Minnesota. 16 of them have a current bid on this page; the rest show when their board was last read.

Is AGSIST part of Farmers Win Cooperative?

No. AGSIST is not affiliated with Farmers Win Cooperative. These bids are read from Farmers Win Cooperative's public bid board (farmerswin.com). For a contract, a price later in the day or anything else, call the location.

Where these come from. The AGSIST elevator network reads each Farmers Win Cooperative location’s posted bid board directly; no third-party bid feed is used. A bid shows only if the board was read successfully in the last 6 hours and did not fail on the latest pass, the price was posted in the last 72 hours, and its delivery period has not ended. Each price is the location’s nearest delivery month for the grade it posts most; the town page has every month and the futures contract each basis is quoted against. A basis far off the elevators around it is left out here and flagged on the town page. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day.
