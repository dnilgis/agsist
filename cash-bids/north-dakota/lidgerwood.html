This page is a snapshot and is rebuilt through the day; the live page has the newest board and nearby elevators. Basis is cash minus the futures contract named under it. An elevator whose board names a site rather than a town is listed here when its ZIP belongs to Lidgerwood; its card shows the board’s own name and the ZIP.
