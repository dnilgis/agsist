How these numbers are made. Source: each elevator’s own posted bid board, read by AGSIST; no third-party bid feed is used on this page. A bid counts only if its board was read successfully in the last 6 hours and did not fail on the latest pass, and its price was posted within 72 hours, long enough that a board unchanged over a weekend still counts on Monday. Specialty bids (white, organic, non-GMO, high-oleic, identity-preserved) are left out of the basis. A middle number needs at least 3 elevators on the same futures month.
