How these numbers are made. Source: each elevator’s own posted bid board, read by AGSIST; no third-party bid feed is used on this page. A bid counts only if its board was read successfully in the last 6 hours and did not fail on the latest pass, and its price was posted within 72 hours, long enough that a board unchanged over a weekend still counts on Monday.
