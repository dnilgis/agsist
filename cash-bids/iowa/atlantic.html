Bids come from each elevator’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open. An elevator whose board names a site rather than a town is listed here when its ZIP belongs to Atlantic; its card shows the board’s own name and the ZIP.
