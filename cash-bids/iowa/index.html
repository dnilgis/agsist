Sources and terms

Bids come from each elevator’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions. Specialty bids (white, organic, non-GMO, high-oleic, identity-preserved) are left out of the basis, and a middle number shows only when enough elevators post the same futures month. Change in a week compares each bid with the same elevator’s same delivery and futures month a week earlier. Prices move through the day; always call the elevator to confirm before you haul.
