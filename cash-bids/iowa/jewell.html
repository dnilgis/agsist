Sources and terms

Bids come from each elevator’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day; the live page has the newest bids and nearby elevators. Always call the elevator to confirm before you haul. Basis is cash minus the futures contract named under it. A “Hold to” line names the later month that pays best once storage and interest are taken off, net of storage at 3.5¢ a month (default), interest at 7% a year (default), no shrink (default); put in your own costs on Store or sell.
