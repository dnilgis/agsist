Where these come from. The AGSIST elevator network reads each elevator’s posted bid board directly; no third-party bid feed is used for the bids on this page. A bid shows only if the board was confirmed on the latest read, the price was posted in the last 72 hours, and its delivery period has not ended. This page is a snapshot and is rebuilt through the day; the live page has the newest board and nearby elevators. Basis is cash minus the futures contract named under it. A “Hold to” line is the elevator’s best later posted bid minus its nearest one, net of storage at 3.5¢ a month (default), interest at 7% a year (default), no shrink (default); put in your own costs on Store or sell. “Basis since June” reads AGSIST’s daily basis log, which keeps the day each elevator’s basis changed; until Oct 11, 2026 that log also drew on a licensed bid feed.
