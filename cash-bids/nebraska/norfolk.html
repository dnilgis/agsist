Bids come from each elevator’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day; the live page has the newest bids and nearby elevators. Always call the elevator to confirm before you haul. Basis is cash minus the futures contract named under it. An elevator whose board names a site rather than a town is listed here when its ZIP belongs to Norfolk; its card shows the board’s own name and the ZIP.
