Sources and terms

Bids come from each elevator’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day; the live page has the newest bids and nearby elevators. Always call the elevator to confirm before you haul. Basis is cash minus the futures contract named under it. An elevator whose board names a site rather than a town is listed here when its ZIP belongs to Farmer City; its card shows the board’s own name and the ZIP. A “Hold to” line names the later month that pays best once storage and interest are taken off, net of storage at 3.5¢ a month (default), interest at 7% a year (default), no shrink (default); put in your own costs on Store or sell. The “Basis since” box shows the day each elevator’s basis last changed; before Oct 11, 2026 it also drew on a licensed bid feed.
