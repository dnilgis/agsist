Sources and terms

Bids come from each elevator’s own posted bid board, read in the last 6 hours, with the price posted within the last two trading sessions and the delivery period still open. This page is a snapshot rebuilt through the day; the live page has the newest bids and nearby elevators. Always call the elevator to confirm before you haul.
