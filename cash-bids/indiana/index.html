How these numbers are made. Source: the AGSIST elevator network (dnilgis/bids), boards read directly; no third-party bid feed is used on this page. A bid counts only if its board was confirmed live on the latest read and its price was posted within 72 hours, long enough that a board unchanged over a weekend still counts on Monday.
