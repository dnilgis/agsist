Test strip

Print this to try the reader before the AGSIST gauge exists. It fits any 10:1 tube that reads 0.00 to 1.00 in over 10 inches, like the inner tube of a CoCoRaHS 4-inch gauge.

AGSIST rain gauge test strip. Print at 100% (Actual size), not Fit to page.

Check this line with a ruler. It should be exactly 1 inch. If not, print again at 100%.
  1. Cut out both pieces on the gray outline. Lay piece B over the top of piece A so the tick marks line up, and tape or glue them.
  2. Tape the strip flat behind the tube, blue stripe centered behind it. Line up the strip's 0.00 line with the tube's 0.00 mark.
  3. Keep all four black squares in view beside the tube. Don't cover or trim them.

How it reads The four corner squares fix the strip's position, so the photo can be straightened. Water in the tube magnifies the blue stripe, and the stripe pinches to a point at the surface. The reader finds that point, takes inches of water in the tube and divides by 10. On 38 made-up test photos it read within 0.01 in or said it couldn't read. It hasn't been tried on real photos yet, so check every reading before you log it.

Your data The photo is read on this phone and isn't uploaded. The log is kept in this browser only. Download the CSV to keep a copy.
