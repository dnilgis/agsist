Sources and terms

Every state

A sale is listed when it carries a price and acres and a whole interest changed hands in a normal sale: gifts, family and related-party sales, quit claims, foreclosures and the like are left out, the same rule as Field Scout and the Farmland Atlas. Township and town are where the state places the sale; no parcel is pinned. None of this is an appraisal.

Unusual prices

A sale of 20 acres or more is marked unusual when its price per acre is far above or below the usual for its county, which is the middle half of that county's sales of that size over the two years from Oct 1, 2024 to Sep 30, 2026. Possible reasons above the usual: a house or buildings, timber or hunting ground, a building site or development land, or a neighbor paying more for the piece next door. Possible reasons below: a family or neighbor sale, a part interest, wooded, wet or poorer ground, no road access, or a land contract. The return does not say which, and a price is not wrong because it is unusual.

Best value per acre

Best value lists sales of 40 acres or more with the lowest price per acre against their county's usual, leaving out the unusual prices and anything well under the usual range. The record has no soil, tillable acres, buildings or drainage, so a low price can just mean poorer ground.

Dates

* Indiana sales are dated by the deed for now, so a sale signed late in the month may still be filed after this was read and show up next month.

Names

Buyer and seller names are public record on the state's transfer return. They load only when you tap, and are not shown to search engines. If one is yours and you want it off this site, tap Remove my name beside it. New Jersey and Florida sales carry no buyer or seller names in the state's file; the deed at the county has them.

Illinois

Illinois Department of Revenue, MyDec transfer declarations (PTAX-203), farm parcels, read Nov 3, 2026. A farm sale is a farm parcel on a MyDec transfer declaration. Left out: gifts, family and related-party sales and anything less than the whole interest.

Wisconsin

Wisconsin Department of Revenue, electronic real estate transfer returns, farm and forest classes, read Oct 18, 2026. A farm sale is a transfer return for land in the farm or forest classes. Left out: gifts, family and related-party sales and anything less than the whole interest.

Indiana

Indiana Department of Local Government Finance, sales disclosure forms (from STATS Indiana), farm parcels, read Nov 3, 2026. A farm sale is a sale of land the county classes as farm. Left out: gifts, family and related-party sales and anything less than the whole interest. The date is the sale date on the disclosure form, and the town is the parcel's mailing town, placed at the middle of its ZIP code.

Vermont

Vermont Department of Taxes, property transfer tax returns (published by VCGI), farm, forest and current use land, read Nov 3, 2026. A farm sale is a return where the land's use before or after the sale is an operating farm or timberland, or open land enrolled in current use. Left out: a buyer related to the seller, a return that claims a transfer tax exemption, transfers without pay, by divorce or by foreclosure, and part interests such as a share, a life estate, a lease or an easement.

New Jersey

New Jersey Division of Taxation, SR-1A deed abstracts with acres from the MOD-IV 2026 assessment list, farm lots (classes 3A and 3B), read Oct 29, 2026. A farm sale is a deed whose lot is classed farm, either the farmhouse lot or farmland-assessed land; acres are today's lot on the 2026 assessment list, which may not be the lot as it sold, and a deed whose lot is not on the list has no acres and is kept out of the middle. Left out: gifts and family sales, sales between related companies, part interests, tax liens, sheriff, bankruptcy and short sales, quit claims, government and charity transfers, deeds tied to a foreclosure, exchanges, token-price sales and sales that were not between a willing buyer and seller. The state's file names no buyer or seller.

Florida

Florida Department of Revenue, sale data files joined to the assessment roll, agricultural land (sale months, not days), read Nov 13, 2026. A farm sale is land the assessment roll codes agricultural, timberland included. Left out: any deed the county property appraiser did not mark as a qualified sale. The files give the month of each sale, not the day, and name no buyer or seller.
