Above the usual, possible reasons: a house or buildings, timber or hunting ground, a building site or development land, or a neighbor paying more for the piece next door. Below, possible reasons: a family or neighbor sale, a part interest, wooded, wet or poorer ground, no road access, or a land contract. The return does not say which, and a price is not wrong because it is unusual.
