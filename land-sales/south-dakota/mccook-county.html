Sources and terms

Auction results

An auction result is what an auction company reported for a sale it ran: the auction day, the acres offered, the total sold and, for each tract, the price per acre the company printed. It is not a state transfer return and carries no recording, township, buyer or seller. Only auctions the company reported as sold are shown; whether each sale later closed is not in the company's report. Auction results sit beside the state's sales and are marked apart; where the state has transfer returns they stay out of the market middle. Where it has none, the middle comes from the per-acre figures the companies printed and says so (5 figures from 3 auctions of 20 acres or more, at least). Reported by Wieman Land & Auction.
