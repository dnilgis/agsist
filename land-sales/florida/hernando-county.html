Type: with buildings means the property class on the record shows a house, farm buildings or a business; forest means forest classes with no farm class (Wisconsin), timberland with no operating farm (Vermont) or timberland use codes (Florida); a small parcel is under 20 acres; farmland is the rest. Acres not stated: a return covering several parcels that does not give them.

A sale marked unusual is bare farmland whose price per acre sits well outside the market middle half; possible reasons above it: a house or buildings, timber or hunting ground, a building site or development land, or a neighbor paying more for the piece next door; below: a family or neighbor sale, a part interest, wooded, wet or poorer ground, no road access, or a land contract.

Sources and terms

Every state

A sale is listed when it carries a price and acres and a whole interest changed hands in a normal sale: gifts, family and related-party sales, quit claims, foreclosures and the like are left out, the same rule as Field Scout and the Farmland Atlas. Township and town are where the state places the sale; no parcel is pinned. None of this is an appraisal.

Names

Florida sales carry no buyer or seller names in the state's file; the deed at the county has them.

Florida

Florida Department of Revenue, sale data files joined to the assessment roll, agricultural land (sale months, not days), read Nov 13, 2026. A farm sale is land the assessment roll codes agricultural, timberland included. Left out: any deed the county property appraiser did not mark as a qualified sale. The files give the month of each sale, not the day, and name no buyer or seller.
