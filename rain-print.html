Rain Print

A year of rain on the farm, printed for the wall

Farmers remember years by the rain. Pick the one they still talk about, and this draws every rain of it at the NOAA gauge nearest the farm, with the farm's name and up to three dates on it. Drawing one is free. A print file is $19; a 12 x 18 print is $45, or $89 framed.

For Christmas, pick 2026. You print a gift card for under the tree now, and the full-year print comes in January.

The year in numbers

Which year to pick

If it's a gift for a farmer, for Christmas, a retirement or an anniversary, the year usually picks itself.

  • The drought, when the gauge sat empty for weeks
  • The flood, or the spring you couldn't get in the field
  • The first crop on new ground
  • The year the farm changed hands
  • A mom's or dad's last crop
  • The year you got married
  • The last year before retiring

How ordering works

  1. Draw it here. Type the town nearest the farm, pick the year, and add a name and dates if you want them. Drawing is free.
  2. Sig emails you a proof. Within a day you get a proof and a payment link. Nothing is charged until you pay it, and nothing is printed until you OK the proof.
  3. He prints it and ships it. Printed on his own Epson, framed if you ordered the frame, and shipped from Wisconsin.

Questions

Where does the rain data come from?

From the NOAA weather station nearest the town you type that has a record for the whole year. The print names the station and how far it is from town. It's that station's gauge, so a storm that hit the farm and missed the station won't show. If there's a station closer to the farm, pick it under Weather station.

What if the station missed days?

The page tries the three nearest stations and takes the first with 10 or fewer missing days. Any missing days are counted on the print. A station missing more than 31 days in the year gets no print, because the gaps would look like dry weeks that may not have been dry.

Can I give this year's print for Christmas?

Yes. Pick this year in the Year box and order at the usual price. Then print a gift card for under the tree, with their name, your message and a preview of the year so far. Sig makes the full-year print once NOAA's December numbers are in and sends it in January, with a proof to you first.

When will it arrive?

Prints and framed prints ship within 7 business days after you approve the proof, to US addresses only. To have it for Christmas, order by the date on the price card. The digital file comes by email.

What is the red square in the corner?

Sig's maker's mark. It's also a working QR code. Scan it with a phone and this page opens the same design, with the place, year, colors, name and dates.
