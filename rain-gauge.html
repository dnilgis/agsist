Available now

Rain Print

One year of rain at the NOAA station nearest you, drawn day by day on a 12 x 18 print, printed and framed for the wall. Type your town and a year: a drought year, the wet spring, the year you bought the place. Drawing it is free. You pay only if you want it printed.

  • Every rain and snow of the year, from NOAA’s daily records, next to what a usual year brings.
  • Your name and dates on it. You see a proof before I print.
  • Printed, framed and shipped by me from Chetek. Every print is numbered, and I initial each one by hand.

Digital file, print or framed print. Shipping included in the 50 states.

The AGSIST rain gauge

A gauge you put on a post out in the field and leave there. It keeps the record so you don’t have to.

  • It reports on its own. You don’t drive out to read it after every rain.
  • Built to run through a Wisconsin winter on batteries. The first winter is part of the test.
  • Every tip is kept with its time, so you see how hard it rained, and when it started and quit, not just the total.
  • No console, no app, no subscription. Nothing to install, nothing to pay every month.
  • Your rain stays yours. Nobody sees it unless you share it.

Questions

What won’t the gauge do?

It is not an 8-inch National Weather Service gauge, and I won’t call it one. It measures rain. Snow is not measured as snow, so keep your snow board or ruler for that. Like any gauge, it reads best out in the open, away from trees and buildings.

When can I get one?

Before planting 2027. I’m getting manufacturing going this winter. That’s my plan, not a promise of a ship date. People on the waitlist hear first.

What will it cost?

I haven’t set a price yet. The first batch will be priced barely over what it costs me to make them, as a thank-you to the people who try it with me early. I’ll set the price before orders open. Everyone on the waitlist gets that first-batch price, even if the first batch runs out before your turn.

Who makes it?

I do, in Chetek, Wisconsin. I’m a crop adviser and I run AGSIST. It’s designed, built, sold and shipped from here. If you write to me about yours, I’m the one who writes back.

How accurate is it?

I’m not making any claim yet. It’s being tested next to a glass gauge now. Each month I’ll post how far apart they were and how often it missed, good or bad. The first comparison goes up once a full month is in.

Who can see my rain?

Only you, unless you share it. Your rain stays yours, and I don’t sell it.

What if it breaks?

As long as I’m alive and the business is running, if your gauge breaks in normal use, I’ll fix it or replace it. Send me a photo and tell me what happened. Write to me at sig@farmers1st.com.

Will it work with Rain Print?

That’s the plan. Next year, your own gauge can draw your Rain Print instead of the nearest NOAA station. Nothing is built for that yet.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You’ll hear from me when the first batch is ready, with the first-batch price, before anyone else. Only rain gauge news goes to that address. It does not sign you up for AGSIST Daily.
