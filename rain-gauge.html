What it is

This is the prototype I’ve designed. The printed parts are charcoal.

  • A clear tube on the outside. It’s a store-bought clear tube, so you see the water without opening anything.
  • A white scale behind it. A printed back holds the white scale. You read it through the clear tube, with big numbers on both sides.
  • A funnel on top, the code on the collar. The top funnel is printed. The QR code sits on the collar just under it.
  • Lift it out to empty it. The tube lifts straight up out of its printed holder. Dump it and set it back in.

Wood post or T-post

It screws onto a wood post. On a steel T-post, two hose clamps hold it. Put it on the fence line you already drive past.

Like any rain gauge, it reads best out in the open, away from trees and buildings.

How it would work

It works like any good rain gauge. Walk out after a rain, look at the scale through the tube, read it. The difference is what happens next.

  1. 1Scan the code

    Each gauge has its own code on the collar, so your phone knows which field it’s standing in. No app to install; it opens in the browser.

  2. 2Take a photo of the scale

    Take a photo through the clear tube. The phone shows you the number, like 0.42 in. If it looks off, nudge it up or down before you save.

  3. 3It lands in your rain log

    Every reading is kept by field, with the date and time. Your season total is always up to date.

  4. 4Share it if you want

    Send a view-only link to the landlord or the agronomist. They see only the fields you share with them.

The phone screens are concept screens. The numbers are made up.

Why I’m building it

  • No notebook. The reading goes from the gauge to the log in the same minute you read it.
  • No forgetting. You don’t have to remember what it said by the time you get back to the house.
  • A record for every field. One gauge per field means North 80 and the home place each get their own season total, not one number for the whole farm.
  • It’s still a plain gauge. No battery, no phone needed. If you never scan it, you read it with your eyes and empty it, like you always have.

Who it’s for

  • Farmers who want rain by field, not one number from a station miles away.
  • Landlords and tenants who settle up on what actually fell.
  • Agronomists and crop consultants who keep notes on a lot of fields.
  • Anyone who reads a gauge every morning and is tired of writing it down.

Does it need a battery?

No. There is nothing in the gauge to charge or replace. The code on the collar is printed, and your phone does the rest.

Does it work without a phone?

Yes. It’s a rain gauge first. You can read it by eye and empty it like any other gauge. The phone part is only there if you want the log.

How do I empty it?

Lift the clear tube straight up out of its holder, dump it, and set it back in.

What does it mount on?

A wood post or a steel T-post. It screws onto a wood post. On a T-post, two hose clamps hold it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Scanning the code opens a page in your phone’s web browser. There’s nothing to download.
