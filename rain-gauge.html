A better rain gauge, in the works

I am designing a rain gauge for the farm. It is not for sale yet, and there is no date or price. If you want to hear when there is something real, leave your email below.

The idea: it reads like any good rain gauge, so you can walk out and look at it the way you always have. It also carries a QR code. Scan it, take a picture of the gauge with your phone, and the reading is logged for that gauge, with no typing. No phone, no problem: it still works as a plain gauge.

Nothing has been built or tested in rain yet. The first ones get set next to a standard gauge for a season before I would sell one.

Designed, made, sold and shipped from Chetek, Wisconsin.
