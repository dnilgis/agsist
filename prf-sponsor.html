Sponsor PRF in your state

AGSIST has a free PRF calculator and a page for every state and county where USDA offers Pasture, Rangeland, Forage insurance. Ranchers use them to price coverage before the Dec 1 sales closing. One business per state can put its name on those pages.

See your block and ask or email Sig: sig@agsist.com

Who it is for

Businesses that sell to people running cattle on grass or putting up hay:

  • Crop insurance agencies
  • Ag lenders
  • Ranch supply
  • Cattle buyers and sale barns
  • Seed dealers
  • Fencing dealers

What a sponsor gets

  • The state PRF page. Your block near the top of that state’s page, under the opening answer, on the page with every county’s USDA base values and the key dates.
  • Every county page in the state. 3,070 county pages across 48 states. A smaller version of your block near the top of each one in your state.
  • The calculator result. Your block beside the result on /prf whenever a rancher’s grid is in your state.
  • Your name, logo, one line, phone and a link, labeled Sponsor.
  • A word for you from AGSIST. Each block tells readers that AGSIST is free because businesses like yours pay for it, and asks them to tell you they saw you there.

The figures stay USDA’s

  • Every figure on the PRF pages comes from USDA RMA’s own rates, county base values and PRF index record.
  • The pages stay neutral: no lead forms, no “best” months, no routing to an agent.
  • A sponsor has no say over any figure. Every block is labeled Sponsor, and its link is marked as sponsored.
  • The PRF pages tell readers that a sponsor may sell crop insurance.

The terms

  • One business per state. Take as many states as you want.
  • Prepaid. The placement is guaranteed through Dec 31, and you get the first say on renewing for the next year.
  • Price on request. It depends on the state. Sig replies with a price for the states you ask about.
  • Live once paid. Sig puts the block up himself and it shows on every page in the state at once. He can turn a block down.
  • Traffic on request. The PRF pages are new this fall, so ask for the latest figures for your state.
  • The advertising rules set by your company and your state apply to your block.

Questions

What does it cost to sponsor PRF in a state?

The price is on request, and it depends on the state. Send the form on this page or email Sig, and he replies with a price for the states you ask about.

How long does a PRF sponsorship run?

It is prepaid, and the placement is guaranteed through December 31. You get the first say on renewing for the next year. If you do not renew, your block comes down after December 31.

Can one business sponsor more than one state?

Yes. Each state has one sponsor, and one business can take as many states as it wants.

Does a sponsor have any say over the PRF figures?

No. The figures come from USDA RMA's rates, county base values and PRF index record. A sponsor has no say over any figure, and every block is labeled Sponsor.

Can a crop insurance agency sponsor PRF?

Yes. The PRF pages tell readers that a sponsor may sell crop insurance. The advertising rules set by your company and your state apply to your block.
