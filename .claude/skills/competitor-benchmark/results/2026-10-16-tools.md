## Where we win
- **Breadth with live data behind one free page.** Cash bids with their age, ARC/PLC with FSA benchmarks, PRF back to 1951, LRP, sale barn and hay prices, a hail map. farmdoc's list is mostly downloadable Excel files and databases; UNL's is Nebraska-focused.
- **First phone screen is now the money tools,** with no login and no ads.

## Where they beat us, and how
- **UNL CropWatch** puts a full sentence or paragraph under every tool, even in the saved text: "Users input the bin diameter and grain depth, and the tool calculates the volume in cubic feet and bushels." Our phone cards drop the description entirely.
- **farmdoc** dates every tool: "Last Updated: March 29, 2026", "Last Updated: March 1, 2024". A farmer can see what is fresh. Our cards carry no date, even for data tools that refresh daily.
- **UNL** has an irrigation group (ET/GDD tool, CornSoyWater, irrigation meter calculator, Irricost) and a nitrogen tool that gives "product needed per acre, total product needed for the field and product costs". We have nothing for irrigators.

Best on the internet today? **No.** The hub now opens with the right tools, but on a phone the cards still have no descriptions, and UNL describes every tool better. Changes 1 and 2 are small and add no page weight.
