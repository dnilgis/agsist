## Bugs found
1. **Medium: "Best corn" can name the wrong elevator after an overnight read.**
   - What happens: a day-old board on a DTN feed cannot be re-marked to today's futures, so it drops below every re-marked board even when it pays more.
   - Cause: cb-rank.js contractKey() reads b.symbol first. For DTN boards that symbol is a DTN ticker ("@C6Z"), and neither of its regexes matches one:
     - `contractKey({category:'corn',symbol:'@C6Z',basisMonth:'Dec 2026'})` returns "".
     - netContract() in cash-bids.js already understands the @ form; contractKey does not.
   - Seen at ZIP 54728:
     - Rooney Grain, 25 mi, Oct 2026 corn at -62¢, re-marks to ≈$4.12.
     - It was ranked below River Country Boyd, 40 mi, at ≈$4.10, which got the BEST CORN card.
   - Scope: 178 of 2,038 network shards carry "@" tickers.
   - When it bites: only when the board's latest price predates today, Central time. That is overnight and early morning, or a board that was not reached today.
2. **Low: raw delivery codes.**
   - Bloomer's page prints "HAR 26" and "HAR 27" for River Country Co-op.
   - The ZIP search shows the same board's HAR 26 as "Fall 2026" but leaves HAR 27 as "HAR 27".
3. **Low: two meanings of red on one page.**
   - The basis key under the results says green and red are relative to the middle of the search.
   - The three BEST cards color basis by its sign (cash-bids.js line 1320 uses formatBasis().cls), so every negative basis is red. On the 54728 search the best corn, soybean and wheat basis all show red.
4. **Fixed since Oct 15:**
   - The delivery cell no longer wraps one letter per line on desktop.
   - The basis color has a key.
   - Town pages now lead with the answer and carry FAQPage JSON-LD.
   - The meta description has the price in it.
   - Town pages have "Basis since June".

1. **Read more boards where farmers are (L, dnilgis/bids).**
   - Why: coverage is the one score holding this page at "close", and it fell 193 boards since Oct 15. Rank the 6,336 known-not-read elevators by how many town pages and ZIPs have no read board within 25 mi. Build platform readers first.
   - Honesty risk: none, as long as counts stay from data/elevator-counts.json.
2. **Make contractKey() read DTN tickers (S).**
   - What: map "@C6Z" to ZCZ26, "@S6X" to ZSX26, and "@W6Z" or "@KW6Z" to ZWZ26 or KEZ26. Add a cb-rank test with Rooney's row.
   - Why: the BEST card is the page's headline. It must not pass over a higher bid because of how the board spells its contract.
   - Honesty risk: none; it restores the stated ranking rule.
3. **One delivery label from one place (S).**
   - What: turn "HAR 26" and "HAR 27" into "Harvest 2026" and "Harvest 2027" everywhere. The ZIP search and the town pages should use the same function.

4. **Color the BEST card basis by the same rule as the key (S).**
   - What: use the relative-to-middle tone the list uses, or plain gray.
   - Why: three red "best" prices next to a key that says red is weak tells the farmer the wrong thing.
