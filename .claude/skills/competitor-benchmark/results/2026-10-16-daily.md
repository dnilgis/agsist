## Where we win
- **Every issue has a named, credentialed author and a reply address.** There is no paywall, no partner email, and no sign-in needed to read it.
- **The harvest price clock,** set against RMA's own running figure, every morning in October. Pro Farmer's free index has nothing like it.
- **Prices labeled honestly:** "Yahoo Finance, delayed, not CME settlements", with the quote time.
- **Short.** A lead, three sections, a watch list and one policy item.
- **Sources named on the issue page:** "AgWeb, feedstuffs.com, beefmagazine.com, oilprice.com".

## Where they beat us
- **Same-day report reaction (Pro Farmer).** "Report Snapshot" posts land within about an hour of a WASDE or Grain Stocks release, with the number against the trade. Our briefing comes once, at 5:47 am. The What's priced in page grades the report, but the briefing page does not post it the same day.
- **Breadth of topics (Pro Farmer).** It covers weather, cotton quality, land values and cattle supply, each with its own story. Our one issue carries 3 to 4 topics.

## Five changes, ranked
1. **Consistency check across lead, sections, watch list and the homepage teaser (S).** Use the generator's critic pass. Fail the build when the watch list describes a move the lead contradicts (selling vs short-covering), or when a trigger price is within a tick of the current price. Honesty risk: none.
2. **Date both harvest-price figures (S).** Write "Ours, through Oct 15: $4.92. RMA, through Oct 14: $4.93." The data is already there (series length, and RMA's figure equals our 10-day average). Honesty risk: none.
3. **Report-day afternoon line on the briefing page (M).** On WASDE, Crop Production and Grain Stocks days, post one dated paragraph after the release: the headline numbers against the trade and the What's priced in grade. Post it on the page only, with no extra email. Honesty risk: medium. Use USDA's numbers and the stated trade-average source only, with no forecast of price.
4. **Local bid line for readers with a ZIP (M).** "Your nearest bid: $X, basis Y, read at Z." This would be on the page from the saved ZIP, and in the email for subscribers who gave one. Honesty risk: low. Show the read time and the elevator name, and print nothing if the bid is older than a day.
5. **One "number check" footer (S).** Under the price strip, link each figure to its source row (the settle series, the RMA mirror), so a reader can recheck the clock themselves. Honesty risk: none.

Copy consistency and the undated RMA figure keep it from "yes". Pro Farmer posts report reaction the same day, and we do not.
