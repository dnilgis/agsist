## Where we win
- **We are the only one current past August.** Every readable copy stops at the Aug 6 markup or earlier. We carry:
  - the Sep 16 committee vote
  - the Sep 30 lapse
  - what stopped and what did not on Oct 1
  - the Jan 1, 2027 permanent-law date
  - a dated next-90-days strip
- **The answer comes before the politics.** The first screen says "What it changes for a corn and soybean farm this year: not much", gives the reference prices and names what is stuck. NALC, AFBF and NSAC open with process.
- **A per-operation answer.** Corn and soy, wheat, dairy (DMC dates), cattle and hogs (Prop 12), CRP and specialty crops each get 3 to 5 sourced lines. Nobody in the set does this.
- **Neutral, with a record of its own mistakes.** AFBF and NSAC each argue a side; NSAC tags each amendment "NSAC supported". We take no side, and the update log keeps our wrong calls.

## Where they beat us
- **NSAC: the vote record.** NSAC lists each Aug 6 amendment with its sponsor and outcome. Examples: Bennet #6 to undo the base bill's EQIP and CSP cut failed on party lines; Thune #1 on COOL passed 17-6; Welch #17 on actively engaged rules failed. It also explains the proxy rule: a proxy cannot be the deciding vote, which is why McConnell's absence sank Aug 6. We say "failed along party lines" without the mechanism or the amendments.
- **AFBF: Senate title by title, in one list.** About 45 one-line provisions, from the CRP rental-rate reform to a crop input economist position at USDA. Our title section sits behind a button and leans on the House CBO score.
- **NALC: House specifics we skip.** These include:
  - precision agriculture made EQIP-eligible
  - "a decline in the market price of the insured commodity" added as an insurable cause of loss in the Federal Crop Insurance Act
  - the foreign-adversary farmland purchase ban amendment
  - the exact House loan-limit figures
- **Everyone is shorter.** NALC's whole overview is about 4,000 words of plain text; our phone page is 17,900 px.
