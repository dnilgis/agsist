## 1. Is anyone doing the same thing?

**No one found sells what Rain Print sells:** one chosen station's daily rain and snow for a chosen US place and year,
every day drawn and labeled, with names and dates, printed and framed by a person. An AI search summary for
"personalized rainfall poster year town print" ended with "I did not find a service that offers this directly", and
the same happened for "gift for farmer rainfall data print" and "rainfall poster custom town year gift farmer".

AgDaily, HPJ) own "gift for farmer".

## 4. The 5 changes, ranked

1. **Put the search words on the page (S).**
   - Title: `Rain Print: a custom rainfall poster of any US town and year | AGSIST`
   - Description: `Every rain and snow of one year at the station nearest a farm or hometown, on a 12 x 18 print with a name and dates. Free to draw; $45 print, $89 framed.`
   - og/twitter title: `Rain Print: a year of rain and snow at the farm, printed and framed`
   - H1 stays Sig's voice, add the words: `A year of rain and snow on the farm, or back home, printed for the wall`
   - Lead line add: `A gift for a farmer, or for the year you got married or a child was born.`
2. **Show a real finished print without waiting on NOAA (M).** Ship 3 to 4 static images made from real data (the
   2012 Ames drought and 1993 Des Moines flood already offered as examples): Staircase framed, Roots framed, a close-up
   of labeled days and snow, the shop-wall photo. Put the first one in the hero as a real `<img>` (eager, fetchpriority
   high, width/height set) that the live draw replaces. Alt text, e.g.
   `Framed 12 x 18 Rain Print of 2012 at Ames, Iowa: daily rain from NOAA station records stepping up through the drought year, with a name and three dates`
   and `Close-up of a Rain Print: labeled rain days and snow drawn above the line`. List them all in Product.image.
   Images live in /img, one set, so the page budget is not touched. This also replaces the red error with a picture
   when ACIS is down.
3. **Finish the Product schema honestly (S).** On each Offer: `"availability": "https://schema.org/MadeToOrder"`,
   `"sku"` (rp-file, rp-print, rp-framed), `"seller": {"@type":"Person","name":"Sigurd Lindquist"}`, `shippingDetails`
   (US, $0 rate, handling 0 to 3 business days after proof OK, all 50 states) on the two physical offers,
   `hasMerchantReturnPolicy` stating only what Sig has decided (reprint if wrong; $15 reprint if lost or damaged;
   needs Sig's word on refunds first, and the Terms page has no Rain Print section yet). No Review or AggregateRating
   until real buyers leave reviews; then use their own words with permission.
4. **Add 4 FAQ items that match what buyers type (S), visible and in FAQPage:**
   - `Can I make one for our wedding year or the year a child was born?` Yes; any year the station has, with up to
     three dates labeled on the print.
   - `Does it show snow?` Yes; snow days draw above the line so you can tell them from rain at a glance, where the
     station kept snowfall.
   - `How much rain did we get that year? Do I have to buy to see it?` Drawing is free and shows the year's total
     against a usual year; link "rainfall by county" to /rain.
   - `Is it a good gift for a farmer who has everything?` Plain answer in Sig's voice: it is the one year they still
     talk about, from the gauge down the road, framed and ready to hang; order-by dates for Christmas.
5. **Aim the links in and earn the first outside ones (M).**
   - County rain pages (2,754): keep the button, add one plain-text link to `/rain-print` (no query string) under it
     with the county in the words: `Get Faribault County's year of rain and snow as a framed rainfall print`.
     Generated template change only a few hundred bytes a page, so under the 150 KB cap (check_site_budget.py).
   - /gifts-for-farmers: anchor `a custom rainfall print of the year they still talk about`; /rain and /hail-map
     footers: `rainfall poster of any year`.
   - Outreach, honest pitch with a free print of the editor's own hometown year, no paid placement: Farm Progress
     ("18 things to buy a farmer for Christmas" is refreshed yearly), AgDaily gift guide, High Plains Journal farm and
     ranch gift guide, Successful Farming, Audrey's Little Farm, Wisconsin Farm Bureau and other state Farm Bureau
     magazines, CoCoRaHS (prints name volunteer gauges; their newsletter features observers), state climatology
     offices, and the Barron News-Shield / local papers' Christmas shopping pages. Deadline matters: guides lock in
     early to mid November.

Not proposed: an Etsy or Amazon listing (Sig's no-store-names rule on the page does not forbid selling there, but it
is his call; it would put Rain Print where Weathered Moments already sells), separate wedding/birth landing pages
(one page with the words is enough for now and keeps the budget clean).

## Verdict

Best on the internet today? **Yes for the product, no for being found:** nobody else sells a real station's year of
rain and snow by day, named and framed, but the page is not indexed yet, its title misses every word buyers type, and
its first screen shows no print to a crawler.
