  - 181.2 - 173.2 = **8.0**.
  - Past low misses are 9.5 (2017), 5.3, 5.2, 4.7, 4.4, 4.1 and 3.8, so 8.0 would be **second-biggest**. Match.
  - 181.2 matches the NASS Oct 9 briefing copy.
- **Corn record, against January finals:**
  - Absolute misses sum to 45.2 / 11 = **4.1**.
  - USDA August misses sum to 34.1 / 11 = **3.1**.
  - USDA was closer in **7 of 11** years.
  - Match.
- **Soybean record (production, billion bu):**
  - Tour mean absolute miss is 1.440 / 11 = **0.131**, shown as 0.13. USDA August is 1.370 / 11 = **0.125**, shown as 0.12. Match.
  - Unrounded, the tour was closer 5 times and USDA 5 times, with 2023 drawn (0.05 each). Under the final in **6** years, with 2021 (-0.004) counted as "landed on the final". Match.
- **Against the revised finals:** the NASS Oct 9 briefing chart labels show revised finals for 2019-2022: 167.5, 171.4, 176.7 and 173.4. The page uses the January finals (168.0, 172.0, 177.0, 173.3) and says so under "What counts as the final".
  - Redone on the revised finals, the tour's average miss is 45.7 / 11 = 4.2 and USDA August's is 34.8 / 11 = 3.2. USDA is still closer in 7 of 11. The conclusion holds.
- **No calculation errors.**
