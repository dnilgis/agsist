## Where we win
- **We turn the quote into a farmer's answer.** Each futures page shows where price sits in its 52-week range (43% for Dec corn), a 5-year percentile, the corn/bean ratio on new-crop months (2.44), the RP projected price against the board, fund positioning as a percentile since 2010, carry net of storage and interest, and an entry for your own cost of production. None of the quote sites puts that together.
- **Carry in bushel terms.** "Hold to Jul '27? +27¢ futures carry, net of storage and interest: −17¢" is the store-or-sell math in one line. Barchart and CME show the curve but not what it nets.
- **Crush and class spreads with the method shown.** Soy gross crush is $2.62, the KC-Chicago spread is +41¾¢ and the wheat-corn feed spread is $1.89. Each has a Method line, and every band is labeled an AGSIST rule of thumb.
- **Cattle cost-of-gain calculator** is pre-filled from the board and matches the sale month to days on feed.
- **Clean and fast.** There are no ads. The TradingView chart loads on tap, and the change line carries its own close date.

## Where competitors beat us, and how
- **Freshness signals.** Barchart, CME and Investing.com show intraday quotes and an official settle. Ours is Yahoo last trade, stated honestly. But the titles and meta descriptions were baked from the 8:31 a.m. CT Oct 14 quote and still stood 18 hours later. The soybean title reads "$12.88¼" and its description says "+0.35%", but the day closed $12.72¼, −0.90%. In search, our snippet shows a move the market did not end with.
- **Every contract, settled.** Barchart and CME list every month with settle, volume and open interest. We show six months on the forward curve, with no settlement label and no volume or OI.
- **Why it moved today.** DTN and Barchart publish a dated close story every day ("corn futures steady to 4 cents lower"). Our "What's moving it" box is rules logic: the ratio, the season, the funds. It never says what happened today, for example "Oct 9: USDA raised corn yield 2.7 bu to 181.2; Dec fell 20½¢."
- **Charts and options.** TradingView shows the forward curve, options chain and drawing tools. CME and Barchart publish options and implied volatility. Our chart is a TradingView CFD embed.
- **Local, in the app.** FBN shows local bids net of freight by target price. We have the nearest bid by ZIP (1,923 of 8,404 elevators read), which is good but thinner.
