## The 5 changes, ranked (farmers helped x gap / effort)
1. **One dated "what moved it" line per crop, from what the site already has on file (M).** The latest WASDE result (whats-priced-in data), the latest Crop Progress harvest pace, and the COT week, each with date and source. Example: "Oct 9 WASDE: corn yield 181.2, up 2.7; Dec fell 20½¢ that day." When there is no sourced cause, write no line. This closes the main gap to DTN without a paywall. Honesty risk: medium; no cause without a source, never a guess about why today's tick moved.
2. **Harvest pace and in-season crop cards (S).** Corn and soybean Crop Progress cards lead with % harvested vs last year and the 5-year average in Sep to Nov. The wheat card shows winter wheat planted/emerged in the fall, or hides when the latest report is over 10 days old (F1). Honesty risk: low.
3. **One quote per contract across the site (S).** Make every card that names a contract read that contract's own key (ZWZ26, KEZ26, ZMZ26), not the continuous ticker, and figure spreads from the numbers shown (F2, F3). Fix F4 and F6 in the same pass. Honesty risk: none; it removes two prices for one thing.

A "Show all months" table rendered from prices.json, which already holds every month's close, change and 52-week range. Volume only if a free, licensed source is on file; otherwise leave it out and say so. Page weight does not grow. Honesty risk: low; label it "delayed, not CME settlement" as now.
5. **Give markets.html its own reason to visit (S).** Saved-ZIP nearest bid and basis in the Cash Bids card, contract months on every card, 52-week bars on every grain card, and one linked line per row from the futures page's read ("Corn: lower half of range, funds 81st pct") (F5). Honesty risk: none.
