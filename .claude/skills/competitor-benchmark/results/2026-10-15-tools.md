# Head to head: agsist.com/tools

Checked Oct 15, 2026, from the read-only checkout. I rendered tools.html at 390x844 and 1280x800. No sideways scroll, no JS errors, about 57-150 KB loaded. The page has 21 cards in 6 groups and then 23 links under "Everything else on the site". It computes nothing itself.

**What I could not see:** I could not open any competitor host (DNS refusal). Competitor cells are from search-result text and are marked *.

## Checks
- **No math on this page.** Card claims checked against the pages they link to: fertilizer prices are dated, PRF goes back to 1951, and the hail map covers five years. They match.
- **Labeling (low):**
  - "Cash Bids: Live local cash bid prices" overstates it. The homepage shows bids "7 hours old" and 1,923 of 8,404 elevators read directly.
  - "GDU Tracker: Wisconsin 30-year normals" tells a farmer outside Wisconsin it is not for them.
- **Order:** on a phone the first screen is Spray Timing, Urea Risk, Fertilizer and Manure. Cash bids, break-even and ARC/PLC are a screen or more down. Phone cards drop their one-line descriptions, so only the names and tags show.
- **Missing from the hub:** /store-or-sell ("Sell now or store?") has no card or link in the page body, only in the menu.

## Where we win
- **Breadth with live data behind it.** Few hubs put cash bids, ARC/PLC with FSA benchmarks, PRF history since 1951, a dated fertilizer sheet and a hail map behind one free page with no login. University hubs list one university's tools, often as spreadsheets or PDFs.
- **Clean and fast,** with no ads.

## Where a competitor beats us, and how
- **Purdue's apps\*** put the field calculators (seeding rate, lime, N-P-K) in an app that works in the field. Ours are spread over several pages.
- **University hubs\*** carry the land-grant name, which is a strong trust signal for a farmer choosing between two N calculators.
- **Our hub is a list, not an answer.** There is no "what do you need to do?" entry and no search on the page. On a phone it is about five screens of cards with no descriptions.

## Five changes, ranked by impact
1. **Put the most-used tools first, by site-usage.json (S).** Cash bids, break-even and ARC/PLC should come before urea risk. Order the groups by what farmers open.
2. **Keep a one-line description on phone cards (S).** Use one short line under each name. Today a farmer sees "Urea Volatilization Risk / NITROGEN MANAGEMENT" and has to guess.
3. **Add the missing pages and fix the labels (S).**
   - Add a "Sell now or store?" card.
   - Rename "Live local cash bid prices" to "Today's local cash bids, with the age of each".
   - Drop "Wisconsin" from the GDU card, or say which states it covers.
4. **A filter row at the top (M):** "Selling grain / Inputs / Field / Insurance and programs / Land". It works like the homepage "Sort the page", in plain JS with no page weight.
5. **"Works offline in the field" marks (L, later).** Mark the calculators that work with no signal, for example the yield estimator and the bin calculator, if they do. That takes on Purdue's app edge.

## Verdict
Best on the internet today? **No.** The tools behind it are strong, but the hub page is a long list in an order nobody chose, and on a phone the cards lose their descriptions. The fixes are small and cost no page weight.
