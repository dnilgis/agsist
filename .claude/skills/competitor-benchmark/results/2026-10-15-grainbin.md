## Where we win
- We are the only tool that goes from bin size to bushels to shrink, drying cost and net dollars on one screen, with a sticky total on the phone.
- It has the most bin shapes: round, hopper, rectangular, plus peaked, level and draw-cone surfaces and a partial-fill slider. The draw-cone partial fill uses the correct annular-wedge integral; I have not seen that on any competitor.
- The bin-sizing box, the common-sizes table, Copy, Print Record and editable shrink factor are all unique together.
- It uses the US bushel (1.244 ft3). Per its snippet, Omni uses the imperial bushel.

## Where competitors beat us, and how
- **Manufacturer basis.** GSI, Brock and Sukup all state that their capacities use a 28 deg angle of repose and 6% compaction (ASABE S413.1 / EP413). We use 25 deg and 0% pack. So our number runs **5 to 6.5% under the dealer chart** for the same bin, and the page never says why. A farmer checking a GSI 30' 9-ring (15,337 bu) gets 14,484 from us and concludes we are wrong.
- **Sourcing.** Brock and Sukup cite ASABE standards. UNL shows its constants. Our page has no source links: pack factor, moisture targets and the 1.3 shrink factor all go uncited (the audit flagged this too).
- **Trust signals.** UNL carries an extension byline. We have only a meta author tag, with no visible "Sigurd Lindquist, CCA", no "updated" date and no sources list.

## Math check (worked by hand, Python, and in the live page)

Volume: our 30x24 peaked = 14,957 and level = 13,633, which match the page and the formulas (pi r^2 h x 0.8036; cone h = r tan 25). UNL's constants are equivalent (0.628 = pi/4 x 0.8). The volume geometry itself is correct.

**Three real bins vs manufacturer tables** (grain wall height = eave minus a 12" plenum for GSI, minus 1" for Brock, per their footnotes):

| Bin | Mfr capacity | Ours as shipped (25 deg, 0 pack) | Ours if farmer types eave | 28 deg + 6% pack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSI 30' 9-ring, 24'2" eave | 15,337 | 14,484 (-5.6%) | 15,052 (-1.9%) | 15,550 (+1.4%) |
| GSI 36' 12-ring, 32'2" eave | 29,404 | 27,782 (-5.5%) | 28,600 (-2.7%) | 29,789 (+1.3%) |
| Brock 48' 8-ring, 21'6" eave | 39,053 | 36,568 (-6.4%) | 36,689 (-6.1%) | 39,569 (+1.3%) |
| (extra) Brock 30' 13-ring, 34'10" | 22,232 | 21,063 (-5.3%) | 21,111 (-5.0%) | 22,524 (+1.3%) |

All four imply the same 4.6% effective pack at 28 deg, so the charts are internally consistent. In practice, "28 deg + 5% pack" lands within 0.5% of every chart.

Effort S. This alone turns "your number is wrong" into "this page explains the dealer's number".
2. **Test weight and market bushels (M2):** add a test weight input, show both "volume bushels" and "market bushels at X lb", and run shrink and value on market bushels. Effort S-M.
3. **Sources block + visible byline/date:** add "Sigurd Lindquist, CCA · Updated Oct 2026" and a short Sources list: ASABE S413.1/EP413.2, D241.4, Bhadra et al. 2015, NCH-61 shrink, ISU A2-32 drying, SDSU/NDSU storage moisture, GSI/Brock/Sukup chart basis. Effort S. This fixes the audit note and lifts trust from 3 to 5.
4. **Sizing on real rings (M3):** add a ring-height choice (32" GSI/Brock, 44" Sukup), a peaked-and-packed option, and fix the 50,000 bu FAQ wording. Effort S.
5. **Shrink and drying honesty (M5, M6, M8):** split shrink into water + handling, charge drying per wet bushel, rename the row, and add a "carry to Sell now or store?" link that passes bushels and net value so storage months live in one place (M11). Effort S-M.

None of this needs data files, and the page stays about 72 KB.

**Best on the internet today? Close.** It is the most complete free grain bin tool I found, and its geometry is right. But it reads 5 to 6% under every manufacturer chart without saying why, has no test weight, and cites nothing. Fixes 1 to 3 (all small) would make it clearly the best.
