## Where we win
- **The tour's track record against the final crop, year by year, against USDA's August number.** I found no other public page with it. The tour's own coverage does not grade itself.
- **Pod counts handled honestly** ("a direction, not a yield"), with a working paper on what they predict.
- **Not affiliated, and says so.** "Tour figures are theirs; the comparison and the arithmetic are ours."

## Where a competitor beats us, and how
- **Live during tour week (AgWeb\*).** Nightly video, scout notes and route-by-route stories. Ours posts the state figures but cannot match field color.
- **It goes quiet after August.** In October the hero still reads like an August page. A harvest-time reader wants "how is the tour's 173.2 holding up against harvest reports".

## Five changes, ranked by impact
1. **An October hero (S):** "Tour said 173.2 in August. USDA now 181.2. If USDA's October number holds, this would be the tour's Nth biggest low miss since 2015." Compute it from the existing table and label it as conditional until January.
2. **Fix the dateline and sources line (S).** Use "Record updated Oct 9 (USDA)" or the latest data date, and "Pro Farmer national estimates (2015-2025)".
3. **Add the soybean bushel record beside the corn one on the first screen (S).** It is in a fold now.
4. **Shorten the phone page (S).** Fold the per-night state cards into one table after tour week. 8,900 px is long.
5. **Add a state-level record (M):** each state's tour sample against NASS final state yield. Load from data on demand. It is the "local" question farmers ask ("is the tour low on my state every year?").

## Verdict
Best on the internet today? **Yes.** No other page I found grades the Pro Farmer tour against the final crop with USDA's August number beside it, and every figure I checked is right. It needs an October framing so it does not read as an August page.
