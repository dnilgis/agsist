## Where we win
- **Today's RMA offers turned into a cost for your head and weight,** with sales-close times and each step of the math. Every coverage level is available on demand. The answer gives the premium billing date and the subsidy share, cited to the handbook section. K-State quotes one rate ($9.95 a cwt, steers weight 2) from Feb 2024.
- **A tested record.** "Has it paid" gives what each premium dollar got back since the current subsidy began (July 2020), with every test on /lrp/record. I found no other free page that publishes this for LRP.
- **No agenda.** It sells nothing, routes to no agent, discloses the author's agency ownership and states that basis is not covered.

## Where they beat us
- **Put cost, side by side (K-State).** K-State puts a real put premium ($13.20 a cwt for a $270 October strike) next to the LRP cost ($9.95) for the same sale month. Our "LRP or a put?" box compares terms (subsidy, size, payment, records) but gives no put price.
- **Local adoption (K-State).** About 322,000 Kansas feeder head were covered in 2023, set against the calf crop. That is a "do people like me use it" figure we do not give.
- Otherwise none of the saved copies beat us. The RMA daily reports and the USU tool could not be read, so this verdict rests on thin competitor evidence.

## Five changes, ranked
1. **Put a dated example on the first screen (S).**
   - What: show feeder steers, 550 lb, the nearest end date, the 100% and 95% cost per cwt and per head, labeled "example, change it below". Keep the closed-offers notice.
   - Why: the first screen is a form, and the most common question ("LRP rates today") has an answer we already hold.
   - Honesty risk: low, if it is labeled as an example, with the offer date.
2. **Show the premium trend for the same end date and coverage (S-M).**
   - What: the last 10 sales days, from the daily files RMA already posts, so a buyer sees whether today's cost is up or down.
   - Honesty risk: low, if it is labeled as past cost, not a buy signal.
3. **Show your barn's basis against the index, from our sale-barn data (M-L, after the cattle-auction history change).**
   - What: the past spread between the nearest barn's class price and the CME Feeder Cattle Index on sale days, so the floor reads in barn dollars.
   - Why: LRP "does not cover basis", and nobody shows the farmer what basis has been at his barn.
   - Honesty risk: medium. It needs matched weight classes, head counts and a "past spread, not a forecast" label.
4. **Put a real put price next to LRP (M).**
   - What: show the CME settle for the put nearest the coverage price and end month, dated, per cwt and per head.
   - Why: it is the K-State comparison, kept current.
   - Honesty risk: medium. It needs a licensed or public settle source with a date. Do not show it if the data is missing.
5. **Compute beginning/veteran subsidy and insured share (S).**
   - What: one checkbox and one box. The page names both but does not compute them.
   - Why: beginning farmers get more subsidy and their real cost is lower than shown.
   - Honesty risk: low. Cite the RMA rate.

Also fix the "closed at" wording for intraday futures (S).
