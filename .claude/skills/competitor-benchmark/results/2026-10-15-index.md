## Where we win
- **One screen answers "what is my grain worth and what is moving it", free and with no login.** It has ZIP cash bids (20,106 bids from 1,746 locations, with age shown), Dec and Nov futures with time stamps, and the morning briefing line.
- **Things no farm media homepage I found carries:**
  - the insurance harvest price running against the projected price, settle by settle;
  - the October WASDE graded against the trade;
  - a ratings-based yield nowcast with its backtest error printed.
- **Honest data ages everywhere.** It shows "7 hours old" and "Delayed 15 min", and says "No quote on file, not shown" for MGEX instead of showing a stale number.
- **No ads, and no paywall in front of any number.**

## Where a competitor beats us, and how
- **Bid coverage and depth (DTN\*).** DTN says it calls 4,200 buyers a day. AGSIST reads 1,923 of 8,404 elevators directly. A farmer whose elevator is not in our feed falls back to DTN or the elevator's own site.
- **News breadth (DTN\*, Farm Progress\*, Successful Farming\*).** They run many stories a day. Our homepage has one briefing line. That is a deliberate choice, but "what happened in ag today" is still a top reason to open a farm homepage.
- **Weather (DTN\*).** DTN's weather is its core product. Ours needs a ZIP and an outside call, and the first-screen cell can sit empty.
- **Length.** The homepage runs 13 sections. The bottom half (positioning, land, tools, FAQ) repeats the menu, and on a phone that is about six screens.

## Five changes, ranked by impact
1. **Fix the trend label on the homepage nowcast card (S).** Use the trend window ("16-year trend, 2010-2025: 188.7"), or show USDA's 183.0 trend beside it as conditions-yield does. This one is a wrong label on the most-read page.
2. **Make the first-screen "Today" cell honest without a ZIP (S).** With no ZIP, show "Set ZIP for weather" (as Local Conditions does), not a skeleton and then "didn't load".
3. **Show where your elevator stands after a ZIP (M).** When the nearest elevator is not read directly, say so on the bid card and offer the elevator's own bid page link. This closes the coverage gap with DTN honestly instead of hiding it.
4. **Trim the bottom of the phone page (S).** Fold Land and More Tools and the FAQ into one "More" link, and keep the Price Alert collapsed. It would save one to two phone screens with no loss.
5. **One "today in ag" line set under the briefing teaser (M).** Use 3 headline links from data/news.json with source names, so the homepage answers "what happened" without becoming a news site.

## Verdict
Best on the internet today? **Close.** For a grain farmer, no free homepage I found puts local cash bids, dated futures, the harvest price clock, graded USDA reports and a backtested yield model on one screen without ads or a login. DTN still wins on bid coverage, weather and news volume. The trend label is the one wrong label on the page.
