## Where we win
- **An answer with no download.** Every competitor tool in this set is an Excel file (farmdoc, K-State, AFPC's ARC-IC aid, MSU) or sits behind a listing page. We give a verdict for each county, crop and practice in the HTML, with the expected dollars and the yield that triggers ARC.
- **2026 and 2027 together.** We give both 2027 ERP estimates and the math. farmdoc's Aug 5 article and K-State's Aug 19 sheet are about 2026.
- **Checks out by hand.** Every figure I recomputed matched, and the page says what it does not do.

## Where they beat us
- **ARC-IC (AFPC, farmdoc).** AFPC's listing: "2026 ARC-IC Decision Aid, Updated: Oct 2026 ... combines every FSA farm you have enrolled in ARC-IC in one state into a single ARC-IC farm, for up to fifty farms." farmdoc's What-If tool "calculates ... ARC at the Individual Level (ARC-IC)" and was updated today. We say we do not model it.
- **Probabilities (AFPC).** "A stochastic simulation characterizing the probabilities of different levels of expected FSA payments based on 500 possible future realizations of market prices and county yields" (updated Sep 2026). We give a count of past price years ("PLC did not come out ahead in any of the 11 price years we ran").
- **A tested rule of thumb (farmdoc daily, Aug 5).** "Choose PLC if the program crop's average US cash price for January of the current crop marketing year is less than the crop's effective reference price." They report it picked the higher-paying program in 32 of 36 crop-years, 2021 to 2024, and that it would have captured about 93% of possible payments against 67% actually paid. It is simple and published with a record. For the 2027 decision, whose signup runs to Mar 15, the January 2027 price will be known before the deadline. We do not mention it.
