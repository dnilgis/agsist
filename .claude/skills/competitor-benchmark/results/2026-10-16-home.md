## Bugs found
1. **Trivial: the soybean COT row is a quarter cent off the soybean price shown at the top of the page, and names no contract.**
   - It shows "−45 1/4¢ ... front month" with no contract named. Corn and wheat name theirs.
   - The reason: it compares against the continuous ZS=F quote (12.5775), while the ticker and the first screen show Nov '26 at 12.57 1/2. A reader working it from the page gets −45 1/2¢.
   - `contractOf` finds no match because the two snapshots differ by a quarter cent.
2. **Low: the upcoming-reports strip skips the livestock reports.** It builds its monthly rows from usda-calendar.ics, which has none of the reports the calendar page now lists. So the strip goes from Oct 22 Export Sales to Oct 26 and misses Oct 22 Milk Production and Oct 23 Cattle on Feed. See the usda-calendar report, bug 1.
