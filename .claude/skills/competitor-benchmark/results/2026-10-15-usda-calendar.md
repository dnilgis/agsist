# Head to head: agsist.com/usda-calendar

Checked Oct 15, 2026, from the read-only checkout. I rendered usda-calendar.html at 390x844 and 1280x800. No sideways scroll, no JS errors (one blocked analytics call). The first screen shows the next WASDE (Tue Nov 10), the next report (Export Sales, Thu Oct 15, 8:30 a.m. ET) with a countdown, and calendar add buttons.

**What I could not see:** I could not open any competitor host (DNS refusal). Competitor cells are from search-result text and are marked *.

## Dates checked
- **2026 WASDE dates:** Jan 12, Feb 10, Mar 10, Apr 9, May 12, Jun 11, Jul 10, Aug 12, Sep 11, Oct 9, Nov 10, Dec 10. These match the USDA list as quoted in search.
- **Weekdays:**
  - Oct 1 and Oct 15 are Thursdays.
  - Oct 13 Crop Progress is a Tuesday, after Columbus Day.
  - Nov 10 is a Tuesday.
  - Nov 27 Export Sales is a Friday, after Thanksgiving.
  - Dec 4 StatCan is a Friday.
  - All are correct.
- **Countdown:** 7 h 03 min to 8:30 a.m. ET at a 05:27 UTC render. Correct.
- **No wrong dates found.**
- **Missing reports:**
  - Cattle on Feed (NASS 2026 schedule, from snippets): Oct 23, Nov 20, Dec 18.
  - Milk Production, Cold Storage, and quarterly Hogs and Pigs (December).
  - CFTC COT on Fridays.
  - The calendar also jumps from December 2026 to March 2027 (only the ARC/PLC deadline). There is nothing in January, the month of USDA's annual Crop Production, Dec 1 Grain Stocks and Winter Wheat Seedings.
  - Before adding January, someone should confirm whether USDA has posted its 2027 dates. I could not.

## Where we win
- **One calendar across agencies:** WASDE, NASS, FAS export sales, FSA signup dates, CONAB and StatCan, with a countdown in your own time zone and calendar export. USDA splits these across OCE, NASS, FAS and FSA pages.
- **Market impact tags** (low to extreme, with the reason), plus links from each WASDE to the "What's priced in" grade.
- **FSA dates with consequences** ("No election means you keep your 2025 choice but get no 2026 payment").

## Where a competitor beats us, and how
- **Completeness (NASS).** The NASS calendar has every livestock and dairy report. AGSIST has cattle and milk pages but none of their reports on the calendar.
- **Next year (USDA OCE, once posted).** Our page stops at December, apart from the Mar 15 deadline.

## Five changes, ranked by impact
1. **Add the livestock and dairy reports (S).** From the NASS 2026 PFEI schedule: Cattle on Feed (Oct 23, Nov 20, Dec 18), monthly Milk Production, Cold Storage, and December Hogs and Pigs. Add a "Livestock" filter, and link each one to the cattle and milk pages.
2. **January 2027 (S).** Add Jan 12-ish only once USDA posts the 2027 schedule. Until then, show a line: "USDA has not posted 2027 report dates yet. The January report (annual Crop Production, Dec 1 Grain Stocks, Winter Wheat Seedings) is usually the biggest of the winter."
3. **CFTC COT on Fridays (S),** linking to /cot. Note the holiday shifts.
4. **"What it did" on past reports (M).** With "Show past" on, each past WASDE or Crop Production shows the corn and soybean move from the prior close, plus the What's priced in grade, from data the site already has.
5. **Let the subscribe feed include weekly reports (S).** Today it is monthly reports only. Offer a second ICS with Crop Progress and Export Sales for people who want them.

## Verdict
Best on the internet today? **Close.** For a grain farmer it is the cleanest single calendar I found: right dates, a countdown in local time, impact tags and FSA deadlines. But it leaves out the livestock and dairy reports the site's own cattle and milk pages depend on, and it has nothing for January.
