Where this comes from

Parcel lines and owner of record: the state or county's own free public parcel service, read when this page opened. Transfers and sales: the state's public transfer returns. Soils: USDA NRCS soil survey (SSURGO). Crop history: USDA NASS Cropland Data Layer. Flood: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer. Wetlands: USFWS National Wetlands Inventory. Ground height: USGS 3DEP. Each piece shows as missing when its source did not answer; nothing is filled in by estimate. This is a starting read, not an appraisal, a title search or a survey.
