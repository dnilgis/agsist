63.4% of Arkansas is in exceptional drought

63.4%of Arkansas's land area in exceptional drought (D4), map valid Nov 24, 2026

Exceptional drought (D4) is the worst of the Drought Monitor's five drought levels. We show how much of each state's land was at that level on four maps.

Save the phone picture

The facts

  • 63.4% of Arkansas was in exceptional drought (D4) on the Nov 24 map, up from 55.8% a week earlier.
  • It was 37.1% four weeks earlier (Oct 27) and 0% a year earlier (Nov 25, 2025).
  • 86.5% of Arkansas was in extreme drought or worse (D3 or D4), the same as 86.5% a week earlier.
  • 62 of Arkansas's 75 counties had some exceptional drought, up from 60 a week earlier and 48 four weeks earlier.
  • In 47 of those counties, at least half the land was in exceptional drought.
  • Louisiana: 22.3% in exceptional drought, 66.5% in D3 or worse. Mississippi: 27.8% in exceptional drought, 66.9% in D3 or worse.

Source: U.S. Drought Monitor (National Drought Mitigation Center, USDA, NOAA), map valid Nov 24, 2026, released Nov 26, 2026. droughtmonitor.unl.edu.
