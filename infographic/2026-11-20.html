Corn funds' net position fell 9 weeks in a row

−33,862 contracts net shortManaged money in corn futures, positions Nov 17, 2026

The facts

  • In the week to Nov 17, funds' net corn position fell 11,234 contracts, to 33,862 net short. CFTC Commitments of Traders, Disaggregated Futures-Only, positions Nov 17, 2026, released Nov 20, 2026.
  • From Nov 10 to Nov 17, shorts rose by 12,250 contracts. Longs rose by 1,016. CFTC Commitments of Traders, Disaggregated Futures-Only, positions Nov 17, 2026, released Nov 20, 2026.
  • Over nine weeks, funds cut longs by 239,561 contracts and added 208,761 shorts. CFTC Commitments of Traders, Disaggregated Futures-Only, positions Nov 17, 2026, released Nov 20, 2026.
  • Record high: 414,460 net long on Sep 15, 2026, the largest net long since our series began in 2010, in contracts. CFTC Commitments of Traders, Disaggregated Futures-Only, positions Nov 17, 2026, released Nov 20, 2026.
