Land in Providence County carries a census value of $18,606 per acre, up 30.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 5.5 percent a year. July nights average 63.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.2 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.88 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved by period: 18 percent in 1989-1999, 10 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, 38 percent in 2020-2025. The county saw 11 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 50 weeks total from 2000-2025. Solar operating capacity stands at 130.9 MW, with 3.5 MW more proposed to EIA.
