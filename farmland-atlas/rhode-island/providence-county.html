Providence County, Rhode Island

Rain is at 101% of normal. Land and buildings run $18,606 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.26 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.07 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 191 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,586 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $18,606 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 18% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 82.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 3.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.26 per base acre, PLC $2.69.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (107.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $53.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.9%.
