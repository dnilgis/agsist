Bristol County had 1,625 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land here carries a census value of $49,530 per acre, up 9.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 1.9 percent a year. July nights average 66.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.87 F per decade, warming. The 2010s was the first decade the July average low reached 66 F, and only 1 July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Drought has touched this county lightly: 38 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 9 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland. Solar operating capacity stands at 15.7 MW, with 0 MW wind operating and none proposed to EIA.
