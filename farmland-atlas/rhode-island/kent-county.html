Kent County had 8,694 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land in Kent County carries a census value of $14,822 per acre, up 4.7 percent from 2017-2022, or 0.9 percent a year. July nights average 64.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.88 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched the county in 48 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 18 weeks and 11 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 17 percent of harvested cropland, and the county hosts 132.9 MW of operating solar and 15 MW of operating wind, with no new capacity proposed to EIA.
