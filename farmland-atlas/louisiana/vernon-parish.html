Cash rent in Vernon Parish was $21 an acre in 2026, tied for 6th lowest of 46 in Louisiana, against a state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings sat at the other end, at $5,108 an acre in 2022 and 8th highest of 63. That was up 37.9% from $3,703 in 2017, or 6.6% a year. In 2022, 29% of the farmland was rented.
