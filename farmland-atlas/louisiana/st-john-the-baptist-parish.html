St. John the Baptist Parish, Louisiana

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,674 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.66 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

96.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,306 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,674 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.51 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 1.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,563 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.70 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.98, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 2006 to 2024; too much rain caused 13%.

The census value of land and buildings in St. John the Baptist Parish stood at $5,674 an acre in 2022, up 62.1% from $3,500 in 2017. That works out to 10.1% a year, and it puts the parish 7th highest of 63 in Louisiana, well over the state median of $3,628. Back in 2012 the census value was $2,910. 44% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for St. John the Baptist Parish

What is farmland worth in St. John the Baptist Parish, Louisiana?

Land and buildings were worth $5,674 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
