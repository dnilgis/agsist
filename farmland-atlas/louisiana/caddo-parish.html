Cash rent in Caddo Parish ran $71 an acre in 2026, up 23.5% from $58 in 2016 and above the state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings was $4,525 an acre in 2022, up 51.7% from 2017. That census value ranks 11th highest of 63 in Louisiana. Corn made 160.3 bu in 2025, and 58% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
