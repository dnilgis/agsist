Avoyelles Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $115 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,193 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
