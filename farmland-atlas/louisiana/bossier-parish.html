As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Bossier Parish runs $38 an acre for 2026, up 15.2% from $33 in 2016 and ranked 20th lowest of 46, under the state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings was $3,746 an acre in 2022, up 20.0% from $3,122 in 2017, which ranks 28th highest of 63. Pasture rents for $20 an acre in 2026, and 29% of farmland was rented in 2022.
