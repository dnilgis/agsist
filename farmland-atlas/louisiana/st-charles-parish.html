Cropland in St. Charles Parish rented for $27 an acre in 2023, and pasture went for $18. The census value of land and buildings was $4,463 an acre in 2022, up 27.8% from $3,491 in 2017, or 5.0% a year. That puts the parish 12th highest of 63 against a state median of $3,628. Renters farm a lot of the ground here, with 78% of farmland rented in 2022.
