Tangipahoa Parish, Louisiana

Tangipahoa Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,045 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22105. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tangipahoa Parish stands at $37 per acre, up 60.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, was $7,045 per acre in 2022, having climbed 39.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low of 73.1 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 71.9 F, part of a 0.49 F per decade trend since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved unevenly across periods, at 100 percent in 1989-1999, 86 percent in 2000-2009, 32 percent in 2010-2019, and 28 percent in 2020-2025.
