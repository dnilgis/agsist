Cropland rent in Jackson Parish runs $35 an acre in 2026, tied for 17th lowest of 46 and under the state median of $56. Pasture rents for $19 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,299 an acre in 2022, up 7.4% from $4,003 in 2017, which ranks 15th highest of 63 against a state median of $3,628. 24% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
