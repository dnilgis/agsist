Non-irrigated cash rent in Winn Parish averages $34 per acre in 2023. Land value sits at $2,837 per acre in the 2022 census, up 1.2 percent from 2017-2022, 0.2 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed 0.5 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 72.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 71.6 F. Only 6 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
