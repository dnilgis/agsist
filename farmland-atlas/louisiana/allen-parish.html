As of Oct 16, cash rent in Allen Parish runs $31 an acre for 2025, up 79.4% from $17 in 2013. Irrigated rent is $76 an acre for 2026, 2nd lowest of 21 in Louisiana, against a state median of $134. The census value of land and buildings was $3,103 an acre in 2022, up 20.9% from $2,566 in 2017, and 15th lowest of 63 in the state. 54% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
