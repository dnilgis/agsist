Lafourche Parish, Louisiana

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,909 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Lafourche Parish vs Louisiana: $26 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 27% of the other 45 Louisiana counties with a 2026 rent (13th lowest of 46).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

77% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.36 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 76% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,446 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,909 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 51% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 83.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 759 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $48.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.20 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.86, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 1.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.6%.
