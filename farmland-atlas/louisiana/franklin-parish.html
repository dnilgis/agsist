Franklin Parish, Louisiana

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.13 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 75% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 19 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,086 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,219 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $78 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 82% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 9.3% from 2016 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains under 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 80.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 4.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,996 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.97, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. 62.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 25.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 52.2%.

Quick answers for Franklin Parish

What is cash rent in Franklin Parish, Louisiana?

The county average is $78 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $160 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Franklin Parish, Louisiana?

Land and buildings were worth $3,219 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Franklin Parish, Louisiana?

The county's 2021-25 average is 188.7 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
