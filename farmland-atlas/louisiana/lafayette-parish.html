Lafayette Parish cash rent on cropland runs $74 an acre for 2026, up 11.4% from $66 in 2016 and 19th highest of 46 in Louisiana. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,291 an acre in 2022, up 48.6% from $5,581 in 2017. That puts the parish 2nd highest of 63 in the state, well above the $3,628 state median. 50% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
