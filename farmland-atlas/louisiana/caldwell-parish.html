Cash rent on Caldwell Parish cropland was $49 an acre in 2026, down 32.6% from $72 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $56 and ranks 22nd lowest of 46. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 25.5% from $2,507 an acre in 2017 to $3,147 in 2022, which still ranks 16th lowest of 63 in Louisiana.
