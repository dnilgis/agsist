Terrebonne Parish, Louisiana

Terrebonne Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2017); land and buildings $4,411 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Terrebonne Parish was $70 per acre in 2017. Land value in 2022 was $4,411 per acre, up 7.1 percent since 2017, or 1.4 percent a year. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 131 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2000 the worst year at 37 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 42 such weeks. July nightly lows have warmed since 1976, running 0.68 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 76.8 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 75.4 F.
