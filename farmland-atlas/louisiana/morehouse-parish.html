Morehouse Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,492 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.34 per base acre for corn.

Morehouse Parish vs Louisiana: $75 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 62% of the other 45 Louisiana counties with a 2026 rent (18th highest of 46).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,019 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,492 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $75 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.69 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 74% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 16.2% from 2016 to 2026. 68% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 33.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 216.4 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 87,809 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.34 per base acre, PLC $3.42.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (136.19 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $73.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $30.10 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.74 per base acre, PLC $7.06. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $50.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 150 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.48 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.47, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 88% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 65.3%.

Quick answers for Morehouse Parish

What is cash rent in Morehouse Parish, Louisiana?

The county average is $75 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $150 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Morehouse Parish, Louisiana?

Land and buildings were worth $3,492 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Morehouse Parish, Louisiana?

The county's 2021-25 average is 192.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
