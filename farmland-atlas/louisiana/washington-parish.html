Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington Parish runs $34 per acre, up 46.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value stands at $3,980 per acre, up 10.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 72.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 71.5 F normal, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.55 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved 89 percent, 86 percent, 40 percent, and 27 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, while the loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 1.31 against 1.21 for 1995-2025. Drought weeks fell from 37 in 2000 to 18 across 2021-2025, and 4 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation.
