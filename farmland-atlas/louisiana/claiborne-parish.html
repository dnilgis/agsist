Claiborne Parish, Louisiana

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,379 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 37.62 inches, 78% of normal (48.43 inches) through Nov 18, at Homer, 1 mi N. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,932 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,379 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $19 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 40.7% from 2016 to 2026. 13% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 152.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $61.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Claiborne Parish runs $19 an acre in 2026, up 40.7% from $14 in 2016. That ranks 4th lowest of 46 in Louisiana, well under the state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,379 an acre in 2022, up 41.4% from $2,389 in 2017 and 25th lowest of 63. Only 13% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
