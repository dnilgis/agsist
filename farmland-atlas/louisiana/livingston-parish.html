Livingston Parish, Louisiana

Livingston Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2010); land and buildings $6,940 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22063. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in 2010 stood at $15 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $6,940 per acre, with land value change of 29.7 percent from 2017-2022, or 5.3 percent a year. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. July nightly lows have averaged 73.8 F from 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 72.6 F, with a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. The county spent 99 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 25 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed for this county.
