Livingston Parish, Louisiana

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,940 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 53.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 28 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,200 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,940 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 177.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 81 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Livingston Parish was $6,940 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 63 in Louisiana against a state median of $3,628. That is up 29.7% from $5,351 in 2017, 5.3% a year. Pasture rented for $18 an acre in 2023, and just 14% of the parish farmland was rented in 2022.
