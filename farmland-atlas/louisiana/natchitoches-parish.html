Non-irrigated cash rent in Natchitoches Parish runs $58 per acre in 2026, up 8.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $134 per acre, a multiple of 2.33 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026, compared with 1.66 in 2010; the direction of that multiple shows no clear pattern. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 0.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2024, with a median of 144.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 117.1 in 2016. Crop insurance data for 1989-2025 show $59.7 million in indemnities, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 61 percent; the loss ratio was 1.57 overall and 2.04 for 2016-2025. Census land value stands at $3,223 per acre, up 38 percent from 2017 to 2022.
