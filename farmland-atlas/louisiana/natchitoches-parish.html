U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Natchitoches Parish rents for $58 an acre in 2026, up 8.5% from $53 in 2016 and a touch over the state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings was $3,223 an acre in 2022, 22nd lowest of 63 in the state, up 38.0% from $2,335 in 2017. Corn made 160.6 bu in 2024, well above the 144.2 bu median for 2008 through 2024.

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
