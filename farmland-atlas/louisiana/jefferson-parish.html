Jefferson Parish, Louisiana

Jefferson Parish, LA: land and buildings $2,176 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value here runs $2,176 per acre, and it moved down 6.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or down 1.4 percent a year. July nights average 76.1 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 75.2 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.39 F per decade, warming. Drought weeks at severe or worse ran 125 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst year 2000 with 42 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 29 such weeks. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
