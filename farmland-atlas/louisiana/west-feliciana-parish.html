Cropland rent in West Feliciana Parish runs $20 an acre for 2026, the 5th lowest of 46 in Louisiana against a state median of $56. That rent sits 11.4% below the $22 paid in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,197 an acre in 2022, up 8.9% from $2,935 in 2017, or 1.7% a year. 27% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
