Non-irrigated cash rent in West Feliciana Parish averages $20 per acre in 2026, down 11.4 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, and excess moisture and flood account for 82 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1994-2025, the largest cause by far. Census land value runs $3,197 per acre, up 8.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
