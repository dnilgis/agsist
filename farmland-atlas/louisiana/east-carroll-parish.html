USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in East Carroll Parish runs $130 an acre in 2026, 2nd highest of 46 in Louisiana and well over the $56 state median. That is up 13.0% from $115 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,631 an acre in 2022, up 24.7% from $3,713 in 2017 and 10th highest of 63 in the state. Corn made 202.3 bu in 2025, above the 184.9 bu average for 2021 to 2025.
