Catahoula Parish, Louisiana

Catahoula Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $88 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,908 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Catahoula Parish averages $88 per acre in 2026, up 27.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $147 per acre in 2025, 1.71 times the non-irrigated rate, though that multiple was only 1.14 in 2009 and shows no clear direction since. Corn yield has trended up 3.4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 174.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 128 in 2009. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has declined across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, while excess moisture and flood account for 56 percent of the $135.9 million paid out since 1989. Census land value sits at $2,908 per acre, up 7.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
