As of Oct 16, cash rent in Jefferson Davis Parish runs $18 an acre in 2026, tied for 2nd lowest of 46 in Louisiana, against a state median of $56. That is up 44.0% from $13 in 2016. Irrigated rent sits at $71 an acre in 2026, lowest of 21. The census value of land and buildings was $2,980 an acre in 2022, up 33.8% from 2017.
