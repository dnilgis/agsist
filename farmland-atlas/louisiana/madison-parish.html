Madison Parish, Louisiana

Madison Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $143 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,764 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 176.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22065. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison Parish sits at $143 per acre in 2026, up 17.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $159 per acre, a multiple of 1.11 over non-irrigated, and that multiple showed no clear direction against 1.19 in 2009. Corn yields carried a fitted trend of 3.2 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 176.2 bushels per acre and a low of 115 in 2009. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 were dominated by excess moisture and flood at 57 percent, not heat and drought, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.04. Census land values reached $3,764 per acre in 2022, up 36.9 percent from 2017. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed in the county.
