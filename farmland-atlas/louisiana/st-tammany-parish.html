St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana

St. Tammany Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $14 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,879 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 averages $14 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $10,879 per acre, with a land value change of 1.4 percent from 2017-2022, 0.3 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed since 1976 at 0.49 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 74 F runs above the 1991-2020 normal of 72.7 F. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 106 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 18 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Crop insurance indemnities from 1997-2006 totaled $113,301, with wind the largest cause at 52 percent. No solar or wind capacity operates in the county, and none is proposed to EIA.
