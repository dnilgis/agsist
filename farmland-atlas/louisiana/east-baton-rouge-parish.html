Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $18 per acre, up 56.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings value came in at $8,027 per acre in the census year 2022, down 2.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 74 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.7 F, with a warming trend of 0.57 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks were 111 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 42 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025, and heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 14 percent in 1989-1999, 0 percent in 2010-2019, and 21 percent in 2020-2025.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
