Tensas Parish, Louisiana

Tensas Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $104 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,843 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22107. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tensas Parish runs $104 per acre in 2026, down 1 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $175 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 1.03 in 2009 to 1.68 in 2026, direction rising. Groundwater supplies 86 percent of irrigation water as of 2015. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 46 percent of $109.0 million paid, with the loss ratio over 2016-2025 at 0.94 against 0.73 for the full 1989-2025 period. Census value of land and buildings stood at $2,843 per acre in 2022, up 1.4 percent from 2017.
