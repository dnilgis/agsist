Stands out against its own record

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Tensas Parish ran $104 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 46 in Louisiana and well over the $56 state median. That is down 1.0% from $105 in 2016, so rent has hardly moved. The census value of land and buildings was $2,843 an acre in 2022, up 1.4% from $2,803 in 2017 and 8th lowest of 63. Corn made 193.4 bu in 2025, against an 18 year median of 172.8 bu.
