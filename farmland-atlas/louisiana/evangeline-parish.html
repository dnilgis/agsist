Evangeline Parish, Louisiana

Evangeline Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $44 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,186 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Evangeline Parish is $44 per acre in 2026, down 57.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $90 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent moved from 1.49 in 2009 to 2.06 in 2026, with no clear direction overall. Irrigation covers 68 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and 90 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. July nights have warmed from a 72.9 F normal to a 73.7 F average for 2017-2026, part of a 0.41 F per decade trend since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has risen across each listed period: 7 percent in 1989-1999, 12 percent in 2000-2009, 18 percent in 2010-2019, and 25 percent in 2020-2025.
