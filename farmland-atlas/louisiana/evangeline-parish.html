As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Evangeline Parish runs $44 an acre for 2026, down 57.4% from $102 in 2016, and that is 21st lowest of 46 against a state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 30.5% to $3,186 an acre in 2022 from $2,441 in 2017. Irrigated ground rents for $90 an acre in 2026, and 48% of farmland was rented in 2022.
