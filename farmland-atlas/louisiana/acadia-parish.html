Non-irrigated cash rent in Acadia Parish runs $54 per acre in 2026, up 67.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $88 per acre, a multiple of 1.64 over non-irrigated in 2026, against 1.31 in 2009, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Seventy percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 70 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. Crop insurance losses since 1989 total $48.3 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 58 percent; heat and drought shares moved 12 percent, 21 percent, 10 percent, then 23 percent across the four listed periods. Land value reached $3,046 per acre in 2022, up 10.3 percent from 2017.
