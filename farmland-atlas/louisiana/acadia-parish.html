Acadia Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,046 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.18 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 43.93 inches, 78% of normal (56.49 inches) through Nov 17, at Crowley, 2 mi NE. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,281 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,046 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $54 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 67.2% from 2016 to 2026. 72% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 37.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.18 per base acre, PLC $1.67.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (66.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.67 per base acre, PLC $6.28. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $49.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.93 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.68, 2016 to 2025. 70% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 17.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 58%.

Cropland rent in Acadia Parish runs $54 an acre for 2026, up 67.2% from $32 in 2016. That sits just under the Louisiana median of $56 and ranks 23rd lowest of 46. The census value of land and buildings was $3,046 an acre in 2022, up 10.3% from $2,762 in 2017. 72% of the farmland here is rented, so the rent figure matters to plenty of operators.
