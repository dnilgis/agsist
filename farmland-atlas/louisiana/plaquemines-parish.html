Plaquemines Parish, Louisiana

Plaquemines Parish, LA: land and buildings $2,130 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value here sits at $2,130 per acre in the 2022 census, up 51 percent from 2017, or 8.6 percent a year. July nights average 77.1 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 76.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.46 F per decade, warming. Irrigation covers 43 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Drought has held severe levels or worse for 142 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 41 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 and the worst year, 2000, logging 37 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
