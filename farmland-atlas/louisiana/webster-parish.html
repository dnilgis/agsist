Webster Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,320 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 263 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,999 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,320 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 175.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 977 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $63.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 72.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1991 to 2001; too much rain caused 25.9%.

Cash rent in Webster Parish runs $22 an acre for 2025, up 209.9% from $7 in 2014. That is a big jump on a small number. The census value of land and buildings was $4,320 an acre in 2022, up 47.0% from $2,939 in 2017. That puts it 14th highest of 63 in Louisiana, against a state median of $3,628.
