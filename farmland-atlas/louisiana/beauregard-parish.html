Cropland rent in Beauregard Parish runs $22 an acre for 2026, up 12.8% from $20 in 2016. That is tied for 9th lowest of 46 parishes, against a state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings sits higher in the order at $4,024 an acre in 2022, up 32.4% from $3,039 in 2017 and 20th highest of 63. 37% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
