Beauregard Parish, Louisiana

Beauregard Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,024 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Beauregard Parish runs $22 per acre in 2026, up 12.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents for $54 per acre in 2025. Census land value stands at $4,024 per acre in 2022, up 32.4 percent from 2017, or 5.8 percent a year. July nights average 73.7 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.53 F per decade, warming. Drought weeks totaled 32 in 2021-2025, against 140 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse across 2000-2025. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 56 percent over 1989-2025.
