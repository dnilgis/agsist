Beauregard Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,024 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.52 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 44.9 inches, 80% of normal (55.95 inches) through Nov 17, at Ragley, 5 mi SE. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 29 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,171 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,024 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 12.8% from 2016 to 2026. 36.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 180.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 171,988 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.52 per base acre, PLC $2.21.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (88.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $49.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.79 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.87 over the same years. 7.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 56.4%.

Cropland rent in Beauregard Parish runs $22 an acre for 2026, up 12.8% from $20 in 2016. That is tied for 9th lowest of 46 parishes, against a state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings sits higher in the order at $4,024 an acre in 2022, up 32.4% from $3,039 in 2017 and 20th highest of 63. 37% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
