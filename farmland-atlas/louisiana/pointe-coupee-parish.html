Cash rent in Pointe Coupee Parish runs $85 an acre for 2026, up 20.7% from $70 in 2016 and tied for 12th highest of 46 in Louisiana. The census value of land and buildings was $3,727 an acre in 2022, up 20.6% from $3,091 in 2017, which is 3.8% a year. Corn made 177.8 bu in 2025, right about the 2021-25 average of 178.6 bu, and 64% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
