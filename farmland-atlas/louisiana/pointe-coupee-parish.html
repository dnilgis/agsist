Pointe Coupee Parish, Louisiana

Pointe Coupee Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $85 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,727 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22077. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pointe Coupee Parish averages $84 per acre in 2026, up 20.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent in 2024 runs $182 per acre, though only 9 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census, and all of that water comes from groundwater. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 179.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 149.6 in 2011. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $73.7 million, driven mostly by excess moisture and flood at 39 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.05, higher than the 0.85 ratio for the full 1989-2025 period. Census land value reached $3,727 per acre in 2022, up 20.6 percent from 2017.
