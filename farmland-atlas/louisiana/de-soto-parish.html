Non-irrigated cash rent in De Soto Parish averages $30 per acre in 2026, up 5.4 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $3,577 per acre in 2022, up 20.6 percent from 2017, a pace of 3.8 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 73.3 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.2 F, part of a trend of 0.6 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks in severe category or worse ran 161 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks and 20 weeks logged from 2021-2025; excess moisture and flood, not heat, drove the largest share of indemnities at 38 percent over 1989-2025.
