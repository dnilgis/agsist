St. Bernard Parish, Louisiana

St. Bernard Parish, LA: land and buildings $2,086 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22087. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in St. Bernard Parish stood at $2,086 per acre in the 2022 census, up 19.1 percent from 2017, or 3.6 percent a year. July nights have averaged 76.6 F from 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 75.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.43 F per decade, warming. Of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 123 were spent with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, 9.1 percent of weeks, with 2000 the worst year at 42 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 23 such weeks. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census.
