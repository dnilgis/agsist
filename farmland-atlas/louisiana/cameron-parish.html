Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,390 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,737 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $12 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.53 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 63% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 48% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 102.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 994 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (22.79 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 25, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $49.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $28.19 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 3.94, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 35.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 48.5%.

Quick answers for Cameron Parish

What is cash rent in Cameron Parish, Louisiana?

The county average is $12 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024 and $84 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cameron Parish, Louisiana?

Land and buildings were worth $2,737 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
