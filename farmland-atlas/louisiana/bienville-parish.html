Bienville Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,628 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.00 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 75% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 255 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,993 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,628 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 150.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 4.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 49,579 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $61.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 4.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cash rent in Bienville Parish was $20 an acre in 2021, down 4.8% from $21 in 2011. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,628 an acre in 2022, up 42.4% from $2,547 in 2017. That puts the parish right on the state median of $3,628 and ranks it 32nd lowest of 63. Only 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
