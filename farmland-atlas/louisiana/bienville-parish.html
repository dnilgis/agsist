Bienville Parish, Louisiana

Bienville Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2021); land and buildings $3,628 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bienville Parish was $20 per acre in 2021, down 4.8 percent from 2011. Land value stood at $3,628 per acre in 2022, up 42.4 percent from 2017, a 7.3 percent a year pace. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, 2022 census. July nights average 73.4 F, 2017-2026, against a 71.5 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.76 F per decade since 1976. Drought hit severe or worse for 183 of 1357 weeks, 2000-2025, with 34 weeks in 2021-2025. No solar or wind operates in the parish now; 4.5 MW solar is proposed to EIA.
