Cropland rent in St. Mary Parish runs $81 an acre for 2026, up 66.0% from $49 in 2014. That puts it 14th highest of 46 in Louisiana, against a state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings was $3,498 an acre in 2022, up 6.1% from $3,298 in 2017, or 1.2% a year. Renting is the rule here, with 93% of farmland rented in 2022.
