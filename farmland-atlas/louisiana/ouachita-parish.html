Cropland rent in Ouachita Parish runs $85 an acre in 2026, up 8.3% from $78 in 2016 and tied for 12th highest of 46 in Louisiana. The census value of land and buildings was $4,018 an acre in 2022, down 5.7% from $4,261 in 2017. Corn made 195.9 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 186.1 bu.
