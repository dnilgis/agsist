West Baton Rouge Parish, Louisiana

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,693 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.92 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,225 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,693 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $66 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 73% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 31.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 594 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.92 per base acre, PLC $2.36.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (94 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.79 per base acre, PLC $6.79. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $51.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.93 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.47, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 54.9%.

Cash rent in West Baton Rouge Parish was $67 an acre in 2023, up 6.4% from $63 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $4,693 an acre in 2022, up 66.4% from $2,820 in 2017. That puts the parish 9th highest of 63 in the state, against a state median of $3,628. 73% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
