Cash rent in West Baton Rouge Parish was $67 an acre in 2023, up 6.4% from $63 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $4,693 an acre in 2022, up 66.4% from $2,820 in 2017. That puts the parish 9th highest of 63 in the state, against a state median of $3,628. 73% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
