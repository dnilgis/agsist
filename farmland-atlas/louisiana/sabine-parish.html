Sabine Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,692 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.50 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 74% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,060 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,692 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 223.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 220 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 113,305 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $54.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
