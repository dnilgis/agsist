Richland Parish cash rent was $80 an acre in 2026, up 34.7% from $59 in 2016 and 15th highest of 46 in Louisiana, against a state median of $56. The census value of land and buildings was $3,401 an acre in 2022, up 20.1% from $2,831 in 2017, and that sits 26th lowest of 63. Corn made 188.7 bu in 2024, a bit over the 17 year median of 180.2 bu. Renters farm 61% of the ground.
