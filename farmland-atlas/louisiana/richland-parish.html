Richland Parish, Louisiana

Richland Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $80 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,401 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 180.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22083. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Richland Parish runs $80 per acre in 2026, up 34.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $148 per acre, and the irrigated premium over non-irrigated has widened, from a multiple of 1.44 in 2009 to 1.86 in 2026. Sixty-four percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with 70 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Corn yields have trended up 3.5 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2024, with a median of 180.2 bushels per acre and a low of 108 in 2009.
