Cash rent in Iberville Parish runs $32 an acre in 2026, down 11.3% from $36 in 2016 and under the state median of $56. That puts it 16th lowest of 46. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,562 an acre in 2022, up 42.7% from $1,796 in 2017, which ranks 4th lowest of 63 against a state median of $3,628. 45% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
