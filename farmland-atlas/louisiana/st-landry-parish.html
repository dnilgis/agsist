St. Landry Parish, Louisiana

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,540 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.79 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 44.92 inches, 86% of normal (52.29 inches) through Nov 17, at Grand Coteau, 2.7 mi E.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,540 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $89 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.79 per base acre, PLC $2.60.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (103.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $51.70 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.35 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.23 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 57% of the median yield. 21% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in St. Landry Parish runs $89 an acre for 2026, up 45.9% from $61 in 2016 and 9th highest of 46 in Louisiana. The census value of land and buildings was $3,540 an acre in 2022, up 21.3% from $2,919 in 2017, or 3.9% a year. Corn made 180.2 bu in 2025, above the 2021 to 2025 average of 168.6 bu, and 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
