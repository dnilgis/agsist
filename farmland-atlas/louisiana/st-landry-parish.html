Non-irrigated cash rent in St. Landry Parish runs $89 per acre in 2026, up 45.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent sits close behind at $90 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has fallen from 1.56 in 2009 to 1.01 in 2026. Corn yields carry a rising trend of 2.9 bushels per acre a year against a median of 167.4 bushels per acre, with 2011 a low point at 94.7. Land value is $3,540 per acre in 2022, up 21.3 percent from 2017 to 2022.
