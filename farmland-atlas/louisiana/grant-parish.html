Grant Parish, Louisiana

Grant Parish, LA: non-irrigated cash rent $78 an acre (2025); land and buildings $3,170 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 22043. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Grant Parish runs $78 per acre in 2025, up 10.7 percent from 2014. Census land value sits at $3,170 per acre, up 15.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield has a median of 148.2 bushels per acre from 2008-2025, with a worst year of 68.8 in 2011, the same year that brought 47 weeks of severe drought or worse, the most of any year in 2000-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 26 percent in 1989-1999, 25 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, then 49 percent in 2020-2025. Excess moisture and flood remains the largest cause of indemnities overall, at 44 percent.
