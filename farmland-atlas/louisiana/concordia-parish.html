Concordia Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,245 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.96 per base acre for corn.

Concordia Parish vs Louisiana: $119 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 96% of the other 45 Louisiana counties with a 2026 rent (3rd highest of 46).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.48 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 20 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,116 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,245 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $119 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.56 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 45% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 20.2% from 2016 to 2026. 56% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 17,811 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 40 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 25: $4.79 at Vidalia, 18 miles. 1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.96 per base acre, PLC $2.87.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (114.18 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $48.88 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.23 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $51.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 852 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.82 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.93, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. 23.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 49.1%.
