Cash rent in Concordia Parish ran $119 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 46 in Louisiana and well over the state median of $56. That is up 20.2% from $99 in 2016. Irrigated rent was $185 an acre in 2026, highest of 21 in the state. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,245 an acre in 2022, up 15.8% from $2,802 in 2017.
