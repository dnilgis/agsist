West Carroll Parish, Louisiana

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,030 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 22 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,050 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,030 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $98 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 47.7% from 2016 to 2026. 36.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 40.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,473 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $50.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2,661 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.72 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.12 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 66% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 20.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 66.4%.
