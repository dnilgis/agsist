Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2013 (15 surveys on record since 2009); up 46% from $14 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Calcasieu Parish runs $21 an acre in 2026, up 46.4% from $14 in 2016 and tied for 6th lowest of 46 in the state. Irrigated ground rents for $80 an acre in 2026, 3rd lowest of 21. The census value of land and buildings was $3,891 an acre in 2022, up 11.8% from 2017 and 24th highest of 63. Renters farm 58% of the ground.
