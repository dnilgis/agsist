Non-irrigated cash rent in Windham County averaged $84 per acre in 2024, down 26.1 percent from 2014. July nights average 57.6 F over 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 55.7 F, and the long-term trend is 0.84 F per decade, warming. Freeze and frost caused 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 2000-2025, while heat and drought accounted for 0 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009, 0 percent in 2010-2019, and 20 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 was 0.81, above the 1995-2025 ratio of 0.63. Census land value stood at $5,142 per acre in 2022, up 2.6 percent from 2017.
