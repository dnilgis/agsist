The census value of land and buildings in Essex County was $2,357 an acre in 2022. That was up 20.5% from $1,956 in 2017, or 3.8% a year. It ranks lowest of 13 in Vermont, well under the state median of $3,906. 25% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
