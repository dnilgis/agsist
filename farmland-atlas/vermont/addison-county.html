Addison County, Vermont

Addison County, VT: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,832 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 50001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Addison County runs $66 per acre, up 28.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,832 per acre in 2022, up 14.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.8 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value was 1.54 percent. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 totaled $22.3 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 51 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 13 percent in 1989-1999, 2 percent in 2000-2009, 3 percent in 2010-2019, and 40 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 57.1 F normal to a 58.4 F average over 2017-2026, continuing a trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976.
