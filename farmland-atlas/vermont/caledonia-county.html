Stands out against its own record

$52

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2010; up 52.9% from $34 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Caledonia County rented for $52 an acre in 2026, up 52.9% from $34 in 2016. That rent sits 3rd lowest of 10 in Vermont, under the state median of $59. The census value of land and buildings was $3,395 an acre in 2022, up 6.4% from 2017, or 1.2% a year. Only 15% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
