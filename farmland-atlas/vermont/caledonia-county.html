Caledonia County, Vermont

Caledonia County, VT: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,395 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 50005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $52 per acre, up 52.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Water risk has been low over 2000-2025, with severe drought or worse in 1.5 percent of weeks, though 2021-2025 saw 18 such weeks, most in 2025. Indemnities since 2000 total $310,624, driven by excess moisture and flood at 92 percent, with heat and drought at 0 percent of losses in 2010-2019 and 4 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed to 55.3 F from a 1991-2020 normal of 54.1 F.
