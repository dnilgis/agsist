Cash rent in Grand Isle County runs $62 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 10 in Vermont and a bit over the state median of $59. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,445 an acre in 2022, up 73.0% from $4,304 in 2017, and that is the highest of 13 in the state. Only 18% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
