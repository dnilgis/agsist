Orange County, Vermont

Orange County, VT: non-irrigated cash rent $71 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,431 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 50017. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $71 per acre, down 3.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $4,431 per acre, up 20.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.8 percent a year. Excess moisture and flood account for 60 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1998-2025, and the loss ratio over that span is 0.44. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 3 percent in 2000-2009, 3 percent in 2010-2019, and 19 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 54.6 F normal to a 56 F average over 2017-2026, a trend of 0.69 F per decade. Drought weeks stayed rare: 19 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 17 of those in 2021-2025.
