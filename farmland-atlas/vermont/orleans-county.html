Cash rent in Orleans County runs $59 an acre for 2026, right at the state median and 5th lowest of 10. That is up 91.8% from $31 in 2016. Sources and terms

Every figure is a county average from the sources below, not an appraisal of any farm. "Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed. Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. Downloads: all counties, CSV.

This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields. Loss ratio and claims: USDA RMA Summary of Business. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor. July lows: NOAA nClimDiv county monthly minimum temperature. A missing figure is named with its reason (in a table it reads n/a).

Rent against price: the county's non-irrigated cash rent (USDA NASS county survey, not more than three survey years older than the newest) divided by the median price per acre of recorded market sales of bare farmland, 20 acres or more, over the 24 months the sales line names. Years of rent is that price divided by the rent. We show it only with 5 or more sales, and only where the rent is at least 1% of the price (sales priced well above farm value, such as building ground, get no line). The two sides are different land: the sales include pasture and any irrigated ground, the rent is non-irrigated cropland only. Taxes, upkeep and interest are not counted.
