Washington County, Vermont

Washington County, VT: non-irrigated cash rent $56 an acre (2024); land and buildings $5,057 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 50023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County averages $56 per acre in 2024, up 19.1 percent from 2014. Census land and buildings value stands at $5,057 per acre, up 18.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.4 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1996-2025 total $384,611, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 76 percent; heat and drought accounted for 8 percent of indemnities in 2010-2019 and 14 percent in 2020-2025. Drought has touched this county rarely: 13 of 1357 weeks since 2000 saw severe drought or worse, 11 of those weeks in 2021-2025. July nights average 56.1 F for 2017-2026 against a 54.8 F normal for 1991-2020, warming at 0.67 F per decade since 1976.
