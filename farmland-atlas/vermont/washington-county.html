As of Oct 12, cash rent in Washington County runs $56 an acre for 2024, up 19.1% from $47 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,057 an acre in 2022, up 18.1% from $4,283 in 2017, or 3.4% a year. That sits above the state median of $3,906 and ranks 4th highest of 13. 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
