Rutland County, Vermont

Rutland County, VT: non-irrigated cash rent $58 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,393 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 50021. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $58 per acre, up 71.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stands at $3,393 per acre, up 22.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.2 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $2.1 million in indemnities over the closed crop years 1995-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 81 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 11 percent in 2000-2009 to 6 percent in 2010-2019 to 21 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows averaged 56.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 55.5 F, and the county saw 8 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2021-2025.
