Cash rent in Lamoille County runs $62 an acre for 2025, up 65.3% from $38 in 2013. Pasture rented for $50 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings was $3,508 an acre in 2022, up 0.6% from 2017 and still below the $3,645 of 2012. That ranks 4th lowest of 13 in Vermont, under a state median of $3,906.
