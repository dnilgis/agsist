Cash rent in Ashland County runs $129 an acre for 2026, up 13.2% from $114 in 2016. That sits under the Ohio median of $168 and ranks tied for 28th lowest of 76. The census value of land and buildings was $7,886 an acre in 2022, up 29.8% from $6,075 in 2017, close to the state median of $7,956. Corn trends at 176.3 bu, short of the state median of 192.3.
