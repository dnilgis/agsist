Non-irrigated cash rent in Ashland County runs $129 per acre, up 13.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yield trend over 2008-2025 is 1.7 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 149.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 118.3 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood account for 50 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, more than heat and drought in any of the periods listed. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 6 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 0 weeks from 2021-2025. Census land value stood at $7,886 per acre in 2022, up 29.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
