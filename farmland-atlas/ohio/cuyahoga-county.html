Cuyahoga County, Ohio

Cuyahoga County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2022); land and buildings $32,555 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Cuyahoga County had 1,564 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value rose 99.6 percent from 2017-2022, or 14.8 percent a year. Drought has been a minor factor here: 1 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, none of it in 2021-2025, and heat and drought made up 0 percent of indemnities in 2010-2019. Crop insurance indemnities from 1990-2025 total $260,478, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 69 percent. July nights have warmed from a 63.7 F normal to 65.1 F for 2017-2026, a trend of 0.55 F per decade.
