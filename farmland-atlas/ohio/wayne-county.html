Wayne County, Ohio

Wayne County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $154 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,012 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 161.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39169. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County averages $154 per acre in 2026, up 38.7 percent from 2016. Land value under the census sits at $12,012 per acre, up 34.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.1 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 161.4 bushels and a worst year of 125.9 in 2012. Drought has been rare here: 6 of 1357 weeks since 2000 saw severe drought or worse, none from 2021-2025, and July nights average 62.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.2 F.
