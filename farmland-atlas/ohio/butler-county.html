Butler County, Ohio

Rain is at 125% of normal. Land and buildings run $9,017 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.65 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.20 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 125% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 196 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,103 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,017 an acre in the 2022 census.

OSU's survey covers western Ohio's northwest and southwest regions only, so no figure for this county. Western Ohio Cropland Values and Cash Rents 2025-26, Ohio State University Extension, survey of January to April 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $165 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 37.5% from 2016 to 2026. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 31% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 99.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 9.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 241 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.23 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.35, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 38% of the median yield. 1.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 19.4%.

Stands out against its own record

$165

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 37.5% from $120 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Butler County runs $165 an acre for 2026, up 37.5% from $120 in 2016 and just under the state median of $168. That puts it tied for 37th lowest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $9,017 an acre in 2022, up 9.2% from $8,255 in 2017, and it ranks 24th highest of 87. Renters work 29% of the farmland here.
