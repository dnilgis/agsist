Stands out against its own record

$165

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 37.5% from $120 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Butler County runs $165 an acre for 2026, up 37.5% from $120 in 2016 and just under the state median of $168. That puts it tied for 37th lowest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $9,017 an acre in 2022, up 9.2% from $8,255 in 2017, and it ranks 24th highest of 87. Renters work 29% of the farmland here.
