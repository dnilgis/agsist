Cash rent in Putnam County ran $172 an acre in 2022, up 30.3% from $132 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings was $8,403 an acre in 2022, up 25.5% from $6,694 in 2017, and that ranks 34th highest of 87 against a state median of $7,956. Corn trend yield sits at 201.4 bu, 18th highest of 81 in Ohio, and 47% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
