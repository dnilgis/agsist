Non-irrigated cash rent in Warren County stands at $140 per acre, up 30.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value reached $10,500 per acre in 2022, up 34.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.1 percent a year. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 176.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 127.2 in 2012. Drought weeks stayed low too, at 22 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025.
