Perry County, Ohio

Perry County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $103 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,172 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 164.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39127. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Perry County averages $103 per acre in 2026, up 25.6 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $6,172 per acre, up 34.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, a 6.2 percent a year pace. Corn yield trend runs 2.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 164.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.7 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood drove 52 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share moved from 59 percent in 1989-1999, to 40 percent in 2000-2009, to 29 percent in 2010-2019, to 47 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland in the county is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
