Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.09 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,908 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

The census value of land and buildings came to $7,859 an acre in 2022, up 20.0% from 2017, and sits just under the state median of $7,956 at 39th lowest of 87. Corn carries more weight here, with a 14-yr trend of 206.5 bu that ranks 11th highest of 81, though the 2025 crop came in at 188.9 bu.
