USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 6, cash rent in Mahoning County ran $102 an acre in 2026, up 28.3% from $80 in 2016 and still under the $168 state median, 22nd lowest of 76. The census value of land and buildings was $8,291 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from $6,626 in 2017 and a bit over the $7,956 state median. Corn trends at 171.4 bu for 2026, which sits 20th lowest of 81 in the state.
