USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Williams County runs $206 an acre in 2026, up 44.1% from $143 in 2014 and 21st highest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $7,565 an acre in 2022, up 46.6% from $5,161 in 2017, which still sits 33rd lowest of 87 against a state median of $7,956. Corn trends at 193.2 bu for 2026, and 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
