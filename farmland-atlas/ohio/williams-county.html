Non-irrigated cash rent in Williams County averages $206 per acre for 2026, up 44.1 percent from 2014. Census land and buildings value stood at $7,565 per acre in 2022, after a 46.6 percent rise from 2017, 7.9 percent a year. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 167.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 96.1 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood drove 47 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, the largest single cause, while heat and drought's share moved 23 percent, 36 percent, 23 percent, 36 percent across the four decades listed. Drought weeks stayed low, 32 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 9 of those in 2021-2025.
