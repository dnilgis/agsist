$51

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 13.5% from $45 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Harrison County runs $51 an acre for 2026, up 13.5% from $45 in 2016, and that sits tied for 4th lowest of 76 against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,421 an acre in 2022, up 63.8% from $3,310 in 2017. The corn trend projects 165.9 bu for 2026, below the state median of 192.3 bu.
