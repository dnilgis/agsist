Cash rent in Clinton County runs $170 an acre for 2026, up 8.3% from $157 in 2016 and about even with the state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $8,247 an acre in 2022, up 38.4% from $5,958 in 2017. Corn is where this county pulls ahead, with a 14 year trend yield of 219.2 bu projected for 2026, 3rd highest of 81 in Ohio. In 2025 it made 206.7 bu.
