Portage County, Ohio

Portage County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $82 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,439 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 144.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39133. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Portage County runs $82 per acre, up 8.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $7,439 per acre, up 14.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 2.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 144.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 110.5 in 2015. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 37 percent in 1989-1999, 15 percent in 2000-2009, 12 percent in 2010-2019, and 48 percent in 2020-2025, while excess moisture and flood remains the largest overall cause at 62 percent.
