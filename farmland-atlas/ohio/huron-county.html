Non-irrigated cash rent in Huron County is $187 per acre in 2026, up 45 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 stood at $7,432 per acre, up 22.2 percent from 2017, 4.1 percent a year. July nightly lows rose from a 62.1 F normal to 63.7 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.55 F per decade warming trend since 1976. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, so this reads mainly as a rain-fed county.
