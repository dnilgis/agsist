As of Oct 16, cash rent in Huron County runs $187 an acre in 2026, up 45.0% from $129 in 2016 and 31st highest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $7,432 an acre in 2022, up 22.2% from $6,083 in 2017, which still sits 30th lowest of 87 against a state median of $7,956. Corn trends at 193.9 bu for 2026, a touch over the state median of 192.3.
