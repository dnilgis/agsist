Non-irrigated cash rent in Seneca County runs $178 per acre in 2026, up 17.1 percent from 2014. Land value carries at $7,606 per acre, the operator's own estimate, rising 29.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 3.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2024, with a median of 166.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 114.4 in 2012. No irrigation is in use, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
