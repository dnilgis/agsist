Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 42.8% from $152 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wyandot County runs $217 an acre for 2026, up 42.8% from $152 in 2016 and tied for 14th highest of 76 in Ohio, against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $7,721 an acre in 2022, up 35.3% from $5,706 in 2017, which sits 36th lowest of 87. Corn trends at 200.3 bu for 2026, 20th highest of 81, though 2025 came in at 187.6 bu.
