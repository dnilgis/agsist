Wyandot County, Ohio

Wyandot County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $217 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,721 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39175. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wyandot County runs $217 per acre in 2026, up 42.8 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $7,721 per acre, up 35.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.2 percent a year. Corn yields hold a median of 172.4 bushels per acre from 2008-2025, with a fitted trend of 2.8 bushels per acre a year and a worst year of 123.2 in 2012. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows $77.4 million in indemnities, led by excess moisture and flood at 46 percent; drought weeks stay rare, 8 of 1357 from 2000-2025. July nights have warmed from a 62.6 F normal to 64.6 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.57 F per decade rise since 1976.
