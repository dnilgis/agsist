Cash rent in Carroll County runs $65 an acre for 2026, down 1.5% from $66 in 2016, and 11th lowest of 76 against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,992 an acre in 2022, up 27.2% from $4,710 in 2017. Corn here is thin ground by Ohio standards, with a trend yield of 134.9 bu that sits 2nd lowest of 81.
