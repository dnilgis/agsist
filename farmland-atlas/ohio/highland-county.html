Highland County, Ohio

Highland County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $173 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,081 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Highland County runs $173 per acre in 2026, up 37.3 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 174.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 118.4 in 2012. Heat and drought stands as the largest cause of indemnities across 1989-2025 at 41 percent, and its share by period reads 37 percent, 33 percent, 18 percent, then 56 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low overall, 28 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 7 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No cropland in the county is irrigated. Solar operating capacity is 667 MW, with 200 MW more proposed; wind is not present.
