Madison County, Ohio

Madison County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $237 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,260 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39097. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County runs $237 per acre, up 24.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value sits at $9,260 per acre, up 33.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 2.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 175.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 140.9 in 2012. Drought has been light: 7 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 4 of those in 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities fell from 59 percent in 1989-1999 to 47 percent in 2000-2009 to 35 percent in 2010-2019, then rose to 50 percent in 2020-2025. Solar operating capacity stands at 957.2 MW, with 155 MW solar proposed to EIA.
