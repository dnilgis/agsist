Non-irrigated cash rent in Pike County averages $147 per acre in 2026, up 28.9 percent from 2016. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 0.1 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 170.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 128.7 in 2024. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 51 percent, and its share rose from 38 percent in 1989-1999 to 47 percent in 2000-2009, dropped to 36 percent in 2010-2019, then rose to 63 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.98, higher than the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.79. Land value per acre fell 6.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
