Cash rent in Pike County runs $147 an acre for 2026, up 28.9% from $114 in 2016 and under the state median of $168. The 2022 census value of land and buildings came in at $3,899 an acre, down 6.2% from $4,156 in 2017, and that ranks 4th lowest of 87 in Ohio. Corn trends at 162.1 bu for 2026, 12th lowest of 81, against a state median of 192.3 bu.
