Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 76.5% from $68 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Tuscarawas County runs $120 an acre for 2026, up 76.5% from $68 in 2016, and tied for 25th lowest of 76 in Ohio against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $7,082 an acre in 2022, up 27.5% from $5,555 in 2017. Corn trends at 167.2 bu for 2026, 18th lowest of 81 in the state, and 35% of farmland was rented in 2022.
