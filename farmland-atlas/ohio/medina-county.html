Medina County, Ohio

Rain is at 101% of normal. Land and buildings run $9,400 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.01 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 38.66 inches, 101% of normal (38.39 inches) through Nov 18, at Brunswick, 0.5 mi NE. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,778 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,400 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $99.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 35.4% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 45% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 71.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.01 per base acre, PLC $3.11.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (124 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.36 per base acre, PLC $8.31. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $75.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.97 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.45, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. 1.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 65.2%.
