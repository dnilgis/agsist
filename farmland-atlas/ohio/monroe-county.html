Cash rent in Monroe County runs $27 an acre for 2024, up 10.4% from $24 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $4,184 an acre in 2022, up 28.0% from 2017, and that sits 6th lowest of 87 against a state median of $7,956. The corn trend is 118.8 bu, lowest of 81 in Ohio. Only 15% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
