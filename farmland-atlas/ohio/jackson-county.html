Jackson County, Ohio

Rain is at 101% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,273 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.82 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.36 inches, 101% of normal (40.01 inches) through Nov 17, at Jackson, 3 mi NW.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,273 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $74 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.82 per base acre, PLC $2.78.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $54.20 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.04 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.13 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 93% of the median yield. 0.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).
