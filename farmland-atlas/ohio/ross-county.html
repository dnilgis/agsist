Non-irrigated cash rent in Ross County averages $174 per acre for 2026, up 16.8 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $6,424 per acre, with a change of 40.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 1.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 174.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 123 in 2008. Heat and drought drove 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and the share moved 65 percent in 1989-1999, 41 percent in 2000-2009, 54 percent in 2010-2019, 61 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low at 24 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2024 the worst year at 9 weeks.
