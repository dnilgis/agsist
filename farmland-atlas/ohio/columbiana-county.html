Columbiana County, Ohio

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,379 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.03 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 45.18 inches, 116% of normal (38.82 inches) through Nov 18, at Hanoverton, 0.4 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 177 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,679 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,379 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $94 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 24.5% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 30% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 162.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $3.84 at Deerfield, 24 miles.

2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.70 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.49, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 6.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 40.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.9%.
