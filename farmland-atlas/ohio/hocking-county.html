Hocking County, Ohio

Hocking County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $101 an acre (2025); land and buildings $6,488 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39073. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hocking County averages $101 per acre in 2025, up 8.6 percent from 2014. Corn yields show a median of 153.6 bushels per acre over 2008-2022, with a worst year of 110 in 2012. July nights average 63.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.3 F, part of a warming trend of 0.45 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been rare: 17 of 1357 weeks since 2000 crossed the severe threshold, 13 of them in 2024. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
