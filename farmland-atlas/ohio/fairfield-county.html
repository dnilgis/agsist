Fairfield County, Ohio

Fairfield County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $188 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,551 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $188 per acre, up 34.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $7,551 per acre, though that figure fell 2 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 64.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.9 F, with no irrigation recorded on harvested cropland in the 2022 census.
