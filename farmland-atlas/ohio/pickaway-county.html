Pickaway County, Ohio

Pickaway County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $218 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,962 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39129. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pickaway County runs $218 per acre, up 31.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value in 2022 was $8,962 per acre, up 48.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 174.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 93.9 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 61 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and its share by period was 74 percent in 1989-1999, 48 percent in 2000-2009, 62 percent in 2010-2019, and 62 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were concentrated late, with 18 of the 27 weeks since 2000 falling in 2021-2025.
