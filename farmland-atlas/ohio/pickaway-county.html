As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pickaway County runs $218 an acre for 2026, tied for 12th highest of 76 in Ohio and well above the state median of $168. That rent is up 31.3% from $166 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,962 an acre in 2022, up 48.5% from $6,033 in 2017. 64% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: NASS county corn yields. The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008.
