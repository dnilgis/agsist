Quick answers for Clark County

What is cash rent in Clark County, Ohio?

The county average is $247 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Clark County, Ohio?

Land and buildings were worth $9,200 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Clark County, Ohio?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 208.1 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
