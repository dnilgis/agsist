Morgan County, Ohio

Morgan County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $56 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,363 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 140.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39115. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Morgan County averages $56 per acre for 2026, up 28.7 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 2.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 140.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 103.9 in 2012. July nights have warmed to an average low of 63.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62 F, continuing a trend of 0.56 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought made up 38 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009, 24 percent in 2010-2019, and 67 percent in 2020-2025, while excess moisture and flood remains the largest cause of losses over 1989-2025 at 52 percent. Solar and wind capacity here are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
