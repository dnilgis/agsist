Cropland in Morgan County rents for $56 an acre in 2026, up 28.7% from $44 in 2016 and tied for 8th lowest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $4,363 an acre in 2022, up 26.2% from $3,458 in 2017 and 10th lowest of 87 against a state median of $7,956. Corn runs on a 14-yr trend of 163.1 bu, 13th lowest of 81 in the state.
