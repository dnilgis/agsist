Lucas County, Ohio

Lucas County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $175 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,863 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 177.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39095. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lucas County is $175 per acre for 2026, up 26.8 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $8,863 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 7.1 percent, 1.4 percent a year. The 2022 rent-to-value figure sits at 1.94 percent. Crop insurance data show excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities across 1989-2025, at 51 percent, with a loss ratio of 0.58 over those closed crop years. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 32 percent in 1989-1999, to 27 percent in 2000-2009, to 7 percent in 2010-2019, to 36 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse, 2021-2025, totaled 25, out of 28 for the full 2000-2025 span.
