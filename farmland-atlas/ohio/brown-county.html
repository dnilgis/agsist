Brown County, Ohio

Brown County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $161 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,850 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Brown County runs $161 per acre, up 43.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $5,850 per acre, up 32.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield trend runs 2.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 172.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 123.8 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood account for 53 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, more than heat and drought in any of the listed periods. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 29 of 1357 from 2000-2025, and none from 2021-2025. Irrigated cropland share, 2022 census, is 0 percent.
