Cash rent in Brown County runs $161 an acre for 2026, up 43.8% from $112 in 2016 and a bit under the $168 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $5,850 an acre in 2022, up 32.7% from $4,408 in 2017, and that ranks 21st lowest of 87 in Ohio. Corn trends at 187.4 bu for 2026, against 163.3 bu last year.
