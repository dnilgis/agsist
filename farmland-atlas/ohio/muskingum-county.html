Stands out against its own record

$120

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 55.8% from $77 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Muskingum County rents for $120 an acre in 2026, up 55.8% from $77 in 2016. That rent is tied for 25th lowest of 76 in Ohio, under the state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $5,530 an acre in 2022, up 34.8% from $4,101 in 2017, and 18th lowest of 87 in the state. Corn ran 184.4 bu in 2025, a little over the 14-yr trend of 183.2 bu.
