Cash rent in Geauga County runs $66 an acre for 2026, up 33.7% from $49 in 2016 and still 13th lowest of 76 in Ohio against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $9,272 an acre in 2022, up 28.0% from $7,245 in 2017 and 18th highest of 87. Corn trends to 176.0 bu for 2026, under the state median of 192.3, and 25% of farmland was rented in 2022.
