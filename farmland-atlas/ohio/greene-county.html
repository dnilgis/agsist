Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 26.3% from $179 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Greene County runs $226 an acre for 2026, up 26.3% from $179 in 2016 and tied for 8th highest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $9,290 an acre in 2022, up 30.1% from $7,140 in 2017. Corn holds up its end too, with a 14-yr trend of 208.7 bu that sits 9th highest of 81 against a state median of 192.3 bu.
