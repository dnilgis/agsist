As of Oct 16, cash rent in Scioto County runs $107 an acre for 2026, up 71.2% from $63 in 2016, and that is 24th lowest of 76 against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $4,857 an acre in 2022, up 54.8% from $3,138 in 2017 and 13th lowest of 87 in Ohio. Corn trends at 183.4 bu for 2026, and 28% of farmland was rented in 2022.
