Excess moisture and flood is the story here. Insurance payouts went to that cause 69 percent of the time from 1989 to 2025, totaling $52.7 million. Corn yields have climbed 3 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2023, with a median of 158.1 bushels per acre across those years. Heat and drought claims fell from 32 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 20 percent in 2020-2025. Cash rent is $137 per acre in 2026, up 45 percent from 2016.
