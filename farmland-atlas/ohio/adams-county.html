Adams County, Ohio

Adams County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $136 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,254 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 150.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Adams County runs $136 per acre in 2026, up 57.2 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census sits at $5,254 per acre, up 35.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.2 percent a year. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 150.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 80.5 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 49 percent of indemnities paid since 1989, and that share moved 44 percent, 53 percent, 31 percent, 62 percent across the decades listed. Drought weeks were rare overall, 25 of 1357 weeks since 2000, and none from 2021-2025. Solar and wind capacity: none operating, none proposed.
