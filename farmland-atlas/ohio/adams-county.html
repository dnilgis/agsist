Cropland in Adams County rents for $136 an acre in 2026, up 57.2% from $87 in 2016 and below the Ohio median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $5,254 an acre in 2022, up 35.3% from 2017, and that sits 15th lowest of 87 in the state. Corn trends at 163.3 bu for 2026, well under the state median of 192.3 bu. Only 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
