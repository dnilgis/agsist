Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 28.2% from $163 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Crawford County runs $209 an acre in 2026, up 28.2% from $163 in 2016 and 19th highest of 76 in Ohio. The census value of land and buildings was $8,056 an acre in 2022, up 25.0% from $6,446 in 2017, close to the state median of $7,956. Corn is where this county stands out, with a 14-yr trend of 216.7 bu, 4th highest of 81 in the state. 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
