Ashtabula County, Ohio

Ashtabula County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $53 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,435 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 160.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39007. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent averages $53 per acre in 2026, down 27.9 percent from 2016. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, was $5,435 per acre in 2022, up 24.3 percent from 2017, 4.4 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 160.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 114.6 in 2015. No cropland is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
