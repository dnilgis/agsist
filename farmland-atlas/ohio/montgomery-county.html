$215

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 40.5% from $153 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Montgomery County runs $215 an acre for 2026, up 40.5% from $153 in 2016 and 16th highest of 76 in Ohio, where the state median is $168. The census value of land and buildings was $7,918 an acre in 2022, up 9.8% from $7,214 in 2017, or 1.9% a year. Corn trends at 195.2 bu for 2026, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
