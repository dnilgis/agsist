Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 18.6% from $183 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Champaign County runs $217 an acre for 2026, up 18.6% from $183 in 2016 and tied for 14th highest of 76 in Ohio, where the state median is $168. The census value of land and buildings was $9,173 an acre in 2022, up 27.9% from $7,170 in 2017, or 5.1% a year. Corn trends at 198.8 bu, a touch over the state median of 192.3 bu.
