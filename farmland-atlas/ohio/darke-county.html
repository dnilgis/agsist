Darke County, Ohio

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (18 counties), OSU survey 2026: top cropland $20,286, average $15,941, bottom $12,640 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.22 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.54 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 114% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 183 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,905 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Loss ratio 0.29, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 57% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025.
