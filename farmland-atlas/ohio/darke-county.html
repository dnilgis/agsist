Corn yield, all practices, 2023: the highest of 16 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2023

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Darke County was $287 an acre in 2025, up 48.7% from $193 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,913 an acre in 2022, up 31.2% from 2017. That census value ranks 6th highest of 87 in Ohio, against a state median of $7,956. The corn trend projects 222.6 bu for 2026, and that trend yield ranks 2nd highest of 81.
