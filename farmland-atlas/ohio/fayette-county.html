Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 34.8% from $178 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Fayette County runs $240 an acre for 2026, up 34.8% from $178 in 2016. That rent is 4th highest of 76 in Ohio, well over the state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $8,758 an acre in 2022, up 22.6% from $7,142 in 2017. Renters work 58% of the farmland here as of 2022.
