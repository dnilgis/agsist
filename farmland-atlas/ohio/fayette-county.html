Fayette County, Ohio

Fayette County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $240 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,758 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 180.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39047. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $240 per acre in 2026, up 34.8 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $8,758 per acre, having climbed 22.6 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 63 F to 64.7 F over 2017-2026, with a longer trend of 0.41 F per decade since 1976, warming. No cropland here was irrigated as of the 2022 census. Heat and drought stand as the largest cause of indemnities since 1989, at 45 percent.
