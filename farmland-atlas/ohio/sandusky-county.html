Cash rent in Sandusky County runs $201 an acre for 2026, up 32.2% from $152 in 2016 and 23rd highest of 76 against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,839 an acre in 2022, up 32.3% from $5,926 in 2017, which sits 38th lowest of 87. Corn trends at 199.1 bu, 22nd highest of 81, and 43% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
