Sandusky County, Ohio

Sandusky County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $201 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,839 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 170.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39143. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sandusky County averages $201 per acre in 2026, up 32.2 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $7,839 per acre in 2022, after rising 32.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield trend runs 2.6 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 170.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 132 in 2008. Excess moisture and flood account for 50 percent of the $52.6 million in crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, closed crop years, while heat and drought shares moved from 45 percent in 1989-1999 to 43 percent in 2000-2009, down to 10 percent in 2010-2019, then up to 39 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
