Coshocton County cash rent sits at $95 an acre for 2026, up 2.7% from $93 in 2016 and ranked 20th lowest of 76 against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings ran $6,755 an acre in 2022, up 28.0% from $5,279 in 2017, or 5.1% a year. The 14-yr corn trend comes to 175.4 bu for 2026, ranked 23rd lowest of 81 next to a state median of 192.3 bu.
