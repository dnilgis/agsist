Land

Land and buildings: $9,808 an acre in the 2022 census.

OSU's survey covers western Ohio's northwest and southwest regions only, so no figure for this county. Western Ohio Cropland Values and Cash Rents 2025-26, Ohio State University Extension, survey of January to April 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $127 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.58 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 10% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 15.5% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 34% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 185.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 184 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Holmes County

What is cash rent in Holmes County, Ohio?

The county average is $127 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Holmes County, Ohio?

Land and buildings were worth $9,808 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Holmes County, Ohio?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 164.9 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
