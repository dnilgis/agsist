Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 15.5% from $110 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Holmes County cash rent is $127 an acre for 2026, up 15.5% from $110 in 2016 and 27th lowest of 76 against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings ran $9,808 an acre in 2022, up 26.7% from $7,741 in 2017, and that is 12th highest of 87 in Ohio. The corn yield trend for 2026 sits at 164.9 bu, 15th lowest of 81 next to a state median of 192.3 bu.
