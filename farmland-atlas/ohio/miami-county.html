Miami County, Ohio

Rain is at 123% of normal. Land and buildings run $10,549 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.07 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 45.82 inches, 123% of normal (37.16 inches) through Nov 17, at Tipp City. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,001 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $10,549 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $212 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 30.9% from 2016 to 2026. 49.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 53.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 32.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,695 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 10 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.43 at Versailles, 18 miles. 7 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 30 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.07 per base acre, PLC $3.65.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (145.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $89.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 105 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.09 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.30 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 60% of the median yield. 1.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 52.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 19.9%.
