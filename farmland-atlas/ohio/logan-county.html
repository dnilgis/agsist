Logan County, Ohio

Logan County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $202 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,497 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 168.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39091. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Logan County averages $202 per acre in 2026, up 18.8 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 stood at $8,497 per acre, with a 40.4 percent change from 2017 to 2022, 7 percent a year. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 2.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 168.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 131 in 2008. Wind operating capacity is 253.2 MW; solar operating capacity is 0 MW.
