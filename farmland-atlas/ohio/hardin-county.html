As of Oct 16, cash rent in Hardin County runs $250 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 76 in Ohio and well over the $168 state median. That rent is up 34.4% from $186 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings reached $9,197 an acre in 2022, up 56.2% from $5,888 in 2017, which ranks 21st highest of 87. Corn sits close to the state, with a 14-yr trend of 193.1 bu against a 192.3 bu state median.
