Summit County, Ohio

Summit County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $80 an acre (2026); land and buildings $21,036 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39153. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 averages $80 per acre. Census land and buildings value in 2022 stood at $21,036 per acre, with land value change of 57.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 9.5 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal average low was 62.5 F, against 64.3 F over 2017-2026, with a trend of 0.68 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been light: 4 of 1357 weeks reached severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, none from 2021-2025. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 totaled $2.1 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 42 percent.
