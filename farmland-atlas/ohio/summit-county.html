As of Oct 6, Summit County cash rent sits at $80 an acre for 2026, well under the Ohio median of $168 and 15th lowest of 76. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story. It reached $21,036 an acre in 2022, highest of 87 in the state, up 57.4% from $13,365 in 2017, or 9.5% a year. Only 20% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
