Cash rent in Defiance County runs $177 an acre for 2026, up 18.8% from $149 in 2014. That puts it 34th highest of 76 in Ohio, a bit over the state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,824 an acre in 2022, up 36.4% from $5,736 in 2017, and 37th lowest of 87 in the state. 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Defiance County

What is cash rent in Defiance County, Ohio?

The county average is $177 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Defiance County, Ohio?

Land and buildings were worth $7,824 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Defiance County, Ohio?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 189.5 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
