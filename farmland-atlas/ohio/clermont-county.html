Cropland rent in Clermont County runs $129 an acre for 2026, under the Ohio median of $168 and tied for 28th lowest of 76. The census value of land and buildings was $7,599 an acre in 2022, up 35.6% from $5,605 in 2017, which works out to 6.3% a year. Corn has run a median 170.4 bu an acre from 2008 to 2022, and 45% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
