Clermont County, Ohio

Clermont County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $129 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,599 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 170.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 averages $129 per acre. Census land value in 2022 stood at $7,599 per acre, up 35.6 percent from 2017, or 6.3 percent a year. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, and water stress has been minor: 25 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000, with 0 weeks from 2021-2025. Excess moisture and flood, not heat, has driven claims, accounting for 39 percent of the $32.0 million paid out over 1989-2025; heat and drought's share fell from 50 percent in 1989-1999 to 16 percent in 2000-2009, then rose to 22 percent in 2010-2019 and 27 percent in 2020-2025. Solar operating capacity is 80 MW, with 0 MW proposed.
