Cash rent in Delaware County runs $190 an acre for 2026, up 38.7% from $137 in 2016 and above the state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $9,771 an acre in 2022, up 24.9% from $7,824 in 2017 and 13th highest of 87 in Ohio. Corn made 189.4 bu in 2025, and 57% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
