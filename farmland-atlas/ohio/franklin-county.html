Franklin County, Ohio

Franklin County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $218 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,462 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 166.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39049. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County averages $218 per acre in 2026, up 50.3 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $11,462 per acre, the operators' own estimate, up 42.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. The county corn yield trend runs 1.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2022, with a median of 166.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 131 in 2008. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 44 percent of $21.2 million paid out over 1989-2025, though drought weeks stayed low, 11 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025. July nightly lows have warmed, 64.1 F normal against 65.4 F over 2017-2026. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland.
