$208

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 24.6% from $167 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wood County runs $208 an acre for 2026, up 24.6% from $167 in 2016 and 20th highest of 76 in Ohio, above the state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,146 an acre in 2022, up 22.3% from $6,659 in 2017, which is 4.1% a year. Corn trends at 198.6 bu projected for 2026, and 52% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
