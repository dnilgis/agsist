Non-irrigated cash rent in Ottawa County averages $183 per acre in 2026, up 25.3 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $7,882 per acre, up 45.7 percent from 2017, 7.8 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 160.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 125.8 in 2015. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 26 of that toll in 2021-2025 alone, against 27 of 1357 weeks counted for the full 2000-2025 period. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
