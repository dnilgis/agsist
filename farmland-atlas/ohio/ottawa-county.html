USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Ottawa County runs $183 an acre for 2026, up 25.3% from $146 in 2016 and 32nd highest of 76 in Ohio, against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $7,882 an acre in 2022, up 45.7% from $5,409 in 2017, which sits a bit under the state median of $7,956. Corn came in at 196.0 bu in 2025, and 46% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
