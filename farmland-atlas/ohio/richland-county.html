As of Oct 16, cash rent in Richland County runs $165 an acre for 2026, up 42.2% from $116 in 2014. That sits just under the state median of $168, tied for 37th lowest of 76. The census value of land and buildings was $8,823 an acre in 2022, up 17.6% from $7,505 in 2017, and 28th highest of 87 in Ohio. Corn made 182.2 bu in 2025, against a 14-yr trend of 188.5 bu projected for 2026.
