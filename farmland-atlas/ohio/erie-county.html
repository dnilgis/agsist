Cash rent in Erie County runs $191 an acre for 2026, up 36.4% from $140 in 2016. That sits above the Ohio median of $168 and ranks 27th highest of 76. The census value of land and buildings came in at $9,030 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from $6,544 in 2017. Corn did its part too, with 204.3 bu in 2025 against a 2021-25 average of 190.2 bu.
