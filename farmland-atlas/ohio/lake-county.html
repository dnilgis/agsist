Cash rent in Lake County runs $116 an acre for 2025, and irrigated ground went for $211 in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $9,645 an acre in 2022, up 18.2% from $8,162 in 2017, or 3.4% a year. That sits above the state median of $7,956 and ranks 15th highest of 87. 33% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
