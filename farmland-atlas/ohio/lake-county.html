Lake County, OH: non-irrigated cash rent $116 an acre (2025); land and buildings $9,645 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.18 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 39085. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lake County runs $116 per acre, against a census land value of $9,645 per acre. July nights average 64.7 F for 2017-2026, up from 63.1 F in the 1991-2020 normal, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.67 F per decade, warming. Drought has been rare here: 7 weeks of severe drought or worse out of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, none from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
