USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Union County runs $194 an acre in 2026, up 14.8% from $169 in 2016 and 25th highest of 76 in Ohio, against a state median of $168. The census value of land and buildings was $8,151 an acre in 2022, up 28.4% from $6,350 in 2017. Corn sits on a 14-yr trend of 195.5 bu, a little over the state median of 192.3, and 44% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
