Cash rent in Knox County ran $149 an acre in 2025, up 39.3% from $107 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $7,956 an acre in 2022, up 31.6% from 2017, which puts it right on the Ohio median at 44th lowest of 87. The 14 year corn trend projects 191.3 bu for 2026, close to the state median of 192.3 bu, and 35% of farmland was rented in 2022.
