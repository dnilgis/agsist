Knox County, Ohio

Rain is at 107% of normal. Land and buildings run $7,956 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.29 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 41 inches, 107% of normal (38.43 inches) through Nov 17, at Fredericktown, 4 mi NNW. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,727 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $7,956 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $149 an acre (USDA, 2025). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 35.3% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 29.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 105.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,588 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 30 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.21 at Mt Gilead, 20 miles. 4 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 35 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 2 elevators.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.36 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.33 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36%.
