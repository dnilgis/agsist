Marion County, Ohio

Land and buildings run $8,262 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.25 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,913 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $8,262 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $195 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 16.8% from 2016 to 2026. 54.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 59.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 25.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 100 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,939 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 15 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 4 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.52 at Harpster, 13 miles. 13 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 30 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 6 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.25 per base acre, PLC $3.58.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (142.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $85.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 550 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.81 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.56 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51.5%.
