Trumbull County, Ohio

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,640 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.49 per base acre for corn.

Trumbull County vs Ohio: $82.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 21% of the other 75 Ohio counties with a 2026 rent (17th lowest of 76).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

6.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.52 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,645 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,640 an acre in the 2022 census.

OSU's survey covers western Ohio's northwest and southwest regions only, so no figure for this county. Western Ohio Cropland Values and Cash Rents 2025-26, Ohio State University Extension, survey of January to April 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $82.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 12, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 57% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 68.4% from 2016 to 2026. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 42% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 91.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 948 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.99 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.47, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 69% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 73%.

Quick answers for Trumbull County

What is cash rent in Trumbull County, Ohio?

The county average is $83 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Trumbull County, Ohio?

Land and buildings were worth $5,640 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Trumbull County, Ohio?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 192.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
