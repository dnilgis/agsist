Robeson County, North Carolina

Robeson County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $75 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,897 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 110.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37155. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Robeson County averages $75 per acre in 2026, up 36.4 percent from 2016. Land value carries at $3,897 per acre in the 2022 census, an operator estimate rather than a sale price, up 17.6 percent from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance losses ran hot relative to premium over the closed years 1989-2025, with a loss ratio of 0.99, and over 2016-2025 the ratio rose to 1.15, with $17.73 paid per $100 of insured value. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved unevenly across periods: 32 percent in 1989-1999, 61 percent in 2000-2009, 44 percent in 2010-2019, 26 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 71.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 70.6 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.57 F per decade since 1976.
