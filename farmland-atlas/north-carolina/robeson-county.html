Robeson County cash rent was $75 an acre in 2026, up 36.4% from $55 in 2016, and 40th lowest of 86 in North Carolina against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $3,897 an acre in 2022, up 17.6% from $3,315 in 2017, which puts it 10th lowest of 92 in the state. Corn made 137.2 bu in 2025, above the long run county median of 110.5 bu.
