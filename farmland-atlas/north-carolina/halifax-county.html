Halifax County, North Carolina

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,339 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.65 per base acre for corn.

Halifax County vs North Carolina: $121 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 84% of the other 85 North Carolina counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 14th highest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.67 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 67% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 228 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,543 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,339 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $121 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.72 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 55% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 9% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 22.2% from 2016 to 2026. 49% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 22% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 34.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 173 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,166 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 1,126 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.65 per base acre, PLC $1.97.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (78.65 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $16.04 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.38 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30.86 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.05 per base acre, PLC $6.96. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.56 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $58.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 107 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.21 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.67, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 53% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.7%.

Quick answers for Halifax County

What is cash rent in Halifax County, North Carolina?

The county average is $121 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Halifax County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $4,339 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Halifax County, North Carolina?

The county's 2019-23 average is 124.8 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
