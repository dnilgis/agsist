Cash rent in Halifax County runs $121 an acre in 2026, up 22.2% from $99 in 2016 and tied for 14th highest of 86 in North Carolina, where the state median is $81. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,339 an acre in 2022, up 69.1% from $2,566 in 2017, yet it still sits 25th lowest of 92 against a state median of $5,711. Corn made 128.5 bu in 2023, and 49% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
