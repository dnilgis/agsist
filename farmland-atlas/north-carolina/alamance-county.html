Cash rent in Alamance County runs $53 an acre for 2026, up 69.4% from $31 in 2016, and that sits 20th lowest of 86 against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,217 an acre in 2022, up 36.9% from $6,000 in 2017, and ranks 13th highest of 92. Corn made 150.7 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 140.3 bu.
