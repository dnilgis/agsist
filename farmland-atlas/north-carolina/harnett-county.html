Harnett County, North Carolina

Non-irrigated cash rent in Harnett County averages $121 per acre in 2026, up 26 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $6,229 per acre, with a change of 11.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 2.2 percent a year. Corn yields show no trend over 2008-2025, holding at a median of 107 bushels per acre, though 2024 fell to 43.2. Heat and drought drove 38 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, with the share at 25 percent in 1989-1999, 49 percent in 2000-2009, 38 percent in 2010-2019, and 37 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.55 F per decade since 1976, and irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
