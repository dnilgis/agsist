Wayne County, North Carolina

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.39 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 29.58 inches, 62% of normal (47.66 inches) through Nov 14, at Goldsboro, 4.4 mi E. Frost-free season: 233 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,699 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $134 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 40.3% from 2016 to 2026. 57% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 10% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 47.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 80 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 194 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.39 per base acre, PLC $2.24.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (89.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $59.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 10 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.94 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.03, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 43% of the median yield. 2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 26.5%.
