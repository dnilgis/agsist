Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County averages $134 per acre for 2026, up 40.3 percent from 2016. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, so most of this rent tracks non-irrigated ground. Heat and drought drove 37 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and this share was 29 percent in 1989-1999, 52 percent in 2000-2009, 33 percent in 2010-2019, and 37 percent in 2020-2025. The corn yield trend on 2008-2025 runs 2.4 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 107.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 46 in 2008.
