Cash rent in Vance County runs $47 an acre for 2025, up 2.2% from $46 in 2014. That is about as flat as rent gets over that stretch. The census value of land and buildings was $4,200 an acre in 2022, up 41.0% from $2,978 in 2017, and it sits under the state median of $5,711 at 20th lowest of 92. Renters work 40% of the farmland here as of 2022.
