Brunswick County, North Carolina

Brunswick County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $78 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,930 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 119.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Brunswick County runs $78 per acre, up 14.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value sits at $4,930 per acre, up 25.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. The county corn yield trend is 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 119.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 40 in 2011. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 72.8 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.5 F, part of a warming trend of 0.58 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought drove 43 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2010-2019, the highest of the periods given, and only 4 percent of harvested cropland here is irrigated.
