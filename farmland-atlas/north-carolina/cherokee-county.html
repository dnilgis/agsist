Farmland in Cherokee County rented for $74 an acre in 2026, tied for 37th lowest of 86 and a bit under the state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $6,855 an acre in 2022, up 39.7% from $4,906 in 2017, and 28th highest of 92. Corn does well here, with a trend yield of 186.4 bu that ranks 2nd highest of 37 against a state median of 134.6 bu.
