Cash rent in Perquimans County runs $112 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $95 in 2016 and above the state median of $81, tied for 22nd highest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $3,918 an acre in 2022, up 10.1% from $3,560 in 2017, and that sits 12th lowest of 92 against a state median of $5,711. Corn trends at 148.0 bu, 12th highest of 37, and 69% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
