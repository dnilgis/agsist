Macon County, North Carolina

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,663 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.39 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 47.12 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 81% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,583 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,663 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $53.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.41 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 176.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.39 per base acre, PLC $2.24.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (89.09 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 25, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.69 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $37.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 3 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1992 to 2009; too much rain caused 35.7%.

Cropland in Macon County rented for $54 an acre in 2025, and pasture went for $23. The census value of land and buildings was $8,663 an acre in 2022, up 8.7% from $7,966 in 2017, or 1.7% a year. That puts the county 10th highest of 92 in North Carolina, well above the state median of $5,711. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Macon County

What is cash rent in Macon County, North Carolina?

The county average is $54 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Macon County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $8,663 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
