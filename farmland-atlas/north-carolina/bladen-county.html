Cash rent in Bladen County is $83 an acre for 2026, down 7.3% from $89 in 2016. That ranks 43rd highest of 86, a hair over the state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $4,093 an acre in 2022, up 22.8% from $3,334 in 2017, and still ranks 17th lowest of 92 in the state. Corn made 134.3 bu in 2025, well above the county median of 111.9 bu going back to 2008.
