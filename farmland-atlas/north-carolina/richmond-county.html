Stands out against its own record

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Richmond County runs $49 an acre for 2025, up 27.6% from $38 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,944 an acre in 2022, up 13.3% from $4,365 in 2017, or 2.5% a year. That sits under the state median of $5,711 and ranks 34th lowest of 92. Only 13% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
