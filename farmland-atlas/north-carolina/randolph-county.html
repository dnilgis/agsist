Randolph County, North Carolina

Randolph County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $68 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,225 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 112.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37151. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Randolph County averages $68 per acre in 2026, up 58.1 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $6,225 per acre, up 23.2 percent from 2017, 4.3 percent a year. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 1.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 112.5 bushels per acre and a low of 48.9 in 2024. Drought weeks fell sharply in the most recent stretch: 131 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, but only 1 such week occurred in 2021-2025.
