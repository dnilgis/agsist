Cabarrus County, North Carolina

Cabarrus County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,055 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 125.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cabarrus County averages $70 per acre in 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 125.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 58.4 in 2015. Heat and drought caused 55 percent of $6.9 million in crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.05, above the 1989-2025 figure of 0.92. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. July nightly lows have moved from a 68.4 F normal to a 69.1 F average over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.41 F per decade since 1976.
