Mecklenburg County, North Carolina

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.43 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 28.96 inches, 75% of normal (38.59 inches) through Nov 17, at Charlotte Douglas Airport. Frost-free season: 225 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,609 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land: no recorded sale median, census value or published rent for this county.

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 198.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 206 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 2 days a year and damaging wind on 9.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 27.8%.
