Henderson County, North Carolina

Henderson County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $117 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,065 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37089. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henderson County is $117 per acre in 2026, up 33 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $190 per acre. Land value in the 2022 census sits at $11,065 per acre, up 42.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.4 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $223.2 million, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 55 percent; heat and drought stayed a small share across each decade shown, from 4 percent in 1989-1999 to 1 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 210 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with only 8 weeks in 2021-2025. Solar operating capacity is 7 MW; no wind is operating or proposed.
