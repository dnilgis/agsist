Cash rent in Henderson County runs $117 an acre for 2026, up 33.0% from $88 in 2016 and 17th highest of 86 in North Carolina, where the median is $81. The census value of land and buildings came in at $11,065 an acre in 2022, up 42.7% from $7,756 in 2017 and 3rd highest of 92 in the state. Corn made 180.9 bu in 2023, well above the county median of 156.1 bu since 2008.
