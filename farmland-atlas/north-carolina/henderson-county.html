Henderson County, North Carolina

Rain is at 92% of normal. Land and buildings run $11,065 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.82 per base acre for corn.

Henderson County vs North Carolina: $117 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 81% of the other 85 North Carolina counties with a 2026 rent (17th highest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 49.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,845 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $11,065 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $117 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.53 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 9% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 7% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 33% from 2016 to 2026. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 72.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 128 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.82 per base acre, PLC $3.21.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (127.96 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $81.48 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $28.28 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $37.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $55.53 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.28, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 53% of the median yield. 14.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 3.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.5%.

Quick answers for Henderson County

What is cash rent in Henderson County, North Carolina?

The county average is $117 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $190 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Henderson County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $11,065 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Henderson County, North Carolina?

The county's 2008-2023 median is 156.1 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
