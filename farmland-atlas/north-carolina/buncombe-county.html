Stands out against its own record

19 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2008 (53 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Buncombe County runs $46 an acre for 2026, down 8.0% from $50 in 2016 and 12th lowest of 86 counties in the state. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $11,802 an acre in 2022, up 18.6% from $9,949 in 2017 and 2nd highest of 92. Corn came in at 116.0 bu in 2024, under the 2020-24 average of 138.1 bu.
