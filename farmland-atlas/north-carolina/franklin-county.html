Cash rent in Franklin County is $66 an acre in 2026, tied for 26th lowest of 86 and under the state median of $81. That is up 11.9% from $59 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,829 an acre in 2022, up 63.9% from $3,556 in 2017 and a bit over the state median of $5,711. Corn made 126.2 bu in 2025, above the county median of 108.2 bu going back to 2008.
