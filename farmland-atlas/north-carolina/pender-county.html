Pender County, North Carolina

Pender County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $93 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,274 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pender County stands at $93 per acre, up 50 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent in 2021 was $150 per acre, and only 13 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. Land value is $5,274 per acre, down 1.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights have warmed, averaging 71.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 70.6 F normal, with a trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities ran 8 percent in 1989-1999, 32 percent in 2000-2009, 34 percent in 2010-2019, and 16 percent in 2020-2025.
