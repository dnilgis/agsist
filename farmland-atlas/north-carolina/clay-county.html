Clay County, North Carolina

Clay County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $65 an acre (2024); land and buildings $6,386 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37043. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clay County averages $65 per acre in 2024. Census value of land and buildings runs $6,386 per acre, up 1.7 percent from 2017-2022, or 0.3 percent a year. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. The July nightly low has warmed 0.53 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 62.6 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 61 F; no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Drought was heaviest in 2008, with 53 weeks at severe or worse, against 16 weeks in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
