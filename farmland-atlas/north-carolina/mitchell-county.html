Non-irrigated cash rent in Mitchell County runs $56 per acre, against a census land value of $6,687 per acre. July nights average 60 F over 2017-2026, up from a 59.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.3 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Drought weeks ran 138 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2021-2025 at 0 weeks. Crop insurance losses from 1989-2012 totaled $4.8 million, mostly excess moisture and flood at 39 percent; heat and drought's share fell from 45 percent in 1989-1999 to 26 percent in 2000-2009 and 29 percent in 2010-2019. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
