As of Oct 16, cash rent in Mitchell County runs $56 an acre for 2026, 22nd lowest of 86 in North Carolina against a state median of $81. Pasture goes for $35 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $6,687 an acre in 2022, up 15.0% from $5,815 in 2017, or 2.8% a year, and that sits 31st highest of 92 in the state. In 2022, 27% of the farmland here was rented.
