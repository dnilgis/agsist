Surry County, North Carolina

Surry County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $92 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,529 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 144.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37171. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Surry County averages $92 per acre in 2026, up 23.5 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $6,529 per acre in 2022, after a change of 46 percent from 2017 to 2022. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, so most ground here answers to rain. Heat and drought account for 38 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share moved from 26 percent in 1989-1999 to 39 percent in 2000-2009 to 42 percent in 2010-2019 to 36 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed crop years is 1.28. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 64.6 F to 66.1 F over 2017-2026, with the 2020s the first decade the average low reached 66 F.
