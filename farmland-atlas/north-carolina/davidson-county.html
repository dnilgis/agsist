Non-irrigated cash rent in Davidson County is $50 per acre, up 40.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in 2022 was $7,144 per acre, up 23.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights have warmed from a 67.7 F normal to a 68.7 F average, 2017-2026, and drought weeks fell to 2 in 2021-2025 from 132 of 1357 weeks, 2000-2025. Solar operating capacity is 9 MW, with 135 MW proposed.
