U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Watauga County was $62 an acre in 2026, down 1.6% from $63 in 2016 and under the state median of $81. That puts it tied for 24th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, $8,870 an acre in 2022, up 37.8% from $6,439 in 2017 and 7th highest of 92 in the state. 34% of farmland was rented in 2022.
