Cash rent in Davie County runs $67 an acre for 2026, up 21.8% from $55 in 2016 and under the state median of $81. That puts it tied for 28th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $5,867 an acre in 2022, up 15.3% from $5,089 in 2017, and 44th highest of 92. Corn trends at 146.6 bu, 13th highest of 37 in the state, after 161.7 bu in 2025.
