Dare County had 115 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nightly low in Dare County runs 73.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 72.4 F normal for 1991-2020. The trend since 1976 is 0.47 F per decade, warming. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 59 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 9 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
