Dare County, North Carolina

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.60 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the last spring freeze comes around Mar 12 in the median year; most years have no fall freeze before December. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,830 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land: no recorded sale median, census value or published rent for this county.

The 2022 census land value rose while next door fell; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.70 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Cattle: NASS withholds this county's count (D). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,059 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.60 per base acre, PLC $2.18.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (87 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.23 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (24.39 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.39 per base acre, PLC $7.70. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42.65 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $58.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

66.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 24.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1999 to 2025; too much rain caused 60.2%.
