Cash rent in Rutherford County runs $44 an acre for 2026, up 37.5% from $32 in 2016 and tied for 7th lowest of 86 in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,087 an acre in 2022, up 48.2% from $4,782 in 2017, which puts it 24th highest of 92. Corn is thin here, with a trend yield of 104.6 bu against a state median of 134.6 bu.
