Rutherford County, North Carolina

Rutherford County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $44 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,087 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 96.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37161. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rutherford County stands at $44 per acre in 2026, up 37.5 percent from 2016. Census land value reached $7,087 per acre in 2022, up 48.2 percent from 2017. Corn yields have trended up 1.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2012-2025, with a median of 96.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 75.2 in 2015. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities ran 6 percent in 1989-1999, 43 percent in 2000-2009, 25 percent in 2010-2019, and 16 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse fell from 212 of 1357 over 2000-2025 to 8 in 2021-2025.
