Cash rent in Lee County runs $80 an acre for 2026, up 13.6% from $70 in 2014 and just under the state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $7,066 an acre in 2022, up 22.6% from $5,764 in 2017, and that ranks 25th highest of 92. Corn made 107.2 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 87.9 bu for 2008 to 2025.
