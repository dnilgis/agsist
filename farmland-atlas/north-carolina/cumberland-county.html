Cash rent in Cumberland County runs $105 an acre for 2026, up 59.1% from $66 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $81 and ranks tied for 25th highest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $5,708 an acre in 2022, up 10.5% from $5,166 in 2017 and right about at the state median of $5,711. Corn came in at 69.3 bu in 2024, well under the 104.7 bu it averaged from 2020 to 2024.
