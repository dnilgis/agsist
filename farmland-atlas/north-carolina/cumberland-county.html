Cumberland County, North Carolina

Cumberland County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $105 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,708 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 99.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37051. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cumberland County averages $105 per acre for 2026, up 59.1 percent from 2016. Land value was $5,708 per acre in the 2022 census, up 10.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 1.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 99.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 55.5 in 2011. July nights average 71.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.7 F, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.47 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought caused 40 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, the largest single cause, with the loss ratio at 1.49 over that period and 1.42 over 2016-2025.
