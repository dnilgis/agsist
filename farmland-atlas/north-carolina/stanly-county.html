Cash rent in Stanly County runs $83 an acre for 2026, up 9.2% from $76 in 2016 and tied for 41st highest of 86 against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,580 an acre in 2022, up 21.3% from $4,599 in 2017, which ranks 44th lowest of 92. Corn trends at 116.0 bu, short of the state median of 134.6 bu and 13th lowest of 37.

Sources and terms

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
