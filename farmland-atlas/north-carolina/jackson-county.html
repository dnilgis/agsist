Jackson County, North Carolina

Jackson County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $59 an acre (2025); land and buildings $8,324 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37099. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land values rose 1.6 percent from 2017-2022, or 0.3 percent a year. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 59.2 F to 60.8 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.56 F per decade since 1976; no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought has touched the county in 193 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 8 weeks in 2021-2025. Crop insurance losses from 1995-2013 totaled $0.3 million, mostly from excess moisture and flood at 52 percent. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
