Jackson County, North Carolina

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,324 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 47.41 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 183 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,408 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,324 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $58.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.41 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.7% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 91.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 327 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $55.15 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $38.75 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $37.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2013; too much rain caused 52.3%.

Quick answers for Jackson County

What is cash rent in Jackson County, North Carolina?

The county average is $59 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Jackson County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $8,324 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
