Stands out against its own record

31 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2008 (46 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Jackson County rents for $59 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $8,324 an acre in 2022, up 1.6% from $8,195 in 2017, or 0.3% a year. That is still under the $9,656 the census showed in 2012. The 2022 figure sits well above the state median of $5,711 and ranks 12th highest of 92.

Sources and terms

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
