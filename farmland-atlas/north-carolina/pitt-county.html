Cash rent in Pitt County runs $123 an acre for 2026, up 33.7% from $92 in 2016, and that rent ranks 12th highest of 86 in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,126 an acre in 2022, up 7.3% from $3,845 in 2017, which sits 18th lowest of 92 in the state. Corn trends to 96.2 bu for 2026, 3rd lowest of 37, though last year made 123.9 bu.
