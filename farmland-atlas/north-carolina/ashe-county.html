Non-irrigated cash rent in Ashe County averages $92 per acre, up 1.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings carry a census value of $6,859 per acre, with the 2017-2022 change at 30.4 percent, 5.5 percent a year. The July nightly low has moved from a 58.1 F normal to 59.4 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.43 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been light in recent years: 4 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 112 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has swung across periods, from 48 percent in 1989-1999 to 31 percent in 2000-2009, 64 percent in 2010-2019, and 1 percent in 2020-2025.
