Cash rent in Wilson County runs $154 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 86 counties in North Carolina and well above the state median of $81. That rent is up 52.5% from $101 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $4,325 an acre in 2022, 22nd lowest of 92, under the state median of $5,711. Corn came in at 152.2 bu in 2025, against a 2021-25 average of 115.5 bu.

Sources and terms

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.
