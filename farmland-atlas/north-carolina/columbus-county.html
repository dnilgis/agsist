Cash rent in Columbus County sits at $86 an acre for 2026, up 0.6% from $85 in 2016, which is tied for 39th highest of 86 in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $3,906 an acre in 2022, up 19.7% from $3,264 in 2017, and that ranks 11th lowest of 92 in the state. Corn trends at 112.2 bu for 2026, below the state median of 134.6 bu.
