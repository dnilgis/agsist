As of Oct 14, cash rent in Pamlico County runs $99 an acre for 2026, up 18.7% from $83 in 2016 and above the state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $3,222 an acre in 2022, down 11.8% from $3,652 in 2017, and that ranks 3rd lowest of 92 in North Carolina. Corn did better, with 169.9 bu last year and a 14-yr trend of 144.7 bu that sits 14th highest of 37.
