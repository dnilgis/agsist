Pamlico County, North Carolina

Pamlico County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $99 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,222 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 144.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37137. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pamlico County averages $98 per acre for 2026, up 18.7 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,222 per acre in 2022, down 11.8 percent from 2017. Corn yields show a trend of 1.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 144.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 97.5 in 2011. July nights average 72.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 72 F, with a warming trend of 0.36 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance indemnities total $33.0 million for closed crop years 1989-2025, with wind the largest cause at 35 percent, and heat and drought shares by period were 26 percent, 30 percent, 27 percent, and 25 percent.
