Stands out against its own record

13 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Washington County runs $136 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th highest of 86 in North Carolina and well over the state median of $81. That is up 7.9% from $126 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,152 an acre in 2022, up 13.2% from 2017, yet still 19th lowest of 92 against a state median of $5,711. The corn trend sits at 154.5 bu, 9th highest of 37, and 70% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
