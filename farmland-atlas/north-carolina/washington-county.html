Washington County, North Carolina

Washington County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $136 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,152 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37187. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County averages $136 per acre in 2026, up 7.9 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry an operator estimate of $4,152 per acre, with a 13.2 percent change from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance losses since 1989 run to $77.1 million, and excess moisture and flood account for 41 percent of that, well ahead of heat and drought in any decade listed. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and most of that draws on groundwater. Solar operating capacity stands at 149.6 MW, with nothing proposed to EIA for solar or wind.
