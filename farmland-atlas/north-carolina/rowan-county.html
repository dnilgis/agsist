Rowan County cash rent runs $74 an acre for 2026, up 32.1% from $56 in 2016 and tied for 37th lowest of 86 against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $6,780 an acre in 2022, up 15.9% from 2017 and 30th highest of 92. The corn trend projects 118.8 bu for 2026, under the state median of 134.6 bu, and 39% of farmland was rented in 2022.
