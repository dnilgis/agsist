Sampson County, North Carolina

Sampson County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $120 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,166 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 109.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37163. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sampson County runs $120 per acre in 2026, up 23.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $125 per acre, and the irrigated premium has been falling, from 1.55 in 2009 to 1.04 in 2026. Only 6 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and corn yields have trended up 2.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 109.9 and a low of 50.3 in 2011. July nights average 70.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.2 F. Land value per the 2022 census sits at $5,166 per acre, up 5.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
