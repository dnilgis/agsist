Cash rent in Sampson County was $120 an acre in 2026, up 23.7% from $97 in 2016 and 16th highest of 86 in North Carolina, where the state median is $81. The census value of land and buildings was $5,166 an acre in 2022, up 5.6% from 2017, which works out to 1.1% a year and still sits under the state median of $5,711. Corn made 131.1 bu in 2025, well above the 17 year county median of 109.9 bu.
