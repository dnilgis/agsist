Cash rent in Tyrrell County runs $181 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 86 in North Carolina against a state median of $81. That is up 49.6% from $121 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, down 7.5% to $3,836 an acre in 2022 from $4,148 in 2017, and it sits 9th lowest of 92. Corn holds up well here, with a trend yield of 167.7 bu that ranks 5th highest of 37.
