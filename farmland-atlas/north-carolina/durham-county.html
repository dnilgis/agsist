Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 in Durham County stands at $47 per acre. Corn yield over 2008-2025 runs at a median of 110.5 bushels per acre, with 2024 at 30.8, the worst year in that span. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 67.9 F to 69.3 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.76 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought has been the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025 at 47 percent, though its share by period reads 49 percent, 59 percent, 43 percent, and 35 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025.
