As of Oct 16, cash rent in Haywood County runs $69 an acre for 2026, down 20.8% from $87 in 2014 and under the state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up to $8,046 an acre in 2022 from $6,461 in 2017. That puts it 14th highest of 92 in North Carolina. Operators rented 30% of the farmland here in 2022.
