Haywood County, North Carolina

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,046 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

21.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.36 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,213 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,046 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $68.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Aug 28, 2026 to Sep 2, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 20.8% from 2014 to 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 221.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 83 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $55.83 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $42.62 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $37.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.88 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.01, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 28.6%.

Cash rent in Haywood County runs $69 an acre for 2026, down 20.8% from $87 in 2014 and under the state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up to $8,046 an acre in 2022 from $6,461 in 2017. That puts it 14th highest of 92 in North Carolina. Operators rented 30% of the farmland here in 2022.
