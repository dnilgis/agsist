Cash rent in Nash County runs $126 an acre for 2026, up 23.5% from $102 in 2016. That rent is tied for 10th highest of 86 in North Carolina, where the state median is $81. The census value of land and buildings was $4,436 an acre in 2022, down 4.1% from $4,627 in 2017, and sits 28th lowest of 92. Corn made 132.5 bu in 2025, against a 2021-25 average of 115.8 bu.
