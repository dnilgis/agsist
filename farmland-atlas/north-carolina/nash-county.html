Nash County, North Carolina

Nash County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $126 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,436 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 102.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37127. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Nash County is $126 per acre in 2026, up 23.5 percent from 2016. Land value moved the other way, down 4.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, so rent has climbed while the underlying land value slipped. The July nightly low has warmed at 0.55 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 69.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 68.4 F. Drought weeks stayed low recently, with 2 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025 against 88 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000.
