Cash rent in Montgomery County runs $46 an acre for 2026, against a state median of $81. That puts it 11th lowest of 86 counties in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $5,267 an acre in 2022, up 11.0% from $4,743 in 2017, or 2.1% a year. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
