As of Oct 16, cash rent in Iredell County runs $86 an acre for 2026, up 52.7% from $56 in 2016 and tied for 39th highest of 86 in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $7,124 an acre in 2022, up 31.0% from $5,438 in 2017, which puts it 23rd highest of 92 against a state median of $5,711. Corn made 156.4 bu in 2023, well over the 14 year median of 119.2 bu.
