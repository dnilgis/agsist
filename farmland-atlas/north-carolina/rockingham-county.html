Cash rent in Rockingham County runs $49 an acre for 2026, tied for 16th lowest of 86 in North Carolina against a state median of $81. That is up 38.6% from $35 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,089 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from $3,873 in 2017 and 36th lowest of 92. Corn came in at 45.8 bu in 2024, well under the county median of 104.5 bu since 2008.
