Northampton County, North Carolina

Northampton County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $116 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,568 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 122.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37131. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Northampton County is $116 per acre in 2026, up 20.8 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $3,568 per acre, up 27.6 percent from 2017. July nightly lows average 70.1 F for 2017-2026, above the 68.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.76 F per decade, warming.
