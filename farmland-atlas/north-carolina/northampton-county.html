Cash rent in Northampton County runs $116 an acre for 2026, up 20.8% from $96 in 2016 and tied for 18th highest of 86 against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $3,568 an acre in 2022, up 27.6% from $2,797 in 2017, yet still 5th lowest of 92 in the state. Corn came in at 153.9 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year median of 122.0 bu.
