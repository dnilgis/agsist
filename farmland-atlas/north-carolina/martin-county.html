Martin County, North Carolina

Martin County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $116 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,011 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 98.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37117. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Martin County is $116 per acre for 2026, against land value of $3,011 per acre in 2022, a rent-to-value share of 3.59 percent. Land value rose 12.4 percent from 2017-2022, 2.4 percent a year. No cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census.
