Bertie County, North Carolina

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,072 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.09 per base acre for corn.

Bertie County vs North Carolina: $133 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 92% of the other 85 North Carolina counties with a 2026 rent (8th highest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.10 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 73% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 234 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,619 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,072 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $133 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.73 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 26% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 20% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 52% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 1.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 453 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

PRF grazing county base value: $58.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.56 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.61, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 42% of the median yield. 7.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 32.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36.5%.

Quick answers for Bertie County

What is cash rent in Bertie County, North Carolina?

The county average is $133 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bertie County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $4,072 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Bertie County, North Carolina?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 134.6 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
