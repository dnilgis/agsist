Madison County, North Carolina

Madison County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $74 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,294 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37115. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County runs $74 per acre, down 7.5 percent since 2014. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, stands at $7,294 per acre, up 47.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 8 percent a year. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Drought weeks were heavy in 2008, with 45 weeks that year, but 2021-2025 recorded zero weeks in severe drought or worse. Heat and drought caused 54 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 37 percent in 2000-2009, and 43 percent in 2010-2019. The July nightly low has warmed since 1976 at 0.61 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 63.7 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 62.1 F.
