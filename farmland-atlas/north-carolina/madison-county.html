Cropland rent in Madison County runs $74 an acre for 2026, down 7.5% from $80 in 2014 and tied for 37th lowest of 86 in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,294 an acre in 2022, up 47.1% from $4,959 in 2017, which ranks 21st highest of 92 in the state. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
