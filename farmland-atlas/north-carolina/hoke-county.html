Hoke County, North Carolina

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,488 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.68 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.89 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 236 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,728 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,488 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $61 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.47 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 18% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 7% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 41.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 40 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,580 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.68 per base acre, PLC $2.32.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (92.58 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $5.82 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $20.46 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (26.34 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $52.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 200 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.65 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.69, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.2%.

The census value of land and buildings came to $5,488 an acre in 2022, up 24.7% from $4,402 in 2017, and that ranks 42nd lowest of 92 against a state median of $5,711. Corn has run a median 103.0 bu over 9 years of county yields from 2008 to 2022, and 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
