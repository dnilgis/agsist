Cash rent in Carteret County sits at $88 an acre for 2026, ranked 38th highest of 86 in North Carolina and a bit above the state median of $81. That rent is down 3.8% from $92 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings dropped 50.8% to $2,231 an acre in 2022, from $4,536 in 2017, well under the state median of $5,711.

Carteret County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
