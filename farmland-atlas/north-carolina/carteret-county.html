Carteret County, North Carolina

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,231 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.25 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.55 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 73% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the last spring freeze comes around Mar 9 in the median year; most years have no fall freeze before December. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,922 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,231 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $88 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 19% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 3.8% from 2016 to 2026. Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 51,280 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.25 per base acre, PLC $2.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.51 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $1.76 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $24.42 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.61 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.54 per base acre, PLC $8.24. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.62 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $59.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.22 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.77, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 39%.

Cash rent in Carteret County sits at $88 an acre for 2026, ranked 38th highest of 86 in North Carolina and a bit above the state median of $81. That rent is down 3.8% from $92 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings dropped 50.8% to $2,231 an acre in 2022, from $4,536 in 2017, well under the state median of $5,711.

Carteret County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
