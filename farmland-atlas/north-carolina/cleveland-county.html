Cleveland County, North Carolina

Rain is at 75% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,086 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.99 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 33.45 inches, 75% of normal (44.36 inches) through Nov 18, at Shelby, 2 mi NW. Frost-free season: 213 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,451 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,086 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $42.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 32.8% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 201.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 28.9 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 26 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.99 per base acre, PLC $1.72.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (68.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (28.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.58 per base acre, PLC $9.12. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (50.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $59.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.40 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.96, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 46% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 6.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 35% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.9%.
