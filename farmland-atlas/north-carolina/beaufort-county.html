Stands out against its own record

15 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, more than any full year since 2000.

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 14, cash rent in Beaufort County runs $130 an acre in 2026, up 49.4% from $87 in 2016 and 9th highest of 86 in North Carolina against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $4,329 an acre in 2022, up 26.6% from $3,420 in 2017, yet it sits 24th lowest of 92 and under the state median of $5,711. Corn came in at 163.5 bu in 2025, and 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
