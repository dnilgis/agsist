Jones County, North Carolina

Jones County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $91 an acre (2025); land and buildings $3,602 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 109.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jones County is $91 per acre in 2025, up 9.6 percent from 2014. Land value fell over the same recent period, down 11.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, so rent has grown while the land under it lost value; cash rent is now 2.33 percent of land value. July nightly lows averaged 70.6 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.9 F, part of a warming trend of 0.55 F per decade since 1976.
