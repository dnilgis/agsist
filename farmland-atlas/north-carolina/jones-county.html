Jones County, North Carolina

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,602 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.93 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 233 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,728 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,602 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $91 an acre (USDA, 2025). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 53.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 7.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 160 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,114 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.93 per base acre, PLC $2.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (103.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $59.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 50 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $26.43 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.36 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 23% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29%.
