Non-irrigated cash rent in Onslow County averages $109 per acre in 2026, up 38 percent since 2014. Corn yields show a rising trend of 1.1 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2024, with a median of 105 bushels per acre and a low of 42.1 in 2011. Crop insurance has paid $88.0 million over 1989-2025, driven mostly by excess moisture and flood at 36 percent, with heat and drought shares by period at 9 percent, 30 percent, 29 percent, and 20 percent. Severe drought or worse covered the county in 71 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 20 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
