Gaston County, North Carolina

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,015 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.54 per base acre for corn.

Gaston County vs North Carolina: $35 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 1% of the other 85 North Carolina counties with a 2026 rent (2nd lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.64 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 225 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,536 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,015 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.64 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 40% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 6% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 27.3% from 2016 to 2026. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 198.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 73 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 24 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.54 per base acre, PLC $1.93.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (76.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $61.57 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.97 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (28.69 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $59.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.29 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.77, 2016 to 2025. 3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.6%.
