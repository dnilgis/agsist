As of Oct 16, cropland in Anson County rents for $72 an acre in 2026, up 7.5% from $67 in 2016 and below the state median of $81. That puts it 36th lowest of 86 in North Carolina. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,138 an acre in 2022, up 25.7% from $4,086 in 2017. Corn trends at 122.6 bu, under the state median of 134.6 bu.
