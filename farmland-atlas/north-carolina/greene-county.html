As of Oct 14, Greene County cash rent sits at $136 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th highest of 86 in North Carolina and well over the $81 state median. That is up 43.2% from $95 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,677 an acre in 2022, 29th lowest of 92 and under the $5,711 state median. A good share of the ground is farmed by renters, with 61% of farmland rented in 2022.
