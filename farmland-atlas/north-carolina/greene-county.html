Greene County, North Carolina

Greene County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $136 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,677 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 104.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37079. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greene County averages $136 per acre in 2026, up 43.2 percent from 2016. Census land value per acre is $4,677, with land value up 18.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.5 percent a year. Cash rent equals 2.29 percent of that census value. July nightly lows run 70.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 70 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 is warming at 0.55 F per decade. Heat and drought caused 34 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with that share at 18 percent in 1989-1999, 41 percent in 2000-2009, 27 percent in 2010-2019, and 42 percent in 2020-2025. The county held 29 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 100 weeks over 2000-2025.
