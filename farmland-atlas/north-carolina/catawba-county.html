Cash rent in Catawba County runs $47 an acre for 2026, down 3.1% from $48 in 2016 and 13th lowest of 86 in North Carolina, where the median is $81. The census value of land and buildings was $7,785 an acre in 2022, up 19.0% from $6,543 in 2017 and 17th highest of 92 in the state. Corn made 101.0 bu in 2024, well under the county median of 131.4 bu going back to 2008.
