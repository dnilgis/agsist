Granville County, North Carolina

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,366 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.13 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 34.21 inches, 80% of normal (42.96 inches) through Nov 16, at Oxford, 2.5 mi WSW. Frost-free season: 215 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,449 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,366 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $66 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 32% from 2016 to 2026. 27.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 71.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,369 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.13 per base acre, PLC $1.67.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (66.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $55.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 3 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.41 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.31 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 22% of the median yield. 3.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.7%.
