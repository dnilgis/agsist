Person County, North Carolina

Person County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $68 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,957 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 98.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37145. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Person County averages $68 per acre in 2026, up 66.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $115 per acre, a multiple of 1.70 over non-irrigated, versus 1.92 in 2009, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so most ground here depends on rainfall. Corn yields trend up 2.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 98 bushels per acre and a worst year of 52.8 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 43 percent, 58 percent, 36 percent, 30 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, and there were zero weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
