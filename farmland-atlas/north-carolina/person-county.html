Person County, North Carolina

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,957 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.46 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 29.16 inches, 72% of normal (40.46 inches) through Nov 11, at Roxboro, 7 mi ESE. Frost-free season: 207 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,371 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,957 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $68 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 66.7% from 2016 to 2026. 39.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 76.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 80 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 75 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.46 per base acre, PLC $2.04.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (81.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (24.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $55.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 11 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.32 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.27 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 54% of the median yield. 2.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 24.9%.
