Gates County, North Carolina

Gates County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $112 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,274 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 130.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37073. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Gates County runs $112 per acre in 2026, up 23.1 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $4,274 per acre in 2022, up 17.9 percent from 2017, or 3.3 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value was 2.19 percent in 2022. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 70 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.8 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.66 F per decade. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved 66 percent in 1989-1999, 70 percent in 2000-2009, 26 percent in 2010-2019, and 40 percent in 2020-2025.
