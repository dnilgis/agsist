Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 23.1% from $91 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Gates County runs $112 an acre for 2026, up 23.1% from $91 in 2016 and tied for 22nd highest of 86 counties against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $4,274 an acre in 2022, up 17.9% from $3,625 in 2017, which puts it 21st lowest of 92 and under the $5,711 state median. Corn made 169.2 bu in 2025, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
