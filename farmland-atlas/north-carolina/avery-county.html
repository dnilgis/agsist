Non-irrigated cash rent in Avery County runs $80 per acre, a figure the census puts at 0.8 percent of the $8,717 per acre operators report their land and buildings are worth. July nights average 59.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 58.5 F, and the county has never recorded a July average low at or above 70 F since 1900. Drought has been light lately: 3 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 146 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025, with 2008 the worst year at 45 weeks. Crop insurance losses from 1989-2014 total $538,252, led by freeze and frost at 35 percent, with heat and drought shares of 19 percent, 6 percent, and 5 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019.
