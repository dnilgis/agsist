Burke County, North Carolina

Burke County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $43 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,651 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 103.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37023. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Burke County runs $43 per acre, down 2.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent stood at $220 per acre in 2023, a multiple of 5.87 over non-irrigated that year, up from 3.69 in 2014, though the multiple's direction shows no clear pattern. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 0.6 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 103.5 bushels per acre and a low of 62.1 in 2024. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 13 percent in 1989-1999, to 6 percent in 2000-2009, to 4 percent in 2010-2019, to 62 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value reached $6,651 per acre in 2022, up 16.7 percent from 2017.
