U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Burke County ran $43 an acre in 2026, down 2.3% from $44 in 2016 and 6th lowest of 86 counties in North Carolina against a state median of $81. The census value of land and buildings was $6,651 an acre in 2022, up 16.7% from $5,701 in 2017, which ranks 32nd highest of 92. Corn made 113.8 bu in 2025, a bit under the 2021-25 average of 116.3 bu but above the 18 year median of 103.5 bu.
