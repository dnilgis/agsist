Pasquotank County, North Carolina

Pasquotank County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $135 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,382 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 167.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37139. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $135 per acre, up 26.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025, at 43 percent of $39.3 million paid. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 39 percent, 33 percent, 35 percent, then 50 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed at 0.71 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 71.9 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 70.4 F. Land value, per the operators' own 2022 estimate, stands at $4,382 per acre.
