As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pasquotank County runs $135 an acre for 2026, 6th highest of 86 in North Carolina against a state median of $81. That rent is up 26.2% from $107 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,382 an acre in 2022, up 10.0% from 2017 and 27th lowest of 92 in the state. Corn trends to 181.0 bu for 2026, 3rd highest of 37, and 74% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
