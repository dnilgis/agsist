Non-irrigated cash rent in Johnston County averages $114 per acre in 2026, up 27.4 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 stood at $6,813 per acre, up 43.7 percent from 2017, a pace of 7.5 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 35 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, with the share at 45 percent in 2000-2009, 34 percent in 2010-2019, and 39 percent in 2020-2025. The county saw 0 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, after 84 of 1357 weeks since 2000. July nightly lows have warmed 0.67 F per decade since 1976, and only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census.
