Johnston County, North Carolina

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,813 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.50 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 36.89 inches, 84% of normal (44.1 inches) through Nov 18, at Clayton, 6.8 mi ESE. Frost-free season: 232 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,656 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,813 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $114 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 27.4% from 2016 to 2026. 49% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 15% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 53.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 73 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 514 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.50 per base acre, PLC $1.88.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (75.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (26.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.21 per base acre, PLC $8.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $59.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 16 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.48 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.32, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 31% of the median yield. 3.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 8.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 35.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 30.8%.
