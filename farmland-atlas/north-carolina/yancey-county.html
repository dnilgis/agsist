Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.93 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 177 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,309 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,261 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.9% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 151.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 615 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Yancey County

What is cash rent in Yancey County, North Carolina?

The county average is $45 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Yancey County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $6,261 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
