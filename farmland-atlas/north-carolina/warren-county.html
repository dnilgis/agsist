Warren County, North Carolina

Warren County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $48 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,721 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 87.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37185. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Warren County is $48 per acre for 2026, up 3.3 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $4,721 per acre, with land value up 62.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 10.2 percent a year. July average nightly low ran 69.5 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.2 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 is 0.73 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought caused 60 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, with shares of 58 percent in 1989-1999, 68 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 69 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were rare in 2021-2025, at 3, well below the 2002 peak of 41. Solar operating capacity is 154.4 MW; wind operating is 0 MW.
