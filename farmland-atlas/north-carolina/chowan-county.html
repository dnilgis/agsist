Chowan County, North Carolina

Chowan County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $99 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,973 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 141.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Chowan County averages $99 per acre in 2026, up 12.5 percent from 2016. Land value comes in at $3,973 per acre in 2022, up 13.7 percent from 2017, or 2.6 percent a year. Excess moisture and flood is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 at 42 percent of $14.7 million paid, and the loss ratio over that span is 0.51, with 0 of 12 crop years paying out more than premium. July nightly lows average 71.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.74 F per decade, warming. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 76 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with only 6 such weeks from 2021-2025.
