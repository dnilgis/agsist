Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 55.89 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,621 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,429 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $100 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 6% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 90.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.73 per base acre, PLC $2.90.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (115.56 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $77.31 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.54 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $37.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 44% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 4.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2024; too much rain caused 66.5%.

Quick answers for Transylvania County

What is cash rent in Transylvania County, North Carolina?

The county average is $100 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Transylvania County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $9,429 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Transylvania County, North Carolina?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 198.1 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 8 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
