Camden County, North Carolina

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,007 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.38 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 247 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,634 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,007 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $120 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.75 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 29% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 76% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 3.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.38 per base acre, PLC $3.60.

PRF grazing county base value: $58.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 8 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.58 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.42, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 79% of the median yield. 0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51%.

Quick answers for Camden County

What is cash rent in Camden County, North Carolina?

The county average is $120 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Camden County, North Carolina?

Land and buildings were worth $4,007 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Camden County, North Carolina?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 163.7 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
