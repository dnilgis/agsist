USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Camden County runs $120 an acre for 2025, up 15.4% from $104 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $4,007 an acre in 2022, up 25.9% from 2017, and sits 15th lowest of 92 against a state median of $5,711. Corn does better, with a trend yield of 163.7 bu projected for 2026 that ranks 7th highest of 37. In 2022, 76% of the farmland was rented.
