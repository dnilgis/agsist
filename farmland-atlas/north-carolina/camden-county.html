Camden County, North Carolina

Camden County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $120 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,007 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 166.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37029. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Camden County, NC runs $120 per acre in 2025, up 15.4 percent from 2014. Land and buildings carry a value of $4,007 per acre, up 25.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.7 percent a year, so rent sits at 2.8 percent of land value. Drought has been rare: 75 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000, and just 4 weeks from 2021-2025. No cropland here is irrigated.
