Swain County, North Carolina

Swain County, NC: land and buildings $10,139 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37173. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Swain County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low ran 60.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 59 F, with a warming trend of 0.41 F per decade since 1976. Drought has touched the county in 151 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2008 the worst year at 42 weeks, and only 14 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and heat and drought made up 1 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed to EIA.
