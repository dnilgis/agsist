Hertford County, North Carolina

Hertford County, NC: non-irrigated cash rent $115 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,623 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 120.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 37091. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hertford County is $115 per acre for 2026, up 16.2 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $3,623 per acre in 2022, up 13.3 percent from 2017. July nights average 70.2 F over 2017-2026, up from 68.7 F in the 1991-2020 normal, with a warming trend of 0.8 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks fell to 4 in 2021-2025, against 83 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025.
