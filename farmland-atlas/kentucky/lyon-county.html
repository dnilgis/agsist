Lyon County, Kentucky

Lyon County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $144 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,987 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.62 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21143. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lyon County averages $144 per acre in 2025, up 73.5 percent from 2014. Census land value reached $4,987 per acre in 2022, after a 59.8 percent change from 2017 to 2022, and the 2022 cash rent was 2.65 percent of that value. Heat and drought caused 53 percent of the $12.7 million in crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, with the share rising to 64 percent in 2010-2019 before falling to 45 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 67 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 26 weeks and 14 weeks logged in 2021-2025. July nights average 70.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 69.1 F normal for 1991-2020.
