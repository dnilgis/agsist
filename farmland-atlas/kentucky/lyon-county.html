Cash rent in Lyon County runs $144 an acre for 2025, up 73.5% from $83 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $4,987 an acre in 2022, up 59.8% from $3,121 in 2017, or 9.8% a year. That sits above the Kentucky median of $4,449 and ranks 41st highest of 114. 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
