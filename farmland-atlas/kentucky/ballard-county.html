USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Ballard County cash rent stands at $161 an acre for 2026, up 45.0% from $111 in 2016 and tied for 26th highest of 86 in Kentucky, against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $4,478 an acre in 2022, up 23.9% from 2017 and about even with the state median of $4,449. Corn is where the county stands out, with a 14-yr trend of 216.4 bu, 6th highest of 76.
