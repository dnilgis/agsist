Cumberland County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,793 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.91 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

8.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 47.41 inches, 102% of normal (46.56 inches) through Nov 17, at Dubre, 1 mi NE. Frost-free season: 202 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,342 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,793 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $80 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 20.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 7.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 93 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.91 per base acre, PLC $2.83.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (112.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $69.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.26 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.57 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 89% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 31.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 45.5%.
