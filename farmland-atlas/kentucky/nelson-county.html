Cropland in Nelson County rents for $180 an acre in 2026, up 23.3% from $146 in 2016. That rent is tied for 13th highest of 86 in Kentucky, well over the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,930 an acre in 2022, up 46.0% from $4,061 in 2017, which ranks 20th highest of 114.
