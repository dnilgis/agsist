Cash rent in McCracken County runs $140 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $105 in 2016 and 37th highest of 86 in Kentucky. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,673 an acre in 2022, up 27.3% from $4,456 in 2017 and 25th highest of 114 in the state. 60% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
