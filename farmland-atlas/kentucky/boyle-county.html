Stands out against its own record

8 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Boyle County ran $126 an acre in 2024, up 152.0% from $50 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,423 an acre in 2022, up 45.8% from $3,720 in 2017. That value ranks 32nd highest of 114 in Kentucky and sits above the state median of $4,449. Corn has held a median of 173.7 bu an acre over 14 years.
