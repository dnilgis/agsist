Boyle County, Kentucky

Boyle County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $126 an acre (2024); land and buildings $5,423 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21021. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Boyle County averages $126 per acre in 2024, up 152 percent from 2013. Census land value stands at $5,423 per acre, up 45.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trended up 3.7 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2022, with a median of 173.7 bushels per acre and a low of 116 in 2008. Heat and drought has driven indemnities across every decade measured: 69 percent of losses in 1989-1999, 49 percent in 2000-2009, 43 percent in 2010-2019, and 44 percent in 2020-2025, out of $9.8 million paid since 1989. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
