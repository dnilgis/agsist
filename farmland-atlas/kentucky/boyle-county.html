Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.48 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.88, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 67% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 46% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 26%.

Cash rent in Boyle County ran $126 an acre in 2024, up 152.0% from $50 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,423 an acre in 2022, up 45.8% from $3,720 in 2017. That value ranks 32nd highest of 114 in Kentucky and sits above the state median of $4,449. Corn has held a median of 173.7 bu an acre over 14 years.
