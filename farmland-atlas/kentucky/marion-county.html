Marion County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.78 per base acre for corn.

Marion County vs Kentucky: $173 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 79% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (19th highest of 86).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.13 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Quick answers for Marion County

What is cash rent in Marion County, Kentucky?

The county average is $173 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Marion County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $4,582 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Marion County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 186.2 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
