Rain is at 90% of normal. South East subregion (27 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $3,100 an acre; hay $2,800, pasture $2,400, woodland $1,600.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 198 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,201 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).
