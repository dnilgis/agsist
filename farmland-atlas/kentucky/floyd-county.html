Floyd County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2022); land and buildings $2,988 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21071. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Floyd County had 6,898 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights have warmed, averaging 66.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.8 F, with a trend of 0.51 F per decade since 1976; the 2020s is the first decade to reach 66 F. Drought was rare and getting rarer: 50 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, none since 2021. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland.
