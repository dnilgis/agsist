Cropland rent in Bullitt County runs $83 an acre for 2026, down 2.4% from $85 in 2016 and well under the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $7,883 an acre in 2022, up 52.7% from $5,161 in 2017 and 7th highest of 114 in Kentucky. Corn trends at 176.9 bu, 26th lowest of 76, and 19% of farmland was rented in 2022.
