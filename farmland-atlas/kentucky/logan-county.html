Cash rent in Logan County runs $232 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. That rent is up 6.4% from $218 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $6,404 an acre in 2022, up 32.6% from $4,829 in 2017. Corn trends at 203.9 bu for 2026, above the state median of 188.0 bu.
