Grayson County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Near West subregion (12 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,500, pasture $6,000, woodland $4,100. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.66 per base acre for corn.

Grayson County vs Kentucky: $110 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 44% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 38th lowest of 86).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.22 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.74, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 47% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 46.9%.
