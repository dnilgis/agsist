Grayson County, Kentucky

Grayson County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $110 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,703 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21085. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Grayson County is $110 per acre for 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $3,703 per acre, up 25.3 percent from 2017, 4.6 percent a year. Cropland here has 0 percent irrigated, so crop outcomes track rainfall and heat directly. July nights average 67 F over 2017-2026 against a 65.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the county saw 11 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2021-2025.
