Johnson County, Kentucky

South East subregion (27 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $3,100 an acre; hay $2,800, pasture $2,400, woodland $1,600. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.27 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 190 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,152 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 40.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2015; too much rain caused 53.6%.

The census value of land and buildings in Johnson County sat at $3,144 an acre in 2022. That was up 4.8% from $3,001 in 2017, 0.9% a year, after a bigger jump from $1,750 in 2012. It ranks 20th lowest of 114 in Kentucky, under the state median of $4,449. Cash rent ran $31 an acre in 2022, and 14% of the farmland was rented.
