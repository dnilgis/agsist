Trigg County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,078 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

51.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 29.97 inches, 68% of normal (44.16 inches) through Nov 17, at Cadiz.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,078 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $175 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.77 per base acre, PLC $3.19.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (127.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $60.80 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.61 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.87 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 32% of the median yield. 4.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).

Trigg County cash rent sits at $175 an acre for 2026, 17th highest of 86 in Kentucky and above the state median of $125. That is down 10.3% from $195 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings ran $5,078 an acre in 2022, up 23.6% from $4,110 in 2017. Corn trends at 191.4 bu, a little over the state median of 188.0 bu.
