Trigg County, Kentucky

Trigg County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $175 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,078 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 170.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21221. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Trigg County averages $175 per acre in 2026, down 10.3 percent from 2014. Census land value stood at $5,078 per acre in 2022, up 23.6 percent from 2017, and 2026 rent equals a small share of that value. Corn yield trended up 3.2 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 170.4 bushels per acre and a low of 54.5 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 50 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share fell across the four listed periods: 57 percent, 57 percent, 52 percent, 44 percent. July nights have warmed, averaging 70.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 68.7 F normal for 1991-2020. Solar and wind capacity in the county is 0 MW, operating or proposed.
