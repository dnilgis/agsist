Todd County, Kentucky

Todd County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $200 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,606 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21219. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Todd County runs $200 per acre in 2026, down 5.2 percent from 2016. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, is $6,606 per acre, up 27.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 3.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 175.4 bushels per acre and a low of 80.8 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 57 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share has moved 56 percent, 43 percent, 59 percent, 63 percent across the periods 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, 2020-2025. No cropland in the county is irrigated. Solar and wind capacity are not present here.
