Wayne County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.16 per base acre for corn.

Wayne County vs Kentucky: $130 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 54% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (40th highest of 86).

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 197 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,189 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.23 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.62, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 48% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 62.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.3%.
