Quick answers for Owsley County

What is farmland worth in Owsley County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $2,657 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
