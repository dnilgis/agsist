Owsley County, Kentucky

Owsley County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2022); land and buildings $2,657 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21189. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Owsley County is $34 per acre, against a land value of $2,657 per acre in 2022. That land value moved up 98 percent from 2017-2022. Excess moisture and flood, not heat, drove indemnities from 1992-2021, at 56 percent of the $1.8 million paid, and the heat and drought share of indemnities fell from 65 percent in 1989-1999 to 39 percent in 2000-2009 to 11 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks were 59 of 1357 from 2000-2025, and 0 from 2021-2025. The July average nightly low moved from 64.8 F normal to 66.5 F over 2017-2026, warming at 0.46 F per decade since 1976.
