Quick answers for Fayette County

What is cash rent in Fayette County, Kentucky?

The county average is $80 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Fayette County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $16,231 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Fayette County, Kentucky?

The county's 2008-2025 median is 164.8 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
