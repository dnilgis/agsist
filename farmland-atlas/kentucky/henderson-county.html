Henderson County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $236 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,088 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21101. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henderson County is $236 per acre for 2026, up 31.8 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $6,088 per acre, up 19.5 percent from 2017, or 3.6 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 172.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 69.6 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 43 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, though the share fell to 24 percent in 2020-2025 after reaching 60 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks were low from 2021-2025, at 2 of the period's total.
