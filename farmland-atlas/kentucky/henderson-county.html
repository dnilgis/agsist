Henderson County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Mid West subregion (15 counties, 11 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,100, pasture $5,400, woodland $4,400. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.37 per base acre for corn.

Henderson County vs Kentucky: $236 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 99% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (2nd highest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

64.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.56 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 213 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,492 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Solar and wind proposed but not built: 346.1 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,181 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.19 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 40% of the median yield. 5.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 37%.
