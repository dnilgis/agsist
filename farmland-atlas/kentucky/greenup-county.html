$43

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest since 2010 (15 surveys on record since 2009); down 5.6% from $45 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Greenup County runs $43 an acre for 2026, down 5.6% from $45 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,615 an acre in 2022, up 46.3% from $2,471 in 2017, or 7.9% a year. That sits 30th lowest of 114 against a state median of $4,449. Corn has run a median 128.6 bu from 2010 to 2022, and 18% of farmland was rented in 2022.
