Cropland rent in Nicholas County runs $120 an acre for 2025, up 51.9% from $79 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,706 an acre in 2022, up 102.7% from $2,322 in 2017. That puts the county 49th highest of 114 in Kentucky, a bit above the state median of $4,449. Corn has held a median of 148.5 bu over 8 years of county yields from 2009 to 2022.
