Magoffin County cash rent sits at $35 an acre for 2026, up 12.9% from $31 in 2016 and 6th lowest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $2,713 an acre in 2022, up 31.1% from $2,070 in 2017, or 5.6% a year. That still ranks 9th lowest of 114 against a state median of $4,449. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
