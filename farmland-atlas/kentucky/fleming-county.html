As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Fleming County runs $132 an acre for 2026, up 5.6% from $125 in 2016 and 38th highest of 86 in Kentucky. The census value of land and buildings was $3,780 an acre in 2022, up 43.0% from $2,643 in 2017, which still sits 35th lowest of 114 in the state. The corn trend points to 162.7 bu for 2026, 14th lowest of 76 against a state median of 188.0 bu.
