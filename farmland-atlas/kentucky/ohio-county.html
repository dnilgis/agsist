Ohio County, Kentucky

Ohio County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $168 an acre (2024); land and buildings $3,919 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 180.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21183. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Ohio County averaged $168 per acre in 2024, up 35.5 percent from 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,919 per acre in 2022, up 2.8 percent a year from 2017 to 2022, and the 2022 rent was 4.03 percent of that value. Corn yields have trended up 2.7 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 180 bushels per acre and a low of 101.4 in 2012. Drought has stayed limited: 47 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, and only 9 weeks from 2021-2025. Excess moisture and flood drove 68 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, the largest cause on record for this county. Solar and wind capacity here: 0 MW.
