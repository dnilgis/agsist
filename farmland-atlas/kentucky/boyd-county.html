Boyd County, Kentucky

Boyd County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2024); land and buildings $3,181 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Boyd County averages $38 per acre in 2024, up 31 percent from 2013. Census land and buildings values stand at $3,181 per acre, up 32.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.8 percent a year. Crop insurance paid $119,641 in indemnities across 1995-2019, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 77 percent. Drought has been a small factor: severe drought or worse covered 3.6 percent of weeks from 2000-2025, with 7 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights are warming, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 64.3 F to 66.3 F in 2017-2026, a trend of 0.56 F per decade. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
