Cropland rent in Boyd County runs $38 an acre for 2024, up 31.0% from $29 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,181 an acre in 2022, up 32.3% from 2017, or 5.8% a year. That sits under the state median of $4,449 and ranks 22nd lowest of 114 in Kentucky. Only 6% of farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Boyd County

What is cash rent in Boyd County, Kentucky?

The county average is $38 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Boyd County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $3,181 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
