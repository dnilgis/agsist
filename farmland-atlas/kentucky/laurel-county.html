Cash rent in Laurel County was $33 an acre in 2026, down 18.8% from $40 in 2016 and 5th lowest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $4,682 an acre in 2022, up 40.1% from $3,343 in 2017 and a bit over the state median of $4,449. The corn trend yield projects 150.2 bu for 2026, 4th lowest of 76 against a state median of 188.0 bu.
