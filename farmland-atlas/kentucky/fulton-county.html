Non-irrigated cash rent in Fulton County runs $191 per acre in 2026, up 27.3 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $5,381 per acre, with a 46.5 percent gain from 2017 to 2022, 7.9 percent a year, and the 2022 rent-to-value ratio was 3.31 percent. Corn yields have trended up 2.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 177.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 72.3 in 2012. Crop insurance history shows $60.4 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, driven mostly by excess moisture and flood at 56 percent; heat and drought shares moved 35 percent, 15 percent, 35 percent, and 33 percent across the four listed periods. Only 7 percent of cropland is irrigated, and solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW.
