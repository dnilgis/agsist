Cash rent in Fulton County runs $191 an acre for 2026, tied for 10th highest of 86 counties in Kentucky and well above the state median of $125. That rent is up 27.3% from $150 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,381 an acre in 2022, up 46.5% from $3,674 in 2017, against a state median of $4,449. 63% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
