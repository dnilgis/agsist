Cash rent in Simpson County runs $217 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $184 in 2016 and 5th highest of 86 in Kentucky, where the state median is $125. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,821 an acre in 2022, up 20.2% from $5,675 in 2017, or 3.7% a year. Corn yields here trend toward 204.3 bu for 2026, against a state median of 188.0 bu.
