Simpson County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Mid West subregion (15 counties, 11 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,100, pasture $5,400, woodland $4,400. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.46 per base acre for corn.

Simpson County vs Kentucky: $217 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 95% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (5th highest of 86).

Quick answers for Simpson County

What is cash rent in Simpson County, Kentucky?

The county average is $217 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Simpson County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $6,821 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Simpson County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 204.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
