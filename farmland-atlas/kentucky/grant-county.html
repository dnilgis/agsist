Grant County, Kentucky

Grant County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $76 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,003 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21081. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Grant County runs $76 per acre, up 60.6 percent from 2014 to 2026. Corn yields carry a trend of 3.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 135.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 87 in 2012. No cropland here is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Crop insurance history shows $3.7 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 66 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities fell across the listed decades from 68 percent in 1989-1999 to 26 percent in 2000-2009 to 20 percent in 2010-2019, then rose to 25 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 65.3 F to 67.2 F over 2017-2026.
