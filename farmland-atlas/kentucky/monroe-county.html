Monroe County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,027 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.72 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

62.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 200 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,332 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,027 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $150 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 17.2% from 2016 to 2026. 19.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 340.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.72 per base acre, PLC $2.62.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (104.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $69.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 58 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $34.84 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.50 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 30% of the median yield. 0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 58.7%.
