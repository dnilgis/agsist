As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Webster County runs $229 an acre for 2026, up 8.5% from $211 in 2016 and 4th highest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $4,351 an acre in 2022, up 18.1% from $3,683 in 2017, sitting a bit under the state median of $4,449. Corn trends at 203.7 bu for 2026, 16th highest of 76, and 51% of farmland was rented in 2022.
