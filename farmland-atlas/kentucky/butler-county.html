Butler County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Mid West subregion (15 counties, 11 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,100, pasture $5,400, woodland $4,400. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.37 per base acre for corn.

Butler County vs Kentucky: $144 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 61% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (34th highest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

98% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.04 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 88% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 210 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,431 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.37 per base acre, PLC $3.37.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $100.33 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $29.67 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $69.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 372 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.42 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.69, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 61% of the median yield. 0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 55.6%.
