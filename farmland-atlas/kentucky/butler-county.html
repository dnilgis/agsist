Cash rent in Butler County runs $144 an acre for 2026, up 25.2% from $115 in 2016 and 34th highest of 86 in Kentucky. The census value of land and buildings was $4,286 an acre in 2022, up 61.9% from $2,648 in 2017, against a state median of $4,449. Corn trends at 205.5 bu for 2026, 11th highest of 76 in the state.
