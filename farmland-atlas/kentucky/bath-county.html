A separate source from USDA's; nothing here is averaged with USDA or census figures. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.63 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 12% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 14% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 13% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 210.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,623 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 439 acres, so read the change with care.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.14 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.59, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 61% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.2%.
