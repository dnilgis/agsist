Bourbon County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Bluegrass subregion (11 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,300 an acre; hay $7,600, pasture $6,800, woodland $4,800. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.44 per base acre for corn.

Bourbon County vs Kentucky: $105 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 41% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 36th lowest of 86).

Quick answers for Bourbon County

What is cash rent in Bourbon County, Kentucky?

The county average is $105 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bourbon County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $7,039 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Bourbon County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 222.7 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 10 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
