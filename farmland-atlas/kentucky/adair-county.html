Non-irrigated cash rent in Adair County runs $129 per acre in 2026, up 14.2 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 4.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 158.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 71.6 in 2012. July nights average 67.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 65.5 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.73 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought stood at 62 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, fell to 38 percent in 2000-2009 and 35 percent in 2010-2019, then rose to 60 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind operating and proposed capacity are both 0 MW.
