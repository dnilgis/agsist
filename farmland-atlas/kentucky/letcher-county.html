Letcher County is mostly a dry-weather place where the notable thing is the land price. The census put land and buildings at $3,563 per acre in 2022, and that value rose 18.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.5 percent a year. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so the ground depends on rain. Drought has been present but not heavy: 61 of 1357 weeks since 2000 had at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with the worst year being 2007 at 27 weeks. July nights have warmed slightly, with the 2017-2026 average low at 64.2 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.1 F. There are no solar or wind projects here.
