Letcher County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Letcher County was $3,563 per acre in the 2022 census, up 18.8 percent from 2017, or 3.5 percent a year. July nights average 64.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.1 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.44 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought was rare here: 61 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, with 2007 the worst year at 27 weeks, and only 3 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigated cropland stood at 5 percent in the 2022 census. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
