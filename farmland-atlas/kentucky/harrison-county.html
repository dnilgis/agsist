Cash rent in Harrison County runs $101 an acre for 2026, up 34.7% from $75 in 2016. That sits under the Kentucky median of $125 and ranks 35th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $4,169 an acre in 2022, up 34.5% from $3,100 in 2017. Corn made 195.8 bu in 2023, well above the 2019-23 average of 167.5 bu.
