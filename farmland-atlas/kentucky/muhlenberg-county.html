Muhlenberg County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Mid West subregion (15 counties, 11 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,100, pasture $5,400, woodland $4,400. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.02 per base acre for corn.

Muhlenberg County vs Kentucky: $148 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 62% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 32nd highest of 86).

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.28 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.85, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 39% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.8%.
