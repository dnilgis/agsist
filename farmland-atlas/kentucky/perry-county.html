Land value in 2022 stood at $2,578 per acre, the operators' own estimate, not a sale price, with a change of 125 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 17.6 percent a year. July nights average 65.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.4 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.47 F per decade, warming, and no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Drought weeks in 2021-2025 number 4, against 67 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst year 2007 with 27 weeks. No solar or wind operates in the county now; 490 MW solar is proposed to EIA.
