The census value of land and buildings in Bell County was $2,647 an acre in 2022, up 33.0% from $1,990 in 2017. That works out to 5.9% a year, after it barely moved from $1,945 in 2012. The county sits 7th lowest of 114 in Kentucky, against a state median of $4,449. Only 7% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
