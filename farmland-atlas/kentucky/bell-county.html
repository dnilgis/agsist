Land in Bell County carried a census value of $2,647 per acre in 2022, up 33 percent from 2017, or 5.9 percent a year. July nights average 64.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.6 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.39 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched this county in 67 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 29 weeks; from 2021-2025 the count is 4 weeks. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
