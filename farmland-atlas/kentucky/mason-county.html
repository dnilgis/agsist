Cropland rent in Mason County runs $90 an acre for 2026, down 7.7% from $98 in 2016 and 29th lowest of 86 against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 37.1% from 2017 to $4,053 an acre in 2022, which is 43rd lowest of 114. Corn trends at 169.9 bu, 16th lowest of 76, and last year came in at 135.2 bu.
