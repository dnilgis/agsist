USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Jessamine County is $60 an acre for 2026, up 4.3% from $58 in 2016. That is tied for 14th lowest of 86 against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $7,170 an acre in 2022 and 9th highest of 114, up 8.0% from $6,640 in 2017. Corn trends at 100.0 bu, lowest of 76, and came in at 82.0 bu in 2025.
