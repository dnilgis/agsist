Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 27% from $131 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Shelby County runs $166 an acre for 2026, up 26.7% from $131 in 2016 and above the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $7,994 an acre in 2022, 6th highest of 114 in Kentucky, up 37.6% from $5,809 in 2017. 39% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
