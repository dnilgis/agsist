Corn yields have climbed 3 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2025, though 2012 was a rough year at 64.6 bushels. The county paid out $138.9 million in crop insurance since 1989, and the loss ratio is 1.77, meaning the indemnity exceeded the premium in 15 of the 16 closed crop years. Excess moisture and flood caused 53 percent of those claims. Heat and drought dropped from 68 percent of claims in 1989-1999 to 33 percent in 2020-2025. Cash rent is $150 per acre in 2026, up 20 percent from 2016.
