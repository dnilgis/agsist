Scott County, Kentucky

Rain is at 97% of normal. Bluegrass subregion (11 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,300 an acre; hay $7,600, pasture $6,800, woodland $4,800. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.34 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.87 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 200 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,204 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

A separate source from USDA's; nothing here is averaged with USDA or census figures. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 3.5% from 2016 to 2026. 31% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 215.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,619 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.71, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 37% of the median yield. 1.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 42.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.8%.
