Quick answers for Menifee County

What is cash rent in Menifee County, Kentucky?

The county average is $41 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Menifee County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $2,876 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
