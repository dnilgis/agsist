Oldham County, Kentucky

Rain is at 96% of normal. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.27 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.88 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,267 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.36 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.25, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 56% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 58.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 27.4%.
