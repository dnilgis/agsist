Clay County, Kentucky

Clay County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $51 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,956 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.55 since 1995. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clay County stands at $51 per acre in 2025, down 5.6 percent from 2013. Land value reached $2,956 per acre in 2022, up 63 percent from 2017, 10.3 percent a year. July nights average 66.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 64.5 F normal, warming at 0.51 F per decade since 1976; the 2020s is the first decade reaching 66 F. Drought was severe in only 59 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with just 4 weeks from 2021-2025.
