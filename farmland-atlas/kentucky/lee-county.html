July nightly lows average 66.5 F for 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 64.9 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.4 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought caused 65 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2006, with the heat and drought share at 92 percent for 1989-1999 and 20 percent for 2000-2009. Drought weeks stayed low across 2000-2025, with 54 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse and zero such weeks in 2021-2025. Land value is $3,452 per acre in 2022, up 68.6 percent from 2017-2022. No cropland is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in the county.
