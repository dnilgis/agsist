Cropland rent in Livingston County runs $168 an acre for 2026, up 40.0% from $120 in 2016 and tied for 22nd highest of 86 in Kentucky. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,592 an acre in 2022, up 63.3% from $2,812 in 2017, a bit above the state median of $4,449. Corn here trends at 184.0 bu, and last year came in at 134.8 bu.
