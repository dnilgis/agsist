Henry County, Kentucky

Henry County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $95 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,807 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henry County runs $95 per acre, up 28.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value follows the same path: $4,807 per acre in 2022, up 24.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.5 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 67.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 65.4 F normal for 1991-2020, warming at 0.48 F per decade since 1976. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with none proposed to EIA.
