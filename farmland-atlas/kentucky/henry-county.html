Henry County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.93 per base acre for corn.

Henry County vs Kentucky: $95 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 36% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (32nd lowest of 86).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.63 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 52% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 37.1%.
