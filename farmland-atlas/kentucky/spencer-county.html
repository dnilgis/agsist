Spencer County, Kentucky

Spencer County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $122 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,499 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 169.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21215. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Spencer County runs $122 per acre in 2026, up 22 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census sits at $6,499 per acre, with a 43.1 percent gain from 2017 to 2022. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, so yield rides on rain and heat. Corn yields trended up 3.1 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, median 169.7 bushels, though 2008 fell to 92. Heat and drought drove 54 percent of the $9.8 million in crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share rose to 70 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows average 67.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 65.9 F normal, with no clear trend direction since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
