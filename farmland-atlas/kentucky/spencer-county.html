Spencer County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.79 per base acre for corn.

Spencer County vs Kentucky: $122 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 49% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (43rd lowest of 86).

Quick answers for Spencer County

What is cash rent in Spencer County, Kentucky?

The county average is $122 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Spencer County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $6,499 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Spencer County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 172.8 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 11 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
