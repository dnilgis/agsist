Breckinridge County, Kentucky

Breckinridge County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $127 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,848 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21027. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Breckinridge County averages $127 per acre for 2026, up 21 percent from 2016. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, stood at $3,848 per acre in 2022, after a change of 24.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 3.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 149.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 49.1 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 45 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and its share by period was 60 percent in 1989-1999, 39 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 31 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates yet, though 300 MW of solar has been proposed to EIA.
