USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Bracken County runs $71 an acre in 2026, down 6.6% from $76 in 2016 and 19th lowest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $3,229 an acre in 2022, up 29.3% from $2,498 in 2017, or 5.3% a year. Corn trends at 157.4 bu, 10th lowest of 76 in the state, though last year came in at 177.1 bu. Only 12% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
