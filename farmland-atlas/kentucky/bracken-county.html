Non-irrigated cash rent in Bracken County runs $71 per acre, down 6.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value under the census sits at $3,229 per acre, up 29.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.3 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 1.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2014-2025, median 142.1 bushels per acre, with a worst year of 126.8 in 2024. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved 58 percent, 31 percent, 20 percent, 47 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Excess moisture and flood account for 54 percent of the $5.0 million paid out from 1989-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
