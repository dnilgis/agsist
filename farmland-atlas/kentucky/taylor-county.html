Taylor County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.72 per base acre for corn.

Taylor County vs Kentucky: $162 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 72% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (25th highest of 86).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.55 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.38, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 27% of the median yield. 0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 48.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 39.1%.
