Taylor County, Kentucky

Taylor County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $162 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,547 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 168.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21217. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Taylor County is $162 per acre, up 1.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $33.6 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 49 percent; that share was 46 percent in 1989-1999, 38 percent in 2000-2009, 42 percent in 2010-2019, and 64 percent in 2020-2025. The July average nightly low has moved from 65.6 F (1991-2020 normal) to 67.6 F (2017-2026), part of a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. No cropland here is irrigated (0 percent, 2022 census). Census land value is $4,547 per acre, up 49.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. Solar and wind: 0 MW operating, 0 MW proposed.
