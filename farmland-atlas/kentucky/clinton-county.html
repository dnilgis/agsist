Stands out against its own record

194.9 bu

Corn yield, all practices, 2023: the highest of 12 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2023

Cropland in Clinton County rents for $85 an acre in 2026, up 41.7% from $60 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $125 and ranks 28th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,298 an acre in 2022, up 54.2% from $2,788 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a 14 year trend of 201.6 bu that ranks 19th highest of 76.
