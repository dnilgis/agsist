Non-irrigated cash rent in Allen County averages $129 per acre in 2026, up 32.3 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $5,163 per acre in 2022, up 49.1 percent from 2017, 8.3 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 runs 2.4 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 153.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 107.2 in 2010. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $66.0 million, largest cause excess moisture and flood at 50 percent, with a loss ratio of 1.53 and 13 of 17 crop years paying out more than premium.
