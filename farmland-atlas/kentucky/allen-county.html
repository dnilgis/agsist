Allen County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Near West subregion (12 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,500, pasture $6,000, woodland $4,100. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.83 per base acre for corn.

Allen County vs Kentucky: $129 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 52% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 41st highest of 86).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.09 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.10, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 70% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 49.9%.

Quick answers for Allen County

What is cash rent in Allen County, Kentucky?

The county average is $129 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Allen County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $5,163 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Allen County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 160.0 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
