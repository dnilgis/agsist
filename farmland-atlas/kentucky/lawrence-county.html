Cash rent in Lawrence County runs $44 an acre in 2025, up 41.9% from $31 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,800 an acre in 2022, up 75.1% from $1,599 in 2017. That still sits under the state median of $4,449 and ranks 11th lowest of 114 in Kentucky. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
