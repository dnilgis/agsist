Cash rent in Lawrence County runs $44 an acre in 2025, up 41.9% from $31 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,800 an acre in 2022, up 75.1% from $1,599 in 2017. That still sits under the state median of $4,449 and ranks 11th lowest of 114 in Kentucky. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Lawrence County

What is cash rent in Lawrence County, Kentucky?

The county average is $44 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Lawrence County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $2,800 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
