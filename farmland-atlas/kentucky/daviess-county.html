Daviess County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Mid West subregion (15 counties, 11 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,100, pasture $5,400, woodland $4,400. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.05 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

34.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.67 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 210 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,405 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.96 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.51, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 51% of the median yield. 5.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 32.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 54.7%.
