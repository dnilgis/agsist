Non-irrigated cash rent in Daviess County averages $207 per acre in 2026, up 12.5 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $4,683 per acre in 2022, down 6.1 percent from 2017. Crop insurance history shows $58.9 million in indemnities from 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 55 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 53 percent in 1989-1999 to 22 percent in 2000-2009, 43 percent in 2010-2019, and 19 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were rare: 45 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 in severe drought or worse, with 2 weeks in 2021-2025. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
