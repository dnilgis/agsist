Cropland in Anderson County rents for $73 an acre in 2026, down 3.9% from $76 in 2016. That sits well under the state median of $125 and ranks 21st lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, up 46.1% to $5,426 an acre in 2022 from $3,713 in 2017, which ranks 31st highest of 114. 22% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
