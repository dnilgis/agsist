Cash rent in Wolfe County runs $55 an acre for 2026, up 65.2% from $33 in 2016. That is 13th lowest of 86 in Kentucky, against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,864 an acre in 2022, up 42.1% from $2,016 in 2017, or 7.3% a year. Only 11% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
