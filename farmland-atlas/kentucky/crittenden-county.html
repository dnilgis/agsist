Crittenden County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Far West subregion (12 counties, 9 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,100 an acre; hay $4,100, pasture $3,500, woodland $3,500. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.02 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.63 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 95% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 213 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,470 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.12 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.58, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 28% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 48.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 39.3%.
