McCreary County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.86 per base acre for corn.

Cash rent in McCreary County stood at $44 an acre in 2022. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,161 an acre in 2022, up 28.8% from $2,455 in 2017, or 5.2% a year. That sits below the state median of $4,449 and ranks 21st lowest of 114. Only 13% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for McCreary County

What is farmland worth in McCreary County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $3,161 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
