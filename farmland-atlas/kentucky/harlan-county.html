Harlan County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here is $2,816 per acre, up 80.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 12.5 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed since 1976 at 0.44 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 63.7 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 62.7 F. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county in 67 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2007 with 29 weeks, and only 4 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
