Cropland rent in Russell County runs $92 an acre for 2026, down 8.0% from $100 in 2016 and below the state median of $125, which puts it 30th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings held flat near $3,080 in 2012 and $3,093 in 2017, then climbed 40.4% to $4,342 by 2022. Corn trends at 204.5 bu for 2026, 13th highest of 76 in the state.
