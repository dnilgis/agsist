Russell County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.02 per base acre for corn.

Russell County vs Kentucky: $92 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 34% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (30th lowest of 86).

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

29% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 74% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 196 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.70 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.62, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 40% of the median yield. 0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 45.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.8%.
