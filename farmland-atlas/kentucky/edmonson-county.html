Stands out against its own record

8 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Edmonson County cash rent sits at $118 an acre for 2026, up 40.5% from $84 in 2016 and a little under the state median of $125. That ranks 42nd lowest of 86 in Kentucky. The census value of land and buildings was $4,420 an acre in 2022, up 39.1% from 2017, or 6.8% a year. The corn trend line points to 191.2 bu for 2026, ranked 34th highest of 76, though 2025 came in at 167.6 bu.
