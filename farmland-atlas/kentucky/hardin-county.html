As of Oct 14, cash rent in Hardin County runs $142 an acre in 2026, tied for 35th highest of 86 in Kentucky and up 2.2% from $139 in 2016. That is about as flat as rent gets over ten years. The census value of land and buildings moved more, reaching $5,886 an acre in 2022, up 28.1% from $4,594 in 2017 and 22nd highest of 114 in the state. Corn trends at 188.5 bu for 2026, close to the state median of 188.0 bu.
