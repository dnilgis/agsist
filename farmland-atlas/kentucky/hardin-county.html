Hardin County, Kentucky

Hardin County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $142 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,886 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hardin County runs $142 per acre in 2026, up 2.2 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,886 per acre in 2022, with land value up 28.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.1 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value sits at 2.11 percent. Corn yields have trended up 3.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 159.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 49.6 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 63 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and the share by period ran 56 percent, 68 percent, 70 percent, and 47 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
