Pike County, Kentucky

Pike County, KY: land and buildings $1,983 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21195. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land here carries a census value of $1,983 per acre, and that value moved 40.2 percent from 2017-2022, 7 percent a year. July nights average 65.7 F for 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 64.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.48 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched this county in 51 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2007 with 24 weeks, and none from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
