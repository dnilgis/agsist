Campbell County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.35 per base acre for corn.

Campbell County vs Kentucky: $45 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 9% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 9th lowest of 86).
