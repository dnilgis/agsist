Campbell County, Kentucky

Campbell County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $45 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,228 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21037. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Campbell County runs $45 per acre in 2026, up 12.5 percent from 2016. Census land and building value stood at $6,228 per acre in 2022, up 23.1 percent from 2017, a pace of 4.2 percent a year. July nights average 66.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 65.2 F, with the July average low first reaching 66 F in the 2010s; the long trend since 1976 runs 0.34 F per decade with no clear direction. Drought has been light: 25 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, none from 2021-2025, though heat and drought still account for 44 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1995-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
