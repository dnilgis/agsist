Madison County, Kentucky

Rain is at 100% of normal. Bluegrass subregion (11 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,300 an acre; hay $7,600, pasture $6,800, woodland $4,800. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.88 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.5%.
