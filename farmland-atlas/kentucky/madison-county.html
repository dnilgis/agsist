Cash rent in Madison County sits at $40 an acre for 2026, the same $40 it was in 2016, and 7th lowest of 86 in the state against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,386 an acre in 2022, up 26.5% from $3,468 in 2017. Corn runs light here, with a 14-yr trend of 151.6 bu, 6th lowest of 76 in the state.
