$174

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 28.9% from $135 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Meade County rents for $174 an acre in 2026, up 28.9% from $135 in 2016. That rent ranks 18th highest of 86 in Kentucky, well over the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,432 an acre in 2022, up 25.5% from $4,328 in 2017. Corn runs a 14-yr trend of 206.4 bu, 10th highest of 76 in the state.
