Meade County, Kentucky

Meade County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $174 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,432 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 166.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21163. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Meade County stands at $174 per acre for 2026, up 28.9 percent from 2016. Land value carries at $5,432 per acre in the 2022 census, with the change from 2017 to 2022 at 25.5 percent, 4.6 percent a year. No cropland here is irrigated, so the corn yield trend of 4.1 bushels per acre a year rides on rainfall, and 2012 still shows the risk at 54.6 bushels against a median of 166.5. Heat and drought drove 71 percent of the $21.5 million in indemnities paid out from 1989-2025, though drought weeks have run at 0 for 2021-2025. July nights average 66.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 65.8 F normal, with the long-term trend at 0.16 F per decade showing no clear direction.
