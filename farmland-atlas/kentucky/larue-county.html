Cash rent in Larue County stood at $181 an acre in 2026, up 9.7% from $165 in 2016 and 12th highest of 86 in Kentucky. The census value of land and buildings was $5,124 an acre in 2022, up 44.4% from $3,549 in 2017, against a state median of $4,449. The county corn trend projects 238.0 bu for 2026, highest of 76 in the state.
