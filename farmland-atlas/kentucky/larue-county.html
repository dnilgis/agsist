Larue County, Kentucky

Larue County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $181 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,124 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 154.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21123. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Larue County runs $181 per acre in 2026, up 9.7 percent from 2016. The census put land and buildings at $5,124 per acre in 2022, after a 44.4 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, 7.6 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 5.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 154.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 62.7 in 2012. July nights average 67.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 65.7 F, and the county saw 10 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. Heat and drought account for 57 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
