Cash rent in Robertson County runs $75 an acre for 2025, up 86.2% from $40 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,643 an acre in 2022, up 66.6% from $2,187 in 2017, or 10.7% a year. Quick answers for Robertson County

What is cash rent in Robertson County, Kentucky?

The county average is $75 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Robertson County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $3,643 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
