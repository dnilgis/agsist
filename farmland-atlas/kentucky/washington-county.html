Washington County, Kentucky

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County, KY runs $62 per acre, against a census land value of $4,727 per acre. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, so corn yields track weather directly: the median 2008-2024 stands at 163 bushels per acre, but 2012 fell to 66.6. Heat and drought have driven crop insurance losses, rising from 54 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 80 percent in 2020-2025, with a loss ratio of 0.94 over 1989-2025 and 0.9 over 2016-2025. Drought weeks stayed low, 6 of them in 2021-2025, and the July nightly low has moved from a 65.5 F normal to 67 F in 2017-2026, though the long trend since 1976 shows no clear direction. Solar is proposed at 120 MW; none is built yet.
