Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.95 per base acre for corn.

Washington County vs Kentucky: $61.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 19% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (17th lowest of 86).

Quick answers for Washington County

What is cash rent in Washington County, Kentucky?

The county average is $62 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Washington County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $4,727 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Washington County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 199.6 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
