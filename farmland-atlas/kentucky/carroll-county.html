Carroll County, Kentucky

Carroll County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $80 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,769 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carroll County is $80 per acre in 2026, up 31.1 percent from 2014. Land value in 2022 was $4,769 per acre, up 40.6 percent from 2017, 7 percent a year. July nights average 68.1 F over 2017-2026, against a 65.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.58 F per decade, warming. Drought weeks were 33 of 1357 from 2000-2025, and 2 from 2021-2025.
