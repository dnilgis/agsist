Union County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Mid West subregion (15 counties, 11 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,100, pasture $5,400, woodland $4,400. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.29 per base acre for corn.

Union County vs Kentucky: $259 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 100% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (highest of 86).

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

11.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.55 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,487 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.13 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.31, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. 1.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 40.6%.
