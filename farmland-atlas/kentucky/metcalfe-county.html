Metcalfe County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,990 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.50 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.28 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 95% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 197 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,287 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,990 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $180 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 26.8% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 244.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,724 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 181 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.50 per base acre, PLC $2.46.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (98 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $69.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 12 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $23.94 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.40, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 30% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 54.2%.
