Stands out against its own record

7 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Metcalfe County runs $180 an acre for 2026, tied for 13th highest of 86 in Kentucky and well above the state median of $125. That rent is up 26.8% from $142 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,990 an acre in 2022, up 49.0% from $2,677 in 2017, though still 40th lowest of 114 in the state. Corn trends at 173.2 bu, short of the state median of 188.0 bu.
