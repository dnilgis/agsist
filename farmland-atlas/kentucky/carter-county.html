As of Oct 16, cash rent in Carter County runs $28 an acre for 2025, down 12.5% from $32 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 58.5% from $1,933 in 2017 to $3,064 in 2022, still 17th lowest of 114 against a state median of $4,449. Corn trends at 117.4 bu, 2nd lowest of 76 in the state, and only 11% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
