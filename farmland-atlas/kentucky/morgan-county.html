Cropland rent in Morgan County runs $25 an acre in 2026, 3rd lowest of 86 counties in Kentucky against a state median of $125. That rent is down 16.7% from $30 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,458 an acre in 2022, up 26.2% from $1,948 in 2017, and still 4th lowest of 114. Only 11% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
