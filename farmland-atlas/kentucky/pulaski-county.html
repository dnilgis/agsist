Cash rent in Pulaski County runs $105 an acre for 2026, up 10.5% from $95 in 2016 and under the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $4,741 an acre in 2022, up 46.4% from $3,238 in 2017, which puts it 47th highest of 114. Corn trends at 174.6 bu, 22nd lowest of 76, and 2025 came in at 124.1 bu.
