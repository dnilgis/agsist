Jefferson County farmland rents for $53 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $13,280 an acre in 2022, up 8.0% from $12,296 in 2017, or 1.6% a year. That sits well over the state median of $4,449 and ranks 2nd highest of 114. Only 16% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
