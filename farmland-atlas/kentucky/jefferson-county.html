Jefferson County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.66 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Jefferson County

What is cash rent in Jefferson County, Kentucky?

The county average is $53 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Jefferson County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $13,280 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
