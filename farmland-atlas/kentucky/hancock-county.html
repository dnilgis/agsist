Hancock County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Near West subregion (12 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,500 an acre; hay $6,500, pasture $6,000, woodland $4,100. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.44 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

9.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 210 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,329 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.51 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.59, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 40% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 54.2%.
