Martin County, Kentucky

Martin County, KY: land and buildings $2,372 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21159. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Martin County had 9,488 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low, 2017-2026, is 65.6 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.4 F, with a warming trend since 1976 of 0.5 F per decade. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 38 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst year 2007 with 25 weeks, and only 2 weeks from 2021-2025. Solar operating stands at 111 MW; wind operating is 0 MW.
