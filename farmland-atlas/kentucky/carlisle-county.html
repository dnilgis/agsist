Carlisle County, Kentucky

Land and buildings run $5,470 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.70 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 219 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,575 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,470 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $180 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 29.5% from 2016 to 2026. 62.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 15.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 245 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.63 at Mound City, 19 miles.

4 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $60.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 52 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.46 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.60 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 39% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 40.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1994 to 2025; too much rain caused 43.2%.
