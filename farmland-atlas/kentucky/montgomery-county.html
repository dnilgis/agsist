Montgomery County, Kentucky

Montgomery County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,254 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 130.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21173. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Montgomery County is $60 per acre in 2026, down 14.3 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value is $4,254 per acre, up 21.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.9 percent a year. July average nightly low was 64.2 F under the 1991-2020 normal, and 65.6 F for 2017-2026, warming at 0.41 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks fell from 49 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025 to 1 in 2021-2025. Solar and wind operating and proposed capacity are 0 MW.
