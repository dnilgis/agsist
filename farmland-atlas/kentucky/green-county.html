Rain is at 93% of normal. South Central subregion (16 counties, 10 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,900 an acre; hay $5,800, pasture $4,500, woodland $3,000. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.24 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Green County

What is cash rent in Green County, Kentucky?

The county average is $142 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Green County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $4,242 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Green County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 184.4 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
