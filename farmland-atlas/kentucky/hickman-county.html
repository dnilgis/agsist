Hickman County, Kentucky

Rain is at 84% of normal. Far West subregion (12 counties, 9 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $7,100 an acre; hay $4,100, pasture $3,500, woodland $3,500. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.17 per base acre for corn.

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.73 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 43% of the median yield. 7.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 31.7%.
