Cash rent in Hickman County runs $180 an acre for 2026, up 16.9% from $154 in 2016. That sits well above the Kentucky median of $125 and ranks tied for 13th highest of 86. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,670 an acre in 2022, up 41.3% from $4,014 in 2017, or 7.2% a year. Corn trends toward 195.2 bu for 2026, against a state median of 188.0.
