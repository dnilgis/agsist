Cash rent in Woodford County runs $113 an acre for 2026, up 32.9% from $85 in 2016. That rent ranks 41st lowest of 86 in Kentucky, a bit under the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $8,841 an acre in 2022, up 9.0% from $8,111 in 2017, and it ranks 4th highest of 114. Corn trends at 181.5 bu for 2026, short of the state median of 188.0.
