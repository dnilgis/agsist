Pendleton County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.89 per base acre for corn.

Pendleton County vs Kentucky: $50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 13% of the other 85 Kentucky counties with a 2026 rent (12th lowest of 86).

Quick answers for Pendleton County

What is cash rent in Pendleton County, Kentucky?

The county average is $50 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Pendleton County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $4,055 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Pendleton County, Kentucky?

The county's 2020-24 average is 162.8 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
