Owen County, Kentucky

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central subregion (15 counties, 7 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,700 an acre; hay $6,600, pasture $6,100, woodland $5,200. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.24 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

6.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.01 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 204 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,248 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Quick answers for Owen County

What is cash rent in Owen County, Kentucky?

The county average is $87 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Owen County, Kentucky?

Land and buildings were worth $3,637 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Owen County, Kentucky?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 167.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
