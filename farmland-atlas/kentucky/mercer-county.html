USDA NASS county yields, 2023

Cash rent in Mercer County runs $79 an acre for 2026, down 36.8% from $125 in 2016, and it sits 23rd lowest of 86 against a state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 35.7% to $5,326 an acre in 2022 from $3,925 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 237.0 bu that ranks 2nd highest of 76 in the state.
