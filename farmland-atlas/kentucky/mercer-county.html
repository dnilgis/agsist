Rain is at 103% of normal. Bluegrass subregion (11 counties, 8 agent surveys), UK survey 2025: cropland $8,300 an acre; hay $7,600, pasture $6,800, woodland $4,800. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.94 per base acre for corn.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.89 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.94, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 32% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 52.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 21.2%.
