Knott County had 9,659 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Knott County stands at $2,596 per acre in 2022, up 103 percent from 2017, a change of 15.2 percent a year. July nights average 65.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64 F, with a warming trend of 0.48 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought has touched the county in 67 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2007 with 27 weeks, and only 4 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
