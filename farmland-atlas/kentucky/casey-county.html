Cropland rent in Casey County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 40.5% from $79 in 2016 and under the state median of $125. The census value of land and buildings was $3,369 an acre in 2022, up 43.8% from $2,343 in 2017, and ranks tied for 26th lowest of 114 in Kentucky. Corn trends at 174.0 bu for 2026, though the 2025 crop came in at 118.7 bu.
