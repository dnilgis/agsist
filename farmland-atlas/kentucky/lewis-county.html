As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Lewis County runs $91 an acre for 2025, up 28.2% from $71 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,517 an acre in 2022, up 20.1% from $2,096 in 2017, and that ranks 5th lowest of 114 against a state median of $4,449. Only 10% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
