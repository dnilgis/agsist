Lewis County, Kentucky

Lewis County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $91 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,517 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 131.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21135. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lewis County is $91 per acre in 2025, up 28.2 percent from 2014. Corn yield trend runs 4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 131.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 99.4 in 2012.
