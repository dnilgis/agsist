Gallatin County, Kentucky

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,786 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.46 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 39.53 inches, 93% of normal (42.29 inches) through Nov 17, at Warsaw Markland Dam. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,250 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,786 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 36.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 163 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 122 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.46 per base acre, PLC $3.30.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (131.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $53.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.78 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.07 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 59%.
