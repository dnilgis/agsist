Gallatin County, Kentucky

Gallatin County, KY: non-irrigated cash rent $110 an acre (2017); land and buildings $3,786 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 21077. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Gallatin County ran $110 per acre in 2017. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,786 per acre in 2022, up 33.1 percent from 2017, or 5.9 percent a year. Corn yield had a median of 157.1 bushels per acre over 2009-2025, with the worst year, 2011, at 125.7. Crop insurance indemnities totaled $4.0 million across closed crop years 1993-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 59 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities rose from 12 percent in 2000-2009 to 30 percent in 2010-2019 to 70 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio was 1.01 over 1989-2025 and 1.07 over 2016-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
