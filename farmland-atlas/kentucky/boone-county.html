Boone County cash rent sits at $45 an acre for 2026, tied for 9th lowest of 86 in Kentucky against a state median of $125. That rent is down 22.4% from $58 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings runs the other way, at $8,920 an acre in 2022, up 48.6% from $6,004 in 2017 and 3rd highest of 114 in the state. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
