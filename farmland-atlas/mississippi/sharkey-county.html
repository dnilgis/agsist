$145

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 43.6% from $101 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Sharkey County runs $145 an acre for 2026, up 43.6% from $101 in 2016 and 9th highest of 71 in Mississippi against a state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $3,746 an acre in 2022, up 13.2% from $3,310 in 2017. Corn made 209.0 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 202.6 bu, and 80% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
