As of Oct 16, cash rent in Panola County runs $120 an acre for 2026, up 48.1% from $81 in 2016. That sits well above the state median of $67 and ranks 15th highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $3,142 an acre in 2022, up 27.6% from 2017, which comes to 5.0% a year. Corn has run a median of 157.1 bu from 2008 to 2021, and 34% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
