$66

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 13.8% from $58 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Union County cropland rented for $66 an acre in 2026, up 13.8% from $58 in 2016 and tied for 33rd lowest of 71 against a state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $2,286 an acre in 2022, up 14.4% from $1,999 in 2017, and that sits 5th lowest of 82 in the state. Corn has run a median of 156.1 bu over 2012 to 2022, and 28% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
