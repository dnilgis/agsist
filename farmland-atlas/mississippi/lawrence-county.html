Cropland in Lawrence County rents for $19 an acre in 2026, 3rd lowest of 71 in Mississippi against a state median of $67. Pasture goes for $15 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,498 an acre in 2022, up 8.9% from $3,211 in 2017, or 1.7% a year, and 32nd highest of 82. Only 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
