Cropland rent in Grenada County runs $112 an acre in 2026, up 23.1% from $91 in 2016 and 16th highest of 71 in Mississippi, well over the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $2,691 an acre in 2022, up 22.3% from $2,200 in 2017, yet still 19th lowest of 82 against a state median of $3,313. Corn has yielded a median 146.7 bu over the years on record, and 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
