Cropland rent in Oktibbeha County runs $55 an acre for 2026, up 81.7% from $30 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $67 and ranks 28th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $2,845 an acre in 2022, up 1.8% from $2,796 in 2017, or 0.3% a year. Only 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
