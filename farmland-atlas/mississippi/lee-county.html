Lee County, Mississippi

Lee County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $90 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,286 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 116.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28081. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lee County averages $90 per acre in 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. Corn yields trend up 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 116.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 85.9 in 2009. July nights average 72 F over 2017-2026, against a 70.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade, warming. Solar and wind operating and proposed capacity are both 0 MW.
