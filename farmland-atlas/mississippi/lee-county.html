Lee County's cropland has been hit more by wet years than dry ones. Flood and excess moisture make up 57 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and the county has paid out more than it took in premium in only 2 of 19 closed crop years. The loss ratio over the ten closed years 2016-2025 is 0.95, so the same pattern holds recently. Heat and drought claims went from 44 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 40 percent in 2020-2025, and the county's worst drought year was 2007 with 21 weeks. Non-irrigated cash rent is $90 per acre for 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value was $3,286 per acre in 2022.
