As of Oct 16, cash rent in Tippah County ran $65 an acre in 2026, up 3.2% from $63 in 2016 and ranked 32nd lowest of 71 in Mississippi. The census value of land and buildings was $2,378 an acre in 2022, up 14.8% from $2,072 in 2017, which put it 10th lowest of 82 in the state. Corn trends at 146.7 bu for 2026, below the state median of 155.9 bu, and only 19% of farmland was rented in 2022.
