Tippah County, Mississippi

Tippah County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $65 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,378 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 138.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28139. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tippah County runs $65 per acre, up 3.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yields have trended up 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 138.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 93 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 46 percent, and its share rose from 14 percent in 1989-1999 to 54 percent in 2000-2009, then fell to 21 percent in 2010-2019, then rose to 56 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.07, above the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.76. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland.
