Cash rent in Perry County was $39 an acre in 2020, up 188.9% from $14 in 2009. The census value of land and buildings was $3,656 an acre in 2022, up 13.9% from $3,210 in 2017, or 2.6% a year. That ranks 26th highest of 82 in Mississippi and sits above the state median of $3,313. Only 9% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
