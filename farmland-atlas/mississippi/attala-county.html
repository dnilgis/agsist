Attala County, Mississippi

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,239 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.83 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.48 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 72% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 233 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,828 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,239 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $85 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 50.4% from 2016 to 2026. 8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 35.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 67,136 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.83 per base acre, PLC $1.68.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (66.84 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $66.74 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.77 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.78 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.44 per base acre, PLC $5.85. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $39.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 36 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.59 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.25, 2016 to 2025. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 5.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 20.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 57.2%.

Cropland in Attala County rents for $85 an acre in 2026, up 50.4% from $57 in 2016. That rent sits above the state median of $67 and ranks 26th highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $2,239 an acre in 2022, up 21.3% from $1,846 in 2017, yet it still ranks 4th lowest of 82 in Mississippi. Only 8% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
