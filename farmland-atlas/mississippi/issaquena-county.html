Non-irrigated cash rent in Issaquena County is $153 per acre in 2026, up 17.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $202 per acre, a multiple of 1.32 over non-irrigated this year, against 1.13 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Irrigation covers 43 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and 95 percent of that water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Crop insurance history leans on water, not heat: excess moisture and flood account for 71 percent of the $64.0 million paid out over 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.27, higher than the 0.93 mark for the full 1989-2025 period. Census land value sits at $2,878 per acre, up 5.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
