Pontotoc County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $67 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,758 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 105.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28115. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pontotoc County runs $66 per acre, up 7.3 percent from 2016 to 2026.
