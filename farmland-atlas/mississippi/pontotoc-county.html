Cropland rent in Pontotoc County runs $67 an acre for 2026, right at the state median and 36th lowest of 71. That is up 7.3% from $62 in 2016, not much movement for ten years. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,758 an acre in 2022, up 40.1% from $1,968 in 2017, and still 22nd lowest of 82 in the state. Corn trends at 135.4 bu, 3rd lowest of 18 against a state median of 155.9 bu.
