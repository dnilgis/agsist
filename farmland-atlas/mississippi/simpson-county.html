As of Oct 16, cash rent in Simpson County runs $45 an acre for 2025, up 34.8% from $33 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,201 an acre in 2022, up 16.1% from $2,758 in 2017. That sits a bit under the state median of $3,313 and ranks 35th lowest of 82. In 2022, 40% of the farmland here was rented.
