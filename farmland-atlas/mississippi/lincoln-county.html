Cropland rent in Lincoln County runs $26 an acre in 2026, the same $26 it was in 2016. That sits well under the state median of $67 and ranks 12th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,880 an acre in 2022, up 26.3% from $3,071 in 2017, and ranks 20th highest of 82. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
