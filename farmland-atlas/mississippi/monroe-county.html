Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 39.5% from $60 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Monroe County runs $83 an acre for 2026, up 39.5% from $60 in 2016 and 27th highest of 71 in Mississippi. The census value of land and buildings was $2,359 an acre in 2022, up 7.3% from 2017, and that ranks 8th lowest of 82 against a state median of $3,313. Corn trends at 136.4 bu for 2026, 5th lowest of 18 in the state.
