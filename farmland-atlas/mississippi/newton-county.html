$32

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 68% from $19 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 14, cash rent in Newton County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 68.4% from $19 in 2016 and still well under the state median of $67, tied for 17th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $3,337 an acre in 2022, up 40.7% from 2017, which sits close to the state median of $3,313 and ranks 40th highest of 82. Pasture rents for $25 an acre in 2026, and 17% of farmland was rented in 2022.
