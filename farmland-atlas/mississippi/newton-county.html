Newton County, Mississippi

Newton County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,337 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28101. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Newton County stands at $32 per acre, up 68.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value in 2022 was $3,337 per acre, up 40.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.1 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal low was 69.7 F, against 71.9 F for 2017-2026, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.63 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance losses from 2014-2025 totaled $482,451, mostly excess moisture and flood at 77 percent, while heat and drought's share rose from 2 percent in 2010-2019 to 32 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 8 percent of harvested cropland. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
