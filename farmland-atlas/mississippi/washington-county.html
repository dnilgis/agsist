Washington County, Mississippi

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,488 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.66 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 59% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 21 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,046 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,488 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $172 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 62.3% from 2016 to 2026. 66% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 1.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 31,991 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.66 per base acre, PLC $3.91.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (155.72 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $60.13 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $34.41 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.47 per base acre, PLC $9.60. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (53.16 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $37.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 366 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.96 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.75, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 72.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51.1%.
