Washington County, Mississippi

Washington County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $172 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,488 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 192.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28151. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County is $172 per acre in 2026, up 62.3 percent from 2016. Irrigation is widespread: 73 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, drawing 95 percent of its water from groundwater. Land value stood at $4,488 per acre in 2022, up 21 percent from 2017.
