Washington County cash rent runs $172 an acre in 2026, highest of 71 in Mississippi against a state median of $67. That rent was $106 in 2016 and is up 62.3% since. The census value of land and buildings was $4,488 an acre in 2022, 7th highest of 82, up 21.0% from $3,708 in 2017. Corn came in at 174.3 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 196.3 bu.
