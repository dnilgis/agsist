As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Alcorn County runs $60 an acre for 2026, up 16.7% from $51 in 2016 and below the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $2,375 an acre in 2022, up 11.5% from $2,131 in 2017, which ranks 9th lowest of 82 in Mississippi. Corn has made a median 145.2 bu over 11 years from 2008 to 2020, and 31% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
