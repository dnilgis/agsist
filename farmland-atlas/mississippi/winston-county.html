Cropland rent in Winston County runs $27 an acre in 2025, up 47.2% from $18 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,658 an acre in 2022, up 16.7% from $2,277 in 2017. That sits under the state median of $3,313 and ranks 18th lowest of 82. Only 15% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
