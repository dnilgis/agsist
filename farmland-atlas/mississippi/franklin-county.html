Franklin County, Mississippi

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,207 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.27 per base acre for corn.

Franklin County vs Mississippi: $22 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 7% of the other 70 Mississippi counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 6th lowest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: at least 269 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,006 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,207 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.40 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). 17% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 13% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 15% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 69.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,701 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 4.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2020; too much rain caused 38.8%.

Quick answers for Franklin County

What is cash rent in Franklin County, Mississippi?

The county average is $22 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Franklin County, Mississippi?

Land and buildings were worth $3,207 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
