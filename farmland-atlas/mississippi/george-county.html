George County, Mississippi

George County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $59 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,441 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 101.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28039. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in George County runs $59 per acre, up 9.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value sits at $4,441 per acre in the 2022 census, up 19 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.5 percent a year. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 34 percent of the $9.4 million paid since 1992, and the loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 1.03, above the 1992-2025 figure of 0.79. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 72.7 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.2 F, and the long-term trend since 1976 runs 0.56 F per decade, warming. Only 4 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated here, and solar and wind development stand at 0 MW.
