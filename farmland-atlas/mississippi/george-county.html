$59

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2010; up 9.3% from $54 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

George County cash rent runs $59 an acre for 2026, up 9.3% from $54 in 2016 and under the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,441 an acre in 2022, up 19.0% from 2017 and 8th highest of 82 in Mississippi. Corn is another story, with a trend yield of 136.3 bu that sits 4th lowest of 18 against a state median of 155.9 bu.
