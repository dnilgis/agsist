Amite County, Mississippi

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,315 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.06 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 46.7 inches, 81% of normal (57.85 inches) through Nov 17, at Liberty, 6.4 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 257 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,015 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,315 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 50% from 2016 to 2026. 10.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 164.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.06 per base acre, PLC $1.75.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (69.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (27.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $38.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 58.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.1%.

Cash rent in Amite County sits at $21 an acre for 2026, up 50.0% from $14 in 2016. That rent ranks 5th lowest of 71 in Mississippi, well under the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $3,315 an acre in 2022, up 3.9% from $3,192 in 2017, close to the state median of $3,313. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
