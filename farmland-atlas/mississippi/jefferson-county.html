Jefferson County, Mississippi

Jefferson County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,384 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28063. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jefferson County averages $50 per acre for 2026. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,384 per acre in 2022, up 34.3 percent from 2017, 6.1 percent a year. Crop insurance losses since 1990 total $12.8 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 94 percent; heat and drought stayed at 0 percent of indemnities through 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019, then rose to 4 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 87 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2000 with 29 weeks, and 31 weeks from 2021-2025. July nighttime lows have warmed 0.34 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 72.4 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.3 F.
