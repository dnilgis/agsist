Cropland in Jefferson County rents for $50 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $67, which puts it 27th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $3,384 an acre in 2022, up 34.3% from $2,520 in 2017, or 6.1% a year. That sits near the state median of $3,313 and ranks 37th highest of 82. Renters farm 29% of the land here.
