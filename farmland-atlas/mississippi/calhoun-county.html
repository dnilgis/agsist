Cash rent in Calhoun County is $132 an acre for 2026, down 0.8% from $133 in 2016. That $132 ranks 11th highest of 71 in Mississippi, where the state median is $67. The census value of land and buildings was $2,428 an acre in 2022, up 36.7% from $1,776 in 2017, yet it still sits 13th lowest of 82 in the state. Corn made 161.6 bu in 2025.
