Calhoun County, Mississippi

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,428 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.18 per base acre for corn.

Calhoun County vs Mississippi: $132 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 86% of the other 70 Mississippi counties with a 2026 rent (11th highest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.23 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 71% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 229 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,796 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,428 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $132 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 30% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 0.8% from 2016 to 2026. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 54.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 160 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,150 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.18 per base acre, PLC $2.32.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (92.41 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $88.15 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $20.42 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.42 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.50 per base acre, PLC $6.93. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.41 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $41.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 118 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.39 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.54, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 60% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 61.9%.
