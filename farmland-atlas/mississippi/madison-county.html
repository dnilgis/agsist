Madison County, Mississippi

Madison County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,486 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28089. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County runs $70 per acre, up 12 percent from 2016 to 2026. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Corn yields have trended up 4.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 153.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 71.1 in 2011. July nights average 72.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.9 F, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.5 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought account for 41 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, with the loss ratio at 0.82 over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 versus 0.71 over 1989-2025.
