Webster County, Mississippi

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,215 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 229 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,786 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,215 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $125 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 40.4% from 2016 to 2026. 32.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 40.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 7,500 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.96 per base acre, PLC $2.57.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (102.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $39.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 201 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.88 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.65 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 67% of the median yield. 1.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 17.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 54.8%.
