Excess moisture and flood is the top insurance claim here, running 55 percent of indemnities paid from 1989 to 2025. Cash rent is $125 per acre in 2026, up 40.4 percent from 2016. Corn yields have climbed 3.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 150 bushels. CRP ground covers 26.3 percent of the cropland base. July nights have warmed 0.38 F per decade since 1976, and 50 Julys since 1900 have had average lows at or above 70 F.
