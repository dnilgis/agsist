Clarke County, Mississippi

Clarke County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,758 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clarke County runs $23 per acre, up 9.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, stood at $3,758 per acre in 2022, up 68 percent from 2017, or 10.9 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 72.5 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.5 F, with a trend of 0.61 F per decade since 1976. Drought has stayed limited, with severe drought or worse in only 73 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, and just 11 weeks from 2021-2025.
