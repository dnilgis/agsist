Cropland rent in Clarke County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 9.5% from $21 in 2016 and tied for 8th lowest of 71 against a state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,758 an acre in 2022, up 68.0% from $2,237 in 2017. That puts it 22nd highest of 82 in the state. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
