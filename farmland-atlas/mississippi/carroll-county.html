Cash rent in Carroll County runs $77 an acre for 2026, down 19.9% from $96 in 2016. That still sits above the state median of $67 and ranks 31st highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 35.9% to $2,905 an acre in 2022 from $2,138 in 2017. Corn made 172.6 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 171.3 bu.
