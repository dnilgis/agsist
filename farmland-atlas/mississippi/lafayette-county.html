Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 132% from $44 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Lafayette County runs $101 an acre for 2026, up 132.2% from $44 in 2016 and 21st highest of 71 in Mississippi. The census value of land and buildings was $3,239 an acre in 2022, up 18.2% from $2,740 in 2017, against a state median of $3,313. Corn trends to 191.8 bu for 2026, 2nd highest of 18 in the state. Only 14% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
