Lafayette County, Mississippi

Lafayette County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $101 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,239 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 132.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lafayette County is $101 per acre in 2026, up 132.2 percent from 2016. Median corn yield 2009-2023 is 132.5 bushels per acre, with a worst year of 96.5 in 2012. July nightly lows average 71.1 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.9 F, with a long-term trend of 0.29 F per decade and no clear direction. Irrigated cropland is 3 percent of harvested acres, and severe drought or worse covered 80 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2000 at 24 weeks. Land value is $3,239 per acre in 2022, up 18.2 percent from 2017-2022.
