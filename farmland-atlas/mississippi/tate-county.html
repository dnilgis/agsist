Cash rent in Tate County runs $74 an acre for 2026, down 2.0% from $75 in 2016 and above the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $3,310 an acre in 2022, up 17.2% from $2,825 in 2017, about even with the state median of $3,313. Corn trends at 192.3 bu for 2026, highest of 18 in the state, after 187.0 bu in 2025.
