Hinds County, Mississippi

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,474 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.03 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 39.95 inches, 74% of normal (53.81 inches) through Nov 17, at Raymond, 2.7 mi E. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,992 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,474 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $66 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 9.1% from 2016 to 2026. 30.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 125.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,712 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.03 per base acre, PLC $2.39.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (95.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $38.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 611 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.10 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.13 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 53% of the median yield. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 10.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 45.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 30.7%.
