Cropland rent in Itawamba County runs $66 an acre for 2024, up 36.5% from $48 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,570 an acre in 2022, up 50.1% from $1,712 in 2017, which works out to 8.5% a year. That still sits under the state median of $3,313 and ranks 15th lowest of 82. Pasture rents for $16 an acre in 2026.
