Wilkinson County, Mississippi

Wilkinson County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,905 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28157. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Land value rose 21 percent from 2017-2022, 3.9 percent a year. Drought weeks in severe or worse conditions ran 102 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 35 in 2021-2025 alone, matching the worst year, 2000. The July average nightly low has moved from 71.7 F normal to 73 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976.
