Jasper County, Mississippi

Cash rent in Jasper County ran $20 an acre in 2026, 4th lowest of 71 in Mississippi, against a state median of $67. That is up 39.3% from $14 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,476 an acre in 2022, up 23.9% from $2,805 in 2017 and 34th highest of 82 in the state.
