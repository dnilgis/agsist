Jasper County, Mississippi

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,476 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.92 per base acre for corn.

Jasper County vs Mississippi: $19.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 4% of the other 70 Mississippi counties with a 2026 rent (4th lowest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 244 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,916 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,476 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $19.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.55 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). 18% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 39.3% from 2016 to 2026. 11% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 183.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,075 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.92 per base acre, PLC $1.68.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (66.82 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $20.07 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.77 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.71 per base acre, PLC $5.49. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $39.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Quick answers for Jasper County

What is cash rent in Jasper County, Mississippi?

The county average is $20 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Jasper County, Mississippi?

Land and buildings were worth $3,476 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
