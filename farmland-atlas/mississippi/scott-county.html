Cropland rent in Scott County runs $46 an acre for 2026, up 55.9% from $30 in 2016. That is 25th lowest of 71 counties, under the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $3,382 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $2,546 in 2017, and it sits 38th highest of 82 against a state median of $3,313.
