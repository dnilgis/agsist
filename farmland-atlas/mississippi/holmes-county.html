Holmes County, Mississippi

Holmes County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $146 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,992 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 183.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28051. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Holmes County runs $146 per acre, up 35.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated land rents at $197 per acre, a multiple of 1.35 over non-irrigated in 2026, compared with 1.14 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Corn yields trend up 3.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 183.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 131 in 2008. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 58 percent, and the heat and drought share has fallen across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, then risen slightly in 2020-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with none proposed to EIA.
