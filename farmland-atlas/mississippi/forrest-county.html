Forrest County, Mississippi

Forrest County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2024); land and buildings $5,040 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Forrest County averages $60 per acre in 2024, up 22.7 percent from 2012. Land value under the census stands at $5,040 per acre, up 18.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.5 percent a year. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Heat and drought accounted for 5 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999 and 37 percent in 2020-2025, and the July average nightly low has moved from a 70.6 F normal to 72.1 F over 2017-2026. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 runs at 1.21, against 0.97 for the full 1989-2025 record.
