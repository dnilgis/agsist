Cash rent in Bolivar County runs $123 an acre in 2026, up 23.6% from $100 in 2016 and tied for 13th highest of 71 in Mississippi, where the median is $67. The census value of land and buildings was $4,310 an acre in 2022, up 42.5% from $3,025 in 2017 and 9th highest of 82. Corn made 199.7 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 190.9 bu.
