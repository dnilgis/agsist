Cash rent in DeSoto County was $110 an acre in 2026, 18th highest of 71 in Mississippi and up 73.2% from $64 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,266 an acre in 2022, 11th highest of 82, up 41.8% from $3,009 in 2017. Corn made 140.5 bu in 2025, under the county's long run median of 148.5 bu.
