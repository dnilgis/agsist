Non-irrigated cash rent in DeSoto County stands at $110 per acre in 2026, up 73.2 percent from 2016. Census land value reached $4,266 per acre in 2022, a change of 41.8 percent from 2017, or 7.2 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 2.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 148.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 100.2 in 2011. Crop insurance shows a loss ratio of 0.87 over 1991-2025, closed crop years, rising to 1.11 over 2016-2025; excess moisture and flood account for 61 percent of indemnities paid since 1991. Drought held at 7 percent of weeks from 2000-2025, with 23 weeks recorded in 2021-2025.
