DeSoto County, Mississippi

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,266 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.82 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 43.17 inches, 86% of normal (50.47 inches) through Nov 18, at Olive Branch. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,918 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,266 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $110 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 73.2% from 2016 to 2026. 52% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 33.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 190 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,851 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.82 per base acre, PLC $3.01.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (120 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.17 per base acre, PLC $7.98. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $41.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 50 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.98 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.11, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 30.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1991 to 2025; too much rain caused 61.2%.
