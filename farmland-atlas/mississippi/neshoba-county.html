Cropland rent in Neshoba County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $20 in 2016. That sits well under the state median of $67 and ranks tied for 8th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $3,021 an acre in 2022, up 13.4% from $2,664 in 2017, and 30th lowest of 82 in the state. 20% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
