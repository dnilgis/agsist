Neshoba County, Mississippi

Neshoba County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,021 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28099. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Neshoba County runs $23 per acre in 2026, up 17.9 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $3,021 per acre, with a 13.4 percent change from 2017 to 2022, 2.5 percent a year. July nights average 71.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 69.5 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.45 F per decade since 1976; 32 Julys since 1900 have reached that 70 F mark or above. Drought held severe or worse for 19 weeks from 2021-2025, out of 87 weeks total from 2000-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
