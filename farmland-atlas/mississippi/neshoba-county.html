Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 229 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,827 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,021 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $23 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.59 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 17.9% from 2016 to 2026. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 189.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 25,495 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.59 per base acre, PLC $5.64. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.24 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 25, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $46.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 40 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Quick answers for Neshoba County

What is cash rent in Neshoba County, Mississippi?

The county average is $23 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Neshoba County, Mississippi?

Land and buildings were worth $3,021 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
