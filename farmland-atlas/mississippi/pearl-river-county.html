Pearl River County, Mississippi

Rain is at 119% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,891 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.44 per base acre for corn.

Pearl River County vs Mississippi: $44.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 33% of the other 70 Mississippi counties with a 2026 rent (24th lowest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

31.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 68.95 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 119% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 22 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,085 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,891 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.56 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 28% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 64.8% from 2016 to 2026. 13% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 209.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,271 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.44 per base acre, PLC $1.41.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (56.15 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $12.41 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.45 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). PRF grazing county base value: $39.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

5.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 42.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.6%.

Stands out against its own record

$45

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 11 surveys since 2011; up 64.8% from $27 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Pearl River County runs $45 an acre for 2026, up 64.8% from $27 in 2016. That still sits under the state median of $67 and ranks 24th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,891 an acre in 2022, up 9.8% from $3,545 in 2017, and it ranks 19th highest of 82. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
