With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, more than any full year since 2000.

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Sunflower County sits at $123 an acre for 2026. That is up 32.3% from $93 in 2016 and tied for 13th highest of 71 in Mississippi. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,042 an acre in 2022, up 26.9% from $3,184 in 2017 and 15th highest of 82 in the state. Plenty of this ground is farmed by renters, with 69% of the farmland rented in 2022.
