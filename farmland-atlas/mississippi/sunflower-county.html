Non-irrigated cash rent in Sunflower County runs $123 per acre in 2026, up 32.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $178 per acre, a multiple of 1.45 over non-irrigated, against 1.22 in 2008, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Irrigation covers 69 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, drawing 95 percent of its water from groundwater. Census land value sits at $4,042 per acre, up 26.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
