Cash rent in Wayne County runs $23 an acre for 2026, down 45.2% from $42 in 2016. That sits tied for 8th lowest of 71 counties, against a state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $3,372 an acre in 2022, up 8.9% from $3,096 in 2017, and ranks 39th highest of 82. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
