Wayne County, Mississippi

Wayne County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,372 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.27 since 1995. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28153. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County stands at $23 per acre, down 45.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. July nights average 71.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.51 F per decade, warming. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2025 total $2.1 million, led by freeze and frost at 39 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities fell from 40 percent in 1989-1999 to 21 percent in 2010-2019 to 0 percent in 2020-2025. Land value rose 8.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
