Jones County, Mississippi

Jones County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,661 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.17 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28067. Figures as of 2026-10-16.

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Jones County runs $42 an acre in 2026, up 133.3% from $18 in 2016 and 22nd lowest of 71 in Mississippi against a state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings sits higher in the order, at $4,661 an acre in 2022, 4th highest of 82. That figure rose 23.1% from $3,786 in 2017, or 4.2% a year. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
