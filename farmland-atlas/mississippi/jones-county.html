$42

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 133.3% from $18 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Jones County runs $42 an acre in 2026, up 133.3% from $18 in 2016 and 22nd lowest of 71 in Mississippi against a state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings sits higher in the order, at $4,661 an acre in 2022, 4th highest of 82. That figure rose 23.1% from $3,786 in 2017, or 4.2% a year. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
