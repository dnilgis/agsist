Marshall County, Mississippi

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,043 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.92 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure.

Land and buildings: $3,043 an acre in the 2022 census.

Program
