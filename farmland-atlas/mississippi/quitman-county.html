U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Quitman County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 6.7% from $104 in 2016 and 17th highest of 71 counties in Mississippi, well over the $67 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $2,971 an acre in 2022, up 3.8% from 2017, still 27th lowest of 82. Corn made 181.6 bu in 2025, near the 182.3 bu average for 2021-25. 46% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
