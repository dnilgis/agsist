As of Oct 16, cash rent in Lowndes County is $48 an acre in 2026, 26th lowest of 71 in Mississippi and under the state median of $67. That is down 3.1% from $49 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,174 an acre in 2022, up 25.5% from $2,530 in 2017. Corn trends at 140.9 bu projected for 2026, 6th lowest of 18, and 41% of farmland was rented in 2022.
