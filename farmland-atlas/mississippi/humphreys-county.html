Cash rent in Humphreys County runs $153 an acre for 2026, up 44.3% from $106 in 2016. That is tied for 4th highest of 71 in Mississippi, where the state median is $67. The census value of land and buildings was $3,509 an acre in 2022, up 7.3% from $3,270 in 2017. Corn made 198.7 bu in 2025, above the 18 year county median of 185.8 bu.
