Lamar County, Mississippi

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,521 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.46 per base acre for corn.

Lamar County vs Mississippi: $34.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 26% of the other 70 Mississippi counties with a 2026 rent (19th lowest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 57.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 103% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: at least 267 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,013 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,521 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $34.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). 32% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 25.8% from 2016 to 2026. 13% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 206.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 366 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.46 per base acre, PLC $1.05.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $2.34 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $9.28 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $41.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.7%.

Quick answers for Lamar County

What is cash rent in Lamar County, Mississippi?

The county average is $35 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Lamar County, Mississippi?

Land and buildings were worth $4,521 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
