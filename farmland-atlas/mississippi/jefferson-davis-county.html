Non-irrigated cash rent in Jefferson Davis County runs $22 per acre, up 29.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stood at $3,188 per acre in 2022, up 24 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.4 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 62 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989 to 2025, and that share climbed across the decades: 27 percent in 1989-1999, 50 percent in 2000-2009, 93 percent in 2010-2019, 86 percent in 2020-2025. The July average nightly low has moved from 70.5 F in the 1991-2020 normal to 72 F in 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.54 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
