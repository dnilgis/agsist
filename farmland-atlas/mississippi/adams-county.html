Adams County, Mississippi

Adams County, MS: non-irrigated cash rent $62 an acre (2023); land and buildings $3,265 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.17 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 28001. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Adams County averaged $62 per acre in 2023. Census land and building values were $3,265 per acre in 2022, up 10.7 percent from 2017, or 2 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 totaled $11.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 84 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 2 percent in 1989-1999, 3 percent in 2000-2009, 3 percent in 2010-2019, and 21 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.45 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 72.9 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 71.7 F. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
