Cash rent in Adams County ran $62 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $3,265 an acre in 2022, up 10.7% from $2,950 in 2017, or 2.0% a year. That sits a little under the state median of $3,313 and ranks 39th lowest of 82. In 2022, 31% of the farmland here was rented.
