The census value of land and buildings in Jackson County was $5,657 an acre in 2022. That ranks 2nd highest of 82 in Mississippi, well over the state median of $3,313. It was up 12.9% from $5,012 in 2017, 2.5% a year. The last cash rent on the books is $37 an acre from 2010, and 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
