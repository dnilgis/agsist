$70

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2014 (16 surveys on record since 2009); up 73.8% from $40 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Rankin County runs $70 an acre for 2026, up 73.8% from $40 in 2016 and a shade over the state median of $67. The census value of land and buildings was $4,054 an acre in 2022, up 22.8% from $3,300 in 2017, and that sits 14th highest of 82 in Mississippi. In 2022, 29% of the county's farmland was rented.
