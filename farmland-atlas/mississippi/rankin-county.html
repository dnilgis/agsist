Non-irrigated cash rent in Rankin County stands at $70 per acre for 2026, up 73.8 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, so yield rests on rainfall and heat. Corn yield trend runs 3.8 bushels per acre a year on 2009-2025, with a median of 144.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 70 in 2009. Heat and drought account for 49 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and July nights have warmed to a 72.1 F average low for 2017-2026 against a 70.5 F normal for 1991-2020. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, was $4,054 per acre in 2022, up 22.8 percent from 2017.
