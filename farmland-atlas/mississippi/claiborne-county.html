Non-irrigated cash rent in Claiborne County runs $102 per acre in 2026, up 48.9 percent from 2016. Corn yields have trended up 3 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 156.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 90 in 2009. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved 11 percent, 23 percent, 19 percent, then 60 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The county carries a crop insurance loss ratio of 0.64 over 1989-2025, and 0.68 over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025. Irrigation covers 5 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
