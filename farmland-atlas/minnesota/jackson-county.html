Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the highest of 18 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 14, cropland in Jackson County rents for $243 an acre in 2026, up 22.1% from $199 in 2016 and above the state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $8,449 an acre in 2022, up 31.2% from $6,438 in 2017, which ranks 13th highest of 84 in Minnesota. Corn has been good here, with a 2021-25 average of 202.5 bu and 226.7 bu in 2025. 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
