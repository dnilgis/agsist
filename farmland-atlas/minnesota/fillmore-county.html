Cash rent in Fillmore County runs $263 an acre in 2026, tied for 12th highest of 81 in Minnesota and well over the state median of $189. That is up 22.9% from $214 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,919 an acre in 2022, up 6.6% from 2017 and 38th lowest of 84 in the state. The corn trend yield is projected at 209.2 bu for 2026, 6th highest of 44.
