As of Oct 16, cash rent in Isanti County runs $60 an acre for 2026, up 5.3% from $57 in 2016 and 13th lowest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $5,934 an acre in 2022, up 52.7% from $3,887 in 2017. Corn trends at 168.3 bu for 2026, 10th lowest of 44 in the state.
