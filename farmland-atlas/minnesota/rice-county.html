Rice County, Minnesota

Rain is at 90% of normal. Farmers here paid $271.39 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.06 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.93 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 165 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,539 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.68, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. 1.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 42.9%.

Cash rent in Rice County runs $276 an acre for 2026, tied for 6th highest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. That is up 34.0% from $206 in 2016. Irrigated ground rents for $350 in 2026, the highest of 17. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,330 an acre in 2022, up 21.5% from $6,857 in 2017, and 47% of farmland was rented in 2022.
