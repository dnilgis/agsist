Itasca County, Minnesota

Itasca County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,363 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Itasca County runs $39 per acre in 2026, up 77.3 percent from 2016. Land value was $3,363 per acre in 2022, up 18.3 percent from 2017, 3.4 percent a year. The county saw 123 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 55 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. July nights average 56.4 F over 2017-2026 against a 55.5 F normal, warming at 0.47 F per decade since 1976.
