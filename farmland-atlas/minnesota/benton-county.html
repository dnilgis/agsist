Stands out against its own record

$128

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 53.3% from $84 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Benton County runs $128 an acre for 2026, up 53.3% from $84 in 2016. That ranks tied for 28th lowest of 81 in Minnesota, under the state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $5,147 an acre in 2022, up 17.2% from $4,390 in 2017. Corn made 180.5 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year county median of 154.3 bu.
