Benton County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Farmers here paid $144.61 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.10 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Benton County

What is cash rent in Benton County, Minnesota?

The county average is $128 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Benton County, Minnesota?

Land and buildings were worth $5,147 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Benton County, Minnesota?

The county's 2021-25 average is 175.1 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
