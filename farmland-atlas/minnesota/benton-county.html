Benton County, Minnesota

Benton County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $128 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,147 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 154.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27009. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Benton County averages $128 per acre in 2026, up 53.3 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $5,147 per acre, up 17.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.2 percent a year. Corn yields carry a trend of 3.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 154.3 bushels per acre and a low of 90.3 in 2014. Heat and drought account for 51 percent of indemnities since 1989, and their share moved 10 percent, 57 percent, 27 percent, 88 percent across the four measured periods, with no steady direction across the four. The loss ratio was 0.94 for all closed years, 0.7 for 2016-2025.
