Dakota County, Minnesota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,295 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.83 per base acre for corn.

Dakota County vs Minnesota: $252 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 76% of the other 80 Minnesota counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 17th highest of 81).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

58.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 173 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,618 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,295 an acre in the 2022 census.

University of Minnesota FINBIN, 2025: too few farms here (under 10 with cash rented land), so no figure. Cropland Rental Rates for Minnesota Counties, Center for Farm Financial Management, University of Minnesota, Aug 2026. Rents paid from FINBIN farm records, family leases included; a separate source from USDA's. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $252 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.73 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 6, 2026). 46% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 9% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 33.3% from 2016 to 2026. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 20% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 159.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 170 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $36.31 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42.63 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $65.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 92 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.30 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.57, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 34.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.6%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Dakota County runs $252 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $189 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,295 an acre in 2022, up 20.2% from $6,902 in 2017 and 18th highest of 84. Corn made 198.2 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 187.7 bu. 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
