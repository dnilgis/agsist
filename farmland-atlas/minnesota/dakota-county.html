$252

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 33.3% from $189 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Dakota County runs $252 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $189 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,295 an acre in 2022, up 20.2% from $6,902 in 2017 and 18th highest of 84. Corn made 198.2 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 187.7 bu. 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
