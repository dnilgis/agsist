Dakota County, Minnesota

Dakota County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $252 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,295 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 189.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27037. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dakota County averages $252 per acre in 2026, up 33.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $285 per acre, a multiple of 1.13 over non-irrigated, down from 1.26 in 2013, with no clear direction over that span. Census value of land and buildings stood at $8,295 per acre in 2022, up 20.2 percent from 2017, or 3.7 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities since 1989 total $99.3 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 50 percent; that share was 20 percent in 1989-1999, 60 percent in 2000-2009, 31 percent in 2010-2019, and 65 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.57, and the county held severe drought or worse for 28 weeks in 2021-2025.
