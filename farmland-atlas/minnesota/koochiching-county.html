Cash rent in Koochiching County sits at $24 an acre for 2024, up 14.3% from $21 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,595 an acre in 2022, up 79.8% from $1,443 in 2017. That ranks 2nd lowest of 84 against a state median of $6,372. Pasture rents for $11 an acre in 2025.
