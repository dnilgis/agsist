Koochiching County, Minnesota

Koochiching County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2024); land and buildings $2,595 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Koochiching County averages $24 per acre in 2024, up 14.3 percent from 2014. Land value stood at $2,595 per acre in 2022, up 79.8 percent from 2017, a rate of 12.5 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $8.3 million in indemnities over 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 69 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities ran 4 percent in 1989-1999, 5 percent in 2000-2009, 5 percent in 2010-2019, and 18 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in this county.
