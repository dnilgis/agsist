Wadena County, Minnesota

Wadena County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,230 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 143.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27159. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wadena County averages $39 per acre in 2026, up 21.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $215 per acre, and 89 percent of irrigation water comes from groundwater. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $22.3 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 59 percent; that share was 14 percent in 1989-1999, 54 percent in 2000-2009, 42 percent in 2010-2019, and 80 percent in 2020-2025. The county saw 58 weeks in severe drought or worse in 2021-2025, against 132 of 1357 weeks for 2000-2025 overall. Corn yield trend is 2.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 143.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 111.1 in 2013.
