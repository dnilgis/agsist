Wadena County, Minnesota

Cash rent in Wadena County was $39 an acre in 2026, tied for 6th lowest of 81 in Minnesota and well under the state median of $189. That is up 21.9% from $32 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings ran $3,230 an acre in 2022, up 46.8% from $2,200 in 2017 and 12th lowest of 84. Corn made 172.5 bu in 2025, above the 18 year median of 143.6 bu.
