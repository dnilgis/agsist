Cook County had 1,940 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Cook County land, per the 2022 census operators' own estimate, carries a value of $7,495 per acre, up 26.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.8 percent a year. The July average nightly low sits at 52.1 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 51.4 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.36 F per decade with no clear direction. Drought pressure has run 143 of 1357 weeks at severe level or worse from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 35 weeks, and 40 such weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 8 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
