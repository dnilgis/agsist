USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Marshall County runs $114 an acre for 2026, up 44.3% from $79 in 2016, and that sits 25th lowest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $3,168 an acre in 2022, up 28.1% from $2,474 in 2017, which is 10th lowest of 84 in Minnesota. Corn came in at 183.1 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 157.1 bu. The county rented out 47% of its farmland in 2022.
