Cash rent in Lincoln County runs $223 an acre for 2026, up 26.7% from $176 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $189 and ranks 32nd highest of 81. The census value of land and buildings was $6,365 an acre in 2022, up 31.6% from $4,836 in 2017, right about at the state median of $6,372. Renters farm 46% of the ground here.
