Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 28.9% from $149 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wright County runs $192 an acre for 2026, up 28.9% from $149 in 2016 and 39th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings was $7,935 an acre in 2022, up 23.2% from $6,441 in 2017 and 26th highest of 84 in the state. Corn made 183.5 bu in 2025, a bit above the 176.0 bu median for 2008 to 2025. In 2022, 51% of the farmland was rented.
