Wright County, Minnesota

Rain is at 88% of normal. Farmers here paid $214.70 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.44 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

3.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.61 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 88% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 166 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,477 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.71 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.51, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. 4.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 42.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 37.7%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wright County runs $192 an acre for 2026, up 28.9% from $149 in 2016 and 39th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings was $7,935 an acre in 2022, up 23.2% from $6,441 in 2017 and 26th highest of 84 in the state. Corn made 183.5 bu in 2025, a bit above the 176.0 bu median for 2008 to 2025. In 2022, 51% of the farmland was rented.
