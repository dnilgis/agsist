Sibley County, Minnesota

Land and buildings run $8,265 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.99 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 168 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,593 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $8,265 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $278 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 12.6% from 2016 to 2026. 52.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 64.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 75.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

County median corn basis: 64 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $3.71 at Hcp, 9 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.99 per base acre, PLC $4.06.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (161.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $47.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 824 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.43 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.78 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 47.8%.
