Non-irrigated cash rent in Sibley County averages $278 per acre for 2026, up 12.6 percent from 2016. Land value carries at $8,265 per acre, the operators' own estimate, up 22.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 187.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 135.7 in 2014. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
