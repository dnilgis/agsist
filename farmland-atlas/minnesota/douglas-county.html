Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 37.3% from $118 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Douglas County runs $162 an acre for 2026, up 37.3% from $118 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $189 and ranks 33rd lowest of 81. The census value of land and buildings was $4,553 an acre in 2022, up 15.4% from $3,946 in 2017, against a state median of $6,372. Corn trends at 175.8 bu, and 38% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
