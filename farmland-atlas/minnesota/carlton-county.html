Carlton County, Minnesota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,040 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.15 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

63.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 25.62 inches, 84% of normal (30.48 inches) through Nov 17, at Cloquet. Frost-free season: 136 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,973 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,040 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $19 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 31% from 2016 to 2026. 14.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.8% of the cropland, CRP left out. 57.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 716 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.15 per base acre, PLC $2.25.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (89.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). PRF grazing county base value: $26.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 4 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 81% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.9%.
