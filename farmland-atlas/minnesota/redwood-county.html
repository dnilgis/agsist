Cash rent in Redwood County ran $251 an acre in 2025, up 13.1% from $222 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $8,408 an acre in 2022, up 34.8% from $6,239 in 2017. That ranks 14th highest of 84 in Minnesota, against a state median of $6,372. In 2022, 61% of the farmland here was rented.

Quick answers for Redwood County

What is cash rent in Redwood County, Minnesota?

The county average is $251 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Redwood County, Minnesota?

Land and buildings were worth $8,408 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Redwood County, Minnesota?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 184.8 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
