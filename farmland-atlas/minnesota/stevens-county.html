Stevens County cash rent sits at $189 an acre for 2026, right on the state median and 41st lowest of 81. That rent is up 19.6% from $158 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,446 an acre in 2022, up 27.0% from $5,077 in 2017, and 41st highest of 84. Corn made 182.8 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 182.4 bu, and 65% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
