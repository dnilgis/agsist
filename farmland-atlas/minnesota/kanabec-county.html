USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Kanabec County runs $71 an acre for 2026, up 18.5% from $60 in 2016. That sits tied for 15th lowest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $3,246 an acre in 2022, up 22.4% from $2,652 in 2017 and 14th lowest of 84. Corn came in at 182.8 bu in 2025, and 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
