Non-irrigated cash rent in Le Sueur County averages $258 per acre in 2026, up 11.7 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 Census sits at $7,494 per acre, with rent equal to 2.98 percent of that value. Excess moisture and flood is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 39 percent of $98.9 million paid out. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 8 percent in 1989-1999 to 46 percent in 2000-2009, 20 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were fewer in 2021-2025, at 22, than in the worst year, 2012, at 31.
