Cash rent in Waseca County runs $263 an acre for 2026, tied for 12th highest of 81 in Minnesota and up 13.4% from $232 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,825 an acre in 2022, up 33.3% from $6,621 in 2017 and 8th highest of 84 in the state. Corn trends at 207.9 bu, 9th highest of 44, and 52% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
