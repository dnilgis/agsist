Carver County, Minnesota

Rain is at 92% of normal. Farmers here paid $231.25 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.00 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 27.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 173 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,588 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.48, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 66% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 25% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51.3%.

The census value of land and buildings was $8,140 an acre in 2022, up 20.7% from $6,742 in 2017, which ranks 23rd highest of 84. Corn trends at 195.2 bu for 2026, and 47% of farmland was rented in 2022.
