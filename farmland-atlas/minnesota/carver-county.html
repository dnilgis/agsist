Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 29.2% from $195 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Carver County runs $252 an acre for 2026, up 29.2% from $195 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $189 and is tied for 17th highest of 81. The census value of land and buildings was $8,140 an acre in 2022, up 20.7% from $6,742 in 2017, which ranks 23rd highest of 84. Corn trends at 195.2 bu for 2026, and 47% of farmland was rented in 2022.
