Cash rent in Kittson County runs $90 an acre for 2026, up 8.5% from $83 in 2016. That ranks 21st lowest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,361 an acre in 2022, up 49.4% from $2,249 in 2017, or 8.4% a year. Even so it sits 16th lowest of 84 next to a state median of $6,372.
