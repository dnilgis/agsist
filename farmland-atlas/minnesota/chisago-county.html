As of Oct 16, cash rent in Chisago County was $81 an acre in 2026, up 17.4% from $69 in 2016, and 19th lowest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $7,018 an acre in 2022, up 53.4% from $4,575 in 2017, and 38th highest of 84 in the state. The corn trend yield sits at 168.6 bu, 11th lowest of 44 against a state median of 188.3 bu.
