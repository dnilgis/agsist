Otter Tail County, Minnesota

Rain is at 100% of normal. Farmers here paid $110.26 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.39 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.54 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 156 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,285 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.57 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. 14.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22%.
