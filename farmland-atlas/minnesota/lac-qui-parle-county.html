Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,362 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.30 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 21.64 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,568 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,362 an acre in the 2022 census.

University of Minnesota FINBIN, 2025: too few farms here (under 10 with cash rented land), so no figure. Cropland Rental Rates for Minnesota Counties, Center for Farm Financial Management, University of Minnesota, Aug 2026. Rents paid from FINBIN farm records, family leases included; a separate source from USDA's. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $215 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.71 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 6, 2026). 42% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 43% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 18.8% from 2016 to 2026. 54% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 47% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 19.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 134 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 32 acres, so read the change with care.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.10 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.54, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36%.

Corn made 194.0 bu in 2025, well over the 177.3 bu median since 2008. Renters farm 54% of the ground here.
