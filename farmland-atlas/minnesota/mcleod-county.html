Cash rent in McLeod County runs $233 an acre for 2026, down 6.0% from $248 in 2016, and that sits tied for 28th highest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,217 an acre in 2022, up 26.1% from $5,724 in 2017. Corn went 203.4 bu in 2025, and 52% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
