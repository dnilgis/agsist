McLeod County, Minnesota

McLeod County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $233 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,217 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 183.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27085. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in McLeod County averages $233 per acre for 2026, down 6 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $7,217 per acre, up 26.1 percent from 2017, 4.7 percent a year. Corn yield trend from 2008-2025 runs 1.1 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 183.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 2014 at 128.6. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $164.3 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 54 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.59, below the 0.88 rate for the full 1989-2025 period. Drought weeks ran 56 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2022 the worst year at 16 weeks.
