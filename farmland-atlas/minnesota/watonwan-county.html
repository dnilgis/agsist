Watonwan County, Minnesota

Farmers here paid $276.94 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 171 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,617 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.51 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.89, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. 3.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51%.
