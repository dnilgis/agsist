As of Oct 16, cash rent in Watonwan County ran $252 an acre in 2026, up 10.0% from $229 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $8,091 an acre in 2022, up 14.0% from $7,097 in 2017, or 2.7% a year. Corn came in at 212.3 bu in 2025, and 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
