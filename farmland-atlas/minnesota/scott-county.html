Cash rent in Scott County runs $236 an acre for 2026, up 14.0% from $207 in 2016 and above the state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $9,547 an acre in 2022, 3rd highest of 84 in Minnesota and up 25.8% from $7,590 in 2017. Corn yields trend at 203.0 bu for 2026, 13th highest of 44, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
