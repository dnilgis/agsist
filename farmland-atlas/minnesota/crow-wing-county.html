Crow Wing County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,187 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.41 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 24.46 inches, 84% of normal (29.04 inches) through Nov 17, at Brainerd. Frost-free season: 152 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,223 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,187 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 32.8% from 2016 to 2026. 28.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 71.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 260 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.41 per base acre, PLC $2.51.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (100.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $26.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.66 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.75 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.3%.
