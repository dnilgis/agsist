$277

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 17% from $237 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 15, Rock County cash rent stands at $277 an acre for 2026, tied for 3rd highest of 81 in Minnesota and well above the state median of $189. That rent is up 16.9% from $237 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,831 an acre in 2022, 7th highest of 84, up 15.7% from $7,631 in 2017. Renters work 54% of the farmland here.
