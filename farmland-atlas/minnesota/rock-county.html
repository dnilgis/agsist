Rock County, Minnesota

Rock County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $277 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,831 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 181.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27133. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rock County averages $277 per acre in 2026, up 16.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,831 per acre in 2022, up 15.7 percent from 2017. Corn yield trend runs 1.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2022, with a median of 181.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 150.2 in 2019. Excess moisture and flood drove crop insurance losses over 1989-2025, the largest cause at 33 percent, while heat and drought's share moved from 2 percent in 1989-1999 to 41 percent in 2000-2009, 16 percent in 2010-2019, and 46 percent in 2020-2025. Wind operating capacity stands at 318.8 MW, with no solar and no proposed capacity to EIA.
