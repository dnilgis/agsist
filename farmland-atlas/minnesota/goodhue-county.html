Goodhue County, Minnesota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Farmers here paid $261.48 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.91 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

58.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 26.29 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 165 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,508 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.44 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.40, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 88% of the median yield. 1.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 2.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 24.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.4%.
