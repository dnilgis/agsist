Cash rent in Goodhue County runs $277 an acre for 2026, up 42.1% from $195 in 2016 and tied for 3rd highest of 81 in Minnesota, against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,303 an acre in 2022, up 33.8% from $6,204 in 2017 and 17th highest of 84. Corn made 212.5 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 201.8 bu.
