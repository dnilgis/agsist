Goodhue County, Minnesota

Goodhue County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $277 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,303 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 194.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27049. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Goodhue County runs $277 per acre in 2026, up 42.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $284 per acre, but only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015. Corn yields have trended up 1.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 194.5 bushels per acre and a low of 172 in 2008. Crop insurance losses since 1989 total $125.1 million, mostly from excess moisture and flood at 33 percent; heat and drought's share of that has moved 18 percent, 34 percent, 16 percent, 36 percent across the four periods listed. Severe drought or worse covered the county in 42 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 9 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
