Cropland rent in Renville County runs $268 an acre for 2026, up 17.5% from $228 in 2016 and 11th highest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $8,138 an acre in 2022, up 24.1% from $6,560 in 2017. Corn did its part too, with 213.8 bu in 2025 and a trend of 206.5 bu that sits 10th highest of 44.
