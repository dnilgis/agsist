Quick answers for Mower County

What is cash rent in Mower County, Minnesota?

The county average is $276 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $286 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Mower County, Minnesota?

Land and buildings were worth $8,240 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Mower County, Minnesota?

The county's 2021-25 average is 214.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
