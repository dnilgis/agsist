Mower County, Minnesota

Mower County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $276 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,240 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 194.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27099. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mower County runs $276 per acre for 2026, up 15.5 percent from 2016. Land value came in at $8,240 per acre in the 2022 Census, the operators' own estimate, up 20.9 percent from 2017, 3.9 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 2.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 194.1 bushels per acre and a worst year in 2013 at 154.5. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 6 percent in 1989-1999 to 40 percent in 2000-2009, 19 percent in 2010-2019, and 79 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 41 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 96 weeks total from 2000-2025.
