Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 11.5% from $165 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Big Stone County runs $184 an acre for 2026, up 11.5% from $165 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $4,825 an acre in 2022, up 11.0% from $4,348 in 2017, and that sits 30th lowest of 84 in Minnesota. Corn made 172.3 bu in 2025, close to the 18 year median of 171.6 bu. In 2022, 51% of the farmland was rented.
