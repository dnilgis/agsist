Hennepin County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $17,237 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.40 per base acre for corn.

Hennepin County vs Minnesota: $141 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 36% of the other 80 Minnesota counties with a 2026 rent (30th lowest of 81).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

31.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.55 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 173 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,600 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $17,237 an acre in the 2022 census.

University of Minnesota FINBIN, 2025: too few farms here (under 10 with cash rented land), so no figure. Cropland Rental Rates for Minnesota Counties, Center for Farm Financial Management, University of Minnesota, Aug 2026. Rents paid from FINBIN farm records, family leases included; a separate source from USDA's. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $141 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.79 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 6, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 14% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 6% from 2016 to 2026. 31% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 33% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 101.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 320 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. 2.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51.9%.
