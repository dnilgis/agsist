Hennepin County, Minnesota

Hennepin County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $141 an acre (2026); land and buildings $17,237 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 162.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
