Cash rent in Clay County runs $172 an acre for 2026, up 31.3% from $131 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $189 and ranks 35th lowest of 81. The census value of land and buildings was $4,738 an acre in 2022, up 20.0% from $3,948 in 2017, still below the state median of $6,372. 62% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
