Clay County, Minnesota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Farmers here paid $168.93 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.21 per base acre for corn.

Clay County vs Minnesota: $172 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 43% of the other 80 Minnesota counties with a 2026 rent (35th lowest of 81).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

71.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 19.15 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 79% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 154 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,398 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Quick answers for Clay County

What is cash rent in Clay County, Minnesota?

The county average is $172 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Clay County, Minnesota?

Land and buildings were worth $4,738 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Clay County, Minnesota?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 175.4 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
