Freeborn County, Minnesota

Freeborn County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $272 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,698 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 182.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27047. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 averages $272 per acre, up 11.9 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $7,698 per acre, up 27.7 percent from 2017, or 5 percent a year, and 2022 rent stood at 3.17 percent of that value. Corn yield has trended up 1.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 182.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 157.8 in 2013. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 51 percent, with the loss ratio easing to 0.56 over 2016-2025 from 0.74 across the full record. Drought weeks ran 126 of 1357 since 2000, with 40 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
