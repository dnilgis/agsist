Cash rent in Houston County runs $217 an acre for 2026, up 15.4% from $188 in 2016 and tied for 34th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,209 an acre in 2022, up 21.4% from $4,290 in 2017, which still sits 33rd lowest of 84 in the state. Corn holds up well here, with a trend yield of 204.0 bu that ranks 12th highest of 44.
