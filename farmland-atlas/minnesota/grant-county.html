Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.01 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.54, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 70% of the median yield. 2.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 17% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 53.1%.
