Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 42% from $125 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pope County runs $177 an acre for 2026, up 41.6% from $125 in 2016. That sits a bit under the state median of $189 and ranks 36th lowest of 81. The census value of land and buildings was $5,298 an acre in 2022, up 27.8% from $4,144 in 2017, or 5.0% a year. Corn made 174.9 bu in 2025, close to the 2021 to 2025 average of 178.4 bu.
