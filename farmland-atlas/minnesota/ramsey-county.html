Ramsey County, Minnesota

Ramsey County, MN: land and buildings $46,973 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27123. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Ramsey County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land here is valued at $46,973 per acre by operators, up 76.3 percent from 2017-2022, or 12 percent a year. Irrigation covers 65 percent of harvested cropland. Drought weeks were 97 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2022 the worst year at 20 weeks, and 37 weeks fell in that category from 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 62.7 F in the 1991-2020 normal and 63.5 F for 2017-2026, with a trend of 0.35 F per decade since 1976 and no clear direction. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
