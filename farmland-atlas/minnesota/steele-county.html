Cash rent in Steele County runs $258 an acre for 2026, tied for 14th highest of 81 in Minnesota and up 20.6% from $214 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $9,183 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 84, up 48.1% from $6,201 in 2017. Corn made 216.7 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 203.9 bu.
