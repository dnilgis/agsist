Cash rent in Cottonwood County ran $231 an acre in 2026, up 19.1% from $194 in 2016 and 30th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,224 an acre in 2022, up 30.1% from $6,321 in 2017 and 21st highest of 84. Corn holds up its end too, with a 14 year trend of 209.9 bu that sits 5th highest of 44 in the state.
