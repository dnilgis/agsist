Sherburne County, Minnesota

Sherburne County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,181 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 163.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation shapes this county. Non-irrigated rent rose 13.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought account for 53 percent of the $31.0 million in indemnities paid from 1989-2025, closed crop years, with the share at 64 percent in 2020-2025, compared to 37 percent in 2010-2019, 66 percent in 2000-2009, and 11 percent in 1989-1999. Census land value stands at $7,181 per acre, up 39.3 percent from 2017-2022. Wind development: not present.
