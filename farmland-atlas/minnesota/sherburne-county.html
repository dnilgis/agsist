$66

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 13.8% from $58 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent here runs $66 an acre in 2026, 14th lowest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. That is up 13.8% from $58 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,181 an acre in 2022, up 39.3% from $5,155 in 2017 and above the state median of $6,372. Corn made 174.1 bu in 2025, over the county's 17 year median of 163.1 bu.
