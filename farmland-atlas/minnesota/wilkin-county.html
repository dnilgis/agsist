Wilkin County, Minnesota

Wilkin County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $180 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,418 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 154.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27167. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wilkin County is $180 per acre in 2026, up 20 percent from 2016. Land value reached $5,418 per acre in 2022, up 39.9 percent from 2017. Irrigation covers 0 percent of harvested cropland, so the crop depends on rain; excess moisture and flood account for 56 percent of indemnities paid since 1989.
