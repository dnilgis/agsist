USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, Mahnomen County cash rent runs $128 an acre in 2026, up 28.0% from $100 in 2016 and tied for 28th lowest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,169 an acre in 2022, up 41.1% from $2,955 in 2017, and sits 25th lowest of 84. Corn made 185.3 bu in 2025, and 44% of farmland was rented in 2022.
