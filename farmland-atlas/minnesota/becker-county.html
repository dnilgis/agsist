Becker County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $127 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,413 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
