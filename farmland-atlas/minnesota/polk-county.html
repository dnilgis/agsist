Polk County, Minnesota

Polk County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $166 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,321 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 142.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27119. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Polk County runs $166 per acre in 2026, up 40.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent sits at $211 per acre, though irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland. Corn yields trend up 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 142.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.2 in 2021. Solar and wind capacity are both at 0 MW, operating and proposed.
