Excess moisture and flood is the largest claim on crop insurance here, running 55 percent of all indemnities from 1989 to 2025. Corn yields have gained 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 175.9 bushels per acre. The worst year was 2011 at 111.5 bushels per acre. Heat and drought claims jumped from 2 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 39 percent in 2000-2009, then fell to 11 percent in 2010-2019 and rose to 21 percent in 2020-2025. Cash rent is $182 per acre in 2026, up 9 percent from 2016.
