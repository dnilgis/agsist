Cropland rent in Meeker County runs $217 an acre for 2026, up 21.9% from $178 in 2016 and tied for 34th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings was $5,951 an acre in 2022, up 14.7% from 2017 and 40th lowest of 84, a bit under the state median of $6,372. Corn made 178.7 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 179.9 bu.
