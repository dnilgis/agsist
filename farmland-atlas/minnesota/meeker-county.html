Meeker County, Minnesota

Meeker County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $217 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,951 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 176.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Meeker County is $217 per acre in 2026, up 21.9 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $5,951 per acre, up 14.7 percent from 2017, and non-irrigated rent is 3.58 percent of that value. The county saw 67 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 26 weeks in 2013 alone.
