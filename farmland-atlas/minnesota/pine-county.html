Pine County, Minnesota

Pine County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,269 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 139.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27115. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pine County averages $40 per acre for 2026, up 20.9 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $3,269 per acre, up 38.3 percent from 2017, 6.7 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 2.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 139.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 97.3 in 2014. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $25.7 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 44 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.57. No irrigated cropland was reported in the 2022 census. Solar operating capacity stands at 3 MW, with no wind operating and nothing proposed to EIA.
