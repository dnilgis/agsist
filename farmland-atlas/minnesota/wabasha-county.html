Wabasha County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Farmers here paid $240.16 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.67 per base acre for corn.

Wabasha County vs Minnesota: $241 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 70% of the other 80 Minnesota counties with a 2026 rent (25th highest of 81).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

12% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 161 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,525 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.33 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.67, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 93% of the median yield. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.9%.
