Wabasha County, Minnesota

Wabasha County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $241 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,636 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 187.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27157. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wabasha County runs $241 per acre, up 26.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yields have trended up 1.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 187.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 174.1 in 2013. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows $86.0 million in indemnities, led by excess moisture and flood at 30 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 11 percent in 1989-1999 to 47 percent in 2000-2009, 13 percent in 2010-2019, and 37 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and drought weeks stayed low, 9 of the last 2021-2025 span. Census land value sits at $6,636 per acre, up 20.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
