As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Wabasha County runs $241 an acre for 2026, up 26.2% from $191 in 2016 and ranked 25th highest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $6,636 an acre in 2022, up 20.2% from $5,519 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 205.4 bu that ranks 11th highest of 44 in the state.
