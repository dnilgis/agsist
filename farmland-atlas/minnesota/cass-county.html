Cass County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,049 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.12 per base acre for corn.

Cass County vs Minnesota: $30 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 5% of the other 80 Minnesota counties with a 2026 rent (5th lowest of 81).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 26.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. Risk

Quick answers for Cass County

What is cash rent in Cass County, Minnesota?

The county average is $30 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cass County, Minnesota?

Land and buildings were worth $3,049 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
