Aitkin County, Minnesota

Aitkin County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $19 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,854 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 129.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value moved the other direction, up 35.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.2 percent a year, putting the market value of land and buildings at $2,854 per acre in 2022. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of loss, at 60 percent of $18.3 million in indemnities from 1989-2025.
