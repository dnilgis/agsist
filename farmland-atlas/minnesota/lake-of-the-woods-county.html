$59

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 12 surveys since 2009; up 85.7% from $32 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Lake of the Woods County runs $59 an acre for 2026, up 85.7% from $32 in 2016. That rent sits 12th lowest of 81 in Minnesota, well under the state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $2,618 an acre in 2022, up 54.3% from $1,697 in 2017, and it ranks 3rd lowest of 84 against a state median of $6,372.
