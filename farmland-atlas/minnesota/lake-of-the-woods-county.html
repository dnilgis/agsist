Lake of the Woods County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,618 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.77 per base acre for corn.

Lake of the Woods County vs Minnesota: $58.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 14% of the other 80 Minnesota counties with a 2026 rent (12th lowest of 81).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 140 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,003 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land and buildings: $2,618 an acre in the 2022 census.

University of Minnesota FINBIN, 2025: too few farms here (under 10 with cash rented land), so no figure. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.55 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 10% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 85.7% from 2016 to 2026. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 16% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 15.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Lake of the Woods County runs $59 an acre for 2026, up 85.7% from $32 in 2016. That rent sits 12th lowest of 81 in Minnesota, well under the state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $2,618 an acre in 2022, up 54.3% from $1,697 in 2017, and it ranks 3rd lowest of 84 against a state median of $6,372.
