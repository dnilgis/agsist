Norman County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,636 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.68 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

41.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 19.8 inches, 80% of normal (24.79 inches) through Nov 16, at Twin Valley, 0.1 mi NE.

Land and buildings: $4,636 an acre in the 2022 census.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.68 per base acre, PLC $3.03.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (120.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $30.10 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.77 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.63 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).

Norman County's story right now is the insurance claims. Heat and drought went from 1 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 34 percent in 2020-2025, while excess moisture and flood still account for 47 percent of the total since 1989. The loss ratio over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 was 0.63, so claims have run below what farmers paid in premiums. Corn yields have trended up 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 141.7 bushels. Non-irrigated cash rent is $161 per acre in 2026, up 33.1 percent from 2016. Irrigation is not part of the picture here, with 0 percent of harvested cropland irrigated in 2022.
