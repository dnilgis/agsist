Cash rent in Washington County runs $145 an acre for 2026, up 9.0% from $133 in 2016 and 31st lowest of 81 in the state. The census value of land and buildings tells another story at $11,320 an acre in 2022, up 30.2% from 2017 and 2nd highest of 84. Corn came in at 203.0 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 176.0 bu.
