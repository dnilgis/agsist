Washington County, Minnesota

Washington County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $145 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,320 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 176.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27163. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County runs $145 per acre in 2026, up 9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $11,320 per acre, with non-irrigated rent equal to 1.35 percent of that value in 2022. Land value rose 30.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.4 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 1.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 176.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 129 in 2013. Heat and drought drove 58 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and drought weeks ran higher in 2021-2025 than the 2000-2025 average, with 2022 the worst single year at 20 weeks.
