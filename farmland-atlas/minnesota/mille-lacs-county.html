Mille Lacs County, Minnesota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,934 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.51 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 28.2 inches, 97% of normal (29.11 inches) through Nov 18, at Milaca. Frost-free season: 155 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,275 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.33 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.62, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 67% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 44.6%.
