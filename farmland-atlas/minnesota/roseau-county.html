Roseau County, Minnesota

Farmers here paid $70.73 an acre in cash rent in 2025, by University of Minnesota farm records. ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.62 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 140 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,041 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Loss ratio 0.56, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 69% of the median yield. 0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Heat and drought caused 19.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 66.1%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 39.6% from $51 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Roseau County runs $71 an acre for 2026, tied for 15th lowest of 81 in Minnesota and well under the state median of $189. That rent is up 39.6% from $51 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,551 an acre in 2022, the lowest of 84, up 46.4% from $1,743 in 2017. Corn has held a median of 120.2 bu across 12 years from 2008 to 2021.
