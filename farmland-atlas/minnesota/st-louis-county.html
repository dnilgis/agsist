As of Oct 16, cash rent in St. Louis County runs $21 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 81 in Minnesota against a state median of $189. That is up 46.4% from $14 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,927 an acre in 2022, up 97.1% from $1,992 in 2017, and 23rd lowest of 84 against a state median of $6,372. 21% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
