Martin County, Minnesota

Martin County, MN: non-irrigated cash rent $280 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,968 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 188.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 27091. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Martin County averages $280 per acre in 2026, up 15.7 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $8,968 per acre, with land value up 33.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6 percent a year. Excess moisture and flood account for 59 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, the largest single cause. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 1 percent in 1989-1999 to 34 percent in 2000-2009, then to 13 percent in 2010-2019 and 12 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and wind operating capacity stands at 256.8 MW against 0 MW of solar.
