USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cropland rent in Brown County was $236 an acre in 2026, up 12.9% from $209 in 2016 and tied for 26th highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,310 an acre in 2022, up 26.4% from $6,574 in 2017 and 16th highest of 84. Corn made 221.1 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year county median of 185.1 bu.
