As of Oct 16, cash rent in Swift County runs $220 an acre for 2026, up 24.3% from $177 in 2016 and 33rd highest of 81 in Minnesota, against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings was $6,379 an acre in 2022, up 26.5% from 2017 and right about at the state median of $6,372. Corn made 182.2 bu in 2025, a touch under the 2021-25 average of 187.9 bu. 45% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
