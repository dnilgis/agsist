As of Oct 16, cropland cash rent in Lyon County runs $230 an acre for 2026, up 16.8% from $197 in 2016 and 31st highest of 81 in Minnesota. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,146 an acre in 2022, up 22.1% from $5,852 in 2017, or 4.1% a year. Corn has held a 172.2 bu median from 2008 to 2022, and 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: NASS county corn yields. The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out. Claims paid per $100 of coverage: claims divided by liability (the insured value), times 100, same source. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor, weeks with half the county or more in D2 or worse.
