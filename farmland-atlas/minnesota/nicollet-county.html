Cash rent in Nicollet County ran $275 an acre in 2026, tied for 8th highest of 81 in Minnesota and up 17.5% from $234 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,617 an acre in 2022, up 23.1% from $7,002 in 2017, against a state median of $6,372. The corn trend yield for 2026 sits at 217.2 bu, 2nd highest of 44, and 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
