Cash rent in Morrison County runs $94 an acre for 2026, up 45.7% from $65 in 2016, and that sits 23rd lowest of 81 against a state median of $189. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,889 an acre in 2022, up 17.5% from $3,310 in 2017, which puts it 22nd lowest of 84. Corn made 180.3 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year median of 154.1 bu.
