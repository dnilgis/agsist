Camden County, New Jersey

Camden County, NJ: irrigated cash rent $84 an acre (2024); land and buildings $18,360 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.82 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Camden County had 7,431 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land here carries a census value of $18,360 per acre, up 11.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 2.3 percent a year. July nightly lows average 68.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.1 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.85 F per decade, warming. Drought has been uneven: 61 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2002 alone at 38 weeks, and 21 weeks came in 2021-2025. Crop insurance history shows a loss ratio of 1.82 over 1989-2025, and heat and drought caused 1 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 22 percent in 2000-2009, 11 percent in 2010-2019, and 14 percent in 2020-2025.
