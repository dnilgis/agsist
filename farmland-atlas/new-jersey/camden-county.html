The census value of land and buildings in Camden County sat at $18,360 an acre in 2022, up 11.8% from $16,419 in 2017, or 2.3% a year. That stands above the state median of $14,983. Back in 2012 the census value was $12,583. Irrigated cash rent ran $84 an acre in 2024, and 34% of farmland was rented in 2022.

Camden County had 7,431 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
