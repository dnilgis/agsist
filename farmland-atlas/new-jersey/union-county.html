Union County, New Jersey

Union County, NJ: land and buildings $105,823 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Union County had 139 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 68.5 F for 2017-2026, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 66.1 F, with a warming trend of 1.17 F per decade since 1976. The 2010s was the first decade the July average low reached 66 F. Drought has stayed rare: 10 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 49 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000, with 2002 the worst year at 24 weeks. Solar operating capacity stands at 29.3 MW, with 2.7 MW more proposed to EIA; no wind is operating or proposed.
