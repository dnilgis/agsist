Cash rent in Gloucester County runs $108 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 10 counties in New Jersey against a state median of $84. The census value of land and buildings was $14,091 an acre in 2022, up 11.2% from $12,676 in 2017, which works out to 2.1% a year. That value sits 6th lowest of 13. 42% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
