Cumberland County, New Jersey

Cumberland County, NJ: non-irrigated cash rent $100 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,056 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 141.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cumberland County stands at $100 per acre in 2026, up 73 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent is $159 per acre, a multiple of 1.60 over non-irrigated rent in 2026, compared with 1.75 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Land value per the 2022 census is $11,056 per acre, up 12.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nightly lows have warmed to 68.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.2 F, a trend of 0.96 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $12.2 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 69 percent, and the loss ratio for 2016-2025 closed years runs higher, at 0.65, than the 0.49 seen across the full 1989-2025 period.
