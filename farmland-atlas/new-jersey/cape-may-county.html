Cape May County had 7,821 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here is $19,256 per acre, up 32.2 percent from 2017-2022, or 5.7 percent a year. July nights average 69.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.7 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.88 F per decade, warming. Groundwater supplied 77 percent of irrigation water in 2015. Solar operating capacity stands at 2.5 MW, with no wind and no proposed solar or wind capacity on file.
