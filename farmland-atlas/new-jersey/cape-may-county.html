Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.79 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 224 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,157 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $19,256 an acre in the 2022 census.

Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.33 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 24, 2026). 23% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 26.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 567 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

The census value of land and buildings in Cape May County stood at $19,256 an acre in 2022. That is up 32.2% from $14,561 in 2017, or 5.7% a year, and well past the $11,534 of 2012. It runs above the New Jersey median of $14,983 across 13 counties. In 2022, 23% of the farmland was rented.

Cape May County had 7,821 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Quick answers for Cape May County

What is farmland worth in Cape May County, New Jersey?

Land and buildings were worth $19,256 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
