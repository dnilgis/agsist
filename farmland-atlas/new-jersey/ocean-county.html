Ocean County had 6,961 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Heat and drought caused 75 percent of crop insurance indemnities in Ocean County from 1989-2025, closed crop years, totaling $523,341. That share rose from 56 percent in 2010-2019 to 80 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows averaged 65.9 F in the 1991-2020 normal period and 67.7 F in 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.87 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks in severe category or worse ran 21 in 2021-2025, against 61 of 1357 weeks countywide from 2000-2025. Census land value reached $23,893 per acre in 2022, up 25.5 percent from 2017-2022, 4.7 percent a year. Solar operating capacity stands at 76.5 MW; wind operating is 0 MW.
