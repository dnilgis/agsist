As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Ocean County was $23,893 an acre in 2022. That is up 25.5% from $19,031 in 2017, 4.7% a year, and well above the New Jersey state median of $14,983. Back in 2012 the census value stood at $15,446. 22% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Ocean County had 6,961 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
