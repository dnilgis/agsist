$66

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2009; up 27% from $52 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Hunterdon County runs $66 an acre for 2026, up 27.2% from $52 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $84 and ranks 3rd lowest of 10. The census value of land and buildings came to $17,886 an acre in 2022, up 14.5% from $15,617 in 2017, and ranks 6th highest of 13. 35% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
