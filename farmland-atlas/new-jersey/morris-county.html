Morris County, New Jersey

Morris County, NJ: non-irrigated cash rent $55 an acre (2022); land and buildings $30,022 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34027. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Cash rent in Morris County runs $55 per acre non-irrigated, 2022, and $130 per acre irrigated, 2024. Irrigation covers 25 percent of harvested cropland, 2022 census. The July nightly low has moved from 62.1 F, 1991-2020 normal, to 64.5 F, 2017-2026, a warming trend of 1.05 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought caused 55 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1999-2025, closed crop years, with the share at 73 percent for 2010-2019 and 41 percent for 2020-2025. The loss ratio held at 0.34 for both 1995-2025 and 2016-2025, and land value rose 40.1 percent from 2017-2022.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

"Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed; it moves only when a figure on the page does. Source files and their retrieval dates are listed on the data page.
