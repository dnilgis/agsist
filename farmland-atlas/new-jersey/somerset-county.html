As of Oct 16, cash rent in Somerset County runs $35 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 10 counties in New Jersey and well under the state median of $84. The census value of land and buildings came to $21,660 an acre in 2022, up 9.5% from $19,776 in 2017, or 1.8% a year. That value sits 5th highest of 13, above the state median of $14,983. 32% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
