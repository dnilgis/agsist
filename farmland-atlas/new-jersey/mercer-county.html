As of Oct 16, cash rent in Mercer County runs $55 an acre for 2026, the 2nd lowest of 10 in New Jersey and under the state median of $84. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $26,811 an acre in 2022, 3rd highest of 13 in the state. That figure climbed 48.0% from $18,114 in 2017, 8.2% a year. Renters work 44% of the county's farmland.
