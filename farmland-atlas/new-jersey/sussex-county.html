Sussex County, New Jersey

Sussex County, NJ: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2025); land and buildings $13,039 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34037. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sussex County runs $42 per acre in 2025, down 8.8 percent from 2014. Land and buildings carry a census value of $13,039 per acre, with that value up 13 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.5 percent a year. Heat and drought account for 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1998, and their share has moved from 25 percent in 2000-2009, to 35 percent in 2010-2019, to 75 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 62.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 60.5 F, with a warming trend of 0.9 F per decade since 1976. Drought reached severe or worse in 19 of the weeks from 2021 to 2025.
