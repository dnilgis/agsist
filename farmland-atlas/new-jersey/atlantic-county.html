Irrigated cropland in Atlantic County rents for $236 an acre in 2026, highest of 6 in New Jersey and well over the state median of $160. The census value of land and buildings was $14,983 an acre in 2022, up 17.4% from 2017, or 3.3% a year. That lands right on the state median and ranks 7th lowest of 13. Only 18% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
