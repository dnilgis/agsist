Atlantic County, New Jersey

Atlantic County, NJ: irrigated cash rent $236 an acre (2026); land and buildings $14,983 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34001. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigation covers 77 percent of harvested cropland here, and groundwater supplies 95 percent of that water, 2015. The July average nightly low has moved from 66.7 F, 1991-2020 normal, to 68.4 F, 2017-2026, and the trend since 1976 is 0.85 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved this way across the periods: 8 percent in 1989-1999, 50 percent in 2000-2009, 2 percent in 2010-2019, 12 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 2.48, above the 1989-2025 figure of 1.39. Census value of land and buildings, 2022, is $14,983 per acre, up 17.4 percent from 2017.
