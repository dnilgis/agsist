Cash rent in Monmouth County runs $101 an acre for 2026, up 50.7% from $67 in 2014. That sits 3rd highest of 10 in New Jersey, against a state median of $84. The census value of land and buildings came in at $25,361 an acre in 2022, up 20.9% from $20,982 in 2017, and ranks 4th highest of 13. 26% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
