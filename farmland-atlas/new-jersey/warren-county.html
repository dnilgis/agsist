Warren County, New Jersey

Warren County, NJ: non-irrigated cash rent $129 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,941 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 145.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 34041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Warren County stands at $129 per acre for 2026, up 68.6 percent from 2016. Land value reached $13,941 per acre in the 2022 census, up 14.4 percent from 2017, or 2.7 percent a year, and that rent equals 0.62 percent of that value. July nights average 63.5 F for 2017-2026, above the 61.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 1.05 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance shows a loss ratio of 0.65 over 1989-2025 but 1.02 over 2016-2025, with heat and drought at 98 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 60 percent in 2000-2009, 21 percent in 2010-2019, and 87 percent in 2020-2025. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation, and severe drought covered the county for 11 weeks between 2021-2025.
