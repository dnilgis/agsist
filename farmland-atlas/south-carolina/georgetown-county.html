Georgetown County, South Carolina

Georgetown County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,960 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 99.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45043. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Georgetown County runs $39 per acre for 2026, up 18.2 percent from 2014. Land value sits at $2,960 per acre, the operators' own estimate, up 44.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7.7 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 1.2 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 99.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 34.4 in 2011. Heat and drought accounted for 33 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 47 percent in 2000-2009, 42 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025, the largest cause overall at 38 percent. The county spent 86 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, but only 4 of those weeks fell in 2021-2025.
