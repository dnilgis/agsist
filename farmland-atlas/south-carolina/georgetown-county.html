Stands out against its own record

35 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2002 (35 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Georgetown County was $39 an acre in 2026, up 18.2% from $33 in 2014 and just under the state median of $40. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,960 an acre in 2022, up 44.9% from $2,043 in 2017, and still 7th lowest of 46 in South Carolina. The corn trend projects 108.7 bu for 2026, 7th highest of 14.
