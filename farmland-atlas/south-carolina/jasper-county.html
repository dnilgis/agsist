Jasper County, South Carolina

Jasper County, SC: land and buildings $4,579 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.97 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Water in Jasper County comes entirely from groundwater, at 100 percent of irrigation supply in 2015. Severe drought or worse hit 145 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 35 weeks; only 9 weeks reached that mark in 2021-2025. July nights average 73.5 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 63 percent in 1989-1999, 79 percent in 2000-2009, 28 percent in 2010-2019, and 27 percent in 2020-2025. Land value rose 25.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 4.6 percent a year, reaching $4,579 per acre in the 2022 Census operator estimate.
