Jasper County, South Carolina

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,579 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

74.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 30.7 inches, 67% of normal (46.05 inches) through Nov 18, at Ridgeland, 5.8 mi ESE. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,081 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,579 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 5.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,610 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.96 per base acre, PLC $1.63.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (65.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (26.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.09 per base acre, PLC $5.94. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $45.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.33 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.91, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 8.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 38.5%.

The census value of land and buildings in Jasper County was $4,579 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from $3,661 in 2017. That works out to 4.6% a year, and the figure was $2,717 back in 2012. The 2022 value sits above the state median of $3,802 and ranks 15th highest of 46. Only 5% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
