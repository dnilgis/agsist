Orangeburg County, South Carolina

Orangeburg County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,291 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 139.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Orangeburg County runs $50 per acre in 2026, up 25.3 percent since 2016. Irrigated land rents at $115 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 1.74 in 2009 to 2.32 in 2026, a rising premium for water. Only 24 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census, with 72 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 57 percent in 1989-1999, to 62 percent in 2000-2009, to 36 percent in 2010-2019, to 20 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $3,291 per acre, up 9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
