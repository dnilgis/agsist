Cropland rent in Orangeburg County runs $50 an acre for 2026, up 25.3% from $40 in 2016 and tied for 8th highest of 38 in South Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $3,291 an acre in 2022, up 9.0% from $3,018 in 2017, which sits 13th lowest of 46 in the state. Corn made 159.1 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 150.8 bu.
