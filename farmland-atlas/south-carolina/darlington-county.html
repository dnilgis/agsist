Darlington County, South Carolina

Darlington County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $47 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,794 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 111.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45031. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Darlington County averages $47 per acre in 2026, up 16 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $101 per acre in 2025, but irrigation covers only 4 percent of harvested cropland, with 68 percent of that water from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought account for 43 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.98, close to breakeven on premium paid. The July nightly low has warmed from a 70.6 F normal to 72.2 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.61 F per decade trend since 1976. Census land value sits at $2,794 per acre, up 7.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
