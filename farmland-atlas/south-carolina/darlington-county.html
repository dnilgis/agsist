Darlington County, South Carolina

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,794 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.32 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 30.25 inches, 70% of normal (42.99 inches) through Nov 18, at Hartsville. Frost-free season: 239 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,850 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,794 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $47 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 16% from 2016 to 2026. 46% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 17.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 424.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,281 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.32 per base acre, PLC $2.20.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (87.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (24.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.41 per base acre, PLC $8.09. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $48.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.59 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.98, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. 3.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 5.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.6%.
