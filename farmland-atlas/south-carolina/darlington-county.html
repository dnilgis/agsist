As of Oct 14, cash rent in Darlington County was $47 an acre in 2026, up 16.0% from $41 in 2016 and tied for 12th highest of 38 in South Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $2,794 an acre in 2022, up 7.6% from 2017 and 4th lowest of 46 in the state. Corn made 122.7 bu in 2025, above the county's 18 year median of 111.2 bu.
