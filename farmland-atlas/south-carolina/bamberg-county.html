Stands out against its own record

28 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2012 (28 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent on Bamberg County cropland was $39 an acre in 2026, up 22.2% from $32 in 2016 and 18th lowest of 38 in the state. Irrigated ground is another matter at $147 an acre in 2026, highest of 7. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,550 an acre in 2022, up 21.1% from $2,932 in 2017. Corn made 140.6 bu in 2025, a good bit over the 128.4 bu median going back to 2008.
