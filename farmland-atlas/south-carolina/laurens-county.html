Cropland rent in Laurens County runs $20 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 38 in South Carolina and well under the state median of $40. That is up 29.0% from $16 in 2016, and pasture rents for more at $26 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $4,583 an acre in 2022, up 19.5% from 2017 and 14th highest of 46 in the state.
