U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Greenville County runs $28 an acre for 2026, up 19.1% from $24 in 2016 and under the state median of $40, which puts it 9th lowest of 38. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story. It was $12,103 an acre in 2022, up 31.7% from $9,188 in 2017, and that ranks highest of 46 against a state median of $3,802. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
