Greenville County, South Carolina

Greenville County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,103 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45045. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greenville County averages $28 per acre in 2026, up 19.1 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stands at $12,103 per acre, up 31.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.7 percent a year. The July average nightly low has moved from 68.5 F over 1991-2020 to 69.4 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.35 F per decade since 1976. Drought has eased in the recent stretch: 9 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 251 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2008 the worst year at 53 weeks. Freeze and frost, not heat, drives most crop insurance losses here, at 73 percent of indemnities paid from 1989-2025.
