Barnwell County, South Carolina

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,749 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.54 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

71.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 40.72 inches, 95% of normal (42.74 inches) through Nov 18, at Barnwell, 5 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 247 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,878 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,749 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 12.8% from 2016 to 2026. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 25% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 40 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 124.9 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,202 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.54 per base acre, PLC $1.90.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (75.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.55 per base acre, PLC $6.36. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $45.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.64 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.58, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 28% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 16.5%.
