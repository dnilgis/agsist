Barnwell County, South Carolina

Barnwell County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $44 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,749 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 120.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Barnwell County runs $44 per acre in 2026, up 12.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $72 per acre, a multiple of 1.64 over non-irrigated in 2026, compared with 1.27 in 2009, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. July nights average 71.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 70.4 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.41 F per decade, warming. Drought was heaviest in 2002, with 45 weeks in severe drought or worse, and only 3 weeks over 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 43 percent, 75 percent, 43 percent, 12 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Census land value stands at $3,749 per acre, up 34.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
