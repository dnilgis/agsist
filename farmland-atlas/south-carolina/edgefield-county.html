Cropland rent in Edgefield County runs $26 an acre for 2025, down 10.3% from $29 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,696 an acre in 2022, up 33.1% from $3,527 in 2017. That puts it 13th highest of 46 in the state, above the state median of $3,802. Only 18% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
