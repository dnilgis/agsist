Non-irrigated cash rent in Edgefield County is $26 per acre in 2025, down 10.3 percent from 2014. Drought has eased in recent years: 2 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 242 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 45 weeks. The July average nightly low is 71.6 F for 2017-2026, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 70.2 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.53 F per decade, warming.
