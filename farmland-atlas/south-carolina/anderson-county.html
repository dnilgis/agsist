Cropland rent in Anderson County runs $24 an acre for 2026, up 23.1% from $20 in 2016 and 6th lowest of 38 in the state against a $40 state median. The census value of land and buildings tells another story at $7,011 an acre in 2022, up from $5,675 in 2017 and 7th highest of 46. Corn is thin here, with a 14 year trend of 78.8 bu that sits lowest of 14 counties.
