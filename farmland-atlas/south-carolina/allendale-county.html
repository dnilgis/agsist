Allendale County, South Carolina

Allendale County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,964 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 91.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Allendale County stands at $38 per acre in 2026, up 17.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents higher, at $78 per acre in 2025, and 68 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater as of 2015. July nightly lows average 71.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 70.7 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.43 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance shows heat and drought as the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 33 percent, though that share ran higher in 2000-2009 at 53 percent than in 2020-2025 at 25 percent. Census land value reached $2,964 per acre in 2022, up 34.4 percent from 2017.
