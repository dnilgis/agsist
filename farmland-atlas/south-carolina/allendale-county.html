Stands out against its own record

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Allendale County runs $38 an acre for 2026, up 17.2% from $32 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $40. The census value of land and buildings was $2,964 an acre in 2022, up 34.4% from $2,206 in 2017, and that ranks 8th lowest of 46. Corn has held to a median of 91.0 bu over 15 years, and only 12% of farmland was rented in 2022.
