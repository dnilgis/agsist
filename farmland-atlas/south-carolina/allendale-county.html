Allendale County, South Carolina

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,964 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.19 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

95.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 247 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,898 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,964 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $38 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 17.2% from 2016 to 2026. 11.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 59.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 50 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,337 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.19 per base acre, PLC $2.23.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (88.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $45.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 288 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.63 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.02 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 23% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 32.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 26.6%.
