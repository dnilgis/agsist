As of Oct 14, cash rent in Dillon County runs $61 an acre for 2026, up 19.8% from $51 in 2016 and 3rd highest of 38 in the state against a $40 median. The census value of land and buildings went the other way on rank, at $2,757 an acre in 2022, 3rd lowest of 46 and under the $3,802 state median, though still up 20.3% from 2017. Corn holds up its end, with a 14-yr trend of 129.8 bu, 2nd highest of 14.
