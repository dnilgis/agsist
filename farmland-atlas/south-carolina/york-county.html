Non-irrigated cash rent in York County runs $34 per acre in 2026, up 52.3 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stands at $8,629 per acre, with a 23.6 percent change from 2017 to 2022, or 4.3 percent a year. The July average nightly low is 69.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 68.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.39 F per decade. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 48 percent in 1989-1999, to 47 percent in 2000-2009, to 23 percent in 2010-2019, to 13 percent in 2020-2025.
