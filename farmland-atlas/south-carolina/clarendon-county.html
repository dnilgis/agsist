Clarendon County, South Carolina

Clarendon County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $54 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,051 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clarendon County averages $54 per acre in 2026, up 27.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $150 per acre in 2023, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.73 in 2013 to 2.97 in 2023, a rising multiple. Irrigation water is 100 percent groundwater as of 2015. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 4.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 136.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 57 in 2008. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 60 percent in 1989-1999, 52 percent in 2000-2009, 44 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025.
