Cash rent in Oconee County ran $33 an acre in 2026, up 41.3% from $23 in 2016 and 12th lowest of 38 in the state. The census value of land and buildings was $7,485 an acre in 2022, up 17.2% from 2017 and 6th highest of 46. So the ground is valued high and rents low. Corn trends at 92.0 bu, 4th lowest of 14, with 102.4 bu last year in 2025.
