Cash rent in Union County runs $15 an acre for 2026, tied for lowest of 38 in South Carolina and well under the $40 state median. That is down 11.8% from $17 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 50.5% from $2,534 in 2017 to $3,814 in 2022, which is 23rd highest of 46 and close to the $3,802 state median. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
