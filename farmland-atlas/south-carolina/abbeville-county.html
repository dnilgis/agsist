Cash rent in Abbeville County runs $21 an acre for 2026, 4th lowest of 38 in South Carolina and well under the state median of $40. That rent was $14 back in 2014, so it is up 51.9% since then. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,079 an acre in 2022, up 31.8% from $3,096 in 2017 and 18th highest of 46.
