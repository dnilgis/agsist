Cash rent in Hampton County runs $49 an acre in 2026, up 102.1% from $24 in 2016 and 10th highest of 38 in South Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $3,227 an acre in 2022, up 32.3% from $2,439 in 2017, and it sits 11th lowest of 46 in the state. Corn made 140.2 bu in 2025, a bit above the 2021-25 average of 138.5 bu.
