Chester County, South Carolina

Chester County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,702 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Chester County runs $22 per acre in 2026, up 22.2 percent from 2016. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, so this is a rain-fed county. July nights average 69.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.1 F, with a warming trend of 0.39 F per decade since 1976.
