U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland in Chester County rents for $22 an acre in 2026, up 22.2% from $18 in 2016 and 5th lowest of 38 in South Carolina, where the median is $40. Pasture goes for $16 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,702 an acre in 2022, up 11.4% from $3,323 in 2017, or 2.2% a year. Only 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
