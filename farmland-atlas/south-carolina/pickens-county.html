U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pickens County runs $29 an acre for 2026, down 10.8% from $33 in 2016, and ranks 11th lowest of 38 against a state median of $40. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, reaching $9,117 an acre in 2022, up 29.3% from $7,050 in 2017. That value is 4th highest of 46 in the state, where the median sits at $3,802. Only 16% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
