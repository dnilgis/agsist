Pickens County, South Carolina

Rain is at 84% of normal. Land and buildings run $9,117 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.51 per base acre for corn.

Pickens County vs South Carolina: $29 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 27% of the other 37 South Carolina counties with a 2026 rent (11th lowest of 38).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.12 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 234 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,489 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,117 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $29 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 7% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 10.8% from 2016 to 2026. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 150.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,104 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.51 per base acre, PLC $1.30.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (51.78 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $65.01 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $11.44 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (12.84 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $64.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 6.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cash rent in Pickens County runs $29 an acre for 2026, down 10.8% from $33 in 2016, and ranks 11th lowest of 38 against a state median of $40. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, reaching $9,117 an acre in 2022, up 29.3% from $7,050 in 2017. That value is 4th highest of 46 in the state, where the median sits at $3,802. Only 16% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
