McCormick County, South Carolina

McCormick County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2021); land and buildings $3,216 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45065. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in 2021 averaged $15 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $3,216 per acre, up 60.5 percent from 2017, or 9.9 percent a year. No cropland here was irrigated as of the 2022 census. July nightly lows have averaged 71.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 70.2 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.4 F per decade, warming. Drought weeks ran high in 2000-2025, with 282 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse and 2008 the worst year at 50 weeks, but only 3 weeks reached that mark in 2021-2025.
