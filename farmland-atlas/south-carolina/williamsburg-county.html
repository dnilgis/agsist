Non-irrigated cash rent in Williamsburg County runs $40 per acre, up 23.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated land rents higher, at $56 per acre in 2025, though only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Heat and drought drove 36 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, but that share has moved down across each decade listed: 48 percent in 1989-1999, 49 percent in 2000-2009, 38 percent in 2010-2019, and 25 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks tell a similar story: 84 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, with just 5 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. July nights have warmed, averaging 71.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 70.8 F normal, on a long-term trend of 0.5 F per decade.
