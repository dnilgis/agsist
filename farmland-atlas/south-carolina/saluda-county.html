As of Oct 16, Saluda County cash rent stands at $33 an acre for 2026, up 57.1% from $21 in 2016 and tied for 13th lowest of 38 counties in South Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $4,440 an acre in 2022, up 43.5% from $3,093 in 2017 and 17th highest of 46. Corn runs light here, with a 14 year trend of 79.4 bu that ranks 2nd lowest of 14.
