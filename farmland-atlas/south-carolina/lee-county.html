Stands out against its own record

16 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2007 (19 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 14, cash rent in Lee County runs $52 an acre for 2026, up 25.3% from $42 in 2016 and 7th highest of 38 in the state. The census value of land and buildings was $2,655 an acre in 2022, up 9.9% from $2,416 in 2017, and it ranks lowest of 46 against a state median of $3,802. Corn made 146.4 bu in 2025, a little under the 2021 to 2025 average of 151.5 bu but well above the 18 year median of 131.8 bu.
