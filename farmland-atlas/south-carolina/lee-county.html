Lee County, South Carolina

Lee County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,655 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 131.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lee County stands at $52 per acre, up 25.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. The land itself carries a census value of $2,655 per acre, with that value up 9.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Rent takes 1.92 percent of that value. Crop insurance data show heat and drought falling as a share of indemnities across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025: 62 percent, 59 percent, 35 percent, 24 percent. Over 2016-2025, the loss ratio was 0.8, and indemnities per $100 of insured value came to $11.15. Solar stands at 44 MW operating, with 60 MW solar proposed to EIA; wind is 0 MW on both counts.
