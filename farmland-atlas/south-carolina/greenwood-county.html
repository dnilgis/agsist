Greenwood County, South Carolina

Rain is at 112% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,514 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.12 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

1.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 46.12 inches, 112% of normal (41.24 inches) through Nov 17, at Greenwood, 8 mi NE. Frost-free season: 237 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,764 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,514 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $15 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 16.7% from 2016 to 2026. 17.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 118.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 729 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.12 per base acre, PLC $1.17.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $69.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cropland rent in Greenwood County was $15 an acre in 2026, tied for lowest of 38 in South Carolina against a state median of $40. That rent is down 16.7% from $18 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,514 an acre in 2022, up 2.9% from $3,416 in 2017, or 0.6% a year, and it ranks 18th lowest of 46. 18% of farmland was rented in 2022.
