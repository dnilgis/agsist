Greenwood County, South Carolina

Greenwood County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,514 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45047. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greenwood County averages $15 per acre in 2026, down 16.7 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 Census stood at $3,514 per acre, up 2.9 percent from 2017, or 0.6 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 70.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.9 F, part of a warming trend of 0.29 F per decade since 1976, with 46 Julys at or above 70 F since 1900. Drought pressure has eased in recent years: only 2 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 264 of 1357 weeks countywide from 2000-2025. Solar capacity operating stands at 7.5 MW; no wind is operating or proposed.
