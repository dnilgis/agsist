U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Newberry County was $35 an acre in 2025, up 16.9% from $30 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,891 an acre in 2022, up 17.4% from 2017, which ranks 21st highest of 46 and sits just above the state median of $3,802. Corn runs thin here, with a trend yield of 83.9 bu that is 3rd lowest of 14 in the state.
