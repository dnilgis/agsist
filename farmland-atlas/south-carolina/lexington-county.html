Lexington County, South Carolina

Lexington County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,537 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 132.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45063. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lexington County runs $33 per acre in 2026, up 43.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $120 per acre, a multiple of 3.64 over non-irrigated in 2026, up from 1.85 in 2009, and the multiple is rising. Irrigation covers 38 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 86 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Corn yields carried a fitted trend of 4.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2022, with a median of 132.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 57.8 in 2011. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved 21 percent, 32 percent, 41 percent, 52 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025.
