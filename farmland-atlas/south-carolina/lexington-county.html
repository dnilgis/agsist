Stands out against its own record

7 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2012 (32 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent on Lexington County cropland runs $33 an acre for 2026, up 43.5% from $23 in 2016 and tied for 13th lowest of 38 against a state median of $40. Irrigated ground tells another story at $120 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 7. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,537 an acre in 2022, up 18.1% from $5,533 in 2017 and 8th highest of 46 in the state.
