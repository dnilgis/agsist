Cash rent in Horry County runs $75 an acre for 2026, highest of 38 in South Carolina against a state median of $40. That is still down 6.9% from $80 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,912 an acre in 2022, up 8.9% from $4,510 in 2017, and sits 12th highest of 46. Corn made 132.4 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year county median of 115.4 bu.
