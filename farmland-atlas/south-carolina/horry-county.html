Horry County, South Carolina

Horry County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $75 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,912 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 115.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45051. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Horry County is $74 per acre in 2026, down 6.9 percent from 2016. Land value, at $4,912 per acre from the 2022 census, rose 8.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 37 percent, and its share by decade moved from 33 percent in 1989-1999 to 40 percent in 2000-2009 to 46 percent in 2010-2019, then down to 25 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks eased too: 87 of 1357 weeks were in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, against 4 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 6 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and all of that water comes from groundwater as of 2015.
