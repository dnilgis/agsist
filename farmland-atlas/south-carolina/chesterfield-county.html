Chesterfield County, South Carolina

Chesterfield County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $48 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,318 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 105.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45025. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Chesterfield County averages $48 per acre in 2026, up 13.1 percent since 2014. Land carries a census value of $3,318 per acre, with a 12.4 percent change from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 42 percent, though its share has moved unevenly by decade: 34 percent in 1989-1999, 57 percent in 2000-2009, 39 percent in 2010-2019, and 45 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.02, above the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.95. July nights have warmed from a 69.7 F normal to 71.1 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.61 F per decade since 1976.
