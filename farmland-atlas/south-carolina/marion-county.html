Marion County, South Carolina

Marion County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $64 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,868 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 121.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45067. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Marion County runs $64 per acre in 2026, up 52.4 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $2,868 per acre, up 23 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.2 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 121.6 bushels per acre and a low of 51 in 2011. July nights average 72.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.1 F. Irrigation draws 99 percent from groundwater as of 2015.
