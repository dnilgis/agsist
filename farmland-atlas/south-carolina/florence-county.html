As of Oct 16, cash rent in Florence County runs $47 an acre for 2026, up 11.9% from $42 in 2016 and tied for 12th highest of 38 counties in South Carolina. The census value of land and buildings was $3,382 an acre in 2022, up 6.8% from $3,166 in 2017, which is 1.3% a year. That still sits 16th lowest of 46 against a state median of $3,802. 54% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
