Florence County, South Carolina

Florence County, SC: non-irrigated cash rent $47 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,382 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 103.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 45041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Florence County runs $47 per acre, up 11.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stands at $3,382 per acre, up 6.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 1.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 103.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 42.9 in 2011. July nights average 72.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 70.9 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.53 F per decade since 1976. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. Heat and drought caused 38 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, the largest single cause.
