Kalawao County, Hawaii

Kalawao County, HI: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 15005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Kalawao County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 69.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.5 F. Only 3 Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F, and the July average low first reached 66 F in the 1990s. Drought has touched this county in 99 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, or 7.3 percent, with 2010 the worst year at 27 weeks; from 2021-2025 the count is 13 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
