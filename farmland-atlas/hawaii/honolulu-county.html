The 2022 census value of land and buildings in Honolulu County runs $29,887 an acre, highest of 4 in Hawaii. That is up 20.5% from $24,794 in 2017, 3.8% a year, and well above the state median of $12,660. Renters work a good share of it, with 56% of farmland rented in 2022.
