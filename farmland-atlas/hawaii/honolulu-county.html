Land value here runs $29,887 per acre, up 20.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.8 percent a year. Irrigation covers 86 percent of harvested cropland, with 18 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. The July average nightly low has moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 69.8 F to 71.2 F over 2017-2026, and the July average low first reached 66 F in the 1990s. Drought has stayed limited: 11 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, all 11 of them falling in 2021-2025, with 2023 the worst single year at 4 weeks.
