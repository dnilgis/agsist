Ferry County, Washington

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $929 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 11.85 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 69% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 138 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,792 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $929 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.37 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 100,040 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.62 per base acre, PLC $6.86. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $30.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

The census value of land and buildings in Ferry County was $929 an acre in 2022, up 79.3% from $518 in 2017, which works out to 12.4% a year. Even so, it sits well under the Washington median of $5,067. Cash rent ran $21 an acre in 2020, and pasture rent was $3 an acre in 2025.

Ferry County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
