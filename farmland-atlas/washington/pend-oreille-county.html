Pend Oreille County, Washington

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,486 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 15.44 inches, 67% of normal (22.97 inches) through Nov 17, at Boundary Dam. Frost-free season: 144 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,661 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,486 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $38 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 13.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 89.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,851 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $30.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

8.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
