Wahkiakum County, Washington

Wahkiakum County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2021); land and buildings $6,630 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53069. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wahkiakum County averaged $20 per acre in 2021. Census data put land and buildings at $6,630 per acre in 2022, up 38 percent from 2017, or 6.7 percent a year. July nights average 51.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 51.4 F, with a warming trend of 0.49 F per decade since 1976; no July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county lightly: 34 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, 7 of those weeks between 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
