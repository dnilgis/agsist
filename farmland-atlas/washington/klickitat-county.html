Cropland rent in Klickitat County runs $42 an acre for 2026, 4th lowest of 25 in Washington against a state median of $71. The census value of land and buildings was $2,482 an acre in 2022, up 39.6% from $1,778 in 2017, or 6.9% a year. That still sits 8th lowest of 34 next to a state median of $5,067. 41% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
