Klickitat County, Washington

Klickitat County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,482 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53039. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Klickitat County runs $42 per acre; irrigated land leased for $383 per acre in 2024. Census land and buildings value came to $2,482 per acre in 2022, up 39.6 percent from 2017, 6.9 percent a year. July nightly lows have averaged 54.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 52.9 F, with a warming trend of 0.81 F per decade since 1976. Freeze and frost, not heat, has driven the most crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 at 36 percent of $106.9 million paid; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.83, with $8.03 paid per $100 of insured value. The county spent 48 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 163 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025.
