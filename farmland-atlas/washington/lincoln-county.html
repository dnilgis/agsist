Cropland rent in Lincoln County runs $57 an acre for 2026, up 34.1% from $43 in 2016 and under the state median of $71. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,603 an acre in 2022, up 31.2% from $1,222 in 2017, and that ranks lowest of 34 in Washington. Rented ground made up 62% of the farmland in 2022.
