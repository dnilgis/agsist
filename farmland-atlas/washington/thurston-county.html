Non-irrigated cash rent in Thurston County runs $90 per acre, with irrigated land at $198 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $17,717 per acre, up 49.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 8.3 percent a year. The irrigated-to-non-irrigated rent multiple has fallen from 6.12 in 2009 to 2.20 in 2026. Drought has touched the county in 44 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2015 the worst year at 17 weeks and just 7 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights average 52.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 52.3 F normal, warming at 0.29 F per decade since 1976.
