Pacific County, Washington

Pacific County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2025); land and buildings $5,238 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53049. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pacific County averages $39 per acre in 2025, down 36.6 percent from 2014. Census land and buildings value is $5,238 per acre, up 51.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.6 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities for 1997-2022 total $284,052, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 90 percent; heat and drought account for 0 percent of indemnities from 2010-2019. Drought has been rare here: 50 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 10 such weeks from 2021-2025. July nights average 51.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 51.5 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 is 0.44 F per decade, warming.
