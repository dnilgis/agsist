Pacific County, Washington

Pacific County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2025); land and buildings $5,238 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Cash rent in Pacific County runs $39 an acre for 2025, down 36.6% from $62 in 2014. Pasture rents for $14 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $5,238 an acre in 2022, up 51.2% from 2017, and that ranks 15th highest of 34 in Washington. Only 10% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
