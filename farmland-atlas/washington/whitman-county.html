As of Oct 16, cash rent in Whitman County was $79 an acre in 2026, up 0.6% from 2016 and right about where it sat then. That ranks 11th highest of 25 in Washington, against a state median of $71. The census value of land and buildings ran $2,528 an acre in 2022, up 44.8% from $1,746 in 2017, and still 9th lowest of 34. In 2022, 63% of the farmland here was rented.
