Yakima County, Washington

Yakima County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $150 an acre (2021); land and buildings $2,782 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 209.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53077. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Yakima County stands at $231 per acre for 2026. Non-irrigated rent averaged $150 per acre in 2021, up 170.3 percent from 2011. Census land and buildings value was $2,782 per acre in 2022, up 1 percent from 2017, or 0.2 percent a year. July nights have warmed, averaging 53.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 51.7 F, part of a warming trend of 0.86 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought accounted for 33 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2020-2025, up from 12 percent in 2010-2019, 3 percent in 2000-2009, and 13 percent in 1989-1999. Freeze and frost remains the largest cause of indemnities overall, at 46 percent of $577.0 million paid across closed crop years 1989-2025.
