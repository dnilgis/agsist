Pierce County, Washington

Pierce County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2023); land and buildings $28,343 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pierce County runs $70 per acre in 2023, down 58.1 percent from 2013. Irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $466 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.79 in 2009 to 4.99 in 2023, a rising multiple. Irrigation covers 27 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 85 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Drought weeks at severe level or worse ran 37 from 2021-2025, against 62 of 1357 weeks for 2000-2025, with 2025 the worst year at 23 weeks. July nights have warmed 0.61 F per decade since 1976, and land carries a census value of $28,343 per acre, up 31.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
