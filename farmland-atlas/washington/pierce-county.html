Cash rent on cropland in Pierce County runs $70 an acre for 2023, down 58.1% from $167 in 2013. Irrigated ground rents for $466 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 14 in the state against a state median of $350. The census value of land and buildings came in at $28,343 an acre in 2022, up 31.9% from 2017 and 2nd highest of 34 in Washington.
