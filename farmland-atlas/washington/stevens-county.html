Non-irrigated cash rent in Stevens County runs $50 per acre, up 35.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated rent stood at $63 per acre in 2024, a multiple of 1.56 over non-irrigated; that multiple was 2.41 in 2017, with no clear direction since. Land value came in at $2,014 per acre in the 2022 census, up 28.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.1 percent a year. Crop insurance shows a loss ratio of 1.52 over 1989-2025, with heat and drought the largest cause of indemnities at 57 percent.
