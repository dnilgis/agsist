$50

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 35.6% from $37 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Stevens County runs $50 an acre in 2026, up 35.6% from $37 in 2016 and tied for 5th lowest of 25 in the state, where the median is $71. The census value of land and buildings was $2,014 an acre in 2022, up 28.4% from $1,569 in 2017, and that sits 5th lowest of 34 against a state median of $5,067. 33% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
