Skagit County, Washington

Skagit County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $256 an acre (2026); land and buildings $14,280 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Skagit County runs $256 per acre in 2026, up 11.3 percent from 2014. Irrigated land rents at $425 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved with no clear direction, from 1.17 in 2009 to 1.66 in 2026. Land value under the operators' own estimate stands at $14,280 per acre, up 41 percent from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025, at 42 percent, while heat and drought's share moved from 7 percent in 1989-1999 to 23 percent in 2000-2009, 11 percent in 2010-2019, and 53 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed 0.51 F per decade since 1976.
