Skagit County, Washington

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $14,280 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.91 per base acre for corn.

Skagit County vs Washington: $256 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 100% of the other 24 Washington counties with a 2026 rent (highest of 25).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

89.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 55.10 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 192 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,374 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $14,280 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $256 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 8, 2026 to Sep 10, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 19% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 11.3% from 2014 to 2026. 46% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 14% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 246.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 28,461 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.91 per base acre, PLC $3.76.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (149.87 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $122.49 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $33.12 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $32.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 38 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.39 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.93, 2016 to 2025. 43.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 42%.

Quick answers for Skagit County

What is cash rent in Skagit County, Washington?

The county average is $256 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $425 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Skagit County, Washington?

Land and buildings were worth $14,280 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
