Whatcom County cropland rent sits at $181 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 25 in Washington, against a state median of $71. That rent is up 10.4% from $164 in 2014. Irrigated ground rents for $514 an acre in 2026, highest of 14. The census value of land and buildings came to $20,475 an acre in 2022, up 21.9% from $16,794 in 2017 and 4th highest of 34.
