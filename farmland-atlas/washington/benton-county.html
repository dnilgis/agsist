Cash rent in Benton County ran $45 an acre in 2024, and irrigated ground rented for $700 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $4,153 an acre in 2022, up 6.5% from 2017, or 1.3% a year. That sits under the state median of $5,067, and the county's census value ranks 15th lowest of 34 in Washington. In 2022, 44% of the farmland was rented.
