Snohomish County, Washington

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $22,374 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

63.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 55.80 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 79% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 193 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,398 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $22,374 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $140 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.65 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 21% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 24% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 22.8% from 2014 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 12% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 390.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,707 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $32.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 48 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.59 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.29, 2016 to 2025. 42.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 3.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1997 to 2025; too much rain caused 78.2%.

Cash rent in Snohomish County runs $140 an acre in 2026, up 22.8% from $114 in 2014 and 5th highest of 25 in Washington, where the state median is $71. The census value of land and buildings was $22,374 an acre in 2022, up 15.7% from $19,332 in 2017, or 3.0% a year. That is 3rd highest of 34 against a state median of $5,067. Renters work 37% of the farmland here.
