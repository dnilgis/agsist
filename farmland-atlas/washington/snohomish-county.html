Snohomish County, Washington

Snohomish County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $140 an acre (2026); land and buildings $22,374 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Snohomish County stands at $140 per acre for 2026, up 22.8 percent from 2014. Irrigated rent runs $402 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent moved from 2.00 in 2010 to 2.87 in 2026, with no clear direction over that span. Land value and buildings were estimated by operators at $22,374 per acre in 2022, up 15.7 percent from 2017. Crop insurance losses since 1997 total $1.2 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 78 percent; heat and drought accounted for 1 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009, 7 percent in 2010-2019, and 0 percent in 2020-2025.
