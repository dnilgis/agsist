The census value of land and buildings in Mason County was $7,916 an acre in 2022, down 7.5% from $8,562 in 2017. That works out to 1.6% a year. The 2022 figure still sits above the Washington median of $5,067 and ranks 13th highest of 34. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
