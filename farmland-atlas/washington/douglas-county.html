Douglas County, Washington

Douglas County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $53 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,784 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53017. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent runs $285 per acre against $53 per acre non-irrigated, and the non-irrigated rate is up 27.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Groundwater supplied 18 percent of irrigation water in 2015, so most irrigated ground here depends on surface supply. Heat and drought caused 42 percent of $248.6 million in indemnities since 1989, with the share at 49 percent in 1989-1999, 71 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 37 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 stands at 1.79, above the 1989-2025 figure of 1.44. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
