As of Oct 16, cropland in Douglas County rents for $53 an acre in 2026, up 27.7% from $42 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $71 and comes in 8th lowest of 25. The census value of land and buildings was $2,784 an acre in 2022, up 135.9% from $1,180 in 2017, still below the state median of $5,067. 54% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
