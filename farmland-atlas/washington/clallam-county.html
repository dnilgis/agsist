Clallam County, Washington

Clallam County, WA: irrigated cash rent $108 an acre (2023); land and buildings $19,353 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53009. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Land value here is $19,353 per acre in 2022, up 51.6 percent from 2017, or 8.7 percent a year. July nights average 50.5 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 49.9 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched this county in 44 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2015 the worst year at 16 weeks, and 12 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 40 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 6 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
