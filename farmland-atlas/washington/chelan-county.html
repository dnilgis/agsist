As of Oct 16, irrigated rent in Chelan County runs $758 an acre for 2025. The census value of land and buildings came in at $18,894 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $15,400 in 2017, which works out to 4.2% a year. That puts the county 6th highest of 34 in Washington, well over the state median of $5,067. Only 8% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
