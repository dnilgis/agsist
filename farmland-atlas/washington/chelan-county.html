Chelan County, Washington

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $18,894 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

93.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 24.37 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 156 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,539 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $18,894 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.28 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 8, 2026 to Sep 10, 2026). 0.1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 5.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,682 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $24.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.37 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 2.64, 2016 to 2025. 96.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.8%.

Irrigated rent in Chelan County runs $758 an acre for 2025. The census value of land and buildings came in at $18,894 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $15,400 in 2017, which works out to 4.2% a year. That puts the county 6th highest of 34 in Washington, well over the state median of $5,067. Only 8% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
