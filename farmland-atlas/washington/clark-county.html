Stands out against its own record

$90

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 89.5% from $48 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Clark County runs $90 an acre for 2026, up 89.5% from $48 in 2014 and tied for 8th highest of 25 in Washington. Irrigated ground rents for $418 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 14. The census value of land and buildings came in at $18,755 an acre in 2022, up 109.6% from $8,946 in 2017 and 7th highest of 34, against a state median of $5,067. Only 14% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
