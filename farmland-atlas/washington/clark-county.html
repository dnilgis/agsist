Clark County, Washington

Clark County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $90 an acre (2026); land and buildings $18,755 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clark County averages $90 per acre in 2026, up 89.5 percent from 2014. Irrigated cash rent is $418 per acre, a multiple of 4.64 over non-irrigated rent in 2026, up from 3.11 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Census value of land and buildings runs $18,755 per acre, up 109.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 16 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1996-2022 total $169,315, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 63 percent, and heat and drought account for 0 percent of indemnities from 2020-2025. July nights have warmed 0.67 F per decade since 1976, and the county saw 35 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
