Garfield County, Washington

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,814 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.92 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.67 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 172 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,136 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,814 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $70.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 10.2% from 2016 to 2026. 45% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 28 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 210,779 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.92 per base acre, PLC $0.93.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $29.52 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $8.18 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.16 per base acre, PLC $11.04. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (61.13 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $27.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 666 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.66 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.14, 2016 to 2025. 0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.7%.

$71

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 10.2% from $64 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Garfield County runs $71 an acre for 2026, up 10.2% from $64 in 2016 and right at the state median of $71. The census value of land and buildings was $1,814 an acre in 2022, up 15.3% from $1,573 in 2017, or 2.9% a year. That sits 2nd lowest of 34 in Washington, well under the state median of $5,067. 45% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
