Stands out against its own record

$71

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 10.2% from $64 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Garfield County runs $71 an acre for 2026, up 10.2% from $64 in 2016 and right at the state median of $71. The census value of land and buildings was $1,814 an acre in 2022, up 15.3% from $1,573 in 2017, or 2.9% a year. That sits 2nd lowest of 34 in Washington, well under the state median of $5,067. 45% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
