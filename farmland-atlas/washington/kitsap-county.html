Kitsap County had 9,539 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kitsap County averages $172 per acre; irrigated cash rent in 2021 was $342 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $50,663 per acre, with a change of 44.1 percent, 7.6 percent a year, from 2017-2022. The July average nightly low was 54 F for the 1991-2020 normal and 55.1 F for 2017-2026, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.67 F per decade, warming. Drought was rare: 25 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, with 2 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
