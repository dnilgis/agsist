Cash rent in Walla Walla County runs $74 an acre for 2025, up 75.0% from $42 in 2014. Irrigated ground rents for $297 in 2026, 7th lowest of 14 against a state median of $350. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,786 an acre in 2022, up 49.6% from $2,531 in 2017, and it sits 14th lowest of 34 against a state median of $5,067. 55% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
