Non-irrigated cash rent in Walla Walla County averages $74 per acre in 2025, up 75 percent from 2014. Irrigated rent runs $297 per acre for 2026, a multiple of 4.20 times the non-irrigated rate in 2025, against 3.97 in 2011, with no clear direction in that multiple. The county logged 89 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 193 weeks total from 2000-2025.
