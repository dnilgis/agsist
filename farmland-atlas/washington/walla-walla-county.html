Walla Walla County, Washington

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,786 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.63 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 12.04 inches, 77% of normal (15.67 inches) through Nov 18, at Walla Walla Regional Airport. Frost-free season: 202 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,606 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,786 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $73.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 55% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Cattle: NASS withholds this county's count (D). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 20,201 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.63 per base acre, PLC $4.91.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (195.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.88 per base acre, PLC $12.98. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (71.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $27.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 93 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.58 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.30, 2016 to 2025. 27.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 48.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.3%.
