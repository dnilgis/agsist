Island County, Washington

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $16,100 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.91 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 72% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 252 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,657 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $16,100 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $111 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 235 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.91 per base acre, PLC $2.84.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (113.13 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $122.49 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.00 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $32.00 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

29% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 2002 to 2025; too much rain caused 18.3%.

Cash rent in Island County runs $111 an acre for 2026, against a state median of $71, which puts it 6th highest of 25. Pasture rent was $139 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings came to $16,100 an acre in 2022, up 46.6% from $10,979 in 2017, or 8.0% a year. That sits well above the state median of $5,067.

Island County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
