Adams County, Washington

Adams County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $57 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,133 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 211.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated rent is up 68.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. July nights have warmed 0.92 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 55.9 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 54.2 F. Groundwater supplies 48 percent of irrigation water as of 2015.
