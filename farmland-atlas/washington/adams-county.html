$57

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 11 surveys since 2011; up 69% from $34 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, rent in Adams County runs $57 an acre for 2026, up 68.7% from $34 in 2016. Irrigated ground is another story at $467 an acre in 2026, 2nd highest of 14 in Washington. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,133 an acre in 2022, up 49.4% from $1,428 in 2017.
