Jefferson County, Washington

Jefferson County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,294 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53031. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Jefferson County had 8,717 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jefferson County, WA runs $60 per acre. Land value stood at $13,294 per acre in 2022, up 74.7 percent over 2017-2022, or 11.8 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 49.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 48.5 F, with a warming trend of 0.55 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been light: 34 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, worst year 2015 with 16 weeks, and only 2 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
