USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Grant County runs $52 an acre for 2026, 7th lowest of 25 in Washington against a state median of $71. Irrigated ground is another story at $449 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $5,216 an acre in 2022, up 52.5% from $3,421 in 2017. Corn made 261.8 bu in 2025, well above the county median of 217.2 bu.
