Grant County, Washington

Grant County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,216 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 217.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53025. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Grant County runs $449 per acre in 2025, against $52 per acre for non-irrigated land in 2026. Irrigation covers 80 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 25 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Land value rose 52.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 8.8 percent a year.
