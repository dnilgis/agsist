Columbia County, Washington

Columbia County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $110 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,280 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Columbia County averages $110 per acre for 2026, up 279.3 percent from 2014. Land value in the 2022 census was $2,280 per acre, up 40 percent from 2017, 7 percent a year. Heat and drought caused 71 percent of $97.5 million in crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share rose across 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, at 30 percent, 69 percent, and 81 percent. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 86 over 2021-2025, against 179 of 1357 weeks for the full 2000-2025 span. Wind operating capacity stands at 657.4 MW, with no solar and no new capacity proposed to EIA.
