$110

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 10 surveys since 2012; up 279.3% from $29 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Columbia County runs $110 an acre for 2026, up 279.3% from $29 in 2014 and 7th highest of 25 in Washington. The census value of land and buildings was $2,280 an acre in 2022, up 40.0% from $1,628 in 2017, yet still 7th lowest of 34 in the state. 46% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
