Cash rent in Spokane County runs $50 an acre for 2026, tied for 5th lowest of 25 in Washington against a state median of $71. That rent is down 1.0% from 2016, when it also stood at $50. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,951 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $3,728 in 2017. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
