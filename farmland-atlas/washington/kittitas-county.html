Irrigated cash rent in Kittitas County runs $202 an acre for 2026. That is 3rd lowest of 14 in Washington and well under the $350 state median. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,287 an acre in 2022, up 3.9% from $4,127 in 2017, and sits 16th lowest of 34. In 2022, 45% of the farmland here was rented.
