Cover crops go on 0.1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 117.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Cash rent in Cowlitz County runs $84 an acre for 2026, 10th highest of 25 in Washington and above the state median of $71. That is up 32.5% from $63 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $10,824 an acre in 2022, up 20.9% from $8,955 in 2017, and 11th highest of 34 against a state median of $5,067. Only 17% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Cowlitz County

What is cash rent in Cowlitz County, Washington?

The county average is $84 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cowlitz County, Washington?

Land and buildings were worth $10,824 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
