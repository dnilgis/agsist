Franklin County, Washington

Franklin County, WA: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,182 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 219.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 53021. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Franklin County runs $553 per acre in 2025, against $34 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2026. That gap in use points to water as the deciding factor here, and only 25 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015, leaving most supply exposed to surface conditions. Heat and drought drove 39 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share rose to 51 percent in 2020-2025 after 29 percent in 2010-2019. July nightly lows have warmed 1 F per decade since 1976, running 59.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 57.5 F normal for 1991-2020. Land values rose 12.8 percent from 2017-2022, 2.4 percent a year.
