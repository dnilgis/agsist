Franklin County is an irrigated county, and that shapes everything else here. Non-irrigated ground rents for $34 per acre in 2026, while irrigated ground rented for $553 per acre in 2025, so the water is where the money is. The loss ratio of 1.09 over 1989-2025 means the insurance has paid out more than the premiums taken in, and 13 of 29 closed crop years paid out more than premium. Heat and drought was the largest cause of claims at 40 percent, and its share went from 35 percent in 1989-1999 to 53 percent in 2020-2025. Julys have been warmer at night, averaging 59.3 F lows from 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 57.5 F.
