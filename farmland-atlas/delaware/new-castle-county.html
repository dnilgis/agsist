New Castle County, Delaware

New Castle County, DE: non-irrigated cash rent $102 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,212 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 10003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in New Castle County is $102 per acre, up 8.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings carry a census value of $11,212 per acre, up 18 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought account for 63 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, though that share fell from 78 percent in 1989-1999 to 37 percent in 2010-2019, then rose to 72 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 67.2 F to 69 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.93 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks stayed low, at 68 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 23 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
