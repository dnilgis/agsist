USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in New Castle County runs $102 an acre for 2026, up 8.5% from $94 in 2016 and the lowest of 3 in Delaware. The census value of land and buildings tells it the other way, at $11,212 an acre in 2022, up 18.0% from 2017 and the highest of 3. Dryland corn trends at 156.8 bu, also the highest of 3, and 72% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
