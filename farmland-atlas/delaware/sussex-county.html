Stands out against its own record

13 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2012 (21 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Sussex County sits at $106 an acre for 2026, 2nd lowest of 3 in Delaware and right on the state median. That is up 19.8% from $89 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $9,642 an acre in 2022, up 13.8% from $8,473 in 2017. Corn made 184.9 bu in 2025, and 46% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
