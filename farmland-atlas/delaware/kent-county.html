Kent County, Delaware

Kent County, DE: non-irrigated cash rent $119 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,679 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 10001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kent County is $119 per acre, up 33 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census sits at $8,679 per acre, up 9.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. The July nightly low has warmed to 69.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.4 F. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed years is 0.28, below the full-period 1989-2025 ratio of 0.56.
