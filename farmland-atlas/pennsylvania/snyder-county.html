Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 68.6% from $70 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Snyder County rents for $118 an acre in 2026, up 68.6% from $70 in 2016 and 11th highest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings came to $9,076 an acre in 2022, up 23.8% from $7,332 in 2017, or 4.4% a year. Corn runs a little behind the state, with a trend yield of 129.9 bu that sits 13th lowest of 50.
