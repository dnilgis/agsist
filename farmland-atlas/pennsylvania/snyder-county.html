Snyder County, Pennsylvania

Snyder County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $118 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,076 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 125.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42109. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Snyder County runs $118 per acre in 2026, up 68.6 percent from 2016. Land value carries at $9,076 per acre in 2022, up 23.8 percent from 2017 (4.4 percent a year), and 2022 rent stood at 0.9 percent of that value. Corn yield trend runs 0.7 bushels per acre a year on 2008-2025, with a median of 125.7 and a worst year of 82.2 in 2022. Heat and drought drove 77 percent of $11.2 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, with the share at 86 percent in 1989-1999, 73 percent in 2000-2009, 56 percent in 2010-2019, and 86 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio was 0.85 over 1989-2025 but 1.15 over 2016-2025. Drought weeks were rare: 1 of 1357 weeks since 2000, none since 2021.
