Beaver County, Pennsylvania

Rain is at 114% of normal. Land and buildings run $7,099 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.72 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 114% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,756 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,099 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $70.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.53 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 143.1% from 2016 to 2026. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 11% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 101.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 232 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 22: $3.81 at Mt Jackson, 20 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.72 per base acre, PLC $2.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.17 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $74.04 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $24.35 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.23 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $70.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 7 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.73 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.72, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 28.1%.
