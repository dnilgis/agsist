$56

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 72.3% from $33 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Crawford County runs $56 an acre for 2026, up 72.3% from $33 in 2016 and still under the state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $3,981 an acre in 2022, up 19.2% from 2017, and that ranks 4th lowest of 63 in the state. Corn does better than the dirt costs, with a trend yield of 155.0 bu that ranks 10th highest of 50.
