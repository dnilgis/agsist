Centre County, Pennsylvania

Centre County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $107 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,311 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Centre County averages $107 per acre in 2026, up 37.2 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $8,311 per acre in 2022, up 24 percent from 2017, 4.4 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 149.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 92.7 in 2011. Drought has been light: 6 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, none from 2021-2025. Heat and drought drive 72 percent of the county's crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share reached 89 percent in 2020-2025, the highest of any period listed. July nights have warmed to a 61.4 F average low for 2017-2026, against a 59.7 F normal for 1991-2020.
