As of Oct 6, cash rent in Northumberland County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 45.1% from $77 in 2016, and that ranks 13th highest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,292 an acre in 2022, up 41.2% from $5,872 in 2017. Corn trends at 145.3 bu, 15th highest of 50 in the state, though last year came in at 135.7 bu.
