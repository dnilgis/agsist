Northumberland County, Pennsylvania

Northumberland County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $111 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,292 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 140.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42097. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $111 per acre, up 45.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value came in at $8,292 per acre, up 41.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.1 percent a year, and that $111 rent is 1.36 percent of the 2022 census value. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 0.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 140.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 86.9 in 2022. Heat and drought stayed the largest cause of indemnities across 1989-2025 at 65 percent, though its share by period moved 95 percent, 71 percent, 49 percent, then 67 percent. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.97, close to breakeven on premium paid.
