Wyoming County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,125 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.87 per base acre for corn.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,125 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $33 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.38 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 9% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 53.5% from 2014 to 2026. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 64.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 10,332 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 54% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
