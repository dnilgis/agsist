As of Oct 6, cash rent in Wyoming County runs $33 an acre for 2026, up 53.5% from $22 in 2014. That sits 15th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania, where the median is $68. The census value of land and buildings was $5,125 an acre in 2022, up 23.7% from $4,144 in 2017. Corn trends at 122.4 bu, 8th lowest of 50 against a state median of 138.8 bu.
