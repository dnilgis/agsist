Non-irrigated cash rent in Wyoming County averages $33 per acre in 2026, up 53.5 percent from 2014. Land is valued at $5,125 per acre by operators as of the 2022 census, with that value up 23.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.3 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed, from 59.2 F normal to 60.9 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.71 F per decade trend since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 68 percent in 1989-1999 to 49 percent in 2000-2009, 17 percent in 2010-2019, and 61 percent in 2020-2025.
