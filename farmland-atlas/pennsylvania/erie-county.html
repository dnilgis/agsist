Erie County, Pennsylvania

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Northwestern Pennsylvania region (7 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: larger agricultural land sold for $3,800 to $5,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.57 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.88 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 172 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,466 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.55 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.40, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 5.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.8%.

$68

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2008; up 49.5% from $46 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Erie County sits at $68 an acre for 2026, right on the state median and 31st lowest of 61. That rent is up 49.5% from $46 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,301 an acre in 2022, up 17.5% from $4,511 in 2017 and still under the state median of $7,064. Corn runs better than the land value lets on, with a trend yield of 153.4 bu that ranks 11th highest of 50.
