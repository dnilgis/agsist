USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 12, cash rent in Clarion County runs $30 an acre for 2026, up 20.4% from $25 in 2016 and tied for 8th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $4,159 an acre in 2022, up 29.6% from $3,210 in 2017, and that sits 5th lowest of 63. The corn trend comes out at 140.8 bu for 2026, a bit over the state median of 138.8.
