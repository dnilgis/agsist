Mercer County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Northwestern Pennsylvania region (7 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: larger agricultural land sold for $3,800 to $5,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.00 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.28 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,567 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 7.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 64.3%.
