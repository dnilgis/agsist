Fulton County, Pennsylvania

Fulton County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $77 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,350 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 116.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42057. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Fulton County is $77 per acre, up 75 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value in 2022 was $5,350 per acre, up 28.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.1 percent a year. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 116.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 71.1 in 2011. Heat and drought drove 77 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share at 78 percent in 1989-1999, 77 percent in 2000-2009, 68 percent in 2010-2019, and 86 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 61.5 F normal to a 62.8 F average over 2017-2026, and drought weeks stayed low at 3 out of the 2021-2025 period.
