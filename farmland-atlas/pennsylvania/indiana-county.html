Cash rent in Indiana County runs $46 an acre for 2026, up 16.7% from $39 in 2016 and 21st lowest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $4,312 an acre in 2022, up 19.8% from $3,600 in 2017, and that ranks 8th lowest of 63. Corn trends at 141.4 bu, a bit over the state median of 138.8 bu, though last year came in at 122.9 bu.
