Non-irrigated cash rent in Indiana County averages $46 per acre in 2026, up 16.7 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $4,312 per acre, with rent equal to 1.17 percent of that value in 2022. Corn yield trend runs 0.7 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 135.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 108.1 in 2011. Heat and drought caused 56 percent of the $19.9 million in indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and that share moved 79 percent, 50 percent, 26 percent, 77 percent across the four periods listed. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.56 F per decade since 1976, and the county recorded 0 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2000-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
