Land in Pike County carries a value of $3,130 per acre by the 2022 census operators' own estimate, up 40.6 percent from 2017, or 7.1 percent a year. July nights average 60.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 58.5 F normal for 1991-2020, and the county has warmed 0.83 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 27 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 10 weeks, and 5 weeks from 2021-2025.
