Cash rent in Cumberland County runs $132 an acre for 2026, tied for 8th highest of 61 in Pennsylvania and up 36.1% from $97 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $10,509 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from 2017 and 10th highest of 63. Corn holds up too, with a trend yield of 159.3 bu that sits 5th highest of 50 against a state median of 138.8 bu.
