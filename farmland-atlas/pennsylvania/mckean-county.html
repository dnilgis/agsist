McKean County, Pennsylvania

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,268 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.17 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

80.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.63 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 149 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,189 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,268 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.41 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 22% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 7.5% from 2016 to 2026. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 65.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 123 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 88,213 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.17 per base acre, PLC $2.69.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (107.21 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $70.39 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.69 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $49.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 16 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.2%.

Quick answers for McKean County

What is cash rent in McKean County, Pennsylvania?

The county average is $19 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in McKean County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $4,268 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
