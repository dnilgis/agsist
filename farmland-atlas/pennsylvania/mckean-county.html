As of Oct 6, cash rent in McKean County runs $19 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. That is down 7.5% from $20 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 49.9% from $2,848 in 2017 to $4,268 in 2022, still 7th lowest of 63. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
