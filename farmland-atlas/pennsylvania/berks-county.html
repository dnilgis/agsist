Berks County, Pennsylvania

Berks County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $155 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,449 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 162.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent here is $155 per acre in 2026, up 36 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $13,449 per acre in 2022, up 20 percent from 2017. July nightly lows have moved from 63.6 F to 65.7 F, and irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland.
