Lancaster County, Pennsylvania

Lancaster County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $289 an acre (2026); land and buildings $21,633 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42071. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lancaster County is $289 per acre in 2026, up 29 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $21,633 per acre, with an 18.3 percent gain from 2017 to 2022. Irrigation is a small factor here, covering 2 percent of harvested cropland, and the corn yield trend runs 2 bushels per acre a year on a median of 179.8 bushels per acre over 2008-2025. Heat and drought drove 71 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, though that share has moved differently across periods: 87 percent in 1989-1999, 82 percent in 2000-2009, 58 percent in 2010-2019, and 68 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.51, against 0.44 for the full 1989-2025 record.
