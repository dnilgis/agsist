Allegheny County, Pennsylvania

Allegheny County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2022); land and buildings $10,009 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 122.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Allegheny County ran $24 per acre in 2022, up 6.7 percent from 2012. Land and buildings carried a census value of $10,009 per acre, with that value up 14.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.7 percent a year. Heat and drought caused 59 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that cause's share moved from 30 percent in 2010-2019 to 76 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows averaged 61.9 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 63.5 F in 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.61 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought covered none of the county across all tracked weeks from 2000-2025.
