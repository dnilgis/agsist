Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.96 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 120% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,860 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,009 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.59 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 18% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 58.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 631 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 9.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 26.1%.

Quick answers for Allegheny County

What is farmland worth in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $10,009 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 128.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
