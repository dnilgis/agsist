Lebanon County, Pennsylvania

Lebanon County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $206 an acre (2026); land and buildings $16,709 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42075. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lebanon County averages $206 per acre in 2026, up 6.7 percent from 2016. Census land value runs $16,709 per acre, the operators' own estimate, not a sale price, up 16 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trended up 2.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 173 bushels per acre and a worst year of 116.7 in 2012. July nightly lows have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 63.8 F to 66 F over 2017-2026, and the 2020s are the first decade to reach that mark. Heat and drought drove 86 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1993-2025, and severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county for 25 weeks between 2021 and 2025.
