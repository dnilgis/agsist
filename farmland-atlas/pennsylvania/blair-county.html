Cash rent in Blair County sits at $175 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. That is up 43.4% from $122 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $6,711 an acre in 2022, down 0.5% from $6,747 in 2017. Corn runs a 14-yr trend of 177.2 bu, 2nd highest of 50 in the state.
