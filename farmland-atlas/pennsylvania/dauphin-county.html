Dauphin County's corn is mostly dryland, and the thing that stands out is heat and drought claims. Their share of crop insurance indemnities went from 95 percent in 1989-1999 to 86 percent in 2020-2025, with 78 percent across all closed years. The county paid out more than it took in premium in one of the last fifteen closed crop years, and the loss ratio over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 is 0.59. July nights have been warming too: the average low for 2017-2026 is 65.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.9 F. Non-irrigated cash rent is $88 per acre in 2026, up 27.7 percent from 2016.
