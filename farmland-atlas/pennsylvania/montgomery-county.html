Montgomery County, Pennsylvania

Rain is at 92% of normal. Land and buildings run $21,260 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.99 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.44 inches, 92% of normal (43.81 inches) through Nov 16, at Norristown. Frost-free season: 204 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,104 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $21,260 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $68 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 33% from 2014 to 2026. 42.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 54.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 293 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.99 per base acre, PLC $2.40.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (95.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (27 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $98.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.29 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.89 over the same years. 4.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 7.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.3%.
