Cambria County, Pennsylvania

Rain is at 111% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,911 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.86 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 47.62 inches, 111% of normal (42.9 inches) through Nov 16, at Ebensburg Sewage Plant. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,378 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,911 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $48 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 60% from 2016 to 2026. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 155.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 114 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,153 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.86 per base acre, PLC $2.55.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (101.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.85 per base acre, PLC $8.16. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $64.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 43 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.02 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.80, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 73% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.9%.

Cropland rent in Cambria County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 60.0% from $30 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $68 and ranks tied for 22nd lowest of 61. The census value of land and buildings was $4,911 an acre in 2022, up 6.0% from $4,631 in 2017, and 17th lowest of 63 in Pennsylvania. Corn trends at 138.6 bu for 2026, about even with the state median of 138.8.
