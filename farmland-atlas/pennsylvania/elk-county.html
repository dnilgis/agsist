The census value of land and buildings in Elk County runs $5,329 an acre in 2022, up 26.9% from $4,198 in 2017. That sits under the state median of $7,064 and ranks 23rd lowest of 63. Cash rent was $18 an acre in 2021, and 16% of farmland was rented in 2022. The corn trend comes to 141.4 bu, tied for 20th highest of 50, though last year came in at 118.2 bu.
