Cameron County had 5,578 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 58.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 57 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.39 F per decade, warming. Drought has stayed light here: only 1 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, and 2021-2025 had none. No cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in the county.
