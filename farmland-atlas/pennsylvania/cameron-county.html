Cameron County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,594 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.46 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.46 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 160 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,387 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,594 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.42 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 1, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 47 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 39,400 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.46 per base acre, PLC $2.85.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (113.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $67.22 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.07 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $49.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.
