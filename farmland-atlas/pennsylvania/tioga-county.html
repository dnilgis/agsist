Non-irrigated cash rent in Tioga County stands at $48 per acre, up 88.2 percent from 2014 to 2026. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 0.9 bushels per acre a year over 2010-2025, with a median of 128.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 104.2 in 2020. July nights average 57.5 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 55.9 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.4 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 28 percent in 1989-1999, 21 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, and 48 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 17 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with zero from 2021-2025, and census land value reached $4,552 per acre in 2022, up 19.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
