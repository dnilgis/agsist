Carbon County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,381 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.57 per base acre for corn.

Carbon County vs Pennsylvania: $60 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 45% of the other 60 Pennsylvania counties with a 2026 rent (28th lowest of 61).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.96 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 171 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,454 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,381 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $60 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.50 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 16% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 47.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 60 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Quick answers for Carbon County

What is cash rent in Carbon County, Pennsylvania?

The county average is $60 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Carbon County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $8,381 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
