Carbon County, Pennsylvania

Carbon County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,381 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carbon County runs $60 per acre. Land carries a census value of $8,381 per acre, up 26.6 percent from 2017-2022, 4.8 percent a year. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $6.8 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 44 percent; that share moved 78 percent in 1989-1999, 51 percent in 2000-2009, 22 percent in 2010-2019, and 64 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over the same closed years stands at 1.63, rising to 2.43 over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025. July nightly lows have warmed from a 59.6 F normal to 61.5 F over 2017-2026, a trend of 0.96 F per decade since 1976.
