Union County, Pennsylvania

Union County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $106 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,462 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42119. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Union County is $106 per acre in 2026, up 32.5 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 reached $12,462 per acre, up 29.8 percent from 2017. July nightly lows have moved from 61.2 F to 62.9 F, and the long trend since 1976 is 0.71 F per decade, warming.
