Cash rent in Adams County runs $91 an acre for 2026, up 34.8% from $68 in 2016 and 18th highest of 61 counties in Pennsylvania. The census value of land and buildings was $9,138 an acre in 2022, up 32.7% from $6,886 in 2017, which ranks 15th highest of 63 against a state median of $7,064. Corn came in at 118.5 bu in 2025, well under the 14-yr trend of 140.2 bu.
