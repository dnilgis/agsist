USDA NASS county yields, 2025

York County cash rent runs $166 an acre for 2026, up 48.2% from $112 in 2016. That is 5th highest of 61 in Pennsylvania, against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $9,224 an acre in 2022, up 12.5% from 2017. Corn made 209.8 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year median of 156.1 bu.
