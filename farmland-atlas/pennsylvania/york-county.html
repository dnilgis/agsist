Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,989 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,224 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $166 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.57 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 36% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 48.2% from 2016 to 2026. 43% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 146.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 157.1 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,663 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $86.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 374 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.52 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.46, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 8.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 17.7%.

Quick answers for York County

What is cash rent in York County, Pennsylvania?

The county average is $166 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $324 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in York County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $9,224 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in York County, Pennsylvania?

The county's 2021-25 average is 170.7 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
