Stands out against its own record

$15

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 11 surveys since 2009; down 32% from $22 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 12, cash rent in Wayne County runs $15 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. That is down 31.8% from $22 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 48.0% from $4,294 in 2017 to $6,354 in 2022, 8.2% a year. That leaves it 27th lowest of 63, under the state median of $7,064.
