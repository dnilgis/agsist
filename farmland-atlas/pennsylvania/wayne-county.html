Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County is $15 per acre, down 31.8 percent from 2014 to 2026. Land and buildings carry a census value of $6,354 per acre, with an 8.2 percent a year increase from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 59.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 57.5 F normal for 1991-2020, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.79 F per decade. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 37 percent, with heat and drought's share falling from 38 percent in 2000-2009 to 11 percent in 2010-2019.
