Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.52 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 99% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 158 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,284 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,354 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $15 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.37 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 31.8% from 2014 to 2026. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 100.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Cash rent in Wayne County runs $15 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. That is down 31.8% from $22 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 48.0% from $4,294 in 2017 to $6,354 in 2022, 8.2% a year. That leaves it 27th lowest of 63, under the state median of $7,064.

Quick answers for Wayne County

What is cash rent in Wayne County, Pennsylvania?

The county average is $15 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Wayne County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $6,354 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
