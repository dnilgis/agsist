As of Oct 6, cash rent in Clearfield County ran $26 an acre in 2026, up 73.3% from $15 in 2016 and still 6th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $4,418 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from $3,608 in 2017. Corn trends at 110.6 bu, 3rd lowest of 50 in the state, and last year came in at 81.3 bu.
