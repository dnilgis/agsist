Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.19 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 108% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,730 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,840 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $132 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.57 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 67.1% from 2016 to 2026. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 324.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.87 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.44, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 58% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 75.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.7%.

Quick answers for Mifflin County

What is cash rent in Mifflin County, Pennsylvania?

The county average is $132 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Mifflin County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $7,840 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Mifflin County, Pennsylvania?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 147.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
