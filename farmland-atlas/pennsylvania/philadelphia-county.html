Philadelphia County, Pennsylvania

Philadelphia County, PA: land and buildings $77,080 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42101. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Philadelphia County had 326 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here runs $77,080 per acre, and the Census recorded a change of 31.3 percent from 2017-2022, 5.6 percent a year. July nights average 69.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.7 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 1 F per decade, warming, and the July average low first reached 66 F in the 1980s. Drought has touched this county in 51 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 34 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 16 such weeks.
