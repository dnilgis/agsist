Franklin County, Pennsylvania

Land and buildings run $8,855 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.55 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

28.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 190 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,945 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,855 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $146 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 46% from 2016 to 2026. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 21% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 455.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 60.2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 272 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 116 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

County median corn basis: 45 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.79 at Shippensburg, 13 miles. 1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.55 per base acre, PLC $2.79.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (111 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.29 per base acre, PLC $9.66. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (53.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $86.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 146 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.43, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 53% of the median yield. 2.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 80.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6%.
