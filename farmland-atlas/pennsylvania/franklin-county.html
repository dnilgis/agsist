Franklin County, Pennsylvania

Franklin County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $146 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,855 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County stands at $146 per acre for 2026, up 46 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $8,855 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 17.6 percent, 3.3 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 runs 3.1 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 146.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 77.7 in 2010. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025 at 81 percent, and its share held near that level across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 13 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 0 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2021-2025.
