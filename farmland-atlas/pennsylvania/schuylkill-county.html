As of Oct 16, cash rent in Schuylkill County runs $82 an acre for 2026, up 59.8% from $51 in 2016 and 23rd highest of 61 in Pennsylvania. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,075 an acre in 2022, up 21.5% from $6,645 in 2017, and sits 25th highest of 63. Corn made 113.7 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 122.5 bu.
