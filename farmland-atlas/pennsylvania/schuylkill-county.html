Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania

Schuylkill County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $82 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,075 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 128.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42107. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $82 per acre, up 59.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $8,075 per acre, up 21.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4 percent a year. Corn yields hold a median of 128.7 bushels per acre over 2008-2025, with a trend of 0.3 bushels per acre a year and a worst year of 78 in 2011. July nights average 63.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.4 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.84 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought drove 87 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 66 percent in 2000-2009, 48 percent in 2010-2019, and 81 percent in 2020-2025.
