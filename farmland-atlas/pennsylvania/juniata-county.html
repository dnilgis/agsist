Non-irrigated cash rent in Juniata County averages $113 per acre for 2026, up 51.7 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 120.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 76.7 in 2016. July nights have warmed to an average low of 64.3 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.8 F, part of a warming trend of 0.58 F per decade since 1976. Census land value reached $9,082 per acre in 2022, up 39.3 percent from 2017.
