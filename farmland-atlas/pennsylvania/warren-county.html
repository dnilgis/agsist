Warren County, Pennsylvania

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Northwestern Pennsylvania region (7 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: larger agricultural land sold for $3,800 to $5,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.25 per base acre for corn.

Warren County vs Pennsylvania: $29.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 12% of the other 60 Pennsylvania counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 8th lowest of 61).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

55.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.17 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 158 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,319 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 69% of the median yield. 0.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 52.7%.
