Cash rent in Huntingdon County runs $89 an acre for 2026, up 56.6% from $57 in 2016 and 20th highest of 61 in Pennsylvania. The census value of land and buildings was $6,474 an acre in 2022, up 34.5% from $4,814 in 2017, and that sits 28th lowest of 63 against a state median of $7,064. Corn trends at 139.0 bu, about level with the state median of 138.8 bu.
