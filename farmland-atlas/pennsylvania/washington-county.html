Cash rent in Washington County runs $30 an acre for 2026, up 57.9% from $19 in 2016 and tied for 10th lowest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,188 an acre in 2022, up 10.4% from $6,511 in 2017, which puts it 29th highest of 63. Corn trends at 136.1 bu for 2026, a bit under the state median of 138.8 bu. Only 17% of farmland was rented in 2022.
