Lycoming County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,578 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.02 per base acre for corn.

Lycoming County vs Pennsylvania: $65 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 47% of the other 60 Pennsylvania counties with a 2026 rent (29th lowest of 61).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.26 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 164 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,496 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,578 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $65 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 13% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 71.1% from 2016 to 2026. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 55.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,337 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.02 per base acre, PLC $2.92.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (116.37 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $36.72 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.72 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.47 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $49.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 335 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.92 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.22, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 32.9%.
