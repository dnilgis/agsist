Cash rent in Lycoming County runs $65 an acre for 2026, up 71.1% from $38 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $68. The 2022 census value of land and buildings came to $6,578 an acre, up 28.5% from $5,119 in 2017, and ranks 29th lowest of 63. Corn trends at 143.6 bu for 2026, 19th highest of 50, though 2025 came in at 134.6 bu. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
