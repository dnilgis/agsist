Venango County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Northwestern Pennsylvania region (7 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: larger agricultural land sold for $3,800 to $5,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.30 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,506 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).
