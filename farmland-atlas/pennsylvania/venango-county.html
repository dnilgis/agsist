Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 12 surveys since 2010; up 81.8% from $22 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Venango County runs $40 an acre in 2026, up 81.8% from $22 in 2016 and still under the state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $4,234 an acre in 2022, up 11.5% from 2017 and 6th lowest of 63 in Pennsylvania. The corn trend sits at 145.2 bu for 2026, 16th highest of 50, though the 2025 crop came in at 123.5 bu.
