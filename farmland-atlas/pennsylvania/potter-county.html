Potter County, Pennsylvania

Potter County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,653 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42105. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Potter County averages $22 per acre in 2026, down 14 percent from 2016. Excess moisture and flood is the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025, closed crop years, at 40 percent of $4.4 million, but heat and drought's share has moved from 13 percent in 1989-1999, to 21 percent in 2000-2009, to 27 percent in 2010-2019, to 58 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 57 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 55.4 F. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county for 20 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, and none of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. Wind operating capacity stands at 90 MW, with 38.7 MW of solar proposed to EIA and none operating.
