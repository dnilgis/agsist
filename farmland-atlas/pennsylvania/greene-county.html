USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, Greene County cash rent sits at $49 an acre for 2026, up 234.5% from $15 in 2016, and still 25th lowest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $5,257 an acre in 2022, up 44.3% from $3,644 in 2017, which ranks 20th lowest of 63. Corn here trends at 122.2 bu, 7th lowest of 50 in the state, and only 21% of farmland was rented in 2022.
