Cash rent in Perry County runs $94 an acre for 2026, up 21.4% from $77 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $68 and ranks 17th highest of 61. The census value of land and buildings was $8,722 an acre in 2022, up 31.6% from $6,629 in 2017, or 5.6% a year. Corn trends at 135.7 bu for 2026, a touch under the state median of 138.8.
