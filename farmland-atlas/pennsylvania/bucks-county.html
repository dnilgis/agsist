Bucks County, Pennsylvania

Bucks County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $45 an acre (2026); land and buildings $15,277 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 113.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value here runs $15,277 per acre as of 2022, up 62.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, while non-irrigated cash rent sits at $45 per acre in 2026, up only 2.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Rent covers 0.32 percent of land value. July nights average 66.5 F over 2017-2026, up from the 64.3 F normal of 1991-2020, and the first decade reaching 66 F was the 2020s. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse totaled 14 from 2021-2025, against 53 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000.
