Cash rent in Bucks County was $45 an acre in 2026, up 2.3% from $44 in 2016. That rent ranks tied for 19th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania. The census value of land and buildings was $15,277 an acre in 2022, up 62.4% from $9,408 in 2017, and 4th highest of 63 in the state. Corn made 155.8 bu in 2025, well above the county median of 113.3 bu since 2008.
