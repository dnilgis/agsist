Land

Land and buildings: $7,134 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $39 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 16% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 18.2% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 15% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 104.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 897 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Butler County

What is cash rent in Butler County, Pennsylvania?

The county average is $39 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Butler County, Pennsylvania?

Land and buildings were worth $7,134 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Butler County, Pennsylvania?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 133.8 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 11 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
