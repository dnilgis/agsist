Butler County, Pennsylvania

Butler County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,134 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 131.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Butler County runs $39 per acre in 2026, up 18.2 percent from 2016. Land value sits at $7,134 per acre, up 34.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, so rent equals 0.67 percent of land value. July nights have warmed to a 61.2 F average low for 2017-2026 against a 59.6 F normal for 1991-2020, continuing a 0.62 F per decade trend since 1976. The county logged 0 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025.
