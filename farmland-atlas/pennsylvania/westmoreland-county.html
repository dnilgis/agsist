Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 86% from $38 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Westmoreland County was $71 an acre in 2026, up 85.5% from $38 in 2016 and tied for 27th highest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $6,940 an acre in 2022, up 17.4% from $5,909 in 2017, which ranks 31st lowest of 63. Corn runs light here, with a trend yield of 120.3 bu that sits 5th lowest of 50 in the state.
