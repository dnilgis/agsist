Lehigh County, Pennsylvania

Lehigh County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $121 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,925 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42077. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lehigh County averages $121 per acre in 2026, up 40.7 percent from 2016. Land value in the census sits at $9,925 per acre, with the 2017-2022 change at 26.4 percent, 4.8 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 1.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 152.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 87.6 in 2011. Heat and drought account for 74 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share was 79 percent in 2020-2025, against 97 percent in 1989-1999, 67 percent in 2000-2009, and 67 percent in 2010-2019. July nightly lows have warmed to 64.9 F over 2017-2026 from a 62.6 F normal. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland here.
