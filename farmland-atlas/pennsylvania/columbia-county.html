Cash rent in Columbia County runs $78 an acre for 2026, up 39.3% from $56 in 2016 and above the state median of $68. That puts it 24th highest of 61 in Pennsylvania. The census value of land and buildings was $7,413 an acre in 2022, up 23.0% from $6,029 in 2017. Corn is the soft spot here, with a 14-yr trend of 123.9 bu that sits 10th lowest of 50 in the state.
