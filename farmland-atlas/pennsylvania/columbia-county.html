Columbia County, Pennsylvania

Columbia County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $78 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,413 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 133.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42037. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Columbia County averages $78 per acre for 2026, up 39.3 percent from 2016. July nights average 62.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 60.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the longer trend since 1976 runs 0.6 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought drove 60 percent of the $19.5 million in indemnities paid from 1989-2025, with the share at 86 percent in 1989-1999, 66 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 69 percent in 2020-2025. Land value sits at $7,413 per acre in 2022, up 23 percent from 2017.
