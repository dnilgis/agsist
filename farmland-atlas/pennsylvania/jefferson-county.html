Cash rent in Jefferson County runs $27 an acre for 2026, up 71.0% from $16 in 2016. That is 7th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania, where the state median is $68. The census value of land and buildings was $4,424 an acre in 2022, up 33.5% from $3,314 in 2017, and 11th lowest of 63 against a state median of $7,064. 35% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Sources and terms

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields.
