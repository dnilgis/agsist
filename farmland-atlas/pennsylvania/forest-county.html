Cash rent in Forest County runs $45 an acre for 2026, tied for 19th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania and under the state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,579 an acre in 2022, up 34.3% from $4,900 in 2017, or 6.1% a year. Corn trends at 131.1 bu, a bit shy of the state median of 138.8 bu, and 18% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Forest County had 5,781 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
