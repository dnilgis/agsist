Chester County, Pennsylvania

Chester County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $199 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,004 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42029. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Chester County is $199 per acre in 2026, up 35.4 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings was $13,004 per acre in 2022, up 7.1 percent from 2017, 1.4 percent a year. The July average nightly low has moved from 65 F normal to 66.8 F over 2017-2026, and the county did not reach a 66 F average low until the 2010s. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 90 percent, 73 percent, 47 percent, 53 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, 2020-2025. The county saw 24 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2021-2025, against 60 weeks total since 2000. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland here.
