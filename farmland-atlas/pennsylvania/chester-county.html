Chester County cash rent stood at $199 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 61 in Pennsylvania against a state median of $68. That rent was up 35.4% from $147 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $13,004 an acre in 2022, 6th highest of 63, up 7.1% from 2017. Corn made 205.6 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 195.2 bu.
