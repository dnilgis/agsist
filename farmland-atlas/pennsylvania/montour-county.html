As of Oct 6, cash rent in Montour County runs $100 an acre for 2026, up 88.7% from $53 in 2016. That puts it 16th highest of 61 counties, against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $8,863 an acre in 2022, up 37.6% from $6,442 in 2017. Corn trends at 143.9 bu for 2026, a bit over the state median of 138.8.
