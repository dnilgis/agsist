Armstrong County, Pennsylvania

Armstrong County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,502 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 128.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42005. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Armstrong County averages $32 per acre in 2026, up 26 percent from 2016. Land value is $4,502 per acre, based on the operators' own estimate, not a sale price, up 23.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield has trended up 0.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 128.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 96.8 in 2011. Heat and drought has driven crop insurance losses at rates of 74 percent, 75 percent, 33 percent, and 81 percent across the four periods on record, with the loss ratio rising from 0.72 over 1989-2025 to 0.79 over 2016-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and the county has recorded 0 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2000-2025.
