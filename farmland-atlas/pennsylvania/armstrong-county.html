Armstrong County, Pennsylvania

Rain is at 120% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,502 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.16 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 120% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 172 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,655 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,502 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers northwestern Pennsylvania only. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $31.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 26% from 2016 to 2026. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 111.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 17.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 100 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.16 per base acre, PLC $2.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (104.18 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $73.47 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.02 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34.49 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.64 per base acre, PLC $6.09. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.74 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $70.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 120 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.02 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.79, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 75% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 62.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 26.7%.
