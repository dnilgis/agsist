USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Armstrong County cash rent stands at $32 an acre for 2026, up 26.0% from $25 in 2016. That rent is 12th lowest of 61 in Pennsylvania, against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings was $4,502 an acre in 2022, up 23.5% from $3,644 in 2017, and 13th lowest of 63 in the state. Corn trend yield sits at 134.2 bu, a bit under the state median of 138.8 bu.
