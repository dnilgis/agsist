Northampton County, Pennsylvania

Northampton County, PA: non-irrigated cash rent $90 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,794 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 42095. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Northampton County runs $90 per acre in 2026, up 12.5 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $11,794 per acre for 2022, up 57.2 percent from 2017, or 9.5 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 0.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 148.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 100.9 in 2022. Heat and drought drive 80 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share was 98 percent in 1989-1999, 71 percent in 2000-2009, 50 percent in 2010-2019, and 91 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 stands at 1.08, above the 0.73 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record.
