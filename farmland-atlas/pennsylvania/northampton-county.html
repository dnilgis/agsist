Northampton County, Pennsylvania

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $11,794 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.61 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 176 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,756 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $11,794 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $90 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 12.5% from 2016 to 2026. 61% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 57.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,992 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.61 per base acre, PLC $2.72.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (108.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $57.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.19 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.08, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 80% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.4%.

Heat and drought claims are the story in Northampton County. They were 98 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999 and 91 percent in 2020-2025, and the county paid out $21.3 million on closed crop years from 1989 to 2025. Over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 the loss ratio was 1.08, so claims ran above premium. Corn has still held up in most years, with a median of 148.3 bushels per acre for 2008-2025, but 2022 fell to 100.9. Non-irrigated cash rent is $90 per acre for 2026, up 12.5 percent from 2016. The July nightly low has averaged 64 F since 2017, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.7 F.
