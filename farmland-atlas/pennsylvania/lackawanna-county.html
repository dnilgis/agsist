Cash rent in Lackawanna County was $33 an acre in 2026, up 20.4% from $27 in 2016 and tied for 13th lowest of 61 against a state median of $68. The census value of land and buildings ran $7,310 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $5,301 in 2017 and a little over the state median of $7,064. Corn here trends at 122.0 bu, 6th lowest of 50 in the state.
