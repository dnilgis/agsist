Carbon County, Utah

Carbon County, UT: irrigated cash rent $89 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,617 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All harvested cropland in Carbon County is irrigated, and none of that water comes from groundwater, so surface supply is what carries this land. Drought weeks tell that story too: severe drought or worse hit the county 421 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2021 alone logging 52 weeks, and the 2021-2025 stretch added up to 139 weeks. July nights have warmed, with the 2017-2026 average low at 56.1 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 54.4 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.77 F per decade, warming. Land value moved fast, up 111.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 16.1 percent a year.
