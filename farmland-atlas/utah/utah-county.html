Irrigated cash rent in Utah County runs $122 per acre in 2026, against $33 per acre non-irrigated, a premium of $89 per acre. Non-irrigated rent is down 34 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value sits at $15,104 per acre, up 72.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 11.6 percent a year.
