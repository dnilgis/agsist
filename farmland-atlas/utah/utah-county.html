As of Oct 16, cash rent on Utah County cropland runs $33 an acre for 2026, down 34.0% from $50 in 2016 and a hair under the state median of $34. Irrigated ground rents for $122 an acre in 2026, 7th highest of 24 in the state. The census value of land and buildings came in at $15,104 an acre in 2022, up 72.9% from $8,734 in 2017 and 2nd highest of 28 counties in Utah.
