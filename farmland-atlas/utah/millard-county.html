Millard County, Utah

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,334 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.70 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 10.99 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 120% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 160 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,819 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,334 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $15 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.15 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 10, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 6% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 133.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 924 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,018 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.70 per base acre, PLC $3.34.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (133.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $29.42 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.62 per base acre, PLC $9.41. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (52.12 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $5.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.62 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.15, 2016 to 2025. 92% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 9.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.3%.
