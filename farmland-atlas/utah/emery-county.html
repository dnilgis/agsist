Emery County, Utah

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

31.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 12.25 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 142% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 171 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,840 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value rose while next door fell; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.12 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 10, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 73.9% from 2016 to 2026. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 160.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 400 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,216 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.97 per base acre, PLC $2.76.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (109.97 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $24.30 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $3.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

90.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cropland rent in Emery County ran $20 an acre in 2026, up 73.9% from $12 in 2016. That sits 3rd lowest of 10 in Utah, under a state median of $34. Irrigated ground rented for $106 an acre in 2026, 12th lowest of 24 and close to the $107 state median. In 2022, 28% of the farmland here was rented.

Quick answers for Emery County

What is cash rent in Emery County, Utah?

The county average is $20 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $106 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.
