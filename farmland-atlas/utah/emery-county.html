Emery County, Utah

Emery County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,319 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated land rents for $106 per acre in 2026, against $20 per acre for non-irrigated ground. Irrigated cropland covers 91 percent of harvested acres here, per the 2022 census, and none of that water comes from groundwater. Drought has covered the county at severe level or worse for 462 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 148 of those weeks packed into 2021-2025.
