Davis County, Utah

Davis County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2021); land and buildings $14,480 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in 2026 is $153 per acre, and 86 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, so water access shapes most farmland here. Non-irrigated rent reached $42 per acre in 2021, up 80.4 percent from 2009. Census land value hit $14,480 per acre in 2022, up 55.3 percent from 2017, or 9.2 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 63.2 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 60.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.95 F per decade, warming. Drought was heaviest in 2021, with 52 weeks of severe drought or worse that year and 115 weeks total across 2021-2025.
