Sanpete County, Utah

Sanpete County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2025); land and buildings $5,012 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49039. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland dominates here: 89 percent of harvested acres, with irrigated cash rent at $128 per acre against $23 per acre non-irrigated. Irrigation failure is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across closed years, $1.8 million of $2.9 million total, at 62 percent. Drought has been present in this county, with severe drought or worse recorded in 428 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, and 143 weeks from 2021-2025 alone. July nightly lows have moved from 52.7 F (1991-2020 normal) to 54.4 F (2017-2026), with a warming trend of 0.74 F per decade since 1976. Land value rose 77.7 percent from 2017-2022.
