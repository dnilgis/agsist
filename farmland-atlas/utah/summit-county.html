Summit County, Utah

Summit County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,336 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Summit County cash rent on cropland was $31 an acre in 2025, up 260.5% from $9 in 2013. Irrigated rent ran $91 in 2026, which ranks 6th lowest of 24 against a state median of $107. The census value of land and buildings was $4,336 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $3,265 in 2017, and that sits 13th highest of 28 in Utah.
