Salt Lake County, Utah

Salt Lake County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2023); land and buildings $13,993 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49035. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Salt Lake County is $41 per acre in 2023, up 148.5 percent from 2013. Irrigated cash rent in 2026 is $121 per acre. Land value stands at $13,993 per acre, up 44.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7.6 percent a year. Sixty-seven percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with only 3 percent of irrigation water from groundwater in 2015. July nightly lows average 63.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 60.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 1 F per decade, warming. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 356 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 117 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
