Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

73.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.30 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 116% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 120 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,855 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,020 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.12 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 94% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 399.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Quick answers for Piute County

What is cash rent in Piute County, Utah?

The county average is $32 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $90 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Piute County, Utah?

Land and buildings were worth $4,020 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
