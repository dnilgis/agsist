Piute County, Utah

Piute County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,020 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49031. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Piute County stands at $90 per acre in 2026, and non-irrigated rent is $32 per acre in 2025, up 128.6 percent since 2013. Irrigation covers 95 percent of harvested cropland, with only 4 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Drought has been present here: 392 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, and 2021-2025 alone logged 132 such weeks, with 2021 itself hitting 52 weeks. The July average nightly low has moved from 51.2 F (1991-2020 normal) to 52.6 F (2017-2026), part of a warming trend of 0.56 F per decade since 1976. Census land value reached $4,020 per acre in 2022, up 93.2 percent from 2017, or 14.1 percent a year.
