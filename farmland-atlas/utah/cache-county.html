Cache County, Utah

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,620 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.19 per base acre for corn.

Cache County vs Utah: $37 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 67% of the other 9 Utah counties with a 2026 rent (4th highest of 10).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

62.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 95% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 130 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,961 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,620 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $37 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.41 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 9, 2026 to Sep 10, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 15% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 7.5% from 2016 to 2026. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 192 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,269 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.19 per base acre, PLC $3.79.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (151.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $33.42 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.63 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.20, 2016 to 2025. 70% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.8%.

Quick answers for Cache County

What is cash rent in Cache County, Utah?

The county average is $37 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $105 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cache County, Utah?

Land and buildings were worth $7,620 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
