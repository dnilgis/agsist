Grand County, Utah

Grand County, UT: irrigated cash rent $61 an acre (2021); land and buildings $1,110 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All harvested cropland in Grand County is irrigated, and only 2 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. That makes surface water supply the fact to watch here. Drought weeks back this up: 471 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, and 133 of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. July nights have warmed, running 64 F for 2017-2026 against a 62 F normal, with a 0.78 F per decade trend since 1976. Census land value rose 34.7 percent from 2017-2022, 6.1 percent a year, to $1,110 per acre.
