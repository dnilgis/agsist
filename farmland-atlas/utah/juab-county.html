Non-irrigated cash rent in Juab County is $11 per acre in 2024, down 26.7 percent from 2014. Irrigated cropland covers 79 percent of harvested cropland, and 51 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse ran 442 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2021 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 142 such weeks fell in 2021-2025.
