The census value of land and buildings in Wayne County was $10,311 an acre in 2022, up 127.2% from $4,539 in 2017. That ranks 5th highest of 28 in Utah, well over the state median of $3,900. Irrigated cropland rented for $117 an acre in 2026, tied for 9th highest of 24, while pasture went for $9. 33% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
