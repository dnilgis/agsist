Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County runs $44 per acre; irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $117 per acre. Cropland here leans on water: 93 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with only 2 percent of that water from groundwater in 2015. Drought has been a recurring feature, with 462 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 52 of those weeks in 2021 alone, and 145 more from 2021-2025. July nights have warmed too, averaging 63.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.9 F, with a warming trend of 0.9 F per decade since 1976. Census land value sits at $10,311 per acre, up 127.2 percent from 2017-2022.
