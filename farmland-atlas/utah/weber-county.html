Weber County, Utah

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $18,674 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.68 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

29.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 21.78 inches, 104% of normal (21.02 inches) through Nov 17, at Ogden NE Bench. Frost-free season: 150 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,233 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $18,674 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $58 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 88.7% from 2016 to 2026. 25.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 11.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 211.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.68 per base acre, PLC $2.90.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (115.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: leans PLC, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

86.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 0%.
