Washington County, Utah

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.46 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 123% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 204 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,215 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,684 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.20 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 7% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 105.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

94.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cash rent in Washington County runs $40 an acre in 2025, up 166.7% from $15 in 2013. Irrigated ground rents for $148 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,684 an acre in 2022, up 132.4% from $3,737 in 2017, and that is 7th highest of 28 against a state median of $3,900. In 2022, 39% of the farmland was rented.
