Washington County, Utah

Washington County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2025); land and buildings $8,684 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland dominates here: 94 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigated cash rent runs $148 per acre against $40 per acre non-irrigated. Non-irrigated rent is up 166.7 percent from 2013 to 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $8,684 per acre in 2022, up 132.4 percent from 2017, 18.4 percent a year. Drought has been a recurring presence, with 425 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, and 155 weeks from 2021-2025 alone; 2003 was the worst single year at 52 weeks. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 66.2 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 65 F, a 0.71 F per decade trend since 1976, with the 66 F mark first reached in the 2020s.
