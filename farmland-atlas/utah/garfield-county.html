Garfield County, Utah

Rain is at 149% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,398 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

2.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 14.44 inches, 149% of normal (9.67 inches) through Nov 18, at Escalante. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,497 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,398 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 129.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 303 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

92% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
