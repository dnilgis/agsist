Beaver County, Utah

Beaver County, UT: irrigated cash rent $97 an acre (2024); land and buildings $3,779 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.76 since 1992. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation is the story in Beaver County. 98 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 59 percent of that water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Drought pressure has been present: 319 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 52 weeks in 2021 alone, and 134 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights have warmed 0.74 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average nightly low at 59 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 57.3 F.
