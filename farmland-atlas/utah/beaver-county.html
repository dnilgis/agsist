Beaver County, Utah

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,779 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.67 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

50.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.40 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 123% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 149 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,568 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,779 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.20 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 15% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 184.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 310 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,946 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 27,533 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.67 per base acre, PLC $4.07.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (162.09 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $35.82 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

98.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 3.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1992 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.7%.

Irrigated cropland in Beaver County rented for $97 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings was $3,779 an acre in 2022, up 69.8% from $2,226 in 2017, or 11.2% a year. That sits a bit under the Utah median of $3,900 and ranks 14th lowest of 28. Only 15% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
