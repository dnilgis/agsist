Cash rent in Sevier County ran $12 an acre in 2026, the lowest of 10 counties in Utah and well under the $34 state median. That rent fell 68.8% from $39 in 2016. Irrigated ground rented for $97 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 25.0% to $5,351 an acre in 2022 from $4,282 in 2017, which put it 9th highest of 28.
