Kane County, Utah

Kane County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $9 an acre (2010); land and buildings $4,475 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Kane County land sold at $4,475 per acre in the 2022 census. Land value rose 146.7 percent from 2017-2022, or 19.8 percent a year. All harvested cropland, 100 percent, is irrigated, with only 7 percent of irrigation water from groundwater in 2015. Drought has covered at least 50 percent of the county in 469 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 163 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone and 2003 the worst year at 52 weeks. July nightly lows have warmed 0.8 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 62.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 61.1 F. Corn yield and insurance loss records are not available.
