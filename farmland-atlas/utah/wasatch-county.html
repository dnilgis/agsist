Irrigated cash rent in Wasatch County runs $111 per acre, against $15 per acre for non-irrigated land in 2019. Irrigation covers 96 percent of harvested cropland, with 1 percent of water drawn from groundwater in 2015, so surface supply carries this county. July nights have warmed, averaging 50.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 49.1 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.68 F per decade since 1976. Census land value sits at $8,960 per acre, up 61.2 percent from 2017-2022. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
