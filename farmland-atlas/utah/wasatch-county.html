The census value of land and buildings in Wasatch County stood at $8,960 an acre in 2022, up 61.2% from $5,557 in 2017. That ranks 6th highest of 28 in Utah, against a state median of $3,900. Irrigated cropland rented for $111 an acre in 2026, 11th highest of 24, near the state median of $107. 37% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
