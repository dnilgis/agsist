Tooele County, Utah

Tooele County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2025); land and buildings $3,447 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49045. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigation covers 92 percent of harvested cropland, 2022 census, with 24 percent of irrigation water from groundwater in 2015. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, 2000-2025, were 510 of 1357, and 2021-2025 saw 141 such weeks. July nightly lows averaged 63.6 F for 2017-2026, against 61.6 F for the 1991-2020 normal, with a warming trend of 0.7 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities rose from 13 percent in 1989-1999 to 32 percent in 2010-2019.
