Tooele County, Utah

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,447 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.46 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 10.08 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 171 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,824 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,447 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.09 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 64.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,619 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.46 per base acre, PLC $1.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (64.32 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 25, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.21 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $7.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

92.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 12.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 24.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2022; too much rain caused 0%.

Quick answers for Tooele County

What is cash rent in Tooele County, Utah?

The county average is $27 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $103 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Tooele County, Utah?

Land and buildings were worth $3,447 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
