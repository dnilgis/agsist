The census value of land and buildings in San Juan County was $748 an acre in 2022, the lowest of 28 in Utah against a state median of $3,900. That was up 103.8% from $367 in 2017, or 15.3% a year. Cash rent was $34 an acre in 2010 and pasture rent was $3 an acre in 2025. Only 6% of farmland was rented in 2022.
