San Juan County, Utah

San Juan County, UT: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2010); land and buildings $748 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 49037. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Cash rent moved from $34 per acre non-irrigated in 2010 to $59 per acre irrigated in 2020. Land value rose 103.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 15.3 percent a year. Crop insurance shows heat and drought as the largest cause of indemnities across 1989-2025, at 67 percent, and the share by period is 38 percent for 1989-1999, 72 percent for 2000-2009, 76 percent for 2010-2019, and 74 percent for 2020-2025. Drought weeks ran 463 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2021 alone at 52 weeks and 131 weeks over 2021-2025. July nightly lows average 64.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.3 F, with a warming trend of 0.73 F per decade since 1976.
