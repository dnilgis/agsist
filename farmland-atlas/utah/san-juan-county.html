San Juan County, Utah

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 13.15 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 142% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,047 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $748 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.18 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 10, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). 0.1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.8% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 10.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 320 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $3.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $21.42 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.04, 2016 to 2025. 9.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 66.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1%.

The census value of land and buildings in San Juan County was $748 an acre in 2022, the lowest of 28 in Utah against a state median of $3,900. That was up 103.8% from $367 in 2017, or 15.3% a year. Cash rent was $34 an acre in 2010 and pasture rent was $3 an acre in 2025. Only 6% of farmland was rented in 2022.
