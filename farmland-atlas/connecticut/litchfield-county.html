Cash rent in Litchfield County runs $47 an acre for 2025, up 42.4% from $33 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $14,512 an acre in 2022, up 28.2% from $11,322 in 2017, or 5.1% a year. That puts the county 4th highest of 8 in Connecticut, a little over the state median of $13,767.
