Cash rent in Tolland County runs $90 an acre for 2026, up 8.4% from $83 in 2016 and 2nd highest of 6 in Connecticut. The census value of land and buildings came in at $13,021 an acre in 2022, up 34.2% from $9,703 in 2017. That still sits 4th lowest of 8 in the state, under the $13,767 state median. 25% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
