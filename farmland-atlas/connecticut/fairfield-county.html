Fairfield County, Connecticut

Fairfield County, CT: land and buildings $11,310 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 09001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in the 2022 census stands at $11,310 per acre, up 4.8 percent from 2017. July nights average 65.7 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.7 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 1.05 F per decade. No July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night, and the 2020s is the first decade to reach 66 F. Crop insurance losses from 1999-2015 total $546,631, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 67 percent; heat and drought accounted for 0 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009 and 2 percent in 2010-2019. Irrigated cropland covers 8 percent of harvested acres, and solar operating capacity is 3.9 MW with no wind.
