Cash rent in Windham County runs $56 an acre in 2026, up 30.2% from $43 in 2016. That is the lowest of 6 in Connecticut, against a state median of $83. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,546 an acre in 2022, up 11.6% from $9,452 in 2017, and it is the lowest of 8 against a state median of $13,767. In 2022, 19% of the farmland was rented.
