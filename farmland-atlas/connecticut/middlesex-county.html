Cash rent in Middlesex County runs $68 an acre for 2026, 2nd lowest of 6 in Connecticut against a state median of $83. The census value of land and buildings was $15,325 an acre in 2022, up 2.8% from $14,911 in 2017, 0.5% a year. That value sits 2nd highest of 8 in the state, well over the $13,767 state median. 26% of the county's farmland was rented in 2022.
