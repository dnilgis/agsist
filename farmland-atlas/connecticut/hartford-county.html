Cash rent in Hartford County runs $152 an acre for 2026, the highest of 6 in Connecticut and well over the state median of $83. That rent is up 33.3% from $114 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $15,160 an acre in 2022, 3rd highest of 8, though it slipped 6.0% from $16,126 in 2017. 27% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
