As of Oct 16, cash rent in Huntington County runs $200 an acre for 2026, up 11.1% from $180 in 2014 and a bit under the state median of $217, tied for 34th lowest of 89. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,495 an acre in 2022, up 23.9% from $6,854 in 2017, and that ranks 38th highest of 89. Corn trends at 214.5 bu, close to the state median of 213.9, and 56% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
