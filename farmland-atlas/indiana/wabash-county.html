Non-irrigated cash rent in Wabash County averages $201 per acre for 2026, up 2 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $8,802 per acre, up 39.9 percent from 2017, 7 percent a year. Corn yield trended up 2.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 176.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 105.4 in 2012. Heat and drought made up 24 percent of indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share moved 33 percent, 13 percent, 23 percent, 42 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Irrigated cropland is 3 percent of the county, and solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
