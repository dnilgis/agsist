Corn yields here have climbed 3.1 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2025, with a median of 179.3 bushels. The worst year was 2012 at 90.5 bushels. Heat and drought have paid out 40 percent of all crop insurance indemnities since 1989, though the share has moved around, 52 percent in 1989-1999, down to 22 percent in 2000-2009, then back up to 44 percent in 2010-2019 and 42 percent in 2020-2025. Land value has climbed 26.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Cash rent is $240 per acre in 2026, up 11.6 percent from 2016.
