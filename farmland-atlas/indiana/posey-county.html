Posey County, Indiana

Posey County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $225 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,200 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18129. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Posey County is $225 per acre in 2026, up 18.4 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,200 per acre in 2022, up 18.6 percent from 2017, 3.5 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $94.5 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent. Heat and drought made up 15 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 13 percent in 2000-2009, 35 percent in 2010-2019, and 13 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 38 of 1357 from 2000-2025, and none from 2021-2025. Solar operating capacity stands at 190.8 MW, with 250 MW proposed to EIA.
