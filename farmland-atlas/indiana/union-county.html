Union County, Indiana

Union County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $220 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,913 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18161. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Union County stands at $220 per acre in 2026, up 17 percent from 2016. The census-estimated land value is $9,913 per acre in 2022, after a 56.4 percent increase from 2017 to 2022, or 9.4 percent a year. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 2.9 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 195.8 bushels per acre and a low of 91.7 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025, at 67 percent, with its share rising from 39 percent in 1989-1999, 40 percent in 2000-2009, 78 percent in 2010-2019, and 61 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were absent from 2021-2025, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
