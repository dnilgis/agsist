Ohio County, Indiana

Ohio County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $90 an acre (2025); land and buildings $6,126 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 150.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18115. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Ohio County runs $90 per acre, against a census land value of $6,126 per acre in 2022. Land value rose 40.2 percent from 2017-2022, 7 percent a year. Cropland here is not irrigated; the 2022 census found 0 percent of harvested cropland irrigated. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 at 45 percent, though its share by period moved 49 percent, 41 percent, 50 percent, then 33 percent, not a steady climb. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal low is 65.2 F, the 2017-2026 average is 67.4 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.56 F per decade. Solar and wind operating capacity is 0 MW.
