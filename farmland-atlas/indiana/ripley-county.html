As of Oct 16, Ripley County cash rent runs $174 an acre in 2026, up 11.5% from $156 in 2016 and 16th lowest of 89 in Indiana, under the state median of $217. The census value of land and buildings was $6,590 an acre in 2022, up 27.6% from $5,163 in 2017, or 5.0% a year. Corn trends at 190.7 bu for 2026, 4th lowest of 60 against a state median of 213.9 bu.
