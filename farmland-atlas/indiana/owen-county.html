Non-irrigated cash rent in Owen County runs $162 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $5,396 per acre, up 18.8 percent from 2017, or 3.5 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $43.4 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 51 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 22 percent in 1989-1999 to 9 percent in 2000-2009, up to 46 percent in 2010-2019, then down to 19 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and there were 0 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
