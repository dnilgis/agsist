Allen County, Indiana

Non-irrigated cash rent in Allen County runs $219 per acre in 2026, up 36.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $7,803 per acre, though this figure moved down 1.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 2.9 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 178.8 bushels per acre and a low of 109 in 2012. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in the county.
