Pike County, Indiana

Pike County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $194 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,920 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 160.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18125. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pike County runs $194 per acre in 2026, up 4.9 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $5,920 per acre, up 20.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.7 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $63.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 52 percent. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 35 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, and there were 0 weeks from 2021-2025. Corn yield trended up 3.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 160.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 57.8 in 2012. Solar stands at 210 MW operating, with 250 MW proposed to EIA.
