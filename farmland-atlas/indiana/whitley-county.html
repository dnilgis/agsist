Non-irrigated cash rent in Whitley County runs $199 per acre for 2026, up 38.2 percent from 2014. The census value of land and buildings stood at $8,939 per acre in 2022, with land value change 2017-2022 at 40.9 percent, or 7.1 percent a year. Corn yield trend over 2008-2025 runs 3.2 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 174.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 96.4 in 2012. Heat and drought made up 31 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share moved 20 percent, 16 percent, 35 percent, 33 percent across the listed periods. Irrigated cropland covers 1 percent of harvested acres, and severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county for 46 of 1357 weeks since 2000.
