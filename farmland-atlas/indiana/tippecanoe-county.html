As of Oct 16, cash rent in Tippecanoe County runs $267 an acre for 2026, up 18.7% from $225 in 2016 and 9th highest of 89 in Indiana, where the state median is $217. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,601 an acre in 2022, down 4.7% from $9,027 in 2017. Corn made 233.4 bu in 2025, well over the 185.1 bu county median going back to 2008, and 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
