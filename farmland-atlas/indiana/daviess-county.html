Daviess County, Indiana

Daviess County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $255 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,072 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 187.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Daviess County averages $255 per acre for 2026, up 34.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents higher, at $285 per acre, though only 4 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and all of that draws from groundwater. Corn yields have trended up 3.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 187.7 bushels per acre and a low of 72.9 in 2012. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 41 percent over 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share moved from 36 percent in 1989-1999, to 23 percent in 2000-2009, to 46 percent in 2010-2019, to 15 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 sits at 0.32, well below the full period's 0.65.
