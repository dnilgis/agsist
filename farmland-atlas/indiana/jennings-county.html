Jennings County cropland rent sits at $209 an acre for 2026, up 26.7% from $165 in 2016 and a bit under the $217 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $6,620 an acre in 2022, up 45.0% from $4,564 in 2017, and ranks 19th lowest of 89 in Indiana. Corn runs below the state too, with a 14-yr trend of 197.5 bu that is 11th lowest of 60. Renters farm 45% of the ground here.
