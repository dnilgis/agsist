Benton County, Indiana

Benton County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $300 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,011 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 194.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Benton County averages $300 per acre in 2026, up 20 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $10,011 per acre, up 29.4 percent from 2017, or 5.3 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 3.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 194.6 and a worst year of 116.9 in 2012. Crop insurance losses have stayed low over 2016-2025, with a loss ratio of 0.1 and $0.49 paid per $100 of insured value. Drought weeks were few over 2000-2025, 29 of 1357, with 9 of those falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, and wind operating capacity stands at 1175.2 MW, with 200.2 MW more proposed to EIA.
