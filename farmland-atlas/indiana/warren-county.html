Non-irrigated cash rent in Warren County is $303 per acre for 2026, up 21.2 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $7,338 per acre, with rent at 3.37 percent of that value. Corn yield trend runs 4.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 191.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 110.4 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $52.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 50 percent; that share was 62 percent in 1989-1999, 19 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, and 35 percent in 2020-2025. Wind operating capacity stands at 398.6 MW, with 0 MW solar operating and none proposed to EIA.
