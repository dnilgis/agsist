Marion County, Indiana

Marion County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $185 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,101 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 178.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18097. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 in Marion County is $185 per acre. Census land and buildings value in 2022 stood at $11,101 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 5.1 percent, 1 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities for closed crop years 1989-2025 totaled $10.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 50 percent; that share moved from 37 percent in 1989-1999 to 17 percent in 2000-2009, up to 63 percent in 2010-2019, then down to 35 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse ran 25 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 11 weeks, and only 2 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigated cropland share, 2022 census, was 1 percent.
