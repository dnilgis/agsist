As of Oct 16, Pulaski County cash rent sits at $217 an acre for 2026, right on the state median and tied for 44th highest of 89. That is up 17.3% from $185 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings ran $9,173 an acre in 2022, up 49.2% from $6,150 in 2017 and 23rd highest of 89. Corn averaged 176.0 bu from 2020 through 2024, and 61% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
