Perry County, Indiana

Perry County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $127 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,209 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18123. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Perry County stands at $127 per acre against a census land value of $5,209 per acre, a yield ratio of 1.76 percent. Land value rose 29.8 percent from 2017-2022, or 5.4 percent a year. The median corn yield runs 172.4 bushels per acre over 2008-2025, with the worst year at 129.8 bushels per acre in 2011.
