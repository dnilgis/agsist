Miami County, Indiana

Miami County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $218 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,928 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $218 per acre, up 10.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value in 2022 sat at $7,928 per acre, with a land value change of 17.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.3 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 runs 3 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 172.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 2012 at 126.8. Heat and drought share of indemnities moved 39 percent in 1989-1999, 17 percent in 2000-2009, 27 percent in 2010-2019, and 46 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse totaled 25 over 2021-2025, against 56 of 1357 for the full 2000-2025 record. Irrigated share of harvested cropland is 2 percent.
