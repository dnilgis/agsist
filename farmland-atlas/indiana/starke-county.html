As of Oct 16, cash rent in Starke County runs $170 an acre in 2026, up 6.2% from $160 in 2016 and tied for 14th lowest of 89 in Indiana, under the state median of $217. The census value of land and buildings was $7,178 an acre in 2022, up 43.4% from $5,004 in 2017, and sits 27th lowest of 89 against a state median of $8,158. Corn made 162.3 bu in 2024, a bit under the 171.7 bu the county averaged from 2020 through 2024.
