Starke County, Indiana

Starke County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $170 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,178 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18149. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Starke County runs $170 per acre, up 6.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land and buildings value came in at $7,178 per acre in 2022, up 43.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.5 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 2.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 149.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 97.6 in 2015. Heat and drought drove 38 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share moved from 39 percent in 1989-1999 to 34 percent in 2000-2009, 35 percent in 2010-2019, and 49 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 was 0.53, below the 0.81 ratio for the full 1989-2025 period.
