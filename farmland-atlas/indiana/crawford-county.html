Crawford County, Indiana

Crawford County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $115 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,347 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Crawford County, IN stands at $115 per acre in 2025, up 64.3 percent since 2014. Land value reached $4,347 per acre in 2022, a rise of 40.5 percent over 2017-2022. The county recorded 0 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2021-2025, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 shows no clear direction, holding at 65.1 F for 2017-2026 against a 64.8 F normal.
