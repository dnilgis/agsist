Decatur County, Indiana

Non-irrigated cash rent in Decatur County stands at $235 per acre for 2026, up 29.1 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census was $9,415 per acre, with a 32.9 percent change from 2017 to 2022, or 5.9 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 4.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 184.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 86.1 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $79.4 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 49 percent; that share ran 36 percent in 1989-1999, 37 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, and 34 percent in 2020-2025. No irrigation is recorded here, and no weeks of severe drought or worse were logged from 2021-2025.
