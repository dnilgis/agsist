Fayette County, Indiana

Fayette County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $255 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,205 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 182.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Fayette County averages $255 per acre for 2026, up 40.9 percent from 2016. Census land value for 2022 stood at $9,205 per acre, after a 62.7 percent change from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield has trended up 3.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 182.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 74.9 in 2012. Drought weeks fell to 0 in 2021-2025, down from 23 of 1357 weeks measured over 2000-2025. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
