Cropland rent in Knox County runs $229 an acre in 2026, up 14.5% from $200 in 2016 and 33rd highest of 89 in Indiana. The census value of land and buildings was $8,068 an acre in 2022, up 29.6% from $6,224 in 2017 and a bit under the state median of $8,158. Corn made 207.1 bu in 2024, well above the county median of 180.2 bu. Renters farmed 58% of the ground in 2022.
