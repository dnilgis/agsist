Cash rent in Tipton County runs $275 an acre for 2026, up 25.0% from $220 in 2014 and tied for 6th highest of 89 in Indiana. The census value of land and buildings came to $11,110 an acre in 2022, up 36.0% from $8,167 in 2017, and that ranks 3rd highest of 89 against a state median of $8,158. Corn followed suit, with 221.6 bu last year and a 14 year trend of 224.4 bu, and 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
