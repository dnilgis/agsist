Washington County, Indiana

Washington County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $168 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,753 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18175. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County, IN sits at $168 per acre for 2026, up 46.1 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $5,753 per acre, with a 27.8 percent change from 2017 to 2022, or 5 percent a year. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, and water stress has been mild: 34 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, and 2021-2025 recorded 0 such weeks. Heat and drought caused 47 percent of $66.2 million in indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share running 53 percent in 1989-1999, 43 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 35 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.27. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
