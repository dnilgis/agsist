Fountain County, Indiana

Fountain County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $265 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,277 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 183.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Fountain County stands at $265 per acre for 2026, up 17.8 percent from 2016. Corn yield trend runs 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2022, with a median of 183.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.9 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 49 percent of indemnities over 1989-2025, with that share at 55 percent in 1989-1999, 12 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, and 37 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value reached $8,277 per acre in 2022, up 23 percent from 2017.
