Non-irrigated cash rent in Elkhart County stands at $230 per acre in 2026, up 43.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $307 per acre; the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent was 1.55 in 2009 and is 1.33 in 2026, direction falling. Corn yield trend is up 2.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 173.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 92.5 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved 14 percent, 21 percent, 32 percent, 33 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Census land value is $11,769 per acre, up 5.3 percent from 2017 to 2022.
