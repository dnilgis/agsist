Cash rent in Switzerland County runs $134 an acre for 2026, tied for 2nd lowest of 89 in Indiana against a state median of $217. That rent is down 8.2% from $146 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 85.6% from $4,098 in 2017 to $7,606 in 2022, which still sits 37th lowest of 89. Only 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
