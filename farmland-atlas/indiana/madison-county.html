Madison County, Indiana

Madison County, IN: non-irrigated cash rent $245 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,322 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 181.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 18095. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
