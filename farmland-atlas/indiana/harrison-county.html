USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Harrison County runs $170 an acre for 2026, up 73.5% from $98 in 2016 and tied for 14th lowest of 89 in Indiana, under the state median of $217. The census value of land and buildings was $5,877 an acre in 2022, up 28.5% from $4,575 in 2017, which ranks 6th lowest of 89. Corn trends at 195.4 bu, short of the state median of 213.9.
