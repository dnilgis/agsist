Cash rent in Adams County runs $235 an acre for 2026, up 23.7% from $190 in 2016 and above the state median of $217. The census value of land and buildings was $10,771 an acre in 2022, up 30.2% from $8,274 in 2017 and 6th highest of 89. Corn trends at 207.6 bu for 2026, a bit under the state median of 213.9, and 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
