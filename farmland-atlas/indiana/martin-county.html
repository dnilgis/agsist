Martin County, Indiana

Non-irrigated cash rent in Martin County stands at $190 per acre for 2026, up 46.2 percent from 2016. Census land value came in at $5,972 per acre in 2022, up 53.7 percent from 2017, or 9 percent a year. Cash rent equals 2.95 percent of that census value. Corn yields trended up 4.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2022, with a median of 166.3 bushels per acre and a low of 53.1 in 2012.
