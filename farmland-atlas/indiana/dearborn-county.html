As of Oct 16, cash rent in Dearborn County runs $147 an acre for 2026, the 5th lowest of 89 in Indiana against a state median of $217. The census value of land and buildings was $6,735 an acre in 2022, up 38.6% from $4,861 in 2017. Corn trends to 191.3 bu for 2026, 6th lowest of 60 and under the state median of 213.9 bu. Just 18% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
