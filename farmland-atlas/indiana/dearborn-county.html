Non-irrigated cash rent in Dearborn County for 2026 is $147 per acre. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $6,735 per acre, up 38.6 percent from 2017, 6.7 percent a year. Corn yield trend from 2008-2024 runs 3.7 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 160.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 88 in 2012. Drought has been light: 2 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 32 weeks total from 2000-2025. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $13.9 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
