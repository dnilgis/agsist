Cash rent in Sioux County runs $24 an acre in 2026, the lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $144 an acre in 2026, also the lowest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $1,283 an acre in 2022, up 42.4% from $901 in 2017, or 7.3% a year. That still sits 9th lowest of 93 in the state.
