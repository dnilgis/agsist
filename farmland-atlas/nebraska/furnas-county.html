Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 23% from $71 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Furnas County runs $88 an acre in 2026, up 23.2% from $71 in 2016 and tied for 22nd lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $2,895 an acre in 2022, up 17.3% from $2,467 in 2017. Corn made 155.1 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 144.4 bu.
