McPherson County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,006 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31117. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in McPherson County averages $40 per acre in 2025, down 23.1 percent from 2014. Irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $146 per acre. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 0 percent in 1989-1999, to 15 percent in 2000-2009, to 17 percent in 2010-2019, to 61 percent in 2020-2025. Hail remains the largest single cause of indemnities across 1989-2025, closed crop years, at 56 percent, out of a total of $4.8 million paid. The county saw 316 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 50 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation draws 89 percent of its water from groundwater, 2015, from a base of 966 registered wells.
