Cash rent in McPherson County runs $40 an acre for 2025, down 23.1% from $52 in 2014. Irrigated ground rents for $146 in 2026, 2nd lowest of 78 against a state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $1,006 an acre in 2022, up 22.8% from $819 in 2017, and that sits lowest of 93 in Nebraska.
