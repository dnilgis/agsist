McPherson County, Nebraska

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,006 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.39 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 18.87 inches, 81% of normal (23.38 inches) through Nov 17, at Tryon, 11.3 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 155 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,654 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,006 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2025). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 40.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 4.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 97.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.18 at Hershey, 25 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.39 per base acre, PLC $3.19.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (127.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $22.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.38 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.27 over the same years. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.7 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.3%.
