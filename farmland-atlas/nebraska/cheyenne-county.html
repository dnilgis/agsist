Cheyenne County, Nebraska

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,455 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 16.35 inches, 86% of normal (19.11 inches) through Nov 17, at Lodgepole, 8 mi N. Frost-free season: 154 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,555 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,455 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $42 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 23.2% from 2016 to 2026. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 68.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,265 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 15 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.54 at Dix, 25 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $15.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 766 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.56 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.92, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 84% of the median yield. 11.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 5.4 days a year and damaging wind on 4.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.1%.
