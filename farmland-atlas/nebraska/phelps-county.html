Phelps County, Nebraska

Phelps County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $135 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,776 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 216.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31137. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Phelps County runs $313 per acre in 2026, against $135 per acre for non-irrigated ground. Non-irrigated rent rose 33.7 percent from 2016 to 2026, and land value rose 12.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Solar and wind capacity are not present in this county.
