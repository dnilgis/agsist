USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Irrigated cropland in Phelps County rents for $313 an acre in 2026, 13th highest of 78 in Nebraska and above the state median of $272. Cash rent went from $101 in 2016 to $135 in 2026, up 33.7%. The census value of land and buildings was $5,776 an acre in 2022, up 12.9% from $5,114 in 2017. Corn made 240.5 bu in 2025, against a county median of 216.9 bu since 2008.
