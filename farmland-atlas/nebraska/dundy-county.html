Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2011 (16 surveys on record since 2009); up 16% from $44 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Dundy County was $51 an acre in 2026, up 15.9% from $44 in 2016 and 13th lowest of 80 in Nebraska, where the median is $141. Irrigated ground rented for $237 in 2026 against a state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $1,991 an acre in 2022, up 30.1% from $1,530 in 2017. Corn made 160.6 bu in 2025, a little above the 153.5 bu median going back to 2008.
