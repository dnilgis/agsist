Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Northeast district (13 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $7,670 an acre; high grade $9,705, low grade $5,830. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.56 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Knox County

What is cash rent in Knox County, Nebraska?

The county average is $217 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $300 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Knox County, Nebraska?

Land and buildings were worth $5,161 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Knox County, Nebraska?

The county's 2021-25 average is 175.7 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
