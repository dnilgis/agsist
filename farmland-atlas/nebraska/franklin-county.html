Franklin County, Nebraska

Franklin County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $115 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,265 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 182.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31061. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County is $115 per acre in 2026, up 11.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $284 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved without clear direction, from 2.15 in 2008 to 2.47 in 2026. Corn yields trend up 1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 182.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 153.3 in 2022. Irrigation covers 56 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, drawing 91 percent of water from groundwater, with 1,139 irrigation wells on record showing no decommission date.
