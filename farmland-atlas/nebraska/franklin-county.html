Cash rent in Franklin County runs $115 an acre for 2026, up 11.7% from $103 in 2016 and 29th lowest of 80 in Nebraska. Irrigated ground rents for $284 in 2026, above the state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $4,265 an acre in 2022, up 23.2% from $3,462 in 2017. Corn made 205.6 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 196.0 bu.
