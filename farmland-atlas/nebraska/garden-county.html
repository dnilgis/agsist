Garden County cash rent on cropland runs $44 an acre in 2026, up 22.2% from $36 in 2016 and 9th lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $189 in 2026, 11th lowest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $1,256 an acre in 2022, up 45.0% from $866 in 2017, and still 8th lowest of 93. Corn made 141.2 bu in 2023, about where the county usually lands.
