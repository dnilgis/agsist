Cash rent in Dawes County runs $35 an acre for 2026, up 50.0% from $23 in 2016, and that is tied for 3rd lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $170 in 2026, 4th lowest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $1,397 an acre in 2022, up 57.1% from $889 in 2017, still 13th lowest of 93. 38% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
