Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.39 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.63, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 90% of the median yield. 67.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.1%.
