Kearney County, Nebraska

Kearney County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $129 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,608 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 202.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31099. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Kearney County land depends on irrigation. 68 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 90 percent of that water comes from groundwater. Non-irrigated rent is down 3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census is $7,608 per acre, up 45.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7.8 percent a year.
