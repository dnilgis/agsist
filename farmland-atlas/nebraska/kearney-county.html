USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Kearney County runs $129 an acre in 2026, down 3.0% from $133 in 2016, and below the state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 45.4% to $7,608 an acre in 2022 from $5,232 in 2017, which ranks 7th highest of 93. Corn made 235.4 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 200.3 bu.
