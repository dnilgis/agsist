Hall County, Nebraska

Cash rent in Hall County ran $130 an acre in 2025, up 31.3% from $99 in 2014. Irrigated ground rented for $279 an acre in 2026, a bit over the state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $5,923 an acre in 2022, up 16.5% from 2017 and 32nd highest of 93 in Nebraska. In 2022, 54% of the farmland was rented.
