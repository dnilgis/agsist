Howard County, Nebraska

Howard County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $132 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,372 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 183.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland covers 67 percent of harvested acres in Howard County, and irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $245 per acre against $132 per acre for non-irrigated ground. The irrigated multiple has moved from 2.34 in 2008 to 1.86 in 2026, direction falling. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $86.8 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 40 percent; that share was 10 percent in 1989-1999, 51 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 44 percent in 2020-2025. The county held 300 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2023 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 114 of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. Census land value sits at $4,372 per acre in 2022, up 25.2 percent from 2017.
