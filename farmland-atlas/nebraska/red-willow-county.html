Red Willow County cash rent is $67 an acre for 2026, up 20.9% from $55 in 2016, and that sits 20th lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated rent is $214 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $2,366 an acre in 2022, up 21.5% from $1,947 in 2017, which ranks 30th lowest of 93. Corn made 153.0 bu in 2025, above the 141.8 bu median since 2008.
