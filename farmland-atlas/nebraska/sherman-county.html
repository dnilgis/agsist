Cash rent in Sherman County ran $134 an acre in 2026, up 51.4% from $89 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $141. Irrigated ground rented for $299 in 2026, which ranks 22nd highest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $3,905 an acre in 2022, up 45.9% from $2,676 in 2017. Corn made 181.1 bu in 2025, below the 2021-25 average of 192.6 bu.
