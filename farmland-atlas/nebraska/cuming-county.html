Cuming County, Nebraska

Cuming County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $289 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,508 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 193.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31039. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cuming County averages $289 per acre for 2026, up 9.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $344 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved with no clear direction, 1.19 in 2026 against 1.25 in 2008. Corn yields carry a trend of 1.9 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 193.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 95.7 in 2012. Heat and drought has taken a growing share of crop insurance indemnities by period: 27 percent in 1989-1999, 54 percent in 2000-2009, 75 percent in 2010-2019, and 84 percent in 2020-2025, against total indemnities of $219.0 million. Census value of land and buildings sits at $7,508 per acre, up 23.3 percent from 2017 to 2022.
