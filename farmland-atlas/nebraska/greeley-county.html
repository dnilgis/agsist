Greeley County, Nebraska

Greeley County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $145 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,034 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 185.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31077. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greeley County stands at $145 per acre in 2026, up 39.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $267 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 2.11 in 2008 to 1.84 in 2026, a falling trend. Irrigation covers 61 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 93 percent of its water from groundwater across 1,722 registered wells, 935 of them with no decommission date on record. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $60.0 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 44 percent, and that share has moved from 15 percent in 1989-1999, to 45 percent in 2000-2009, 42 percent in 2010-2019, and 52 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value reached $4,034 per acre in 2022, up 36.1 percent from 2017.
