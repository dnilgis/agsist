Custer County, Nebraska

Custer County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $100 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,892 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 176.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation draws 93 percent of its water from groundwater, with 2322 irrigation wells not decommissioned and a median static water level of 79 feet. The county spent 318 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 100 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
