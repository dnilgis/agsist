Cash rent in Richardson County runs $211 an acre for 2026, down 1.9% from $215 in 2016, and that ranks 21st highest of 80 against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $5,087 an acre in 2022, up 19.2% from $4,267 in 2017. Corn made 185.1 bu in 2025, well over the 162.3 bu median since 2008.
