USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Saline County cash rent sits at $152 an acre for 2026, up 2.0% from $149 in 2016, and that ranks 37th highest of 80 in Nebraska. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,669 an acre in 2022, up 20.2% from $4,716 in 2017, against a state median of $4,499. Corn made 189.1 bu in 2025, well over the 2008 to 2025 median of 163.7 bu.
