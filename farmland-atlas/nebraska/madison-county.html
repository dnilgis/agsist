Madison County, Nebraska

Rain is at 104% of normal. Northeast district (13 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $7,670 an acre; high grade $9,705, low grade $5,830. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.43 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 27.51 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,850 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.71 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.65, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. 45.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.7%.
