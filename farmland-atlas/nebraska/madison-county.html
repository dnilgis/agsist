Cash rent in Madison County ran $214 an acre in 2026, up 9.7% from $195 in 2016. That sits 19th highest of 80 in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $7,527 an acre in 2022, up 29.4% from $5,819 in 2017 and 10th highest of 93. In 2022, 55% of the farmland here was rented.
