Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 13.5% from $111 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Boyd County runs $126 an acre for 2026, up 13.5% from $111 in 2016 and 32nd lowest of 80 counties in Nebraska. The census value of land and buildings was $2,664 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from $2,028 in 2017, which ranks 36th lowest of 93. Corn made 167.2 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 156.9 bu.
