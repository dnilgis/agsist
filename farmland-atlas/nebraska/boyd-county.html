Boyd County, Nebraska

Boyd County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $126 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,664 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 150.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31015. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Boyd County runs $126 per acre in 2026, up 13.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground carried $207 per acre back in 2017, but irrigation covers only 3 percent of harvested cropland here, so most rent decisions rest on dryland performance. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 2 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 150.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 72.2 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 total $65.3 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 86 percent; that share ran 19 percent in 1989-1999, 92 percent in 2000-2009, 78 percent in 2010-2019, and 90 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value reached $2,664 per acre in 2022, up 31.4 percent from 2017.
