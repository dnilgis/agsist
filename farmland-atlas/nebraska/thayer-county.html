Thayer County, Nebraska

Cash rent in Thayer County is $132 an acre for 2026, up 12.8% from $117 in 2016 and a little under the state median of $141. Irrigated rent runs $291 for 2026, tied for 28th highest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $6,039 an acre in 2022, up 54.1% from $3,920 in 2017 and 30th highest of 93. In 2022, 53% of the farmland here was rented.
