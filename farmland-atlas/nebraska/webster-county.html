USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Webster County runs $117 an acre for 2026, up 13.6% from $103 in 2016 and tied for 30th lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,213 an acre in 2022, up 53.8% from $2,739 in 2017. Corn made 189.2 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 158.4 bu. 59% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
