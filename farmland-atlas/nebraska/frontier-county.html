Cash rent in Frontier County sits at $68 an acre for 2026, up 21.6% from $56 in 2016. That is 21st lowest of 80 in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $2,370 an acre in 2022, up 26.1% from $1,879 in 2017. Corn made 162.4 bu in 2024, above the 2008 to 2024 median of 136.0 bu.
