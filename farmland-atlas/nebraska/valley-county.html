Valley County, Nebraska

Valley County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $132 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,037 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31175. Figures as of 2026-10-16.

As of Oct 16, Valley County cash rent sits at $132 an acre for 2026, up 38.2% from $96 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $302 in 2026, 20th highest of 78 in Nebraska. The census value of land and buildings was $3,037 an acre in 2022, up 5.5% from $2,878 in 2017, or 1.1% a year.

The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out.
