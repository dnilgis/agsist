Morrill County, Nebraska

Morrill County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,682 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 165.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31123. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Morrill County runs $40 per acre in 2025; irrigated cash rent is $178 per acre in 2026. Irrigation covers 64 percent of harvested cropland, and 63 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Registered wells number 2585, with 1005 irrigation wells not decommissioned, median depth 120 feet, median static water level 26 feet. Land and buildings were valued at $1,682 per acre in the 2022 census, after a change of 46.9 percent, 8 percent a year, from 2017-2022. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse ran 429 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 115 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
