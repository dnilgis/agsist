Dawson County, Nebraska

Cash rent in Dawson County runs $128 an acre for 2026, up 31.3% from $98 in 2016, and sits 33rd lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated rent is $273 for 2026, about even with the state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $4,897 an acre in 2022, up 43.5% from $3,412 in 2017. Corn made 212.0 bu in 2024, above the 2020-24 average of 205.0 bu.
