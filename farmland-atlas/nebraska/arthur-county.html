Irrigated ground in Arthur County rented for $174 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $1,091 an acre in 2022, up 36.2% from $801 in 2017, or 6.4% a year, and well under the state median of $4,499. That puts it 4th lowest of 93 in Nebraska, and 32% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
