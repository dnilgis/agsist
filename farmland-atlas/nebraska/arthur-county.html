Arthur County, Nebraska

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North district (16 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $1,915 an acre; high grade $2,445, low grade $1,380. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.67 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.81 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 155 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,624 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.25 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.80, 2016 to 2025. 35.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2024; too much rain caused 8.5%.

Quick answers for Arthur County

What is cash rent in Arthur County, Nebraska?

The county average is $174 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Arthur County, Nebraska?

Land and buildings were worth $1,091 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
