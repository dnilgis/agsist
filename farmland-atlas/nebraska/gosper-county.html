Gosper County, Nebraska

Gosper County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $98 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,514 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 183.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31073. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Water comes mostly from groundwater, 88 percent as of 2015, drawn from 804 active irrigation wells with a median static water level of 128 feet. The county spent 85 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. Land values rose 60.5 percent from 2017-2022.
