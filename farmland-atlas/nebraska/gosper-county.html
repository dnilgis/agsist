Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.46 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.57, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 84% of the median yield. 60% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 56% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.6%.
