Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 25.6% from $78 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Gosper County cash rent ran $98 an acre in 2026, up 25.6% from $78 in 2016, and ranked 26th lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rented for $280 an acre in 2026, a little over the state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $4,514 an acre in 2022, up 60.5% from $2,812 in 2017, about even with the state median of $4,499.
