Lincoln County, Nebraska

Lincoln County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,340 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31111. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland in Lincoln County rents for $263 per acre in 2026, against $66 per acre for non-irrigated ground, and 58 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. Groundwater supplies 84 percent of that irrigation, drawn from 8,358 registered wells, with a median depth of 300 feet and a median static water level of 66 feet at drilling. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 4 percent, 32 percent, 34 percent, 53 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Hail remains the largest cause of indemnities overall at 44 percent. Solar and wind operating capacity is 0 MW, with none proposed to EIA.
