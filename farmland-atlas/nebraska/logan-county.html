Logan County, Nebraska

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North district (16 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $1,915 an acre; high grade $2,445, low grade $1,380.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

92.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 20.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 156 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,691 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.07 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.75, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 88% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.5%.
