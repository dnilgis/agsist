USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, rent here runs $49 an acre in 2026, up 5.4% from $47 in 2014, and that is 12th lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $1,355 an acre in 2022, up 12.9% from $1,200 in 2017, and ranks 11th lowest of 93. Corn came in at 162.7 bu in 2025, a little under the 170.2 bu median for 2008 to 2025.
