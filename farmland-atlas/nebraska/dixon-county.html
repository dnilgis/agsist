Dixon County, Nebraska

Rain is at 77% of normal. Land and buildings run $5,376 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.08 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 22.33 inches, 77% of normal (28.95 inches) through Nov 16, at Wakefield, 3 mi NW. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,820 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,376 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $256 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 2% from 2016 to 2026. 46.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.8% of the cropland, CRP left out. 137.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 43,544 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 47 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 3 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.08 at Hinton, 28 miles. 11 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 68 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 2 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.08 per base acre, PLC $3.78.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (150.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $58.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,419 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.83 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.46 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 43% of the median yield. 16.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 71.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3%.

$256

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2014 (13 surveys on record since 2009); up 2.0% from $251 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Dixon County runs $256 an acre for 2026, 6th highest of 80 in Nebraska and well over the state median of $141. That is up 2.0% from $251 in 2016, so rent has barely moved in ten years. Irrigated ground rents for $343 in 2026, 3rd highest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $5,376 an acre in 2022, up 15.0% from 2017, and corn made 205.5 bu in 2025 against a median of 179.3 bu.
