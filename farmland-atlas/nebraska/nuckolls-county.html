Cash rent in Nuckolls County runs $148 an acre in 2026, up 9.6% from $135 in 2016 and 38th highest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $296 an acre in 2026, 23rd highest of 78. The census value of land and buildings reached $5,127 an acre in 2022, up 42.9% from $3,588 in 2017. Corn made 181.2 bu in 2024, above the 166.5 bu median since 2008.
