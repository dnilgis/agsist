Otoe County, Nebraska

Otoe County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $186 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,550 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31131. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Otoe County runs $186 per acre, up 6.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated ground rented for $241 per acre in 2022, but irrigation covers only 3 percent of harvested cropland, with 63 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought drove 66 percent of the $127.7 million in crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and that cause's share rose from 13 percent in 1989-1999 to 70 percent in 2000-2009 to 63 percent in 2010-2019 to 78 percent in 2020-2025. County corn yield trended up 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, though 2012 dropped to 81.3 against a median of 157.9. Census land value stood at $5,550 per acre in 2022, up 12 percent from 2017.
