Cash rent in Otoe County runs $186 an acre for 2026, up 6.3% from $175 in 2016. That puts it 28th highest of 80 counties in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $5,550 an acre in 2022, up 12.0% from $4,957 in 2017. Corn made 174.7 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 157.9 bu since 2008.
