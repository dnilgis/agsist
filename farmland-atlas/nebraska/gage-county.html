Gage County, Nebraska

Rain is at 110% of normal. Land and buildings run $5,474 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.08 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 32.83 inches, 110% of normal (29.76 inches) through Nov 17, at Beatrice, 1 mi N. Frost-free season: 183 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,122 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,474 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $176 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 12.8% from 2016 to 2026. 51.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 16.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 62.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,040 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 42 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 10 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.21 at E Energy Adams, LLC, 16 miles. 27 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 72 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 8 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.08 per base acre, PLC $3.25.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (129.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2,439 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.47 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.44 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 59% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 68.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.4%.
