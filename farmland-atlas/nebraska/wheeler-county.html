Wheeler County, Nebraska

Wheeler County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $88 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,116 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 185.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31183. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wheeler County runs $88 per acre in 2026, down 11.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $262 per acre, and that gap has moved without a clear direction, from 3.80 in 2013 to 2.99 in 2026. Median corn yield 2008-2024 is 185.2 bushels per acre, with the worst year, 2008, at 151. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $40.4 million, led by excess moisture and flood at 43 percent, while heat and drought's share has moved from 7 percent in 1989-1999 to 12 percent in 2000-2009, 11 percent in 2010-2019, and 16 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $2,116 per acre, up 38.5 percent from 2017 to 2022.
