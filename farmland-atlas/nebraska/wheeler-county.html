Wheeler County, Nebraska

Cash rent on Wheeler County cropland ran $88 an acre in 2026, down 11.6% from $99 in 2016 and tied for 22nd lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rented for $262 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 38.5% from $1,528 in 2017 to $2,116 in 2022. Corn made 226.1 bu in 2024, well over the 185.2 bu median since 2008.
