Adams County, Nebraska

Adams County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $144 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,544 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31001. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Adams County is $144 per acre in 2026, down 3.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent is $307 per acre, and 73 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 98 percent of its water from groundwater. Corn yields show a trend of 2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, with a median of 195.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 181 in 2008. Crop insurance indemnities total $133.2 million across 1989-2025, with hail as the largest cause at 44 percent; the loss ratio for the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 is 0.74. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 288 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2023 the worst year at 52 weeks.
