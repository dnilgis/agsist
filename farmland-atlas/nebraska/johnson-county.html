Johnson County, Nebraska

Johnson County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $184 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,389 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31097. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Johnson County stands at $184 per acre for 2026, up 21.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $298 per acre in 2025, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.78 in 2010 to 1.54 in 2025, direction falling. Only 12 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 94 percent of that water draws from groundwater, with 1,012 registered wells at a median depth of 179.5 feet. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $53.9 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 64 percent; that share ran 20 percent in 1989-1999, 67 percent in 2000-2009, 62 percent in 2010-2019, and 74 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $4,389 per acre for 2022, up 20.9 percent from 2017.
