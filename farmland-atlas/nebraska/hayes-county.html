$62

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 11.8% from $55 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Hayes County was $62 an acre in 2026, up 11.8% from $55 in 2016, which is 18th lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $2,196 an acre in 2022, up 55.2% from $1,415 in 2017, and that is tied for 27th lowest of 93. Corn made 146.0 bu in 2025, close to the 18 year median of 147.7 bu.
