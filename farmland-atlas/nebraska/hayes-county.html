Hayes County, Nebraska

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Southwest district (9 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $1,745 an acre; high grade $2,230, low grade $1,195.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

52.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 19.28 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 95% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,860 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.80 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.75, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 53.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.2%.
