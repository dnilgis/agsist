Cedar County, Nebraska

Cedar County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $247 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,435 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 183.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31027. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cedar County averages $247 per acre in 2026, up 2.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $352 per acre, a multiple of 1.43 over non-irrigated in 2026, close to the 1.47 multiple of 2008, with no clear direction in between. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, at 73 percent of $188.0 million paid, and its share has climbed from 15 percent in 1989-1999 to 64 percent in 2000-2009 to 77 percent in 2010-2019 and 77 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 215 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 107 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Land value per the 2022 census sits at $6,435 per acre, up 24.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
