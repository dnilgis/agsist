USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Cedar County was $247 an acre in 2026, 11th highest of 80 in Nebraska and well over the state median of $141. That is up 2.1% from $242 in 2016, which is not much of a move. Irrigated rent ran $352 in 2026, the highest of 78 in the state. The census value of land and buildings was $6,435 an acre in 2022, up 24.2% from $5,182 in 2017.
