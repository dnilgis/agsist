As of Oct 16, cash rent in Washington County runs $229 an acre for 2026, up 5.0% from $218 in 2016 and 12th highest of 80 in Nebraska, where the median is $141. The census value of land and buildings was $7,365 an acre in 2022, up 13.0% from $6,519 in 2017. Corn made 176.6 bu in 2024, a bit above the 17 year median of 173.1 bu.
