Washington County, Nebraska

Washington County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $229 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,365 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31177. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County is $229 per acre in 2026, up 5 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $265 per acre. The irrigated multiple over non-irrigated is 1.16 in 2026, down from 1.27 in 2011, and the direction is falling. Census value of land and buildings stands at $7,365 per acre, up 13 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.5 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $104.7 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 50 percent, and that cause's share has moved from 22 percent in 1989-1999, to 42 percent in 2000-2009, 48 percent in 2010-2019, and 60 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 192 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 80 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
