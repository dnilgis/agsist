Non-irrigated cash rent in Sarpy County averages $223 per acre in 2026, up 6.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $295 per acre, a multiple of 1.32 over non-irrigated, close to the 1.28 multiple seen in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Corn yields have trended up 2.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 186.2 bushels per acre against a worst year of 110.1 in 2012. Census land value sits at $7,646 per acre, up 2.8 percent from 2017 to 2022.
