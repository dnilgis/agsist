As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Sarpy County runs $223 an acre for 2026, up 6.2% from $210 in 2016. That sits tied for 16th highest of 80 in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,646 an acre in 2022, 6th highest of 93, up 2.8% from $7,439 in 2017. Corn made 197.7 bu in 2025, and 65% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
