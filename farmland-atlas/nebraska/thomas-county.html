Groundwater supplies 92 percent of irrigation water in Thomas County, drawn from 749 registered wells, with 50 irrigation wells showing no decommission date on record. Median well depth is 220.5 feet, and median static water level when the wells were drilled was 33.5 feet. Land value rose 36.5 percent from 2017-2022, 6.4 percent a year, against a census value of $1,065 per acre in 2022. Drought was present in 348 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 25.6 percent of weeks, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 103 such weeks. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2020 totaled $267,532, with hail the largest cause at 72 percent, and heat and drought at 32 percent of indemnities from 2010-2019.
