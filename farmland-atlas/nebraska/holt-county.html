USDA NASS county yields, 2023

Cash rent in Holt County runs $90 an acre in 2026, up 51.7% from $59 in 2016, and that sits tied for 24th lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $274 in 2026, close to the state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,650 an acre in 2022, up 11.3% from $2,380 in 2017. Corn made 223.7 bu in 2023, above the 2019-23 average of 208.2 bu.
