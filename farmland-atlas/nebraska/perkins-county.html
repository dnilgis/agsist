Cash rent in Perkins County runs $54 an acre for 2026, up 10.2% from $49 in 2016 and 14th lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $212 in 2026, 19th lowest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $2,196 an acre in 2022, up 2.6% from 2017 and tied for 27th lowest of 93. Renters farm 53% of the ground here.
