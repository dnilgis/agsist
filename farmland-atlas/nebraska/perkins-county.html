Perkins County, Nebraska

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Southwest district (9 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $1,745 an acre; high grade $2,230, low grade $1,195.

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.73 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.99, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 82% of the median yield. 42.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.5%.
