Clay County, Nebraska

Clay County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $157 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,744 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31035. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clay County runs $157 per acre in 2026, up 2.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents higher at $320 per acre, a multiple of 2.04 over non-irrigated, against 1.85 in 2008, with no clear direction in that multiple. Irrigation is the base of this county: 79 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 95 percent of its water from groundwater, with 3,939 registered wells at a median depth of 190 feet. Crop insurance indemnities since 1989 total $108.4 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 40 percent, and that share ran 8 percent in 1989-1999, 61 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, and 50 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $6,744 per acre, up 39.1 percent from 2017 to 2022.
