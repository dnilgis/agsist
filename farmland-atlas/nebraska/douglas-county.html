Douglas County, Nebraska

Douglas County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $248 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,113 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 180.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Douglas County is $248 per acre in 2026, up 25.3 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $9,113 per acre, up 19.2 percent from 2017. The county saw 194 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 72 of those weeks in 2021-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity is 0 MW.
