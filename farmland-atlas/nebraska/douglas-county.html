USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Douglas County cropland rents for $248 an acre in 2026, up 25.3% from $198 in 2016 and 10th highest of 80 in Nebraska. The census value of land and buildings was $9,113 an acre in 2022, highest of 93 in the state and up 19.2% from 2017. Corn came in at 179.0 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 179.9 bu. Renters farm 46% of the ground.
