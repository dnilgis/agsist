Stanton County cash rent stood at $252 an acre in 2026, tied for 7th highest of 80 in Nebraska and up 16.7% from $216 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,551 an acre in 2022, up 51.3% from $4,991 in 2017 and 9th highest of 93. Corn made 216.1 bu in 2025, well above the county median of 180.6 bu since 2008.
