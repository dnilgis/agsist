Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.72 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Quick answers for Sheridan County

What is cash rent in Sheridan County, Nebraska?

The county average is $36 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $162 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sheridan County, Nebraska?

Land and buildings were worth $1,394 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Sheridan County, Nebraska?

The county's 2008-2025 median is 151.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
