Sheridan County, Nebraska

Sheridan County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,394 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31161. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sheridan County is $36 per acre in 2026, up 38.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $162 per acre, and 28 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 94 percent of its water from groundwater. Corn yield has trended down 0.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 151.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 124.7 in 2024. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 1 percent in 1989-1999, 24 percent in 2000-2009, 16 percent in 2010-2019, and 46 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value was $1,394 per acre in 2022, up 43.7 percent from 2017.
