Sheridan County cash rent sits at $36 an acre in 2026, up 38.5% from $26 in 2016, and that is the 5th lowest of 80 against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,394 an acre in 2022, up 43.7% from $970 in 2017, still well under the state median of $4,499. Corn made 169.3 bu in 2025, above the county median of 151.4 bu.
