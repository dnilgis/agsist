Quick answers for Keya Paha County

What is cash rent in Keya Paha County, Nebraska?

The county average is $43 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $193 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Keya Paha County, Nebraska?

Land and buildings were worth $1,790 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Keya Paha County, Nebraska?

The county's 2021-25 average is 192.5 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
