Cash rent in Brown County runs $62 an acre for 2025, up 148.0% from $25 in 2014. Irrigated ground rents for $186 in 2026, against a state median of $272, which puts it 10th lowest of 78. The census value of land and buildings was $1,674 an acre in 2022, up 42.8% from $1,172 in 2017 and 18th lowest of 93 in the state.
