Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 156 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.52, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 82% of the median yield. 43.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 14.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.7%.
