Cash rent in Thurston County runs $269 an acre for 2026, tied for 2nd highest of 80 in Nebraska and well over the state median of $141. That is up 11.2% from $242 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,255 an acre in 2022, up 50.3% from $4,827 in 2017, and 17th highest of 93 in the state. Corn has held a median of 177.7 bu an acre, and 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
