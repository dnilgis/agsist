Quick answers for Thurston County

What is cash rent in Thurston County, Nebraska?

The county average is $269 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $345 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Thurston County, Nebraska?

Land and buildings were worth $7,255 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
