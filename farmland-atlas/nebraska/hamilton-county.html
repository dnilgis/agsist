USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Hamilton County ran $174 an acre in 2026, up 15.2% from $151 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,963 an acre in 2022, up 41.3% from $6,341 in 2017, and that sits 2nd highest of 93 in Nebraska. Corn made 233.0 bu in 2025, ahead of the 2021 to 2025 average of 221.5 bu. In 2022, 64% of the farmland here was rented.
