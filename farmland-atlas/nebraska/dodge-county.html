Dodge County, Nebraska

Rain is at 108% of normal. East district (16 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $8,320 an acre; high grade $10,375, low grade $6,255. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.08 per base acre for corn.

Dodge County vs Nebraska: $225 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 82% of the other 79 Nebraska counties with a 2026 rent (15th highest of 80).

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.85 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.45, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 38.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29%.
