Cash rent in Blaine County was $39 an acre in 2023, down 9.3% from $43 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $1,317 an acre in 2022, up 32.6% from $993 in 2017, or 5.8% a year. That sits well under the state median of $4,499 and ranks 10th lowest of 93. Pasture rent was $22 an acre in 2026, and 42% of farmland was rented in 2022.
