Blaine County, Nebraska

Blaine County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2023); land and buildings $1,317 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Blaine County ran $187 per acre in 2025. Non-irrigated rent stood at $39 per acre in 2023, down 9.3 percent from 2013. Irrigation water comes 92 percent from groundwater, and among 725 registered wells, 159 irrigation wells remain, with median depth 300 feet and median static water level 16 feet. Drought was present in 313 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, and 114 weeks from 2021-2025, with 2022 the worst year at 40 weeks.
