Colfax County cash rent ran $249 an acre in 2026, up 20.3% from $207 in 2016 and 9th highest of 80 in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,261 an acre in 2022, up 33.4% from $6,194 in 2017, and that ranks 3rd highest of 93. Corn made 209.1 bu in 2025, well above the 18 year county median of 186.3 bu.
