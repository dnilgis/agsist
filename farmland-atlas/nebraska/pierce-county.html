Pierce County, Nebraska

Pierce County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $197 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,080 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31139. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pierce County runs $197 per acre in 2026, up 2.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $291 per acre, and the irrigated multiple sits at 1.48 in 2026 against 1.65 in 2008, with no clear direction over that span. Water is present here: 49 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, all of it from groundwater, drawn from 3,021 registered wells at a median depth of 208 feet. The county spent 44 weeks in severe drought or worse in 2022, the worst year in the 2000-2025 record.
