Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 2% from $193 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, Pierce County cash rent sits at $197 an acre for 2026, 25th highest of 80 in Nebraska and well over the state median of $141. That is up 2.1% from $193 in 2016, which is not much movement for a decade. The census value of land and buildings ran $7,080 an acre in 2022, up 45.3% from $4,872 in 2017 and 20th highest of 93 in the state.
