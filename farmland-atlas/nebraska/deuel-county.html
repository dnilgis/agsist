USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Deuel County runs $45 an acre for 2026, 10th lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $172 an acre in 2026, 5th lowest of 78. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,003 an acre in 2022, up 53.5% from $1,305 in 2017. Corn made 114.0 bu in 2024, and 59% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
