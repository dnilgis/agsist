Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.96 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.95, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 9.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).
