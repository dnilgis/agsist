Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the highest of 15 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, Keith County cash rent on non irrigated cropland runs $56 an acre for 2026, up 28.7% from $44 in 2016 and 15th lowest of 80 in Nebraska against a state median of $141. Irrigated ground rents for $218 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,431 an acre in 2022, up 49.5% from $1,626 in 2017. Corn made 181.5 bu in 2025, well over the 158.0 bu median since 2008.
