Banner County, Nebraska

Banner County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,242 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated rent rose 10.9 percent from 2014 to 2026, and land value rose 42.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nightly lows have warmed 0.42 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 58.1 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 57 F. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 125 from 2021-2025, out of 413 of 1357 weeks counted from 2000-2025.
