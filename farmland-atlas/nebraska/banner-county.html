Banner County, Nebraska

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Northwest district (11 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $890 an acre; high grade $1,120, low grade $665.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 12.17 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 72% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 147 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,427 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.97, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3.8%.
