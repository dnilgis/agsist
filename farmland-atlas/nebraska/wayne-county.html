Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.58 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.83, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. 34.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 84.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.4%.

County corn made 191.4 bu in 2024, above the 186.6 bu median going back to 2008.
