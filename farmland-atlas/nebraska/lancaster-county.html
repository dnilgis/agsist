Lancaster County, Nebraska

Lancaster County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $190 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,481 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in 2026 runs $190 per acre, up 16.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $274 per acre, a multiple of 1.44 over non-irrigated, down from 1.63 in 2011, with no clear direction over that span. Most cropland here is dryland: only 7 percent of harvested acres carry irrigation, and 86 percent of that water comes from groundwater, drawn from wells with a median static water level of 66 feet at drilling.
