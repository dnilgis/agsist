Lancaster County, Nebraska

Rain is at 126% of normal. East district (16 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $8,320 an acre; high grade $10,375, low grade $6,255. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.05 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.44 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 126% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 182 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,091 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.73, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 52% of the median yield. 6.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.4 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 72.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.8%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Lancaster County was $190 an acre in 2026, up 16.6% from $163 in 2016 and 26th highest of 80 in Nebraska, where the state median is $141. The census value of land and buildings was $6,481 an acre in 2022, up 15.8% from $5,598 in 2017. Corn made 164.5 bu in 2024, a little over the 156.8 bu median since 2008. In 2022, 47% of the farmland was rented.
