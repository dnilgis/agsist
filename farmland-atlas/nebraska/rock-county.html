Corn yield, all practices, 2023: the highest of 10 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2023

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Rock County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 33.8% from $36 in 2016. That rent ranks 11th lowest of 80 in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $1,482 an acre in 2022, up 30.2% from $1,138 in 2017. Corn made 232.3 bu in 2023, well above the county's 187.9 bu median.
