Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.68 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.47, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. 19.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 38.2%.
