Boone County, Nebraska

Boone County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $204 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,452 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 187.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Boone County averages $204 per acre in 2026, up 17.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $295 per acre. Irrigation covers 55 percent of harvested cropland, with 99 percent of that water drawn from groundwater, and 3,108 wells are registered, 1,836 with no decommission date on record. Heat and drought has driven a rising share of crop insurance indemnities across each listed period: 26 percent in 1989-1999, 45 percent in 2000-2009, 53 percent in 2010-2019, 59 percent in 2020-2025, against total indemnities of $108.1 million since 1989. The county spent 212 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000, including 40 weeks in the worst year, 2013, and land value rose 45 percent from 2017 to 2022.
