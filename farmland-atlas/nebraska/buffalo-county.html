Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 26.07 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 105% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.21 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.48, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. 73.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.7%.
