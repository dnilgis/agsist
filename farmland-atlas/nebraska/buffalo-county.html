Buffalo County, Nebraska

Buffalo County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $138 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,857 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Buffalo County stands at $138 per acre in 2026, up 36.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $271 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved without clear direction, 2.08 in 2008 and 1.96 in 2026. Irrigation covers 73 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 98 percent of its water from groundwater, and the county carries 8,974 registered wells at a median depth of 212.5 feet. Corn yields have trended up 1.4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 195.4 bushels per acre and a low of 165.8 in 2022. Hail, not heat, is the largest driver of the $180.2 million in indemnities paid across 1989-2025, at 43 percent of the total.
