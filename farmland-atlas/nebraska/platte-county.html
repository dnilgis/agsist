Platte County, Nebraska

Platte County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $258 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,054 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Platte County runs $258 per acre for 2026, up 27.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $316 per acre, a multiple of 1.22 in 2026 against 1.37 in 2008, with no clear direction over that span. Irrigation covers 56 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, drawing 98 percent of its water from groundwater, and the county carries 6,453 registered wells at a median depth of 154 feet. Corn yields have trended up 1.4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2024, with a median of 195.4 bushels per acre and a low of 154.3 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 49 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and weeks in severe drought or worse reached 101 of the 2021-2025 span.
