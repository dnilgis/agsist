Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 27.1% from $203 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Platte County runs $258 an acre for 2026, 5th highest of 80 in Nebraska and well over the state median of $141. That rent is up 27.1% from $203 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,054 an acre in 2022, up 11.5% from $6,328 in 2017. Corn made 196.4 bu in 2024, close to the 2020-24 average of 198.6 bu.
