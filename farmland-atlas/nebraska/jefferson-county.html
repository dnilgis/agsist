Cash rent in Jefferson County runs $159 an acre for 2026, up 19.5% from $133 in 2016 and above the state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $5,724 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $4,151 in 2017, and that ranks 34th highest of 93 in Nebraska. Corn made 174.5 bu in 2025, better than the county median of 164.9 bu over 17 years. In 2022, 52% of the farmland here was rented.
