Irrigated cropland in Fillmore County rents for $314 an acre in 2026, 12th highest of 78 in Nebraska, against a state median of $272. Rent on cropland overall went from $133 in 2016 to $174 in 2026, up 30.8%. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,359 an acre in 2022, up 35.1% from $5,448 in 2017, and that ranks 16th highest of 93.
