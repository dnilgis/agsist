Merrick County, Nebraska

Merrick County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $121 an acre (2022); land and buildings $5,476 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 185.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31121. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Merrick County irrigates 82 percent of its harvested cropland, and all of that water comes from groundwater. There are 8087 registered wells, 5495 of them irrigation wells still active, with a median depth of 48 feet and a median static water level of 10 feet. Irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $272 per acre, against a non-irrigated cash rent of $121 per acre in 2022. Excess moisture and flood remains the largest indemnity cause overall, at 39 percent.
