Cass County, Nebraska

Rain is at 112% of normal. East district (16 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $8,320 an acre; high grade $10,375, low grade $6,255. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.90 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.77 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 112% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 184 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,094 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.39, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 48% of the median yield. 0.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 66.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.6%.
