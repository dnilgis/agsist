Cash rent in Cass County runs $226 an acre for 2026, up 12.4% from $201 in 2016. That is tied for 13th highest of 80 in Nebraska, against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings was $6,946 an acre in 2022, up 23.9% from $5,607 in 2017 and 22nd highest of 93. Corn made 196.7 bu in 2025, well over the 176.6 bu county median going back to 2008.
