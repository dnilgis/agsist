Scotts Bluff County, Nebraska

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Northwest district (11 counties), UNL survey 2026: dryland without irrigation potential $890 an acre; high grade $1,120, low grade $665.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 11.70 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 150 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,561 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 218.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.29 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.80, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 87% of the median yield. 82.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 9.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.7%.
