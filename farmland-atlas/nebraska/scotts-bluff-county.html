Irrigated ground in Scotts Bluff County rented for $180 an acre in 2026, 9th lowest of 78 in Nebraska against a state median of $272. The census value of land and buildings was $2,573 an acre in 2022, up 26.3% from $2,038 in 2017. Corn made 160.5 bu in 2024, close to the county median of 161.6 bu going back to 2008.
