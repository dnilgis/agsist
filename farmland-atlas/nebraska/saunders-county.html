Cash rent in Saunders County runs $223 an acre for 2026, up 18.0% from $189 in 2016 and tied for 16th highest of 80 in Nebraska. The census value of land and buildings was $7,483 an acre in 2022, up 32.7% from $5,641 in 2017 and 14th highest of 93 against a state median of $4,499. Corn made 192.5 bu in 2025, above the 182.9 bu median since 2008, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
