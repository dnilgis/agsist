Cherry County, Nebraska

Cherry County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2022); land and buildings $1,224 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 180.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31031. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cherry County is $36 per acre in 2022, up 75.6 percent from 2012. Irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $176 per acre. Only 18 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 93 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater, drawn from 5,768 registered wells with a median depth of 300 feet. Census land value sits at $1,224 per acre, up 31.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.6 percent a year. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 1 percent in 1989-1999, to 12 percent in 2000-2009, to 19 percent in 2010-2019, to 24 percent in 2020-2025.
