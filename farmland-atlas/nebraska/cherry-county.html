U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Cherry County was $36 an acre in 2022, up 75.6% from $21 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings was $1,224 an acre in 2022, up 31.2% from $933 in 2017, and it ranks 6th lowest of 93 against a state median of $4,499. Irrigated rent ran $176 an acre in 2026, 6th lowest of 78, and pasture went for $18 an acre that year.
