Butler County, Nebraska

Butler County, NE: non-irrigated cash rent $226 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,462 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 186.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 31023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Butler County runs $226 per acre in 2026, up 18.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $324 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated land is 1.43 in 2026, against 1.36 in 2008, with no clear direction in between. Corn yields carry a trend of 1.7 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 186.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 141.2 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 62 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share ran 18 percent in 1989-1999, 71 percent in 2000-2009, 56 percent in 2010-2019, and 66 percent in 2020-2025. Land value is $6,462 per acre by the 2022 census estimate, up 13.5 percent from 2017 to 2022.
