USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Nance County runs $204 an acre for 2026, up 39.7% from $146 in 2016 and tied for 23rd highest of 80 in Nebraska. The census value of land and buildings was $4,829 an acre in 2022, up 14.2% from $4,227 in 2017, or 2.7% a year. Corn made 191.8 bu in 2025, above the county median of 178.1 bu. 48% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
