As of Oct 16, cash rent in Nemaha County runs $213 an acre for 2026, up 4.9% from $203 in 2016, and that sits 20th highest of 80 against a state median of $141. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,778 an acre in 2022, up 49.0% from $4,548 in 2017. Corn made 186.7 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 185.7 bu and well over the long median of 169.3 bu.
