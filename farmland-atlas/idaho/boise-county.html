The census value of land and buildings in Boise County was $2,053 an acre in 2022, up 43.3% from $1,433 in 2017. That works out to 7.5% a year, after barely moving from $1,419 in 2012. Even so, it sits well under the Idaho median of $3,689 and ranks 2nd lowest of 42. In 2022, 36% of the county's farmland was rented.
