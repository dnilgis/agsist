Boise County, Idaho

Boise County, ID: land and buildings $2,053 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Land value here sits at $2,053 per acre as of the 2022 census estimate, up 43.3 percent from 2017, a 7.5 percent a year pace. July nights average 52.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 50.3 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.91 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched this county in 183 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2001 the worst year at 42 weeks, and 46 weeks landed in 2021-2025 alone.
