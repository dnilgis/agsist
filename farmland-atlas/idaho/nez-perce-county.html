Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 15.6% from $77 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Nez Perce County ran $89 an acre in 2026, the highest of 18 in Idaho, against a state median of $52. That is up 15.6% from $77 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,156 an acre in 2022, up 51.8% from $2,079 in 2017, and that still sits 14th lowest of 42 in the state.
