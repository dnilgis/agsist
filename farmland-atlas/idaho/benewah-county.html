Non-irrigated cash rent in Benewah County averages $80 per acre in 2026, up 15.9 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census sits at $2,747 per acre, up 41.9 percent from 2017, 7.2 percent a year. Heat and drought has driven the largest share of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 55 percent, and its share has moved from 14 percent in 1989-1999 to 26 percent in 2000-2009, to 37 percent in 2010-2019, to 81 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 50.2 F to 51.6 F over 2017-2026, continuing a trend of 0.93 F per decade since 1976. No irrigation, solar, or wind capacity is present in this county.
