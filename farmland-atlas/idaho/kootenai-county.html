As of Oct 16, cash rent in Kootenai County runs $65 an acre for 2025, up 225.0% from $20 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $11,050 an acre in 2022, up 100.0% from $5,525 in 2017, and 3rd highest of 42 in Idaho against a state median of $3,689. Irrigated rent sits at $70 for 2026, tied for 2nd lowest of 33. Renters work 31% of the farmland here.
