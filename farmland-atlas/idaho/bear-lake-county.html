Cash rent in Bear Lake County runs $35 an acre for 2026, up 66.7% from $21 in 2016, against a state median of $52. That puts it 7th lowest of 18. Irrigated ground rents for $79 in 2026, 5th lowest of 33. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,768 an acre in 2022, up 92.4% from $1,439 in 2017, still under the state median of $3,689.
