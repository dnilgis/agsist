Bannock County carries a 2026 cash rent of $23 an acre, the lowest of 18 in Idaho against a state median of $52. Irrigated ground rents for $167 in 2026, 15th lowest of 33. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,753 an acre in 2022, up 41.0% from $1,953 in 2017, or 7.1% a year. Only 12% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
