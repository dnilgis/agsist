Clark County, Idaho

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,102 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

63.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 104 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,724 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,102 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.11 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Aug 28, 2026 to Aug 31, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 16% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 57.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.09 per base acre, PLC $13.49. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (74.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 24, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $17.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 74 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.67 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.97, 2016 to 2025. 83.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 2.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14%.

Quick answers for Clark County

What is cash rent in Clark County, Idaho?

The county average is $138 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Clark County, Idaho?

Land and buildings were worth $3,102 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
