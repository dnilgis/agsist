As of Oct 16, irrigated cash rent in Clark County ran $138 an acre in 2026, 11th lowest of 33 in Idaho, against a state median of $178. The census value of land and buildings was $3,102 an acre in 2022, up 76.8% from $1,755 in 2017, or 12.1% a year. That still sits 13th lowest of 42 against a state median of $3,689. 18% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
