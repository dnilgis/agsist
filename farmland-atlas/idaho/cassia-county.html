Cassia County, Idaho

Cassia County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $35 an acre (2024); land and buildings $4,486 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16031. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland dominates here: 91 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigated cash rent for 2026 runs $405 per acre against $35 per acre for non-irrigated ground. Groundwater supplies 59 percent of irrigation water as of 2015. July nights have warmed, averaging 56.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 54.6 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.94 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been present but not constant: 383 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2004 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 101 weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Freeze and frost, not heat, has been the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 37 percent of $59.4 million paid.
