Irrigated cropland in Jefferson County rents for $168 an acre in 2026, 16th lowest of 33 against a state median of $178. The census value of land and buildings ran $5,087 an acre in 2022, up 44.4% from $3,522 in 2017, or 7.6% a year. That puts it 14th highest of 42, above the $3,689 state median. 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
