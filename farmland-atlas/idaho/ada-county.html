Ada County, Idaho

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $13,084 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.11 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

68.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 9.69 inches, 103% of normal (9.41 inches) through Nov 17, at Boise Air Terminal. Frost-free season: 180 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,718 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $13,084 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 40.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 515.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 525 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,886 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 638 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.11 per base acre, PLC $3.63.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (144.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $16.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.26 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.87 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. 95.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 30.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.4%.
