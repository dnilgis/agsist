Bonner County, Idaho

Bonner County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $53 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,142 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bonner County averages $53 per acre in 2026, up 45.2 percent from 2014. Land and buildings carry a census value of $6,142 per acre, up 22 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.1 percent a year. Heat and drought caused 46 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2010-2019 and 67 percent in 2020-2025, out of $477,316 in indemnities from 2014-2025. The county logged 71 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 176 weeks total from 2000-2025, with 2001 the worst single year at 42 weeks. July nightly lows average 52 F for 2017-2026, above the 50.2 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long-term trend runs 0.78 F per decade, warming.
