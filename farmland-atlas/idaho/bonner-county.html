Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.01 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 74% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 151 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,722 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,142 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $53 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 9% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 45.2% from 2014 to 2026. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 39 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,582 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $29.10 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

4.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 2014 to 2025; too much rain caused 19.7%.

Quick answers for Bonner County

What is cash rent in Bonner County, Idaho?

The county average is $53 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bonner County, Idaho?

Land and buildings were worth $6,142 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
