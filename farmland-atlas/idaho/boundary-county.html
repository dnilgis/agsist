Cropland rent in Boundary County runs $81 an acre for 2026, up 14.9% from $71 in 2016 and 3rd highest of 18 in Idaho against a state median of $52. The census value of land and buildings was $5,691 an acre in 2022, up 19.0% from $4,784 in 2017, or 3.5% a year. That value ranks 10th highest of 42 in the state. 38% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
