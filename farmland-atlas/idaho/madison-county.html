Madison County, Idaho

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,444 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 11.82 inches, 111% of normal (10.63 inches) through Nov 17, at Rexburg Madison County Airport. Frost-free season: 133 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,954 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,444 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 16.1% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 48.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $17.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 738 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.51 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.14 over the same years. 64.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.1%.
