Irrigated cash rent in Gem County runs $143 an acre for 2026. That is 13th lowest of 33 in Idaho and under the state median of $178. The census value of land and buildings was $4,014 an acre in 2022, up 42.6% from $2,815 in 2017, which had barely moved from $2,761 in 2012. Corn made 221.8 bu in 2025 and averaged 223.1 bu from 2021 through 2025.
