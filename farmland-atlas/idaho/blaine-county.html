Cropland in Blaine County rents for $32 an acre in 2026, tied for 4th lowest of 18 in Idaho against a state median of $52. Irrigated ground runs $120 an acre in 2026, 9th lowest of 33. The census value of land and buildings was $3,556 an acre in 2022, up 40.6% from $2,530 in 2017, which works out to 7.0% a year. 29% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
