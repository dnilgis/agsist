Irrigated cash rent in Gooding County runs $425 an acre for 2026, highest of 33 in Idaho and well over the state median of $178. The census value of land and buildings was $7,101 an acre in 2022, up 18.0% from $6,016 in 2017 and 5th highest of 42. Corn made 196.1 bu in 2025, a little under the recent average of 202.8 bu. 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
