Dryland cash rent in Power County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 18.5% from $27 in 2016 and tied for 4th lowest of 18 against a state median of $52. Irrigated ground is another matter at $290 an acre in 2026, 8th highest of 33. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,930 an acre in 2022, up 59.3% from $2,467 in 2017, or 9.8% a year. 37% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
