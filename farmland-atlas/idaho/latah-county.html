Latah County, Idaho

Latah County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $80 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,464 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.19 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16057. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Latah County runs $80 per acre, up 6.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent stood at $165 per acre in 2020. Heat and drought has been the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 56 percent of $107.8 million paid, and its share by period moved 33 percent, 23 percent, 39 percent, then 75 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.33, above the 1989-2025 ratio of 1.19. July nightly lows averaged 50.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 49.5 F, with a warming trend of 0.67 F per decade since 1976.
