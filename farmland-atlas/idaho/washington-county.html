Washington County, Idaho

Washington County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2023); land and buildings $2,299 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16087. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation carries this county. Ninety-six percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigated cash rent in 2026 runs $221 per acre against $30 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2023. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 11 percent, 14 percent, 34 percent, 96 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.68, above the 1.07 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record. July nightly lows have warmed 1.19 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 59.6 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 57 F.
