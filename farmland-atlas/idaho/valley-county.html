Irrigated cropland in Valley County rents for $106 an acre in 2026, tied for 7th lowest of 33 against a state median of $178. Pasture goes for $21 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,900 an acre in 2022, up 50.3% from $2,594 in 2017, or 8.5% a year. That sits a bit above the state median of $3,689.

Valley County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
