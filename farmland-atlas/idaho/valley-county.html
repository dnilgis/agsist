Valley County, Idaho

Valley County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2013); land and buildings $3,900 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16085. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Valley County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Irrigated cash rent in 2026 is $106 per acre. Non-irrigated cash rent in 2013 was $18 per acre. Irrigated cropland covers 81 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census, with 1 percent of irrigation water from groundwater in 2015. Land value rose 50.3 percent from 2017-2022, or 8.5 percent a year, against a census value of $3,900 per acre. July nights have warmed, with the 2017-2026 average low at 45.6 F against the 1991-2020 normal of 43.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.87 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance losses from 1995-2023 total $180,933, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 62 percent.
