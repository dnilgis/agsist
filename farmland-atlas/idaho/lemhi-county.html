Lemhi County, Idaho

Lemhi County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2023); land and buildings $3,947 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16059. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Harvested cropland is 95 percent irrigated, 2022 census, with only 2 percent of irrigation water from groundwater in 2015, so surface supply carries this county. Land value was $3,947 per acre in 2022, up 56.3 percent from 2017-2022, 9.3 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 48.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 46.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.81 F per decade, warming. Drought held severe or worse for 87 weeks from 2021-2025, against 322 of 1357 weeks, 23.7 percent, for 2000-2025.
