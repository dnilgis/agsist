Lemhi County, Idaho

Lemhi County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2023); land and buildings $3,947 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Irrigated ground in Lemhi County rents for $74 an acre in 2026, 4th lowest of 33 in Idaho against a state median of $178. Pasture runs $25 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,947 an acre in 2022, up 56.3% from $2,526 in 2017 and 19th highest of 42 in the state. Only 18% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
