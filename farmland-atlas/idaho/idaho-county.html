Idaho County, Idaho

Idaho County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,367 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.15 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16049. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Idaho County runs $70 per acre in 2026, up 11.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent was $86 per acre in 2020, but irrigation covers only 1 percent of harvested cropland, so most land here is dryland and priced that way. Census land and buildings value stood at $2,367 per acre in 2022, up 44 percent from 2017, a 7.6 percent a year pace. Heat and drought has taken a growing share of crop insurance indemnities, from 27 percent in 1989-1999 to 33 percent in 2000-2009, 35 percent in 2010-2019, and 52 percent in 2020-2025, against $93.5 million in total indemnities across 1989-2025. July nightly lows have warmed from 46.5 F normal to 47.8 F over 2017-2026.
