Custer County, Idaho

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.51 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 83 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,462 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,588 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.06 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 20% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 196.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $17.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

98.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.1%.

Irrigated cropland in Custer County rents for $90 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $178. That puts it 6th lowest of 33 Idaho counties. Quick answers for Custer County

What is cash rent in Custer County, Idaho?

The county average is $90 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Custer County, Idaho?

Land and buildings were worth $3,588 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
