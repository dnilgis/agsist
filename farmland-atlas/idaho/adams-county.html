Adams County, Idaho

Adams County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2022); land and buildings $2,752 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Adams County ran $30 an acre in 2022, up 66.7% from $18 in 2012. Irrigated ground rented for $70 an acre in 2026, tied for 2nd lowest of 33 counties against a state median of $178. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,752 an acre in 2022, up 77.5% from $1,550 in 2017, and that ranks 9th lowest of 42 in Idaho.
