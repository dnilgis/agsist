Shoshone County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Shoshone County stands at $8,542 per acre as of the 2022 Census, with a change of 27.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.9 percent a year. July nights average 51.5 F for 2017-2026, up from a 49.9 F normal over 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.78 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought was present in 246 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2021-2025 holding 110 such weeks and 2001 the worst single year at 42 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
