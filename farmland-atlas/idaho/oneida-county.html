As of Oct 16, cash rent in Oneida County was $26 an acre in 2026, 2nd lowest of 18 against a state median of $52. That rent is down 31.6% from $38 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 58.3% from 2017 to $2,625 an acre in 2022, still 7th lowest of 42 in Idaho. Irrigated ground rented for $127 an acre in 2026 and pasture for $10.
