Irrigated cropland in Payette County rents for $184 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $178, which puts it 15th highest of 33. The census value of land and buildings was $5,191 an acre in 2022, up 48.1% from $3,505 in 2017. Corn made 204.1 bu in 2024, close to its median of 205.0 bu since 2008. In 2022, 49% of the farmland here was rented.
