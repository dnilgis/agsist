Cash rent on Fremont County cropland runs $58 an acre for 2026, up 22.1% from $48 in 2016 and above the state median of $52. Irrigated ground rents for $180 an acre in 2026, ranked 16th highest of 33. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,813 an acre in 2022, up 73.9% from $2,767 in 2017. That is 11.7% a year and ranks 16th highest of 42.
