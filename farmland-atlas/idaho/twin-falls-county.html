Irrigated cash rent in Twin Falls County runs $319 an acre for 2026, 6th highest of 33 in Idaho against a state median of $178. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,129 an acre in 2022, up 38.1% from $4,439 in 2017, or 6.7% a year. That sits 8th highest of 42, well over the state median of $3,689. 29% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
