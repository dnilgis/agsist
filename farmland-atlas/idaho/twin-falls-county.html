Twin Falls County, Idaho

Twin Falls County, ID: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2014); land and buildings $6,129 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 16083. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in 2026 stands at $319 per acre, and irrigation covers 99 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Groundwater supplies 25 percent of irrigation water as of 2015, so most water comes from surface sources. Irrigation failure is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across closed crop years, at 42 percent of $56.0 million paid, with $23.4 million tied to that cause alone. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved differently across periods: 26 percent in 1989-1999, 32 percent in 2000-2009, 14 percent in 2010-2019, and 6 percent in 2020-2025. Land value rose 38.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.9 F per decade since 1976.
