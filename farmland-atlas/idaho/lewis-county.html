As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Lewis County runs $88 an acre for 2026, up 13.6% from $77 in 2016. That rent is 2nd highest of 18 in Idaho, well over the $52 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $2,819 an acre in 2022, up 43.5% from $1,964 in 2017, and it sits 12th lowest of 42 against a $3,689 state median. 50% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
