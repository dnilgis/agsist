Irrigated cash rent in Butte County ran $178 an acre in 2026, right on the state median and 17th lowest of 33. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,430 an acre in 2022, up 67.1% from $2,053 in 2017, or 10.8% a year. That still sits under the state median of $3,689. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
