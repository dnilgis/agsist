Greene County, New York

Rain is at 103% of normal. Capital Region (9 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $7,000 to $21,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.56 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.89 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 103% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 159 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,276 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.56 per base acre, PLC $2.39.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 24.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2021; too much rain caused 69.6%.

Cash rent in Greene County ran $36 an acre in 2025, up 44.9% from $25 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,674 an acre in 2022, up 79.8% from $3,156 in 2017, or 12.4% a year. That ranks 10th highest of 50 in New York, against a state median of $3,819. Just 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
