$107

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 65.9% from $65 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Ontario County runs $107 an acre for 2026, up 65.9% from $65 in 2016. That rent sits 8th highest of 47 in New York, where the state median is $73. The census value of land and buildings was $5,135 an acre in 2022, up 27.5% from $4,027 in 2017, which works out to 5.0% a year. Corn trends at 163.2 bu, though the 2025 crop came in at 147.5 bu.
