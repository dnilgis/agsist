Ontario County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,135 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 36.32 inches, 117% of normal (31 inches) through Nov 17, at Bristol Harbour. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,464 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,135 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $107 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 65.9% from 2016 to 2026. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 45% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 339.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 30 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 414 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.84 per base acre, PLC $3.26.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (129.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.54 per base acre, PLC $10.11. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (56 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $67.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 116 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.22 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.10, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 70% of the median yield. 0.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 17.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 70.5%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 65.9% from $65 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Ontario County runs $107 an acre for 2026, up 65.9% from $65 in 2016. That rent sits 8th highest of 47 in New York, where the state median is $73. The census value of land and buildings was $5,135 an acre in 2022, up 27.5% from $4,027 in 2017, which works out to 5.0% a year. Corn trends at 163.2 bu, though the 2025 crop came in at 147.5 bu.
