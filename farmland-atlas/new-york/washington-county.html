Washington County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Capital Region (9 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $7,000 to $21,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.66 per base acre for corn.

Washington County vs New York: $88 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 74% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (13th highest of 47).
