Broome County cash rent sits at $66 an acre in 2026, up 101.5% from $33 in 2016, and ranks 20th lowest of 47 against a state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings was $3,269 an acre in 2022, up 5.8% from $3,089 in 2017, or 1.1% a year. Corn trends at 161.9 bu, 13th highest of 35, and only 17% of farmland was rented in 2022.
