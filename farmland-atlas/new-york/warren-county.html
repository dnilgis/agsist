Warren County, New York

Warren County, NY: land and buildings $6,662 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36113. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Warren County had 8,159 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Warren County was $6,662 per acre in 2022, up 59.6 percent from 2017, or 9.8 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 57.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 56.3 F, with a warming trend of 0.78 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Solar operating capacity is 6.9 MW, with 5 MW proposed; wind operating capacity is 0 MW.
