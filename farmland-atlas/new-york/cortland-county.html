Cropland rent in Cortland County was $76 an acre in 2026, up 24.8% from $61 in 2016 and 21st highest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $3,103 an acre in 2022, up 33.7% from $2,320 in 2017, but still 13th lowest of 50 against a state median of $3,819. Corn trends at 157.9 bu, right on the state median.
