Cortland County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Central New York region (11 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $2,000 to $8,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.96 per base acre for corn.

Cortland County vs New York: $75.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 57% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (21st highest of 47).

Quick answers for Cortland County

What is cash rent in Cortland County, New York?

The county average is $76 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cortland County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $3,103 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Cortland County, New York?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 157.9 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 12 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
