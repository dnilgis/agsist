Delaware County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Central New York region (11 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $2,000 to $8,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.97 per base acre for corn.

Delaware County vs New York: $49.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 11% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 6th lowest of 47).

Cropland rent in Delaware County runs $50 an acre for 2026, up 80.0% from $28 in 2016. That is tied for 6th lowest of 47 in New York, under the state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings was $3,035 an acre in 2022, up 5.4% from $2,880 in 2017, or 1.1% a year. Corn has held near a median of 127.2 bu from 2008 to 2022.
