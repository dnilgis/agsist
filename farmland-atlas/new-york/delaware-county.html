$50

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 80% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 6, cropland rent in Delaware County runs $50 an acre for 2026, up 80.0% from $28 in 2016. That is tied for 6th lowest of 47 in New York, under the state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings was $3,035 an acre in 2022, up 5.4% from $2,880 in 2017, or 1.1% a year. Corn has held near a median of 127.2 bu from 2008 to 2022.
