Non-irrigated cash rent here is $45 per acre for 2026, up 233.3 percent from 2014. Land sold at $3,086 per acre in 2022, up 29.6 percent from 2017, 5.3 percent a year. July nights average 56.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 54.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.64 F per decade, warming. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and drought at severe or worse held the county for 6 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025.
