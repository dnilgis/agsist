Schuyler County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Western New York region (14 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $3,000 to $13,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.70 per base acre for corn.

Schuyler County vs New York: $56 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 26% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (13th lowest of 47).

Quick answers for Schuyler County

What is cash rent in Schuyler County, New York?

The county average is $56 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Schuyler County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $3,972 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Schuyler County, New York?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 148.1 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 10 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
