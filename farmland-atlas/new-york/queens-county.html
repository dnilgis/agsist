Queens County, New York

Queens County, NY: land and buildings $113,746 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36081. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Queens County had 69 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here runs $113,746 per acre, up 134.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, an 18.6 percent a year pace. July nights average 69.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.83 F per decade, warming. Twelve Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F, and the July average low first reached 66 F in the 1930s. Drought has touched this county in 44 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 27 weeks, and 12 weeks fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
