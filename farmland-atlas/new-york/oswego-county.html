Oswego County, New York

Oswego County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,848 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 131.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Oswego County averages $60 per acre in 2026, up 122.2 percent from 2016. Water risk here has run mostly wet: excess moisture and flood account for 54 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, closed crop years, and severe drought covered just 11 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with zero weeks from 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 10 percent, 16 percent, 2 percent, 8 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. July nights average 61.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 59.5 F normal from 1991-2020, with warming of 0.68 F per decade since 1976. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, and irrigation-failure indemnities total $373,915 across all closed years.
