Wayne County, New York

Wayne County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $94 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,862 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36117. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County is $94 per acre in 2026, up 59.3 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 stands at $4,862 per acre, having risen 41.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7.2 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 1.7 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 147.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 132.1 in 2012. Drought has stayed light: 16 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, and 0 weeks from 2021-2025.
