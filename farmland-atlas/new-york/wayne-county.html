Wayne County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,862 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.46 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

53.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 34 inches, 101% of normal (33.52 inches) through Nov 17, at Palmyra, 1.6 mi NW. Frost-free season: 180 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,457 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,862 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $94 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 59.3% from 2016 to 2026. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 30% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 167.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 141 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.46 per base acre, PLC $2.97.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (118.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.18 per base acre, PLC $8.50. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (47.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $67.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.62 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.16, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 89% of the median yield. 2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 4.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 21.2%.
