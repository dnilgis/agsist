Fulton County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Adirondack region (8 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $5,000 to $9,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.14 per base acre for corn.

Fulton County vs New York: $49.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 11% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 6th lowest of 47).

Quick answers for Fulton County

What is cash rent in Fulton County, New York?

The county average is $50 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Fulton County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $2,916 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
