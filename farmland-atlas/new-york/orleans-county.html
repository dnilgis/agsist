Orleans County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Western New York region (14 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $3,000 to $13,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.54 per base acre for corn.

Orleans County vs New York: $128 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 91% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (5th highest of 47).

Quick answers for Orleans County

What is cash rent in Orleans County, New York?

The county average is $128 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Orleans County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $4,834 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Orleans County, New York?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 183.7 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 8 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
