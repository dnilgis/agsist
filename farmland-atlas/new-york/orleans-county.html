Orleans County, New York

Orleans County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $128 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,834 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 165.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36073. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Orleans County averages $128 per acre in 2026, up 85.5 percent from 2016. Land value under the Census operator estimate is $4,834 per acre, up 52.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.8 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 1.9 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 165.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 134.3 in 2011.
