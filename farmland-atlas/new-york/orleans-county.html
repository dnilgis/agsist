USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Orleans County runs $128 an acre for 2026, up 85.5% from $69 in 2016 and 5th highest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $4,834 an acre in 2022, up 52.3% from $3,173 in 2017. The corn trend for 2026 sits at 183.7 bu, highest of 35 in the state, against a state median of 157.9 bu.
