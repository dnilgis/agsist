Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 66% from $45 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Niagara County was $75 an acre in 2026, up 65.6% from $45 in 2016 and 23rd highest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $3,796 an acre in 2022, up 23.6% from $3,070 in 2017, which sits 25th lowest of 50 in the state. Corn made 147.0 bu in 2025, short of the 2021 to 2025 average of 162.1 bu.
