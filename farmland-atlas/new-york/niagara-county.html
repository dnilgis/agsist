Niagara County, New York

Niagara County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $75 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,796 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36063. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in 2022 was $3,796 per acre, up 23.6 percent from 2017, and rent equals 1.51 percent of that value. Drought weeks were rare, 33 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 17 weeks. July nights have warmed 0.52 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 63.2 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 62.2 F.
