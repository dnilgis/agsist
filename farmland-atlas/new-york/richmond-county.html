Richmond County, New York

Richmond County, NY: land and buildings $2,551,600 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36085. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Richmond County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land here carries a census value of $2,551,600 per acre, with land value change 2012-2022 of 1347.2 percent, 30.6 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal is 68.3 F, the 2017-2026 average is 70.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 1.03 F per decade, warming. Twelve Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F, and the July average low first reached 66 F in the 1970s. Drought has touched the county in 51 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2002 with 27 weeks, and 16 weeks from 2021-2025.
