Land and buildings: $2,551,600 an acre in the 2022 census.

No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

The census value of land and buildings in Richmond County came to $2,551,600 an acre in 2022. That is up 1347.2% from $176,311 in 2012, which works out to 30.6% a year. The state median is $3,819, so this county does not look much like the rest of New York farm country.

Richmond County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
