Dutchess County, New York

Dutchess County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $54 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,698 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $54 per acre, up 77 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census sits at $10,698 per acre, up 18.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.4 percent a year. Corn yield has a median of 146.5 bushels per acre over 2008-2022, with the worst year, 2022, at 118.1. July nights average 64.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.7 F, warming at 1.1 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought covered 11 weeks of the county from 2021-2025.
