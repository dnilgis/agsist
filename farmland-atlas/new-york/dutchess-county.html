Cash rent in Dutchess County runs $54 an acre for 2026, up 77.0% from $31 in 2016, and that rent sits tied for 9th lowest of 47 against a state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $10,698 an acre in 2022, 2nd highest of 50 in New York, up 18.3% from $9,040 in 2017. Corn has run a median of 146.5 bu over 9 years of county figures from 2008 to 2022.
