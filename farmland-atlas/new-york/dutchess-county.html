Dutchess County, New York

Rain is at 94% of normal. Hudson Valley region (7 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $8,500 to $30,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Fewer Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.33 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 177 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,674 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $25.78 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.49, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 81% of the median yield. 3.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 7.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.4%.
