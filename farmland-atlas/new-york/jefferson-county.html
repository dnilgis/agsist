Jefferson County cash rent sits at $49 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th lowest of 47 in New York and under the state median of $73. That rent is up 12.6% from $44 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,496 an acre in 2022, down 6.8% from $2,677 in 2017 and 4th lowest of 50 in the state. Corn trends at 146.5 bu, below the state median of 157.9.
