Quick answers for Orange County

What is cash rent in Orange County, New York?

The county average is $77 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $216 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Orange County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $10,494 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Orange County, New York?

The county's 2020-24 average is 156.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
