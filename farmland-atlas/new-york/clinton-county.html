As of Oct 12, cash rent in Clinton County runs $63 an acre for 2026, up 27.6% from $49 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $73 and ranks 16th lowest of 47. The census value of land and buildings was $3,464 an acre in 2022, up 31.0% from $2,645 in 2017, or 5.5% a year. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
