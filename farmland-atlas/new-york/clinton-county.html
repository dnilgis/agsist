Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Adirondack region (8 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $5,000 to $9,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.71 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Clinton County

What is cash rent in Clinton County, New York?

The county average is $63 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Clinton County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $3,464 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
