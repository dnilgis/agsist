Nassau County had 665 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Census value of land and buildings runs $62,463 per acre for 2022, with a change of 259.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 29.2 percent a year. July nights average 69 F for 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 67.2 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.91 F per decade, warming. The county logged 58 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 27 weeks, and 12 such weeks from 2021-2025. Solar operating stands at 1.8 MW, wind at 0 MW, with no proposed capacity to EIA for either.
