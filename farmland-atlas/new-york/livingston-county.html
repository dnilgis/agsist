Livingston County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,585 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.45 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 30.09 inches, 106% of normal (28.49 inches) through Nov 18, at Dansville. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,456 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,585 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $141 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 108.9% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 31% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 283.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 224.1 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,404 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 1,108 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.45 per base acre, PLC $3.32.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (132.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.68 per base acre, PLC $10.29. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (57 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $67.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 132 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.75, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 65.8%.
