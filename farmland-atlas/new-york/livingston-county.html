As of Oct 16, cash rent in Livingston County is $141 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 47 in New York and well over the state median of $73. It was $68 in 2016, so rent is up 108.9% since then. The census value of land and buildings was $4,585 an acre in 2022, up 23.5% from $3,712 in 2017. The corn trend projects 173.6 bu for 2026, 3rd highest of 35 in the state.
