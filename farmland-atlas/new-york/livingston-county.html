Livingston County, New York

Livingston County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $141 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,585 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 161.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36051. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Livingston County averages $141 per acre in 2026, up 108.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $4,585 per acre in 2022, after a change of 23.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.3 percent a year. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 161.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 2016 at 122.8. None of the county's harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
