Chemung County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,542 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.17 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

15.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 33.73 inches, 98% of normal (34.32 inches) through Nov 17, at Elmira. Frost-free season: 161 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,366 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,542 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $55 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 74.6% from 2016 to 2026. 19.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 123.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 671 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.17 per base acre, PLC $2.92.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (116.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (43.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $49.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.49 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.58 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 87% of the median yield. 2.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 42.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1992 to 2025; too much rain caused 48.7%.
