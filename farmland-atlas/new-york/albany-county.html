Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. 1.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 5.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 44.8%.

Cash rent in Albany County ran $48 an acre in 2024, up 174.3% from $18 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,894 an acre in 2022, up 32.6% from $4,446 in 2017, or 5.8% a year. That sits well above the state median of $3,819 and ranks 9th highest of 50. Some 40% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
