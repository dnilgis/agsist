Albany County, New York

Albany County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $48 an acre (2024); land and buildings $5,894 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 137.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Albany County averages $48 per acre in 2024, up 174.3 percent from 2013. Census land value stands at $5,894 per acre, up 32.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.8 percent a year. Corn yields have a 2010-2021 median of 137.7 bushels per acre, with the worst year, 2011, at 93.3. Crop insurance history for 1989-2025 shows $3.1 million in indemnities, mostly from excess moisture and flood at 45 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 0 percent in 2000-2009, to 7 percent in 2010-2019, to 19 percent in 2020-2025.
