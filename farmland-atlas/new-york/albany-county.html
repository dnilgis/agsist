As of Oct 6, cash rent in Albany County ran $48 an acre in 2024, up 174.3% from $18 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,894 an acre in 2022, up 32.6% from $4,446 in 2017, or 5.8% a year. That sits well above the state median of $3,819 and ranks 9th highest of 50. Some 40% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
