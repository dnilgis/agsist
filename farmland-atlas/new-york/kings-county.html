Kings County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Kings County land and buildings were worth $588,578 per acre in 2022, up 32.7 percent from 2017, or 5.8 percent a year. July nights have averaged 70.9 F over 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 69 F, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.86 F per decade since 1976. Sixteen Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched the county in 42 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 27 weeks, and 13 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind operates in the county now, but 810 MW of wind has been proposed to EIA. Cash rent, corn yield, and insurance loss records are not available.
