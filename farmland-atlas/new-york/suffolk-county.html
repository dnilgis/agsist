AGSIST/Farmland Atlas/New York/Suffolk County

Suffolk County, New York

Rain is at 110% of normal. Land and buildings run $18,696 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.18 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.73 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 110% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 215 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,784 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $18,696 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: New York City and Long Island are not in the report's regions. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $146 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 24.8% from 2014 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 22% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 7.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 935.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 299 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.18 per base acre, PLC $2.21.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (88.15 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $4.81 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.48 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (27.87 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.11 per base acre, PLC $8.15. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.13 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $57.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.26 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.16, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. 46.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 31.5%.
