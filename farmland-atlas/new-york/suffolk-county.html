Non-irrigated cash rent in Suffolk County runs $146 per acre in 2026, up 24.8 percent from 2014. Irrigated cash rent is $288 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 2.58 in 2010 to 1.97 in 2026, with no clear direction. Land value carries at $18,696 per acre in 2022, up 53 percent from 2017, or 8.9 percent a year. Crop insurance loss ratio was 0.4 over 1995-2025 but rose to 1.16 over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025.
