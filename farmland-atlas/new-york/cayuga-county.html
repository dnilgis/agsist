USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Cayuga County runs $133 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 47 in New York and well over the state median of $73. That rent is up 39.3% from $96 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,355 an acre in 2022, up 10.1% from $3,955 in 2017, or 1.9% a year. Corn trends at 166.2 bu for 2026, tied for 8th highest of 35, though 2025 came in at 140.0 bu.
