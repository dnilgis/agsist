USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Chenango County runs $63 an acre for 2026, up 37.0% from $46 in 2016 and 17th lowest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $2,434 an acre in 2022, up 18.4% from $2,056 in 2017, and that sits 3rd lowest of 50 against a state median of $3,819. Corn comes in at a 136.8 bu trend, 3rd lowest of 35, with 24% of farmland rented in 2022.
