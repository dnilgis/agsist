Schenectady County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Capital Region (9 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $7,000 to $21,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.48 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,448 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2025; too much rain caused 63.8%.

Cash rent in Schenectady County ran $35 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $5,989 an acre in 2022, up 39.7% from $4,288 in 2017, or 6.9% a year. That ranks 8th highest of 50 in the state, well over the state median of $3,819. In 2022, 25% of the farmland was rented.
