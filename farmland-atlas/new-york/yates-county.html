Yates County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Western New York region (14 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $3,000 to $13,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.46 per base acre for corn.

Yates County vs New York: $111 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 87% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (7th highest of 47).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.97 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,430 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.30 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.92, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36%.
