Cash rent in Ulster County runs $104 an acre for 2026, up 65.1% from $63 in 2016 and 9th highest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,701 an acre in 2022, up 26.1% from 2017 and 4th highest of 50. Corn is another story. The 14-yr trend sits at 131.8 bu, 2nd lowest of 35 against a state median of 157.9 bu.
