Hamilton County, New York

Hamilton County, NY: land and buildings $4,967 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Hamilton County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land and buildings in Hamilton County are valued at $4,967 per acre in the 2022 census, up 48.7 percent from 2017, or 8.3 percent a year. July nights average 54.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.61 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Irrigated cropland stood at 29 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census, with no solar or wind operating or proposed.
