Putnam County, New York

Putnam County, NY: land and buildings $11,485 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36079. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Putnam County had 3,992 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land in Putnam County was valued by operators at $11,485 per acre in the 2022 Census, up 91.4 percent from 2017, a rise of 13.9 percent a year. July nights average 65.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 1.14 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought was present in 79 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 17 weeks; 31 weeks fell in 2021-2025. Solar operating stands at 2.3 MW; wind operating is 0 MW.
