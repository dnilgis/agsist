107.4 bu

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Rensselaer County was $53 an acre in 2026, down 28.6% from $74 in 2016. That rent is 8th lowest of 47 in New York, under the state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 26.6% to $4,449 an acre in 2022 from $3,513 in 2017, and sits 20th highest of 50. Corn runs a 14-yr trend of 149.9 bu, 13th lowest of 35.
