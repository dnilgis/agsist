Cropland rent in Tompkins County runs $69 an acre for 2026, up 55.1% from $45 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $73. That puts it 22nd lowest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $3,994 an acre in 2022, up 5.4% from $3,789 in 2017, or 1.1% a year. Corn trends at 152.2 bu for 2026, against a 2019-23 average of 145.5 bu.
