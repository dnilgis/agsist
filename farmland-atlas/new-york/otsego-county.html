$49

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2008; up 36.6% from $36 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Otsego County runs $49 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 47 counties in New York against a state median of $73. That rent is up 36.6% from $36 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,070 an acre in 2022, up 15.2% from $2,666 in 2017, and 10th lowest of 50 in the state. Corn trends at 161.8 bu for 2026, a bit above the state median of 157.9 bu.
