Otsego County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Central New York region (11 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $2,000 to $8,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.05 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 153 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,151 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.64 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.67, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 14.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 73%.

The census value of land and buildings was $3,070 an acre in 2022, up 15.2% from $2,666 in 2017, and 10th lowest of 50 in the state. Corn trends at 161.8 bu for 2026, a bit above the state median of 157.9 bu.
