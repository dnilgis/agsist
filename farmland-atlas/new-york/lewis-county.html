Stands out against its own record

$79

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 27% from $62 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 12, cropland in Lewis County rents for $79 an acre in 2026, up 27.4% from $62 in 2016 and above the state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings was $2,328 an acre in 2022, up 15.9% from 2017, and that ranks lowest of 50 in New York. Corn trends at 145.5 bu, 7th lowest of 35, and 28% of farmland was rented in 2022.
