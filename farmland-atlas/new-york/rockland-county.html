Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 198 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,864 in the median year.

Land

Land: no recorded sale median, census value or published rent for this county.

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 60.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 16.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. Cattle: NASS withholds this county's count (D). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.
