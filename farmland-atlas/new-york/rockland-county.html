July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 66.5 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.1 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 1.04 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F, and the 2020s is the first decade to reach 66 F.
