AGSIST/Farmland Atlas/New York/Steuben County

Steuben County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,076 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.35 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

98.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 33.12 inches, 97% of normal (34.28 inches) through Nov 18, at Hornell Almond Dam. Frost-free season: 158 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,282 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,076 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $57.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 64.3% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 162.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 139.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 45,841 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.35 per base acre, PLC $2.53.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (101 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.84 per base acre, PLC $8.34. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $57.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 73 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.24, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 75% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 30.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.5%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 64.3% from $35 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Steuben County cash rent sits at $58 an acre for 2026, up 64.3% from $35 in 2016 and still under the state median of $73, 14th lowest of 47. The census value of land and buildings was $3,076 an acre in 2022, up 46.5% from $2,099 in 2017. Corn runs on the light side here, with a 14-yr trend of 141.7 bu that ranks 4th lowest of 35. Only 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
