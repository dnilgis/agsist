Steuben County, New York

Steuben County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $58 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,076 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36101. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Steuben County averages $58 per acre in 2026, up 64.3 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,076 per acre in 2022, up 46.5 percent from 2017, 7.9 percent a year. Cash rent in 2022 ran at 1.5 percent of that census value. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 closed crop years total $34.2 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 51 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 43 percent in 1989-1999, 17 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, to 43 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 1.24, above the 1989-2025 figure of 1.17.
