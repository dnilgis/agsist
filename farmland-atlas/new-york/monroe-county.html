Monroe County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Western New York region (14 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $3,000 to $13,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.33 per base acre for corn.

Monroe County vs New York: $80.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 63% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 17th highest of 47).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

77.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.69 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 121% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 185 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,527 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.70 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.01, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 57.8%.
