Cropland rent in Monroe County runs $81 an acre for 2026, up 41.2% from $57 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 47 counties in New York. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,622 an acre in 2022, up 7.3% from $4,306 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a trend yield of 180.1 bu that ranks 2nd highest of 35, though 2025 came in at 153.4 bu.
