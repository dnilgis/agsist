Schoharie County is a rain county more than a heat county. The biggest cause of crop insurance payouts here since 1990 is excess moisture and flood, at 49 percent. Heat and drought claims went from 4 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009 to 22 percent in 2020-2025, so that share is the one to watch. Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $38 per acre, up 55.1 percent from 2016. The census land value was $3,551 per acre in 2022, and the land value change over 2017-2022 was 45.8 percent. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.55.

Quick answers for Schoharie County

What is cash rent in Schoharie County, New York?

The county average is $38 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Schoharie County, New York?

Land and buildings were worth $3,551 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Schoharie County, New York?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 166.2 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 6 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
