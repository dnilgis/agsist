Schoharie County, New York

Schoharie County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,551 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 138.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36095. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Schoharie County averages $38 per acre in 2026, up 55.1 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,551 per acre in 2022, up 45.8 percent from 2017, 7.8 percent a year. The 2026 rent figure runs at 0.84 percent of that 2022 land value. Crop insurance history from 1989-2025 shows a loss ratio of 0.78, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause of indemnities at 49 percent over 1990-2025; heat and drought's share rose from 4 percent in 2000-2009 to 6 percent in 2010-2019 to 22 percent in 2020-2025.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

"Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed; it moves only when a figure on the page does. Source files and their retrieval dates are listed on the data page.

Yield: NASS county corn yields, trend fitted here over every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out.
