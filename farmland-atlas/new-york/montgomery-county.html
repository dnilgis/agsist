Montgomery County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Capital Region (9 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $7,000 to $21,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.07 per base acre for corn.

Montgomery County vs New York: $55 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 22% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 11th lowest of 47).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.73 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.64, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 67% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 11% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 77%.
