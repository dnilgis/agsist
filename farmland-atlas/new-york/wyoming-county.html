Cash rent in Wyoming County runs $126 an acre for 2026, up 31.2% from $96 in 2016. That rent sits 6th highest of 47 counties in New York, well over the state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings was $4,920 an acre in 2022, up 42.1% from $3,463 in 2017. Corn trend yield comes in at 163.7 bu, 11th highest of 35, though 2025 came in at 129.6 bu.
