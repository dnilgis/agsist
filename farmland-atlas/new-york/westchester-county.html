Westchester County, New York

Westchester County, NY: land and buildings $11,506 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36119. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Westchester County had 6,311 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Census market value of land and buildings in Westchester County was $11,506 per acre in 2022, up 8.1 percent from 2017, or 1.6 percent a year. July nights have warmed. The 1991-2020 normal average low was 64.5 F; 2017-2026 averages 66.9 F. The trend since 1976 runs 1.05 F per decade, warming, and the 2010s was the first decade the July average low reached 66 F. Drought has touched the county in short stretches: 76 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2002 the worst year at 22 weeks, and 23 such weeks fell in 2021-2025. Solar operating capacity stands at 22.4 MW; wind operating is 0 MW.
