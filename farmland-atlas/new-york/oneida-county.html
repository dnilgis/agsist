Oneida County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Central New York region (11 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $2,000 to $8,500 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.24 per base acre for corn.

Oneida County vs New York: $73 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 50% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (24th lowest of 47).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.20 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. 0.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 16.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 61.2%.
