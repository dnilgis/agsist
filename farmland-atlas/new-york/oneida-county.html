Oneida County, New York

Oneida County, NY: non-irrigated cash rent $73 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,359 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 36065. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $73 per acre, up 37.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census value of land and buildings stood at $3,359 per acre in 2022, with rent equal to 1.52 percent of that value. Excess moisture and flood drove 61 percent of the $10.1 million in indemnities paid from 1993-2025, though heat and drought's share of indemnities climbed across 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, at 4 percent, 9 percent, and 29 percent. Corn yields trended up 1.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 149.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 115.6 in 2013.
