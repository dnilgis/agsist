Onondaga County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.01 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

87.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 33.76 inches, 96% of normal (35.24 inches) through Nov 17, at Syracuse Hancock International Airport. Frost-free season: 166 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,388 in the median year.

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $80 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 24.8% from 2016 to 2026. 27.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 25.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 274.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 45.3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 474 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.01 per base acre, PLC $2.98.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (118.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $54.00 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.78 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.02 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. 1.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 35.1%.

Cash rent in Onondaga County runs $81 an acre for 2026, tied for 17th highest of 47 in New York and above the $73 state median. That is up 24.8% from $65 in 2016. Corn made 140.9 bu in 2025, a bit under the 146.7 bu it averaged from 2021 through 2025. In 2022, 28% of the farmland here was rented.
