Cash rent in Onondaga County runs $81 an acre for 2026, tied for 17th highest of 47 in New York and above the $73 state median. That is up 24.8% from $65 in 2016. Corn made 140.9 bu in 2025, a bit under the 146.7 bu it averaged from 2021 through 2025. In 2022, 28% of the farmland here was rented.
