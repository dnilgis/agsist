Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2017; too much rain caused 36%.

Cash rent in Sullivan County ran $48 an acre in 2023, up 200.0% from $16 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings was $5,224 an acre in 2022, up 24.0% from $4,213 in 2017, which works out to 4.4% a year. That value sits 11th highest of 50 in New York, against a state median of $3,819. In 2022, 37% of the farmland here was rented.
