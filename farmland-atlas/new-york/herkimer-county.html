As of Oct 12, cash rent in Herkimer County runs $73 an acre in 2026, up 48.0% from $49 in 2016 and right at the state median of $73, 23rd lowest of 47. The census value of land and buildings was $3,172 an acre in 2022, up 38.6% from $2,289 in 2017, and sits 14th lowest of 50 against a state median of $3,819. Corn trends at 146.2 bu for 2026, 8th lowest of 35, and came in at 124.0 bu in 2025.
