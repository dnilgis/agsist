Herkimer County, New York

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Adirondack region (8 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $5,000 to $9,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.05 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 152 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,041 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.94 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.67, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 5.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 64.5%.
