Cash rent in St. Lawrence County runs $75 an acre for 2026, up 64.8% from $46 in 2016, and it sits 22nd highest of 47 against a state median of $73. The census value of land and buildings was $2,386 an acre in 2022, up 30.2% from $1,832 in 2017. That still ranks 2nd lowest of 50 in New York, well under the state median of $3,819. Corn trends at 153.0 bu for 2026, a bit shy of the state median of 157.9 bu.
