Non-irrigated cash rent in Cattaraugus County averages $84 per acre in 2026, up 49.1 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $2,954 per acre for 2022, up 28.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.2 percent a year. Rent equals 2.06 percent of that census value. Corn yield trend runs 1.6 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2024, with a median of 146.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 106 in 2008. Drought has stayed rare, with severe drought or worse in 10 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, and heat and drought's share of indemnities rose from 3 percent in 1989-1999 to 33 percent in 2020-2025, though the years between did not move in one direction. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland.
