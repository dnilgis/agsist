Cattaraugus County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,954 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.36 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 38.64 inches, 98% of normal (39.47 inches) through Nov 17, at Franklinville. Frost-free season: 150 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,183 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,954 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $84 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 49.1% from 2016 to 2026. 18.3% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 10% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 219.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 47,919 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.36 per base acre, PLC $2.78.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $57.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.38 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.73 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 5.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 17.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 52.7%.
