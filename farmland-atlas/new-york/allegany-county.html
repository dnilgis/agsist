As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Allegany County runs $85 an acre for 2026, up 39.3% from $61 in 2016 and 15th highest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $3,316 an acre in 2022, up 64.7% from $2,013 in 2017, though still 18th lowest of 50. Corn trends at 128.0 bu, the lowest of 35 counties in the state.
