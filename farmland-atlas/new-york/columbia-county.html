Columbia County, New York

Land and buildings run $6,536 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.52 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,595 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,536 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $54 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 4.9% from 2016 to 2026. 45.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 188.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 47 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,998 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.52 per base acre, PLC $2.45.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (97.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $54.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 3 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.28 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.59 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 82% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 6.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.2%.
