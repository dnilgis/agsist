Cash rent in Columbia County was $54 an acre in 2026, up 4.9% from $52 in 2016 and tied for 9th lowest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings was $6,536 an acre in 2022, up 56.1% from $4,188 in 2017, which puts it 6th highest of 50 in the state. Corn trends at 145.3 bu for 2026, under the state median of 157.9 bu.
