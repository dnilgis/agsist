Bronx County is a city county, and the notable thing here is the heat at night. July nightly lows averaged 70.1 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.4 F. Since 1976 the July low has been warming at 0.89 F per decade. Eleven Julys since 1900 averaged a low at or above 70 F, and the 1930s was the first decade the July average low reached 66 F. Drought has been rare here. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse came to 43 of 1357 weeks from 2000 to 2025, with 2002 the worst year at 25 weeks. Those weeks were 7 in 2021-2025. There is no CRP ground, and no solar or wind operating or proposed to EIA.
