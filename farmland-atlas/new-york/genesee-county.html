$131

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 79.5% from $73 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent here runs $131 an acre for 2026, up 79.5% from $73 in 2016, and that ranks 4th highest of 47 in New York. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,099 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from $3,345 in 2017. The corn trend sits at 171.7 bu for 2026, 4th highest of 35 in the state, though the 2025 crop came in at 136.8 bu.
