Genesee County, New York

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Western New York region (14 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: high-quality cropland sold for $3,000 to $13,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Limited Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.29 per base acre for corn.

Genesee County vs New York: $131 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 93% of the other 46 New York counties with a 2026 rent (4th highest of 47).

Risk
