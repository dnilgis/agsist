Cash rent in Lee County ran $100 an acre in 2025, up 38.9% from $72 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,431 an acre in 2022, up 8.1% from $3,173 in 2017 and 28th lowest of 75 in Arkansas. In 2022, 74% of the farmland here was rented.
