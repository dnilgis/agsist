Lee County, Arkansas

Lee County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $100 an acre (2025); land and buildings $3,431 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05077. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation covers 73 percent of harvested cropland here, with 98 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. Non-irrigated cash rent in 2025 averages $100 per acre, up 38.9 percent from 2014, while irrigated rent runs $152 per acre in 2026. The ten closed crop years 2016-2025 carry a loss ratio of 1.15, higher than the 1989-2025 figure of 0.96. Census land value sits at $3,431 per acre in 2022, up 8.1 percent from 2017.
