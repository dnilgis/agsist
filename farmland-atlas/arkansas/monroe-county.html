Monroe County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,754 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 250 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,948 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,754 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $80 an acre (USDA, 2023). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 73.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 3.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,208 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $54.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 343 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.24 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.56 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 81% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 16.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 73.9%.
