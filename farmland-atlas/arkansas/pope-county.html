Pope County, Arkansas

Pope County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $19 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,861 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.42 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05115. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pope County averages $19 per acre in 2026, down 7.3 percent from 2016. Land value under the operators' own estimate stands at $3,861 per acre, up 20.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.8 percent a year. Irrigation covers 21 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Crop insurance history shows $13.8 million in indemnities over 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 53 percent; the loss ratio over that span is 1.42, rising to 1.82 over 2016-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
