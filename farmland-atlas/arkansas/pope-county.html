Cropland rent in Pope County runs $19 an acre for 2026, tied for 5th lowest of 61 in Arkansas and under the state median of $30. That rent is down 7.3% from $21 in 2016. Pasture rents for $27 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,861 an acre in 2022, up 20.3% from $3,210 in 2017, and 32nd highest of 75 in the state.
