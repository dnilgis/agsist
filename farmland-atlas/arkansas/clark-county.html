Clark County cash rent runs $40 an acre in 2026, up 146.9% from $16 in 2016. That puts it 16th highest of 61 in Arkansas, against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,036 an acre in 2022, up 25.4% from $2,421 in 2017, and 16th lowest of 75 in the state. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
