Chicot County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,081 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.78 per base acre for corn.

Chicot County vs Arkansas: $159 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 98% of the other 60 Arkansas counties with a 2026 rent (2nd highest of 61).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: at least 267 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,007 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,081 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $159 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.59 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 13% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 65% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 63.9% from 2016 to 2026. 62% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 12.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 77,296 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.79 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.17, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 55.5%.

Quick answers for Chicot County

What is cash rent in Chicot County, Arkansas?

The county average is $159 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $165 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Chicot County, Arkansas?

Land and buildings were worth $4,081 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Chicot County, Arkansas?

The county's 2021-25 average is 178.7 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
