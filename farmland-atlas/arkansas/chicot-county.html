Cash rent in Chicot County runs $159 an acre for 2026, up 63.9% from $97 in 2016, and that ranks 2nd highest of 61 in Arkansas against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,081 an acre in 2022, up 37.4% from $2,971 in 2017. Corn made 173.5 bu in 2025, a touch under the 2021-25 average of 178.7 bu. 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
