Cropland in Sharp County rents for $25 an acre in 2026, tied for 15th lowest of 61 in Arkansas against a state median of $30. That rent was $20 in 2016, up 28.2% over ten years. The census value of land and buildings was $3,164 an acre in 2022, up 48.4% from $2,132 in 2017, which ranks 23rd lowest of 75. Pasture rents for $19 an acre in 2026.

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
