Sharp County, Arkansas

Sharp County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,164 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05135. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sharp County averages $25 per acre in 2026, up 28.2 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $3,164 per acre, with land value up 48.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.2 percent a year. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, so this is a rain-fed county. July nights have averaged 69.5 F over 2017-2026, against a 68.1 F normal for 1991-2020, with warming since 1976 at 0.43 F per decade. The county spent 29 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
