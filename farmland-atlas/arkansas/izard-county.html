Non-irrigated cash rent in Izard County stands at $28 per acre in 2026, up 133.3 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $2,778 per acre in 2022, a gain of 35.7 percent over 2017-2022, or 6.3 percent a year. Severe drought touched the county 104 of 1357 weeks during 2000-2025, with 2012 carrying 28 of those weeks; the 2021-2025 period added 32 weeks. The July nightly low averaged 68.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.6 F, with no clear direction in the trend since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed.
