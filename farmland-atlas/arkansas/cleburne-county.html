U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland here rents for $23 an acre in 2026, tied for 9th lowest of 61 counties in Arkansas, against a state median of $30. That rent has barely moved in ten years, down 2.1% from $24 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings ran $3,631 an acre in 2022, up 23.6% from $2,938 in 2017 and right on the state median. 28% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
