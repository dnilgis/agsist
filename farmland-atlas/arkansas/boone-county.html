Boone County, Arkansas

Boone County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,933 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.36 since 2009. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Boone County runs $36 per acre in 2026, up 24.6 percent from 2016. Land values, by the operators' own estimate, stand at $3,933 per acre, up 49.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.3 percent a year. Crop insurance losses since 2009 total $2.2 million, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 89 percent; heat and drought accounted for 0 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009, 0 percent in 2010-2019, and 13 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 67.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 66.8 F normal, with no clear direction in the longer trend since 1976. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
