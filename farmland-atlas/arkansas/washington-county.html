Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County, AR runs $53 per acre, up 96.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $7,460 per acre, up 45.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.8 percent a year. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. July nights average 68.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 67.3 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.44 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance losses from 1996-2023 totaled $457,510, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 53 percent; heat and drought accounted for 20 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 and 9 percent in 2000-2009.
