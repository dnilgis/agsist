U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Randolph County cash rent stands at $35 an acre for 2026, up 52.2% from $23 in 2016 and tied for 21st highest of 61 in Arkansas. Irrigated ground rents for $172 in 2026, 10th highest of 27. The census value of land and buildings was $4,053 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from $2,936 in 2017 and 23rd highest of 75. 41% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
