Union County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,912 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.95 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 61% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,900 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,912 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $7.80 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.72 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 17% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 8% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 204.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,679 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $59.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
