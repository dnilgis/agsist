Cash rent in Lincoln County runs $25 an acre for 2026, down 41.9% from $43 in 2014 and tied for 15th lowest of 61 in Arkansas. Irrigated ground rents for $156 an acre in 2026, just under the state median of $158. The census value of land and buildings was $3,517 an acre in 2022, up 14.1% from $3,082 in 2017. In 2022, 65% of the farmland here was rented.
