Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County averages $53 per acre in 2026, up 158.5 percent from 2016. Census land and building value stood at $4,232 per acre in 2022, up 35.1 percent from 2017, or 6.2 percent a year. The county logged 93 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with the worst year 2012 at 28 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 16 such weeks. July nightly lows averaged 67.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 66.4 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.3 F per decade and no clear direction.
