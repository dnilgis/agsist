Madison County, Arkansas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,232 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,310 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,232 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $53 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 158.5% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 243 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 230 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Madison County runs $53 an acre for 2026, up 158.5% from $21 in 2016. That puts it tied for 12th highest of 61 in Arkansas, against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $4,232 an acre in 2022, up 35.1% from $3,133 in 2017, and 17th highest of 75. 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
