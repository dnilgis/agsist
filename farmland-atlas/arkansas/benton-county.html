Stands out against its own record

17 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Benton County is $53 an acre for 2026, 14th highest of 61 in Arkansas and well over the $30 state median. That is up 1.0% from $52 in 2014, so rent has barely moved. The census value of land and buildings is another story. It was $9,796 an acre in 2022, highest of 75 in the state and up 51.2% from $6,478 in 2017.
