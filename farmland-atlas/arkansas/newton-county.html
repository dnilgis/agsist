Newton County, Arkansas

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.47 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 208 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,305 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,159 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $25.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 27.5% from 2014 to 2026. 18% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 173.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Quick answers for Newton County

What is cash rent in Newton County, Arkansas?

The county average is $26 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Newton County, Arkansas?

Land and buildings were worth $3,159 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
