Non-irrigated cash rent in Newton County averages $26 per acre for 2026, up 27.5 percent since 2014. Census land and buildings value came in at $3,159 per acre in 2022, with land value up 29.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.3 percent a year. No cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. Drought reached severe or worse in 6.7 percent of weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 28 weeks, and 16 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights have averaged 67.5 F over 2017-2026, against a 66.5 F normal from 1991-2020, with no clear direction in the trend since 1976.
