Bradley County, Arkansas

Bradley County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $12 an acre (2022); land and buildings $3,664 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in 2022 averaged $12 per acre. Census land and building value in 2022 stood at $3,664 per acre, up 11.5 percent from 2017, or 2.2 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 totaled $34.8 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 48 percent; heat and drought's share moved 4 percent, 3 percent, 24 percent, 23 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran to 131 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 38 weeks in 2011 alone and 21 weeks over 2021-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
