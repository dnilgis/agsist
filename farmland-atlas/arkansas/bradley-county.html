U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 14, cash rent in Bradley County was $12 an acre in 2022, and pasture rent was $13 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $3,664 an acre in 2022, up 11.5% from $3,287 in 2017, or 2.2% a year. That sits just over the state median of $3,631 and ranks 37th highest of 75. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
