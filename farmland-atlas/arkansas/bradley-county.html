Bradley County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,664 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 250 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,904 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,664 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 27% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 28% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 173.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 47,009 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $59.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $65.66 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.38, 2016 to 2025. 12.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 17.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 48.2%.

Cash rent in Bradley County was $12 an acre in 2022, and pasture rent was $13 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $3,664 an acre in 2022, up 11.5% from $3,287 in 2017, or 2.2% a year. That sits just over the state median of $3,631 and ranks 37th highest of 75. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
