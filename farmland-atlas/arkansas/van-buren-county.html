Van Buren County, Arkansas

Van Buren County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $21 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,436 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Van Buren County averages $21 per acre in 2026, up 44.8 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $3,436 per acre, with land value up 24.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.5 percent a year. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland. Drought has held at severe or worse for 94 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 17 weeks from 2021-2025 and the worst year, 2012, at 28 weeks. July nightly lows have moved 0.31 F per decade since 1976, with no clear direction.
