Hempstead County, Arkansas

Hempstead County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,902 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hempstead County stands at $30 per acre, up 50 percent from 2016 to 2026. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so this land depends on rainfall and summer heat patterns. July nights have averaged 71.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 70.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.6 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 2 percent in 1989-1999 to 13 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks in severe or worse conditions ran 138 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 14 of those weeks in 2021-2025.
