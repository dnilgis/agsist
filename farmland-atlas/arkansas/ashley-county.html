Ashley County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,172 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.78 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 26.96 inches, 56% of normal (48.21 inches) through Nov 17, at Portland. Frost-free season: 257 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,985 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,172 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 64.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 19 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 75 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 69,155 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.78 per base acre, PLC $3.86.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (153.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $51.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 380 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.90 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.21 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 65.8%.
