Crawford County, Arkansas

Crawford County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,236 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Crawford County, AR stands at $31 per acre in 2026, up 34.8 percent since 2016. Land value reached $4,236 per acre in 2022, a gain of 38.1 percent from 2017. July nightly lows are warming at 0.55 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 70.3 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 69 F.
