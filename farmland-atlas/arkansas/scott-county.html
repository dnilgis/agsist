Scott County, Arkansas

Scott County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,109 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05127. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Scott County, AR stands at $23 per acre in 2026, up 31.4 percent from 2016. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and all irrigation water in 2015 came from groundwater. The county spent 139 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 28 weeks; the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming.
