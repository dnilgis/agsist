Cropland rent in Scott County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 31.4% from $18 in 2016 and tied for 9th lowest of 61 in Arkansas. Pasture rents for $22 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,109 an acre in 2022, up 26.7% from $2,454 in 2017, and that puts it 19th lowest of 75. 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
