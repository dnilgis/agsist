Cropland cash rent in Garland County was $11 an acre in 2022, down 44.7% from $19 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, at $7,941 an acre in 2022, up 52.0% from $5,226 in 2017. That puts it 2nd highest of 75 in Arkansas, well over the state median of $3,631. Only 12% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
