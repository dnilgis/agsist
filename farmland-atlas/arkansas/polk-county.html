Polk County, Arkansas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,901 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 37.05 inches, 71% of normal (52.22 inches) through Nov 10, at Vandervoort, 1 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 218 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,536 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,901 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $31 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 77.1% from 2016 to 2026. 17.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 249.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,833 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $48.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
