Non-irrigated cash rent in Nevada County runs $19 per acre in 2026, up 11.8 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $2,507 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 6 percent, 1.2 percent a year. That 2022 rent figure works out to 0.5 percent of that census value. On drought, the county spent 121 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 8.9 percent of weeks, with 2011 the worst year at 33 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 10 such weeks. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 71.4 F against a 70.6 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a 0.5 F per decade warming trend since 1976.
