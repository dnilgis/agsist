Non-irrigated cash rent in Perry County averages $27 per acre in 2026, down 6.9 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $3,212 per acre in 2022, up 16.5 percent from 2017, or 3.1 percent a year. The July average nightly low has moved from 70.2 F (1991-2020 normal) to 71 F (2017-2026), part of a warming trend of 0.35 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
