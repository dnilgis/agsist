Cropland rent in Perry County runs $27 an acre for 2026, down 6.9% from $29 in 2016 and under the state median of $30. That ranks 24th lowest of 61. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,212 an acre in 2022, up 16.5% from $2,758 in 2017, or 3.1% a year. 31% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
