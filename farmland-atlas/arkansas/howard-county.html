Non-irrigated cash rent in Howard County averages $32 per acre in 2026, up 18.5 percent from 2016. Census land and building value stood at $4,175 per acre in 2022, up 46.2 percent from 2017, a pace of 7.9 percent a year. The July nightly low averaged 70.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 69.5 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.56 F per decade since 1976. Drought was present in 154 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 32 weeks, and 19 weeks from 2021-2025. Crop insurance losses from 1989-2023 totaled $127,814, mostly from freeze and frost at 58 percent, with heat and drought at 0 percent of indemnities from 2020-2025.
