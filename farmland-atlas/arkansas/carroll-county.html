Carroll County, Arkansas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,992 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Carroll County vs Arkansas: $50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 77% of the other 60 Arkansas counties with a 2026 rent (15th highest of 61).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.92 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,392 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,992 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 47.1% from 2016 to 2026. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 304.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 183 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 21 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Quick answers for Carroll County

What is cash rent in Carroll County, Arkansas?

The county average is $50 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Carroll County, Arkansas?

Land and buildings were worth $3,992 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
