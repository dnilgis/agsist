Poinsett County, Arkansas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,440 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.73 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 244 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,833 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,440 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $110 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 26.4% from 2014 to 2026. 81% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 14.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 5.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 4.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,930 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.73 per base acre, PLC $3.49.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (139.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $60.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 126 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.51 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.12 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. 88.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 11.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 72%.
