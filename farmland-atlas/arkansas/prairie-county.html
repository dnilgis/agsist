Prairie County, Arkansas

Prairie County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $26 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,560 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 185.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05117. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Prairie County averages $26 per acre in 2026, down 38.1 percent from 2014. Irrigated cash rent is $140 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved from 1.53 in 2009 to 5.38 in 2026, rising. Irrigation covers 85 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 62 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $56.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 72 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities fell each period listed, from 30 percent in 1989-1999 to 14 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $3,560 per acre in 2022, up 21.9 percent from 2017.
