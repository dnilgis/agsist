Cropland rent in Montgomery County runs $29 an acre for 2026, up 39.0% from $21 in 2016. That sits just under the state median of $30 and ranks tied for 27th lowest of 61. The census value of land and buildings was $3,481 an acre in 2022, up 33.9% from $2,599 in 2017, and tied for 30th lowest of 75. Pasture rents for $23 an acre in 2026.
