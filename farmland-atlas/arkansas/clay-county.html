Stands out against its own record

40 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, more than any full year since 2000.

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, irrigated cropland in Clay County rents for $195 an acre in 2026, the highest of 27 in Arkansas against a state median of $158. The census value of land and buildings was $4,788 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 75, up 11.1% from $4,310 in 2017. Corn made 189.9 bu in 2025, a little under the recent average of 192.1 bu. In 2022, 67% of the farmland here was rented.
