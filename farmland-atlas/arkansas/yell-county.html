Cash rent in Yell County runs $26 an acre for 2026, up 92.6% from $14 in 2016. That still sits under the state median of $30 and comes in tied for 20th lowest of 61. The census value of land and buildings was $3,691 an acre in 2022, up 37.0% from $2,695 in 2017, and ranks 36th highest of 75. Pasture rents for $19 an acre in 2026, and 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
