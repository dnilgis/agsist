Non-irrigated rent in Independence County fell 25 percent from 2016 to 2026, now $28 per acre. Irrigated ground brought $143 per acre in 2023, a gap that's grown over time: irrigated was 1.84 times the non-irrigated rent in 2009, but 4.21 times in 2023. Corn yields have trended up 1.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, though the worst year was 2011 at 112.1 bushels. The county has paid out $41.8 million in crop insurance indemnities since 1989, mostly for excess moisture and flood, which made up 81 percent of the total. The loss ratio from 2016 to 2025 was 1.22, meaning claims ran above premiums paid in nine of eighteen crop years.
