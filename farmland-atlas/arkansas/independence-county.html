Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $28 per acre, down 25 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated land rents at $143 per acre in 2023, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.84 in 2009 to 4.21 in 2023, direction rising. Excess moisture and flood account for 81 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid out from 1989-2025, a total of $41.8 million, with a loss ratio of 1.05 over that span and 1.22 over 2016-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 21 percent in 1989-1999, 25 percent in 2000-2009, 10 percent in 2010-2019, and 13 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $3,067 per acre for 2022, up 33.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. Solar and wind capacity, operating or proposed, is 0 MW.
