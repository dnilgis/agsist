Arkansas County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,364 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.52 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.38 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 68% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 257 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,962 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,364 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $98 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.64 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 34% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 28% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 66% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 4.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 155 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,476 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

County median soybean basis: 20 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 2 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.52 per base acre, PLC $4.16.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (165.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $36.62 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.98 per base acre, PLC $11.14. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (61.71 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $54.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,099 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.04 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.01, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 15.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 69.1%.

Quick answers for Arkansas County

What is cash rent in Arkansas County, Arkansas?

The county average is $98 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $158 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Arkansas County, Arkansas?

Land and buildings were worth $4,364 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Arkansas County, Arkansas?

The county's 2021-25 average is 189.3 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
