Phillips County, Arkansas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,345 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 258 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,972 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,345 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $119 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 37.6% from 2016 to 2026. 80% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 4.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 4.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,772 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 29 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.62 at Helena, 16 miles. 1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $54.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 89 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.59 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.31, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. 67.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 51.8%.
