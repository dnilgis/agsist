Cash rent in Jefferson County runs $83 an acre for 2026, down 9.3% from $91 in 2016, and that is 11th highest of 61 in Arkansas against a state median of $30. Irrigated ground rents for $185 in 2026, tied for 3rd highest of 27. The census value of land and buildings was $3,494 an acre in 2022, up 6.9% from 2017, which works out to 1.4% a year. 65% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
