Hot Spring County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,629 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.94 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 39.2 inches, 78% of normal (50.38 inches) through Nov 17, at Degray Lake State Park. Frost-free season: 237 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,802 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,629 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 70% from 2016 to 2026. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 220.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 39,006 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.94 per base acre, PLC $1.31.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (52.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (15.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $48.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 6.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2024; too much rain caused 89.8%.
