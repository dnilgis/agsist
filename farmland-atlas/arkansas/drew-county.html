Drew County, Arkansas

Drew County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,711 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 187.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05043. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland in Drew County covers 78 percent of harvested acres, and irrigated cash rent runs $150 per acre against $39 per acre non-irrigated in 2026. That gap reflects groundwater dependence: 82 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Non-irrigated rent rose 143.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value sits at $3,711 per acre, up 28.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.1 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities from 1989-2025 at 67 percent, while heat and drought's share fell across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio was 1.13 over 1989-2025 and 1.38 over 2016-2025.
