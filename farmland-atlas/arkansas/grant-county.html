Grant County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,912 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 28.1 inches, 64% of normal (43.77 inches) through Nov 18, at Sheridan. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,875 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,912 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $13 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 13.3% from 2014 to 2026. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 197.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 100 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 98,021 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $48.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Grant County runs $13 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 61 in Arkansas against a state median of $30. That rent is down 13.3% from $15 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,912 an acre in 2022, up 8.3% from $3,612 in 2017, or 1.6% a year. That puts it 29th highest of 75, a bit above the state median of $3,631.
