Non-irrigated cash rent in Greene County averages $105 per acre in 2026, up 150 percent from 2014. Irrigated rent runs $192 per acre, a multiple of 1.83 over non-irrigated, versus 1.67 in 2009; the direction of that multiple shows no clear direction. Eighty-two percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 97 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. Census land value sits at $4,818 per acre, up 13.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
