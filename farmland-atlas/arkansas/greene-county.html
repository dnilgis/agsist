Cash rent in Greene County ran $105 an acre in 2026, 9th highest of 61 in Arkansas and well over the state median of $30. That is up 150.0% from $42 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,818 an acre in 2022, up 13.7% from 2017 and 4th highest of 75. In 2022, 73% of the farmland here was rented.
