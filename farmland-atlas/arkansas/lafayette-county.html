Cash rent in Lafayette County runs $58 an acre for 2025, up 79.7% from $32 in 2014. Irrigated ground rents for $174 in 2026, tied for 8th highest of 27 in Arkansas against a state median of $158. The census value of land and buildings was $2,940 an acre in 2022, up 15.4% from 2017 and 14th lowest of 75. Corn made 159.4 bu in 2024, above the county median of 148.4 bu.
