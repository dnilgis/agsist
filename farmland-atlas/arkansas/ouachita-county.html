Ouachita County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,230 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 231 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,873 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,230 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 14% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 50.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 18,094 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $59.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

6.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
