Stands out against its own record

38 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, more than any full year since 2000.

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Pulaski County runs $39 an acre for 2026, tied for 17th highest of 61 in Arkansas and above the state median of $30. That is down 25.0% from $52 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 17.1% from 2017 to $4,602 an acre in 2022. That works out to 3.2% a year and ranks 8th highest of 75 in the state.
