Sevier County, Arkansas

Sevier County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,481 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05133. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sevier County, AR sits at $24 per acre in 2026, up 6.7 percent from 2016, against a census land value of $3,481 per acre in 2022. Land value itself rose 30.5 percent from 2017-2022, at 5.5 percent a year. The July nightly low trend runs 0.51 F per decade warming since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 71.4 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.1 F.
