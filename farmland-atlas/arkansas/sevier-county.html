U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Sevier County runs $24 an acre for 2026, 12th lowest of 61 in Arkansas and under the state median of $30. That is up 6.7% from $23 in 2016, which is not a lot of movement. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,481 an acre in 2022, up 30.5% from $2,668 in 2017 and tied for 30th lowest of 75 in the state.
