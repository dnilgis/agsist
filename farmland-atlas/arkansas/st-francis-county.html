St. Francis County, Arkansas

St. Francis County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $136 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,503 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05123. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in St. Francis County runs $136 per acre in 2026, up 142.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $152 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved from 1.52 in 2009 to 1.12 in 2026, with no clear direction over the full record. Irrigation covers 74 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 94 percent of that water. Crop insurance shows $107.3 million in indemnities from 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 59 percent; heat and drought's share fell from 33 percent in 1989-1999 and 2000-2009 to 20 percent in 2010-2019 and 21 percent in 2020-2025. Land value sits at $3,503 per acre, up 12.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
