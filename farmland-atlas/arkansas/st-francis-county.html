As of Oct 14, cash rent in St. Francis County runs $136 an acre for 2026, up 142.9% from $56 in 2016. That rent ranks 4th highest of 61 in Arkansas, where the state median sits at $30. The census value of land and buildings was $3,503 an acre in 2022, up 12.7% from $3,108 in 2017. In 2022, 75% of the farmland here was rented.
