23 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Dallas County rents for $14 an acre in 2026, 2nd lowest of 61 in Arkansas, against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $2,085 an acre in 2022, lowest of 75 in the state, up 7.0% from $1,949 in 2017. That works out to 1.4% a year. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
