Pike County, Arkansas

Pike County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,015 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pike County runs $38 per acre in 2026, up 85.4 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $3,015 per acre, with land value up 15.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 2.9 percent a year. July nights average 70.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 69.3 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976. Drought was present 142 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2011 at 30 weeks, and 14 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
