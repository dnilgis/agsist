Pike County, Arkansas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,015 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 36.72 inches, 77% of normal (47.93 inches) through Nov 17, at Antoine, 1 mi SW. Frost-free season: 223 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,722 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,015 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $38 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 85.4% from 2016 to 2026. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 186.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 54,918 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $59.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cropland rent in Pike County runs $38 an acre for 2026, up 85.4% from $21 in 2016. That ranks 19th highest of 61 in Arkansas, above the state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $3,015 an acre in 2022, up 15.6% from $2,608 in 2017, and 15th lowest of 75 in the state. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
