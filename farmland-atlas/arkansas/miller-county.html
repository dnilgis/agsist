Stands out against its own record

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cropland in Miller County rents for $32 an acre in 2026, tied for 24th highest of 61 in Arkansas and a bit over the state median of $30. That rent was $20 in 2016, so it is up 64.1% since then. The census value of land and buildings was $3,500 an acre in 2022, up 35.8% from $2,578 in 2017, and it sits 33rd lowest of 75 in the state. In 2022, 56% of the county's farmland was rented.
