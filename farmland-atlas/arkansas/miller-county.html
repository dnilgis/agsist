Non-irrigated cash rent in Miller County averages $32 per acre in 2026, up 64.1 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,500 per acre in 2022, up 35.8 percent from 2017, or 6.3 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025 total $135.3 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 53 percent; heat and drought's share ran 33 percent in 1989-1999, 39 percent in 2000-2009, 35 percent in 2010-2019, and 17 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low averaged 73.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 72 F normal for 1991-2020, warming at 0.66 F per decade since 1976.
