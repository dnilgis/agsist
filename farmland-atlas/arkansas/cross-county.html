Cross County, Arkansas

Cross County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $140 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,223 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 186.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05037. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cross County runs $140 per acre in 2026, up 79.5 percent from 2014. Irrigated rent stands at $149 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved from 1.31 in 2009 to 1.06 in 2026, with no clear direction over that span. Land value carries at $4,223 per acre, up 25.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.6 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $205.1 million paid out over 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 77 percent; heat and drought's share fell from 27 percent in 1989-1999 to 24 percent in 2000-2009, then to 7 percent in 2010-2019 and 10 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 83 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 92 percent from groundwater.
