As of Oct 16, cash rent in Cross County was $140 an acre in 2026, up 79.5% from $78 in 2014. That ranks 3rd highest of 61 in Arkansas, where the state median is $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,223 an acre in 2022, up 25.3% from $3,370 in 2017 and 18th highest of 75. Renters work much of the ground here, with 81% of farmland rented in 2022.
