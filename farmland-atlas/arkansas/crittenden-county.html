Cash rent in Crittenden County runs $109 an acre for 2026, up 26.0% from $87 in 2016 and 8th highest of 61 in Arkansas, where the state median is $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,236 an acre in 2022, up 10.0% from $3,851 in 2017, or 1.9% a year. Corn made 176.2 bu in 2025, a bit under the recent average of 184.4 bu. In 2022, 86% of the farmland here was rented.
