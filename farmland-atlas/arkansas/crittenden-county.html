Crittenden County, Arkansas

Crittenden County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $109 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,236 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Crittenden County runs $109 per acre in 2026, up 26 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents at $157 per acre, and 65 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015. Water risk shows up more than heat risk here: excess moisture and flood account for 67 percent of the $156.6 million in indemnities paid across 1989-2025, while severe drought or worse covered 107 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 21 weeks. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 32 percent in 1989-1999 to 43 percent in 2000-2009, then down to 12 percent in 2010-2019 and 18 percent in 2020-2025. Land value sits at $4,236 per acre, up 10 percent from 2017 to 2022.
