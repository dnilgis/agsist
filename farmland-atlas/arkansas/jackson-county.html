Jackson County, Arkansas

Jackson County, AR: non-irrigated cash rent $114 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,007 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 168.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 05067. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jackson County averages $114 per acre in 2025, up 52 percent from 2014. Irrigated rent runs $185 per acre for 2026, a multiple of 1.34 over non-irrigated, versus 1.47 in 2009, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Irrigation covers 83 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and groundwater supplies 94 percent of that water as of 2015. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows $366.9 million in indemnities, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 81 percent; heat and drought shares by period were 20 percent, 30 percent, 5 percent, and 10 percent. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.51, above the 1.37 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record.
