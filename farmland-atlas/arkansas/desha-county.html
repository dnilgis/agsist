U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Desha County ran $73 an acre in 2024, up 12.3% from $65 in 2014. Irrigated rent was $177 an acre in 2026, 7th highest of 27 in Arkansas against a state median of $158. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,113 an acre in 2022, up 26.4% from 2017 and 21st highest of 75. Corn made 182.8 bu in 2025, a shade under the 186.0 bu median since 2008.
