Cash rent in Johnson County runs $24 an acre for 2026, up 80.8% from $13 in 2016 and 11th lowest of 61 against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $3,931 an acre in 2022, up 40.8% from $2,792 in 2017 and 27th highest of 75. Corn last year made 131.8 bu, close to a trend of 135.5 bu.
