Non-irrigated cash rent in Marion County, AR stands at $30 per acre in 2026, up 76.5 percent from 2016. Land value reached $2,692 per acre in 2022, a gain of 10.4 percent over 2017-2022, or 2 percent a year. Severe drought touched the county during 87 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025, with 2012 carrying 28 of those weeks; the July nightly low trend since 1976 shows no clear direction, and the 2017-2026 average of 68.1 F runs above the 1991-2020 normal of 67 F.
