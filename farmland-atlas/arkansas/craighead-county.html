Dryland cash rent in Craighead County was $55 an acre in 2023, down 12.8% from $63 in 2012. Irrigated ground rented for $159 in 2026, 13th highest of 27 in the state. The census value of land and buildings was $4,451 an acre in 2022, down 3.5% from $4,613 in 2017 and 11th highest of 75. Corn made 170.3 bu in 2025, under the 184.4 bu median since 2008, and 83% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
