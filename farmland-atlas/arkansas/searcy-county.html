Land value in Searcy County reached $2,700 per acre in 2022, up 43.4 percent from 2017, or 7.5 percent a year. The county carried 0 percent irrigated cropland in the 2022 census, and the 2021-2025 period recorded 10 weeks in severe drought or worse against a 2000-2025 rate of 7.2 percent of weeks. The July nightly low averaged 68.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 67 F; the trend since 1976 shows no clear direction.
