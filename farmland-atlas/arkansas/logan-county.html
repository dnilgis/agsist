Cropland in Logan County rented for $33 an acre in 2026, up 120.0% from $15 in 2016. That ranks 23rd highest of 61 in Arkansas, a little over the $30 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $3,129 an acre in 2022, up 25.2% from $2,499 in 2017, and it ranks 20th lowest of 75. Renters worked 33% of the farmland in 2022.
