Lafayette County, Florida

Lafayette County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $49 an acre (2024); land and buildings $4,741 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12067. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lafayette County runs $49 per acre in 2024, up 8.9 percent from 2014. Irrigated cash rent in 2023 is $166 per acre, and 59 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 100 percent of its water from groundwater. July nights average 73.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.5 F, with a warming trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance indemnities for 1998-2025 total $9.4 million, with wind the largest cause at 39 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 3 percent in 2000-2009 to 27 percent in 2010-2019 to 24 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $4,741 per acre in 2022, up 56.8 percent from 2017.
