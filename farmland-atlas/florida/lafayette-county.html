Cropland in Lafayette County rents for $49 an acre in 2024, up 8.9% from $45 in 2014. Irrigated ground ran $166 in 2023. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,741 an acre in 2022, up 56.8% from $3,024 in 2017, which ranks 14th lowest of 61 against a state median of $6,645. 59% of the farmland here is rented.
