Cropland in Walton County rents for $41 an acre in 2026, down 8.9% from $45 in 2016 and tied for 5th lowest of 21 in Florida. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 46.0% to $5,504 an acre in 2022 from $3,771 in 2017. That still sits below the state median of $6,645 and ranks 19th lowest of 61. Only 8% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
