Walton County, Florida

Walton County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,504 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12131. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Walton County averages $41 per acre in 2026, down 8.9 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census was $5,504 per acre, up 46 percent from 2017, 7.9 percent a year. Heat and drought account for 44 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and that share moved from 21 percent in 1989-1999, to 63 percent in 2000-2009, to 37 percent in 2010-2019, to 54 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low averaged 73.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 72.1 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.49 F per decade since 1976. Only 12 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
