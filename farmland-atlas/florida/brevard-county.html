The census value of land and buildings in Brevard County stood at $7,712 an acre in 2022, up 53.4% from $5,027 in 2017. That works out to 8.9% a year, after a small slip from $5,414 in 2012. The 2022 figure sits above the state median of $6,645 and ranks 24th highest of 61. In 2022, 56% of the farmland here was rented.
