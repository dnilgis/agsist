Brevard County, Florida

Brevard County, FL: land and buildings $7,712 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.96 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation covers 81 percent of harvested cropland in Brevard County, with 93 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. Land was valued at $7,712 per acre in 2022, up 53.4 percent from 2017. The July average nightly low ran 75.4 F during 2017-2026 against a 74.1 F normal, with a warming trend of 0.63 F per decade since 1976.
