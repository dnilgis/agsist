The census value of land and buildings in Taylor County was $4,478 an acre in 2022. That is up 22.7% from $3,650 in 2017, or 4.2% a year, after sitting at $3,805 in 2012. It ranks 3rd lowest of 61 in Florida, well under the state median of $6,645. Only 19% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
