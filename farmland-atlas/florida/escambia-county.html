Non-irrigated cash rent in Escambia County runs $96 per acre, up 3.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census puts land and buildings at $7,806 per acre, with rent at 1.1 percent of that value as of 2022. Irrigation covers 6 percent of harvested cropland, all of it drawn from groundwater. Crop insurance history leans on excess moisture and flood, the largest cause of the $22.2 million paid out over 1989-2025, with a loss ratio of 0.72 across that span and 0.77 over 2016-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 15 percent, 22 percent, 36 percent, and 24 percent across the four decades listed. Drought weeks stayed low in 2021-2025, at 18.
