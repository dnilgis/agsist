Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

63.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.01 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,215 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Recorded farm sales: median $11,536 an acre, 8 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $9,445 and $19,449. Land and buildings: $8,209 an acre in the 2022 census. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $42.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 11.8% from 2016 to 2026. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 226.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 33,789 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.10 per base acre, PLC $2.84.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (113.21 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $38.10 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.02 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.85 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.14 per base acre, PLC $7.03. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.96 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $41.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.68 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.40, 2016 to 2025. 29.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 5.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 40%.
