Cropland rent in Marion County runs $30 an acre in 2026, up 7.1% from $28 in 2016 and the 3rd lowest of 21 against a state median of $44. The census value of land and buildings tells it the other way, at $12,964 an acre in 2022, up 16.6% from $11,114 in 2017 and 9th highest of 61. Pasture rent was $22 an acre in 2025, and 27% of farmland was rented in 2022.
