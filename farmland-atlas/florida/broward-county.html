Broward County, Florida

Broward County, FL: land and buildings $35,434 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.75 since 1990. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land here is valued by operators at $35,434 per acre, and that value rose 6.9 percent from 2017-2022, 1.3 percent a year. The July nightly low averaged 75.5 F over 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 74.4 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.51 F per decade, warming. Wind, not heat or drought, has driven crop insurance losses: wind accounts for 66 percent of the $8.7 million paid out over 1990-2025, while heat and drought made up 0 percent of indemnities in every period from 1989-1999 through 2020-2025. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 112 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 31 weeks, and 24 weeks fell in that category from 2021-2025.
