The census value of land and buildings in Putnam County ran $4,904 an acre in 2022, up 23.3% from $3,977 in 2017, or 4.3% a year. That still sits under the state median of $6,645, and it ranks 16th lowest of 61. It also has not caught back up to the $5,529 operators put down in 2012. Irrigated cropland rented for $260 an acre in 2021, and 40% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
