Putnam County, Florida

Putnam County, FL: irrigated cash rent $260 an acre (2021); land and buildings $4,904 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.64 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12107. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Putnam County land was valued at $4,904 per acre in 2022, up 23.3 percent from 2017. The July nightly low has been warming at 0.66 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 73.8 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.4 F, and severe drought exposure ran 149 of 1357 weeks during 2000-2025 but fell to 0 weeks during 2021-2025.
