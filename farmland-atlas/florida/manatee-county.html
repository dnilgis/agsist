Cropland rent in Manatee County runs $41 an acre for 2026, tied for 5th lowest of 21 counties in Florida and under the state median of $44. That rent is down 5.7% from $44 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $9,523 an acre for 2022, 16th highest of 61, and up 22.3% from $7,784 in 2017. 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
