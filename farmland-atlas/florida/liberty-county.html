Land value in Liberty County stood at $4,646 per acre in 2022, up 18.6 percent from 2017, a pace of 3.5 percent a year. July nights average 72.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.5 F, with warming since 1976 at 0.5 F per decade. Drought weeks ran 140 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 25 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Crop insurance indemnities from 2001-2020 totaled $437,552, with other causes at 54 percent the largest driver; heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 0 percent in 2000-2009 to 12 percent in 2020-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
