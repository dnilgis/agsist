Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,133 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,646 an acre in the 2022 census.

Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.47 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 0.9% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 34.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,543 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.
