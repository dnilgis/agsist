Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 51.67 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 108% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,289 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Recorded farm sales: median $20,866 an acre, 18 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $12,456 and $54,889. Land and buildings: $9,390 an acre in the 2022 census. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $51.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). USDA cash rent rose 87.3% from 2016 to 2026. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 225.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 74.9 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,205 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.
