As of Oct 16, Hernando County cash rent sits at $51 an acre in 2025, up 131.8% from $22 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $11,831 an acre in 2022, up 12.8% from $10,488 in 2017, or 2.4% a year. That ranks 14th highest of 61 in Florida, well above the state median of $6,645. 37% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
