Hernando County, Florida

Hernando County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $51 an acre (2025); land and buildings $11,831 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hernando County averages $51 per acre in 2025, up 131.8 percent from 2014. Land value and buildings stood at $11,831 per acre in 2022, up 12.8 percent from 2017, a change of 2.4 percent a year. July nights average 74.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.8 F, with a warming trend of 0.67 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance losses have stayed low: a loss ratio of 0.11 across 1995-2025 closed crop years, and heat and drought made up 0 percent of indemnities in both 2010-2019 and 2020-2025.
