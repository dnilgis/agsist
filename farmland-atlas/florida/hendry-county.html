Hendry County, Florida

Hendry County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,646 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12051. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent runs $310 per acre in 2025, against a non-irrigated rate of $42 per acre for 2026. Land value fell 5.2 percent from 2017-2022, a drop of 1.1 percent a year, with the 2022 census figure at $4,646 per acre. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $323.0 million, mostly from wind at 47 percent; heat and drought stayed at 0 percent of indemnities from 1989-1999 through 2010-2019, then rose to 3 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio climbed to 1.6 over 2016-2025, above the 1.13 ratio for the full 1989-2025 span, and July nights have warmed to a 74.4 F average low for 2017-2026 against a 73.2 F normal for 1991-2020.
