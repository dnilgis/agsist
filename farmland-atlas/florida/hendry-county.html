Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 53.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 108% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,408 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,646 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $41.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.25 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 26, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 48% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 110.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 223.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,065 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Hendry County

What is cash rent in Hendry County, Florida?

The county average is $42 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $310 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Hendry County, Florida?

Land and buildings were worth $4,646 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
