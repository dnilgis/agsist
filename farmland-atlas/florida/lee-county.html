Non-irrigated cash rent in Lee County averaged $70 per acre in 2022. Land value and buildings reached $13,080 per acre in 2022, up 7.2 percent from 2017, or 1.4 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $62.5 million, with wind the largest cause at 60 percent; heat and drought accounted for 0 percent of indemnities in every period listed, from 1989-1999 through 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 1989-2025 stands at 1, with 9 of 26 crop years paying out more than premium; the ratio over 2016-2025 is 2.23, and indemnity paid per $100 of insured value in that span is $6.21.
