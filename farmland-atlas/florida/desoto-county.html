As of Oct 16, DeSoto County cropland rented for $41 an acre in 2025, up 19.1% from $34 in 2014. Irrigated ground ran $191 in 2025 and pasture $18 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $6,531 an acre in 2022, up 30.4% from 2017, which ranks 29th lowest of 61 against a state median of $6,645. 47% of farmland was rented in 2022.
