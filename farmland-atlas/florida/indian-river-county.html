The census value of land and buildings in Indian River County was $6,645 an acre in 2022. That is up 15.7% from $5,745 in 2017, or 3.0% a year. It sits right at the Florida median of $6,645 and ranks 31st lowest of 61. Pasture rented for $25 an acre in 2026, and 25% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
