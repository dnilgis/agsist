Indian River County, Florida

Indian River County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $62 an acre (2009); land and buildings $6,645 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Ninety percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with 51 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. The July average nightly low measured 74.5 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.2 F, trending warmer at 0.49 F per decade since 1976. Census land value stood at $6,645 per acre in 2022, up 15.7 percent from 2017.
