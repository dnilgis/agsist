St. Lucie County, Florida

St. Lucie County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $29 an acre (2016); land and buildings $6,408 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12111. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent ran $255 per acre in 2020, and census market value of land and buildings reached $6,408 per acre in 2022, up 7.2 percent over 2017-2022. The county carries 95 percent of harvested cropland under irrigation, with 34 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. July nightly lows are trending warmer at 0.51 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 75.1 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.6 F.
