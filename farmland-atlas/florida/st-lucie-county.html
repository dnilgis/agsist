Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 69.13 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 135% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,422 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Recorded farm sales: median $24,983 an acre, 26 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $18,455 and $42,950. Land and buildings: $6,408 an acre in the 2022 census. Soils: NCCPI 0.28 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 41% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 195.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 298 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,581 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.44 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.78, 2016 to 2025. 94.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 2.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.7%.
