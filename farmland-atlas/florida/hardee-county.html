Hardee County, Florida

Hardee County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $14 an acre (2024); land and buildings $6,616 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12049. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated ground covers 80 percent of harvested cropland here, and 99 percent of that water comes from groundwater, making aquifer access the central fact of this land. Non-irrigated cash rent fell down 50.9 percent from 2012 to 2024, landing at $14 per acre, while irrigated rent stood at $145 per acre in 2021. Land value reached $6,616 per acre in 2022, up 24 percent from 2017.
