Cash rent in Hardee County was $14 an acre in 2024, down 50.9% from $29 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,616 an acre in 2022, up 24.0% from $5,337 in 2017, or 4.4% a year. That sits just under the state median of $6,645 and ranks 30th lowest of 61. In 2022, 24% of the county's farmland was rented.
