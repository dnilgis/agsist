As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Pinellas County runs $25,955 an acre for 2022. That is down 54.8% from $57,464 in 2017, or 14.7% a year, and below the $30,143 of 2012. It still sits well above the state median of $6,645 across 61 counties. Only 2% of farmland here was rented in 2022.

Pinellas County had 3,744 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Sources and terms

Every figure is a county average from the sources below, not an appraisal of any farm. "Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed. Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields. Loss ratio and claims: USDA RMA Summary of Business. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor. July lows: NOAA nClimDiv county monthly minimum temperature.
