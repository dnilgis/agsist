Pinellas County had 3,744 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Pinellas County was $25,955 per acre in the 2022 census, the operators' own estimate. July nights average 77.2 F for 2017-2026, up from a 75.8 F normal in 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.56 F per decade, warming. Drought held at severe level or worse for 132 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, with 2000 the worst year at 33 weeks, and 42 weeks fell in that range during 2021-2025.
