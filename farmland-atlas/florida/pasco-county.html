Cash rent in Pasco County ran $30 an acre in 2025, up 11.1% from $27 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $12,190 an acre in 2022, up 70.2% from $7,161 in 2017. That ranks 12th highest of 61 in Florida, against a state median of $6,645.
