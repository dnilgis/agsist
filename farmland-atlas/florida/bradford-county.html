Bradford County, Florida

Bradford County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $17 an acre (2025); land and buildings $6,042 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bradford County averages $17 per acre in 2025, down 62.2 percent from 2014. Census value of land and buildings stood at $6,042 per acre in 2022, up 26.5 percent from 2017, or 4.8 percent a year. July nights average 72.4 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.5 F, part of a warming trend of 0.5 F per decade since 1976. Drought held severe or worse levels for 142 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with the worst year, 2000, at 35 weeks, and only 2 such weeks from 2021-2025. All irrigation water came from groundwater as of 2015. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed for this county.
