Irrigation defines farming here: 93 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 83 percent of that water from groundwater. The July average nightly low ran 75.4 F during 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 74 F, trending up at 0.57 F per decade since 1976.
