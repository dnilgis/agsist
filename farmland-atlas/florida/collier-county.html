Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

24.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 50.72 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,468 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,076 an acre in the 2022 census.

Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.28 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 26, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 56.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 149 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,397 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $50.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.69, 2016 to 2025. 93.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 39.3%.

Quick answers for Collier County

What is cash rent in Collier County, Florida?

The county average is $351 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Collier County, Florida?

Land and buildings were worth $6,076 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
