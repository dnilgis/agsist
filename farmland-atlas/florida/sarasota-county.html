The census value of land and buildings in Sarasota County was $12,958 an acre in 2022, up 40.9% from $9,195 in 2017, or 7.1% a year. Back in 2012 it was $4,814. That puts the county 10th highest of 61 in Florida, well above the state median of $6,645. 27% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
