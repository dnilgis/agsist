Wakulla County, Florida

Wakulla County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2021); land and buildings $9,146 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12129. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

The July average nightly low ran 73.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 72 F, with a warming trend of 0.59 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought or worse covered the county during 135 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2000 the worst year at 36 weeks.
