Clay County, Florida

Clay County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2016); land and buildings $6,932 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Clay County land and buildings were valued at $6,932 per acre in the 2022 census, up 21.1 percent from 2017, or 3.9 percent a year. Non-irrigated cash rent came in at $39 per acre in 2016. Only 7 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in 2022, with 19 percent of that irrigation water drawn from groundwater in 2015. The July average nightly low ran 73.3 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.1 F, on a warming trend of 0.57 F per decade since 1976; severe drought touched the county in 146 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, though the 2021-2025 period recorded 0 such weeks.
