Flagler County, Florida

Flagler County, FL: irrigated cash rent $264 an acre (2020); land and buildings $6,070 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.98 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land in Flagler County carries a census value of $6,070 per acre, up 9.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 1.8 percent a year. Irrigation covers 74 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 98 percent of that water, as of the 2015 measure. The July nightly low has moved from a 72.8 F normal (1991-2020) to 74.2 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.62 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance history since 1989 shows excess moisture and flood driving 65 percent of the $11.6 million in indemnities, with heat and drought shares staying low across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025.
