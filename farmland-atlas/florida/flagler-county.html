Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 44.46 inches, 92% of normal (48.07 inches) through Nov 17, at Palm Coast, 0.8 mi NNW. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,328 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,070 an acre in the 2022 census.

Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. 43% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 68 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 40,545 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk
