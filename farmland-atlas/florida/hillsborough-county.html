Hillsborough County, Florida

Hillsborough County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $43 an acre (2026); land and buildings $15,276 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Hillsborough County runs $569 per acre against $43 per acre non-irrigated, a multiple of 13.23 in 2026 versus 13.24 in 2010, with no clear direction over that span. Non-irrigated rent is up 83 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value carries at $15,276 per acre, up 30.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.5 percent a year. July nights average 75.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 73.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.68 F per decade, warming.
