Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Farmland sold for a median $48,075 an acre.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

7.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.75 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,453 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Recorded farm sales: median $48,075 an acre, 17 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $29,626 and $59,073. Land and buildings: $15,276 an acre in the 2022 census. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $43 an acre (USDA, 2026). USDA cash rent rose 83% from 2016 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 163.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 74.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,981 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $41.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.21 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.34, 2016 to 2025. 58.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 4.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 53.6%.
