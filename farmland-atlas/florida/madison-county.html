Cash rent in Madison County runs $22 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 21 counties, against a state median of $44. That rent is down 48.2% from $43 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 27.8% from $3,527 in 2017 to $4,506 in 2022, and that still sits 5th lowest of 61.
