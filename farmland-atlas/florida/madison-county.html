Land

Recorded farm sales: median $7,931 an acre, 6 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $6,059 and $8,750. Land and buildings: $4,506 an acre in the 2022 census. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2026). Soils: NCCPI 0.53 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 48.2% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 14% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 166 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 74.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 40,232 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.
