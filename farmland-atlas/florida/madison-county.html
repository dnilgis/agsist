Madison County, Florida

Madison County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,506 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12079. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County is $22 per acre, down 48.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent in 2024 was $278 per acre. Census land and buildings value in 2022 stood at $4,506 per acre, up 27.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5 percent a year. July average nightly low has moved from 71.3 F normal to 72.8 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.61 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought's share of indemnities ran 26 percent in 1989-1999, 9 percent in 2000-2009, 47 percent in 2010-2019, and 6 percent in 2020-2025. The county's loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.26, above the 1989-2025 ratio of 1.09.
