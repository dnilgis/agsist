As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Dixie County was $4,694 an acre in 2022, up 26.1% from $3,722 in 2017, or 4.7% a year. That figure sits under the state median of $6,645 and ranks 13th lowest of 61. The last cash rent on record is $53 an acre from 2012. In 2022, 37% of the county's farmland was rented.
