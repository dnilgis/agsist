Gulf County, Florida

Gulf County, FL: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

July nights average 73.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.7 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.44 F per decade, warming. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. Drought has touched at least 50 percent of the county in 130 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, with 2000 the worst year at 37 weeks, and 22 weeks fell in that category between 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates in Gulf County, and none is proposed to EIA.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
