Cropland in Okeechobee County rents for $41 an acre in 2026, a touch under the $44 state median and tied for 5th lowest of 21. Pasture goes for $18 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,713 an acre in 2022, up 35.3% from $4,223 in 2017. That puts it 21st lowest of 61 against a state median of $6,645, with 32% of farmland rented in 2022.
