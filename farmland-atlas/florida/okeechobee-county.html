Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,364 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Recorded farm sales: median $15,001 an acre, 23 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $10,694 and $19,248. Land and buildings: $5,713 an acre in the 2022 census. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $41 an acre (USDA, 2026). Soils: NCCPI 0.26 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 12% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 482.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 149 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,521 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk
