The census value of land and buildings in Martin County was $7,304 an acre in 2022, up 66.2% from $4,396 in 2017. That puts it 26th highest of 61 in Florida, above the state median of $6,645. Cash rent was $37 an acre in 2019, and pasture rent was $10 in 2026. 58% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
