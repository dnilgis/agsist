Martin County, Florida

Martin County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2019); land and buildings $7,304 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12085. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Land value in Martin County stood at $7,304 per acre in 2022, up 66.2 percent from 2017, a pace of 10.7 percent a year. Cash rent was $37 per acre non-irrigated in 2019 and $150 per acre irrigated in 2016. Irrigation covers 68 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with only 9 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Crop insurance losses since 1989 total $59.2 million, driven mostly by wind at 64 percent; heat and drought made up 0 percent of indemnities through 2019 and 5 percent from 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.67 F per decade since 1976, reaching 75.7 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.9 F.
