Franklin County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land in Franklin County carried a census market value of $6,207 per acre in 2022, up 219.5 percent from 2012, or 12.3 percent a year. The July average nightly low ran 73.7 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.9 F, with a warming trend of 0.51 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought touched 125 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025, or 9.2 percent, with 2000 the worst year at 36 weeks; 24 of those drought weeks fell in 2021-2025.
