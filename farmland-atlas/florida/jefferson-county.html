Cropland rent in Jefferson County runs $44 an acre for 2025, up 12.8% from $39 in 2014. Pasture rents for $42 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $5,049 an acre in 2022, up 45.6% from $3,468 in 2017, and it ranks 18th lowest of 61 against a state median of $6,645. 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
