Recorded prices. Not an appraisal. No adjustments. A recorded price is what the state's transfer record says was paid once the sale closed, auction or private; never an asking price.

Sales: Florida Department of Revenue, sale data files joined to the assessment roll, agricultural land (sale months, not days). Rent: USDA NASS.

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,049 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.19 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.58 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,153 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,049 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44 an acre (USDA, 2025). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.61 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 21% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 14% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 153.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 74.9 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,083 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.05 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.59 per base acre, PLC $6.73. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.26 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $26.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.45 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.45, 2016 to 2025. 25.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 6.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 24.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.1%.

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
