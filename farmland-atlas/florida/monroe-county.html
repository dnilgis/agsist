Monroe County, Florida

Monroe County, FL: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12087. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

July nights average 77.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 76.1 F normal for 1991-2020. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.58 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched at least 50 percent of the county in 81 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2011 with 18 weeks, and 21 weeks from 2021-2025. There is no solar or wind operating or proposed in this county.
