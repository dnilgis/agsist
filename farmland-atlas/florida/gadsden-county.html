Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,409 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.67 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 55.50 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 111% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,093 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,409 an acre in the 2022 census.

Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. 6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 63.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,004 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Cash rent in Gadsden County sat at $37 an acre in 2019, up 17.7% from $31 in 2009. The census value of land and buildings was $4,409 an acre in 2022, up 3.6% from $4,256 in 2017, which works out to 0.7% a year. That puts the county 2nd lowest of 61 in Florida, well under the state median of $6,645. Only 6% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
