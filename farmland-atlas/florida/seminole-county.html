The census value of land and buildings in Seminole County sits at $12,610 an acre for 2022. That is up 52.5% from $8,271 in 2017, or 8.8% a year, after $7,872 back in 2012. It runs well above the state median of $6,645 and ranks 11th highest of 61 in Florida. Only 17% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
