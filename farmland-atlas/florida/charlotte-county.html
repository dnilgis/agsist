Cropland in Charlotte County rents for $27 an acre in 2026, the 2nd lowest of 21 in Florida against a state median of $44. Pasture goes for $12 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $8,300 an acre in 2022, up 15.7% from $7,176 in 2017, or 3.0% a year, and that ranks 19th highest of 61. Renters worked 55% of the farmland in 2022.
