As of Oct 16, cropland in Holmes County rents for $48 an acre in 2026, up 17.1% from $41 in 2016. That puts it 9th highest of 21 against a state median of $44. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,558 an acre in 2022, up 36.7% from 2017, still 7th lowest of 61 in Florida. Only 15% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
