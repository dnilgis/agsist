Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

67.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 47.14 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,194 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Recorded farm sales: median $9,054 an acre, 8 sales.

Sales from Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026; half fell between $6,384 and $10,684. Land and buildings: $6,666 an acre in the 2022 census. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $55 an acre (USDA, 2026). USDA cash rent fell 19.1% from 2016 to 2026. 49% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains under 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 18% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 353.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 37,844 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.64 per base acre, PLC $2.64.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (105 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $41.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.99 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.44, 2016 to 2025. 56.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 32.1%.
