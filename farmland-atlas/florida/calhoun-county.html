Cash rent in Calhoun County runs $54 an acre for 2024, down 12.3% from $61 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, climbing 109.0% from $2,154 in 2017 to $4,502 in 2022. That still sits under the state median of $6,645 and ranks 4th lowest of 61 in Florida. Only 11% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
