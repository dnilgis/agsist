Nassau County, Florida

Nassau County, FL: land and buildings $4,919 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12089. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value here runs $4,919 per acre, up 31.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 5.6 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 2012-2024 total $507,266, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 52 percent; heat and drought made up 11 percent of indemnities in 2010-2019 and 0 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low averaged 72.3 F in the 1991-2020 normal and 73.4 F in 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been light lately: 1 week in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 157 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000. Irrigation covers 4 percent of harvested cropland, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015.
