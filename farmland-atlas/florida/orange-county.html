Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 56.81 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 117% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,410 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,106 an acre in the 2022 census.

Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.28 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 69% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 138.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 32,930 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Irrigated cropland in Orange County rents for $300 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,106 an acre in 2022, up 19.9% from $8,432 in 2017, or 3.7% a year. That puts the county 15th highest of 61 in Florida, against a state median of $6,645. Renters work a lot of this ground, with 69% of farmland rented in 2022.
