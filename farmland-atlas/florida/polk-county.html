Cash rent in Polk County ran $26 an acre in 2023, up 48.6% from $18 in 2013. Irrigated ground rented for $190 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,140 an acre in 2022, up 17.7% from 2017, which ranks 28th highest of 61 against a state median of $6,645.
