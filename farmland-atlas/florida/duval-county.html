Duval County, Florida

Duval County, FL: land and buildings $13,486 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12031. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value here sits at $13,486 per acre, up 79.3 percent from 2017-2022, or 12.4 percent a year. July nights average 74 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. Drought has been light in recent years: zero weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 134 of 1357 weeks (9.9 percent) for 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 38 weeks. Irrigation reaches 28 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and 42 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015.
