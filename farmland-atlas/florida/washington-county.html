As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Washington County runs $44 an acre for 2026, down 13.9% from $51 in 2016 and right at the state median of $44. The census value of land and buildings was $4,787 an acre in 2022, up 25.3% from $3,820 in 2017, which ranks 15th lowest of 61 against a state median of $6,645. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
