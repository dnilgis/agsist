Glades County carried 92 percent of its harvested cropland under irrigation in 2022, drawing 7 percent of that water from groundwater. Land and buildings were valued at $4,623 per acre in 2022, up 7.3 percent from 2017. The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 was 74.2 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.8 F, continuing a warming trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976.
