As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Glades County was $4,623 an acre in 2022. That is up 7.3% from $4,308 in 2017, or 1.4% a year, and it was $3,056 back in 2012. It sits under the state median of $6,645 and ranks 9th lowest of 61. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
