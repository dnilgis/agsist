Land

Land and buildings: $7,637 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 0.5% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 19.5% from 2016 to 2026. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 328 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 74.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,704 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Columbia County

What is cash rent in Columbia County, Florida?

The county average is $35 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $129 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Columbia County, Florida?

Land and buildings were worth $7,637 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
