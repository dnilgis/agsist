Columbia County, Florida

Columbia County, FL: non-irrigated cash rent $35 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,637 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Columbia County stands at $35 per acre in 2026, down 19.5 percent from 2016, while irrigated rent reached $129 per acre in 2023. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.48 F per decade since 1976, reaching a 2017-2026 average of 72.2 F against the 1991-2020 normal of 71.3 F.
