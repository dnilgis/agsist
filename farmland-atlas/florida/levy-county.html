Quick answers for Levy County

What is cash rent in Levy County, Florida?

The county average is $58 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $133 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Levy County, Florida?

Land and buildings were worth $6,275 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
