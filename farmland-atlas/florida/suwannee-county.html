Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

26.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.83 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 30 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,130 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.13 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).
