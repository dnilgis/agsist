Land value per the census stands at $13,626 per acre, with a change of 60.2 percent from 2017-2022, or 9.9 percent a year. Irrigation covers 86 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 100 percent of that water, so the aquifer carries the load here. July nights have warmed from a 72.9 F normal to 74.3 F over 2017-2026, continuing a trend of 0.63 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been light lately: 0 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 134 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025 counting back to 2000. Crop insurance losses lean toward excess moisture and flood, at 48 percent of the $19.0 million paid across closed crop years 1989-2025.
