Osceola County, Florida

Osceola County, FL: irrigated cash rent $200 an acre (2023); land and buildings $4,578 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.84 since 1995. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 12097. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land in Osceola County was valued at $4,578 per acre in 2022, up 18 percent from 2017 at 3.4 percent a year. Severe drought touched 108 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, though only 4 weeks fell in 2021-2025. The county draws 81 percent of irrigation water from groundwater, and the July nightly low has warmed at 0.65 F per decade since 1976, reaching a 2017-2026 average of 74.7 F against the 73.2 F normal.
