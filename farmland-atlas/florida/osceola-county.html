As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Osceola County was $4,578 an acre in 2022, up 18.0% from $3,880 in 2017. That sits 8th lowest of 61 in Florida, well under the state median of $6,645. Irrigated ground rented for $200 an acre in 2023, and 59% of the county's farmland was rented in 2022.
