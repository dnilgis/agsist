Wilkes County, Georgia

Wilkes County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,614 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13317. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wilkes County runs $24 per acre in 2026, up 9.3 percent from 2016. July nights average 70.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 69.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.47 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2025 total $341,598, with heat and drought the largest cause at 68 percent, a share that holds steady at 68 percent for 2020-2025. Drought weeks at severe level or worse ran 301 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst year 2008 with 53 weeks, but only 7 weeks from 2021-2025. Solar and wind operating and proposed both stand at 0 MW.
