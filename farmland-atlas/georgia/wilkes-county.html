Cropland rent in Wilkes County runs $24 an acre for 2026, the 3rd lowest of 105 against a state median of $58. That rent sat at $22 in 2016, so it is up 9.3% in ten years. The census value of land and buildings was $3,614 an acre in 2022, up 4.8% from $3,447 in 2017, or 1.0% a year. That value ranks 52nd lowest of 135 against a state median of $4,057.
