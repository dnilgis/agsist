Stands out against its own record

21 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2016 (23 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Rent in Franklin County runs $34 an acre for 2026, up 38.8% from $25 in 2016, and that is tied for 17th lowest of 105 in Georgia against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings sits the other way, at $7,127 an acre in 2022, up 24.0% from $5,748 in 2017 and 19th highest of 135. Corn trends at 138.7 bu for 2026, about even with the state median of 138.2 bu.
