Franklin County, Georgia

Franklin County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,127 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13119. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County averages $34 per acre in 2026, up 38.8 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $7,127 per acre, up 24 percent from 2017, 4.4 percent a year. Cropland here is mostly dry-farmed, with only 3 percent irrigated as of the 2022 census. July nights have warmed from a 68.9 F normal to 70.1 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.37 F per decade trend since 1976. Drought weeks ran 274 of 1357 from 2000-2025, but only 14 from 2021-2025, and the median corn yield for 2013-2025 was 151.1 bushels per acre against a low of 100 in 2020.
