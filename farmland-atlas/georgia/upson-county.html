Upson County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,824 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.49 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

2.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 244 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,758 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,824 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 62.8% from 2016 to 2026. 9.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 135.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,310 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.49 per base acre, PLC $3.46.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (138 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $29.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

4.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 2005 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.2%.
