As of Oct 16, cash rent in Burke County runs $48 an acre in 2026, up 20.3% from $40 in 2016 and tied for 45th lowest of 105 in Georgia. Irrigated ground rents for $182 an acre in 2026, 2nd lowest of 39. The census value of land and buildings was $2,857 an acre in 2022, barely up from $2,843 in 2017 and 16th lowest of 135. Corn made 163.8 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 181.2 bu.
