U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland in White County rents for $53 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $58, which puts it 48th lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings was $11,468 an acre in 2022, up 41.1% from $8,126 in 2017, or 7.1% a year. That ranks 3rd highest of 135 in Georgia, well over the $4,057 state median. Pasture rents for $40 an acre in 2026.
