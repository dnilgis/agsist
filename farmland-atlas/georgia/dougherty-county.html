Dougherty County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,051 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.73 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 36.04 inches, 91% of normal (39.82 inches) through Nov 16, at Albany SW Georgia Regional Airport. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 28 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,086 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,051 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 13.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 12.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 43.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,729 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.73 per base acre, PLC $4.08.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (162.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (17.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $47.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.02 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 2.18 over the same years. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 31.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.8%.

The census value of land and buildings in Dougherty County runs $4,051 an acre for 2022, up 4.7% from $3,869 in 2017. That works out to 0.9% a year, and it sits right about at the state median of $4,057, ranked 67th lowest of 135. Dry cropland rent was $58 an acre in 2017, against $243 for irrigated ground that year. Only 14% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
