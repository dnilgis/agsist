Cash rent in Lowndes County runs $92 an acre for 2026, 24th highest of 105 in Georgia and above the state median of $58. That is up 32.4% from $70 an acre in 2014. Irrigated ground rented for $182 an acre in 2024, and pasture went for $50. In 2022, 26% of the farmland here was rented.
