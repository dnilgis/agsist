Spalding County, Georgia

Spalding County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,320 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13255. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

The 2022 census puts land and buildings at $6,320 per acre, up 23.4 percent over 2017-2022, or 4.3 percent a year. Non-irrigated cash rent runs $27 per acre for 2026. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.45 F per decade since 1976, reaching a 2017-2026 average of 70.8 F against the 1991-2020 normal of 69.3 F. Severe drought or worse covered the county during 303 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, though only 7 such weeks fell in 2021-2025.
