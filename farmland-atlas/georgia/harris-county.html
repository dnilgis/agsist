Harris County, GA: land and buildings $4,181 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13145. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

The county spent 304 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2000-2025, or 22.4 percent of that span, with 2012 reaching 52 weeks; the 2021-2025 period added 15 more such weeks. The July average nightly low ran 70.1 F during 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.4 F, with a warming trend of 0.32 F per decade since 1976.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

Yield: NASS county corn yields, trend fitted here over every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out.
