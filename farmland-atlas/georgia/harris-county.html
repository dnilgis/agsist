U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

The census value of land and buildings in Harris County was $4,181 an acre in 2022. That is down 3.3% from $4,322 in 2017, 0.7% a year, and below the $4,802 of 2012. It still sits a little over the state median of $4,057 and ranks 63rd highest of 135 in Georgia. Just 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
