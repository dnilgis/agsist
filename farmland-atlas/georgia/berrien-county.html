Non-irrigated cash rent in Berrien County runs $103 per acre in 2026, up 36.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $269 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 2.20 in 2009 to 2.61 in 2026, rising. Census land value sits at $4,188 per acre, up 33.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1 bushels per acre a year across 2010-2025, with a median of 158.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.8 in 2024. Crop insurance loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 1.55, higher than the 1.29 ratio across 1989-2025.
