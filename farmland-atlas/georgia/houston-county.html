Cropland rent in Houston County runs $59 an acre for 2026, up 55.3% from $38 in 2016 and ranked 52nd highest of 105 against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,027 an acre in 2022, up 25.0% from $4,021 in 2017, or 4.6% a year. That ranks 47th highest of 135, above the state median of $4,057. 21% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
