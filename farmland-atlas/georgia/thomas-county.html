Thomas County cash rent sits at $121 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 105 in Georgia and well over the $58 state median. That rent is up 50.3% from $81 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,110 an acre in 2022, up 12.9% from 2017 and just over the $4,057 state median. Corn came in at 202.2 bu in 2025, above the county's recent average of 176.6 bu.
