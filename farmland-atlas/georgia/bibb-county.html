Land and buildings in Bibb County, GA were valued at $8,544 per acre in 2022, up 113.3 percent from 2017, or 16.4 percent a year. The July average nightly low ran 71.1 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.1 F, with a warming trend of 0.48 F per decade since 1976.
