As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Elbert County runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 62.2% from $23 in 2016. That still sits under the state median of $58 and ranks 28th lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,492 an acre in 2022, up 14.4% from $3,925 in 2017, and ranks 55th highest of 135. 21% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
