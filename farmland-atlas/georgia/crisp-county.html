Crisp County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,063 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.37 per base acre for corn.

Crisp County vs Georgia: $83 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 70% of the other 104 Georgia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 31st highest of 105).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 19 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,997 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,063 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $83 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.57 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 50% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 25.8% from 2014 to 2026. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 95.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,394 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.37 per base acre, PLC $2.63.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (104.59 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $110.99 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.11 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.58 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.56 per base acre, PLC $7.01. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.84 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $39.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 232 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.80 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.74, 2016 to 2025. 36% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 52.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.5%.
