As of Oct 16, cash rent in Crisp County runs $83 an acre for 2026, tied for 31st highest of 105 in Georgia and above the state median of $58. That is up 25.8% from $66 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,063 an acre in 2022, down 0.1% from $3,065 in 2017 and 28th lowest of 135 in the state. In 2022, 44% of the farmland here was rented.
