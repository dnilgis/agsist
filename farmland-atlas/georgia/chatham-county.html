Chatham County had 7,742 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 74.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.2 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.53 F per decade, warming. Land value fell 6.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 1.3 percent a year, with the 2022 census figure at $4,753 per acre. Irrigation covers 28 percent of harvested cropland. Drought weeks were 162 of 1357 (11.9 percent) from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 45 weeks, and only 9 weeks from 2021-2025. Solar operating stands at 2.2 MW, matching the 2.2 MW proposed to EIA; wind is 0 MW on both counts.
