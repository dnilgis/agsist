DeKalb County, Georgia

DeKalb County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in DeKalb County was $99,639 per acre in the 2022 census, the operators' own estimate, not a sale price. That value changed 112.3 percent from 2017-2022, 16.2 percent a year. July nights average 70.5 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.2 F. The July nightly low has trended warming since 1976 at 0.49 F per decade, and 24 Julys since 1900 have averaged 70 F or above. Drought was rare in recent years: 14 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 287 of 1357 weeks (21.1 percent) for 2000-2025, with 2008 the worst year at 50 weeks.
