As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Fannin County was $10,817 an acre in 2022. That is up 83.3% from $5,902 in 2017, or 12.9% a year, after $6,505 back in 2012. It ranks 4th highest of 135 in Georgia, well over the state median of $4,057. Only 12% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
