Banks County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,926 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.56 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

4.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 38.89 inches, 74% of normal (52.52 inches) through Nov 16, at Baldwin, 1.4 mi ESE. Frost-free season: 243 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,601 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,926 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $28 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 25% from 2016 to 2026. 16.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 23.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 248.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 461 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.56 per base acre, PLC $1.76.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (70.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cash rent in Banks County runs $28 an acre for 2026, up 25.0% from $22 in 2016 and tied for 5th lowest of 105 in Georgia. Pasture rents a touch higher at $30 an acre for 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,926 an acre in 2022, up 28.7% from $5,381 in 2017 and 23rd highest of 135 in the state. Only 17% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
