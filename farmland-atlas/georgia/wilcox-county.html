Wilcox County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,832 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.96 per base acre for corn.

Wilcox County vs Georgia: $125 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 99% of the other 104 Georgia counties with a 2026 rent (2nd highest of 105).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 24 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,016 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,832 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $125 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.58 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 31% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 86.6% from 2016 to 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 103.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 220 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,882 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.96 per base acre, PLC $2.96.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (117.83 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $39.38 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $26.04 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (24.68 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.23 per base acre, PLC $7.57. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.94 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $39.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 20 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.07, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15%.
