Wilcox County, Georgia

Wilcox County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $125 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,832 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 186.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13315. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
