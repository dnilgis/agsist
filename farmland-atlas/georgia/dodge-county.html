Cropland rent in Dodge County runs $82 an acre in 2026, up 30.4% from $63 in 2016 and 33rd highest of 105 in Georgia, where the median is $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,010 an acre in 2022, up 15.8% from $2,600 in 2017 and tied for 23rd lowest of 135 in the state. Corn made 229.0 bu in 2025, well over the 2008 to 2025 median of 170.4 bu.
