Washington County, Georgia

Washington County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $57 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,691 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 186.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13303. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County averages $57 per acre in 2026, up 70.1 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stands at $2,691 per acre, up 27.8 percent from 2017, or 5 percent a year. Median corn yield 2009-2023 is 186.5 bushels per acre, with 2010 as the worst year at 98.6. Heat and drought has driven a falling share of crop insurance indemnities: 78 percent in 1989-1999, 46 percent in 2000-2009, 38 percent in 2010-2019, and 10 percent in 2020-2025, against a 1989-2025 total of $17.4 million in indemnities. Drought weeks have also eased, from 239 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025 to 2 weeks in 2021-2025.
