As of Oct 16, cash rent in Jenkins County runs $74 an acre for 2026, up 50.0% from $49 in 2016 and tied for 43rd highest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings was $2,983 an acre in 2022, up 13.9% from $2,620 in 2017, and that sits 21st lowest of 135 in the state. Corn made 181.9 bu in 2023, a bit over the 175.8 bu median since 2008.
