As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Putnam County runs $40 an acre for 2026, up 46.3% from $27 in 2016, and that sits tied for 29th lowest of 105 against a state median of $58. Pasture rented for $28 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,713 an acre in 2022, up 12.1% from $4,206 in 2017, or 2.3% a year. That ranks 50th highest of 135, above the state median of $4,057.
