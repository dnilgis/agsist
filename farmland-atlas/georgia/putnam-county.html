Putnam County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,713 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.53 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

88% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.58 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,787 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,713 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $39.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 46.3% from 2016 to 2026. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 455.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 28,007 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.53 per base acre, PLC $1.23.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (49.08 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $10.85 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $51.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 46.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 2020 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.3%.
