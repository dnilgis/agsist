Putnam County, Georgia

Non-irrigated cash rent in Putnam County averages $40 per acre in 2026, up 46.3 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $4,713 per acre in 2022, up 12.1 percent from 2017, or 2.3 percent a year. The July average nightly low has moved from 69.7 F, the 1991-2020 normal, to 71.3 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.69 F per decade since 1976. Drought pressure has eased in recent years: 6 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 301 of 1357 weeks (22.2 percent) over 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 52 weeks. Crop insurance paid $684,835 in indemnities for 2020-2025, closed crop years, with heat and drought the largest cause at 47 percent.
