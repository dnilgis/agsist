Appling County, Georgia

Appling County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $101 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,332 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 130.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13001. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Appling County runs $101 per acre, up 20.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated ground rents higher, at $254 per acre in 2025, and 90 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Corn yields show a trend of 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2020, with a median of 130 bushels per acre and a worst year of 79 in 2008. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved the same direction across every period given: 67 percent in 1989-1999, 45 percent in 2000-2009, 33 percent in 2010-2019, and 11 percent in 2020-2025. Land value, per the Census operator estimate, sits at $3,332 per acre, up 2.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
