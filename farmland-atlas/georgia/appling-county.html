Appling County, Georgia

Land and buildings run $3,332 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 25 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,053 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,332 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $101 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 20.2% from 2016 to 2026. 31% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 15% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 60.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 54,285 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $6.68. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $29.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 353 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.41 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.35, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 61% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 24.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22%.
