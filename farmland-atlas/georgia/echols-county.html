Cropland rent in Echols County runs $95 an acre for 2026, tied for 22nd highest of 105 in Georgia against a state median of $58. Pasture rent was $51 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $3,971 an acre in 2022, up 42.3% from $2,791 in 2017, which puts it 62nd lowest of 135 next to a state median of $4,057. 44% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
