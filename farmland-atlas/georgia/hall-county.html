Hall County, Georgia

Hall County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,855 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 112.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13139. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Heat and drought drove 63 percent of the 1 million dollars in crop insurance indemnities paid 1995-2025, and the county spent 248 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2008 alone logging 53 such weeks. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade warming, with the 2017-2026 average at 68.9 F against the 1991-2020 normal of 67.8 F. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated at the 2022 census.
