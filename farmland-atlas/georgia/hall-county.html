Cash rent in Hall County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 57.7% from $26 in 2016 and tied for 33rd lowest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings was $13,855 an acre in 2022, up 48.5% from $9,332 in 2017 and highest of 135 in the state. Corn runs thin here, with a trend yield of 98.5 bu against a state median of 138.2 bu, 2nd lowest of 12.
