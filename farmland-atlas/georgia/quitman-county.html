The census value of land and buildings in Quitman County was $3,653 an acre in 2022. That is up 58.3% from $2,307 in 2017, which works out to 9.6% a year. Back in 2012 it was $2,133. Even after that climb, the county still sits under the Georgia state median of $4,057.

Quitman County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
