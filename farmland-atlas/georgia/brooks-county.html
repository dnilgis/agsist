Cash rent in Brooks County runs $106 an acre for 2026, up 28.5% from $83 in 2016 and tied for 16th highest of 105 in Georgia, where the state median is $58. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,723 an acre in 2022, up 35.1% from $3,496 in 2017. Corn made 190.6 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 143.6 bu.
