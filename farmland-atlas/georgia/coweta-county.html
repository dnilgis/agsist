Non-irrigated cash rent in Coweta County runs $29 per acre for 2026, up 31.8 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $6,209 per acre, up 28.7 percent from 2017, or 5.2 percent a year. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. July nights average 70.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.69 F per decade. Drought weeks were low in 2021-2025 at 8, against 300 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 52 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
