U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Coweta County runs $29 an acre for 2026, tied for 8th lowest of 105 in Georgia and well under the state median of $58. That rent is up 31.8% from $22 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits higher, at $6,209 an acre in 2022, 34th highest of 135, and up 28.7% from $4,825 in 2017. Only 11% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
