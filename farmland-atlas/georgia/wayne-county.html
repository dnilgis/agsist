Cash rent in Wayne County runs $76 an acre for 2026, up 67.8% from $45 in 2016 and above the state median of $58. That puts it 39th highest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,579 an acre in 2022, up 72.1% from $2,080 in 2017, though still under the state median of $4,057. Corn made 132.8 bu in 2025, better than the county median of 110.0 bu.
