Dade County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,527 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.30 per base acre for corn.

Dade County vs Georgia: $29.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 9% of the other 104 Georgia counties with a 2026 rent (10th lowest of 105).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

41.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.66 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 223 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,453 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,527 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $29.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 26, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 13% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 92 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.30 per base acre, PLC $3.10.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (123.56 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $73.20 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $27.31 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $48.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 2012 to 2025; too much rain caused 8.4%.

Quick answers for Dade County

What is cash rent in Dade County, Georgia?

The county average is $30 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Dade County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $4,527 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
