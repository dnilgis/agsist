Cash rent in Lee County runs $74 an acre for 2026, up 3.5% from $71 in 2014, against a state median of $58. Irrigated ground rents for $270 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 39 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,563 an acre in 2022, up 5.3% from $3,384 in 2017, or 1.0% a year, and 49th lowest of 135. 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
