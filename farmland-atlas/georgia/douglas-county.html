Douglas County had 7,323 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Douglas County land carries a census value of $7,432 per acre, with the change from 2017-2022 at 11 percent, 2.1 percent a year. July nights have averaged 70.1 F over 2017-2026, up from a 68.6 F normal in 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.67 F per decade, warming. Ten Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F. Drought pressure has eased in recent years: severe drought or worse held the county for 256 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2000 with 46 weeks, versus just 6 weeks from 2021-2025. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated.
