U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Bleckley County runs $81 an acre for 2026, up 73.1% from $47 in 2016 and tied for 34th highest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings was $2,967 an acre in 2022, up 15.0% from $2,580 in 2017, and that sits 20th lowest of 135 in the state. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022, and county corn has run a median of 189.7 bu over 11 years.
