Cash rent in Toombs County runs $68 an acre for 2026, up 43.6% from $47 in 2016 and above the state median of $58. Irrigated ground rents for $242 an acre in 2026, 18th highest of 39 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,365 an acre in 2022, up 21.0% from $2,780 in 2017, which still sits 41st lowest of 135. Corn made 191.9 bu in 2025, a bit over the 182.5 bu median.
