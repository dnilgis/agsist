Early County, Georgia

Early County vs Georgia: $87 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 73% of the other 104 Georgia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 28th highest of 105).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 46.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 28 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,056 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,557 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $87 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 60% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 34.9% from 2016 to 2026. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 87.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,430 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $38.45 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $29.95 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.72 per base acre, PLC $7.72. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42.75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $47.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 55 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.45 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.20, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 42.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 18.2%.
