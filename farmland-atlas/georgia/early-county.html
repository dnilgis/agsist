As of Oct 16, cropland in Early County rents for $87 an acre in 2026, up 34.9% from $65 in 2016. That sits well over the state median of $58 and is tied for 28th highest of 105. The census value of land and buildings was $3,557 an acre in 2022, up 38.5% from $2,569 in 2017, still under the state median of $4,057. Corn made 225.1 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 201.7 bu.

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
