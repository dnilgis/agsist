Cash rent in Ware County sits at $83 an acre in 2025, up 3.1% from $80 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,041 an acre in 2022, up 18.6% from $2,565 in 2017. That ranks 26th lowest of 135 in Georgia, under the state median of $4,057. Corn made 137.2 bu in 2025, a bit better than the county median of 130.8 bu since 2008.
