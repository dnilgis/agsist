Farmland here rents for $31 an acre in 2026, tied for 12th lowest of 105 and well under the state median of $58. Pasture goes for $24 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings ran $10,026 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 135, up 52.9% from $6,557 in 2017. Just 22% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
