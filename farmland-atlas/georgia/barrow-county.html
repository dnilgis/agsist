Barrow County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $10,026 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.90 per base acre for corn.

Barrow County vs Georgia: $31 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 11% of the other 104 Georgia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 12th lowest of 105).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.89 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 79% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 243 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,624 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,026 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $31 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.69 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 20% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 325.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,335 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $45.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Quick answers for Barrow County

What is cash rent in Barrow County, Georgia?

The county average is $31 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Barrow County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $10,026 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
