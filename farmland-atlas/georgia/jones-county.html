Cropland in Jones County rented for $25 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $3,562 an acre in 2022, up 26.9% from $2,806 in 2017, or 4.9% a year. That sits under the Georgia median of $4,057 and ranks 48th lowest of 135. 19% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
