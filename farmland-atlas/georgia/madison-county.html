Cropland rent in Madison County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 24.6% from $29 in 2016 and under the state median of $58. That rent sits tied for 25th lowest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $7,085 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from 2017 and 21st highest of 135 in the state. 22% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
