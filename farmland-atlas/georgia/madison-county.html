Madison County, Georgia

Madison County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,085 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13195. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County runs $36 per acre in 2026, up 24.6 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $7,085 per acre in 2022, a rise of 25.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.6 percent a year. Just 3 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. July nights average 70.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 68.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.57 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought caused 66 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2025, with shares of 65 percent for 2010-2019 and 64 percent for 2020-2025; drought weeks fell from 303 of 1357 across 2000-2025 to 16 in 2021-2025.
