Madison County, Georgia

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,085 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.63 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

99.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.20 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 81% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,628 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,085 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 24.6% from 2016 to 2026. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 267.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,262 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.63 per base acre, PLC $1.08.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (17.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.46 per base acre, PLC $7.53. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 66.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.7%.

Cropland rent in Madison County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 24.6% from $29 in 2016 and under the state median of $58. That rent sits tied for 25th lowest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $7,085 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from 2017 and 21st highest of 135 in the state. 22% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
