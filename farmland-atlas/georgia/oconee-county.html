Stands out against its own record

22 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2012 (40 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Oconee County ran $35 an acre in 2025, up 19.0% from $29 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $6,873 an acre in 2022, down 5.0% from $7,235 in 2017. That still ranks 24th highest of 135 in Georgia, above the state median of $4,057. Only 10% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
