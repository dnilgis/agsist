Non-irrigated cash rent in Emanuel County runs $69 per acre, up 34 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated ground rents higher, at $148 per acre in 2025, a multiple of 2.31 times the non-irrigated rate that year, up from 1.78 in 2013 with no clear direction since. Only 15 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 89 percent of that water comes from groundwater. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 75 percent, 61 percent, 52 percent, 15 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed years is 0.69, and land value reached $2,816 per acre in the 2022 census, up 34.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
