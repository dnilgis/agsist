Fayette County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $10,668 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

16.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 34.56 inches, 80% of normal (43.02 inches) through Nov 17, at Peachtree City Falcon Field. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,713 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,668 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 103 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.20 per base acre, PLC $1.15.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $6.14. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $29.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Fayette County's most notable number is how far the summer nights have warmed. The July average low has run 70.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.3 F, and it has been 24 Julys since 1900 at or above 70 F. Land values here jumped 96.1 percent between 2017 and 2022, which is 14.4 percent a year, while the census value still is $10,668 per acre. Water is the bigger pressure: 309 of 1357 weeks since 2000 had at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2012 the worst year at 52 weeks. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated.
