Irwin County, Georgia

Irwin County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $113 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,053 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13155. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
