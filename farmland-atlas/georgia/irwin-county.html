Irwin County cash rent was $113 an acre in 2026, tied for 8th highest of 105 in Georgia and well over the $58 state median. That rent was $96 in 2016, so it is up 17.7% since then. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, down 6.0% from $3,249 in 2017 to $3,053 in 2022. Corn made 172.9 bu in 2024, under the 184.0 bu it averaged from 2020 to 2024.
