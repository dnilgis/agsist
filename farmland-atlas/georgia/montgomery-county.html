Montgomery County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,312 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.60 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,025 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,312 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $80 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 67.7% from 2016 to 2026. 11.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 40.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 791 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.60 per base acre, PLC $1.85.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (73.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $51.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 49 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.09 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.91 over the same years. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 24.5%.

Cash rent in Montgomery County runs $81 an acre for 2026, tied for 34th highest of 105 in Georgia and above the state median of $58. That rent is up 67.7% from $48 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,312 an acre in 2022, up 28.2% from $2,584 in 2017, and it sits 36th lowest of 135 against a state median of $4,057. Only 11% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
