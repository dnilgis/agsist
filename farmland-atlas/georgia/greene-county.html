Cash rent in Greene County sits at $17 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 105 in Georgia against a state median of $58. That is down 35.3% from $26 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 50.9% from $3,586 in 2017 to $5,412 an acre in 2022, which ranks 40th highest of 135 in the state.
