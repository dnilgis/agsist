Cash rent in Candler County runs $75 an acre for 2026, tied for 41st highest of 105 against a state median of $58. That rent is up 43.3% from $52 in 2014. The corn trend sits at 101.9 bu for all practices, 3rd lowest of 12, well under the state median of 138.2 bu. 21% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
