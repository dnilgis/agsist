Mitchell County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,002 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 25 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,064 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,002 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $112 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 37.4% from 2016 to 2026. 40% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 116.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 261 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 15,467 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.08 per base acre, PLC $7.33. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $47.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 54 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.52 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.28, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 59% of the median yield. 60.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 16.4%.
