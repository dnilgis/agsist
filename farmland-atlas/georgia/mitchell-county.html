Cash rent in Mitchell County runs $112 an acre for 2026, up 37.4% from $82 in 2016 and 11th highest of 105 in Georgia, where the median is $58. Irrigated ground rents for $260 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $231. The census value of land and buildings was $4,002 an acre in 2022, up 8.1% from $3,702 in 2017 and a shade under the state median of $4,057.
