Mitchell County, Georgia

Mitchell County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $112 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,002 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13205. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Sixty percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 96 percent of that water from groundwater, so access to a well is the central land question here.
