Cash rent in Effingham County runs $32 an acre for 2026, 14th lowest of 105 in Georgia and well under the state median of $58. That is down 4.5% from $33 in 2016, so rent has barely budged in a decade. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 35.3% from $3,250 in 2017 to $4,398 in 2022, which sits 57th highest of 135 and above the state median of $4,057.
