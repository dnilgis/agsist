Effingham County, Georgia

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,398 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.72 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 66% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,053 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,398 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $31.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 4.5% from 2016 to 2026. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 29 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 10,685 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.74 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (24.28 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.27 per base acre, PLC $6.55. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.29 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $43.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.29 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 5.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 38.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.5%.

Cash rent in Effingham County runs $32 an acre for 2026, 14th lowest of 105 in Georgia and well under the state median of $58. That is down 4.5% from $33 in 2016, so rent has barely budged in a decade. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 35.3% from $3,250 in 2017 to $4,398 in 2022, which sits 57th highest of 135 and above the state median of $4,057.
