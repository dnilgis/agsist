Stephens County, Georgia

Stephens County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,529 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13257. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Stephens County averages $40 per acre for 2026. Census land and buildings value stood at $7,529 per acre in 2022, up 30.4 percent from 2017, 5.5 percent a year. Only 7 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. Drought weeks fell from 258 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025 to 7 weeks between 2021-2025, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.32 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 69.6 F against a 68.6 F normal for 1991-2020. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
