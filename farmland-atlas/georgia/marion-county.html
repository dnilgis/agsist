Cash rent in Marion County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 4.3% from $35 in 2016 and 27th lowest of 105 against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $2,843 an acre in 2022, up 19.7% from $2,375 in 2017, or 3.7% a year. Only 15% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
