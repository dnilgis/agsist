Rabun County, Georgia

Rabun County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,517 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13241. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Rabun County had 6,201 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

The July nightly low trend runs 0.58 F per decade warming since 1976, with no July since 1900 averaging at or above 70 F; no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed.
