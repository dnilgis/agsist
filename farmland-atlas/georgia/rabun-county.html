U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Rabun County runs $66 an acre for 2026, 47th highest of 105 counties and above the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came in at $11,517 an acre in 2022, up 39.8% from $8,240 in 2017. That is 6.9% a year, and well over the state median of $4,057. 23% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Rabun County had 6,201 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
