Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 53.01 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 207 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,028 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $11,517 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $65.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.66 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 180.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.53 per base acre, PLC $1.80.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (71.73 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $15.85 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2018; too much rain caused 11%.

Cash rent in Rabun County runs $66 an acre for 2026, 47th highest of 105 counties and above the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came in at $11,517 an acre in 2022, up 39.8% from $8,240 in 2017. That is 6.9% a year, and well over the state median of $4,057. 23% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Rabun County had 6,201 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
