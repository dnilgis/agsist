Cash rent in Oglethorpe County runs $34 an acre for 2026, tied for 15th lowest of 105 in Georgia and under the state median of $58. That is up 59.5% from $21 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,655 an acre in 2022, 38th highest of 135, up 29.9% from $4,353 in 2017. Just 17% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
