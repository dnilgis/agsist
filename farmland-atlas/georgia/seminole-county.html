Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 55.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 114% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 30 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,112 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,993 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $108 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.54 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 48% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 31.7% from 2016 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 19% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 48.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 19,151 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Seminole County

What is cash rent in Seminole County, Georgia?

The county average is $108 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $274 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Seminole County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,993 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Seminole County, Georgia?

The county's 2020-24 average is 210.1 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
