Seminole County, Georgia

Seminole County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $108 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,993 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 206.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13253. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Seminole County runs $108 per acre in 2026, up 31.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $274 per acre, and irrigated rent has held at 2.54 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026 versus 2.91 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Fifty-six percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and 99 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1 bushel per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 206.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 164 in 2009. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 totaled $97.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 35 percent of that total.
