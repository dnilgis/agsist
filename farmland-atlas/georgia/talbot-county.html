Talbot County, Georgia

Talbot County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2022); land and buildings $2,460 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13263. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Talbot County averaged $23 per acre in 2022. July average nightly low ran 69.3 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 70.2 F over 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.28 F per decade since 1976. Drought at severe level or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 280 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 52 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 12 such weeks. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
