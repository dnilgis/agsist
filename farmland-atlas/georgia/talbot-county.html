Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

15.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 248 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,781 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land: no recorded sale median, census value or published rent for this county.

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.66 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 11% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 41.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 17,366 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $29.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Talbot County's heat is the thing to look at first. The July nightly low has averaged 70.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs warm at 0.32 F per decade. July has averaged a low at or above 70 F in 16 years since 1900. Drought is the other side of it: 280 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 had at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, and 2012 hit 52 weeks. Over 2021-2025 there were 12 such weeks. Cropland in CRP is 12.7 percent, with 160 acres and none expiring through fiscal 2029. Non-irrigated cash rent was $23 per acre in 2022.
