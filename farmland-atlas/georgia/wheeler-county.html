The census value of land and buildings in Wheeler County ran $3,641 an acre in 2022. That was up 99.1% from $1,829 in 2017, or 14.8% a year, and $1,493 back in 2012. It still sits under the state median of $4,057. Cash rent was last reported at $63 an acre in 2013, and irrigated rent at $174 in 2009.

Wheeler County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
