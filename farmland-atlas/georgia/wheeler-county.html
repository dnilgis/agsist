Wheeler County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,641 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 24 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,040 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,641 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 80 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,516 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.84 per base acre, PLC $2.22.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (88.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $51.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 57 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $34.68 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.90, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.4%.

The census value of land and buildings in Wheeler County ran $3,641 an acre in 2022. That was up 99.1% from $1,829 in 2017, or 14.8% a year, and $1,493 back in 2012. It still sits under the state median of $4,057. Cash rent was last reported at $63 an acre in 2013, and irrigated rent at $174 in 2009.

Wheeler County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
