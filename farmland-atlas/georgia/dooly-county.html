Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

14.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: at least 261 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,965 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,211 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $75 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 58% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 18.1% from 2016 to 2026. 40% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 30% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 13.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,187 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $39.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 328 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.38 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.93, 2016 to 2025. 30.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 62.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.5%.

Quick answers for Dooly County

What is cash rent in Dooly County, Georgia?

The county average is $75 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Dooly County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,211 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
