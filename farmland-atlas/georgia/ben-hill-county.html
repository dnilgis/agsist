As of Oct 16, cropland in Ben Hill County rents for $95 an acre in 2026, up 21.0% from $79 in 2016 and tied for 22nd highest of 105 counties in Georgia. Irrigated ground runs $273 an acre in 2026, 4th highest of 39. The census value of land and buildings was $3,024 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from $2,469 in 2017 and 25th lowest of 135. Corn made 154.1 bu in 2024, under the county median of 172.6 bu.
