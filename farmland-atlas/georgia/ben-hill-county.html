Ben Hill County, Georgia

Ben Hill County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $95 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,024 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated land in Ben Hill County rents for $273 per acre in 2026, against $95 per acre non-irrigated. That gap has widened: the irrigated multiple was 2.31 in 2009 and is 2.87 in 2026, and the direction is rising. Corn yields have a median of 172.6 bushels per acre from 2010-2024, with 2014 the worst year at 135.8. Heat and drought drove 36 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, though that share fell across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019 before dropping further to 13 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 203 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with zero such weeks in 2021-2025. Land value rose 22.5 percent from 2017-2022, at 4.1 percent a year.
