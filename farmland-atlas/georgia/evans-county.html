Stands out against its own record

23 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Evans County cash rent stood at $57 an acre in 2023, up 11.8% from $51 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,486 an acre in 2022, up 29.2% from $2,699 in 2017. That sits under the state median of $4,057 and ranks 45th lowest of 135. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
