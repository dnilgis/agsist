Evans County, Georgia

Evans County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $57 an acre (2023); land and buildings $3,486 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.71 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13109. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Evans County averaged $57 per acre in 2023, up 11.8 percent from 2013. Census land value reached $3,486 per acre in 2022, up 29.2 percent from 2017, or 5.3 percent a year. Irrigation covers 39 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 43 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Crop insurance history shows $18.3 million in indemnities over the closed crop years 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 35 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities fell from 62 percent in 1989-1999 to 25 percent in 2000-2009, then rose to 30 percent in 2010-2019, then fell to 6 percent in 2020-2025.
