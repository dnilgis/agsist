Whitfield County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,983 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.89 per base acre for corn.

Whitfield County vs Georgia: $27.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 4% of the other 104 Georgia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 5th lowest of 105).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 228 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,570 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,983 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 16% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 14.6% from 2016 to 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 203.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 139 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $48.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1998 to 2025; too much rain caused 53.4%.

Quick answers for Whitfield County

What is cash rent in Whitfield County, Georgia?

The county average is $28 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Whitfield County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $6,983 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
