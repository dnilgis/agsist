Cropland rent in Habersham County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 54.7% from $27 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $58, tied for 33rd lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $8,912 an acre in 2022, 11th highest of 135 and up 24.5% from $7,160 in 2017. Pasture rents for $43 an acre in 2026, a touch above cropland.
