Cash rent in Sumter County runs $65 an acre in 2026, up 22.6% from $53 in 2016 and above the state median of $58, which puts it 48th highest of 105. The census value of land and buildings was $3,216 an acre in 2022, up 12.5% from $2,859 in 2017, and that sits 33rd lowest of 135. Corn made 189.7 bu in 2023, a little under the 2019-23 average of 193.7 bu.
