Glynn County, Georgia

Glynn County, GA: land and buildings $32,706 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13127. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Glynn County had 1,894 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Glynn County stands at $32,706 per acre, with a change of 112.2 percent from 2017-2022, or 16.2 percent a year. July nightly lows average 73.9 F for 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 73 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.41 F per decade, warming. Irrigation covers 42 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and 18 MW of solar operates in the county with no wind.
