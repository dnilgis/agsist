As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Twiggs County ran $63 an acre in 2022, up 37.4% from $46 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings was $2,361 an acre in 2022, down 2.3% from $2,417 in 2017, and it sits 3rd lowest of 135 against a state median of $4,057. Irrigated rent was $221 an acre in 2026, 16th lowest of 39. 22% of farmland was rented in 2022.
