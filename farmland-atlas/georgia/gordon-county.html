As of Oct 16, cash rent in Gordon County runs $34 an acre for 2026, tied for 15th lowest of 105 in Georgia and well under the state median of $58. That is down 4.3% from $35 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,746 an acre in 2022, 26th highest of 135, up 3.1% from $6,545 in 2017. Pasture rent sits at $42 an acre for 2026.
