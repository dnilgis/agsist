Newton County land and buildings averaged $6,257 per acre in the 2022 census, up 39.8 percent from 2017. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.67 F per decade warming, with the 2017-2026 average at 70.6 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 69 F; the county sat in severe drought or worse for 313 of 1357 weeks during 2000-2025.
