July nightly lows in Richmond County average 72.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 71 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade, warming. Irrigation covers 5 percent of harvested cropland, 2022 census. Heat and drought made up 90 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 63 percent in 2000-2009, 10 percent in 2010-2019, and 25 percent in 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 223 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 0 such weeks from 2021-2025. Land value stood at $4,332 per acre in 2022, up 17.7 percent from 2017. Solar operating capacity is 37.8 MW; no wind is operating or proposed.
