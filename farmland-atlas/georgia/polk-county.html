Polk County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,165 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.77 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 231 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,602 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,165 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $53.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 81.4% from 2016 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 123.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,465 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.96 per base acre, PLC $2.79.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (111.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $9.75. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (54 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $48.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.04 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.28, 2016 to 2025. 1.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 38.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 42.9%.

Stands out against its own record

$54

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 81.4% from $30 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Polk County ran $54 an acre in 2026, up 81.4% from $30 in 2016 and a bit under the $58 state median, 49th lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,165 an acre in 2022, up 42.8% from $3,616 in 2017 after barely moving from $3,606 in 2012. That 2022 figure sits 44th highest of 135, above the $4,057 state median.
