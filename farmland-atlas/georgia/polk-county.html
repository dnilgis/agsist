Stands out against its own record

$54

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 81.4% from $30 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Polk County ran $54 an acre in 2026, up 81.4% from $30 in 2016 and a bit under the $58 state median, 49th lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,165 an acre in 2022, up 42.8% from $3,616 in 2017 after barely moving from $3,606 in 2012. That 2022 figure sits 44th highest of 135, above the $4,057 state median.
