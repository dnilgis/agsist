Pickens County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $9,671 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.20 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

15.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.81 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 217 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,367 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,671 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.64 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 244.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,858 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.20 per base acre, PLC $2.16.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (86 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $66.70 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.01 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $45.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cropland in Pickens County rented for $25 an acre in 2022, and pasture went for $32 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $9,671 an acre in 2022, up 13.4% from $8,529 in 2017, or 2.5% a year. That sits well above the state median of $4,057 and ranks 8th highest of 135. 41% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Pickens County

What is farmland worth in Pickens County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $9,671 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
