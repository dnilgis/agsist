As of Oct 16, cash rent in Jackson County ran $32 an acre in 2024, up 14.3% from $28 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $8,356 an acre in 2022, 13th highest of 135 in Georgia and well over the state median of $4,057. That census value was up 31.2% from $6,370 in 2017.

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
