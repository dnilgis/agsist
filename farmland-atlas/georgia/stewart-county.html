Stewart County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,010 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 260 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,898 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,010 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 18% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 14 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 25,534 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $21.02 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.25, 2016 to 2025. 16.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 66.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 12.2%.
