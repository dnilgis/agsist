Camden County, Georgia

Camden County, GA: land and buildings $3,536 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Camden County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Camden County land carried a value of $3,536 per acre in the 2022 census, up 23.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 4.3 percent a year. July nights average 73.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. Drought pressure has eased in recent years: only 2 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 150 of 1357 weeks countywide from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks. Irrigation reaches 42 percent of harvested cropland, and 30 MW of solar operates in the county with no wind.
