Stands out against its own record

19 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2012 (52 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Crawford County rented for $49 an acre in 2024, down 1.0% from $50 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings ran $5,223 an acre in 2022, up 29.6% from $4,029 in 2017. That puts the county 42nd highest of 135 in Georgia, above the state median of $4,057. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
