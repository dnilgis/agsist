Cropland rent in Jeff Davis County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 19.4% from $93 in 2016. That rent is tied for 12th highest of 105 in Georgia, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,127 an acre in 2022, up 24.8% from $2,505 in 2017 but still under the $3,321 of 2012. Corn made 177.1 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 141.1 bu.
