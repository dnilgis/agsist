As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Jeff Davis County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 19.4% from $93 in 2016. That rent is tied for 12th highest of 105 in Georgia, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,127 an acre in 2022, up 24.8% from $2,505 in 2017 but still under the $3,321 of 2012. Corn made 177.1 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 141.1 bu.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

"Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed; it moves only when a figure on the page does. Source files and their retrieval dates are listed on the data page.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: NASS county corn yields. The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out. Claims paid per $100 of coverage: claims divided by liability (the insured value), times 100, same source. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor, weeks with half the county or more in D2 or worse. July lows: NOAA nClimDiv county monthly minimum temperature. A missing figure is named with its reason (in a table it reads n/a).
