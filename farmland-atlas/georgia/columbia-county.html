As of Oct 16, cash rent in Columbia County runs $26 an acre for 2025. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,571 an acre in 2022, up 47.6% from $4,452 in 2017, or 8.1% a year. That puts the county 28th highest of 135 in Georgia, against a state median of $4,057. 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
