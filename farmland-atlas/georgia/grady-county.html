Grady County cash rent on cropland runs $113 an acre in 2026, tied for 8th highest of 105 in Georgia and well over the state median of $58. That is up 52.7% from $74 in 2016. Irrigated rent sits at $303 in 2026, the highest of 39. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,955 an acre in 2022, up 5.2% from $3,760 in 2017, a little under the state median of $4,057.
