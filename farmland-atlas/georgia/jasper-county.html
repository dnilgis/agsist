Jasper County, Georgia

Jasper County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2019); land and buildings $4,573 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13159. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jasper County averaged $40 per acre in 2019, up 122.2 percent from 2009. All harvested cropland here is non-irrigated; the 2022 census puts irrigated share at 0 percent. Land value, as operators estimate it, stood at $4,573 per acre in 2022, up 31.4 percent from 2017, or 5.6 percent a year. Crop insurance paid out $569,968 across 1995-2025, with heat and drought the largest cause at 49 percent of claims, rising to 51 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed to a 71 F average low for 2017-2026, against a 69.2 F normal for 1991-2020, continuing a warming trend of 0.77 F per decade since 1976.
