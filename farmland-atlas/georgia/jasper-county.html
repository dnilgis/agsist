Jasper County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,573 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.93 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

88.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,739 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,573 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.69 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 11% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 9% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 162.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,356 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.93 per base acre, PLC $1.09.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (43.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $9.61 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $6.15. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34.06 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $51.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.8%.

Cash rent in Jasper County ran $40 an acre in 2019, up 122.2% from $18 in 2009. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,573 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from $3,481 in 2017, or 5.6% a year. That sits above the state median of $4,057 and ranks 53rd highest of 135. Only 9% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
