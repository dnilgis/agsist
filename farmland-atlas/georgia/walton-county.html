Walton County, Georgia

Walton County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,750 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13297. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Walton County runs $34 per acre, up 51.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $6,750 per acre, up 19.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.6 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal low is 68.7 F, the 2017-2026 average is 70.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.58 F per decade, warming. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $881,810, with heat and drought the largest cause at 61 percent; that share ran 67 percent in 1989-1999, 66 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, and 61 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were low from 2021-2025, at 6.
