Stands out against its own record

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Butts County rented for $26 an acre in 2024, and pasture went for $25 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,127 an acre in 2022, up 31.6% from $3,897 in 2017. That puts the county 45th highest of 135 in Georgia, above the state median of $4,057. Only 8% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
