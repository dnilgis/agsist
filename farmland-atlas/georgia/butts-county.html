Butts County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,127 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.89 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,742 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,127 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $25.50 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.66 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 16% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 175.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 614 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.84 per base acre, PLC $1.13.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.08 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $9.96 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $6.58. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.46 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $51.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Quick answers for Butts County

What is cash rent in Butts County, Georgia?

The county average is $26 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Butts County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $5,127 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
