Hart County, Georgia

Hart County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,267 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13147. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hart County stands at $40 per acre in 2026, up 40.4 percent from 2016, against a Census land value of $7,267 per acre in 2022, which itself rose 29.3 percent from 2017-2022. The July average nightly low over 2017-2026 was 70.2 F, above the 1991-2020 normal of 69 F, on a warming trend of 0.42 F per decade since 1976.
