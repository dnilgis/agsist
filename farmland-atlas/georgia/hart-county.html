Cash rent in Hart County runs $40 an acre for 2026, up 40.4% from $29 in 2016 and still under the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $7,267 an acre in 2022, up 29.3% from $5,622 in 2017, and that ranks 18th highest of 135 in Georgia. Corn trends at 163.3 bu, 3rd highest of 12 counties, and last year came in at 192.5 bu.
