Land in Charlton County was valued at $3,119 per acre in 2022, up 28.5 percent over 2017-2022, or 5.1 percent a year. The July average nightly low ran 72.5 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.4 F, with a warming trend of 0.67 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought or worse covered the county during 178 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks.
