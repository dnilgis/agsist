Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

2.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.62 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 105% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 251 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,829 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,338 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.54 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 57.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,194 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $29.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $26.67 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.71, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.6%.

Quick answers for Taylor County

What is cash rent in Taylor County, Georgia?

The county average is $50 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $160 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Taylor County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,338 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
