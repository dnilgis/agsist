Wilkinson County, Georgia

Wilkinson County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2024); land and buildings $2,292 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13319. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Land value rose 11.8 percent over 2017-2022, at 2.3 percent a year. The county spent 262 of 1,357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2012 recording 52 such weeks.
