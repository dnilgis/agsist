Wilkinson County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,292 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.73 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

12.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 255 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,918 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,292 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40.50 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 52.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 14,145 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 74.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.7%.

Quick answers for Wilkinson County

What is cash rent in Wilkinson County, Georgia?

The county average is $41 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Wilkinson County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $2,292 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
