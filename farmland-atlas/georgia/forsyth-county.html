Cropland in Forsyth County rents for $47 an acre in 2026, under the Georgia median of $58 and tied for 43rd lowest of 105. Pasture goes for $37 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings ran $12,514 an acre in 2022, 2nd highest of 135 in the state and up 58.8% from $7,879 in 2017. That is 9.7% a year, while about 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
