Miller County, Georgia

Miller County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $96 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,973 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 198.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13201. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Miller County runs $96 per acre for 2026, up 20.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $259 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated sits at 2.71 in 2026 versus 2.61 in 2009, with no clear direction over that stretch. Irrigation is widespread here: 63 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 99 percent of its water from groundwater. Census land value stands at $3,973 per acre for 2022, up 22.5 percent from 2017.
