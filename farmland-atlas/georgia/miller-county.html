Land

Land and buildings: $3,973 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $95.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.56 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 57% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 20.9% from 2016 to 2026. 50% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 116.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,750 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Miller County is a county where the irrigated ground is the story. Irrigated cash rent is $259 per acre in 2026, and the 2026 rent is $96 per acre for non-irrigated ground, so the irrigation premium is there every year you look. Heat and drought has been the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities here, at 48 percent of the $163.8 million paid over 1989-2025. Its share went from 53 percent in 1989-1999 to 44 percent in 2020-2025. Over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025, the loss ratio was 1.15, so claims ran above premiums. Groundwater supplies 99 percent of irrigation water, and 63 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated.
