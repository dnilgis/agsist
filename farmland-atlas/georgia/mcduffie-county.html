McDuffie County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,810 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.14 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

61.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 245 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,789 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,810 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $77.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 49% from 2016 to 2026. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 155.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 250 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,731 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.14 per base acre, PLC $1.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (64 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $6.88. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $43.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

11.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cash rent in McDuffie County runs $78 an acre for 2026, up 49.0% from $52 in 2016 and 37th highest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $2,810 an acre in 2022, down 6.1% from $2,994 in 2017. That puts the county 11th lowest of 135 in the state, against a state median of $4,057. Only 19% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
