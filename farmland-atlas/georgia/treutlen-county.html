Cash rent in Treutlen County runs $75 an acre for 2026, tied for 41st highest of 105 in Georgia and above the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $2,356 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from $1,883 in 2017. That still sits 2nd lowest of 135 in the state, well under the $4,057 median. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
