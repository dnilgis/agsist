Cropland rent in Meriwether County runs $44 an acre for 2026, up 11.5% from $39 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $58 and ranks 36th lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings was $4,592 an acre in 2022, up 40.8% from $3,262 in 2017, and it ranks 52nd highest of 135 in Georgia. Only 15% of farmland was rented in 2022.
