Gwinnett County, Georgia

Gwinnett County, GA: land and buildings $16,242 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13135. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Gwinnett County had 5,392 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Gwinnett County stood at $16,242 per acre in 2022, up 73.9 percent over 2017-2022, or 11.7 percent a year. The July average nightly low ran 69.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.5 F, with a warming trend of 0.5 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought or worse covered the county during 280 of 1,357 weeks from 2000-2025, or 20.6 percent of that span, with 2008 the worst year at 53 weeks.
