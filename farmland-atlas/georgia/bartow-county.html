USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Bartow County runs $46 an acre in 2026, up 7.0% from $43 in 2016 and tied for 41st lowest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings was $7,089 an acre in 2022, up 17.7% from 2017 and 20th highest of 135. The corn yield trend sits at 192.4 bu, highest of 12.
