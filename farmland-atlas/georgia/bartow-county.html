Bartow County, Georgia

Bartow County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $46 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,089 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13015. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bartow County runs $46 per acre in 2026, up 7 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $7,089 per acre in 2022, having moved 17.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.3 percent a year. Corn yields carry a trend of 3.8 bushels per acre a year across 2009-2025, with a median of 159.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 116.1 in 2014. Heat and drought has driven crop insurance losses, at 61 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 67 percent in 2000-2009, 52 percent in 2010-2019, and 46 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 12 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 222 weeks total from 2000-2025. Only 15 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation as of the 2022 census.
