As of Oct 16, cash rent in Tift County runs $111 an acre for 2026, tied for 12th highest of 105 in Georgia, well over the state median of $58. That is up 11.0% from $100 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,310 an acre in 2022, up 35.8% from $3,174 in 2017. In 2022, 37% of the county's farmland was rented.
