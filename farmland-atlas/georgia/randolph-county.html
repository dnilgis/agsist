Randolph County, Georgia

Land and buildings run $2,912 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,951 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,912 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $62 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 56.2% from 2016 to 2026. 26.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 7.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 62.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,504 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $47.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.77 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.14 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 87% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 48.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 17.8%.
