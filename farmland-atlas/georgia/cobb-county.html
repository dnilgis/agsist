Cobb County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $21,524 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.41 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 237 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,632 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $21,524 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.65 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 20.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 363 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $45.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

The census value of land and buildings in Cobb County was $21,524 an acre in 2022. That is up 88.4% from $11,425 in 2017, or 13.5% a year, after the 2012 census put it at $12,235. The state median across 135 Georgia counties is $4,057. Only 7% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Cobb County had 2,830 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
