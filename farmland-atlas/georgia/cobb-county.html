Cobb County, Georgia

Cobb County, GA: land and buildings $21,524 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13067. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Cobb County had 2,830 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here runs $21,524 per acre, with a change of 88.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 13.5 percent a year. July nights average 70 F for 2017-2026, above the 68.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade, warming. Eleven Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F. Drought was widespread in 2008, with 50 weeks of severe drought or worse, but 2021-2025 saw only 5 such weeks. Solar operating stands at 1.5 MW, with no wind and no proposed capacity listed.
