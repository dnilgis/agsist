Cropland in Hancock County rents for $29 an acre in 2026, tied for 8th lowest of 105 in Georgia against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $2,478 an acre in 2022, up 18.5% from $2,092 in 2017, though still below the $3,424 of 2012. That ranks 7th lowest of 135 in the state, where the median is $4,057. Only 8% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
