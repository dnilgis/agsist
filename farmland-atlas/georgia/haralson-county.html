Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 12 surveys since 2009.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Haralson County ran $31 an acre in 2026, tied for 12th lowest of 105 in Georgia, against a state median of $58. Pasture went for $35 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $6,674 an acre in 2022, up 40.5% from $4,750 in 2017 and 27th highest of 135. Only 14% of farmland was rented in 2022.
