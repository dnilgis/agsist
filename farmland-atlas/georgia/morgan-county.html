Morgan County, Georgia

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,796 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.10 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

97.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,722 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,796 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 22.8% from 2016 to 2026. 24.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 8.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 198.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,250 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.10 per base acre, PLC $1.17.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $51.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.21 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.11 over the same years. 2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1992 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.7%.

Cash rent in Morgan County runs $35 an acre in 2026, up 22.8% from $29 in 2016. That rent is tied for 20th lowest of 105 in Georgia, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,796 an acre in 2022, up 24.0% from $4,673 in 2017, and that ranks 37th highest of 135. 25% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
