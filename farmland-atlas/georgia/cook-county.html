Cook County, Georgia

Cook County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $101 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,057 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 163.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cook County runs $101 per acre in 2026, up 14.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $243 per acre, a multiple of 2.41 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026, against 1.69 in 2009; the direction of that multiple shows no clear direction. Corn yields have trended up 4.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 163.2 bushels per acre and a low of 68.1 in 2011. Crop insurance data show heat and drought as the largest cause of indemnities at 33 percent across 1989-2025, with the loss ratio over 2016-2025 at 1.36. Census land value stands at $4,057 per acre in 2022, up 33.5 percent from 2017.
