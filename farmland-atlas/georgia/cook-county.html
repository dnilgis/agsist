Cash rent in Cook County runs $101 an acre for 2026, up 14.1% from $89 in 2016 and tied for 19th highest of 105 in Georgia, against a state median of $58. Irrigated ground rents for $243 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $4,057 an acre in 2022, up 33.5% from $3,038 in 2017. Corn made 206.8 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 163.2 bu since 2008.
