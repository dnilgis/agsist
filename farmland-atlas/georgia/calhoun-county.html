Cropland rent in Calhoun County runs $87 an acre for 2026, up 70.6% from $51 in 2016 and tied for 28th highest of 105 in Georgia. The census value of land and buildings was $2,997 an acre in 2022, down 0.6% from $3,014 in 2017 and 22nd lowest of 135 in the state. Corn made 225.6 bu in 2025, above the 18 year median of 216.2 bu.
