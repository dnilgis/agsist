Cropland rent in Atkinson County runs $120 an acre in 2026, tied for 4th highest of 105 in Georgia and well over the state median of $58. That rent is up 55.8% from $77 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,362 an acre in 2022, up 15.8% from 2017 but still under the state median of $4,057. Corn made 201.8 bu in 2025 against a median of 162.1 bu since 2009.
