Johnson County, Georgia

Johnson County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $46 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,716 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 164.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13167. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Johnson County averages $46 per acre in 2026, up 51.7 percent from 2016. Land value under the census stands at $2,716 per acre, up 29.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.3 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 1.8 bushels per acre a year across 2010-2025, with a median of 164.6 bushels per acre and a low of 68 in 2010. Crop insurance shows heat and drought as the largest cause of indemnities at 59 percent over 1989-2025, though its share has moved from 91 percent in 1989-1999, 81 percent in 2000-2009, 54 percent in 2010-2019, to 24 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks dropped from 212 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025 to 3 weeks in 2021-2025.
