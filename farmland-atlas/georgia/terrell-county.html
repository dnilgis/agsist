Cash rent in Terrell County runs $77 an acre in 2026, up 19.4% from $65 in 2016 and 38th highest of 105 in Georgia, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,290 an acre in 2022, up 17.4% from $2,803 in 2017 and 34th lowest of 135. Corn made 204.6 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 194.8 bu.
