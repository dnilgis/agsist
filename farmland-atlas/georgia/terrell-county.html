Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,996 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,290 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $77 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 62% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 19.4% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 9.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,322 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Terrell County

What is cash rent in Terrell County, Georgia?

The county average is $77 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $234 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Terrell County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,290 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Terrell County, Georgia?

The county's 2021-25 average is 194.8 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
