Peach County, Georgia

Peach County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,440 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.93 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13225. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Irrigated land dominates Peach County, with 73 percent of harvested cropland under irrigation and 96 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. Irrigated cash rent stands at $197 per acre, while non-irrigated rent held flat at $60 per acre from 2016 to 2026, a change of 0 percent.
