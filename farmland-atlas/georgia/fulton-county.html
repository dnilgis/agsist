Fulton County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $25,039 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

23.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.09 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,647 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $25,039 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.69 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 4, 2026). 5% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 96.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,346 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $45.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

38.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2 days a year and damaging wind on 5.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Fulton County sat at $25,039 an acre in 2022. That is up 31.3% from $19,063 in 2017, or 5.6% a year, and a long way past the $6,780 counted in 2012. The Georgia county median is $4,057. Only 5% of farmland here was rented in 2022.

Fulton County had 9,150 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
