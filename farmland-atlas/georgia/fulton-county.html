Fulton County, Georgia

Fulton County, GA: land and buildings $25,039 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13121. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Fulton County had 9,150 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land here carries a census value of $25,039 per acre, up 31.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.6 percent a year. July nights average 70.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.53 F per decade, warming. Drought has held at 288 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2008 the worst year at 50 weeks, and only 15 weeks from 2021-2025.
