Monroe County, Georgia

Monroe County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2023); land and buildings $4,275 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13207. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Monroe County averaged $36 per acre in 2023, up 50 percent from 2012. Census land value stood at $4,275 per acre in 2022, up 30.4 percent from 2017, a change of 5.4 percent a year. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in 2022, so most production here depends on rainfall. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 70.6 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.2 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.53 F per decade, warming.
