As of Oct 16, cash rent in Clay County runs $83 an acre for 2026, up 36.1% from $61 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $58 and ranks tied for 31st highest of 105. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,401 an acre in 2022, up 43.7% from $2,367 in 2017, and that ranks 42nd lowest of 135. Corn made 192.7 bu in 2023, close to the county median of 191.3 bu.
