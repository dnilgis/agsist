Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 246 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,859 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,858 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 28% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 13% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 55.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,509 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.
