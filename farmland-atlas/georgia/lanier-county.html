Cash rent in Lanier County runs $87 an acre for 2026, down 10.8% from $97 in 2016. That still sits above the state median of $58 and ranks 30th highest of 105. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,152 an acre in 2022, up 37.0% from $3,030 in 2017, or 6.5% a year. 26% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
