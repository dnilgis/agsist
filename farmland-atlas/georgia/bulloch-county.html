U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Bulloch County ran $79 an acre in 2026, up 17.0% from $68 in 2016 and above the state median of $58, which puts it 36th highest of 105. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,810 an acre in 2022, up 33.0% from 2017. Corn made 169.4 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 140.6 bu. 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
