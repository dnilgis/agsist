Lincoln County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,791 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.37 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

84% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 31.76 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 246 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,786 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,791 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $23 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 196 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 303 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 44 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.37 per base acre, PLC $1.31.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (52 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $41.39 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $11.49 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $6.42. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.54 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
