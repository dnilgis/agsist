Cropland rent in Baker County runs $106 an acre for 2026, up 48.3% from $72 in 2016 and tied for 16th highest of 105 counties in Georgia. Irrigated ground rents for $271 in 2026, 5th highest of 39. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,716 an acre in 2022, up 67.2% from $2,222 in 2017. Corn made 226.8 bu in 2025, well over the 191.1 bu county median.
