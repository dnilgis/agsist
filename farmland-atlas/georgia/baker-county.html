Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 25 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,080 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,716 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $106 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.59 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 36% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 48.3% from 2016 to 2026. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 15% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 57.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,974 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.56 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.12, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 87% of the median yield. 56.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 19.2%.

Quick answers for Baker County

What is cash rent in Baker County, Georgia?

The county average is $106 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $271 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Baker County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,716 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Baker County, Georgia?

The county's 2021-25 average is 199.9 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
