Baker County, Georgia

Baker County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $106 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,716 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 191.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland rents $271 per acre and non-irrigated $106 per acre in 2026, a gap that has held without clear direction since 2009. Irrigation covers 57 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 93 percent of its water from groundwater. Heat and drought caused 52 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, but that share has moved down each decade since: 66 percent in 1989-1999, 60 percent in 2000-2009, 52 percent in 2010-2019, 40 percent in 2020-2025. Corn yields trended up 1.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 191.1 bushels per acre. Census land value reached $3,716 per acre in 2022, up 67.2 percent from 2017.
