Henry County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,949 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.17 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

44.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.48 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 244 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,723 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,949 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.66 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 33% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 140.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,915 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.17 per base acre, PLC $1.29.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $7.23. PRF grazing county base value: $29.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

4.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.2%.

The census value of land and buildings in Henry County sat at $8,949 an acre in 2022. That is up 19.6% from $7,482 in 2017, 3.6% a year, and well above the $5,735 of 2012. It ranks 10th highest of 135 in Georgia, against a state median of $4,057. Only 18% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Henry County

What is farmland worth in Henry County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $8,949 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
