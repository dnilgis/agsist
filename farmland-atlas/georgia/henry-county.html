Henry County land was valued at $8,949 per acre in 2022, up 19.6 percent from 2017, or 3.6 percent a year. The July average nightly low ran 70.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.4 F, with a warming trend of 0.47 F per decade since 1976.
