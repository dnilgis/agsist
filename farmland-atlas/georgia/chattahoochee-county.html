Chattahoochee County, Georgia

Chattahoochee County, GA: land and buildings $8,235 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Chattahoochee County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Drought pressure is real: 284 of 1357 weeks during 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, 20.9 percent of that span, with 2012 reaching 52 weeks. The July average nightly low ran 71 F during 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.4 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.22 F per decade.
