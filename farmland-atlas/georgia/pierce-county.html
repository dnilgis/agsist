U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pierce County runs $119 an acre for 2026, 6th highest of 105 in Georgia and well over the state median of $58. That rent is up 41.7% from $84 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings sat at $3,076 an acre in 2022, up 6.9% from $2,877 in 2017, which is 29th lowest of 135 against a state median of $4,057. Corn made 172.7 bu in 2023, above the 2019-23 average of 158.7 bu.
