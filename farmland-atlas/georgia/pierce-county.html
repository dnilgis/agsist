Pierce County, Georgia

Pierce County, GA: non-irrigated cash rent $119 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,076 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 13229. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pierce County runs $119 per acre in 2026, up 41.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent was $248 per acre in 2022, and the irrigated-to-non-irrigated multiple was 2.36 that year against 1.86 in 2009, with no clear direction over time. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 0.5 bushels per acre a year across 2011-2023, with a median of 159.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 119.6 in 2019. Crop insurance closed years 1989-2025 paid $81.8 million, led by excess moisture and flood at 32 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities ran 44 percent in 1989-1999, 23 percent in 2000-2009, 28 percent in 2010-2019, and 9 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value stood at $3,076 per acre in 2022, up 6.9 percent from 2017.
