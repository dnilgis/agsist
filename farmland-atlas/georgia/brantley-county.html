Cash rent in Brantley County runs $48 an acre for 2026, tied for 45th lowest of 105 and under the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,316 an acre in 2022, up 13.6% from $2,919 in 2017, and that sits 37th lowest of 135. The corn trend projects 79.3 bu for 2026, lowest of 12, against a state median of 138.2 bu.
