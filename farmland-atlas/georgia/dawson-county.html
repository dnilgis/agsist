Dawson County, Georgia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $9,260 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

46.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 46.13 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 220 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,317 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,260 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $45.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 248.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 82 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $45.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cropland rent in Dawson County ran $46 an acre in 2025, and pasture rented for $39 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $9,260 an acre in 2022, up 32.6% from $6,983 in 2017, or 5.8% a year. Quick answers for Dawson County

What is cash rent in Dawson County, Georgia?

The county average is $46 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Dawson County, Georgia?

Land and buildings were worth $9,260 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
