U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Lamar County sits at $35 an acre for 2026, the same $35 it was in 2016. That is tied for 20th lowest of 105 counties, under the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings went from $3,501 an acre in 2017 to $5,215 in 2022, up 49.0%, which ranks 43rd highest of 135. 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
