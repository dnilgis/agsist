Cash rent in Lamar County has not moved in the past decade, holding at $35 per acre in 2026. Heat and drought has been the dominant claim in crop insurance, running 72 percent of the $1.2 million paid out from 1997 to 2025. That share shifted around, 86 percent in 2000-2009, down to 60 percent in 2010-2019, then back up to 84 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed. The average July low was 68.8 F in the 1991-2020 normal, and 70.4 F in 2017-2026, trending up 0.47 F per decade since 1976. Land value climbed 49 percent from 2017 to 2022.
