Lamar County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,215 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.56 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 238 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,727 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,215 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent was the same in 2016 and 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 107.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,143 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.56 per base acre, PLC $2.45.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (97.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.73 per base acre, PLC $9.35. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (51.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $29.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.51 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.60, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 72.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1997 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.1%.

Cropland rent in Lamar County sits at $35 an acre for 2026, the same $35 it was in 2016. That is tied for 20th lowest of 105 counties, under the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings went from $3,501 an acre in 2017 to $5,215 in 2022, up 49.0%, which ranks 43rd highest of 135. 30% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
