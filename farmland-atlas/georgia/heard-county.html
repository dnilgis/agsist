U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Heard County rents for $31 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $58, which puts it 11th lowest of 105. Pasture goes for $27 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,425 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from $3,611 in 2017, or 4.1% a year, and that sits 56th highest of 135 in Georgia. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
