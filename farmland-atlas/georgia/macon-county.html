Cash rent in Macon County runs $89 an acre for 2026, up 101.1% from $44 in 2016 and 26th highest of 105 in Georgia against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,435 an acre in 2022, up 6.8% from $3,217 in 2017, which puts it 43rd lowest of 135. Corn made 124.2 bu in 2025, well under the 2021-25 average of 199.1 bu.
