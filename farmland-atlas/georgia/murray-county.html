Cropland rent in Murray County runs $43 an acre for 2026, up 34.4% from $32 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $58 and ranks 35th lowest of 105. The census value of land and buildings was $6,452 an acre in 2022, up 27.3% from $5,068 in 2017, and that ranks 29th highest of 135. Only 17% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
