Warren County, Georgia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,933 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.61 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

74.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 247 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,764 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,933 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 122.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 107 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 14,676 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.61 per base acre, PLC $1.56.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (62.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (24.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $43.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.14 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.57, 2016 to 2025. 4.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.6%.

Cash rent in Warren County runs $21 an acre for 2023, up 20.0% from $18 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $2,933 an acre in 2022, down 1.6% from $2,982 in 2017, or 0.3% a year. That sits under the Georgia median of $4,057 and ranks 19th lowest of 135. In 2022, 23% of the farmland here was rented.
