Elko County, Nevada

All harvested cropland in Elko County is irrigated, 100 percent, and only 6 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater in 2015. Land value sits at $647 per acre in the 2022 census, up 17.9 percent from 2017-2022, 3.3 percent a year. Drought pressure has been present: 429 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 were at severe drought or worse, 31.6 percent, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 109 such weeks came in 2021-2025. July nights are trending warmer, up 0.8 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 53.3 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 51.4 F.
