Irrigated cash rent in Churchill County runs $199 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 5 in Nevada against a state median of $123. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,955 an acre in 2022, up 54.3% from $1,915 in 2017, or 9.1% a year. That puts it 5th highest of 13 in the state. Only 9% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
