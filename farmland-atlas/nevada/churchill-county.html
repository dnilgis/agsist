Churchill County, Nevada

Churchill County, NV: irrigated cash rent $199 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,955 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Cropland here is 100 percent irrigated, and groundwater supplies only 2 percent of that water, so this county's farming depends on surface water. July nights have warmed too, up 0.88 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average low at 58.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 57.2 F. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 26 percent in 2010-2019 to 32 percent in 2020-2025.
