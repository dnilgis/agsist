Churchill County, Nevada

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,955 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.12 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

82.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 3.56 inches, 106% of normal (3.35 inches) through Nov 16, at Lahontan Dam. Frost-free season: 159 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,684 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,955 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 9.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 364.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 400 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,694 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.12 per base acre, PLC $2.53.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (101 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $8.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.64 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.63 over the same years. 100% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2025; too much rain caused 3.5%.

Irrigated cash rent in Churchill County runs $199 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 5 in Nevada against a state median of $123. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,955 an acre in 2022, up 54.3% from $1,915 in 2017, or 9.1% a year. That puts it 5th highest of 13 in the state. Only 9% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
