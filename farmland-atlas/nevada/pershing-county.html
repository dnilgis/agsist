Irrigation drives risk in Pershing County. Ninety-nine percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigation failure accounts for 77 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1993-2025, closed crop years, totaling $56.5 million of $73.8 million paid. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved differently across the periods given: 10 percent in 1989-1999, 5 percent in 2000-2009, 5 percent in 2010-2019, and 4 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 600 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 108 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
