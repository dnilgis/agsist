Clark County, Nevada

Clark County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Irrigation covers 100 percent of harvested cropland here, and 65 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. The July average nightly low sits at 76.2 F for 2017-2026, up from a 74.2 F normal in 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.84 F per decade, warming. Drought has been a regular presence: 434 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2021 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 170 such weeks fell in 2021-2025 alone. Land value carried at $3,863 per acre in the 2022 census, up 24.3 percent from 2017-2022.
