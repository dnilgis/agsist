Lincoln County, Nevada

Irrigated cropland in Lincoln County rented for $207 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,230 an acre in 2022, up 25.4% from $2,576 in 2017, or 4.6% a year. That is the highest of 13 in Nevada, well over the state median of $1,791. 21% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
