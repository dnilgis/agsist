Lincoln County, Nevada

In Lincoln County, all harvested cropland is irrigated, and 96 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater in 2015. That leaves this county exposed to drought weeks: 444 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, and 2021 alone had 52 weeks. Between 2021-2025, 174 weeks reached that mark. July nightly lows have moved from 62.9 F (1991-2020 normal) to 64.6 F (2017-2026), part of a warming trend of 0.88 F per decade since 1976. Land value rose 25.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 4.6 percent a year, with the 2022 census estimate at $3,230 per acre.
