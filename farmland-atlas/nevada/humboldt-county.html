Irrigated cropland in Humboldt County rents for $123 an acre in 2026, which is the state median and 3rd lowest of 5. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,164 an acre in 2022, up 31.2% from $887 in 2017, or 5.6% a year. That still sits under the state median of $1,791 and ranks tied for 5th lowest of 13. In 2022, 23% of the farmland here was rented.
