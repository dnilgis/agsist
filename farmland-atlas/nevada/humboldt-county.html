Humboldt County, Nevada

Humboldt County, NV: irrigated cash rent $123 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,164 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.06 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32013. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

All harvested cropland in Humboldt County is irrigated, and 82 percent of that irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. The July average nightly low ran 56 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 58.4 F over 2017-2026, with warming since 1976 at 0.86 F per decade. Freeze and frost caused 38 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, closed crop years, more than heat and drought, which ran 46 percent in 2000-2009, 14 percent in 2010-2019, and 25 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 536 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 98 of those weeks in 2021-2025 alone. Census value of land and buildings reached $1,164 per acre in 2022, up 31.2 percent from 2017-2022.
