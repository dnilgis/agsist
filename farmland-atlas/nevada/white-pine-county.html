White Pine County, Nevada

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,791 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

30.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 12.03 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 106% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 123 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,241 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,791 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.09 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 2, 2026 to Sep 10, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 88.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 280 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $11.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

100% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Irrigated cropland in White Pine County rented for $96 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $1,791 an acre in 2022, up 35.1% from $1,326 in 2017, or 6.2% a year. That sits right on the state median of $1,791 and ranks 7th lowest of 13 in Nevada. In 2022, 24% of the farmland here was rented.
