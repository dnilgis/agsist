White Pine County, Nevada

White Pine County, NV: irrigated cash rent $96 an acre (2023); land and buildings $1,791 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All harvested cropland here is irrigated, and 94 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater, 2015. Drought weeks at severe or worse ran 404 of 1357 (29.8 percent) from 2000-2025, with 2021 at 52 weeks and 134 weeks across 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 55 F in 2017-2026, up from the 53.3 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.72 F per decade, warming. Land value rose 35.1 percent from 2017-2022, 6.2 percent a year, to $1,791 per acre. Wind operating stands at 150 MW, with 300 MW solar and 0 MW wind proposed to EIA.
