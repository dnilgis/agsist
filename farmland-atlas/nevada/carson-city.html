Carson City, Nevada

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $13,675 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

94.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 8.99 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 159 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,373 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $13,675 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $8.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
