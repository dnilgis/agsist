Eureka County, Nevada

Irrigation defines this county: 100 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 98 percent of that water comes from groundwater. July nights average 53.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 51.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.89 F per decade, warming. Drought pressure has been present here: 527 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 sat at severe drought or worse, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 107 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Census land and building value stood at $577 per acre in 2022, up 15.4 percent from 2017-2022, or 2.9 percent a year.
