Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 9.52 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 119 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,271 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $577 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.09 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 2, 2026 to Sep 9, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 13% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 33.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 137,755 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Eureka County

What is cash rent in Eureka County, Nevada?

The county average is $76 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Eureka County, Nevada?

Land and buildings were worth $577 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
