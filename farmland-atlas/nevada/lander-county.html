Lander County, Nevada

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,115 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

27.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 8.61 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 133 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,382 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,115 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.07 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 2, 2026 to Sep 9, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 9% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 14 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 51,635 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $11.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

100% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Lander County was $1,115 an acre in 2022. That is up 6.3% from $1,049 in 2017, which works out to 1.2% a year. Back in 2012 it stood at $743. The Nevada median across 13 counties is $1,791, so Lander sits well under it.

Lander County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Quick answers for Lander County

What is farmland worth in Lander County, Nevada?

Land and buildings were worth $1,115 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
