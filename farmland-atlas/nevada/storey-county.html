Storey County, Nevada

Storey County, NV: land and buildings $77,197 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32029. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Storey County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Storey County reached $77,197 per acre in the census year 2022, the operators' own estimate. That value climbed 101.1 percent from 2012-2022, a pace of 7.2 percent a year. July nights average 60.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 58.3 F, and the nightly low trend since 1976 runs 1.26 F per decade, warming. Drought has held ground here too: 499 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2015 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 97 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone.
