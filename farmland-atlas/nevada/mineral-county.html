Mineral County, Nevada

Mineral County, NV: irrigated cash rent $79 an acre (2026); land and buildings $374 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation water in Mineral County came entirely from groundwater in 2015, at 100 percent. The July average nightly low was 56.6 F under the 1991-2020 normal and has risen to 58.8 F in 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 1.01 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has been a recurring presence: 520 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 sat at severe drought or worse, 38.3 percent, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 104 such weeks from 2021-2025. Land value stood at $374 per acre in 2022, up 27.6 percent from 2017-2022, 5 percent a year.
