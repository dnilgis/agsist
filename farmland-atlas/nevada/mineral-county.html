Irrigated cash rent in Mineral County runs $79 an acre for 2026, lowest of 5 in Nevada against a state median of $123. The census value of land and buildings came in at $374 an acre in 2022, up 27.6% from $293 in 2017, which works out to 5.0% a year. That 2022 figure still sits under the $429 of 2012, and it ranks lowest of 13 against a state median of $1,791.
