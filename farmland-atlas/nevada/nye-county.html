Nye County, Nevada

Nye County, NV: land and buildings $3,089 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Nye County irrigates 100 percent of its harvested cropland, and 96 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. The July nightly low has moved from a 59.5 F normal to 61.4 F, part of a warming trend of 0.95 F per decade since 1976. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $3,089 per acre, up 78.3 percent from 2017-2022, or 12.3 percent a year.
