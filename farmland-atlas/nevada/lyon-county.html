Lyon County, Nevada

Lyon County, NV: irrigated cash rent $178 an acre (2023); land and buildings $3,107 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.47 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 32019. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Cropland here is 100 percent irrigated, and groundwater supplies only 6 percent of that water, so the county depends on surface delivery. Drought exposure is present: severe drought or worse covered the county 491 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 96 weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. July nights have warmed, running 58.8 F over 2017-2026 against a 56.5 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 1.06 F per decade since 1976.
