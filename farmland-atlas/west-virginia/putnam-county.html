Cash rent in Putnam County ran $44 an acre in 2024, down 42.8% from $76 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings was $3,285 an acre in 2022, up 14.6% from 2017, or 2.8% a year. That ranks 21st highest of 47 in West Virginia, a bit above the state median of $3,215. Only 12% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
