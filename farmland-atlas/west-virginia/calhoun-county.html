Calhoun County, West Virginia

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,029 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,800 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.51 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 50.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.54 per base acre, PLC $1.91.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (76 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $32.97 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.80 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Calhoun County runs $27 an acre for 2025, down 8.6% from $29 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,800 an acre in 2022, down 1.3% from $1,823 in 2017. That puts it lowest of 47 against a state median of $3,215. Only 17% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
