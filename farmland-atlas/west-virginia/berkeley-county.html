Berkeley County, West Virginia

Berkeley County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $46 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,055 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 119.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Berkeley County, WV runs $46 per acre in 2026, up 24.3 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings sits at $7,055 per acre in 2022, up 31.9 percent from 2017, 5.7 percent a year. The July nightly low averaged 63.4 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 64.4 F over 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.61 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought made up 20 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 27 percent in 2000-2009, 87 percent in 2010-2019, and 19 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 stands at 1.59, against 1.1 for the full 1989-2025 record, and 27 of the 1357 weeks since 2000 fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025 alone.
