Berkeley County, West Virginia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,055 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.92 per base acre for corn.

Berkeley County vs West Virginia: $46 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 83% of the other 29 West Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (6th highest of 30).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

1.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.72 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 93% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 196 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,046 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,055 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $46 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.54 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 18% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 24.3% from 2016 to 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 120.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 100 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.92 per base acre, PLC $2.58.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (102.74 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $22.71 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.62 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $30.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 37 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $22.07 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.59, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 28% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 25% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2%.

Quick answers for Berkeley County

What is cash rent in Berkeley County, West Virginia?

The county average is $46 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Berkeley County, West Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $7,055 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
