Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.48 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 108% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 181 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,821 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,944 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.51 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 5% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 63.6% from 2014 to 2026. 9% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 87.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 10 surveys since 2010; up 63.6% from $11 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Monongalia County runs $18 an acre for 2026, up 63.6% from $11 in 2014 and 3rd lowest of 30 in West Virginia. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $5,944 an acre in 2022, 3rd highest of 47 in the state against a state median of $3,215. That value rose 31.2% from $4,530 in 2017, 5.6% a year. Only 9% of farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Monongalia County

What is cash rent in Monongalia County, West Virginia?

The county average is $18 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Monongalia County, West Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $5,944 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
