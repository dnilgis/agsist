$18

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 10 surveys since 2010; up 63.6% from $11 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Monongalia County runs $18 an acre for 2026, up 63.6% from $11 in 2014 and 3rd lowest of 30 in West Virginia. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $5,944 an acre in 2022, 3rd highest of 47 in the state against a state median of $3,215. That value rose 31.2% from $4,530 in 2017, 5.6% a year. Only 9% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
