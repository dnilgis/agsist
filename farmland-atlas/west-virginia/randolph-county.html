Randolph County, West Virginia

Randolph County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,785 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54083. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Randolph County averages $34 per acre in 2026, up 47.8 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $2,785 per acre in 2022, up 14.8 percent from 2017, or 2.8 percent a year. July nights average 59.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 57.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.55 F per decade, warming. Drought held severe or worse for 37 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 28 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No irrigation, solar, or wind capacity is present in this county.
