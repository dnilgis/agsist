Cropland in Randolph County rents for $34 an acre in 2026, up 47.8% from $23 in 2016. That rent ranks 14th highest of 30 in West Virginia, a bit over the state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $2,785 an acre in 2022, up 14.8% from $2,425 in 2017, and it ranks 16th lowest of 47. 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
