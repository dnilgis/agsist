Logan County, West Virginia

Logan County, WV: land and buildings $3,077 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Logan County had 752 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Logan County was $3,077 per acre in the 2022 Census, up 24.4 percent from 2017, or 4.5 percent a year. July nights average 65.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.1 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.48 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county in 23 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 13 weeks, and 7 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
