Clay County, West Virginia

Clay County, WV: land and buildings $2,599 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in Clay County stands at $2,599 per acre in the 2022 census, up 50.9 percent from 2017, or 8.6 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed from 63.3 F to 64.6 F against the 1991-2020 normal, part of a trend of 0.54 F per decade since 1976, and no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Drought has touched this county in 28 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 26 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 and 2024 alone accounting for 19 weeks.
