Cropland in Taylor County rents for $24 an acre in 2026, up 84.6% from $13 in 2014. That still sits under the state median of $30 and ranks 7th lowest of 30. The census value of land and buildings ran $4,194 an acre in 2022, up 36.4% from $3,075 in 2017, and ranks 10th highest of 47. Only 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
