Wetzel County, West Virginia

Wetzel County, WV: land and buildings $2,290 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land in Wetzel County was valued by operators at $2,290 per acre in the 2022 Census, up 18.8 percent from 2017, or 3.5 percent a year. July nights average 63.1 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.7 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.54 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county in 20 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2024 the worst year at 15 weeks, and all 20 of those weeks fall within 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
