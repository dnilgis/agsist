Cropland in Mercer County rents for $47 an acre in 2026, up 62.1% from $29 in 2016. That rent sits 5th highest of 30 counties in West Virginia, well over the state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $2,733 an acre in 2022, up 8.7% from $2,515 in 2017 and 15th lowest of 47. Pasture rents for $14 an acre in 2026.
