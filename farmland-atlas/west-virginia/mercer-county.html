Mercer County, West Virginia

Mercer County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $47 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,733 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54055. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mercer County averages $47 per acre in 2026, up 62.1 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 is $2,733 per acre, with land value up 8.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 1.7 percent a year. Drought has touched this county in 33 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 2.4 percent of the time, with 2008 the worst year at 9 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 4 such weeks. July nights average 60.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 60.3 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.37 F per decade, warming. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
