McDowell County, West Virginia

McDowell County, WV: land and buildings $4,027 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54047. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

McDowell County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 62.8 F for 2017-2026, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 61.9 F, part of a warming trend of 0.43 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Drought has been light: 43 of 1357 weeks reached severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with only 1 such week from 2021-2025.
