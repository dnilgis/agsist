McDowell County is a quiet one on most measures. Its July nights have warmed a little, with the average low up from 61.9 F in the 1991-2020 normal to 62.8 F over 2017-2026, and the trend since 1976 is warming at 0.42 F per decade. No July since 1900 has averaged a low of 70 F or higher. Drought has been rare here, with 43 of 1357 weeks since 2000 seeing at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, and the worst year was 2007 with 17 weeks. The land is valued at $4,027 per acre, and there are only 2 CRP acres, none of them with wind or solar on the ground.
