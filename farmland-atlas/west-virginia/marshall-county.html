As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Marshall County was $3,668 an acre in 2022, up from $2,587 in 2017 and $2,199 in 2012. That is a 41.8% climb from 2017 to 2022, or 7.2% a year. It sits above the West Virginia median of $3,215 and ranks 17th highest of 47. Just 7% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
