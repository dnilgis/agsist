Marshall County, West Virginia

Marshall County, WV: land and buildings $3,668 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in Marshall County came in at $3,668 per acre in 2022, up 41.8 percent from 2017, or 7.2 percent a year. July nights average 63.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.68 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought weeks are concentrated: 24 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, and all 24 fall within 2021-2025, with 2024 alone accounting for 17. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
