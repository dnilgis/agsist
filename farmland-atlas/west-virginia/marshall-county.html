Marshall County, West Virginia

Rain is at 113% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,668 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.70 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 46.97 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 113% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 184 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,842 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,668 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.57 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 81 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 293 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.70 per base acre, PLC $1.97.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (78.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $77.32 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.33 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $38.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

The census value of land and buildings in Marshall County was $3,668 an acre in 2022, up from $2,587 in 2017 and $2,199 in 2012. That is a 41.8% climb from 2017 to 2022, or 7.2% a year. It sits above the West Virginia median of $3,215 and ranks 17th highest of 47. Just 7% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
