Non-irrigated cash rent in Mineral County is $27 per acre in 2026, up 3.8 percent from 2016. Land value stands at $3,232 per acre, up 15.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3 percent a year. Drought weeks have also concentrated recently: 33 of the 2000-2025 total of 47 fell in 2021-2025, with 24 weeks in 2024 alone.
