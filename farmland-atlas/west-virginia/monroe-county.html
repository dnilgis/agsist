Cash rent in Monroe County runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 5.7% from $35 in 2016. That sits tied for 10th highest of 30 in West Virginia, above the state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,501 an acre in 2022, up 31.5% from $2,662 in 2017. 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
