Non-irrigated cash rent in Harrison County stands at $19 per acre in 2026, down 24 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings was $3,496 per acre in 2022, up 39.6 percent from 2017, or 6.9 percent a year. July nights have averaged 63.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.9 F, with a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates here, and none is proposed to EIA.
