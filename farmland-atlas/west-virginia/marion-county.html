Marion County, West Virginia

Marion County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,858 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54049. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Marion County averages $40 per acre in 2026, up 252.2 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $3,858 per acre, with land value up 30.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.4 percent a year. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. July nights have averaged 62.9 F from 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.6 F, with a warming trend of 0.43 F per decade since 1976. Drought has touched the county in 15 weeks of the 2000-2025 span, all of them falling in the 2021-2025 window, with 2024 the worst year at 15 weeks.
