Marion County, West Virginia

Land and buildings run $3,858 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.16 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 181 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,882 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,858 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 252.2% from 2016 to 2026. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 79 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.16 per base acre, PLC $2.08.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (83 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $38.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Marion County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 252.2% from $12 in 2016. That puts it 9th highest of 30 counties in West Virginia, against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $3,858 an acre in 2022, up 30.2% from $2,964 in 2017, or 5.4% a year. Only 12% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
