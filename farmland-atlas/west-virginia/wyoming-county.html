Wyoming County, West Virginia

Wyoming County, WV: land and buildings $3,991 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Wyoming County had 1,494 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land here is valued at $3,991 per acre by operators, up 19.2 percent from 2017-2022, or 3.6 percent a year. July nights average 63 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62 F, with a warming trend of 0.42 F per decade since 1976; no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Drought has touched this county in 30 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 2.2 percent of the time, with 2007 the worst year at 10 weeks, and 6 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates here, and none is proposed to EIA.
