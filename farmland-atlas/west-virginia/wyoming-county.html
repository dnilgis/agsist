Wyoming County, West Virginia

Rain is at 106% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,991 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.29 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 106% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,828 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,991 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.28 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 0.7% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 26.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 79,341 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

The census value of land and buildings in Wyoming County was $3,991 an acre in 2022. That is up 19.2% from $3,347 in 2017, 3.6% a year, and well past the $2,072 figure from 2012. It sits above the West Virginia county median of $3,215. Only 12% of farmland here was rented in 2022.

Wyoming County had 1,494 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
