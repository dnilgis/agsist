Mingo County, West Virginia

Mingo County, WV: land and buildings $1,361 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54059. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Mingo County had 4,083 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land in Mingo County, WV sold for $1,361 per acre in the census year 2022, up 22 percent from 2017, 4 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 65.7 F for 2017-2026, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 64.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.5 F per decade, warming. Drought was rare here: 36 of 1357 weeks hit severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 23 weeks, and only 2 weeks in 2021-2025.
