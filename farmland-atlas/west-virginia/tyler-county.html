The census value of land and buildings in Tyler County was $2,816 an acre in 2022. That is up 47.2% from $1,913 in 2017, or 8.0% a year, after $1,836 in 2012. It sits under the state median of $3,215 and ranks 17th lowest of 47. The last cash rent on record was $18 an acre in 2013, and 21% of farmland was rented in 2022.
