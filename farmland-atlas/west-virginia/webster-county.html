Webster County, West Virginia

Webster County, WV: land and buildings $1,960 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54101. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Webster County had 7,354 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 60.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 59.5 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.44 F per decade. Drought has touched the county in 34 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2024 the worst year at 19 weeks, and 27 weeks fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
