Non-irrigated cash rent in Pocahontas County averages $37 per acre in 2026, down 10.8 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stands at $3,089 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 64.7 percent, 10.5 percent a year. July nights average 58.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 56.9 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.54 F per decade since 1976. Drought has touched the county lightly: 29 of 1357 weeks since 2000 at severe drought or worse, with 18 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed.
