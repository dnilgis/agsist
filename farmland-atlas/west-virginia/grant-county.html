Grant County, West Virginia

Rain is at 107% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,008 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.64 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.88 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 107% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 171 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,630 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,008 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.41 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 3.6% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 108.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 22,768 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.64 per base acre, PLC $3.02.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (120.33 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $26.59 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $30.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 16 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 82.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 8.5%.

Stands out against its own record

$27

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest since 2013 (15 surveys on record since 2009); down 3.6% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Grant County runs $27 an acre for 2026, down 3.6% from $28 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $30 and ranks tied for 12th lowest of 30. The census value of land and buildings was $3,008 an acre in 2022, up 14.7% from $2,622 in 2017, or 2.8% a year. Pasture rents for $10 an acre in 2026, and 23% of farmland was rented in 2022.
