Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 176 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,736 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,673 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $31.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent fell 1.6% from 2016 to 2026. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 107.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 67 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

PRF grazing county base value: $38.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
