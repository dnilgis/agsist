The census value of land and buildings in Lincoln County was $2,003 an acre in 2022, up 8.7% from $1,843 in 2017. That about gets it back to the $2,021 of 2012. It sits 2nd lowest of 47 in West Virginia, well under the state median of $3,215. Only 5% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
