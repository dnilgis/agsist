Gilmer County, West Virginia

Gilmer County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,104 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Gilmer County runs $20 per acre in 2025, up 95 percent from 2014. Census land and building value stands at $2,104 per acre, up 59.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 9.8 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 62.9 F to 64.5 F over 2017-2026, part of a longer trend of 0.54 F per decade warming since 1976. No irrigation is in use on harvested cropland, and drought has touched the county in 20 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, all 20 falling in the 2021-2025 stretch, with 2024 the worst year at 20 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
