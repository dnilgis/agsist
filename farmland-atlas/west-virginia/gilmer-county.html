Cropland rent in Gilmer County was $20 an acre in 2025, up 95.0% from $10 in 2014. Pasture went for $5 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $2,104 an acre in 2022, up 59.8% from $1,317 in 2017, which ranks 4th lowest of 47 against a state median of $3,215. 22% of farmland was rented in 2022.
