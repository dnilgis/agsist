Jefferson County is a small corn county where heat and drought is the main thing that costs farmers money. Heat and drought went from 58 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999 to 77 percent in 2020-2025, and the loss ratio over the last ten closed crop years is 0.81. Non-irrigated cash rent is $55 per acre in 2026, down 26.7 percent from 2016. The July nightly low averaged 65.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.1 F, and the county has seen 35 weeks of severe drought or worse since 2021.
