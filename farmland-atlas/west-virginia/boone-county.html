Boone County, West Virginia

Rain is at 107% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,129 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.43 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 46.59 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 107% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 196 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,046 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,129 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.30 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,690 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.43 per base acre, PLC $1.73.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (69 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $25.67 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $15.25 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
