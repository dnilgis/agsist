Doddridge County, West Virginia

Doddridge County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $26 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,479 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Doddridge County averages $26 per acre in 2026, up 136.4 percent from 2014. Land value under the 2022 census stands at $2,479 per acre, up 16.4 percent from 2017, or 3.1 percent a year. July nights have warmed since 1976 at 0.62 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average nightly low of 63.9 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 62.1 F. Drought has touched the county in 15 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, all 15 falling in the 2021-2025 span, with 2024 the worst year at 15 weeks.
