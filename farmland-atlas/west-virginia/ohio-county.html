Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.04 inches, 118% of normal (33.81 inches) through Nov 16, at Pike Island. Frost-free season: 183 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,831 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,459 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 16.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 127 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.08 per base acre, PLC $2.09.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $38.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.
