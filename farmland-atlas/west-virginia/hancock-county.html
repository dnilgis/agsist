Hancock County's land value stands out more than its weather. The census put cropland and buildings at $4,294 per acre in 2022, and that figure fell 6.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 1.3 percent a year. That is a county where the land price has been sliding while the rest of the picture stays quiet. July nights have warmed some, with the 2017-2026 average low at 62.7 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.7 F. Severe drought has been rare, with 12 of 1357 weeks since 2000 at 50 percent of the county or more, though 2025 had 6 of those weeks. There is no CRP ground and no operating wind or solar yet, only 8.4 MW of solar proposed.
