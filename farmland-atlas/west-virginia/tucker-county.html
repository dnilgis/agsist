Tucker County, West Virginia

Rain is at 112% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,965 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.61 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 54.36 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 112% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 160 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,255 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,965 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 75.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 11.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.61 per base acre, PLC $3.51.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (139.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $78.36 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $30.90 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $30.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Tucker County ran $35 an acre in 2026, 12th highest of 30 in West Virginia, against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,965 an acre in 2022, up 38.5% from $2,863 in 2017, or 6.7% a year. That value sits 13th highest of 47 in the state, above the $3,215 median. In 2022, 24% of the farmland was rented.
