Wayne County, West Virginia

Wayne County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,644 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54099. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County runs $31 per acre. Land value stood at $2,644 per acre in the 2022 census, up 31.6 percent from 2017-2022, or 5.6 percent a year. The July average nightly low was 64.5 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 66.2 F for 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.51 F per decade since 1976; the 2020s is the first decade this figure reached 66 F. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, and drought was rare, with 34 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 6 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
