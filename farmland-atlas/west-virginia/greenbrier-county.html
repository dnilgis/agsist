Cash rent in Greenbrier County runs $51 an acre for 2026, down 4.7% from $53 in 2016. That still ranks 4th highest of 30 in West Virginia, well above the state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,317 an acre in 2022, up 27.3% from $2,606 in 2017, and sits 20th highest of 47 against a state median of $3,215.
