Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.21 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,441 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,317 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 4.7% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 226 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 62,500 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Quick answers for Greenbrier County

What is cash rent in Greenbrier County, West Virginia?

The county average is $51 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Greenbrier County, West Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,317 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
