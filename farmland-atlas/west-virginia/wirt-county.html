Cash rent in Wirt County runs $12 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 30 in West Virginia against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,622 an acre in 2022, up 45.0% from $1,808 in 2017, and that is 10th lowest of 47 in the state. Only 13% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
