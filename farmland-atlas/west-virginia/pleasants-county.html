The census value of land and buildings in Pleasants County was $3,209 an acre in 2022, up 39.0% from $2,308 in 2017. That works out to 6.8% a year. It sits about even with the West Virginia median of $3,215 and ranks 23rd lowest of 47. Only 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
