Jackson County, West Virginia

Rain is at 112% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,620 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.62 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 47.11 inches, 112% of normal (41.96 inches) through Nov 18, at Ripley, 5.8 mi S. Frost-free season: 188 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,075 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,620 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $25 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 39.8% from 2016 to 2026. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 81.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.62 per base acre, PLC $2.73.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (108.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2024; too much rain caused 53.5%.

The census value of land and buildings was $2,620 an acre in 2022, up 18.5% from $2,211 in 2017, which ranks 9th lowest of 47 in the state.
