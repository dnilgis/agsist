Hampshire County cash rent runs $43 an acre for 2026, up 123.7% from $19 in 2016 and 8th highest of 30 in West Virginia. Pasture rents for $17 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,713 an acre in 2022, up 23.6% from $3,004 in 2017 and 16th highest of 47 in the state. 26% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
