Non-irrigated cash rent in Raleigh County stands at $25 per acre for 2026, unchanged from 2016, a 0 percent move over that period. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $4,225 per acre, with a land value change of 13.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 2.6 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low of 61.7 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 60.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.36 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched the county lightly, with 17 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 at severe drought or worse, 8 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
