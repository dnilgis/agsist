Raleigh County, West Virginia

Rain is at 111% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,225 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.33 per base acre for corn.

Raleigh County vs West Virginia: $25 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 24% of the other 29 West Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 8th lowest of 30).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 46.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 111% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,628 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,225 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $25 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.36 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent was the same in 2016 and 2026. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 106.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 19,529 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.33 per base acre, PLC $2.26.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (90 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $26.61 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.89 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cropland rent in Raleigh County stood at $25 an acre in 2026, the same $25 it was in 2016, and tied for 8th lowest of 30 against a state median of $30. Pasture rent ran $24 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,225 an acre in 2022, up 13.9% from $3,709 in 2017, and 8th highest of 47 in the state. Only 14% of farmland was rented in 2022.
