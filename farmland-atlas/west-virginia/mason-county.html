Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,096 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,972 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $103 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.46 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 12% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 24.1% from 2014 to 2026. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 19% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 92.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 644 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (43.49 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 8 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.11 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 89% of the median yield. 0.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 37.6%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Mason County cropland rent stands at $103 an acre for 2026, the highest of 30 in West Virginia against a state median of $30. That rent is up 24.1% from $83 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,972 an acre in 2022, up 22.3% from 2017 at 4.1% a year, which ranks 18th lowest of 47. County corn ran a median of 138.0 bu an acre from 2008 to 2016.
