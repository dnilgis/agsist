Non-irrigated cash rent in Hardy County stands at $45 per acre, up 18.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, so yield rides on rainfall and heat. Corn's median 2008-2016 was 161.3 bushels per acre, with 2010 falling to 116.8. July nights average 61.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 60 F normal, warming at 0.37 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought caused 70 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.09, above the 0.73 ratio for the full 1989-2025 period. Drought weeks in severe or worse conditions ran 29 of 2021-2025, against 51 of 1357 weeks for 2000-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
