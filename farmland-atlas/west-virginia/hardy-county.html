Cash rent in Hardy County runs $45 an acre for 2026, up 18.4% from $38 in 2016, and that ranks 7th highest of 30 in West Virginia against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,212 an acre in 2022, up 31.1% from $3,213 in 2017, which is 5.6% a year. That value ranks 9th highest of 47 in the state. 31% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
