Morgan County, West Virginia

Cropland rent in Morgan County runs $21 an acre for 2026, against a state median of $30, which puts it 5th lowest of 30. The census value of land and buildings tells it the other way, at $5,715 an acre in 2022, 4th highest of 47 and well over the $3,215 state median. That value is up 9.2% from 2017, 1.8% a year. Pasture rents for $11 an acre in 2025, and 20% of farmland was rented in 2022.
