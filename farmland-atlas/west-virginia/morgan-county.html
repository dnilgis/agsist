Non-irrigated cash rent in Morgan County averages $21 per acre for 2026. Land value rose 9.2 percent from 2017-2022, or 1.8 percent a year. July nightly lows average 63 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 62.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched the county in 46 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2010 the worst year at 14 weeks, and 20 weeks recorded in 2021-2025.
