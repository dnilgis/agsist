Brooke County, West Virginia

Brooke County, WV: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,024 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Census land and buildings value stood at $4,024 per acre in 2022, up 42.4 percent from 2017, a 7.3 percent a year pace. July nights average 63 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.8 F, with warming of 0.43 F per decade since 1976; no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought has touched the county in 17 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2024 the worst year at 15 weeks, and those same 17 weeks fall within 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates here, and none is proposed to EIA.
