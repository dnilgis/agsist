Fayette County, West Virginia

Land and buildings run $4,123 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.66 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 188 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,713 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,123 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 16.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 76.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,714 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.66 per base acre, PLC $2.14.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (85.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $36.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Fayette County stood at $4,123 an acre in 2022. That is up 41.5% from $2,913 in 2017, or 7.2% a year. The county ranks 11th highest of 47 in West Virginia, above the state median of $3,215. In 2022, 16% of the farmland here was rented.
