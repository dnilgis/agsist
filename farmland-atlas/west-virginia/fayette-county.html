Fayette County, West Virginia

Fayette County, WV: land and buildings $4,123 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 54019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in 2022 stood at $4,123 per acre, up 41.5 percent from 2017, a 7.2 percent a year pace. July nightly lows averaged 62.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.3 F, with a warming trend of 0.38 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has been light: 11 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 9 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
