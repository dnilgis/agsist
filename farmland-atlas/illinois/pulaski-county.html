Pulaski County, Illinois

Pulaski County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $152 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,494 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 169.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17153. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pulaski County runs $152 per acre in 2026, up 16.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $5,494 per acre for 2022, and land value rose 33.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6 percent a year. Most cropland here is not irrigated: only 6 percent of harvested cropland carried irrigation in the 2022 census, and where irrigation runs, it draws on groundwater at 100 percent. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $25.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 54 percent. The July average nightly low has moved from 68.8 F under the 1991-2020 normal to 70.2 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.5 F per decade since 1976.
