Non-irrigated cash rent in Coles County runs $286 per acre for 2026, up 8.7 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $10,272 per acre, with a 30.4 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, or 5.5 percent a year. Corn yield trend is up 4.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 195.6 and a worst year of 89.3 in 2012. Crop insurance shows a loss ratio of 0.5 across 1989-2025, with only 2 of 27 years paying out more than premium; heat and drought caused 53 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 19 percent in 2000-2009, 72 percent in 2010-2019, and 13 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland here is irrigated.
