Woodford County, Illinois

Woodford County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $290 an acre (2025); land and buildings $10,987 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 206.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17203. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Woodford County averages $290 per acre in 2025, up 18.4 percent from 2014. Census value of land and buildings was $10,987 per acre in 2022, up 22.5 percent from 2017, or 4.1 percent a year. The corn yield trend runs 3.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 206 bushels per acre and a worst year of 102.5 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 58 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, with the share at 50 percent in 1989-1999, 41 percent in 2000-2009, 68 percent in 2010-2019, and 23 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 88 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 16 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
