Woodford County cash rent stands at $290 an acre for 2025, up 18.4% from $245 in 2014. That rent is 3rd highest of 10 in Illinois, against a state median of $193. The census value of land and buildings was $10,987 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from 2017 and 9th highest of 91. Corn runs a 14-yr trend of 247.3 bu, with 235.1 bu in 2025.
