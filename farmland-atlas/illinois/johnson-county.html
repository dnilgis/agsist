Johnson County, Illinois

Johnson County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $129 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,327 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17087. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Johnson County runs $129 per acre in 2025, up 61.3 percent since 2014. Land carries a census value of $4,327 per acre, up 16.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. No harvested cropland is irrigated here, so the corn yield, with a median of 147.1 bushels per acre from 2008-2025 and a worst year of 93.9 in 2016, rides on rainfall. Excess moisture and flood drove 58 percent of the $25.1 million in crop insurance indemnities paid out from 1989-2025, well above the heat and drought share, which ranged from 19 percent to 39 percent across the listed periods. Drought weeks stayed low, 42 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 2012 the worst year at 17 weeks.
