Adams County, Illinois

Adams County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $238 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,631 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Adams County averages $238 per acre in 2026, up 36 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census is $8,631 per acre, up 32.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 37 percent, and its share of indemnities rose from 18 percent in 1989-1999 and 18 percent in 2000-2009 to 41 percent in 2010-2019, then 39 percent in 2020-2025. The recent loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 0.28, below the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.79. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland, and all of that draws on groundwater.
