Non-irrigated cash rent in Champaign County stands at $316 per acre for 2026, up 19.2 percent from 2016. Corn yields carry a rising trend of 3.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 191.3 bushels per acre and a low of 108.9 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 58 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, though that share moved differently across periods: 69 percent in 1989-1999, 22 percent in 2000-2009, 72 percent in 2010-2019, and 16 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, all of it drawn from groundwater as of 2015.
