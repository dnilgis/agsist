As of Oct 16, cash rent in Monroe County runs $151 an acre for 2026, the 8th lowest of 86 in Illinois against a state median of $254. That rent is up 23.8% from $122 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,117 an acre in 2022, up 21.9% from $6,659 in 2017. Corn made 156.5 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 170.0 bu.
