Monroe County, Illinois

Monroe County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $151 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,117 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17133. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Monroe County runs $151 per acre in 2026, up 23.8 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued by operators at $8,117 per acre in 2022, with land value change 2017-2022 at 21.9 percent, 4 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $74.1 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 52 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 10 percent in 1989-1999, 38 percent in 2000-2009, 31 percent in 2010-2019, and 38 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 27 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 12 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Solar and wind capacity in the county are both 0 MW operating and 0 MW proposed.
