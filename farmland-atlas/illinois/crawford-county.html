As of Oct 14, cash rent in Crawford County runs $200 an acre for 2026, up 19.8% from $167 in 2016 and 23rd lowest of 86 in Illinois, where the state median is $254. The census value of land and buildings was $6,710 an acre in 2022, up 27.7% from $5,254 in 2017 and still under the state median of $8,638. Corn made 188.0 bu in 2025, a bit shy of the 2021-25 average of 198.5 bu. 61% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
