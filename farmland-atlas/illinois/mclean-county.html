McLean County, Illinois

McLean County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $326 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,074 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17113. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in McLean County averages $326 per acre in 2026, up 19.9 percent from 2016. Corn yields trend up 4.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 203.2 bushels per acre and a low of 109.5 in 2012. Irrigation is not a factor here: 0 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. Heat and drought has driven 66 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, though its share fell from 77 percent in 2010-2019 to 25 percent in 2020-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 sits at 0.11.
