$326

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 20% from $272 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 14, cash rent in McLean County was $326 an acre in 2026, tied for 5th highest of 86 in Illinois against a state median of $254. That is up 19.9% from $272 in 2016. The corn trend projects 250.9 bu for 2026, 3rd highest of 66 in the state, and last year came in at 243.1 bu. In 2022, 69% of the farmland here was rented.
