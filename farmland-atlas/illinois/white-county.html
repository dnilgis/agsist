Cash rent in White County runs $175 an acre for 2026, up 34.6% from $130 in 2016. That sits well under the state median of $254 and comes in 16th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $5,590 an acre in 2022, up 12.2% from $4,983 in 2017, and 13th lowest of 91 against a state median of $8,638. Corn made 175.7 bu in 2024, a little under the 2020-24 average of 180.9 bu.
