Winnebago County, Illinois

Winnebago County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $264 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,082 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 187.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17201. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Winnebago County averages $264 per acre in 2026, up 30 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $8,082 per acre, with 2017-2022 gains of 13.2 percent, 2.5 percent a year. Corn yields trended up 3.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 187.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 99.6 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 49 percent of $82.6 million paid, and its share by period was 26 percent, 36 percent, 57 percent, then 45 percent. No cropland here is irrigated. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 83 of 1357 since 2000, with 16 of those in 2021-2025.
