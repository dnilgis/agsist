Non-irrigated cash rent in Boone County stands at $267 per acre for 2026, up 7.7 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $9,226 per acre, with that rent equal to 2.85 percent of value. Corn yield trend runs 2.9 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 192.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 148 in 2009. Heat and drought accounts for 40 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, with its share moving 35 percent, 26 percent, 49 percent, and 28 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 94 of 1357 since 2000, with 21 of those coming in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015.
