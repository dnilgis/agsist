USDA NASS county yields, 2025

DeKalb County cash rent stood at $318 an acre in 2025, 2nd highest of 10 in Illinois against a state median of $193. That rent is up 14.4% from $278 in 2014. Corn made 244.4 bu in 2025, and the trend line projects 234.0 bu for 2026, 22nd highest of 66. In 2022, 72% of the farmland here was rented.
