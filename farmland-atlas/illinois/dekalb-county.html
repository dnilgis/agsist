DeKalb County, Illinois

DeKalb County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $318 an acre (2025); land and buildings $7,704 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17037. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in DeKalb County stands at $318 per acre in 2025, up 14.4 percent from 2014. Corn yields have trended up 3.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 190.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 159.3 in 2012. Heat and drought made up 25 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 12 percent in 2000-2009, 20 percent in 2010-2019, and 32 percent in 2020-2025. Excess moisture and flood remains the largest cause of indemnities overall, at 31 percent, and no cropland in the county is irrigated.
