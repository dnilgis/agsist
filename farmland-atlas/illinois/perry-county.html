USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, Perry County cash rent stands at $172 an acre for 2026, up 60.7% from $107 in 2016 and tied for 13th lowest of 86 in Illinois against a state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings was $6,023 an acre in 2022, up 26.0% from $4,782 in 2017. Corn runs light here, with a 14-yr trend of 166.5 bu that ranks 3rd lowest of 66 in the state.
