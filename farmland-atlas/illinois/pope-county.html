Pope County, Illinois

Pope County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $92 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,224 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 158.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17151. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pope County averages $92 per acre in 2025, down 10.7 percent from 2013. Median corn yield 2012-2025 is 158.6 bushels per acre, with 2012 the worst year at 47.6. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $13.2 million, and excess moisture and flood is the largest cause at 53 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 29 percent in 1989-1999 to 37 percent in 2000-2009, 39 percent in 2010-2019, then 27 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 46 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 19 weeks; 13 of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. Census land value is $4,224 per acre in 2022. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
