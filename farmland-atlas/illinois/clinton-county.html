Clinton County, Illinois

Clinton County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $218 an acre (2023); land and buildings $9,036 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 163.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17027. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clinton County averaged $218 per acre in 2023, up 47.3 percent from 2013. Census land value came in at $9,036 per acre in 2022, up 30.4 percent from 2017, or 5.5 percent a year. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, and heat and drought account for 51 percent of crop insurance indemnities of $158.6 million paid out over 1989-2025. That share moved 30 percent, 28 percent, 59 percent, and 42 percent across the four periods on record, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 stood at 0.39. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 19 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 2012 the worst year at 8 weeks.
