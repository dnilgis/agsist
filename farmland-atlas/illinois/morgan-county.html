USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, Morgan County cash rent stands at $281 an acre for 2026, up 17.1% from $240 in 2016 and tied for 27th highest of 86 in Illinois. The census value of land and buildings was $9,869 an acre in 2022, up 19.7% from $8,247 in 2017. Corn made 228.5 bu in 2024, above the 2020-24 average of 210.5 bu.
