USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Kane County cash rent sits at $274 an acre for 2026, up 8.3% from $253 in 2016 and 32nd highest of 86 in Illinois. The census value of land and buildings was $12,276 an acre in 2022, up 16.3% from 2017 and 2nd highest of 91 in the state, well over the $8,638 state median. Corn made 233.7 bu in 2024, against a 17 year median of 185.6 bu. In 2022, 72% of the farmland was rented.
