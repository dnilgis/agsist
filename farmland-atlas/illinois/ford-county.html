Ford County, Illinois

Ford County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $294 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,956 an acre in the 2022 census; corn 14-yr trend 252.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17053. Figures as of 2026-10-14.

As of Oct 14, cash rent in Ford County runs $294 an acre for 2026. That is up 23.5% from $238 in 2016 and ranks 14th highest of 86 in Illinois. The census value of land and buildings came in at $9,956 an acre in 2022, up 28.2% from $7,769 in 2017. The corn trend projects 252.8 bu for 2026, which is highest of 66 in the state, against a state median of 225.2 bu.
