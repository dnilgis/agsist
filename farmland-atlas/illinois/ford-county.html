Ford County, Illinois

Ford County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $294 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,956 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 186.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Ford County averages $294 per acre for 2026, up 23.5 percent from 2016. The census puts land and buildings value at $9,956 per acre in 2022, with that value rising 28.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.1 percent a year. Rent as a share of that census value is 2.64 percent. Corn yield trend runs 4.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 186.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 63.9 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 total $157.6 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 61 percent, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 sits at 0.19. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, all from groundwater.
