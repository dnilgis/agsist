Non-irrigated cash rent in McHenry County stands at $234 per acre for 2026, up 17 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 177.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 124.4 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 38 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, the largest single cause, and the share by period was 26 percent in 1989-1999, 38 percent in 2000-2009, 39 percent in 2010-2019, and 35 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 30 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 107 of 1357 weeks (7.9 percent) since 2000.
