Jo Daviess County, Illinois

Jo Daviess County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $255 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,940 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17085. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jo Daviess County runs $255 per acre in 2026, up 18.6 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $7,940 per acre, up 30 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.4 percent a year. Corn yield trend rises 3.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 190.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 97.4 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 35 percent of indemnities across 1989-2025, closed crop years, with shares of 22 percent in 1989-1999, 12 percent in 2000-2009, 45 percent in 2010-2019, and 26 percent in 2020-2025. The county carries no irrigation, 0 percent of harvested cropland in 2022, and July nightly lows show no clear direction since 1976.
