Cass County, Illinois

Cass County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $306 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,711 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 198.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17017. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cass County runs $306 per acre in 2026, up 23.4 percent from 2016. Census land value came in at $7,711 per acre in 2022, up 20.5 percent from 2017, 3.8 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 3.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 198.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 113.2 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved 18 percent in 1989-1999, 24 percent in 2000-2009, 52 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025. The county drew severe drought or worse for 65 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2000 the worst year at 17 weeks and 10 weeks logged in 2021-2025.
