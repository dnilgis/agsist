$87

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest since 2011 (14 surveys on record since 2009); down 27% from $119 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Union County runs $87 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 86 in Illinois. That rent is down 27.3% from $119 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,116 an acre in 2022, up 20.4% from $4,248 in 2017, and it ranks 9th lowest of 91. The corn trend yield projects 172.2 bu for 2026, 4th lowest of 66 in the state.
