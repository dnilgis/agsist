USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Williamson County runs $101 an acre in 2026, up 18.1% from $86 in 2016 and 2nd lowest of 86 in Illinois against a state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings was $4,701 an acre in 2022, up 18.9% from $3,953 in 2017, which ranks 5th lowest of 91. Corn trends at 181.3 bu for 2026, 7th lowest of 66, and 40% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
