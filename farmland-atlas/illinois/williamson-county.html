Williamson County, Illinois

Williamson County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $101 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,701 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17199. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $101 per acre, up 18.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Corn yields have trended up 2.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, with a median of 153.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 33.7 in 2012. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $54.5 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 51 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 45 percent in 1989-1999 to 20 percent in 2000-2009, 50 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 69 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.5 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.55 F per decade, warming.
