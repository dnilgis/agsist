Vermilion County, Illinois

Vermilion County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $267 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,777 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17183. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Vermilion County stands at $267 per acre for 2026, up 14.6 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 4.1 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 194.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 100.1 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, though its share by period varied: 67 percent in 1989-1999, 15 percent in 2000-2009, 65 percent in 2010-2019, and 24 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed crop years was 0.22.
