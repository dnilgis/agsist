Sangamon County, Illinois

Sangamon County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $334 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,261 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17167. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sangamon County averages $334 per acre for 2026, up 11 percent from 2016. Corn yield trend runs 4.1 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 210.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 131.3 in 2012. Crop insurance losses totaled $156.5 million over 1989-2025, with heat and drought at 37 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 7 percent in 2000-2009, 26 percent in 2010-2019, and 11 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 8 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2021-2025, against 57 weeks total across 2000-2025. Solar operating capacity stands at 592.8 MW, wind at 302 MW.
