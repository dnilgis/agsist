Schuyler County, Illinois

Schuyler County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $249 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,519 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 182.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17169. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $249 per acre, up 26.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $6,519 per acre, up 18.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, and that rent equals 3.33 percent of the 2022 census value. Corn yield trend is 3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 182.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 75.9 in 2012. Heat and drought has driven 41 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and its share moved from 8 percent in 1989-1999 to 20 percent in 2000-2009 to 46 percent in 2010-2019 to 33 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, and no solar or wind capacity operates in the county now.
