Edgar County, Illinois

Edgar County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $275 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,162 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 191.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Edgar County runs $275 per acre, up 1.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $10,162 per acre, up 36.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.5 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 4.3 bushels per acre a year on the fitted years 2008-2024, with a median of 191.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 96.7 in 2012. Heat and drought made up 61 percent of indemnities over 1989-2025, but its share by period moved 45 percent, 10 percent, 81 percent, then 18 percent. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.12, well below the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.49. No cropland in the county is irrigated.
