Randolph County, Illinois

Randolph County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $166 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,679 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 144.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17157. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Randolph County averages $166 per acre in 2026, up 43.1 percent from 2016. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $84.5 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 48 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved from 11 percent in 1989-1999, to 32 percent in 2000-2009, to 41 percent in 2010-2019, to 43 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 31 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 13 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.45 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 69 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 67.8 F.
