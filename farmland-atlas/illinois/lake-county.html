Lake County cash rent stands at $125 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 86 in Illinois against a state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings runs the other way at $15,913 an acre in 2022, highest of 91 and up 31.0% from $12,149 in 2017. Corn still does fine here, with 201.4 bu in 2025 against a 155.2 bu median since 2008.
