Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $125 per acre. Census land value in 2022 stood at $15,913 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 31 percent, 5.5 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 runs 3.4 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 155.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 2009 at 121. Crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, closed crop years, total $11.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 42 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 3 percent in 1989-1999 to 40 percent in 2000-2009, 20 percent in 2010-2019, and 41 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse, 2021-2025, reached 51 out of the 2000-2025 count of 106.
