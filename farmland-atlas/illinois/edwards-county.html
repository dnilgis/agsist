Cash rent in Edwards County runs $156 an acre for 2026, up 24.8% from $125 in 2016. That sits 11th lowest of 86 counties, under the state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings was $5,893 an acre in 2022, up 9.7% from $5,372 in 2017, or 1.9% a year. 56% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
