Cash rent in Wabash County ran $202 an acre in 2026, up 26.2% from $160 in 2016. That is 25th lowest of 86 in Illinois, where the state median sits at $254. The census value of land and buildings was $5,778 an acre in 2022, up 4.4% from $5,533 in 2017, or 0.9% a year, and 17th lowest of 91 against a state median of $8,638. Corn made 162.8 bu in 2025, right on the county median since 2008.
