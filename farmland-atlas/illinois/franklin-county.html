Franklin County, Illinois

Franklin County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $126 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,677 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County runs $126 per acre in 2026, up 15.6 percent from 2016. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Corn yield trend from 2008-2025 runs 2.9 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 152.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 30.1 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 45 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, with the share at 51 percent in 1989-1999, 26 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, and 37 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.46 F per decade since 1976, and the county saw 16 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
