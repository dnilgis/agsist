McDonough County, Illinois

McDonough County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $293 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,948 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 198.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $293 per acre, up 8.1 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $9,948 per acre, after a rise of 35.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.3 percent a year. Corn yield trend from 2008-2023 runs 3.5 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 198.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 139 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 at 33 percent, and its share by period was 12 percent in 1989-1999, 26 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 26 percent in 2020-2025. The county recorded 16 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 91 weeks total from 2000-2025.
