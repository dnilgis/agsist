As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Henry County ran $272 an acre in 2023, up 10.6% from $246 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings reached $10,039 an acre in 2022, up 27.0% from $7,902 in 2017, and sits 22nd highest of 91 against a state median of $8,638. Corn made 223.5 bu in 2023, well over the 2019-23 average of 211.3 bu.
