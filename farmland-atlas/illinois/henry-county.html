Henry County, Illinois

Henry County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $272 an acre (2023); land and buildings $10,039 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 193.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17073. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henry County averaged $272 per acre in 2023, up 10.6 percent from 2013. The census value of land and buildings stood at $10,039 per acre in 2022, up 27 percent from 2017, or 4.9 percent a year. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 3.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 193.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 140 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 39 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share rising to 43 percent in 2010-2019 before falling to 29 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and wind capacity operating in the county totals 508.5 MW against 4.9 MW of solar.
