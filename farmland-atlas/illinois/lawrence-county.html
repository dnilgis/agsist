Lawrence County cash rent stood at $219 an acre in 2026, up 52.1% from $144 in 2016. That rent sits under the state median of $254 and ranks tied for 27th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,987 an acre in 2022, up 23.7% from $5,650 in 2017. Renters work a good share of the ground here, with 61% of farmland rented in 2022.
