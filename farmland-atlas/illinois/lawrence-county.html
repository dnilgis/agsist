Non-irrigated cash rent here is $219 per acre in 2026, up 52.1 percent since 2016. Irrigated ground is $305 per acre. Corn yields have trended up 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 166 bushels and a low of 142 in 2011. Heat and drought claims were heavy in 2010-2019 at 56 percent of indemnities, but excess moisture and flood is the largest cause overall at 49 percent of the $91.6 million paid out since 1989. The loss ratio over the last ten crop years is 0.28.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor, weeks with half the county or more in D2 or worse. July lows: NOAA nClimDiv county monthly minimum temperature.
