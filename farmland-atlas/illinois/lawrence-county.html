Lawrence County, Illinois

Lawrence County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $219 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,987 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 166.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17101. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lawrence County stands at $219 per acre for 2026, up 52.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent is $305 per acre in 2025, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.46 in 2009 to 1.37 in 2025, direction falling. Corn yield trend runs 2.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 166 bushels per acre and a worst year of 142 in 2011. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $91.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent; heat and drought's share moved 26 percent, 17 percent, 56 percent, and 13 percent across the four listed periods. Census land value is $6,987 per acre in 2022, up 23.7 percent from 2017.
