Cash rent in Saline County runs $150 an acre for 2026, up 26.1% from $119 in 2016. That rent is 7th lowest of 86 counties in Illinois, against a state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings was $4,910 an acre in 2022, up 1.8% from $4,824 in 2017, or 0.4% a year, and 6th lowest of 91. 45% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
