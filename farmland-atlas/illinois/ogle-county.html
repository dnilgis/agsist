Cash rent in Ogle County runs $282 an acre for 2026, up 16.5% from $242 in 2016 and 26th highest of 86 in Illinois. The census value of land and buildings came to $9,818 an acre in 2022, up 14.2% from $8,599 in 2017, against a state median of $8,638. The corn trend sits at 233.0 bu for 2026, and 59% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
