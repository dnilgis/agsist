Quick answers for Iroquois County

What is farmland worth in Iroquois County, Illinois?

Land and buildings were worth $8,638 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Iroquois County, Illinois?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 230.8 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
