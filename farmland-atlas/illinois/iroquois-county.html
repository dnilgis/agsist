Stands out against its own record

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Iroquois County was $205 an acre in 2022, up 20.6% from $170 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,638 an acre in 2022, up 17.6% from $7,348 in 2017. That sits right at the Illinois median and ranks 46th lowest of 91. The corn trend projects 230.8 bu for 2026, 28th highest of 66 in the state, and 60% of farmland was rented in 2022.
