Wayne County, Illinois

Wayne County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $176 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,608 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 160.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17191. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wayne County stands at $176 per acre for 2026, up 28.5 percent from 2016. Land value from the 2022 census was $5,608 per acre, up 17.7 percent from 2017, or 3.3 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 3.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 160.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 39.7 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities across closed years 1989-2025 total $292.5 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 56 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 19 percent in 1989-1999, to 33 percent in 2000-2009, to 45 percent in 2010-2019, to 17 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind capacity are not present in this county.
