Will County, Illinois

Non-irrigated cash rent in Will County averages $231 per acre in 2026, up 22.9 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $10,259 per acre in 2022, and the 2022 rent-to-value figure was 1.99 percent. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 177.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 126 in 2012. Crop insurance history shows $95.5 million paid out over 1989-2025, closed crop years, led by excess moisture and flood at 43 percent; heat and drought's share moved 47 percent, 39 percent, 30 percent, 47 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 42 of 1357 since 2000, with 7 of those in 2021-2025.
