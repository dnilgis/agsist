Cash rent in Hardin County was $100 an acre in 2020, up 20.5% from $83 in 2009. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,632 an acre in 2022, up 41.2% from $3,281 in 2017, or 7.1% a year. That sits well under the state median of $8,638 and ranks 4th lowest of 91 in Illinois. Only 15% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Hardin County

What is farmland worth in Hardin County, Illinois?

Land and buildings were worth $4,632 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
