Jersey County cash rent sits at $221 an acre for 2026, down 7.9% from $240 in 2016, and 29th lowest of 86 against a state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 32.2% to $8,366 an acre in 2022 from $6,330 in 2017. Corn trends at 220.0 bu for 2026, a bit under the state median of 225.2 bu. 41% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
