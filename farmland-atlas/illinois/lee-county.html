Lee County, Illinois

Lee County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $283 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,639 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 188.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17103. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lee County runs $283 per acre in 2026, up 19.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $323 per acre, and the irrigated multiple sits at 1.14 in 2026 versus 1.13 in 2009, with no clear direction. Corn yield trend is 3.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 188.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 142.3 in 2012. Drought weeks were low from 2000-2025, at 90 of 1357, and only 5 weeks from 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 40 percent, 20 percent, 21 percent, then 37 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Census land value stands at $10,639 per acre, up 22.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
