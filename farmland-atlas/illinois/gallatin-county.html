As of Oct 16, cash rent in Gallatin County runs $155 an acre for 2026, 10th lowest of 86 in Illinois and under the state median of $254. That is down 3.1% from $160 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,746 an acre in 2022, up 8.3% from $5,307 in 2017, and 16th lowest of 91. Renters farm 54% of the ground here.
