Whiteside County, Illinois

Whiteside County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $270 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,976 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 188.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17195. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Whiteside County averages $270 per acre in 2026, up 15.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $303 per acre, a multiple of 1.12 over non-irrigated, down from 1.27 in 2008, with no clear direction over that span. Corn yield trend runs 3.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 188.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 139.8 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $106.4 million, led by excess moisture and flood at 34 percent, and heat and drought's share moved 34 percent, 29 percent, 28 percent, then 39 percent across the four periods listed. Census land value sits at $8,976 per acre in 2022, up 13.7 percent from 2017.
