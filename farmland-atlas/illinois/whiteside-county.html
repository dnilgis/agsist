Cash rent in Whiteside County was $270 an acre in 2026, up 15.4% from $234 in 2016 and 35th highest of 86 in Illinois against a state median of $254. The census value of land and buildings was $8,976 an acre in 2022, up 13.7% from $7,892 in 2017. Corn made 229.8 bu in 2025, above the 217.4 bu average for 2021 through 2025. In 2022, 55% of the farmland was rented.
