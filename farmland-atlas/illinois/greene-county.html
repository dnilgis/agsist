Greene County, Illinois

Greene County, IL: non-irrigated cash rent $280 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,667 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 191.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 17061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greene County stands at $280 per acre for 2026, up 23.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,667 per acre in 2022, after a 24 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, 4.4 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $87.7 million paid out over 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 35 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 25 percent in 1989-1999, to 16 percent in 2000-2009, to 35 percent in 2010-2019, to 11 percent in 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 46 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with only 4 such weeks in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
