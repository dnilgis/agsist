U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Lauderdale County cash rent runs $135 an acre for 2026, up 13.4% from $119 in 2016. That sits well above the state median of $65 and ranks tied for 15th highest of 88. The census value of land and buildings was $3,838 an acre in 2022, up 13.4% from $3,385 in 2017, though that is 18th lowest of 93 and under the state median of $5,131. 63% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
