Stands out against its own record

6 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Washington County cropland rent sits at $64 an acre for 2026, up 24.5% from $51 in 2016 and just under the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings ran $9,728 an acre in 2022, up 23.6% from 2017 and 4th highest of 93 in Tennessee. Corn is another story, with a 14-yr trend of 141.9 bu that ranks 3rd lowest of 42.
