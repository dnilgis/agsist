Non-irrigated cash rent in Williamson County stands at $53 per acre, down 23.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Cropland here is not irrigated in any meaningful way, with only 2 percent of harvested cropland under irrigation in the 2022 census. Heat and drought account for 71 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share has moved from 65 percent in 1989-1999 to 80 percent in 2000-2009 to 53 percent in 2010-2019 to 90 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have moved from a 67.3 F normal to a 69.1 F average over 2017-2026, and the county has logged 15 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. Census land value sits at $7,148 per acre, up 17.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
