Houston County, Tennessee

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,906 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.81 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 42.58 inches, 90% of normal (47.29 inches) through Nov 15, at Tennessee Ridge, 3 mi S. Frost-free season: 210 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,520 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,906 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $64 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 276.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,361 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.81 per base acre, PLC $2.32.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (92.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $56.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.5%.
