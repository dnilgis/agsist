$110

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 48.6% from $74 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Hardeman County runs $110 an acre for 2026, up 48.6% from $74 in 2016. That ranks 23rd highest of 88 in Tennessee, against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $2,804 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from 2017, yet still 2nd lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131. Corn trends at 174.9 bu for 2026, and only 18% of farmland was rented in 2022.
