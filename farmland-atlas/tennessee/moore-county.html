Moore County, Tennessee

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,198 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.81 per base acre for corn.

Moore County vs Tennessee: $55 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 33% of the other 87 Tennessee counties with a 2026 rent (30th lowest of 88).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.01 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,442 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,198 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $55 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.73 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 14.1% from 2016 to 2026. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 320.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.81 per base acre, PLC $2.70.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (107.41 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $70.17 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.74 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.76 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 73.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1991 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.8%.

Quick answers for Moore County

What is cash rent in Moore County, Tennessee?

The county average is $55 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Moore County, Tennessee?

Land and buildings were worth $4,198 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
