As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Monroe County runs $53 an acre for 2026, up 31.2% from $40 in 2016 and below the state median of $65, tied for 26th lowest of 88. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,749 an acre in 2022, up 35.2% from $4,252 in 2017 and 35th highest of 93. Corn has held a median of 161.7 bu from 2009 to 2021, and 29% of farmland was rented in 2022.
