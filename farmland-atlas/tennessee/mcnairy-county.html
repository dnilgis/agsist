McNairy County, Tennessee

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,954 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.65 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 40.08 inches, 81% of normal (49.34 inches) through Nov 17, at Finger, 4.1 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 216 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,636 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,954 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $65 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 47.7% from 2014 to 2026. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 73.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 25 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 27,764 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.65 per base acre, PLC $2.68.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (106.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $56.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 20 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.15, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 66% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 71%.
