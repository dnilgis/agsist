USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cropland in Cannon County rents for $94 an acre in 2026, up 6.2% from $88 in 2016 and 28th highest of 88 against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,095 an acre in 2022, up 69.9% from $3,587 in 2017 and 25th highest of 93. Corn runs a 14-yr trend of 169.8 bu, 19th lowest of 42, though 2024 came in at just 92.2 bu.
