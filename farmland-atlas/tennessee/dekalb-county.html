Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2010; up 112.0% from $50 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in DeKalb County runs $106 an acre for 2026, up 112.0% from $50 in 2016. That rent ranks 24th highest of 88 in Tennessee, well over the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,806 an acre in 2022, up 42.0% from $3,384 in 2017, which sits 43rd lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131.
