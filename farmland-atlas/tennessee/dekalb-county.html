DeKalb County, Tennessee

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,806 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.48 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 28.96 inches, 56% of normal (52.03 inches) through Nov 16, at Smithville, 4.9 mi WSW. Frost-free season: 207 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,402 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,806 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $106 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 112% from 2016 to 2026. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 185.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 373 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.48 per base acre, PLC $2.97.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (118.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $56.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.89 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.08, 2016 to 2025. 1.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6%.

Cropland rent in DeKalb County runs $106 an acre for 2026, up 112.0% from $50 in 2016. That rent ranks 24th highest of 88 in Tennessee, well over the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,806 an acre in 2022, up 42.0% from $3,384 in 2017, which sits 43rd lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131.
