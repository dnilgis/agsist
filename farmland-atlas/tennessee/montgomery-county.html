As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Montgomery County runs $132 an acre for 2026, up 11.9% from $118 in 2016. That sits well above the Tennessee median of $65 and ranks 17th highest of 88. The census value of land and buildings was $5,228 an acre in 2022, up 6.3% from 2017 and close to the state median of $5,131. Corn made 165.2 bu in 2025, right on the county's long run median.
