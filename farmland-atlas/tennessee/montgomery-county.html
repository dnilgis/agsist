Montgomery County, Tennessee

Montgomery County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $132 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,228 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 165.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47125. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $132 per acre, up 11.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $5,228 per acre, with that value up 6.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 1.2 percent a year. Heat and drought account for 43 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share moved from 41 percent in 1989-1999 to 37 percent in 2000-2009, then to 44 percent in 2010-2019, then to 46 percent in 2020-2025. The corn yield trend for 2009-2025 runs 3.8 bushels per acre a year, against a median of 165.2 and a worst year of 63.7 in 2012. July nights average 70 F over 2017-2026, compared with 68.3 F for the 1991-2020 normal. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
