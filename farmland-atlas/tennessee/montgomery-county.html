Montgomery County, Tennessee

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,228 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.18 per base acre for corn.

Montgomery County vs Tennessee: $132 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 82% of the other 87 Tennessee counties with a 2026 rent (17th highest of 88).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.45 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 76% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 214 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,554 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,228 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $132 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.75 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 32% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 11.9% from 2016 to 2026. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 127.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,264 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.88 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.78, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 39% of the median yield. 1.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 42.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 34.2%.

Quick answers for Montgomery County

What is cash rent in Montgomery County, Tennessee?

The county average is $132 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Montgomery County, Tennessee?

Land and buildings were worth $5,228 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Montgomery County, Tennessee?

The county's 2021-25 average is 172.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
