Macon County's crop insurance record leans toward wet years, not dry ones. Excess moisture and flood is the largest cause of indemnities at 48 percent, and insurers have paid out more than they took in premiums in 22 of the 27 closed crop years from 1989 to 2025, with a loss ratio of 1.59. Heat and drought share fell from 58 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 40 percent in 2020-2025. Nightly July lows have warmed a little, and the 2017-2026 average is 67.7 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 66.5 F. Non-irrigated cash rent is $135 per acre for 2026, up 3.8 percent since 2016.
