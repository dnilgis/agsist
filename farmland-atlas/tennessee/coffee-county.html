Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 61.7% from $81 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Coffee County was $131 an acre in 2026, up 61.7% from $81 in 2016 and tied for 18th highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,382 an acre in 2022, up 31.1% from $4,105 in 2017 and a little over the state median of $5,131. The corn trend yield projects 183.0 bu for 2026, tied for 8th highest of 42.
