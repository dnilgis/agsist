Non-irrigated cash rent in Cocke County averages $75 per acre in 2026, up 41.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $275 per acre in 2024. The July nightly low has warmed from a 65.3 F normal to 66.8 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.51 F per decade trend since 1976, and the county did not reach a 66 F July average low until the 2010s. Drought weeks in severe or worse conditions fell to 2 between 2021-2025, down from a 2000-2025 total of 133 of 1357, with 2008 the worst year at 41 weeks. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $8.9 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 45 percent, and the loss ratio for 2016-2025 stands at 0.79.
