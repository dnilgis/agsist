As of Oct 16, cash rent in Weakley County runs $145 an acre for 2026, 11th highest of 88 in Tennessee and well over the state median of $65. That is up 9.8% from $132 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $4,680 an acre in 2022, up 29.4% from $3,616 in 2017, and ranks 41st lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131. In 2022, 42% of the farmland here was rented.
