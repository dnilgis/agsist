Hamblen County, Tennessee

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,809 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,362 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,809 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $48.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.69 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 8% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 30.7% from 2016 to 2026. 47% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 260.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

$49

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest since 2011 (14 surveys on record since 2009); down 30.7% from $70 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Hamblen County rents for $49 an acre in 2026, down 30.7% from $70 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $65 and ranks 25th lowest of 88. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, up 23.6% to $6,809 an acre in 2022 from $5,507 in 2017, which is 14th highest of 93 in Tennessee. In 2022, 47% of the farmland was rented.

Quick answers for Hamblen County

What is cash rent in Hamblen County, Tennessee?

The county average is $49 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Hamblen County, Tennessee?

Land and buildings were worth $6,809 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
