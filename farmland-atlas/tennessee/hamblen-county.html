Stands out against its own record

$49

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest since 2011 (14 surveys on record since 2009); down 30.7% from $70 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Hamblen County rents for $49 an acre in 2026, down 30.7% from $70 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $65 and ranks 25th lowest of 88. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, up 23.6% to $6,809 an acre in 2022 from $5,507 in 2017, which is 14th highest of 93 in Tennessee. In 2022, 47% of the farmland was rented.
