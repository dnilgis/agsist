Cash rent in Fentress County runs $40 an acre for 2026, down 35.2% from $61 in 2016. That sits 19th lowest of 88 against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 21.3% to $4,211 an acre in 2022 from $3,472 in 2017, which ranks 27th lowest of 93. Corn trends at 145.3 bu, 6th lowest of 42 in the state.
