$70

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 29.6% from $54 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Loudon County cropland rents for $70 an acre in 2026, up 29.6% from $54 in 2016 and tied for 40th highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings was $7,931 an acre in 2022, up 39.4% from $5,690 in 2017, or 6.9% a year. That census value ranks 8th highest of 93, well over the state median of $5,131.
