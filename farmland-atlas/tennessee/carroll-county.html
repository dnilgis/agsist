Carroll County, Tennessee

Carroll County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $127 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,459 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carroll County averages $127 per acre in 2026, up 15.5 percent from 2016. Land value from the 2022 census sits at $3,459 per acre, up 26.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.8 percent a year. Cropland here is mostly not irrigated: irrigation covers 5 percent of harvested cropland. Corn yield trend runs 2.7 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 152.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 92 in 2012. July nightly lows average 70.3 F over 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 68.9 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.46 F per decade, warming. Excess moisture and flood account for 44 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid out from 1989-2025, the largest single cause.
