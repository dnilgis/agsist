Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 38.6% from $66 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Hardin County runs $92 an acre for 2026, up 38.6% from $66 in 2016 and 31st highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings was $3,021 an acre in 2022, up 35.3% from $2,232 in 2017, and still 6th lowest of 93 in the state. Corn trend yield sits at 165.0 bu for 2026, 13th lowest of 42. Just 18% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
