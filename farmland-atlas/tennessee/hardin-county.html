Hardin County, Tennessee

Hardin County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $92 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,021 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 143.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hardin County runs $92 per acre, up 38.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stands at $3,021 per acre, up 35.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.2 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 3.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, with a median of 143.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 88.6 in 2010. July nightly lows average 69.8 F over 2017-2026 against a 68.2 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 is 0.43 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of indemnities fell 1989-1999 to 2000-2009 to 2010-2019, then rose in 2020-2025 to 49 percent, and only 8 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated.
