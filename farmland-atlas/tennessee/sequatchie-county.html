Sequatchie County, Tennessee

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,508 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.48 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 41.69 inches, 79% of normal (52.82 inches) through Nov 17, at Dunlap, 3.1 mi NW. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,263 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,508 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $48 an acre (USDA, 2025). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 17.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 16.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 267.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 951 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.48 per base acre, PLC $2.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $48.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.98 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.09 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 53% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 15.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 2001 to 2025; too much rain caused 47.3%.
