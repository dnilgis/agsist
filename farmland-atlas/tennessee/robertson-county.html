Robertson County, Tennessee

Robertson County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $187 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,424 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47147. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Robertson County runs $187 per acre in 2026, up 31.7 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $7,424 per acre in 2022, after a 40.5 percent rise from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 69.3 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.9 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. Drought weeks stayed low: 8 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 50 weeks total across 2000-2025. Heat and drought drove 51 percent of indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share rose to 67 percent in 2020-2025, up from 41 percent in 2010-2019.
