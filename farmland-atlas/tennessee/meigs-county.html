Cash rent in Meigs County runs $25 an acre for 2026, tied for 3rd lowest of 88 in Tennessee and down 30.6% from $36 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, rising 60.1% from $3,327 in 2017 to $5,327 in 2022, which sits 40th highest of 93. Corn holds up fine here, with a 14-yr trend of 212.3 bu that ranks highest of 42 in the state.
