Cropland in Anderson County rents for $27 an acre in 2026, the 7th lowest of 88 in Tennessee against a state median of $65. Pasture goes for $26 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $7,843 an acre in 2022, up 43.5% from $5,465 in 2017, which ranks 9th highest of 93. 37% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
