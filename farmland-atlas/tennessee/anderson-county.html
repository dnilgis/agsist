Anderson County, Tennessee

Anderson County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,843 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
