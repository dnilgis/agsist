Scott County, Tennessee

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

75.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.08 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 88% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,022 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,689 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.58 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Aug 31, 2026 to Sep 1, 2026). 8% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 34.4% from 2014 to 2026. 15% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 71.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 80 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.81 per base acre, PLC $2.25.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (89.55 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $79.67 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.79 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $48.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Quick answers for Scott County

What is cash rent in Scott County, Tennessee?

The county average is $20 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Scott County, Tennessee?

Land and buildings were worth $3,689 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
