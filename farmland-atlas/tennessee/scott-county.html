Non-irrigated cash rent in Scott County averages $20 per acre in 2026, down 34.4 percent from 2014. The census value of land and buildings stands at $3,689 per acre in 2022, up 38.1 percent from 2017, or 6.7 percent a year. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland. July nights have warmed to an average low of 65.2 F for 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 63.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.57 F per decade, warming. Drought was present 75 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 30 weeks, and 10 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
