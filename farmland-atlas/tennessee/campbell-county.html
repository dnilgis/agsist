Campbell County, Tennessee

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,131 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.12 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 196 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,076 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,131 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 218.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.12 per base acre, PLC $1.80.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (71.82 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $30.99 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $15.87 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $47.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 48.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2004; too much rain caused 23%.
