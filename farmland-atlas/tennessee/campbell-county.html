Campbell County, Tennessee

Campbell County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2025); land and buildings $5,131 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47013. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Campbell County averages $22 per acre in 2025, down 6.4 percent from 2014. Census land and buildings value stands at $5,131 per acre, up 24 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.4 percent a year. July nights average 65.3 F over 2017-2026, against a 64.3 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.38 F per decade, warming. Drought held at least 50 percent of the county at severe level or worse for 76 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 30 weeks and 6 weeks from 2021-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
