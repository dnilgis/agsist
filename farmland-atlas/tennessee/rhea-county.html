Rhea County, Tennessee

Rhea County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $26 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,301 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.53 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47143. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rhea County runs $26 per acre, down 21.2 percent from 2014 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $5,301 per acre, up 23 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.2 percent a year. No cropland here is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 44 percent of $7.4 million paid; its share ran 50 percent in 1989-1999, 34 percent in 2000-2009, 43 percent in 2010-2019, and 53 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 66.8 F normal to 68.3 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.55 F per decade warming trend since 1976.
