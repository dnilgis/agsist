Cropland rent in Rhea County runs $26 an acre for 2026, 6th lowest of 88 counties in Tennessee and well under the state median of $65. That rent is down 21.2% from $33 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 23.0% from 2017 to $5,301 an acre in 2022, which ranks 41st highest of 93. 32% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
