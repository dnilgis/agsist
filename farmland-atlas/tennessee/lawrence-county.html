Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 59.8% from $82 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland cash rent in Lawrence County runs $131 an acre for 2026, up 59.8% from $82 in 2016 and tied for 18th highest of 88 in Tennessee. The state median sits at $65. The census value of land and buildings was $4,579 an acre in 2022, up 38.9% from $3,296 in 2017, and that ranks 38th lowest of 93. Corn came in at 170.3 bu in 2025, against a 14 year trend of 161.7 bu.
