Stands out against its own record

$25

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 12 surveys since 2009; down 26.5% from $34 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Union County cropland rent was $25 an acre in 2026, tied for 3rd lowest of 88 in Tennessee and well under the $65 state median. That rent is down 26.5% from $34 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings ran $4,605 an acre in 2022, up 12.5% from $4,092 in 2017, or 2.4% a year. Only 11% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
