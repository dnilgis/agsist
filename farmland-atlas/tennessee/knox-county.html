Knox County, Tennessee

Knox County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,302 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47093. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Knox County runs $36 per acre, up 31.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings carry a census value of $12,302 per acre, up 25 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.6 percent a year. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities ran 22 percent in 1989-1999, 24 percent in 2000-2009, and 2 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks were 112 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 39 in 2008 alone and 9 from 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 68.8 F from 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.5 F, warming 0.47 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
