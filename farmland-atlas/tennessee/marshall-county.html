Marshall County, Tennessee

Marshall County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,660 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47117. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Marshall County averages $70 per acre in 2026, up 40 percent from 2016. Heat and drought caused 79 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share has stayed above 63 percent in every decade counted since. Drought weeks came to 134 of 1357 between 2000-2025, with 40 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. July nights have warmed to a 68.7 F average low for 2017-2026, above the 67 F normal for 1991-2020. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
