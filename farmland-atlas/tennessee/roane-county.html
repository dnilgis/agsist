Roane County, Tennessee

Roane County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,039 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47145. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Roane County averages $38 per acre in 2026, up 22.6 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings stood at $6,039 per acre in 2022, up 14.7 percent from 2017, or 2.8 percent a year. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. July nightly lows have averaged 68.7 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.2 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.57 F per decade, warming. The county spent 132 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 39 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
