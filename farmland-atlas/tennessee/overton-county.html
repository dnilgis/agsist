Cash rent in Overton County runs $69 an acre for 2026, up 80.3% from $38 in 2016 and 43rd highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings was $4,036 an acre in 2022, 21st lowest of 93 and under the state median of $5,131. Corn trends at 183.0 bu for 2026, tied for 8th highest of 42 counties.
