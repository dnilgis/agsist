As of Oct 16, cash rent in Decatur County runs $75 an acre for 2026, up 29.3% from $58 in 2016 and tied for 35th highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings was $2,946 an acre in 2022, up 33.4% from 2017, yet still 3rd lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131. Corn trends at 172.4 bu for 2026, about even with the state median of 172.5 bu.
