Non-irrigated cash rent in Tipton County is $156 per acre in 2026, up 44.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents at $172 per acre. Land and buildings carry a census value of $4,367 per acre in 2022, with land value up 32.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.8 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $35.2 million in indemnities from 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 28 percent in 1989-1999 to 41 percent in 2000-2009, 15 percent in 2010-2019, and 57 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low, 106 of 1357 weeks since 2000, worst year 2012 with 20 weeks. Solar and wind capacity is not present in this county.
