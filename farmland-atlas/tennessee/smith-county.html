Non-irrigated cash rent in Smith County is $130 per acre in 2026, up 52.9 percent from 2016. Land value has climbed faster, rising 55.3 percent since 2017 to $5,358 per acre. The county's crop insurance shows a long-term loss ratio of 1.25 over closed crop years from 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood driving 52 percent of indemnities. Heat and drought made up 45 percent of claims in 1989-1999 and 48 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed 0.51 F per decade since 1976.
