Rutherford County, Tennessee

Rutherford County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $65 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,250 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 161.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47149. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rutherford County averages $65 per acre in 2026, down 13.3 percent from 2016. Heat and drought account for 69 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid over 1989-2025, and that share was 42 percent in 1989-1999, 80 percent in 2000-2009, 73 percent in 2010-2019, and 65 percent in 2020-2025. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, 2022 census. July nights average 69.6 F over 2017-2026, up from the 67.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the county has logged 7 Julys at or above 70 F since 1900. Census land value stands at $10,250 per acre, up 39.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
