Shelby County, Tennessee

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,264 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.30 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.72 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,905 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,264 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $120 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.69 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 54% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 49% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Cattle: NASS withholds this county's count (D). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 240.7 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,485 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

County median corn basis: 28 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 26: $4.68 at Memphis, 14 miles. 1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 45 cents over Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.30 per base acre, PLC $3.09.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (123.25 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $60.21 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $27.24 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.58 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.13 per base acre, PLC $8.90. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (49.29 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $60.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 14 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.31 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.99, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 7.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 30.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.5%.

Quick answers for Shelby County

What is cash rent in Shelby County, Tennessee?

The county average is $120 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Shelby County, Tennessee?

Land and buildings were worth $5,264 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
