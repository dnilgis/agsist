Hickman County, Tennessee

Hickman County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,098 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 163.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47081. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hickman County averages $40 per acre in 2026, down 37.5 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 Census sits at $4,098 per acre, up 29.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.4 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 4.1 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 163.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 65.7 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 49 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share rose to 54 percent in 2020-2025 after standing at 41 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks ran 24 out of the 2021-2025 stretch, against 70 total since 2000. No solar or wind capacity operates in the county now; 5.5 MW of solar is proposed to EIA.
