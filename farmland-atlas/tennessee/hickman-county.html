Hickman County, Tennessee

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,098 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.75 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 208 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,468 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,098 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 37.5% from 2016 to 2026. 24.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out. 126.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 5.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,960 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.75 per base acre, PLC $2.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (103.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $56.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 20 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.17 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.32 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 40% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 48.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 45.5%.

Hickman County's heat and drought claims stand out. They were 44 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999 and 54 percent in 2020-2025, and the county's loss ratio over the last ten closed crop years is 1.32, meaning insurers paid out more than farmers paid in premiums. Non-irrigated cash rent is $40 per acre in 2026, down 37.5 percent from 2016. The corn yield trend is up 4.1 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, though the worst year, 2012, was 65.7 bushels. July nights are warmer too: the 2017-2026 average low is 68.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.3 F.
