Cumberland County, Tennessee

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,023 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.99 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

50.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 39.19 inches, 79% of normal (49.65 inches) through Nov 17, at Crossville Memorial Airport.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,023 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $94 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.99 per base acre, PLC $2.41.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (96.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $48.20 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 57% of the median yield. 0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).
