Johnson County, Tennessee

Johnson County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,031 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47091. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Johnson County runs $30 per acre, down 6.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $6,031 per acre, up 51.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 8.7 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $4.7 million in indemnities over 1989-2020, with heat and drought the largest cause at 40 percent; that share moved 46 percent in 1989-1999, 28 percent in 2000-2009, and 64 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks were 113 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 39 weeks in the worst year, 2008, and none from 2021-2025. July nights have warmed from a 59.6 F normal to 60.7 F over 2017-2026, a trend of 0.47 F per decade.
