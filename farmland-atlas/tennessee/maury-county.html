Cropland rent in Maury County runs $87 an acre for 2026, up 15.3% from $75 in 2014 and 32nd highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,916 an acre in 2022, up 46.5% from $4,037 in 2017, which puts it 30th highest of 93 against a state median of $5,131. Corn trends at 180.2 bu an acre, 11th highest of 42, though the 2025 crop came in at 146.3 bu.
