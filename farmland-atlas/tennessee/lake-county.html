Lake County, Tennessee

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,535 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.78 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

27.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 236 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,693 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,535 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $196 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.72 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 52% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 34.2% from 2016 to 2026. 72% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Cattle: NASS withholds this county's count (D). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,361 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.78 per base acre, PLC $3.28.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (130.73 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $94.45 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $28.89 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.93 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.47 per base acre, PLC $10.05. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (55.66 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $60.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.40 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.66, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 22.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 61.4%.
