Non-irrigated cash rent in Lake County runs $196 per acre for 2026, up 34.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $223 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has fallen from 1.56 in 2009 to 1.14 in 2026. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 161.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 116.8 in 2012. Census land value sits at $4,535 per acre, up 29.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
