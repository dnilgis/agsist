Franklin County, Tennessee

Franklin County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $173 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,951 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County stands at $173 per acre, up 44.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value reached $5,951 per acre in 2022, after a 45.1 percent change from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 3.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 172.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 83.3 in 2012. Heat and drought made up 36 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 11 percent in 2000-2009, 55 percent in 2010-2019, and 40 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have moved from a 66.7 F normal to 68.1 F over 2017-2026, and irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland.
