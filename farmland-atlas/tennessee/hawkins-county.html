Stands out against its own record

85.0 bu

Corn yield, all practices, 2024: the lowest of 15 published years since 2009.

USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cash rent in Hawkins County runs $26 an acre for 2026, 5th lowest of 88 in Tennessee and down 17.7% from $31 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 34.6% to $5,015 an acre in 2022 from $3,727 in 2017, close to the state median of $5,131. Corn is thin ground here, with a 14-yr trend of 128.6 bu that ranks 2nd lowest of 42 against a state median of 172.5 bu.
