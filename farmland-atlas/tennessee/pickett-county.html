Pickett County, Tennessee

Pickett County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,393 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47137. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pickett County is $40 per acre in 2026, down 16.7 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $4,393 per acre, up 25.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 67.3 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 65.5 F, with a trend of 0.67 F per decade since 1976, and the 66 F mark was first reached in the 2010s. Heat and drought drove 48 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and no cropland here was irrigated as of the 2022 census. Drought weeks fell from 29 in 2007 to 8 across 2021-2025.
