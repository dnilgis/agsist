Bradley County, Tennessee

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,324 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.94 per base acre for corn.

Bradley County vs Tennessee: $38 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 18% of the other 87 Tennessee counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 17th lowest of 88).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.39 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 224 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,529 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,324 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $38 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 22% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 11.8% from 2016 to 2026. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 257.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 656 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 188 acres, so read the change with care.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.94 per base acre, PLC $2.51.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $47.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 4 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $23.59 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.29, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 38% of the median yield. 1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 72.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.5%.

Quick answers for Bradley County

What is cash rent in Bradley County, Tennessee?

The county average is $38 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bradley County, Tennessee?

Land and buildings were worth $8,324 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
