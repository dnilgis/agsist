Bradley County, Tennessee

Bradley County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,324 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 133.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47011. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bradley County runs $38 per acre, up 11.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings carry a census value of $8,324 per acre, up 37.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.5 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 73 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1990-2025, and that share has moved up in every decade listed: 56 percent in 1989-1999, 70 percent in 2000-2009, 73 percent in 2010-2019, 79 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed from a 67.7 F normal (1991-2020) to 69.4 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.52 F per decade trend since 1976. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed in the county.
