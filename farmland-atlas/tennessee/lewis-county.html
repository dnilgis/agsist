As of Oct 16, cropland in Lewis County rents for $31 an acre in 2026, 12th lowest of 88 counties in Tennessee against a state median of $65. Pasture goes for $17 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,635 an acre in 2022, up 27.1% from $2,859 in 2017, which ranks 15th lowest of 93. Only 18% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
