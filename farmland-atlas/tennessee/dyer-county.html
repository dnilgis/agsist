Dyer County cash rent sits at $161 an acre for 2026, up 23.8% from $130 in 2016 and 6th highest of 88 in Tennessee, well over the $65 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $4,619 an acre in 2022, up 37.3% from $3,364 in 2017. Corn made 156.6 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 165.9 bu. 69% of farmland was rented in 2022.
