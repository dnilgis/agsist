Dyer County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $161 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,619 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 160.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dyer County is $161 per acre for 2026, up 23.8 percent from 2016.
