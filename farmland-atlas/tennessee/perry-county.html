Perry County, Tennessee

Perry County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $94 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,130 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 107.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47135. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Perry County runs $94 per acre, up 193.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $3,130 per acre, up 41.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields rose 3.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2022, with a median of 107.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 75 in 2008. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal low is 67.9 F against 69.5 F for 2017-2026, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.65 F per decade. Crop insurance losses lean toward excess moisture and flood, at 52 percent of the $3.3 million paid from 1989-2025. Solar and wind operating capacity is 0 MW each.
