Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 10 surveys since 2011; up 193.8% from $32 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Perry County runs $94 an acre for 2026, up 193.8% from $32 in 2016 and tied for 26th highest of 88 in Tennessee. The census value of land and buildings was $3,130 an acre in 2022, up 41.2% from $2,216 in 2017, yet still 8th lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
