Wilson County, Tennessee

Wilson County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $56 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,533 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47189. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $56 per acre, up 11 percent from 2016 to 2026. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so the crop depends on rainfall and on July heat. July nights average 69.3 F over 2017-2026, up from a 67.9 F normal in 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.45 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 63 percent of $4.9 million paid out over 1989-2025, and its share has moved from 40 percent in 1989-1999 to 71 percent in 2000-2009 to 60 percent in 2010-2019 to 68 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $6,533 per acre, up 33 percent from 2017 to 2022. Solar and wind capacity here are both 0 MW.
