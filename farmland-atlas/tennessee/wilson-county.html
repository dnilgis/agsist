Cash rent in Wilson County runs $56 an acre for 2026, up 11.0% from $50 in 2016 and under the state median of $65. That puts it 31st lowest of 88. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,533 an acre in 2022, up 33.0% from $4,911 in 2017 and 19th highest of 93. Corn trends at 126.6 bu, the lowest of 42 counties.
