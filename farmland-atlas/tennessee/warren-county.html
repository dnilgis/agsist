Non-irrigated cash rent in Warren County runs $115 per acre, up 69.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $6,580 per acre in 2022, after a 90.4 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, 13.7 percent a year. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 3.6 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 167.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 73.6 in 2012. Drought weeks were 145 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst year 2008 with 50 weeks, and 28 weeks from 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 45 percent, 13 percent, 27 percent, 22 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, 2020-2025. Solar and wind, operating and proposed, are 0 MW.
