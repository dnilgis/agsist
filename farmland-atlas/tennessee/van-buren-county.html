Cropland rent in Van Buren County ran $26 an acre in 2025, down 38.8% from $43 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 34.6% to $4,510 an acre in 2022 from $3,350 in 2017. That sits under the state median of $5,131 and ranks 36th lowest of 93. Only 13% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
