Van Buren County, Tennessee

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,510 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.42 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.50 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 208 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,204 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,510 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.77 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 13% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 216.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 234 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.42 per base acre, PLC $3.45.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (137.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $89.79 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $30.37 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). PRF grazing county base value: $48.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 31.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 8%.

Cropland rent in Van Buren County ran $26 an acre in 2025, down 38.8% from $43 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 34.6% to $4,510 an acre in 2022 from $3,350 in 2017. That sits under the state median of $5,131 and ranks 36th lowest of 93. Only 13% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
