Non-irrigated cash rent in Van Buren County averages $26 per acre in 2025, down 38.8 percent from 2014. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, stands at $4,510 per acre in 2022, up 34.6 percent from 2017, or 6.1 percent a year. July nights average 66.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 65.4 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.55 F per decade, warming. Drought hit 147 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 at severe level or worse, with 29 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025, and heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 20 percent in 2000-2009, to 41 percent in 2010-2019, to 35 percent in 2020-2025.
