Cropland rent in Sevier County runs $35 an acre in 2026, up 12.9% from $31 in 2016 and tied for 14th lowest of 88 in Tennessee, against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings tells another story at $7,730 an acre in 2022, up 26.4% from $6,114 in 2017 and 10th highest of 93. Pasture rents for $20 an acre in 2026, and 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
