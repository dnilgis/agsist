Benton County, Tennessee

Benton County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $73 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,196 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $73 per acre, up 9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yields trended up 5 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2023, with a median of 153.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 72 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 47 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share moved 27 percent in 1989-1999, 50 percent in 2000-2009, 30 percent in 2010-2019, and 60 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 68.6 F normal to a 70.4 F average over 2017-2026, and only 2 percent of cropland here is irrigated.
