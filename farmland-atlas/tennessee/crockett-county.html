Crockett County, Tennessee

Crockett County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $143 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,729 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Crockett County runs $143 per acre in 2026, up 19.2 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $4,729 per acre in 2022, after a 40.5 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, 7 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 156.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 88.8 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood account for 48 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, more than heat and drought in any of the four decades listed. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
