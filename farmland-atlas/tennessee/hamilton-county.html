Hamilton County, Tennessee

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.41 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

13.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 36.3 inches, 76% of normal (47.57 inches) through Nov 18, at Chattanooga Airport. Frost-free season: 226 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,544 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $28.50 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 5% from 2014 to 2026. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 161.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,332 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.41 per base acre, PLC $2.43.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (96.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $47.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 8.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1997 to 2025; too much rain caused 48.8%.
