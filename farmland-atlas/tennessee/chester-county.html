Chester County, Tennessee

Chester County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $92 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,781 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 150.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Chester County runs $92 per acre, up 22.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $2,781 per acre, up 9.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 150 bushels per acre against a low of 85.2 in 2012. July nights average 70.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.9 F, and the county has logged 15 Julys at or above 70 F since 1900. Drought held severe or worse for 22 weeks over 2021-2025, and crop insurance indemnities since 1990 point mostly to excess moisture and flood, at 59 percent of losses. Solar and wind operating capacity: 0 MW.
