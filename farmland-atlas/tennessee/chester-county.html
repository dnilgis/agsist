Cash rent in Chester County runs $92 an acre for 2026. That is up 22.7% from $75 in 2016 and above the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $2,781 an acre in 2022, up 9.5% from $2,540 in 2017, and that ranks lowest of 93 in Tennessee. Corn trends at 173.5 bu, a hair over the state median of 172.5 bu.
