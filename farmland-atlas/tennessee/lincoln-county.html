$155

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 96% from $79 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Lincoln County runs $155 an acre for 2026, up 96.2% from $79 in 2016. That puts it 8th highest of 88 in Tennessee, well over the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $5,222 an acre in 2022, up 42.6% from $3,663 in 2017, and sits 46th highest of 93. Corn trends at 173.0 bu for 2026, close to the state median of 172.5 bu.
