Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 26.7% from $45 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Greene County runs $57 an acre in 2026, up 26.7% from $45 in 2016 and under the state median of $65, which puts it 34th lowest of 88. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,423 an acre in 2022, up 43.0% from $4,493 in 2017 and 20th highest of 93 in Tennessee. Corn trends at 161.8 bu, 10th lowest of 42, and 28% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
