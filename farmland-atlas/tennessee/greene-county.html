Greene County, Tennessee

Greene County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $57 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,423 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47059. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greene County averages $57 per acre for 2026, up 26.7 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census stands at $6,423 per acre, up 43 percent from 2017, or 7.4 percent a year. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, so corn yields track rainfall; the yield trend runs 3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 146.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 92 in 2008. Drought weeks at severe or worse ran 112 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst in 2008 at 41 weeks, and fell to 0 in 2021-2025. Heat and drought caused 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.48.
