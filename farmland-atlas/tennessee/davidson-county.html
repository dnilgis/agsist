Davidson County's census value of land and buildings stood at $12,414 an acre in 2022, the highest of 93 counties in Tennessee and well over the state median of $5,131. That value rose 38.3% from $8,978 in 2017, 6.7% a year. Cash rent for cropland was $77 an acre in 2023, up 27.5% from $60 in 2013. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
