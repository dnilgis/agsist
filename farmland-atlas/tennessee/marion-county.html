Marion County cropland rents for $59 an acre in 2026, down 21.3% from $75 in 2016 and under the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 61.3% to $5,249 an acre in 2022 from $3,254 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 193.1 bu that sits 4th highest of 42 in the state.

Sources and terms

Every figure is a county average from the sources below, not an appraisal of any farm. "Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed. Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. What each column means: county data and how to cite.

This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields. Loss ratio and claims: USDA RMA Summary of Business. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor. July lows: NOAA nClimDiv county monthly minimum temperature.
