McMinn County, Tennessee

McMinn County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,610 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 158.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47107. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in McMinn County runs $70 per acre, up 60.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value sits at $5,610 per acre, up 20.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 69.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.3 F, part of a 0.63 F per decade warming trend since 1976. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, so most fields depend on rainfall.
