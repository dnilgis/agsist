Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.69 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 214 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,491 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,610 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $70 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.77 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 60.9% from 2016 to 2026. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 200.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 165 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.48 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.78, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. 0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 75% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 18.5%.

McMinn County is a dryland county with most of its ground rained on, and the notable part is how much of the insurance money has gone to dry weather. Heat and drought was 70 percent of indemnity dollars in 1989-1999 and 84 percent in 2020-2025, and the loss ratio over the full run of closed crop years is 1.02, so claims have paid out more than premiums came in. Corn yields have a slight upward trend, with a median of 158.1 bushels per acre, and the worst year, 2010, was 97.7. Non-irrigated cash rent is $70 per acre in 2026, and census land value was $5,610 per acre in 2022.
