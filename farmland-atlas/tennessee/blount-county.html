Stands out against its own record

$56

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 55.6% from $36 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Blount County runs $56 an acre for 2026, up 55.6% from $36 in 2016, and still under the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings tells another story at $8,030 an acre in 2022, up 24.8% from 2017 and 7th highest of 93 in Tennessee. Corn is the soft spot, with a trend yield of 148.2 bu that sits 7th lowest of 42.
