Non-irrigated cash rent in Dickson County is $61 per acre in 2026, down 3.2 percent from 2016. Land value was $5,919 per acre in 2022, up 40.9 percent from 2017, 7.1 percent a year. July nightly lows average 69.1 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.64 F per decade, warming. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and severe drought or worse held at least 50 percent of the county for 58 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025.
