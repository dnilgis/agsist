Cropland rent in Dickson County was $61 an acre in 2026, down 3.2% from $63 in 2016 and under the state median of $65. That puts it 38th lowest of 88. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, climbing 40.9% from $4,201 an acre in 2017 to $5,919 in 2022, 29th highest of 93. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
