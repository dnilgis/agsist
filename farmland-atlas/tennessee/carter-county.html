Carter County, Tennessee

Carter County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $35 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,540 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carter County averages $35 per acre in 2026, up 40 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $6,540 per acre, with a change of 25.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.7 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 61.1 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 62.1 F for 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.39 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks ran 124 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst in 2008 with 41 weeks, and 0 weeks from 2021-2025. No cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
