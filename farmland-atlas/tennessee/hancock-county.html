Cropland rent in Hancock County runs $23 an acre for 2026, the 2nd lowest of 88 in Tennessee and well under the $65 state median. That rent is down 4.2% from $24 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 19.8% from $2,662 in 2017 to $3,190 in 2022, which still sits 10th lowest of 93 against a $5,131 state median.
