Non-irrigated cash rent in Claiborne County averages $29 per acre in 2026, down 9.4 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $4,395 per acre, with a change of 40.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7 percent a year. July nights average 65.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.3 F, with a warming trend of 0.36 F per decade and no clear direction. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
