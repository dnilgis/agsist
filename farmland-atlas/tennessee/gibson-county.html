Cash rent in Gibson County runs $153 an acre for 2026, up 20.5% from $127 in 2016 and 9th highest of 88 in Tennessee, where the median is $65. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,365 an acre in 2022, up 23.3% from 2017, yet it sits 30th lowest of 93 against a state median of $5,131. Corn made 156.8 bu in 2025, a bit under the 2021-25 average of 161.7 bu, and 61% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
