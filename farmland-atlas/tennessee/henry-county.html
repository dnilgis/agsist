Henry County, Tennessee

Henry County, TN: non-irrigated cash rent $141 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,384 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 169.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 47079. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henry County runs $141 per acre in 2026, up 25.9 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $4,384 per acre, having moved 31.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.7 percent a year. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 51 percent of $69.4 million paid, and its share of losses by period was 48 percent in 1989-1999, 33 percent in 2000-2009, 56 percent in 2010-2019, and 61 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal low is 68.9 F, against 70.7 F for 2017-2026. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland here is irrigated, and irrigation failure claims total just $36,118 across all closed years.
