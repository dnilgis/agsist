As of Oct 14, cash rent in Henry County runs $141 an acre for 2026, up 25.9% from $112 in 2016 and well over the $65 state median, 14th highest of 88. The census value of land and buildings was $4,384 an acre in 2022, up 31.9% from $3,324 in 2017, which still sits 32nd lowest of 93 in Tennessee. Corn trends at 179.6 bu for 2026, 12th highest of 42.
