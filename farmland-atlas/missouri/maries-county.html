Maries County, Missouri

Maries County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $47 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,134 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 125.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29125. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Maries County averages $47 per acre in 2026, up 54.1 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended down 0.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 125 bushels per acre and a low of 45 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 60 percent of $3.5 million paid, and its share has moved from 24 percent in 2000-2009 to 45 percent in 2010-2019 to 78 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has averaged 68.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 66.7 F. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Solar interconnection queue shows 200 MW proposed; nothing is operating yet.
