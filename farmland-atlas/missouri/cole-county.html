Cash rent in Cole County runs $84 an acre for 2026, up 67.0% from $50 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $117 and ranks 43rd lowest of 109. The census value of land and buildings was $5,078 an acre in 2022, up 39.3% from $3,646 in 2017, and ranks 35th highest of 113. Corn made 171.8 bu in 2024, against a 2020-24 average of 146.2 bu.
