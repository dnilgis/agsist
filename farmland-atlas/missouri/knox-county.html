Rain is at 102% of normal. Northeast region (10 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $10,314, average $8,167, poor $6,593 an acre (30 to 33 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.81 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.25 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 188 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk
