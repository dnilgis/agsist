Lawrence County, Missouri

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (10 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,357, average $7,248, poor $6,321 an acre (13 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.89 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.00 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 201 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,375 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $23.38 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.12, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 59% of the median yield. 2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 4.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 78.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.4%.
