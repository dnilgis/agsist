Lawrence County, Missouri

Lawrence County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $68 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,465 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 118.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29109. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lawrence County averages $68 per acre for 2026, up 72.2 percent from 2014. Cropland here is dryland: irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 98 percent of what irrigation exists. Corn yields have trended down 0.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 118.8 bushels per acre and a low of 70.2 in 2012. Heat and drought has driven a growing share of crop insurance indemnities: 40 percent in 1989-1999, 44 percent in 2000-2009, 79 percent in 2010-2019, and 84 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value stands at $4,465 per acre, up 39.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
