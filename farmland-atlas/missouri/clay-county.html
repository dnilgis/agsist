Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.07 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 115% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 201 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,340 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,629 an acre in the 2022 census.

MU's 2025 survey collected no data for the Kansas City urban region, so no figure here. Missouri Farmland Values Opinion Survey (G401), University of Missouri Extension, survey of March to May 2025, page reviewed Jul 2025. An opinion survey of landowners, lenders, farm managers and others; a separate source from USDA's. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $175 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.73 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 25% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 25.9% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 205.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,029 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.78 per base acre, PLC $3.23.

Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $63.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.71 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.56, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 54% of the median yield. 4.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3 days a year and damaging wind on 4.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 46.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 46.3%.

Quick answers for Clay County

What is cash rent in Clay County, Missouri?

The county average is $175 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Clay County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $6,629 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Clay County, Missouri?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 208.4 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 10 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
