Clay County, Missouri

Clay County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $175 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,629 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 161.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29047. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent for Clay County runs $175 per acre in 2026, up 25.9 percent from 2016. Land value reached $6,629 per acre in 2022, up 59 percent from 2017. July nightly lows sit at 67.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 67.9 F normal, with no clear direction since 1976.
