USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cropland rent in Daviess County runs $175 an acre for 2026, up 15.1% from $152 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 109 in Missouri, where the state median is $117. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,517 an acre in 2022, up 38.3% from $3,265 in 2017 and close to the state median of $4,459. Corn trends at 154.2 bu, which sits 15th lowest of 64 in the state, though 2024 came in at 182.4 bu.
