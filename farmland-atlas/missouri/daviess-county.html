Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.66 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.47, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 37%.
