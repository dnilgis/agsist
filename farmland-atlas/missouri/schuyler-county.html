$97

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 15 surveys since 2009; down 14.9% from $114 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Schuyler County cropland rents at $97 an acre in 2026, down 14.9% from $114 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $117 and ranks 47th lowest of 109. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 60.9% to $4,152 an acre in 2022 from $2,581 in 2017. Renters work 35% of the farmland here.
