Schuyler County, Missouri

Schuyler County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $97 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,152 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29197. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Schuyler County averages $97 per acre for 2026, down 14.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $4,152 per acre in 2022, up 60.9 percent from 2017. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $36.0 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 52 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 16 percent in 1989-1999, 13 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, to 63 percent in 2020-2025. Wind operations in the county total 400 MW, with 0 MW solar and no projects of either kind proposed to EIA.
