Cropland rent in Platte County runs $168 an acre for 2026, up 12.0% from $150 in 2016 and 23rd highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $5,713 an acre in 2022, up 29.0% from $4,429 in 2017, against a state median of $4,459. Corn trends at 199.1 bu for 2026, 12th highest of 64, and 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
