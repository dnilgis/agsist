Cash rent in Worth County runs $188 an acre for 2026. That ranks 8th highest of 109 in Missouri and sits well over the state median of $117. Rent is up only 3.3% from $182 in 2016, so it has not moved much. The census value of land and buildings was $3,888 an acre in 2022, up 36.7% from $2,844 in 2017 but still under the state median of $4,459.
