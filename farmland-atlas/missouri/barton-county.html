Non-irrigated cash rent in Barton County averages $83 per acre for 2026, up 12.2 percent from 2016. Land value under the census reached $4,126 per acre in 2022, up 49.9 percent from 2017, and non-irrigated rent that year worked out to 1.88 percent of that value. Heat and drought has driven a growing share of crop insurance indemnities, rising from 26 percent in 1989-1999 and 23 percent in 2000-2009 to 54 percent in 2010-2019 and 61 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 59 out of the 2021-2025 span, against 141 of 1357 weeks for 2000-2025 overall. Wind operating capacity stands at 149.4 MW; solar operating capacity is 0 MW.
