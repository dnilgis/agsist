Cash rent in Barton County runs $83 an acre for 2026, up 12.2% from $74 in 2016 and 42nd lowest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $4,126 an acre in 2022, up 49.9% from $2,753 in 2017. Corn made 171.2 bu in 2025, well above the county's 17 year median of 148.7 bu.
