Laclede County, Missouri

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Central region (20 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,204, average $6,404, poor $4,911 an acre (48 to 55 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.11 per base acre for corn.

Laclede County vs Missouri: $38.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 14% of the other 108 Missouri counties with a 2026 rent (16th lowest of 109).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,316 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $20.76 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.78, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. 2.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1992 to 2025; too much rain caused 31.1%.
