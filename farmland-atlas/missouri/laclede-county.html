Cash rent in Laclede County runs $39 an acre for 2026, up 13.2% from $34 in 2016 and 16th lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $3,518 an acre in 2022, up 39.8% from $2,517 in 2017, and 24th lowest of 113. Only 18% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
