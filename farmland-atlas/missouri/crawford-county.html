As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Crawford County runs $25 an acre for 2026, the 6th lowest of 109 against a state median of $117. That rent is up 44.1% from $17 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,882 an acre in 2022, up 11.6% from $2,583 in 2017, or 2.2% a year. Pasture rents for $18 an acre in 2026, and 22% of farmland was rented in 2022.
