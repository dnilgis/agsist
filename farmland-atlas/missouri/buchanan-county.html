USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cash rent in Buchanan County runs $164 an acre for 2026, up 13.9% from $144 in 2016 and 26th highest of 109 counties in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $5,382 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $4,053 in 2017, or 5.8% a year. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 200.9 bu that sits 9th highest of 64 against a state median of 181.1 bu.
