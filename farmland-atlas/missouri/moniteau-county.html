USDA NASS county yields, 2024

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Moniteau County runs $85 an acre for 2026, up 5.0% from $81 in 2016 and under the state median of $117. That ranks 44th lowest of 109. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,680 an acre in 2022, up 31.1% from $3,570 in 2017, and sits 49th highest of 113. Corn trends at 156.3 bu, below the state median of 181.1 bu.
