Moniteau County, Missouri

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Central region (20 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,204, average $6,404, poor $4,911 an acre (48 to 55 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.91 per base acre for corn.

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

1.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Quick answers for Moniteau County

What is cash rent in Moniteau County, Missouri?

The county average is $85 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Moniteau County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $4,680 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Moniteau County, Missouri?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 156.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 10 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
