St. Francois County's story is dryland ground that has paid its way for most of the years on record. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so rain decides the crop. Heat and drought made up 64 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2010-2019, and 52 percent in 2020-2025, so it is still the main claim cause. The loss ratio over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 was 0.94, which means indemnities came in just under premiums. Non-irrigated cash rent is $63 per acre for 2026, and the census value of land and buildings was $5,052 per acre in 2022.
