Scott County, Missouri

Scott County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $176 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,405 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 174.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29201. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Scott County averages $176 per acre in 2026, up 0.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $260 per acre, a multiple of 1.48 over non-irrigated, compared with 1.27 in 2009; the direction of that multiple shows no clear direction. Irrigation covers 59 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 60 percent of $61.5 million paid out from 1989-2025, with the loss ratio rising to 0.79 over 2016-2025 from 0.63 over the full period. Census land value reached $6,405 per acre in 2022, up 25.9 percent from 2017.
