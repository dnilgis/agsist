Scott County is mostly dryland with a sizable irrigated share, and the water comes from groundwater. Heat and drought claims have grown: they were 32 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 and 38 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 was 0.79, so insurance has paid out more than the premiums taken in over that stretch, and in 3 of 23 closed crop years it paid more than premium. Non-irrigated cash rent is $176 per acre in 2026, and irrigated rent is $260 per acre. Census land and buildings value was $6,405 per acre in 2022, and CRP covers 3.7 percent of the cropland base.

Quick answers for Scott County

What is cash rent in Scott County, Missouri?

The county average is $176 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $260 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Scott County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $6,405 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Scott County, Missouri?

The county's 2020-24 average is 183.5 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
