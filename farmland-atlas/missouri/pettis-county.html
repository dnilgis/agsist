Pettis County, Missouri

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,906 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.86 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 47.77 inches, 119% of normal (40.1 inches) through Nov 17, at Sedalia Water Plant. Frost-free season: 202 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,346 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,906 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $149 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 60.2% from 2016 to 2026. 30.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 137.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

County median corn basis: 25 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.61 at Smithton, MO Feed Mill, 10 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.86 per base acre, PLC $3.06.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (121.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $47.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 433 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.20 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.49 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 32% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.8%.
