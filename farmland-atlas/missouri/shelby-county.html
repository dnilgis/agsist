Shelby County, Missouri

Shelby County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $177 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,127 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29205. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Shelby County averages $177 per acre in 2026, up 14.2 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $6,127 per acre in 2022, with land value change of 64.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 10.4 percent a year. Cash rent as a share of census value runs at 2.53 percent. Heat and drought account for 51 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid out from 1989-2025, and that share has moved unevenly across periods: 43 percent in 1989-1999, 22 percent in 2000-2009, 56 percent in 2010-2019, 56 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.31, below the 1989-2025 figure of 0.82. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland here, so this is a county where rainfall carries the crop.
