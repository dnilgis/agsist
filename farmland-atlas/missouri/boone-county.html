Boone County, Missouri

Boone County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $143 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,608 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Boone County is $143 per acre, up 1.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 48 percent of $44.7 million, and its share by period runs 41 percent, 36 percent, 48 percent, then 55 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.51, below the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.74. Only 4 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and that water is 100 percent groundwater. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 23 over 2021-2025, against 94 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000.
