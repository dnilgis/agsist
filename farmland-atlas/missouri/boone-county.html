Boone County, Missouri

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Central region (20 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,204, average $6,404, poor $4,911 an acre (48 to 55 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.41 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

50.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.64 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 117% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 206 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,315 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.18 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.51, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 30% of the median yield. 4.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 46.1%.
