Putnam County, Missouri

Putnam County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $118 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,789 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 128.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29171. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Putnam County runs $118 per acre in 2026, up 21.6 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census sits at $3,789 per acre, up 50.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance losses since 1989 lean toward excess moisture and flood, 55 percent of $53.7 million paid, but the heat and drought share has moved higher across recent decades: 9 percent in 1989-1999, 6 percent in 2000-2009, 49 percent in 2010-2019, 54 percent in 2020-2025. Median corn yield 2008-2024 is 128.7 bushels per acre, with 2012 the low year at 65.6. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
