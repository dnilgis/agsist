As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Putnam County runs $118 an acre for 2026, up 21.6% from $97 in 2016 and ranked 54th highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $3,789 an acre in 2022, up 50.4% from $2,520 in 2017, yet still 29th lowest of 113 against a state median of $4,459. Corn made 175.9 bu in 2024, well over the county median of 128.7 bu.
