Cropland rent in Hickory County runs $58 an acre for 2026, up 57.5% from $37 in 2016 and 30th lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,566 an acre in 2022, up 69.2% from $2,108 in 2017. Corn is thin ground here, with a 14-yr trend of 99.4 bu for 2026, the lowest of 64 counties against a state median of 181.1 bu.
