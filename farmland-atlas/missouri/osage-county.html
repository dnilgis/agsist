Cash rent in Osage County runs $69 an acre for 2026, down 25.1% from $92 in 2016, and that sits 37th lowest of 109 against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 43.7% to $3,470 an acre in 2022 from $2,415 in 2017. Corn came in at 176.2 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 135.8 bu.
