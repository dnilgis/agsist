Greene County, Missouri

Greene County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,004 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29077. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greene County stands at $50 per acre, up 35.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought drove 86 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1995-2025, and that share rose to 89 percent in 2020-2025 from 82 percent in 2010-2019. The corn yield trend on published years 2008-2021 ran down 0.5 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 135.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 66.7 in 2018. July nightly lows averaged 67.9 F over 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 66.5 F, and the county has warmed 0.42 F per decade since 1976. No cropland here is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Land value rose 47.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 8.1 percent a year.
