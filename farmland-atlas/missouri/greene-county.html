Greene County, Missouri

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (10 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,357, average $7,248, poor $6,321 an acre (13 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.26 per base acre for corn.

Greene County vs Missouri: $49.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 22% of the other 108 Missouri counties with a 2026 rent (25th lowest of 109).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.07 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. Quick answers for Greene County

What is cash rent in Greene County, Missouri?

The county average is $50 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Greene County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $7,004 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
