Henry County, Missouri

Henry County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $97 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,028 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 124.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29083. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Land value reached $4,028 per acre in 2022, up 49.7 percent from 2017, and rent equals 2.58 percent of that value. Solar development is proposed at 400 MW; none is operating yet.
