Henry County, Missouri

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,028 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.03 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.96 inches, 99% of normal (41.49 inches) through Nov 17, at Windsor, 4.2 mi SSE.

Land and buildings: $4,028 an acre in the 2022 census.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.03 per base acre, PLC $2.48.
