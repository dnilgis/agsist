Taney County, Missouri

Taney County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,385 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29213. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Taney County stands at $24 per acre, down 27.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $3,385 per acre, up 51.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.7 percent a year. Drought has touched the county in 72 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 18 weeks, and 22 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights average 67.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 66.8 F normal for 1991-2020, with no clear direction in the trend since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
