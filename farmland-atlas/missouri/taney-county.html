U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Taney County runs $24 an acre for 2026, 5th lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. That is down 27.3% from $33 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 51.5% from $2,235 in 2017 to $3,385 in 2022. Even so, that value sits 22nd lowest of 113 against a state median of $4,459.
