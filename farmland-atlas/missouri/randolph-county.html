Randolph County, Missouri

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Central region (12 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,226, average $6,376, poor $5,073 an acre (36 to 38 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.31 per base acre for corn.

Randolph County vs Missouri: $144 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 63% of the other 108 Missouri counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 40th highest of 109).
