Cash rent in Douglas County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 28.6% from $25 in 2016. That is tied for 9th lowest of 109 in Missouri, against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,936 an acre in 2022, up 39.8% from $2,100 in 2017, and that is 15th lowest of 113. Pasture rents for $30 an acre in 2026, and 23% of farmland was rented in 2022.
