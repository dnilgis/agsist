Atchison County, Missouri

Atchison County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $247 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,221 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 184.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29005. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Atchison County averages $247 per acre in 2026, up 28.6 percent from 2016. Census land value came to $6,221 per acre in 2022, up 26.9 percent from 2017, 4.9 percent a year. Non-irrigated rent in 2022 ran 3.26 percent of that census value. Flood and excess moisture drove 61 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid out from 1989-2025, more than heat and drought in any of the listed periods. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse fell from 106 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025 to 3 in 2021-2025. Wind operating capacity stands at 800.4 MW; no solar operates and no new solar or wind is proposed to EIA.
