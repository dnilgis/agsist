Atchison County, Missouri

Rain is at 137% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,221 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.67 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.27 inches, 137% of normal (29.31 inches) through Nov 17, at Rockport, 0.5 mi E. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,143 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,221 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $247 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 28.6% from 2016 to 2026. 52% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 21% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 19.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 103,893 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 10 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.24 at Mill 240 at Tarkio, MO Feed Mill, 13 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.67 per base acre, PLC $3.75.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (149.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.19 per base acre, PLC $7.53. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $63.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 92 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.61 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.30, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 58% of the median yield. 7.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 61.4%.
