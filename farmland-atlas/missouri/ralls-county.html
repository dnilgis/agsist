USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cropland rent in Ralls County runs $165 an acre in 2026, tied for 24th highest of 109 counties against a state median of $117. That rent is up 19.6% from $138 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,816 an acre in 2022, up 51.1% from $3,850 in 2017 and 18th highest of 113. Corn trends at 191.0 bu for 2026, and 54% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
