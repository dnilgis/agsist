Callaway County, Missouri

Callaway County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $131 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,653 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 145.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Callaway County is $131 per acre in 2026, up 14.9 percent from 2016. The July average nightly low was 68.4 F over 2017-2026, above the 67 F normal for 1991-2020. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 13 out of 2021-2025.
