Mississippi County, Missouri

Mississippi County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $183 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,746 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 180.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29133. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mississippi County runs $183 per acre in 2026, up 21.2 percent from 2016. Irrigation covers 46 percent of harvested cropland, all of it from groundwater. Land value reached $6,746 per acre in 2022, up 15.6 percent from 2017.
