USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Mississippi County runs $183 an acre for 2026, up 21.2% from $151 in 2016 and 12th highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $6,746 an acre in 2022, up 15.6% from $5,837 in 2017 and 5th highest of 113. Corn made 189.9 bu in 2025, a touch over the 184.5 bu average for 2021-25, and 74% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
