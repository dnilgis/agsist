Cropland rent in Bollinger County sits at $68 an acre for 2026, tied for 35th lowest of 109 in Missouri and well under the state median of $117. That is down 0.7% from $69 in 2016, so rent has hardly moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 44.0% from $2,507 in 2017 to $3,609 in 2022, which ranks 27th lowest of 113. 29% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
