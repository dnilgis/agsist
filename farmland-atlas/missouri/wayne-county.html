U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Wayne County runs $42 an acre for 2026, up 16.9% from $36 in 2016 and 17th lowest of 109 counties against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $2,495 an acre in 2022, up 6.2% from $2,350 in 2017 and 3rd lowest of 113 in Missouri. Corn made 89.9 bu in 2023, under the county median of 105.5 bu.
