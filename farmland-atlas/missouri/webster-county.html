Webster County, Missouri

Webster County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,559 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 123.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29225. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Webster County runs $37 per acre, up 5.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, so yield leans on rain and heat. Crop insurance indemnities for 1994-2025 total $6.7 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 81 percent, rising to 84 percent for 2020-2025. July nights average 66.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 65.5 F normal for 1991-2020. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
