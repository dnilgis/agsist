Dallas County, Missouri

Dallas County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,179 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 111.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29059. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dallas County averages $32 per acre in 2026, up 14.5 percent from 2016. Land value under the census sits at $4,179 per acre, with a 64.7 percent change from 2017 to 2022, 10.5 percent a year. Corn yield median for 2011-2023 is 111.5 bushels per acre, with 2011 as the worst year at 42.5. Crop insurance indemnities for closed crop years 1995-2025 total $2.2 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 75 percent of that total, and its share rose from 74 percent in 2010-2019 to 78 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 67.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 66.6 F normal for 1991-2020. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
