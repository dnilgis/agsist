Butler County, Missouri

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Southeast region (8 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $9,500, average $8,100, poor $6,300 an acre (6 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.60 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.42 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 74% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 222 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,580 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.60 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.06, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 63.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 79.7%.
