USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Jasper County runs $96 an acre for 2026, up 50.4% from $64 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $117 and ranks 45th lowest of 109. The census value of land and buildings was $4,178 an acre in 2022, up 29.7% from $3,221 in 2017. Corn trends at 143.8 bu for 2026, which ranks 11th lowest of 64 in the state.
