Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,515 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.88 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.92, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 27% of the median yield. 2.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 63.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 31%.
