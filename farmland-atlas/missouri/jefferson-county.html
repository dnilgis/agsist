Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

75.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.82 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.51, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 34% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 40.4%.

Quick answers for Jefferson County

What is cash rent in Jefferson County, Missouri?

The county average is $52 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Jefferson County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $5,915 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Jefferson County, Missouri?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 159.0 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 12 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
