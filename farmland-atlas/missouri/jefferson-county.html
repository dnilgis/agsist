Jefferson County, Missouri

Jefferson County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,915 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 130.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29099. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jefferson County runs $52 per acre, up 2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value stood at $5,915 per acre in 2022, up 40.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.1 percent a year. Corn yields rose 1.6 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 130.4 bushels per acre and a worst year in 2012 at 44.7. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, at 52 percent of $3.0 million paid, and its share has risen across each period listed: 4 percent in 1989-1999, 35 percent in 2000-2009, 53 percent in 2010-2019, and 57 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind development is not present in this county.
