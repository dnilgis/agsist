Non-irrigated cash rent in Oregon County runs $28 per acre, up 7.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land and buildings value stood at $2,560 per acre in 2022, up 43.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7.5 percent a year. Drought held at severe or worse for 94 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, with 28 weeks in the worst year, 2012, and 26 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights average 68.4 F over 2017-2026, above the 66.7 F normal from 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.62 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
