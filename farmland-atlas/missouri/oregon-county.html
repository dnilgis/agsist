Cropland rent in Oregon County runs $28 an acre for 2026, up 7.8% from $26 in 2016. That is 7th lowest of 109 in Missouri, against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,560 an acre in 2022, up 43.7% from $1,781 in 2017, and still 4th lowest of 113. Pasture rents for $33 an acre in 2026, a little above cropland.
