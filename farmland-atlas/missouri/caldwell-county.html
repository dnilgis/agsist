Caldwell County, Missouri

Rain is at 94% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,467 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.34 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 35.32 inches, 94% of normal (37.55 inches) through Nov 17, at Kingston, 0.1 mi WNW. Frost-free season: 195 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,206 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,467 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $158 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent fell 0.6% from 2016 to 2026. 31% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 13% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 108.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 25 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $3.98 at Cameron Coop, 15 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.34 per base acre, PLC $2.87.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (114.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $63.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,415 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.62 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.48, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 29% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 54.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 39.3%.

Cash rent in Caldwell County ran $158 an acre in 2026, tied for 29th highest of 109 in Missouri and above the state median of $117. That is down 0.6% from $159 in 2016, so rent has barely moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,467 an acre in 2022, up 40.9% from $3,170 in 2017 and close to the state median of $4,459. Corn trends at 184.1 bu for 2026, a bit over the state median of 181.1.
