Caldwell County, Missouri

Caldwell County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $158 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,467 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 143.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29025. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Caldwell County is $158 per acre in 2026, down 0.6 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings was $4,467 per acre in 2022, up 40.9 percent from 2017, 7.1 percent a year. Corn yield trend from 2008-2025 runs 3.6 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 143.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 41.1 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 55 percent of $59.8 million in indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share by period at 20 percent in 1989-1999, 49 percent in 2000-2009, 60 percent in 2010-2019, and 41 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 125 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 7 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
