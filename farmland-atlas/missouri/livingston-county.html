Livingston County cash rent runs $185 an acre for 2026, tied for 10th highest of 109 in Missouri and well over the state median of $117. That is up 9.5% from $169 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,690 an acre in 2022, up 39.1% from $3,372 in 2017. Corn trends at 205.8 bu for 2026, 7th highest of 64 in the state.
