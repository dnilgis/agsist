Pulaski County, Missouri

Pulaski County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $29 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,020 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29169. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pulaski County averages $29 per acre for 2026, up 34.9 percent from 2016. Land value under the census operators' own estimate stands at $3,020 per acre, up 35 percent from 2017 to 2022, a pace of 6.2 percent a year. Cropland here runs 0 percent irrigated, so yield outcomes track rainfall and heat directly. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 58 percent over 1994-2025, but the heat and drought share moved from 20 percent in 2010-2019 to 54 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.44 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 68.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 66.8 F.
