Cropland rent in Pulaski County runs $29 an acre for 2026, up 34.9% from $22 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 109 against a state median of $117. Pasture rents for $17 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,020 an acre in 2022, up 35.0% from $2,237 in 2017, and sits 16th lowest of 113. 25% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
