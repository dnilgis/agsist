USDA NASS county yields, 2023

Cash rent in DeKalb County runs $156 an acre for 2026, down 6.6% from $167 in 2016, and tied for 34th highest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 36.1% to $4,456 an acre in 2022 from $3,273 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a trend yield of 208.0 bu that sits 6th highest of 64 in the state.
