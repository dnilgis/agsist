DeKalb County, Missouri

DeKalb County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $156 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,456 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 127.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29063. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in DeKalb County runs $156 per acre for 2026, down 6.6 percent from 2016. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, stood at $4,456 per acre in 2022, up 36.1 percent from 2017, or 6.4 percent a year. Cropland here is not irrigated, so production rests on rainfall, and drought weeks over 2000-2025 came to 144 of 1357, with zero from 2021-2025. Heat and drought has driven crop insurance losses, rising in share from 1989-1999 through 2010-2019 before falling in 2020-2025. Corn yields trended up 4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, though 2012 dropped to 56.4 against a median of 127.
