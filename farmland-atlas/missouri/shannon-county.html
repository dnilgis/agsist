Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 29.69 inches, 66% of normal (45.19 inches) through Nov 18, at Winona, 3 mi SW. Cropland rent in Shannon County runs $22 an acre for 2025, and pasture rents for $12 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $2,396 an acre in 2022, up 22.1% from $1,963 in 2017, or 4.1% a year. That sits well under the state median of $4,459 and ranks 2nd lowest of 113. Only 14% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
