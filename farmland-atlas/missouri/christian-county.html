As of Oct 16, cash rent in Christian County runs $46 an acre for 2026, up 44.4% from $32 in 2016, and that sits 20th lowest of 109 against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $5,535 an acre in 2022, up 37.4% from $4,027 in 2017, which ranks 26th highest of 113. Pasture rents for $38 an acre in 2026, and 31% of farmland was rented in 2022.
