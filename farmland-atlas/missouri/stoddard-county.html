Stoddard County, Missouri

Stoddard County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $113 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,814 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29207. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Stoddard County averages $113 per acre in 2026, up 4.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents at $208 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 1.71 in 2008 to 1.84 in 2026 with no clear direction. Irrigation covers 55 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 99 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Census land value sits at $6,814 per acre in 2022, up 27.3 percent from 2017.
