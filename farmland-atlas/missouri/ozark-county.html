Ozark County, Missouri

Ozark County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,762 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29153. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Ozark County averages $32 per acre for 2026, down 39.4 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $2,762 per acre, with land value up 29.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.3 percent a year. None of the harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. July nights average 67.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 66.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.33 F per decade with no clear direction. The county held 19 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 61 weeks total from 2000-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
