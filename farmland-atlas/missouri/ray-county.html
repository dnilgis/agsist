Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 27.2% from $147 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Ray County cash rent runs $187 an acre in 2026, 9th highest of 109 in Missouri and well over the $117 state median. That is up 27.2% from $147 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,276 an acre in 2022, up 50.1% from $3,516 in 2017. Corn trend yield sits at 190.5 bu, 19th highest of 64 in the state.
