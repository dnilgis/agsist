Cass County, Missouri

Cass County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $130 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,459 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 140.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29037. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cass County is $130 per acre in 2026, up 44.4 percent from 2016. Land value change from 2017 to 2022 was 18.6 percent, 3.5 percent a year, and the census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $4,459 per acre. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 61 percent, and its share by period runs 56 percent in 1989-1999, 52 percent in 2000-2009, 58 percent in 2010-2019, and 68 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 91 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025, with 33 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
