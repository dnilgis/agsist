Howell County, Missouri

Howell County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $47 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,894 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29091. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Howell County averages $47 per acre in 2026, up 64.9 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $2,894 per acre in the 2022 Census, operator estimate, up 35 percent from 2017, a 6.2 percent annual pace. Heat and drought drove 96 percent of the county's $1.6 million in crop insurance indemnities across the 2015-2025 closed crop years, and that share rose from 87 percent in 2010-2019 to 97 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.98, close to break-even between premium and indemnity. July nights have warmed from a 66.1 F normal (1991-2020) to 68.1 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.57 F per decade trend since 1976.
