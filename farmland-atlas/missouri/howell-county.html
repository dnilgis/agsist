Cropland rent in Howell County ran $47 an acre in 2026, up 64.9% from $29 in 2016, and that still sits tied for 22nd lowest of 109 against a state median of $117. Pasture rented for $30 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $2,894 an acre in 2022, up 35.0% from $2,144 in 2017, and that is 12th lowest of 113 in Missouri. Only 21% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
