USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cropland rent in Montgomery County runs $119 an acre for 2026, up 22.7% from $97 in 2016 and just over the state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $5,802 an acre in 2022, up 53.9% from $3,771 in 2017, and that ranks 19th highest of 113 in Missouri. Corn trends at 182.2 bu for 2026, with 169.9 bu harvested in 2025, and 39% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
