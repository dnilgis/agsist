Audrain County, Missouri

Audrain County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $179 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,387 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Audrain County averages $179 per acre in 2026, up 35.6 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings sits at $6,387 per acre, with land value up 50.1 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought drive crop insurance losses here, at 62 percent of $200.0 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share has moved from 53 percent in 1989-1999, to 47 percent in 2000-2009, to 65 percent in 2010-2019, to 69 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 6 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
