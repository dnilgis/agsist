Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 35.6% from $132 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Audrain County runs $179 an acre for 2026, up 35.6% from $132 in 2016. That rent is 13th highest of 109 in Missouri, against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,387 an acre in 2022, up 50.1% from $4,256 in 2017, and that ranks 11th highest of 113. Corn made 183.0 bu in 2025, well above the 17 year median of 146.0 bu.
