Lafayette County, Missouri

Lafayette County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $226 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,328 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29107. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lafayette County is $226 per acre for 2026, up 59.2 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,328 per acre in 2022, and land value rose 61.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 10.1 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $80.6 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 51 percent; that share moved 21 percent, 33 percent, 59 percent, 51 percent across the listed periods. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 was 0.22, below the 1989-2025 figure of 0.43. Irrigation draws entirely on groundwater as of 2015, and irrigation failure claims across all closed years total $14,750.
