0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.22, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 46% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29%.
