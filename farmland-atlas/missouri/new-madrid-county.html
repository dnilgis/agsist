Cash rent in New Madrid County runs $161 an acre for 2026, up 30.9% from $123 in 2016 and 27th highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $6,704 an acre in 2022, up 19.9% from $5,591 in 2017 and 6th highest of 113 in the state. Corn made 196.8 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 183.9 bu. 74% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
