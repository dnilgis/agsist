As of Oct 16, cash rent in Grundy County runs $165 an acre for 2026, up 16.2% from $142 in 2016. That sits tied for 24th highest of 109 in Missouri, against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,305 an acre in 2022, up 47.0% from $2,928 in 2017, and ranks 51st lowest of 113. 44% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
