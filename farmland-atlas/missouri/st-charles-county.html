Cash rent in St. Charles County runs $157 an acre for 2026, up 22.7% from $128 in 2016 and tied for 31st highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,162 an acre in 2022, up 26.1% from $4,885 in 2017, and that ranks 15th highest of 113. The corn trend projects 199.7 bu for 2026, 11th highest of 64, against a state median of 181.1 bu.
