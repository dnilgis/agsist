Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. East Urban (St. Louis) region (2 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $15,625, average $15,875, poor $16,000 an acre (5 observations, poor above good, as MU printed it). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.93 per base acre for corn.

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

45% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.00 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.55, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.3 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025.
