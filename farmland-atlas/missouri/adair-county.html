USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Adair County runs $121 an acre for 2026, up 31.5% from $92 in 2016 and a bit over the state median of $117. That ranks 52nd highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,098 an acre in 2022, up 46.8% from $2,792 in 2017, and still under the state median of $4,459. Corn has a median yield of 134.0 bu over 13 years from 2008 to 2022.
