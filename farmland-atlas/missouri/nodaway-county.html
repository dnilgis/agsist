Nodaway County, Missouri

Nodaway County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $192 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,499 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 129.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29147. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Nodaway County runs $192 per acre, up 8.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings were valued by operators at $5,499 per acre in 2022, after a change of 37.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.5 percent a year. Corn yield trend for 2008-2018 was 3.7 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 129.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 88.1 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 at 46 percent, and its share moved 10 percent in 1989-1999, 39 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 38 percent in 2020-2025. Wind operates at 528.9 MW; solar operating and proposed capacity are both 0 MW.
