Pike County, Missouri

Pike County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $148 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,561 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29163. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pike County runs $148 per acre in 2026, up 23.3 percent from 2016. Land value under the census sits at $4,561 per acre, up 29.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.3 percent a year. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 50 percent, and its share has moved up across each of the four periods listed: 42 percent, 45 percent, 50 percent, 60 percent. The county drew 58 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 26 of those weeks in 2012 alone. Irrigation depends entirely on groundwater, and irrigation failure has cost $192,920 in indemnities across all closed years.
