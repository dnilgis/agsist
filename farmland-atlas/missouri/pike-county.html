Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 23.3% from $120 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Pike County rents for $148 an acre in 2026, up 23.3% from $120 in 2016 and 39th highest of 109 in Missouri. The state median is $117. The census value of land and buildings was $4,561 an acre in 2022, up 29.7% from $3,516 in 2017. Corn runs a 14-yr trend of 183.9 bu, and 35% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
