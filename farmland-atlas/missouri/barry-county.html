Barry County, Missouri

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (10 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,357, average $7,248, poor $6,321 an acre (13 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.62 per base acre for corn.

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.80 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 202 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Quick answers for Barry County

What is cash rent in Barry County, Missouri?

The county average is $45 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Barry County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $4,384 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Barry County, Missouri?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 122.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 10 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
