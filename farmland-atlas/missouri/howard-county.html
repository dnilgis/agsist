Howard County, Missouri

Howard County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $142 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,853 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29089. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Howard County runs $142 per acre, up 17.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated ground rents at $243 per acre for 2025, and all of the county's irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Crop insurance history shows $37.1 million in indemnities from 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 60 percent, but heat and drought's share has climbed each period: 17 percent in 1989-1999, 26 percent in 2000-2009, 35 percent in 2010-2019, 38 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 stands at 0.69, matching the 1989-2025 figure of 0.69. Census land value reached $3,853 per acre in 2022, up 27.5 percent from 2017.
