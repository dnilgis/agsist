As of Oct 16, cash rent in Howard County runs $142 an acre for 2026. That is up 17.4% from $121 in 2016, and it is tied for 43rd highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $3,853 an acre in 2022, up 27.5% from 2017 but still 32nd lowest of 113 against a state median of $4,459. Corn made 149.1 bu in 2023, ahead of the 135.1 bu median for the years on record since 2008.
