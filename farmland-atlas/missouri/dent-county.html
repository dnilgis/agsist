Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. South Central region (17 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $6,444, average $5,100, poor $3,889 an acre (9 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.16 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Dent County

What is cash rent in Dent County, Missouri?

The county average is $20 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Dent County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $2,920 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
