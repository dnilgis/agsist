Bates County, Missouri

Bates County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $155 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,006 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bates County runs $155 per acre for 2026, up 69.4 percent from 2016. Corn yield has trended up 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 153.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 119 in 2010. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025 at 58 percent of $166.4 million paid, and its share rose from 44 percent in 1989-1999 to 63 percent in 2010-2019 before easing to 55 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, so this yield and rent record was built mostly on rainfall. Census land value stands at $4,006 per acre, up 29.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
