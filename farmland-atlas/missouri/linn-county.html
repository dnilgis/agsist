Non-irrigated cash rent in Linn County is $134 per acre in 2026, up 14.5 percent from 2016. Land value came in at $4,261 per acre in 2022, up 47.8 percent from 2017, 8.1 percent a year, and rent is 3.1 percent of land value. Cropland here is not irrigated: 0 percent of harvested cropland and 0 percent of irrigation water from groundwater, so drought exposure runs through the crop itself.
