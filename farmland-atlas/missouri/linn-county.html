As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Linn County runs $134 an acre for 2026, up 14.5% from $117 in 2016 and 48th highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,261 an acre in 2022, up 47.8% from $2,883 in 2017, which sits 50th lowest of 113 against a state median of $4,459. The 14-yr corn trend projects 192.4 bu for 2026, 15th highest of 64, though the 2019-23 average was 161.5 bu.
