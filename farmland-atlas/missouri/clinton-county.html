As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Clinton County runs $200 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. That is up 28.2% from $156 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,692 an acre in 2022, up 53.0% from $3,721 in 2017. The corn trend line projects 202.2 bu for 2026, 8th highest of 64, though the 2025 crop came in at 185.1 bu.
