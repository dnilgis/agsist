Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.34, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 35% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33%.
