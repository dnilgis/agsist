Scotland County, Missouri

Scotland County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $175 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,438 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29199. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Scotland County runs $175 per acre for 2026, up 31.6 percent from 2016. Land value sits at $5,438 per acre, up 61.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
