Washington County, Missouri

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. East region (11 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,492, average $7,458, poor $7,444 an acre (20 to 21 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.17 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 39.44 inches, 96% of normal (40.97 inches) through Nov 18, at Potosi, 4 mi SW. Frost-free season: 190 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,212 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 94.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 2018 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.6%.

Cash rent in Washington County runs $22 an acre for 2026, 4th lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. That rent is down 38.0% from $36 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 23.4% from $2,372 in 2017 to $2,926 in 2022, which is 14th lowest of 113. Pasture rents for $31 an acre in 2026, a bit above cropland.
