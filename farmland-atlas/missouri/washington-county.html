Washington County, Missouri

Washington County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,926 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29221. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County, MO stands at $22 per acre in 2026, down 38 percent from 2016. Crop insurance indemnities from 2018-2025 closed crop years total $122,877, with heat and drought the largest cause at 95 percent, and heat and drought account for 95 percent of indemnities in 2020-2025. The county saw 53 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, worst in 2012 with 12 weeks, and 15 such weeks in 2021-2025. Census land and buildings value reached $2,926 per acre in 2022, up 23.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.3 percent a year. No cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
