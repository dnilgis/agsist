Washington County, Missouri

Washington County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,926 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Cash rent in Washington County runs $22 an acre for 2026, 4th lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. That rent is down 38.0% from $36 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 23.4% from $2,372 in 2017 to $2,926 in 2022, which is 14th lowest of 113. Pasture rents for $31 an acre in 2026, a bit above cropland.
