Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

97.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 68% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 236 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,741 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.37 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.11, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 87% of the median yield. 64% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 67.3%.
