Non-irrigated cash rent in Lincoln County averages $149 per acre in 2026, up 43.3 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,019 per acre in 2022, with land value up 15.1 percent from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $68.7 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 56 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved differently across periods: 35 percent in 1989-1999, 27 percent in 2000-2009, 45 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025. There were 0 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, and the county corn yield trend for 2008-2025 runs 3.2 bushels per acre a year.
