USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Lincoln County cash rent runs $149 an acre in 2026, up 43.3% from $104 in 2016 and tied for 37th highest of 109 in Missouri, against a state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $5,019 an acre in 2022, up 15.1% from $4,361 in 2017. Corn hit 176.1 bu in 2025, and the trend line projects 187.7 bu for 2026.
