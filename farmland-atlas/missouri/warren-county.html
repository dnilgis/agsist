Cash rent in Warren County sits at $107 an acre for 2026, the same $107 it was in 2016, and under the state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings ran $5,779 an acre in 2022, up 42.8% from $4,048 in 2017, and that ranks 20th highest of 113. Corn came in at 162.4 bu in 2025, and 29% of farmland was rented in 2022.
