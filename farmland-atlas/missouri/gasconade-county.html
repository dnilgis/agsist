Gasconade County, Missouri

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. East region (11 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,492, average $7,458, poor $7,444 an acre (20 to 21 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.70 per base acre for corn.

Gasconade County vs Missouri: $38 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 13% of the other 108 Missouri counties with a 2026 rent (15th lowest of 109).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

59.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.15 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 193 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,280 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.99 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.79, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 50% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 60.6%.
