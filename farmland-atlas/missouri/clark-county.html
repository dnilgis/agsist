Clark County, Missouri

Clark County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $157 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,859 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 154.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29045. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clark County averages $157 per acre in 2026, up 11.3 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 3 percent irrigated, so yields track rain and heat rather than pumped water. Flood risk dominates the insurance record: excess moisture and flood account for 56 percent of the $105.4 million paid out over 1989-2025, closed crop years. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved from 22 percent in 1989-1999 to 24 percent in 2000-2009, 38 percent in 2010-2019, and 50 percent in 2020-2025.
