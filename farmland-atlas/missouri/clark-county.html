Cropland in Clark County rented for $157 an acre in 2026, tied for 31st highest of 109 in Missouri and above the state median of $117. That rent is up 11.3% from $141 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,859 an acre in 2022, up 35.9% from 2017. Corn made 205.5 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 154.0 bu.
