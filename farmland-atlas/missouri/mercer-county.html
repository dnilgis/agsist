Mercer County, Missouri

Mercer County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $139 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,903 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29129. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mercer County averages $139 per acre for 2026, up 23 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census was $3,903 per acre, up 44.9 percent from 2017, or 7.7 percent a year. No harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. Crop insurance indemnities since 1989 total $57.7 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 15 percent in 1989-1999 to 16 percent in 2000-2009 to 50 percent in 2010-2019 to 47 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 91 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 3 from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
