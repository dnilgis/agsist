Harrison County, Missouri

Rain is at 129% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,799 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.74 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 46.86 inches, 129% of normal (36.25 inches) through Nov 16, at Bethany, 1.3 mi NE. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,124 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,799 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $136 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 7.5% from 2016 to 2026. 28.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 7.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 67 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,017 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 35 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.07 at Coffey, 18 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 75 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.74 per base acre, PLC $2.91.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (115.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $63.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2,011 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.41 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.33 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 32% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 54.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 39.4%.
