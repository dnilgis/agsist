Harrison County, Missouri

Harrison County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $136 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,799 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29081. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Harrison County stands at $136 per acre for 2026, down 7.5 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,799 per acre in 2022, and that rent-to-value figure sat at 3.84 percent that year. Corn yields rose over 2008-2025 by a fitted 4.2 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 135.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 43.8 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved 22 percent, 30 percent, 64 percent, 54 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Drought weeks dropped to 5 in 2021-2025 from a county count of 103 of 1357 weeks since 2000. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
