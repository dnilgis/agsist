U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in McDonald County was $46 an acre in 2026, 21st lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. That is down 15.6% from $55 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 45.8% to $3,910 an acre in 2022 from $2,682 in 2017. Pasture rent ran $35 an acre in 2026.
