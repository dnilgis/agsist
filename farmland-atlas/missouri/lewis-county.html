USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Lewis County runs $176 an acre for 2026, up 30.4% from $135 in 2016 and tied for 15th highest of 109 in Missouri, where the state median is $117. The census value of land and buildings was $4,861 an acre in 2022, up 46.8% from $3,312 in 2017. Corn came in at 192.7 bu in 2025, and 46% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
