Iron County, Missouri

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. South Central region (17 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $6,444, average $5,100, poor $3,889 an acre (9 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.17 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.06 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,161 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.17 per base acre, PLC $1.88.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $65.72 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.58 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $37.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Cash rent in Iron County sits at $20 an acre for 2025, down 13.3% from $23 in 2013. Pasture rent runs $22 an acre for 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $2,697 an acre in 2022, up 30.6% from $2,065 in 2017, and that ranks 7th lowest of 113 against a state median of $4,459. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
