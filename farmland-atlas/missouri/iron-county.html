Iron County, Missouri

Non-irrigated cash rent in Iron County averages $20 per acre in 2025, down 13.3 percent from 2013. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 stands at $2,697 per acre, up 30.6 percent from 2017, a rate of 5.5 percent a year. No cropland here is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Drought at severe level or worse touched the county in 53 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 12 weeks, and 2021-2025 has already logged 12 weeks.
