Monroe County, Missouri

Monroe County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $171 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,186 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 138.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29137. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Monroe County is $171 per acre in 2026, up 23.9 percent from 2016. Census land value for 2022 stands at $5,186 per acre, with land value up 45.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7.8 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $156.3 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 66 percent. That share was 61 percent in 1989-1999, 48 percent in 2000-2009, 66 percent in 2010-2019, and 77 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.63, below the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.92. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
