Cropland rent in Benton County runs $75 an acre for 2026, up 9.6% from $68 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $117 and ranks tied for 40th lowest of 109. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,020 an acre in 2022, up 46.9% from $2,737 in 2017, or 8.0% a year. 29% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
