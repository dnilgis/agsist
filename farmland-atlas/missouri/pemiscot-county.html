Pemiscot County, Missouri

Pemiscot County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $157 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,687 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29155. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pemiscot County runs $157 per acre in 2026, up 41.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $185 per acre, a multiple of 1.18 over non-irrigated, down from 1.25 in 2009 with no clear direction since. Irrigation draws 100 percent from groundwater. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $106.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 66 percent; heat and drought's share has moved from 39 percent in 1989-1999 to 35 percent in 2000-2009, 21 percent in 2010-2019, and 15 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $6,687 per acre, up 27.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
