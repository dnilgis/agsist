Sullivan County, Missouri

Sullivan County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $123 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,213 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 122.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29211. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sullivan County runs $123 per acre, up 34.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $3,213 per acre, up 37.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.6 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 4.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 122.8 bushels per acre and a low of 45.9 in 2012. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 48 percent of $57.8 million paid since 1989, while heat and drought's share has climbed across the four listed decades, from 16 percent in 1989-1999 to 62 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed.
