Cash rent in Sullivan County runs $123 an acre for 2026, up 34.4% from $92 in 2016 and a bit over the state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $3,213 an acre in 2022, up 37.4% from 2017, yet still 18th lowest of 113 in Missouri. Corn trends at 178.1 bu for 2026, and the county rented 28% of its farmland in 2022.
