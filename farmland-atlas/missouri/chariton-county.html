As of Oct 16, cash rent in Chariton County runs $156 an acre for 2026, up 31.1% from $119 in 2016 and tied for 34th highest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $4,955 an acre in 2022, up 44.1% from $3,439 in 2017. The 14-yr corn trend projects 231.1 bu for 2026, the highest of 64 counties, against a state median of 181.1 bu.
