Ripley County, Missouri

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,855 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.92 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 215 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,499 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,855 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $74 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 92.2% from 2016 to 2026. 36.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 20.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 221.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.92 per base acre, PLC $3.11.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (124 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $37.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 58 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.90 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.68 over the same years. 42.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 7.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 84.7%.
