Cropland rent in Carroll County runs $192 an acre for 2026, tied for 6th highest of 109 in Missouri and well above the state median of $117. That rent is up 18.5% from $162 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,366 an acre in 2022, up 49.9% from $3,580 in 2017. Corn holds up its end too, with a trend yield of 200.7 bu that ranks 10th highest of 64.
