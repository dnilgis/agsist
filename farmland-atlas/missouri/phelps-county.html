Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Cash rent in Phelps County runs $17 an acre for 2025, down 55.8% from $39 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 48.4% to $3,912 an acre in 2022 from $2,636 in 2017. That puts the county 37th lowest of 113 in Missouri, under the state median of $4,459. In 2022, 22% of the farmland here was rented.

Quick answers for Phelps County

What is cash rent in Phelps County, Missouri?

The county average is $17 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Phelps County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $3,912 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
