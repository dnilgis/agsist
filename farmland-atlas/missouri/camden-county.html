Camden County, Missouri

Camden County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $16 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,851 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29029. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Camden County averages $16 per acre for 2026, down 25.6 percent from 2016. Census land and building values reached $2,851 per acre in 2022, up 29.1 percent from 2017, or 5.2 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2025 total $121,674, with heat and drought the largest cause at 60 percent. The county spent 40 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 80 such weeks across 2000-2025, and 2023 alone carried 25 weeks. July nights average 68.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 67.4 F normal for 1991-2020, with no clear direction in the longer trend.
