Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 11 surveys since 2011; down 25.6% from $22 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Camden County runs $16 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 109 in Missouri against a state median of $117. That rent is down 25.6% from $22 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,851 an acre in 2022, up 29.1% from $2,208 in 2017, and still 9th lowest of 113. Only 16% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
