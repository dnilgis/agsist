Marion County, Missouri

Marion County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $185 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,203 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 164.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29127. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
