Cropland rent in Gentry County runs $160 an acre for 2026, which is 28th highest of 109 in Missouri and down 14.4% from $187 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 43.6% from $3,098 in 2017 to $4,448 in 2022. That 2022 figure sits just under the state median of $4,459. Corn made 160.0 bu in 2023, right about at the recent average of 159.8 bu.
