Cropland rent in Jackson County runs $158 an acre for 2026, tied for 29th highest of 109 in Missouri and above the state median of $117. That rent is up 58.0% from $100 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $6,658 an acre in 2022, 8th highest of 113, up 14.5% from $5,814 in 2017. Corn trends at 184.8 bu, a little over the state median of 181.1 bu.
