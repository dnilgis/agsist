USDA NASS county yields, 2023

Holt County cash rent sits at $217 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 109 in Missouri and well over the state median of $117. That rent is up 20.6% from $180 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,713 an acre in 2022, up 16.8% from $4,893 in 2017. Corn made 219.0 bu in 2023, against a county median of 174.0 bu.
