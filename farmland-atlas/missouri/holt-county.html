Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.71 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Quick answers for Holt County

What is cash rent in Holt County, Missouri?

The county average is $217 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Holt County, Missouri?

Land and buildings were worth $5,713 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Holt County, Missouri?

The county's 2008-2023 median is 174.0 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
