Cropland rent in Ste. Genevieve County runs $104 an acre for 2026, up 26.8% from $82 in 2016 and below the state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $4,393 an acre in 2022, up 48.9% from $2,951 in 2017, which ranks 54th lowest of 113. The corn trend sits at 180.5 bu for 2026, close to the state median of 181.1 bu.
