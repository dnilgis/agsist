Non-irrigated cash rent in Saline County runs $196 per acre, up 30.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value sits at $6,325 per acre, up 57.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 9.5 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 4.1 bushels per acre a year against a median of 170.3, with 2012 the worst year at 84.3. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has climbed across the periods given: 24 percent in 1989-1999, 27 percent in 2000-2009, 53 percent in 2010-2019, 55 percent in 2020-2025, and it accounts for 49 percent of all indemnities paid from 1989-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and drought weeks ran higher in 2021-2025 than the 2000-2025 count suggests on its own: 42 of that later stretch against 114 total since 2000.
