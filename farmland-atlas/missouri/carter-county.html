Carter County, Missouri

Carter County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $19 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,342 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carter County runs $19 per acre in 2026, down 13.6 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, and July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 68 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 66.7 F, part of a warming trend of 0.44 F per decade since 1976. Drought has touched the county in 80 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 21 weeks, and 24 weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Census land value stands at $2,342 per acre in 2022, up 22.7 percent from 2017.
