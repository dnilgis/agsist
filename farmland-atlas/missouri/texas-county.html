Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. South Central region (17 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $6,444, average $5,100, poor $3,889 an acre (9 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.18 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 81% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 193 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,250 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 94.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 2017 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.4%.
