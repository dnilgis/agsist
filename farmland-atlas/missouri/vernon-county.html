Vernon County, Missouri

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. West region (8 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $7,671, average $6,823, poor $6,107 an acre (21 to 24 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.55 per base acre for corn.

Vernon County vs Missouri: $115 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 48% of the other 108 Missouri counties with a 2026 rent (53rd lowest of 109).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

84.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.08 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,500 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk
