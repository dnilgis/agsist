Franklin County, Missouri

Franklin County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,647 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County runs $70 per acre in 2026, down 5.4 percent from 2016. Land value carries at $5,647 per acre in the 2022 census, up 46.1 percent from 2017, a rate of 7.9 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 151.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 48.5 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood account for 53 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, while the heat and drought share of indemnities has moved from 4 percent in 1989-1999 to 32 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 62 percent in 2020-2025.
