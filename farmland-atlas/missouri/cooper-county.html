Cooper County, Missouri

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Central region (20 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $8,204, average $6,404, poor $4,911 an acre (48 to 55 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 49.55 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 130% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 205 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,348 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.07 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.60, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. 1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.4%.
