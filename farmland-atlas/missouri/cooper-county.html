Cooper County, Missouri

Cooper County, MO: non-irrigated cash rent $144 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,376 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 141.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 29053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cooper County runs $144 per acre in 2026, up 50.8 percent from 2016. Land value came in at $5,376 per acre in 2022, up 64.5 percent from 2017, a pace of 10.5 percent a year. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so yield rides on rain and heat. Corn yields have trended up 1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 141.1 bushels per acre and a low of 57.2 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has climbed across each decade listed: 54 percent in 1989-1999, 57 percent in 2000-2009, 69 percent in 2010-2019, 73 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind development, proposed or operating, is not present in this county.
