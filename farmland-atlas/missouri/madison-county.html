U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Madison County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 22.4% from $29 in 2016. That sits 13th lowest of 109 in Missouri, well under the state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,627 an acre in 2022, up 25.2% from $2,099 in 2017, and ranks 5th lowest of 113 against a state median of $4,459. Only 14% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
