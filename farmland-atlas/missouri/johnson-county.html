Stands out against its own record

192.4 bu

Corn yield, all practices, 2024: the highest of 16 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2024

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Johnson County sits at $105 an acre for 2026, up 51.1% from $70 in 2016 and 49th lowest of 109 in Missouri. The census value of land and buildings was $4,787 an acre in 2022, up 43.2% from $3,344 in 2017, and that ranks 44th highest of 113. Corn made 192.4 bu in 2024, well over the 2008 to 2024 county median of 136.1 bu.
