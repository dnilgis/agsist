Johnson County, Missouri

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. West region (8 counties), MU survey 2025: good cropland $7,671, average $6,823, poor $6,107 an acre (21 to 24 observations). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.92 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

11.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.37 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 110% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,366 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.73 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.57, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 42% of the median yield. 1.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.9%.
