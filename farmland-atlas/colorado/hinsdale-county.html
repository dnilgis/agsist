Hinsdale County had 4,903 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here rose 77.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 12.2 percent a year, and the Census puts land and buildings at $3,273 per acre. July nights average 43.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 43.1 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.41 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Irrigation here draws 1 percent of its water from groundwater in 2015, and drought has held severe or worse in 319 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 61 weeks from 2021-2025.
