Non-irrigated cash rent fell 63.5 percent from 2011 to 2021, dropping to $9 per acre. Irrigated ground rents for $210 per acre in 2025, reflecting the county's dependence on water: 77 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigation failure has paid out $10.5 million in crop insurance indemnities since 1989. Heat and drought claims have risen from 0 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 13 percent in 2020-2025.
