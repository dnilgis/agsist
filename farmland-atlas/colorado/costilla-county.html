Costilla County, Colorado

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Western region (35 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $160 to $300 an acre a year, median $285.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

14% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.33 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 125 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,727 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 26% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 10.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 30,482 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Costilla County

What is cash rent in Costilla County, Colorado?

The county average is $210 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.
