As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Denver County was $122,408 an acre in 2022. That is up 92.1% from $63,728 in 2017, which works out to 13.9% a year. In 2012 it stood at $54,228. The state median across Colorado's 58 counties is $2,765.

Denver County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
