Denver County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here sits at $122,408 per acre, the operators' own estimate, and it rose 92.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 13.9 percent a year. July nights average 59.9 F over 2017-2026, up from the 58.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.54 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched this county at times, with 160 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000 and 41 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Heat and drought drove 58 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 2000-2025, and that share moved from 51 percent in 2000-2009 to 47 percent in 2010-2019 to 77 percent in 2020-2025.
