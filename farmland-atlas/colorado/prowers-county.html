Cash rent on Prowers County cropland runs $34 an acre for 2026, a bit over the $30 state median and 9th highest of 24. Irrigated ground rents for $154 in 2026, close to the $155 state median. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,393 an acre in 2022, up 72.8% from $806 in 2017, yet still 9th lowest of 58 in the state. Corn made 187.8 bu in 2024, well above the 159.3 bu median since 2008.
