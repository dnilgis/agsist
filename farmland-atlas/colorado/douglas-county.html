Douglas County, Colorado

Douglas County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $14 an acre (2012); land and buildings $12,290 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Douglas County ran $14 per acre in 2012. Census value of land and buildings stood at $12,290 per acre in 2022, up 82.2 percent from 2017, or 12.7 percent a year. Irrigated cropland was a small share of the total, 13 percent, in the 2022 census. July nights have warmed, averaging 55.4 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 54.3 F, with a trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. Drought was present but not constant: the county spent 179 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 38 weeks, and 25 such weeks from 2021-2025.
