The census value of land and buildings in Douglas County was $12,290 an acre in 2022, 4th highest of 58 in Colorado. That is up 82.2% from $6,747 in 2017, or 12.7% a year, and well over the state median of $2,765. Pasture rented for $12 an acre in 2026. Only 17% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
