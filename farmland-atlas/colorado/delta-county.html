Delta County, Colorado

Land and buildings run $7,364 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.26 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 155 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,308 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,364 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $16 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 164 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,590 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.26 per base acre, PLC $3.39.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (135.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $10.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.33 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.88, 2016 to 2025. 92.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 1.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.3%.
