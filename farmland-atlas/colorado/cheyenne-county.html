Cash rent in Cheyenne County runs $32 an acre for 2026, the same $32 it was in 2016, and that sits 10th highest of 24 in Colorado against a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,281 an acre in 2022, up 52.9% from $838 in 2017, yet still 7th lowest of 58 in the state. Corn made 85.5 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 53.3 bu.
