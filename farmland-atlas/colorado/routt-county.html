Routt County, Colorado

Routt County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $17 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,101 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08107. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated rent reached $17 per acre in 2026, up 25.9 percent from 2016. Census land value was $4,101 per acre in 2022, a rise of 30.6 percent from 2017. The county draws 0 percent of irrigation water from groundwater, and heat and drought drove 77 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2010-2019, up from 65 percent in 2000-2009, against a July nightly low trend of 0.5 F per decade warming since 1976.
