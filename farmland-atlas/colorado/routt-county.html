Cash rent in Routt County runs $17 an acre for 2026, up 25.9% from $14 in 2016. That sits 4th lowest of 24 in Colorado, under the state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $4,101 an acre in 2022, up 30.6% from $3,141 in 2017, and ranks 17th highest of 58. 34% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
