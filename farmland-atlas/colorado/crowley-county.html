Crowley County, Colorado

Crowley County, CO: land and buildings $1,003 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.71 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Land values in Crowley County reached $1,003 per acre in 2022, up 97.1 percent from 2017, or 14.5 percent a year. The county sat in severe drought or worse during 476 of 1357 weeks across 2000-2025, or 35.1 percent of that span, with 2013 reaching 53 weeks. July nightly lows averaged 60.9 F during 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 60 F, trending warmer at 0.48 F per decade since 1976.
