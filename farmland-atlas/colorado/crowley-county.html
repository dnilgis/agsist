Crowley County, Colorado

Rain is at 104% of normal. Eastern region (29 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $50 to $300 an acre a year, median $137. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.70 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 12.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 165 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,867 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

The census value of land and buildings in Crowley County stood at $1,003 an acre in 2022, up 97.1% from $509 in 2017. That works out to 14.5% a year, and it started from $342 in 2012. Even so, the county ranks 2nd lowest of 58 in Colorado, well under the state median of $2,765. Just 11% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
