Mesa County carries 94 percent irrigated cropland, and every drop of that irrigation water comes from surface sources, not groundwater. Irrigated cash rent reached $113 per acre in 2026, with non-irrigated rent up 47.4 percent from 2009 to 2021.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
