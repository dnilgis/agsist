Mesa County cash rent on cropland stood at $42 an acre in 2021, up 47.4% from $29 in 2009. The census value of land and buildings was $8,081 an acre in 2022, up 46.3% from $5,523 in 2017, and 7th highest of 58 against a state median of $2,765. Irrigated rent ran $113 an acre in 2026, 8th lowest of 30 against a state median of $155.
