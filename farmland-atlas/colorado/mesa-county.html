Mesa County, Colorado

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Western region (35 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $160 to $300 an acre a year, median $285. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.50 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Mesa County

What is cash rent in Mesa County, Colorado?

The county average is $113 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Mesa County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $8,081 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
