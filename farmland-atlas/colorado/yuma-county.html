Yuma County, Colorado

Yuma County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $45 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,580 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 187.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08125. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Yuma County runs $290 per acre against $45 per acre non-irrigated, and that multiple has moved from 4.68 in 2009 to 6.44 in 2026, rising. Non-irrigated rent is up 15.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Groundwater supplies 100 percent of irrigation water, and 52 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so the water layer carries most of the weight here. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 6 percent in 1989-1999, 35 percent in 2000-2009, 31 percent in 2010-2019, and 32 percent in 2020-2025. Hail remains the largest single cause of indemnities at 53 percent over 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.87 against 0.71 for the full 1989-2025 record.
