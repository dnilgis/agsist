Chaffee County, Colorado

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,178 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 9.05 inches, 97% of normal (9.29 inches) through Nov 17, at Buena Vista, 2 mi S. Frost-free season: 112 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,364 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,178 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 26.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 100.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $9.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

87.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
