As of Oct 16, cash rent in Chaffee County ran $31 an acre in 2026, right by the state median of $30 and 12th highest of 24. Irrigated rent was $61 an acre in 2026, 3rd lowest of 30 and well under the $155 state median. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,178 an acre in 2022, up 21.7% from $5,075 in 2017, and 11th highest of 58.
