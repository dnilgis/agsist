Ouray County, Colorado

Irrigated cropland in Ouray County rented for $40 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $2,949 an acre in 2022, down 3.4% from $3,054 in 2017, or 0.7% a year. That sits above the state median of $2,765 and ranks 24th highest of 58 in Colorado. Just 17% of the county's farmland was rented in 2022.
