As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Moffat County was $20 an acre in 2024, up 5.4% from $19 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $1,560 an acre in 2022, up 95.2% from $799 in 2017. That sits under the state median of $2,765 and ranks 11th lowest of 58. Irrigated ground rented for $91 an acre in 2023.
