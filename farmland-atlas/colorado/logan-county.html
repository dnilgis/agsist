Logan County cash rent runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 19.7% from $31 in 2016 and 6th highest of 24 in Colorado. Irrigated ground rents for $234 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 30 in the state. The census value of land and buildings was $2,141 an acre in 2022, up 80.2% from $1,188 in 2017, and still 19th lowest of 58. Corn made 153.2 bu in 2025.
