Custer County, Colorado

Custer County, CO: irrigated cash rent $62 an acre (2020); land and buildings $3,662 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Cropland here is 86 percent irrigated, and none of that irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 is $3,662 per acre, with a land value change of 88.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 13.5 percent a year. Drought has touched this county often: 316 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least severe drought countywide, 23.3 percent, with 2003 the worst year at 52 weeks; 2021-2025 has logged 24 such weeks. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 47.2 F to 48.1 F in 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.44 F per decade since 1976.
