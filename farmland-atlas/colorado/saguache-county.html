Irrigated cropland in Saguache County rents for $189 an acre in 2026, 10th highest of 30 in Colorado, against a state median of $155. Pasture goes for $12 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,951 an acre in 2022, up 55.5% from $1,898 in 2017, or 9.2% a year, and 23rd highest of 58. 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
