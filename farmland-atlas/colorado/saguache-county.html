Saguache County, Colorado

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Western region (35 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $160 to $300 an acre a year, median $285. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.40 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

57.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.17 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 115 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,553 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.40 per base acre, PLC $0.45.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $3.98 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $10.10 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Quick answers for Saguache County

What is cash rent in Saguache County, Colorado?

The county average is $189 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Saguache County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $2,951 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
