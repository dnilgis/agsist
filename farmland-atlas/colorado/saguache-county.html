Saguache County, Colorado

Saguache County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2020); land and buildings $2,951 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent stands at $189 per acre, and 90 percent of harvested cropland here is irrigated, making water supply the central fact for any buyer. July nightly lows are warming at 0.41 F per decade since 1976, though no July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F.
