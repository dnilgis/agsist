Weld County, Colorado

Weld County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,798 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08123. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Weld County is $25 per acre in 2026, down 2 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent is $202 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 3.92 in 2009 to 8.08 in 2026, direction rising. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 0.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 175.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 129.5 in 2022. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved from 7 percent in 1989-1999, to 29 percent in 2000-2009, to 17 percent in 2010-2019, to 44 percent in 2020-2025. Census land and building value is $3,798 per acre, up 46.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
