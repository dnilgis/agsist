Weld County, Colorado

Cash rent in Weld County was $25 an acre in 2026, down 2.0% from $26 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 24 in Colorado. Irrigated ground ran $202 an acre in 2026, 7th highest of 30. The census value of land and buildings was $3,798 an acre in 2022, up 46.9% from $2,586 in 2017. Corn made 190.0 bu in 2025, against a 17 year median of 175.8 bu.
