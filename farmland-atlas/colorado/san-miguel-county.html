Cash rent in San Miguel County stood at $14 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 24 in Colorado against a state median of $30. Pasture rent ran $6 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings was $2,179 an acre in 2022, up 46.2% from $1,490 in 2017, or 7.9% a year. That still ranks 20th lowest of 58 against a state median of $2,765.
