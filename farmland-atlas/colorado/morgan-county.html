As of Oct 16, cash rent in Morgan County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 80.0% from $20 in 2016 and 7th highest of 24 in Colorado. Irrigated ground rents for $206 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 30. The census value of land and buildings was $2,717 an acre in 2022, up 56.6% from $1,735 in 2017, and still a bit under the state median of $2,765.
