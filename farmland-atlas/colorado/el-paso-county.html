As of Oct 16, cash rent in El Paso County was $7 an acre in 2024, down 50.4% from $14 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings ran $2,688 an acre in 2022, up 91.0% from $1,407 in 2017, or 13.8% a year. That puts the county 25th lowest of 58, just under the state median of $2,765. Renters worked 42% of the farmland in 2022.
