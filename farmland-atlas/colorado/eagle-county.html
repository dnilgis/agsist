Eagle County carries a land value of $6,632 per acre as of 2022, up 100.4 percent over 2017-2022, or 14.9 percent a year. Irrigation covers 99 percent of harvested cropland, drawing entirely from surface water, not groundwater. The county spent 256 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025, with 2021 reaching 51 weeks. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.41 F per decade warming, and the 2017-2026 average sits at 47.5 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 46.5 F.
