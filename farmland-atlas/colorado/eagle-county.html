Irrigated cropland in Eagle County rented for $74 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,632 an acre in 2022, up 100.4% from $3,309 in 2017, or 14.9% a year. That ranks 9th highest of 58 in Colorado, well over the state median of $2,765. 30% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
