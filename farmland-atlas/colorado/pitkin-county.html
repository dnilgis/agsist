Land value here climbed 110.8 percent from 2017-2022, or 16.1 percent a year, reaching $16,058 per acre by the 2022 census. All harvested cropland is irrigated, and none of that irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015. July nightly lows average 46.2 F for 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 45.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.34 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched the county in 271 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 43 weeks, and 65 weeks fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
