The census value of land and buildings in Pitkin County stands at $16,058 an acre for 2022, 3rd highest of 58 in Colorado. That is up 110.8% from $7,617 in 2017, 16.1% a year, and a long way from $5,597 in 2012. The state median is $2,765. Only 7% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
