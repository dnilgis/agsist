Cash rent in Kiowa County runs $29 an acre for 2026, up 7.4% from $27 in 2016 and tied for 11th lowest of 24 in Colorado. The census value of land and buildings was $1,238 an acre in 2022, up 64.2% from $754 in 2017, and that ranks 6th lowest of 58 in the state. Corn made 86.5 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 60.3 bu.
