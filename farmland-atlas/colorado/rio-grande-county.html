Quick answers for Rio Grande County

What is cash rent in Rio Grande County, Colorado?

The county average is $26 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2023 and $220 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Rio Grande County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $5,672 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
