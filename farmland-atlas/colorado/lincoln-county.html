Lincoln County, Colorado

Lincoln County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $900 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 46.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08073. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lincoln County averages $28 per acre in 2026, up 16.7 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so the corn yield record depends on rain and heat. The median corn yield 2008-2025 is 46.6 bushels per acre, with 2024 down at 25.8. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 64 percent of $163.3 million paid out over 1989-2025, and that share has moved from 11 percent in 1989-1999 to 65 percent in 2000-2009, 61 percent in 2010-2019, and 71 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 57.6 F to 58.6 F over 2017-2026. Wind operates at 852.6 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
