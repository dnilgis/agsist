Alamosa County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $21 an acre (2022); land and buildings $4,456 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08003. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigation drives this county. Ninety-one percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigation failure is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 58 percent, totaling $60.7 million of the $104.5 million paid out over 1989-2025, closed crop years. Non-irrigated cash rent sits at $21 per acre and fell 40 percent from 2011 to 2022, while irrigated cash rent in 2026 is $191 per acre. Groundwater supplies 62 percent of irrigation water as of 2015. Land value per the 2022 census stands at $4,456 per acre, up 110.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 16 percent a year.
