Alamosa County, Colorado

Rain is at 93% of normal. Western region (35 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $160 to $300 an acre a year, median $285.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 9.92 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 93% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 124 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,027 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Irrigated cropland in Alamosa County rents for $191 an acre in 2026, 9th highest of 30 in Colorado against a state median of $155. Dry cropland rent went the other way, down 40.0% from $35 in 2011 to $21 in 2022. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,456 an acre in 2022, up 110.2% from $2,120 in 2017, and it sits 15th highest of 58 in the state.
