Garfield County, Colorado

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Western region (35 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $160 to $300 an acre a year, median $285. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

19% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 19.21 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 99% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 141 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,887 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Cash rent in Garfield County stood at $31 an acre in 2020, up 14.8% from $27 in 2009. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,654 an acre in 2022, up 52.3% from $2,399 in 2017. That ranks 20th highest of 58 in Colorado, against a state median of $2,765. In 2022, 44% of the county's farmland was rented.
