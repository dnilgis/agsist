Garfield County, Colorado

Garfield County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2020); land and buildings $3,654 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08045. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Garfield County ran $68 per acre in 2025, and non-irrigated rent averaged $31 per acre in 2020, up 14.8 percent from 2009. Land value carried $3,654 per acre in 2022, up 52.3 percent from 2017, or 8.8 percent a year. Irrigation covers 89 percent of harvested cropland, and none of that water came from groundwater as of 2015. Drought has been a factor: the county spent 376 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2021 alone logging 51 weeks, and 85 weeks fell in 2021-2025. July nights have warmed 0.71 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low, 54.9 F, sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 53 F.
