U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

The census value of land and buildings in Lake County stood at $2,701 an acre in 2022. That is up 31.4% from $2,055 in 2017, or 5.6% a year, though still under the $2,916 operators put down in 2012. It ranks 26th lowest of 58 in Colorado, a little below the state median of $2,765. 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
