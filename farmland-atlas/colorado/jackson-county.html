Quick answers for Jackson County

What is cash rent in Jackson County, Colorado?

The county average is $15 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024 and $100 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Jackson County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $2,216 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
