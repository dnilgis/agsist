Jefferson County, Colorado

Land value in Jefferson County, CO stands at $16,602 per acre, and the change from 2017-2022 was 115.2 percent, 16.6 percent a year. Non-irrigated cash rent in 2021 averaged $35 per acre, while irrigated cash rent in 2014 was $120 per acre. Irrigation covers 25 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and none of that water came from groundwater in 2015. Drought has touched this county in 157 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 39 weeks, and 30 weeks fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.52 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 53.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 53 F.
