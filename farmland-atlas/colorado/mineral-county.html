Mineral County, Colorado

Mineral County, CO: land and buildings $4,334 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08079. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Cropland here is 93 percent irrigated, with 2 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. The July average nightly low sits at 43.5 F against a 43 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.26 F per decade with no clear direction. Drought has marked 345 of 1357 weeks at severe or worse from 2000-2025, worst year 2003 with 52 weeks, and 79 weeks from 2021-2025. Census land and building value stands at $4,334 per acre, up 26 percent from 2017-2022, or 4.7 percent a year. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
