Dolores County, Colorado

Rain is at 133% of normal. Western region (35 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $160 to $300 an acre a year, median $285. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.26 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 133% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 131 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,782 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

The census value of land and buildings in Dolores County stood at $3,270 an acre in 2022, up 83.4% from $1,783 in 2017. That works out to 12.9% a year, and the figure was $1,443 back in 2012. It sits above the Colorado county median of $2,765 and ranks 21st highest of 58. In 2022, 23% of the farmland was rented.
