As of Oct 16, irrigated cropland in Gunnison County rents for $54 an acre in 2026. That ranks 2nd lowest of 30 against a state median of $155. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,924 an acre in 2022, up 13.9% from $2,567 in 2017, or 2.6% a year, and it ranks 26th highest of 58. 31% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
