Gunnison County, Colorado

Gunnison County, CO: irrigated cash rent $54 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,924 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All harvested cropland in Gunnison County is irrigated, and none of that water comes from groundwater. Land value stood at $2,924 per acre in 2022, up 13.9 percent from 2017, or 2.6 percent a year. Drought has touched this county in 316 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, with 2003 the worst year at 51 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 54 weeks in severe drought or worse. July nightly lows have warmed 0.47 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 46.8 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 46 F.
