Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.46 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 114 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,546 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

The census value of land and buildings in Teller County was $2,307 an acre in 2022. That is up 14.0% from $2,023 in 2017, or 2.7% a year, after barely moving from $2,006 in 2012. It sits under the Colorado median of $2,765 and ranks 22nd lowest of 58. In 2022, 52% of the farmland was rented.
