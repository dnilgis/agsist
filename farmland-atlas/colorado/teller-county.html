Census value of land and buildings in Teller County stood at $2,307 per acre in 2022, up 14 percent from 2017, a gain of 2.7 percent a year. The July average nightly low ran 45.6 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 46.8 F for 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.61 F per decade since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above for its nightly low. Drought was present in 205 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 39 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 17 such weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates in the county, and none is proposed to EIA.
