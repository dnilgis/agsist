Otero County, Colorado

Rain is at 138% of normal. Eastern region (29 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $50 to $300 an acre a year, median $137. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.13 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 138% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 175 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,992 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.13 per base acre, PLC $3.87.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (154.09 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $26.22 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $34.05 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.21 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $11.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $26.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.29, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. 90.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.2 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.5%.
