Irrigated cropland here rents for $135 an acre in 2026, under the state median of $155 and 12th lowest of 30. The census value of land and buildings was $1,736 an acre in 2022, up 43.4% from $1,211 in 2017. That value sits 17th lowest of 58 in Colorado, well below the state median of $2,765. Just 8% of farmland was rented in 2022.
