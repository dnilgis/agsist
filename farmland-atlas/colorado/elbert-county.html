Elbert County, Colorado

Rain is at 95% of normal. Eastern region (29 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $50 to $300 an acre a year, median $137.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

9.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.69 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 95% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 145 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,391 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $23.39 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.92, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 51% of the median yield. 9.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 5.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 63.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.2%.
