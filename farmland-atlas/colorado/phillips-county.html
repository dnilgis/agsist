Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 44% from $33 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Phillips County ran $48 an acre in 2026, highest of 24 in Colorado against a state median of $30. That is up 43.9% from $33 in 2016. Irrigated ground rented for $257 an acre in 2026, 2nd highest of 30. The census value of land and buildings was $2,779 an acre in 2022, up 64.2% from $1,692 in 2017.
