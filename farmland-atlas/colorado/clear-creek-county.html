Clear Creek County, Colorado

Clear Creek County, CO: land and buildings $3,896 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Clear Creek County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in this county was $3,896 per acre in the 2022 Census, up 23.4 percent from 2017, 4.3 percent a year. July nights average 45.4 F for 2017-2026 against a 44.6 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.5 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county in 160 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 43 weeks, and 29 weeks fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
