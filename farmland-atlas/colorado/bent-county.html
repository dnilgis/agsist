Bent County, Colorado

Rain is at 163% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,087 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.57 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 21.29 inches, 163% of normal (13.07 inches) through Nov 17, at Las Animas. Frost-free season: 175 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,045 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,087 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 33.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,570 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.57 per base acre, PLC $3.04.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (121 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $11.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $34.41 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.22, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 81.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3.1%.
