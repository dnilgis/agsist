Bent County, Colorado

Bent County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,087 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 sits at $18 per acre against $178 per acre for irrigated land. Only 4 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015, so surface supply carries this county. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 483 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 55 weeks logged in 2021-2025 alone and the worst year, 2013, at 53 weeks. Census land value was $1,087 per acre in 2022, after a 57.5 percent change, 9.5 percent a year, from 2017-2022.
