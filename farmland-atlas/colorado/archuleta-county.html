Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 133 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,895 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,985 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20.50 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 41.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $10.10 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

96.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Archuleta County was $4,985 an acre in 2022, up 60.6% from $3,104 in 2017. That works out to 9.9% a year and ranks 14th highest of 58 counties in Colorado, against a state median of $2,765. Cash rent was $21 an acre in 2023, and pasture rent was $12 an acre in 2026. 28% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
