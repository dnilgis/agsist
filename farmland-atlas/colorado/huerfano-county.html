Land value in Huerfano County stood at $1,360 per acre in 2022, up 41.4 percent over 2017-2022, or 7.2 percent a year. Seventy percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and all irrigation water came from surface sources, with groundwater at 0 percent. The county logged 339 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025, 25 percent of that span, with 2003 reaching 52 weeks; 2021-2025 added 32 more weeks. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.51 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 53.8 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 52.8 F; no July since 1900 has averaged at or above 70 F.
