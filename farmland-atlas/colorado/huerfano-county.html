Huerfano County, Colorado

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Eastern region (29 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $50 to $300 an acre a year, median $137. ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.79 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

65.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.51 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 88% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,168 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

The census value of land and buildings in Huerfano County runs $1,360 an acre for 2022. That is up 41.4% from $962 in 2017, or 7.2% a year, and well above the $864 of 2012. Against the state median of $2,765, Huerfano sits 8th lowest of 58. The last irrigated rent on record is $75 an acre in 2013, and 33% of the farmland was rented in 2022.

What is farmland worth in Huerfano County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $1,360 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
