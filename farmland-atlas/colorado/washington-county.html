Washington County, Colorado

Washington County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,171 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 74.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08121. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County runs $40 per acre, up 41.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated rent is $214 per acre, 5.42 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026 against 4.57 in 2009, with no clear direction to that multiple. Only 6 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 88 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. Corn yields have trended down 1.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 74.3 and a low of 44.1 in 2022. Heat and drought drove 60 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and its share rose across 2000-2009 at 58 percent, 2010-2019 at 52 percent, and 2020-2025 at 69 percent. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating or proposed.
