Adams County, Colorado

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Eastern region (29 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $50 to $300 an acre a year, median $137.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

62.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 11.35 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 76% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 156 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,610 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.09, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 59% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 55.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.9%.
