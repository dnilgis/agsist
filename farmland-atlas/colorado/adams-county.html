Adams County cash rent was $20 an acre in 2026, down 7.1% from $21 in 2016. That sits 6th lowest of 24 in Colorado, under a state median of $30. The census value of land and buildings was $2,918 an acre in 2022, up 80.9% from $1,613 in 2017 and 27th highest of 58. Corn made 76.2 bu in 2025, well above the 53.5 bu it averaged from 2021 to 2025.
