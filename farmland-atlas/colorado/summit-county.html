Land value in Summit County stood at $8,928 per acre in 2022, up 111.3 percent from 2017, or 16.1 percent a year. Irrigation supplies 90 percent of harvested cropland, drawing entirely from surface water, not groundwater. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.44 F per decade warming, with the 2017-2026 average at 43 F against the 1991-2020 normal of 42.3 F; no July since 1900 has averaged at or above 70 F at night. Severe drought touched 202 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 43 weeks.
