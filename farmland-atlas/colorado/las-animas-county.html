Las Animas County, Colorado

Las Animas County, CO: non-irrigated cash rent $6 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,005 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Las Animas County is $6 per acre. Irrigated cash rent is $122 per acre. Irrigation covers 57 percent of harvested cropland, and none of that water comes from groundwater. The July nightly low has warmed 0.28 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 58.1 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 57.5 F. Drought has been present: 467 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 49 such weeks came in 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved 26 percent, 56 percent, 27 percent, 20 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025.
