As of Oct 16, cropland here rents for $6 an acre in 2026, the lowest of 24 Colorado counties against a state median of $30. Irrigated ground runs $122 an acre in 2026 and pasture $4. The census value of land and buildings was $1,005 an acre in 2022, up 66.9% from $602 in 2017, and still 3rd lowest of 58 in the state.
