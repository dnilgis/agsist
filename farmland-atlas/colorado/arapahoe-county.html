Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

36.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 13.35 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 153 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,537 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Quick answers for Arapahoe County

What is cash rent in Arapahoe County, Colorado?

The county average is $14 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Arapahoe County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $2,773 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
