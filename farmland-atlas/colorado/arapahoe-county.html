Cash rent in Arapahoe County runs $14 an acre for 2026, the 2nd lowest of 24 in Colorado against a state median of $30. That is down from $15 an acre in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,773 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $2,260 in 2017 and right about even with the state median of $2,765. Pasture rents for $8 an acre in 2026.
