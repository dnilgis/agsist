San Juan County, Colorado

San Juan County, CO: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08111. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

San Juan County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 42.7 F, close to the 1991-2020 normal of 42.4 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.33 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has posted an average low at or above 70 F. Drought was present in 315 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 23.2 percent, with 2018 the worst year at 52 weeks; 2021-2025 logged 49 weeks in severe drought or worse. There is no solar or wind operating or proposed here.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
