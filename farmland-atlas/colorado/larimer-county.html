Cash rent in Larimer County ran $29 an acre in 2026, down 1.7% from $30 in 2016 and tied for 11th lowest of 24 in Colorado. Irrigated ground is another story at $195 an acre in 2026, 8th highest of 30 against a state median of $155. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,276 an acre in 2022, up 35.3% from $4,637 in 2017 and 10th highest of 58.
