Larimer County, Colorado

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Eastern region (29 counties), CSU survey 2025: irrigated alfalfa land rents for $50 to $300 an acre a year, median $137. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.36 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 13.06 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 71% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 126 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,732 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.36 per base acre, PLC $3.44.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (137.08 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $50.56 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $30.29 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $13.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.68, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 70% of the median yield. 74.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.4%.
