Montrose County, Colorado

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Quick answers for Montrose County

What is cash rent in Montrose County, Colorado?

The county average is $138 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Montrose County, Colorado?

Land and buildings were worth $5,909 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
