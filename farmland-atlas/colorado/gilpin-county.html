Gilpin County, Colorado

Gilpin County, CO: land and buildings $8,979 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08047. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Gilpin County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here is $8,979 per acre by the 2022 census, the operators' own estimate. That figure moved 112.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 16.3 percent a year. Irrigated share of harvested cropland is 15 percent in the 2022 census. July nightly lows averaged 47.3 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 46.6 F, with a warming trend of 0.39 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks ran 137 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 38 weeks, and 25 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
