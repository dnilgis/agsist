Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the highest of 17 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Kit Carson County runs $40 an acre for 2026, the 4th highest of 24 in Colorado, and up just 2.6% from $39 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $1,406 an acre in 2022, up 15.3% from $1,219 in 2017, yet still the 10th lowest of 58 in the state. Corn made 136.8 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 95.4 bu.
