Park County, Colorado

Park County, CO: irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,330 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 08093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland in Park County stood at 82 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census. Land value here was $2,330 per acre in 2022, up 38.7 percent from 2017, 6.8 percent a year. Drought has touched this county over 2000-2025: 205 of 1357 weeks at severe or worse, with 2002 the worst year at 43 weeks; the 2021-2025 span held 26 such weeks. July nights average 44.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 44 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
