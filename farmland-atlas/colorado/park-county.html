Irrigated rent in Park County runs $33 an acre for 2026. That is the lowest of 30 counties in Colorado, against a state median of $155. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,330 an acre in 2022, up 38.7% from $1,680 in 2017, or 6.8% a year. That census value ranks 23rd lowest of 58, and 39% of farmland was rented in 2022.
