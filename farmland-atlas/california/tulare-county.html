Cash rent on Tulare County cropland runs $42 an acre for 2026, up 68.0% from $25 in 2016 and above the state median of $35. Irrigated ground rents for $450 an acre in 2026, tied for 17th highest of 41. The census value of land and buildings came to $15,617 an acre in 2022, up 33.2% from 2017 and 14th highest of 52 in the state. 26% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
