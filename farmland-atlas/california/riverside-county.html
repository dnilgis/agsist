Riverside County, California

Riverside County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2024); land and buildings $19,831 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06065. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Riverside County runs $259 per acre for 2026, and irrigation covers 90 percent of harvested cropland, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. July nights average 75.4 F for 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 72.8 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.97 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought account for 54 percent of crop insurance indemnities across closed years 1989-2025, with that share at 30 percent in 1989-1999, 64 percent in 2000-2009, 53 percent in 2010-2019, and 52 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 132 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 448 such weeks over 2000-2025.
