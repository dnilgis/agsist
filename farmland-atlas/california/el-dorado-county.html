El Dorado County, California

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $11,768 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

3.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.18 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 207 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,601 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $11,768 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $33 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 45% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 15% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 80.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,964 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $17.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.78 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 2.45, 2016 to 2025. 88.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.6%.

El Dorado County rent on cropland runs $33 an acre for 2026, tied for 9th lowest of 20 in California against a state median of $35. Irrigated ground is another story at $900 an acre for 2026, 10th highest of 41. The census value of land and buildings was $11,768 an acre in 2022, up 18.6% from $9,926 in 2017, or 3.5% a year. 45% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
