Contra Costa County, California

Rain is at 107% of normal. Land and buildings run $9,199 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.79 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 107% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,849 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,199 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.38 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 15, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 26% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 35.6% from 2016 to 2026. 59% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 141.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 5.7 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,309 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.79 per base acre, PLC $2.94.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (117.01 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $25.86 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $22.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.36 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. 87.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36.8%.
