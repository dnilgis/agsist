$31

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 12 surveys since 2009; up 35.6% from $23 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Contra Costa County cropland rented for $31 an acre in 2026, up 35.6% from $23 in 2016 and 7th lowest of 20 against a state median of $35. Irrigated ground ran $379 an acre in 2026, 15th lowest of 41. The census value of land and buildings was $9,199 an acre in 2022, up 32.9% from $6,922 in 2017, under the state median of $11,471. In 2022, 59% of the farmland here was rented.
