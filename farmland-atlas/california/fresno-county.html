Fresno County, California

Rain is at 89% of normal. Land and buildings run $16,378 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.43 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.02 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 219 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,847 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $16,378 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 45% from 2016 to 2026. 31% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 281.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,711 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 73,714 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.43 per base acre, PLC $3.87.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (154.22 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $21.36 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $34.08 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $16.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.96 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.21, 2016 to 2025. 98.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.6%.
