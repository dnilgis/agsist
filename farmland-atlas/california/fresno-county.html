Fresno County, California

Fresno County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $16,378 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06019. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Fresno County runs $305 per acre, against $22 per acre for non-irrigated ground, which fell 45 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigation covers 98 percent of harvested cropland, and 53 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. July nights have warmed to a 60.8 F average low over 2017-2026, up from 58.7 F in the 1991-2020 normal, part of a 1.1 F per decade warming trend since 1976.
