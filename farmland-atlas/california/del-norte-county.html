Del Norte County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land in Del Norte County carried a census market value of $10,206 per acre in 2022, up 22.7 percent over 2017-2022 at 4.2 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 54.9 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.1 F, with a warming trend of 0.87 F per decade since 1976; no July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Groundwater supplied 71 percent of irrigation water in 2015, and the county spent 180 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2020 the worst year at 38 weeks.
