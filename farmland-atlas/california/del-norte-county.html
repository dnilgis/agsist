Irrigated cash rent in Del Norte County runs $620 an acre for 2026, which is 14th highest of 41 in California and well above the state median of $437. The census value of land and buildings was $10,206 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $8,315 in 2017, or 4.2% a year. That 2022 value still sits below the state median of $11,471.

Del Norte County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Quick answers for Del Norte County

What is cash rent in Del Norte County, California?

The county average is $620 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Del Norte County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $10,206 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
