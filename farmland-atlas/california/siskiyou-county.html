Siskiyou County, California

Siskiyou County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $21 an acre (2023); land and buildings $4,174 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06093. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent runs $250 per acre against $21 per acre non-irrigated, and 91 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with 71 percent of that water from groundwater. Irrigation failure is the largest cause of indemnities across closed crop years, at 52 percent of $93.3 million, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 2.39 against 1.7 for all closed years. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 3 percent, 5 percent, 13 percent, 8 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. July nightly lows average 54.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 52.6 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 1.01 F per decade since 1976.
