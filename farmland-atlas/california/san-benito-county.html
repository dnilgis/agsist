San Benito County, California

San Benito County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $16 an acre (2024); land and buildings $5,362 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06069. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in San Benito County stands at $651 per acre in 2026, against a non-irrigated rate of just $16 per acre. Land value under the operators' own estimate reached $5,362 per acre in 2022, up 50.2 percent from 2017. Irrigation covers 87 percent of harvested cropland, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Drought has been a real presence: 366 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 90 weeks logged from 2021-2025. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 58.7 F to 60.5 F over 2017-2026.
