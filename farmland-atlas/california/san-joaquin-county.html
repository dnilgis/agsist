Irrigated cropland in San Joaquin County rents for $425 an acre in 2026, just under the state median of $437 and 20th lowest of 41. The census value of land and buildings was $20,649 an acre in 2022, up 37.5% from $15,020 in 2017, which ranks 8th highest of 52. Corn made 206.8 bu in 2023, above the county median of 180.1 bu since 2008. 43% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
