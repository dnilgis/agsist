Orange County, California

Orange County, CA: irrigated cash rent $2,360 an acre (2026); land and buildings $15,003 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06059. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation leans heavily on groundwater, which supplied 71 percent of irrigation water in 2015. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.59 F per decade warming, and heat and drought accounted for 80 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2010-2019, up from 0 percent in 2000-2009. The county spent 106 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2021-2025, and 454 of 1357 weeks were in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025.
