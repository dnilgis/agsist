Merced County, California

Rain is at 90% of normal. Land and buildings run $16,527 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 6.65 inches, 90% of normal (7.36 inches) through Nov 17, at Los Banos. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,361 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $16,527 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $33 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 8.2% from 2014 to 2026. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 679.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 202.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 18,413 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $16.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.98, 2016 to 2025. 98% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 20.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 38.8%.
