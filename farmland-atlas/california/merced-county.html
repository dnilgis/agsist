Irrigated cash rent sits at $411 per acre in 2026, and 98 percent of harvested cropland here carries irrigation, making water access the central fact of farming in Merced County. The county logged 366 weeks in severe drought or worse across 2000-2025, with 97 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone.
