Amador County, California

Amador County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $65 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,386 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Amador County stood at $324 per acre in 2025, against a non-irrigated rent of $65 per acre in 2026. Land value carries at $6,386 per acre in the 2022 census, up 43.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.5 percent a year. Irrigation covers 95 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. July nights have warmed from a 59.5 F normal to a 62.1 F average over 2017-2026, part of a 0.98 F per decade warming trend since 1976. Heat and drought caused 26 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2025, and the county spent 331 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025.
