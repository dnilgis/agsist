Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 99% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,959 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,386 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $65 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 1, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 261.1% from 2016 to 2026. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 98 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 868 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 2.87, 2016 to 2025. 94.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.8%.

Quick answers for Amador County

What is cash rent in Amador County, California?

The county average is $65 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $324 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Amador County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $6,386 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
