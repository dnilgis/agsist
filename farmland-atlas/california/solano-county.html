Solano County, California

Solano County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,901 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 193.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06095. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated rent reached $30 per acre in 2026, up 15.4 percent from 2016. The county spent 386 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 in severe drought or worse, with 2021 filling all 52 weeks.
