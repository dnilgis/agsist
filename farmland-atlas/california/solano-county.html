Solano County, California

Cash rent on Solano County cropland ran $30 an acre in 2026, up 15.4% from $26 in 2016 and tied for 4th lowest of 20 in California. Irrigated rent was $257 an acre in 2026, 5th lowest of 41. The census value of land and buildings reached $12,901 an acre in 2022, up 41.0% from $9,148 in 2017, and 20th highest of 52. Corn yields had a median of 193.1 bu over 2008 to 2018, and 66% of farmland was rented in 2022.
