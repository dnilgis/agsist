Irrigated cash rent in San Luis Obispo County is $811 per acre in 2026, against $27 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2023. Land value under the census carries $12,118 per acre, up 60.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 9.9 percent a year. Irrigation covers 86 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with 71 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Crop insurance losses since 1989 total $118.6 million, led by freeze and frost at 38 percent, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 stands at 1.24. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 363 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 80 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
