Nonirrigated cropland rent in San Luis Obispo County was $27 an acre in 2023, up 92.9% from $14 in 2013. Irrigated ground rented for $811 an acre in 2026, 11th highest of 41 in the state and well over the state median of $437. The census value of land and buildings reached $12,118 an acre in 2022, up 60.6% from $7,547 in 2017, and sits 24th highest of 52.
