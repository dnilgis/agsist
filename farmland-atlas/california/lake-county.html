Lake County, California

Lake County, CA: irrigated cash rent $491 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,833 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 2.59 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

July nights average 61 F now against a 58.9 F normal, warming at 0.65 F per decade since 1976, though no July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved from 1 percent in 1989-1999, to 6 percent in 2000-2009, to 16 percent in 2010-2019, to 31 percent in 2020-2025. Cropland here leans on irrigation, 81 percent of harvested acres, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Land value rose 19.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, and the county logged 112 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2021-2025 against 342 of 1357 weeks since 2000.
