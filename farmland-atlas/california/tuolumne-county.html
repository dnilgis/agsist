Tuolumne County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,337 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06109. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Ninety-two percent of harvested cropland in Tuolumne County is irrigated, and 71 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. The 2022 census put land and buildings at $4,337 per acre, a gain of 16.7 percent from 2017. Severe drought or worse covered the county during 350 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2016 seeing 52 such weeks, and 94 weeks fell in that range during 2021-2025 alone.
