Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2009 (10 surveys on record since 2009); up 62.2% from $19 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Tehama County ran $30 an acre in 2026, up 62.2% from $19 in 2016 and tied for 4th lowest of 20 in California. Irrigated ground rented for $272 in 2026, 7th lowest of 41. The census value of land and buildings was $5,479 an acre in 2022, up 27.0% from $4,313 in 2017, and 14th lowest of 52 against a state median of $11,471.
