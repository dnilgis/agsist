Alameda County, California

Alameda County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,473 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06001. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent 2025 runs $566 per acre, and 85 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, with 70 percent of that water coming from groundwater in 2015. Non-irrigated rent stands at $27 per acre in 2026, up 68.8 percent from 2016. Census land value reached $13,473 per acre in 2022, up 89.6 percent from 2017, or 13.6 percent a year. The county spent 356 of 1357 weeks, 26.2 percent, in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2015 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 94 such weeks came in 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 7 percent in 1989-1999 to 41 percent in 2010-2019 to 14 percent in 2020-2025.
