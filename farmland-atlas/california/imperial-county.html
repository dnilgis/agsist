As of Oct 16, irrigated cropland in Imperial County rents for $350 an acre in 2026, against a state median of $437, which puts it 12th lowest of 41. The census value of land and buildings was $11,892 an acre in 2022, up 6.8% from $11,135 in 2017, or 1.3% a year, and up from $7,002 in 2012. That value ranks 25th highest of 52. Renters work a lot of this ground, with 73% of farmland rented in 2022.
