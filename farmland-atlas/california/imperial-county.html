Imperial County, California

Imperial County, CA: irrigated cash rent $350 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,892 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Imperial County carries 98 percent irrigated cropland, drawing 71 percent of that water from groundwater. The July average nightly low ran 81.6 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 79.1 F, on a warming trend of 0.94 F per decade since 1976.
