Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.14 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,937 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $12,311 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50.50 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 15, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 57.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 862 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.
