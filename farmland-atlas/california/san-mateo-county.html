San Mateo County, California

San Mateo County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $51 an acre (2023); land and buildings $12,311 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06081. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent stands at $550 per acre in 2026, and census land value reached $12,311 per acre in 2022, up 26 percent from 2017 at 4.7 percent a year. The county spent 276 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 94 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025; 71 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.48 F per decade since 1976, reaching 53.9 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.5 F.
