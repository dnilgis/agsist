Madera County, California

Madera County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,239 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Madera County runs $410 per acre against $66 per acre non-irrigated, and that gap has widened since 2010, when the multiple stood at 3.90 versus 6.26 in 2026. Groundwater supplies 98 percent of irrigation water, so land value here rests on wells and aquifer access, not surface allocations. Census value of land and buildings reached $13,239 per acre in 2022, up 20.8 percent from 2017. Crop insurance shows the heat and drought share of indemnities climbing from 1 percent in 1989-1999 to 27 percent in 2020-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.03, above the 0.83 figure for the full 1989-2025 record. July nights have warmed from a 58.8 F normal to a 60.6 F average over 2017-2026.
