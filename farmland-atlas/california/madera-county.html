As of Oct 16, cash rent on Madera County cropland runs $66 an acre in 2026, up 111.3% from $31 in 2016 and 2nd highest of 20 counties in the state. Irrigated ground rents for $410 an acre in 2026, a bit under the state median of $437. The census value of land and buildings was $13,239 an acre in 2022, up 20.8% from $10,958 in 2017, and 19th highest of 52.
