The census value of land and buildings in Sierra County was $2,427 an acre in 2022, up 42.6% from $1,702 in 2017. That works out to 7.4% a year. Even so, that value ranks 2nd lowest of 52 in California, well under the state median of $11,471. In 2022, 27% of the county's farmland was rented.
