Land value here was $2,427 per acre in 2022, up 42.6 percent from 2017, or 7.4 percent a year. July nights average 51.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 49.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.98 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Groundwater supplied 71 percent of irrigation water in 2015, and the county spent 98 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 349 such weeks total from 2000-2025, with 2015 the worst year at 52 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
