Sonoma County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $58 an acre (2026); land and buildings $30,475 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06097. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Sonoma County runs $1,300 per acre against $58 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2026. The census value of land and buildings sits at $30,475 per acre, up 37.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.6 percent a year. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 3 percent in 1989-1999 to 18 percent in 2000-2009 to 21 percent in 2010-2019 to 25 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 105 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 328 such weeks across 2000-2025. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.32 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 53.7 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.2 F.
