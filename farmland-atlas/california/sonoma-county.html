Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 27.82 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,301 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $30,475 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $57.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.59 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 15, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 10% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 49.4% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 27% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 112.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 15,721 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.89 per base acre, PLC $9.35. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (51.78 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 25, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $22.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.09 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.96, 2016 to 2025. 77.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 12.9%.

Quick answers for Sonoma County

What is cash rent in Sonoma County, California?

The county average is $58 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $1,300 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sonoma County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $30,475 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
