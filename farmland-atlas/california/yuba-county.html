Yuba County, California

Yuba County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2016); land and buildings $11,174 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.02 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06115. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Yuba County reached $365 per acre in 2026, against a census land value of $11,174 per acre and a land value gain of 35.9 percent from 2017-2022. Irrigation is central here: 98 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. The July nightly low trend since 1976 is warming at 0.79 F per decade, with the 2017-2026 average sitting at 63.8 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 62 F.
