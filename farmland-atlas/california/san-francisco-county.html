San Francisco County, California

San Francisco County, CA: land and buildings $48,425 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

San Francisco County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in San Francisco County stood at $48,425 per acre in 2022, down 37.7 percent from 2017. The July average nightly low ran 54.3 F for 2017-2026, just above the 54.1 F normal for 1991-2020, with no clear direction in the trend since 1976. The county spent 94 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2021-2025, and 279 of 1357 weeks during 2000-2025, with 2014 reaching 52 weeks.
