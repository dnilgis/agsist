Nevada County, California

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,650 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

10.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.98 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,438 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,650 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $19.50 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 1, 2026 to Sep 2, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 50% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 75.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 750 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $17.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

84.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 0.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 2015 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.3%.

Cash rent in Nevada County ran $20 an acre in 2024, 3rd lowest of 13 in California and under the $22 state median. Irrigated ground is another story at $1,540 an acre in 2026, 5th highest of 41. Quick answers for Nevada County

What is cash rent in Nevada County, California?

The county average is $20 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024 and $1,540 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Nevada County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $8,650 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
