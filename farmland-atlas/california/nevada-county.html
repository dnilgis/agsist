Nevada County, California

Nevada County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2024); land and buildings $8,650 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Nevada County reached $1,540 per acre in 2026, the figure a land buyer will want to anchor on first. The 2022 census put market value of land and buildings at $8,650 per acre, up 16.5 percent over 2017-2022, or 3.1 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.91 F per decade since 1976, reaching a 2017-2026 average of 57 F, though no July since 1900 has averaged at or above 70 F at night.
