Monterey County, California

Monterey County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $14 an acre (2024); land and buildings $12,799 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06053. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

The census market value of land and buildings was $12,799 per acre in 2022, a rise of 73.7 percent from 2017. Groundwater supplies 71 percent of irrigation water, and the county spent 317 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2000-2025.
