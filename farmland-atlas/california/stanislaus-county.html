Stanislaus County, California

Stanislaus County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $55 an acre (2026); land and buildings $20,208 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06099. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated land here rents for $450 per acre against $55 for non-irrigated ground, and 98 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so water access is the line that separates value in this county. Groundwater supplies 58 percent of irrigation water as of 2015. Crop insurance history runs $267.0 million in indemnities across closed crop years 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 43 percent, though heat and drought's share has moved from 2 percent in 1989-1999 to 9 percent in 2000-2009 to 16 percent in 2010-2019 and 16 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.14, above the 0.83 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record. July nights have warmed at 0.64 F per decade since 1976.
