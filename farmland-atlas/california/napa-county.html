Land

Land and buildings: $61,295 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 16, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 12% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 18% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 17% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 39% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 49.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 26.4 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 31,067 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Napa County

What is cash rent in Napa County, California?

The county average is $21 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024 and $4,000 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Napa County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $61,295 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
