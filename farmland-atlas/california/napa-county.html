The census value of land and buildings in Napa County sits at $61,295 an acre for 2022, highest of 52 in California, against a state median of $11,471. That figure was $44,154 in 2017 and is up 38.8% since, 6.8% a year. Irrigated rent runs $4,000 an acre for 2026, highest of 41, while plain cash rent was $21 for 2024. Only 18% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
