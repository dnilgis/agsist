Napa County, California

Napa County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $21 an acre (2024); land and buildings $61,295 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.44 since 1993. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06055. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent reaches $4,000 per acre in 2026, which tells a buyer this is among the most intensively valued farmland in the atlas. The 2022 census puts land and buildings at $61,295 per acre, up 38.8 percent from 2017, or 6.8 percent a year. With 99 percent of harvested cropland irrigated and 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater, the 334 weeks of severe drought or worse recorded across 2000-2025 carry direct operational weight.
