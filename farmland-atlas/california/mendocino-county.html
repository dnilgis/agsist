Mendocino County, California

Mendocino County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2022); land and buildings $6,511 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06045. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent runs $1,290 per acre, while non-irrigated rent sits at $32 per acre, down 37.3 percent from 2011 to 2022. Eighty percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 71 percent of that water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought has grown as a share of crop insurance indemnities across each period listed: 1 percent in 1989-1999, 6 percent in 2000-2009, 18 percent in 2010-2019, and 43 percent in 2020-2025, out of $47.6 million in total indemnities. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 was 1.61, above the 1989-2025 ratio of 1.19. Land value rose 70 percent from 2017 to 2022, reaching $6,511 per acre.
