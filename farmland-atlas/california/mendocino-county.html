Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.28 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 73% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the last spring freeze comes around Mar 22 in the median year; most years have no fall freeze before December. Land

Land and buildings: $6,511 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 14% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 21.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 4.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 44,342 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Mendocino County

What is cash rent in Mendocino County, California?

The county average is $1,290 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Mendocino County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $6,511 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
