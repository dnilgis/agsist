Non-irrigated cash rent in Humboldt County runs $22 per acre; irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $405 per acre. Land value in the 2022 census was $4,578 per acre, up 48.2 percent from 2017-2022, or 8.2 percent a year. Irrigation covers 76 percent of harvested cropland, and 71 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. Drought was present in 274 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2014 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 77 weeks from 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 54.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.8 F, warming at 0.46 F per decade since 1976.
