Humboldt County, California

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,578 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

1.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 24.83 inches, 64% of normal (38.84 inches) through Nov 17, at McKinleyville, 7.3 mi ESE. Frost: the last spring freeze comes around Mar 23 in the median year; most years have no fall freeze before December. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,221 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,578 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2024). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 48% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 109.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 20,450 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $38.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

75.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
