Humboldt County, California

Humboldt County cropland rents for $22 an acre in 2024, right at the state median of $22 and tied for 6th lowest of 13. Pasture runs $19 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,578 an acre in 2022, up 48.2% from $3,090 in 2017, but still 11th lowest of 52 in the state. 48% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
