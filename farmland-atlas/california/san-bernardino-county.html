San Bernardino County, California

San Bernardino County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2024); land and buildings $47,339 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

The county is 99 percent irrigated cropland, with 71 percent of that water coming from groundwater in 2015, so supply security is the central land question.
