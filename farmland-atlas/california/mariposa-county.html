Cropland in Mariposa County rents for $30 an acre in 2026, tied for 4th lowest of 20 against a state median of $35. Pasture goes for $20. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,306 an acre in 2022, up 108.6% from $2,064 in 2017, and still 8th lowest of 52 in a state where the median sits at $11,471. 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
