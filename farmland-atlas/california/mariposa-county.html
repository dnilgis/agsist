Mariposa County, California

Mariposa County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,306 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06043. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 runs $30 per acre. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $4,306 per acre, up 108.6 percent from 2017-2022, or 15.8 percent a year. Irrigated cropland covers 93 percent of harvested acres, and 71 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater as of 2015. Drought was present in severe form or worse for 349 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 98 weeks fell in 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 59.9 F from 2017-2026, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 57.9 F, warming at 0.93 F per decade since 1976. Corn yield and insurance loss records are not available.
