Kern County, California

Rain is at 77% of normal. Land and buildings run $10,539 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 5.61 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,495 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,539 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $48 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.12 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 1, 2026 to Sep 15, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 30% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 136 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,995.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 189,520 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $78.63 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $31.62 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $16.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.95 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.96, 2016 to 2025. 98.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 30.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 28.8%.
