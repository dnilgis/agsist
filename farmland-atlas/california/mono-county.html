The census value of land and buildings in Mono County sits at $3,138 an acre for 2022. That is up 63.4% from $1,921 in 2017, 10.3% a year, though it ran $2,817 back in 2012. It ranks 4th lowest of 52 counties, well under the state median of $11,471. Irrigated rent was $152 an acre in 2017, and 45% of farmland was rented in 2022.
