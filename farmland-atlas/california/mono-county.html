Mono County, California

Mono County, CA: irrigated cash rent $152 an acre (2017); land and buildings $3,138 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06051. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Land and buildings in Mono County were valued at $3,138 per acre in 2022, up 63.4 percent from 2017, or 10.3 percent a year. The county recorded 410 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 30.2 percent of that span, with 2016 seeing 52 weeks at that level. Irrigation covers 98 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 71 percent of its water from groundwater, and heat and drought claimed 100 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 2010-2019. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.87 F per decade warming, with the 2017-2026 average at 49.9 F against the 1991-2020 normal of 48.3 F.
