Glenn County, California

Glenn County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,262 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 196.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06021. Figures as of 2026-09-25.
