Santa Cruz County, California

Santa Cruz County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $75 an acre (2014); land and buildings $38,401 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06087. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Santa Cruz County reached $2,220 per acre in 2025, and census value of land and buildings stood at $38,401 per acre in 2022, up 79.8 percent since 2017. Irrigation covers 95 percent of harvested cropland, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 53.5 F to 53.9 F over 2017-2026, a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities was 0 percent in 1989-1999, 17 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 13 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 89 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 267 of 1357 weeks total since 2000.
