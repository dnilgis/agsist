Irrigated cropland in Ventura County rented for $3,340 an acre in 2026, which ranks 2nd highest of 41 in California against a state median of $437. The census value of land and buildings was $28,278 an acre in 2022, up 10.9% from $25,498 in 2017, or 2.1% a year. That value ranks 5th highest of 52, well over the state median of $11,471. 36% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
