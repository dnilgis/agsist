Ventura County, California

Ventura County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $63 an acre (2024); land and buildings $28,278 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06111. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Ventura County runs $3,340 per acre, against a non-irrigated average of $63 per acre. That gap reflects how much of the farming here depends on water: 97 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 71 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1991-2025, at 44 percent of $118.1 million paid out. That share moved from 13 percent in 1989-1999, to 38 percent in 2000-2009, to 53 percent in 2010-2019, to 39 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 121 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
