Irrigated cropland in Colusa County rents for $375 an acre in 2026, 14th lowest of 41 in California against a state median of $437. Dryland rent was $28 an acre in 2021, down 52.5% from $59 in 2011. The census value of land and buildings reached $11,119 an acre in 2022, up 39.6% from $7,967 in 2017, and 54% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
