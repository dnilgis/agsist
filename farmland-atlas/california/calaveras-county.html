Calaveras County, California

Calaveras County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $11 an acre (2022); land and buildings $4,909 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06009. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in 2026 runs $700 per acre, while non-irrigated rent averages $11 per acre in 2022, down 63.3 percent from 2011. Land value in the 2022 census stands at $4,909 per acre, up 38.1 percent from 2017, 6.7 percent a year. Irrigation covers 93 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 70 percent of that water, 2015. July nightly lows have warmed to 62.4 F, 2017-2026, from a 1991-2020 normal of 59.8 F, a warming trend of 0.93 F per decade since 1976.
