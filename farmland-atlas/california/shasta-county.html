Shasta County, California

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

1.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.98 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,761 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $36.50 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value rose while next door fell; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.50 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 10, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 7% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent was the same in 2014 and 2026. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 62.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 24,326 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.74 per base acre, PLC $8.48. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46.96 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 24, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $32.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.10 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.29, 2016 to 2025. 92.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.4%.

Quick answers for Shasta County

What is cash rent in Shasta County, California?

The county average is $37 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $205 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.
