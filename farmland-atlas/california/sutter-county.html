Sutter County is an irrigated county first. Irrigation covers 96 percent of harvested cropland, and 73 percent of the water comes from groundwater. The irrigated cash rent was $420 per acre in 2026, against $38 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2023. Irrigated rent was 12.11 times the non-irrigated rent in 2023, up from 4.00 in 2010. Crop insurance has paid out more than it took in: the loss ratio is 1.42 over closed crop years 1989-2025, and 1.74 over 2016-2025. Irrigation failure is the largest cause of claims, at 34 percent. Heat and drought went from 13 percent of claims in 1989-1999 to 10 percent in 2020-2025.
