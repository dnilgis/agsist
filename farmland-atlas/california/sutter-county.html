Sutter County, California

Sutter County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2023); land and buildings $14,191 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 202.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06101. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigation carries this county. 96 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 73 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. Irrigated cash rent has moved to $420 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has gone from 4.00 in 2010 to 12.11 in 2023, direction rising. Non-irrigated rent itself sits at $38 per acre, down 45.3 percent from 2013 to 2023. Irrigation failure is the largest cause of indemnities across 1989-2025 at 34 percent, with $171.9 million paid out, and land and buildings are valued at $14,191 per acre in the 2022 census.
