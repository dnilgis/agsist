Los Angeles County, California

Los Angeles County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $84 an acre (2024); land and buildings $21,834 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06037. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent averages $84 per acre; irrigated cash rent reaches $1,000 per acre. Land value stood at $21,834 per acre in 2022, up 17.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 3.3 percent a year. Irrigation covers 68 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and groundwater supplied 70 percent of irrigation water in 2015. The county spent 477 of 1357 weeks, 35.2 percent, in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 52 weeks; 2021-2025 logged 122 such weeks. July nightly lows have warmed 0.78 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 65 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 63.1 F.
