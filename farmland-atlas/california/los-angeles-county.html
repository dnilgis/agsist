Nonirrigated cash rent in Los Angeles County was $84 an acre in 2024, highest of 13 against a state median of $22. Irrigated rent ran $1,000 an acre in 2026, 9th highest of 41. The census value of land and buildings was $21,834 an acre in 2022, up 17.5% from $18,580 in 2017, or 3.3% a year, and 6th highest of 52. 76% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
