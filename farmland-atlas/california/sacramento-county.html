Non-irrigated cash rent in Sacramento County runs $68 per acre in 2026, up 68.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $276 per acre, a multiple of 4.09 over non-irrigated in 2026, against 6.28 in 2010, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Irrigated cropland covers 89 percent of harvested acres, and groundwater supplied 64 percent of irrigation water in 2015. Census land value reached $15,766 per acre in 2022, up 56.9 percent from 2017.
