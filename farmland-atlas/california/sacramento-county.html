$68

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2010; up 68.8% from $40 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Sacramento County runs $68 an acre in 2026, up 68.8% from $40 in 2016 and the highest of 20 in the state. The census value of land and buildings was $15,766 an acre in 2022, up 56.9% from $10,048 in 2017, which ranks 13th highest of 52. Corn made 201.6 bu in 2023, above the 2019-23 average of 194.5 bu. 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
