Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.05 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 108% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Land

Land and buildings: $15,766 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $67.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.42 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 1, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 26% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 68.8% from 2016 to 2026. 54% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 143.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,942 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Sacramento County

What is cash rent in Sacramento County, California?

The county average is $68 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $276 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sacramento County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $15,766 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Sacramento County, California?

The county's 2019-23 average is 194.5 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
