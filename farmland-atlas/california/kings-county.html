Kings County cash rent sat at $22 an acre in 2024, down 55.7% from $49 in 2012 and 5th lowest of 13 in the state. Irrigated ground rented for $305 an acre in 2026, tied for 10th lowest of 41. The census value of land and buildings came in at $10,391 an acre in 2022, down 3.9% from $10,815 in 2017.
