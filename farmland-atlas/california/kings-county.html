Kings County, California

Kings County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2024); land and buildings $10,391 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06031. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Water drives this county. Irrigation covers 99 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 95 percent of that water, so irrigation failure is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 40 percent of $531.7 million in indemnities, paid out as $213.3 million. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 436 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 52 weeks. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved from 1 percent in 1989-1999 to 13 percent in 2000-2009 to 15 percent in 2010-2019 to 16 percent in 2020-2025. Non-irrigated cash rent stands at $22 per acre in 2024, down 55.7 percent from 2012.
