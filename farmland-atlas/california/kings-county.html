Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 4.88 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,595 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,391 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21.50 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.10 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 1, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 19% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 547.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 499.1 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,822 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $38.41 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $31.62 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $16.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.89 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.52, 2016 to 2025. 98.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 14.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 30.9%.

Quick answers for Kings County

What is cash rent in Kings County, California?

The county average is $22 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024 and $305 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Kings County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $10,391 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
