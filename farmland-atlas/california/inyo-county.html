Inyo County, California

Inyo County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $5 an acre (2017); land and buildings $961 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Inyo County was $106 per acre in 2017, against $5 per acre for non-irrigated land. All harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and 71 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Land value rose 55 percent from 2017-2022, or 9.2 percent a year. The county spent 511 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 53 weeks in the worst year, 2013, and 116 weeks from 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 70.7 F from 2017-2026, up from a 68.5 F normal in 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 1.04 F per decade since 1976.
