Cash rent in Modoc County was $22 an acre in 2024, down 21.4% from $28 in 2014 and tied for 6th lowest of 13 in California. The census value of land and buildings ran $4,267 an acre in 2022, up 118.1% from $1,956 in 2017. That still left it 7th lowest of 52, well under the state median of $11,471. 27% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
