Modoc County, California

Modoc County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2024); land and buildings $4,267 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.61 since 1990. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06049. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland carries this county: 84 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigated rent runs $303 per acre against $22 per acre non-irrigated. Groundwater supplies 71 percent of irrigation water, and irrigation failure is the largest cause of indemnities at 64 percent, $49.3 million of the $77.2 million paid out since 1990. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 2.36, higher than the 1.61 ratio for all closed years back to 1990. July nights have warmed from a 50 F normal to a 52.5 F average for 2017-2026, a trend of 0.99 F per decade since 1976.
