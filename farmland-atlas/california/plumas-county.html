Plumas County, California

Plumas County, CA: land and buildings $4,222 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06063. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land in Plumas County last measured at $4,222 per acre in 2022, up 83.7 percent over 2017-2022, or 12.9 percent a year. Irrigation is central to farming here: 69 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in 2022, and 71 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. The county spent 342 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025, 25.2 percent of that span, with 2015 reaching 52 weeks; 97 of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 1.01 F per decade warming, with the 2017-2026 average at 51.8 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 50 F.
