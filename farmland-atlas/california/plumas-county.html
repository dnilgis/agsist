The census value of land and buildings in Plumas County ran $4,222 an acre in 2022. That is up 83.7% from $2,298 in 2017, or 12.9% a year, and well above the $1,776 of 2012. It still sits under the state median of $11,471 and ranks 6th lowest of 52 in California. Only 14% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
