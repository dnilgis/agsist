Lassen County, California

Lassen County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,493 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06035. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland in Lassen County rents for $225 per acre in 2026, against $32 per acre for non-irrigated ground. That gap points to water as the working asset here. Irrigation covers 91 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and 71 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Drought has been present: 431 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 97 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 4 percent in 1989-1999, to 9 percent in 2000-2009, to 8 percent in 2010-2019, to 43 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed at 1.01 F per decade since 1976.
