Yolo County, California

Rain is at 96% of normal. Land and buildings run $10,780 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.79 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.76 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,180 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,780 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $43 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 48% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 7.5% from 2014 to 2026. 50% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 110.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 13 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 36,190 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.79 per base acre, PLC $3.98.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (158.66 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 25, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $24.56 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $35.06 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.49 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.84, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 88.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 30.6%.

Quick answers for Yolo County

What is cash rent in Yolo County, California?

The county average is $43 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $440 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Yolo County, California?

Land and buildings were worth $10,780 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Yolo County, California?

The county's 2008-2023 median is 196.0 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
