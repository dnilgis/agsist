Yolo County, California

Yolo County, CA: non-irrigated cash rent $43 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,780 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 196.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 06113. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Yolo County runs $440 per acre against $43 per acre non-irrigated, and that multiple has moved from 5.09 in 2009 to 10.23 in 2026, rising. Irrigation covers 89 percent of harvested cropland, with 33 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Drought pressure has been present: 398 of 1357 weeks since 2000 saw severe drought or worse, with 2021 alone posting 52 weeks and 114 weeks falling in 2021-2025. Crop insurance history shows $308.5 million in indemnities since 1989, led by excess moisture and flood at 31 percent, with irrigation failure adding $80.3 million. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 stands at 1.84, above the 1.41 rate for the full 1989-2025 period.
