$47

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 47% from $32 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, Ford County cropland rented for $47 an acre in 2026, up 46.9% from $32 in 2016, and that sits tied for 25th lowest of 102 against a state median of $58. Irrigated ground runs higher at $169 an acre in 2026, tied for 11th highest of 28. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,718 an acre in 2022, up 11.1% from $1,547 in 2017. 58% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
