Ford County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,708 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Ford County vs Kansas: $47 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 24% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 25th lowest of 102).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

18.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 22.58 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,328 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.64, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 79% of the median yield. 16.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 5.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 69.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.1%.
