Jefferson County, Kansas

Jefferson County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $82 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,906 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20087. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jefferson County averages $82 per acre in 2026, up 7.9 percent from 2016. Land value came in at $3,906 per acre in the 2022 census, rising 34.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.1 percent a year; cash rent equals 1.83 percent of that value. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 2.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 146.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 109.5 in 2010. Heat and drought drove 73 percent of $39.5 million in indemnities since 1989, and its share of losses moved 26 percent, 71 percent, 80 percent, 73 percent across the decades listed. Irrigation covers 4 percent of harvested cropland, with 91 percent of that water drawn from groundwater.
