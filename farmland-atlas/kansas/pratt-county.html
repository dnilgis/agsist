Pratt County, Kansas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,400 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

70% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.61 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 200 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,394 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.14 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.97, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 88% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025.
