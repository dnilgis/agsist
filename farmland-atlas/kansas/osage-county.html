Osage County, Kansas

Rain is at 128% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $4,131 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.23 per base acre for corn.

Osage County vs Kansas: $70 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 68% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 32nd highest of 102).

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.56 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.73, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 26% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
