USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Chautauqua County was $31 an acre in 2025, down 7.6% from $33 in 2014. Pasture rent ran $17 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,007 an acre in 2022, up 23.1% from $1,631 in 2017, and that ranks 40th lowest of 104 against a state median of $2,272. The corn trend sits at 94.3 bu, lowest of 20 in the state.
