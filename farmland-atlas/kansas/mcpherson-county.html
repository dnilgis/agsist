Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $4,645 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

14.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.28 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 198 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,473 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).
