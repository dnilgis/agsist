Nemaha County, Kansas

Rain is at 125% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,538 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.65 inches, 125% of normal (32.48 inches) through Nov 17, at Goff, 3.9 mi SSW. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,174 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,538 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $159 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 20.5% from 2016 to 2026. 41.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 17.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 214.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 69,887 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.06 at Pawnee City, 24 miles.

7 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.84 per base acre, PLC $2.99.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (119 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $52.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,280 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.15 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.43 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 49% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 81.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.5%.
