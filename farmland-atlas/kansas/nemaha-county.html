Nemaha County cash rent runs $159 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 102 in Kansas against a state median of $58. That is up 20.5% from $132 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,538 an acre in 2022, up 51.0% from $3,006 in 2017. Corn made 191.5 bu in 2024, well over the 144.9 bu county median going back to 2008.
