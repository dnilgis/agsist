Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 18.5% from $62 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Crawford County runs $74 an acre in 2026, up 18.5% from $62 in 2016 and 25th highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,097 an acre in 2022, up 53.8% from $2,014 in 2017 and 22nd highest of 104. Corn made 121.4 bu in 2025 against a county median of 111.4 bu, and 49% of farmland was rented in 2022.
