Crawford County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,097 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.27 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

33.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 39.22 inches, 94% of normal (41.59 inches) through Nov 17, at Farlington, 0.8 mi NNE. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,547 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,097 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $74 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 18.5% from 2016 to 2026. 49.3% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 134.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 7,297 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 30 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.08 at Columbus East, 21 miles. 6 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 30 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.27 per base acre, PLC $2.42.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (96.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $43.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 107 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.00 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.01 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 28% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 66.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 27.9%.
