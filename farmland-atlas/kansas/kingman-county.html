Cropland rent in Kingman County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 20.3% from $40 in 2016 and under the state median of $58, 28th lowest of 102. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,172 an acre in 2022, up 34.2% from $1,619 in 2017. Corn made 168.4 bu in 2024, well over the 136.0 bu median since 2008. 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
