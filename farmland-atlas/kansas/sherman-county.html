Sherman County, Kansas

Sherman County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $63 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,965 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 131.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20181. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sherman County runs $63 per acre in 2026, up 37 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $193 per acre, and the irrigated multiple is 3.06 in 2026 against 3.18 in 2010, with no clear direction over that span. Heat and drought drove 56 percent of $465.9 million in indemnities across closed crop years 1989-2025, with the share at 5 percent in 1989-1999, 68 percent in 2000-2009, 50 percent in 2010-2019, and 62 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigated cropland covers 28 percent of harvested acres, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015.
