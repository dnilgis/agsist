Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 19.05 inches, 100% of normal (19.05 inches) through Nov 17, at Goodland, 0.5 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,903 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,965 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $63 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 37% from 2016 to 2026. 58.3% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 33.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 45,333 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 14,471 acres, so read the change with care.

3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.90 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.97 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. 27.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 5.9 days a year and damaging wind on 8.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 55.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.9%.
