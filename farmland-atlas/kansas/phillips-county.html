Non-irrigated cash rent in Phillips County runs $58 per acre, up 30.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought drove 77 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share climbed across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019 before landing at 77 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 240 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2013 the worst year at 43 weeks. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, so most acres carry the drought exposure directly.
