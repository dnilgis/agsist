Barber County, Kansas

Barber County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $43 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,941 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 132.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20007. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Barber County averages $43 per acre in 2026, up 17.8 percent from 2016. Only 6 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so the corn yield trend of 3.4 bushels per acre a year, on a median of 132.8 bushels per acre from 2009-2022, rides mostly on rainfall. Heat and drought has driven a growing share of crop insurance indemnities: 39 percent in 1989-1999, 40 percent in 2000-2009, 80 percent in 2010-2019, 91 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 306 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 with at least 50 percent of its area in severe drought or worse, and 80 of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. Census land value reached $1,941 per acre in 2022, up 35.2 percent from 2017.
