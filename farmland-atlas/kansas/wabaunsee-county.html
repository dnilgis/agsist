USDA NASS county yields, 2024

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Wabaunsee County runs $63 an acre for 2026, up 15.6% from $55 in 2016 and tied for 42nd highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $2,694 an acre in 2022, up 39.8% from $1,927 in 2017. Corn made 163.1 bu in 2024, well over the 13 year median of 118.1 bu. In 2022, 51% of the farmland was rented.
