Cash rent in Stanton County runs $33 an acre for 2026, up 10.2% from $30 in 2016 and tied for 3rd lowest of 102 against a state median of $58. Irrigated rent is $101 for 2026, the lowest of 28. The census value of land and buildings was $1,446 an acre in 2022, up 35.9% from $1,064 in 2017 and 5th lowest of 104. Renters farm 66% of the ground.
