Cash rent in Sheridan County runs $64 an acre in 2026, up 41.1% from $45 in 2016 and above the state median of $58. Irrigated ground rents for $199 in 2026, 6th highest of 28 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,767 an acre in 2022, up 13.0% from 2017 but still under the $1,919 of 2012, and 24th lowest of 104. Corn made 96.3 bu in 2023, short of the 120.0 bu median.
