Osborne County, Kansas

Osborne County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $58 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,710 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 102.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
