Cheyenne County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,630 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

4.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 172 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,931 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,630 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $55 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 23.6% from 2016 to 2026. 55.3% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 60.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,448 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 8 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.41 at Benkelman, 21 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 110 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

PRF grazing county base value: $35.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,990 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.61 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.68 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.6 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.6%.
