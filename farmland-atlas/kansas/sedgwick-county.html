Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the highest of 15 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 14, Sedgwick County cash rent sits at $56 an acre for 2026, up 10.9% from $51 in 2016 and 47th lowest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings ran $3,923 an acre in 2022, up 13.3% from $3,464 in 2017, and that is 10th highest of 104. Corn made 160.4 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 129.9 bu. In 2022, 56% of the farmland here was rented.
