Jackson County, Kansas

Rain is at 125% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $4,775 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.52 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.35 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 125% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 192 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,296 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.50 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.33, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 40% of the median yield. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 75.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.5%.

Cropland rent in Jackson County runs $92 an acre for 2026, up 46.4% from $63 in 2016. That sits well above the state median of $58 and ranks tied for 11th highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $3,276 an acre in 2022, up 22.4% from $2,677 in 2017. Corn made 170.5 bu in 2024, ahead of the 137.1 bu median for 2009 to 2024.
