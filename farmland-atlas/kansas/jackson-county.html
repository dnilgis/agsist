Jackson County, Kansas

Cropland rent in Jackson County runs $92 an acre for 2026, up 46.4% from $63 in 2016. That sits well above the state median of $58 and ranks tied for 11th highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $3,276 an acre in 2022, up 22.4% from $2,677 in 2017. Corn made 170.5 bu in 2024, ahead of the 137.1 bu median for 2009 to 2024.
