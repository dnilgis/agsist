Washington County, Kansas

Washington County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $92 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,737 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 118.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20201. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County averages $92 per acre, up 14.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent was $173 per acre in 2021, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent was 2.35 in 2021 versus 1.59 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Heat and drought drove 66 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share moved from 24 percent in 1989-1999, to 75 percent in 2000-2009, to 54 percent in 2010-2019, to 90 percent in 2020-2025. County corn yield trended up 3.2 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2022, though 2012 brought the worst yield on record at 79.7 bushels per acre. Census land value stood at $2,737 per acre in 2022, up 12.8 percent from 2017.
