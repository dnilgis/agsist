Washington County, Kansas

Rain is at 126% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $5,919 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.24 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.44 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 126% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,233 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 14.4% from $80 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Washington County runs $92 an acre for 2026, tied for 11th highest of 102 in Kansas and well above the $58 state median. That rent is up 14.4% from $80 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,737 an acre in 2022, up 12.8% from 2017, or 2.4% a year. Corn has held a median of 118.8 bu from 2008 to 2022, and 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
