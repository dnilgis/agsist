USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Butler County runs $54 an acre for 2026, up 16.1% from $47 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $58 and ranks 41st lowest of 102. The census value of land and buildings was $2,796 an acre in 2022, up 23.3% from $2,268 in 2017, and ranks 28th highest of 104. Corn came in at 132.9 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 113.6 bu.
