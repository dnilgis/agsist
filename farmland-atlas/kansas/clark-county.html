Clark County, Kansas

Clark County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,288 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.06 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20025. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clark County runs $38 per acre, up 21 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value sits at $1,288 per acre by the 2022 census estimate, up 8.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought drove 82 percent of $61.5 million in crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share rose in each successive period: 51 percent in 1989-1999, 68 percent in 2000-2009, 83 percent in 2010-2019, 87 percent in 2020-2025. All irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015, drawn through 121 active irrigation points of diversion with a median priority year of 1977.
