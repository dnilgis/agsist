$38

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 21.0% from $31 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Clark County rents for $38 an acre in 2026, tied for 9th lowest of 102 in Kansas and under the state median of $58. That rent is up 21.0% from $31 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $1,288 an acre in 2022, 2nd lowest of 104 in the state, up 8.5% from $1,187 in 2017. 61% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
