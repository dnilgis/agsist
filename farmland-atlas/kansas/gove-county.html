Gove County, Kansas

Cash rent in Gove County ran $50 an acre in 2026, down 2.0% from $51 in 2016 and under the Kansas median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,729 an acre in 2022, up 32.9% from $1,301 in 2017, and that value ranks 21st lowest of 104 in the state. Corn made 73.9 bu in 2024, well short of the 95.4 bu median for 2008 through 2024. In 2022, 57% of the farmland here was rented.
