Neosho County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $3,954 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.18 per base acre for corn.

Neosho County vs Kansas: $55 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 41% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 42nd lowest of 102).

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

5.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 119% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal.
