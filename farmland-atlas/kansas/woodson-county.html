Woodson County, Kansas

Rain is at 118% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,005 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.32 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 45.86 inches, 118% of normal (38.95 inches) through Nov 17, at Yates Center. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,469 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,005 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $58 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 17% from 2016 to 2026. 51.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4.8% of the cropland, CRP left out. 99 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

County median corn basis: 60 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $3.75 at Piqua, 11 miles. 2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 72 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.32 per base acre, PLC $2.27.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (90.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $43.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 163 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.85 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.85 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 30% of the median yield. 0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 77.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 16.5%.
