Lyon County, Kansas

Lyon County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $72 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,515 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 109.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20111. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lyon County runs $72 per acre, up 33.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $2,515 per acre, up 26.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so yield rests on rain and heat. Heat and drought has driven a rising share of crop insurance indemnities each decade counted: 32 percent in 1989-1999, 51 percent in 2000-2009, 82 percent in 2010-2019, 79 percent in 2020-2025. The corn yield trend for 2008-2025 is 0.3 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 109.4 and a low of 40.8 in 2011. Drought weeks ran 129 of 1357 from 2000-2025, worst in 2018 at 30 weeks; 21 weeks came in 2021-2025.
