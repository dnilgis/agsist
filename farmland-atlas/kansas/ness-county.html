Ness County, Kansas

Ness County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,424 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 90.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20135. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 stands at $42 per acre, up 36.1 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so this is a heat and water story tied to rainfall more than to wells. Heat and drought caused 5 percent of indemnity dollars in 1989-1999, 74 percent in 2000-2009, 85 percent in 2010-2019, and 90 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 306 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 with at least 50 percent of the area in severe drought or worse, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks.
