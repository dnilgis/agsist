Cash rent in Ness County runs $42 an acre for 2026, up 36.1% from $31 in 2016, against a state median of $58 and tied for 16th lowest of 102. The census value of land and buildings was $1,424 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from $1,084 in 2017, and 4th lowest of 104 in Kansas. Corn made 61.8 bu in 2024, under the 2020-24 average of 68.6 bu and well off the long run median of 90.3 bu.
