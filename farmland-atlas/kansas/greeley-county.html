Greeley County, Kansas

Rain is at 126% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,072 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 22.61 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 126% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,984 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).
