Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2009; up 58% from $31 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Greeley County runs $49 an acre for 2026, up 58.1% from $31 in 2016, and that sits 31st lowest of 102 against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $1,795 an acre in 2022, up 26.8% from $1,416 in 2017. Corn made 70.8 bu in 2023, still under the 83.8 bu median for 2010 to 2023. 57% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
