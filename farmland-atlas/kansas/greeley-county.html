Greeley County, Kansas

Greeley County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $49 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,795 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 83.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greeley County averages $49 per acre in 2026, up 58.1 percent from 2016. Heat and drought is the largest cause of indemnities across 1989-2025, at 73 percent of $262.1 million paid, and its share by period was 28 percent in 1989-1999, 70 percent in 2000-2009, 77 percent in 2010-2019, and 75 percent in 2020-2025. All irrigation water comes from groundwater, measured in 2015 at 100 percent, and the county held 355 active irrigation points of diversion with a median priority year of 1969. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
