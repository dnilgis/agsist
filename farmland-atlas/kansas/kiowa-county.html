Kiowa County, Kansas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,048 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

56.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 21.64 inches, 88% of normal (24.7 inches) through Nov 18, at Greensburg. Frost-free season: 195 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,376 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.25 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 31.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 69% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.1%.
