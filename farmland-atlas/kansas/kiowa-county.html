Kiowa County, Kansas

Kiowa County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,601 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 178.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20097. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kiowa County sits at $39 per acre, unchanged from 2016 to 2026 at 0 percent. Irrigated cash rent is $144 per acre in 2025, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 3.42 in 2010 to 4.24 in 2025 with no clear direction overall. Groundwater supplies 100 percent of irrigation water, and 32 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 35 percent in 1989-1999, to 51 percent in 2000-2009, to 69 percent in 2010-2019, to 79 percent in 2020-2025. Median corn yield 2010-2023 is 178.1 bushels per acre, with 2023 the worst year at 139.8.
