Hodgeman County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,127 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Weather

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. $38

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 5.6% from $36 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Hodgeman County ran $38 an acre in 2026, tied for 9th lowest of 102 in Kansas against a state median of $58. That rent was $36 in 2016, up 5.6% over ten years. The census value of land and buildings was $1,488 an acre in 2022, up 26.5% from $1,176 in 2017, and still 7th lowest of 104 in the state. 46% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
