Hodgeman County, Kansas

Hodgeman County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,488 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20083. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hodgeman County runs $38 per acre in 2026, up 5.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents higher, at $88 per acre in 2023, and 16 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, with all of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Water rights show 858 active points of diversion, 598 for irrigation, with a median priority year of 1968. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $117.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 70 percent overall, and that share by period runs 19 percent in 1989-1999, 63 percent in 2000-2009, 72 percent in 2010-2019, and 79 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $1,488 per acre for 2022, up 26.5 percent from 2017.
