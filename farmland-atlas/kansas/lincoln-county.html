Lincoln County, Kansas

Rain is at 114% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,023 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 31.91 inches, 114% of normal (28.06 inches) through Nov 17, at Beverly, 4.6 mi S. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,337 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,023 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $68 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 29.2% from 2016 to 2026. 41.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 127.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 21,820 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 48 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.16 at Hunter, 17 miles. 10 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 80 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 2 elevators.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

PRF grazing county base value: $44.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 345 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.79 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.97 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 46% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 69.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Lincoln County cash rent runs $69 an acre in 2026, up 29.2% from $53 in 2016 and above the state median of $58. That rent ranks 34th highest of 102. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,023 an acre in 2022, up 17.1% from $1,727 in 2017, still under the state median of $2,272. Corn made 68.0 bu in 2023 against a county median of 89.7 bu.
