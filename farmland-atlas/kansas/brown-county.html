Brown County, Kansas

Brown County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $184 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,136 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20013. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Brown County averages $184 per acre for 2026, up 0.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent was $232 per acre in 2023. Census land value stood at $5,136 per acre in 2022, up 46.4 percent from 2017, or 7.9 percent a year; the 2022 non-irrigated rent was 3.52 percent of that value. Corn yield trend runs 3.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 173.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 114.9 in 2012. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
