Chase County, Kansas

Chase County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $63 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,385 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 124.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20017. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Chase County runs $63 per acre in 2026, up 35.5 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 2 percent irrigated, so this is a rain and heat county. Heat and drought account for 77 percent of crop insurance indemnities since the 1989-1999 period, and that share has moved higher in each period listed: 27 percent in 1989-1999, 58 percent in 2000-2009, 78 percent in 2010-2019, 88 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low sits at 67.9 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.8 F, with no clear direction in the longer trend since 1976. Median corn yield 2009-2024 is 124.5 bushels per acre, with the worst year, 2013, at 85.5. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 35 over 2021-2025.
