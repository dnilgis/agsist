Cash rent in Miami County runs $87 an acre for 2026, up 44.2% from $60 in 2014 and 14th highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,190 an acre in 2022, up 35.4% from 2017 and 8th highest of 104. The corn trend yield sits at 154.2 bu, 4th highest of 20, against a state median of 120.3 bu.
