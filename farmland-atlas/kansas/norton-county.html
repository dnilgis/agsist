Norton County, Kansas

Norton County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $55 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,785 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 84.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20137. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Norton County runs $55 per acre, up 35.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated rent was $178 per acre in 2023. Groundwater supplies 98 percent of irrigation water, drawn through 722 irrigation points of diversion with a median priority year of 1998. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $220.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 80 percent overall; that share was 20 percent in 1989-1999, 77 percent in 2000-2009, 84 percent in 2010-2019, and 82 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 335 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 with at least 50 percent of the area in severe drought or worse, including 53 weeks in 2013 alone. Census land value stood at $1,785 per acre in 2022, up 31.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
