Wilson County, Kansas

Rain is at 103% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $4,389 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.74 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.79 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 103% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,536 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).
