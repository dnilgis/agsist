Cash rent in Scott County runs $57 an acre for 2026, up 31.4% from $43 in 2016 and tied for 48th lowest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $2,012 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $1,515 in 2017. Corn made 74.6 bu in 2024, well under the county median of 119.5 bu for 2009 to 2024.
