USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Linn County runs $97 an acre in 2026, up 65.8% from $59 in 2016 and 10th highest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,041 an acre in 2022, up 19.1% from $2,554 in 2017. That puts the county 23rd highest of 104 in the state. Renters work 48% of the farmland here.

Quick answers for Linn County

What is cash rent in Linn County, Kansas?

The county average is $97 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Linn County, Kansas?

Land and buildings were worth $3,041 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
