As of Oct 16, Marion County cash rent runs $56 an acre in 2026, up 20.7% from $46 in 2016 and tied for 45th lowest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,804 an acre in 2022, up 37.3% from $2,042 in 2017, and that ranks 27th highest of 104. Corn made 131.7 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 111.0 bu.
