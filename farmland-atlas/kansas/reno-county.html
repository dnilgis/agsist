Cash rent in Reno County runs $53 an acre for 2026, up 3.9% from $51 in 2016 and under the state median of $58. Irrigated ground rents for $181 in 2026, 9th highest of 28. The census value of land and buildings was $2,641 an acre in 2022, up 23.5% from $2,139 in 2017. Corn made 165.7 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 165.3 bu.
