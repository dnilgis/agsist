USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Smith County runs $76 an acre for 2026, up 17.1% from $65 in 2016 and 22nd highest of 102 counties in Kansas, where the median is $58. The census value of land and buildings was $2,263 an acre in 2022, up 18.5% from $1,909 in 2017. Corn made 148.3 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 117.8 bu. 54% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
