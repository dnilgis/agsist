Smith County, Kansas

Rain is at 119% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $3,736 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.62 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.52 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 119% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 180 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,126 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.60, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 33% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 79.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.7%.
