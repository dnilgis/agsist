Comanche County, Kansas

Comanche County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,550 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Comanche County averages $36 per acre, up 14.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent runs $107 per acre in 2025, and all irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015, drawn from 175 irrigation points of diversion with a median priority year of 1976. Heat and drought account for 80 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and their share has moved up each period: 44 percent in 1989-1999, 68 percent in 2000-2009, 85 percent in 2010-2019, and 89 percent in 2020-2025.
