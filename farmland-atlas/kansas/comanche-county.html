Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2009; up 15% from $31 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Comanche County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 14.5% from $31 in 2016 and 6th lowest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,550 an acre in 2022, up 33.7% from $1,159 in 2017, and that sits 9th lowest of 104. Pasture rents for $13 an acre in 2026, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
