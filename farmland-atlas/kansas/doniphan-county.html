Cash rent in Doniphan County runs $193 an acre for 2026, the highest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. That is up 17.7% from $164 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,754 an acre in 2022, 4th highest of 104, up 38.6% from $3,431 in 2017. Corn made 207.3 bu in 2025, above the county median of 186.6 bu.
