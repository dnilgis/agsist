Cash rent in Wichita County runs $56 an acre for 2026, up 24.7% from $45 in 2014 and tied for 45th lowest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $1,673 an acre in 2022, up 19.4% from 2017 and 14th lowest of 104 against a state median of $2,272. Corn made 78.5 bu in 2024, well under the county median of 124.8 bu since 2008. In 2022, 71% of the farmland here was rented.
