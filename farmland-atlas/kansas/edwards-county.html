Cash rent in Edwards County runs $41 an acre in 2026, up 5.2% from $39 in 2016 and 12th lowest of 102 in Kansas. Irrigated ground rents for $167 an acre in 2026, 13th highest of 28 and above the $159 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $1,957 an acre in 2022, up 6.2% from $1,843 in 2017 but still under the $2,134 of 2012. Corn made 167.2 bu in 2025, against a 2021-25 average of 158.1 bu.
