Coffey County, Kansas

Coffey County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $62 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,681 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 115.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20031. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Coffey County runs $62 per acre, up 20.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so corn yields track weather directly: the trend line rises 2.8 bushels per acre a year against a median of 115, but 2012 fell to 41.1. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities at 73 percent of $75.5 million paid out since 1989, and its share has moved higher across the last two periods, 76 percent in 2010-2019 and 79 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.62, with $8.54 paid per $100 of insured value. Wind capacity operating stands at 199 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
