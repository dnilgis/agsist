USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Coffey County cropland rented for $62 an acre in 2026. That is up 20.6% from $51 in 2016 and tied for 46th highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $2,681 an acre in 2022, up 46.9% from $1,825 in 2017 and 36th highest of 104. Corn made 152.4 bu in 2025, well over the 115.0 bu median going back to 2009.
