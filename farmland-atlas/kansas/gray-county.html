Gray County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,796 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

6.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 20.18 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 183 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,277 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 521.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,836 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.89 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.87, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. 37.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.3%.
