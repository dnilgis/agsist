Gray County, Kansas

Cash rent in Gray County runs $50 an acre for 2026, up 20.7% from $41 in 2016 and tied for 32nd lowest of 102 in Kansas. Irrigated ground rents for $154 in 2026, a touch under the state median of $159. The census value of land and buildings was $1,985 an acre in 2022, up 24.4% from $1,596 in 2017. Corn made 181.0 bu in 2025, short of the 2021-25 average of 193.9 bu.
