Cash rent in Stafford County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 9.1% from $44 in 2016 and 29th lowest of 102 in Kansas. Irrigated ground rents for $151 in 2026, under the state median of $159. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,086 an acre in 2022, up 15.7% from 2017. Corn made 178.3 bu in 2025, well over the 149.9 bu median since 2008.
