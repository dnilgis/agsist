Kearny County, Kansas

Rain is at 132% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,740 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 23.4 inches, 132% of normal (17.77 inches) through Nov 13, at Lakin. Frost-free season: 180 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,177 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,740 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 63% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 130.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,580 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 10 cents over Dec '26 futures, median of 5 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.63 at Ulysses Elevator, 6 miles. 10 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,016 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.62 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.73, 2016 to 2025. 22.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.8%.
