Stands out against its own record

$76

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 23.6% from $62 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Ottawa County runs $76 an acre for 2026, up 23.6% from $62 in 2016. That sits above the Kansas median of $58 and ranks tied for 20th highest of 102. The census value of land and buildings was $2,577 an acre in 2022, up 28.1% from $2,011 in 2017. Corn made 128.5 bu in 2025, a touch over the 125.2 bu median since 2008, and 56% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
