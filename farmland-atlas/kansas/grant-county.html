Grant County cash rent sits at $31 an acre for 2026, the 2nd lowest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. That rent is down 18.7% from $38 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,711 an acre in 2022, up 8.6% from $1,576 in 2017, or 1.7% a year. Corn ran 181.1 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 212.1 bu.
