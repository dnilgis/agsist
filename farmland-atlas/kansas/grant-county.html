Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.28 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 71% of the median yield. 46.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).
