Grant County, Kansas

Grant County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,711 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 189.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20067. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent was $96 per acre in 2019. The county has spent 448 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 83 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Irrigation draws entirely from groundwater, 100 percent as of 2015, on land where 46 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and water rights carry a median priority year of 1969.
