As of Oct 16, cash rent in Douglas County runs $85 an acre for 2026, up 21.6% from $70 in 2016, and 16th highest of 102 against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $4,700 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 104, up 15.4% from $4,072 in 2017. Corn here trends at 154.6 bu, 3rd highest of 20, and 61% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
