Cherokee County, Kansas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $5,759 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.26 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Cherokee County

What is cash rent in Cherokee County, Kansas?

The county average is $70 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cherokee County, Kansas?

Land and buildings were worth $2,676 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Cherokee County, Kansas?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 116.4 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 12 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
