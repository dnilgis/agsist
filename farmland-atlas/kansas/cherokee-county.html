Cash rent in Cherokee County runs $70 an acre for 2026, up 26.1% from $56 in 2016 and tied for 32nd highest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $2,676 an acre in 2022, up 21.0% from $2,211 in 2017, or 3.9% a year. Corn trends at 116.4 bu for 2026, a bit under the state median of 120.3 bu, and 52% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
