Cash rent in Rooks County runs $49 an acre for 2026, up 21.2% from $40 in 2016 and 30th lowest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $1,521 an acre in 2022, up 21.6% from $1,251 in 2017. That ranks 8th lowest of 104 in the state, against a state median of $2,272. Corn made 71.3 bu in 2023, under the county median of 82.7 bu going back to 2008.
