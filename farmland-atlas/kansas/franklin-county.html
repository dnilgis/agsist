Franklin County, Kansas

Franklin County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $75 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,444 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 124.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20059. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County runs $74 per acre in 2026, down 2.6 percent from 2016. Census land value came in at $3,444 per acre in 2022, up 43.3 percent from 2017, 7.5 percent a year, while cash rent that year sat at 2.13 percent of that value. Corn yields trended up 5.1 bushels per acre a year over 2010-2024, with a median of 124.8 bushels per acre and a low of 46.2 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 77 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share moved from 45 percent in 1989-1999 to 60 percent in 2000-2009 to 84 percent in 2010-2019 to 80 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
