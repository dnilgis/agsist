Atchison County, Kansas

Atchison County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $131 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,435 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Atchison County runs $131 per acre for 2026, up 34.4 percent from 2016. Land value carries the same direction: the census figure reached $4,435 per acre in 2022, up 45.9 percent from 2017, a rate of 7.8 percent a year. Cropland is 1 percent irrigated, so heat and drought carry the risk here: that cause has held 60 percent of all indemnities since 1989, and its share by period reads 26 percent, 66 percent, 61 percent, 69 percent. The loss ratio for the whole record is 0.44, against 0.12 for the closed years 2016-2025. Drought weeks stayed low recently, 13 since 2021, against 118 total since 2000. No solar or wind capacity is built or proposed here.
