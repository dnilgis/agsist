Cash rent in Riley County runs $88 an acre for 2026, up 16.7% from $75 in 2016. That puts it 13th highest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,371 an acre in 2022, up 11.5% from $3,022 in 2017. Corn made 163.4 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 151.7 bu.
