Riley County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $5,900 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.35 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 44.55 inches, 131% of normal (33.99 inches) through Nov 18, at Manhattan. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,357 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).
