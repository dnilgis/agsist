Cropland rent in Leavenworth County runs $86 an acre for 2026, against a state median of $58, which puts it 15th highest of 102. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,077 an acre in 2022, up 48.4% from $3,421 in 2017 and 3rd highest of 104 in Kansas. The 14-yr corn trend sits at 170.8 bu for 2026, highest of 20 counties reporting. 43% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
