Leavenworth County, Kansas

Rain is at 126% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $9,384 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.70 per base acre for corn.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.20 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.29, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 43.2%.
