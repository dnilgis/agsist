Corn yield, all practices, 2024: the lowest of 11 published years since 2009.

USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cash rent in Hamilton County runs $34 an acre in 2026, up 21.4% from $28 in 2016 and 5th lowest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $1,478 an acre in 2022, up 38.6% from $1,066 in 2017, which still ranks 6th lowest of 104. Corn made 66.7 bu in 2024, well under the 106.8 bu median for the years on record.
