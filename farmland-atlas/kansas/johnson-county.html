Cropland in Johnson County rents for $71 an acre in 2026, up 10.2% from $64 in 2014. That rent is tied for 28th highest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings ran $6,558 an acre in 2022, the highest of 104, and up 73.8% from $3,773 in 2017. 39% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
