Ellsworth County is a dryland county where drought has become the main thing paying claims. Heat and drought went from 12 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 89 percent in 2020-2025, and the closed crop years 2016-2025 show a loss ratio of 1.01, so insurers paid out slightly more than farmers paid in premium. Non-irrigated cash rent is $46 per acre for 2026, down 5.2 percent since 2014, while the census puts land and buildings at $2,106 per acre in 2022. Most of the irrigation water comes from groundwater, 91 percent in 2015.
