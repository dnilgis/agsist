Cropland rent in Montgomery County runs $68 an acre for 2026, up 61.9% from $42 in 2014 and 35th highest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,685 an acre in 2022, up 33.6% from $2,010 in 2017. Corn trends at 120.1 bu for 2026, about even with the state median of 120.3 bu, and 37% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
