Bourbon County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $4,178 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.24 per base acre for corn.

Bourbon County vs Kansas: $63.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 60% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 40th highest of 102).

Quick answers for Bourbon County

What is cash rent in Bourbon County, Kansas?

The county average is $64 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bourbon County, Kansas?

Land and buildings were worth $2,844 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Bourbon County, Kansas?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 123.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 13 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
