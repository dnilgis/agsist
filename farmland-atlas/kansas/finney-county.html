Finney County, Kansas

Finney County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,333 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland in Finney County rents for $210 per acre in 2026, non-irrigated for $50 per acre, a multiple of 4.24; in 2016 the multiple was 3.97, with no clear direction over that span. Non-irrigated rent is up 41.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Groundwater supplies 100 percent of irrigation water, drawn through 2666 irrigation points of diversion with a median priority year of 1972, and 37 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $348.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 70 percent; that share was 12 percent in 1989-1999, 53 percent in 2000-2009, 78 percent in 2010-2019, and 71 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value reached $2,333 per acre in 2022, up 49.1 percent from 2017.
