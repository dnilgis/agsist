Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 12 surveys since 2009; up 41% from $35 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent on Finney County cropland is $50 an acre for 2026, up 41.4% from $35 in 2016 and tied for 32nd lowest of 102 in Kansas. Irrigated ground tells a different story at $210 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 28 against a state median of $159. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,333 an acre in 2022, up 49.1% from $1,565 in 2017. Renters work 62% of the farmland here.
