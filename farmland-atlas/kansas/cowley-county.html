Non-irrigated cash rent for Cowley County stands at $52 per acre for 2026, up 13 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so the corn yield trend, up 2.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, rests on rainfall. That exposure shows in the insurance record: heat and drought caused 37 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 22 percent in 2000-2009, 78 percent in 2010-2019, and 85 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 74 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 210 such weeks across 2000-2025. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, reached $2,281 per acre in 2022, a rise of 23.4 percent from 2017. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
