Cowley County, Kansas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $3,145 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.98 per base acre for corn.

Cowley County vs Kansas: $52 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 36% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (37th lowest of 102).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

84.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 31.41 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 208 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,532 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.94, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 34% of the median yield. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.7 days a year and damaging wind on 5.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 71% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 17.3%.
