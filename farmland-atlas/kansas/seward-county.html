Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 35% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Seward County runs $37 an acre in 2026, up 34.5% from $28 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 102 in Kansas against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,215 an acre in 2022, up 57.1% from $1,410 in 2017, which sits close to the state median of $2,272. Corn made 181.1 bu in 2023, under the 2019-23 average of 197.5 bu. Renters farm 64% of the ground.
