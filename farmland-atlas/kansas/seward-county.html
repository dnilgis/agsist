Seward County, Kansas

Seward County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,215 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 185.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20175. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent runs $137 per acre against $37 per acre non-irrigated in Seward County, and 54 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, all of it from groundwater. Non-irrigated rent is up 34.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought account for 57 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that cause's share by period runs 26 percent, 42 percent, 66 percent, then 56 percent. The county spent 385 of 1357 weeks since 2000 in severe drought or worse, with 81 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Water rights carry a median priority year of 1976 across 1004 irrigation points of diversion. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with none proposed to EIA.
