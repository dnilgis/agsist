Cash rent in Dickinson County runs $62 an acre for 2026, up 21.6% from $51 in 2016 and 45th highest of 102 against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $2,714 an acre in 2022, up 26.8% from $2,140 in 2017 and 31st highest of 104. Corn made 133.0 bu in 2024, a bit over the 2020-24 average of 126.8 bu.
