Dickinson County, Kansas

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dickinson County is $62 per acre for 2026, up 21.6 percent from 2016. Cropland here is mostly rain-fed: only 2 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, so land value and yield outcomes ride on rainfall and heat more than on water rights. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved up through each listed period: 17 percent in 1989-1999, 54 percent in 2000-2009, 75 percent in 2010-2019, 93 percent in 2020-2025. The county's corn yield trend is 0.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2009-2024, with a median of 127.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 63.3 in 2011. Census land value stands at $2,714 per acre for 2022, up 26.8 percent from 2017.
