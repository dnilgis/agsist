Marshall County, Kansas

Rain is at 135% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $6,710 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.90 per base acre for corn.

Marshall County vs Kansas: $126 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 96% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (5th highest of 102).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.42 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 135% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,208 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.47 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.34, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 57% of the median yield. 1.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 69.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.4%.
