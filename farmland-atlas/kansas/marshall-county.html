Marshall County, Kansas

Marshall County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $126 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,880 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 132.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20117. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Marshall County runs $126 per acre, up 22.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Cropland here is mostly rain-fed: only 2 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. Heat and drought account for 69 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and that share has moved higher across recent decades: 19 percent in 1989-1999, 79 percent in 2000-2009, 70 percent in 2010-2019, 79 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 closed crop years is 0.34, lower than the 0.46 ratio over the full 1989-2025 period. Census land value stood at $3,880 per acre in 2022, up 25.4 percent from 2017 to 2022.
