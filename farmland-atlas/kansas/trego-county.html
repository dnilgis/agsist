Cropland in Trego County rents for $41 an acre in 2026, up 30.2% from $32 in 2016. That is tied for 13th lowest of 102 in Kansas, under the state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,600 an acre in 2022, up 40.2% from $1,141 in 2017 and 10th lowest of 104. 54% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
