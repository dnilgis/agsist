Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 100.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 159 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.38 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.82, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 82% of the median yield. 57.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.7 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.2%.
