Pottawatomie County, Kansas

Pottawatomie County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $98 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,188 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20149. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pottawatomie County averages $98 per acre in 2026, up 30.9 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census sits at $3,188 per acre, up 29.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 1.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 147 bushels and a worst year of 101.5 in 2012. Heat and drought drive 72 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share has moved from 22 percent in 1989-1999, to 72 percent in 2000-2009, to 75 percent in 2010-2019, to 86 percent in 2020-2025. The county carries 765 active water diversion points, 440 for irrigation, with a median priority year of 1979.
