USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Pottawatomie County runs $98 an acre for 2026, up 30.9% from $75 in 2016, and that sits 9th highest of 102 against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $3,188 an acre in 2022, up 29.5% from $2,461 in 2017. Corn came in at 168.9 bu in 2025, above the county median of 147.0 bu since 2008.
