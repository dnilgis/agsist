Cash rent in Barton County runs $45 an acre for 2026, down 6.3% from $48 in 2016 and 20th lowest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. The census value of land and buildings was $1,821 an acre in 2022, up 16.0% from $1,570 in 2017. Corn made 150.6 bu in 2025, a touch over the long median of 150.2 bu. Renters farm 61% of the ground here.
