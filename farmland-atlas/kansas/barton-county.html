Barton County, Kansas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $2,788 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book.

Barton County vs Kansas: $44.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 19% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (20th lowest of 102).

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

25.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 22.15 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.37 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.94, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 82% of the median yield. 9.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 73.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.9%.
