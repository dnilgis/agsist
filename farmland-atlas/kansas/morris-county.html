Morris County, Kansas

Non-irrigated cash rent in Morris County stands at $62 per acre for 2026, up 24.2 percent from 2016. All harvested cropland here is non-irrigated, per the 2022 census, so rent and yield both ride on rainfall and heat. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $62.2 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 84 percent. That share has moved through the periods: 22 percent in 1989-1999, 74 percent in 2000-2009, 89 percent in 2010-2019, 95 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value reached $2,161 per acre in 2022, up 33.2 percent from 2017.
