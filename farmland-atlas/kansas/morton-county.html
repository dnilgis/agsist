Morton County cash rent stood at $30 an acre in 2026, the lowest of 102 in Kansas against a state median of $58. That rent was $24 in 2016, up 25.5% over ten years. The census value of land and buildings was $1,234 an acre in 2022, up 20.5% from $1,024 in 2017 and the lowest of 104 in the state. Corn has run a median of 157.2 bu over 14 years, and 49% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
