Haskell County, Kansas

As of Oct 16, Haskell County cash rent ran $41 an acre in 2026, tied for 13th lowest of 102 in Kansas against a state median of $58. That is up 7.9% from $38 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,527 an acre in 2022, up 65.5% from $1,527 in 2017 and above the state median of $2,272. In 2022, 76% of the farmland here was rented.
