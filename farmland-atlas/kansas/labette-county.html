Labette County, Kansas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,412 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.91 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

73.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 33.15 inches, 77% of normal (43.2 inches) through Nov 16, at Mound Valley, 3 mi WSW. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,593 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,412 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $61 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 34.1% from 2014 to 2026. 44.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 209.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

County median corn basis: 30 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.05 at Faulkner, 17 miles. 6 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 30 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.91 per base acre, PLC $2.34.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (93.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $43.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 61 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.65 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.02 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 31% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 58.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 35.3%.

Labette County's notable number is the crop insurance record. Indemnities have paid out more than premiums over the full closed period, with a loss ratio of 1.08 for 1989-2025 and 6 of 22 crop years paying more than premium. Heat and drought went from 36 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 68 percent in 2020-2025, and the worst corn year, 2022, was 27.7 bushels per acre. Non-irrigated cash rent is $61 per acre for 2026, up 34.1 percent from 2014, against a 2022 census land value of $2,412 per acre.
