Labette County, Kansas

Labette County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $61 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,412 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 89.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20099. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Labette County stands at $61 per acre in 2026, up 34.1 percent from 2014. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, was $2,412 per acre in 2022, up 13.7 percent from 2017. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $128.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 59 percent; that share was 36 percent in 1989-1999, 19 percent in 2000-2009, 63 percent in 2010-2019, and 68 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 61 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 171 of 1357 weeks for the full 2000-2025 period. Corn yield trend runs 0.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, with a median of 89.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 27.7 in 2022.
