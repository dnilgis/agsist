Jewell County, Kansas

Jewell County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $83 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,492 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20089. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jewell County runs $82 per acre in 2026, up 10 percent from 2016. Land value under the census stands at $2,492 per acre, up 21 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.9 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 73 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share has moved higher across the recent decades: 17 percent in 1989-1999, 59 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, 89 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for the ten closed years 2016-2025 was 0.88, against 0.68 for the full 1989-2025 record.
