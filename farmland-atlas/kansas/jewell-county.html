Cash rent in Jewell County runs $83 an acre for 2026, 17th highest of 102 counties in Kansas and well over the state median of $58. That rent is up 10.0% from $75 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,492 an acre in 2022, up 21.0% from $2,060 in 2017. 59% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
