Non-irrigated cash rent in Rice County runs $56 per acre in 2026, up 14.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $164 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated has moved from 1.93 in 2012 to 2.90 in 2026, direction rising. All irrigation water here comes from groundwater, share 100 percent in 2015, with 686 irrigation points of diversion carrying a median priority year of 1985. Heat and drought has taken a growing share of crop insurance indemnities across the periods 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025: 14 percent, 27 percent, 72 percent, 87 percent. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with none proposed to EIA.
