Cash rent in Rice County runs $57 an acre for 2026, up 14.1% from $50 in 2016 and tied for 48th lowest of 102 in Kansas. The census value of land and buildings was $2,366 an acre in 2022, up 16.2% from $2,036 in 2017 and a bit over the state median of $2,272. Plenty of the ground is farmed by renters, with 62% of farmland rented in 2022.
