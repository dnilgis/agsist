Cloud County, Kansas

Cloud County, KS: non-irrigated cash rent $115 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,142 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 20029. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cloud County runs $115 per acre for 2026; irrigated land commands $236 per acre. Heat and drought drove 71 percent of $153.9 million in indemnities since 1989, and that cause's share climbed from 12 percent in 1989-1999 to 58 percent in 2000-2009, 53 percent in 2010-2019, and 91 percent in 2020-2025. Only 8 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed crop years was 1.14. Census land value stood at $3,142 per acre in 2022, up 36.5 percent from 2017.
