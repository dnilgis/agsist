Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 14.4% from $87 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Republic County ran $100 an acre in 2026, 7th highest of 102 in Kansas, against a state median of $58. That is up 14.4% from $87 in 2016. Irrigated rent was $237 an acre in 2026, highest of 28. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,259 an acre in 2022, up 26.0% from $2,586 in 2017.
