Republic County, Kansas

Rain is at 125% of normal. Non-irrigated cropland sold for an average $4,454 an acre in 2025, by the Kansas Land Values book. ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.70 per base acre for corn.

Republic County vs Kansas: $99.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 94% of the other 101 Kansas counties with a 2026 rent (7th highest of 102).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.51 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.74, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.9%.
