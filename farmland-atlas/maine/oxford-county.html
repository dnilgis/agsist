Oxford County, Maine

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,166 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.55 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 33.69 inches, 75% of normal (44.88 inches) through Nov 17, at Hartford. Frost-free season: 145 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,939 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,166 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $24 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 11.1% from 2014 to 2026. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 28.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,902 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.55 per base acre, PLC $3.15.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (125.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $35.10 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.10 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.29 over the same years. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 9.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.5%.

Stands out against its own record

19 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, more than any full year since 2000.

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Oxford County cash rent sits at $24 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 10 in Maine and under the state median of $51. That rent is down 11.1% from $27 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,166 an acre in 2022, up 8.6% from $2,914 in 2017, or 1.7% a year. That still ranks 5th lowest of 16, below the state median of $4,123, and only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
