Aroostook County, Maine

Aroostook County, ME: non-irrigated cash rent $108 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,403 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 23003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Aroostook County is $108 per acre in 2026, up 46.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $2,403 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 of 38 percent, 6.7 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value stood at 3.56 percent. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $147.4 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 61 percent of that total. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 30 percent in 1989-1999 to 8 percent in 2000-2009, 14 percent in 2010-2019, and 20 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.57, below the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.74.
