Stands out against its own record

$108

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 46.9% from $74 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Aroostook County runs $108 an acre for 2026, the highest of 10 in Maine and well over the state median of $51. That rent is up 46.9% from $74 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,403 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from $1,741 in 2017, yet it still sits 3rd lowest of 16 against a state median of $4,123. Only 21% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
