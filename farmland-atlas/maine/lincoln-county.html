Lincoln County, Maine

Lincoln County, ME: non-irrigated cash rent $45 an acre (2024); land and buildings $6,410 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 23015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lincoln County is $45 per acre. Census market value of land and buildings, 2022, is $6,410 per acre. Land value change 2017-2022 is 38 percent, 6.7 percent a year. The July nightly low averaged 60.5 F for 2017-2026, up from 58.6 F for the 1991-2020 normal, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.86 F per decade, warming. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 74 of 1357 weeks, 2000-2025, with 28 weeks in 2021-2025. Solar operating capacity is 12.5 MW; wind operating capacity is 0 MW.
