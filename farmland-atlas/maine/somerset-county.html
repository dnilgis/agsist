Cropland rent in Somerset County runs $26 an acre in 2026, up 10.9% from $23 in 2016 and 2nd lowest of 10 in Maine, where the state median is $51. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,253 an acre in 2022, up 52.6% from $1,476 in 2017. That is still 2nd lowest of 16 against a state median of $4,123. 24% of farmland here was rented in 2022.

Sources and terms

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
