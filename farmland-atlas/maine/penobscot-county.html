Stands out against its own record

$53

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2010; up 52.2% from $35 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Penobscot County runs $53 an acre for 2026, up 52.2% from $35 in 2016 and just over the state median of $51. That puts it 5th highest of 10 in Maine. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,836 an acre in 2022, up 55.6% from $2,466 in 2017, or 9.2% a year. Only 14% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
