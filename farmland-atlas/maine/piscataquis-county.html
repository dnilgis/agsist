The census value of land and buildings in Piscataquis County was $2,593 an acre in 2022, up 84.2% from $1,408 in 2017. That works out to 13.0% a year, and it still ranks 4th lowest of 16 in Maine, under the state median of $4,123. Cash rent was $43 an acre in 2021, and 11% of farmland was rented in 2022.
