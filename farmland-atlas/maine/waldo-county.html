Non-irrigated cash rent in Waldo County runs $28 per acre, up 31 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $4,188 per acre, up 39.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, a 6.9 percent a year pace. July nights average 59.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 57.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.78 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance paid $127,860 in indemnities across 2000-2025 closed crop years, with heat and drought the largest cause at 40 percent, and drought weeks ran higher in 2021-2025 than the 2000-2025 average of 66 of 1357 weeks.
