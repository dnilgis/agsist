Androscoggin County, Maine

Androscoggin County, ME: non-irrigated cash rent $44 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,591 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 23001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Androscoggin County runs $44 per acre, up 42.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value in 2022 stood at $5,591 per acre, up 58.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 9.7 percent a year. July nights have warmed to a 59.9 F average low for 2017-2026 against a 58.2 F normal, with a trend of 0.84 F per decade since 1976. Freeze and frost, not heat, caused 31 percent of the $4.3 million in indemnities paid over 1989-2025.
