U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Androscoggin County runs $44 an acre in 2026, up 42.6% from $31 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $51 and ranks 4th lowest of 10. The census value of land and buildings was $5,591 an acre in 2022, up 58.6% from $3,526 in 2017, and ranks 5th highest of 16. Only 11% of farmland was rented in 2022.
