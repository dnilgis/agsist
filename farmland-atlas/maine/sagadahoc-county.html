Sagadahoc County, Maine

Sagadahoc County, ME: land and buildings $4,880 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 23023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land in Sagadahoc County is valued at $4,880 per acre, with a change of 18 percent from 2017-2022, or 3.4 percent a year. July nights average 60.8 F for 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 59 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.9 F per decade. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought weeks at severe or worse ran 73 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 19 weeks, and 26 weeks from 2021-2025.
