Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County runs $72 per acre. Land value stands at $3,583 per acre, with a change of 44.2 percent from 2017-2022. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 53.8 F to 55.3 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976. Drought has touched the county in 70 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2025 the worst year at 18 weeks and 29 weeks logged in 2021-2025 alone. Freeze and frost drove crop insurance claims from 1995-2014 at 41 percent, while heat and drought's share of indemnities rose from 0 percent in 2000-2009 to 8 percent in 2010-2019.
