Cash rent in Franklin County runs $72 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 10 in Maine and above the state median of $51. The census value of land and buildings was $3,583 an acre in 2022, up 44.2% from $2,484 in 2017. That still ranks 6th lowest of 16 against a state median of $4,123. Only 9% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
