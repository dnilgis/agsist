Land in Knox County is valued at $5,875 per acre, up 24.3 percent from 2017-2022, or 4.5 percent a year. July nights average 59.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 58.2 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.78 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland. Severe drought or worse held at least 50 percent of the county for 77 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2025 the worst year at 20 weeks; from 2021-2025 that count was 28 weeks. Solar operating stands at 11.5 MW and wind at 4.5 MW, with 74.5 MW solar and 0 MW wind proposed to EIA.
