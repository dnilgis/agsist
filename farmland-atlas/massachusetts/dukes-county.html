Dukes County had 6,404 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dukes County is $51 per acre in 2026. Irrigated cash rent was $86 per acre in 2021. July average nightly low, 2017-2026, is 65.4 F, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 63.9 F, with a warming trend of 0.82 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 38 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2020 the worst year at 13 weeks, and 12 weeks from 2021-2025.
