Worcester County, Massachusetts

Rain is at 90% of normal. Southern New England region (27 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: cropland sold for $6,500 to $25,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.94 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.56 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 168 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,456 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

PRF grazing county base value: $46.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.15 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.11, 2016 to 2025. 6.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 6.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 11.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 132.7% from $26 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Worcester County runs $61 an acre for 2026, up 132.7% from $26 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $78 and ranks 4th lowest of 11. The census value of land and buildings was $15,447 an acre in 2022, up 25.6% from $12,297 in 2017, and ranks 4th highest of 9 in the state. Only 13% of farmland was rented in 2022.
