Non-irrigated cash rent in Berkshire County runs $58 per acre in 2026, up 96.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $114 per acre, though only 2 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 8 percent in 2000-2009, to 18 percent in 2010-2019, to 41 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 1.35, above the 1.13 ratio for the full 1995-2025 period, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause of indemnities overall at 38 percent.
