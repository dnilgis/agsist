Barnstable County, Massachusetts

Barnstable County, MA: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $58,917 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 25001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Barnstable County had 3,956 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $38 per acre, but irrigated land rents at $433 per acre. Cropland leans on that irrigation: 87 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with 83 percent of that water coming from groundwater as of 2015. Land value climbed 92.8 percent over 2017-2022, 14 percent a year, and the operators' own 2022 estimate puts land and buildings at $58,917 per acre. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 6 percent in 1989-1999, to 5 percent in 2000-2009, to 12 percent in 2010-2019, to 23 percent in 2020-2025.
