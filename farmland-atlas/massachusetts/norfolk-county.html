Norfolk County, Massachusetts

Norfolk County, MA: non-irrigated cash rent $73 an acre (2021); land and buildings $20,134 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 25021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Norfolk County had 9,435 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Norfolk County ran $73 per acre in 2021, and irrigated cash rent reached $451 per acre by 2026. Land value in the Census stood at $20,134 per acre in 2022, up 17.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.3 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1996-2025 totaled $167,045, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 29 percent. July nights have warmed since 1976 at 0.94 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average nightly low of 64 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 62.2 F; drought held at 4.2 percent of weeks from 2000-2025, with 2016 the worst year at 22 weeks and 18 weeks logged from 2021-2025.
