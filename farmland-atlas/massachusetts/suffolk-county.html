Suffolk County, Massachusetts

Suffolk County, MA: land and buildings $52,340 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 25025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Suffolk County had 206 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nightly lows average 64.7 F for 2017-2026, up from the 63.3 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.89 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Drought was rare here: 63 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 21 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 and 2016 the worst year at 23 weeks.
