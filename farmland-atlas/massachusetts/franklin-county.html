Franklin County, Massachusetts

Franklin County, MA: non-irrigated cash rent $141 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,637 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 25011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County runs $141 per acre in 2026, up 66.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent is $331 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent stands at 2.35 in 2026, against 2.60 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Only 9 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. July nights have warmed to a 60.7 F average low over 2017-2026, up from a 58.6 F normal, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.92 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched the county in short stretches, with 14 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025 out of 60 such weeks total from 2000-2025.
