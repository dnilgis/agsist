Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 66.9% from $85 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Franklin County cash rent stood at $141 an acre in 2026, highest of 11 in Massachusetts and well above the $78 state median. That rent is up 66.9% from $85 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,637 an acre in 2022, up 34.6% from $6,419 in 2017, yet still 2nd lowest of 9 against a $14,377 state median.
