Nantucket County, Massachusetts

Nantucket County, MA: land and buildings $14,302 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 25019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Nantucket County had 911 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low, 2017-2026 stands at 64.7 F, up from the July average nightly low, 1991-2020 normal of 62.8 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 is 0.79 F per decade, direction warming, though no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Severe drought or worse held at least 50 percent of the county for 11 of the weeks in 2021-2025, close to the 11 weeks logged in 2020 alone.
