Rain is at 86% of normal. Southern New England region (27 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: cropland sold for $6,500 to $25,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.00 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Cropland rent in Hampshire County runs $129 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 11 in Massachusetts and well over the state median of $78. That rent is up 95.5% from $66 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,568 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $7,662 in 2017, yet still 3rd lowest of 9 against a state median of $14,377. Only 15% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
