Cropland rent in Hampshire County runs $129 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 11 in Massachusetts and well over the state median of $78. That rent is up 95.5% from $66 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,568 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $7,662 in 2017, yet still 3rd lowest of 9 against a state median of $14,377. Only 15% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
