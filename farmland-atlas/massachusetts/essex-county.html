Essex County, Massachusetts

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Southern New England region (27 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: cropland sold for $6,500 to $25,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.02 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.10 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,497 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.02 per base acre, PLC $2.73.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (108.86 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $71.15 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $24.06 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $46.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

13.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 40.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 27%.
