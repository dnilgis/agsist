Hampden County, Massachusetts

Rain is at 86% of normal. Southern New England region (27 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: cropland sold for $6,500 to $25,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.95 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 37.09 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,534 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.95 per base acre, PLC $3.13.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (124.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $46.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.28 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.12, 2016 to 2025. 16.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 5.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1991 to 2025; too much rain caused 44.9%.
