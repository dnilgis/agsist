Hampden County, Massachusetts

Hampden County, MA: non-irrigated cash rent $130 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,611 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 25013. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 averages $130 per acre. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $13,611 per acre, up 31.6 percent from 2017, 5.6 percent a year. July nights have warmed from a 59.9 F normal to 61.8 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.87 F per decade warming trend since 1976, though no July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has stayed limited, with severe drought or worse in only 77 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 20 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025; crop insurance losses in the county have run mostly to excess moisture and flood, at 45 percent of $34.9 million paid out since 1991.
