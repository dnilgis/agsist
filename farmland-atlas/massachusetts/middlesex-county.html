Middlesex County, Massachusetts

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Southern New England region (27 counties), ASFMRA Northeast 2026: cropland sold for $6,500 to $25,000 an acre in 2025 sales (Less Sales). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.02 per base acre for corn.

Middlesex County vs Massachusetts: $64.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 40% of the other 10 Massachusetts counties with a 2026 rent (5th lowest of 11).

Quick answers for Middlesex County

What is cash rent in Middlesex County, Massachusetts?

The county average is $65 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $122 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Middlesex County, Massachusetts?

Land and buildings were worth $22,138 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
