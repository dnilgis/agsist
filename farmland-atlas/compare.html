Compare counties

Up to three counties, side by side. Every figure names its survey year and source on the county page. A dash is a figure the source withheld or the record does not carry, never a zero. Nothing is added into a score and no county is declared the winner.

Pick a county from the list to add it.

On a phone, swipe the table sideways to see every county.

Rents are USDA NASS cash rent survey county averages. Land value is the Census of Agriculture, operators' own estimate of land and buildings, not a sale price. A land value that moved against its neighbours since 2017 is withheld here and read with care on the county page. Claims are RMA crop insurance indemnity per $100 of liability over the last ten closed crop years. Columns, units and sources. Link ends in the counties you picked, so you can share it.
