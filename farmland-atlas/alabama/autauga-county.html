As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Autauga County ran $51 an acre in 2026, up 50.7% from $34 in 2016 and 24th highest of 59 in Alabama. Pasture rented for $33 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $2,636 an acre in 2022, up 19.5% from $2,205 in 2017, and that sits 18th lowest of 67 in the state. 34% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
