U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cash rent in Escambia County runs $101 an acre for 2026, up 18.1% from $86 in 2016, and that is 4th highest of 59 counties against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,763 an acre in 2022, up 12.4% from 2017 and 22nd lowest of 67 in the state. Corn made 146.5 bu in 2025, a bit above the county median of 141.8 bu since 2009.
