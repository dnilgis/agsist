Escambia County, Alabama

Escambia County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $101 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,763 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 141.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Escambia County runs $101 per acre, up 18.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census value of land and buildings sits at $2,763 per acre, with that value up 12.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 6 bushels per acre a year across 2009-2025, but the worst year in that span, 2011, hit only 45.7 bushels against a median of 141.8. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 38 percent, and its share has moved 33 percent, 25 percent, 41 percent, 47 percent across the four decades listed. July nights average 72.6 F for 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 70.9 F. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
