Fayette County, Alabama

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,541 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.91 per base acre for corn.

Fayette County vs Alabama: $46.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 52% of the other 58 Alabama counties with a 2026 rent (29th highest of 59).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 45.28 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 226 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,771 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,541 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $46.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.65 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 106.7% from 2016 to 2026. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 18% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 88.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 10 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,392 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.91 per base acre, PLC $2.09.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (83.15 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $12.36 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.38 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $50.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.35 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.47, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 44.8%.
