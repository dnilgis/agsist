Cash rent in Hale County runs $53 an acre for 2026, up 56.7% from $34 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $46 and ranks 22nd highest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $2,543 an acre in 2022, up 12.5% from $2,260 in 2017, and ranks 13th lowest of 67 against a state median of $3,400. 26% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
