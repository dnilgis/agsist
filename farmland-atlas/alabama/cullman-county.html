Cash rent in Cullman County runs $48 an acre in 2026, up 14.5% from $42 in 2016 and a bit over the state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $5,356 an acre in 2022, up 34.7% from $3,977 in 2017, and it ranks 8th highest of 67 in Alabama. Corn does fine here too, with a projected 2026 trend yield of 161.7 bu that ranks 3rd highest of 23.
