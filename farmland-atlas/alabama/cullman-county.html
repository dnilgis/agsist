Non-irrigated cash rent in Cullman County averages $48 per acre, up 14.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Cropland is 1 percent irrigated, so yields track weather closely. The July average nightly low has moved from 68.6 F (1991-2020 normal) to 70.5 F (2017-2026), and heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities ran 24 percent in 1989-1999, 9 percent in 2000-2009, 76 percent in 2010-2019, and 82 percent in 2020-2025. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 3.1 bushels per acre a year across 2009-2025, with a median of 157.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 90.9 in 2010.
