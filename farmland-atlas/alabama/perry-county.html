Cash rent in Perry County runs $46 an acre in 2025, up 40.0% from $33 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,354 an acre in 2022, up 6.6% from 2017, and sits 6th lowest of 67 against a state median of $3,400. Corn made 141.6 bu in 2024, well above the 14 year trend of 116.4 bu.
