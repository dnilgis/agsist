Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 243 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,892 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,002 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 20% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 11.1% from 2016 to 2026. 18% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 196.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 84,514 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Quick answers for Crenshaw County

What is cash rent in Crenshaw County, Alabama?

The county average is $30 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Crenshaw County, Alabama?

Land and buildings were worth $3,002 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
