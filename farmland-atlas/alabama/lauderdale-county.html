Cropland in Lauderdale County rents for $79 an acre in 2026, up 12.9% from $70 in 2016 and 8th highest of 59 in Alabama against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $5,183 an acre in 2022, up 42.9% from $3,626 in 2017, which runs 7.4% a year. Corn trends at 144.9 bu, 8th highest of 23, and 48% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
