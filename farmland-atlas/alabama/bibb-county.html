Cropland rent in Bibb County runs $24 an acre for 2026, up 140.0% from $10 in 2014. That still sits under the state median of $46 and is tied for 5th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $2,611 an acre in 2022, down 7.0% from $2,807 in 2017, and 15th lowest of 67. Only 13% of farmland was rented in 2022.
