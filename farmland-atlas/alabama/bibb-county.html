Bibb County, Alabama

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,611 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Bibb County vs Alabama: $24 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 7% of the other 58 Alabama counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 5th lowest of 59).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

56.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 235 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,822 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,611 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $24 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.68 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 9% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 140% from 2014 to 2026. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 12% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 125.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 51,963 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $50.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 58.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 31.3%.

Quick answers for Bibb County

What is cash rent in Bibb County, Alabama?

The county average is $24 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bibb County, Alabama?

Land and buildings were worth $2,611 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
