Cropland rent in Chilton County runs $25 an acre for 2026, down 12.5% from $28 in 2016. That sits 7th lowest of 59 in Alabama, well under the state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $3,464 an acre in 2022, down 1.8% from $3,529 in 2017 and 32nd highest of 67. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
