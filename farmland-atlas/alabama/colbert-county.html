Cash rent in Colbert County runs $105 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 59 in Alabama against a state median of $46. That rent is up 19.3% from $88 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,528 an acre in 2022, up 32.4% from $2,665 in 2017, and it sits 28th highest of 67 in the state. Corn last year made 157.5 bu, above the 14-yr trend of 141.8 bu.
