$60

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 15.4% from $52 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

DeKalb County cash rent sits at $60 an acre for 2026, up 15.4% from $52 in 2016 and 16th highest of 59 in Alabama. The census value of land and buildings was $5,032 an acre in 2022, up 27.9% from $3,935 in 2017, which ranks 12th highest of 67. Corn holds up well here too, with a 14 year trend of 159.9 bu that is 4th highest of 23.
