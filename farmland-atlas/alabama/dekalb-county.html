Non-irrigated cash rent in DeKalb County averages $60 per acre in 2026, up 15.4 percent from 2016. Land value carries $5,032 per acre in the 2022 census, an operator estimate rather than a sale price, and this rose 27.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield trend runs 3.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 142.4 bushels per acre and a low of 71 in 2008. Heat and drought caused 61 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 60 percent in 2000-2009, 60 percent in 2010-2019, and 47 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
