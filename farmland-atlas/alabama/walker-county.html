Walker County, Alabama

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,512 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.52 per base acre for corn.

Walker County vs Alabama: $24 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 7% of the other 58 Alabama counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 5th lowest of 59).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

22.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.92 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 226 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,765 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,512 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $24 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 11.1% from 2016 to 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 98.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 20,490 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.52 per base acre, PLC $1.17.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (46.54 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $59.87 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $10.29 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $50.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 59% of the median yield. 1.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 2016 to 2025; too much rain caused 32.9%.

Walker County is a non-irrigated county, with only 2 percent of harvested cropland under irrigation, so the rain it gets is the crop's water. Heat and drought are the story in the insurance claims: they were 9 percent of indemnities over 2010-2019 and 69 percent over 2020-2025, and they are the largest cause over 2016-2025 at 37 percent. Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $24 per acre, down 11.1 percent from 2016. The median corn yield for 2013-2025 is 123.8 bushels per acre, with 2024 the worst year at 73.1. The July nightly low has been warming at 0.39 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 70.6 F is above the 1991-2020 normal of 69.6 F.

Quick answers for Walker County

What is cash rent in Walker County, Alabama?

The county average is $24 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Walker County, Alabama?

Land and buildings were worth $3,512 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Walker County, Alabama?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 89.6 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
