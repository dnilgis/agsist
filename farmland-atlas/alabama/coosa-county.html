Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,950 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.66 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 90.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 88,012 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Cash rent in Coosa County ran $16 an acre in 2021, down 20.5% from $20 in 2011. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,950 an acre in 2022, up 28.7% from $2,292 in 2017, or 5.2% a year. That sits under the state median of $3,400 and ranks 24th lowest of 67. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
