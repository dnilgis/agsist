Randolph County, Alabama

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,005 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.61 per base acre for corn.

Randolph County vs Alabama: $25.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 12% of the other 58 Alabama counties with a 2026 rent (8th lowest of 59).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

9.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.40 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 226 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,586 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,005 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $25.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.70 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 2% from 2016 to 2026. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 235.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 18,229 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.61 per base acre, PLC $1.94.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (77.41 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $9.91 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.11 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (15.56 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $50.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 61% of the median yield. 5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 2002 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.5%.

Cropland rent in Randolph County runs $26 an acre in 2026, up 2.0% from $25 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 59 in Alabama against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,005 an acre in 2022, up 26.9% from $3,157 in 2017 and 19th highest of 67. Only 12% of farmland was rented in 2022. Rent has barely moved in ten years while the census value climbed.
