Cropland rent in Morgan County runs $65 an acre for 2026, up 132.1% from $28 in 2016 and 12th highest of 59 in Alabama. The census value of land and buildings was $6,130 an acre in 2022, up 39.2% from $4,403 in 2017 and 4th highest of 67 in the state. Corn on the 14-yr trend projects 154.9 bu for 2026, though the 2024 crop came in at 102.1 bu.
