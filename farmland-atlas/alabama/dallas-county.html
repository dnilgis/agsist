Cash rent in Dallas County runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 17.7% from $31 in 2016, and that sits tied for 19th lowest of 59 against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,402 an acre in 2022, up 28.1% from $1,875 in 2017, which ranks 9th lowest of 67. Corn made 140.4 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 129.8 bu.
