Cropland rent in Shelby County runs $42 an acre for 2026, down 9.8% from $46 in 2016 and tied for 26th lowest of 59 against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,118 an acre in 2022, up 28.7% from $3,976 in 2017 and 11th highest of 67. Corn made 135.3 bu in 2025, well over the 107.9 bu median.
