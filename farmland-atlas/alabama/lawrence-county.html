Lawrence County, Alabama

Lawrence County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $113 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,865 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01079. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lawrence County runs $113 per acre in 2026, up 11.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $152 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved without clear direction, 1.43 in 2009 to 1.35 in 2026. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so most ground here depends on rain. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has swung across periods, 32 percent in 1989-1999, 10 percent in 2000-2009, 67 percent in 2010-2019, and 54 percent in 2020-2025, with 152 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000. Census land value sits at $4,865 per acre, up 28.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, and 393 MW of solar has been proposed to EIA against none operating now.
