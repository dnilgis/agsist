Corn yields here have climbed 2.6 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2025, but heat and drought have been eating into insurance payouts. Those losses went from 32 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 67 percent in 2010-2019, then fell back to 54 percent in 2020-2025. Non-irrigated ground rented for $113 per acre in 2026, up 11.9 percent from 2016. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated here. Land value jumped 28.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, while July nighttime lows have warmed 0.41 F per decade since 1976.

The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out.
