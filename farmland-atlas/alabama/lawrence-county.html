Cash rent in Lawrence County runs $113 an acre in 2026, the 2nd highest of 59 in Alabama against a state median of $46. That is up 11.9% from $101 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,865 an acre in 2022, up 28.2% from $3,796 in 2017. Corn made 177.7 bu in 2025, above the county median of 157.2 bu since 2008.
