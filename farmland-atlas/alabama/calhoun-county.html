Cash rent in Calhoun County runs $65 an acre for 2026, up 95.5% from $33 in 2016, and 13th highest of 59 in Alabama. The census value of land and buildings was $5,855 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $4,246 in 2017, 5th highest of 67 in the state. Corn holds up well here too, with a 14-yr trend of 167.1 bu that sits 2nd highest of 23 in the state.
