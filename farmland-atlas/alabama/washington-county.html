Cash rent in Washington County ran $39 an acre in 2025, up 48.1% from $26 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,714 an acre in 2022, up 34.0% from $2,026 in 2017, or 6.0% a year. That sits under the state median of $3,400 and ranks 21st lowest of 67. Only 13% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
