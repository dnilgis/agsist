As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Cherokee County runs $95 an acre in 2026, 5th highest of 59 in Alabama and well over the state median of $46. That rent is up 12.5% from $84 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,646 an acre in 2022, up 15.1% from $3,167 in 2017. Corn made 121.5 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 137.7 bu.
