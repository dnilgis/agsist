Macon County, Alabama

Land and buildings run $2,687 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.86 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 248 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,927 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,687 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $41 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.65 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 29% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 13.9% from 2016 to 2026. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 72 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,905 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.86 per base acre, PLC $2.06.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (81.92 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $68.13 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.10 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $55.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.46 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.72, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25%.

Cash rent in Macon County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 13.9% from $36 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $46 and ranks 25th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,687 an acre in 2022, up 14.5% from $2,347 in 2017, still short of the $3,400 state median. Only 12% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
