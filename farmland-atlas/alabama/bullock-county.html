As of Oct 16, cash rent in Bullock County runs $33 an acre for 2025, up 10.0% from $30 in 2014. Pasture rents for $15 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $2,258 an acre in 2022, up 5.4% from 2017, and ranks 4th lowest of 67 in Alabama against a state median of $3,400. In 2022, 27% of the farmland was rented.
