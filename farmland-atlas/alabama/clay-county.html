Clay County, Alabama

Cropland rent in Clay County runs $18 an acre in 2026, the same $18 it was in 2016, and that sits 3rd lowest of 59 against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 41.8% from 2017 to $3,980 an acre in 2022, which ranks 20th highest of 67. 23% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
