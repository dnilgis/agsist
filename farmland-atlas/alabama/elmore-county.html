Cropland rent in Elmore County runs $45 an acre for 2026, up 15.6% from $39 in 2016 and just under the state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $3,839 an acre in 2022, up 28.1% from $2,996 in 2017, and that ranks 24th highest of 67 in Alabama. Corn trends low here, with a 14-yr trend of 104.9 bu that sits 3rd lowest of 23.
