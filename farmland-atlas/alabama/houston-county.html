Houston County, Alabama

Houston County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $61 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,685 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01069. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Houston County averages $61 per acre, up 38.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent runs $203 per acre in 2025, a multiple of 3.59 over non-irrigated, compared with 2.50 in 2009; the multiple shows no clear direction. Only 11 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 82 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $194.6 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 59 percent; that share was 52 percent in 1989-1999, 69 percent in 2000-2009, 57 percent in 2010-2019, and 57 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $3,685 per acre in 2022, up 4.3 percent from 2017 to 2022.
