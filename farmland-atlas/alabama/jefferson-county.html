As of Oct 16, cash rent in Jefferson County was $20 an acre in 2017, and pasture rent was $13 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,943 an acre in 2022, up 57.3% from $4,413 in 2017, or 9.5% a year. That value ranks highest of 67 in Alabama, against a state median of $3,400. Renters worked 19% of the farmland here in 2022.
