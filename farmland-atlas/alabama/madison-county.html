As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Madison County ran $113 an acre in 2025, up 69.9% from $67 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $6,510 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $5,306 in 2017. That ranks 2nd highest of 67 in Alabama, against a state median of $3,400. Corn made 181.0 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year county median of 158.7 bu.
