Madison County, Alabama

Madison County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $113 an acre (2025); land and buildings $6,510 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 158.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01089. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County averages $113 per acre in 2025, up 69.9 percent from 2014. Irrigated land rented for $131 per acre in 2024, and 14 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation, with 75 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought account for 52 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share has moved from 18 percent in 1989-1999 to 58 percent in 2000-2009 to 68 percent in 2010-2019 to 66 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows average 70.2 F over 2017-2026, up from the 68.6 F normal for 1991-2020, consistent with a warming trend of 0.65 F per decade since 1976. Census-reported land value stands at $6,510 per acre, up 22.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
