Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 50.0% from $41 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Jackson County runs $62 an acre for 2026, up 50.0% from $41 in 2016. That sits 14th highest of 59 in Alabama, where the state median is $46. The census value of land and buildings was $3,511 an acre in 2022, up 15.4% from $3,042 in 2017. Corn trends at 134.7 bu, 10th lowest of 23 against a state median of 138.8 bu.
