Non-irrigated cash rent in Tallapoosa County averages $21 per acre in 2024, down 8.7 percent from 2014. July nights average 69.7 F over 2017-2026, against a 68.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.53 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance indemnities for 1995-2025 closed crop years total $333,190, with heat and drought the largest cause at 57 percent, compared with 62 percent for 1989-1999. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 269 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, versus 5 weeks in 2021-2025. Census land and building value stands at $3,062 per acre, up 7.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
