Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 200.0% from $17 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Pickens County runs $50 an acre for 2026, up 200.0% from $17 in 2016, and that puts it tied for 25th highest of 59 against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $2,633 an acre in 2022, up 4.2% from $2,528 in 2017, or 0.8% a year. That figure ranks 17th lowest of 67 in Alabama, under the $3,400 state median. Renters worked 29% of the county's farmland in 2022.
