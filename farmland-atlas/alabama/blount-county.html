$48

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2008; up 95.9% from $25 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Blount County runs $48 an acre in 2026, up 95.9% from $25 in 2016 and a shade over the state median of $46. That puts it 27th highest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $5,006 an acre in 2022, up 39.0% from $3,602 in 2017, and it ranks 14th highest of 67 against a state median of $3,400. Corn made 124.1 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 135.5 bu.
