Etowah County, Alabama

Etowah County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,646 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 121.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Etowah County runs $60 per acre in 2026, up 63 percent from 2016. Corn yields have trended up 3.2 bushels per acre a year across 2009-2025, with a median of 121 bushels per acre and a worst year of 83.5 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 82 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 71 percent in 2000-2009, 72 percent in 2010-2019, and 26 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed at 0.47 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 70.2 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 69.3 F. Drought weeks fell from 40 in 2007 to 18 across 2021-2025.
