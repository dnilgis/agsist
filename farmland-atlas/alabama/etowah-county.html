$60

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 63.0% from $37 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Etowah County rents for $60 an acre in 2026, up 63.0% from $37 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 59 counties in Alabama. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,646 an acre in 2022, up 47.2% from $3,836 in 2017 and 7th highest of 67 in the state. Corn ran 142.8 bu in 2025, and 31% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
