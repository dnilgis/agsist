As of Oct 16, cash rent in Lowndes County runs $42 an acre for 2026, up 95.3% from $22 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $46 and ranks 28th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $2,343 an acre in 2022, up 23.4% from $1,898 in 2017, and ranks 5th lowest of 67 against a state median of $3,400. 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
