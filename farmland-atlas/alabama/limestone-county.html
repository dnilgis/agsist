Non-irrigated cash rent in Limestone County sits at $139 per acre for 2026, up 51.9 percent from 2016. Land value carries at $4,683 per acre under the 2022 census estimate, rising 13.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. The county held 42 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2007, its worst year, against 38 weeks total across 2021-2025. July nights average 70.3 F for 2017-2026, up from the 68.6 F normal of 1991-2020. Corn yields carry a median of 159.8 bushels per acre, with 2012 low at 71.9.
