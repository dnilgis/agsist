Cropland rent in Lamar County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 52.8% from $27 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $46 and ranks 24th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $1,910 an acre in 2022, lowest of 67 in Alabama, and it gained only 2.7% from $1,859 in 2017, 0.5% a year. Only 10% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
