Stands out against its own record

$37

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 55.3% from $24 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in St. Clair County runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 55.3% from $24 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $46 and is tied for 19th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $5,176 an acre in 2022, up 21.0% from $4,277 in 2017, and it ranks 10th highest of 67 against a state median of $3,400. Renters farmed 31% of the ground in 2022.
