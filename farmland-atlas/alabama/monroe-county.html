Monroe County, Alabama

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,399 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.17 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

97% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 260 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,993 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,399 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $66 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 10.7% from 2016 to 2026. 23.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 88.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 127,307 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.17 per base acre, PLC $1.94.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (77.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $50.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 108 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.70 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.93 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 55% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 26.6%.
