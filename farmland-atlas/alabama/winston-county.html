Winston County, Alabama

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,005 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

5.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 224 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,596 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,005 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 43.2% from 2016 to 2026. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 197.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 48,285 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.96 per base acre, PLC $1.45.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (57.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (28.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $51.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

6.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 2008 to 2025; too much rain caused 27.5%.

Stands out against its own record

$27

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2012 (15 surveys on record since 2009); up 43.2% from $19 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Winston County rents for $27 an acre in 2026, up 43.2% from $19 in 2016. That still sits well under the state median of $46 and ranks 10th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings was $3,005 an acre in 2022, up 8.7% from $2,765 in 2017, or 1.7% a year. Only 17% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
