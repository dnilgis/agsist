As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pike County runs $40 an acre for 2026, up 37.9% from $29 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $46 and ranks tied for 22nd lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,381 an acre in 2022, up 29.0% from $2,620 in 2017. Corn trends at 130.2 bu for 2026, and only 15% of farmland was rented in 2022.
