Cash rent in Covington County runs $67 an acre for 2026, tied for 10th highest of 59 in Alabama and well above the state median of $46. That rent is up 18.8% from $56 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,400 an acre in 2022, up 26.7% from $2,684 in 2017 and right at the state median. Corn trends lower, with a 14-yr trend of 112.3 bu that sits 4th lowest of 23.
