As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Cleburne County runs $31 an acre for 2026, down 21.8% from $39 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $46 and comes in 15th lowest of 59. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 20.2% to $4,160 an acre in 2022 from $3,460 in 2017, 3.8% a year. That puts it 18th highest of 67, above the state median of $3,400.
