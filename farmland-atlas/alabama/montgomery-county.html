Montgomery County, Alabama

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,146 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.34 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 35.7 inches, 80% of normal (44.53 inches) through Nov 18, at Montgomery Airport. Frost-free season: 253 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,981 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,146 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 7.3% from 2016 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 214.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 160 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,046 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.34 per base acre, PLC $2.06.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (82 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (23.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $55.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 810 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.66 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.82, 2016 to 2025. 2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.4%.
