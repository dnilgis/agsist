$34

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 11 surveys since 2013; up 37% from $25 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland in Wilcox County rented for $34 an acre in 2026, up 36.7% from $25 in 2016. That sits 16th lowest of 59 counties in Alabama, under the state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $2,163 an acre in 2022, up 19.0% from $1,818 in 2017, and it ranks 2nd lowest of 67 against a state median of $3,400. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
