Baldwin County, Alabama

Rain is at 69% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,202 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.29 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

22.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 42.05 inches, 69% of normal (61.23 inches) through Nov 17, at Fairhope, 1.5 mi WSW. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,182 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,202 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $90 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 27.7% from 2016 to 2026. 32.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 15.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 111.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 80 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 104,500 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.29 per base acre, PLC $2.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $50.00 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.53 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.83 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 43% of the median yield. 20.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 4.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36.9%.
