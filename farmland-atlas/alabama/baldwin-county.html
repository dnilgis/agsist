Baldwin County, Alabama

Baldwin County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $90 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,202 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01003. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Baldwin County runs $90 per acre in 2026, up 27.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $143 per acre, and the irrigated premium sits at 1.59 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026, against 1.72 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Land value carries at $6,202 per acre, the operators' own estimate, up 6.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Drought has stayed light in recent years: 11 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 123 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000. Corn yields trend up 3.1 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 152 bushels per acre and a worst year of 65.9 in 2011.
