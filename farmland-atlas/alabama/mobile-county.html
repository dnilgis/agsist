Mobile County, Alabama

Mobile County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $84 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,768 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01097. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mobile County averages $84 per acre for 2026, up 89.8 percent from 2016. Land value under the census stands at $4,768 per acre, changing 0.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, so rent moved far faster than land value over these periods. July nights have averaged 73.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.3 F, with a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. Excess moisture and flood drove 41 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share rose from 11 percent in 1989-1999 to 27 percent in 2020-2025, with 16 percent in 2000-2009 and 11 percent in 2010-2019 between them. Irrigation covers 8 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
