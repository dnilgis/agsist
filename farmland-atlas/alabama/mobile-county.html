Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 90% from $44 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Mobile County ran $84 an acre in 2026, up 89.8% from $44 in 2016. That puts it 7th highest of 59 counties in Alabama, against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $4,768 an acre in 2022, barely moved from $4,738 in 2017, a gain of 0.1% a year. 25% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
