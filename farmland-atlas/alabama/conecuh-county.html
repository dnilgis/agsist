Conecuh County's notable number is heat and drought. They made up 52 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2020-2025, up from 46 percent in 1989-1999, and the county's largest cause over the full 1989-2025 record at 50 percent. The 2011 drought was the worst year, with 45 weeks of severe drought or worse. Non-irrigated rent is $28 per acre in 2026, down 24.7 percent from 2016. The county's corn yield trend has been up 4.9 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2025, with a median of 116.2 bushels.
