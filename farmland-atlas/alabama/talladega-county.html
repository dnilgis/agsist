$54

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 25.9% from $43 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Talladega County runs $54 an acre for 2026, up 25.9% from $43 in 2016 and 21st highest of 59 in Alabama. The census value of land and buildings was $3,914 an acre in 2022, up 18.3% from 2017, or 3.4% a year. Corn made 156.8 bu in 2025, close to the 2021-25 average of 155.9 bu.
