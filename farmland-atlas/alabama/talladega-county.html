Non-irrigated cash rent in Talladega County runs $54 per acre, up 25.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value under the census sits at $3,914 per acre, up 18.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields carry a trend of 1.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2009-2025, with a median of 139.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 113.6 in 2024. Heat and drought stands as the largest cause of indemnities at 55 percent over 1989-2025, though its share fell across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019 before dropping further in 2020-2025 to 33 percent. Solar is not yet built here, with 200 MW proposed to EIA.
