Butler County, Alabama

Butler County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2024); land and buildings $3,116 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Butler County, AL reached $33 per acre in 2024, up 32 percent from 2014. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,116 per acre in 2022, a 27 percent rise from 2017 at 4.9 percent a year. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.57 F per decade warming, with the 2017-2026 average nightly low at 71.5 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.8 F.
