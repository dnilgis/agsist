Marion County, Alabama

Marion County, AL: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,186 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 01093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Marion County averages $34 per acre in 2026, up 28.3 percent from 2016. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, per the 2022 census. July nights have warmed: the 1991-2020 normal low is 68.5 F, against 70 F for 2017-2026, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.5 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved 45 percent, 56 percent, 43 percent, 36 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 0.71, above the 1989-2025 figure of 0.58. Census land value reached $3,186 per acre in 2022, up 36.2 percent from 2017.
