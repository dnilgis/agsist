Cropland rent in Coffee County runs $50 an acre for 2026, up 15.1% from $43 in 2016 and tied for 25th highest of 59 against a state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,859 an acre in 2022, up 46.6% from $2,633 in 2017 and 23rd highest of 67. Corn made 80.1 bu in 2024, under the county median of 93.3 bu.
