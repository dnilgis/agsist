Cash rent in Marengo County runs $40 an acre for 2026, up 23.4% from $32 in 2016 and under the state median of $46. The census value of land and buildings was $2,247 an acre in 2022, up 12.0% from $2,007 in 2017, which ranks 3rd lowest of 67 in Alabama. Corn trends at 102.7 bu, 2nd lowest of 23, well short of the state median of 138.8 bu.
