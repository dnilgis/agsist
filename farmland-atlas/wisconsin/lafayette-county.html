Lafayette County, Wisconsin

Lafayette County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $240 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,655 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55065. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lafayette County stands at $240 per acre for 2026, up 5.7 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $7,655 per acre, with that value up 28.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.1 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 4.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 195.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 77.6 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 18 percent, 24 percent, 39 percent, 21 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and drought weeks ran 53 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 11 from 2021-2025.
