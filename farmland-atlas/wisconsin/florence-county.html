Florence County, Wisconsin

Florence County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,734 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55037. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Florence County averages $25 per acre in 2026. Census land and buildings value stood at $3,734 per acre in 2022, up 47.8 percent over 2017-2022, or 8.1 percent a year. Heat and drought caused 36 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, closed crop years, with a 70 percent share in 2000-2009 and a 28 percent share in 2010-2019. The July nightly low averaged 54.1 F in 2017-2026 against a 53.3 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.44 F per decade since 1976 and no clear direction. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 86 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 23 weeks in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
