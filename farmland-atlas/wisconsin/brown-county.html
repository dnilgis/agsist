Non-irrigated cash rent in Brown County averages $213 per acre for 2026, up 17.7 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $9,088 per acre in 2022, up 7 percent from 2017, 1.4 percent a year. Drought itself stays rare: severe drought or worse held for 6 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with only 1 week in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and July nights have warmed 0.54 F per decade since 1976.
