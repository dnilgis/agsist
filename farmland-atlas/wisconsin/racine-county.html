As of Oct 16, cash rent in Racine County runs $175 an acre for 2026, up 31.6% from $133 in 2016 and above the Wisconsin median of $136. The census value of land and buildings came to $9,656 an acre in 2022, the highest of 68 in the state, up 25.7% from $7,682 in 2017. Corn made 205.1 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year median of 174.2 bu. Renters farm 53% of the ground.
