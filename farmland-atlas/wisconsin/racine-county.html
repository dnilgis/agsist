Non-irrigated cash rent in Racine County runs $175 per acre in 2026, up 31.6 percent from 2016. Census land and building value in 2022 stood at $9,656 per acre, after a 25.7 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, and the 2022 cash rent equaled 1.51 percent of that value. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 totals $33.0 million in indemnities, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent; heat and drought's share moved 28 percent, 13 percent, 13 percent, 21 percent across the four periods listed. The loss ratio held at 0.58 for both the full 1989-2025 record and the 2016-2025 window. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 81 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 37 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
