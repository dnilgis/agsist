As of Oct 16, cash rent in Dodge County runs $212 an acre for 2026, 10th highest of 68 in Wisconsin and well over the state median of $136. That rent is up 20.5% from $176 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,275 an acre in 2022, up 24.5% from $5,844 in 2017. Corn holds up its end too, with a 14-yr trend of 207.6 bu that sits 4th highest of 39.
