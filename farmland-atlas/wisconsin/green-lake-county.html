Green Lake County, Wisconsin

Green Lake County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $195 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,669 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 168.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55047. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $195 per acre, up 36.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings carried a census value of $7,669 per acre in 2022, with that rent equal to 2.2 percent of value. Corn yield trend is up 2.7 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, median 168.3, with 2012 the worst year at 97.3. Heat and drought is the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 50 percent, and its share by decade was 29 percent, 45 percent, 53 percent, then 51 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 42 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 27 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
