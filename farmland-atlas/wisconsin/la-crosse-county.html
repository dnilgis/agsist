Non-irrigated cash rent in La Crosse County is $165 per acre in 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. Census land value came in at $6,527 per acre in 2022, up 30.9 percent from 2017, 5.5 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 1.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 166.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 137.1 in 2012. Heat and drought is the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 52 percent, with shares of 28 percent in 1989-1999, 66 percent in 2000-2009, 50 percent in 2010-2019, and 49 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 44 of 1357 over 2000-2025, with 4 of those in 2021-2025.
