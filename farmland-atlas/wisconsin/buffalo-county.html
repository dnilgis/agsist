Non-irrigated cash rent in Buffalo County stands at $139 per acre in 2026, up 14.9 percent from 2016. Land value reached $5,134 per acre in 2022, up 30.3 percent from 2017, and rent takes 2.61 percent of that value. Drought weeks were rare in 2021-2025, just 1 of the period, and irrigation there draws entirely from groundwater.
