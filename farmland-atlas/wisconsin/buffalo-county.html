Buffalo County cash rent runs $139 an acre for 2026, up 14.9% from $121 in 2016 and 34th highest of 68 in Wisconsin. The census value of land and buildings was $5,134 an acre in 2022, up 30.3% from $3,940 in 2017. That value is 28th lowest of 68, under the state median of $5,565. Corn made 179.6 bu in 2025, well above the 18 year county median of 164.4 bu.
