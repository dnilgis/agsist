Lincoln County, Wisconsin

Lincoln County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $70 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,790 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 134.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55069. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lincoln County averages $70 per acre for 2026, up 58 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $3,790 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 16.9 percent, 3.2 percent a year. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 0.9 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 134.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 111 in 2009. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $12.6 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 38 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 10 percent in 1989-1999 to 64 percent in 2000-2009 to 19 percent in 2010-2019 to 25 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind capacity, operating and proposed, are all 0 MW.
