USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Lincoln County runs $70 an acre for 2026, up 58.0% from $44 in 2016 and 10th lowest of 68 in Wisconsin against a state median of $136. The census value of land and buildings was $3,790 an acre in 2022, up 16.9% from $3,241 in 2017. Corn here trends at 141.3 bu, the lowest of 39 counties, with the state median at 177.4 bu.
