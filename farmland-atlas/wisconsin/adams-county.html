Adams County, Wisconsin

Adams County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $74 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,155 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 129.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Adams County stands at $74 per acre in 2026, unchanged from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $341 per acre, and irrigation covers 67 percent of harvested cropland, with 81 percent of that water drawn from groundwater. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $90.9 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 55 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.88, up from 0.78 across the full 1989-2025 period. Land value per the 2022 census stands at $5,155 per acre, up 14.8 percent from 2017 to 2022.
