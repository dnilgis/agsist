Marinette County, Wisconsin

Marinette County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $124 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,176 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 134.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Marinette County averages $124 per acre in 2026, up 29.2 percent from 2016. Census land value reached $5,176 per acre in 2022, up 33.5 percent from 2017, 5.9 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 2 bushels per acre a year on a median of 134.1 bushels per acre, with 2009 the worst year at 111. Heat and drought account for 40 percent of indemnities since 1989, and drought weeks were 12 out of the 2021-2025 stretch, against 28 in 2009 alone. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with none proposed.
