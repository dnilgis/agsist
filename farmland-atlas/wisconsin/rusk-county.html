Rusk County, Wisconsin

Rusk County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $104 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,399 an acre in the 2022 census; corn 14-yr trend 161.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55107. Figures as of 2026-10-16.

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Rusk County runs $104 an acre for 2026, up 57.6% from $66 in 2016. That still sits 19th lowest of 68 counties, under the state median of $136. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,399 an acre in 2022, up 36.8% from $2,485 in 2017, and ranks 6th lowest of 68 against a state median of $5,565. Corn made 158.3 bu in 2025, close to the 14 year trend of 161.7 bu.
