Vilas County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Freeze and frost is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities in Vilas County, at 66 percent, out of 3 million dollars paid 1989-2023. July nightly lows have stayed flat, 53.4 F in 2017-2026 against a 53.4 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.11 F per decade and no clear direction since 1976. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
