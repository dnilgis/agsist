Non-irrigated cash rent in Columbia County stands at $215 per acre, up 20.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent runs higher, at $246 per acre in 2025, and 98 percent of irrigation water here draws from groundwater. Land value rose 29.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, reaching $7,544 per acre, with cash rent at 2.44 percent of that value.
