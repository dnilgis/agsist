Sources and terms

Every figure is a county average from the sources below, not an appraisal of any farm. "Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed. Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. What each column means: county data and how to cite.

This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields. Loss ratio and claims: USDA RMA Summary of Business. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor. July lows: NOAA nClimDiv county monthly minimum temperature. A missing figure is named with its reason (in a table it reads n/a).

Rent against price: the county's non-irrigated cash rent (USDA NASS county survey, a recent survey year) set against the median price per acre of recorded market sales of bare farmland, 20 acres or more, over the 24 months the sales line names. Years of rent is how many years of that rent it takes to equal the price. A county with few sales, or with sale prices well above farm value such as building ground, gets no line. The two sides are different land: the sales include pasture and any irrigated ground, the rent is non-irrigated cropland only. Taxes, upkeep and interest are not counted.
