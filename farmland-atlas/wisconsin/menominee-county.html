Menominee County is a small county with few of the usual pressures showing up in the numbers. July nights have warmed a little, with the average low at 57.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 56.6 F, and the trend since 1976 is 0.5 F per decade. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Severe drought has covered at least 50 percent of the county in 50 of 1357 weeks since 2000, and 2009 was the worst year with 24 weeks. The 2021-2025 stretch recorded 11 weeks in severe drought or worse. There is no CRP ground, no operating wind or solar, and nothing proposed to the EIA. Those are the facts the county has on the record.
