Trempealeau County, Wisconsin

Trempealeau County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $144 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,892 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 158.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55121. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Trempealeau County averages $144 per acre in 2026, up 10.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated cropland, 6 percent of harvested acres, rents at $264 per acre, a multiple of 1.83 over non-irrigated rent in 2026 versus 1.64 in 2013, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Corn yields trend up 1.4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 158.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 122.4 in 2013. Severe drought covered the county in only 1 of the weeks measured from 2021-2025.
