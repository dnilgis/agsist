Pepin County, Wisconsin

Pepin County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $185 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,613 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 167.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55091. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pepin County is $185 per acre in 2026, up 42.3 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $5,613 per acre, up 44.9 percent from 2017, 7.7 percent a year, and rent now runs at 2.55 percent of land value.
