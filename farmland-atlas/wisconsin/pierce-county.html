USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pierce County runs $179 an acre for 2026, up 32.6% from $135 in 2016 and 19th highest of 68 in Wisconsin. The census value of land and buildings was $5,863 an acre in 2022, up 26.9% from $4,620 in 2017, a bit over the $5,565 state median. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 198.8 bu that sits 6th highest of 39.
