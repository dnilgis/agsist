As of Oct 16, cash rent in Waushara County ran $78 an acre in 2026, up 39.3% from $56 in 2016 and 12th lowest of 68 in Wisconsin, where the state median is $136. Irrigated ground is another story at $301 an acre in 2026, 2nd highest of 11. The census value of land and buildings was $4,925 an acre in 2022, up 16.4% from $4,231 in 2017. Corn made 172.6 bu in 2025, a little under the 175.7 bu average for 2021 through 2025.
