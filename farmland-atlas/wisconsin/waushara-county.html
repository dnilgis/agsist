Non-irrigated cash rent in Waushara County averages $78 per acre in 2026, up 39.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated cropland rents at $301 per acre, a multiple of 3.86 in 2026 versus 4.56 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Irrigation covers 38 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 99 percent of its water from groundwater. Crop insurance data show excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 55 percent of the $86.4 million paid out from 1989-2025, with heat and drought shares of 31 percent, 37 percent, 19 percent, and 23 percent across the four listed periods. Census land value stands at $4,925 per acre, up 16.4 percent from 2017 to 2022.
