Most of Ozaukee's ground is dry-land, and the county's notable story is wet years, not heat. Flood and excess moisture cause 60 percent of the crop insurance indemnities paid here since 1989, and the loss ratio over that stretch is 0.63. Heat and drought claims were 6 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 and 17 percent in 2020-2025. Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $118 per acre, against a census land value of $8,047 per acre from 2022, so the rent is a small share of the value. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated.
