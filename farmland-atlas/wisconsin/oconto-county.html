Cash rent in Oconto County runs $154 an acre for 2026, up 40.0% from $110 in 2016 and tied for 27th highest of 68 in Wisconsin. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,367 an acre in 2022, up 28.8% from $4,166 in 2017, which puts it 33rd lowest of 68. Corn last year made 177.1 bu, while the 14-yr trend projects 166.1 bu for 2026, 11th lowest of 39 in the state.
