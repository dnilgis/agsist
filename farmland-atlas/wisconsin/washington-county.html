Cash rent in Washington County is $119 an acre for 2026, down 0.8% from $120 in 2016 and under the Wisconsin median of $136. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, reaching $9,471 an acre in 2022, up 34.3% from $7,053 in 2017. That value ranks 2nd highest of 68 in the state. In 2022, 45% of the farmland here was rented.
