Manitowoc County, Wisconsin

Manitowoc County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $199 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,751 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55071. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Manitowoc County is $199 per acre in 2026, up 25.2 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $7,751 per acre in the 2022 census, with land value up 13.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.6 percent a year. Drought has been rare: 8 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, none from 2021-2025.
