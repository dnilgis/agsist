Walworth County, Wisconsin

Walworth County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $236 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,759 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55127. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Walworth County stands at $236 per acre for 2026, up 24.2 percent from 2016. Census land value for 2022 was $8,759 per acre, with land value up 26.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.8 percent a year. Corn yields show a trend of 3.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 179.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 102 in 2012. The county drew 25.5 MW of operating solar capacity and no operating wind.
