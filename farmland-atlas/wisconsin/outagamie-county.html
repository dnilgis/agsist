Outagamie County, Wisconsin

Outagamie County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $161 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,559 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55087. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $161 per acre, up 49.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings value in the 2022 census stood at $7,559 per acre, with rent at 2.09 percent of that value. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and irrigation failure has never produced an indemnity.
