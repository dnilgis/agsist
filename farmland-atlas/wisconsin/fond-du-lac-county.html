Fond du Lac County, Wisconsin

Fond du Lac County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $203 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,870 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Fond du Lac County runs $203 per acre, up 30.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census values land and buildings at $7,870 per acre in 2022, with land value change 2017-2022 at 8.2 percent, 1.6 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 is 2.6 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 179.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 120.4 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 closed crop years total $59.1 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 59 percent; heat and drought shares run 19 percent in 1989-1999, 27 percent in 2000-2009, 19 percent in 2010-2019, and 18 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks with severe drought or worse, 2000-2025, are 14 of 1357.
