Sheboygan County, Wisconsin

Sheboygan County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $151 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,332 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55117. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sheboygan County runs $151 per acre, up 41.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census value of land and buildings sits at $8,332 per acre, with land value up 26.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.8 percent a year. Water risk here has stayed low: 10 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000, none from 2021-2025, and no irrigation in use. Excess moisture and flood drove 64 percent of the county's $30.3 million in crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share fell across the four listed periods from 25 percent to 30 percent to 12 percent to 12 percent. Corn yields trended up 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 157.4 bushels per acre.
