Jackson County, Wisconsin

Jackson County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $143 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,363 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 159.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jackson County is $143 per acre in 2026, up 43.7 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $4,363 per acre, up 14.8 percent from 2017, or 2.8 percent a year. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 58.9 F to 60.4 F in 2017-2026. Drought weeks stayed low in 2021-2025, at 4.
