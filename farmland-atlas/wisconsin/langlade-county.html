Langlade County, Wisconsin

Langlade County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $103 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,988 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55067. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Langlade County runs $103 per acre in 2026, up 25.6 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $173 per acre, a multiple of 1.68 over non-irrigated, down from 2.37 in 2011. Census land value stands at $3,988 per acre, up 21.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance history shows freeze and frost as the largest cause of indemnities at 34 percent over 1989-2025, with heat and drought's share moving from 10 percent in 1989-1999 to 54 percent in 2000-2009, 11 percent in 2010-2019, and 30 percent in 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered 66 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2009 the worst year at 24 weeks.
