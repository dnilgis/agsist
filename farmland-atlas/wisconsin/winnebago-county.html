Winnebago County, Wisconsin

Winnebago County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $123 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,641 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55139. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Winnebago County averages $123 per acre in 2026, up 25.5 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $8,641 per acre in 2022, up 24 percent from 2017, a 4.4 percent a year pace. Corn yields have trended up 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 151 bushels per acre and a worst year of 109.8 in 2012. Solar stands at 0 MW operating with 6 MW proposed; wind is 0 MW operating and proposed.
