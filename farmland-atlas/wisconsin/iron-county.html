Iron County had 8,578 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Iron County was $4,086 per acre in 2022, up 20.1 percent from 2017, or 3.7 percent a year. July nightly lows sit at 54.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 54.1 F, and no July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought weeks ran to 100 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 26 weeks, and 41 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
