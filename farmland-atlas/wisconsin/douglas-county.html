Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.85 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 139 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,030 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,828 an acre in the 2022 census.

UW-Madison Extension, 2025: no farmland sales in this county, so no price. Wisconsin Agricultural Land Prices 2025, UW-Madison Division of Extension, May 2026. Bare farmland sold between unrelated parties, from Department of Revenue transfer returns; a separate source from USDA's. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $16 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.40 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 6.7% from 2016 to 2026. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 91.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 80,246 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 14.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2020; too much rain caused 63.5%.

Quick answers for Douglas County

What is cash rent in Douglas County, Wisconsin?

The county average is $16 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Douglas County, Wisconsin?

Land and buildings were worth $2,828 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
