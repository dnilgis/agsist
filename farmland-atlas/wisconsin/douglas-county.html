Douglas County, Wisconsin

Douglas County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $16 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,828 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55031. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Douglas County runs $16 per acre, up 6.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings are valued by operators at $2,828 per acre, with the value rising 44.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.6 percent a year. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated. The July nightly low averaged 55.6 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 56.8 F over 2017-2026, a warming trend of 0.49 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance losses from 1989-2020 were driven mainly by excess moisture and flood, at 64 percent of $1.1 million in indemnities, while heat and drought fell from 31 percent of losses in 2000-2009 to 8 percent in 2010-2019.
