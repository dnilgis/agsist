As of Oct 16, cash rent in Milwaukee County sits at $101 an acre for 2026. That is 16th lowest of 68 in Wisconsin and under the state median of $136. Corn tells a different story. The county yield trend projects 222.4 bu for 2026 against a state median of 177.4 bu, and the 2023 crop came in at 197.3 bu.

Milwaukee County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
