Juneau County, Wisconsin

Juneau County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $120 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,941 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Juneau County averages $120 per acre in 2026, up 31.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated cropland rents at $272 per acre. Corn yields carry a trend of 1.7 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 153.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.7 in 2013. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $73.5 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 30 percent; heat and drought's share moved 13 percent, 25 percent, 32 percent, 26 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $4,941 per acre, up 33.4 percent from 2017 to 2022.
