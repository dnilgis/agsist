$154

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 16.7% from $132 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Monroe County runs $154 an acre for 2026, tied for 27th highest of 68 in Wisconsin and above the state median of $136. That is up 16.7% from $132 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,240 an acre in 2022, up 32.1% from $3,967 in 2017, and sits 32nd lowest of 68 against a state median of $5,565. Corn came in at 167.9 bu in 2025.
