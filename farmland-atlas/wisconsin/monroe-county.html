Monroe County, Wisconsin

Non-irrigated cash rent in Monroe County averages $154 per acre for 2026, up 16.7 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings runs $5,240 per acre, with a 32.1 percent gain from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield trend is 1.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 155.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 121.4 in 2013. Irrigated cropland is 3 percent of the harvested total, and drought weeks from 2000-2025 held at 63 of 1357.
