Waukesha County, Wisconsin

Waukesha County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $171 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,589 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 169.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55133. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
