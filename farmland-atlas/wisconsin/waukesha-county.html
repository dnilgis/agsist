USDA cash rent rose 50% from 2016 to 2026. 40% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 9% of the cropland (2022 census). Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.30 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.43, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 57% of the median yield. 2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).
