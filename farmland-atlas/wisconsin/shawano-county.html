Shawano County, Wisconsin

Shawano County, WI: non-irrigated cash rent $142 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,983 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 55115. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Shawano County averages $142 per acre in 2026, up 46.4 percent from 2016. Land value carries a census figure of $5,983 per acre in 2022, up 28.3 percent from 2017, or 5.1 percent a year, and 2022 rent runs 1.82 percent of that value. The corn yield trend rises 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 152.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 103 in 2008. Drought has stayed light: 12 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2025 the worst year at 7 weeks. July nights run warmer than the 1991-2020 normal, 58.9 F against 57.5 F, and the loss ratio for closed crop years 2016-2025 sits at 0.39.
