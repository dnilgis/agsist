Day County, South Dakota

Rain is at 90% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,549 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.37 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 20.71 inches, 90% of normal (22.91 inches) through Nov 18, at Andover No. 2. Frost-free season: 156 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,376 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,549 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $159 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 13.6% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 50.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 23,311 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 80 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $3.75 at Groton, 27 miles. 3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 80 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 2 elevators.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.43 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.69, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 67% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 14.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 53.5%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Day County runs $159 an acre for 2026. That is up 13.6% from $140 in 2016, and it ranks 20th highest of 63 in South Dakota against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings was $3,549 an acre in 2022, up 34.2% from $2,645 in 2017. The corn trend yield projects to 181.5 bu for 2026, highest of 20 in the state, where the median sits at 147.8 bu.
