Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,550, average $1,850, low $1,413 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.93 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.25 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 144 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,562 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $22.49 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. 18.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 5.5 days a year and damaging wind on 6.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 64.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.2%.
