Cash rent in Fall River County runs $24 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 63 in South Dakota, against a state median of $112. Pasture rents for $10 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,082 an acre in 2022, up 51.1% from $716 in 2017, and that still ranks 3rd lowest of 66. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
