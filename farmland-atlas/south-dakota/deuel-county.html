Deuel County, South Dakota

Land and buildings run $5,470 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.82 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 153 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,313 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,470 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $181 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 20.7% from 2016 to 2026. 46.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 29.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 230.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 89,111 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $3.66 at Canby, 19 miles.

3 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.82 per base acre, PLC $3.47.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (138.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $47.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,271 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.01 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.46 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 66% of the median yield. 1.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 20.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 43.3%.
