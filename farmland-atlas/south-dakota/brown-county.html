Brown County, South Dakota

Brown County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $166 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,295 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 154.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Brown County runs $166 per acre in 2026, up 20.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents higher at $200 per acre, though only 0 percent of harvested cropland here is irrigated. Corn yield has trended up 1.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 154.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 130.5 in 2013. Crop insurance history is dominated by excess moisture and flood, at 69 percent of $870.0 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share has moved 3 percent, 15 percent, 17 percent, 11 percent across the four listed periods. Census land value sits at $4,295 per acre, up 27.8 percent from 2017 to 2022.
