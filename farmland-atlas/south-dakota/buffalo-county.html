Buffalo County, South Dakota

Buffalo County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $75 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,941 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Buffalo County runs $75 per acre in 2025, up 16.3 percent from 2014. Land value sits at $1,941 per acre as of the 2022 census, up 25.6 percent from 2017, or 4.7 percent a year. July nights average 61.3 F for 2017-2026 against a 60.8 F normal for 1991-2020, with no clear trend since 1976.
