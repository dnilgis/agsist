Clark County, South Dakota

Clark County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $170 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,741 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 161.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clark County sits at $170 per acre in 2026, up 27.8 percent from 2016. Land value reached $4,741 per acre in 2022, a change of 50.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 8.5 percent a year, and cash rent is 2.95 percent of that value. Wind operating capacity is 219.5 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
