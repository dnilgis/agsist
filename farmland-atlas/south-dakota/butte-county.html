Butte County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,359 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.34 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 10.69 inches, 67% of normal (16.04 inches) through Nov 16, at Newell. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,442 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,359 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 19.4% from 2016 to 2026. 30.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 60.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 12,330 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 70 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $3.65 at Belle Fourche, 23 miles. 1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.34 per base acre, PLC $2.52.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (100.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $12.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 889 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.24 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.91 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 75% of the median yield. 42.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.6 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.4%.
