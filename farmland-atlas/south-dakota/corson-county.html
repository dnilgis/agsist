Cash rent in Corson County runs $51 an acre for 2026, up 20.2% from $42 in 2016. That sits 16th lowest of 63 in South Dakota, well under the state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings was $1,338 an acre in 2022, up 45.6% from $919 in 2017 and 12th lowest of 66 against a state median of $3,173. The corn trend projects 101.1 bu for 2026, 5th lowest of 20, with the state median at 147.8 bu.
