Corson County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Northwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,175, average $1,731, low $1,417 an acre.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

89% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.25 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 145 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,433 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.21 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.68, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.5, 2010 to 2025.
