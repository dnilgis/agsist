Clay County cash rent stands at $241 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 63 in South Dakota and well over the state median of $112. That is up 26.2% from $191 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,445 an acre in 2022, up 58.3% from $4,702 in 2017. Corn made 188.7 bu in 2025, above the county median of 175.6 bu.
