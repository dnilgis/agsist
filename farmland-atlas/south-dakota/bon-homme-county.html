Bon Homme County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $160 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,007 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46009. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bon Homme County runs $160 per acre in 2026, up 16.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $235 per acre, a multiple of 1.47 over non-irrigated in 2026, against 1.75 in 2010, with no clear direction over that span. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $246.8 million, with heat and drought as the largest cause at 64 percent; that share was 22 percent in 1989-1999, 57 percent in 2000-2009, 58 percent in 2010-2019, and 77 percent in 2020-2025. The county saw 74 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 141 such weeks going back to 2000-2025. Census land value sits at $5,007 per acre in 2022, up 25.7 percent from 2017.
