Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 14.5% from $220 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Union County cash rent runs $252 an acre for 2026. That is tied for highest of 63 in South Dakota and well over the $112 state median, up 14.5% from $220 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,151 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 66 and up 21.7% from $5,875 in 2017. Corn made 181.0 bu in 2024, a touch over the 177.7 bu median for 2008 through 2024.
