Non-irrigated cash rent in Union County runs $252 per acre in 2026, up 14.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $269 per acre, a multiple of 1.07 over non-irrigated; that multiple was 1.13 in 2009, with no clear direction since. The county spent 85 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 189 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025. Census land value sits at $7,151 per acre, up 21.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
