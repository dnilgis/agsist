Davison County, South Dakota

Davison County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $153 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,642 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 138.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Davison County is $153 per acre in 2026, up 16.8 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $4,642 per acre, up 36.6 percent from 2017, 6.4 percent a year. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 113 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 67 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Solar and wind capacity are not present in this county.
