Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 40% from $100 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Aurora County runs $139 an acre for 2026, up 39.7% from $100 in 2016 and tied for 29th highest of 63 in South Dakota. The census value of land and buildings was $3,731 an acre in 2022, up 40.6% from $2,654 in 2017, against a state median of $3,173. Corn trends at 152.5 bu for 2026, 8th highest of 20 in the state, and 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
