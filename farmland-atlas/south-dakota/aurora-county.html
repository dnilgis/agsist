Non-irrigated cash rent in Aurora County runs $139 per acre for 2026, up 39.7 percent from 2016. Land value under the census stands at $3,731 per acre, up 40.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 7 percent a year. No harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, so this is a dryland county. Corn yield has trended up 2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 124.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 63.9 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 54 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, rising to 67 percent in the 2020-2025 span. Wind operating capacity stands at 20 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
