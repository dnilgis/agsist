Pennington County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,513 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $4.37 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

93.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,412 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,513 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $37 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 7.2% from 2016 to 2026. 31% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains under 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 53.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,511 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 90 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $3.50 at Rapid City Elevator, 8 miles. 1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.37 per base acre, PLC $1.40.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (55.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (13.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.05 per base acre, PLC $6.08. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $15.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 362 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.76 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. 4.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 13.7 days a year and damaging wind on 10.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.7%.
