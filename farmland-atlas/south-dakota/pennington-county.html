Cropland in Pennington County rents for $37 an acre in 2026, up 7.2% from $35 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 63 counties in South Dakota, where the median is $112. The census value of land and buildings was $1,513 an acre in 2022, up 43.0% from $1,058 in 2017. That still sits 16th lowest of 66 against a state median of $3,173. Pasture rents for $17 an acre in 2026.
