Hutchinson County, South Dakota

Hutchinson County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $165 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,615 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 151.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46067. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hutchinson County averages $165 per acre in 2026, up 7.1 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $5,615 per acre in 2022, up 24.9 percent from 2017. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 1.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 151.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 30 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 19 percent, 46 percent, 55 percent, 75 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Drought weeks ran higher in 2021-2025, at 81, against 143 weeks total for 2000-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
