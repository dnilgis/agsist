USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cropland rent in Hutchinson County runs $165 an acre for 2026, up 7.1% from $154 in 2016 and 18th highest of 63 in South Dakota. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,615 an acre in 2022, 10th highest of 66, up 24.9% from $4,495 in 2017. Corn made 176.0 bu in 2025, well over the 137.4 bu average for 2021 through 2025. In 2022, 47% of the farmland here was rented.
