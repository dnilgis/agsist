Cropland in Miner County rents for $146 an acre in 2026, up 20.7% from $121 in 2016. That sits 25th highest of 63 counties in South Dakota, against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,428 an acre in 2022, up 25.3% from $3,533 in 2017. Corn trends at 149.2 bu, close to the state median of 147.8 bu.
