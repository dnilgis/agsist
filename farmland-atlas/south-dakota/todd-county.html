$45

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 28.6% from $35 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Todd County runs $45 an acre for 2026, up 28.6% from $35 in 2016. That is 12th lowest of 63 in South Dakota, against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,168 an acre in 2022, up 37.4% from $850 in 2017, or 6.6% a year. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
