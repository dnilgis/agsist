Cropland rent in Hamlin County ran $176 an acre in 2026, up 3.5% from $170 in 2016. That puts it 14th highest of 63 in South Dakota, well over the state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,101 an acre in 2022, up 29.9% from $3,926 in 2017. Corn made 180.0 bu in 2025, a touch over the 2021-25 average of 177.1 bu.
