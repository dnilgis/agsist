Cash rent in Tripp County runs $71 an acre for 2026, up 36.9% from $52 in 2014. That sits under the state median of $112 and ranks 18th lowest of 63. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,124 an acre in 2022, up 31.1% from $1,620 in 2017. Corn made 110.6 bu in 2025, a bit over the county median of 106.7 bu across 17 years.
