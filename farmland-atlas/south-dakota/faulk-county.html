Faulk County, South Dakota

Faulk County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $112 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,320 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 126.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46049. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Faulk County averages $112 per acre in 2026, up 14.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $3,320 per acre in 2022, after a 30.3 percent rise from 2017 to 2022, 5.4 percent a year. Heat and drought caused 42 percent of indemnities paid from 1989-2025, with the share at 6 percent in 1989-1999, 75 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 35 percent in 2020-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in this county.
