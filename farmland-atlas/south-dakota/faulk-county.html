Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 14.9% from $98 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Faulk County runs $112 an acre in 2026, up 14.9% from $98 in 2016 and right on the state median of $112, tied for 31st lowest of 63. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,320 an acre in 2022, up 30.3% from $2,547 in 2017, which ranks 33rd highest of 66. 52% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
