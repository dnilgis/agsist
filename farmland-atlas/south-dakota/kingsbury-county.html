Cash rent in Kingsbury County runs $158 an acre for 2026, up 1.9% from $155 in 2016, and that sits 21st highest of 63 in South Dakota against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,005 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from $3,810 in 2017. That puts it 16th highest of 66 in the state. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
