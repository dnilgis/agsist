Kingsbury County, South Dakota

Kingsbury County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $158 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,005 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 158.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46077. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kingsbury County averages $158 per acre for 2026, up 1.9 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $5,005 per acre, up 31.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.6 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value is 3.04 percent. Excess moisture and flood account for 52 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, the largest single cause, while irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland. Weeks in severe drought or worse totaled 56 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 16 weeks and a corn yield of 115 bushels per acre. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed in the county.
