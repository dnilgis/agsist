Hanson County, South Dakota

Hanson County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $184 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,155 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hanson County runs $184 per acre in 2026, up 8.9 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $5,155 per acre in 2022, with that value up 19.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.6 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 148 bushels per acre and a worst year of 67.4 in 2012. Solar and wind operating capacity is 0 MW each, with none proposed to EIA.
