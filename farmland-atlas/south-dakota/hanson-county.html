Cropland rent in Hanson County runs $184 an acre for 2026, up 8.9% from $169 in 2016 and 10th highest of 63 in South Dakota, where the median is $112. The census value of land and buildings was $5,155 an acre in 2022, up 19.3% from $4,322 in 2017, or 3.6% a year. Corn trends at 173.7 bu for 2026, 3rd highest of 20 in the state. 47% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
