Rain is at 103% of normal. Central region (9 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $6,642, average $5,517, low $4,247 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.40 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

1.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 152 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025.
