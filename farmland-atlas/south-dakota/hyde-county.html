Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 46% from $63 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Hyde County runs $92 an acre for 2026, up 46.4% from $63 in 2014. That sits under the state median of $112 and ranks 22nd lowest of 63. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,117 an acre in 2022, up 38.6% from $1,527 in 2017, or 6.8% a year. 57% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
