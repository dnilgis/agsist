Perkins County, South Dakota

Perkins County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,179 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46105. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Perkins County averages $39 per acre for 2026, up 69.6 percent from 2014. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $1,179 per acre, with a 41.9 percent change from 2017 to 2022, 7.2 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 62 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share moved unevenly across periods: 20 percent in 1989-1999, 70 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 74 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 292 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 59 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
