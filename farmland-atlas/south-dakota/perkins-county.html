Cash rent in Perkins County runs $39 an acre for 2026, up 69.6% from $23 in 2014, and that sits 9th lowest of 63 against a state median of $112. Pasture rents for $16 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,179 an acre in 2022, up 41.9% from $831 in 2017, or 7.2% a year. That ranks 9th lowest of 66 next to a state median of $3,173.
