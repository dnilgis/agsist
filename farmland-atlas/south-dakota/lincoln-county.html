Lincoln County cash rent runs $244 an acre in 2026, up 15.6% from $211 in 2016 and 3rd highest of 63 in South Dakota, where the median is $112. The census value of land and buildings came to $8,445 an acre in 2022, up 22.3% from $6,906 in 2017 and 2nd highest of 66. Corn made 187.5 bu in 2025, well over the 160.4 bu average for 2021 through 2025. Renters farm 61% of the ground.
