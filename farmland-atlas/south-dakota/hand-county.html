Cash rent in Hand County was $108 an acre in 2026, the same $108 it was in 2016, and 28th lowest of 63 against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings rose 39.8% from $2,056 in 2017 to $2,875 in 2022. Corn made 150.3 bu in 2024, above the 2020-24 average of 144.5 bu. 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
