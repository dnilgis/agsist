Non-irrigated cash rent in Hand County sits at $108 per acre in 2026, unchanged from 2016. Land value rose 39.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.9 percent a year, putting cash rent at 3.62 percent of land value. Drought has covered at least 50 percent of the county in severe conditions or worse for 96 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2004 the worst year at 19 weeks; excess moisture and flood, not heat, drive the largest share of indemnities at 44 percent. Wind operates at 225 MW; solar operating and proposed capacity are both 0 MW.
