As of Oct 14, cash rent in Meade County runs $31 an acre for 2026, up 6.9% from $29 in 2016 and 4th lowest of 63 counties in South Dakota against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,388 an acre in 2022, up 46.0% from $951 in 2017, still 15th lowest of 66. Corn is thin here, with a 14-yr trend of 74.8 bu that sits 2nd lowest of 20 against a state median of 147.8 bu.
