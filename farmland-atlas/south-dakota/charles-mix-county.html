Cash rent in Charles Mix County runs $142 an acre for 2026, up 4.4% from $136 in 2016. That ranks 27th highest of 63 in the state, against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,849 an acre in 2022, up 38.1% from $2,788 in 2017. Corn made 170.4 bu in 2025, well above the 18 year median of 136.9 bu.
