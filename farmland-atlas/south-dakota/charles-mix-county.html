Charles Mix County, South Dakota

Charles Mix County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $142 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,849 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46023. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Charles Mix County sits at $142 per acre in 2026, up 4.4 percent from 2016. Land value reached $3,849 per acre in 2022, up 38.1 percent from 2017, 6.7 percent a year, so rent equals 3.33 percent of land value.
