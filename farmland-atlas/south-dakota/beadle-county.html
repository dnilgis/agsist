Cropland rent in Beadle County runs $140 an acre for 2026, up 15.7% from $121 in 2016 and 28th highest of 63 against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,820 an acre in 2022, up 41.8% from $2,693 in 2017. Corn made 178.8 bu in 2025, well over the 137.7 bu median since 2008, and 52% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
