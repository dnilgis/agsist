Cash rent in Turner County runs $218 an acre for 2026, up 19.1% from $183 in 2016 and 6th highest of 63 in South Dakota, where the state median is $112. The census value of land and buildings came in at $7,125 an acre in 2022, up 42.0% from $5,019 in 2017 and 6th highest of 66. Corn made 192.0 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 154.6 bu. 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
