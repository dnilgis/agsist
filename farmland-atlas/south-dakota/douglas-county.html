Douglas County, South Dakota

Douglas County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $143 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,554 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Cropland rent in Douglas County runs $143 an acre for 2026, 26th highest of 63 in South Dakota and above the state median of $112. That rent is up 3.6% from $138 in 2016, so it has barely moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, climbing 22.4% to $4,554 an acre in 2022 from $3,721 in 2017. Corn came in at 172.3 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 144.7 bu.
