Codington County, South Dakota

Rain is at 92% of normal. Land and buildings run $4,311 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.54 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 20.09 inches, 92% of normal (21.78 inches) through Nov 17, at Watertown Regional Airport. Frost-free season: 154 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,360 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,311 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $175 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 14.4% from 2016 to 2026. 39.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 8.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out. 140.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,073 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.54 per base acre, PLC $3.57.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (142.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $47.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,193 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.05 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.61 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 49.3%.
