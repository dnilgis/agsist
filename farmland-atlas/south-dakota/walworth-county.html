Walworth County is a dryland county. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and the rest waits on rain. The heat and drought share of crop insurance claims went from 8 percent in 1989-1999 to 66 percent in 2020-2025, and the loss ratio is 0.49 over 1989-2025, with 5 of 25 crop years paying out more than premium. Corn yields have a trend of 2.5 bushels per acre a year, and the county's median is 125.6 bushels, with 2010 the worst year at 101.1. Non-irrigated cash rent is $105 per acre in 2026, up 21.4 percent since 2016. CRP covers 19.7 percent of the cropland base, and 3594 of those acres expire in fiscal years 2027-2029.
