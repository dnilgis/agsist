Walworth County has seen heat and drought claims grow sharply since 2000. These made up 8 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999 but jumped to 84 percent in 2000-2009, then settled to 66 percent in 2020-2025. The county paid out $99.3 million in indemnities over 1989-2025, with a loss ratio of 0.49. Corn yield has climbed 2.5 bushels per acre a year from 2008-2025, with a median of 125.6 bushels. Cash rent is $105 per acre in 2026, up 21.4 percent from 2016.
