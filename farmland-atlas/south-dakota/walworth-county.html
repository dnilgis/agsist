Walworth County, South Dakota

Walworth County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $105 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,513 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 125.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46129. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Walworth County averages $105 per acre for 2026, up 21.4 percent from 2016. Corn yields trend up 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 125.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.1 in 2010. Heat and drought drove 62 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with that share at 8 percent in 1989-1999, 84 percent in 2000-2009, 41 percent in 2010-2019, and 66 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks ran 148 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 45 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Solar and wind capacity, operating or proposed, are not present in this county.
