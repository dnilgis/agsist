Cropland rent in Oglala Lakota County was $34 an acre in 2024, up 39.6% from $24 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings came in at $871 an acre in 2022, up 29.2% from $674 in 2017, or 5.3% a year. That is the lowest of 66 counties in South Dakota, against a state median of $3,173. Renters worked 38% of the farmland in 2022.
