Oglala Lakota County, South Dakota

Oglala Lakota County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2024); land and buildings $871 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46102. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Oglala Lakota County averages $34 per acre in 2024, up 39.6 percent from 2012. Census land and buildings value stood at $871 per acre in 2022, itself up 29.2 percent from 2017, 5.3 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 51 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and that share was 70 percent in 2000-2009 and 70 percent again in 2020-2025, with 27 percent in 2010-2019 between them. The county spent 57 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2021-2025, out of 283 weeks over 2000-2025. July nights have averaged 60.8 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 59.7 F, with no clear direction in the longer trend.
