Oglala Lakota County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,550, average $1,850, low $1,413 an acre.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

80.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 151 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,615 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Quick answers for Oglala Lakota County

What is cash rent in Oglala Lakota County, South Dakota?

The county average is $34 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Oglala Lakota County, South Dakota?

Land and buildings were worth $871 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
