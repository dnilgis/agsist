Non-irrigated cash rent in McPherson County is $100 per acre for 2026, up 27.6 percent from 2016. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 117 bushels per acre and a worst year of 70.2 in 2021. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025 at 51 percent, and its share rose from 10 percent in 1989-1999 to 58 percent in 2000-2009, then 41 percent in 2010-2019, then 61 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland here, so this exposure runs through dryland yields and rent, not water infrastructure.
