USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Yankton County ran $194 an acre in 2026, up 8.4% from $179 in 2016. That is 9th highest of 63 in South Dakota, against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,549 an acre in 2022, up 25.5% from $4,422 in 2017. Corn made 188.0 bu in 2025, above the 169.8 bu median the county has held since 2008.
