Jerauld County, South Dakota

Jerauld County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $105 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,678 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 127.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46073. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jerauld County is $105 per acre in 2026, down 1.9 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $2,678 per acre, up 12 percent from 2017, and rent is 3.58 percent of that value. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 112 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 57 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
