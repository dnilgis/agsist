USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cash rent in Jerauld County is $105 an acre in 2026, tied for 26th lowest of 63 and under the $112 state median. That is down 1.9% from $107 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,678 an acre in 2022, up 12.0% from $2,391 in 2017. The corn trend sits at 154.5 bu for 2026, 6th highest of 20, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
