Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 44% from $71 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Lyman County runs $102 an acre for 2026, up 43.7% from $71 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $112 and ranks 25th lowest of 63. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,967 an acre in 2022, up 19.1% from $1,651 in 2017, against a state median of $3,173. Corn made 115.5 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 100.3 bu.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: NASS county corn yields. The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out.
