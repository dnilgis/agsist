Lyman County, South Dakota

Lyman County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $102 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,967 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 105.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46085. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lyman County averages $102 per acre for 2026, up 43.7 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $1,967 per acre, up 19.1 percent from 2017, 3.6 percent a year. Corn yields have a fitted trend of 0.6 bushels per acre a year on 2008-2025, with a median of 105 bushels per acre and a low of 46.6 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 58 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with that share at 7 percent in 1989-1999, 65 percent in 2000-2009, 52 percent in 2010-2019, and 67 percent in 2020-2025. Groundwater supplied 1 percent of irrigation water in 2015, and there is no solar or wind capacity operating or proposed in the county.
