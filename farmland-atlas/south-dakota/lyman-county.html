Cropland rent in Lyman County runs $102 an acre for 2026, up 43.7% from $71 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $112 and ranks 25th lowest of 63. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,967 an acre in 2022, up 19.1% from $1,651 in 2017, against a state median of $3,173. Corn made 115.5 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 100.3 bu.
