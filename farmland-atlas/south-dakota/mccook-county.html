McCook County, South Dakota

Rain is at 85% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,395 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 21.95 inches, 85% of normal (25.86 inches) through Nov 16, at Salem, 5 mi NE. Frost-free season: 159 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,665 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,395 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $214 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 28.1% from 2016 to 2026. 46.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 13% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 101.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 99 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,292 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.03 at Marion, 18 miles.

5 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 55 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.96 per base acre, PLC $3.48.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (138.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $57.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,345 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.73 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.99 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 42% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29%.
