Custer County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,550, average $1,850, low $1,413 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.08 per base acre for corn.

Custer County vs South Dakota: $28.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 3% of the other 62 South Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (3rd lowest of 63).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.44 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 77% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 144 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,276 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.08 per base acre, PLC $2.48.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (98.83 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $45.17 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $21.84 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $16.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.30 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.01, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 9.6 days a year and damaging wind on 6.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.2%.

Quick answers for Custer County

What is cash rent in Custer County, South Dakota?

The county average is $29 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Custer County, South Dakota?

Land and buildings were worth $1,832 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
