Haakon County, South Dakota

Haakon County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $54 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,313 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 75.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Haakon County runs $54 per acre, up 18.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $1,313 per acre, up 42.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought has driven a rising share of crop insurance indemnities: 6 percent in 1989-1999, 50 percent in 2000-2009, 56 percent in 2010-2019, and 77 percent in 2020-2025, within a total of $136.0 million paid out over 1989-2025. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 0.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 75.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 34.2 in 2012. Solar and wind operating capacity is 0 MW, with none proposed to EIA.
