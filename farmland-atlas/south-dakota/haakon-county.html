Haakon County, South Dakota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Southwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,550, average $1,850, low $1,413 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $4.75 per base acre for corn.

Haakon County vs South Dakota: $53.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 26% of the other 62 South Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (17th lowest of 63).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

49.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.14 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 146 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,624 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.50 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.81, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 46% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 6.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.3%.
