Cash rent in Bennett County runs $32 an acre in 2026, 5th lowest of 63 in South Dakota against a state median of $112. That rent is down 4.5% from $34 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 41.5% from $952 in 2017 to $1,347 in 2022, or 7.2% a year. Pasture rents for $17 an acre in 2026.
