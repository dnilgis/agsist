Non-irrigated cash rent in Bennett County runs $32 per acre, down 4.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought stand as the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 51 percent of $66.9 million paid out, and that share was 71 percent in 2020-2025, against 4 percent in 1989-1999. July nights average 60.4 F over 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 59.3 F, with a trend of 0.38 F per decade and no clear direction since 1976. The county logged 48 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2021-2025, against 249 weeks of 1357 for the full 2000-2025 stretch.
