Cash rent in Campbell County runs $80 an acre for 2026, up 13.6% from $70 in 2016. That sits 20th lowest of 63 in South Dakota, under the state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings was $2,316 an acre in 2022, up 26.5% from $1,831 in 2017. Corn made 153.8 bu in 2024, above the 2020-24 average of 133.7 bu.
