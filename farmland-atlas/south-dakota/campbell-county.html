Campbell County, South Dakota

Campbell County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $80 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,316 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 112.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46021. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Campbell County averages $80 per acre in 2026, up 13.6 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $2,316 per acre in 2022, up 26.5 percent from 2017, or 4.8 percent a year. Heat and drought have driven indemnities across closed crop years 1989-2025, at 63 percent of the $114.9 million paid, though the share moved differently across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse ran 160 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 63 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Wind operating capacity stands at 94.3 MW; solar operating capacity is 0 MW.
