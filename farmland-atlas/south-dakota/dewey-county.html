Dewey County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Northwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,175, average $1,731, low $1,417 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.56 per base acre for corn.

Dewey County vs South Dakota: $47 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 19% of the other 62 South Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (13th lowest of 63).
