As of Oct 16, cash rent in Jones County runs $36 an acre for 2026, 7th lowest of 63 in South Dakota, where the state median is $112. That rent is down 9.0% from $39 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $1,596 an acre in 2022, up 39.6% from $1,143 in 2017. Corn here has a median yield of 85.9 bu an acre over 2008 to 2020.
