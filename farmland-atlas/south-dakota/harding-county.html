Cash rent in Harding County runs $33 an acre for 2026, 6th lowest of 63 counties in South Dakota against a state median of $112. Pasture rents for $12 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $1,006 an acre in 2022, up 51.3% from $665 in 2017 and 2nd lowest of 66. The corn trend sits at 64.4 bu, the lowest of 20 counties reporting.
