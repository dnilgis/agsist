Stanley County, South Dakota

Rain is at 96% of normal. Southwest region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $2,550, average $1,850, low $1,413 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.52 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

24.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.72 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 155 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,677 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $19.48 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.75, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.7 days a year and damaging wind on 4.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.5%.
