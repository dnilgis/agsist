Minnehaha County, South Dakota

Minnehaha County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $221 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,346 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46099. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Minnehaha County averages $221 per acre in 2026, up 23.5 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $8,346 per acre in 2022, up 29.4 percent from 2017, or 5.3 percent a year, and 2022 rent equaled 2.41 percent of that value. Corn yields have trended up 2.5 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 172 bushels per acre and a worst year of 117.5 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has risen across each listed period: 3 percent in 1989-1999, 25 percent in 2000-2009, 42 percent in 2010-2019, and 56 percent in 2020-2025.
