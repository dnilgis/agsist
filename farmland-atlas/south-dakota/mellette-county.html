Mellette County, South Dakota

Rain is at 107% of normal. South Central region (6 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $4,414, average $3,519, low $3,250 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $4.19 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 21.46 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 107% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 154 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $23.43 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.08, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 57% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 62.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.3%.
