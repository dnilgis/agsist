Potter County, South Dakota

Potter County, SD: non-irrigated cash rent $111 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,861 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 121.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 46107. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Potter County averages $111 per acre for 2026, up 15.6 percent from 2016. The county logged 165 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2021 the worst year at 33 weeks, and 45 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Corn yield trend runs 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, against a median of 121.3 bushels per acre and a low of 93.3 in 2012. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
