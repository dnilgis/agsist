Potter County, South Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Central region (8 counties), SDSU survey 2026: non-irrigated cropland high $7,184, average $5,387, low $3,908 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.38 per base acre for corn.

Potter County vs South Dakota: $111 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 47% of the other 62 South Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (30th lowest of 63).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.24 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.72, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025.
