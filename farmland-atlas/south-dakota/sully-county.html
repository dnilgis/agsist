Cash rent in Sully County is $97 an acre for 2026, up 7.2% from $90 in 2016. That sits 23rd lowest of 63 against a state median of $112. The census value of land and buildings was $2,501 an acre in 2022, up 16.1% from $2,154 in 2017. The corn trend yield comes out at 148.2 bu for 2026, 10th highest of 20, after 143.8 bu in 2025.
