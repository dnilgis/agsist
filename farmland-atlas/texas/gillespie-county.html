Cropland rent in Gillespie County runs $18 an acre for 2026, up 24.1% from $15 in 2016 and tied for 26th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $3,993 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from $3,039 in 2017 and 77th highest of 246 in the state. Corn made 48.5 bu in 2025, well under the 74.5 bu median for 2010 to 2025.
