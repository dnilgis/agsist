Cash rent in Martin County runs $42 an acre for 2026, tied for 33rd highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $2,051 an acre in 2022, up 130.4% from $890 in 2017, or 18.2% a year. That value still sits under the state median of $2,782 and ranks 97th lowest of 246. In 2022, 40% of the farmland here was rented.
