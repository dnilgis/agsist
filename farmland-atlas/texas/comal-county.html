Comal County, Texas

Comal County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $13 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,507 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.06 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48091. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Comal County runs $12 per acre, up 13.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value carries at $5,507 per acre under the 2022 census estimate, up 61.3 percent from 2017 to 2022. Only 9 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so most production depends on rainfall. Drought has been a persistent presence: 465 of 1357 weeks since 2000 sat at severe drought or worse, with 162 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Heat and drought account for 88 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.45 F per decade since 1976.
