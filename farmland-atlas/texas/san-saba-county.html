Non-irrigated cash rent in San Saba County is $11 per acre, down 26.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value runs $3,033 per acre, up 23.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. The July average nightly low sits at 72.6 F for 2017-2026 against a 71.6 F normal for 1991-2020, warming at 0.42 F per decade since 1976. The county logged 354 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 78 of those weeks in 2021-2025. Irrigated cropland covers 16 percent of harvested acres.
