San Saba County cash rent sits at $11 an acre for 2026, tied for 3rd lowest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. That rent is down 26.7% from $15 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,033 an acre in 2022, up 23.8% from $2,449 in 2017, which works out to 4.4% a year.
