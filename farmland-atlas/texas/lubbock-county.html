Lubbock County, Texas

Lubbock County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,673 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48303. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland here rents for $76 per acre, non-irrigated for $42 per acre, and the census puts land and buildings at $2,673 per acre in 2022. All of the county's irrigation water comes from groundwater, and irrigated ground makes up 65 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Heat and drought has driven crop insurance losses, rising from 24 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 36 percent in 2000-2009, 70 percent in 2010-2019, and 66 percent in 2020-2025, out of $804.7 million paid across closed crop years.
