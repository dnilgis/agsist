Marion County, Texas

Marion County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $17 an acre (2016); land and buildings $2,245 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48315. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Census market value of land and buildings stood at $2,245 per acre in 2022, with land value change of 12.1 percent, 2.3 percent a year over 2017-2022. Drought pressure has been present but limited: 203 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks; the 2021-2025 stretch saw 23 weeks. July nights averaged 72.7 F in 2017-2026 against a 72.8 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.22 F per decade since 1976 and no clear direction.
