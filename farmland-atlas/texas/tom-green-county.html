USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cropland rent in Tom Green County runs $50 an acre in 2026, up 66.7% from $30 in 2016 and tied for 16th highest of 190 in Texas, where the median is $27. The census value of land and buildings was $2,271 an acre in 2022, up 44.5% from $1,572 in 2017, still under the state median of $2,782. Corn made 84.4 bu in 2025, short of the 2021-25 average of 107.7 bu.
