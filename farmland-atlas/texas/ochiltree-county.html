Non-irrigated cash rent in Ochiltree County runs $28 per acre in 2026, up 31 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents for $99 per acre, and 40 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, drawing 100 percent of its water from groundwater. Heat and drought drove 51 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and the county spent 73 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2021-2025. Census land value sits at $1,893 per acre, up 53.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. Wind operating capacity is 282.3 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
