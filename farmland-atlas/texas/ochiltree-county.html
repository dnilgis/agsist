Ochiltree County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,893 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.63 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

46.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,452 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,893 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 31% from 2016 to 2026. 59% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 166.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 15,069 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 52 cents over Dec '26 futures, median of 6 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.93 at Conley Tx, 8 miles. 10 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 100 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 4 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.63 per base acre, PLC $4.38.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (174.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 210 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $21.97 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.33, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. 39.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.5%.
