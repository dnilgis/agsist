Cash rent here runs $28 an acre in 2026, up 31.0% from $21 in 2016 and tied for 91st highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $1,893 an acre in 2022, up 53.7% from $1,232 in 2017, and that sits 81st lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. Corn came in at 178.0 bu in 2023, short of the 192.0 bu average for 2019 through 2023.
