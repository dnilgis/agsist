Stands out against its own record

$27

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest since 2013 (16 surveys on record since 2009); down 8.5% from $30 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Concho County was $27 an acre in 2026, down 8.5% from $30 in 2016 and right at the state median, tied for 94th highest of 190. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, up 48.7% to $2,193 an acre in 2022 from $1,475 in 2017. That still sits under the state median of $2,782, 105th lowest of 246. 62% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
