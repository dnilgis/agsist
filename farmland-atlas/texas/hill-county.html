Cash rent in Hill County runs $42 an acre for 2026, up 23.5% from $34 in 2016 and tied for 33rd highest of 190 counties in Texas. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,494 an acre in 2022, up 38.2% from $2,529 in 2017. Corn yields sit 5th highest of 26 counties, with the 14 year trend projecting 100.3 bu for 2026 and last year at 127.9 bu.
