Hill County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,494 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.43 per base acre for corn.

Hill County vs Texas: $42 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 82% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 33rd highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

52.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.92 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 27 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,181 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,494 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $42 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 45% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 23.5% from 2016 to 2026. 58% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 90.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,604.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,602 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 20,989 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.43 per base acre, PLC $2.29.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (91.06 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $20.12 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (11.95 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.93 per base acre, PLC $7.48. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.42 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 12 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $19.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.95, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 52% of the median yield. 5.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 24.5%.

Quick answers for Hill County

What is cash rent in Hill County, Texas?

The county average is $42 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Hill County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $3,494 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Hill County, Texas?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 100.3 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
