Cash rent in Delta County runs $24 an acre for 2026, down 9.4% from $27 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $27 and is tied for 76th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, climbing 110.6% from $1,968 an acre in 2017 to $4,144 in 2022, 68th highest of 246. 43% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
