Delta County, Texas

Delta County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,144 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.08 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48119. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Delta County averages $24 per acre, down 9.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value under the census stood at $4,144 per acre in 2022, up 110.6 percent from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows $44.2 million in indemnities, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 45 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.26, against 1.08 for all closed years 1989-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 56 percent in 1989-1999, 35 percent in 2000-2009, 26 percent in 2010-2019, then 54 percent in 2020-2025. Solar operating capacity is 204.5 MW, with 110.1 MW more proposed to EIA; wind operating and proposed capacity are both 0 MW.
