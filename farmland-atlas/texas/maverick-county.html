The census value of land and buildings in Maverick County was $1,705 an acre in 2022, up 21.4% from $1,405 in 2017. That sits under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 68th lowest of 246. Cash rent ran $15 an acre in 2016, irrigated ground went for $50 in 2019 and pasture brought $3 in 2024. 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
