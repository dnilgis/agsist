La Salle County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,050 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 22.35 inches, 102% of normal (21.89 inches) through Nov 17, at Cotulla, 1.6 mi NE. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,407 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,050 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 21% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 21.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 700 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,299 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $18.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

29.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.8%.

Cash rent in La Salle County ran $15 an acre in 2022, down 21.1% from $19 in 2011. Pasture rent was $6 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,050 an acre in 2022, up 29.4% from $1,584 in 2017. That sits 96th lowest of 246 in Texas, under the state median of $2,782.
