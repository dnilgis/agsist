As of Oct 16, Borden County cropland rented for $36 an acre in 2026, tied for 49th highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. Pasture went for $5 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $1,141 an acre in 2022, up 29.2% from $883 in 2017, yet still 11th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. Renters worked 43% of the farmland in 2022.
