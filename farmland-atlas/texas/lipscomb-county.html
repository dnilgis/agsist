Cropland rent in Lipscomb County runs $19 an acre in 2026, up 32.1% from $14 in 2016 and tied for 32nd lowest of 190 against a state median of $27. Irrigated ground rents for $113 an acre in 2026, 15th highest of 43. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,349 an acre in 2022, up 20.0% from $1,124 in 2017. Renters work 44% of the farmland here.
