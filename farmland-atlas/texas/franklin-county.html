Franklin County, Texas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,816 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Franklin County vs Texas: $26 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 43% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 83rd lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 261 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,001 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,816 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 10.6% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 343.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 409 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 7,682 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.00 per base acre, PLC $5.82. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.24 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 24, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $34.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 36.5%.
