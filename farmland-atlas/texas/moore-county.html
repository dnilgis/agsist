Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the highest since 2010 (17 published years on record since 2008).

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Moore County ran $29 an acre in 2025, up 28.9% from $23 in 2014 and 4th highest of 16 in the state. Irrigated ground rented for $146 an acre in 2026, 9th highest of 43. The census value of land and buildings was $1,836 an acre in 2022, up 61.9% from $1,134 in 2017, and still 77th lowest of 246. Corn made 226.9 bu in 2025, above the county median of 211.0 bu going back to 2008.
