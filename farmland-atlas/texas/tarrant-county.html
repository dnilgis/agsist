Tarrant County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,750 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $4.14 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

46.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 28.81 inches, 84% of normal (34.12 inches) through Nov 17, at Ft Worth Wsfo. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 27 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,218 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,750 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $29 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 31.8% from 2016 to 2026. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 59.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,442 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.14 per base acre, PLC $1.19.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (47.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 6.5 days a year and damaging wind on 7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2023; too much rain caused 36.4%.

Cropland rent in Tarrant County runs $29 an acre for 2026, up 31.8% from $22 in 2016 and tied for 84th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $6,750 an acre in 2022, up 10.6% from 2017. That ranks 14th highest of 246, well over the state median of $2,782. Only 20% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
