Tarrant County, Texas

Tarrant County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $29 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,750 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48439. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tarrant County is $29 per acre in 2026, up 31.8 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $6,750 per acre, up 10.6 percent from 2017. The county logged 280 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 57 weeks in 2021-2025. July nightly lows have warmed 0.5 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 75.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 74.6 F.
