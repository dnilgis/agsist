Mason County, Texas

Mason County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,655 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48319. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mason County is $22 per acre, up 53.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cash rent in 2022 was $115 per acre, and 71 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated, with 98 percent of that water from groundwater in 2015. July nights average 71.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 70.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.43 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $354,057, with heat and drought the largest cause at 59 percent overall and 81 percent of losses in 2020-2025. Land value rose 14.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 2.8 percent a year, reaching $2,655 per acre.
