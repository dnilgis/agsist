Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the lowest of 16 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Uvalde County is $20 an acre in 2026, down 22.0% from $25 in 2016 and tied for 38th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $2,157 an acre in 2022, up 66.1% from $1,299 in 2017 and still under the state median of $2,782. Corn made 93.5 bu in 2025, short of the 110.3 bu average for 2021 to 2025.
