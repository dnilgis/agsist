Uvalde County, Texas

Rain is at 180% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,157 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 43.31 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 180% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,242 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,157 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $19.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 22% from 2016 to 2026. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 38.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 480 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,908 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $20.10 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.19 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.10, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 53.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 53.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 17.6%.
