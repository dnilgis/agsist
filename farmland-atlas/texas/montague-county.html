Cropland rent in Montague County was $23 an acre in 2026, up 7.0% from $22 in 2016, and below the state median of $27, tied for 65th lowest of 190. Pasture went for $14 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings ran $3,879 an acre in 2022, up 42.0% from $2,732 in 2017, which put it 79th highest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. 34% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
