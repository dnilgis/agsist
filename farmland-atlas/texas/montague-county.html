Montague County, Texas

Montague County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,879 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48337. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Montague County runs $23 per acre, up 7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value is $3,879 per acre by the 2022 census operator estimate, up 42 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.3 percent a year. Only 13 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so most production depends on rainfall. Drought has been a recurring cost: 258 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2006 the worst year at 39 weeks, and 52 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $3.0 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 44 percent, and the loss ratio over that span is 1.47.
