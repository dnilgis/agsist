Knox County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,983 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.25 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 22.48 inches, 90% of normal (24.99 inches) through Nov 17, at Munday, 1 mi W. Frost-free season: 243 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,959 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,983 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 3.8% from 2016 to 2026. 50.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 38.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 450 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 38,539 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.25 per base acre, PLC $1.47.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (58.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $23.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.02 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.22 over the same years. 16.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 73.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.4%.
