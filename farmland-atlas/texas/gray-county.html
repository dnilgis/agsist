U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Gray County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $20 in 2016 and tied for 65th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $1,579 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from $1,144 in 2017, which ranks 59th lowest of 246. Corn made 154.3 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 189.4 bu. Renters farm 66% of the ground.
