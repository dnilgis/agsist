Cropland rent in Cochran County runs $34 an acre for 2026, up 21.4% from $28 in 2016 and tied for 57th highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $140 an acre in 2023, 2nd highest of 10. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,417 an acre in 2022, up 53.0% from $926 in 2017 but still 39th lowest of 246 in the state. 43% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
