Crosby County, Texas

Crosby County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $55 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,357 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48107. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Crosby County averages $55 per acre in 2026, up 41 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $69 per acre, a multiple of 1.25 over non-irrigated, down from 1.75 in 2009, with no clear direction in between. Heat and drought drive crop insurance losses here: 24 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 34 percent in 2000-2009, 69 percent in 2010-2019, and 58 percent in 2020-2025, out of $540.1 million paid across all closed years. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 is 1.68, above the 1.44 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record. July nights have warmed to 69.6 F on average for 2017-2026, above the 68.6 F normal, with 99 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater.
