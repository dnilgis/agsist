$55

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 41.0% from $39 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Crosby County runs $55 an acre for 2026, up 41.0% from $39 in 2016. That sits 12th highest of 190 counties in Texas, well over the state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $1,357 an acre in 2022, up 39.9% from $970 in 2017, yet still 34th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. 47% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
