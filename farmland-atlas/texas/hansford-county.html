Hansford County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,037 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.36 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

89.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 18.23 inches, 94% of normal (19.33 inches) through Nov 17, at Gruver. Frost-free season: 202 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,420 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,037 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $34 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 25.9% from 2016 to 2026. 58.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 442.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 15,612 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 55 cents over Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.96 at Texhoma, 28 miles. 7 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 100 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.36 per base acre, PLC $4.63.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (184.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.99 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.28 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. 58.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.4%.
