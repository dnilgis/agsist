Hansford County, Texas

Hansford County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,037 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 205.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48195. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland covers 59 percent of harvested acres here, and irrigated rent runs $160 per acre against $34 per acre non-irrigated, so water access sets the value of ground in Hansford County. All of that irrigation draws from groundwater. Non-irrigated rent is up 25.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed crop years is 1.28, above the 1.14 mark for the full 1989-2025 record. Wind operates at 1220.8 MW; solar is not present.
