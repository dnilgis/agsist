Cooke County, Texas

Cooke County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,391 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 76.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48097. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cooke County runs $31 per acre in 2026, up 55 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $4,391 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 32.8 percent, 5.8 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2010-2025 runs 0.9 bushels per acre a year, median 76 bushels per acre, with 2022 the worst year at 64.1. Heat and drought share of indemnities moved from 2 percent in 1989-1999 to 32 percent in 2000-2009, 37 percent in 2010-2019, and 34 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 221 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 36 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Solar operating capacity is 64.2 MW and wind operating capacity is 413.4 MW, with 150 MW solar proposed to EIA.
