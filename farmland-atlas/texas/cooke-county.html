$31

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 55% from $20 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Cooke County runs $31 an acre for 2026, up 55.0% from $20 in 2016 and tied for 70th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,391 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $3,307 in 2017 and 59th highest of 246 in the state. Corn trends at 89.2 bu, which sits 11th lowest of 26 counties, though 2025 came in at 116.3 bu.
