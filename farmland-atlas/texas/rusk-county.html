Rusk County, Texas

Rusk County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,819 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48401. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rusk County runs $15 per acre, up 30.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census value of land and buildings stood at $3,819 per acre in 2022, up 49.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.3 percent a year. July nights average 72.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.6 F, with a warming trend of 0.32 F per decade since 1976. Drought was present 152 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2011 with 51 weeks, and 24 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland. No solar or wind capacity operates now; 200 MW solar is proposed to EIA.
