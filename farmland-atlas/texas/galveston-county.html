Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.38 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 93% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,569 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $14.50 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value rose while next door fell; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 22% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 3.3% from 2016 to 2026. 51% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 165.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,008 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.52 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.93, 2016 to 2025. 18.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 11% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2024; too much rain caused 39.8%.
