The census value of land and buildings in Terrell County stood at $1,263 an acre in 2022, up 68.2% from $751 in 2017. That works out to 11.0% a year, and it was $389 back in 2012. Even so, it ranks 22nd lowest of 246 in Texas, well under the state median of $2,782. 57% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
