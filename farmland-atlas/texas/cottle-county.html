As of Oct 16, cash rent in Cottle County is $24 an acre in 2026, down 2.1% from 2016 and under the Texas median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $1,059 an acre in 2022, 5th lowest of 246 in the state, and down 4.6% from $1,110 in 2017. In 2022, 42% of the farmland here was rented.
