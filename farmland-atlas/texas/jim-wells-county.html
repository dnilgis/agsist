Jim Wells County, Texas

Jim Wells County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,446 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48249. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jim Wells County is $31 per acre, down 27.9 percent from 2014 to 2025. July nights average 75.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 74.6 F normal, warming 0.39 F per decade since 1976.
