Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.97 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 164% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,317 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,573 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $11 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.46 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 22% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 12.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 389.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,055 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.47 per base acre, PLC $1.80.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (71.72 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $15.85 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.15 per base acre, PLC $3.63. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20.13 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 76.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2023; too much rain caused 7.3%.
