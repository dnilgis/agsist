Edwards County, Texas

Edwards County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $5 an acre (2016); land and buildings $1,467 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48137. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in 2016 averaged $5 per acre. Land value in 2022 was $1,467 per acre, up 25.4 percent from 2017, or 4.6 percent a year. Sixty-three percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. The county spent 519 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 38.2 percent of that span, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks; from 2021-2025 it logged 87 such weeks. July nightly lows have warmed 0.41 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 71.4 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 70.5 F. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
