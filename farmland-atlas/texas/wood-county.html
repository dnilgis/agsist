Stands out against its own record

27 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2011 (52 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Wood County cropland rented for $19 an acre in 2026, down 11.9% from $21 in 2016 and tied for 32nd lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings ran the other way, reaching $5,395 an acre in 2022, up 60.5% from $3,362 in 2017. That put it 30th highest of 246 counties, well above the state median of $2,782. 25% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
