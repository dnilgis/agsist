Harrison County, Texas

Harrison County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,360 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48203. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Harrison County stands at $18 per acre in 2026, up 56.5 percent from 2016. Drought has touched the county 188 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks; 23 weeks fell in that category from 2021-2025. July nights average 72.7 F for 2017-2026, close to the 72.9 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.16 F per decade and no clear direction since 1976.
