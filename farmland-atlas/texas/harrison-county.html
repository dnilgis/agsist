Harrison County, Texas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,360 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Harrison County vs Texas: $18 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 13% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 26th lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.01 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 73% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 21 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,102 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,360 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.76 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 22% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 56.5% from 2016 to 2026. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 178.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 14,280 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $34.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Quick answers for Harrison County

What is cash rent in Harrison County, Texas?

The county average is $18 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Harrison County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $4,360 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
