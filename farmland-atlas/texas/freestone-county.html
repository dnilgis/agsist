Non-irrigated cash rent in Freestone County averages $25 per acre in 2026, up 138.1 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census sits at $4,299 per acre, with a 67.6 percent change from 2017 to 2022, or 10.9 percent a year. July nights average 74.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 73.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.64 F per decade, warming. Drought weeks were 205 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 49 weeks, and only 16 weeks from 2021-2025. Heat and drought caused 5 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 0 percent in 2010-2019, and 0 percent in 2020-2025.
