USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Castro County runs $49 an acre in 2026, up 75.0% from $28 in 2016 and tied for 18th highest of 190 counties in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $145 an acre in 2026, 10th highest of 43. The census value of land and buildings was $2,130 an acre in 2022, up 54.3% from 2017, which still sits 100th lowest of 246. Corn here has a median of 207.4 bu from 2008 to 2017.
