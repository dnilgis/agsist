Castro County, Texas

Castro County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $49 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,130 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 207.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48069. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland covers 57 percent of harvested acres here, drawing on groundwater for 100 percent of that irrigation. Non-irrigated cash rent is $49 per acre for 2026, up 75 percent from 2016, and irrigated rent runs $145 per acre. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities out of $444.6 million paid across closed crop years 1989-2025, at 47 percent, and its share has moved higher each period listed: 15 percent in 1989-1999, 36 percent in 2000-2009, 50 percent in 2010-2019, and 53 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 357 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 in severe drought or worse, with 102 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Census land value sits at $2,130 per acre for 2022, up 54.3 percent from 2017.
