Non-irrigated cash rent in Denton County runs $22 per acre, up 10 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land and buildings value stood at $12,287 per acre in 2022, up 28.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.2 percent a year. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and July nights have warmed from a 74.3 F normal to a 75.6 F average over 2017-2026, part of a 0.57 F per decade warming trend since 1976. Crop insurance shows $32.0 million paid out over 1989-2025, closed crop years, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 47 percent.
