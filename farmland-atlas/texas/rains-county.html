Rains County, Texas

Cash rent in Rains County is $22 an acre in 2026, up 69.2% from $13 in 2014. That is tied for 58th lowest of 190 in Texas, under the state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings went the other way on the list, $5,667 an acre in 2022, up 62.9% from $3,479 in 2017 and 23rd highest of 246. Rented ground made up 38% of the farmland in 2022.
