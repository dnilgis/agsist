Cropland in Hardeman County rents for $23 an acre in 2026, up 24.3% from $19 in 2016. That sits under the Texas median of $27 and ranks tied for 65th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,525 an acre in 2022, up 46.1% from $1,044 in 2017, still well short of the state median of $2,782. 35% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
