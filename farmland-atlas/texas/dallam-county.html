Dallam County, Texas

Dallam County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $21 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,909 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 179.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48111. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dallam County stands at $20 per acre in 2026, down 63.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent is $127 per acre, and irrigation covers 70 percent of harvested cropland, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015. Corn yields have moved down 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 179.9 bushels per acre and a low of 120 in 2011. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.75, and land value rose 68.3 percent from 2017 to 2022.
