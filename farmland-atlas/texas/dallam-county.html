As of Oct 16, cash rent in Dallam County was $21 an acre in 2026, down 63.1% from $56 in 2016 and tied for 48th lowest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rented for $127 an acre in 2026, 12th highest of 43 against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $1,909 an acre in 2022, up 68.3% from $1,134 in 2017.
