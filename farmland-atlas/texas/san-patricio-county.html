San Patricio County, Texas

San Patricio County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $84 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,388 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 81.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48409. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in San Patricio County stands at $84 per acre for 2026, up 24.6 percent from 2016. Cropland here runs on rain: only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and where irrigation exists, 98 percent of the water comes from groundwater. Corn yields show no trend either way from 2010-2025, with a median of 81.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 34.9 in 2022. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $408.8 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 83 percent; that share was 46 percent in 1989-1999, 97 percent in 2000-2009, 88 percent in 2010-2019, and 73 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 312 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 56 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
