As of Oct 16, cash rent in San Patricio County runs $84 an acre for 2026, the highest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. That is up 24.6% from $67 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,388 an acre in 2022, up 27.8% from 2017. That same year, 75% of the farmland here was rented.
