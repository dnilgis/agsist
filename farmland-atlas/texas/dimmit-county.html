Dimmit County, Texas

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Dimmit County was $18 an acre in 2020, up 20.7% from $15 in 2010. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,777 an acre in 2022, up 26.0% from $1,410 in 2017. That puts it 72nd lowest of 246 in Texas, well under the state median of $2,782. In 2022, 26% of the farmland was rented.
