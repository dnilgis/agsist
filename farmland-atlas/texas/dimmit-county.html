Dimmit County, Texas

Dimmit County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2020); land and buildings $1,777 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48127. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Cropland here leans on irrigation, with 53 percent of harvested cropland irrigated in 2022 and 94 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. July nights average 75.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 74.2 F, with a warming trend of 0.55 F per decade since 1976. Land value rose 26 percent from 2017 to 2022.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
