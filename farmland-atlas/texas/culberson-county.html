Culberson County, Texas

Culberson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $13 an acre (2013); land and buildings $1,192 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48109. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation covers 66 percent of harvested cropland here, and all of it draws on groundwater. July nights have warmed since 1976 at 0.69 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average low of 68.2 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 67 F. Land value rose 62 percent from 2017 to 2022, a period that also saw 182 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
