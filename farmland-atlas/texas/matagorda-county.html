Corn yield, all practices, 2025: the highest of 18 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Matagorda County runs $62 an acre for 2026, the 6th highest of 190 in Texas and well over the state median of $27. That is up 41.4% from $44 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,351 an acre in 2022, up 39.8% from $2,397 in 2017. Corn made 131.2 bu in 2025, better than the 104.2 bu median for 2008 through 2025.
