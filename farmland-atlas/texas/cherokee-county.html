Cherokee County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,007 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.90 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 86% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,120 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,007 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.66 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 8% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 24.1% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 181.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 102,975 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $34.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2 days a year and damaging wind on 4.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2021; too much rain caused 5.5%.
