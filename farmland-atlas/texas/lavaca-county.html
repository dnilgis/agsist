Lavaca County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,783 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.58 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 37.69 inches, 104% of normal (36.31 inches) through Nov 16, at Hallettsville, 2 mi N. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,352 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,783 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $28 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 93.1% from 2016 to 2026. 47.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 196.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,411 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.58 per base acre, PLC $1.57.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (62.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.24 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.10 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 42% of the median yield. 14.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.3%.
