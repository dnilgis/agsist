Cash rent in Sutton County runs $8 an acre for 2024, down 24.0% from $10 back in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,268 an acre in 2022, about flat against $1,270 in 2017 and well under the state median of $2,782. That puts the county 23rd lowest of 246 in Texas.
