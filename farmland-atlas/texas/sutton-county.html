Land

Land and buildings: $1,268 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $7.60 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 9% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 13.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 476 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Quick answers for Sutton County

What is cash rent in Sutton County, Texas?

The county average is $8 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sutton County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,268 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
