Non-irrigated cash rent in Bosque County runs $26 per acre, up 79.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value under the census stood at $3,405 per acre in 2022, up 37.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.5 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 50 percent of the $6.9 million in indemnities paid over 1989-2025, and that share climbed to 60 percent in 2020-2025 after running 45 percent, 44 percent, and 40 percent in the three prior decades.
