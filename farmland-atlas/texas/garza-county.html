Garza County, Texas

Garza County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $29 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,244 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.40 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48169. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Garza County averages $28 per acre, up 9.6 percent from 2014 to 2026. Heat and drought account for 80 percent of crop insurance indemnities across closed crop years 1989-2025, and that share was 72 percent in 1989-1999, 75 percent in 2000-2009, 87 percent in 2010-2019, and 77 percent in 2020-2025. The county saw 354 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 86 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. All irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Land value per the 2022 Census stood at $1,244 per acre, up 23.8 percent from 2017 to 2022.
