Lynn County, Texas

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

9.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.05 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 235 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,686 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,306 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $47 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.46 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 39% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 16% from 2014 to 2026. 55% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 21% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 15.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 250 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 616 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (12.02 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $16.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 803 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $40.65 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.21, 2016 to 2025. 36.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.2 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 74% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.6%.

Quick answers for Lynn County

What is cash rent in Lynn County, Texas?

The county average is $47 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $92 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Lynn County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,306 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
