Jackson County, Texas

Jackson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $56 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,107 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 104.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48239. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jackson County averages $56 per acre in 2026, up 14.3 percent from 2016. Corn yields have trended up 2.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 104.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 45.3 in 2009. Heat and drought account for 46 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and the county spent 44 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2021-2025. July nightly lows have warmed from a 74.7 F normal to 76.1 F over 2017-2026. Irrigation covers 7 percent of harvested cropland, drawn 99 percent from groundwater.
