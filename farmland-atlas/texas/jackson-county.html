Jackson County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,107 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.97 per base acre for corn.

Jackson County vs Texas: $56 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 95% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 10th highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

21.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.54 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 105% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,444 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,107 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $56 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 46% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 14.3% from 2016 to 2026. 59% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains under 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 136.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 206 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 47,035 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.97 per base acre, PLC $2.13.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (84.84 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $12.48 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.75 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (22.29 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.64 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.93, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 43% of the median yield. 6.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 46.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.5%.

Quick answers for Jackson County

What is cash rent in Jackson County, Texas?

The county average is $56 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Jackson County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $4,107 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Jackson County, Texas?

The county's 2021-25 average is 115.8 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
