Cash rent in Fort Bend County was $42 an acre in 2026, the same $42 it was in 2016. That ranks 36th highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $4,398 an acre in 2022, up 41.7% from $3,103 in 2017. Corn made 144.5 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 105.2 bu.
