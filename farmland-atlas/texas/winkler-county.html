Winkler County, Texas

Winkler County, TX: land and buildings $1,520 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48495. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value here is $1,520 per acre, up 35.6 percent over 2017-2022, or 6.3 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities for 2023-2025 totaled $382,390, mostly from freeze and frost at 67 percent, not heat, which with drought made up only 11 percent of the 2020-2025 losses. July nights average 71.6 F for 2017-2026 against a 70.3 F normal, part of a warming trend of 0.75 F per decade since 1976.
