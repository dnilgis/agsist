Cash rent in Madison County runs $15 an acre for 2025, down 23.1% from $20 in 2014. That sits under the state median of $24 and ranks tied for 3rd lowest of 16. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 34.4% from $2,996 in 2017 to $4,028 in 2022, which is 74th highest of 246 in Texas. 50% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
