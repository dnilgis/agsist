As of Oct 16, cropland in Nolan County rents for $27 an acre in 2026, right at the Texas median and tied for 94th highest of 190. Irrigated ground rented for $109 in 2023, tied for 3rd highest of 10. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,581 an acre in 2022, up 43.5% from $1,102 in 2017 and 60th lowest of 246 in the state. 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
