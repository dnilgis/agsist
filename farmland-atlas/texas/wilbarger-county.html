Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 17.3% from $26 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wilbarger County runs $31 an acre for 2026, up 17.3% from $26 in 2016. That is tied for 73rd highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $2,155 an acre in 2022, up 68.5% from $1,279 in 2017, and it sits 101st lowest of 246. Pasture rents for $17 an acre in 2026.
