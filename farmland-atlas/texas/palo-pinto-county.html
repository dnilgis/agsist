Cash rent in Palo Pinto County was $21 an acre in 2026, down 10.6% from $24 in 2016. That rent ranks tied for 52nd lowest of 190 in Texas, under the state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $2,869 an acre in 2022, up 17.6% from $2,440 in 2017. 40% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
