Palo Pinto County, Texas

Rain is at 102% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,869 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.02 per base acre for corn.

Palo Pinto County vs Texas: $21 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 27% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 52nd lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.50 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 250 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,044 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,869 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.42 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 9% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 10.6% from 2016 to 2026. 40% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 77.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.02 per base acre, PLC $0.80.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $7.07 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $3.88 per base acre, PLC $3.62. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20.07 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $27.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

5.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 78.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.4%.
