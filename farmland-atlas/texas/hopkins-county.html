Hopkins County, Texas

Hopkins County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,732 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48223. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hopkins County averages $30 per acre, up 15.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $4,732 per acre in 2022, after a change of 62.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 10.2 percent a year. Crop insurance history shows $4.4 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 65 percent. Heat and drought made up 7 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 31 percent in 2000-2009, 36 percent in 2010-2019, and 13 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 5 percent of harvested cropland. Solar capacity operating stands at 1105.9 MW, with 250 MW solar proposed and no wind.
