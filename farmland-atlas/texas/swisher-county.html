Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 81.4% from $22 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Swisher County runs $39 an acre for 2026, up 81.4% from $22 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $27 and ranks 40th highest of 190. Irrigated rent is $72 for 2026, under the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $1,428 an acre in 2022, up 36.5% from 2017 and still below the state median of $2,782.
