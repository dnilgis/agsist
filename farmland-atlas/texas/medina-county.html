Stands out against its own record

$21

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 17 surveys since 2008; down 10.6% from $24 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Medina County runs $21 an acre for 2026, down 10.6% from $24 in 2016 and tied for 52nd lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,805 an acre in 2022, up 42.3% from $2,674 in 2017. Corn made 90.2 bu in 2025, well short of the 117.1 bu county median for 2008 through 2025.
