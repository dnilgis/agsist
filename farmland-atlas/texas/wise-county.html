Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.67 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 255 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,060 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,259 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $15.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 30% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 6.1% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 154.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 126.2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,893 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 28.7%.

Quick answers for Wise County

What is cash rent in Wise County, Texas?

The county average is $16 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Wise County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $6,259 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
