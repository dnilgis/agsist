Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.84 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 158% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: at least 264 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,000 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,994 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 12.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

36.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Real County was $1,994 an acre in 2022, up from $1,921 in 2017 and $1,478 in 2012. The 3.8% gain from 2017 to 2022 works out to 0.7% a year. That 2022 value ranks 91st lowest of 246 in Texas, under the state median of $2,782. In 2022, 26% of the farmland was rented.
