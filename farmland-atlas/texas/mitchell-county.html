Mitchell County, Texas

Mitchell County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,428 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48335. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mitchell County runs $23 per acre, up 17.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Crop insurance tells the harder story: heat and drought caused 77 percent of $186.5 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.62, with $58.00 paid per $100 of insured value in that span. Drought weeks confirm the pattern, 310 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse since 2000, with 83 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. July nights have warmed too, averaging 72.4 F over 2017-2026 against a 71.3 F normal, a 0.5 F per decade rise since 1976. Land values rose 43.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, to $1,428 per acre by the operators' own estimate.
