Cropland rent in Mitchell County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $20 in 2016 and under the state median of $27. That puts it tied for 65th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $1,428 an acre in 2022, up 43.2% from $997 in 2017. 67% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
