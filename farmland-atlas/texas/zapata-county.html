Zapata County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,569 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.10 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 123% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,570 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,569 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.35 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 14% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 43 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 56,433 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $18.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $62.17 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.33, 2016 to 2025. 89.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 21.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 2005 to 2025; too much rain caused 52.5%.
