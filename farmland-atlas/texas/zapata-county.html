Zapata County, Texas

Zapata County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2023); land and buildings $1,569 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48505. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent averaged $50 per acre in 2023. Irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $45 per acre. Cropland here leans heavily on irrigation, with 89 percent of harvested cropland irrigated as of the 2022 census, and indemnities for irrigation failure across all closed years total $798,854. The July nightly low has warmed since 1976 at 0.39 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 76.4 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 75.5 F. Excess moisture and flood account for 53 percent of the $6.2 million paid in indemnities over the closed crop years 2005-2025, more than heat and drought, which fell from 33 percent of indemnities in 2010-2019 to 12 percent in 2020-2025.
