Nueces County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,963 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $4.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

76.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 29.6 inches, 102% of normal (29.06 inches) through Nov 17, at Corpus Christi International Airport. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,565 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,963 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $70 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 14.8% from 2016 to 2026. 59.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 13.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 500 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 16,392 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.77 per base acre, PLC $1.59.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (63.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $15.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.76 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.50 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 45% of the median yield. 0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 82.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.7%.
