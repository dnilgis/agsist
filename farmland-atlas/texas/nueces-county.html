Cropland rent in Nueces County runs $70 an acre for 2026, up 14.8% from $61 in 2016 and 2nd highest of 190 counties in Texas against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $2,963 an acre in 2022, down 2.9% from $3,052 in 2017. Corn on the 14-yr trend projects 109.8 bu for 2026, 2nd highest of 26, and 60% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
