Milam County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,490 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.67 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 34.77 inches, 100% of normal (34.89 inches) through Nov 18, at Gause, 2.8 mi NNW. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 29 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,194 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,490 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $48 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 6.7% from 2016 to 2026. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 202.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,516.9 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,866 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.67 per base acre, PLC $2.09.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (83.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (26.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.90 per base acre, PLC $6.37. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.60 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.78, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 35% of the median yield. 2.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 24.3%.
