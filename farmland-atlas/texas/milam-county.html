Cropland in Milam County rents for $48 an acre in 2026, up 6.7% from $45 in 2016 and tied for 21st highest of 190 counties in Texas. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,490 an acre in 2022, up 42.0% from $3,163 in 2017, and that is 53rd highest of 246. Corn made 114.1 bu in 2024, above the 2020-24 average of 99.8 bu and well over the 82.3 bu long run median.
