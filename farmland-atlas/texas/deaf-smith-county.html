Deaf Smith County, Texas

Deaf Smith County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,838 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48117. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland in Deaf Smith County rents at $101 per acre against $32 per acre non-irrigated, and irrigation runs entirely on groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought has driven the largest share of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and its share by period has moved 36 percent, 50 percent, 66 percent, 69 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The county spent 102 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, with 2022 alone reaching 52 weeks. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 64 F to 65.6 F over 2017-2026, and the 2020s is the first decade with a July average low reaching 66 F. Non-irrigated rent rose 37 percent from 2016 to 2026, and land value rose 62.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
