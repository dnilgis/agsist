Cash rent in Deaf Smith County runs $32 an acre in 2026, up 37.0% from $23 in 2016 and tied for 67th highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $101 in 2026, a touch over the $99 state median. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,838 an acre in 2022, up 62.9% from $1,128 in 2017. Corn made 211.2 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 178.6 bu.
