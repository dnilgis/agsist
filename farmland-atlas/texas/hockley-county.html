Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.55 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 217 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,516 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,594 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $45.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 23% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 19.7% from 2016 to 2026. 51% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 13.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 385 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $42.64 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.42, 2016 to 2025. 42.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 56.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.7%.

Quick answers for Hockley County

What is cash rent in Hockley County, Texas?

The county average is $46 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $75 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Hockley County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,594 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
