Cash rent in Blanco County runs $14 an acre for 2026, tied for 11th lowest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. Rent was $14 in 2016 too and is up 3.7% over the ten years. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,342 an acre in 2022, up 12.1% from $2,982 in 2017. In 2022, 39% of the farmland was rented.
