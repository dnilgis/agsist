Cropland rent in Fisher County runs $31 an acre for 2026, up 24.0% from $25 in 2016. That sits a bit over the state median of $27 and ranks tied for 70th highest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $1,380 an acre in 2022, up 22.0% from $1,131 in 2017, and ranks 38th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
