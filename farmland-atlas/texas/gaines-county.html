Cropland rent in Gaines County runs $51 an acre in 2026, up 68.3% from $30 in 2016 and 15th highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $190 an acre in 2026, 2nd highest of 43. The census value of land and buildings was $1,436 an acre in 2022, up 24.3% from $1,155 in 2017, yet still 44th lowest of 246 in the state.
