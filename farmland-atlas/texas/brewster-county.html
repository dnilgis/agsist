The census value of land and buildings in Brewster County was $1,376 an acre in 2022, up 102.1% from $681 in 2017. That works out to 15.1% a year, and it was $469 back in 2012. Even so, that value sits under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 37th lowest of 246. In 2022, 55% of the county's farmland was rented.
