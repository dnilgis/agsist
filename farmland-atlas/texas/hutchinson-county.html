As of Oct 16, cash rent in Hutchinson County runs $20 an acre in 2025, down 4.8% from $21 in 2014 and under the state median of $24, 7th lowest of 16. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,274 an acre in 2022, up 31.6% from $968 in 2017 and tied for 24th lowest of 246. Corn has held a median of 194.2 bu from 2008 to 2022, and 63% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
