Hutchinson County, Texas

Hutchinson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,274 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 194.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48233. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigation covers 51 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, drawing 96 percent of its water from groundwater as of 2015. Drought pressure has been present: 301 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2022 the worst year at 48 weeks. Land value rose 31.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
