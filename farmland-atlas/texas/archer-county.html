Archer County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,017 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $3.07 per base acre for corn.

Archer County vs Texas: $33.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 68% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (61st highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 26.40 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 252 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,028 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,017 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $33.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 28% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 18% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 21.8% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 128.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $3.07 per base acre, PLC $0.95.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $26.51 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $8.40 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 19 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $37.64 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.36, 2016 to 2025. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.7 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 76.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.8%.

Quick answers for Archer County

What is cash rent in Archer County, Texas?

The county average is $34 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Archer County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $2,017 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
