Cropland rent in Archer County runs $34 an acre for 2026, up 21.8% from $28 in 2016 and above the Texas median of $27, which puts it 61st highest of 190. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,017 an acre in 2022, up 35.7% from $1,486 in 2017, or 6.3% a year. That still sits under the state median of $2,782 and ranks 93rd lowest of 246. 39% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
