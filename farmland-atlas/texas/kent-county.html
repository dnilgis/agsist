Cash rent in Kent County ran $10 an acre in 2024, down 47.0% from $19 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 105.1% to $1,758 an acre in 2022 from $857 in 2017. That value still sits under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 71st lowest of 246.
