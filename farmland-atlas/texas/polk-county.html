Polk County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,083 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

96.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 36.38 inches, 81% of normal (45.01 inches) through Nov 17, at Livingston, 8.4 mi W. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 29 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,198 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,083 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $16 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 26.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 106.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 170 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 273,933 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $31.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.
