Bowie County, Texas

Bowie County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,614 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.33 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48037. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bowie County stands at $24 per acre, up 17.5 percent from 2016 to 2026. Excess moisture and flood account for 59 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, closed crop years, the largest cause listed, with total indemnities of $47.6 million. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 29 percent in 1989-1999, to 27 percent in 2000-2009, to 23 percent in 2010-2019, to 38 percent in 2020-2025. The July average nightly low has moved from 71.7 F in the 1991-2020 normal to 73.3 F over 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 was $3,614 per acre, up 20.3 percent from 2017 to 2022.
