Non-irrigated cash rent in Gregg County ran $16 per acre in 2020. Census value of land and buildings stood at $6,992 per acre in 2022, with land value change 2017-2022 at 23.8 percent, 4.4 percent a year. Irrigated cropland share was 3 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census. Drought was present but not constant: the county spent 182 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 51 weeks, and only 18 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights average 72.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 72.7 F normal for 1991-2020, with no clear direction in the trend since 1976.
