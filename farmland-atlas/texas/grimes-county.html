Grimes County, Texas

Grimes County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,448 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48185. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Grimes County stands at $18 per acre in 2026, up 28.6 percent from 2016. July nights average 74.5 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.3 F, with warming of 0.61 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 175 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 52 weeks in 2011 alone.
