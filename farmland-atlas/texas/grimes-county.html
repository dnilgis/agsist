Cropland in Grimes County rented for $18 an acre in 2026, up 28.6% from $14 in 2016. That sits under the Texas median of $27 and comes in tied for 26th lowest of 190. Pasture rented for $17 an acre in 2026, close to the cropland figure. 20% of farmland here was rented in 2022, so the rest was worked by the folks who hold it.
