Cash rent in Wheeler County runs $21 an acre in 2026, up 100.0% from $11 in 2016. That still sits under the state median of $27, tied for 52nd lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,373 an acre in 2022, up 26.1% from $1,089 in 2017, and 36th lowest of 246 in Texas. Renters work 46% of the farmland here.
