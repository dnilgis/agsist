Cash rent in Motley County ran $17 an acre in 2024, up 50.0% from $11 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $1,118 an acre in 2022, up 32.0% from $847 in 2017. That value sits under the state median of $2,782 and ranks 9th lowest of 246 in Texas. In 2022, 34% of the farmland here was rented.
