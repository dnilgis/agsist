Stands out against its own record

20 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cash rent in Brooks County was $8 an acre in 2023, down 46.0% from $15 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 25.3% to $1,945 an acre in 2022 from $1,552 in 2017. That value ranks 86th lowest of 246 in Texas, under the state median of $2,782.
