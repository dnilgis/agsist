Cropland in Red River County rents for $16 an acre in 2026, tied for 16th lowest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. Pasture rents for $15 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,398 an acre in 2022, up 76.3% from $1,927 in 2017, or 12.0% a year, and it sits 102nd highest of 246 in the state. Renters work 22% of the farmland here.
