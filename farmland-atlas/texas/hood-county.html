Hood County, Texas

Hood County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,910 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48221. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hood County runs $28 per acre for 2026, up 55.6 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $5,910 per acre in the 2022 census, up 72.4 percent from 2017, 11.5 percent a year. July nights average 74.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 73 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.44 F per decade since 1976. Drought has touched the county in 288 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2014 the worst year at 43 weeks and 57 weeks logged from 2021-2025. Irrigated cropland is a small part of the base, at 14 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census.
