Stands out against its own record

$57

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 232.4% from $17 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Colorado County runs $57 an acre for 2026, 9th highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. That is up 232.4% from $17 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,396 an acre in 2022, up 46.1% from $3,009 in 2017. Corn made 139.6 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 102.2 bu going back to 2008.
