Victoria County, Texas

Victoria County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,797 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 95.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48469. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Victoria County sits at $36 per acre for 2026, unchanged from 2016. Land value carries a different story: $3,797 per acre in 2022, up 30 percent from 2017, a rate of 5.4 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 95.9 bushels per acre and a low of 55 in 2009. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 63 percent, 50 percent, 35 percent, 44 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. July nights average 75.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 74.4 F normal for 1991-2020, and the county logged 56 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
