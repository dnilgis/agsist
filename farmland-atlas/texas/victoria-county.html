Cash rent in Victoria County runs $36 an acre for 2026, the same $36 it was in 2016, and 48th highest of 190 against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,797 an acre in 2022, up 30.0% from $2,920 in 2017. The corn trend sits at 115.4 bu, highest of 26 in the state, and 63% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
