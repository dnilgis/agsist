Cropland rent in Rockwall County was $26 an acre in 2025, against a state median of $24, which ranks 7th highest of 16. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,370 an acre in 2022, up 33.1% from $5,536 in 2017, and that ranks 9th highest of 246 in Texas. 54% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
