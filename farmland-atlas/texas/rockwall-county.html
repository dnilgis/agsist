Rockwall County, Texas

Rockwall County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $26 an acre (2025); land and buildings $7,370 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48397. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Crop insurance shows $10.0 million in indemnities across closed crop years 1989-2025, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 67 percent. Heat and drought's share moved 22 percent, 33 percent, 11 percent, 27 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.47 F per decade since 1976, with the 2017-2026 average at 74.9 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.5 F. Severe drought or worse covered 233 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2006 the worst year at 48 weeks.
