Loving County, Texas

Loving County, TX: land and buildings $1,671 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48301. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Loving County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 71.6 F for 2017-2026, against a 70.2 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.73 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been a recurring visitor: 338 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, and 107 weeks did in 2021-2025 alone, with 2011 the worst year at 48 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here, per EIA.
