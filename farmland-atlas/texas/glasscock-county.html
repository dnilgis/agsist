Cropland rent in Glasscock County runs $35 an acre for 2026, up 15.0% from $30 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $27 and ranks 56th highest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $1,420 an acre in 2022, up 54.5% from $919 in 2017, yet still 40th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. Renters work 48% of the county's farmland.
