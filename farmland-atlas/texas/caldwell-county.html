Non-irrigated cash rent in Caldwell County stands at $33 per acre in 2026, up 65 percent from 2014. The county spent 101 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 375 weeks of the same designation across 2000-2025. July nightly lows have warmed 0.58 F per decade since 1976, running 74.8 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.3 F. Irrigation covers 5 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
