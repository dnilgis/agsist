Cash rent in Hall County was $27 an acre in 2025, 5th highest of 16 and above the state median of $24. That rent is down 23.2% from $35 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,274 an acre in 2022, up 38.6% from $919 in 2017, and still tied for 24th lowest of 246 in Texas.
