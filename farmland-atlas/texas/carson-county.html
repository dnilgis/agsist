Carson County, Texas

Carson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $48 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,561 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 186.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48065. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carson County is $48 per acre for 2026, up 100 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $80 per acre in 2025, and 41 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 100 percent of its water from groundwater as of 2015. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $250.8 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 50 percent; that share moved from 37 percent in 1989-1999 to 60 percent in 2000-2009, 55 percent in 2010-2019, and 42 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 52 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2021-2025, against 325 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse for 2000-2025. Census land and building value stands at $1,561 per acre for 2022, up 14.9 percent from 2017.
