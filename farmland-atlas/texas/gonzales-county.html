Gonzales County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,484 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.09 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 32.01 inches, 98% of normal (32.82 inches) through Nov 16, at Gonzales, 4.4 mi NNW.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,484 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet.

Grain

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.09 per base acre, PLC $2.01.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (80.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.87 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.65 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 31% of the median yield. 17.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).

Cropland rent in Gonzales County runs $27 an acre for 2026, up 107.7% from $13 in 2016 and right at the state median, tied for 94th highest of 190. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,484 an acre in 2022, up 41.0% from $3,180 in 2017, and that ranks 54th highest of 246. Corn trends at 90.0 bu for 2026, a bit under the state median of 94.0.
