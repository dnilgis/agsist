Gonzales County, Texas

Gonzales County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,484 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 95.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48177. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Gonzales County stands at $27 per acre, up 107.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. July nights average 75.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.6 F, with a warming trend of 0.64 F per decade since 1976. Drought reached severe levels in 359 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 110 weeks from 2021-2025. Heat and drought caused 89 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025.
