Haskell County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,928 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 19.02 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 244 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,988 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,928 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 20.8% from 2016 to 2026. 50% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 36.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,790.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 21,841 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.20 per base acre, PLC $1.20.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (47.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $23.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 49 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $43.04 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.35, 2016 to 2025. 12.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 74.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.9%.
