Haskell County, Texas

Haskell County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,928 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48207. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Haskell County is $32 per acre, up 20.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigation covers 12 percent of harvested cropland, drawn entirely from groundwater as of 2015. The county spent 339 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 39 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
