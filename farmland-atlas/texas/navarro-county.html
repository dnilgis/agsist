As of Oct 16, cash rent in Navarro County runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 37.7% from $27 in 2016 and tied for 46th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,770 an acre in 2022, up 60.5% from $2,349 in 2017, or 9.9% a year. Corn made 119.0 bu in 2025, against a 2021-25 average of 98.7 bu.
