Cropland rent in Runnels County runs $35 an acre for 2026, up 16.7% from $30 in 2016 and tied for 54th highest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,978 an acre in 2022, up 43.0% from $1,383 in 2017. That still sits at 89th lowest of 246, under the state median of $2,782. Renters work 44% of the farmland.
