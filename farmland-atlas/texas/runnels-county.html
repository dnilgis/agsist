Non-irrigated cash rent in Runnels County averages $35 per acre in 2026, up 16.7 percent from 2016. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $297.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 83 percent. That cause's share moved 47 percent, 91 percent, 84 percent, 82 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The county spent 271 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 60 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. July nights have warmed at 0.37 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 71.4 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 71 F.
