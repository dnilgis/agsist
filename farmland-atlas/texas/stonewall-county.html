$36

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 10 surveys since 2009.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Stonewall County rents for $36 an acre in 2026, tied for 49th highest of 190 counties in Texas and above the state median of $27. Pasture goes for $9 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $1,450 an acre in 2022, up 58.6% from $914 in 2017 and still 46th lowest of 246 in the state. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
