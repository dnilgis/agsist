As of Oct 16, cash rent in Grayson County ran $32 an acre in 2026, up 50.0% from $21 in 2014 and tied for 67th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $7,774 an acre in 2022, 7th highest of 246 in the state and well above the $2,782 state median. A lot of it is farmed by renters, with 60% of farmland rented in 2022.
