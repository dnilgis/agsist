Grayson County, Texas

Grayson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,774 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 72.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48181. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Grayson County runs $32 per acre, up 50 percent since 2014. Land value came in at $7,774 per acre in 2022, up 14.8 percent from 2017, 2.8 percent a year. Cropland here is mostly rain-fed: only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Corn yield has trended up 1.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 72.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 51.5 in 2015.
