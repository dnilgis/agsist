Bexar County, Texas

Rain is at 121% of normal. Land and buildings run $7,540 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

11.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.96 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 121% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,278 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,540 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.42 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 29% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 13% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 71.4% from 2016 to 2026. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 127.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,072 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $39.70 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.35 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.08 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.00, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. 13.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 75.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7%.
