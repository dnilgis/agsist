Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 71% from $18 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 14, cash rent in Bexar County was $30 an acre in 2026, up 71.4% from $18 in 2016 and tied for 77th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings ran $7,540 an acre in 2022, up 27.0% from $5,939 in 2017 and 8th highest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. Corn made 61.9 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 68.1 bu.
