Non-irrigated cropland here rents for $16 per acre against a census land value of $4,564 per acre. All harvested cropland is dryland; 0 percent is irrigated, 2022 census. Drought has touched this county in 149 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks; 37 weeks came in 2021-2025. July nights average 72.6 F for 2017-2026, above the 71.6 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.46 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
