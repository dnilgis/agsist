Schleicher County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,569 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 25.72 inches, 133% of normal (19.41 inches) through Nov 17, at Eldorado. Frost-free season: 234 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,898 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,569 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 39.1% from 2016 to 2026. 47.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 21.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 21,694 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $57.89 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.59 over the same years. 78.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 0.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 86.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.6%.
