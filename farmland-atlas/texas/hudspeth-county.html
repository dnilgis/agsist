As of Oct 16, irrigated cropland in Hudspeth County rents for $106 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $1,068 an acre in 2022, up 18.3% from $903 in 2017, or 3.4% a year. That sits well under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 6th lowest of 246. 55% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
