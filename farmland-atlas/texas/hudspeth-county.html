Hudspeth County, Texas

Hudspeth County, TX: irrigated cash rent $106 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,068 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 2.05 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48229. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Water drives this county. Irrigation supplies 90 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 91 percent of that water, so the aquifer carries the operation. Crop insurance history shows irrigation failure as the largest cause of loss at 66 percent of $95.6 million in indemnities, with $62.7 million paid for that cause alone. The heat and drought share of indemnities has moved from 1 percent in 1989-1999 to 3 percent in 2000-2009 to 7 percent in 2010-2019 to 25 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 387 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 137 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
