As of Oct 16, cash rent in Roberts County was $10 an acre in 2022, down 31.4% from $14 in 2012. The census value of land and buildings was $950 an acre in 2022, up 24.8% from $761 in 2017, or 4.5% a year. That census value ranks lowest of 246 in Texas, against a state median of $2,782.
