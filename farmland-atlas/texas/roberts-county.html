Roberts County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $950 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.57 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

33.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 208 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,526 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $950 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 33.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 500 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,593 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 25: $4.95 at Holt Tx, 25 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.57 per base acre, PLC $4.82.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (192.05 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $93.81 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $42.44 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.31 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.12, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10%.

Quick answers for Roberts County

What is farmland worth in Roberts County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $950 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
