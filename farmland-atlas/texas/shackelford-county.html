Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 73.9% from $12 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Shackelford County runs $20 an acre for 2026, up 73.9% from $12 in 2016. That still sits under the Texas median of $27 and ranks tied for 42nd lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $1,885 an acre in 2022, up 46.1% from $1,290 in 2017. 62% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
