Lamb County, Texas

Rain is at 100% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,505 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 17.99 inches, 100% of normal (17.91 inches) through Nov 17, at Olton. Frost-free season: 204 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,419 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,505 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $66 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 45.6% from 2016 to 2026. 37.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 17.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 306.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 29,330 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (27.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $16.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 708 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $45.05 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.73 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. 55.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.7%.
