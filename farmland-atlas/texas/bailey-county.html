$48

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 28.0% from $38 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Bailey County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 28.0% from $38 in 2016 and tied for 21st highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $124 an acre in 2026, 13th highest of 43, against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,912 an acre in 2022, up 124.4% from $852 in 2017, though still 83rd lowest of 246 in the state.
