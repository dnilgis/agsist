Crane County, Texas

Crane County, TX: land and buildings $1,444 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in Crane County stands at $1,444 per acre in 2022, up 70.3 percent from 2017, a change of 11.2 percent a year. July nights average 72.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.9 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.63 F per decade, warming. Drought has covered at least 50 percent of the county for 323 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 50 weeks, and 81 weeks from 2021-2025. Solar operating capacity is 150 MW; wind operating is 0 MW; no wind or solar is proposed to EIA.
