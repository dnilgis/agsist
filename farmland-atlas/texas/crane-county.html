As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Crane County was $1,444 an acre in 2022, up 70.3% from $848 in 2017. That works out to 11.2% a year, and the figure was $590 back in 2012. It still sits well under the Texas state median of $2,782 and ranks 45th lowest of 246. In 2022, 71% of the farmland here was rented.
