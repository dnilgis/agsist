Cash rent in DeWitt County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 55.2% from $15 in 2014 and 64th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $3,823 an acre in 2022, up 24.4% from $3,072 in 2017. That sits 80th highest of 246 and above the state median of $2,782.
