Potter County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,217 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 18.02 inches, 96% of normal (18.68 inches) through Nov 17, at Amarillo Airport. Frost-free season: 208 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,463 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,217 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $37 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 138.7% from 2016 to 2026. 71% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 65.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 167.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,178 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $39.31 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.43, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.8 days a year and damaging wind on 6.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 80.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1%.

Stands out against its own record

$37

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2009; up 138.7% from $16 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Potter County runs $37 an acre for 2026, up 138.7% from $16 in 2016. That puts it tied for 43rd highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings was $1,217 an acre in 2022, up 19.8% from $1,016 in 2017, and it sits 19th lowest of 246 in the state. Renters work 70% of the farmland here.
