Cash rent in Limestone County is $16 an acre for 2025, 5th lowest of 16 in the state, where the 2025 median is $24. That rent is down 38.5% from $26 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,658 an acre in 2022, up 44.3% from $1,842 in 2017. The corn trend for 2026 sits at 95.0 bu, 9th highest of 26.
