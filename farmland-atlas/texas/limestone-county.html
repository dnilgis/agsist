Limestone County, Texas

Limestone County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $16 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,658 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 82.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48293. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Limestone County averages $16 per acre in 2025, down 38.5 percent from 2014. Land value, by the operators' own estimate, stands at $2,658 per acre in 2022, up 44.3 percent from 2017, 7.6 percent a year. Corn yields trend upward at 2.2 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2024, with a median of 82 bushels per acre and a worst year of 34.3 in 2015. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has fallen across every decade listed: 75 percent in 1989-1999, 44 percent in 2000-2009, 34 percent in 2010-2019, 33 percent in 2020-2025. The county carried a loss ratio of 0.96 over 1989-2025, closed crop years, and 1.1 over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025.
