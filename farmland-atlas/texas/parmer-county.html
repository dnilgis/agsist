As of Oct 16, cash rent in Parmer County runs $37 an acre in 2026, up 32.7% from $28 in 2016 and tied for 46th highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $116 an acre in 2026, 14th highest of 43. The census value of land and buildings was $1,651 an acre in 2022, up 46.8% from $1,125 in 2017, and still 65th lowest of 246. Corn came in at 120.5 bu in 2024, well under the 171.3 bu median since 2008.
