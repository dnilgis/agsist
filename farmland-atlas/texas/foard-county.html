Foard County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,694 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 24.13 inches, 95% of normal (25.49 inches) through Nov 18, at Crowell. Frost-free season: 243 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,902 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,694 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 43.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 37,295 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $2.97 per base acre, PLC $3.59. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $23.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $41.50 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.17, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 79.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.4%.

Cash rent in Foard County was $19 an acre in 2025, down 27.5% from $26 in 2014. That sits under the state median of $24 and ranks 6th lowest of 16. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 51.8% from $1,116 an acre in 2017 to $1,694 in 2022, 8.7% a year. That 2022 value ranks 67th lowest of 246 in Texas, where the median is $2,782.
