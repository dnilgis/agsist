Bee County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,674 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 29.71 inches, 103% of normal (28.89 inches) through Nov 17, at Beeville, 9 mi S. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,417 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,674 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $56 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 40% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 69.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 274.8 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,521 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (12 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $21.95 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.89, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 35% of the median yield. 7.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 78.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.1%.
