Pecos County, Texas

Pecos County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $5 an acre (2017); land and buildings $1,178 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.19 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48371. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent averaged $5 per acre in 2017; irrigated cash rent was $38 per acre in 2014. Groundwater supplies 98 percent of irrigation water, 2015. Drought has been present: 304 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2011 the worst year at 49 weeks, and 85 weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. July nightly lows averaged 70.4 F over 2017-2026 against a 69.4 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.5 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance indemnities for closed crop years, 1989-2025, totaled $26.9 million, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 29 percent; the loss ratio was 1.19 overall and 1.59 for the ten closed years 2016-2025.
