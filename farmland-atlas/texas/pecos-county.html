Pecos County, Texas

Rain is at 112% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,178 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.75 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.82 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 112% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 257 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,923 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,178 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 8.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 860.1 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 26,098 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.75 per base acre, PLC $3.23.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (128.65 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $28.43 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $8.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.39 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.59, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 15% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.8%.

The census value of land and buildings in Pecos County was $1,178 an acre in 2022, up 70.2% from $692 in 2017, or 11.2% a year. That value sits well under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 13th lowest of 246. Cash rent was $5 an acre in 2017, and pasture rent was $2 an acre in 2026. In 2022, 42% of the farmland was rented.

What is farmland worth in Pecos County, Texas?
