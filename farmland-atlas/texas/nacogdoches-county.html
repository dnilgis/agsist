U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Nacogdoches County runs $18 an acre for 2026, up 6.1% from $17 in 2016 and 25th lowest of 190 against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $4,382 an acre in 2022, up 51.4% from $2,895 in 2017, and 60th highest of 246. Pasture rents for $13 an acre in 2026, and 29% of farmland was rented in 2022.
