Non-irrigated cash rent in Nacogdoches County runs $18 per acre, up 6.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in 2022 stood at $4,382 per acre, the operators' own estimate, up 51.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.6 percent a year. Drought has hit the county in 141 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks; from 2021-2025 the county logged 27 such weeks. July nights have averaged 73.2 F from 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.5 F, with warming of 0.53 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates in the county now, though 609.1 MW of solar has been proposed to EIA.
