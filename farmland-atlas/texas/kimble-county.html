Kimble County, Texas

Kimble County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $8 an acre (2024); land and buildings $2,623 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48267. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kimble County runs $8 per acre in 2024, up 43.6 percent from 2014. Land value sits at $2,623 per acre in the 2022 census, the operators' own estimate, and that value rose 31.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.7 percent a year. July nights average 70.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 69.6 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.49 F per decade since 1976. Crop insurance losses from 1989-2018 totaled $198,132, with heat and drought the largest cause at 40 percent overall and 44 percent in 1989-1999. The county spent 91 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 407 weeks of 1357 counted from 2000-2025.
