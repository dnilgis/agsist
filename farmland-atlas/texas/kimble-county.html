Cash rent in Kimble County runs $8 an acre for 2024, up 43.6% from $6 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,623 an acre in 2022, up 31.7% from $1,992 in 2017, or 5.7% a year. That sits under the state median of $2,782 and ranks 116th lowest of 246. 21% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
