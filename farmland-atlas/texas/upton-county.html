As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Upton County was $1,323 an acre in 2022. That is up 63.7% from $808 in 2017, or 10.4% a year, and it sits 30th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. Cash rent was $31 an acre in 2017, pasture went for $6 an acre in 2026, and 57% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
