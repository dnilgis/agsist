Upton County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,323 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.39 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: at least 259 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,037 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,323 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.30 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 61% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 57% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 7.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 175 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 17,484 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 3,283 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.39 per base acre, PLC $1.72.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (68.46 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $15.13 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $62.50 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.51, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 84.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.2%.

What is farmland worth in Upton County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,323 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
