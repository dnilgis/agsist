Henderson County, Texas

Henderson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,699 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48213. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henderson County averages $37 per acre in 2026, up 146.7 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value stood at $4,699 per acre in 2022, up 47.4 percent from 2017, or 8.1 percent a year. The July nightly low has moved from a 72.8 F normal to 73.6 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.48 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks were 182 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2011 the worst year at 52 weeks; 23 weeks fell in that category from 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities rose from 14 percent in 1989-1999 to 29 percent in 2000-2009 to 47 percent in 2010-2019, then fell to 31 percent in 2020-2025.
