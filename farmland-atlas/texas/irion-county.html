The census value of land and buildings in Irion County was $1,345 an acre in 2022, up 35.2% from $995 in 2017. That works out to 6.2% a year, and it still sits 31st lowest of 246 in Texas, well under the state median of $2,782. Pasture rent ran $5 an acre in 2026, and 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
