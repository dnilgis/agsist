USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent on cropland in Wichita County runs $39 an acre for 2026, up 18.5% from $33 in 2016 and 41st highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground rents for $87 in 2025 and pasture for $11 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,084 an acre in 2022, up 41.4% from $1,474 in 2017, and sits 98th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782.
