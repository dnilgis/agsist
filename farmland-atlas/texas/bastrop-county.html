Cropland rent in Bastrop County runs $25 an acre in 2026, up 81.5% from $14 in 2016 and a shade under the $27 state median, tied for 78th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings is another story at $6,225 an acre in 2022, up 51.3% from $4,114 in 2017 and 16th highest of 246. Pasture rents for $13 an acre in 2026, and 37% of farmland was rented in 2022.
