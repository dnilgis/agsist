Bastrop County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,225 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.49 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 33.18 inches, 102% of normal (32.47 inches) through Nov 17, at Red Rock, 1.2 mi N. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,253 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,225 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $24 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 81.5% from 2016 to 2026. 36.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 177.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,222 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.49 per base acre, PLC $1.42.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (56.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $23.18 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.10 over the same years. 24.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23%.
