$22

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 18.9% from $19 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Chambers County runs $22 an acre for 2026, up 18.9% from $19 in 2016 and under the state median of $27. Irrigated ground rents for $65 an acre in 2026, 7th lowest of 43 counties. The census value of land and buildings was $3,315 an acre in 2022, up 40.1% from $2,367 in 2017. Renters work 57% of the farmland here.
