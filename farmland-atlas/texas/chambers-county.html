Chambers County, Texas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,315 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.14 per base acre for corn.

Chambers County vs Texas: $22 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 30% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 58th lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.58 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,451 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,315 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 28% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 18.9% from 2016 to 2026. 57% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 133.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 188.2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,842 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.14 per base acre, PLC $1.58.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (63 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $56.44 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $13.92 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (12.73 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $55.34 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 2.08, 2016 to 2025. 65.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 0.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 57.7%.
