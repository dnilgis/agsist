Cropland in Yoakum County rents for $30 an acre in 2026, up 68.6% from $18 in 2016 and tied for 80th highest of 190 in Texas. Irrigated ground runs $158 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 43 against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $1,606 an acre in 2022, up 71.0% from $939 in 2017, and still 63rd lowest of 246 in the state.
