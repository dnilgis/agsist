Yoakum County, Texas

Yoakum County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,606 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48501. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland in Yoakum County rents for $158 per acre in 2026, while non-irrigated ground rents for $30 per acre, up 68.6 percent from 2016. Forty-five percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and all of that irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought account for 55 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share has moved from 45 percent in 1989-1999 to 55 percent in 2000-2009, 52 percent in 2010-2019, and 59 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 93 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 419 such weeks total from 2000-2025. Census land value reached $1,606 per acre in 2022, up 71 percent from 2017.
