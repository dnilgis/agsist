Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.69 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 219 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,538 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,606 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $29.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.39 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 0.1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 68.6% from 2016 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 24.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,822 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $22.12 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $16.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 806 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $47.05 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.73, 2016 to 2025. 45% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 54.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.4%.

Quick answers for Yoakum County

What is cash rent in Yoakum County, Texas?

The county average is $30 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $158 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Yoakum County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,606 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
