Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.14 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 147% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 240 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,910 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,968 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.47 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 7% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 130.4% from 2016 to 2026. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 28 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 16,673 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 78 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

24.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 70.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.3%.

Quick answers for Menard County

What is cash rent in Menard County, Texas?

The county average is $27 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Menard County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,968 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
