Brazoria County, Texas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,440 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.69 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 37.1 inches, 92% of normal (40.34 inches) through Nov 17, at Angleton Lake Jackson Brazoria County Airport. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,481 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,440 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $34 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 38.8% from 2014 to 2026. 51% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 142.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,338 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 22,147 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.69 per base acre, PLC $2.03.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (80.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.39 per base acre, PLC $3.86. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (21.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.30 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.19, 2016 to 2025. 27% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 34.1%.
