Travis County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,127 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.16 per base acre for corn.

Travis County vs Texas: $23.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 38% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 72nd lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

62.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 31.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,207 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,127 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $23.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 20% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 4.4% from 2016 to 2026. 52% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 117.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 2.3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,245 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.16 per base acre, PLC $1.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (64.19 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.19 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.16 per base acre, PLC $5.73. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.74 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.49 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.49, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 32% of the median yield. 4.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 63.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 24.6%.

Quick answers for Travis County

What is cash rent in Travis County, Texas?

The county average is $24 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Travis County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $7,127 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Travis County, Texas?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 98.7 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 12 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
