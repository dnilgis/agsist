Smith County, Texas

Smith County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,161 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.38 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48423. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Smith County runs $22 per acre in 2026, up 18.9 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $7,161 per acre, up 36.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.4 percent a year. July nightly lows average 72.6 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.2 F, with a trend of 0.26 F per decade and no clear direction since 1976. Heat and drought made up 44 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999, 4 percent in 2010-2019, and 0 percent in 2020-2025; freeze and frost is the largest cause overall at 56 percent of $1.4 million paid out. The county saw 20 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 190 of 1357 weeks countywide from 2000-2025.
