Refugio County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,528 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.30 per base acre for corn.

Refugio County vs Texas: $48.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 90% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (20th highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.95 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 126% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,505 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,528 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $48.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.39 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 55% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 19.8% from 2016 to 2026. 63% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. The 2022 census reports no cover crops here. 65.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 394.4 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 18,808 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.30 per base acre, PLC $1.67.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (66.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.68 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20.11 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $15.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.49 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.90, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.3%.
