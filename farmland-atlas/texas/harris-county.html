Harris County, Texas

Harris County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,000 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48201. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Harris County runs $34 per acre in 2026, up 74.4 percent from 2016. Land value sits at $9,000 per acre by the operators' own 2022 estimate, up 4.2 percent from 2017, or 0.8 percent a year. July nights average 75.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 74.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought drove 47 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, with the share at 71 percent in 2010-2019 before falling to 26 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 21 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 131 of 1357 weeks, 9.7 percent, over 2000-2025.
