Callahan County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,550 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.37 per base acre for corn.

Callahan County vs Texas: $16 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 8% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 16th lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

70.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.98 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 246 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,940 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,550 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $16 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 25% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 8% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 73.9% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 72.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 415 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 25,280 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.37 per base acre, PLC $0.76.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30.27 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $6.69 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $39.25 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.30, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 75.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 8%.

Stands out against its own record

$16

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 73.9% from $9 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Callahan County was $16 an acre in 2026, tied for 16th lowest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. That rent is up 73.9% from $9 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,550 an acre in 2022, up 47.2% from $1,732 in 2017, or 8.0% a year. In 2022, 35% of the county's farmland was rented.
