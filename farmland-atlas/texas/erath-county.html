Cropland rent in Erath County runs $24 an acre for 2026, up 41.2% from $17 in 2016. That sits under the Texas median of $27 and ranks tied for 76th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $4,099 an acre in 2022, up 29.5% from $3,166 in 2017, and it ranks 70th highest of 246 in the state.
