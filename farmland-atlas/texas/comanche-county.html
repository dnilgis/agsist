Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.14 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 109% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 247 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,001 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,754 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.45 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 29% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.7% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 89.2% from 2016 to 2026. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 196.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150.7 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 30,779 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 7,865 acres, so read the change with care.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $24.73 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Quick answers for Comanche County

What is cash rent in Comanche County, Texas?

The county average is $35 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Comanche County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $3,754 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
