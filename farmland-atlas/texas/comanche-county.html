Stands out against its own record

$35

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 89.2% from $19 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Comanche County runs $35 an acre for 2026, up 89.2% from $19 in 2016. That sits above the Texas median of $27 and ranks tied for 54th highest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $3,754 an acre in 2022, up 42.3% from $2,639 in 2017, and 87th highest of 246 in the state. 32% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
