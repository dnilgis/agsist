Washington County, Texas

Washington County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $29 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,646 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48477. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County is $29 per acre, up 75.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. The July nightly low averaged 74.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.69 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance indemnities totaled $3.6 million over 1995-2025, with heat and drought the largest cause at 47 percent; that share moved 64 percent in 1989-1999, 61 percent in 2000-2009, 32 percent in 2010-2019, and 51 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 240 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 40 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Census land value came to $4,646 per acre in 2022, down 2.8 percent from 2017 to 2022.
