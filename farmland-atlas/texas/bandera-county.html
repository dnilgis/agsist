Bandera County, Texas

Cash rent in Bandera County is $10 an acre for 2025, which ranks lowest of 16 in Texas, against a state median of $24. That is up 13.6% from $9 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings was $4,341 an acre in 2022, up 71.5% from $2,531 in 2017, and ranks 62nd highest of 246. In 2022, 28% of the farmland was rented.
