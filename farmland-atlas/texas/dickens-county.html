Cash rent in Dickens County runs $27 an acre for 2026, up 165.0% from $10 in 2016, and that sits tied for 88th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $1,791 an acre in 2022, up 68.2% from $1,065 in 2017, and ranks 74th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. In 2022, 28% of the farmland here was rented.
