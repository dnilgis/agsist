As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Young County runs $20 an acre for 2026, up 25.0% from $16 in 2016 and tied for 42nd lowest of 190 in Texas. Pasture rents for $9 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,496 an acre in 2022, up 47.6% from $1,691 in 2017, and that sits 113th lowest of 246. 52% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
