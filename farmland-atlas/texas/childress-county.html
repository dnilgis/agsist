As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Childress County runs $25 an acre for 2026, up 38.9% from $18 in 2016 and a shade under the $27 state median. Irrigated rent is $26 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 43 counties against a $99 state median. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,687 an acre in 2022, up 82.4% from $925 in 2017, or 12.8% a year.
