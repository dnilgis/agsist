McMullen County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,940 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $4.84 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

98.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.38 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 101% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,398 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,940 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.42 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 0.2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 27% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 22.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 328 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.84 per base acre, PLC $1.13.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $16.24 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $9.95 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $18.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 76.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.4%.

The census value of land and buildings in McMullen County was $1,940 an acre in 2022. That is up 6.8% from $1,816 in 2017, or 1.3% a year, and well above the $1,405 of 2012. It sits under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 85th lowest of 246. 26% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
