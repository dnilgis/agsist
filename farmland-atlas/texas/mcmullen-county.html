McMullen County, Texas

McMullen County, TX: land and buildings $1,940 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.63 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48311. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Heat and drought account for 76 percent of crop insurance indemnities in McMullen County across closed crop years 1989-2025, and the loss ratio for that period stands at 1.63. That share moved from 69 percent in 1989-1999, 76 percent in 2000-2009, 91 percent in 2010-2019, to 70 percent in 2020-2025. The July average nightly low has moved from 73.9 F under the 1991-2020 normal to 74.6 F in 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.35 F per decade since 1976. Census land value sits at $1,940 per acre, up 6.8 percent from 2017-2022.
