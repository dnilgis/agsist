Austin County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,189 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.46 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

61.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.11 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,335 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,189 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $38 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 35% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 65.2% from 2016 to 2026. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 238 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 300 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 239 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.61 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.00, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 21% of the median yield. 4.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 46.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 32.8%.

Quick answers for Austin County

What is cash rent in Austin County, Texas?

The county average is $38 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Austin County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $6,189 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Austin County, Texas?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 94.1 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
