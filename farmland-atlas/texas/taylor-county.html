Cash rent in Taylor County runs $17 an acre for 2026, down 15.0% from $20 in 2016, and that ranks 24th lowest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, reaching $3,181 an acre in 2022, up 54.9% from $2,053 in 2017. That puts it 113th highest of 246, a bit above the state median of $2,782. 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
