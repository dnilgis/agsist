As of Oct 16, cash rent in Brown County ran $17 an acre in 2026, up 37.5% from $12 in 2014. That rent is tied for 22nd lowest of 190 in Texas, under the state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,221 an acre in 2022, up 33.6% from $2,411 in 2017. 36% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
