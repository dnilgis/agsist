Aransas County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 78.8 F for 2017-2026, above the 77.7 F normal for 1991-2020. The July nightly low has warmed 0.53 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought caused 53 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1993-2025, and the share by period runs 98 percent for 1989-1999, 82 percent for 2000-2009, 63 percent for 2010-2019, and 29 percent for 2020-2025. The county logged 237 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 44 of those weeks in 2021-2025 and 2009 the worst year at 46 weeks. Land value rose 8.7 percent from 2017-2022, or 1.7 percent a year, reaching $1,831 per acre in 2022.
