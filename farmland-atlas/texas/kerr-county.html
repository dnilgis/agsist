Kerr County, Texas

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.17 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 146% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 243 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,930 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,597 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.49 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 20.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,170 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

43.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,597 an acre in 2022, up 44.1% from $2,497 in 2017. That puts it 94th highest of 246 in Texas. 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
