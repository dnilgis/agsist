Kerr County, Texas

Kerr County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $26 an acre (2025); land and buildings $3,597 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48265. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kerr County averages $26 per acre in 2025, up 8.3 percent from 2014. Census land and buildings value reached $3,597 per acre in 2022, a change of 44.1 percent from 2017, or 7.6 percent a year. Water carries the heavier signal here: the county spent 614 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 175 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. The July average nightly low sits at 70.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.5 F, warming at 0.47 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
