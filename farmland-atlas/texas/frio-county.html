Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 95.2% from $21 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Frio County runs $41 an acre in 2026, up 95.2% from $21 in 2016 and 37th highest of 190 against a state median of $27. Irrigated ground rents for $173 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 43. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,702 an acre in 2022, up 46.2% from $1,848 in 2017 and 121st lowest of 246. Corn made 134.8 bu in 2024, under the 143.8 bu median since 2009.
