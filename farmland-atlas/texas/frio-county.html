Frio County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,702 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.17 per base acre for corn.

Frio County vs Texas: $41 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 81% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (37th highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 113% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,312 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,702 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $41 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 27% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 41% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 95.2% from 2016 to 2026. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 93.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,437 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.17 per base acre, PLC $2.44.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (97.14 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $80.89 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $21.47 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $18.20 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.89, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 77.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 35.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.1%.

Quick answers for Frio County

What is cash rent in Frio County, Texas?

The county average is $41 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $173 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Frio County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $2,702 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Frio County, Texas?

The county's 2009-2024 median is 143.8 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
