Titus County, Texas

Exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,394 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Titus County vs Texas: $20 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 22% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 42nd lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: exceptional drought (D4) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 68% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 257 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,983 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,394 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.72 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 23% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 90.5% from 2014 to 2026. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 198 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,849 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $34.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 31.9%.
