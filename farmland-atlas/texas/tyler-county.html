Tyler County, Texas

Tyler County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2024); land and buildings $4,622 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48457. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tyler County runs $22 per acre in 2024, up 65.4 percent from 2013. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $4,622 per acre, up 35.5 percent from 2017, or 6.3 percent a year. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland. July nights average 72.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.9 F, with warming of 0.41 F per decade since 1976. The county held 26 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 118 of 1357 weeks (8.7 percent) since 2000. No solar or wind operates here now; 140 MW solar is proposed.
