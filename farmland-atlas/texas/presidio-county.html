As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Presidio County runs $1,070 an acre for 2022. That is up 36.7% from $783 in 2017, 6.4% a year, and well above the $461 of 2012. It still sits 7th lowest of 246 in Texas, against a state median of $2,782. 51% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
