Presidio County land carried a census value of $1,070 per acre in 2022, up 36.7 percent from 2017, or 6.4 percent a year. July nights average 68.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.67 F per decade, warming. Drought has been a steady presence: 460 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, 33.9 percent, with 2024 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 190 weeks fell in that range from 2021-2025 alone. Irrigation covers 30 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
