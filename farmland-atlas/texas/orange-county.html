Non-irrigated cash rent in Orange County averaged $24 per acre in 2022, up 140 percent from 2010. July nights average 74.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.3 F, with a warming trend of 0.36 F per decade since 1976. Drought was present in 137 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 44 weeks in the worst year, 2011, and 48 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
