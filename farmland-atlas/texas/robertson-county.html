As of Oct 16, cash rent in Robertson County ran $11 an acre in 2025, down 59.3% from $27 in 2014 and 2nd lowest of 16 against a state median of $24. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 21.1% to $3,500 an acre in 2022 from $2,889 in 2017. That puts it 98th highest of 246. 43% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
