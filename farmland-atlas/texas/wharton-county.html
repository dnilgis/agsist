Wharton County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,064 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.12 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 37.27 inches, 86% of normal (43.54 inches) through Nov 17, at Wharton, 0.3 mi E. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,385 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,064 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $63 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 48.2% from 2016 to 2026. 59% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 100.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,609.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 22,940 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.12 per base acre, PLC $2.58.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (102.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.83 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.24, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 42% of the median yield. 37.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.8%.
