The 2022 census value of land and buildings in Jeff Davis County runs $1,101 an acre. That is up 60.3% from $687 in 2017, or 9.9% a year, and it was $480 back in 2012. Even so, it sits well under the Texas median of $2,782 and ranks 8th lowest of 246. 32% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
