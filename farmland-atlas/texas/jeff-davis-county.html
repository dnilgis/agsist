Land value in Jeff Davis County ran $1,101 per acre in 2022, up 60.3 percent from 2017, or 9.9 percent a year. Drought has been a steady presence: 442 of 1357 weeks since 2000 saw severe drought or worse, with 2024 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 160 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. July nightly lows have moved from a 64.2 F normal to 65.4 F now, part of a 0.67 F per decade warming trend since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
