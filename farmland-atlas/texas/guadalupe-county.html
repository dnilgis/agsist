Cash rent in Guadalupe County runs $30 an acre for 2026, up 31.1% from $23 in 2016 and tied for 80th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,941 an acre in 2022, up 52.6% from $3,893 in 2017 and 19th highest of 246. Corn is thin here, with a trend yield of 66.2 bu that sits 3rd lowest of 26 and a 2025 crop of 47.0 bu.
