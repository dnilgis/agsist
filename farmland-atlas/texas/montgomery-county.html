Montgomery County, Texas

Montgomery County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $16 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,070 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48339. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Montgomery County is $16 per acre in 2026, up 10.3 percent from 2016. The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 74.9 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 73.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.48 F per decade, warming. Land value stood at $13,070 per acre in 2022, up 14.5 percent from 2017. Drought weeks at severe or worse ran 134 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 17 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 8 percent of harvested cropland, and 58 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Crop insurance paid out little against premium from 1998-2023, a loss ratio of 0.02.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
