Lee County, Texas

Lee County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $13 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,061 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 65.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48287. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lee County is $13 per acre in 2026, down 23.5 percent from 2016. Heat and drought caused 69 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and drought covered 33 weeks of the county in 2021-2025. July nightly lows averaged 74.5 F in 2017-2026, up from 73.1 F in the 1991-2020 normal.
