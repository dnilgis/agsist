Lee County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,061 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.32 per base acre for corn.

Lee County vs Texas: $13 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 4% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (8th lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.51 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,247 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,061 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $13 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 15% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 23.5% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 267.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,328 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.32 per base acre, PLC $1.23.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (48.83 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $10.79 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 20% of the median yield. 2.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 69.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 12.8%.

Quick answers for Lee County

What is cash rent in Lee County, Texas?

The county average is $13 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Lee County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $5,061 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Lee County, Texas?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 101.1 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 8 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
