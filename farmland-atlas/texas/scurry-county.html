Scurry County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,279 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Scurry County vs Texas: $32 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 66% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 64th highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.68 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 255 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,895 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,279 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.48 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 42% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 25.5% from 2016 to 2026. 51% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 23.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 180 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 139,037 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.26 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $23.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $57.63 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.37, 2016 to 2025. 13.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 82% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.2%.
