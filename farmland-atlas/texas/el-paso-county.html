El Paso County, Texas

El Paso County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,912 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cropland here rents for $60 per acre, irrigated for $140 per acre. That gap reflects water, not soil. Irrigation drives this county: 73 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and irrigation failure accounts for 64 percent of all crop insurance indemnities since closed crop years began in 1992, totaling $53.7 million of the $84.1 million paid out. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 0 percent in 1989-1999, 4 percent in 2000-2009, 3 percent in 2010-2019, and 17 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 69.4 F to 71 F over 2017-2026, a trend of 0.92 F per decade.
