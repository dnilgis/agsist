Cash rent in El Paso County runs $60 an acre in 2026, tied for 7th highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. Irrigated ground rents for $140 an acre in 2026, 11th highest of 43, where the state median is $99. In 2022, 81% of the farmland here was rented.

Sources and terms

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
