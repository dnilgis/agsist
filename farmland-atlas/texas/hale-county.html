Hale County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,573 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

33.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 17.89 inches, 103% of normal (17.44 inches) through Nov 17, at Plainview Water Production. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,476 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,573 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 29.9% from 2014 to 2026. 47.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 15.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 211.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,631 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 80 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $5.16 at Petersburg Tx, 19 miles. 2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 592 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $35.31 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.45 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.3%.
