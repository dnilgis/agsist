Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 251 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,008 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,129 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.46 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 23% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 6% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 30% from 2016 to 2026. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 32.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 90 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,773 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $11.94 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $13.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 70 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $47.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.46, 2016 to 2025. 5.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 82.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3.2%.
