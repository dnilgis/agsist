Cropland rent in McCulloch County runs $26 an acre in 2026, up 30.0% from $20 in 2016. That sits just under the state median of $27 and ranks tied for 83rd lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $2,129 an acre in 2022, up 8.0% from $1,971 in 2017, or 1.6% a year. Pasture rents for $7 an acre in 2026.
