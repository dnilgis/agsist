Coleman County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,174 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.34 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 114% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 249 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,977 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,174 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 26% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 6% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 68.2% from 2016 to 2026. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 53.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 396 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,352 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.38 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $2.59 per base acre, PLC $3.34. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (18.52 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $23.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $50.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.76, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 76.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.5%.
