As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Oldham County runs $21 an acre for 2026, up 13.9% from $18 in 2016 and under the state median of $27. That puts it tied for 48th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings was $1,053 an acre in 2022, up 29.2% from $815 in 2017, and it ranks 4th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. 26% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
