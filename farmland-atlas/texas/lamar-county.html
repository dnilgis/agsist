Lamar County, Texas

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,557 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 244 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,936 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,557 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $22 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 4.4% from 2016 to 2026. 27.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 171.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,358.7 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 34,804 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $26.12 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.13 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 54% of the median yield. 6.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.9%.

Cash rent in Lamar County sits at $22 an acre for 2026, down 4.4% from $23 in 2016 and tied for 55th lowest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings ran $3,557 an acre in 2022, up 41.9% from $2,507 in 2017, which ranks 97th highest of 246 in the state. Rent barely moved in ten years while the census value climbed. Corn yields have run a median of 80.0 bu from 2008 to 2022.
