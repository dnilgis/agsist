Bell County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,587 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.93 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

76.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.04 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 105% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 29 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,172 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,587 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $46 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 34% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 29.6% from 2016 to 2026. 54% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 103.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 884.2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,474 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.93 per base acre, PLC $2.02.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (80.37 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.76 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (22.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.07 per base acre, PLC $7.03. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.92 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.88 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.96, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 41% of the median yield. 0.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 53.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23%.

Quick answers for Bell County

What is cash rent in Bell County, Texas?

The county average is $46 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Bell County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $4,587 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Bell County, Texas?

The county's 2021-25 average is 90.2 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
