Cash rent in Collin County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 90.9% from $17 in 2016 and tied for 67th highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $13,066 an acre in 2022, up 31.4% from 2017 and 2nd highest of 246 in the state. Corn is another story, with a 14-yr trend of 68.0 bu that sits 4th lowest of 26.
