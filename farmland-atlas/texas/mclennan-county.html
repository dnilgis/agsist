Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 25% from $35 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in McLennan County runs $43 an acre for 2026, up 24.6% from $35 in 2016 and 32nd highest of 190 in Texas. The census value of land and buildings was $5,592 an acre in 2022, up 55.0% from $3,608 in 2017, or 9.2% a year. Corn last year made 104.3 bu, above the 2021-25 average of 98.6 bu. 52% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
