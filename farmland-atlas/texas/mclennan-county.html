McLennan County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,592 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.37 per base acre for corn.

McLennan County vs Texas: $43 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 84% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (32nd highest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

80.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.90 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 29 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,178 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,592 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $43 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 41% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 24.6% from 2016 to 2026. 52% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 156.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 435.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,617 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.37 per base acre, PLC $2.03.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (80.95 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.89 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (25.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.11 per base acre, PLC $6.88. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $19.37 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.09, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 45% of the median yield. 1.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.7%.
