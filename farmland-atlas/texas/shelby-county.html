Shelby County, Texas

Shelby County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,584 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48419. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Shelby County is $18 per acre, up 2.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in 2022 was $3,584 per acre, up 2.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 0.4 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 72.7 F over 2017-2026, against a 72.2 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.45 F per decade since 1976. Drought hit 50 percent of the county or worse in 167 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 52 weeks in 2011 alone and 39 weeks from 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 2 percent of harvested cropland, all from groundwater. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
