Liberty County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,253 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.56 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 48.57 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 95% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,327 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,253 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $23 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 20.7% from 2016 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 179 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 48,361 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.56 per base acre, PLC $1.50.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (59.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (15 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.85 per base acre, PLC $4.19. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (23.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $27.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $37.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.69, 2016 to 2025. 20% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 63.3%.

Cropland rent in Liberty County runs $23 an acre in 2026, down 20.7% from $29 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $27 and ranks tied for 65th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 41.4% to $4,253 an acre in 2022 from $3,008 in 2017, which ranks 67th highest of 246. 34% of farmland was rented in 2022.
