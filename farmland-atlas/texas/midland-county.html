The census value of land and buildings in Midland County sat at $1,618 an acre in 2022, up 0.6% from $1,609 in 2017. That works out to 0.1% a year, and it ranks 64th lowest of 246 against a Texas median of $2,782. Cash rent was $23 an acre in 2016, and 44% of farmland was rented in 2022.
