Starr County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,289 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

25% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.23 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 123% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,572 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,289 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 39.3% from 2016 to 2026. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 108.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 700 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 30,362 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $20.81 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $12.47 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $16.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $34.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.93, 2016 to 2025. 10.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.8%.

$19

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 16 surveys since 2009; down 39.3% from $31 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Starr County rents for $19 an acre in 2026, tied for 32nd lowest of 190 in Texas. That rent is down 39.3% from $31 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings ran $2,289 an acre in 2022, up 24.8% from $1,834 in 2017 and still under the state median of $2,782. 27% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
