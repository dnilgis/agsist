Somervell County, Texas

Land and buildings run $4,819 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,127 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,819 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $19.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 50% from 2014 to 2026. 60% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 68.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 170 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,622 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.08 per base acre, PLC $4.15. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (23 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $27.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

13.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.
