Cropland rent in Zavala County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 31.4% from $18 in 2016 and under the state median of $27. Irrigated rent is $51 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th lowest of 43 against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,156 an acre in 2022, up 23.8% from $1,741 in 2017.
