Randall County, Texas

Randall County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,838 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48381. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

The county spent 93 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, including 47 weeks in 2022 alone. July nights have warmed since 1976 at 0.41 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average low of 67 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 65.6 F. Wind operates at 166.5 MW; solar operating and proposed wind and solar are all 0 MW.
