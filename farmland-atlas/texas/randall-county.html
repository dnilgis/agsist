Stands out against its own record

$36

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 121.9% from $16 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Randall County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 121.9% from $16 in 2016 and tied for 49th highest of 190 in Texas, where the median is $27. The census value of land and buildings was $1,838 an acre in 2022, up 16.2% from $1,582 in 2017. That sits tied for 78th lowest of 246 against a state median of $2,782. Renters work 39% of the farmland here.
