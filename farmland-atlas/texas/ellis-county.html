Cash rent in Ellis County runs $27 an acre for 2026, up 8.2% from $25 in 2016 and right at the state median, tied for 88th lowest of 190. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,374 an acre in 2022, up 67.4% from $3,211 in 2017, and that ranks 31st highest of 246. Corn trends at 78.7 bu, below the state median of 94.0, though 2025 came in at 94.7 bu.
