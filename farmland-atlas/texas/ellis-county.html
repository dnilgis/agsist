Ellis County, Texas

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,374 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.61 per base acre for corn.

Ellis County vs Texas: $26.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 46% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 88th lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 27.39 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 76% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,204 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,374 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 35% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 8.2% from 2016 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 125.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 448.3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 19,386 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.61 per base acre, PLC $1.86.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (74.25 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.41 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.90 per base acre, PLC $6.42. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.54 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $21.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.14, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 63% of the median yield. 2.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 43.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 42%.
