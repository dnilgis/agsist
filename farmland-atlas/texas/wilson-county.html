Wilson County, Texas

Wilson County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,471 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 99.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48493. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wilson County averages $31 per acre in 2026, up 44.2 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $4,471 per acre in 2022, up 40.9 percent from 2017, 7.1 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 75 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, with the share rising in 2000-2009 at 89 percent, 2010-2019 at 77 percent, and 2020-2025 at 93 percent, though 1989-1999 sat lower at 50 percent. The county spent 122 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 363 weeks total from 2000-2025. Only 23 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census, and 91 percent of that irrigation draws on groundwater as of 2015.
