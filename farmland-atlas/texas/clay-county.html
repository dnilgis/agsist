Cash rent in Clay County runs $27 an acre for 2026, up 1.9% from $26 in 2016, and that sits right on the state median of $27, tied for 88th lowest of 190. Pasture rents for $13 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,259 an acre in 2022, up 17.4% from $1,924 in 2017, which ranks 108th lowest of 246 in Texas. 28% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
