Cropland rent in Leon County runs $16 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $12 in 2016, and that sits tied for 16th lowest of 190 against a state median of $27. Pasture goes for $15 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,776 an acre in 2022, up 24.3% from $3,037 in 2017, which ranks 85th highest of 246. 33% of farmland was rented in 2022.
