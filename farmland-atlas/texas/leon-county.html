Leon County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,776 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Leon County vs Texas: $16 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 8% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 16th lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.99 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 21 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,150 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,776 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $16 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.52 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 10% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 33.3% from 2016 to 2026. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 207.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 689 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 7,001 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $31.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.03 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.73, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 42% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 55%.
