Coryell County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,621 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.00 per base acre for corn.

Coryell County vs Texas: $20 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 22% of the other 189 Texas counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 42nd lowest of 190).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.62 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years have no spring freeze after February; the first fall freeze comes around Nov 25 in the median year. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,109 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,621 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 25% from 2016 to 2026. 46% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 123.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 56 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,395 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $26.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $19.91 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.98, 2016 to 2025. 4.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.3%.

Quick answers for Coryell County

What is cash rent in Coryell County, Texas?

The county average is $20 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Coryell County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $3,621 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
