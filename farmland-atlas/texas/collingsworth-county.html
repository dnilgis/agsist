Collingsworth County, Texas

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,213 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.98 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

21.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 218 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,686 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,213 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 161.3% from 2016 to 2026. 39.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 13.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 35.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.98 per base acre, PLC $1.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (70.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $23.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 282 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $30.19 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.48 over the same years. 23.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 72.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.4%.

Cash rent in Collingsworth County ran $41 an acre in 2026, up 161.3% from $16 in 2016. That ranks 38th highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,213 an acre in 2022, up 19.6% from $1,014 in 2017, which sits 18th lowest of 246. Operators rented 39% of the farmland in 2022.
