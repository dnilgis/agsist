Cash rent in Collingsworth County ran $41 an acre in 2026, up 161.3% from $16 in 2016. That ranks 38th highest of 190 in Texas, against a state median of $27. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,213 an acre in 2022, up 19.6% from $1,014 in 2017, which sits 18th lowest of 246. Operators rented 39% of the farmland in 2022.
