Parker County, Texas

Rain is at 98% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,899 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 31.35 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 255 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,079 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,899 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $16.50 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.46 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 24% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 23% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 130.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 773 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 792 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.80 per base acre, PLC $4.12. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (22.82 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 24, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $27.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

4.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 66.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.1%.

Cropland rent in Parker County ran $17 an acre in 2024, up 32.0% from $13 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $6,899 an acre in 2022, up 60.6% from $4,296 in 2017, or 9.9% a year. That sits well over the state median of $2,782 and ranks 13th highest of 246 in Texas. Pasture rent was $13 an acre in 2026.
