Armstrong County, Texas

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

72.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,506 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,307 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.47 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 46% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 39.2% from 2016 to 2026. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 82.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 265.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 92,113 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.95 per base acre, PLC $3.13.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (124.79 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 25, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $27.58 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (30 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $39.46 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.29, 2016 to 2025. 8.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 74% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.8%.

Quick answers for Armstrong County

What is cash rent in Armstrong County, Texas?

The county average is $36 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $61 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Armstrong County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $1,307 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
