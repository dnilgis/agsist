Non-irrigated cash rent in Hemphill County averages $23 per acre in 2026, up 53.3 percent from 2016. Land value under the census estimate stands at $1,181 per acre, with a 6 percent change from 2017 to 2022. Crop insurance losses since 1989 total $5.3 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 74 percent of that total. That share moved 64 percent, 76 percent, 81 percent, and 63 percent across the four periods measured, so no steady direction holds. Groundwater supplied 100 percent of irrigation water in 2015, and irrigated rent was last recorded at $60 per acre in 2017.
