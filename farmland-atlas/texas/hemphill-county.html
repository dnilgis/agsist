Hemphill County, Texas

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,181 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

67% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.66 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 207 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,524 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,181 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $23 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.49 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). 9% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 53.3% from 2016 to 2026. 46% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 99.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 17,218 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (14 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $20.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $30.06 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.25, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 74.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 12%.
