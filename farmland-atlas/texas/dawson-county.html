As of Oct 16, cash rent in Dawson County ran $48 an acre in 2025, the highest of 16 against a state median of $24. That rent was $43 in 2014, up 12.9% since. The census value of land and buildings was $1,359 an acre in 2022, up 30.7% from $1,040 in 2017, and still 35th lowest of 246 in Texas. 57% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
