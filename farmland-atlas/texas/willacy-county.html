Willacy County, Texas

Willacy County, TX: non-irrigated cash rent $66 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,789 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 48489. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Willacy County runs $66 per acre in 2026, up 4.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $98 per acre, a multiple of 1.49 in 2026 versus 1.72 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Only 8 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated as of the 2022 census, and none of that water came from groundwater in 2015. Crop insurance history shows heat and drought driving losses hard: 84 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009 and 74 percent in 2010-2019, though that share dropped to 30 percent in 2020-2025. Land value rose 58.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, and wind capacity in the county stands at 1444.6 MW operating.
