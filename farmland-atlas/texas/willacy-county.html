Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.33 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 128% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: most years bring no killing frost from March through November, so no season length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 4,624 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,789 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $65.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 26% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 12% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 4.8% from 2016 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 47.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 116,996 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.92 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.98, 2016 to 2025. 7.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.5%.

Quick answers for Willacy County

What is cash rent in Willacy County, Texas?

The county average is $66 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $98 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Willacy County, Texas?

Land and buildings were worth $2,789 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
