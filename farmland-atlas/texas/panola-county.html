Cash rent in Panola County runs $14 an acre in 2026, down 18.2% from $17 in 2016 and tied for 9th lowest of 190 in Texas against a state median of $27. Pasture rents for $8 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $3,130 an acre in 2022, up 17.2% from $2,670 in 2017, which works out to 3.2% a year. That sits above the state median of $2,782 and ranks 114th highest of 246.
