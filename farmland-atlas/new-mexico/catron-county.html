Catron County, New Mexico

Catron County, NM: land and buildings $620 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in Catron County rose 47.6 percent from 2017-2022, or 8.1 percent a year, putting the 2022 census figure at $620 per acre. Irrigated cropland stands at 64 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census, with groundwater supplying 2 percent of irrigation water in 2015. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 492 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, a 36.3 percent share, with 2021 the worst year at 52 weeks; 2021-2025 logged 147 such weeks. July nightly lows averaged 53.3 F for 2017-2026 against a 51.9 F normal for 1991-2020, with warming of 0.9 F per decade since 1976.
