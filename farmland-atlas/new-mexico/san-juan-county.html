San Juan County, New Mexico

Rain is at 148% of normal. Land and buildings run $745 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 12.04 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 148% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 168 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,831 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $745 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.11 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 3, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 6.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 370 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $10.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.65 per $100 of coverage a year, 2017 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.58, 2017 to 2025. 99.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 16.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 2001 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.1%.

Irrigated cash rent in San Juan County runs $177 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 10 in New Mexico and above the state median of $166. The census value of land and buildings came in at $745 an acre in 2022, up 85.3% from $402 in 2017, or 13.1% a year. That value still sits 11th lowest of 30, under the state median of $784. Only 5% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
