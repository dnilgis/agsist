Irrigated cash rent in San Juan County runs $177 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 10 in New Mexico and above the state median of $166. The census value of land and buildings came in at $745 an acre in 2022, up 85.3% from $402 in 2017, or 13.1% a year. That value still sits 11th lowest of 30, under the state median of $784. Only 5% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
