As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Otero County runs $754 an acre for 2022. That is up 71.8% from $439 in 2017, or 11.4% a year. It sits a bit under the state median of $784 and ranks 12th lowest of 30 in New Mexico. 38% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
