Luna County, New Mexico

Rain is at 145% of normal. Land and buildings run $796 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.22 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 12.94 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 145% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 221 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,539 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $796 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.14 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 70% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 52.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 141.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 32,348 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.22 per base acre, PLC $3.91.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (155.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $32.95 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $34.39 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $7.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $20.46 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 3.71, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.7%.

Irrigated rent in Luna County runs $248 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 10 in New Mexico against a state median of $166. The census value of land and buildings came to $796 an acre in 2022, up 54.6% from $515 in 2017, which works out to 9.1% a year. That value sits 14th highest of 30, close to the state median of $784. 70% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
