Hidalgo County, New Mexico

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $414 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.40 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 13.75 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 127% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 211 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,434 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $414 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 27.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 5.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,319 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.40 per base acre, PLC $3.88.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (154.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $43.80 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $34.13 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $7.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.52 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.60, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2024; too much rain caused 3.5%.

The census value of land and buildings in Hidalgo County runs $414 an acre for 2022, up 66.9% from $248 in 2012. That works out to 5.3% a year, and it still sits 5th lowest of 30 against a state median of $784. Irrigated ground rented for $117 an acre in 2020, and 27% of the county's farmland was rented in 2022.
