As of Oct 16, Santa Fe County irrigated land rents for $41 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,245 an acre in 2022, up 41.3% from $881 in 2017, or 7.2% a year. That sits well above the state median of $784 and ranks 5th highest of 30 in New Mexico. Only 11% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
