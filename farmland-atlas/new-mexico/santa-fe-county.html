Santa Fe County, New Mexico

Santa Fe County, NM: irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,245 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35049. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland makes up 63 percent of harvested cropland here, and 52 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Land value ran $1,245 per acre in 2022, up 41.3 percent since 2017, 7.2 percent a year. July nights average 56.5 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 55.3 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.66 F per decade, warming. Drought held at least 50 percent of the county at severe or worse for 538 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks; from 2021-2025 that count was 147 weeks. Solar operating capacity is 9.5 MW, with 5 MW solar proposed to EIA.
