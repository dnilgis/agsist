Grant County, New Mexico

Grant County, NM: land and buildings $575 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Grant County irrigates 84 percent of harvested cropland, with only 12 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater in 2015. July nightly lows averaged 62.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 60.4 F, with a warming trend of 0.72 F per decade since 1976. Land value rose 18.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 3.4 percent a year, against a census value of $575 per acre.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
