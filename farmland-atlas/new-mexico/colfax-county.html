Colfax County, New Mexico

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $977 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

97.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.80 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 148 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,169 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $977 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.47 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 0.4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 40% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 19.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 18,810 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $11.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

84.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Irrigated cropland in Colfax County rented for $47 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings came in at $977 an acre in 2022, up 93.5% from $505 in 2017, or 14.1% a year. That ranks the county 8th highest of 30 in New Mexico, against a state median of $784. In 2022, 40% of the farmland was rented.
