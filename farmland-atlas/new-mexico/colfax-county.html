Colfax County, New Mexico

Colfax County, NM: irrigated cash rent $47 an acre (2024); land and buildings $977 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35007. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigation covers 85 percent of harvested cropland in Colfax County, with 7 percent of that water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Land value stood at $977 per acre in 2022, up 93.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 14.1 percent a year. Drought has held the county for 556 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 41 percent of that stretch, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 98 weeks in severe drought or worse. July nightly lows averaged 52.7 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 52.3 F, with warming since 1976 at 0.36 F per decade.
