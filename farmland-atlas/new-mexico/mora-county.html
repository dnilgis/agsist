Cash rent in Mora County was $15 an acre in 2022, up 87.5% from $8 in 2011. The census value of land and buildings was $1,359 an acre in 2022, up 146.2% from $552 in 2017. That ranks 4th highest of 30 in New Mexico, against a state median of $784. Only 12% of the farmland here is rented.
