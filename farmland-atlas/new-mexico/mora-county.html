Mora County, New Mexico

Mora County, NM: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2022); land and buildings $1,359 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35033. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Mora County stood at $85 per acre in 2025, against a non-irrigated average of $15 per acre in 2022. Land value per the 2022 census sat at $1,359 per acre, and that value changed 146.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, a rate of 19.7 percent a year. Drought has been a steady presence: 567 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 129 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. July nightly lows have warmed since 1976 at 0.54 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 53.4 F runs above the 1991-2020 normal of 52.5 F.
