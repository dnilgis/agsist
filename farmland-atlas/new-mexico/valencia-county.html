Valencia County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Irrigation covers 95 percent of harvested cropland in Valencia County, with 7 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater in 2015. Land value fell 9.2 percent from 2017-2022, down 1.9 percent a year, and the census puts value at $1,055 per acre. July nights have warmed from a 61.7 F normal to 63.3 F in 2017-2026, a trend of 0.94 F per decade since 1976.
