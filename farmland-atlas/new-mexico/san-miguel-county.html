San Miguel County, New Mexico

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $836 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

71.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 13.68 inches, 91% of normal (15.1 inches) through Nov 17, at Las Vegas Municipal Airport. Frost-free season: 176 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,810 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $836 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 24.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 19.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 150,752 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $11.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

85.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in San Miguel County was $836 an acre in 2022, up 86.2% from $449 in 2017. That works out to 13.2% a year, and it stood at $377 back in 2012. The 2022 figure sits above the state median of $784 and ranks 13th highest of 30 in New Mexico. In 2022, 25% of the county's farmland was rented.
