Los Alamos County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value here runs $24,134 per acre as of 2022, up 58.1 percent over 2012-2022, or 4.7 percent a year. July nights average 54.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.57 F per decade, warming. Drought has been present: 484 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, 35.7 percent, with 2021 alone logging 52 weeks, and 135 weeks came in the 2021-2025 stretch. Solar operating capacity stands at 1 MW, with no wind and no proposed solar or wind on file.
