McKinley County, New Mexico

McKinley County, NM: land and buildings $605 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35031. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland covers 72 percent of harvested acres here, so water access shapes most decisions in McKinley County. Land value rose 41.4 percent from 2017-2022, 7.2 percent a year, with the census estimate at $605 per acre. July nightly lows have moved from a 55.8 F normal (1991-2020) to 57.1 F (2017-2026), part of a 0.76 F per decade warming trend since 1976. Solar capacity stands at 508 MW operating, with 90 MW proposed and no wind operating or proposed.
