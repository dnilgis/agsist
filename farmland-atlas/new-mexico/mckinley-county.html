As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in McKinley County was $605 an acre in 2022, up 41.4% from $428 in 2017. That works out to 7.2% a year, and it was $315 back in 2012. It still sits under the state median of $784 and ranks 8th lowest of 30. Only 5% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
