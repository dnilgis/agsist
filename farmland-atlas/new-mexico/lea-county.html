Lea County, New Mexico

Lea County, NM: irrigated cash rent $129 an acre (2024); land and buildings $979 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.83 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35025. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Water is the story in Lea County. Irrigation covers 82 percent of harvested cropland, and all of it draws from groundwater, 100 percent, as of 2015. The July nightly low has moved from a 65.8 F normal to 67.1 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.72 F per decade warming trend since 1976, and the county first reached a 66 F July average low in the 2010s.
