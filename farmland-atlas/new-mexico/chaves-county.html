Irrigated cropland in Chaves County rents for $175 an acre in 2026, just over the New Mexico median of $166 and 5th highest of 10. The census value of land and buildings came to $887 an acre in 2022, up 87.5% from $473 in 2017, which works out to 13.4% a year. That puts it 11th highest of 30 in the state. In 2022, 37% of the farmland here was rented.
