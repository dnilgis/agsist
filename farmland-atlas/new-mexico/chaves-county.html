Chaves County, New Mexico

Chaves County, NM: irrigated cash rent $175 an acre (2026); land and buildings $887 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.29 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

In Chaves County, 85 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 93 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. The July average nightly low has moved from 64.4 F to 66.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.9 F per decade, warming. Freeze and frost remains the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025, at 61 percent. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 535 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 184 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
