Lincoln County, New Mexico

Lincoln County, NM: land and buildings $915 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value in Lincoln County ran $915 per acre in the census year 2022, up 83.7 percent since 2017, or 12.9 percent a year. July nights average 61 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 58.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.84 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has been present often: 452 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 sat at 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2013 the worst year at 53 weeks, and 134 such weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Wind operates at 727 MW with 2561.2 MW more proposed to EIA; solar operates at 3 MW.
