Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.29 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 133% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 190 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,917 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $768 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 26.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 50,000 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $7.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

72.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Irrigated cropland in Socorro County rented for $181 an acre in 2024. The census value of land and buildings was $768 an acre in 2022, up 22.3% from $628 in 2017, or 4.1% a year. That sits just under the state median of $784 and ranks 14th lowest of 30. 37% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
