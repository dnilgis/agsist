The census value of land and buildings in Sandoval County sits at $704 an acre for 2022, up 31.8% from $534 in 2017, or 5.7% a year. That is under the state median of $784 and ranks 10th lowest of 30 in New Mexico. Irrigated rent ran $95 an acre in 2020. 25% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
