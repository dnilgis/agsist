Eddy County, NM: irrigated cash rent $110 an acre (2026); land and buildings $907 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.65 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35015. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

In Eddy County, 95 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 58 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. That reliance sits against a dry record: 476 of 1357 weeks were in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2024 the worst year at 53 weeks. The July average nightly low has moved from 67.3 F normal to 68.9 F over 2017-2026, warming at 0.82 F per decade since 1976.
