Eddy County, New Mexico

Rain is at 117% of normal. Land and buildings run $907 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.18 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.43 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 117% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 232 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,642 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $907 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.20 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 52 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,999 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.18 per base acre, PLC $2.10.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (83.84 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $81.19 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.53 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $8.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.29 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.49, 2016 to 2025. 94.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 0.3%.

Irrigated cash rent in Eddy County runs $110 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 10 against a state median of $166. The census value of land and buildings came in at $907 an acre in 2022, up 53.5% from $591 in 2017, and it was $447 back in 2012. That 2022 figure sits 10th highest of 30 and above the state median of $784. 43% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
