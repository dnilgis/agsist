Taos County, New Mexico

Taos County, NM: non-irrigated cash rent $24 an acre (2022); land and buildings $2,095 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35055. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated rent fell 37.3 percent from 2012 to 2022. Land value sits at $2,095 per acre, up 28.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.1 percent a year. Drought has touched the county often: 448 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 89 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. July nights have warmed 0.48 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 49.3 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 48.7 F.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
