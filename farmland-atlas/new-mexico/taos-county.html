Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

4.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 18.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 107% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 137 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,747 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,095 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.20 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 10, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). Cattle: NASS withholds this county's count (D). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 17,261 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $10.10 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

83.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Quick answers for Taos County

What is cash rent in Taos County, New Mexico?

The county average is $80 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Taos County, New Mexico?

Land and buildings were worth $2,095 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
