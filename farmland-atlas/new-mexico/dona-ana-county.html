Doña Ana County, New Mexico

Doña Ana County, NM: irrigated cash rent $296 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,316 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.48 since 1995. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35013. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Ninety percent of harvested cropland here is irrigated, and 31 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015. July nightly lows average 69.1 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.3 F, warming at 0.99 F per decade since 1976. The county spent 455 of 1357 weeks at 50 percent or more in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 177 of those weeks in 2021-2025 alone.
