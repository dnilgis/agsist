Irrigated cash rent in Doña Ana County runs $296 an acre for 2026, the highest of 10 in New Mexico against a state median of $166. The census value of land and buildings was $2,316 an acre in 2022, down 6.8% from $2,484 in 2017, or 1.4% a year. That still sits highest of 30 in the state, well over the $784 median. 26% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
