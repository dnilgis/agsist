Sierra County, New Mexico

Rain is at 136% of normal. Land and buildings run $406 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.73 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 14.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 136% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 204 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,248 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $406 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.13 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 32% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.8% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 13.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,533 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.73 per base acre, PLC $4.20.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (167.34 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $25.83 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $36.98 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $7.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

72.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 2013 to 2025; too much rain caused 0%.

Quick answers for Sierra County

What is cash rent in Sierra County, New Mexico?

The county average is $173 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sierra County, New Mexico?

Land and buildings were worth $406 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
