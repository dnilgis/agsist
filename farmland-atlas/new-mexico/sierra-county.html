Sierra County, New Mexico

Sierra County, NM: irrigated cash rent $173 an acre (2025); land and buildings $406 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 35051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cropland makes up 73 percent of harvested acres in Sierra County, with 53 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. July nightly lows averaged 64.9 F for 2017-2026, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 62.9 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.86 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought caused 0 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 2010-2019 and 44 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 455 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 158 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone.
