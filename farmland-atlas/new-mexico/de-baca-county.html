De Baca County, New Mexico

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

1.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 132% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 199 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,313 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $385 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.26 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 3, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 0.2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 19.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 90 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 11,670 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $11.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 530 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

87.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 32.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 2011 to 2021; too much rain caused 14%.
