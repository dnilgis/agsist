How these numbers work

Each quarter, ag bankers in the district say whether each item was higher, lower or the same as a year earlier. The index is the percent saying higher, minus the percent saying lower, plus 100. Over 100 means more said higher; under 100, more said lower; 100 is an even split.

The Fed districts do not follow state lines. A state wholly inside one district shows that district; a state split between districts shows each one that reports. The Richmond and San Francisco districts have no reading in the Kansas City Fed's table after 2021 Q4 and 2020 Q2, so they are not shown. Chicago and Dallas quarters are checked against those banks' own files; a quarter where the two disagree is left out rather than guessed. Read 2026-10-28.
