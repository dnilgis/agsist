The census value of land and buildings in Harding County was $447 an acre in 2022, up 22.1% from $366 in 2017, or 4.1% a year. Back in 2012 it was $283. That 2022 figure ranks 6th lowest of 30 in New Mexico, well under the state median of $784. Just 26% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
