Land value in Harding County was $447 per acre in 2022, up 22.1 percent from 2017, or 4.1 percent a year. July nights average 61.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 60.8 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.44 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has covered at least severe severe status in 422 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 108 weeks from 2021-2025 alone and 52 weeks in 2022. Irrigation draws 100 percent from groundwater in 2015. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
