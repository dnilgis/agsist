Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 12 surveys since 2011; up 53% from $18 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, Curry County cropland rent sits at $28 an acre for 2026, up 52.8% from $18 in 2016 and the highest of 3 in New Mexico against a state median of $19. Irrigated ground rents for $81 an acre in 2026, 2nd lowest of 10. The census value of land and buildings was $763 an acre in 2022, up 9.8% from $695 in 2017, or 1.9% a year. Renters worked 28% of the farmland in 2022.
