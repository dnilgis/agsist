Curry County, New Mexico

Rain is at 134% of normal. Land and buildings run $763 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 21.89 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 134% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 194 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,233 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $763 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.46 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 7, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 50% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 52.8% from 2016 to 2026. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 272.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 3.4 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 93,744 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 4,926 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $20.73 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $32.43 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.28 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $11.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 5,667 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $32.45 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.51, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 73.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.2%.
