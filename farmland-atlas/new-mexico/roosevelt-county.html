Cropland in Roosevelt County rents for $19 an acre in 2026, up 46.2% from $13 in 2014 and level with the state median of $19. Irrigated ground rents for $122 an acre in 2026, 4th lowest of 10 against a state median of $166. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,039 an acre in 2022, up 127.4% from $457 in 2017, and that ranks 6th highest of 30 in the state.
