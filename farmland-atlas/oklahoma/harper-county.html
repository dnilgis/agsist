Cropland rent in Harper County runs $27 an acre for 2026, up 26.2% from $21 in 2016 and tied for 26th lowest of 74 in Oklahoma. The census value of land and buildings was $1,147 an acre in 2022, up 4.2% from $1,101 in 2017, and that ranks 2nd lowest of 77 in the state. Pasture rents for $11 an acre in 2026, and 43% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
