Wagoner County, Oklahoma

Wagoner County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,764 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 72.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40145. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wagoner County runs $26 per acre in 2026, down 5.4 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $3,764 per acre, up 32.9 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trended up 3.5 bushels per acre a year over 2010-2024, with a median of 72.3 bushels per acre and a low of 21.5 in 2011. Heat and drought drove 47 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and its share rose across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019 before easing to 45 percent in 2020-2025. Drought held severe level or worse for 130 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 25 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
