Woods County, Oklahoma

Woods County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $35 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,550 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.13 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40151. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Woods County runs $34 per acre, down 8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stands at $1,550 per acre, up 16.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.2 percent a year. Heat and drought account for 75 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share moved 60 percent, 59 percent, 77 percent, 92 percent across the four periods listed. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 366 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 96 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Irrigation covers 3 percent of harvested cropland, all of it drawn from groundwater.
