Cropland rent in Woods County runs $35 an acre for 2026, down 8.0% from $38 in 2016, and that sits tied for 22nd highest of 74 against a state median of $31. Pasture rents for $10 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $1,550 an acre in 2022, up 16.9% from $1,326 in 2017, yet still 11th lowest of 77 in the state. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
