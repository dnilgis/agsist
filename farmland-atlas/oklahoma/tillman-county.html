Tillman County, Oklahoma

Tillman County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $38 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,639 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40141. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Only 8 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 96 percent of irrigation water comes from groundwater. The county spent 445 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 99 of those weeks in 2021-2025.
