Non-irrigated cash rent in Beckham County runs $28 per acre, up 27.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought account for 72 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share was 49 percent in 1989-1999, 74 percent in 2000-2009, 73 percent in 2010-2019, and 75 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 97 of 1357 weeks... rather, 400 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 97 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 20 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, drawing 97 percent of its water from groundwater as of 2015. Census land value stands at $1,768 per acre for 2022, up 32.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
