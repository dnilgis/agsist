Grady County, Oklahoma

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,107 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.92 per base acre for corn.

Grady County vs Oklahoma: $32.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 59% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 29th highest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 26.85 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 226 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,804 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,107 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.58 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 46% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 36% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 185.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 250 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 25,241 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.13 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.64, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 78% of the median yield. 7.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.8 days a year and damaging wind on 5.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 52.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.1%.

Quick answers for Grady County

What is cash rent in Grady County, Oklahoma?

The county average is $33 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $66 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Grady County, Oklahoma?

Land and buildings were worth $3,107 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Grady County, Oklahoma?

The county's 2008-2023 median is 106.0 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
