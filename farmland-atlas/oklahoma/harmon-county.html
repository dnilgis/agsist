Harmon County, Oklahoma

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,346 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

93.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 23.18 inches, 87% of normal (26.53 inches) through Nov 17, at Vinson, 6 mi NW. Frost-free season: 223 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,805 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,346 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $33 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 34.7% from 2016 to 2026. 48% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 13% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 119.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 376 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.21 at Standard (Altus), 30 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $19.00 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $41.80 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.47, 2016 to 2025. 16% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 72.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4%.
