Harmon County, Oklahoma

Harmon County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,346 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40057. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Harmon County stands at $33 per acre for 2026, up 34.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent was $141 per acre in 2023, and irrigation covers 16 percent of harvested cropland, with 99 percent of that water coming from groundwater as of 2015. Crop insurance closed years 1989-2025 paid out $210.7 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 72 percent; that share was 39 percent in 1989-1999, 77 percent in 2000-2009, 79 percent in 2010-2019, and 70 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 464 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, 126 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Census land value reached $1,346 per acre in 2022, up 7.5 percent from 2017.
