Cotton County, Oklahoma

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,876 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Cotton County vs Oklahoma: $35.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 73% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (21st highest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.96 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 87% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 242 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,943 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,876 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.52 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 48% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 14.5% from 2014 to 2026. 53% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 148 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $30.29 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.80 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (9.56 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $19.00 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $30.65 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.97, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 63.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.8%.

$36

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 14.5% from $31 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Cotton County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 14.5% from $31 in 2014. That is 21st highest of 74 in Oklahoma, where the state median is $31. The census value of land and buildings was $1,876 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from $1,359 in 2017, and it sits 20th lowest of 77. In 2022, 53% of the farmland here was rented.
