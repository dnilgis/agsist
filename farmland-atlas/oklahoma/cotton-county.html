Cotton County, Oklahoma

Cotton County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,876 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

The county spent 357 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 72 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
