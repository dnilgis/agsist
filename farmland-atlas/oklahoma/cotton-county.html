$36

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 14.5% from $31 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Cotton County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 14.5% from $31 in 2014. That is 21st highest of 74 in Oklahoma, where the state median is $31. The census value of land and buildings was $1,876 an acre in 2022, up 38.0% from $1,359 in 2017, and it sits 20th lowest of 77. In 2022, 53% of the farmland here was rented.
