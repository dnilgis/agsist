Cropland rent in McIntosh County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 15.4% from $20 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $31 and comes in tied for 16th lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings was $2,601 an acre in 2022, up 36.8% from $1,901 in 2017, which ranks 32nd highest of 77. 25% of farmland was rented in 2022.
