Cleveland County, Oklahoma

Cleveland County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,757 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cleveland County is $41 per acre in 2026, up 57.7 percent from 2016. Land value runs $6,757 per acre, up 39.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.9 percent a year. The county saw 39 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2011, and 59 weeks total across 2021-2025. July nights average 71.3 F for 2017-2026 against a 71.1 F normal, with a trend of 0.19 F per decade and no clear direction.
