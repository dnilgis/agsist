$41

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 57.7% from $26 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Cleveland County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 57.7% from $26 in 2016. That ranks 9th highest of 74 against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,757 an acre in 2022, up 39.3% from $4,851 in 2017, and sits 3rd highest of 77 in Oklahoma. 32% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
