Love County, Oklahoma

Love County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $19 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,908 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.34 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40085. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Love County is $19 per acre in 2026, up 2.7 percent from 2016. Land value per the 2022 census stood at $2,908 per acre, up 18.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.4 percent a year. July nights average 73.3 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 72.4 F, with the long trend since 1976 at 0.31 F per decade and no clear direction. Drought reached severe or worse in 265 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 76 weeks in 2021-2025 alone and 42 weeks in 2022. Heat and drought caused 42 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with shares of 43 percent in 1989-1999, 54 percent in 2000-2009, 36 percent in 2010-2019, and 27 percent in 2020-2025.
