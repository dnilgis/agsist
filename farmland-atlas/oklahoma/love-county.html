Cash rent in Love County runs $19 an acre for 2026, tied for 7th lowest of 74 in Oklahoma and well under the state median of $31. That rent is up 2.7% since 2016, so it has barely moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,908 an acre in 2022, up 18.5% from $2,455 in 2017 and 23rd highest of 77 in the state.
