Cash rent in Haskell County runs $20 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $15 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $31 and ranks tied for 10th lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,498 an acre in 2022, up 31.3% from $1,902 in 2017, close to the state median of $2,515. Renters work 27% of the farmland.
