Haskell County, Oklahoma

Haskell County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,498 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40061. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Haskell County averages $20 per acre, up 33.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value under the census sits at $2,498 per acre, up 31.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.6 percent a year. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so returns ride on rain and heat. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $1.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 44 percent; that share was 28 percent in 1989-1999, 46 percent in 2000-2009, 41 percent in 2010-2019, and 56 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low averaged 71.8 F in 2017-2026 against a 70.7 F normal for 1991-2020, with no clear direction in the longer trend since 1976.
