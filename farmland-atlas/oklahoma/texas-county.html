As of Oct 16, cash rent in Texas County runs $28 an acre for 2026, up 19.1% from $24 in 2016. That sits tied for 29th lowest of 74, a bit under the state median of $31. Irrigated ground rents for $113 an acre in 2026, 5th highest of 11. The census value of land and buildings was $1,212 an acre in 2022, up 19.6% from $1,013 in 2017 and 5th lowest of 77 in the state.

Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms.
