Texas County, Oklahoma

Texas County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,212 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 182.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40139. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All of that irrigation water comes from groundwater, at 100 percent as of 2015. The county spent 425 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 81 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Wind capacity operating stands at 200 MW, solar at 1 MW, with 102 MW solar proposed to EIA and no additional wind proposed.
