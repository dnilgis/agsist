Texas County, Oklahoma

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,212 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.51 per base acre for corn.

Texas County vs Oklahoma: $28 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 38% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 29th lowest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

52.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.78 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 199 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,317 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,212 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $28 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.51 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 45% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 19.1% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 155.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 102 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 210,707 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 50 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 25: $5.00 at Texhoma, 24 miles. 2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.51 per base acre, PLC $4.39.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (175.04 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $30.07 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $38.68 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $22.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 107 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.24 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.90, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 79% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 4.6 days a year and damaging wind on 6.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 53.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3.9%.

Quick answers for Texas County

What is cash rent in Texas County, Oklahoma?

The county average is $28 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $113 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Texas County, Oklahoma?

Land and buildings were worth $1,212 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Texas County, Oklahoma?

The county's 2021-25 average is 196.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
