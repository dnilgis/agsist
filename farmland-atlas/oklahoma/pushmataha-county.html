Non-irrigated cash rent in Pushmataha County stands at $34 per acre for 2026, up 235 percent from 2014. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, and none of that irrigation draws on groundwater, so this is a rain-fed county. July nights average 71.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 70 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.67 F per decade, warming. Drought has held severe levels or worse for 182 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2006 the worst year at 37 weeks, and 32 weeks fell in that category from 2021-2025. Census land and buildings value reached $2,244 per acre in 2022, up 45.5 percent from 2017.
