Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 235.0% from $10 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Pushmataha County was $34 an acre in 2026, up 235.0% from $10 in 2014. That rent is tied for 25th highest of 74 in Oklahoma, a little over the $31 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $2,244 an acre in 2022, up 45.5% from $1,542 in 2017, and it ranks 32nd lowest of 77 against a state median of $2,515.
