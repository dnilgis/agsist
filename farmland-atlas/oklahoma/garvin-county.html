Cropland rent in Garvin County runs $26 an acre for 2026, up 18.2% from $22 in 2016 and under the state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,714 an acre in 2022, up 41.6% from $1,917 in 2017, and that ranks 28th highest of 77. Corn here trends at 101.2 bu, a touch over the state median of 99.3, though 2025 came in at 85.5 bu.
