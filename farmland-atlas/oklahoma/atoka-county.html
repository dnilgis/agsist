Atoka County, Oklahoma

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,051 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 35.4 inches, 85% of normal (41.57 inches) through Nov 18, at McGee Creek Dam. Frost-free season: 229 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,859 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,051 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $23 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 100% from 2016 to 2026. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 175.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,323 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $32.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29%.

Cash rent in Atoka County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up from $12 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $31 and ranks tied for 18th lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,051 an acre in 2022, up 11.8% from $1,834 in 2017. Just 16% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
