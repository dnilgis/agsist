Atoka County, Oklahoma

Atoka County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,051 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40005. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Atoka County runs $23 per acre in 2026, up 100 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $2,051 per acre, up 11.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.3 percent a year. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so yields lean on rainfall and on nighttime cooling. The July average nightly low sits at 72.8 F for 2017-2026, above the 71.4 F normal, and heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 22 percent, 35 percent, 70 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2020-2025. The county logged 211 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse since 2000, with 56 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
