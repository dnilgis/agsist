Stands out against its own record

$25

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2010; up 113.0% from $12 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Pittsburg County runs $25 an acre for 2026, up 113.0% from $12 in 2016. That still sits under the state median of $31 and ranks 21st lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,168 an acre in 2022, up 23.7% from $1,752 in 2017, or 4.4% a year. Renters work 22% of the farmland here.
