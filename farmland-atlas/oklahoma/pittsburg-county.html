Pittsburg County, Oklahoma

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,168 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.27 per base acre for corn.

Pittsburg County vs Oklahoma: $24.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 27% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (21st lowest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.29 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 85% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 228 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,813 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,168 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $24.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.61 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 19% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 113% from 2016 to 2026. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 152.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 19,863 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.27 per base acre, PLC $2.53.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (100.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $36.64 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $22.30 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (12.69 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). PRF grazing county base value: $32.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2013; too much rain caused 18.4%.

Stands out against its own record

$25

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2010; up 113.0% from $12 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Pittsburg County runs $25 an acre for 2026, up 113.0% from $12 in 2016. That still sits under the state median of $31 and ranks 21st lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,168 an acre in 2022, up 23.7% from $1,752 in 2017, or 4.4% a year. Renters work 22% of the farmland here.
