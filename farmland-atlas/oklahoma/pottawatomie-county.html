Pottawatomie County, Oklahoma

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,181 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.87 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

72.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 28.75 inches, 77% of normal (37.2 inches) through Nov 17, at Shawnee. Frost-free season: 225 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,743 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,181 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21 an acre (USDA, 2025). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 19.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 148.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,444 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.87 per base acre, PLC $1.90.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (75.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (16.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $31.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.18 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.53 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 21% of the median yield. 2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 32.3%.

Cash rent in Pottawatomie County runs $21 an acre for 2025, down 33.3% from $32 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 41.1% from 2017 to $3,181 an acre in 2022, which ranks 15th highest of 77 in Oklahoma against a state median of $2,515. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 163.2 bu, 2nd highest of 14 in the state.
