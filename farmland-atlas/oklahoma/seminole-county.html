Seminole County, Oklahoma

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,633 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.53 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

74.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 35.12 inches, 88% of normal (39.81 inches) through Nov 17, at Seminole. Frost-free season: 223 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,757 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,633 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $13 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 25.7% from 2016 to 2026. 28.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 119.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 240 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.77 at Shawnee, 21 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.53 per base acre, PLC $1.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (70.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $31.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 46.8%.

Cash rent in Seminole County runs $13 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 74 in Oklahoma and well under the state median of $31. That rent is down 25.7% from $18 in 2016.
