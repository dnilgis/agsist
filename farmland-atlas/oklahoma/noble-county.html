USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Noble County runs $37 an acre for 2026, tied for 18th highest of 74 in Oklahoma and up 4.3% from $35 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,423 an acre in 2022, up 36.2% from $1,779 in 2017, which sits 37th lowest of 77 in the state. Corn trends at 75.3 bu an acre, 3rd lowest of 14, against a state median of 99.3 bu.
