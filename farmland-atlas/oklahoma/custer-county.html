Custer County, Oklahoma

Cropland rent in Custer County runs $39 an acre for 2026, tied for 13th highest of 74 in Oklahoma and above the state median of $31. That rent is down 2.5% from $40 in 2016, so it has barely moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 28.7% from $1,452 in 2017 to $1,869 in 2022, which still sits 19th lowest of 77 against a state median of $2,515.
