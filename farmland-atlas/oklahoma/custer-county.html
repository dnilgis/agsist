Custer County, Oklahoma

Custer County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,869 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Custer County runs $39 per acre for 2026, down 2.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $136 per acre in 2025, and irrigation covers 6 percent of harvested cropland, drawing 99 percent of its water from groundwater. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $97.2 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 72 percent, and that share has moved from 53 percent in 1989-1999 to 63 percent in 2000-2009 to 68 percent in 2010-2019 to 86 percent in 2020-2025. Wind capacity operating stands at 1146 MW, with no solar and no new wind proposed to EIA.
