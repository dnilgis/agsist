Custer County, Oklahoma

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,869 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.20 per base acre for corn.

Custer County vs Oklahoma: $39 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 82% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 13th highest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 19.81 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 73% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 207 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,672 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,869 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $39 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.56 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 55% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 2.5% from 2016 to 2026. 47% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 101.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 197,771 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 20 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 24: $4.54 at Weatherford, 18 miles. 2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 70 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.20 per base acre, PLC $2.20.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (87.75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $62.71 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.39 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $27.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.67 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.96, 2016 to 2025. 6.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.2 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 71.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.8%.

Cropland rent in Custer County runs $39 an acre for 2026, tied for 13th highest of 74 in Oklahoma and above the state median of $31. That rent is down 2.5% from $40 in 2016, so it has barely moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 28.7% from $1,452 in 2017 to $1,869 in 2022, which still sits 19th lowest of 77 against a state median of $2,515.
