Muskogee County, Oklahoma

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,977 an acre (2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 28.18 inches, 72% of normal (38.9 inches) through Nov 17, at Muskogee Davis Field. Frost-free season: 227 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,782 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,977 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $34 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 15.3% from 2016 to 2026. 31.9% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 0.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 189.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 400 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,390 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

PRF grazing county base value: $32.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.43 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.85 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 30% of the median yield. 5.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 48.2%.
