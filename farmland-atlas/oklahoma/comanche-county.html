Stands out against its own record

$36

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2008; up 18.0% from $31 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Comanche County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 18.0% from $31 in 2016, and that sits 20th highest of 74 counties in Oklahoma. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,715 an acre in 2022, up 40.8% from $1,928 in 2017. That puts it 27th highest of 77 in the state. Renters work 48% of the farmland here.
