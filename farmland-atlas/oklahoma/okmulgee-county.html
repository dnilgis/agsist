Cropland rent in Okmulgee County runs $19 an acre for 2026, down 26.9% from $26 in 2014 and tied for 7th lowest of 74 against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 40.4% to $3,106 an acre in 2022 from $2,212 in 2017, which ranks 18th highest of 77. Pasture rents for $15 an acre in 2026, and 29% of farmland was rented in 2022.
