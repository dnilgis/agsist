Johnston County, Oklahoma

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,523 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.86 per base acre for corn.

Johnston County vs Oklahoma: $15 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 3% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (3rd lowest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,897 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,523 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $15 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.54 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 43% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 18.9% from 2014 to 2026. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 135.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,736 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.86 per base acre, PLC $2.05.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (81.74 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $35.22 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.06 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.71 per base acre, PLC $5.90. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (32.68 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $32.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 42.1%.

Cropland rent in Johnston County runs $15 an acre for 2026, 3rd lowest of 74 in Oklahoma, against a state median of $31. That rent is down 18.9% from $19 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 34.8% from $1,872 in 2017 to $2,523 in 2022, which puts the county 38th highest of 77 and close to the state median of $2,515.

Quick answers for Johnston County

What is cash rent in Johnston County, Oklahoma?

The county average is $15 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Johnston County, Oklahoma?

Land and buildings were worth $2,523 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
