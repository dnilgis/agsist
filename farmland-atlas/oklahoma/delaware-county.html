Cash rent in Delaware County runs $44 an acre for 2026, up 54.4% from $29 in 2016 and 5th highest of 74 in Oklahoma. The state median sits at $31. The census value of land and buildings was $3,409 an acre in 2022, up 24.9% from $2,729 in 2017, which ranks 10th highest of 77. Only 21% of farmland was rented in 2022.
