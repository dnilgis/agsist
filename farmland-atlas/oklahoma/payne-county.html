$31

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 59.0% from $20 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Payne County runs $31 an acre for 2026, up 59.0% from $20 in 2016. That sits right at the state median of $31 and is tied for 35th highest of 74. The census value of land and buildings was $3,264 an acre in 2022, up 34.8% from $2,421 in 2017, which ranks 12th highest of 77 against a state median of $2,515. 38% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
