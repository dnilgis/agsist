$38

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 33% from $29 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Jackson County runs $38 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $29 in 2016 and 16th highest of 74 in Oklahoma. Irrigated ground rents for $138 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 11. The census value of land and buildings was $1,760 an acre in 2022, up 25.9% from $1,398 in 2017, yet still 16th lowest of 77 in the state. 57% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
