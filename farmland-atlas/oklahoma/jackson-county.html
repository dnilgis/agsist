Jackson County, Oklahoma

Non-irrigated cash rent in Jackson County runs $38 per acre for 2026, up 33.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents for $138 per acre, a multiple of 3.63 over non-irrigated in 2026 versus 3.64 in 2010, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Only 8 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and of the water used for irrigation in 2015, 16 percent came from groundwater. Crop insurance history shows heat and drought driving 54 percent of $706.7 million in indemnities since 1989, with that share at 26 percent in 1989-1999, 67 percent in 2000-2009, 55 percent in 2010-2019, and 55 percent in 2020-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
