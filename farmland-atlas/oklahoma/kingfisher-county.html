Kingfisher County, Oklahoma

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,658 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 29.72 inches, 87% of normal (34.14 inches) through Nov 18, at Kingfisher. Frost-free season: 214 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,770 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,658 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $39 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 39.3% from 2016 to 2026. 50% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 144.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 62,416 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: even with Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.36 at Kingfisher, 6 miles. 5 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 80 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.55 per base acre, PLC $5.76. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $31.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.2 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 63.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.5%.

Stands out against its own record

$39

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 39.3% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Kingfisher County runs $39 an acre for 2026, up 39.3% from $28 in 2016. That puts it tied for 13th highest of 74 in Oklahoma, against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,658 an acre in 2022, up 38.2% from $1,923 in 2017, or 6.7% a year. 50% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
