Stands out against its own record

$39

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 39.3% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Kingfisher County runs $39 an acre for 2026, up 39.3% from $28 in 2016. That puts it tied for 13th highest of 74 in Oklahoma, against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,658 an acre in 2022, up 38.2% from $1,923 in 2017, or 6.7% a year. 50% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
