Cash rent in Roger Mills County runs $29 an acre for 2026, up 39.0% from $21 in 2016 and a touch under the state median of $31. That puts it tied for 31st lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings was $1,374 an acre in 2022, up 7.2% from $1,282 in 2017, or 1.4% a year. Against a state median of $2,515, that ranks 7th lowest of 77.
