Stands out against its own record

$39

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2014 (17 surveys on record since 2008); up 6.9% from $36 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Canadian County cropland rents for $39 an acre in 2026, up 6.9% from $36 in 2016 and 15th highest of 74 in Oklahoma against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,406 an acre in 2022, up 45.0% from $2,349 in 2017, which ranks 11th highest of 77. Irrigated ground runs $47 an acre in 2026, lowest of 11, and 52% of farmland was rented in 2022.
