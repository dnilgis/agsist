$14

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the lowest of 12 surveys since 2009; down 39% from $23 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Okfuskee County runs $14 an acre in 2026, 2nd lowest of 74 in Oklahoma against a state median of $31. That rent is down 39.1% from $23 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 48.6% from $1,713 in 2017 to $2,545 in 2022, 8.2% a year. Pasture rents for $18 an acre in 2026, a little more than cropland.
