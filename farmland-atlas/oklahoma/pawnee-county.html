Pawnee County, Oklahoma

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,155 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.64 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

48.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.8 inches, 119% of normal (34.25 inches) through Nov 17, at Ralston. Frost-free season: 210 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,725 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,155 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $26 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 37.8% from 2016 to 2026. 44.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 133 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.64 per base acre, PLC $1.25.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (50 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (10.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $37.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.95 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.96 over the same years. 2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.9%.

Cash rent in Pawnee County runs $26 an acre for 2026, up 37.8% from $19 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $31 and comes in tied for 22nd lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings was $2,155 an acre in 2022, up 20.9% from $1,783 in 2017, or 3.9% a year. Renters work 44% of the farmland here.
