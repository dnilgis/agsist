Cash rent in Pawnee County runs $26 an acre for 2026, up 37.8% from $19 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $31 and comes in tied for 22nd lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings was $2,155 an acre in 2022, up 20.9% from $1,783 in 2017, or 3.9% a year. Renters work 44% of the farmland here.
