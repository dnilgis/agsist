Cash rent in Cherokee County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 21.2% from $26 in 2016 and tied for 33rd highest of 74 counties against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings was $3,676 an acre in 2022, up 48.0% from $2,483 in 2017, and that ranks 7th highest of 77. Pasture rents for $28 an acre in 2026, and 25% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
