Carter County, Oklahoma

Carter County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $13 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,570 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40019. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carter County runs $12 per acre, up 4.2 percent from 2014 to 2025. Land value sits at $2,570 per acre in the 2022 census, up 26.6 percent from 2017 to 2022. Drought is the recurring feature here: the county spent 267 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 in severe drought or worse, with 2022 alone reaching 45 weeks, and 75 weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. Heat and drought drove 55 percent of crop insurance indemnities in 1989-1999 and 71 percent in 2010-2019, out of $423,974 in total indemnities across 1989-2015. Only 6 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
