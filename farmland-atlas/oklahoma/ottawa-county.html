Ottawa County, Oklahoma

Ottawa County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,225 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 98.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40115. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Ottawa County runs $52 per acre in 2026, up 62.5 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $3,225 per acre in the 2022 census, up 16.2 percent from 2017. Heat and drought drove 61 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and its share rose from 27 percent in 1989-1999 to 22 percent in 2000-2009 to 62 percent in 2010-2019 to 77 percent in 2020-2025. The county carried at least 50 percent coverage in severe drought or worse for 126 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 39 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and irrigation failure indemnities total $21,311 across all closed years.
