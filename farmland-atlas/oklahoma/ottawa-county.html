Heat and drought is a growing issue. Insurance indemnities for heat and drought were 27 percent of all payouts in 1989-1999, then jumped to 62 percent in 2010-2019 and 77 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over the past ten crop years is 1.04, meaning the county paid out $1.04 in indemnities for every dollar of premium collected. Non-irrigated cash rent is $52 per acre and has risen 62.5 percent since 2016. Corn yield has gained 0.7 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2025, with a median of 98.3 bushels, though 2011 was rough at 10.4. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated.
