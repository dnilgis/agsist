Cropland rent in Nowata County runs $26 an acre for 2026, up 6.2% from $24 in 2016. That puts it tied for 22nd lowest of 74 against a state median of $31, and pasture rents a bit higher at $28 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,515 an acre in 2022, up 21.9% from $2,063 in 2017, which sits right at the state median and ranks 39th lowest of 77.
