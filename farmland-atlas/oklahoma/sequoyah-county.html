As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Sequoyah County runs $18 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th lowest of 74 against a state median of $31. Pasture rents for $19 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,093 an acre in 2022, up 41.2% from $2,191 in 2017, and that ranks 19th highest of 77. Renters work 24% of the farmland here.
