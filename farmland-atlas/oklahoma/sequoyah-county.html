Sequoyah County, Oklahoma

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,093 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.24 per base acre for corn.

Sequoyah County vs Oklahoma: $17.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 4% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 4th lowest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.52 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 74% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 227 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,738 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,093 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $17.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.71 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 20% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 183.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.24 per base acre, PLC $2.53.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (100.99 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $61.81 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $22.32 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (23.51 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $32.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.06, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 50% of the median yield. 12.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 27.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 66.9%.

Quick answers for Sequoyah County

What is cash rent in Sequoyah County, Oklahoma?

The county average is $18 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sequoyah County, Oklahoma?

Land and buildings were worth $3,093 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
