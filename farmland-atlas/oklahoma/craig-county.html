Craig County, Oklahoma

Cropland cash rent in Craig County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 34.0% from $24 in 2016. That puts it tied for 33rd highest of 74 in Oklahoma, a dollar over the state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,579 an acre in 2022, up 22.5% from $2,105 in 2017. Corn trends at 97.3 bu, but last year it made 63.8 bu.
