As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Logan County runs $34 an acre for 2026, up 42.6% from $24 in 2016 and tied for 25th highest of 74 in Oklahoma. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,204 an acre in 2022, up 43.0% from $2,240 in 2017. That ranks 14th highest of 77 and sits above the state median of $2,515. 44% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
