As of Oct 16, cash rent in Mayes County runs $31 an acre for 2026, right at the state median and tied for 35th highest of 74. That rent is up 17.0% from $27 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,529 an acre in 2022, up 26.9% from 2017 and 8th highest of 77 in Oklahoma. Corn trends at 87.2 bu, 4th lowest of 14 counties.
