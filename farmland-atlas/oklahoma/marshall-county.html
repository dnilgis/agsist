Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 48% from $16 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 12, cash rent in Marshall County runs $23 an acre for 2026. That is tied for 18th lowest of 74 in Oklahoma, under a state median of $31. Rent is still up 48.4% from $16 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $2,584 an acre in 2022, up 6.7% from $2,422 in 2017, or 1.3% a year.
