USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Kiowa County rents for $40 an acre in 2026, up 29.5% from $31 in 2016 and 12th highest of 74 in Oklahoma. The census value of land and buildings was $1,819 an acre in 2022, up 45.1% from $1,254 in 2017, yet still 18th lowest of 77 in the state. 53% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
