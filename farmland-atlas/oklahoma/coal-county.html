Coal County, Oklahoma

Cash rent in Coal County was $19 an acre in 2026, 6th lowest of 74 counties in Oklahoma and under the state median of $31. That rent was $18 in 2016, up 5.7% over ten years. The census value of land and buildings ran $2,116 an acre in 2022, up 16.2% from $1,821 in 2017, or 3.0% a year. 25% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
