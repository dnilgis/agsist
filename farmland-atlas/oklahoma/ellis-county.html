Stands out against its own record

$24

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 50.0% from $16 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Ellis County rents for $24 an acre in 2026, up 50.0% from $16 in 2016 and ranked 20th lowest of 74 in Oklahoma against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings was $1,200 an acre in 2022, up 20.7% from $994 in 2017. That puts the county 4th lowest of 77, well under the $2,515 state median. Renters work 47% of the farmland here.
