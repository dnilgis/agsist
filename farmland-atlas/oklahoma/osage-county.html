Osage County, Oklahoma

Osage County, OK: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,883 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.95 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 40113. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Osage County runs $22 per acre in 2025, down 41.1 percent from 2014. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $14.7 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 61 percent. That cause has climbed by decade: 31 percent in 1989-1999, 44 percent in 2000-2009, 68 percent in 2010-2019, 77 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 99 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 260 weeks total from 2000-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, with 82 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater as of 2015.
