Cropland rent in Greer County runs $31 an acre in 2026, right at the state median and tied for 37th highest of 74. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,439 an acre in 2022, up 24.5% from $1,156 in 2017, or 4.5% a year. That still sits 8th lowest of 77 against a state median of $2,515. Pasture rents for $17 an acre in 2026, and 45% of farmland was rented in 2022.
