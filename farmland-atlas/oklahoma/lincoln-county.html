U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Lincoln County ran $29 an acre in 2026, a bit under the state median of $31 and tied for 31st lowest of 74. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,128 an acre in 2022, up 39.6% from $2,241 in 2017. That put it 16th highest of 77 in Oklahoma. In 2022, 32% of the farmland here was rented.
