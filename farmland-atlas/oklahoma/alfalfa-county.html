Alfalfa County, Oklahoma

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,964 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.36 per base acre for corn.

Alfalfa County vs Oklahoma: $40.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 88% of the other 73 Oklahoma counties with a 2026 rent (10th highest of 74).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 22.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 210 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,661 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,964 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.63 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 55% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent fell 6.9% from 2016 to 2026. 49% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 109.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,016 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 30 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 6 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 25: $4.20 at McWillie, 15 miles. 13 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 50 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 7 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.36 per base acre, PLC $1.89.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (75.45 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $50.07 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.67 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.65 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $31.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 124 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $17.38 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.98, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 54% of the median yield. 0.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 69.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.7%.

Quick answers for Alfalfa County

What is cash rent in Alfalfa County, Oklahoma?

The county average is $41 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Alfalfa County, Oklahoma?

Land and buildings were worth $1,964 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Alfalfa County, Oklahoma?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 89.9 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
