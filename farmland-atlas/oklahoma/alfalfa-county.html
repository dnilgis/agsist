Cash rent in Alfalfa County runs $41 an acre for 2026, down 6.9% from $44 in 2016. That still ranks 10th highest of 74 in Oklahoma, where the county median is $31. The census value of land and buildings was $1,964 an acre in 2022, up 15.3% from $1,703 in 2017, and 24th lowest of 77 against a state median of $2,515. 49% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
