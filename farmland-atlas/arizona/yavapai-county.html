Yavapai County, Arizona

Yavapai County, AZ: irrigated cash rent $93 an acre (2025); land and buildings $2,325 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 04025. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All of Yavapai County's harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and 32 percent of that irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Land sat at $2,325 per acre in 2022, up 40.8 percent over 2017-2022 at 7.1 percent a year. The county spent 463 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, a share of 34.1 percent, with 2007 recording 52 such weeks. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.85 F per decade warming, reaching a 2017-2026 average of 66.5 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.9 F.
