Maricopa County carries 100 percent irrigated cropland, and 79 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater. The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 79.6 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 77.5 F, with a warming trend of 1.04 F per decade since 1976.
