Irrigated cash rent in Maricopa County runs $266 an acre for 2026, a touch under the state median of $271 and 2nd lowest of 4. The census value of land and buildings was $7,203 an acre in 2022, down 22.0% from $9,236 in 2017, or 4.9% a year. That 2022 figure still sits 2nd highest of 12, well above the state median of $1,597. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
