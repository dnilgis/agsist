La Paz County, Arizona

La Paz County, AZ: irrigated cash rent $276 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,604 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.20 since 1990. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 04012. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

La Paz County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Cropland in La Paz County is 100 percent irrigated, with 13 percent of that water coming from groundwater as of 2015. The July average nightly low has risen from a 1991-2020 normal of 79.2 F to 81.5 F in 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.91 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved down across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025: 35 percent, 27 percent, 23 percent, 15 percent. Freeze and frost, not heat, is the largest cause of indemnities from 1990-2025 at 35 percent. Census land and building value stood at $2,604 per acre in 2022, up 29.2 percent from 2017-2022.
