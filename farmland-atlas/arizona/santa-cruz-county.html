Groundwater supplies 100 percent of irrigation water here, so the county depends on it entirely. Land value change 2017-2022 was 29.1 percent, 5.2 percent a year, and the Census value of land and buildings, 2022 stands at $2,575 per acre. Drought pressure has been present: weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, 2000-2025 numbered 431 of 1357, 31.8 percent, with the worst year 2025 at 49 weeks, and weeks in severe drought or worse, 2021-2025 came to 123. July nights are warming, 0.3 F per decade since 1976, with the July average nightly low, 2017-2026 at 64.8 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.6 F.
