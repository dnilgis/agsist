Irrigated cash rent in Pinal County runs $183 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 4 in Arizona against a state median of $271. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,148 an acre in 2022, down 22.3% from $2,764 in 2017, or 4.9% a year. That value still sits 6th highest of 12 in the state, above the $1,597 median. In 2022, 21% of the farmland here was rented.
