Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.33 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 142% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 233 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,400 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $356 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.20 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 0.4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 13% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 11.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,663 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Gila County is a fully irrigated county, and that shapes everything else here. Every acre of harvested cropland in the 2022 census was irrigated. The July nights are getting warmer: the average low has been 66.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 65.2 F, and it has not yet hit 70 F in any July since 1900. Drought has been a steady pressure too. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse came to 439 of 1357 over 2000-2025, with the worst year at 46 weeks in 2014. The census puts land and buildings at $356 per acre, and that figure was down 8.5 percent from 2017 to 2022.

What is farmland worth in Gila County, Arizona?
