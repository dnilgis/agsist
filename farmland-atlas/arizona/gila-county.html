Gila County, Arizona

Gila County, AZ: land and buildings $356 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 04007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

All harvested cropland in Gila County is irrigated, so water carries the weight here. Weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse ran 439 of 1357 (32.4 percent) from 2000-2025, with the worst year 2014 at 46 weeks. From 2021-2025 that count was 96 weeks. Census value of land and buildings, 2022, stood at $356 per acre, down 8.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 1.8 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed 0.73 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 66.8 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 65.2 F.
