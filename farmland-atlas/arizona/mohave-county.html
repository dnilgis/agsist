Mohave County, Arizona

Mohave County, AZ: irrigated cash rent $122 an acre (2019); land and buildings $1,045 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.23 since 1999. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 04015. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

The July average nightly low has moved from 70.8 F in the 1991-2020 normal to 72.8 F in 2017-2026, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.77 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance indemnities for 1999-2018 totaled $628,277, with heat and drought the largest cause at 56 percent, and that cause held at 57 percent in 2000-2009 and 55 percent in 2010-2019. Irrigation drew 44 percent of its water from groundwater in 2015. Land value, per the operators' own 2022 estimate, stands at $1,045 per acre, up 24.6 percent from 2017-2022.
