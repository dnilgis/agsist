Pima County, Arizona

Pima County, AZ: irrigated cash rent $171 an acre (2025); land and buildings $504 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.76 since 1995. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 04019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Pima County had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

All of Pima County's harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and 62 percent of that irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. The census market value of land and buildings stood at $504 per acre in 2022, down 4.4 percent from 2017. The county spent 402 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse during 2000-2025, with 84 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone.
