Irrigated cropland in Apache County rents for $53 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $329 an acre in 2022, up 19.6% from $275 in 2017, or 3.7% a year. That is 2nd lowest of 12 in Arizona, well under the state median of $1,597. Only 1% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
