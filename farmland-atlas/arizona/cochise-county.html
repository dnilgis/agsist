Cochise County, Arizona

Rain is at 141% of normal. Land and buildings run $2,695 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.78 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 20.61 inches, 141% of normal (14.63 inches) through Nov 17, at Paradise. Frost-free season: 231 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,488 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,695 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 36.4% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 1.9% of the cropland, CRP left out. 156.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 335.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 15,963 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.78 per base acre, PLC $4.62.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (184.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $7.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.67 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 2.05 over the same years. 100% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 6.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 3.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4%.
