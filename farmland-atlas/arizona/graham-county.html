Irrigated cropland in Graham County rents for $184 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $736 an acre in 2022, up 13.6% from $648 in 2017, or 2.6% a year. That sits under the state median of $1,597 and ranks 5th lowest of 12. Only 3% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
