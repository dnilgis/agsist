Greenlee County, Arizona

Greenlee County, AZ: irrigated cash rent $73 an acre (2019); land and buildings $5,515 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 04011. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Every acre of harvested cropland here is irrigated, and 64 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. July nights average 62.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.6 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.69 F per decade, warming.
