Land value in Navajo County stood at $325 per acre in 2022, up 47.1 percent from 2017, or 8 percent a year. Virtually all harvested cropland is irrigated, at 99 percent, and 46 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015. The county sat in severe drought or worse during 616 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, which is 45.4 percent of that span, with 2004 seeing 52 such weeks. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.76 F per decade warming, reaching 60.1 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 59 F.
