Navajo County, Arizona

Rain is at 144% of normal. Land and buildings run $325 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 13.83 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 144% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,818 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $325 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 3% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 4.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 1,450 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 23,047 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $7.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

98.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

The census value of land and buildings in Navajo County was $325 an acre in 2022, lowest of 12 in Arizona against a state median of $1,597. That is up 47.1% from $221 in 2017, or 8.0% a year, after sitting at $259 in 2012. Irrigated cash rent ran $113 an acre in 2023, and only 2% of farmland was rented in 2022.
