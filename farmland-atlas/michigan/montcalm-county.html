Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 28.9% from $83 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Montcalm County runs $107 an acre for 2026, up 28.9% from $83 in 2016 and 32nd highest of 71 in Michigan. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,937 an acre in 2022, up 23.7% from $3,991 in 2017 and a bit under the state median of $5,270. Corn made 182.0 bu in 2025, well above the 151.5 bu median for 2008 through 2025.
