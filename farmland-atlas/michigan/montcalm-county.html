Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.10 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 162 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,457 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,937 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $107 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.49 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 8% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 11% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 28.9% from 2016 to 2026. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 17% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 17% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 94.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 446.2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.78 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.37, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. 41.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.1%.

Quick answers for Montcalm County

What is cash rent in Montcalm County, Michigan?

The county average is $107 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $241 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Montcalm County, Michigan?

Land and buildings were worth $4,937 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Montcalm County, Michigan?

The county's 2021-25 average is 172.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
