Non-irrigated cash rent in Montcalm County runs $107 per acre in 2026, up 28.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $241 per acre, a multiple of 2.25 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026, against 2.12 in 2013, with no clear direction to that spread. Census value of land and buildings stands at $4,937 per acre, up 23.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.3 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $38.1 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 36 percent; that share was 18 percent in 1989-1999, 43 percent in 2000-2009, 34 percent in 2010-2019, and 46 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 is 0.37, against 0.43 for the full 1989-2025 record.
