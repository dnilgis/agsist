Cash rent in Gratiot County runs $186 an acre for 2026, up 31.9% from $141 in 2016, and that rent sits 4th highest of 71 in Michigan against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $6,512 an acre in 2022, up 19.9% from $5,432 in 2017. Corn made 193.1 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 178.7 bu. Renters farm 54% of the ground.
