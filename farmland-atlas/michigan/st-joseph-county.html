St. Joseph County, Michigan

St. Joseph County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $131 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,367 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 142.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26149. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in St. Joseph County averages $131 per acre in 2026, up 12.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $321 per acre, and 67 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation, drawing 81 percent of its water from groundwater. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 0.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 142.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 114 in 2008. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $21.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 41 percent; that share was 62 percent in 1989-1999, 36 percent in 2000-2009, 45 percent in 2010-2019, and 39 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $7,367 per acre, up 28.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
