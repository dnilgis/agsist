Cash rent in Ontonagon County runs $22 an acre for 2025, down 25.9% from $29 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $3,563 an acre in 2022, up 123.1% from $1,597 in 2017, or 17.4% a year. That still sits under the state median of $5,270, 15th lowest of 76. 22% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
