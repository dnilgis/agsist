Cash rent in Manistee County runs $35 an acre in 2026, up 25.0% from $28 in 2016 and 10th lowest of 71 in Michigan against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,308 an acre in 2022, up 49.4% from $2,884 in 2017, still under the state median of $5,270. Corn made 129.7 bu in 2024, a little over the 2020-24 average of 128.6 bu.
