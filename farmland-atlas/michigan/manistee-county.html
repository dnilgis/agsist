Manistee County, Michigan

Manistee County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $35 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,308 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 120.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26101. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Manistee County runs $35 per acre in 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents higher, at $78 per acre, though only 16 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation, and 68 percent of that water comes from groundwater. Corn yield has trended up 1.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2009-2024, with a median of 120.4 bushels per acre against a worst year of 83.4 in 2012. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 points to freeze and frost, not heat, as the dominant cause of loss, at 72 percent of indemnities. Drought weeks were low county-wide from 2000-2025 at 3.1 percent, with 2021-2025 holding 31 weeks. Solar and wind capacity here stand at 0 MW, operating and proposed alike.
