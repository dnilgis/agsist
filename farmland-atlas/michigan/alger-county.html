Alger County, Michigan

Alger County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,348 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Alger County is $20 per acre in 2026. Land value was $2,348 per acre in 2022, up 15.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 2.9 percent a year. Cash rent runs at 0.85 percent of land value. July nights averaged 54.7 F over 2017-2026, close to the 54 F normal for 1991-2020, with a trend of 0.25 F per decade and no clear direction since 1976. Drought was rare here: 29 of 1357 weeks since 2000 reached severe drought or worse, with 5 such weeks from 2021-2025. No cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in this county.
