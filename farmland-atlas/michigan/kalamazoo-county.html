Kalamazoo County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,660 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.57 per base acre for corn.

Kalamazoo County vs Michigan: $128 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 63% of the other 70 Michigan counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 26th highest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.88 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 105% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 173 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,632 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,660 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $128 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 41% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 8% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 28% from 2016 to 2026. 58% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 19% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 96.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 220 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,277 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.57 per base acre, PLC $3.56.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (142.01 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $17.73 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $31.38 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.97 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.22 per base acre, PLC $8.69. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (48.13 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $46.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 61 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.40, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 80% of the median yield. 33.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.2%.

Quick answers for Kalamazoo County

What is cash rent in Kalamazoo County, Michigan?

The county average is $128 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $325 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Kalamazoo County, Michigan?

Land and buildings were worth $6,660 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Kalamazoo County, Michigan?

The county's 2019-23 average is 143.4 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
