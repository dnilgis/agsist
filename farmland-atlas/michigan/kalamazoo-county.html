Kalamazoo County, Michigan

Kalamazoo County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $128 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,660 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 150.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26077. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kalamazoo County averages $128 per acre in 2026, up 28 percent from 2016. Irrigated cropland, at 33 percent of harvested acres in the 2022 census, rents for $325 per acre. Land and buildings carry an operator estimate of $6,660 per acre in 2022, down 5.6 percent from 2017, or down 1.1 percent a year. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 totaled $24.2 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 51 percent.
