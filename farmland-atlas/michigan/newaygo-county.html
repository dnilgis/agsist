Cash rent in Newaygo County was $94 an acre in 2026, up 61.2% from $58 in 2016 and a little under the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $5,405 an acre in 2022, up 39.0% from $3,889 in 2017, which ranks 35th highest of 76 in Michigan. Corn made 153.7 bu in 2025, short of the 161.3 bu average from 2021 to 2025.
