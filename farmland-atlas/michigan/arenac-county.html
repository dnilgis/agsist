Arenac County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,500 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.91 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

1.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 31.27 inches, 113% of normal (27.6 inches) through Nov 17, at Standish, 4 mi NE. Frost-free season: 166 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,389 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $4,500 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $128 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 50.6% from 2014 to 2026. 34.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 68.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 131.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 175 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 48 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.91 per base acre, PLC $3.29.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (130.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $62.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 70 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.04 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.45 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 82% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 42.3%.

The census value of land and buildings came to $4,500 an acre in 2022, up 34.1% from $3,355 in 2017, and that ranks 28th lowest of 76 in Michigan. The corn trend yield sits at 170.4 bu for 2026, tied for 12th highest of 36, and the county made 175.0 bu in 2025.
