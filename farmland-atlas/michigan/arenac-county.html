$128

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 50.6% from $85 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Arenac County runs $128 an acre for 2026, up 50.6% from $85 in 2014 and above the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,500 an acre in 2022, up 34.1% from $3,355 in 2017, and that ranks 28th lowest of 76 in Michigan. The corn trend yield sits at 170.4 bu for 2026, tied for 12th highest of 36, and the county made 175.0 bu in 2025.
