Land value here is $3,434 per acre in 2022, up 55.7 percent from 2017, or 9.3 percent a year. July nights average 54.1 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.2 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.34 F per decade with no clear direction. Heat and drought caused 55 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2024, a total of $2.9 million paid, with the loss ratio at 1.79. By period that share ran 74 percent in 1989-1999, 54 percent in 2000-2009, 44 percent in 2010-2019, and 74 percent in 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in only 38 of 1357 weeks since 2000, and just 1 week from 2021-2025.
