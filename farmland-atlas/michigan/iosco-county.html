Iosco County, Michigan

Iosco County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,856 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26069. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Iosco County is $52 per acre in 2026, up 44.4 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $3,856 per acre, up 22.3 percent from 2017, and rent sits at 1.23 percent of that value. July nightly lows averaged 59.4 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 57.4 F, warming 0.82 F per decade since 1976. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW.
