Non-irrigated cash rent in Wexford County averages $56 per acre in 2026, up 36.6 percent from 2016. Land value in the census sits at $3,918 per acre, up 16.3 percent from 2017 to 2022, 3.1 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 2.3 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2022, with a median of 103.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 54.5 in 2013. Heat and drought account for 55 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, and drought weeks rose from 32 total across 2000-2025 to 18 in the shorter 2021-2025 span. July nightly lows average 56.9 F over 2017-2026, up from a 1991-2020 normal of 55.6 F. Solar and wind capacity are both at 0 MW.
