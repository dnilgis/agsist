Non-irrigated cash rent in Chippewa County averages $29 per acre in 2026, up 61.1 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings stood at $2,840 per acre in 2022, with land value up 31.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.6 percent a year. The July nightly low has moved from a 53.8 F normal (1991-2020) to 55 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.59 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought caused 45 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1993-2025, with that share at 49 percent in 2000-2009 and 42 percent in 2010-2019, while severe drought or worse covered the county in only 22 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025.
