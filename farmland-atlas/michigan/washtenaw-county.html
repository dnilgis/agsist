Cash rent in Washtenaw County runs $92 an acre for 2026, up 22.7% from $75 in 2016 and still under the Michigan median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $7,616 an acre in 2022, 9th highest of 76 in the state, though that is down 2.6% from $7,823 in 2017. Corn made 181.2 bu in 2025, right on its recent average and well above the 146.8 bu median going back to 2008.
