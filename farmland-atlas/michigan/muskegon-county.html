Muskegon County's most unusual number is the insurance record. Crop insurance has paid out $37.1 million across 1989-2025, and freeze and frost caused 60 percent of it, so the county's losses have come more from cold than from heat or drought. The loss ratio over the whole period is 0.65, and the county paid out more than it took in premium in 3 of 17 closed crop years. Heat and drought claims went from 31 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 13 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation matters here too: 61 percent of water comes from groundwater. Non-irrigated cash rent is $111 per acre in 2026.
