Cash rent in Iron County ran $17 an acre in 2022. The census value of land and buildings came in at $4,200 an acre in 2022, up 112.6% from $1,976 in 2017, or 16.3% a year. That still sits under the state median of $5,270 and ranks 24th lowest of 76. 21% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
