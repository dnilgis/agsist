Iron County, Michigan

Iron County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $17 an acre (2022); land and buildings $4,200 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26071. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Iron County ran $17 per acre in 2022. Land and buildings were valued at $4,200 per acre in 2022, after a change of 112.6 percent, 16.3 percent a year over 2017-2022. July average nightly low was 53 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 52.2 F; the trend since 1976 runs 0.38 F per decade with no clear direction. Drought was rare here: 54 of 1357 weeks logged severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 5 such weeks in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
