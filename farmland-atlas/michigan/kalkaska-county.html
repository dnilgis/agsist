Kalkaska County, Michigan

Kalkaska County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $59 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,799 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Cropland in Kalkaska County rents for $59 an acre in 2026, the 19th lowest of 71 counties and well under the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $3,799 an acre in 2022, up 43.1% from $2,654 in 2017, or 7.4% a year. That still sits 17th lowest of 76 against a state median of $5,270. Only 12% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
