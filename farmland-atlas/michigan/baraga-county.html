Baraga County, MI: land and buildings $6,098 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

The July average nightly low was 52.9 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 54.3 F for 2017-2026, with a trend since 1976 of 0.46 F per decade and no clear direction. Drought has been light here: 38 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, the worst year was 2007 with 21 weeks, and 2021-2025 had 0 weeks.
