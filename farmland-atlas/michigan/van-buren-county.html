Cash rent in Van Buren County runs $140 an acre for 2026, up 17.6% from $119 in 2016 and tied for 17th highest of 71 in Michigan against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,341 an acre in 2022, up 9.5% from $5,789 in 2017, which ranks 21st highest of 76. Corn made 179.6 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 157.8 bu.
