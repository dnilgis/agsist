Cash rent in Dickinson County runs $22 an acre for 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $3,012 an acre in 2022, up 10.5% from $2,727 in 2017, or 2.0% a year. That puts the county 5th lowest of 76 in Michigan, well under the state median of $5,270. Only 19% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
