Mason County, Michigan

Mason County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $62 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,628 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 145.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26105. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mason County is $62 per acre for 2026, up 6 percent from 2014. Land value in 2022 was $4,628 per acre, up 48.8 percent from 2017, 8.3 percent a year; cash rent is 1.33 percent of land value. July nights have warmed: 58.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 57.5 F normal for 1991-2020, with a 1976 trend of 0.54 F per decade.
