Cass County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,984 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.38 per base acre for corn.

Cass County vs Michigan: $126 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 61% of the other 70 Michigan counties with a 2026 rent (28th highest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 40.94 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 113% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,658 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,984 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $126 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.57 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 26, 2026). 40% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 8% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 9.6% from 2016 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 18.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,040 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Quick answers for Cass County

What is cash rent in Cass County, Michigan?

The county average is $126 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $280 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Cass County, Michigan?

Land and buildings were worth $5,984 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Cass County, Michigan?

The county's 2021-25 average is 162.0 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
