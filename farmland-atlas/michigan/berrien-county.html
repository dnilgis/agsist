Cash rent in Berrien County ran $125 an acre in 2026, down 2.3% from $128 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $99 and ranks 29th highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $6,476 an acre in 2022, up 0.5% from $6,445 in 2017, and ranks 18th highest of 76. Corn made 172.7 bu in 2024 and averaged 171.8 bu from 2020 through 2024.
