Tuscola County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $222 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,465 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 172.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26157. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
