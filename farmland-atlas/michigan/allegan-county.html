Non-irrigated cash rent in Allegan County is $155 per acre for 2026, up 19.2 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $6,684 per acre in the 2022 census, up 11.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Drought has touched the county rarely: 27 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2005 the worst year at 16 weeks.
