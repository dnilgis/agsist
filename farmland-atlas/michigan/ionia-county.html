Ionia County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,594 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.36 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.18 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,543 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,594 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $196 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.59 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 32% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 29% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 39% from 2016 to 2026. 42% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 39% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 204.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,801 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 22: $4.02 at Middleton, 22 miles.

2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.36 per base acre, PLC $3.41.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (135.67 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $29.98 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.26 per base acre, PLC $11.11. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (61.51 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $82.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 148 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.80 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.54, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. 4.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 29%.
