Corn yields in Saginaw County have climbed 1.9 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2025, reaching a median of 164.4 bushels, though 2010 dropped to 142.4. Non-irrigated cash rent is $159 per acre in 2026, up 4.6 percent over the past decade. The county has paid out $94.0 million in crop insurance indemnities since 1989, with excess moisture and flood making up 49 percent of claims. Heat and drought claims moved from 16 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 28 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.63.
