Cash rent in Osceola County runs $79 an acre for 2026, up 43.6% from $55 in 2016. That rent is 27th lowest of 71 in Michigan, where the state median is $99. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,515 an acre in 2022, up 17.0% from 2017 and 13th lowest of 76 in the state. Corn trends at 133.3 bu an acre, 7th lowest of 36 against a state median of 161.7.
