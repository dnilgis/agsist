Cash rent in Livingston County runs $105 an acre for 2026, a bit over the Michigan median of $99 and 34th highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $7,380 an acre in 2022, up 29.5% from $5,701 in 2017, and that sits 10th highest of 76. The corn trend yield projected for 2026 is 175.2 bu, against a state median of 161.7, which puts the county 10th highest of 36.
