Emmet County, Michigan

Rain is at 118% of normal. Land and buildings run $5,871 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.47 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

22% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 36.06 inches, 118% of normal (30.66 inches) through Nov 18, at Petoskey. Frost-free season: 151 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,045 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,871 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent fell 55% from 2016 to 2026. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 64.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 40 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.47 per base acre, PLC $2.12.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (84.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (21 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.26 per base acre, PLC $6.46. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $31.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 2.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 18% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2024; too much rain caused 18.7%.

Cash rent in Emmet County runs $18 an acre for 2026, the 2nd lowest of 71 in Michigan against a state median of $99. That rent is down 55.0% from $40 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, up 55.9% from $3,767 in 2017 to $5,871 in 2022, which is 27th highest of 76. Corn trends at 116.5 bu, 4th lowest of 36, and only 14% of farmland was rented in 2022.
