Emmet County, Michigan

Emmet County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,871 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 116.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26047. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Emmet County sits at $18 per acre in 2026, down 55 percent from 2016. Land value per acre was $5,871 in the 2022 census, up 55.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 9.3 percent a year. Corn yield has a median of 116.5 bushels per acre over 2008-2023, with a worst year in 2009 at 84. July nights average 57.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 56.4 F, with a warming trend of 0.63 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been rare: 13 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, 3 of those since 2021.
