Charlevoix County, Michigan

Charlevoix County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,911 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 114.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26029. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Charlevoix County averages $25 per acre in 2026, down 30.6 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $4,911 per acre in 2022, up 30.3 percent from 2017, 5.4 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 1.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 114.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 79 in 2008. Drought has been rare here: 13 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, and only 3 weeks from 2021-2025. Crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2024 total $487,797, with heat and drought the largest cause at 41 percent, though that share fell from 48 percent in 2000-2009 to 34 percent in 2010-2019. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
