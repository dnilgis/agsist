Kent County, Michigan

Kent County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $145 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,869 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26081. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kent County stands at $145 per acre in 2026, up 26.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents at $247 per acre, a multiple of 1.70 over non-irrigated in 2026, against 2.15 in 2009, with no clear direction over that span. Corn yields have trended up 2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 156.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 129.9 in 2012. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $119.8 million, led by freeze and frost at 59 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities has run 17 percent, 18 percent, 8 percent, and 7 percent across the four periods on record. Census land value sits at $7,869 per acre, up 6.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
