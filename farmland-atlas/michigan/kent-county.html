Kent County cash rent ran $145 an acre in 2026, up 26.1% from $115 in 2016 and 15th highest of 71 against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $7,869 an acre in 2022, up 6.6% from $7,380 in 2017 and 6th highest of 76. Corn made 178.4 bu in 2023, above the 2019-23 average of 165.5 bu.
