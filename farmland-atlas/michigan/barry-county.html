Barry County, Michigan

Barry County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $129 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,027 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 153.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Barry County runs $129 per acre in 2026, up 18.3 percent from 2016. Land value under the census sits at $6,027 per acre, up 25.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.7 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 2.4 bushels per acre a year on a median of 153 bushels per acre, with a worst year of 121 in 2012. Heat and drought accounts for 39 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, and that share moved 44 percent, 35 percent, 37 percent, 47 percent across the four measured periods. Drought weeks stayed low overall, 21 of 1357 weeks at 1.5 percent, but 2021-2025 alone carried 9 of those weeks. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with none proposed.
