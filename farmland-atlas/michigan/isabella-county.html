Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 35.0% from $100 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Isabella County runs $135 an acre in 2026, up 35.0% from $100 in 2016 and 20th highest of 71 in Michigan, where the median is $99. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,954 an acre in 2022, up 20.9% from $4,099 in 2017 and 35th lowest of 76 in the state. Corn trends at 152.7 bu, a bit under the state median of 161.7 bu, and 42% of farmland was rented in 2022.
