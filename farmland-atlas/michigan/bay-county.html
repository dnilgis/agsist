Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 40% from $132 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Bay County runs $185 an acre in 2026, 5th highest of 71 in Michigan and well above the state median of $99. That is up 40.2% from $132 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,313 an acre in 2022, up 5.2% from 2017, or 1.0% a year, and sits about even with the state median of $5,270. Corn made 190.1 bu in 2025, against a county median of 164.4 bu since 2008.
