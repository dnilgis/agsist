Bay County, Michigan

Bay County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $185 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,313 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 164.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26017. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $185 per acre, up 40.2 percent from 2014 to 2026. Land value in 2022 stood at $5,313 per acre, up 5.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. Drought weeks were concentrated late: 31 of the 37 weeks of severe drought or worse recorded from 2000-2025 fell in 2021-2025, with 2025 alone reaching 18 weeks. July nightly lows averaged 62.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 60.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.77 F per decade, warming.
