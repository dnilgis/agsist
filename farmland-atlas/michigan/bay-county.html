Bay County, Michigan

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,313 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.29 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

95.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,529 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,313 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $185 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 40.2% from 2014 to 2026. 47.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 72.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 16.8% of the cropland, CRP left out. 17.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 80 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.29 per base acre, PLC $3.71.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (148 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $62.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 318 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.08 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.45 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 88% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 40%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 40.2% from $132 in 2014.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Bay County runs $185 an acre in 2026, 5th highest of 71 in Michigan and well above the state median of $99. That is up 40.2% from $132 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,313 an acre in 2022, up 5.2% from 2017, or 1.0% a year, and sits about even with the state median of $5,270. Corn made 190.1 bu in 2025, against a county median of 164.4 bu since 2008.
