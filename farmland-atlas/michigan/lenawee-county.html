Non-irrigated cash rent in Lenawee County averages $165 per acre in 2026, up 9.3 percent from 2016. Census land value came in at $5,766 per acre in 2022, up 10.3 percent from 2017, or 2 percent a year. Corn yields trended up 3.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 169 bushels per acre against a worst year of 100.3 in 2012. Heat and drought stayed the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025 at 33 percent, and its share rose to 39 percent in 2020-2025 after 34 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks fell to 0 in 2021-2025 from 15 of 1357 weeks in 2000-2025 overall.
