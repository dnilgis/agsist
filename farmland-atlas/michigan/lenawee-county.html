Lenawee County, Michigan

Rain is at 107% of normal. Land and buildings run $5,766 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.21 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 107% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,714 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,766 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $165 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 9.3% from 2016 to 2026. 51% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 66% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 102.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 80 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 29,481 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 10 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 20: $4.38 at Ottawa Lake, 21 miles. 7 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 55 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 2 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.96 per base acre, PLC $3.40.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (135.45 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $48.84 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $29.93 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.29 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.49 per base acre, PLC $12.21. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (67.65 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $81.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 624 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.99 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.52, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 59% of the median yield. 2.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 32.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 32.6%.
