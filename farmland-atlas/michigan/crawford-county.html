Crawford County had 1,230 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in the 2022 census stands at $4,483 per acre, with a change of 28 percent, 5.1 percent a year over 2017-2022. July nights average 56.3 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 55 F, with a warming trend of 0.65 F per decade since 1976. No Julys since 1900 have averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched the county in 26 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2003 with 13 weeks, and 13 weeks from 2021-2025. No harvested cropland was irrigated in the 2022 census, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
