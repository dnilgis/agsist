The census value of land and buildings in Crawford County stood at $4,483 an acre in 2022. That is up 28.0% from $3,502 in 2017, or 5.1% a year, and still under the Michigan median of $5,270. Back in 2012 it was $3,308. Only 1% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Crawford County had 1,230 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
