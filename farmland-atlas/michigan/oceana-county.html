Oceana County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,348 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.58 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

49.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 37.9 inches, 109% of normal (34.79 inches) through Nov 17, at Hart, 3 mi WSW. Frost-free season: 161 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,329 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $5,348 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $106 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 86% from 2016 to 2026. 35.1% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 11.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8.3% of the cropland, CRP left out. 48.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 120 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 40 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.58 per base acre, PLC $2.45.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (97.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $38.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 56 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.61 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.75 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. 12.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Oceana County runs $106 an acre for 2026, up 86.0% from $57 in 2016 and a bit over the state median of $99. That puts it 33rd highest of 71 in Michigan. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,348 an acre in 2022, up 28.6% from $4,159 in 2017. Corn made 134.3 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 146.6 bu.
