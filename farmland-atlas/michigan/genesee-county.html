Cash rent in Genesee County runs $131 an acre for 2026, up 45.6% from $90 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $99 and ranks tied for 23rd highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $5,538 an acre in 2022, up 5.2% from $5,262 in 2017, or 1.0% a year. 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
