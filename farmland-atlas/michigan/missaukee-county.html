Cash rent in Missaukee County runs $98 an acre for 2026, up 58.1% from $62 in 2016 and just under the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $4,867 an acre in 2022, up 33.1% from $3,658 in 2017, which ranks 31st lowest of 76. Corn trends at 160.5 bu for 2026, close to the state median of 161.7, and 37% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
