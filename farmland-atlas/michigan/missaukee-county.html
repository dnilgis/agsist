Missaukee County, Michigan

Missaukee County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $98 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,867 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 145.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26113. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Missaukee County averages $98 per acre in 2026, up 58.1 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $4,867 per acre in 2022, up 33.1 percent from 2017, or 5.9 percent a year. Corn yields have trended up 1.9 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 145.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 109 in 2009. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 32 percent in 1989-1999, to 41 percent in 2000-2009, to 50 percent in 2010-2019, to 63 percent in 2020-2025. The county drew 88 percent of irrigation water from groundwater in 2015, and July nightly lows have warmed 0.58 F per decade since 1976.
