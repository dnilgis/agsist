Roscommon County had 4,107 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Roscommon County was $4,073 per acre in 2022, up 66.2 percent from 2017, or 10.7 percent a year. July nights average 56.7 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 55.3 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.64 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county in 26 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 13 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. No solar or wind capacity operates here, and none is proposed to EIA.
