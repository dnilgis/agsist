$137

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 30.5% from $105 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Gladwin County runs $137 an acre for 2026, up 30.5% from $105 in 2016. That rent sits above the state median of $99 and ranks 19th highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $4,647 an acre in 2022, up 18.9% from $3,909 in 2017, which is 3.5% a year. That value still ranks 30th lowest of 76, under the state median of $5,270.
