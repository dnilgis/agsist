Non-irrigated cash rent in Gladwin County averages $137 per acre for 2026, up 30.5 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $4,647 per acre in 2022, up 18.9 percent from 2017, 3.5 percent a year. Cash rent in 2022 ran 1.32 percent of that census value. The county corn yield trend is 1.4 bushels per acre a year on 2008-2025, with a median of 151.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 119 in 2009. Excess moisture and flood account for 41 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid over 1989-2025, closed crop years, more than any other cause.
