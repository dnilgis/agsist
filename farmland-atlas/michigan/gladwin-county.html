Gladwin County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,647 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.59 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

46.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,403 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,647 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $137 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 30.5% from 2016 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 36% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 78.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 145 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.59 per base acre, PLC $3.11.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (123.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $60.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 20 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $7.81 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.73, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 79% of the median yield. 1.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 35.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.3%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 30.5% from $105 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Gladwin County runs $137 an acre for 2026, up 30.5% from $105 in 2016. That rent sits above the state median of $99 and ranks 19th highest of 71. The census value of land and buildings was $4,647 an acre in 2022, up 18.9% from $3,909 in 2017, which is 3.5% a year. That value still ranks 30th lowest of 76, under the state median of $5,270.
