Sanilac County, Michigan

Sanilac County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $157 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,380 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 173.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26151. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sanilac County averages $157 per acre in 2026, up 31.9 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $6,380 per acre in 2022, up 29.4 percent from 2017, or 5.3 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $102.5 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 49 percent. Heat and drought's share of indemnities was 15 percent in 1989-1999, 28 percent in 2000-2009, 19 percent in 2010-2019, and 34 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed 0.63 F per decade since 1976, and irrigation covers 0 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Wind capacity operating is 122 MW; solar operating is 0 MW, with 539 MW solar proposed to EIA.
