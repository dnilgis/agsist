Sanilac County, Michigan

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,380 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.82 per base acre for corn.

Sanilac County vs Michigan: $157 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 86% of the other 70 Michigan counties with a 2026 rent (11th highest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

84% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,423 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,380 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $157 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.61 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 1, 2026). 13% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 59% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 31.9% from 2016 to 2026. 39% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 63% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 14% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 141.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 539 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,090 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.69 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.59, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 89% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 25.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 48.8%.

Quick answers for Sanilac County

What is cash rent in Sanilac County, Michigan?

The county average is $157 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Sanilac County, Michigan?

Land and buildings were worth $6,380 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Sanilac County, Michigan?

The county's 2021-25 average is 195.1 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
