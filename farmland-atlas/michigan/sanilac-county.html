Sanilac County cash rent stood at $157 an acre in 2026, up 31.9% from $119 in 2016 and 11th highest of 71 in Michigan, against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $6,380 an acre in 2022, up 29.4% from $4,930 in 2017. Corn came in at 201.0 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 195.1 bu.
