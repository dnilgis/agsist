Midland County's heat and drought claims stand out. Their share of crop insurance indemnities went from 19 percent in 1989-1999 to 41 percent in 2020-2025, and the county has paid out more than premium in only 1 of 19 closed crop years. Flood and excess moisture is still the largest cause at 42 percent of the total. Only 1 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so most of the ground depends on rain. Non-irrigated cash rent is $179 per acre for 2026, up 43.2 percent from 2016.
