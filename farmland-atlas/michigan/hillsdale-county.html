Hillsdale County, Michigan

Hillsdale County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $150 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,493 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26059. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hillsdale County is $150 per acre in 2026, up 10.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $233 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.27 in 2009 to 1.55 in 2026, direction rising. Drought exposure is low: 18 of 1357 weeks since 2000 reached severe drought or worse, with zero such weeks in 2021-2025. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $58.3 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 34 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities ran 23 percent in 1989-1999, 28 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, and 34 percent in 2020-2025. Wind capacity operating is 150 MW, and 150 MW of solar is proposed to EIA.
