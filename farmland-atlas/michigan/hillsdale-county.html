Cash rent in Hillsdale County ran $150 an acre in 2026, up 10.3% from $136 in 2016. That rent is 13th highest of 71 in Michigan, where the county median is $99. The census value of land and buildings was $5,493 an acre in 2022, up 27.5% from $4,309 in 2017. Corn made 190.5 bu in 2025, well over the county's 156.2 bu median going back to 2008.
