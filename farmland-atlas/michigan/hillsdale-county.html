Hillsdale County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,493 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.36 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,596 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,493 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $150 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 10.3% from 2016 to 2026. 47% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 32% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 159.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,575 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 27 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.25 at Hamilton, 27 miles. 4 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 49 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

PRF grazing county base value: $82.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,028 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.53 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.59, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 26.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.8%.
