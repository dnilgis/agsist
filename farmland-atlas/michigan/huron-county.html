Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 11.7% from $214 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Huron County runs $239 an acre for 2026, the highest of 71 counties in Michigan, where the state median sits at $99. That rent is up 11.7% from $214 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,652 an acre in 2022, up 26.5% from $6,048 in 2017 and 8th highest of 76. Corn yields trend at 207.5 bu, the highest of 36, and 50% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
