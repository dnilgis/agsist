Huron County, Michigan

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,652 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.14 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.18 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 170 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,301 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,652 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $239 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 11.7% from 2016 to 2026. 50% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 80% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 23% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 261.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 30,163 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $3.98 at Snover, 29 miles.

1 elevator we read sits within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.14 per base acre, PLC $3.74.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (149.06 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $15.10 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $32.94 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.51 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.27 per base acre, PLC $14.50. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (80.34 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $62.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 455 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.24 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.43, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 83% of the median yield. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 39.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 35.5%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Huron County runs $239 an acre for 2026, the highest of 71 counties in Michigan, where the state median sits at $99. That rent is up 11.7% from $214 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,652 an acre in 2022, up 26.5% from $6,048 in 2017 and 8th highest of 76. Corn yields trend at 207.5 bu, the highest of 36, and 50% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
