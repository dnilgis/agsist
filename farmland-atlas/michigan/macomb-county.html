Macomb County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,741 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.27 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 28.65 inches, 89% of normal (32.2 inches) through Nov 17, at Richmond, 4 mi NNW. Frost-free season: 188 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,742 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $7,741 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $102 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 50% from 2016 to 2026. 46.9% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 29.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 62.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 25 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.27 per base acre, PLC $3.16.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (125.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (35.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $81.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.46 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.05 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 87% of the median yield. 3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 5.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 15% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 65%.
