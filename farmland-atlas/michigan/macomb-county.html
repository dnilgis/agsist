Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 50.0% from $68 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Macomb County runs $102 an acre for 2026, up 50.0% from $68 in 2016 and a little over the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,741 an acre in 2022, 7th highest of 76 in Michigan and up 51.9% from $5,095 in 2017. Corn made 166.7 bu in 2024, a touch better than the county's 17 year median of 160.2 bu.
