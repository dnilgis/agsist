Non-irrigated cash rent in Monroe County averages $172 per acre in 2026, up 21.1 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $6,275 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 1.9 percent, 0.4 percent a year. Corn yields carry a trend of 2.8 bushels per acre a year on the fitted years 2008-2025, with a median of 168.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 2012 at 101.6. Heat and drought's share of indemnities ran 50 percent in 1989-1999, 42 percent in 2000-2009, 29 percent in 2010-2019, and 40 percent in 2020-2025; excess moisture and flood is the largest overall cause, at 41 percent.
