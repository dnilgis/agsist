Monroe County, Michigan

Rain is at 96% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,275 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.15 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 29.28 inches, 96% of normal (30.62 inches) through Nov 18, at Dundee. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,844 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,275 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $172 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 21.1% from 2016 to 2026. 59% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 64% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 14.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 100 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,025 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: even with Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 19: $4.40 at Toledo, 20 miles. 5 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 40 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.15 per base acre, PLC $3.40.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (135.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (39.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.00 per base acre, PLC $12.17. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (67.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $81.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 283 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.35 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.58, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 60% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 33.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.4%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 21.1% from $142 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Monroe County runs $172 an acre for 2026, up 21.1% from $142 in 2016. That ranks 7th highest of 71 in Michigan, against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $6,275 an acre in 2022, up 1.9% from $6,156 in 2017, or 0.4% a year. Corn made 187.0 bu in 2025, close to the recent average of 187.8 bu and well over the 168.1 bu median going back to 2008.
