Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 21.1% from $142 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Monroe County runs $172 an acre for 2026, up 21.1% from $142 in 2016. That ranks 7th highest of 71 in Michigan, against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $6,275 an acre in 2022, up 1.9% from $6,156 in 2017, or 0.4% a year. Corn made 187.0 bu in 2025, close to the recent average of 187.8 bu and well over the 168.1 bu median going back to 2008.
