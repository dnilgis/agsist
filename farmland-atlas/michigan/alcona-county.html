Non-irrigated cash rent in Alcona County averages $45 per acre in 2026, up 87.5 percent from 2016. No cropland here is irrigated. July nights average 58 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 56.4 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.72 F per decade. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 47 percent over 1989-2025, with heat and drought's share moving from 32 percent in 1989-1999 to 17 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, and 27 percent in 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered 37 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2003 the worst year at 13 weeks. Census land value stands at $3,069 per acre, up 18.5 percent from 2017 to 2022.
