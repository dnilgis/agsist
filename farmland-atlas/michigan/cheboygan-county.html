Non-irrigated cash rent in Cheboygan County runs $27 per acre in 2025, up 8 percent from 2014. Land and buildings carry a value of $3,337 per acre, up 30.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.4 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value is 0.87 percent. July nightly lows average 56.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 55.4 F, part of a warming trend of 0.55 F per decade since 1976.
