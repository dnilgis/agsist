Houghton County, Michigan

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,467 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.37 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

69.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 138 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,923 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,467 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 20.2% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 40.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 141,667 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.37 per base acre, PLC $2.05.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (81.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $43.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

The census value of land and buildings in Houghton County was $3,467 an acre in 2022, up 47.3% from $2,354 in 2017. That works out to 8.1% a year, and it was $1,772 back in 2012. Even so, it sits at 12th lowest of 76 in Michigan, under the state median of $5,270. 20% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
