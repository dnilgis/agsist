Antrim County, Michigan

Antrim County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,870 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 115.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Antrim County is $41 per acre, down 18 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census was $5,870 per acre, up 39.7 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nightly lows have warmed from a 56.6 F normal to 58.1 F over 2017-2026. Drought weeks were 12 of 1357 since 2000, with 2 in 2021-2025.
