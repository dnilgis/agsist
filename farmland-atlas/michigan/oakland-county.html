Cash rent in Oakland County runs $71 an acre for 2026, up 18.3% from $60 in 2016 and under the state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings tells another story at $15,247 an acre in 2022, up 31.2% from 2017 and 2nd highest of 76 in Michigan. Corn trends at 161.1 bu, close to the state median of 161.7 bu.
