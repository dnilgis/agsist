Luce County, Michigan

Luce County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $18 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,109 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26095. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Luce County had 8,037 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Luce County runs $18 per acre for 2026. Census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $3,109 per acre, up 23.3 percent from 2017-2022, or 4.3 percent a year. July nights have averaged 54.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 53 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.59 F per decade, warming. Drought has touched the county in 25 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2005 the worst year at 11 weeks, and 5 weeks from 2021-2025. All irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
