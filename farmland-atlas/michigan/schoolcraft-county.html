Land in Schoolcraft County is valued at $2,839 per acre, up 56 percent from 2017-2022, or 9.3 percent a year. July nightly lows average 55.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 54.6 F; the trend since 1976 is 0.28 F per decade with no clear direction. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched the county in 27 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2005 with 11 weeks, and 5 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
