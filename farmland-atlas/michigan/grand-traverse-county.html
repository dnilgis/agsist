Non-irrigated cash rent in Grand Traverse County averages $65 per acre in 2026, up 54.8 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 stands at $9,197 per acre, up 44.6 percent from 2017, or 7.6 percent a year. Freeze and frost is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 at 58 percent, and heat and drought's share of indemnities fell across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019 before dropping again in 2020-2025 at 7 percent. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 runs 3.4 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 113.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 69.5 in 2012. July nightly lows have warmed 0.7 F per decade since 1976, and drought weeks stayed at 3 of the possible weeks in 2021-2025.
