Cash rent in Ogemaw County runs $64 an acre in 2026, up 71.6% from $37 in 2016 and 21st lowest of 71 against a state median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $3,407 an acre in 2022, up 21.8% from $2,797 in 2017 and 10th lowest of 76. Corn tells a different story, with a 14-yr trend of 170.4 bu that is tied for 12th highest of 36.
