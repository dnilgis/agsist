Delta County, Michigan

Delta County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,340 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 115.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26041. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Delta County stands at $20 per acre for 2026, down 25 percent from 2016. Land values, by contrast, rose 31.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.6 percent a year, reaching $2,340 per acre by the operators' own estimate. Corn yield trend runs 1.9 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 115 bushels per acre and a worst year of 64 in 2008. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved unevenly across periods: 21 percent in 1989-1999, 62 percent in 2000-2009, 32 percent in 2010-2019, and 84 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low overall, 28 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 5 weeks in 2021-2025.
