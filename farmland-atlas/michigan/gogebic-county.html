Gogebic County had 5,198 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land value in Gogebic County was $3,602 per acre in the 2022 census, up 38.5 percent from 2017, or 6.7 percent a year. July nightly lows averaged 54.1 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.6 F; the trend since 1976 runs 0.3 F per decade with no clear direction. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Drought was rare here: 90 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw severe drought or worse, with 2007 the worst year at 25 weeks, and 28 such weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
