Benzie County, Michigan

Rain is at 124% of normal. Land and buildings run $6,283 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.09 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.27 inches, 124% of normal (32.54 inches) through Nov 17, at Beulah, 7 mi SSW. Frost-free season: 160 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,231 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,283 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $80 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 19.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 37.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 33 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Soybeans: no elevator we read in the county posts a bid.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.09 per base acre, PLC $2.23.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (88.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $31.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 19 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.78 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.45 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. 11.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 7.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.9%.
