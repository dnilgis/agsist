Wayne County, Michigan

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $20,415 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.24 per base acre for corn.

Wayne County vs Michigan: $90 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 40% of the other 70 Michigan counties with a 2026 rent (29th lowest of 71).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 27.52 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,801 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $20,415 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $90 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.55 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 23% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 14% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 24.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 17 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 41 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $81.50 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $25.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 2.23, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 64% of the median yield. 18.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 6.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 49.8%.
