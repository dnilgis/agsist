Non-irrigated cash rent in Eaton County runs $123 per acre in 2026, down 6.8 percent from 2016. Corn yield trend over 2008-2025 is 2.5 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 163.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 124.2 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood drive crop insurance losses here, accounting for 41 percent of $75.5 million in indemnities paid over 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share moved from 24 percent in 1989-1999 to 23 percent in 2000-2009 to 22 percent in 2010-2019 to 38 percent in 2020-2025. Drought itself stays rare: only 17 of 1357 weeks since 2000 saw severe drought or worse, with 2 weeks in 2021-2025. Census land value sits at $5,160 per acre, up 23.6 percent from 2017 to 2022.
