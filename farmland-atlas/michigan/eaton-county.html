As of Oct 6, cash rent in Eaton County runs $123 an acre for 2026, down 6.8% from $132 in 2016, and still above the Michigan median of $99. The census value of land and buildings was $5,160 an acre in 2022, up 23.6% from $4,176 in 2017. Corn is where this county stands out, with a 14-yr trend of 188.4 bu, 2nd highest of 36 in the state. Last year came in at 195.8 bu.
