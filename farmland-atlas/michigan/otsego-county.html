Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.74 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 144% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 132 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,097 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,545 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 20.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,058 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 66% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.7%.

Quick answers for Otsego County

What is cash rent in Otsego County, Michigan?

The county average is $30 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Otsego County, Michigan?

Land and buildings were worth $3,545 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Otsego County, Michigan?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 154.5 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 9 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
