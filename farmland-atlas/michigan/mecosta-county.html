Cropland rent in Mecosta County runs $70 an acre in 2026, up 7.7% from $65 in 2016 and below the Michigan median of $99, which puts it 24th lowest of 71. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,348 an acre in 2022, up 24.1% from $3,505 in 2017. Corn made 145.2 bu in 2025, a little over the 2021-25 average of 144.6 bu.
