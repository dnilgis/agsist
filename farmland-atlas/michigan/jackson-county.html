Non-irrigated cash rent in Jackson County, MI runs $97 per acre, up 12.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yield trend over 2008-2025 is 3.2 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 140.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 78.7 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 49 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, with that share at 42 percent in 1989-1999, 30 percent in 2000-2009, 59 percent in 2010-2019, and 53 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low, 18 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with just 1 week from 2021-2025. Census land value sits at $4,952 per acre, down 0.7 percent from 2017 to 2022.
