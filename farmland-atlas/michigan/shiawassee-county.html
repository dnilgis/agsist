Shiawassee County, Michigan

Shiawassee County, MI: non-irrigated cash rent $148 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,037 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 26155. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Shiawassee County runs $148 per acre, up 64.4 percent since 2014. Census land value sits at $6,037 per acre, up 33.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2 bushels per acre a year on the published years 2008-2025, with a median of 152.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 131.7 in 2010. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of loss, at 47 percent of $64.6 million paid, while heat and drought's share moved from 23 percent in 1989-1999 to 36 percent in 2000-2009, 24 percent in 2010-2019, and 33 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low, 12 of 1357 weeks since 2000, worst year 2003 with 9 weeks.
