Cash rent in Presque Isle County runs $43 an acre for 2026, up 19.7% from $36 in 2016 and well under the state median of $99. That puts it 12th lowest of 71 in Michigan. The census value of land and buildings was $3,642 an acre in 2022, up 55.3% from $2,345 in 2017. Corn here trends at 115.0 bu, 3rd lowest of 36, against a state median of 161.7 bu.
