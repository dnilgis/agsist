0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Land

Land and buildings: $3,642 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $42.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.43 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 28, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 12% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 19.7% from 2016 to 2026. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 37.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 150 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Quick answers for Presque Isle County

What is cash rent in Presque Isle County, Michigan?

The county average is $43 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Presque Isle County, Michigan?

Land and buildings were worth $3,642 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Presque Isle County, Michigan?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 115.0 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 11 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
