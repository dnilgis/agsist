Cash rent in Calhoun County runs $132 an acre for 2026, up 25.7% from $105 in 2016. That ranks 22nd highest of 71 in Michigan, where the state median is $99. The census value of land and buildings came in at $5,072 an acre in 2022, down 4.5% from $5,309 in 2017. Corn made 168.2 bu in 2025, well over the 18 year county median of 144.4 bu.
