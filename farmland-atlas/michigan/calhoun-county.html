Non-irrigated cash rent in Calhoun County averages $132 per acre in 2026, up 25.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs $242 per acre in 2024, a multiple of 1.67 over non-irrigated; that multiple was 1.58 in 2009, with no clear direction since. Corn yields carry a trend of 2.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 144.4 bushels per acre and a worst year of 87.1 in 2012. Census land value sits at $5,072 per acre, down 4.5 percent from 2017 to 2022.
