The census value of land and buildings in Campbell County was $959 an acre in 2022, up 69.4% from $566 in 2017. That works out to 11.1% a year, and it still sits 7th lowest of 22 against a state median of $1,248. Cash rent was $10 an acre in 2019, and pasture went for $6 an acre in 2026. 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
