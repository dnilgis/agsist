The census value of land and buildings in Sheridan County was $1,582 an acre in 2022, up 29.2% from $1,224 in 2017. That ranks 10th highest of 22 in Wyoming, against a state median of $1,248. Irrigated rent ran $50 an acre in 2026, 5th lowest of 15, while pasture rent was $11. 25% of farmland was rented in 2022.
