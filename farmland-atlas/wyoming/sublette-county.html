Sublette County, Wyoming

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,038 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

23.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 15.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 97% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 77 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,445 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,038 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $21 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 107 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 18,448 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $9.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

97.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.
