Fremont County, Wyoming

Fremont County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,882 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated land dominates here: 94 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and the irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $78 per acre against $15 per acre non-irrigated. July nights have warmed 0.46 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 51.1 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 50 F. Weeks in severe drought or worse ran 379 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 50 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
