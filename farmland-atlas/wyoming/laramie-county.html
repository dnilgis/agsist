As of Oct 16, cash rent in Laramie County runs $28 an acre for 2026, up 37.5% from $20 in 2016 and still the lowest of 4 against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,003 an acre in 2022, up 136.2% from $848 in 2017, and that ranks 5th highest of 22. Irrigated ground rented for $112 an acre in 2025, and pasture went for $6 in 2026.
