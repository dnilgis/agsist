Laramie County, Wyoming

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,003 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 11.85 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,156 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,003 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $27.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.33 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 12% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 26% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 37.5% from 2016 to 2026. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 56.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 765 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 39,611 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 19,472 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

County median corn basis: 15 cents over Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $23.90 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $8.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 12 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $15.66 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.03, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. 24.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 8.8 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.6%.

Quick answers for Laramie County

What is cash rent in Laramie County, Wyoming?

The county average is $28 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $112 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Laramie County, Wyoming?

Land and buildings were worth $2,003 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
