Laramie County, Wyoming

Laramie County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,003 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 117.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Laramie County is $28 per acre for 2026, up 37.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $112 per acre in 2025, and irrigation covers 25 percent of harvested cropland, with 71 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 36 percent of $74.3 million paid, and its share has moved unevenly across periods: 5 percent in 1989-1999, 33 percent in 2000-2009, 27 percent in 2010-2019, and 52 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low averaged 54.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 53.6 F, warming at 0.49 F per decade since 1976. Census land value sits at $2,003 per acre for 2022, up 136.2 percent from 2017.
