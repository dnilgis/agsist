Crook County, Wyoming

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,776 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.86 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 7.91 inches, 44% of normal (18.1 inches) through Nov 16, at Devils Tower No. 2. Frost-free season: 136 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,220 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,776 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $14 an acre (USDA, 2025). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 41.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5.2% of the cropland, CRP left out. 54 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 30,026 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.86 per base acre, PLC $1.82.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (72.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: close, either one, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $8.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $27.97 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.05 over the same years. 8.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 7.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 68.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.2%.

Cropland in Crook County rented for $14 an acre in 2025, and pasture went for $8 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $1,776 an acre in 2022, up 81.4% from $979 in 2017. That puts the county 8th highest of 22 in Wyoming, above the state median of $1,248. In 2022, 42% of the farmland here was rented.
