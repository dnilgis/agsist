Platte County, Wyoming

Platte County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2025); land and buildings $1,028 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56031. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Cash rent in Platte County runs $22 per acre non-irrigated and $120 per acre irrigated, with 60 percent of harvested cropland under irrigation as of the 2022 census. Groundwater supplies 17 percent of that irrigation water, so most of it comes from surface sources. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 1 percent, 20 percent, 24 percent, 32 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. Hail remains the largest single cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 26 percent, and irrigation failure alone has cost $5.7 million across all closed years. Land value rose 17.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.2 percent a year.
