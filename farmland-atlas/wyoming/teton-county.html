Teton County, Wyoming

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 24.82 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 77 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,196 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value rose while next door fell; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.28 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Aug 31, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 0.1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 158.1% from 2016 to 2026. 48% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 137.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,099 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $9.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

66.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.
