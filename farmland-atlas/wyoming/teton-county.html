Non-irrigated cash rent in Teton County stands at $40 per acre in 2026, up 158.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent runs slightly higher, at $45 per acre. Irrigated cropland covers 67 percent of harvested acres, all of it drawn from surface water, with none from groundwater in 2015. Drought has touched the county in 375 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 131 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
