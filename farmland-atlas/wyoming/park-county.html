Park County, Wyoming

Park County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2021); land and buildings $2,842 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.80 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56029. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland in Park County rents for $110 per acre in 2026, against $30 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2021. Irrigation covers 89 percent of harvested cropland, and none of that water comes from groundwater. July nights have warmed from 46.6 F normal to 47.8 F in 2017-2026, a trend of 0.56 F per decade. Land values rose 90.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
