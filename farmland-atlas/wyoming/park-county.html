The census value of land and buildings in Park County was $2,842 an acre in 2022, up 90.2% from $1,494 in 2017. That sits 2nd highest of 22 in Wyoming, against a state median of $1,248. Irrigated rent ran $110 an acre in 2026, 4th highest of 15, and pasture rent was $14 an acre in 2026. Just 17% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
