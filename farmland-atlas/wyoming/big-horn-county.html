As of Oct 16, irrigated cropland in Big Horn County rents for $106 an acre in 2026, 5th highest of 15 in Wyoming against a state median of $78. The census value of land and buildings was $2,313 an acre in 2022, up 45.6% from $1,589 in 2017 and 3rd highest of 22 in the state. Pasture rents for $23 an acre in 2025, and 42% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
