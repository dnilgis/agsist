Big Horn County, Wyoming

Big Horn County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $23 an acre (2020); land and buildings $2,313 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56003. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent here runs $106 per acre, against $23 per acre for non-irrigated ground, and 82 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Groundwater supplies only 1 percent of that irrigation, so this county depends on surface water. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 3 percent in 1989-1999 to 9 percent in 2000-2009, 7 percent in 2010-2019, and 23 percent in 2020-2025. Freeze and frost remains the largest cause of indemnities overall, at 33 percent.
