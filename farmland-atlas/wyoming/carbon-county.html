As of Oct 16, cropland in Carbon County rented for $17 an acre in 2022, and irrigated ground went for $24 in 2026, the lowest of 15 in the state against a $78 state median. Pasture rented for $4 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $971 an acre in 2022, up 76.5% from $550 in 2017, and 8th lowest of 22 against a $1,248 state median. 38% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
