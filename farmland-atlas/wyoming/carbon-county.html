Carbon County, Wyoming

Carbon County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $17 an acre (2022); land and buildings $971 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56007. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Carbon County runs $24 per acre, against $971 per acre for land and buildings in the 2022 census. Irrigation covers 96 percent of harvested cropland, and none of that water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Land value rose 76.5 percent from 2017-2022, 12 percent a year. Drought has touched the county in 327 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2003 the worst year at 52 weeks; 2021-2025 saw 31 such weeks. July nightly lows have warmed 0.4 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 51.6 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 50.6 F. Wind operations already total 1754.6 MW, with 3556.6 MW more proposed.
