U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

The census value of land and buildings in Uinta County ran $1,330 an acre in 2022, up 24.1% from $1,072 in 2017, or 4.4% a year. That puts it 11th highest of 22 in Wyoming, against a state median of $1,248. Irrigated rent was $41 an acre in 2026, 2nd lowest of 15, well under the $78 state median. Pasture went for $8 an acre in 2026, and 41% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
