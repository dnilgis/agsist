Irrigated cropland in Washakie County rents for $162 an acre in 2026. That ranks 2nd highest of 15 in Wyoming, against a state median of $78. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,623 an acre in 2022, up 38.8% from $1,169 in 2017, or 6.8% a year. 26% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
