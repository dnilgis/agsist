Albany County, Wyoming

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,040 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 11.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 119 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,716 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,040 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.16 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 1% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 38.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 504 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 113,732 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $7.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

80.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Quick answers for Albany County

What is cash rent in Albany County, Wyoming?

The county average is $40 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Albany County, Wyoming?

Land and buildings were worth $1,040 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
