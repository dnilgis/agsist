Cash rent in Lincoln County runs $30 an acre for 2026, up 62.2% from $19 in 2016, and that sits 2nd lowest of 4 against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings was $3,963 an acre in 2022, up 116.8% from $1,828 in 2017. That census value ranks highest of 22 in Wyoming, well over the state median of $1,248.
