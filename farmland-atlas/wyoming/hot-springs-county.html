Hot Springs County, Wyoming

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $823 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 8.71 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 72% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 145 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,031 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $823 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 31.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 10,224 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $8.70 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

99.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 1.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1997 to 2022; too much rain caused 20.4%.

That still sits 4th lowest of 22 in the state, under the $1,248 median. 25% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
