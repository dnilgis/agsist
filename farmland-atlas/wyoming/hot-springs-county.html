Every acre of harvested cropland in Hot Springs County is irrigated, and only 3 percent of that irrigation water comes from groundwater, 2015. Irrigation failure is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities, 1997-2022, at 71 percent, totaling $167,408 of $237,346 in closed-year claims. Heat and drought made up 0 percent of indemnities 2000-2009 and 4 percent 2010-2019. The county spent 75 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 421 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 53 weeks. July nightly lows have warmed 0.57 F per decade since 1976, and land value rose 32.5 percent from 2017-2022.
