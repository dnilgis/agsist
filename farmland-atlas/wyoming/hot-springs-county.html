As of Oct 16, irrigated cash rent in Hot Springs County runs $101 an acre for 2026, 6th highest of 15 in Wyoming against a state median of $78. The census value of land and buildings was $823 an acre in 2022, up 32.5% from $621 in 2017, or 5.8% a year. That still sits 4th lowest of 22 in the state, under the $1,248 median. 25% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
