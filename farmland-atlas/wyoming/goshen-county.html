Goshen County, Wyoming

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,871 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 13.4 inches, 94% of normal (14.31 inches) through Nov 18, at Torrington, 29 mi N. Frost-free season: 141 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,404 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,871 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $20.50 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 30% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 87.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 163 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 16,966 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: no elevator we read within 30 miles.

Program

Corn ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Run yours in the ARC or PLC calculator.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $8.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.45 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 90% of the median yield. 66.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 7.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.4%.
