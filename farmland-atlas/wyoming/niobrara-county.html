Niobrara County, Wyoming

Niobrara County, WY: non-irrigated cash rent $14 an acre (2025); land and buildings $791 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 56027. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland here rents for $135 per acre in 2026, against $14 per acre for non-irrigated ground, and irrigation draws 91 percent of its water from groundwater as of 2015. Non-irrigated rent is up 16.7 percent from 2013 to 2025. The heat and drought share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 3 percent, 9 percent, 48 percent, 55 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed 0.54 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 57.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 56.2 F. Census land value rose 26.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.8 percent a year.
