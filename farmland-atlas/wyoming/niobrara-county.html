Cropland rent in Niobrara County runs $14 an acre for 2025, up 16.7% from $12 in 2013. Irrigated ground rents for $135 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 15 and well over the state median of $78. The census value of land and buildings came in at $791 an acre in 2022, up 26.2% from $627 in 2017, yet that still sits 3rd lowest of 22 against a state median of $1,248.
