As of Oct 16, cash rent in Decatur County runs $167 an acre for 2026, tied for lowest of 99 in Iowa against a state median of $266. That rent was $163 in 2016, up 2.5% in ten years. The census value of land and buildings moved more, from $3,845 in 2017 to $5,732 in 2022, up 49.1%, and still 8th lowest of 99. Corn trends to 196.4 bu, 5th lowest of 83, with 2025 at 182.9 bu.
