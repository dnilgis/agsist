Marion County, Iowa

Marion County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $230 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,647 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19125. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent averages $230 per acre in 2026, up 17.9 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $7,647 per acre, with that value up 32.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.8 percent a year. All harvested cropland is non-irrigated. Crop insurance history shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 45 percent, but the heat and drought share has moved from 4 percent in 1989-1999 to 13 percent in 2000-2009, 47 percent in 2010-2019, and 52 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio for 2016-2025 sits at 0.18, below the 1989-2025 ratio of 0.55. Solar and wind capacity are not yet developed in the county.
