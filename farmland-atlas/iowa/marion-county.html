USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Marion County runs $230 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $195 in 2016 and 14th lowest of 99 in Iowa against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $7,647 an acre in 2022, up 32.5% from $5,771 in 2017, and sits 21st lowest of 99. The 14-yr corn trend projects 218.8 bu for 2026, right on the state median. 38% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
