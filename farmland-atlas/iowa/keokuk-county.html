Non-irrigated cash rent in Keokuk County is $236 per acre for 2026, up 7.8 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 was $7,382 per acre, up 26.5 percent from 2017, 4.8 percent a year. The county logged 53 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 179 of 1357 weeks going back to 2000. No irrigation is in use, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
