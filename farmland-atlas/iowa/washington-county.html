Washington County, Iowa

Washington County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $286 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,552 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 185.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19183. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County, IA runs $286 per acre in 2026, up 19.2 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $9,552 per acre in 2022, up 38.7 percent from 2017, 6.8 percent a year, and the 2022 rent-to-value ratio was 2.48 percent. Corn yield trend is 3.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, median 185.6 bushels per acre, with a worst year of 132.1 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved 19 percent, 24 percent, 50 percent, 63 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.18, well under the 1989-2025 figure of 0.48. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, with 0 MW proposed to EIA.
