Osceola County, Iowa

Rain is at 104% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $14,483 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.53 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 30.21 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 104% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 162 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,601 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.53 per base acre, PLC $4.16.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (165.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $2.03 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $36.64 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (48.63 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $77.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,168 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 88% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 19.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.7%.
