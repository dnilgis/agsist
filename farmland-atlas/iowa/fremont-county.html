Fremont County, Iowa

Rain is at 121% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $10,073 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.75 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 39.15 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 121% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 184 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,106 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.75 per base acre, PLC $3.66.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (145.97 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $103.93 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $32.26 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.49 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $80.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,262 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.91 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.37, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. 4.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 15.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 70.2%.
