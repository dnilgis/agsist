As of Oct 16, cash rent in Fremont County runs $215 an acre for 2026, down 1.8% from $219 in 2016. That ranks 10th lowest of 99 in Iowa, under a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $7,991 an acre in 2022, up 33.1% from $6,002 in 2017. Corn made 195.8 bu in 2025, a little under the recent average of 204.5 bu.
