Page County, Iowa

Rain is at 129% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $8,513 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.63 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 44.14 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 129% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 181 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,050 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.23 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.19, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 64% of the median yield. 0.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.5%.
