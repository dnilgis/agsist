Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.83 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.37, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 27.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 41.8%.

Quick answers for Pocahontas County

What is cash rent in Pocahontas County, Iowa?

The county average is $269 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Pocahontas County, Iowa?

Land and buildings were worth $11,081 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Pocahontas County, Iowa?

The county's 2021-25 average is 209.0 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
