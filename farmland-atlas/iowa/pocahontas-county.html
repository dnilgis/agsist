Pocahontas County, Iowa

Pocahontas County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $269 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,081 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19151. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $269 per acre for 2026, up 17.5 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $11,081 per acre in 2022, with land value up 37.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.6 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 190.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 163.8 in 2013. Wind power operates in the county at 366.4 MW; no irrigation is reported on cropland here.
