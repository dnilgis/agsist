Cash rent in Winnebago County runs $262 an acre for 2026, up 12.0% from $234 in 2016 and just under the state median of $266, tied for 44th lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings was $9,707 an acre in 2022, up 38.9% from $6,989 in 2017 and 40th highest of 99. Corn trends at 215.6 bu for 2026, and 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
