Winnebago County, Iowa

Winnebago County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $262 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,707 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19189. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $262 per acre in 2026, up 12 percent from 2016. Land value sits at $9,707 per acre, up 38.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, and rent takes 2.71 percent of that value. Drought weeks were limited too, 110 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2012 the worst year at 41 weeks. No cropland here is irrigated, and July nights show no clear warming direction, 61.8 F now against a 61.1 F normal.
