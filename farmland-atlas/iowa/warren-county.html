Stands out against its own record

$218

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2014 (17 surveys on record since 2008); up 12.4% from $194 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Warren County cropland rents for $218 an acre in 2026, 11th lowest of 99 in Iowa and under the state median of $266. That is up 12.4% from $194 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,820 an acre in 2022, up 38.8% from $5,636 in 2017. Corn runs behind the state too, with the trend at 200.1 bu against a state median of 218.8 bu.
