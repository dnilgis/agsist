Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.02 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.54, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 34% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 54.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 35.3%.
