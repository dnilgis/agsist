Cash rent in Henry County runs $253 an acre for 2026, up 11.0% from $228 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $266 and ranks tied for 32nd lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings was $7,797 an acre in 2022, up 24.4% from $6,267 in 2017, which ranks 25th lowest of 99. Corn trends toward 202.3 bu for 2026, and 39% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
