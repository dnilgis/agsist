USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Adair County cash rent sits at $237 an acre for 2026, up 21.5% from $195 in 2016. That ranks 18th lowest of 99 counties, under the state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $7,226 an acre in 2022, up 38.2% from $5,230 in 2017, and 17th lowest of 99. Corn trends at 201.9 bu, 11th lowest of 83 against a state median of 218.8 bu.
