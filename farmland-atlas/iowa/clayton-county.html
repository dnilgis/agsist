USDA NASS county yields, 2025

Cash rent in Clayton County runs $266 an acre for 2026, right at the state median of $266 and tied for 49th lowest of 99. That is up 9.0% from $244 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $7,648 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $5,545 in 2017, and still 22nd lowest of 99 against a state median of $9,410. Corn came in at 224.0 bu in 2025.

Sources and terms

Every figure is a county average from the sources below, not an appraisal of any farm. "Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed. Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. What each column means: county data and how to cite.

This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS. Averages, trends, ranks and estimates are calculations by AGSIST.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields.
