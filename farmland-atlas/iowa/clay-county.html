Clay County, Iowa

Clay County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $248 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,321 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 184.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Clay County runs $248 per acre for 2026, up 11.2 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $10,321 per acre in 2022, up 29.3 percent from 2017, 5.3 percent a year. Cash rent in 2022 took 2.6 percent of that census value. Corn yield trend runs 1.2 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 184.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 158.5 in 2018. Crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025 total $140.2 million, largest cause excess moisture and flood at 39 percent, with heat and drought share highest in 2000-2009 at 46 percent. No irrigated cropland was reported in the 2022 census.
