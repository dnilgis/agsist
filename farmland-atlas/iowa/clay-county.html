USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Clay County ran $248 an acre in 2026, up 11.2% from $223 in 2016 and below the state median of $266, tied for 28th lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings was $10,321 an acre in 2022, up 29.3% from $7,985 in 2017 and 30th highest of 99. Corn trends at 200.0 bu, 9th lowest of 83, and 57% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
