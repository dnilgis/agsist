Ida County, Iowa

Rain is at 96% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $13,117 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.63 per base acre for corn.

Ida County vs Iowa: $291 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 84% of the other 98 Iowa counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 16th highest of 99).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.35 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,779 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $66.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2,412 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.72 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.46, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 8.7%.
