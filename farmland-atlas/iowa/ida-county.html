Ida County, Iowa

Ida County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $291 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,698 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 203.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19093. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Ida County runs $291 per acre, up 3.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value in 2022 stood at $10,698 per acre, up 45.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 7.8 percent a year, and 2022 rent equaled 2.73 percent of that value. County corn yield trend runs 2.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 203.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 151.4 in 2012. Heat and drought drove 59 percent of indemnities across 1989-2025, and that share moved from 11 percent in 1989-1999 to 67 percent in 2000-2009, 55 percent in 2010-2019, and 67 percent in 2020-2025. Wind power operating in the county totals 502.1 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
