Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.47 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.42, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 79% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.3, 2010 to 2025.
