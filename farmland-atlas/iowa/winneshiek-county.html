Non-irrigated cash rent in Winneshiek County is $274 per acre for 2026, up 14.6 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 was $8,233 per acre, with land value up 28.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5.2 percent a year. The corn yield trend for 2008-2025 runs 2.5 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 192.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 140.6 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved 22 percent, 31 percent, 40 percent, 62 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. The county has no irrigated cropland as of the 2022 census.
