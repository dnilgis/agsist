Cropland rent in Jefferson County runs $244 an acre for 2026, up 15.6% from $211 in 2016 and tied for 24th lowest of 99 against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $6,951 an acre in 2022, up 25.5% from $5,538 in 2017, which ranks 15th lowest of 99. Corn trends at 203.1 bu for 2026, and 42% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
