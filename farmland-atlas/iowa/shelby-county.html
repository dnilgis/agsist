Shelby County, Iowa

Shelby County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $270 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,443 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19165. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Shelby County stands at $270 per acre for 2026, up 13 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census was $9,443 per acre, up 37.8 percent from 2017, a pace of 6.6 percent a year; cash rent runs at 2.77 percent of land value. No irrigation is present on any harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and no solar or wind generation is operating or proposed.
