Kossuth County, Iowa

Rain is at 90% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $12,414 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.68 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.62 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,639 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

USDA cash rent rose 24.2% from 2016 to 2026. 56% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 74% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 36.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,130 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $75.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 5,325 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.78 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 86% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.5%.
