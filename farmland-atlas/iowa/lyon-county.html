Lyon County, Iowa

Rain is at 98% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $15,764 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.50 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 27.19 inches, 98% of normal (27.86 inches) through Nov 17, at Rock Rapids. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,653 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.28 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.45, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 84% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.7%.
