As of Oct 14, Lyon County cash rent sits at $284 an acre for 2026, up 15.0% from $247 in 2016 and tied for 25th highest of 99 counties against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings came in at $12,857 an acre in 2022, up 28.7% from $9,988 in 2017. That ranks 3rd highest of 99 in Iowa. Corn runs the other way, with a 14-yr trend of 210.1 bu that is 21st lowest of 83 and under the state median of 218.8 bu.
