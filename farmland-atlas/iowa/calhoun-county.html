Cash rent in Calhoun County runs $267 an acre for 2026, up 6.4% from $251 in 2016, and it sits tied for 48th highest of 99 against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings came in at $10,686 an acre in 2022, up 34.6% from $7,942 in 2017, good for 25th highest of 99. The corn trend projects 227.2 bu for 2026, 15th highest of 83 in the state.
