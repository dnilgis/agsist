USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cash rent in Wayne County runs $199 an acre for 2026, 8th lowest of 99 in Iowa against a state median of $266. That is up 22.1% from $163 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,741 an acre in 2022, up 35.0% from $4,252 in 2017, and still 9th lowest of 99 against a state median of $9,410.
