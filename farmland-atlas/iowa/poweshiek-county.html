Heat and drought have pulled harder on this county's crop insurance over time. From 1989-1999 through 2000-2009, heat and drought ran 14 percent of indemnities. Since 2010-2019, it jumped to 45 percent of claims. The county has paid out $124.6 million in indemnities over closed crop years 1989-2025, with heat and drought the largest cause. Corn yield has climbed 2.7 bushels per acre a year from 2008-2025, but the worst year was 2020 at 138.2 bushels. Cash rent is $278 per acre in 2026, up 13.9 percent since 2016.
