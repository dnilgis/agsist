Cass County, Iowa

Rain is at 125% of normal. Land and buildings run $8,221 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.59 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 40.65 inches, 125% of normal (32.58 inches) through Nov 13, at Atlantic, 7.1 mi NNE. Frost-free season: 176 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,855 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $8,221 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $259 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 19.9% from 2016 to 2026. 47% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 43.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5.5% of the cropland, CRP left out. 153.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 80 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 30 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 2 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.15 at Adair, 19 miles. 8 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 70 cents under Jan '27 futures, 1 elevator.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.59 per base acre, PLC $3.82.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (152.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (45.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $80.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 3,630 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.65 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.28 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 67% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 52% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.3%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Cass County runs $259 an acre for 2026, up 19.9% from $216 in 2016. That sits just under the Iowa median of $266 and ranks 41st lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings came in at $8,221 an acre in 2022, up 39.5% from $5,895 in 2017, still below the state median of $9,410. Corn trends at 213.1 bu for 2026, and 47% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
