The survey reports this county with Decatur; the figures and responses cover both. Iowa State University cash rent survey, released May 2026; a separate survey from USDA's. Iowa State's land value is down 1.1% from 2024 in the same survey. Fewer than 5 recorded farm sales here in the last two years, so no sale median. Soils: NCCPI 0.72 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 11% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 19% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 14.9% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 19% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 137.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 291 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 748 acres, so read the change with care.

Quick answers for Ringgold County

What is cash rent in Ringgold County, Iowa?

The county average is $201 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Ringgold County, Iowa?

Land and buildings were worth $5,670 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
