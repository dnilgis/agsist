Buena Vista County, Iowa

Buena Vista County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $260 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,332 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 190.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Buena Vista County is $260 per acre in 2026, up 6.1 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $11,332 per acre, with a 40.9 percent change from 2017 to 2022, 7.1 percent a year. None of the county's harvested cropland is irrigated. Corn yield trend runs 2.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 190.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 148.2 in 2012. Heat and drought caused 36 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and drought weeks ran higher in 2021-2025 than the 2000-2025 average share.
