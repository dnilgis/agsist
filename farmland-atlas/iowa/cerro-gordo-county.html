Cash rent in Cerro Gordo County runs $260 an acre for 2026, up 8.3% from $240 in 2016 and a bit under the Iowa median of $266, tied for 42nd lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings was $9,033 an acre in 2022, up 23.0% from $7,341 in 2017. That sits 44th lowest of 99 against a state median of $9,410. Corn trends at 217.2 bu for 2026, close to the state median of 218.8 bu.
