Fayette County, Iowa

Fayette County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $270 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,138 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 191.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19065. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Fayette County is $270 per acre for 2026, up 13 percent from 2016. Land value under the census operators' estimate was $9,138 per acre in 2022, up 27.3 percent from 2017, or 5 percent a year, and 2022 rent equaled 2.77 percent of that value. Corn yields have trended up 3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 191.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 151 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities rose across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, reaching 43 percent in 2020-2025, though the loss ratio for 2016-2025 was 0.39. Irrigation share is 0 percent, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
