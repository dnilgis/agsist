Sioux County, Iowa

Rain is at 88% of normal. Land and buildings run $13,341 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.14 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 24.85 inches, 88% of normal (28.17 inches) through Nov 15, at Sioux Center, 2 mi SE. Frost-free season: 163 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,707 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $13,341 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $336 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 27.8% from 2016 to 2026. 55.8% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 39.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 785.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 80 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

County median corn basis: 35 cents under Dec '26 futures, median of 8 elevators in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $4.22 at POET Ashton, 27 miles. 28 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down. County median soybean basis: 69 cents under Jan '27 futures, median of 6 elevators.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.14 per base acre, PLC $4.33.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (172.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (52.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $77.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,206 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.69 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.49 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. 3.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 49.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.6%.
