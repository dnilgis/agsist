USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in O'Brien County runs $310 an acre for 2026, 3rd highest of 99 in Iowa and above the state median of $266. That rent is up 28.6% from $241 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $13,843 an acre in 2022, highest of 99, up 42.3% from $9,727 in 2017. 57% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
