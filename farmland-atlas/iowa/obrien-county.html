O'Brien County, Iowa

O'Brien County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $310 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,843 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 200.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19141. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in O'Brien County runs $310 per acre for 2026, up 28.6 percent from 2016. The census put land and buildings at $13,843 per acre in 2022, after a 42.3 percent rise from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trended up 1.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 200.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 159.8 in 2012. Crop insurance history over 1989-2025 shows excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 32 percent, with heat and drought's share moving from 1 percent in 1989-1999 to 23 percent in 2000-2009, 15 percent in 2010-2019, and 27 percent in 2020-2025. Wind capacity operating in the county stands at 752.3 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
