Sac County, Iowa

Sac County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $290 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,519 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 196.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19161. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Sac County is $290 per acre in 2026, up 16.9 percent from 2016. Land value reached $11,519 per acre in 2022, up 45 percent from 2017, and rent takes 2.34 percent of that value. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and wind capacity stands at 210.9 MW with no solar operating.
