Guthrie County, Iowa

Guthrie County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $248 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,494 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 176.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19077. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Guthrie County averages $248 per acre for 2026, up 13.8 percent from 2016. Census land value for 2022 stood at $8,494 per acre, up 34.8 percent from 2017, or 6.2 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 3.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2022, with a median of 176.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 113.4 in 2012. Heat and drought has driven indemnities in every decade counted: 13 percent of losses in 1989-1999, 45 percent in 2000-2009, 67 percent in 2010-2019, and 55 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and wind capacity operating in the county is 207.1 MW.
