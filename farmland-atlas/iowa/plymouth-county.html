Plymouth County, Iowa

Cash rent in Plymouth County ran $288 an acre in 2026, tied for 20th highest of 99 in Iowa and up 11.6% from $258 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $11,556 an acre in 2022, 6th highest of 99, up 35.0% from $8,561 in 2017. Corn made 195.6 bu in 2024, a bit over the 192.0 bu median going back to 2008. In 2022, 51% of the farmland was rented.
