Rain is at 123% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $10,775 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.78 per base acre for corn.

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.40 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.35, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 68% of the median yield. 1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 29% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 44.2%.
