Carroll County, Iowa

Carroll County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $305 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,742 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 195.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19027. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carroll County runs $305 per acre for 2026, up 25 percent from 2016. The census value of land and buildings stood at $10,742 per acre in 2022, after a 33.8 percent rise from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields have trended up 3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, with a median of 195.7 bushels per acre and a worst year in 2012 at 105.3. Heat and drought has driven most crop insurance losses over time, rising from 4 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999 to 78 percent in 2010-2019, then 63 percent in 2020-2025. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland here, and wind capacity of 284.8 MW operates in the county with no solar.
