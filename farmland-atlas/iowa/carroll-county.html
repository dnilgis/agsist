Cash rent in Carroll County runs $305 an acre for 2026. That is 4th highest of 99 in Iowa and up 25.0% from $244 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $10,742 an acre in 2022, up 33.8% from $8,030 in 2017. Corn holds up its end too, with a trend yield of 239.1 bu projected for 2026, 3rd highest of 83 in the state.
