Crawford County, Iowa

Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $12,563 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.30 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 168 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,795 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Solar and wind proposed but not built: 252 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.24 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.47, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 66% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 2.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 5.7%.
