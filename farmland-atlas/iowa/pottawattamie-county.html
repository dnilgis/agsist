Cash rent in Pottawattamie County runs $255 an acre for 2026, up 11.4% from $229 in 2016. That sits under the Iowa median of $266 and ranks tied for 34th lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings was $8,718 an acre in 2022, up 28.5% from $6,784 in 2017, still short of the $9,410 state median. In 2022, 56% of farmland here was rented.
