Non-irrigated cash rent in Pottawattamie County averages $255 per acre for 2026, up 11.4 percent from 2016. Land value came in at $8,718 per acre in 2022, up 28.5 percent from 2017. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, so the corn crop rests on rain and the water record: 174 of 1357 weeks, 12.8 percent, logged severe drought or worse since 2000, with 40 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. July nights average 64.7 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.5 F.
