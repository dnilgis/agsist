Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 22.0% from $227 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Johnson County was $277 an acre in 2026, up 22.0% from $227 in 2016 and tied for 36th highest of 99 against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $10,307 an acre in 2022, up 28.2% from $8,037 in 2017, which ranks 32nd highest of 99. Corn yields here track the state close, with a 14-yr trend of 218.7 bu against a state median of 218.8 bu. In 2022, 45% of the farmland was rented.
