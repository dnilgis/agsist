Johnson County, Iowa

Johnson County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $277 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,307 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 184.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19103. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Johnson County is $277 per acre in 2026, up 22 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $10,307 per acre, with a 28.2 percent change from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 21 percent in 1989-1999, to 25 percent in 2000-2009, to 53 percent in 2010-2019, to 51 percent in 2020-2025. The county saw 132 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2023 as the worst year at 25 weeks. July average nightly low sits at 64.3 F for 2017-2026, against a 63.5 F normal for 1991-2020. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
