Monona County, Iowa

Monona County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $272 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,044 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 176.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19133. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Monona County runs $272 per acre in 2026, up 16.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $296 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved from 1.10 in 2011 to 1.09 in 2026, direction rising. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 1.6 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 176.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 115.1 in 2012. Heat and drought stand as the largest cause of indemnities over 1989-2025 at 43 percent, and the share has moved from 11 percent in 1989-1999, to 49 percent in 2000-2009, to 35 percent in 2010-2019, to 56 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 85 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 183 weeks total from 2000-2025.
