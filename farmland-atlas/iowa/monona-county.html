As of Oct 16, Monona County cash rent sits at $272 an acre for 2026, up 16.7% from $233 in 2016 and a touch over the $266 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $8,044 an acre in 2022, up 39.6% from $5,762 in 2017, and that still ranks 31st lowest of 99 in Iowa. Corn came in at 203.1 bu in 2025, well above the 186.1 bu average for 2021 to 2025.
