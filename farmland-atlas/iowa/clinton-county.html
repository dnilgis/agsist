$304

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 26.1% from $241 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Clinton County runs $304 an acre for 2026, 5th highest of 99 in Iowa and above the state median of $266. That is up 26.1% from $241 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $9,600 an acre in 2022, up 27.3% from 2017. Corn trends at 229.1 bu, 11th highest of 83, against a state median of 218.8 bu.
