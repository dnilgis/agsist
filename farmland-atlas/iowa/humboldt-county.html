As of Oct 16, cash rent in Humboldt County runs $262 an acre for 2026, up 4.0% from $252 in 2016 and just under the state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $10,740 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $8,088 in 2017 and 20th highest of 99. Corn trends at 214.4 bu for 2026, a bit under the state median of 218.8 bu. 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
