Humboldt County, Iowa

Humboldt County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $262 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,740 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 192.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19091. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Humboldt County runs $262 per acre, up 4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $10,740 per acre, with a 32.8 percent gain from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield trend runs 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 192.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 146.1 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood drove crop insurance losses, at 40 percent of the $106.3 million paid out over 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share moved from 3 percent in 1989-1999 to 20 percent in 2000-2009 to 28 percent in 2010-2019 and 2020-2025. No irrigated cropland is reported, and no solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed in the county.
