Humboldt County, Iowa

Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $12,719 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.25 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 171 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,697 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Iowa State University cash rent survey, released May 2026; a separate survey from USDA's. Iowa State's land value is down 3.8% from 2024 in the same survey. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.81 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 35% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 47% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 4% from 2016 to 2026. 60% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 74% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 33.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 725 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.35 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.44, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 76% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 25.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 40.1%.
