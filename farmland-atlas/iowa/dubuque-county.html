$303

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 16.1% from $261 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Dubuque County ran $303 an acre in 2026, tied for 6th highest of 99 in Iowa and above the state median of $266. That rent is up 16.1% from $261 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,692 an acre in 2022, up 23.9% from $8,633 in 2017. The corn trend for 2026 sits at 228.3 bu, 12th highest of 83 in the state.
