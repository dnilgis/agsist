Dubuque County, Iowa

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dubuque County runs $303 per acre for 2026, up 16.1 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $10,692 per acre in 2022, up 23.9 percent from 2017, a pace of 4.4 percent a year. Corn yields carry a trend of 3.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 198.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 149.4 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 17 percent in 1989-1999 to 14 percent in 2000-2009 to 55 percent in 2010-2019 to 56 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 0.46, below the 1989-2025 figure of 0.78. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
