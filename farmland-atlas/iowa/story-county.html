Cash rent in Story County runs $290 an acre for 2026, tied for 18th highest of 99 and above the state median of $266. That rent was $230 in 2016, up 26.1% over ten years. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,914 an acre in 2022, 13th highest of 99, up 15.3% from $9,467 in 2017. Corn trends at 217.2 bu for 2026, a hair under the state median of 218.8 bu.

Quick answers for Story County

What is cash rent in Story County, Iowa?

The county average is $290 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Story County, Iowa?

Land and buildings were worth $10,914 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Story County, Iowa?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 217.2 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 14 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
