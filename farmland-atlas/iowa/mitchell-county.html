Mitchell County, Iowa

Mitchell County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $277 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,387 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 193.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19131. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mitchell County runs $277 per acre in 2026, up 11.7 percent from 2016. Census land value in 2022 stood at $10,387 per acre, with a 32.1 percent gain from 2017 to 2022, 5.7 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2008-2025 runs 2.5 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 193.9 bushels per acre and a worst year of 131.4 in 2012. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved through the periods this way: 2 percent in 1989-1999, 26 percent in 2000-2009, 40 percent in 2010-2019, 73 percent in 2020-2025. Weeks in severe drought or worse totaled 48 for 2021-2025, against 113 of 1357 weeks for 2000-2025.
