Non-irrigated cash rent in Dallas County runs $241 per acre, up 5.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $10,688 per acre, up 30.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 2.8 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 183.7 bushels per acre and a low of 129 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 44 percent of indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and the share by period runs 7 percent, 30 percent, 59 percent, then 24 percent. No cropland here is irrigated. Drought weeks ran 118 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 32 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
