Monroe County cash rent ran $174 an acre in 2026, up 4.8% from $166 in 2016 and 4th lowest of 99 in Iowa against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $5,613 an acre in 2022, up 33.5% from $4,206 in 2017, and 5th lowest of 99. Corn trends to 196.1 bu for 2026, 4th lowest of 83, and 30% of farmland was rented in 2022.
