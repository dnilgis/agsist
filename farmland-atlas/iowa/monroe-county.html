Monroe County, Iowa

Monroe County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $174 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,613 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 162.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19135. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Monroe County runs $174 per acre in 2026, up 4.8 percent from 2016. Land value, per the operators' own estimate, stood at $5,613 per acre in 2022, up 33.5 percent from 2017, or 5.9 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 4.4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 162.3 bushels per acre and a low of 67.3 in 2012. Crop insurance shows the shift: heat and drought made up 7 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 14 percent in 2000-2009, 51 percent in 2010-2019, and 61 percent in 2020-2025, while flood and excess moisture still lead over the full 1989-2025 record at 51 percent. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
