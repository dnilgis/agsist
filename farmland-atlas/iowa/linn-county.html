Cash rent in Linn County runs $258 an acre for 2026, up 10.7% from $233 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $266, tied for 38th lowest of 99. The census value of land and buildings was $10,758 an acre in 2022, up 29.2% from $8,328 in 2017 and 16th highest of 99. The corn trend sits at 226.0 bu for 2026, tied for 18th highest of 83, and 49% of farmland was rented in 2022.
