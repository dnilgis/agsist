Cash rent in Black Hawk County runs $257 an acre for 2026, up 1.2% from $254 in 2016, and tied for 36th lowest of 99 against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $9,980 an acre in 2022, up 14.4% from $8,723 in 2017, and 35th highest of 99. Corn trends at 219.2 bu, and 55% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
