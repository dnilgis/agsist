Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 20.1% from $244 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Buchanan County runs $293 an acre for 2026, up 20.1% from $244 in 2016. That sits tied for 14th highest of 99 in Iowa, above the state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $9,819 an acre in 2022, up 24.5% from $7,884 in 2017. Corn trends at 224.7 bu for 2026, and 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
