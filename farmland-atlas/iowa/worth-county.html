Worth County, Iowa

Worth County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $241 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,241 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 192.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19195. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Worth County runs $241 per acre, up 7.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value sits at $9,241 per acre, up 32.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yield trend runs 2.3 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 192.3 bushels per acre and a worst year of 143.1 in 2012. Excess moisture and flood account for 47 percent of indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and heat and drought's share of indemnities has moved from 1 percent in 1989-1999 to 19 percent in 2000-2009, 20 percent in 2010-2019, and 32 percent in 2020-2025. The county carries 350.9 MW of operating wind and no operating solar.
