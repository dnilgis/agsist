Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.50 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 98% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 167 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.84 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.71, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).
