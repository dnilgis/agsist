Cash rent in Marshall County runs $286 an acre for 2026. That is up 17.7% from $243 in 2016 and tied for 23rd highest of 99 in Iowa. The census value of land and buildings was $9,375 an acre in 2022, up 23.4% from 2017, or 4.3% a year. The corn trend projects 240.3 bu for 2026, 2nd highest of 83 in the state, against a state median of 218.8 bu.
