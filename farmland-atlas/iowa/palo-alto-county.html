Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest since 2013 (17 surveys on record since 2008); up 21.0% from $233 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Palo Alto County runs $282 an acre for 2026, up 21.0% from $233 in 2016 and tied for 30th highest of 99 in Iowa. The census value of land and buildings was $9,830 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $8,014 in 2017. Corn made 209.9 bu in 2025, above the 18 year median of 185.2 bu. In 2022, 61% of the farmland was rented.
