Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.19 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 169 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,693 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $46.26 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $35.73 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $77.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 3,854 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.17 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.57, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 85% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 14.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 53.8%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Palo Alto County runs $282 an acre for 2026, up 21.0% from $233 in 2016 and tied for 30th highest of 99 in Iowa. The census value of land and buildings was $9,830 an acre in 2022, up 22.7% from $8,014 in 2017. Corn made 209.9 bu in 2025, above the 18 year median of 185.2 bu. In 2022, 61% of the farmland was rented.
