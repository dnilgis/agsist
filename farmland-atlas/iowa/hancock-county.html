Hancock County, Iowa

Hancock County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $265 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,457 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 194.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19081. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Hancock County averages $265 per acre in 2026, up 15.7 percent from 2016. Land value from the 2022 Census sits at $9,457 per acre, the operators' own estimate, up 24.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 4.4 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 2.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 194.8 bushels per acre and a worst year of 143.3 in 2012. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 total $179.7 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 35 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 closed years is 0.53. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved 1 percent, 13 percent, 32 percent, 14 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025.
