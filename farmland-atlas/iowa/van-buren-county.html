Van Buren County, Iowa

Van Buren County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $197 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,451 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 152.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19177. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Van Buren County runs $197 per acre in 2026, up 16.6 percent from 2016. Land is valued by operators at $5,451 per acre as of 2022, with a 16.6 percent gain from 2017 to 2022, 3.1 percent a year. Corn yield trend is 3.5 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2023, with a median of 152.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 90.8 in 2010. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has climbed across the decades listed: 19 percent in 1989-1999, 17 percent in 2000-2009, 50 percent in 2010-2019, 54 percent in 2020-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed.
