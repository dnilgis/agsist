Cropland rent in Adams County runs $232 an acre for 2026, up 12.1% from $207 in 2016 and 15th lowest of 99 against a state median of $266. The census value of land and buildings was $6,476 an acre in 2022, up 29.9% from $4,987 in 2017, and that sits 11th lowest of 99. 46% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
