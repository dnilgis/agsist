Cash rent in Butler County runs $301 an acre for 2026, tied for 9th highest of 99 in Iowa and above the state median of $266. That rent is up 32.0% from $228 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $8,547 an acre in 2022, up 19.2% from $7,169 in 2017, yet it sits 39th lowest of 99 and below the state median of $9,410. Renters farm 53% of the ground here.
