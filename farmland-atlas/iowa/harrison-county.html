Harrison County, Iowa

Rain is at 109% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $10,994 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $13.62 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 109% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 177 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,959 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $13.62 per base acre, PLC $3.92.

Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $105.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $34.50 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $66.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 4,148 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.28 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.61, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 25.4%.
