USDA NASS county yields, 2024

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Clarke County runs $167 an acre for 2026, tied for lowest of 99 in Iowa against a state median of $266. That rent is down 21.2% from $212 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,329 an acre in 2022, up 24.8% from $4,271 in 2017 and 3rd lowest of 99. Corn trends at 203.9 bu, under the state median of 218.8 bu.
