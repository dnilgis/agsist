Bremer County, Iowa

Bremer County, IA: non-irrigated cash rent $291 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,905 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 200.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 19017. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bremer County runs $291 per acre in 2026, up 11.1 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $9,905 per acre in 2022, up 24 percent from 2017. Heat and drought has been the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 44 percent of $118.4 million paid; its share by period was 3 percent in 1989-1999, 26 percent in 2000-2009, 52 percent in 2010-2019, and 45 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.45, below the 0.75 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record. Drought weeks in severe category or worse ran 108 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 68 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
