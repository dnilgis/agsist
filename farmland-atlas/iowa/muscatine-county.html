Muscatine County, Iowa

Rain is at 131% of normal. Iowa State's 2025 survey puts farmland at $12,125 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $15.35 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 46.21 inches, 131% of normal (35.3 inches) through Nov 17, at Muscatine, 2 mi N. Frost-free season: 184 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,946 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $1.23 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.20, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 79% of the median yield. 9.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 35.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 35.2%.
