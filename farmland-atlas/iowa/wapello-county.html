The survey reports this county with Mahaska; the figures and responses cover both. Iowa State University cash rent survey, released May 2026; a separate survey from USDA's. Iowa State's land value is up 1.4% from 2024 in the same survey. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.77 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 22% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 18% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 21.1% from 2016 to 2026. 35% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 28% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 71.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 46 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.79 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.34, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 56% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025.
