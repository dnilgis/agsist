July average nightly low for 2017-2026 runs 70.8 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.9 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.88 F per decade, warming. Out of all Julys since 1900, 17 have had an average low at or above 70 F, and the first decade to reach a 66 F average low was the 1930s. Drought has touched this county in 71 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 39 weeks, and 26 weeks fell in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
