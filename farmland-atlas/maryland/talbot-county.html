Talbot County, Maryland

Talbot County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $136 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,250 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Talbot County runs $136 per acre, up 25.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stands at $9,250 per acre, up 31.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.6 percent a year. Corn yield trend runs 3.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, with a median of 148.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 101.6 in 2012. Heat and drought account for 72 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, with the share highest at 95 percent in 1989-1999 and lowest at 66 percent in 2010-2019, before rising to 71 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows average 70.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.7 F, with a warming trend of 0.77 F per decade since 1976.
