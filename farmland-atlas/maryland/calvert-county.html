Calvert County, Maryland

Calvert County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $68 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,544 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 113.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Calvert County is $68 per acre, up 5.4 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $11,544 per acre, up 12.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought account for 75 percent of crop insurance indemnities over 1993-2025, closed crop years, and that share moved to 80 percent in 2000-2009, 62 percent in 2010-2019, and 92 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 70.5 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.7 F, with a warming trend of 0.92 F per decade since 1976. No cropland in the county is irrigated as of the 2022 census. Solar and wind capacity, operating or proposed, is 0 MW.
