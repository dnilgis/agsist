Dorchester County, Maryland

Dorchester County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $117 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,614 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 167.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24019. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dorchester County averages $117 per acre, up 9.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated cropland rents at $270 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.32 in 2011 to 2.48 in 2025, a rising multiple. Forty percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, drawing 95 percent of its water from groundwater. Heat and drought has driven crop insurance losses, though its share of indemnities has moved differently across periods: 71 percent in 1989-1999, 57 percent in 2000-2009, 45 percent in 2010-2019, and 53 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $7,614 per acre, up 34.1 percent from 2017 to 2022.
