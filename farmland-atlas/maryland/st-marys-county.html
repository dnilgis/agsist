St. Mary's County, Maryland

Rain is at 80% of normal. Land and buildings run $8,652 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.24 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 33.89 inches, 80% of normal (42.45 inches) through Nov 17, at Mechanicsville, 5 mi NE. Frost-free season: 229 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,451 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $8,652 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $52 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent fell 3.7% from 2014 to 2026. 32.6% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 17.1% of the cropland, CRP left out. 50.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 65 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. There is no 2023 filing to compare.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.24 per base acre, PLC $2.64.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (105.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $68.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 239 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.41 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.35 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 34% of the median yield. 2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 78.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.9%.
