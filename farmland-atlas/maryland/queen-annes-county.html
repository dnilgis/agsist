Queen Anne's County, Maryland

Queen Anne's County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $177 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,163 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 162.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24035. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Queen Anne's County runs $177 per acre in 2026, up 30.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $259 per acre, a multiple of 1.46 in 2026 versus 1.33 in 2010, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Corn yields have trended up 4 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, with a median of 162.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 96.2 in 2012. Census land value stands at $8,163 per acre, up 11.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
