Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.20 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 186 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,873 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,375 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $28 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.49 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 13, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 19.1% from 2016 to 2026. 14% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 102.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 120 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 4.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 34.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 9.8%.

Quick answers for Allegany County

What is cash rent in Allegany County, Maryland?

The county average is $28 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Allegany County, Maryland?

Land and buildings were worth $6,375 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
