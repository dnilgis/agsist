Allegany County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,375 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 121.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.
