As of Oct 16, cash rent in Allegany County runs $28 an acre for 2026, the lowest of 21 in Maryland, against a state median of $117. That rent was $24 in 2016, up 19.1% over ten years. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,375 an acre in 2022, up 14.0% from $5,592 in 2017, and sits 2nd lowest of 23 against a state median of $9,250. Corn here has a median yield of 121.2 bu an acre over 10 years from 2008 to 2019.
