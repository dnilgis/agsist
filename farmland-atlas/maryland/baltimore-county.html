Baltimore County, Maryland

Baltimore County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $156 an acre (2026); land and buildings $13,587 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 161.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24005. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $156 per acre, up 108 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yield trend is fitted at 1.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2024, with a median of 161.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 123 in 2008. Heat and drought drove 98 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 62 percent in 2000-2009, 47 percent in 2010-2019, and 81 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows averaged 66.2 F under the 1991-2020 normal and 68.1 F over 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.89 F per decade since 1976. Only 3 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
