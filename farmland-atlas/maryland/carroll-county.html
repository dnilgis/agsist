Cash rent in Carroll County runs $132 an acre for 2024, up 50.0% from $88 in 2014. The 2022 census value of land and buildings came to $10,420 an acre, up 27.4% from 2017, and that sits 9th highest of 23 against a state median of $9,250. Corn trends at 155.9 bu, 7th lowest of 16 in the state, though 2025 came in at 173.3 bu.
