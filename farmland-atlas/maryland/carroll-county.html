Carroll County, Maryland

Carroll County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $132 an acre (2024); land and buildings $10,420 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 155.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24013. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Carroll County averaged $132 per acre in 2024, up 50 percent from 2014. Land value in the Census sits at $10,420 per acre, up 27.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5 percent a year. The July nightly low has warmed 0.75 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 65.8 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 64.3 F. Corn yield trend is 1.8 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 155.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 107.4 in 2010.
