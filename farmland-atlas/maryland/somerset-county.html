Somerset County, Maryland

Somerset County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $102 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,951 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24039. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Somerset County runs $102 per acre in 2026, up 15.3 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $7,951 per acre in 2022, up 38.9 percent from 2017. Heat and drought has taken a rising share of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.88 F per decade since 1976, now averaging 71.2 F for 2017-2026 against a 69.7 F normal for 1991-2020. The county corn yield trend runs 3.1 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 147.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 90.9 in 2010. Solar operating capacity stands at 122.8 MW; wind operating capacity is 0 MW.
