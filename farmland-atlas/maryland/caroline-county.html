Cash rent in Caroline County runs $140 an acre for 2026. That is up 27.3% from $110 in 2016 and ranks 5th highest of 21 in Maryland. The census value of land and buildings was $9,891 an acre in 2022, up 38.8% from $7,126 in 2017. Corn made 132.9 bu in 2024, well under the county median of 157.1 bu for 2008 through 2024.
