Caroline County, Maryland

Caroline County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $140 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,891 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 157.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24011. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Caroline County is $140 per acre in 2026, up 27.3 percent since 2016. Land value reached $9,891 per acre in 2022, up 38.8 percent from 2017. Irrigation covers 32 percent of harvested cropland, with 77 percent of that water drawn from groundwater as of 2015.
