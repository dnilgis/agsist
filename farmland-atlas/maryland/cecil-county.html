Non-irrigated cash rent in Cecil County is $131 per acre in 2026, up 39.4 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $10,443 per acre, up 30.3 percent from 2017, and rent is 1.5 percent of that value. July nights have warmed to an average low of 69.2 F for 2017-2026, above the 67.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.95 F per decade.
