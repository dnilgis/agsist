Howard County, Maryland

Howard County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $73 an acre (2017); land and buildings $12,622 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 175.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24027. Figures as of 2026-09-25.
