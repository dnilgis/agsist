What stands out in Harford County is the heat and drought claims. Over 1989-1999 heat and drought made up 85 percent of crop insurance indemnities, and over 2020-2025 it was 59 percent, after falling to 28 percent in 2010-2019. Corn yields have been trending up 2.3 bushels per acre a year since 2008, with a median of 165.9 and a bad year in 2010 at 129.7. Non-irrigated cash rent is $171 per acre for 2026, up 42.5 percent from 2016. The loss ratio of 0.19 over the last ten closed crop years is low, and the census value of $11,928 per acre is far above the rent.
