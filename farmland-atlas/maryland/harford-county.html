Non-irrigated cash rent in Harford County averages $171 per acre in 2026, up 42.5 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $11,928 per acre in 2022, with rent equal to 1.39 percent of that value. July nights average 69 F over 2017-2026, against a 66.8 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.96 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance heat and drought shares moved 85 percent in 1989-1999, 43 percent in 2000-2009, 28 percent in 2010-2019, and 59 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks totaled 28 of the county's 2000-2025 count of 68 occurring in 2021-2025 alone. Solar operating capacity stands at 58.9 MW; wind operating capacity is 0 MW.
