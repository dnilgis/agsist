$118

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 33.3% from $89 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Worcester County runs $118 an acre for 2026, up 33.3% from $89 in 2016 and 10th highest of 21 in Maryland. The census value of land and buildings was $8,232 an acre in 2022, up 55.3% from $5,300 in 2017, which ranks 9th lowest of 23 against a state median of $9,250. Corn made 152.9 bu in 2025, a bit under the 2021-25 average of 158.0 bu.
