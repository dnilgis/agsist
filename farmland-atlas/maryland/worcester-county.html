Non-irrigated cash rent in Worcester County, MD is $118 per acre in 2026, up 33.3 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $8,232 per acre, up 55.3 percent from 2017, 9.2 percent a year. July nightly lows average 70.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 68.6 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.87 F per decade, warming. Irrigation covers 8 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and 90 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015.
