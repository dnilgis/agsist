Frederick County, Maryland

Frederick County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $123 an acre (2026); land and buildings $11,000 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Frederick County is $123 per acre in 2026, up 17.1 percent from 2016. Land value in 2022 is $11,000 per acre, up 15.5 percent from 2017, 2.9 percent a year. July nightly lows average 66.2 F for 2017-2026, above the 1991-2020 normal of 64.9 F, warming 0.66 F per decade since 1976.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
