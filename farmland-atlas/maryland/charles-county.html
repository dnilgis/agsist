Corn yields in Charles County have climbed 3.6 bushels per acre a year from 2008 to 2025, with a median of 112.2 bushels per acre over that span, though the worst year was 2012 at 54.7. Heat and drought has driven 70 percent of the $11.2 million in crop insurance indemnities paid since 1989. Non-irrigated cash rent is $44 per acre in 2026, up 12.8 percent from 2016. The county has seen July average nightly lows warm 0.96 F per decade since 1976, and 14 Julys since 1900 have hit 70 F or above. Land value fell 15.9 percent from 2017 to 2022.
