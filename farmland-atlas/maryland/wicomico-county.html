Cash rent in Wicomico County was $114 an acre in 2026, up 39.9% from $82 in 2016. That sits 10th lowest of 21 in Maryland, just under the state median of $117. The census value of land and buildings was $7,641 an acre in 2022, up 8.6% from 2017 and 4th lowest of 23. Corn made 136.8 bu in 2025, a touch above the 135.2 bu median for 2008 to 2025.
