Non-irrigated cash rent in Wicomico County runs $114 per acre, up 39.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Irrigated land rents at $193 per acre, and the irrigated premium has held near a 1.82 to 1.83 multiple over non-irrigated rent since 2010, with no clear direction. Census land value sits at $7,641 per acre, up 8.6 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought drove 67 percent of crop insurance indemnities across closed years 1989-2025, and that share has moved 81 percent, 77 percent, 61 percent, 67 percent across the decades listed. July nights have warmed 0.92 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average low of 69.7 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 68.1 F.
