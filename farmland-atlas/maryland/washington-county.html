Washington County, Maryland

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $8,668 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.22 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

59.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 35.56 inches, 91% of normal (38.88 inches) through Nov 16, at Hagerstown, 1 mi E. Frost-free season: 199 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,062 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $8,668 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $128 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 70.7% from 2016 to 2026. 41% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 16% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 298.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 12 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,038 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.22 per base acre, PLC $2.85.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (113.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: leans ARC-CO, but expected payments are not printed, as wheat has too little price history of its own. PRF grazing county base value: $88.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 137 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.61 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.54, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 45% of the median yield. 0.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 6.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 58.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.4%.
