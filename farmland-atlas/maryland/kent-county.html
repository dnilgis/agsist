Kent County, Maryland

Kent County, MD: non-irrigated cash rent $191 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,782 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 164.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 24029. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Kent County runs $191 per acre in 2026, up 69 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $282 per acre, a multiple of 1.48 over non-irrigated in 2026, compared with 1.56 in 2010, with no clear direction over that span. Corn yields trend up 3.8 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2025, with a median of 164.1 bushels per acre and a worst year of 85.1 in 2011. Heat and drought account for 76 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with shares of 86 percent in 1989-1999, 73 percent in 2000-2009, 73 percent in 2010-2019, and 81 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $7,782 per acre in 2022, up 17.7 percent from 2017.
