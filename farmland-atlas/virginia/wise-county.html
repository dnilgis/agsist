Wise County, Virginia

Wise County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,671 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51195. Figures as of 2026-10-19.

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Wise County runs $28 an acre for 2026, 5th lowest of 86 in Virginia against a state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,671 an acre in 2022, up 18.5% from $3,097 in 2017, or 3.5% a year. That still sits below the state median of $4,596, 22nd lowest of 86. In 2022, 57% of the farmland here was rented.

Sources, dates and arithmetic

"Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed; it moves only when a figure on the page does. Source files and their retrieval dates are listed on the data page.

Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: NASS county corn yields. The headline trend, the one rent per bushel uses, is fitted here over the published years in the 15 years ending this year; the yearly trend card uses every published year since 2008. Loss ratio: claims (indemnity) divided by total premium, USDA RMA Summary of Business, 1989 on, open crop year left out. Claims paid per $100 of coverage: claims divided by liability (the insured value), times 100, same source. Drought: U.S. Drought Monitor, weeks with half the county or more in D2 or worse.
