Wise County, Virginia

Wise County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,671 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51195. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wise County runs $28 per acre, against a census land value of $3,671 per acre. July nightly lows average 62.8 F over 2017-2026, up from the 61.7 F normal for 1991-2020, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.42 F per decade. Drought has been light in recent years: 0 weeks in severe drought or worse in 2021-2025, against 62 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 27 weeks. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities was 51 percent in 1989-1999 and 33 percent in 2000-2009. Land value rose 18.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 3.5 percent a year.
