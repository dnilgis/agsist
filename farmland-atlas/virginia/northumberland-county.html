Northumberland County, Virginia

Northumberland County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $93 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,890 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 142.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51133. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cropland rents at $93 per acre, up 24 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries no irrigation, none of it in 2022, so yield rides on rainfall and July nights. Those nights average 70.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 69 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.76 F per decade, warming. Corn yields have climbed 2.8 bushels per acre a year across 2008-2025, median 142.3 bushels per acre, with 2010 low at 71.9. No solar or wind operates or is proposed here.
