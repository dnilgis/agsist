Rockbridge County, Virginia

Rockbridge County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,763 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 140.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51163. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rockbridge County runs $33 per acre in 2026, down 8.3 percent from 2016. Corn yield has moved up over 2008-2025 at 0.5 bushels per acre a year, with a median of 140.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 99 in 2008. No cropland here is irrigated, per the 2022 census. Heat and drought drove 81 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share rising across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019 before easing in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed, averaging 64.7 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.2 F. Census land value stood at $5,763 per acre in 2022, up 17.2 percent from 2017.
