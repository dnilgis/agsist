Washington County, Virginia

Washington County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,621 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 121.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51191. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Washington County averages $50 per acre in 2026, up 37 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings stands at $5,621 per acre, with a change of 11.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.2 percent a year. Corn yields show a fitted trend of 1.6 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2022, with a median of 121 bushels per acre and a worst year of 106.8 in 2012. July nightly lows average 62.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 61.7 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.51 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought stood at 65 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 28 percent in 2000-2009, 45 percent in 2010-2019, and 96 percent in 2020-2025.
