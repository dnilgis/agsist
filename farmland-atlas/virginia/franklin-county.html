Franklin County, Virginia

Franklin County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $55 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,475 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51067. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Franklin County averages $55 per acre in 2026, up 34.1 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $4,475 per acre, up 24.2 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nightly lows average 66.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 64.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the 2020s is the first decade to reach 66 F. Heat and drought caused 50 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share by period at 29 percent for 1989-1999, 58 percent for 2000-2009, 56 percent for 2010-2019, and 41 percent for 2020-2025. No cropland here is irrigated, and no solar or wind capacity operates in the county, though 12 MW of solar has been proposed to EIA.
