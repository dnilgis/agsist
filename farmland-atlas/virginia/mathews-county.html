Mathews County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,440 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.70 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

94.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 35.31 inches, 77% of normal (45.86 inches) through Nov 17, at Port Haywood, 1 mi SE. Frost-free season: 239 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,595 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $6,440 an acre in the 2022 census.

We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. 53.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 9.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,225 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.70 per base acre, PLC $2.62.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (104.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $64.40 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2025; too much rain caused 42%.

The census value of land and buildings in Mathews County ran $6,440 an acre in 2022. That is up 50.5% from $4,278 in 2017, 8.5% a year, though it was $6,193 back in 2012. The Virginia median sits at $4,596. Renters work 54% of the farmland here as of 2022.
