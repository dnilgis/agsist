Danville City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Danville City's July nights average 67.9 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.3 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.52 F per decade, warming. Only 3 Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F, and the county did not hit 66 F until the 1920s. Drought pressure has stayed low: 82 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 45 weeks, and only 3 such weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
