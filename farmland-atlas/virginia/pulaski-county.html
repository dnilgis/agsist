Cash rent in Pulaski County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 50.0% from $32 in 2014. That puts it 41st highest of 86 counties, a dollar over the state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings was $3,976 an acre in 2022, up 13.0% from $3,518 in 2017, and it sits 29th lowest of 86 against a state median of $4,596.
