Stands out against its own record

16 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Floyd County rents for $41 an acre in 2026, up 20.6% from $34 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $47 and ranks tied for 30th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $4,519 an acre in 2022, up 18.9% from $3,802 in 2017, or 3.5% a year. 33% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
