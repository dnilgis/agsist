Non-irrigated cash rent in Floyd County is $41 per acre in 2026, up 20.6 percent from 2016. Land value is $4,519 per acre in 2022, up 18.9 percent from 2017, 3.5 percent a year. July nights average 62.3 F over 2017-2026 against a 61 F normal, with warming of 0.68 F per decade since 1976; no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or higher at night. Drought has covered severe levels or worse for 56 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2002 at 32 weeks, and only 6 weeks from 2021-2025.
