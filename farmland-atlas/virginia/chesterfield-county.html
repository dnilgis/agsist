Chesterfield County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.98 per base acre for corn.

Chesterfield County vs Virginia: $41.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 36% of the other 85 Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (32nd lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.93 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 92% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 215 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,454 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $41.50 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.61 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 40% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 46.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 167.6 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.98 per base acre, PLC $2.54.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (101.17 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $75.84 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $22.36 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (26.19 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.03 per base acre, PLC $7.53. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (41.71 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $77.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $20.55 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.06, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 17% of the median yield. 2.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.4 days a year and damaging wind on 5.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 62% of crop insurance claims paid from 1990 to 2025; too much rain caused 22.8%.

Quick answers for Chesterfield County

What is cash rent in Chesterfield County, Virginia?

The county average is $42 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is the corn yield in Chesterfield County, Virginia?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 141.0 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 6 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
