Chesterfield County, Virginia

Chesterfield County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $42 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,136 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51041. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 averages $42 per acre. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, so most fields depend on rain. Heat and drought caused 62 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1990-2025, and the loss ratio over the ten closed crop years 2016-2025 was 1.06, up from 0.94 across 1989-2025. July nights have warmed, averaging 69.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 68.2 F normal for 1991-2020, at a trend of 0.47 F per decade. Drought weeks have been absent recently: zero weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 61 of 1357 weeks since 2000.
