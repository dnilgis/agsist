Non-irrigated cash rent in Albemarle County averages $33 per acre in 2026, up 40.4 percent from 2016. Land and buildings carry a census value of $9,048 per acre, down 8.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 67.4 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 65.6 F, with warming of 0.73 F per decade since 1976, and the 66 F mark was first reached in the 2010s. Crop insurance history shows freeze and frost as the largest cause of the $43.6 million paid out over 1989-2025, with heat and drought shares of 11 percent, 31 percent, 28 percent, and 22 percent across the listed periods, and drought at severe level or worse for 8 weeks of 2021-2025.
