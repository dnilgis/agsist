Cash rent in Pittsylvania County runs $41 an acre for 2026, up 28.1% from $32 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $47 and ranks tied for 30th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,197 an acre in 2022, up 12.8% from $2,835 in 2017, and ranks 7th lowest of 86. Corn here has a median yield of 92.2 bu over 11 years from 2008 to 2022.
