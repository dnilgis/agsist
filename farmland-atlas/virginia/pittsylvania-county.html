Pittsylvania County, Virginia

Pittsylvania County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $41 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,197 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 92.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51143. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $41 per acre, up 28.1 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought account for 43 percent of the $99.4 million paid out in indemnities since 1989, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 stands at 1.57, above the 1.49 mark for the full 1989-2025 record. July nightly lows average 67.3 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 66.1 F, with warming of 0.67 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks stayed low from 2021 to 2025, at 6. Solar operating capacity stands at 252.2 MW, with 155 MW more proposed to EIA.
