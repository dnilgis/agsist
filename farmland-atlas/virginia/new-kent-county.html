Stands out against its own record

7 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2010 (10 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

As of Oct 16, cash rent in New Kent County runs $80 an acre for 2026, tied for 16th highest of 86 counties in Virginia and well above the $47 state median. The census value of land and buildings was $5,501 an acre in 2022, up 2.8% from 2017, or 0.5% a year, and still a touch under the $5,525 of 2012. Corn trends at 134.6 bu, 7th lowest of 36, though the 2021-25 average came in at 157.9 bu.
