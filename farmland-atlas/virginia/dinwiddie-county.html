Dinwiddie County, Virginia

Dinwiddie County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $72 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,339 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 138.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dinwiddie County averages $72 per acre in 2026, up 82.3 percent from 2016. Heat and drought has driven $19.6 million in crop insurance indemnities since 1989, but its share of losses has moved down across the four decades measured: 89 percent in 1989-1999, 78 percent in 2000-2009, 62 percent in 2010-2019, and 55 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.41, below the 1989-2025 figure of 0.64. Severe drought weeks fell to 0 during 2021-2025, against 75 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000.
