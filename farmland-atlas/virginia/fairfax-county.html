Fairfax County had 3,616 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Land in Fairfax County carried a census value of $17,494 per acre in 2022, up 4.1 percent from 2017, or 0.8 percent a year. July nights average 69.5 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 67.8 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.85 F per decade, warming. Seven Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F, and the July average low first reached 66 F in the 1930s. Drought has touched the county in 66 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 4.9 percent of weeks, with 2002 the worst year at 26 weeks; 2021-2025 logged 23 such weeks.
