Tazewell County, Virginia

Rain is at 100% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,488 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.40 per base acre for corn.

Tazewell County vs Virginia: $40 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 32% of the other 85 Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 28th lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.36 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 174 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,515 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,488 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.51 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 31, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 6.7% from 2016 to 2026. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 215.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 13.3 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,636 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.40 per base acre, PLC $2.25.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (89.58 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.80 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $44.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 18 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 3.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 45.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2023; too much rain caused 30%.
