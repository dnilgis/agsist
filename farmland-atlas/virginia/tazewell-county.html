Tazewell County, Virginia

Tazewell County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,488 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51185. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tazewell County averages $40 per acre for 2026, up 6.7 percent from 2016. Census land and buildings value for 2022 stands at $3,488 per acre, with land value change of 27.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 5 percent a year. The July average nightly low has moved from 59.5 F (1991-2020 normal) to 60.5 F (2017-2026), with a warming trend of 0.44 F per decade since 1976. Drought pressure has eased in recent years: 2 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 58 weeks total from 2000-2025, with 2007 the worst year at 23 weeks. Crop insurance indemnities from 1995-2023 total $177,931, with heat and drought the largest cause at 46 percent.
