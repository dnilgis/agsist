Dickenson County, Virginia

Dickenson County, VA: land and buildings $2,612 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51051. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

No farm reported irrigated harvested cropland in the 2022 census, so cropland here runs on rain. Drought has been light on record: 58 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, and 0 weeks from 2021-2025. July nights average 63.4 F for 2017-2026 against a 62.2 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.43 F per decade since 1976. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
