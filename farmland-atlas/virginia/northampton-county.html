Cash rent in Northampton County runs $88 an acre for 2026, up 6.0% from $83 in 2016 and 12th highest of 86 in Virginia, well over the state median of $47. Irrigated ground rents for $145 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,745 an acre in 2022, up 3.5% from 2017, or 0.7% a year. Corn made 111.4 bu in 2025, short of the 2021-25 average of 161.5 bu.
