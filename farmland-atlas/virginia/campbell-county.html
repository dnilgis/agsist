Cash rent in Campbell County runs $32 an acre for 2026, up 42.2% from $23 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $47 and ranks tied for 13th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $3,656 an acre in 2022, up 18.5% from $3,086 in 2017, or 3.4% a year. Corn trends at 153.4 bu for 2026, a touch below the state median of 156.0 bu.
