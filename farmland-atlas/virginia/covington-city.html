Covington City has very little farm ground in the Atlas data, and what stands out is the nights. The July average low for 2017-2026 is 64.4 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.1 F, and the nightly low trend since 1976 is 0.52 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. On water, 23 of 1357 weeks since 2000 had at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with the worst year 2002 at 16 weeks. The most recent 2021-2025 period had 5 weeks in severe drought or worse. There is no CRP ground, and no wind or solar operating or proposed.
