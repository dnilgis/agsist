Shenandoah County, Virginia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,334 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.24 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 182 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,899 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $7,334 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $68 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 55.7% from 2016 to 2026. 38.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 8.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 265.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 729 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.24 per base acre, PLC $2.83.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (112.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.9 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $72.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $29.88 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.66 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 49% of the median yield. 0.7% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.7%.
