Fauquier County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,962 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.66 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 38.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 206 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,158 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,962 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $65 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.57 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 20% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 15% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 47.7% from 2016 to 2026. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 169.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 14.2 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,261 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.66 per base acre, PLC $2.46.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (97.89 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $21.63 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34.75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $72.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.21 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.53, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 64% of the median yield. 2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.1 days a year and damaging wind on 8.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 76.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 15.6%.

Fauquier is a dryland county. Only 2 percent of its harvested cropland is irrigated, so the corn yield and the rent both carry the weather. Heat and drought made up 77 percent of the $9.3 million paid out on closed crop years from 1989 to 2025, and their share went from 92 percent in 1989-1999 to 86 percent in 2020-2025. Non-irrigated cash rent is $65 per acre for 2026, up 47.7 percent since 2016. The loss ratio over the ten closed years 2016-2025 is 0.53, which is lower than the 0.6 for the full run, so claims have come in lighter in the recent closed years. The census land value is $7,962 per acre in 2022.
