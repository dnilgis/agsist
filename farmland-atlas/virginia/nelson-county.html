Nelson County, Virginia

Nelson County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,566 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51125. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Nelson County is $40 per acre, up 12.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. The census value of land and buildings in 2022 stood at $5,566 per acre, with land value change of 9.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 1.9 percent a year. July nights have warmed: the 2017-2026 average low is 66.3 F against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.66 F per decade, warming. The 2020s is the first decade the July average low reached 66 F. Crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025 closed crop years total $12.3 million, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 45 percent; the loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 1.12, up from 0.92 across 1989-2025.
