Stands out against its own record

177.8 bu

Corn yield, all practices, 2024: the highest of 16 published years since 2008.

USDA NASS county yields, 2024

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Chesapeake City ran $79 an acre in 2025, up 8.2% from $73 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $5,707 an acre in 2022, down 2.4% from $5,848 in 2017. That ranks 30th highest of 86 in Virginia, against a state median of $4,596. The corn trend yield sits at 168.7 bu, 11th highest of 36, and 52% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
