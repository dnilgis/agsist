Chesapeake City, Virginia

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,707 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.97 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.86 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 241 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,602 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,707 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $79 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.71 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 50% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 52% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 12% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 43.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,656 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.88 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.66, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 72% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 41.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 46.1%.

Quick answers for Chesapeake City

What is cash rent in Chesapeake City, Virginia?

The county average is $79 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Chesapeake City, Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $5,707 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Chesapeake City, Virginia?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 168.7 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 12 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
