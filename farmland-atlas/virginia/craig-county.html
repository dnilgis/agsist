Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,219 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 192 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,784 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,219 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $43.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 141.7% from 2016 to 2026. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 177.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.
