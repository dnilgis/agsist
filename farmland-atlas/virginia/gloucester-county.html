Gloucester County, Virginia

Gloucester County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $72 an acre (2026); land and buildings $6,053 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 144.4 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51073. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 runs $72 per acre. Census land and buildings value was $6,053 per acre in 2022, up 28.3 percent from 2017, 5.1 percent a year. July nights have warmed from a 1991-2020 normal of 70.1 F to 71.7 F in 2017-2026, part of a 0.83 F per decade trend since 1976. No harvested cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
