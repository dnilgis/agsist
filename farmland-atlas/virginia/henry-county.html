Henry County, Virginia

Henry County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $32 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,027 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.56 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51089. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Henry County runs $32 per acre, up 113.3 percent from 2014 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $3,027 per acre, with a 2.2 percent change from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 67.2 F over 2017-2026, against a 65.9 F normal for 1991-2020, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.9 F per decade. Heat and drought made up 9 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 39 percent in 2010-2019, and 42 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low in 2021-2025, at 6, and no cropland here is irrigated.
