Henry County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,027 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.95 per base acre for corn.

Henry County vs Virginia: $32 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 14% of the other 85 Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 13th lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.36 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 76% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 206 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,277 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,027 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.58 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 113.3% from 2014 to 2026. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 93.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 201.1 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,050 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.95 per base acre, PLC $1.91.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (76.23 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.85 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (22.88 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.27 per base acre, PLC $7.27. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.28 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $81.00 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $25.38 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.67, 2016 to 2025. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 8.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 37.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.7%.
