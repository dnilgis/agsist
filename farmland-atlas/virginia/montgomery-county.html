Cropland rent in Montgomery County runs $47 an acre for 2026, right on the state median and tied for 43rd highest of 86. That is up 22.4% from $38 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $5,743 an acre in 2022, up 18.6% from $4,842 in 2017, and 28th highest of 86. 28% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
