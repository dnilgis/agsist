Non-irrigated cash rent in Madison County runs $56 per acre, up 3.7 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought drove 73 percent of indemnities in 1989-1999, 57 percent in 2000-2009, 27 percent in 2010-2019, and 86 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.84, above the 1989-2025 figure of 0.58, and indemnity paid per $100 of insured value over 2016-2025 was $6.03. July nightly lows have warmed 0.67 F per decade since 1976, reaching 65.7 F on average for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.9 F. Solar and wind development, operating or proposed, is 0 MW.
