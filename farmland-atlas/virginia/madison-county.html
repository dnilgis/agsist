As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Madison County runs $56 an acre for 2026, up 3.7% from $54 in 2016, and that sits 35th highest of 86 against a state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings came in at $6,994 an acre in 2022, up 17.6% from $5,949 in 2017, which ranks 19th highest of 86. Corn made 198.1 bu in 2025, well above the 14 year trend of 161.2 bu.
