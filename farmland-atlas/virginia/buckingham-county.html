Cash rent in Buckingham County was $30 an acre in 2026, tied for 7th lowest of 86 in Virginia against a state median of $47. That rent was $18 in 2016, up 63.9% by 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $4,400 an acre in 2022, up 17.9% from $3,731 in 2017. Pasture rent was $15 an acre in 2026.
