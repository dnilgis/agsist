Highland County, Virginia

Highland County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2025); land and buildings $3,382 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51091. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Highland County averages $30 per acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $3,382 per acre in 2022, up 5.8 percent from 2017, or 1.1 percent a year. Cropland irrigation is limited, at 1 percent of harvested cropland in the 2022 census. July nights have warmed, from a 1991-2020 normal of 57.9 F to 59.4 F in 2017-2026, a trend of 0.51 F per decade since 1976, and no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought weeks have run higher recently: 34 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 48 weeks total for 2000-2025.
