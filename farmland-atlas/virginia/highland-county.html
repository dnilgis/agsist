Highland County, Virginia

Cash rent in Highland County ran $30 an acre in 2025, and pasture went for $24. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,382 an acre in 2022, up 5.8% from $3,198 in 2017, or 1.1% a year. That sits under the Virginia median of $4,596 and ranks 13th lowest of 86. In 2022, 34% of the farmland here was rented.
