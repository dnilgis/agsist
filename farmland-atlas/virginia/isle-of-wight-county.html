Cash rent in Isle of Wight County runs $105 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th highest of 86 in Virginia and well over the state median of $47. That rent is up 52.2% from $69 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings sat at $3,877 an acre in 2022, up 4.4% from 2017, or 0.9% a year, and ranks 25th lowest of 86. 54% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
