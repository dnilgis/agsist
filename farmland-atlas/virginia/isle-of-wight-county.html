Isle of Wight County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,877 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.19 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 232 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,583 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,877 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $105 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 52.2% from 2016 to 2026. 54% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 35% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 29% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 36.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 258.4 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 7,169 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.19 per base acre, PLC $2.71.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (108.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (31.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.58 per base acre, PLC $9.46. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (52.4 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $76.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.26 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.45, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 22% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 59.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 18.4%.
