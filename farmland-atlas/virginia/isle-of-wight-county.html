Isle of Wight County, Virginia

Isle of Wight County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $105 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,877 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 135.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $105 per acre, up 52.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $3,877 per acre, up 4.4 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields trend up 5.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2023, with a median of 135.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 30 in 2010. Heat and drought account for 60 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, though that share has fallen across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, and 2010-2019, then risen in 2020-2025. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse dropped to 0 for 2021-2025, down from 67 of 1357 weeks since 2000. Solar operating capacity stands at 301 MW, with 258.4 MW more proposed to EIA.
