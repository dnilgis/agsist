Lexington City, Virginia

Lexington City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51678. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Lexington City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Weeks with at least 50 percent of Lexington City in severe drought or worse, 2000-2025, total 41 of 1357 (3 percent), with the worst year 2002 at 25 weeks. In 2021-2025, the county logged 15 weeks at that drought level. No solar or wind capacity operates in the county, and none is proposed to EIA.
