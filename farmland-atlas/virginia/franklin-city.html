Franklin City, Virginia

Franklin City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51620. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Franklin City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low for 2017-2026 runs 69.4 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.1 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.62 F per decade, warming. Eleven Julys since 1900 have reached an average low at or above 70 F. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 82 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 6 percent, with 2002 the worst year at 31 weeks. From 2021-2025, that count is 0 weeks. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
