Charles City County, Virginia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,221 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.13 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

22% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 232 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,595 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,221 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $77 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 27.3% from 2016 to 2026. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 19% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 3.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 120 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 6,518 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.13 per base acre, PLC $3.01.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (119.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.24 per base acre, PLC $11.23. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (62.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $64.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 10 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.62 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.44, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 36% of the median yield. 9.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 62.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 18.5%.
