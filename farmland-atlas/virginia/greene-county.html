Greene County, Virginia

Greene County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $45 an acre (2026); land and buildings $8,303 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51079. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Greene County is $45 per acre. Land value in 2022 was $8,303 per acre, up 27.1 percent from 2017-2022. July nights average 65.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 64.1 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.72 F per decade, warming. The 2020s is the first decade the July average low reached 66 F. None of the harvested cropland is irrigated, and drought weeks ran 51 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 12 from 2021-2025.
