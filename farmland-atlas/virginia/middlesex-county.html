As of Oct 16, cash rent in Middlesex County runs $87 an acre in 2026, 13th highest of 86 in Virginia against a state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings was $4,041 an acre in 2022, up 1.9% from 2017, and that sits 31st lowest of 86. Corn holds up well here, with a 14 year trend of 178.3 bu that ranks 3rd highest of 36. 53% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
