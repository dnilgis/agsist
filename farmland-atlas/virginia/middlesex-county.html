Middlesex County, Virginia

Middlesex County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $87 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,041 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 147.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51119. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 in Middlesex County, VA runs $87 per acre. Census land value in 2022 stood at $4,041 per acre, with a change of 1.9 percent from 2017-2022, 0.4 percent a year. Corn yields trended up 4.3 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 147.5 bushels per acre and a worst year of 71.3 in 2010. Heat and drought drove 66 percent of crop insurance indemnities across closed years, with the share at 89 percent in 1989-1999, 48 percent in 2000-2009, 75 percent in 2010-2019, and 89 percent in 2020-2025. Only 2 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and irrigation failure indemnities totaled $54,091 across all closed years.
