Charlottesville City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 68.1 F for 2017-2026, up from the 66.3 F normal set in 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.78 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has reached an average low of 70 F or above. Drought has touched at least 50 percent of the county in 59 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, 4.3 percent of the record, with 2002 the worst year at 34 weeks; 2021-2025 logged 13 such weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates here, and none is proposed to EIA.
