U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland rent in Amherst County runs $26 an acre for 2026, up 8.3% from $24 in 2016. That sits well under the state median of $47 and ranks 4th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings tells a different story at $5,795 an acre in 2022, up 24.5% from $4,654 in 2017 and 26th highest of 86. 28% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
