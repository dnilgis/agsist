Giles County, Virginia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,670 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.75 per base acre for corn.

Giles County vs Virginia: $30 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 9% of the other 85 Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (9th lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 31.92 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 184 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,675 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,670 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.53 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 6% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 1.7% from 2016 to 2026. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 175.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 329 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.75 per base acre, PLC $2.24.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (89.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $19.69 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $44.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

Quick answers for Giles County

What is cash rent in Giles County, Virginia?

The county average is $30 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Giles County, Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,670 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
