Giles County, Virginia

Giles County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,670 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51071. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Giles County runs $30 per acre in 2026, up 1.7 percent from 2016. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $3,670 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 19.4 percent, 3.6 percent a year. No cropland here is irrigated, so the county depends on rainfall, and drought weeks have been limited: 38 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, with 5 such weeks from 2021-2025. July nights have warmed, averaging 61.6 F over 2017-2026 against a 60.5 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a 0.51 F per decade warming trend since 1976. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
