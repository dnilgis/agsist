Lancaster County, Virginia

Lancaster County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $60 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,252 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Lancaster County had 9,987 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Non-irrigated cash rent for 2026 is $60 per acre. Land value in 2022 was $5,252 per acre, up 24 percent from 2017, 4.4 percent a year. The July average nightly low has moved from 69.5 F normal to 71.2 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.88 F per decade since 1976. None of the county's harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census.
