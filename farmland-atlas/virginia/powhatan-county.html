Powhatan County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $6,616 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.64 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 34.38 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 209 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,362 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $6,616 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.63 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 26, 2026). 44% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 20% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 136.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.64 per base acre, PLC $2.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $24.42 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.97 per base acre, PLC $8.84. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (48.99 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $77.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.23 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.56, 2016 to 2025. 0.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 2.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 71.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.7%.

Stands out against its own record

14 weeks

With half the county or more in severe drought (D2) or worse in 2026 so far, the most since 2002 (39 weeks).

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (44 maps this year)

Cropland in Powhatan County rented for $30 an acre in 2023, and pasture went for $24 an acre in 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $6,616 an acre in 2022, up 24.9% from $5,299 in 2017, or 4.5% a year. That puts the county 22nd highest of 86 in Virginia, well over the state median of $4,596. Only 17% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
