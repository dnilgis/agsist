Frederick County, Virginia

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $7,861 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.01 per base acre for corn.

Frederick County vs Virginia: $33.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 21% of the other 85 Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (19th lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.72 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 187 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,936 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,861 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $33.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.60 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 14% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 8.1% from 2016 to 2026. 34% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 113.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 30 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 2,121 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.01 per base acre, PLC $2.31.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (91.93 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $30.85 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $20.32 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (19.74 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $72.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $47.13 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.63, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 29% of the median yield. 0.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.3 days a year and damaging wind on 5.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.6%.
