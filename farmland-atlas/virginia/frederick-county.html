Frederick County, Virginia

Frederick County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $34 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,861 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 136.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51069. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land and buildings carry a market value of $7,861 per acre, with that value up 8.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. No cropland here draws on irrigation; the 2022 census puts the irrigated share at 0 percent. Drought has stayed limited: 42 of 1357 weeks since 2000 hit severe drought or worse, and 2021-2025 held 16 such weeks.
