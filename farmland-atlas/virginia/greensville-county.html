Non-irrigated cash rent in Greensville County runs $96 per acre in 2026, up 49.2 percent from 2016. Land carries a census value of $2,861 per acre, with that value up 5.5 percent from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 69.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 68.5 F, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.81 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance indemnities since 1989 total $28.3 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 61 percent overall; by period that share ran 68 percent in 1989-1999, 70 percent in 2000-2009, 56 percent in 2010-2019, and 56 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks have run light lately: zero weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 89 of 1357 weeks since 2000.
