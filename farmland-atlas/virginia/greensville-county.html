Greensville County, Virginia

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,861 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.20 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 224 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,511 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,861 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $96 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 49.2% from 2016 to 2026. 32.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1.5% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 27.6% of the cropland, CRP left out. 19.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,448 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.20 per base acre, PLC $2.02.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (80.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $76.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.82 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.77 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 26% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.4 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 61.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 23.9%.
