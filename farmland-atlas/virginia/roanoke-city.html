Roanoke City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low for 2017-2026 runs 66.9 F, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 65.7 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.63 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought has touched this county in 44 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 32 weeks, and only 6 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
