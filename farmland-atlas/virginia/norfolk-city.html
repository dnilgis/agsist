Norfolk City, Virginia

Norfolk City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51710. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Norfolk City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights in Norfolk City average 73 F now, against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.8 F. The July nightly low has warmed 0.61 F per decade since 1976. Out of all Julys since 1900, 77 have had an average low at or above 70 F. Drought has been light: 63 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, with only 1 such week from 2021-2025, and 2002 was the worst year at 28 weeks. Solar operating capacity is 0.9 MW, with no wind operating and nothing proposed to EIA.
