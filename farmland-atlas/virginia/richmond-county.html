Richmond County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.81 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 225 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,461 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $93.50 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.71 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 27, 2026). 41% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 24.7% from 2016 to 2026. 66% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 35% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 13.4 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 122 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,269 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.81 per base acre, PLC $2.77.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (110.43 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $39.38 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $24.41 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (34.2 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.96 per base acre, PLC $10.04. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (55.63 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $64.40 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.07 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.26, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 36% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 79.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 7.1%.
