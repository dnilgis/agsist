Cash rent in Goochland County runs $45 an acre for 2026, tied for 39th lowest of 86 in Virginia and a shade under the state median of $47. That is down 5.3% from $47 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, $7,139 an acre in 2022, up 31.5% from $5,430 in 2017 and 18th highest of 86. In 2022, 30% of the farmland here was rented.
