Goochland County, Virginia

Goochland County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $45 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,139 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51075. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value stands at $7,139 per acre, up 31.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 5.6 percent a year. The July nightly low has warmed since 1976 at 0.55 F per decade, and the 2017-2026 average of 67.8 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 66.4 F. No cropland in the county was irrigated as of the 2022 census, and drought weeks have dropped to 0 in 2021-2025 from 66 of 1357 weeks over 2000-2025.
