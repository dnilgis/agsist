Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Portsmouth City is a warm place for nights in July. The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 72.7 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 71.7 F, and the July nightly low has warmed 0.54 F per decade since 1976. Julys with an average low at or above 70 F have happened 80 times since 1900. Drought has been a steady but modest part of the picture: 67 of 1357 weeks since 2000 had at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2002 the worst at 28 weeks. Severe drought has not shown up in any week from 2021-2025. There is no CRP, wind, or solar on the books here.
