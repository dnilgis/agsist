Portsmouth City, Virginia

Portsmouth City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51740. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Portsmouth City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 72.7 F, above the 1991-2020 normal of 71.7 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.55 F per decade, warming, and 80 Julys since 1900 have had an average low at or above 70 F. Drought pressure has eased: 2000-2025 shows 67 of 1357 weeks with at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, worst year 2002 with 28 weeks, and 2021-2025 shows 0 such weeks. There is no solar or wind operating or proposed in this county.
