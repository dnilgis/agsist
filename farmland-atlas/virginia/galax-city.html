Galax City, Virginia

Galax City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51640. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Galax City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low ran 62 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 60.2 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.49 F per decade, direction warming. No July since 1900 has closed with an average low at or above 70 F. Drought pressure stayed low: 86 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 24 weeks, and only 4 such weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
