Cash rent in Orange County runs $76 an acre for 2026, up 74.7% from $44 in 2016 and 19th highest of 86 in Virginia, where the state median is $47. The census value of land and buildings was $6,611 an acre in 2022, up 5.0% from $6,297 in 2017. Corn holds up well here, with a 14-yr trend of 178.4 bu that ranks 2nd highest of 36 in the state.
