Rockingham County, Virginia

Rockingham County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $111 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,531 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 149.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51165. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rockingham County averages $111 per acre in 2026, up 30.6 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $9,531 per acre in 2022, with a change of 7.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Corn yields carry a fitted trend of 1.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 149.6 bushels per acre and a worst year of 103.4 in 2024. Crop insurance losses over 1989-2025 total $40.5 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 40 percent; that share ran 89 percent in 1989-1999, 43 percent in 2000-2009, 74 percent in 2010-2019, and 34 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks rose from 52 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000 to 35 in the 2021-2025 span alone.
