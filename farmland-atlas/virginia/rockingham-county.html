Cash rent in Rockingham County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 30.6% from $85 in 2016 and 2nd highest of 86 in Virginia, where the state median is $47. The census value of land and buildings was $9,531 an acre in 2022, up 7.8% from $8,844 in 2017 and 5th highest of 86. Corn made 217.1 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 145.5 bu.
