Culpeper County, Virginia

Culpeper County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $58 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,957 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.1 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51047. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $58 per acre, up 31.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value carries $7,957 per acre, up 38.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, 6.7 percent a year. Corn yields trend up 2.4 bushels per acre a year on a median of 146.1 bushels per acre, with 2011 the worst year at 104.4. Heat and drought account for 65 percent of indemnities since 1989, and that share sat at 74 percent in 1989-1999, fell to 57 percent in 2000-2009 and 58 percent in 2010-2019, then rose back to 74 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 65.5 F normal to 67.5 F over 2017-2026, part of a 0.76 F per decade trend since 1976. Only 2 percent of cropland here is irrigated.
