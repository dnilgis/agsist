Mecklenburg County, Virginia

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $3,268 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.07 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 26.60 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 64% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 215 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,426 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $3,268 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $38 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.67 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 34% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 22.6% from 2016 to 2026. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 20% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 90.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,128 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

PRF grazing county base value: $76.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $9.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.97, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 44% of the median yield. 4.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 4.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 64.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 14.8%.

Quick answers for Mecklenburg County

What is cash rent in Mecklenburg County, Virginia?

The county average is $38 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $95 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Mecklenburg County, Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $3,268 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Mecklenburg County, Virginia?

The county's 2021-25 average is 138.9 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
