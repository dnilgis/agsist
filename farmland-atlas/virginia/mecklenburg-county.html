Cash rent in Mecklenburg County runs $38 an acre for 2026, up 22.6% from $31 in 2016 and tied for 25th lowest of 86 against a state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,268 an acre in 2022, up 18.2% from $2,764 in 2017, which ranks 10th lowest of 86. Corn made 125.9 bu in 2025, under the 2021-25 average of 138.9 bu.
