Rappahannock County, Virginia

Rappahannock County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $31 an acre (2026); land and buildings $7,465 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51157. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rappahannock County averages $31 per acre in 2026. July nights average 65.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 63.5 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.7 F per decade, warming. No irrigation is in use on harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, 0 percent. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $1.7 million, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 37 percent; heat and drought's share moved from 17 percent in 1989-1999 to 38 percent in 2000-2009 to 21 percent in 2010-2019 to 15 percent in 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 58 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 24 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
