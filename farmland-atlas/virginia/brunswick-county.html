Brunswick County, Virginia

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,749 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $7.56 per base acre for corn.

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 28.11 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 66% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 214 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,466 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $2,749 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $30 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.65 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 47% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 24% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 69.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 903 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 20,802 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.56 per base acre, PLC $1.84.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (73.27 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $16.19 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (26.79 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.37 per base acre, PLC $7.75. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (42.94 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $76.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.98 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.74, 2016 to 2025. 13% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 56.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 17.3%.

Cropland in Brunswick County rents for $30 an acre in 2025, down 9.1% from $33 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,749 an acre in 2022, up 9.3% from $2,514 in 2017, or 1.8% a year. That sits well under the state median of $4,596 and comes in 2nd lowest of 86 in Virginia. 26% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
