Stands out against its own record

25 weeks

U.S. Drought Monitor weekly maps, 2000 to 2026 (40 maps this year)

Cropland in Brunswick County rents for $30 an acre in 2025, down 9.1% from $33 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $2,749 an acre in 2022, up 9.3% from $2,514 in 2017, or 1.8% a year. That sits well under the state median of $4,596 and comes in 2nd lowest of 86 in Virginia. 26% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
