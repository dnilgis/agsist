Accomack's corn yields have been climbing at 2.7 bushels per acre a year since 2008, reaching a median of 156.9 bushels per acre over the period, though a severe drought year in 2010 dropped the crop to 66.3 bushels per acre. Non-irrigated cash rent is $112 per acre in 2026, up 33.3 percent from 2016. Heat and drought have paid out 54 percent of the crop insurance indemnities across all closed years from 1992-2025, totaling $73.3 million. Irrigation is light here, 7 percent of harvested cropland, and July nights have been warming at 0.72 F per decade since 1976.
