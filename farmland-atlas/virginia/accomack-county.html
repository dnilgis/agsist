Accomack County, Virginia

Accomack County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $112 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,045 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 156.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51001. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Accomack County runs $112 per acre in 2026, up 33.3 percent from 2016. Irrigated land carries a higher rent, $115 per acre in 2021, though the irrigated premium has no clear direction, running 1.36 in 2021 against 1.83 in 2010. Only 7 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 10 percent of irrigation water comes from groundwater. Heat and drought drove 54 percent of crop insurance indemnities across all closed years, with the share at 65 percent in 1989-1999, 40 percent in 2000-2009, 62 percent in 2010-2019, and 54 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed from a 70.1 F normal to a 71.5 F average over 2017-2026, consistent with a warming trend since 1976.
