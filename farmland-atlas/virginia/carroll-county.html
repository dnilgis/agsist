Cropland in Carroll County rents for $45 an acre in 2026, up 20.3% from $37 in 2016. That sits just under the state median of $47 and ranks tied for 39th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,866 an acre in 2022, up 20.1% from $3,219 in 2017, and 36% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
