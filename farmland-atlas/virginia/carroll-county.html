Carroll County, Virginia

Rain is at 87% of normal. Land and buildings run $3,866 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 39.31 inches, 87% of normal (45.31 inches) through Nov 17, at Galax Water Plant. Frost-free season: 185 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,636 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $3,866 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $44 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 20.3% from 2016 to 2026. 35.7% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.7% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1.4% of the cropland, CRP left out. 282 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 792 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.77 per base acre, PLC $2.33.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (92.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $44.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 44 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $58.69 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 1.34 over the same years. 1.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 3.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.6%.
