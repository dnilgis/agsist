Bedford County, Virginia

Bedford County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,539 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 133.3 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Bedford County runs $28 per acre, up 33.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land carries a census value of $5,539 per acre, up 25.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, 4.7 percent a year. Cropland here is 0 percent irrigated, so yield rides on rain and heat. Corn yields trended up 1.4 bushels per acre a year over 2009-2022, median 133.3 bushels per acre, with 2009 low at 94. Heat and drought drove 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, though that share fell to 42 percent in 2020-2025. July nights average 66.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 65.4 F normal for 1991-2020, and the July low has warmed 0.65 F per decade since 1976.
