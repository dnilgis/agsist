Prince William County, Virginia

Prince William County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $49 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,336 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51153. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $49 per acre, up 63.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Census land value stands at $12,336 per acre, up 15.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.9 percent a year. The July nightly low averaged 69.5 F over 2017-2026, against a 67.4 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.94 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought caused 76 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, with shares of 94 percent in 1989-1999, 70 percent in 2000-2009, 71 percent in 2010-2019, and 71 percent in 2020-2025.
