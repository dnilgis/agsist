Caroline County, Virginia

Caroline County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $64 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,710 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 148.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Caroline County runs $64 per acre, up 10.3 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yields have trended up 3.1 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 148.8 bushels per acre and a low of 43.3 in 2010. Heat and drought account for 78 percent of crop insurance indemnities since 1989, though that share has eased across the decades: 93 percent in 1989-1999, 73 percent in 2000-2009, 77 percent in 2010-2019, 75 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 sits at 0.47, below the full-period 0.81. Only 5 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and there have been 0 weeks of severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. Solar and wind development: 0 MW operating, 0 MW proposed.
