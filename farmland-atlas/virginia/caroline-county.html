As of Oct 16, cash rent in Caroline County runs $64 an acre for 2026, up 10.3% from $58 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $47 and ranks 28th highest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $4,710 an acre in 2022, up 27.4% from $3,696 in 2017. Corn made 170.6 bu in 2025, and 57% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
