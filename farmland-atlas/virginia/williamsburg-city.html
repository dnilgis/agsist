Williamsburg City, Virginia

Williamsburg City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51830. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Williamsburg City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low, 2017-2026, stands at 71.1 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 69.5 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs at 0.72 F per decade, warming. Since 1900, 34 Julys have reached an average low at or above 70 F, and the first decade to reach 66 F was the 1900s. Drought has been light here: 55 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 saw at least 50 percent of the county in severe drought or worse, with 2002 the worst year at 36 weeks, and 2021-2025 recorded 0 such weeks. Solar and wind operating capacity are both 0 MW, and no capacity is proposed to EIA.
