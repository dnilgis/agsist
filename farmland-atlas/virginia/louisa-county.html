Louisa County, Virginia

Louisa County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $56 an acre (2026); land and buildings $5,625 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 113.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51109. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Louisa County averages $56 per acre in 2026, up 111.3 percent from 2016. Census land value stands at $5,625 per acre, with land value change 2017-2022 at 13.6 percent, 2.6 percent a year. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so corn yields track weather closely: the fitted trend on 2008-2019 runs 9.1 bushels per acre a year, median 113.5 bushels per acre, with 2010 as low as 45.2. July nightly lows have moved from 65.7 F (1991-2020 normal) to 67.1 F (2017-2026), part of a warming trend since 1976. Heat and drought drove 65 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share at 86 percent in 1989-1999, 65 percent in 2000-2009, 54 percent in 2010-2019, and 72 percent in 2020-2025.
