Loudoun County, Virginia

Loudoun County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2026); land and buildings $12,276 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 125.0 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51107. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $37 per acre, down 16.9 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought drove 59 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, and that share moved unevenly across the decades: 82 percent in 1989-1999, 52 percent in 2000-2009, 41 percent in 2010-2019, then 76 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed 0.81 F per decade since 1976, and the 1991-2020 normal of 65.4 F sits below the 2017-2026 average of 67.4 F. Irrigation covers only 1 percent of harvested cropland, so this heat exposure runs mostly on rainfall.
