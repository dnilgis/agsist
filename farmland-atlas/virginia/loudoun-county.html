Loudoun County, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $12,276 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.47 per base acre for corn.

Loudoun County vs Virginia: $37 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 25% of the other 85 Virginia counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 22nd lowest of 86).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 35.64 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 94% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 206 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,173 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $12,276 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $37 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.61 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 33% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 3% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent fell 16.9% from 2016 to 2026. 40% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 9% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 118 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 100 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 13,192 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.47 per base acre, PLC $2.44.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (97.15 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $21.47 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (36.83 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.11 per base acre, PLC $7.98. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.21 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $72.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.81 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.11, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 73% of the median yield. 0.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.8 days a year and damaging wind on 9.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 58.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 16.1%.

Cash rent in Loudoun County was $37 an acre in 2026, down 16.9% from $45 in 2016 and tied for 22nd lowest of 86 in Virginia. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $12,276 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from $9,816 in 2017. That puts it 3rd highest of 86, against a state median of $4,596. Corn made 167.5 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 125.0 bu.
