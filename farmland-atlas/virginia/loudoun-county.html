As of Oct 16, cash rent in Loudoun County was $37 an acre in 2026, down 16.9% from $45 in 2016 and tied for 22nd lowest of 86 in Virginia. The census value of land and buildings went the other way, at $12,276 an acre in 2022, up 25.1% from $9,816 in 2017. That puts it 3rd highest of 86, against a state median of $4,596. Corn made 167.5 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 125.0 bu.
