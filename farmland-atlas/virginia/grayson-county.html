Cash rent in Grayson County runs $70 an acre for 2026, up 59.1% from $44 in 2016. That ranks 22nd highest of 86 in Virginia, well over the state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings was $4,673 an acre in 2022, up 12.5% from $4,155 in 2017, and 42% of farmland was rented in 2022.
