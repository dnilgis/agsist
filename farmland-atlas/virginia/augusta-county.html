Cash rent in Augusta County runs $105 an acre for 2026, tied for 4th highest of 86 in Virginia, against a state median of $47. That rent is up 87.5% from $56 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $7,594 an acre in 2022, 15th highest of 86, up 8.5% from $6,999 in 2017. Corn made 198.0 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 151.6 bu.
