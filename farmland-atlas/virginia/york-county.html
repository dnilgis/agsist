Land in York County is valued at $16,424 per acre, with a change of 35.8 percent, 6.3 percent a year from 2017-2022. July nightly lows average 72 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 70.5 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.7 F per decade, warming. Of 53 Julys since 1900 with an average low at or above 70 F, the first decade reaching 66 F was the 1900s. Severe drought or worse covered 67 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, worst in 2002 with 36 weeks, and 0 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
