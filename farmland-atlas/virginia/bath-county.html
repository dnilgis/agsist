Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.61 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 84% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 182 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,697 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,314 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $45 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.50 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 5% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland. USDA cash rent rose 91.5% from 2016 to 2026. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 100.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.00 per base acre, PLC $2.12.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (84.44 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.66 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $44.70 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 5 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 54.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1998 to 2024; too much rain caused 25.6%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2009; up 91.5% from $24 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Bath County runs $45 an acre for 2026, up 91.5% from $24 in 2016. That sits just under the state median of $47 and ranks tied for 41st lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings was $4,314 an acre in 2022, up 17.3% from $3,677 in 2017, or 3.2% a year. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
