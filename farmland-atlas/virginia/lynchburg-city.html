Lynchburg City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low here ran 65.5 F under the 1991-2020 normal, and 67.4 F for 2017-2026. The trend since 1976 runs 0.75 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night, though the 2010s was the first decade to reach 66 F. Drought has touched 52 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 38 weeks, and 9 weeks from 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed here.
