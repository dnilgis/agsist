Non-irrigated cash rent in Suffolk City runs $103 per acre in 2026, up 33.8 percent from 2016. Cropland here is 1 percent irrigated, so the corn yield line depends on rain and heat. The trend rises 3.4 bushels per acre a year on published years 2008-2024, but the median sits at 128.7 bushels per acre against a worst year of 40.1 in 2010. July nights average 69.9 F over 2017-2026, above the 68.9 F normal for 1991-2020. Drought weeks stayed low in 2021-2025, at 4.
