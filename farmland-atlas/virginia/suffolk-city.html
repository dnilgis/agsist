Suffolk City, Virginia

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,816 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.56 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 36.63 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 232 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,549 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,816 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $103 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.54 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 2, 2026). 24% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 30% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 33.8% from 2016 to 2026. 62% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 24% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 27% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 8.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 75 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,963 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $8.56 per base acre, PLC $2.58.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (102.66 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $22.69 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (33.23 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.53 per base acre, PLC $9.98. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (55.28 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $76.50 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $3.89 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.40, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 31% of the median yield. 0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 27.6%.
