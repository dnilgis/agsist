Wythe County, Virginia

Wythe County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $62 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,284 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 144.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51197. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wythe County runs $62 per acre, up 70.8 percent from 2016 to 2026. Corn yields have trended up 2.4 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 144.2 bushels per acre and a worst year of 94.6 in 2011. July nights average 60.8 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 59.4 F, and the long trend since 1976 is warming at 0.47 F per decade. Drought has been mild by the county's own record: 63 of 1357 weeks at severe drought or worse since 2000, with only 5 weeks from 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 43 percent in 1989-1999 to 47 percent in 2000-2009 to 67 percent in 2010-2019 to 66 percent in 2020-2025.
