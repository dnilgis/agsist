Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 225 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 3,462 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,252 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $95 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.71 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Aug 25, 2026). 43% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 23.4% from 2016 to 2026. 54% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 34% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 19 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,860 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.37, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 27% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 86.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1993 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.3%.

Quick answers for Essex County

What is cash rent in Essex County, Virginia?

The county average is $95 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Essex County, Virginia?

Land and buildings were worth $4,252 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Essex County, Virginia?

The county's 2021-25 average is 172.5 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
