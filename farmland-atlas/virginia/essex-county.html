Essex County, Virginia

Essex County, VA: non-irrigated cash rent $95 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,252 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 140.6 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Essex County, VA runs $95 per acre in 2026, up 23.4 percent from 2016. Census land value sits at $4,252 per acre in 2022, up 33.1 percent from 2017, 5.9 percent a year. Rent as a share of that value is 2.01 percent. July nights average 70 F over 2017-2026, against a 68.3 F normal, and the nightly low has warmed 0.78 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought drove 87 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1993-2025, and the loss ratio over 2016-2025 fell to 0.37 from 0.61 across 1989-2025. Drought weeks were 15 over 2021-2025, against 79 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse over 2000-2025.
