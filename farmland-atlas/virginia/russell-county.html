Non-irrigated cash rent in Russell County, VA runs $37 per acre, up 15.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. No cropland here is irrigated, per the 2022 census, so this rent reflects rainfed risk directly. Crop insurance history shows that risk: $10.0 million in indemnities since 1989, with heat and drought the largest cause at 42 percent, and a loss ratio of 3.02. Heat and drought's share moved differently across the decades: 65 percent in 1989-1999, 27 percent in 2000-2009, 70 percent in 2010-2019. Drought weeks in severe category or worse ran 71 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with zero weeks from 2021-2025.
