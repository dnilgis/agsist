The census value of land and buildings in Buchanan County was $4,490 an acre in 2022. That is up 85.7% from $2,418 in 2017, or 13.2% a year, after a dip from $2,642 in 2012. It sits just under the Virginia median of $4,596. In 2022, 22% of farmland here was rented.

Buchanan County had 8,501 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.
