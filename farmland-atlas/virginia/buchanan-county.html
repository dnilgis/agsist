Buchanan County had 8,501 acres in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Buchanan County shows no operating or proposed solar or wind, and no irrigated harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Land value per the 2022 census stands at $4,490 per acre, with a change of 85.7 percent from 2017-2022, or 13.2 percent a year. July nightly lows have moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 62.5 F to 63.6 F in 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.42 F per decade since 1976. Drought has been light in this county: 52 of 1357 weeks fell into severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 0 such weeks from 2021-2025.
