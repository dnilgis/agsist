As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Appomattox County runs $33 an acre for 2026, up 35.4% from $24 in 2016 and 15th lowest of 86 in Virginia against a state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,591 an acre in 2022, up 16.1% from $3,094 in 2017, or 3.0% a year. Corn ran 175.2 bu last year, well above the 2021-25 average of 154.3 bu.
