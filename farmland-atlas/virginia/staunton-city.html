Staunton City, Virginia

Staunton City, VA: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 51790. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Staunton City had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 64.2 F, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 62.5 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.38 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county for 49 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, with 2002 the worst year at 22 weeks, and 26 weeks in 2021-2025. No solar or wind capacity operates in the county, and none is proposed to EIA.
