Cash rent in Patrick County sits at $43 an acre for 2026, the same $43 it was in 2016, and that ranks tied for 34th lowest of 86 against a state median of $47. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,929 an acre in 2022, up 41.6% from $2,775 in 2017, and that is 27th lowest of 86. Only 17% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
