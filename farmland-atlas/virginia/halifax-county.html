Cropland rent in Halifax County runs $38 an acre for 2026, up 38.2% from $28 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $47 and is tied for 25th lowest of 86. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,199 an acre in 2022, up 20.7% from $2,651 in 2017, and ranks 8th lowest of 86. Corn made 134.8 bu in 2023, well over the 96.1 bu median from 2008 to 2023.
