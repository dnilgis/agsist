Cropland in Ward County rents for $60 an acre in 2026, up 17.8% from $51 in 2016 and a little under the state median of $65, which puts it 23rd lowest of 53. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,272 an acre in 2022, up 39.1% from $1,633 in 2017 and 19th highest of 53. Renters work a good share of the ground here, with 59% of farmland rented in 2022.
