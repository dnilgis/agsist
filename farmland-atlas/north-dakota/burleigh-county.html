Burleigh County, North Dakota

Burleigh County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $72 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,991 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 99.8 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38015. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Burleigh County averages $72 per acre in 2026, up 38.5 percent from 2016. Census value of land and buildings runs $1,991 per acre in 2022, with land value change 2017-2022 of 4.8 percent, 0.9 percent a year. Corn yield trend on published years 2009-2025 runs 1.5 bushels per acre a year, median 99.8 bushels per acre, with a worst year of 54 in 2021. Heat and drought share of indemnities moved 21 percent in 1989-1999, 66 percent in 2000-2009, 27 percent in 2010-2019, and 73 percent in 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.49 F per decade since 1976, and weeks in severe drought or worse ran 36 out of the 2021-2025 stretch. Wind operating capacity stands at 201.4 MW; solar operating is 0 MW.
