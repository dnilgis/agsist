Dunn County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,432 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.31 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 11.7 inches, 67% of normal (17.41 inches) through Nov 18, at Killdeer. Frost-free season: 130 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,242 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.00 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.76, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 46% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.6 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 51% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 20.4%.
