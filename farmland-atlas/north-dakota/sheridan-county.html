Sheridan County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,122 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.07 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

70.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 13.89 inches, 81% of normal (17.11 inches) through Nov 16, at Mc Clusky. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,157 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. PRF grazing county base value: $22.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 276 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.38 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.80, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 81% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 0.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 44.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 33.9%.
