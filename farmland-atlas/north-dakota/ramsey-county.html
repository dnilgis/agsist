Cash rent in Ramsey County runs $66 an acre for 2026, up 15.8% from $57 in 2016. That sits right about the state median of $65 and ranks 26th highest of 53. The census value of land and buildings was $1,885 an acre in 2022, up 4.2% from $1,809 in 2017, or 0.8% a year. Corn trends at 116.6 bu for 2026, and 49% of farmland was rented in 2022.
