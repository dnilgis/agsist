Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 13 surveys since 2008; up 28.2% from $66 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Cavalier County runs $84 an acre for 2026, up 28.2% from $66 in 2016 and 16th highest of 53 in North Dakota against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $2,507 an acre in 2022, up 20.5% from $2,080 in 2017. Corn trends at 110.6 bu for 2026, 12th lowest of 28, and 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
