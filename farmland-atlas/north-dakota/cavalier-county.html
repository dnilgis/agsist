Cavalier County, North Dakota

Cavalier County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $84 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,507 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 107.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Cavalier County averages $84 per acre in 2026, up 28.2 percent from 2016. Land value stood at $2,507 per acre in 2022, up 20.5 percent from 2017, 3.8 percent a year. Cropland here is not irrigated; the 2022 census puts irrigated share at 0 percent. The county held 41 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2021 alone, within 81 weeks of that depth across 2021-2025.
