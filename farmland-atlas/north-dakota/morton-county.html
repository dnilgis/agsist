Morton County, North Dakota

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,127 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $6.48 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 9.6 inches, 55% of normal (17.56 inches) through Nov 14, at New Salem, 5 mi NW. Frost-free season: 141 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,320 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $14.09 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.72, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 65% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 3.9 days a year and damaging wind on 4.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 60.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 13.7%.
