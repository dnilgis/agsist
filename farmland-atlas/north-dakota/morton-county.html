$56

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 33.7% from $42 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Morton County cash rent stood at $56 an acre in 2026, up 33.7% from $42 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $65 and ranks 19th lowest of 53. The census value of land and buildings was $1,865 an acre in 2022, up 32.8% from $1,404 in 2017. Corn made 117.1 bu in 2024, well over the county median of 93.4 bu.
