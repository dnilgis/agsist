Mercer County, North Dakota

Mercer County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $54 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,277 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 93.9 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mercer County averages $54 per acre in 2026, up 55.1 percent from 2016. Census land value stood at $1,277 per acre in 2022, down 6.7 percent from 2017, so rent has moved against value in that stretch. Corn yields trended down 0.7 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2022, with a median of 93.9 bushels per acre and a low of 54.4 in 2021, the same year 52 weeks of severe drought or worse were recorded. Heat and drought drive 54 percent of the county's $116.5 million in crop insurance indemnities since 1989, with that share at 68 percent in 2000-2009 and 67 percent in 2020-2025. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
