Wells County, North Dakota

North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $3,870 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.79 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 142 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,164 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.56 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.77, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 64% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 27.2% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 43.5%.

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 25.6% from $65 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wells County runs $81 an acre for 2026, up 25.6% from $65 in 2016. That rent is 17th highest of 53 in North Dakota, against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,385 an acre in 2022, up 39.4% from $1,711 in 2017. Corn holds up well here too, with a 14-yr trend of 147.5 bu that ranks 6th highest of 28.
