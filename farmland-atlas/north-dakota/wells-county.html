$81

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 25.6% from $65 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Wells County runs $81 an acre for 2026, up 25.6% from $65 in 2016. That rent is 17th highest of 53 in North Dakota, against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,385 an acre in 2022, up 39.4% from $1,711 in 2017. Corn holds up well here too, with a 14-yr trend of 147.5 bu that ranks 6th highest of 28.
