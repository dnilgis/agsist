Wells County, North Dakota

Wells County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $81 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,385 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 130.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38103. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wells County is $81 per acre in 2026, up 25.6 percent from 2016. Land value is $2,385 per acre in 2022, up 39.4 percent from 2017. July nightly lows have warmed 0.45 F per decade since 1976, and the county has no irrigation, solar, or wind capacity.
