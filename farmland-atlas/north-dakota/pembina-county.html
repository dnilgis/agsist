Cropland rent in Pembina County runs $131 an acre for 2026, up 43.2% from $92 in 2016 and 7th highest of 53 counties against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came in at $3,915 an acre in 2022, up 42.0% from $2,757 in 2017 and 3rd highest of 53. Corn trends at 148.2 bu for 2026, 5th highest of 28, and 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
