Benson County cash rent sits at $69 an acre for 2026, up 30.5% from $53 in 2016 and tied for 23rd highest of 53 in North Dakota. The census value of land and buildings was $1,514 an acre in 2022, up 10.7% from 2017. That ranks 13th lowest of 53, under the state median of $1,885. Corn came in at 154.5 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 115.2 bu going back to 2008.
