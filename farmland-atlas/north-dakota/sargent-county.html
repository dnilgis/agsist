$135

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 31.1% from $103 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Sargent County runs $135 an acre for 2026, 5th highest of 53 in North Dakota and well over the $65 state median. That is up 31.1% from $103 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $3,454 an acre in 2022, 6th highest of 53, up 23.9% from 2017. Corn made 194.7 bu in 2025, above the 165.2 bu 2021-25 average.
