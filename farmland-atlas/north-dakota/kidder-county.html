Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 37% from $43 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Kidder County runs $58 an acre for 2026, up 36.5% from $43 in 2016 and 22nd lowest of 53 in North Dakota. The census value of land and buildings was $1,884 an acre in 2022, up 49.8% from $1,258 in 2017, right about even with the state median of $1,885. Corn made 153.7 bu in 2025, well above the 2021-25 average of 128.6 bu.
