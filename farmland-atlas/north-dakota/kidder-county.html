Kidder County, North Dakota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,644 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $8.15 per base acre for corn.

Quick answers for Kidder County

What is cash rent in Kidder County, North Dakota?

The county average is $58 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $194 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Kidder County, North Dakota?

Land and buildings were worth $1,884 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Kidder County, North Dakota?

The county's 2021-25 average is 128.6 bushels an acre (all practices), from USDA NASS county yields, a county figure and not any one field.
