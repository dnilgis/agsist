Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 29.3% from $38 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Oliver County cash rent sits at $49 an acre for 2026, up 29.3% from $38 in 2016 and 14th lowest of 53 counties against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,781 an acre in 2022, up 23.4% from $1,443 in 2017. Corn made 125.3 bu in 2024, above the 113.4 bu median for the county. Renters work 67% of the farmland here.
