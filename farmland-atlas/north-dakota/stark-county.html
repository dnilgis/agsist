Stark County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,457 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.97 per base acre for corn.

Stark County vs North Dakota: $47.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 23% of the other 52 North Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (13th lowest of 53).

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Quick answers for Stark County

What is cash rent in Stark County, North Dakota?

The county average is $48 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Stark County, North Dakota?

Land and buildings were worth $1,651 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.

What is the corn yield in Stark County, North Dakota?

The county's 14-yr trend (projected 2026) is 89.2 bushels an acre (all practices), fit to 11 years of USDA NASS county yields. A trend line is a projection from past years, not a forecast of this year's crop.
