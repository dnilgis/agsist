Cash rent in Stark County runs $48 an acre for 2026, up 33.8% from $36 in 2016 and 13th lowest of 53 in North Dakota against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $1,651 an acre in 2022, up 24.0% from $1,331 in 2017, which ranks 18th lowest of 53. Corn trends at 89.2 bu, 7th lowest of 28 counties, and 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
