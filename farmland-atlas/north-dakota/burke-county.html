Burke County, North Dakota

Burke County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $37 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,445 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.04 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38013. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Burke County averages $37 per acre for 2026, up 8.8 percent from 2014. Census land value stands at $1,445 per acre, with a change of 37.1 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.5 percent a year. The 2022 cash rent figure equals 2.8 percent of that census value. Water risk shows up in the record: 209 of 1357 weeks since 2000 landed in severe drought or worse, and 101 of those weeks fall within 2021-2025 alone, with 2021 itself reaching 52 weeks. Crop insurance history points to excess moisture and flood as the largest cause of indemnities at 59 percent, ahead of heat and drought, which peaked at 27 percent in 2000-2009.
