$51

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2008; up 40.3% from $36 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Grant County rents for $51 an acre in 2026, up 40.3% from $36 in 2016. That still sits 16th lowest of 53 against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $1,382 an acre in 2022, up 29.0% from $1,071 in 2017, and 8th lowest of 53. Corn made 109.6 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 91.5 bu.
