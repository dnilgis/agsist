$95

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 34% from $71 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Emmons County runs $95 an acre for 2026, up 33.8% from $71 in 2016 and 14th highest of 53 against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $1,968 an acre in 2022, up 24.2% from $1,585 in 2017, which is 4.4% a year. Corn trends at 130.5 bu for 2026, ahead of the state median of 117.8 bu, and 60% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
