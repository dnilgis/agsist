Griggs County, North Dakota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $3,187 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $10.88 per base acre for corn.

Griggs County vs North Dakota: $80 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 67% of the other 52 North Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (18th highest of 53).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

8.9% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 18.00 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 89% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 144 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,186 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.09 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.89, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 75% of the median yield. 1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 15% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 53.6%.
