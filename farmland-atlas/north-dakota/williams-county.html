Cropland in Williams County rents for $38 an acre in 2026, up 10.1% from $35 in 2016. That is 5th lowest of 53 in North Dakota, where the state median is $65. The census value of land and buildings was $1,383 an acre in 2022, up 26.2% from $1,096 in 2017. Corn made 102.4 bu in 2025, well over the county median of 84.6 bu.
