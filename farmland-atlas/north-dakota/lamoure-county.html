Non-irrigated cash rent in LaMoure County averages $122 per acre in 2026, up 27.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $232 per acre in 2025, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved with no clear direction, from 1.75 in 2011 to 1.98 in 2025. Census land value came in at $3,133 per acre in 2022, up 23.4 percent from 2017. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census.
