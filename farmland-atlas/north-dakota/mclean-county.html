Cash rent in McLean County runs $73 an acre in 2026, up 22.9% from $59 in 2016 and 21st highest of 53 in North Dakota. The census value of land and buildings was $2,556 an acre in 2022, up 43.2% from $1,785 in 2017, against a state median of $1,885. Corn made 125.6 bu in 2025, well above the 109.7 bu average from 2021 to 2025.
