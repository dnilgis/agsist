Barnes County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $2,861 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.75 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

74.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure.

Land

Land and buildings: $2,861 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $111 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet.

Grain

County median corn basis: 73 cents under Dec '26 futures, 1 elevator in the county.

Best corn cash bid within 30 miles on Nov 18: $3.74 at Hannaford, 26 miles. 2 elevators we read sit within 30 miles. The newest bids load further down.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.75 per base acre, PLC $3.09.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (123 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $27.20 an acre, crop year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.93 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.99 over the same years. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. 0.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census).

Cash rent in Barnes County runs $111 an acre for 2026, up 28.3% from $87 in 2016 and 11th highest of 53 in North Dakota against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $2,861 an acre in 2022, up 23.8% from $2,311 in 2017. Corn holds up too, with a 14-yr trend of 161.7 bu that sits 2nd highest of 28 in the state. 58% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
