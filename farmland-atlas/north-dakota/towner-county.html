$64

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2008; up 42.7% from $45 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland in Towner County rents for $64 an acre in 2026, up 42.7% from $45 in 2016 and just under the state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $1,929 an acre in 2022, up 39.3% from $1,385 in 2017, which ranks 26th highest of 53. Corn yields 109.6 bu on the 14 year trend, 11th lowest of 28, though 2025 came in at 143.8 bu.
