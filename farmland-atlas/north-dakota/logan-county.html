USDA NASS county yields, 2024

Cash rent in Logan County runs $69 an acre for 2026, tied for 23rd highest of 53 in North Dakota and a bit above the $65 state median. That is up 17.1% from $59 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,837 an acre in 2022, up 54.2% from $1,191 in 2017. Corn made 146.8 bu in 2024, well over the county median of 98.7 bu.
