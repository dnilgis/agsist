As of Oct 16, cash rent in Traill County runs $161 an acre for 2026, up 42.5% from $113 in 2016 and 3rd highest of 53 in North Dakota, where the state median is $65. The census value of land and buildings was $3,725 an acre in 2022, up 20.8% from 2017 and 4th highest of 53. The corn trend sits at 173.9 bu, highest of 28 counties, and 62% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
