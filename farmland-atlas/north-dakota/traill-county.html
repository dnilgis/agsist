Traill County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $5,591 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.65 per base acre for corn.

Traill County vs North Dakota: $161 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 96% of the other 52 North Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (3rd highest of 53).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

58.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 17.82 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 82% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 152 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,350 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.49 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.73, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 10.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 56.6%.
