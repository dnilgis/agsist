As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Pierce County runs $65 an acre for 2026, right at the state median of $65 and tied for 26th lowest of 53. That is up 27.7% from $51 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $1,765 an acre in 2022, up 25.3% from $1,409 in 2017 and 21st lowest of 53. The corn trend projects 108.4 bu for 2026, 10th lowest of 28 in the state.
