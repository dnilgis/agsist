Steele County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $3,588 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.53 per base acre for corn.

Steele County vs North Dakota: $99 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 79% of the other 52 North Dakota counties with a 2026 rent (12th highest of 53).

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

67.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 145 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,213 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $8.31 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.62, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 77% of the median yield. 1.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 50.6%.
