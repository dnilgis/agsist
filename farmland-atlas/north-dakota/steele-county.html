Cash rent in Steele County runs $99 an acre in 2026, up 15.1% from $86 in 2016 and 12th highest of 53 in North Dakota. The census value of land and buildings was $2,962 an acre in 2022, up 34.8% from $2,197 in 2017, against a state median of $1,885. Corn made 162.1 bu in 2024, and 59% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
