USDA NASS county yields, 2023

Cash rent in Hettinger County runs $49 an acre for 2026, up 18.1% from $42 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $65 and ranks 15th lowest of 53. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,611 an acre in 2022, up 14.7% from $1,405 in 2017, which is 2.8% a year. Corn here is a tougher go, with a 14-yr trend of 84.9 bu that ranks 5th lowest of 28 against a state median of 117.8 bu.
