Cash rent in Cass County runs $163 an acre for 2026, tied for highest of 53 in North Dakota, against a state median of $65. That rent is up 38.1% from $118 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $4,110 an acre in 2022, 2nd highest of 53, up 10.5% from $3,718 in 2017. Corn made 182.3 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 168.5 bu.
