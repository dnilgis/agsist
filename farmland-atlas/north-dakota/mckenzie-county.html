McKenzie County, North Dakota

McKenzie County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $30 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,352 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in McKenzie County runs $30 per acre in 2026, down 1.7 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents at $185 per acre. Crop insurance indemnities for 1989-2025 total $140.9 million, with heat and drought the largest cause at 54 percent. That cause's share moved by period: 24 percent in 1989-1999, 79 percent in 2000-2009, 22 percent in 2010-2019, 79 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were 207 of 1357 over 2000-2025, with 126 of those falling in 2021-2025 alone.
