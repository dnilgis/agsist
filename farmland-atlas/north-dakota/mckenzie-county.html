Heat and drought is the story in McKenzie County. It caused 54 percent of the crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989 to 2025, and its share went from 24 percent in 1989-1999 to 79 percent in 2020-2025. The county has had 207 weeks of severe drought or worse since 2000, and 2021 alone had 52 of them. Irrigation is barely used here, with only 3 percent of water from groundwater, so irrigated cash rent of $185 per acre rests on very little ground. Non-irrigated rent is $30 per acre, down 1.7 percent since 2016.
