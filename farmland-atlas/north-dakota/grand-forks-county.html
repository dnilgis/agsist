USDA NASS county yields, 2025

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Grand Forks County ran $134 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 53 in North Dakota and well over the $65 state median. That is up 46.4% from $92 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $3,568 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 53, up 4.6% from $3,411 in 2017. Corn made 166.7 bu in 2025, above the 147.1 bu average for 2021 to 2025.
