Sioux County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $1,100 an acre.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 144 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,367 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $16.33 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.83, 2016 to 2025. 0% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 19.5%.
