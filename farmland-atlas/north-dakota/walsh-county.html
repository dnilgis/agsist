As of Oct 16, cash rent in Walsh County runs $136 an acre for 2026, up 54.5% from $88 in 2016 and 4th highest of 53 counties in North Dakota, where the state median is $65. The census value of land and buildings was $3,221 an acre in 2022, up 28.2% from $2,513 in 2017. Corn made 154.2 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 142.3 bu.
