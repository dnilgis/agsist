Cropland rent in Mountrail County runs $42 an acre for 2026, up 6.4% from $39 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 53 in North Dakota against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,445 an acre in 2022, up 13.1% from $1,278 in 2017, which ranks tied for 10th lowest of 53. Corn here trends at 86.6 bu, 6th lowest of 28 counties. 59% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
