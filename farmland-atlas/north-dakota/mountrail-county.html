Mountrail County, North Dakota

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,690 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $5.80 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

9.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.36 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 100% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 140 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,124 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.80 per base acre, PLC $1.61.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (64.12 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $50.06 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $14.17 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.32 per base acre, PLC $6.89. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (38.18 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $15.20 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,491 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.73, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. 0.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 2.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 36.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 31.3%.
