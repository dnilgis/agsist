McHenry County cash rent was $57 an acre in 2026, up 35.7% from $42 in 2016 and 21st lowest of 53 in North Dakota. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,533 an acre in 2022, up 40.8% from $1,089 in 2017, under the state median of $1,885. Corn made 129.5 bu an acre in 2024. 51% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
