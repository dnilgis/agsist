Eddy County, North Dakota

North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $2,396 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.92 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,194 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.81 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.86, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.2 days a year and damaging wind on 1.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 27.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 54%.
