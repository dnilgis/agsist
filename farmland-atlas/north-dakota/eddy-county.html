Heat and drought's share of indemnities has climbed across each decade listed: 14 percent in 1989-1999, 16 percent in 2000-2009, 24 percent in 2010-2019, 37 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were concentrated recently: 43 of the 65 weeks of severe drought or worse since 2000 fell in 2021-2025. Solar and wind capacity are both 0 MW, operating and proposed.
