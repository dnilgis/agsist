Cash rent in Foster County runs $91 an acre for 2026, up 17.4% from $78 in 2016 and 15th highest of 53 counties against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $2,646 an acre in 2022, up 31.8% from $2,008 in 2017. Corn trends at 146.5 bu for 2026, 7th highest of 28 in the state.
