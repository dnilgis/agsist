Cropland rent in Richland County runs $163 an acre for 2026, tied for highest of 53 counties in North Dakota and up 27.3% from $128 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,419 an acre in 2022, highest of 53, up 38.6% from $3,189 in 2017. Corn made 184.3 bu in 2025, well over the 2021-25 average of 163.2 bu.
