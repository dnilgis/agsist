Richland County, North Dakota

Richland County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $163 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,419 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 154.2 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38077. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Richland County averages $163 per acre for 2026, up 27.3 percent from 2016. Census land value is $4,419 per acre, up 38.6 percent from 2017 to 2022, and the 2022 rent-to-value ratio is 3.15 percent. Corn yield trend is 3 bushels per acre a year from 2008-2025, with a median of 154.2 bushels per acre and a low of 96.4 in 2011. Excess moisture and flood drive 70 percent of indemnities paid from 1989-2025, while heat and drought's share has moved from 2 percent in 1989-1999 to 6 percent in 2000-2009, 7 percent in 2010-2019, and 17 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks stayed low overall, 32 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 10 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
