Richland County, North Dakota

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $6,783 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.04 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

82.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.05 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 69% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 159 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,488 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.04 per base acre, PLC $3.31.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.78 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.44, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 62% of the median yield. Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.5 days a year and damaging wind on 3.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 8.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 69.6%.
