Cash rent in Ransom County runs $118 an acre for 2026, up 24.2% from $95 in 2016 and 10th highest of 53 in North Dakota against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $2,811 an acre in 2022, up 39.7% from $2,012 in 2017. Corn made 188.4 bu in 2025, above the 2021-25 average of 178.0 bu. In 2022, 47% of the farmland here was rented.
