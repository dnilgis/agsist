Dickey County, North Dakota

Rain is at 83% of normal. North Dakota's 2026 Trust Lands survey puts rented cropland at $4,944 an acre. ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.21 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

7.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 18.08 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 83% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 154 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,350 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.21 per base acre, PLC $3.27.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (130.34 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $18.89 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $28.81 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $7.85 per base acre, PLC $7.27. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.27 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $29.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 1,714 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.58 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.88, 2016 to 2025. The worst corn year since 2008 made 74% of the median yield. 4.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 12.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 68.3%.
