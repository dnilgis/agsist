Dickey County, North Dakota

Dickey County, ND: non-irrigated cash rent $121 an acre (2026); land and buildings $3,238 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 146.7 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 38021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Dickey County runs $121 per acre in 2026, up 26 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $199 per acre, and the irrigated multiple has moved from 1.61 in 2011 to 1.64 in 2026, direction falling. Corn yields trend up 2.2 bushels per acre a year over 2008-2025, with a median of 146.7 bushels per acre and a worst year of 108.3 in 2013. Excess moisture and flood account for 68 percent of the $553.1 million paid out in indemnities since 1989, while heat and drought's share has moved from 2 percent in 1989-1999 to 11 percent in 2000-2009, 12 percent in 2010-2019, and 14 percent in 2020-2025. Wind operates at 330 MW; solar is not present.
