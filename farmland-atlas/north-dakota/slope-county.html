USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Slope County runs $47 an acre for 2026, up 38.2% from $34 in 2016, and ranks 12th lowest of 53 against a state median of $65. The census value of land and buildings was $1,492 an acre in 2022, up 41.2% from $1,057 in 2017. Corn here trends at 77.5 bu, 2nd lowest of 28 in the state, where the median is 117.8 bu.
