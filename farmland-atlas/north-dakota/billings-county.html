As of Oct 16, cash rent in Billings County runs $37 an acre in 2026, tied for 3rd lowest of 53 in North Dakota, against a state median of $65. That rent is up 13.8% from $33 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $1,167 an acre in 2022, the lowest of 53, up 27.0% from $919 in 2017. Corn trends at 78.6 bu, 3rd lowest of 28.
