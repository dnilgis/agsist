Ketchikan Gateway Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 49.4 F for 2017-2026, close to the 49.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend since 1976 runs 0.28 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has reached an average low of 70 F. Drought has touched this county for 38 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025, worst in 2019 with 24 weeks, and 2021-2025 shows 0 weeks.
