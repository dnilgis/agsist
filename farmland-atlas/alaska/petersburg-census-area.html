Petersburg Census Area had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nightly lows in Petersburg Census Area average 45 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 44.4 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.4 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched the county rarely: 10 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, with 2019 the worst year at 10 weeks, and 2021-2025 recorded none. Solar and wind operating capacity stand at 0 MW each, with no proposed capacity to EIA.
