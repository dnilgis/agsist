Aleutians West Census Area, AK: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 02016. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Aleutians West Census Area had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 45.3 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 44.4 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.19 F per decade, with no clear direction. No July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. Solar operating stands at 0 MW and wind operating at 0 MW, with no MW of either proposed to EIA.
