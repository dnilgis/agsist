North Slope Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 44 F, up from the 1991-2020 normal of 43 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.7 F per decade, warming, and no July since 1900 has averaged a nightly low at or above 70 F. There is no solar or wind operating or proposed in this county.
