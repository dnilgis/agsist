Prince of Wales-Hyder Census Area had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 51 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 50.5 F. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.35 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has reached an average low at or above 70 F. Drought has touched this county rarely: 58 of 1357 weeks between 2000-2025 hit severe drought or worse, 4.3 percent, with the worst year 2019 at 44 weeks, and 0 weeks in 2021-2025. Solar operating stands at 0 MW and wind operating at 0 MW, with 0 MW solar and 0 MW wind proposed to EIA.
