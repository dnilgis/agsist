Dillingham Census Area, Alaska

Dillingham Census Area, AK: the public record carries few figures here. Each missing figure is shown with its reason. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 02070. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Dillingham Census Area had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 47.6 F, against a 1991-2020 normal of 47.3 F. The trend since 1976 is 0.22 F per decade, with no clear direction. No July since 1900 has posted an average low at or above 70 F. There is no solar or wind operating in the county, and none proposed to EIA.
