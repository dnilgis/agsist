Kodiak Island Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Kodiak Island Borough runs cool in July, with nightly lows averaging 46.6 F for 2017-2026 against a 45.6 F normal for 1991-2020. The trend since 1976 runs at 0.38 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has posted an average low at or above 70 F. Drought has been a minor factor here: severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 4 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 2019 the worst year at 4 weeks, and no weeks at all from 2021-2025. Wind power stands at 9 MW operating, with no solar operating and nothing proposed to EIA in either category.
