Lake and Peninsula Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nightly lows average 46.5 F for 2017-2026, close to the 46 F normal for 1991-2020, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.1 F per decade with no clear direction. No July since 1900 has averaged a low at or above 70 F. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed in this county.
