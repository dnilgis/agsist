Northwest Arctic Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 47.8 F for 2017-2026, against a 46.7 F normal for 1991-2020. The July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.46 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has reached an average low of 70 F or above. The county has 1.7 MW of solar and 3.3 MW of wind operating, with no proposed additions of either to EIA.
