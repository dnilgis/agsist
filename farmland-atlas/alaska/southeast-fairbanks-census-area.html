Southeast Fairbanks Census Area, Alaska

Southeast Fairbanks Census Area, AK: crop insurance loss ratio 0.83 since 1996. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 02240. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Southeast Fairbanks Census Area had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Crop insurance indemnities here total $1.6 million over 1996-2025, closed crop years, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 41 percent. Heat and drought share of those losses moved from 96 percent in 1989-1999 to 7 percent in 2000-2009 to 32 percent in 2010-2019 to 32 percent in 2020-2025. Drought has been rare on the ground: severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in only 2 of 1357 weeks since 2000, and none of those weeks fell in 2021-2025. July nightly lows average 45 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 44.8 F, with a long-term trend of 0.01 F per decade and no clear direction.
