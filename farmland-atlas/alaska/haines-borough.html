Haines Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

July nights average 45.4 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 44.3 F. The trend since 1976 runs 0.61 F per decade, warming. No July since 1900 has reached an average low of 70 F or above. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
