Bristol Bay Borough had no acres published in farms in the 2022 census. County averages here describe very little farmland; read them with care.

Bristol Bay Borough has never had a July with an average nightly low at or above 70 F, and the current reading confirms that pattern. The July average nightly low for 2017-2026 is 48 F, close to the 1991-2020 normal of 47.7 F. The trend since 1976 runs at 0.2 F per decade with no clear direction. No solar or wind capacity is operating or proposed in this county.
