Cash rent on Flathead County cropland is $43 an acre in 2026, up 19.7% from $36 in 2016 and tied for 3rd highest of 40 in Montana. The census value of land and buildings was $9,225 an acre in 2022, highest of 53 in the state and well above the $1,237 state median. That was up 44.4% from $6,389 in 2017. Irrigated rent runs $71 an acre in 2026, 7th lowest of 29.
