Stands out against its own record

$43

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 11 surveys since 2012; up 60.4% from $27 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Lake County runs $43 an acre for 2026, up 60.4% from $27 in 2016 and tied for 3rd highest of 40 counties in Montana. The census value of land and buildings came in at $2,120 an acre in 2022, up 32.0% from $1,606 in 2017. That puts it 9th highest of 53 against a state median of $1,237. Only 22% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
