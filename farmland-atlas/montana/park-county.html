Park County, Montana

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

29.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 21.65 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 91% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 102 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,465 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $32.50 an acre (USDA, 2026).

The 2022 census land value fell while next door rose; read it with care below. We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 85.7% from 2016 to 2026. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 56.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 9,607 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $8.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.54 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.46, 2016 to 2025. 67.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 3.9, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 40.8% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3%.

Cash rent in Park County runs $33 an acre for 2026, tied for 16th highest of 40 in Montana and a bit over the state median of $31. That is up 85.7% from $18 in 2016. Irrigated ground rents for $92 an acre in 2026, 13th lowest of 29, under the state median of $98. Just 19% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
