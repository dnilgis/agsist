Cash rent in Park County runs $33 an acre for 2026, tied for 16th highest of 40 in Montana and a bit over the state median of $31. That is up 85.7% from $18 in 2016. Irrigated ground rents for $92 an acre in 2026, 13th lowest of 29, under the state median of $98. Just 19% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

Sources and terms

Every figure is a county average from the sources below, not an appraisal of any farm. "Figures as of" is the date this page's numbers last changed. Rent ranks count every county with a published rent in the same survey year, within the state (the same counties as the state rent page); other ranks count counties with 10,000 acres or more in farms. Rent: USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, county. Land value: Census of Agriculture, operators' estimate of land and buildings. Yield: USDA NASS county corn yields.
