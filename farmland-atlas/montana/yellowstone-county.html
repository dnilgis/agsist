Yellowstone County, Montana

Yellowstone County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $28 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,216 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 127.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30111. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Yellowstone County runs $132 per acre against $28 per acre non-irrigated, and irrigation covers 24 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census. Non-irrigated rent is up 31 percent from 2016 to 2026. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities over 1989-2025, at 39 percent of $60.1 million paid, and its share moved 19 percent, 53 percent, 30 percent, 38 percent across 1989-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, and 2020-2025. July nightly lows have warmed at 0.79 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 57.9 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 56.3 F. The county spent 44 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025. Census land value rose 21.2 percent from 2017 to 2022.
