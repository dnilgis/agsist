Meagher County, Montana

Meagher County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2014); land and buildings $1,316 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30059. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland in Meagher County rents for $38 per acre, non-irrigated for $22 per acre, both 2014 figures. Sixty percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and none of that irrigation water comes from groundwater as of 2015. The July nightly low has moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 47 F to 48.9 F over 2017-2026, part of a warming trend of 0.65 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought caused 40 percent of the county's $8.1 million in crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and its share by period was 10 percent in 1989-1999, 51 percent in 2000-2009, 36 percent in 2010-2019, and 66 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value rose 44.9 percent from 2017 to 2022, reaching $1,316 per acre in 2022.
