Stands out against its own record

$33

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 17.9% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Daniels County runs $33 an acre for 2026, up 17.9% from $28 in 2016. That sits a bit above the state median of $31 and ranks tied for 14th highest of 40. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,076 an acre in 2022, up 69.7% from $634 in 2017, which still leaves it 17th lowest of 53 in Montana. 48% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
