Daniels County, Montana

Daniels County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,076 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30019. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Daniels County averages $33 per acre in 2026, up 17.9 percent from 2016. Heat and drought account for 64 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, and that share has moved unevenly by period: 11 percent in 1989-1999, 60 percent in 2000-2009, 41 percent in 2010-2019, and 83 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.22, above the 1989-2025 figure of 1.02. Drought weeks in 2021-2025 totaled 117, against 201 of 1357 weeks countywide since 2000.
