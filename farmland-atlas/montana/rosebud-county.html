Rosebud County, Montana

Rosebud County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2026); land and buildings $716 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.91 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30087. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Rosebud County stands at $25 per acre for 2026, up 47.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated cash rent in 2024 was $106 per acre, and 20 percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, none of it from groundwater as of 2015. Census land and buildings value reached $716 per acre in 2022, up 66.5 percent from 2017, or 10.7 percent a year. Drought has touched the county in 293 of 1357 weeks since 2000, with 2002 the worst year at 53 weeks and 44 weeks logged 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved from 8 percent in 1989-1999 to 19 percent in 2000-2009 to 16 percent in 2010-2019 to 38 percent in 2020-2025.
