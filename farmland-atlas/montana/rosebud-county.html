Cropland rent in Rosebud County runs $25 an acre for 2026, up 47.1% from $17 in 2016 and 8th lowest of 40 in Montana. The census value of land and buildings came to $716 an acre in 2022, up 66.5% from $430 in 2017, and that ranks 3rd lowest of 53 against a state median of $1,237. Pasture rents for $7 an acre in 2026, and 29% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
