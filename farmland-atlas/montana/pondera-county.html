Pondera County, Montana

Non-irrigated cash rent in Pondera County averages $44 per acre in 2026, up 23.9 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent runs $86 per acre, now 1.95 times the non-irrigated rate, against 1.28 in 2009, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Irrigation covers 16 percent of harvested cropland, and none of that water comes from groundwater. Heat and drought caused 52 percent of the $194.9 million in crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, closed crop years, with the share at 42 percent in 1989-1999, 63 percent in 2000-2009, 30 percent in 2010-2019, and 58 percent in 2020-2025. The county spent 115 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, out of 383 weeks over 2000-2025.
