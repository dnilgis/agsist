Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 24% from $36 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cash rent in Pondera County runs $44 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 40 in Montana against a state median of $31. That rent is up 23.9% from $36 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $1,751 an acre in 2022, up 45.1% from $1,207 in 2017, or 7.7% a year. 38% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
