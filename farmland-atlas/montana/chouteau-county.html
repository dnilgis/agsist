Cropland in Chouteau County rents for $37 an acre in 2026, up 17.7% from $31 in 2016. That sits 9th highest of 40 Montana counties, against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,356 an acre in 2022, up 45.3% from $933 in 2017, and 48% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
