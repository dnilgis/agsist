Silver Bow County, Montana

Silver Bow County, MT: irrigated cash rent $59 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,572 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30093. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigation covers 74 percent of harvested cropland here, and 1 percent of that water comes from groundwater, so this county leans on surface water. Drought weeks add up to 377 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 2004 the worst year at 52 weeks, and 116 weeks came in 2021-2025 alone. July nights average 48.5 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 47 F, and the trend since 1976 runs 0.63 F per decade, warming. Land value rose 14.3 percent from 2017-2022, 2.7 percent a year, with land and buildings at $2,572 per acre in the 2022 census.
