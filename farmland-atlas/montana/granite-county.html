Granite County, Montana

Granite County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $15 an acre (2023); land and buildings $2,808 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30039. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent runs $15 per acre; irrigated cash rent is $91 per acre for 2022. Irrigated ground covers 88 percent of harvested cropland, 2022 census, and none of that irrigation water comes from groundwater, 2015. Land value is $2,808 per acre, 2022, with a change of 73.5 percent over 2017-2022, or 11.7 percent a year. July nightly lows average 46.8 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 45.2 F, a warming trend of 0.76 F per decade since 1976. Severe drought or worse covered at least 50 percent of the county in 333 of 1357 weeks, 2000-2025, with 137 weeks in 2021-2025 and 2024 the worst year at 44 weeks. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
