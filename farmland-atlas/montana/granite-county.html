Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 23.75 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 112% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. Land

Land and buildings: $2,808 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $15 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.19 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 6, 2026 to Sep 15, 2026). 0.4% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 27% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 57.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 10,696 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Cash rent in Granite County runs $15 an acre for 2023, and irrigated ground rents for $91 an acre in 2022. The census value of land and buildings came to $2,808 an acre in 2022, up 73.5% from $1,618 in 2017, or 11.7% a year. That value ranks 5th highest of 53 in Montana, well over the state median of $1,237. 27% of farmland in the county was rented in 2022.

Quick answers for Granite County

What is cash rent in Granite County, Montana?

The county average is $15 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Granite County, Montana?

Land and buildings were worth $2,808 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
