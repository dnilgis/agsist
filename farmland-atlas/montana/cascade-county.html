Cascade County, Montana

Rain is at 128% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,234 an acre (2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.39 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 17.85 inches, 128% of normal (13.9 inches) through Nov 17, at Great Falls. Frost-free season: 143 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,870 in the median year.

Land

Land and buildings: $1,234 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $46 an acre (USDA, 2026). We do not read recorded sales in this state yet. Cash rent rose 65.5% from 2016 to 2026. 33.5% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out. 51.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 33,963 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.39 per base acre, PLC $1.26.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (50.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $10.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 7,443 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.82 per $100 of coverage a year, the last ten closed years.

Loss ratio 0.88 over the same years. 10.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.8 days a year and damaging wind on 3.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 52.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.4%.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cascade County cash rent ran $46 an acre in 2026, highest of 40 in Montana, against a state median of $31. That is up 65.5% from $28 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings sat at $1,234 an acre in 2022, up 1.8% from $1,212 in 2017, or 0.4% a year. In 2022, 33% of the farmland here was rented.
