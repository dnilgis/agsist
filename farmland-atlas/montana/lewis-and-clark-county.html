Cropland rent in Lewis and Clark County was $36 an acre in 2025, up 94.6% from $19 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,762 an acre in 2022, up 34.9% from $1,306 in 2017, and that ranks 16th highest of 53 against a state median of $1,237. Irrigated ground rented for $86 an acre in 2026, tied for 10th lowest of 29.
