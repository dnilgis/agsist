Non-irrigated cash rent in Garfield County averages $29 per acre in 2026, up 87.1 percent from 2016. Heat and drought is the largest cause of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, at 40 percent of $32.6 million paid, and its share has moved from 20 percent in 1989-1999 to 39 percent in 2000-2009 to 35 percent in 2010-2019 to 46 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 is 1.28, above the 0.84 ratio for the full 1989-2025 record. July nights have averaged 58.2 F over 2017-2026 against a 56 F normal for 1991-2020, part of a warming trend of 0.86 F per decade since 1976.
