$36

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 15 surveys since 2009; up 44.9% from $25 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Dawson County runs $36 an acre for 2026, up 44.9% from $25 in 2016. That sits above the state median of $31 and ranks tied for 10th highest of 40. The census value of land and buildings was $981 an acre in 2022, up 46.9% from $668 in 2017, and still 10th lowest of 53 against a state median of $1,237. 37% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
