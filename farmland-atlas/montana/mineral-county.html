Cash rent in Mineral County ran $20 an acre in 2021. The census value of land and buildings came to $5,629 an acre in 2022, up 12.5% from $5,002 in 2017, or 2.4% a year. That puts the county 3rd highest of 53 in Montana, well over the state median of $1,237. Only 7% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
