Mineral County, Montana

Mineral County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $20 an acre (2021); land and buildings $5,629 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30061. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Mineral County runs $20 per acre. Census value of land and buildings sits at $5,629 per acre, up 12.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 2.4 percent a year. July nights average 50.2 F for 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 48.6 F, and the July nightly low trend since 1976 runs 0.69 F per decade, warming. Irrigated cropland covers 13 percent of harvested acres. No solar or wind capacity operates or is proposed here.
