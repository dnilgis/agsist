Richland County, Montana

Richland County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,109 an acre in the 2022 census; median corn yield 132.5 bu/ac. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30083. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Richland County runs $33 per acre in 2026, up 40.4 percent from 2016. Irrigated ground rents higher, at $143 per acre, though only 14 percent of harvested cropland carries irrigation, and none of that water comes from groundwater. Corn yields show the risk under dry conditions: a median of 132.5 bushels per acre from 2008-2016, falling to 89.4 in 2012. Crop insurance backs this up. Heat and drought drove 56 percent of $77.9 million in indemnities across 1989-2025, and its share by period reads 13 percent, 66 percent, 43 percent, 70 percent. Land value rose 26.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, at 4.8 percent a year.
