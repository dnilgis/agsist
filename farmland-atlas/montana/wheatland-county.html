Wheatland County, Montana

Wheatland County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $22 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,197 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30107. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $22 per acre, up 53.6 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value follows the same path, $1,197 per acre in 2022, up 72.7 percent from 2017 to 2022, 11.6 percent a year. July nights average 51.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 49.5 F normal, part of a warming trend of 0.57 F per decade since 1976. The county spent 51 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025.
