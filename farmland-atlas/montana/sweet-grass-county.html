Irrigated cash rent in Sweet Grass County stands at $70 per acre for 2026, against a non-irrigated rate of $15 per acre in 2019. Irrigation covers 53 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, and none of that water comes from groundwater as of 2015. Crop insurance history shows heat and drought as the largest cause of indemnities at 57 percent across closed crop years 1989-2024, with the share rising to 75 percent in 2020-2025 after 47 percent in 2010-2019. July nights have warmed, averaging 51.1 F over 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 50 F.
