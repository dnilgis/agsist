Sweet Grass County, Montana

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,499 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Text itFacebook

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

38.5% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 16.27 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 90% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 127 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,755 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,499 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.21 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 3, 2026 to Sep 7, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 4% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 38.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 8,063 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $6.26 per base acre, PLC $5.25. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (29.1 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 24, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $8.80 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

53.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.5 days a year and damaging wind on 1.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 57.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2024; too much rain caused 3.4%.

The census value of land and buildings in Sweet Grass County was $1,499 an acre in 2022, up 32.0% from $1,136 in 2017. That puts it 20th highest of 53 in Montana, above the state median of $1,237. Irrigated rent ran $70 an acre in 2026, 6th lowest of 29 and under the state median of $98, while pasture went for $8. Operators rented 38% of the farmland in 2022.
