Madison County, Montana

Madison County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2019); land and buildings $2,757 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Madison County is $127 per acre for 2026, against $36 per acre for non-irrigated land in 2019. Eighty percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and none of that water comes from groundwater, so surface supply carries the county. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities has moved up each period: 13 percent in 1989-1999, 19 percent in 2000-2009, 32 percent in 2010-2019, 72 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has warmed 0.6 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 48.6 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 47.1 F. Land value per the 2022 census stands at $2,757 per acre, up 61.2 percent from 2017.
