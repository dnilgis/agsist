The census value of land and buildings in Madison County was $2,757 an acre in 2022, up 61.2% from $1,710 in 2017. That puts it 6th highest of 53 in Montana, against a state median of $1,237. Irrigated rent ran $127 an acre in 2026, 7th highest of 29, while pasture rent was $11 an acre in 2026. Only 13% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
