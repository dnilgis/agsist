Stands out against its own record

$33

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 35.4% from $24 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Wibaux County runs $33 an acre for 2026, tied for 16th highest of 40 in Montana. That is up 35.4% from $24 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings was $934 an acre in 2022, up 51.4% from $617 in 2017, and still 7th lowest of 53 in the state. Renters work 38% of the farmland here.
