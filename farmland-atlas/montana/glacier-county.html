Non-irrigated cash rent in Glacier County runs $29 per acre in 2026, up 31.8 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents at $74 per acre, but irrigation covers only 6 percent of harvested cropland, and none of the county's irrigation water came from groundwater as of 2015. Drought has been a real presence: the county spent 343 of 1357 weeks since 2000 at severe drought or worse, with 89 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Crop insurance paid out $146.0 million over 1989-2025, with heat and drought the largest cause at 48 percent, and the 2016-2025 loss ratio stands at 1.
