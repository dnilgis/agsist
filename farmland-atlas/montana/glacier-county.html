As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Glacier County runs $29 an acre in 2026, up 31.8% from $22 in 2016, and tied for 15th lowest of 40 against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings was $1,478 an acre in 2022, up 26.6% from $1,167 in 2017, and 21st highest of 53 in Montana. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
