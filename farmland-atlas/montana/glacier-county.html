Glacier County, Montana

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,478 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

24.6% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.03 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 119% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 121 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,451 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,478 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $29 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.21 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 6, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 6% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 31.8% from 2016 to 2026. 49% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). 32 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 76,905 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $11.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2,353 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $18.68 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.00, 2016 to 2025. 5.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 47.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.4%.

Quick answers for Glacier County

What is cash rent in Glacier County, Montana?

The county average is $29 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $74 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Glacier County, Montana?

Land and buildings were worth $1,478 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
