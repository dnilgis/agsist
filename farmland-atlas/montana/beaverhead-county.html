Cash rent in Beaverhead County runs $19 an acre in 2026, the lowest of 40 in Montana and up 23.3% from $15 in 2016. Irrigated ground tells another story at $121 an acre in 2026, 8th highest of 29. The census value of land and buildings reached $1,883 an acre in 2022, up 43.0% from $1,317 in 2017 and 13th highest of 53. Only 16% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
