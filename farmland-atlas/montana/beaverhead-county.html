Beaverhead County, Montana

Rain is at 102% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,883 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 18.18 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 102% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 92 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,361 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,883 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 23.3% from 2016 to 2026. 16% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 113.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 200 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 31,921 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.30 per base acre, PLC $13.89. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (76.96 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $12.10 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.02 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.66, 2016 to 2025. 90.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 2.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 28.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.4%.

Cash rent in Beaverhead County runs $19 an acre in 2026, the lowest of 40 in Montana and up 23.3% from $15 in 2016. Irrigated ground tells another story at $121 an acre in 2026, 8th highest of 29.
