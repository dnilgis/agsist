Powder River County, Montana

Non-irrigated cash rent in Powder River County runs $18 per acre. Land value and buildings were estimated at $957 per acre in 2022, up 73.7 percent from 2017, or 11.7 percent a year. Heat and drought account for 49 percent of crop insurance indemnities across 1989-2025, and the share has moved higher over time: 30 percent in 1989-1999, 28 percent in 2000-2009, 68 percent in 2010-2019, and 89 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 354 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 72 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. July nightly lows have warmed 0.53 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 57.6 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 56.3 F.
