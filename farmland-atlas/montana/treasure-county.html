Treasure County, Montana

Treasure County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $8 an acre (2025); land and buildings $996 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.67 since 1989. Cropland rent in Treasure County was $8 an acre in 2025, down 61.5% from $21 in 2014. Irrigated ground runs a lot higher at $131 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 29 in Montana against a state median of $98. The census value of land and buildings was $996 an acre in 2022, up 72.3% from $578 in 2017, and that still sits 11th lowest of 53 in the state.
