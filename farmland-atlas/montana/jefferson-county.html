Cash rent in Jefferson County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 87.5% from $12 in 2016. That still sits 6th lowest of 40 in Montana, under the state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings was $1,954 an acre in 2022, up 14.8% from $1,702 in 2017 and 10th highest of 53 in the state. Only 15% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
