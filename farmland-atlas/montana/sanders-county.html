Cropland rent in Sanders County runs $22 an acre for 2026, tied for 3rd lowest of 40 in Montana and under the state median of $31. That is up 4.9% from $21 in 2016, which is not much movement for ten years. Irrigated ground rents for $62 in 2026, 3rd lowest of 29. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,180 an acre in 2022, up 19.4% from $988 in 2017 and about where it sat at $1,187 in 2012.
