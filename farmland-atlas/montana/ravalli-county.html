Ravalli County, Montana

Rain is at 106% of normal. Land and buildings run $9,096 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.39 per base acre for corn.

Ravalli County vs Montana: $35.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 74% of the other 39 Montana counties with a 2026 rent (tied for 10th highest of 40).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

22.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 25.66 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 106% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 96 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,519 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $9,096 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $35.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.23 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 3, 2026 to Sep 6, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 22% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.3% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 101.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 317 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.39 per base acre, PLC $2.12.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (84.36 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $38.03 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $18.64 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (20 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $10.08 per base acre, PLC $8.08. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (44.75 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $15.60 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

89.4% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.2 days a year and damaging wind on 0.7, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 82.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2015; too much rain caused 0%.

Cash rent in Ravalli County ran $36 an acre in 2026, tied for 10th highest of 40 in Montana, against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came in at $9,096 an acre in 2022, 2nd highest of 53 in the state, where the median is $1,237. That was up 58.6% from $5,734 in 2017, or 9.7% a year. In 2022, 22% of the farmland here was rented.
