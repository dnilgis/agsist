Cash rent in Ravalli County ran $36 an acre in 2026, tied for 10th highest of 40 in Montana, against a state median of $31. The census value of land and buildings came in at $9,096 an acre in 2022, 2nd highest of 53 in the state, where the median is $1,237. That was up 58.6% from $5,734 in 2017, or 9.7% a year. In 2022, 22% of the farmland here was rented.
