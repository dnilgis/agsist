Ravalli County, Montana

Ravalli County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $36 an acre (2026); land and buildings $9,096 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30081. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Land value sits at $9,096 per acre, up 58.6 percent from 2017-2022, 9.7 percent a year. July nights average 47.4 F over 2017-2026, up from the 45.8 F normal for 1991-2020, with a warming trend of 0.75 F per decade since 1976. Heat and drought drove 83 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2015, and the county logged 97 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 296 weeks total from 2000-2025.
