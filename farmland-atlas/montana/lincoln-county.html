Lincoln County, Montana

Lincoln County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $25 an acre (2020); land and buildings $4,690 an acre in the 2022 census, flagged to read with care. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30053. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Lincoln County ran $25 per acre in 2020. Irrigation covers 71 percent of harvested cropland as of the 2022 census, with groundwater supplying 2 percent of that water in 2015. July nights have warmed, from 48.2 F in the 1991-2020 normal to 50.1 F in 2017-2026, a trend of 0.8 F per decade since 1976. Drought weeks ran 210 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 46 in 2021-2025 and the worst year, 2001, at 42 weeks.
