Broadwater County, Montana

Land and buildings run $1,894 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 140 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,811 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,894 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $24 an acre (USDA, 2024). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.20 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 5, 2026 to Sep 7, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright, 13% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 29% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains under 0.1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 35 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 7,385 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $10.30 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 149 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $6.78 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.91, 2016 to 2025. 40.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.6 days a year and damaging wind on 1.6, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 67.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 1.9%.

Irrigated cash rent in Broadwater County was $215 an acre in 2026, the highest of 29 in Montana, against a state median of $98. Dryland rent ran $24 an acre in 2024, down 2.0% from $25 in 2013, and pasture went for $14 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings reached $1,894 an acre in 2022, up 61.9% from $1,170 in 2017, and sits 12th highest of 53 in the state.
