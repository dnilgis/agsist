Broadwater County, Montana

Irrigated cash rent in Broadwater County was $215 an acre in 2026, the highest of 29 in Montana, against a state median of $98. Dryland rent ran $24 an acre in 2024, down 2.0% from $25 in 2013, and pasture went for $14 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings reached $1,894 an acre in 2022, up 61.9% from $1,170 in 2017, and sits 12th highest of 53 in the state.
