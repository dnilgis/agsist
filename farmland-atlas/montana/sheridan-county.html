Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 30.8% from $33 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Sheridan County runs $43 an acre for 2026, tied for 3rd highest of 40 in Montana, against a state median of $31. That is up 30.8% from $33 in 2016. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,554 an acre in 2022, up 123.9% from $694 in 2017. In 2022, 50% of the farmland was rented.
