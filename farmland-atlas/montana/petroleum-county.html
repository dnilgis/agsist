Petroleum County, Montana

Rain is at 103% of normal. Land and buildings run $1,059 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.78 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 13.8 inches, 103% of normal (13.39 inches) through Nov 10, at Flatwillow, 4 mi ENE. Frost-free season: 146 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,345 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,059 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 25% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 48.9 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.78 per base acre, PLC $0.94.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (37.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $10.30 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $20.97 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.94, 2016 to 2025. 20% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 1.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 50.1% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 3%.

Cash rent in Petroleum County ran $18 an acre in 2025, down 5.3% from $19 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came to $1,059 an acre in 2022, up 56.2% from $678 in 2017. That still sits under the state median of $1,237, and the county ranks 15th lowest of 53.
