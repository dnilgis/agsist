Non-irrigated cash rent in Powell County runs $28 per acre, irrigated land at $80 per acre for 2026. Cropland here leans on water: 96 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, all of it from surface sources, none from groundwater. The July nightly low has moved from a 1991-2020 normal of 46.1 F to 47.8 F over 2017-2026, a warming trend of 0.76 F per decade since 1976, though no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought pressure has been present: 130 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 356 of 1357 weeks (26.2 percent) for 2000-2025, with 2024 the worst year at 50 weeks. Land value rose 37.7 percent from 2017-2022.
