Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

5.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain: no station here reported nearly every day this year, so we show no rain figure. Frost-free season: 110 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,482 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,783 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $28 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.24 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 3, 2026 to Sep 15, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 19% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 71.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 3,205 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $15.60 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

96.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 0.5, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 23.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2018; too much rain caused 2.1%.

Quick answers for Powell County

What is cash rent in Powell County, Montana?

The county average is $28 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $80 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Powell County, Montana?

Land and buildings were worth $1,783 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
