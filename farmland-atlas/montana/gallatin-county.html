Irrigated rent in Gallatin County runs $173 an acre for 2026, 2nd highest of 29 in Montana, against a state median of $98. Cropland rent sits at $39 for 2026, up 2.6% from $38 in 2016, so it has barely moved in ten years. The census value of land and buildings was $4,069 an acre in 2022, up 34.3% from $3,030 in 2017 and 4th highest of 53 in the state.
