Gallatin County, Montana

Gallatin County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $39 an acre (2026); land and buildings $4,069 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30031. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Gallatin County runs $173 per acre in 2026, against $39 per acre for non-irrigated ground. The irrigated multiple has moved from 2.51 in 2016 to 4.44 in 2026, and the direction is rising. Forty-four percent of harvested cropland was irrigated as of the 2022 census, but only 1 percent of that water came from groundwater in 2015, which ties most irrigation to surface supply. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities was 27 percent in 1989-1999, 38 percent in 2000-2009, 19 percent in 2010-2019, and 56 percent in 2020-2025. Census land value sits at $4,069 per acre in 2022, up 34.3 percent from 2017.
