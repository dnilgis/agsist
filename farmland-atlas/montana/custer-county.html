Custer County, Montana

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,017 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $12.38 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

74.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 9.51 inches, 66% of normal (14.49 inches) through Nov 16, at Powderville, 8 mi NNE. Frost-free season: 142 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,439 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,017 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $18 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 33% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 41 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 17,880 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $12.38 per base acre, PLC $3.25.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (129.3 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Soybeans, 2026: ARC-CO expected $0.00 per base acre, PLC $0.00. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (21 bushels) and past years' price swings around $12.10 soybeans (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $4.37 per base acre, PLC $5.02. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (27.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $8.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $10.56 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.03, 2016 to 2025. 28.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.7 days a year and damaging wind on 4.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 32.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 4.4%.

Cash rent in Custer County was $18 an acre in 2023, up 24.1% from $15 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,017 an acre in 2022, up 89.7% from $536 in 2017. That puts the county 12th lowest of 53 against a state median of $1,237. Irrigated ground rented for $103 an acre in 2026, tied for 11th highest of 29.
