Cash rent in Custer County was $18 an acre in 2023, up 24.1% from $15 in 2013. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,017 an acre in 2022, up 89.7% from $536 in 2017. That puts the county 12th lowest of 53 against a state median of $1,237. Irrigated ground rented for $103 an acre in 2026, tied for 11th highest of 29.
