McCone County, Montana

McCone County, MT: non-irrigated cash rent $27 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,055 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 30055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in McCone County runs $27 per acre, up 20 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land and buildings carry a census value of $1,055 per acre, with a land value change of 99.8 percent from 2017 to 2022. Heat and drought account for 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities paid from 1989-2025, closed crop years, and that share was 14 percent in 1989-1999, 51 percent in 2000-2009, 43 percent in 2010-2019, and 69 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 201 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 119 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025. July nights have warmed from a 55.1 F normal to a 57.2 F average over 2017-2026, a trend of 0.67 F per decade since 1976.
