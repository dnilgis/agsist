$34

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 16 surveys since 2009; up 23.6% from $28 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cash rent in Judith Basin County runs $34 an acre for 2026, up 23.6% from $28 in 2016. That sits a bit over the state median of $31 and ranks 13th highest of 40. The census value of land and buildings was $1,350 an acre in 2022, up 45.3% from $929 in 2017, or 7.8% a year. Renters work 38% of the farmland here.
