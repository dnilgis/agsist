$31

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 14 surveys since 2009; up 32.6% from $23 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

Cropland rent in Phillips County runs $31 an acre in 2026, up 32.6% from $23 in 2016 and right on the state median, tied for 19th highest of 40. Irrigated ground rents for $66 an acre in 2026, 5th lowest of 29 in Montana. The census value of land and buildings was $1,124 an acre in 2022, up 112.1% from $530 in 2017. 32% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
