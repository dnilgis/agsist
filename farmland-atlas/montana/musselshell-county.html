Cash rent in Musselshell County runs $23 an acre for 2026, up 64.3% from $14 in 2016. That sits under the state median of $31 and ranks 7th lowest of 40. The census value of land and buildings was $1,077 an acre in 2022, up 68.5% from $639 in 2017, or 11.0% a year. 27% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
