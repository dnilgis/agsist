Sullivan County, New Hampshire

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $5,219 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $11.05 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

16.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 32.73 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 149 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,133 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $5,219 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers only the New Hampshire towns along the Connecticut River (CT River Valley region), not whole counties. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $80 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 15% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 4% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 17% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 109.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 4 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,754 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $11.05 per base acre, PLC $3.26.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (129.81 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $78.12 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $28.69 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $41.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

1.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.9 days a year and damaging wind on 2.8, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 0.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1999 to 2025; too much rain caused 39.2%.

Cash rent in Sullivan County runs $80 an acre for 2025. The census value of land and buildings was $5,219 an acre in 2022, up 8.9% from $4,792 in 2017, or 1.7% a year. That puts the county 3rd lowest of 10 in the state, under the state median of $6,630. Only 14% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
