Cash rent in Coos County runs $41 an acre for 2026, lowest of 3 in New Hampshire, against a state median of $66. The census value of land and buildings came to $4,071 an acre in 2022, up 58.8% from $2,564 in 2017. That still ranks lowest of 10 against a state median of $6,630. Only 14% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
