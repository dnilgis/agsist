Strafford County, New Hampshire

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $10,359 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.21 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.22 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 165 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,336 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,359 an acre in the 2022 census.

The ASFMRA Northeast report does not place this county: the report covers only the New Hampshire towns along the Connecticut River (CT River Valley region), not whole counties. 2026 US Northeast Land Value Trends, Northeast Chapter ASFMRA with Cornell Cooperative Extension, 2025 sales, published 2026. Cash rent, non-irrigated: $40 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.53 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 8% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 1% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 9% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 4% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 56.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 20 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,735 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.21 per base acre, PLC $1.97.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (78.68 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 24, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $62.14 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $17.39 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. PRF grazing county base value: $41.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

3.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.7 days a year and damaging wind on 2.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 13.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1998 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.8%.

Quick answers for Strafford County

What is cash rent in Strafford County, New Hampshire?

The county average is $40 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2023. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Strafford County, New Hampshire?

Land and buildings were worth $10,359 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
