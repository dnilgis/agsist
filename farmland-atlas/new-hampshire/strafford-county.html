Non-irrigated cash rent averages $40 per acre in Strafford County. Land value in the census stands at $10,359 per acre, up 59.5 percent from 2017-2022, or 9.8 percent a year. July nightly lows have warmed 0.9 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 60.8 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 59.1 F, though no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or higher at night. Crop insurance indemnities from 1998-2025 total $351,360, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 58 percent, while heat and drought account for 7 percent of indemnities from 2020-2025. Severe drought or worse covered 97 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 18 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025.
