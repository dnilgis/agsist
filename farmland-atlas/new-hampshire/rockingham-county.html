Non-irrigated cash rent in Rockingham County is $122 per acre, against a land value of $17,929 per acre in 2022. Land value rose 58.9 percent from 2017-2022, or 9.7 percent a year. July nightly lows have moved from a 60.1 F normal to 61.9 F over 2017-2026, with a warming trend of 0.92 F per decade since 1976, and no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above at night. Drought weeks in severe drought or worse ran 87 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 13 weeks in 2021-2025.
