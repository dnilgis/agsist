Non-irrigated cash rent in Cheshire County runs $71 per acre. Land value under the operators' own estimate stands at $7,375 per acre, up 94.5 percent over 2017-2022. July nightly lows have warmed 0.9 F per decade since 1976, and the 2017-2026 average of 59.5 F sits above the 1991-2020 normal of 57.5 F. Drought weeks were concentrated early, with 15 of the period's worst year, 2002; 2021-2025 logged 19 weeks in severe drought or worse.
