Non-irrigated cash rent in Hillsborough County runs $69 per acre. Land value in the 2022 census stood at $10,594 per acre, up 36.3 percent from 2017, or 6.4 percent a year. The July nightly low averaged 61 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 59 F, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.92 F per decade. Crop insurance indemnities from 1998-2025 totaled $2.8 million, with freeze and frost the largest cause at 50 percent; heat and drought's share of indemnities was 0 percent in 1989-1999, 0 percent in 2000-2009, 23 percent in 2010-2019, and 10 percent in 2020-2025. The loss ratio was 0.81 over 1995-2025 and 1.16 over 2016-2025.
