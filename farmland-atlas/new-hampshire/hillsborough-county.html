Hillsborough County, New Hampshire

Rain is at 88% of normal. Land and buildings run $10,594 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.69 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: no drought level covers half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 36.36 inches, 88% of normal (41.18 inches) through Nov 18, at East Milford. Frost-free season: 165 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,386 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,594 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $69 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 17% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 10% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 42.5 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 703 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.69 per base acre, PLC $2.98.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (118.7 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $41.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $13.90 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.16, 2016 to 2025. 10.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 1.9 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 8.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1998 to 2025; too much rain caused 6.8%.

Cash rent in Hillsborough County runs $69 an acre for 2025. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,594 an acre in 2022, up 36.3% from $7,775 in 2017, or 6.4% a year. That ranks 2nd highest of 10 in New Hampshire, well over the state median of $6,630. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
