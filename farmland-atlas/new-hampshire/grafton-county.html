Cash rent in Grafton County runs $58 an acre in 2025, up 36.9% from $42 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $4,312 an acre in 2022, up 10.4% from $3,907 in 2017, or 2.0% a year. That sits under the state median of $6,630 and ranks 2nd lowest of 10. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
