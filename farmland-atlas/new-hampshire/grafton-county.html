Grafton County, New Hampshire

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,312 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $9.96 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

12.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 34.1 inches, 84% of normal (40.77 inches) through Nov 17, at Bristol, 0.4 mi SSE. Frost-free season: 138 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,969 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,312 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $58 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 1% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 7% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 60.3 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 5,122 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $9.96 per base acre, PLC $3.35.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (133.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $41.10 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

0.5% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 2.1 days a year and damaging wind on 5.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 38.3% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2025; too much rain caused 29.9%.

Cash rent in Grafton County runs $58 an acre in 2025, up 36.9% from $42 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $4,312 an acre in 2022, up 10.4% from $3,907 in 2017, or 2.0% a year. That sits under the state median of $6,630 and ranks 2nd lowest of 10. Only 10% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
