Grafton County, New Hampshire

Grafton County, NH: non-irrigated cash rent $58 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,312 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 33009. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Grafton County runs $58 per acre in 2025, up 36.9 percent from 2014. Land is valued at $4,312 per acre in the 2022 census, with that value up 10.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 2 percent a year. Crop insurance indemnities since 1996 total $994,270, with heat and drought the largest cause at 38 percent; that share moved 46 percent in 2000-2009, 7 percent in 2010-2019, and 58 percent in 2020-2025. Drought weeks were concentrated recently: 18 of the 38 weeks of severe drought or worse since 2000 fell in 2021-2025. July nights average 55.9 F over 2017-2026, against a 54.2 F normal for 1991-2020, warming at 0.82 F per decade since 1976.
