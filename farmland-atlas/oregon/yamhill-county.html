Cropland rent in Yamhill County runs $166 an acre for 2026, up 35.0% from $123 in 2016 and 2nd highest of 15 in Oregon, where the median sits at $100. The census value of land and buildings came to $12,747 an acre in 2022, up 25.2% from $10,178 in 2017, or 4.6% a year. That ranks 10th highest of 36 against a state median of $4,773. 38% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
