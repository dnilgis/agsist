Cropland in Clatsop County rented for $83 an acre in 2025, and pasture went for $48 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $9,702 an acre in 2022, up 35.6% from $7,153 in 2017, or 6.3% a year. That sits well above the state median of $4,773 and ranks 16th highest of 36 in Oregon. 30% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
