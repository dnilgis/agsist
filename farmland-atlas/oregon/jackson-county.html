Jackson County, Oregon

Pasture in Jackson County rents for $48 an acre in 2026, and irrigated ground goes for $152 in 2026. That irrigated rent sits under the state median of $210 and ranks tied for 6th lowest of 23. The census value of land and buildings ran $12,421 an acre in 2022, up 46.2% from $8,494 in 2017 and well past the state median of $4,773, good for 11th highest of 36.
