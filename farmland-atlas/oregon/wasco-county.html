Wasco County, Oregon

Wasco County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $48 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,809 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.46 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41065. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Wasco County runs $358 per acre in 2025, against $48 per acre for non-irrigated ground in 2026. That gap has held without a clear direction, at 7.78 in 2025 versus 8.33 in 2016. Heat and drought drove 56 percent of crop insurance indemnities from 1989-2025, with the share moving unevenly across periods: 63 percent in 1989-1999, 66 percent in 2000-2009, 35 percent in 2010-2019, 58 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed, averaging 54.4 F over 2017-2026 against a 52.3 F normal for 1991-2020, with a 0.85 F per decade trend since 1976. The county spent 245 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 84 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone.
