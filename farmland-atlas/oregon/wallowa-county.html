Wallowa County, Oregon

Wallowa County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $52 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,654 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41063. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Wallowa County runs $52 per acre in 2026, up 21.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated land rents for $109 per acre, and the irrigated multiple over non-irrigated rent has moved from 1.82 in 2016 to 2.12 in 2026, a rising gap between the two. Land and buildings carry a census value of $1,654 per acre, with a 1.3 percent change from 2017 to 2022. July nights average 50.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 49.1 F normal for 1991-2020, and the warming trend since 1976 runs 0.87 F per decade.
