Gilliam County, Oregon

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,338 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

40.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 10, 2026. Rain this year: 6.02 inches, 85% of normal (7.09 inches) through Nov 17, at Arlington. Frost-free season: 172 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,355 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,338 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $55 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Cash rent rose 64.2% from 2016 to 2026. 26% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 18.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 41 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 97,208 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.77 per base acre, PLC $2.81.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (111.8 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $5.70 per base acre, PLC $7.31. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (40.5 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $25.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 2,159 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $12.77 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.06, 2016 to 2025. 4.6% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 78.4% of crop insurance claims paid from 1989 to 2025; too much rain caused 0.8%.

Cropland rent in Gilliam County runs $55 an acre for 2026, up 64.2% from $34 in 2016. That sits 5th lowest of 15 in Oregon, where the state median is $100. The census value of land and buildings was $1,338 an acre in 2022, up 85.8% from $720 in 2017, and still 3rd lowest of 36 against a state median of $4,773. 26% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
