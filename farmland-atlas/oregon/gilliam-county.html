Gilliam County, Oregon

Gilliam County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $55 an acre (2026); land and buildings $1,338 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41021. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent here runs $55 per acre, up 64.2 percent from 2016 to 2026. Land value stood at $1,338 per acre in 2022, up 85.8 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 13.2 percent a year. Heat and drought drove 78 percent of $93.0 million in crop insurance indemnities across closed crop years 1989-2025, and that share has stayed high period to period: 51 percent in 1989-1999, 84 percent in 2000-2009, 71 percent in 2010-2019, 79 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 239 of 1357 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2000-2025, with 70 of those weeks falling in 2021-2025 alone. Cropland here is 5 percent irrigated, and 76 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater as of 2015.
