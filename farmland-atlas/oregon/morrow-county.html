Morrow County, Oregon

Morrow County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $50 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,202 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41049. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Irrigated cash rent in Morrow County ran $528 per acre in 2025, while non-irrigated rent sat at $50 per acre in 2026, up 51.5 percent from 2016. Land and buildings value reached $2,202 per acre in 2022, after a 95.4 percent rise from 2017 to 2022. July nightly lows averaged 55.9 F over 2017-2026 against a 54.3 F normal, warming at 0.88 F per decade since 1976.
