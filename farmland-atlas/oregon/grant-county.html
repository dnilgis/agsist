As of Oct 16, the census value of land and buildings in Grant County was $1,324 an acre in 2022, 2nd lowest of 36 in Oregon against a state median of $4,773. That was up 27.9% from $1,035 in 2017, or 5.0% a year. Cash rent ran $25 an acre in 2021, $95 for irrigated ground in 2025 and $10 for pasture in 2026. In 2022, 29% of the farmland was rented.
