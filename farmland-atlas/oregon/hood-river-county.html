Hood River County, Oregon

Irrigated cropland in Hood River County rents for $759 an acre in 2026, highest of 23 in Oregon, where the state median is $210. The census value of land and buildings was $19,141 an acre in 2022, 5th highest of 36. That is up 37.4% from $13,926 in 2017, or 6.6% a year, and just above the $19,000 of 2012. Only 17% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
