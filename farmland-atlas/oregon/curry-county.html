Curry County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $40 an acre (2022); land and buildings $5,104 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 1.13 since 2009. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41015. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cash rent in Curry County runs $225 per acre, against $40 per acre for non-irrigated ground, and 75 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated. Land value sits at $5,104 per acre, with a change of 44.1 percent from 2017-2022, or 7.6 percent a year. The July nightly low has warmed 0.57 F per decade since 1976, reaching 54 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 52.9 F, and no July since 1900 has averaged 70 F or above. Drought weeks ran 178 of 1357 from 2000-2025, with 31 in 2021-2025.
