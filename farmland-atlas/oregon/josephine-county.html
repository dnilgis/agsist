Josephine County, Oregon

Josephine County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $108 an acre (2025); land and buildings $20,397 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41033. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Josephine County runs $108 per acre, irrigated $132 per acre. Irrigation covers 83 percent of harvested cropland, and groundwater supplies 11 percent of that water. July nights average 55 F over 2017-2026, up from the 53.2 F normal for 1991-2020, and the long trend runs 0.88 F per decade, warming. Heat and drought accounted for 0 percent of indemnities in 2000-2009, 0 percent in 2010-2019, and 23 percent in 2020-2025. The county logged 48 weeks of severe drought or worse in 2021-2025, against 232 weeks total since 2000. Land value rose 13.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 2.5 percent a year, reaching $20,397 per acre by the operators' own estimate.
