Josephine County, Oregon

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $20,397 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

90.2% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 31.12 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 189 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,174 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $20,397 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $108 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.62 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 11, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 2% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 12% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 0.8% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 11% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 201.6 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 43,721 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: FSA lists no contract expirations for this county.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $11.41 per $100 of coverage a year, 2018 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 2.25, 2018 to 2025. 82.9% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.2 days a year and damaging wind on 0.2, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 16.5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1999 to 2025; too much rain caused 0.1%.

Cash rent in Josephine County runs $108 an acre for 2025, with irrigated ground at $132 and pasture at $104. The census value of land and buildings was $20,397 an acre in 2022, up 13.4% from $17,992 in 2017, or 2.5% a year. That puts the county 4th highest of 36 in Oregon, well above the state median of $4,773. Only 12% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
