Lake County, Oregon

Lake County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $33 an acre (2026); land and buildings $2,108 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41037. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Irrigated cropland here rents for $164 per acre, non-irrigated for $33 per acre. That gap reflects water: 85 percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, and 27 percent of irrigation water comes from groundwater. Land value, per the operators' own estimate, rose 95 percent from 2017-2022, or 14.3 percent a year. July nights have warmed, averaging 50.3 F in 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 48.1 F.
