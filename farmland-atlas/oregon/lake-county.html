Cash rent in Lake County was $33 an acre in 2026, lowest of 15 in Oregon, where the state median is $100. Irrigated ground rented for $164 an acre in 2026 and pasture for $15. The census value of land and buildings was $2,108 an acre in 2022, up 95.0% from $1,081 in 2017. Only 28% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
