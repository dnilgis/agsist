Marion County, Oregon

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $17,033 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

99.4% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 33.91 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 64% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 204 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,829 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $17,033 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $181 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.71 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 9, 2026). 34% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 16% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 32.1% from 2016 to 2026. 44% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 41% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 6% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 100 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 1,536 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

PRF grazing county base value: $50.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 4 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $0.88 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.60, 2016 to 2025. 47.1% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 22.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 1996 to 2025; too much rain caused 11.3%.

Quick answers for Marion County

What is cash rent in Marion County, Oregon?

The county average is $181 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $322 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Marion County, Oregon?

Land and buildings were worth $17,033 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
