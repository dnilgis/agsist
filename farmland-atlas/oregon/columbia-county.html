Columbia County, Oregon

Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $10,651 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.77 per base acre for corn.

Columbia County vs Oregon: $99.50 an acre cash rent in 2026, higher than 50% of the other 14 Oregon counties with a 2026 rent (8th lowest of 15).

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.62 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 66% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 218 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,879 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,651 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $99.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.64 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 2% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 42.1% from 2016 to 2026. 21% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 9% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 1% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 126.8 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 24,913 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $50.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Quick answers for Columbia County

What is cash rent in Columbia County, Oregon?

The county average is $100 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Columbia County, Oregon?

Land and buildings were worth $10,651 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
