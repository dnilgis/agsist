Irrigated cropland in Klamath County rents for $258 an acre in 2026. That ranks 8th highest of 23 in Oregon, above the state median of $210. The census value of land and buildings was $2,623 an acre in 2022, up 19.8% from $2,189 in 2017, or 3.7% a year. Even so, it sits 15th lowest of 36 against a state median of $4,773, and 35% of the county's farmland was rented in 2022.
