Sherman County, Oregon

Sherman County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $49 an acre (2010); land and buildings $1,410 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41055. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

July nights average 55.6 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 54.6 F, with warming since 1976 at 0.66 F per decade. Irrigation covers 1 percent of harvested cropland, and 79 percent of irrigation water came from groundwater in 2015. Wind operating capacity is 1256.5 MW, solar operating is 10 MW, and 925 MW of solar is proposed to EIA. Land value rose 65.9 percent from 2017-2022, 10.7 percent a year.
