As of Oct 16, the last cash rent on record for Sherman County is $49 an acre, from 2010. The census value of land and buildings came in at $1,410 an acre in 2022, up 65.9% from $850 in 2017, or 10.7% a year. That still sits well under the state median of $4,773 and ranks 5th lowest of 36 in Oregon. 49% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
