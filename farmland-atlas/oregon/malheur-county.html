Irrigated cropland in Malheur County rents for $276 an acre in 2026, 6th highest of 23 in Oregon against a state median of $210. Pasture goes for $12 an acre in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $1,851 an acre in 2022, up 24.4% from $1,488 in 2017, and that sits 10th lowest of 36 against a state median of $4,773. 20% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
