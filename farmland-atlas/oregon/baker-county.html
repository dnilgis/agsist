Baker County, Oregon

Baker County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $76 an acre (2024); land and buildings $1,942 an acre in the 2022 census; crop insurance loss ratio 0.89 since 1989. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41001. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Land value has risen 55.4 percent from 2017 to 2022, 9.2 percent a year. Ninety-one percent of harvested cropland is irrigated, with 19 percent of irrigation water drawn from groundwater as of 2015. July nights have warmed to an average low of 54.4 F over 2017-2026, against a 1991-2020 normal of 51.9 F, a warming trend of 1 F per decade since 1976.
