Farmland in Baker County rented for $76 an acre in 2024, up 24.8% from $61 in 2012. Irrigated rent was $118 an acre in 2026, 3rd lowest of 23 in Oregon, against a state median of $210. The census value of land and buildings was $1,942 an acre in 2022, up 55.4% from $1,250 in 2017, and that sits 11th lowest of 36.
