Harney County, Oregon

Harney County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $26 an acre (2024); land and buildings $1,268 an acre in the 2022 census. Bids, weather, crops, land and programs for one county. Irrigated cropland in Harney County rents for $78 an acre in 2026, which ranks lowest of 23 against a state median of $210. The census value of land and buildings was $1,268 an acre in 2022, up 85.9% from $682 in 2017, and it ranks lowest of 36. Pasture rents for $9 an acre in 2026, and 18% of farmland was rented in 2022.
