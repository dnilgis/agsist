As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Clackamas County runs $148 an acre for 2026, 4th highest of 15 in Oregon and up 49.5% from $99 in 2016. Irrigated ground rents for $358 an acre in 2026, 3rd highest of 23. The census value of land and buildings came to $29,522 an acre in 2022, highest of 36 in the state and well over the state median of $4,773. That value is up 37.2% from 2017, or 6.5% a year.
