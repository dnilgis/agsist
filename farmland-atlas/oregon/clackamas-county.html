Non-irrigated cash rent in Clackamas County averages $148 per acre in 2026, up 49.5 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $358 per acre, a multiple of 2.42 over non-irrigated rent in 2026, compared with 2.85 in 2008, with no clear direction in that multiple. Land value under the census stands at $29,522 per acre, up 37.2 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 6.5 percent a year. The loss ratio over 2016-2025 was 0.77, above the 1995-2025 ratio of 0.2, and heat and drought's share of indemnities rose from 21 percent in 2010-2019 to 33 percent in 2020-2025.
