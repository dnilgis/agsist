Cropland rent in Linn County runs $150 an acre in 2026, up 53.8% from $98 in 2016. That sits 3rd highest of 15 in Oregon, against a state median of $100. The census value of land and buildings came in at $10,406 an acre in 2022, up 46.7% from $7,092 in 2017, or 8.0% a year. 47% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
