Linn County, Oregon

Linn County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $150 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,406 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41043. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Linn County runs $150 per acre in 2026, up 53.8 percent from 2016. Land and buildings were valued at $10,406 per acre in 2022, after a 46.7 percent gain from 2017 to 2022, 8 percent a year. July nights average 51.8 F over 2017-2026 against a 50.6 F normal for 1991-2020, with warming of 0.56 F per decade since 1976.
