Cropland rent in Lane County runs $89 an acre in 2026, up 17.2% from $76 in 2016 and a bit under the state median of $100. The census value of land and buildings came in at $12,788 an acre in 2022, up 49.5% from 2017 and 9th highest of 36 in Oregon. 37% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
