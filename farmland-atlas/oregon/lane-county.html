Non-irrigated cash rent in Lane County runs $88 per acre in 2026, up 17.2 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent is $165 per acre, a multiple of 1.86 times the non-irrigated rate in 2026, against 2.34 in 2008, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Census land and buildings value is $12,788 per acre, up 49.5 percent from 2017 to 2022, or 8.4 percent a year. Drought has touched the county in 195 of 1357 weeks from 2000-2025, with 34 weeks in the worst year, 2021, and heat and drought account for 0 percent of indemnities in 2010-2019 and 1 percent in 2020-2025. July nights have warmed 0.53 F per decade since 1976.
