Lane County, Oregon

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $12,788 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

88.7% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: 23.49 inches, 65% of normal (35.89 inches) through Nov 17, at Lookout Point Dam. Frost-free season: 203 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,816 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $12,788 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $88.50 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. USDA cash rent rose 17.2% from 2016 to 2026. 37% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 126.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 58,639 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included. The 2023 filings showed 6,112 acres, so read the change with care.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.77 per base acre, PLC $3.65.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (145.6 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 16, 2026. PRF grazing county base value: $50.90 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 14 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $5.96 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 1.20, 2016 to 2025. 19.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.1 days a year and damaging wind on 0.4, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 0.7% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 8.4%.
