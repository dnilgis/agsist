Cropland rent in Washington County runs $135 an acre for 2026, up 6.3% from $127 in 2016 and 6th highest of 15 in Oregon. The census value of land and buildings came to $22,445 an acre in 2022, up 31.3% from $17,099 in 2017 and 3rd highest of 36 in the state. The state median sits at $4,773. 38% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
