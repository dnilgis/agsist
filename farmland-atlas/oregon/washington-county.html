Abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $22,445 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

0% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Land

Land and buildings: $22,445 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $135 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.80 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 18% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 14% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 6.3% from 2016 to 2026. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 50% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 44.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 4,919 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Quick answers for Washington County

What is cash rent in Washington County, Oregon?

The county average is $135 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $251 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Washington County, Oregon?

Land and buildings were worth $22,445 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
