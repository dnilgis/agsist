Polk County, Oregon

Polk County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $146 an acre (2026); land and buildings $10,255 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41053. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Polk County runs $146 per acre in 2026, up 43.1 percent from 2016. Irrigated rent stands at $236 per acre, a multiple of 1.62 over non-irrigated, against 1.20 in 2009, with no clear direction in that multiple over time. Census land and buildings value reached $10,255 per acre in 2022, up 44.1 percent from 2017, 7.6 percent a year. Heat and drought's share of crop insurance indemnities moved from 1 percent in 2000-2009 to 14 percent in 2010-2019 to 46 percent in 2020-2025, while freeze and frost remains the largest single cause over 2000-2025 at 30 percent.
