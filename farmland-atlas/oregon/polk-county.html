Polk County, Oregon

Moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $10,255 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census). ARC-CO is expected to pay $14.77 per base acre for corn.

Weather

Drought now: moderate drought (D1) covers at least half the county.

78% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 42.33 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 239 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,026 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $10,255 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $146 an acre (USDA, 2026). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.72 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). 18% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 5% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. USDA cash rent rose 43.1% from 2016 to 2026. 38% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 31% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 3% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 79.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 3.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 28,498 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Program

Corn, 2026: ARC-CO expected $14.77 per base acre, PLC $3.49.

This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (139.08 bushels) and past years' price swings around $4.60 corn (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Figures of Nov 25, 2026. Corn, 2025 crop: ARC-CO $0.00 per base acre (FSA posted rate), PLC $30.74 (from FSA's posted prices), at the county-average PLC yield; for 2025 the farm gets the higher one, before the sequestration cut. Wheat, 2026: ARC-CO expected $15.08 per base acre, PLC $13.81. This assumes a normal crop, the county-average PLC yield (76.51 bushels) and past years' price swings around $6.20 wheat (USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026). Price range from how far USDA's November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. PRF grazing county base value: $50.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.40 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.47, 2016 to 2025. 23.8% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 27.6% of crop insurance claims paid from 2000 to 2025; too much rain caused 10.5%.

Quick answers for Polk County

What is cash rent in Polk County, Oregon?

The county average is $146 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2026 and $236 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2026. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Polk County, Oregon?

Land and buildings were worth $10,255 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
