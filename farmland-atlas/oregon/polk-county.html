$146

Non-irrigated cash rent, 2026: the highest of 17 surveys since 2008; up 43% from $102 in 2016.

USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, 2026

As of Oct 16, cropland rent in Polk County runs $146 an acre in 2026, up 43.1% from $102 in 2016 and 5th highest of 15 against a state median of $100. The census value of land and buildings came to $10,255 an acre in 2022, up 44.1% from $7,116 in 2017, or 7.6% a year. That ranks 14th highest of 36 in Oregon. 38% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.
