Cash rent in Tillamook County runs $170 an acre for 2025, up 45.3% from $117 in 2014. The census value of land and buildings was $10,065 an acre in 2022, up 29.1% from $7,796 in 2017. That ranks 15th highest of 36 in Oregon, against a state median of $4,773. Only 18% of the county's farmland was rented in 2022.
