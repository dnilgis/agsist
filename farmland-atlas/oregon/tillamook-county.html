Tillamook County, Oregon

Tillamook County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $170 an acre (2025); land and buildings $10,065 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41057. Figures as of 2026-09-20.

Open on the map Compare Printable sheet

Non-irrigated cash rent in Tillamook County is $170 per acre in 2025, up 45.3 percent from 2014. July nights average 51.6 F for 2017-2026 against a 1991-2020 normal of 51 F, with a warming trend of 0.52 F per decade since 1976. The county saw 29 weeks in severe drought or worse from 2021-2025, against 63 weeks total from 2000-2025.

Sources, dates and arithmetic
