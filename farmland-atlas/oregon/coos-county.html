Coos County, Oregon

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

42.1% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 41.99 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 80% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the last spring freeze comes around Mar 22 in the median year; most years have no fall freeze before December. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,831 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,016 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $94 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.44 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey Sep 8, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 20% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 6% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 182.1 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 110,474 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 8 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $2.17 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.79, 2016 to 2025. 62.2% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 5% of crop insurance claims paid from 1995 to 2025; too much rain caused 2.6%.

Quick answers for Coos County

What is cash rent in Coos County, Oregon?

The county average is $94 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $102 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2024. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Coos County, Oregon?

Land and buildings were worth $4,016 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
