Lincoln County, Oregon

Weather

Drought now: abnormally dry (D0) covers at least half the county.

12.3% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 56.76 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 78% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost: the season runs past the March to November window in half the years or more, so no length is shown. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,660 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $7,580 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $46.50 an acre (USDA, 2023). We read recorded sales in Florida, Illinois, Indiana, New Jersey, Vermont and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.58 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 8, 2026 to Sep 9, 2026). None of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland outright. 10% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drainage: NASS withholds this county's figure (D). Cover crops go on 0.5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 96.2 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 42,765 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $50.90 an acre, crop year 2027. No CRP contracts here end in fiscal year 2027.

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

Reported hail an inch or larger on 0 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live.

Cash rent in Lincoln County stood at $47 an acre in 2023. The census value of land and buildings was $7,580 an acre in 2022, up 37.9% from $5,498 in 2017, or 6.6% a year. That ranks 17th highest of 36 in Oregon, against a state median of $4,773. Just 10% of the farmland was rented in 2022.
