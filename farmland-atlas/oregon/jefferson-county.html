Jefferson County, Oregon

Irrigation covers 88 percent of harvested cropland here, with groundwater supplying 3 percent of that water as of 2015. This matters because drought exposure is real: 328 of 1357 weeks since 2000 sat in severe drought or worse, 24.2 percent, and 2021 alone carried 52 weeks, with 149 weeks total across 2021-2025. Heat and drought's share of indemnities moved from 0 percent in 2000-2009 to 12 percent in 2010-2019 to 30 percent in 2020-2025. The July nightly low has moved from 49.5 F (1991-2020 normal) to 51.6 F (2017-2026), with a warming trend of 0.81 F per decade since 1976.
