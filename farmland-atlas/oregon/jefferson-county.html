Irrigated cropland in Jefferson County rents for $152 an acre in 2026. That is tied for 6th lowest of 23 counties, under the state median of $210. The census value of land and buildings was $1,816 an acre in 2022, up 112.4% from $855 in 2017, and it ranks 9th lowest of 36 against a state median of $4,773. Only 16% of farmland here was rented in 2022.
