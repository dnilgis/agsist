Douglas County, Oregon

Douglas County, OR: non-irrigated cash rent $67 an acre (2025); land and buildings $4,442 an acre in the 2022 census. County averages from public records, not an appraisal of any farm. FIPS 41019. Figures as of 2026-09-25.

Non-irrigated cash rent in Douglas County runs $67 per acre; irrigated land commands $158 per acre. Irrigation covers 36 percent of harvested cropland, with only 2 percent of that water coming from groundwater as of 2015. The July nightly low has moved from 52.4 F to 53.4 F, and the longer trend since 1976 runs 0.63 F per decade, warming. Crop insurance losses since 2001 total $3.4 million, with excess moisture and flood the largest cause at 41 percent; heat and drought made up 1 percent of indemnities in 2010-2019 and 15 percent in 2020-2025. Census land values stand at $4,442 per acre, up 30.1 percent from 2017 to 2022.
