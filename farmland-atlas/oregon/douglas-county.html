Douglas County, Oregon

Severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $4,442 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: severe drought (D2) covers at least half the county.

94.8% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 24, 2026. Rain this year: about 29.59 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 71% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 206 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 1,993 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $4,442 an acre in the 2022 census.

Cash rent, non-irrigated: $67 an acre (USDA, 2025). We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. Soils: NCCPI 0.70 acre-weighted across the county's rated farmland (USDA SSURGO, survey dates Sep 9, 2026 to Sep 11, 2026). 3% of the county's mapped acres are prime farmland, 0.9% more are prime farmland if drained or otherwise improved. 28% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). Tile drains 2% of the cropland (2022 census). Cover crops go on 5% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 122 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). Solar and wind proposed but not built: 2.5 megawatts of nameplate capacity (EIA, 2025). Foreign-held land: 118,719 acres filed with USDA under AFIDA for 2024, forest included.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

PRF grazing county base value: $49.00 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 90 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Crop insurance claims: $4.20 per $100 of coverage a year, 2016 to 2025 (the last ten closed crop years, USDA RMA).

Loss ratio 0.87, 2016 to 2025. 36.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.5 days a year and damaging wind on 0.3, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Heat and drought caused 11.9% of crop insurance claims paid from 2001 to 2025; too much rain caused 41%.

Quick answers for Douglas County

What is cash rent in Douglas County, Oregon?

The county average is $67 an acre for non-irrigated cropland in 2025 and $158 an acre for irrigated cropland in 2025. It is the USDA NASS Cash Rents Survey, a county average and not the rent on any one farm.

What is farmland worth in Douglas County, Oregon?

Land and buildings were worth $4,442 an acre in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, the operators' own estimate. It is not an appraisal, and a sale on a given farm can come in well above or below it.
