Cash rent in Douglas County ran $67 an acre in 2025, with irrigated ground at $158 in 2025 and pasture at $21 in 2026. The census value of land and buildings was $4,442 an acre in 2022, up 30.1% from $3,414 in 2017, or 5.4% a year. That sits under the state median of $4,773 and ranks 18th lowest of 36.
