As of Oct 16, cash rent in Umatilla County was $79 an acre in 2025, up 42.7% from $55 in 2014. Irrigated ground rented for $478 an acre in 2026, 2nd highest of 23 in Oregon against a state median of $210. The census value of land and buildings was $2,437 an acre in 2022, up 33.6% from 2017 and 14th lowest of 36 in the state. In 2022, 45% of the farmland here was rented.
