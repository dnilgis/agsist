Wheeler County, Oregon

Extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county. Land and buildings run $1,382 an acre (operators' own estimate, 2022 census).

Weather

Drought now: extreme drought (D3) covers at least half the county.

100% of the county is in drought (D1 or worse) on the U.S. Drought Monitor map of Nov 17, 2026. Rain this year: about 9.16 inches averaged over the county, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 70% of NOAA's 1991-2020 gridded normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km grid, the figure the county rain page leads with. Frost-free season: 145 days in the median year, 1996 to 2025. Corn growing degree days, May through September: 2,034 in the median year, 1996 to 2025 (NOAA).

Land

Land and buildings: $1,382 an acre in the 2022 census.

We read recorded sales in Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Dakota, Vermont, Washington and Wisconsin so far, not in this state. 24% of the land in farms is rented from others (2022 census). The 2022 census reports no tile-drained cropland here. Cover crops go on 2% of the cropland, CRP left out (2022 census). 22.7 cattle and calves per 1,000 acres of land in farms (2022 census). No solar or wind capacity is proposed here (EIA, 2025). No foreign-held acres were filed under AFIDA for 2024; none reported can also mean none filed.

Grain

Grain: we do not read elevator bids in this state yet.

Program

Corn ARC-CO: no expected payment for this county.

Wheat ARC-CO or PLC: it depends on your PLC yield. PRF grazing county base value: $11.80 an acre, crop year 2027. CRP: 22 acres under contract end in fiscal year 2027 (USDA FSA).

Risk

Risk: no crop insurance record for this county.

56.3% of harvested cropland is irrigated (2022 census). Reported hail an inch or larger on 0.3 days a year and damaging wind on 0.1, 2010 to 2025. Reports lean to places where people live. Claims by cause: too few claims here to split them.

The census value of land and buildings in Wheeler County runs $1,382 an acre for 2022. That is up 52.7% from $905 in 2017, or 8.8% a year, and up from $648 back in 2012. It still sits well under the Oregon median of $4,773, at 4th lowest of 36. 24% of the farmland here was rented in 2022.

What is farmland worth in Wheeler County, Oregon?
