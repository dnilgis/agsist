What AGSIST covers

Commodities
Corn, soybeans, wheat, plus specialty grades (white and food-grade corn, food-grade soybeans, milo) where elevators post them.
Geography
Nationwide, wherever elevators post their bids online. Most are in the Corn Belt and the Plains.
Source
Each elevator’s own posted bid board, read by AGSIST and marked direct from the elevator on the results. The map below shows which boards we read.
Freshness
Each board is read on a short repeating cycle, day and night. Every bid shows the time it was read.
Each bid shows
Cash price (futures + basis), contract month, delivery window, and distance from your ZIP.

Common questions
Which grain elevators does AGSIST cover?
Independent elevators, co-ops and grain company locations that post their bids online, most of them in the Corn Belt and Great Plains. AGSIST reads each one's own posted bid board. Enter your ZIP on the cash bids page to see the ones near you.
How do I find my local elevator’s grain price on AGSIST?
Go to agsist.com/cash-bids and enter your 5-digit ZIP code. You get the corn, soybean and wheat bids near you, nearest first, read from each elevator's own posted board.
How current are the cash bid prices?
AGSIST reads each elevator's board on a short repeating cycle, day and night. A bid is the last price that elevator posted, shown with the time it was read, so it can trail the futures if the elevator has not updated its board. Always confirm the bid with the elevator before you load or haul.
My elevator isn’t showing up. What can I do?
It shows up once AGSIST reads its board. Some smaller independents do not post bids online at all, and some we have not added yet. Use the form on this page or email sig@agsist.com and I will see whether it can be added. The network grows most weeks.
How are bids ordered?
Nearest first, by distance from the ZIP you enter. On the Cash Bids page you can sort by best cash price, best basis or name instead. No elevator pays for placement.
Can an elevator pay to rank higher?
No. Listing is free, and payment never changes bid order or ranking. Bids are shown as the elevator posted them, with the time they were read. Elevators can claim their board to check their name, town and phone.
