What AGSIST covers

Commodities
Corn, soybeans, wheat: plus specialty grades (white & food-grade corn, food-grade soybeans, milo) where elevators post them.
Geography
Nationwide. Bids appear wherever elevators post them: Corn Belt, Northern & Southern Plains, Delta, Southeast, and Pacific Northwest.
Source
Two feeds, merged. Where AGSIST reads an elevator’s own posted bid board: marked direct from the elevator on the results: that board wins. Everything else comes from a second bid feed that carries elevator-posted bids. The map below shows which is which.
Freshness
Updated through the trading day, during market hours.
Each bid shows
Cash price (futures + basis), contract month, delivery window, and distance from your ZIP.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which grain elevators does AGSIST cover?
AGSIST displays cash bids from elevators, co-ops, and grain facilities across the Corn Belt, Great Plains, Delta, Southeast, and Pacific Northwest, read from elevators' own posted boards and a second feed. Coverage includes independent elevators, co-operative grain facilities, and major grain company locations. Enter your ZIP code on the cash bids page to see available bids near you.
How do I find my local elevator’s grain price on AGSIST?
Go to agsist.com/cash-bids and enter your 5-digit ZIP code. AGSIST will show available corn, soybean, and wheat bids from grain facilities within your area, sorted by distance. Bids come from elevators' own posted boards and a second feed, and are updated throughout the trading day.
How current are the cash bid prices?
Cash bids come from elevators' own posted boards and a second feed, and reflect elevator-posted prices updated during trading hours. Prices may lag real-time futures by minutes depending on when the elevator last posted. Always confirm the current bid directly with the elevator before loading or hauling grain.
My elevator isn’t showing up. What can I do?
Coverage depends on whether AGSIST reads your elevator's board or it reaches us through a second feed. Some smaller independent elevators may not participate. If your elevator isn’t listed, email sig@farmers1st.com. I’ll investigate whether it’s available and can be added. I’m actively working to expand coverage across the Corn Belt.
How are bids ordered?
Nearest first, by distance from the ZIP you enter. On the Cash Bids page you can sort by best cash price, best basis or name instead. No elevator pays for placement.
Can an elevator pay to rank higher?
No. Listing is free, and payment never changes bid order or ranking. Bids are shown as the elevator posted them, with the time they were read. Elevators can claim their board to check their name, town and phone.
