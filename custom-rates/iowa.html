Questions

What does custom combining cost in Iowa?

In the 2026 Iowa Farm Custom Rate Survey (Ag Decision Maker File A3-10, FM 1698, revised March 2026), combining corn averaged $46.05 per acre ($25.00 to $80.00, 119 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $43.05 per acre ($24.00 to $70.00, 94 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Iowa?

Planting, without attachments: $26.50 per acre in the 2026 survey ($15.00 to $38.00, 69 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Iowa?

Ground, broadcast, not incorporated, self-propelled: $9.35 per acre in the 2026 survey ($5.00 to $18.00, 121 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told Iowa State University Extension and Outreach they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

All rates include fuel, repairs, depreciation, interest, operator labor, and all other machinery costs for the tractor and implement, unless otherwise noted. Diesel assumed at $2.89 a gallon (February 2026).
