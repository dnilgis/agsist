Online survey launched March 2025; 161 respondents who performed, hired or knew 2024 custom rates in Wisconsin. The custom rates reported in this survey include fuel cost unless otherwise stated. Average is the simple average of all reported rates; Range is the minimum and maximum reported values. The range is the lowest and highest answer. Rows whose unit or grouping the report leaves unclear (the baling lines in the forage table, the seeding sub-rows and the first corn harvest line) are left out.

Source: University of Wisconsin-Madison Division of Extension, the survey. AGSIST did not set these rates and does not adjust them. Bordering states with a survey here: Iowa (2026).

Questions

What does custom combining cost in Wisconsin?

In the Custom Rates for 2024 Wisconsin Farm Operations (2024 Wisconsin Custom Rate Survey), combining corn averaged $38.39 per acre ($26.00 to $76.00, 46 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $37.13 per acre ($26.00 to $55.00, 46 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Wisconsin?

Planting corn (without fertilizer), conventional: $23.36 per acre in the 2024 survey ($14.00 to $35.00, 14 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Wisconsin?

Herbicide, boom sprayer: $11.48 per acre in the 2024 survey ($6.75 to $40.00, 42 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told University of Wisconsin-Madison Division of Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

The custom rates reported in this survey include fuel cost unless otherwise stated. Average is the simple average of all reported rates; Range is the minimum and maximum reported values.
