Online survey launched March 2026; 135 Wisconsin farmers, farm managers and custom operators who did, hired or knew custom work in the 2025 season. The reported rates include fuel. Average is the mean of all reported rates; range is the lowest and highest; median is the middle answer; responses is how many usable answers each line had. The range is the lowest and highest answer. The report's fertilizer and chemical table is headed 2024; it reads as a slip, since every other table and its percent changes are 2025. Its small round bale and 4x5 round bale lines print the same five numbers, so neither is lined up with other states. Its "maximum one-way mileage" lines are miles, not dollars, and are left out. Wisconsin's April 2025 preliminary release (2024 answers raised to 2025 with Iowa's year-over-year changes) is not used: nothing here is inflated.

Source: University of Wisconsin-Madison Division of Extension, the survey. AGSIST did not set these rates and does not adjust them. Bordering states with a survey here: Iowa (2026).

Questions

What does custom combining cost in Wisconsin?

In the Custom Rates for 2025 Wisconsin Farm Operations (2026 Wisconsin Custom Rate Survey, of 2025 rates), combining corn averaged $38.47 per acre ($26.00 to $55.00, 34 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $38.45 per acre ($26.00 to $55.00, 29 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Wisconsin?

Planting corn (without fertilizer), conventional: $23.22 per acre in the 2025 survey ($10.00 to $35.00, 9 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Wisconsin?

Herbicide, boom sprayer: $11.01 per acre in the 2025 survey ($8.00 to $18.00, 29 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told University of Wisconsin-Madison Division of Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

The reported rates include fuel. Average is the mean of all reported rates; range is the lowest and highest; median is the middle answer; responses is how many usable answers each line had.
