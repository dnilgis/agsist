Questions

What does custom combining cost in North Dakota?

In the 2024 North Dakota Custom Rates, Part I Early Season Operations (August 2024, marked draft) and Part II Late Season Operations (December 2024), combining corn averaged $43.87 per acre ($20.00 to $100.00, 38 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $36.02 per acre ($12.50 to $60.00, 61 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in North Dakota?

Corn, soybean, sunflower (regular), without fertilizer: $21.02 per acre in the 2024 survey ($12.50 to $35.00, 28 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in North Dakota?

Herbicide, broadcast, liquid (ground): $9.23 per acre in the 2024 survey ($3.00 to $26.50, 111 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told North Dakota State University Extension (late season survey run by USDA NASS) they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

The rates include charges for equipment, power, fuel, and the operator. Exchange work is not custom work for this survey. NDSU advises care with rates from fewer than fifteen reports.
