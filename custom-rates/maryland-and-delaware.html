Mail and online survey in the fall of 2024; 61 custom operators and farmers gave the rates they charge. Reported rates include charges for machines, power, fuel, lube and the labor/operator. They do not include chemicals, seed or other materials, except hay baling materials and where noted (spreading lime includes the lime). The range is the lowest and highest answer. The guide prints the average and the median but not how many answered each job; a job whose lowest, highest, median and average are all one number may rest on a single answer, so it is shown but never lined up with other states. The bed forming and plastic laying line names no unit and is left out.

Source: University of Maryland Extension, with the University of Delaware, Custom Work Charges in Maryland and Delaware (University of Maryland Extension FS-683, updated March 2025), Table 1 (2025 survey). AGSIST did not set these rates and does not adjust them. Bordering states with a survey here: Pennsylvania (2022).

Questions

What does custom combining cost in Maryland and Delaware?

In the Custom Work Charges in Maryland and Delaware (University of Maryland Extension FS-683, updated March 2025), Table 1, combining corn averaged $40.03 per acre (answers ran $30.00 to $52.00, answers not printed) and combining soybeans averaged $39.63 per acre (answers ran $30.00 to $55.00, answers not printed). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Maryland and Delaware?

Corn Planting-Conventional w/o Fertilizer: $27.23 per acre in the 2025 survey (answers ran $18.00 to $75.00, answers not printed). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Maryland and Delaware?

Spraying-Pesticide Ground: $14.01 per acre in the 2025 survey (answers ran $7.00 to $50.00, answers not printed). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told University of Maryland Extension, with the University of Delaware they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and names it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

Reported rates include charges for machines, power, fuel, lube and the labor/operator. They do not include chemicals, seed or other materials, except hay baling materials and where noted (spreading lime includes the lime).
