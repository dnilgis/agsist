Questions

What does custom combining cost in Missouri?

In the Custom Rates for Farm Services in Missouri (MU Extension guide G302, revised April 2023), combining corn averaged $43.29 per acre ($30.00 to $60.00, 17 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $39.42 per acre ($28.00 to $60.00, 19 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Missouri?

Corn or grain sorghum, plant in 30-inch rows: $24.17 per acre in the 2023 survey ($15.00 to $40.00, 15 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Missouri?

Spray with self-propelled crop sprayer: $7.71 per acre in the 2023 survey ($5.00 to $10.00, 28 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told University of Missouri Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

Custom rates cover the cost of machinery, fuel, labor and, occasionally, a product such as lime or bale wrap. Rates exclude the cost of materials being applied.
