2025 rates (survey fall 2025 to winter 2026, published March 2026). MU Extension asked farmers, agribusinesses and land improvement contractors, online and through its specialists, what they charged or paid for custom work in 2025; 953 answers across all jobs. Rates are for machinery, fuel and labor, now and then with a product such as lime or bale wrap. The material being applied is not included unless the row says so. The range is the lowest and highest answer, and the median (the middle answer) is shown under each average, as G302 prints them. For a few jobs with too few answers to report in full, G302 prints only a median, calls it anecdotal and says to give it much less weight; those rows show no average, range or count. Rows priced as a share of the crop are the guide's own percent-of-crop lines.

Source: University of Missouri Extension, the survey. AGSIST did not set these rates and does not adjust them. Bordering states with a survey here: Iowa (2026), Kansas (2026), Nebraska (2026), Oklahoma (2021).

Questions

What does custom combining cost in Missouri?

In the Custom Rates for Farm Services in Missouri (MU Extension guide G302, revised March 2026 by Drew Kientzy and Ben Brown), combining corn averaged $47.56 per acre ($35.00 to $95.00, 9 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $42.33 per acre ($35.00 to $60.00, 9 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Missouri?

Corn or grain sorghum, plant in 30-inch rows: $24.65 per acre in the 2026 survey ($15.00 to $33.00, 23 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Missouri?

Spray with row crop sprayer: $9.42 per acre in the 2026 survey ($4.00 to $12.00, 20 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told University of Missouri Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

Rates are for machinery, fuel and labor, now and then with a product such as lime or bale wrap. The material being applied is not included unless the row says so.
