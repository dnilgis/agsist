Surveys mailed, emailed and handed out at Extension programs through the winter of 2024; 333 farmers, custom operators, farm managers and landowners. These rates, except where noted, include the implement and tractor if required, all variable machinery costs such as fuel, oil, lube, twine, etc., and labor for the operation. Diesel ran about $3.20 to $3.50 a gallon off-road during the survey (January to April 2024). The range is the lowest and highest answer (OSU also prints a one standard deviation band, not shown). OSU released a 2026 edition in late summer 2026; it could not be opened from here yet, so this is the 2024 edition. Rows OSU did not summarize for too few answers are left out.

Source: Ohio State University Extension, the survey. AGSIST did not set these rates and does not adjust them. Bordering states with a survey here: Indiana (2025).

Questions

What does custom combining cost in Ohio?

In the Ohio Farm Custom Rates 2024 (Ward, Richer, Barker and Bennett), combining corn averaged $34.61 per acre ($15.00 to $65.00, 60 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $33.53 per acre ($12.00 to $60.00, 58 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Ohio?

Plant corn, 30 inch rows: $23.51 per acre in the 2024 survey ($6.00 to $40.00, 44 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Ohio?

Spraying, self-propelled sprayer: $9.89 per acre in the 2024 survey ($6.00 to $18.00, 97 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told Ohio State University Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

These rates, except where noted, include the implement and tractor if required, all variable machinery costs such as fuel, oil, lube, twine, etc., and labor for the operation. Diesel ran about $3.20 to $3.50 a gallon off-road during the survey (January to April 2024).
