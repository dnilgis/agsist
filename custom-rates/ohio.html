Questions

What does custom combining cost in Ohio?

In the Ohio Farm Custom Rates 2026 (Ward, Richer, Barker and Bennett, June 29 edition), combining corn averaged $37.35 per acre (answers ran $15.00 to $65.00, 81 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $35.54 per acre (answers ran $15.00 to $65.00, 85 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Ohio?

Plant corn, 30 inch rows: $25.20 per acre in the 2026 survey (answers ran $15.00 to $39.00, 54 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Ohio?

Spraying, self-propelled sprayer: $10.63 per acre in the 2026 survey (answers ran $5.00 to $25.00, 105 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told Ohio State University Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and names it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

These rates, except where noted, include the implement and tractor if required, all variable machinery costs such as fuel, oil, lube, twine, etc., and labor for the operation. Midwest on-road diesel ran $3.36 to $5.81 a gallon during the survey (January to April 2026). OSU assumes most answers used a price in the lower part of that range.
