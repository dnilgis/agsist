Survey of Oklahoma farmers, ranchers and custom operators, fall 2021; about 675 usable surveys. Custom work means the operator furnishes the implement, fuel, labor and other inputs tied to the job; seed or fertilizer only when stated. This report prints the average and the median, not a range. It is the newest Oklahoma edition we could find, from a 2021 survey, so it runs older than its neighbors. Rows with the state average withheld (D) are left out.

Source: Oklahoma State University Extension, with the USDA NASS Oklahoma Field Office mailing, the survey. AGSIST did not set these rates and does not adjust them. Bordering states with a survey here: Kansas (2026), Missouri (2025 rates).

Questions

What does custom combining cost in Oklahoma?

In the Oklahoma Farm and Ranch Custom Rates, 2021-2022 (OSU Extension Current Report CR-205, revised March 2022), combining soybeans averaged $27.11 per acre (not printed, 9 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What does custom spraying cost in Oklahoma?

Ground application, herbicides with boom sprayer: $6.48 per acre in the 2021 survey (not printed, 56 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told Oklahoma State University Extension, with the USDA NASS Oklahoma Field Office mailing they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and links it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

Custom work means the operator furnishes the implement, fuel, labor and other inputs tied to the job; seed or fertilizer only when stated.
