Questions

What does custom combining cost in Indiana?

In the 2025 Indiana Farm Custom Rates (Purdue University, Michael Langemeier, April 2025), combining corn averaged $36.97 per acre (most answers fell between $28.80 and $45.10, 53 answers) and combining soybeans averaged $35.94 per acre (most answers fell between $28.10 and $43.80, 54 answers). Rates are survey averages; your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is the custom planting rate in Indiana?

Conventional till corn, 30 inch rows: $22.97 per acre in the 2025 survey (most answers fell between $16.40 and $29.50, 31 answers). The survey's other planting lines are in the table above.

What does custom spraying cost in Indiana?

Self-propelled sprayer: $8.39 per acre in the 2025 survey (most answers fell between $6.70 and $10.00, 40 answers). Chemical is not included.

Who set these rates?

Nobody set them. They are averages of what farmers and custom operators told Purdue University Extension they charged or paid. AGSIST copied them from the survey and names it. Your local custom operator may charge more or less.

What is included in a custom rate?

Unless otherwise stated, the rates include payments for fuel, operator labor, and machinery ownership costs.
