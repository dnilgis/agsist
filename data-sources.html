Data sources

Where the numbers on AGSIST come from. Last reviewed October 14, 2026. Whether each feed is current right now is on the status page.

Commodity prices

Yahoo Finance (via yfinance)

Live status of this feed →

Used for: Grain, oilseed, livestock, Class III milk, cotton and rice futures; crude oil, natural gas, gold and silver; the Dollar Index, 10-yr Treasury and S&P 500.

Delay: Yahoo lists CBOT and CME futures at a 10-minute exchange delay (Yahoo help, exchanges and data providers, checked Oct 17, 2026; its listing for NYMEX and COMEX energy and metals varies by region, up to 30 minutes). With the 15-minute refresh below, a grain or livestock price on this site is about 10 to 25 minutes old while markets trade.

Update frequency: About every 15 minutes while futures markets trade, Sunday evening to Friday afternoon.

finance.yahoo.com: market data via the yfinance Python library.

Bitcoin, XRP and Kaspa quotes on the dashboard come from the same Yahoo Finance feed.

Weather

Open-Meteo

Used for: Hourly temperature, wind speed, wind direction, humidity, precipitation probability, and derived spray/urea risk calculations.

Update frequency: Fetched by your browser each time you open a page or tool that shows weather.

open-meteo.com: open-source weather API.

Windy.com

Used for: The radar map on the home page, embedded from Windy, a private company. Windy draws its radar layer from national weather services, and AGSIST does not process it.

windy.com

U.S. Drought Monitor (NDMC, USDA, NOAA)

Live status of this feed →

Used for: Weekly U.S. Drought Monitor map embed.

droughtmonitor.unl.edu

Positioning, hail and transport

CFTC Commitments of Traders

Live status of this feed →

Used for: Weekly managed-money and commercial positioning on the COT page, fetched from the CFTC's public reports each Friday.

cftc.gov

NOAA MRMS Radar (MESH)

Live status of this feed →

Used for: Radar-detected maximum hail size swaths on the Hail Map, alongside NWS storm reports and warnings.

nssl.noaa.gov/projects/mrms

USDA AgTransport

Used for: Barge rates, regional grain basis and transport cost indices behind the basis and transport reads.

agtransport.usda.gov

Prediction markets

Polymarket

Used for: Event probabilities in the odds block on the home page. AGSIST collects them once a day from Polymarket's Gamma API (and Kalshi's public market data) on the server, filters them for farm relevance, and the home page reads that one file. Every contract published today is from Polymarket.

polymarket.com

USDA reports

USDA

Live status of this feed →

Used for: Report calendar (WASDE, Crop Progress, Grain Stocks, Acreage, Cattle on Feed), compiled by hand from USDA’s published release schedules (OCE/WAOB and the NASS calendar).

usda.gov: published release schedules, read and entered by hand.

More USDA and government data

USDA NASS Quick Stats and Census of Agriculture

Live status of this feed →

Used for: Yields, acres, cash rents and land values by state and county, including the Census of Agriculture figures on the Farmland Atlas. Pulled from the Quick Stats API.

quickstats.nass.usda.gov

USDA RMA and FSA

Used for: Crop insurance planting dates, prices and Summary of Business data (Risk Management Agency), and Conservation Reserve Program acres (Farm Service Agency).

rma.usda.gov · fsa.usda.gov

USDA FAS Export Sales

Used for: Weekly export sales by commodity, from the Foreign Agricultural Service open data API.

apps.fas.usda.gov

NOAA NCEI and Climate Prediction Center

Used for: Long-run county and state climate records (NCEI) and the 6-10 and 8-14 day outlooks (CPC).

ncei.noaa.gov · cpc.ncep.noaa.gov

Iowa Environmental Mesonet

Used for: National Weather Service local storm reports, including hail reports, on the Hail Map.

mesonet.agron.iastate.edu

U.S. Energy Information Administration

Used for: Power plant data on the Farmland Atlas (survey EIA-860) and the energy news feed behind the briefing.

eia.gov

Field Scout: USDA soils and Copernicus Sentinel

Used for: Soil maps (SSURGO), crop history (Cropland Data Layer) and Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 satellite layers on Field Scout, requested through AGSIST’s own Cloudflare Worker.

Audience numbers

Google Analytics, Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

Used for: The reader and search figures on the sponsor page, read from AGSIST’s own analytics accounts. The Bing Webmaster figure counts Bing and Copilot answers only.

Geocoding

Nominatim / OpenStreetMap

Used for: Reverse geocoding browser lat/lng to city, state, and ZIP code. No personal location data is stored on my servers.

Cash bids

AGSIST elevator network

Live status of this feed →

Cash bids come from AGSIST’s own elevator network: bid boards read directly from each elevator. There is no second feed filling gaps, so an elevator the network does not read shows no bid.
