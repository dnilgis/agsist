Questions about Lawrence County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Lawrence County for 2026?

For a typical farm (non-irrigated): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $3.88 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0.87 PLC vs $4.75 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $207.23 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $84.70 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.87 and ARC-CO $4.75 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Lawrence County, Alabama is 215.8 bu (irrigated); 165.1 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
