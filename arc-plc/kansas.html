Kansas ARC-CO benchmark yields for 2026, by county

Data updated Oct 19, 2026

FSA’s official program year 2026 ARC-CO benchmark yields for 105 Kansas counties, by crop and by practice where FSA splits irrigated and non-irrigated. FSA has not posted 2027 benchmarks; the 2027 benchmark shifts the window one year. FSA file as of Jan 16, 2026

Prices that apply in every county. 2026 effective reference price: corn $4.42, soybeans $10.71, wheat $6.35. 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price: corn $5.03, soybeans $12.17, wheat $6.98. When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, ARC-CO pays when the season-average price ends at $4.52 or lower for corn, $10.95 for soybeans and $6.28 for wheat.

Other crops with FSA benchmarks here: Sorghum · Barley · Oats · Seed cotton · Dry peas · Canola · Flaxseed · Sunflower seed · Mustard seed · Safflower · Sesame seed.

Official benchmark yield and revenue by county

Benchmark yield per acre (FSA, 2026), in bushels or, for peanuts, rice, pulses, seed cotton and most oilseeds, pounds. Practice: All = all practices, Irr. = irrigated, Non-irr. = non-irrigated. Revenue = yield × 2026 benchmark price, $/acre. The most ARC-CO can pay is 12% of revenue on 85% of base, 10.2% of revenue per base acre; each county page works it out.

PLC vs ARC-CO break-even at the benchmark yield

When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, which program pays more depends on the season-average price and on your PLC yield as a share of the county’s official benchmark. Expressed that way, the break-even is the same in every Kansas county. 2026 prices, corn:

PLC yield, % of your official benchmarkWhich pays more, by season-average price
60%PLC pays more at $3.41 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.42 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
70%PLC pays more at $3.55 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.56 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
80%PLC pays more at $3.66 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.67 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
90%PLC pays more at $3.74 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.75 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
100%PLC pays more at $3.81 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.82 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
