Barley: ARC or PLC for 2026 and 2027

2026 signup closesDec 11, 2026
2027 signupNov 2 to Mar 15, 2027

Quick answer. The 2026 PLC effective reference price for barley is $5.58/bu (FSA). PLC pays when the national season-average price ends below that; the loan rate, $2.75/bu, is as low as the price counts. ARC-CO pays when county revenue falls below 90% of benchmark revenue, figured at the 2026 benchmark price of $6.43/bu.

At USDA’s projected 2026/27 season-average price (WASDE, Oct 9, 2026), $5.70/bu, the 2026 PLC rate would be $0.00/bu (none). The real rate comes from the final price after the marketing year ends (June 2026 to May 2027).

2027 (est.): ERP $5.62, benchmark price $6.44.

No ARC or PLC pick for barley yet. FSA’s price table on file starts with 2020/21, so it holds only 4 years of price change to pair with county yields. Across 1,673 county and practice combinations, every typical-farm verdict is withheld (228 have no typical farm because FSA’s county average PLC yield is above the benchmark). The county pages still give the benchmark and where each program pays more by price.

How the barley prices are figured

The effective reference price is the lesser of 115% of the statutory price, or the greater of the statutory price and 88% of the Olympic average season-average price for the five most recent crop years (2020 to 2024 for 2026; 2021 to 2025 for 2027). The ARC-CO benchmark price uses the same five years, each raised to at least the effective reference price.

2026 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2020: $4.75 · 2021: $5.31 · 2022: $7.40 · 2023: $7.39 · 2024: $6.31 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $6.3367. 88% of that is $5.58, above the $5.45 statutory price and under the $6.27 cap (115%).

2026 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2020: $5.58 (raised from $4.75) · 2021: $5.58 (raised from $5.31) · 2022: $7.40 · 2023: $7.39 · 2024: $6.31. Drop the high ($7.40) and low ($5.58); the middle three average $6.43.

2027 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2021: $5.31 · 2022: $7.40 · 2023: $7.39 · 2024: $6.31 · 2025: $5.46 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $6.3867. 88% of that is $5.62, above the $5.45 statutory price and under the $6.27 cap (115%).

2027 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2021: $5.62 (raised from $5.31) · 2022: $7.40 · 2023: $7.39 · 2024: $6.31 · 2025: $5.62 (raised from $5.46). Drop the high ($7.40) and low ($5.62); the middle three average $6.44.

2026 figures are FSA’s and match our math from FSA’s own price column. 2027 is our math (est.); FSA publishes the official figures.

Questions about barley

What is the 2026 effective reference price for barley?

$5.58 per bushel, from FSA's 2026 table. The statutory reference price is $5.45; 88% of the 2020 to 2024 Olympic average season-average price is $5.58, and the cap is $6.27. The 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price is $6.43 and the loan rate is $2.75.

What is the 2027 effective reference price for barley?

$5.62 per bushel by our math from FSA's final prices (est.); the 2027 ARC-CO benchmark price is $6.44. FSA publishes the official 2027 figures.

Will PLC pay on barley for 2026?

At USDA WASDE, Oct 9, 2026's projected 2026/27 price of $5.70/bu, the 2026 PLC rate would be $0.00/bu (none). FSA's own table of Sep 11, 2026 shows $0.00/bu at $5.70. The real rate is set by the final season-average price, published after the marketing year (June 2026 to May 2027) ends.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for barley?

We do not give a pick for barley yet. FSA’s price table on file starts with 2020/21, so it holds only 4 years of price change to pair with county yields. The county pages show FSA's official benchmark, where each program pays more by price, and the calculator runs your own PLC yield and price.

Where do I find my county's barley ARC-CO benchmark yield?

FSA's 2026 county file lists barley benchmarks for 1,704 counties; each one has a page here. A county FSA does not list has no barley benchmark; ask the county FSA office.
