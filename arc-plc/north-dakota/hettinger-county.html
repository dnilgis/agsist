Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Hettinger County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: too small or too uncertain to call (PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $86.51 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $34.99 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, the expected payments are not printed. Wheat has no October futures history of its own on file, so its price swings are borrowed from corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them.
