2026. The 2026 crop is mostly harvested, so each scenario uses a normal LaMoure County crop (FSA’s 2026 benchmark yield) and only the price moves. Prices start from USDA’s projected 2026/27 season-average prices (USDA WASDE, Oct 9, 2026: soybeans $12.00, corn $4.70, wheat $6.30, barley $5.70, oats $3.35, sorghum $4.50; FSA table of Sep 11, 2026: sunflower seed $0.2470, flaxseed $13.00, dry peas $0.1120, canola $0.2450). Around that price we ran each past year with a final USDA price: how far that year’s final season-average price ended from October futures, for corn and soybeans. How we call it. Clear pick when one program averages at least $2.00 more per base acre, the gap is more than t times its standard error (2.26 for 10 years, 2.23 for 11), and it paid more in at least 3 of the years. Leans when the gap is at least $3.00 and one standard error, and it paid more in at least 3 years. Close otherwise. When both round to $0: neither expected to pay.
