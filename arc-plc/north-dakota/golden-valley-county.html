All practices, 2026: Leans PLC: expected about $7.16 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $18.79 PLC vs $11.64 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 5 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 1).
