Missouri ARC-CO benchmark yields for 2026, by county

By Sigurd Lindquist, a Certified Crop Adviser (CCA). Data updated Oct 9, 2026

FSA’s official program year 2026 ARC-CO benchmark yields for 107 Missouri counties, by crop and by practice where FSA splits irrigated and non-irrigated. FSA has not posted 2027 benchmarks; the 2027 benchmark shifts the window one year. FSA file as of Jan 16, 2026

Find your county’s short answer

  • Dec 112026 signup ends Dec 11, 2026.
  • Nov 2 to Mar 152027 signup runs Nov 2, 2026 to Mar 15, 2027.
  • Skip itDo nothing for 2026 and that farm gets no 2026 payment.
  • SignEvery owner and operator on the farm signs.
  • BringBring the farm’s FSA-156EZ. It lists the base acres and PLC yields.

Prices that apply in every county. 2026 effective reference price: corn $4.42, soybeans $10.71, wheat $6.35. 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price: corn $5.03, soybeans $12.17, wheat $6.98. When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, ARC-CO pays when the season-average price ends at $4.52 or lower for corn, $10.95 for soybeans and $6.28 for wheat.

Other crops with FSA benchmarks here: Sorghum · Barley · Oats · Long grain rice · Medium and short grain rice · Seed cotton · Canola · Flaxseed · Sunflower seed.

Revenue = yield × 2026 benchmark price, $/acre. The most ARC-CO can pay is 12% of revenue on 85% of base, 10.2% of revenue per base acre; each county page works it out.

PLC vs ARC-CO break-even at the benchmark yield

When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, which program pays more depends on the season-average price and on your PLC yield as a share of the county’s official benchmark. Expressed that way, the break-even is the same in every Missouri county. 2026 prices, corn:

PLC yield, % of your official benchmarkWhich pays more, by season-average price
60%PLC pays more at $3.41 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.42 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
70%PLC pays more at $3.55 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.56 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
80%PLC pays more at $3.66 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.67 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
90%PLC pays more at $3.74 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.75 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
100%PLC pays more at $3.81 or lower. ARC-CO pays more from $3.82 to $4.52. Neither pays at $4.53 or higher.
