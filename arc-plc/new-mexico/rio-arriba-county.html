Questions about Rio Arriba County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Rio Arriba County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $0.10 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $3.48 PLC vs $3.58 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $42.25 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $16.69 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $3.48 and ARC-CO $3.58 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Rio Arriba County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico is 23.44 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for oats in Rio Arriba County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $10.75 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0 PLC vs $10.75 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $3.05, up to $13.58 per base acre at the $2.20 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $20.90 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $3.35, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $10.75 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for oats in Rio Arriba County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for oats in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico is 52.00 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $3.94 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
