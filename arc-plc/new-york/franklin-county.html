Questions about Franklin County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Franklin County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): ARC-CO expected to pay more on these assumptions (expected $2.81 PLC vs $10.25 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $190.57 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $78.30 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.60, PLC is expected to pay $2.81 and ARC-CO $10.25 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Franklin County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Franklin County, New York is 152.6 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
