Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Wibaux County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $0.25 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $4.92 PLC vs $5.17 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $59.69 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $24.11 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $4.92 and ARC-CO $5.17 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
