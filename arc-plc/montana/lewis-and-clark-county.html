Questions about Lewis and Clark County

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Lewis and Clark County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Lewis and Clark County, Montana is 72.9 bu (irrigated); 31.9 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for barley in Lewis and Clark County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Leans ARC-CO: PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9; expected payments not printed, not enough price history of its own). Too little price history for a clear pick: shown as Leans. PLC pays on any season-average price below $5.58, up to $125.57 per base acre at the $2.75 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $64.35 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $5.60, the expected payments are not printed: this crop does not have enough of its own price history. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for barley in Lewis and Clark County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for barley in Lewis and Clark County, Montana is 98.1 bu (irrigated); 41.1 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.43 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for oats in Lewis and Clark County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for oats in Lewis and Clark County, Montana is 57.8 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $3.94 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

When is my 2025 payment?

FSA's final 2025 ARC-CO county rates are in its file of Nov 2, 2026 and its 2025 prices in its table of Oct 30, 2026. We have no FSA notice of the 2025 payment date that we can check, so see your FSA office for the date. For 2025 a farm gets the higher of ARC-CO or PLC.
