Should I pick ARC or PLC for barley in Garfield County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.20 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $2.21 PLC vs $6.41 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 8). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $5.58, up to $76.49 per base acre at the $2.75 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop.
