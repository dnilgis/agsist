North Carolina ARC-CO benchmark yields for 2026, by county

By Sigurd Lindquist, a Certified Crop Adviser (CCA). Data updated Oct 9, 2026

FSA’s official program year 2026 ARC-CO benchmark yields for 100 North Carolina counties, by crop and by practice where FSA splits irrigated and non-irrigated. FSA has not posted 2027 benchmarks; the 2027 benchmark shifts the window one year. FSA file as of Jan 16, 2026

Find your county’s short answer

  • Nov 2 to Mar 152027 signup runs Nov 2, 2026 to Mar 15, 2027.
  • Skip itDo nothing for 2026 and that farm gets no 2026 payment.
  • SignEvery owner and operator on the farm signs.
  • BringBring the farm’s FSA-156EZ. It lists the base acres and PLC yields.

Prices that apply in every county. 2026 effective reference price: corn $4.42, soybeans $10.71, wheat $6.35. 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price: corn $5.03, soybeans $12.17, wheat $6.98. When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, ARC-CO pays when the season-average price ends at $4.52 or lower for corn, $10.95 for soybeans and $6.28 for wheat.

Other crops with FSA benchmarks here: Sorghum · Barley · Oats · Peanuts · Seed cotton · Dry peas · Canola · Flaxseed · Sunflower seed · Rapeseed · Safflower · Sesame seed.

PLC vs ARC-CO break-even at the benchmark yield

When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, which program pays more depends on the season-average price and on your PLC yield as a share of the county’s official benchmark. Expressed that way, the break-even is the same in every North Carolina county. 2026 prices, corn:
