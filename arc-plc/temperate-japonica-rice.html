Quick answer. The 2026 PLC effective reference price for temperate japonica rice is $0.2433/lb (FSA). PLC pays when the national season-average price ends below that; the loan rate, $0.0770/lb, is as low as the price counts. ARC-CO pays when county revenue falls below 90% of benchmark revenue, figured at the 2026 benchmark price of $0.2685/lb.

At FSA’s projected 2026/27 season-average price (FSA table of Sep 11, 2026), $0.2050/lb, PLC would pay a 2026 rate of $0.0383/lb. The real rate comes from the final price after the marketing year ends (Oct 2026 to Sept 2027).

2027: These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.2000/lb), and the answer depends on it.

How the temperate japonica rice prices are figured

The effective reference price is the lesser of 115% of the statutory price, or the greater of the statutory price and 88% of the Olympic average season-average price for the five most recent crop years (2020 to 2024 for 2026; 2021 to 2025 for 2027). The ARC-CO benchmark price uses the same five years, each raised to at least the effective reference price.

2026 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2020: $0.2260 · 2021: $0.3190 · 2022: $0.4090 · 2023: $0.2230 · 2024: $0.1870 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $0.2560. 88% of that is $0.2253, below the $0.2433 statutory price, so the statutory price holds: $0.2433.

2026 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2020: $0.2433 (raised from $0.2260) · 2021: $0.3190 · 2022: $0.4090 · 2023: $0.2433 (raised from $0.2230) · 2024: $0.2433 (raised from $0.1870). Drop the high ($0.4090) and low ($0.2433); the middle three average $0.2685.

2027. These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.2000/lb), and the answer depends on it.

2026 figures are FSA’s and match our math from FSA’s own price column. 2027 is our math (est.); FSA publishes the official figures.

Questions about temperate japonica rice

What is the 2026 effective reference price for temperate japonica rice?

$0.2433 per pound, from FSA's 2026 table. The statutory reference price is $0.2433; 88% of the 2020 to 2024 Olympic average season-average price is $0.2253, and the cap is $0.2798. The 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price is $0.2685 and the loan rate is $0.0770.

What is the 2027 effective reference price for temperate japonica rice?

Not yet. These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.2000/lb), and the answer depends on it.

Will PLC pay on temperate japonica rice for 2026?

At FSA table of Sep 11, 2026's projected 2026/27 price of $0.2050/lb, PLC would pay a 2026 rate of $0.0383/lb. FSA's own table of Sep 11, 2026 shows $0.0383/lb at $0.2050. The real rate is set by the final season-average price, published after the marketing year (Oct 2026 to Sept 2027) ends.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for temperate japonica rice?

We do not give a pick for temperate japonica rice yet. The county pages show FSA's official benchmark, where each program pays more by price, and the calculator runs your own PLC yield and price.
