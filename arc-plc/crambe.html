Crambe: ARC or PLC for 2026 and 2027

Data updated Oct 19, 2026

Quick answer. The 2026 PLC effective reference price for crambe is $0.2375/lb (FSA). PLC pays when the national season-average price ends below that; the loan rate, $0.1110/lb, is as low as the price counts. ARC-CO pays when county revenue falls below 90% of benchmark revenue, figured at the 2026 benchmark price of $0.2567/lb.

At FSA’s projected 2026/27 season-average price (FSA table of Oct 9, 2026), $0.3660/lb, PLC would pay nothing for 2026. The real rate comes from the final price after the marketing year ends (Sept 2026 to Aug 2027).

2027 (est.): ERP $0.2402, benchmark price $0.2731.

Typical-farm verdicts, 2026: Neither expected to pay 15.

How the crambe prices are figured

The effective reference price is the lesser of 115% of the statutory price, or the greater of the statutory price and 88% of the Olympic average season-average price for the five most recent crop years (2020 to 2024 for 2026; 2021 to 2025 for 2027). The ARC-CO benchmark price uses the same five years, each raised to at least the effective reference price.

2026 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2020: $0.2710 · 2021: $0.2260 · 2022: $0.2590 · 2023: $0.2400 · 2024: $0.3940 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $0.2567. 88% of that is $0.2259, below the $0.2375 statutory price, so the statutory price holds: $0.2375.

2026 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2020: $0.2710 · 2021: $0.2375 (raised from $0.2260) · 2022: $0.2590 · 2023: $0.2400 · 2024: $0.3940. Drop the high ($0.3940) and low ($0.2375); the middle three average $0.2567.

2027 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2021: $0.2260 · 2022: $0.2590 · 2023: $0.2400 · 2024: $0.3940 · 2025: $0.3200 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $0.2730. 88% of that is $0.2402, above the $0.2375 statutory price and under the $0.2731 cap (115%).

2027 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2021: $0.2402 (raised from $0.2260) · 2022: $0.2590 · 2023: $0.2402 (raised from $0.2400) · 2024: $0.3940 · 2025: $0.3200. Drop the high ($0.3940) and low ($0.2402); the middle three average $0.2731.

2026 figures are FSA’s and match our math from FSA’s own price column. 2027 is our math (est.); FSA publishes the official figures.

Questions about crambe

What is the 2026 effective reference price for crambe?

$0.2375 per pound, from FSA's 2026 table. The statutory reference price is $0.2375; 88% of the 2020 to 2024 Olympic average season-average price is $0.2259, and the cap is $0.2731. The 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price is $0.2567 and the loan rate is $0.1110.

What is the 2027 effective reference price for crambe?

$0.2402 per pound by our math from FSA's final prices (est.); the 2027 ARC-CO benchmark price is $0.2731. FSA publishes the official 2027 figures.

Will PLC pay on crambe for 2026?

At FSA table of Oct 9, 2026's projected 2026/27 price of $0.3660/lb, PLC would pay nothing for 2026. FSA's own table of Oct 9, 2026 shows no payment at $0.3660. The real rate is set by the final season-average price, published after the marketing year (Sept 2026 to Aug 2027) ends.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for crambe?

It depends on the county and the farm. Typical-farm verdicts for 2026 across the county pages: Neither expected to pay 15. Open your county's page for its verdict and FSA's official benchmark; the calculator runs your own PLC yield and price.

Where do I find my county's crambe ARC-CO benchmark yield?

FSA's 2026 county file lists crambe benchmarks for 24 counties; each one has a page here. A county FSA does not list has no crambe benchmark; ask the county FSA office.
