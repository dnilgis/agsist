Corn 2026 in Edgar County: leans ARC-CO

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $11.84 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $1.32 PLC vs $13.16 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $270.98 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $115.68 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 3 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.32 and ARC-CO $13.16 per base acre.

2027: Too close to call (expected $13.80 PLC vs $12.08 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 1; the gap is under $5.12). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
