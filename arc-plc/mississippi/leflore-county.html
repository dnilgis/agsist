  • New baseA 2025 law added 30 million base acres nationwide. More acres qualified than that cap, so FSA cut every new allocation by 3.69%.

How it is figured, every number and sources

How the checks are figured. Per base acre at FSA’s county average PLC yield, 85% payment factor in, before sequestration and payment limits. 2025 (paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them; the law pays the higher program): ARC-CO is the FSA posted rate (county file of Nov 2, 2026) times 85%; PLC is from FSA’s posted prices, the 2025 effective reference price minus the final 2025 price (tables of Nov 10, 2026), times the PLC yield, times 85%. 2026, our estimate (paid on or after Oct 1, 2027; only the enrolled program pays): the average over 11 past years’ price swings around USDA’s 2026 price (soybeans $12.10, corn $4.60; USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026), normal county crop; a lower price or a short county crop raises it. Seed cotton: PLC yield 2,368.49 lb, ARC-CO rate $0.00, PLC $0.4200 minus $0.3408 = $0.0792 a pound; Soybeans: PLC yield 46.89 bu, ARC-CO rate $49.98, PLC $10.71 minus $10.50 = $0.21 a bushel; Long grain rice: PLC yield 6,042.60 lb, ARC-CO rate $148.01, PLC $0.1690 minus $0.1010 = $0.0680 a pound; Corn: PLC yield 147.69 bu, ARC-CO rate $0.00, PLC $4.42 minus $4.16 = $0.26 a bushel. FSA figures: FSA ARC/PLC program data. 5.7% is the sequestration rate reported for these payments; the rate is set each fiscal year and can change; source: Farm CPA Report, sequestration and the 2025 limit.

Wheat, 2026: Price range from how far USDA’s November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle.
