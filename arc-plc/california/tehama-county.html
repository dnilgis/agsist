What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for barley in Tehama County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for barley in Tehama County, California is 52.57 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.43 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Tehama County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $1.65 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $10.19 PLC vs $11.84 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $123.62 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $55.20 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $10.19 and ARC-CO $11.84 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
