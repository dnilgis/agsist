Show the math

2026. The 2026 crop is mostly harvested, so each scenario uses a normal Imperial County crop (FSA’s 2026 benchmark yield) and only the price moves. Prices start from USDA’s projected 2026/27 season-average prices (USDA WASDE, Oct 9, 2026: wheat $6.30; FSA table of Sep 11, 2026: seed cotton $0.4114). Around that price we ran each past year with a final USDA price: how far that year’s final season-average price ended from October futures, for corn and soybeans. Wheat, seed cotton have no October futures history of their own, so they borrow corn’s October swings (wheat its own price changes, scaled to corn’s size) and stop at Leans.

2027. Wheat: no 2027 price outlook we can check against past years, so no 2027 answer. Seed cotton wait on USDA’s closing 2025 price, which sets the 2027 reference price.

Typical farm. FSA’s county average PLC yield (wheat 88.8 bu; seed cotton 3,868.5 lb) and FSA’s official benchmark. “Lower” and “Higher” mean 15% under or over the county average. Payments are per base acre and count FSA’s 85% payment acres, before sequestration and payment limits.

How we call it. Clear pick when one program averages at least $2.00 more per base acre, the gap is more than t times its standard error (2.26 for 10 years, 2.23 for 11), and it paid more in at least 3 of the years. Leans when the gap is at least $3.00 and one standard error, and it paid more in at least 3 years. Close otherwise. When both round to $0: neither expected to pay.

Wheat 2026 in Imperial County: Close, either one

All practices, 2026: Close, either one: about $1.29 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $16.36 PLC vs $17.65 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $198.51 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $82.30 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $16.36 and ARC-CO $17.65 per base acre.

2027: No 2027 price yet: there is no 2027 price outlook for this crop we can check against past years, so no call. Type a price in the calculator to see the dollars. The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.

Seed cotton 2026 in Imperial County: Leans PLC
