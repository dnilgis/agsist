Questions about Imperial County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Imperial County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: too small or too uncertain to call (PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $198.51 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $82.30 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, the expected payments are not printed. Wheat has no October futures history of its own on file, so its price swings are borrowed from corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Imperial County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Imperial County, California is 115.6 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for seed cotton in Imperial County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans PLC: ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.4200, up to $394.59 per base acre at the $0.3000 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $184.77 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 1 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $0.4147, the expected payments are not printed. Seed cotton has too few years of its own prices in FSA’s tables, so its price swings are corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them. What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for seed cotton in Imperial County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for seed cotton in Imperial County, California is 4,202 lb (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $0.4311 per pound. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
