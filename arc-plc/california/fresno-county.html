Seed cotton 2026 in Fresno County: Leans PLC

FSA’s seed cotton price is its own weighted average of the upland cotton lint price and the cottonseed price. We use FSA’s seed cotton series as published and never build it from the lint price. All seed cotton counties.

All practices, 2026: Leans PLC: expected about $41.36 more per base acre; ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $44.66 PLC vs $3.30 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings.

PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.4200, up to $306.41 per base acre at the $0.3000 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop.
