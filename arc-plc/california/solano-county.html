Wheat 2026 in Solano County: leans PLC

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (74.1 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (55.61 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans PLC: expected about $19.92 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $38.05 PLC vs $18.13 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 5 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 1).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $165.65 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $38.05 and ARC-CO $18.13 per base acre.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Solano County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Leans PLC: expected about $19.92 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $38.05 PLC vs $18.13 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 5 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 1). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $165.65 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $54.33 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $38.05 and ARC-CO $18.13 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $263.16 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $93.62 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.28 and ARC-CO $41.68 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
