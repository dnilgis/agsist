Questions about Merced County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for seed cotton in Merced County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans PLC: expected about $40.81 more per base acre; ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $43.83 PLC vs $3.03 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.4200, up to $300.75 per base acre at the $0.3000 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $168.21 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 2 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $0.4114, PLC is expected to pay $43.83 and ARC-CO $3.03 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
