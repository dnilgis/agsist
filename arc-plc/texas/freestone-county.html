Questions about Freestone County

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Freestone County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Freestone County, Texas is 32.8 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
