Questions about Bee County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Bee County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Close, either one: about $2.70 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $0.49 PLC vs $3.19 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $117.30 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $56.94 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.49 and ARC-CO $3.19 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
