What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Parmer County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Parmer County, Texas is 160.5 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Parmer County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Close, either one: too small or too uncertain to call (PLC paid more in 4 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $90.09 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $33.48 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, the expected payments are not printed.
