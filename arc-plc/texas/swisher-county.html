Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (27.8 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (14.75 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Too close to call (expected $14.27 PLC vs $13.59 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 5 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3; the gap is under $10.83).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $62.15 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $26.29 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 7 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $14.27 and ARC-CO $13.59 per base acre.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Swisher County for 2026?
