Corn 2026 in Taylor County: leans ARC-CO

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $12.21 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $0.48 PLC vs $12.69 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 9 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $87.72 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $41.04 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 3 of the 9 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.48 and ARC-CO $12.69 per base acre.
