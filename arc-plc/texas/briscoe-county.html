Seed cotton 2026 in Briscoe County: Leans PLC

FSA’s seed cotton price is its own weighted average of the upland cotton lint price and the cottonseed price. We use FSA’s seed cotton series as published and never build it from the lint price. All seed cotton counties.

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (1,264.4 lb) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (521.47 lb), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans PLC: expected about $17.41 more per base acre; ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $18.80 PLC vs $1.39 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings.

With prices centered at $0.4114, PLC is expected to pay $18.80 and ARC-CO $1.39 per base acre.
