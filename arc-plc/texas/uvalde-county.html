Deadlines

  • Dec 112026 signup ends Dec 11, 2026.
  • Nov 2 to Mar 152027 signup is open through Mar 15, 2027.
  • Skip itDo nothing for 2026 and that farm gets no 2026 payment.
  • New baseA 2025 law added 30 million base acres nationwide. More acres qualified than that cap, so FSA cut every new allocation by 3.69%.
  • SignProducers with a share in the crop sign. FSA says a landowner with no share interest, such as a cash-rent landlord, cannot enroll.
  • BringBring the farm’s FSA-156EZ. It lists the base acres and PLC yields.

Dates: USDA Farm Service Agency ARC/PLC page (read Nov 12, 2026) and its Sep 15, 2026 release.

How it is figured, every number and sources

How the checks are figured. Per base acre at FSA’s county average PLC yield, 85% payment factor in, before sequestration and payment limits. 2025 (paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them; the law pays the higher program): ARC-CO is the FSA posted rate (county file of Nov 2, 2026) times 85%; PLC is from FSA’s posted prices, the 2025 effective reference price minus the final 2025 price (tables of Nov 10, 2026), times the PLC yield, times 85%. 2026, our estimate (paid on or after Oct 1, 2027; only the enrolled program pays): the average over 11 past years’ price swings around USDA’s 2026 price (corn $4.60, wheat $6.20; USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026), normal county crop; a lower price or a short county crop raises it. Corn: PLC yield 104.94 bu, ARC-CO rate $23.65, PLC $4.42 minus $4.16 = $0.26 a bushel; Wheat: PLC yield 32.45 bu, ARC-CO rate $13.40, PLC $6.35 minus $5.06 = $1.29 a bushel; Sorghum: PLC yield 54.47 bu, ARC-CO rate $29.11, PLC $4.67 minus $3.67 = $1.00 a bushel; Seed cotton: PLC yield 2,713.25 lb, ARC-CO rate $0.00, PLC $0.4200 minus $0.3408 = $0.0792 a pound. FSA figures: FSA ARC/PLC program data. 5.7% is the sequestration rate reported for these payments; the rate is set each fiscal year and can change; source: Farm CPA Report, sequestration and the 2025 limit.

Wheat, 2026: Price range from how far USDA’s November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle.
