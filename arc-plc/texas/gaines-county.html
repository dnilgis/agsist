Questions about Gaines County

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for seed cotton in Gaines County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for seed cotton in Gaines County, Texas is 1,136.23 lb (irrigated); 306.48 lb (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $0.4311 per pound. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for peanuts in Gaines County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for peanuts in Gaines County, Texas is 2,635.20 lb (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $0.3150 per pound. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Gaines County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Close, either one: about $0.16 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $5.08 PLC vs $5.24 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $61.70 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $24.44 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $5.08 and ARC-CO $5.24 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Gaines County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Gaines County, Texas is 34.33 bu (irrigated); 10.73 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for sorghum in Gaines County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for sorghum in Gaines County, Texas is 28.55 bu (irrigated); 13.84 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.30 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Gaines County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Gaines County, Texas is 81.33 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for oats in Gaines County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $7.44 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0 PLC vs $7.44 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $3.05, up to $16.62 per base acre at the $2.20 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $14.47 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $3.35, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $7.44 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
