Should I pick ARC or PLC for oats in Schleicher County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $6.96 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0 PLC vs $6.96 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $3.05, up to $24.06 per base acre at the $2.20 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $13.53 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $3.35, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $6.96 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
