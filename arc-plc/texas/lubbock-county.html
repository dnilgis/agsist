Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (1,456.7 lb) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (519.05 lb), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans PLC: expected about $20.29 more per base acre; ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $21.66 PLC vs $1.36 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings.

PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.4200, up to $148.58 per base acre at the $0.3000 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. With prices centered at $0.4114, PLC is expected to pay $21.66 and ARC-CO $1.36 per base acre.
