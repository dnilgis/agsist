Sorghum 2026 in Hays County: Leans ARC-CO

All sorghum counties.

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $5.04 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $12.97 PLC vs $18.01 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings.

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.67, up to $116.47 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $34.47 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios.
