Questions about Bell County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Bell County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $2.39 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $0.57 PLC vs $2.97 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $136.68 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $52.92 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.57 and ARC-CO $2.97 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Bell County, Texas is 103.1 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
