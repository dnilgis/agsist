Peanuts

PLCLeans

PLC leads across the 11 price years we ran, but that swings from year to year. ARC-CO did not come out ahead in any of them. Peanuts have too few years of their own prices in FSA’s tables, so their price swings are corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them. So it stops at Leans.

PLC only pays if peanuts average under 31.5 cents a pound for 2026.

2027: no 2027 price yet

ARC-CO pays here for 2026 if the county yield comes in under 3,917 lb at USDA’s price outlook, $0.2400. That is above the 3,316 lb benchmark, so at that price it pays even on a normal crop.

Questions about Young County

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Young County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Young County, Texas is 26.3 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for seed cotton in Young County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for seed cotton in Young County, Texas is 458 lb (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $0.4311 per pound. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for peanuts in Young County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans PLC: ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (PLC paid more in 11 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.3150, up to $239.09 per base acre at the $0.1950 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $106.55 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 11 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $0.2400, the expected payments are not printed. Peanuts have too few years of their own prices in FSA’s tables, so their price swings are corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them.
