Questions about Bailey County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for seed cotton in Bailey County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Leans PLC: expected about $16.23 more per base acre; ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $17.28 PLC vs $1.05 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.4200, up to $118.56 per base acre at the $0.3000 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $58.31 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 2 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $0.4114, PLC is expected to pay $17.28 and ARC-CO $1.05 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for seed cotton in Bailey County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for seed cotton in Bailey County, Texas is 1,325.98 lb (irrigated); 418.66 lb (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $0.4311 per pound. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
