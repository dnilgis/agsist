Oats 2026 in Potter County: Leans ARC-CO

All oats counties.

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $12.55 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0 PLC vs $12.55 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings.

PLC pays on any season-average price below $3.05, up to $29.12 per base acre at the $2.20 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $24.38 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $3.35, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $12.55 per base acre.
