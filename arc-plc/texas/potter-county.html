Should I pick ARC or PLC for oats in Potter County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9; expected payments not printed, not enough price history of its own). Too little price history for a clear pick: shown as Leans. PLC pays on any season-average price below $3.05, up to $29.14 per base acre at the $2.20 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. How it is figured, every number and sources

How the checks are figured. Per base acre at FSA’s county average PLC yield, 85% payment factor in, before sequestration and payment limits. 2025 (paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them; the law pays the higher program): ARC-CO is the FSA posted rate (county file of Nov 2, 2026) times 85%; PLC is from FSA’s posted prices, the 2025 effective reference price minus the final 2025 price (tables of Nov 10, 2026), times the PLC yield, times 85%. 2026, our estimate (paid on or after Oct 1, 2027; only the enrolled program pays): the average over 11 past years’ price swings around USDA’s 2026 price (wheat $6.20; USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026), normal county crop; a lower price or a short county crop raises it. Wheat: PLC yield 26.64 bu, ARC-CO rate $0.00, PLC $6.35 minus $5.06 = $1.29 a bushel. FSA figures: FSA ARC/PLC program data. 5.7% is the sequestration rate reported for these payments; the rate is set each fiscal year and can change; source: Farm CPA Report, sequestration and the 2025 limit.

Wheat, 2026: Price range from how far USDA’s November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle.
