Corn 2026 in Wheeler County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

All practices, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $1.21 PLC vs $32.83 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 9 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $223.89 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $77.35 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 9 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.21 and ARC-CO $32.83 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $13.38 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $12.67 PLC vs $26.05 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 9 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
