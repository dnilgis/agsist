Corn 2026 in Otsego County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

All practices, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $0.69 PLC vs $25.64 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $140.93 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $74.60 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.69 and ARC-CO $25.64 per base acre.
