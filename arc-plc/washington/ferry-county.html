Questions about Ferry County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Ferry County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $0.30 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $7.00 PLC vs $7.30 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $84.95 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $34.03 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $7.00 and ARC-CO $7.30 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Ferry County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Ferry County, Washington is 47.80 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for barley in Ferry County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for barley in Ferry County, Washington is 43.10 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.43 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for oats in Ferry County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for oats in Ferry County, Washington is 65.14 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $3.94 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
