Questions about Grant County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Grant County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.77 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $12.64 PLC vs $17.41 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $153.36 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $81.16 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $12.64 and ARC-CO $17.41 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
