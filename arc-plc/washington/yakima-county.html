Wheat 2026 in Yakima County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (68.7 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (25.06 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $35.28 PLC vs $40.15 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $153.58 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $88.65 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $35.28 and ARC-CO $40.15 per base acre.

2027: Pick ARC-CO (expected $35.28 PLC vs $40.15 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
