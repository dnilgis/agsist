Questions about Walla Walla County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Walla Walla County for 2026?

For a typical farm (non-irrigated): Close, either one: about $1.25 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $13.25 PLC vs $12.00 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 6 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $160.73 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $55.93 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $13.25 and ARC-CO $12.00 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Walla Walla County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Walla Walla County, Washington is 105.52 bu (irrigated); 78.56 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for barley in Walla Walla County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $11.40 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $3.77 PLC vs $15.17 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 8).
