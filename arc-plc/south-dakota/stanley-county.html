Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Stanley County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: too small or too uncertain to call (PLC paid more in 6 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $76.23 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $27.63 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, the expected payments are not printed.
