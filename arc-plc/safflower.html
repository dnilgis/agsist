Quick answer. The 2026 PLC effective reference price for safflower is $0.2438/lb (FSA). PLC pays when the national season-average price ends below that; the loan rate, $0.1110/lb, is as low as the price counts. ARC-CO pays when county revenue falls below 90% of benchmark revenue, figured at the 2026 benchmark price of $0.2773/lb.

At FSA’s projected 2026/27 season-average price (FSA table of Sep 11, 2026), $0.2150/lb, the 2026 PLC rate would be $0.0288/lb. The real rate comes from the final price after the marketing year ends (Sept 2026 to Aug 2027).

How the safflower prices are figured

The effective reference price is the lesser of 115% of the statutory price, or the greater of the statutory price and 88% of the Olympic average season-average price for the five most recent crop years (2020 to 2024 for 2026; 2021 to 2025 for 2027). The ARC-CO benchmark price uses the same five years, each raised to at least the effective reference price.

2026 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2020: $0.2150 · 2021: $0.2550 · 2022: $0.3330 · 2023: $0.3600 · 2024: $0.2430 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $0.2770. 88% of that is $0.2438, above the $0.2375 statutory price and under the $0.2731 cap (115%).

2026 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2020: $0.2438 (raised from $0.2150) · 2021: $0.2550 · 2022: $0.3330 · 2023: $0.3600 · 2024: $0.2438 (raised from $0.2430). Drop the high ($0.3600) and low ($0.2438); the middle three average $0.2773.

2027. These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.2080/lb), and the answer depends on it.

2026 figures are FSA’s and match our math from FSA’s own price column. 2027 is our math (est.); FSA publishes the official figures.

Questions about safflower

What is the 2026 effective reference price for safflower?

$0.2438 per pound, from FSA's 2026 table. The statutory reference price is $0.2375; 88% of the 2020 to 2024 Olympic average season-average price is $0.2438, and the cap is $0.2731. The 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price is $0.2773 and the loan rate is $0.1110.

What is the 2027 effective reference price for safflower?

Not yet. These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.2080/lb), and the answer depends on it.

Will PLC pay on safflower for 2026?

At FSA table of Sep 11, 2026's projected 2026/27 price of $0.2150/lb, the 2026 PLC rate would be $0.0288/lb. FSA's own table of Sep 11, 2026 shows $0.0288/lb at $0.2150. The real rate is set by the final season-average price, published after the marketing year (Sept 2026 to Aug 2027) ends.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for safflower?

We do not give a pick for safflower yet. FSA’s price table on file starts with 2020/21, so it holds only 4 years of price change to pair with county yields. The county pages show FSA's official benchmark, where each program pays more by price, and the calculator runs your own PLC yield and price.

Where do I find my county's safflower ARC-CO benchmark yield?

FSA's 2026 county file lists safflower benchmarks for 159 counties; each one has a page here. A county FSA does not list has no safflower benchmark; ask the county FSA office.
