Corn 2026 in Sherburne County: leans ARC-CO

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (146.1 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (106.73 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $14.51 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $1.21 PLC vs $15.73 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $248.37 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $117.84 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.21 and ARC-CO $15.73 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $7.37 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $12.65 PLC vs $20.02 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
