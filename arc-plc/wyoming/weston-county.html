Wheat 2026 in Weston County: Close, either one

All practices, 2026: Close, either one: about $0.30 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $4.72 PLC vs $4.41 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 6 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $57.23 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop.
