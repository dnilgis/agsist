Nebraska ARC-CO benchmark yields for 2027, by county

Our estimate of each county’s 2027 ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn and soybeans: the Olympic average of USDA NASS county yields for 2021 to 2025. FSA’s official benchmark can differ; it uses RMA data first, a plug yield and a trend adjustment. Your county FSA office has the official number. Data updated Oct 6, 2026

2027 prices that apply in every county. Effective reference price: corn $4.34, soybeans $10.62. ARC-CO benchmark price: corn $4.96, soybeans $12.11. At the county’s benchmark yield, ARC-CO pays when the season-average price ends at $4.46 or lower for corn and $10.89 or lower for soybeans.
