Corn 2026 in McDowell County: leans ARC-CO

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $16.42 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $0.84 PLC vs $17.26 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $171.70 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $65.48 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.84 and ARC-CO $17.26 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $7.94 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $8.74 PLC vs $16.68 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
