Soybeans 2026 in Nash County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

All practices, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $0 PLC vs $10.96 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $10.71, up to $86.30 per base acre at the $6.82 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $40.24 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $12.00, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $10.96 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $7.62 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $2.15 PLC vs $9.78 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).
