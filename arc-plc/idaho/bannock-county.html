Wheat 2026 in Bannock County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (43.6 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (27.60 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $22.39 PLC vs $39.23 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $97.47 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $86.48 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $22.39 and ARC-CO $39.23 per base acre.
