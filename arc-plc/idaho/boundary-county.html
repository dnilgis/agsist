Questions about Boundary County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Boundary County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $1.48 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $13.25 PLC vs $14.72 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $160.73 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $68.65 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $13.25 and ARC-CO $14.72 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Boundary County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Boundary County, Idaho is 96.43 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for canola in Boundary County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for canola in Boundary County, Idaho is 1,964.06 lb (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $0.2595 per pound. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for barley in Boundary County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $11.14 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $4.88 PLC vs $16.03 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 8). Capped at Leans: this crop’s price spread is sized to corn’s October swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $5.58, up to $168.87 per base acre at the $2.75 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $56.48 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 8 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $5.70, PLC is expected to pay $4.88 and ARC-CO $16.03 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
