Corn 2026 in Rooks County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (84.4 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (71.10 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $0.70 PLC vs $16.62 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $143.48 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $77.80 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.70 and ARC-CO $16.62 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $3.60 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $7.31 PLC vs $10.91 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3).
