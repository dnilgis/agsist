Corn 2026 in Barton County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (133.5 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (81.06 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $1.11 PLC vs $25.33 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $226.95 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $104.31 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.11 and ARC-CO $25.33 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $5.19 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $11.56 PLC vs $16.75 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
