Corn 2026 in DeKalb County: too close to call

Non-irrigated, 2026: Too close to call (expected $11.03 PLC vs $20.80 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4; the gap is under $11.95).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $204.17 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop.
