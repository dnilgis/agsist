Corn 2026 in Wood County: Leans ARC-CO

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.79 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $1.02 PLC vs $5.81 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $242.25 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $103.65 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.02 and ARC-CO $5.81 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $8.41 more per base acre; but the gap is within the normal swing, so PLC is a defensible choice (expected $11.07 PLC vs $19.48 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).
