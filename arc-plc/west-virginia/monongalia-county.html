Corn

ARC-COLeans

ARC-CO comes out about $3 more per base acre, but that swings from year to year. PLC did not come out ahead in any of the 11 price years we ran.

PLC only pays if corn averages under $4.42 a bushel for 2026.

2027: ARC-CO

ARC-CO pays here for 2026 if the county yield comes in under 130 bu at USDA’s price outlook, $4.70.

90% of the 135.7 bu benchmark × the $5.03 benchmark price, divided by $4.70.

At $4.20, 50 cents under USDA’s $4.70 for 2026: PLC $17.62, ARC-CO $37.72 per base acre (typical farm).

2025, our estimate, not a USDA figure: higher of PLC $20.82 or ARC-CO $75.96 per base acre, typical farm, before 5.7% sequestration.

Oats

ARC-COLeans

ARC-CO leads across the 11 price years we ran, but that swings from year to year. PLC did not come out ahead in any of them. Oats have too few years of their own prices in FSA’s tables, so their price swings are corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them. So it stops at Leans.

PLC only pays if oats average under $3.05 a bushel for 2026.

2027: no 2027 price yet

ARC-CO pays here for 2026 if the county yield comes in under 64.6 bu at USDA’s price outlook, $3.35. That is above the 61.0 bu benchmark, so at that price it pays even on a normal crop.

90% of the 61.0 bu benchmark × the $3.94 benchmark price, divided by $3.35.

At $2.85, 50 cents under USDA’s $3.35 for 2026: PLC $7.99, ARC-CO $24.53 per base acre (typical farm).

2025, our estimate, not a USDA figure: higher of PLC $0 or ARC-CO $24.68 per base acre, typical farm, before 5.7% sequestration.

Soybeans

Neither expected to payClose

Neither one is likely to pay on soybeans for 2026 at USDA’s price outlook ($12.00).

PLC only pays if soybeans average under $10.71 a bushel for 2026.

2027: ARC-CO

ARC-CO pays here for 2026 if the county yield comes in under 43 bu at USDA’s price outlook, $12.00.

90% of the 47.4 bu benchmark × the $12.17 benchmark price, divided by $12.00.

At $11.50, 50 cents under USDA’s $12.00 for 2026: PLC $0, ARC-CO $0 per base acre (typical farm).

2025, our estimate, not a USDA figure: higher of PLC $5.00 or ARC-CO $61.63 per base acre, typical farm, before 5.7% sequestration.

Questions about Monongalia County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Monongalia County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $3.23 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0.67 PLC vs $3.90 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $160.14 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $69.62 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.67 and ARC-CO $3.90 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Monongalia County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Monongalia County, West Virginia is 135.7 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for oats in Monongalia County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 9; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $3.05, up to $33.96 per base acre at the $2.20 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $24.53 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 9 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $3.35, the expected payments are not printed. Oats have too few years of their own prices in FSA’s tables, so their price swings are corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them. When is my 2025 payment?

FSA's final 2025 ARC-CO county rates are in its file of Sep 30, 2026 and its 2025 prices in its table of Oct 9, 2026. We have no FSA notice of the 2025 payment date that we can check, so see your FSA office for the date. For 2025 a farm gets the higher of ARC-CO or PLC.
