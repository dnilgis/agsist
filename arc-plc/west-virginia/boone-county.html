Corn 2026 in Boone County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

All practices, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $0.57 PLC vs $18.71 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $117.30 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $72.04 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.57 and ARC-CO $18.71 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $11.69 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $5.97 PLC vs $17.66 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Boone County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Pick ARC-CO (expected $0.57 PLC vs $18.71 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $117.30 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Boone County?
