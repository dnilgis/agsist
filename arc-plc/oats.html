Quick answer. The 2026 PLC effective reference price for oats is $3.05/bu (FSA). PLC pays when the national season-average price ends below that; the loan rate, $2.20/bu, is as low as the price counts. ARC-CO pays when county revenue falls below 90% of benchmark revenue, figured at the 2026 benchmark price of $3.94/bu.

At USDA’s projected 2026/27 season-average price (WASDE, Oct 9, 2026), $3.35/bu, PLC would pay nothing for 2026. The real rate comes from the final price after the marketing year ends (June 2026 to May 2027).

2027 (est.): ERP $3.05, benchmark price $3.94.

How the oats prices are figured

The effective reference price is the lesser of 115% of the statutory price, or the greater of the statutory price and 88% of the Olympic average season-average price for the five most recent crop years (2020 to 2024 for 2026; 2021 to 2025 for 2027). The ARC-CO benchmark price uses the same five years, each raised to at least the effective reference price.

2026 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2020: $2.77 · 2021: $4.55 · 2022: $4.57 · 2023: $3.92 · 2024: $3.35 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $3.9400. 88% of that is $3.47, above the $3.05 cap (115%), so the cap holds: $3.05.

2026 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2020: $3.05 (raised from $2.77) · 2021: $4.55 · 2022: $4.57 · 2023: $3.92 · 2024: $3.35. Drop the high ($4.57) and low ($3.05); the middle three average $3.94.

2027 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2021: $4.55 · 2022: $4.57 · 2023: $3.92 · 2024: $3.35 · 2025: $3.23 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $3.9400. 88% of that is $3.47, above the $3.05 cap (115%), so the cap holds: $3.05.

2027 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2021: $4.55 · 2022: $4.57 · 2023: $3.92 · 2024: $3.35 · 2025: $3.23. Drop the high ($4.57) and low ($3.23); the middle three average $3.94.

2026 figures are FSA’s and match our math from FSA’s own price column. 2027 is our math (est.); FSA publishes the official figures.

Questions about oats

What is the 2026 effective reference price for oats?

$3.05 per bushel, from FSA's 2026 table. The statutory reference price is $2.65; 88% of the 2020 to 2024 Olympic average season-average price is $3.47, and the cap is $3.05. The 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price is $3.94 and the loan rate is $2.20.

What is the 2027 effective reference price for oats?

$3.05 per bushel by our math from FSA's final prices (est.); the 2027 ARC-CO benchmark price is $3.94. FSA publishes the official 2027 figures.

Will PLC pay on oats for 2026?

At USDA WASDE, Oct 9, 2026's projected 2026/27 price of $3.35/bu, PLC would pay nothing for 2026. FSA's own table of Sep 11, 2026 shows no payment at $3.35. The real rate is set by the final season-average price, published after the marketing year (June 2026 to May 2027) ends.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for oats?

We do not give a pick for oats yet. The county pages show FSA's official benchmark, where each program pays more by price, and the calculator runs your own PLC yield and price.

Where do I find my county's oats ARC-CO benchmark yield?

FSA's 2026 county file lists oats benchmarks for 2,343 counties; each one has a page here. A county FSA does not list has no oats benchmark; ask the county FSA office.
