Corn 2026 in Hawkins County: ARC-CO expected to pay more

All practices, 2026: Pick ARC-CO (expected $0.80 PLC vs $28.97 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 2 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 5).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $164.39 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $76.34 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.80 and ARC-CO $28.97 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $21.74 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $8.37 PLC vs $30.11 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3).
