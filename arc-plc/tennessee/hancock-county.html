How we call it. Clear pick when one program averages at least $2.00 more per base acre, the gap is more than t times its standard error (2.26 for 10 years, 2.23 for 11), and it paid more in at least 3 of the years. Leans when the gap is at least $3.00 and one standard error, and it paid more in at least 3 years. Close otherwise. When both round to $0: neither expected to pay.

Corn 2026 in Hancock County: Leans ARC-CO

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.01 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0.61 PLC vs $4.62 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $145.52 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $82.53 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.61 and ARC-CO $4.62 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $9.19 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $6.65 PLC vs $15.83 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.

Questions about Hancock County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Hancock County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.01 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0.61 PLC vs $4.62 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $145.52 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $82.53 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.61 and ARC-CO $4.62 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Hancock County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Hancock County, Tennessee is 160.86 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
