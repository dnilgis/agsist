Corn

ARC-COLeans

ARC-CO comes out about $4 more per base acre, but that swings from year to year. PLC did not come out ahead in any of the 11 price years we ran.

PLC only pays if corn averages under $4.42 a bushel for 2026.

2027: leans ARC-CO

ARC-CO pays here for 2026 if the county yield comes in under 172 bu at USDA’s price outlook, $4.70.

90% of the 179.4 bu benchmark × the $5.03 benchmark price, divided by $4.70.

At $4.20, 50 cents under USDA’s $4.70 for 2026: PLC $22.37, ARC-CO $49.87 per base acre (typical farm).

2025, our estimate, not a USDA figure: higher of PLC $26.42 or ARC-CO $103.47 per base acre, typical farm, before 5.7% sequestration.

Questions about Loudon County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Loudon County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.31 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0.85 PLC vs $5.16 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $203.32 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $92.05 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.85 and ARC-CO $5.16 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

When is my 2025 payment?
