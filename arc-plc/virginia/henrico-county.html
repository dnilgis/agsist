Questions about Henrico County

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for soybeans in Henrico County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for soybeans in Henrico County, Virginia is 46.0 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $12.17 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
