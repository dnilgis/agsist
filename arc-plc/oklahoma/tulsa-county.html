How the checks are figured. Per base acre at FSA’s county average PLC yield, 85% payment factor in, before sequestration and payment limits. 2025 (paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them; the law pays the higher program): ARC-CO is the FSA posted rate (county file of Nov 2, 2026) times 85%; PLC is from FSA’s posted prices, the 2025 effective reference price minus the final 2025 price (tables of Nov 10, 2026), times the PLC yield, times 85%. 2026, our estimate (paid on or after Oct 1, 2027; only the enrolled program pays): the average over 11 past years’ price swings around USDA’s 2026 price (wheat $6.20, soybeans $12.10, corn $4.60; USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026), normal county crop; a lower price or a short county crop raises it. Wheat: PLC yield 33.47 bu, ARC-CO rate $28.79, PLC $6.35 minus $5.06 = $1.29 a bushel; Soybeans: PLC yield 15.15 bu, ARC-CO rate $37.15, PLC $10.71 minus $10.50 = $0.21 a bushel; Corn: PLC yield 62.67 bu, ARC-CO rate $31.53, PLC $4.42 minus $4.16 = $0.26 a bushel. FSA figures: FSA ARC/PLC program data. 5.7% is the sequestration rate reported for these payments; the rate is set each fiscal year and can change; source: Farm CPA Report, sequestration and the 2025 limit.

Questions about Tulsa County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for wheat in Tulsa County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Close, either one: about $0.47 apart, too small or too uncertain to call (expected $6.04 PLC vs $5.57 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 6 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2). PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $74.82 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $28.63 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 8 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.20, PLC is expected to pay $6.04 and ARC-CO $5.57 per base acre. Price range from how far USDA’s November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

When is my 2025 payment?

FSA's final 2025 ARC-CO county rates are in its file of Nov 2, 2026 and its 2025 prices in its table of Nov 10, 2026. 2025 payments are paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them. For 2025 a farm gets the higher of ARC-CO (the FSA posted rate) or PLC (from FSA's posted prices). For a typical farm in Tulsa County, per base acre before sequestration: wheat: higher of PLC $36.70 or ARC-CO $24.47; soybeans: higher of PLC $2.70 or ARC-CO $31.58; corn: higher of PLC $13.85 or ARC-CO $26.80.
