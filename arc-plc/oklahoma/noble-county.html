Soybeans 2026 in Noble County: too close to call

All practices, 2026: Too close to call (expected $0 PLC vs $1.66 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 1; the gap is under $3.32).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $10.71, up to $42.98 per base acre at the $6.82 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $17.54 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 1 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $12.00, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $1.66 per base acre.

2027: Too close to call (expected $1.07 PLC vs $2.00 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2; the gap is under $2.24). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
