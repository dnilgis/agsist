Soybeans 2026 in Cotton County: too close to call

All practices, 2026: Too close to call (expected $0 PLC vs $2.06 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 9 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3; the gap is under $2.50).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $10.71, up to $31.74 per base acre at the $6.82 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $32.66 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 3 of the 9 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $12.00, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $2.06 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $5.51 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $0.88 PLC vs $6.39 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 9 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3).
