What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Kiowa County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Kiowa County, Oklahoma is 27.4 bu (non-irrigated). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for seed cotton in Kiowa County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): Leans PLC: ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (PLC paid more in 8 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0; no dollar figure, because the price swings are borrowed from corn’s). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.4200, up to $99.93 per base acre at the $0.3000 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $88.74 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 1 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $0.4147, the expected payments are not printed. Seed cotton has too few years of its own prices in FSA’s tables, so its price swings are corn’s, and the expected dollars would rest on them. We do not print them.
