Questions about Osceola County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Osceola County for 2026?

For a typical farm (non-irrigated): ARC-CO expected to pay more on these assumptions (expected $4.16 PLC vs $14.53 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $281.86 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $111.01 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.60, PLC is expected to pay $4.16 and ARC-CO $14.53 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
