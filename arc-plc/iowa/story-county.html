Corn 2026 in Story County: too close to call

All practices, 2026: Too close to call (expected $14.56 PLC vs $16.79 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2; the gap is under $12.10).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $269.45 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $114.83 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 3 of the 10 past-year scenarios. Centered on USDA’s $4.80 projection, PLC is expected to pay $14.56 and ARC-CO $16.79 per base acre.
