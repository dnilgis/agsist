Corn 2026 in Delaware County: Leans ARC-CO

All practices, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $5.38 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $1.16 PLC vs $6.54 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $275.91 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $116.69 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.16 and ARC-CO $6.54 per base acre.
