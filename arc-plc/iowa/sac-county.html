Questions about Sac County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for soybeans in Sac County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Neither expected to pay (expected $0 PLC vs $0 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). PLC pays on any season-average price below $10.71, up to $157.39 per base acre at the $6.82 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $82.21 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 0 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $12.00, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $0 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for soybeans in Sac County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for soybeans in Sac County, Iowa is 66.2 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $12.17 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
