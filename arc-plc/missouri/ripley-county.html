Soybeans 2026 in Ripley County: leans ARC-CO

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (33.5 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (29.88 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $7.92 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $0 PLC vs $7.92 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $10.71, up to $110.77 per base acre at the $6.82 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $68.29 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 2 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $12.00, PLC is expected to pay $0 and ARC-CO $7.92 per base acre.

2027: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $13.75 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $2.76 PLC vs $16.52 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 3). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
