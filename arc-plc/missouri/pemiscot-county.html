Deadlines

  • Dec 112026 signup ends Dec 11, 2026.
  • Nov 2 to Mar 152027 signup is open through Mar 15, 2027.
  • Skip itDo nothing for 2026 and that farm gets no 2026 payment.
  • New baseA 2025 law added 30 million base acres nationwide. More acres qualified than that cap, so FSA cut every new allocation by 3.69%.
  • SignProducers with a share in the crop sign. FSA says a landowner with no share interest, such as a cash-rent landlord, cannot enroll.
  • BringBring the farm’s FSA-156EZ. It lists the base acres and PLC yields.

Dates: USDA Farm Service Agency ARC/PLC page (read Nov 12, 2026) and its Sep 15, 2026 release.

Contact your county FSA office to complete enrollment; FSA says to make an appointment there to make your elections. Landowners can see their base allocation notice online at fsa.usda.gov/arc-plc with a Login.gov account.

How it is figured, every number and sources

How the checks are figured. Per base acre at FSA’s county average PLC yield, 85% payment factor in, before sequestration and payment limits. 2025 (paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them; the law pays the higher program): ARC-CO is the FSA posted rate (county file of Nov 2, 2026) times 85%; PLC is from FSA’s posted prices, the 2025 effective reference price minus the final 2025 price (tables of Nov 10, 2026), times the PLC yield, times 85%. 2026, our estimate (paid on or after Oct 1, 2027; only the enrolled program pays): the average over 11 past years’ price swings around USDA’s 2026 price (soybeans $12.10, wheat $6.20; USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026), normal county crop; a lower price or a short county crop raises it. Soybeans: PLC yield 38.73 bu, ARC-CO rate $71.28, PLC $10.71 minus $10.50 = $0.21 a bushel; Seed cotton: PLC yield 1,849.73 lb, ARC-CO rate $151.32, PLC $0.4200 minus $0.3408 = $0.0792 a pound; Wheat: PLC yield 53.72 bu, ARC-CO rate $36.44, PLC $6.35 minus $5.06 = $1.29 a bushel; Long grain rice: PLC yield 6,330.35 lb, ARC-CO rate $159.35, PLC $0.1690 minus $0.1010 = $0.0680 a pound. FSA figures: FSA ARC/PLC program data. 5.7% is the sequestration rate reported for these payments; the rate is set each fiscal year and can change; source: Farm CPA Report, sequestration and the 2025 limit.

Wheat, 2026: Price range from how far USDA’s November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle.
