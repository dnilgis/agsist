2026. The 2026 crop is mostly harvested, so each scenario uses a normal Pemiscot County crop (FSA’s 2026 benchmark yield) and only the price moves. Prices start from USDA’s projected 2026/27 season-average prices (USDA WASDE, Oct 9, 2026: soybeans $12.00, wheat $6.30, long grain rice $0.1400, corn $4.70, sorghum $4.50; FSA table of Sep 11, 2026: seed cotton $0.4114, medium and short grain rice $0.1500, sunflower seed $0.2470). Around that price we ran each past year with a final USDA price: how far that year’s final season-average price ended from October futures, for corn and soybeans. Seed cotton, wheat, long grain rice, sorghum, medium and short grain rice, sunflower seed have no October futures history of their own, so they borrow corn’s October swings (wheat its own price changes, scaled to corn’s size) and stop at Leans.

2027. Corn and soybeans start from futures: corn $4.91 (Dec 2027 corn futures $5.12 (Oct 9, 2026) × 0.959, the 2011 to 2025 average of final MYA / February futures); soybeans $12.17 (Nov 2027 soybean futures $12.45 (Oct 9, 2026) × 0.978, the 2011 to 2025 average of final MYA / February futures). Seed cotton, sorghum, medium and short grain rice, sunflower seed wait on USDA’s closing 2025 price, which sets the 2027 reference price.

Typical farm. FSA’s county average PLC yield (soybeans 38.7 bu; seed cotton 1,849.7 lb; wheat 53.7 bu; long grain rice 6,330.3 lb; corn 137.4 bu; sorghum 80.2 bu; medium and short grain rice 5,781.4 lb; sunflower seed 719.0 lb) and FSA’s official benchmark. “Lower” and “Higher” mean 15% under or over the county average. Payments are per base acre and count FSA’s 85% payment acres, before sequestration and payment limits.

How we call it. Clear pick when one program averages at least $2.00 more per base acre, the gap is more than t times its standard error (2.26 for 10 years, 2.23 for 11), and it paid more in at least 3 of the years. Leans when the gap is at least $3.00 and one standard error, and it paid more in at least 3 years. Close otherwise. When both round to $0: neither expected to pay.
