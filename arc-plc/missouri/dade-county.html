Corn 2026 in Dade County: too close to call

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (117.3 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (108.35 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Too close to call (expected $10.77 PLC vs $15.98 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2; the gap is under $10.15).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $199.41 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $94.50 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 3 of the 10 past-year scenarios. Centered on USDA’s $4.80 projection, PLC is expected to pay $10.77 and ARC-CO $15.98 per base acre.

2027: Too close to call (expected $9.98 PLC vs $13.88 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2; the gap is under $7.34). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
