PLC vs ARC-CO break-even at the benchmark yield

When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, which program pays more depends on the season-average price and on your PLC yield as a share of the county’s official benchmark. Expressed that way, the break-even is the same in every Arkansas county. 2026 prices, corn:

PLC yield, % of your official benchmarkWhich pays more, by season-average price
60%PLC: $3.41 or lower
ARC-CO: $3.42 to $4.52
70%PLC: $3.55 or lower
ARC-CO: $3.56 to $4.52
80%PLC: $3.66 or lower
ARC-CO: $3.67 to $4.52
90%PLC: $3.74 or lower
ARC-CO: $3.75 to $4.52
100%PLC: $3.81 or lower
