Prices that apply in every county. 2026 effective reference price: corn $4.42, soybeans $10.71, wheat $6.35. 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price: corn $5.03, soybeans $12.17, wheat $6.98. When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, ARC-CO pays when the season-average price ends at $4.52 or lower for corn, $10.95 for soybeans and $6.28 for wheat.

Benchmark yield per acre (FSA, 2026), in bushels or, for peanuts, rice, pulses, seed cotton and most oilseeds, pounds. Practice: All = all practices, Irr. = irrigated, Non-irr. = non-irrigated. Revenue = yield × 2026 benchmark price, $/acre. The most ARC-CO can pay is 12% of revenue on 85% of base, 10.2% of revenue per base acre; each county page works it out.

PLC vs ARC-CO break-even at the benchmark yield

When the county yield comes in at its benchmark, which program pays more depends on the season-average price and on your PLC yield as a share of the county’s official benchmark. Expressed that way, the break-even is the same in every New Hampshire county. 2026 prices, corn:
