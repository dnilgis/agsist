  • New baseA 2025 law added 30 million base acres nationwide. More acres qualified than that cap, so FSA cut every new allocation by 3.69%.

How it is figured, every number and sources

How the checks are figured. Per base acre at FSA’s county average PLC yield, 85% payment factor in, before sequestration and payment limits. 2025 (paid on or after Oct 1, 2026, as FSA processes them; the law pays the higher program): ARC-CO is the FSA posted rate (county file of Nov 2, 2026) times 85%; PLC is from FSA’s posted prices, the 2025 effective reference price minus the final 2025 price (tables of Nov 10, 2026), times the PLC yield, times 85%. 2026, our estimate (paid on or after Oct 1, 2027; only the enrolled program pays): the average over 11 past years’ price swings around USDA’s 2026 price (wheat $6.20, corn $4.60; USDA WASDE, Nov 10, 2026), normal county crop; a lower price or a short county crop raises it. Wheat: PLC yield 37.71 bu, ARC-CO rate $11.56, PLC $6.35 minus $5.06 = $1.29 a bushel; Corn: PLC yield 77.70 bu, ARC-CO rate $0.00, PLC $4.42 minus $4.16 = $0.26 a bushel; Seed cotton: PLC yield 1,549.78 lb, ARC-CO rate $0.00, PLC $0.4200 minus $0.3408 = $0.0792 a pound; Peanuts: PLC yield 2,653.06 lb, ARC-CO rate $115.40, PLC $0.3150 minus $0.2350 = $0.0800 a pound. FSA figures: FSA ARC/PLC program data. 5.7% is the sequestration rate reported for these payments; the rate is set each fiscal year and can change; source: Farm CPA Report, sequestration and the 2025 limit.

Wheat, 2026: Price range from how far USDA’s November wheat price missed the final season-average price, 2015 to 2025 (11 years); for 2015 to 2018 USDA printed a range, and we use its middle.
