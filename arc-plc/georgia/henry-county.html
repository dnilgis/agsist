Wheat 2026 in Henry County: PLC expected to pay more

All practices, 2026: Pick PLC (expected $20.59 PLC vs $17.12 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 5 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $6.35, up to $89.64 per base acre at the $3.72 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $39.73 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 5 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $6.30, PLC is expected to pay $20.59 and ARC-CO $17.12 per base acre.
