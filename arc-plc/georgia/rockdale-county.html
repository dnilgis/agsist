Questions about Rockdale County

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Rockdale County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Rockdale County, Georgia is 100.67 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for wheat in Rockdale County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for wheat in Rockdale County, Georgia is 55.80 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $6.98 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for oats in Rockdale County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for oats in Rockdale County, Georgia is 59.00 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $3.94 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
