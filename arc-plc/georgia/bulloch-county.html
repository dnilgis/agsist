Should I pick ARC or PLC for peanuts in Bulloch County for 2026?

For a typical farm (non-irrigated): Leans PLC: expected about $91.42 more per base acre; ARC-CO did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $211.06 PLC vs $119.64 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 11 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 0). Capped at Leans: this crop borrows corn’s price swings. PLC pays on any season-average price below $0.3150, up to $337.68 per base acre at the $0.1950 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $137.87 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 11 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $0.2400, PLC is expected to pay $211.06 and ARC-CO $119.64 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
