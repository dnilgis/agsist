Antelope County, Nebraska: ARC or PLC

Data updated Nov 10, 2026

Antelope County, NE: for 2026 on a farm with the county’s typical PLC yield, corn (non-irrigated): ARC-CO, a clear pick; soybeans (non-irrigated): neither program is expected to pay, data updated Nov 10, 2026.

Questions about Antelope County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Antelope County for 2026?

For a typical farm (irrigated): ARC-CO expected to pay more on these assumptions (expected $4.15 PLC vs $16.51 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 6). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $280.84 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $126.13 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 6 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.60, PLC is expected to pay $4.15 and ARC-CO $16.51 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.
