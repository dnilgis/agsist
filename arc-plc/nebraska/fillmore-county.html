Corn 2026 in Fillmore County: leans ARC-CO

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (173.9 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (144.55 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $12.59 more per base acre, but the gap is within the normal swing; either is a defensible choice (expected $1.44 PLC vs $14.03 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $295.63 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $117.03 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 10 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.44 and ARC-CO $14.03 per base acre.

2027: Too close to call (expected $15.06 PLC vs $15.54 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 1 of 10 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 2; the gap is under $9.51). The 2027 view uses FSA’s 2026 benchmark; FSA posts 2027 later.
