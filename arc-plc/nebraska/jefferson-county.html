Corn 2026 in Jefferson County: Leans ARC-CO

Non-irrigated: no typical-farm verdict. FSA’s average PLC yield for the county (145.3 bu) covers all practices and is above this benchmark (129.19 bu), so it does not describe a typical farm here. Run your own PLC yield below.

Irrigated, 2026: Leans ARC-CO: expected about $5.31 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $1.04 PLC vs $6.35 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4).

PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $247.01 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $113.24 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $1.04 and ARC-CO $6.35 per base acre.
