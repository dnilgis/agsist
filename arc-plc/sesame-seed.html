Quick answer. The 2026 PLC effective reference price for sesame seed is $0.2731/lb (FSA). PLC pays when the national season-average price ends below that; the loan rate, $0.1110/lb, is as low as the price counts. ARC-CO pays when county revenue falls below 90% of benchmark revenue, figured at the 2026 benchmark price of $0.4000/lb.

How the sesame seed prices are figured

The effective reference price is the lesser of 115% of the statutory price, or the greater of the statutory price and 88% of the Olympic average season-average price for the five most recent crop years (2020 to 2024 for 2026; 2021 to 2025 for 2027). The ARC-CO benchmark price uses the same five years, each raised to at least the effective reference price.

2026 effective reference price. Season-average prices 2020: $0.3700 · 2021: $0.3900 · 2022: $0.4100 · 2023: $0.4000 · 2024: $0.5400 (struck: the high and low year). Average of the middle three: $0.4000. 88% of that is $0.3520, above the $0.2731 cap (115%), so the cap holds: $0.2731.

2026 ARC-CO benchmark price. 2020: $0.3700 · 2021: $0.3900 · 2022: $0.4100 · 2023: $0.4000 · 2024: $0.5400. Drop the high ($0.5400) and low ($0.3700); the middle three average $0.4000.

2027. These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.3300/lb), and the answer depends on it.

2026 figures are FSA’s and match our math from FSA’s own price column. 2027 is our math (est.); FSA publishes the official figures.

Questions about sesame seed

What is the 2026 effective reference price for sesame seed?

$0.2731 per pound, from FSA's 2026 table. The statutory reference price is $0.2375; 88% of the 2020 to 2024 Olympic average season-average price is $0.3520, and the cap is $0.2731. The 2026 ARC-CO benchmark price is $0.4000 and the loan rate is $0.1110.

What is the 2027 effective reference price for sesame seed?

Not yet. These figures wait on USDA’s closing 2025/26 season-average price; FSA’s table of Sep 11, 2026 still shows it as projected ($0.3300/lb), and the answer depends on it.

Will PLC pay on sesame seed for 2026?

At FSA table of Sep 11, 2026's projected 2026/27 price of $0.3800/lb, the 2026 PLC rate would be $0.0000/lb (none). FSA's own table of Sep 11, 2026 shows $0.0000/lb at $0.3800. The real rate is set by the final season-average price, published after the marketing year (Sept 2026 to Aug 2027) ends.

Should I pick ARC or PLC for sesame seed?

We do not give a pick for sesame seed yet. FSA’s price table on file starts with 2020/21, so it holds only 4 years of price change to pair with county yields.
