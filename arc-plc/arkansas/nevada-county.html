Questions about Nevada County

Should I pick ARC or PLC for corn in Nevada County for 2026?

For a typical farm (all practices): Leans ARC-CO: expected about $4.73 more per base acre; PLC did not pay more in any of the 11 years, but the gap is within the normal swing (expected $0.38 PLC vs $5.11 ARC-CO per base acre; PLC paid more in 0 of 11 past-year scenarios, ARC-CO in 4). PLC pays on any season-average price below $4.42, up to $90.10 per base acre at the $2.42 loan rate, so it protects against a deep price drop. ARC-CO is capped at $91.17 per base acre but also pays when the county’s yield is short; it paid something in 4 of the 11 past-year scenarios. With prices centered at $4.70, PLC is expected to pay $0.38 and ARC-CO $5.11 per base acre. Run your own PLC yield and base acres; the answer can change.

What is the ARC-CO benchmark yield for corn in Nevada County?

FSA's official 2026 benchmark yield for corn in Nevada County, Arkansas is 177.70 bu (all practices). The 2026 benchmark price is $5.03 per bushel. FSA has not posted the 2027 benchmark.
