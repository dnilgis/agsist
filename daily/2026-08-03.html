The Carry Spread Is the Crop Tour's Lie Detector
Corn's nearby at $4.38 versus December at $4.62 is a 24-cent carry. That spread widens when the market doubts the current crop and narrows when supply looks comfortable. Watch whether Pro Farmer yield estimates this week move that spread more than the outright price; the carry tells you what the funds actually believe about the harvest.
