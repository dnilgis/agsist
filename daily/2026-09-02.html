THE TAKEAWAY

Cattle's political supply shock is real; lock beef prices before the import story fully prices.

Cattle at $212.40 and feeders at $320.80 are barely off, but the news under them is worse than the ticks: Trump is opening lean beef imports AND Mexico resumes feeder shipments, two supply hits the same morning. Two markets, two completely different problems.

THE MORE YOU KNOW
The oil-share split: what soybean oil's 3% day says about crush margins
When soybean oil runs hard relative to meal, the oil-share of crush value widens. Today oil rose 3% while meal fell 0.9%. That gap pulls the crush margin higher and incentivizes processors to run beans harder. Watch whether the oil-share holds above 40% -- if it does, crush demand alone can support beans even without a fresh China purchase.
