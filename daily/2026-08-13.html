CATTLE SLIP ON CORN LIFTLOW CONVICTION
●DRIVERCorn ticked higher, pressuring feeder margins; JBS Q2 profitability pressured by cattle prices.
  • Live cattle at $230.50 and feeders at $346.30 both fell roughly 1%, with feeders leading lower as corn ticked higher and direct business stayed soft, per Brownfield's midday trade note.
  • The Cargill multi-plant lockout, ongoing since May 19 and still removing roughly 6,000 head of daily processing capacity, keeps the fed trade from resetting cleanly; processing-constrained dynamics mean a small corn move can rattle feeders disproportionately.
  • JBS reported Q2 revenue at a record but called profitability 'pressured' by cattle prices, which is the packer side of the same story: high cattle, tighter margins.
Cattle drifting lower on input-cost pressure, not demand; the lockout keeps price discovery cloudy.

THE MORE YOU KNOW
The Black Sea Basis No One Prices Until a Strike
Novorossiysk handles roughly 25-30% of Russia's grain export volume. When it goes offline, even briefly, North African and Middle Eastern buyers have to find alternative supply fast. That scramble shows up in US soft red winter wheat basis within 48-72 hours. Today's 3-cent wheat gain may be the opening bid on that shift.
