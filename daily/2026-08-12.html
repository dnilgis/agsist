THE MORE YOU KNOW
Soybean Oil's Quiet Takeover of the Crush
Soybean oil is up 2.1% today while meal added half a percent. When oil's share of crush value expands, processors favor running the crush harder, which pulls more beans through the system. At $69.62, oil is near the top of its 52-week range; if the WASDE shows tight old-crop bean stocks, the oil share story gets a second leg.

DISEASE
USDA Screwworm Sterile Fly Facility Opens 7 Months Early
A Texas facility to breed sterile flies for screwworm control will open well ahead of schedule, accelerating the eradication program that underpins the Aug. 24 phased border reopening at Douglas, AZ. Faster capacity means the reopening timeline could hold or even pull forward, which matters for feeder supply and the feeder/live spread heading into fall.
INPUTS
Fertilizer and Diesel Prices Higher Heading Into Fall Harvest
FarmDoc Daily reports both fertilizer and diesel are running above the prior two-year average as fall fieldwork approaches. Producers locking fall anhydrous now are paying more than 2024 or 2025 buyers did; with the Iran-Hormuz cycle keeping crude unsettled, diesel tightness is the input-cost story that hits combines and grain dryers first.
