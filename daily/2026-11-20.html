Hogs lower in cash, higher in futures
  • December lean hogs settled at $52.42½, up 2.1%, while cash hogs fell on a carcass base.
  • Packers killed about 494,000 hogs Thursday, against 487,000 a week ago.
Grains mixed overnight
  • December corn is at $4.37¼ overnight, down 1¾ cents from Thursday's $4.39 settle, and January soybeans are up 4¼ cents to $12.11¾.
