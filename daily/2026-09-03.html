●DRIVERDrovers profit tracker: packer leverage rising, feedlot margins under pressure; Cargill lockout ongoing.
  • Live cattle fell to $210.35, down 1%, still near the bottom of its 52-week range at 11% from the low. The Cargill multi-plant lockout, ongoing since May 19, is still removing roughly 6,000 head of daily processing capacity, keeping the processing constraint real.

THE MORE YOU KNOW
Harvest Pressure Has a Math Problem
December corn is at $5.305 today, but the cost-of-carry from nearby $5.075 implies harvest storage is only worth 2.3 cents per month -- well below the 4-5 cent commercial rate. When carry goes thin like this, the market is telling you to move grain now, not store it.
