GRAINS SIT STILL, CATTLE SLIP AGAIN BEFORE COF

THE ACTION

Live cattle at $218.95: nothing to do ahead of Cattle on Feed at 2:00 PM CT, but a close under $216.50 is the next sell.

Corn eased to $5.17, beans barely moved at $13.04, and neither market gave you a reason to do anything today. Cattle is the real story: down 0.8% to $218.95, a second straight session lower with USDA's Cattle on Feed still hours away at 2:00 PM CT.

Cattle Slips Again, Report LoomsMEDIUM CONVICTION
  • Live cattle gave back another 0.8% to $218.95, second straight lower close heading into today's Cattle on Feed at 2:00 PM CT.
  • Farm Bureau renewed its push against the extra 300,000 metric tons of beef import allowance Wednesday, a policy fight that hasn't moved cash cattle yet.
Two red closes ahead of data means the market is de-risking, not predicting the report.
Grains Stay ParkedLOW CONVICTION
  • Corn eased 0.4% to $5.17, beans barely ticked down to $13.04, both markets described by feedstuffs.com as quiet amid ongoing US-China trade talks.
  • No fresh confirmed China purchase news today to push beans, and no weather story to push corn; the calendar is empty and the tape knows it.
Quiet grains with US-China talks unresolved means the next move is news-driven, not chart-driven.
