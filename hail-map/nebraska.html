Key figures: Nebraska hail at a glance

  • Nebraska logged 3,950 National Weather Service hail reports statewide over the 4.8 years since January 2022 (2022–2026).
  • Douglas County leads Nebraska with 170 hail reports over that span, about 35.4 per year.
  • Reported hail in Nebraska peaks in June.
  • 3,263 of Nebraska's reports involved stones 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size.

Nebraska hail: the questions people ask

Where does it hail the most in Nebraska?

By reported hail over the 4.8 years since January 2022, the most active counties in Nebraska are Douglas, Cherry, Lincoln. Reports track population and spotter coverage as well as storms, so rural corridors can be under-counted. The table adds up the 4.8 years since January 2022 in one total per county, so it does not show whether a county leads year after year.

When is hail season in Nebraska?

Reported hail in Nebraska peaks around June. May through July hold 69% of reports in Nebraska. Any single year can break the pattern.

How much of Nebraska's hail is severe?

Severe hail is 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size, which dents roofs and vehicles. Crops can be hurt by smaller hail, especially with wind, and the loss depends on growth stage. The per-county severe share is in the table; statewide, hail of any size totaled 3,950 reports over the 4.8 years since January 2022.
