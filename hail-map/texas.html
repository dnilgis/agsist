Key figures: Texas hail at a glance

  • Texas logged 7,688 National Weather Service hail reports statewide over the 4.8 years since January 2022 (2022–2026).
  • Tarrant County leads Texas with 323 hail reports over that span, about 67.3 per year.
  • Reported hail in Texas peaks in May.
  • 6,567 of Texas's reports involved stones 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size.

Texas hail: the questions people ask

Where does it hail the most in Texas?

By reported hail over the 4.8 years since January 2022, the most active counties in Texas are Tarrant, Williamson, Denton. Reports track population and spotter coverage as well as storms, so rural corridors can be under-counted. The table adds up the 4.8 years since January 2022 in one total per county, so it does not show whether a county leads year after year.

When is hail season in Texas?

Reported hail in Texas peaks around May. April through June hold 75% of reports in Texas. Any single year can break the pattern.

How much of Texas's hail is severe?

Severe hail is 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size, which dents roofs and vehicles. Crops can be hurt by smaller hail, especially with wind, and the loss depends on growth stage. The per-county severe share is in the table; statewide, hail of any size totaled 7,688 reports over the 4.8 years since January 2022.
