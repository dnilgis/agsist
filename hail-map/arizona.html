Key figures: Arizona hail at a glance

  • Arizona logged 573 National Weather Service hail reports statewide over the 4.8 years since January 2022 (2022–2026).
  • Maricopa County leads Arizona with 210 hail reports over that span, about 43.8 per year.
  • Reported hail in Arizona peaks in August.
  • 182 of Arizona's reports involved stones 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size.

Arizona hail: the questions people ask

Where does it hail the most in Arizona?

By reported hail over the 4.8 years since January 2022, the most active counties in Arizona are Maricopa, Yavapai, Pima. Reports track population and spotter coverage as well as storms, so rural corridors can be under-counted. The table adds up the 4.8 years since January 2022 in one total per county, so it does not show whether a county leads year after year.

When is hail season in Arizona?

Reported hail in Arizona peaks around August. January, July, August and September hold 63% of reports in Arizona. Any single year can break the pattern.

How much of Arizona's hail is severe?

Severe hail is 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size, which dents roofs and vehicles. Crops can be hurt by smaller hail, especially with wind, and the loss depends on growth stage. The per-county severe share is in the table; statewide, hail of any size totaled 573 reports over the 4.8 years since January 2022.
