South Carolina hail: the questions people ask

Where does it hail the most in South Carolina?

By reported hail over the last 5 years, the most active counties in South Carolina are Lexington, Greenville, Berkeley. Reports track population and spotter coverage as well as storms, so rural corridors can be under-counted; the persistent leaders on this table are real hail geography.

When is hail season in South Carolina?

Reported hail in South Carolina peaks around May. April through June hold 69% of reports in South Carolina. Any single year can break the pattern.

How much of South Carolina's hail is severe?

Severe hail is 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size, which dents roofs and vehicles. Crops can be hurt by smaller hail, especially with wind, and the loss depends on growth stage. The per-county severe share is in the table; statewide, hail of any size totaled 669 reports over 5 years.
