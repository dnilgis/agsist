North Dakota hail: the questions people ask

Where does it hail the most in North Dakota?

By reported hail over the 4.8 years since January 2022, the most active counties in North Dakota are Burleigh, Grand Forks, Cass. Reports track population and spotter coverage as well as storms, so rural corridors can be under-counted. The table adds up the 4.8 years since January 2022 in one total per county, so it does not show whether a county leads year after year.

When is hail season in North Dakota?

Reported hail in North Dakota peaks around July. June and July hold 68% of reports in North Dakota. Any single year can break the pattern.

How much of North Dakota's hail is severe?

Severe hail is 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size, which dents roofs and vehicles. Crops can be hurt by smaller hail, especially with wind, and the loss depends on growth stage. The per-county severe share is in the table; statewide, hail of any size totaled 1,091 reports over the 4.8 years since January 2022.
