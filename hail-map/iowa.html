Key figures: Iowa hail at a glance

  • Iowa logged 2,729 National Weather Service hail reports statewide over the 4.8 years since January 2022 (2022–2026).
  • Linn County leads Iowa with 127 hail reports over that span, about 26.5 per year.
  • Reported hail in Iowa peaks in April.
  • 1,865 of Iowa's reports involved stones 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size.

Iowa hail: the questions people ask

Where does it hail the most in Iowa?

By reported hail over the 4.8 years since January 2022, the most active counties in Iowa are Linn, Scott, Story. Reports track population and spotter coverage as well as storms, so rural corridors can be under-counted. The table adds up the 4.8 years since January 2022 in one total per county, so it does not show whether a county leads year after year.

When is hail season in Iowa?

Reported hail in Iowa peaks around April. March through May hold 65% of reports in Iowa. Any single year can break the pattern.

How much of Iowa's hail is severe?

Severe hail is 1″ or larger, the National Weather Service severe size, which dents roofs and vehicles. Crops can be hurt by smaller hail, especially with wind, and the loss depends on growth stage. The per-county severe share is in the table; statewide, hail of any size totaled 2,729 reports over the 4.8 years since January 2022.
