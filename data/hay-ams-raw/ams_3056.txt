Compared to the last report, demand for all types of hay in Idaho is moderate. Demand for straw is picking up but there aren't a lot of trades 
yet with buyers shopping around. Movement has been slower this week in Idaho with mostly small loads being bought. prices for alfalfa are 
steady. Dairy's and feedlots are buying small loads at a time waiting for prices to come down before buying big lots, with diesel fuel prices 
reaching 
$6
 a gallon, most producers cant afford to deliver hay at cheaper prices. 
According to the U.S. Drought Monitor Website, most of the big hay producing areas in Idaho are under extreme to exceptional drought. 
68%
 
of the state is in moderate drought or worse, 
53%
 of the state is in severe drought or worse, 
34%
 of the state is in extreme drought or worse, 
7%
 of the state is in exceptional drought.
