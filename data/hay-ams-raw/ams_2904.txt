Compared to last report: Trade activity and demand good on retail hay and dairy hay.  In southeast California, Retail #1 alfalfa 3-tie bales sold 
$35-40/per ton higher than two weeks ago.  Growers are attributing the price jump due to a tightening supply.  According to the U.S. Drought 
Monitor, the week of 10/6/26 drought categories have 23.26% of the state in a D1 category, 48.86% of the state in a D0 category, and the rest 
of the state (27.88%) in no drought classification.  The next available hay report will be Friday, October 23, 2026.
Please Note: The above volumes (tonnage, acres, and bales) listed on this USDA LPGMN report are for confirmed trades only, it does not 
include estimated volume (tonnage) for bids or offers to the trade.
