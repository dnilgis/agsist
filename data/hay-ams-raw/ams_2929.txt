The state continues to be two very different stories between the have and have nots when it comes to moisture. North of the Missouri River 
conditions are generally good while south is far from anything good. The area of D3 or extreme drought continues to increase and even some 
D4 or exceptional drought showing now. There was some moisture this week and a few areas small areas did receive 3-4 inches, but as of this 
report most was in 1-2-inch amounts, very welcome but still a long ways from what is needed. In the south several producers are feeding hay 
or some supplement, and water issues also exist. As of this time producers are still easily able to find hay for sale, but fuel prices add a lot to 
getting it to the farm. The supply of hay is moderate to heavy, especially fair to good quality grass types of hay. Hay demand and movement 
have been moderate. Hay prices are steady to firm. The Missouri Department of Agriculture has a hay directory at 
https://apps.mda.mo.gov/haydirectory or visit the University of Missouri Extension feed stuff finder at https://feedstufffinder.org
