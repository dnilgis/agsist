Compared to the last report all reported forages sold steady. Demand was moderate to good. Alfalfa producers are on either fourth or fifth 
cutting with producers out west finishing up on third cutting.  Some reports in the central area that third cutting alfalfa had more tonnage 
than first cutting did. Some reports of 
1.5
 tons per acre. Amazing what water and decent weather can do for a crop!   Some producers are 
bailing cornstalk bales and are somewhat disappointed with the bales per pivot.  Several bales less than most years for some reason.   Fungus 
in the stalks is one reason for the lighter bales?  Fall harvest of corn and soybeans continues across the state. With several producers happy 
with bushels per that are rolling in.
