About AGSIST

Market data and tools for working US producers across grain, livestock, dairy, and specialty. Built by one person who got tired of the good stuff being locked up.

What This Is

AGSIST is an ag dashboard. Live futures across grain, livestock, dairy, and energy, plus local weather, spray and urea-loss conditions, USDA reports, prediction markets, calculators, and a daily briefing. All in one place, no login.

The good ag data has always sat behind subscriptions built for commodity desks, not for the people doing the actual work. I thought that was backwards. My own clients needed something better, so I built it.
