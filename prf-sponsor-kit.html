For crop insurance agencies

Put your agency's name on PRF in your states.

AGSIST runs a free Pasture, Rangeland, Forage calculator and a PRF page for every state. Ranchers use it to price coverage before the Dec 1 sales closing. One agency per state can sponsor that state: its state page and every county page in it. Take one state or as many as you like.

What ranchers get

  • Their grid by ZIP, town, county, coordinates or grid ID.
  • Premium, USDA's share and protection for their acres, coverage and intervals.
  • Every year since 1951 the setup would have paid, interval by interval.
  • Five coverage levels side by side, a share link and a one-page print sheet.
  • A state page with every county's USDA base values and the key dates.

Why agents can trust it

  • Built on USDA RMA's own 2027 rates and base values, refreshed weekly.
  • Its dollar figures matched RMA's own tool on every live policy checked.
  • Neutral by design: no lead forms, no "best" months, no pitch and no routing to an agent. Ranchers buy from the agent they choose.

The sponsor slot

One agency per state, as many states as you want. Your agency's name, logo, a one-line description, phone and a link, on that state's PRF page, on every county page in that state, and next to the result when a rancher picks that state in the calculator. Each one asks readers to support the businesses that keep AGSIST free.
  • Prepaid. Placement guaranteed through Dec 31, with first say on renewing for the next season.
  • Price on request. It depends on the state.
  • The page tells readers a sponsor may sell crop insurance and has no say over any figure. The numbers stay USDA's.
  • Traffic for your state is available on request. The PRF pages are new this fall, so ask for the latest figures.
