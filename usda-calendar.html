About These Reports

WASDE

World Agricultural Supply and Demand Estimates. Monthly global crop and livestock supply/demand balance. Released around the 10th of each month at 12:00 PM ET.

HIGH· Moves on surprises

Prospective Plantings

Farmer survey of intended spring planting acres for corn, soybeans, and wheat. Released late March. It shapes the market's acreage view for the year ahead.

EXTREME· Major price mover

Acreage

Planted and harvested acreage for major crops from the June survey, out the same day as the June 1 Grain Stocks. A surprise here can move the market as much as any report all year.

EXTREME· Can move limit on a surprise

Crop Production (Aug)

The August report has USDA's first survey-based corn and soybean yields of the season, replacing the trend-based numbers in earlier reports.

EXTREME· First survey yields

Grain Stocks (Sep 1)

The September 1 count is the most watched of the four quarterly grain stocks reports. It pins down the old-crop carryout that the new marketing year starts from.

EXTREME· Critical carry-out signal

Grain Stocks (Dec/Mar/Jun)

Quarterly inventory of corn, soybeans, wheat, sorghum, and oats on farms and in commercial facilities. December, March, and June reports track mid-marketing-year pace.

HIGH· Quarterly carry-out signal

Crop Progress

Weekly condition ratings (Excellent/Good/Fair/Poor/VP) and planting or harvest progress by state. Released Mondays at 4:00 PM ET (Tuesday after a Monday holiday) from the first Monday in April through the Monday after Thanksgiving (Nov 30 in 2026).

LOW-MOD· Summer G/E ratings move prices most

Export Sales

Weekly net sales of corn, soybeans, and wheat to foreign buyers. Released Thursdays at 8:30 AM ET by USDA FAS. Large China purchases can move futures at release.

MODERATE· China sales = high impact

Common questions

When is the next USDA crop report?
USDA's next crop reports, WASDE and Crop Production, come out Tuesday, Nov 10, 2026, at 12:00 p.m. ET (11 a.m. CT). Crop Progress comes out Mondays at 4:00 p.m. ET through Nov 30, 2026.
What time do USDA reports come out?
The big crop reports come out at 12:00 p.m. ET (11 a.m. CT): WASDE, Crop Production, Grain Stocks, Prospective Plantings and Acreage. Cattle on Feed, Hogs and Pigs, Milk Production and Cold Storage come out at 3:00 p.m. ET (2 p.m. CT), the time NASS uses for most of its other reports. Crop Progress is out Mondays at 4:00 p.m. ET (3 p.m. CT) in season, and Export Sales Thursdays at 8:30 a.m. ET. Source: the NASS 2026 release calendar and USDA's WASDE schedule.
When is the WASDE report released?
The USDA WASDE report is released monthly, typically around the 10th of each month at 12:00 PM Eastern Time (11:00 AM Central). It can move corn and soybean futures sharply at release.
When is the USDA Export Sales report released?
The USDA Export Sales report usually comes out Thursday at 8:30 AM Eastern Time from the USDA Foreign Agricultural Service. A federal holiday earlier in the week can push it to Friday. It covers weekly net export sales of corn, soybeans, and wheat.
When is the Prospective Plantings report?
The USDA Prospective Plantings report is typically released in late March, usually the last business day of the month. It surveys farmers on intended corn, soybean, and wheat planting acres and is one of the most market-moving reports of the year.
When is Crop Progress released?
USDA Crop Progress is released every Monday at 4:00 PM Eastern Time (Tuesday when Monday is a federal holiday), from the first Monday in April through the Monday after Thanksgiving (Nov 30 in 2026, per the NASS calendar). It provides weekly condition ratings and planting and harvest progress percentages by state.
What happens if I miss the Dec. 11 ARC/PLC deadline for 2026?
FSA says a farm with no 2026 election in by Dec. 11, 2026 keeps its 2025 election for its crops, and the farm gets no payments for the 2026 program year.
Release times are approximate. USDA may shift dates around holidays. Always verify at usda.gov. All times Eastern unless noted.
