How the numbers are counted
  • One weather station inside each county, reporting at least 90% of the days this year. A county without one points to the nearest that has one.
  • A day not reported is unknown, not dry: it adds to neither the total nor the usual year.
  • Usual is NOAA’s daily normal for the station, over the same days. NOAA’s numbers come in a few days late, so the pages are updated every day and each one says the day its numbers run through.
  • Records count only full years. A station 25 points or more from its county average is left off: one gauge, not the county.
