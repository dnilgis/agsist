Corn, soybean and wheat data by state

The U.S. corn yield was 186.5 bushels per acre in 2025 (USDA NASS final, read Nov 10, 2026). Iowa, the top corn state, is at 219 bushels per acre in the USDA November 2026 forecast (read Nov 10, 2026). Soybeans: U.S. 53.0 bushels per acre in 2025; Illinois, the top soybean state, 64 bushels per acre in the forecast. Crop numbers from USDA NASS (the National Agricultural Statistics Service, the federal office that counts U.S. crops) for corn, soybeans and wheat: the 2025 crop, 2026 state forecasts, and yearly series by state you can chart and download. Yields are in bushels per acre (bu/ac).

Common questions

How much corn, soybeans, and wheat did the U.S. produce in 2025?
In the 2025 crop year, U.S. farmers produced a record 17.0 billion bushels of corn (186.5 bu/acre, a record yield), 4.26 billion bushels of soybeans (a record 53.0 bu/acre), and about 1.98 billion bushels of all wheat (53.3 bu/acre), according to USDA NASS Crop Production 2025 Summary and Small Grains 2025 Summary.
How do I get historical corn yield data by state?
AGSIST's Data Explorer shows USDA NASS corn, soybean, and wheat yields and planted acres by state from 2010 to present. For any other commodity, geography, or year, the numbers are in USDA NASS Quick Stats.
