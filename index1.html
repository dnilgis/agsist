Sources and terms

Prices from Yahoo Finance, delayed 10 to 25 min · Cash bids via the AGSIST elevator network · Weather: National Weather Service forecast grid, PRISM daily history (RCC-ACIS), soil at the nearest USCRN, SCAN, Iowa State or Wisconet station · USDA NASS (This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS.) · NOAA CPC

Farmland sale at the top: the highest price per acre among bare farmland sales recorded in the state in its newest 30 days on file, inside the county’s usual range and at no more than twice the state’s middle price, so a higher sale can be left out; no buyer or seller names. Export sales: USDA reports metric tons; shown in bushels (39.368 a ton for corn, 36.744 for soybeans and wheat); commitments are shipments so far plus sales not yet shipped. Funds: the CFTC Commitments of Traders report, managed money (hedge funds and commodity trading advisers), net futures positions as of Tuesday, in contracts; not a headcount of who is long or short, and not a trading signal. The “Since” price line compares the front-month price now with its close on the report’s Tuesday, so a contract roll in between is inside the number. Yield nowcast: built from the weekly USDA crop ratings, state by state, weighted by acres; the 80% band means that in 8 of every 10 of 12 past years, each estimated from earlier years only, the estimate at the same week missed USDA’s final yield by no more than this. The band gets narrower as the season goes on. It is withheld once the ratings are two weeks old. WASDE grades compare each USDA figure with the average of a published trade survey.
