How much rain has Uintah County, Utah, had in 2026?

6.83 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Vernal Municipal Airport in Uintah County, through Nov 16. That is 1.07 inches less than a normal year to date (86% of normal). Averaged over the whole county, about 13.38 inches, 123% of normal.

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 37 points of normal above this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Vernal Municipal Airport, 22 miles from the middle of Uintah County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
