How much rain has Wayne County, Utah, had in 2026?

11.24 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wayne County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 129% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Capitol Reef National Park, 19 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
