How much rain has Juab County, Utah, had in 2026?

12.35 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Juab County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 116% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.07 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 am Mountain, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Levan station (NOAA daily record): 19.25 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station in Levan. That is 6.71 inches more than a normal year to date (154% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 38 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Levan, 50 miles from the middle of Juab County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
