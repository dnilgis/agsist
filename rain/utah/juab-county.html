2026 so far in Juab County

  • Wettest month: October, 2.40 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.74 inches on Oct 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 28 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Mar 4 to Mar 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 4.27 inches at the same station. On the 312 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.29 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 16th wettest by Nov 9 of 86 years on record here (1940 on); the last wetter year was 2019, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 10.80 inches, 106% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Juab County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 20 points below this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Callao, 51 miles from the middle of Juab County. It reported 312 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Juab County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
