17.03 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Carbon County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 133% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Wellington station: 10.91 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 3 miles east of Wellington. That is 2.61 inches more than a normal year to date (131% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles east of Wellington, 11 miles from the middle of Carbon County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
