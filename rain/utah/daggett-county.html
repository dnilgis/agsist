How much rain has Daggett County, Utah, had in 2026?

15.37 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Daggett County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 117% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Flaming Gorge station: 7.76 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Flaming Gorge. That is 2.06 inches less than a normal year to date (79% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Flaming Gorge, 7 miles from the middle of Daggett County. It reported 293 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 29 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 293 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Daggett County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
