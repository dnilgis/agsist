How much rain has Daggett County, Utah, had in 2026?

20.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Hickerson Park, Daggett County, through Oct 15. That is 2.96 inches more than a usual year to date (116% of usual).

Through Oct 10, the weather station in Hickerson Park has 5.10 inches more than last year at this point. August was the wettest month at 4.60 inches. The biggest single day was Jul 22 with 1.60 inches. From May 31 to Jun 27 the gauge went 28 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hickerson Park (station ID USS0009J08S), 24 miles from the middle of Daggett County. It reported every one of the 288 days from Jan 1 to Oct 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Daggett County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
