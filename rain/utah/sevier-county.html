How much rain has Sevier County, Utah, had in 2026?

16.42 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sevier County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 113% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fremont Indian, 31 miles from the middle of Sevier County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Sevier County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
