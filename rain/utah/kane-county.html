The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Kodachrome Basin Park, 17 miles from the middle of Kane County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
