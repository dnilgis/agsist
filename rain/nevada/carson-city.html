How much rain has Carson City, Nevada, had in 2026?

9.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Carson City, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 81% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.66 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 pm Pacific, Nov 25.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Carson City gauge (credited below): 7.54 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles north of Carson City. That is 3.99 inches less than a normal year to date (65% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This gauge is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles north of Carson City, 4 miles from the middle of Carson City. It reported 325 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 325 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Carson City reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
