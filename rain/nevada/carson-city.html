How much rain has Carson City, Nevada, had in 2026?

8.36 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Carson City, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 72% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Carson City gauge: 5.84 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles north of Carson City. That is 5.44 inches less than a normal year to date (52% of normal).

This gauge is 20 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles north of Carson City, 4 miles from the middle of Carson City. It reported 319 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 319 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 5 stations inside Carson City reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
