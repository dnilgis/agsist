The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Smoky Valley - Carvers, 64 miles from the middle of Nye County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Nye County, Nevada, had in 2026?

8.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers in Nye County, through Nov 8. That is 2.68 inches more than a usual year to date (149% of usual).

Is Nye County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers had 8.16 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 149% of the usual 5.48 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Nye County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers, and 3.70 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Nye County in 2026?

October, with 3.30 inches at the weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Nye County in 2026?

1.15 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Nye County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers had 5.60 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 30 days) and 8.16 inches in 2026. On the 282 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.23 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Nye County had in 2026?

3,088 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (20 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Nye County in 2026?

Oct 12 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Smoky Valley - Carvers. Usual: Oct 2, the middle of 21 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Nye County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
