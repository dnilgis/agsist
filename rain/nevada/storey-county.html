2026 so far in Storey County

  • Wettest month: February, 1.54 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.00 inch on Jun 19.
  • Longest dry stretch: 43 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Aug 29 to Oct 10.
  • On record: 37th wettest by Nov 5 of 55 years on record here (1893 on); the last wetter year was 2021, the last drier one 2022. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 5.38 inches, 60% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Storey County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Virginia City, 11 miles from the middle of Storey County. It reported 302 of the 309 days from Jan 1 to Nov 5; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 302 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Storey County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
