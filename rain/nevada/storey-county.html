How much rain has Storey County, Nevada, had in 2026?

5.91 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Storey County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 64% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Virginia City station: 6.46 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station in Virginia City. That is 2.58 inches less than a normal year to date (71% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Virginia City, 11 miles from the middle of Storey County. It reported 313 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
