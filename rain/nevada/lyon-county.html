The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 5.5 miles north-northeast of Wellington (station WELLINGTON 5.5 NNE), 14 miles from the middle of Lyon County. It reported 283 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Lyon County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
