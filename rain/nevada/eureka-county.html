How much rain has Eureka County, Nevada, had in 2026?

8.47 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Eureka County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 86% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Beowawe 49S - U of N Ranch station: 3.72 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 9, at the weather station in Beowawe. That is 3.86 inches less than a normal year to date (49% of normal).

This station is 37 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Beowawe, 18 miles from the middle of Eureka County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
