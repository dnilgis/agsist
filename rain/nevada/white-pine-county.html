How much rain has White Pine County, Nevada, had in 2026?

10.40 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over White Pine County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 93% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Ely Airport station: 9.07 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station at Ely Airport. That is 0.57 inches more than a normal year to date (107% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Ely Airport, 11 miles from the middle of White Pine County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
