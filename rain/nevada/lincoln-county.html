How much rain has Lincoln County, Nevada, had in 2026?

9.22 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lincoln County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 98% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Hiko station (NOAA daily record): 7.97 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 26, at the weather station in Hiko. That is 1.64 inches more than a normal year to date (126% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 28 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hiko, 20 miles from the middle of Lincoln County. It reported 329 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
