How much rain has Esmeralda County, Nevada, had in 2026?

3.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Silverpeak, Esmeralda County, through Oct 13. That is 0.61 inches less than a usual year to date (83% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Silverpeak had caught 3.06 inches, and 1.88 inches of that came in April. The biggest single reading was 0.67 inches on Apr 26. Earlier, the gauge went 43 days in a row with nothing in it, Jan 5 to Feb 16. On the days both years reported, this year has 0.59 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Silverpeak (station ID 267463), 4 miles from the middle of Esmeralda County. It reported 283 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Esmeralda County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
