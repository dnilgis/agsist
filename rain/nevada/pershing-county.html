How much rain has Pershing County, Nevada, had in 2026?

6.49 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Pershing County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 83% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.17 in. NOAA radar county average to 10 am Pacific, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Rye Patch Dam station (NOAA daily record): 7.75 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 21, at the Rye Patch Dam weather station in Rye Patch. That is 0.46 inches more than a normal year to date (106% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rye Patch, 6 miles from the middle of Pershing County. It reported every one of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 325 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Pershing County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
