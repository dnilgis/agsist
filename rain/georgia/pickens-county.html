The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile north-northwest of Jasper, 1 mile from the middle of Pickens County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
