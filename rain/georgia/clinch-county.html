The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles north of Homerville, 13 miles from the middle of Clinch County. It reported 326 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
