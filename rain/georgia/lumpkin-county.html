2026 so far in Lumpkin County

  • Wettest month: January, 7.93 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 3.83 inches on Jan 11.
  • Longest dry stretch: 18 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Mar 18 to Apr 4.
  • 2025 by the same date: 51.14 inches at the same station. 2026 had 3.16 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 76th wettest by Nov 5 of 118 years on record here (1895 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2023. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 49.52 inches, 91% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 11, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Lumpkin County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west-southwest of Dahlonega, 4 miles from the middle of Lumpkin County. It reported every one of the 309 days from Jan 1 to Nov 5. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 309 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Lumpkin County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
