How much rain has Clarke County, Georgia, had in 2026?

31.93 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Athens Ben Epps Airport in Clarke County, through Nov 9. That is 9.91 inches less than a usual year to date (76% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 33.39 inches, 78% of usual.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Athens Ben Epps Airport, 2 miles from the middle of Clarke County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Clarke County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
