How much rain has Tattnall County, Georgia, had in 2026?

38.19 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Tattnall County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 88% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.3 miles north-northeast of Collins, 16 miles from the middle of Tattnall County. It reported 320 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Tattnall County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Tattnall County, about 38.19 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 88% of the normal 43.48 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Tattnall County in the last week?

0.74 inches from Nov 18 to Nov 24 at the volunteer rain gauge 7.3 miles north-northeast of Collins, and 1.38 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 26 to Nov 24. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Tattnall County in 2026?

August, with 9.61 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 7.3 miles north-northeast of Collins, counting Jan 1 through Nov 24.

What was the biggest rain in Tattnall County in 2026?

2.58 inches on Jul 13, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 24. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Tattnall County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 24, the volunteer rain gauge 7.3 miles north-northeast of Collins had 44.78 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 11 days) and 39.62 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 8 days). On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.96 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Tattnall County had in 2026?

4,896 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 24 at a weather station 20 miles from the rain gauge.

Has Tattnall County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 24: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 20 miles from the rain gauge.
