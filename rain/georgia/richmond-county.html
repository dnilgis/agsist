How much rain has Richmond County, Georgia, had in 2026?

39.51 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Richmond County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Augusta Bush Field Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Richmond County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
