The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.1 miles northwest of Moultrie, 8 miles from the middle of Colquitt County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
