The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles north-northeast of Moultrie, 1 mile from the middle of Colquitt County. It reported 327 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Colquitt County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. Averaged over Colquitt County, about 45.84 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 100% of the normal 45.81 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Colquitt County in 2026?

May, with 9.82 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles north-northeast of Moultrie, counting Jan 1 through Nov 25.

What was the biggest rain in Colquitt County in 2026?

4.08 inches on Oct 6, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 25. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Colquitt County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 25, the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles north-northeast of Moultrie had 33.90 inches in 2025 and 43.91 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 2 days). On the 327 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.01 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Colquitt County had in 2026?

Has Colquitt County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 24: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 1 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Nov 30, the middle of 26 years, 1991-2020.
