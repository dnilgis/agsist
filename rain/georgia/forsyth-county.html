How much rain has Forsyth County, Georgia, had in 2026?

37.46 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 2 miles north of Cumming, Forsyth County, through Oct 13. That is 4.74 inches less than a usual year to date (89% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 2 miles north of Cumming had its wettest month in July at 7.88 inches, and its biggest one-day reading came Jul 24 at 2.82 inches. Earlier it went 19 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Apr 7 to Apr 25. On the days both years reported, this year is 13.77 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north of Cumming (station ID 092408), less than a mile from the middle of Forsyth County. It reported 269 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 269 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Forsyth County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
