The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 17 miles northeast of Fargo, 14 miles from the middle of Charlton County. It reported 296 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 31 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
