The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 17 miles northeast of Fargo, 14 miles from the middle of Charlton County. It reported 286 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 30 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
