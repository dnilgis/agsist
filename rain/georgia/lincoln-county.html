How much rain has Lincoln County, Georgia, had in 2026?

32.35 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lincoln County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 80% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles northeast of Tignall, 16 miles from the middle of Lincoln County. It reported 317 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Lincoln County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lincoln County?
