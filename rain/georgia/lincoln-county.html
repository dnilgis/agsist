The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles northeast of Tignall, 16 miles from the middle of Lincoln County. It reported 309 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Lincoln County, Georgia, had in 2026?

31.76 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lincoln County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Tignall gauge had 30.12 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16.

Is Lincoln County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Lincoln County, about 31.76 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of the normal 39.73 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Lincoln County in 2026?

May, with 8.30 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles northeast of Tignall, counting Jan 1 through Nov 16.

What was the biggest rain in Lincoln County in 2026?

1.59 inches on Jul 24, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 16. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lincoln County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 16, the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles northeast of Tignall had 49.69 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 23 days) and 30.12 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 11 days). On the 286 days both years reported, 2026 had 19.66 inches less than 2025.
