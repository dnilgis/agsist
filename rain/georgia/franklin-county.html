The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Carnesville, less than a mile from the middle of Franklin County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Franklin County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
