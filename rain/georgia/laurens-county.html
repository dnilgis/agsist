How much rain has Laurens County, Georgia, had in 2026?

35.30 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Dublin, Laurens County, through Oct 13. That is 3.74 inches less than a usual year to date (90% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Dublin went 18 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Apr 8 to Apr 25, not even a trace. May made up for it as the wettest month at 7.78 inches, and the biggest single day came Aug 3 with 3.50 inches. On the days both years reported, this year sits 7.04 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Dublin (station ID 092844), 6 miles from the middle of Laurens County. It reported 281 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 281 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Laurens County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
