The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles southeast of Adairsville, 7 miles from the middle of Bartow County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Bartow County, Georgia, had in 2026?

Is Bartow County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station 5 miles southeast of Adairsville had 38.04 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 86% of the normal 44.40 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Bartow County in the last week?

0.05 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station 5 miles southeast of Adairsville, and 2.34 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Bartow County in 2026?

May, with 6.46 inches at the weather station 5 miles southeast of Adairsville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Bartow County in 2026?

2.16 inches on Jan 10, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bartow County?

How many growing degree days has Bartow County had in 2026?

4,648 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at a weather station 16 miles from the rain station, 111% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (20 years).

Has Bartow County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 16 miles from the rain station. Typical: Oct 30, the middle of 21 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Bartow County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
