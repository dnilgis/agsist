The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Carrollton, 1 mile from the middle of Carroll County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
