The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles south-southwest of Winston, 4 miles from the middle of Douglas County. It reported 320 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
