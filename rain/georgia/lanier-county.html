Lakeland gauge: 36.51 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.8 miles west of Lakeland. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.8 miles west of Lakeland, 5 miles from the middle of Lanier County. It reported 304 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
