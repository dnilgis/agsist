The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-southeast of Griffin, 2 miles from the middle of Spalding County. It reported 326 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
