The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south-southwest of Clayton, 1 mile from the middle of Rabun County. It reported every one of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
