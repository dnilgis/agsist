The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cordele, 4 miles from the middle of Crisp County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Crisp County, Georgia, had in 2026?

34.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Cordele in Crisp County, through Nov 8. That is 8.56 inches less than a normal year to date (80% of normal).

Is Crisp County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Cordele had 34.06 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 80% of the normal 42.62 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Crisp County in the last week?

0.32 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Cordele, and 0.71 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Crisp County in 2026?

May, with 7.29 inches at the weather station in Cordele, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Crisp County in 2026?

2.70 inches on Oct 5, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Crisp County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station in Cordele had 39.05 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 34.06 inches in 2026. On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.99 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Crisp County had in 2026?

4,866 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Cordele, 103% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Has Crisp County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Cordele. Typical: Nov 12, the middle of 24 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Crisp County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
