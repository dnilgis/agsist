How much rain has Crisp County, Georgia, had in 2026?

33.70 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Cordele, Crisp County, through Oct 14. That is 6.84 inches less than a usual year to date (83% of usual).

Through Oct 8, the weather station in Cordele had 32.62 inches, 7.42 inches less than a usual year. May was the wettest month at 7.29 inches, and the biggest single day came late, 2.70 inches on Oct 5. The gauge went 18 days in a row with nothing from Apr 7 to Apr 24. On the days both years reported, that is 4.60 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cordele (station ID 092266), 4 miles from the middle of Crisp County. It reported every one of the 287 days from Jan 1 to Oct 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 287 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Crisp County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
