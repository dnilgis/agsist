The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Danielsville, 5 miles from the middle of Madison County. It reported 322 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Madison County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Madison County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
