The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Danielsville, 5 miles from the middle of Madison County. It reported 317 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Madison County, Georgia, had in 2026?

Is Madison County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Madison County, about 35.20 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 81% of the normal 43.37 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Madison County in 2026?

May, with 9.44 inches at the weather station in Danielsville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Madison County in 2026?

2.55 inches on Jul 14, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 18.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Madison County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station in Danielsville had 46.46 inches in 2025 and 40.01 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 5 days). On the 317 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.40 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Madison County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 317 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Madison County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
