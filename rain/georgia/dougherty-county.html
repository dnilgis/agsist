How much rain has Dougherty County, Georgia, had in 2026?

46.04 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Dougherty County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 104% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Albany SW Georgia Regional Airport, 1 mile from the middle of Dougherty County. It reported 308 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 308 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Dougherty County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
