How much rain has Brooks County, Georgia, had in 2026?

43.87 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Brooks County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 92% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile north-northeast of Quitman, 3 miles from the middle of Brooks County. It reported 315 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
