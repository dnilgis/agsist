The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jackson, 7 miles from the middle of Butts County. It reported 314 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
