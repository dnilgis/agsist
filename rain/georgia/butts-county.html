How much rain has Butts County, Georgia, had in 2026?

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jackson (station ID 094623), 7 miles from the middle of Butts County. It reported 277 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Butts County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
