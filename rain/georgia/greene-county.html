The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.4 miles southeast of Buckhead, 8 miles from the middle of Greene County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
