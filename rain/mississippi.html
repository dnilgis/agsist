How much rain has Mississippi had in 2026? County by county

46 Mississippi counties have a weather station inside the county that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Oct 25 (15 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 39 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 80% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.
