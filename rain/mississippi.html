How much rain has Mississippi had in 2026? County by county

48 Mississippi counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 18 (14 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 38 counties with a NOAA normal are above 79% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Bolivar County: the driest by Nov 18 of 88 years on record (1912 on), 49% of normal.
  • Carroll County: the driest by Nov 18 of 54 years on record (1970 on), 44% of normal.
  • Rankin County: 3rd driest by Nov 18 of 63 years on record (1964 on), 71% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
