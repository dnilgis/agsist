How much rain has Mississippi had in 2026? County by county

47 Mississippi counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 8 (16 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 39 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 81% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Bolivar County: the driest by Nov 8 of 88 years on record (1912 on), 51% of usual.
  • Stone County: 3rd wettest by Nov 7 of 47 years on record (1948 on), 123% of usual.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily.

Rain and usual are in inches. Usual is NOAA’s daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. A station with no NOAA normal shows none. Each county page has the month by month table.
