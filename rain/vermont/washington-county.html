The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.5 miles north of Northfield, 7 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported 313 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Washington County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Washington County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the volunteer rain gauge 1.5 miles north of Northfield had 36.72 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 32.96 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.08 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Washington County had in 2026?

2,236 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at a weather station 5 miles from the rain gauge, 104% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).
