How much rain has Franklin County, Vermont, had in 2026?

36.34 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Franklin County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 90% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Enosburg Falls 2 station: 34.73 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Enosburg Falls. That is 1.37 inches more than a normal year to date (104% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Enosburg Falls, 6 miles from the middle of Franklin County. It reported 300 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 21 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Franklin County, Vermont, had in 2026?

Is Franklin County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Franklin County, about 36.34 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 90% of the normal 40.46 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Franklin County in 2026?

June, with 8.30 inches at the weather station in Enosburg Falls, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Franklin County in 2026?

1.53 inches on Jun 19, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Franklin County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station in Enosburg Falls had 42.87 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 18 days) and 34.73 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 21 days). On the 284 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.79 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Franklin County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 300 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Franklin County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
