How much rain has Bennington County, Vermont, had in 2026?

41.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Bennington County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 93% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Peru station: 44.50 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station in Peru. That is 0.52 inches more than a normal year to date (101% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Peru, 18 miles from the middle of Bennington County. It reported 314 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Bennington County, Vermont, had in 2026?

41.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Bennington County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 93% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Peru station had 44.50 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16 (101% of normal).

Is Bennington County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Bennington County, about 41.12 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 93% of the normal 44.11 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Bennington County in the last week?

0.71 inches from Nov 10 to Nov 16 at the weather station in Peru, and 1.75 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 18 to Nov 16. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Bennington County in 2026?

August, with 8.32 inches at the weather station in Peru, counting Jan 1 through Nov 16.

What was the biggest rain in Bennington County in 2026?

1.62 inches on Apr 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 16. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bennington County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 16, the weather station in Peru had 52.06 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 44.50 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 6 days). On the 313 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.21 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
