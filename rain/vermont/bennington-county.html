The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Bennington/william H. Morse State Airport (station BENNINGTON/WILLIAM H. MORSE STATE AIRPORT), 13 miles from the middle of Bennington County. It reported 266 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 266 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Bennington County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

Is Bennington County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The NOAA station at Bennington/william H. Morse State Airport had 23.49 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 12, 83% of the usual 28.38 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Bennington County in 2026?

July, with 6.45 inches at the NOAA station at Bennington/william H. Morse State Airport, counting the months through Oct 12.

What was the biggest rain in Bennington County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bennington County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the NOAA station at Bennington/william H. Morse State Airport had 31.01 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 23.49 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 19 days). On the 265 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.8 inches less.
