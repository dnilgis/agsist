Brownington gauge: 36.77 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.7 miles east-southeast of Brownington. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.7 miles east-southeast of Brownington, 8 miles from the middle of Orleans County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
