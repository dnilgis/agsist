The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.7 miles east-southeast of Brownington, 8 miles from the middle of Orleans County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
