The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Gainesboro, 1 mile from the middle of Jackson County. It reported 305 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Jackson County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Jackson County in 2026?
