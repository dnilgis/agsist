The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.3 miles east-southeast of Benton, 6 miles from the middle of Polk County. It reported 326 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
