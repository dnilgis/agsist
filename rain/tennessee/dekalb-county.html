2026 so far in DeKalb County

  • Wettest month: July, 4.70 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.57 inches on Jul 11.
  • Longest dry stretch: 19 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 13 to Oct 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 52.82 inches at the same gauge (it missed 1 day). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 24.04 inches less than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 35.58 inches, 75% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over DeKalb County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 18 points above this gauge: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles west-southwest of Smithville, 5 miles from the middle of DeKalb County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 312 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside DeKalb County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
