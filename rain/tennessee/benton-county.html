The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.2 miles south of Camden, 8 miles from the middle of Benton County. It reported 291 of the 308 days from Jan 1 to Nov 4; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Benton County?
