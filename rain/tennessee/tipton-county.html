The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.2 miles east-southeast of Atoka (station ATOKA 3.2 ESE), 5 miles from the middle of Tipton County. It reported 275 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 275 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Tipton County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Tipton County, Tennessee had in 2026?

Is Tipton County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.2 miles east-southeast of Atoka had 31.52 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 12, 75% of the usual 41.82 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Tipton County in 2026?

June, with 6.52 inches at the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.2 miles east-southeast of Atoka, counting the months through Oct 12.

What was the biggest rain in Tipton County in 2026?

2.70 inches on Jun 23, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 12. Reports that cover more than one day are not counted as one day.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Tipton County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.2 miles east-southeast of Atoka had 50.57 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 6 days) and 31.52 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 10 days). On the 269 days both years reported, 2026 had 19.5 inches less.
