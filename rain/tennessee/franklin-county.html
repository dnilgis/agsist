The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sewanee, 11 miles from the middle of Franklin County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Franklin County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

Is Franklin County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Sewanee had 50.08 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 93% of the normal 53.60 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Franklin County in the last week?

0.75 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Sewanee, and 2.87 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Franklin County in 2026?

July, with 7.99 inches at the weather station in Sewanee, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Franklin County in 2026?

2.19 inches on May 7, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Franklin County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Sewanee had 65.32 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 50.08 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 15.01 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Franklin County had in 2026?

3,972 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Sewanee.

Has Franklin County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Sewanee.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Franklin County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
