The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.8 miles northwest of Ashland City, 11 miles from the middle of Cheatham County. It reported 310 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
