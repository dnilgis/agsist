How much rain has Cheatham County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.3 miles east-northeast of Ashland City, 8 miles from the middle of Cheatham County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
