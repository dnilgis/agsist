The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Greeneville, 5 miles from the middle of Greene County. It reported 319 of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
