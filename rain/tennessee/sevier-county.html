The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg, 7 miles from the middle of Sevier County. It reported 309 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Sevier County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

Is Sevier County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Sevier County, about 46.81 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 24, 90% of the normal 52.16 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Sevier County in 2026?

June, with 8.91 inches at the weather station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg, counting Jan 1 through Nov 24.

What was the biggest rain in Sevier County in 2026?

3.16 inches on Jun 29, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 24. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Sevier County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 24, the weather station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg had 53.30 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 9 days) and 48.01 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 19 days). On the 300 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.05 inches less than 2025.
