How much rain has Sevier County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

46.63 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sevier County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Gatlinburg station: 47.47 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg. That is 1.76 inches less than a normal year to date (96% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg, 7 miles from the middle of Sevier County. It reported 303 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Sevier County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

46.63 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sevier County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Gatlinburg station had 47.47 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17 (96% of normal).

Is Sevier County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Sevier County, about 46.63 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of the normal 51.06 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Sevier County in 2026?

June, with 8.91 inches at the weather station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Sevier County in 2026?

3.16 inches on Jun 29, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Sevier County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station 2 miles southwest of Gatlinburg had 53.04 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 9 days) and 47.47 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 18 days). On the 294 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.36 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 303 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Sevier County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
