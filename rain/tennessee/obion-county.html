The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles south-southeast of Union City (gauge ID US1TNOB0003), 7 miles from the middle of Obion County. It reported 282 of the 290 days from Jan 1 to Oct 17; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Obion County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
