2026 so far in Grainger County

  • Wettest month: June, 8.67 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.34 inches on Feb 26.
  • Longest dry stretch: 15 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Nov 1.
  • 2025 by the same date: 39.16 inches at the same gauge (it missed 11 days). On the 301 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.82 inches more than 2025.
  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 40.55 inches, 95% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Grainger County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.0 miles south-southwest of Rutledge, 7 miles from the middle of Grainger County. It reported 312 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
