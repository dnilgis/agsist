The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Knoxville Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Blount County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Has Blount County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Blount County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
