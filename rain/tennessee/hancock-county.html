The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.5 miles west-southwest of Sneedville, 8 miles from the middle of Hancock County. It reported 318 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
