How much rain has Overton County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

37.84 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Overton County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 77% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Livingston gauge: 37.21 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.1 miles west-southwest of Livingston. That is 13.68 inches less than a normal year to date (73% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.1 miles west-southwest of Livingston, 6 miles from the middle of Overton County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 319 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Overton County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
