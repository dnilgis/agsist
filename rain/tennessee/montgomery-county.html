How much rain has Montgomery County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

24.31 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Clarksville Outlaw Airport, Montgomery County, through Oct 13. That is 12.07 inches less than a usual year to date (67% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Clarksville Outlaw Airport has caught a good share of its rain in July, 8.80 inches. The biggest single day was Jul 5 at 2.32 inches. The gauge went 14 days in a row without even a trace, Mar 19 to Apr 1. On the days both years reported, that is 23.03 inches less than 2025 had by now.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Clarksville Outlaw Airport (station ID 03894), 8 miles from the middle of Montgomery County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Montgomery County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
