How much rain has Davidson County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

29.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Nashville International Airport, Davidson County, through Oct 13. That is 11.17 inches less than a usual year to date (72% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Nashville International Airport had 29.12 inches, 18.20 inches less than last year by the same day. July was the wettest month at 6.59 inches. The biggest single day came early, 1.92 inches on Jan 25. From Mar 19 to Apr 3 the gauge went 16 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Nashville International Airport (station ID 13897), 6 miles from the middle of Davidson County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Davidson County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
