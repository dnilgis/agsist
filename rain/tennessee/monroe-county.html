The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north of Madisonville, 9 miles from the middle of Monroe County. It reported 315 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
