2026 so far in Monroe County

  • Wettest month: May, 7.38 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.90 inches on Sep 26.
  • Longest dry stretch: 22 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Aug 22 to Sep 12.

  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 44.80 inches, 91% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Monroe County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north of Madisonville, 9 miles from the middle of Monroe County. It reported 297 of the 311 days from Jan 1 to Nov 7; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
