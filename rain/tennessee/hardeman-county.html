How much rain has Hardeman County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

33.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Bolivar Water Works, Hardeman County, through Oct 18. That is 9.99 inches less than a usual year to date (77% of usual).

Through Oct 11, the weather station in Bolivar Water Works had May as its wettest month at 8.42 inches. The biggest one-day reading was 2.94 inches on Apr 29. From Aug 24 to Sep 11 the gauge went 19 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, that is 17.10 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bolivar Water Works (station ID 400876), 4 miles from the middle of Hardeman County. It reported 280 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 280 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Hardeman County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Hardeman County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Hardeman County in 2026?
