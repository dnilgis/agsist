The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bolivar Water Works, 4 miles from the middle of Hardeman County. It reported 315 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Hardeman County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Hardeman County in 2026?

May, with 8.42 inches at the weather station in Bolivar Water Works, counting Jan 1 through Nov 15.

What was the biggest rain in Hardeman County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Hardeman County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
