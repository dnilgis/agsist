Chattanooga Airport station: 36.30 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station at Chattanooga Airport. That is 11.10 inches less than a normal year to date (77% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Chattanooga Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Hamilton County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
