The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lawrenceburg, 3 miles from the middle of Lawrence County. It reported 308 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 308 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
