2026 so far in Pickett County

  • Longest dry stretch: 15 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Mar 18 to Apr 1.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Byrdstown, 3 miles from the middle of Pickett County. It reported 313 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
