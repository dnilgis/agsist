How much rain has Weakley County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

33.35 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Dresden, Weakley County, through Oct 18. That is 7.83 inches less than a usual year to date (81% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the station in Dresden had May as its wettest month at 7.95 inches. The biggest single day came May 23 with 2.16 inches. From Mar 17 to Apr 2 the gauge went 17 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. All told it sits 7.31 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Dresden (station ID 402600), 1 mile from the middle of Weakley County. It reported 281 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 281 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Weakley County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
