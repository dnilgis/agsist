How much rain has Cocke County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

38.74 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 1 mile northwest of Newport, Cocke County, through Oct 18. That is 1.02 inches more than a usual year to date (103% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 1 mile northwest of Newport has run a little wetter than a usual year. May was the wettest month at 5.88 inches, and the biggest single day came Jun 28 with 2.21 inches. The gauge then went 14 days in a row with nothing in it, from Aug 22 to Sep 4. On the days both years reported, 2026 is 2.74 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northwest of Newport (station ID 406534), 6 miles from the middle of Cocke County. It reported 288 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Cocke County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
