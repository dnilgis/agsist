The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northwest of Newport, 6 miles from the middle of Cocke County. It reported 310 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms
