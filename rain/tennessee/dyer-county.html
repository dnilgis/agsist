How much rain has Dyer County, Tennessee, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.7 miles east of Dyersburg, 10 miles from the middle of Dyer County. It reported 327 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
