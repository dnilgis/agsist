The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.1 miles west-northwest of Eagleville (gauge ID US1TNWL0112), 11 miles from the middle of Williamson County. It reported 306 of the 309 days from Jan 1 to Nov 5; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: 11 stations inside Williamson County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Williamson County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 5, the volunteer rain gauge 7.1 miles west-northwest of Eagleville had 48.72 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 38.36 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 298 days both years reported, 2026 had 9.75 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Williamson County had in 2026?

4,364 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 5 at a weather station 13 miles from the rain gauge, 113% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

Has Williamson County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 5: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 13 miles from the rain gauge. Usual: Oct 28, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.
