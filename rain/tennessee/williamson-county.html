The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.1 miles west-northwest of Eagleville, 11 miles from the middle of Williamson County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
