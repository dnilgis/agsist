The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.6 miles south-southeast of Savannah, 7 miles from the middle of Hardin County. It reported 310 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
