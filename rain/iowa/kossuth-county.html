How much rain has Kossuth County, Iowa, had in 2026?

28.64 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Kossuth County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 90% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of Burt, 1 mile from the middle of Kossuth County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 326 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Kossuth County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
