How much rain has Monroe County, Iowa, had in 2026?

40.57 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Monroe County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 116% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile south-southeast of Lovilia, 7 miles from the middle of Monroe County. It reported 327 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
