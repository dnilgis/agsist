How much rain has Lyon County, Iowa, had in 2026?

25.10 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Rock Rapids, Lyon County, through Oct 18. That is 1.22 inches less than a usual year to date (95% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Rock Rapids had September as its wettest month by a wide margin, with 9.67 inches. The biggest single day was Jun 28, at 2.49 inches. Back on Feb 5 to Feb 25 the gauge went 21 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. Even so, the station is 1.91 inches ahead of where 2025 stood on the same day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rock Rapids (station ID 137147), 4 miles from the middle of Lyon County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Lyon County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
