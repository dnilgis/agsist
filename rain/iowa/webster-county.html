The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles north-northwest of Fort Dodge, 11 miles from the middle of Webster County. It reported 324 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
