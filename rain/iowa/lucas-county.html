The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile east of Chariton, 3 miles from the middle of Lucas County. It reported 311 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
