How much rain has Lucas County, Iowa, had in 2026?

42.47 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lucas County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 121% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Chariton station: 42.86 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 1 mile east of Chariton. That is 9.54 inches more than a normal year to date (129% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile east of Chariton, 3 miles from the middle of Lucas County. It reported 305 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
