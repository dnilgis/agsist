The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Allison, 2 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 295 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
