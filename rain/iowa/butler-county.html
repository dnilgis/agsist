The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Allison (station ALLISON), 2 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 263 of the 282 days from Jan 1 to Oct 9; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 263 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Butler County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
