The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Osceola, 2 miles from the middle of Clarke County. It reported 324 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
