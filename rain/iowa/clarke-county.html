2026 so far in Clarke County

  • Wettest month: September, 8.91 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 4.10 inches on Aug 5.
  • Longest dry stretch: 18 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 2 to Feb 19.
  • 2025 by the same date: 34.36 inches at the same station (it missed 2 days). On the 306 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.57 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 6th wettest by Nov 8 of 80 years on record here (1897 on); the last wetter year was 2010, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 44.39 inches, 129% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Clarke County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Osceola, 2 miles from the middle of Clarke County. It reported 308 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 308 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Clarke County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
