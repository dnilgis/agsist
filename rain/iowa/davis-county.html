How much rain has Davis County, Iowa, had in 2026?

33.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Bloomfield, Davis County, through Oct 13. That is 0.86 inches less than a usual year to date (97% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Bloomfield had April as its wettest month at 7.56 inches. The biggest single day came Jun 11 with 1.72 inches. Before any of that, the station went 36 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Jan 26 to Mar 2. All told it sits at 33.11 inches, 0.86 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bloomfield (station ID 130753), 1 mile from the middle of Davis County. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Davis County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
