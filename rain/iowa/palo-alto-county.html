The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.4 miles west of West Bend, 10 miles from the middle of Palo Alto County. It reported 308 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 21 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
