The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pocahontas, 1 mile from the middle of Pocahontas County. It reported 299 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 29 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
