How much rain has Ida County, Iowa, had in 2026?

29.37 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Ida County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 96% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.19 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 pm Central, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Battle Creek station (NOAA daily record): 24.37 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23, at the weather station 3 miles northeast of Battle Creek. That is 6.17 inches less than a normal year to date (80% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles northeast of Battle Creek, 3 miles from the middle of Ida County. It reported 325 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
