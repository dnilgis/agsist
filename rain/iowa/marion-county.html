2026 so far in Marion County

  • Wettest month: September, 9.23 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 3.42 inches on Sep 9.
  • Longest dry stretch: 11 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Oct 28.
  • 2025 by the same date: 29.87 inches at the same station (it missed 3 days). On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 14.50 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 6th wettest by Nov 8 of 80 years on record here (1899 on); the last wetter year was 2014, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 46.24 inches, 137% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Marion County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south of Pella, 10 miles from the middle of Marion County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Marion County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
