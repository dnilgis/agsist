How much rain has Appanoose County, Iowa, had in 2026?

35.71 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Rathbun, Appanoose County, through Oct 13. That is 2.59 inches more than a usual year to date (108% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Rathbun has seen its wettest month in September, with 7.11 inches. The biggest one-day reading was 2.22 inches on Mar 6. The gauge also went 25 days in a row with nothing in it, from Jan 26 to Feb 19. On the days both years reported, this year is 3.14 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rathbun (station ID 136910), 6 miles from the middle of Appanoose County. It reported 283 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Appanoose County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Appanoose County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Appanoose County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Appanoose County in 2026?

2.22 inches on Mar 6, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 13. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Appanoose County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station in Rathbun had 32.56 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 9 days) and 35.71 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 275 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.14 inches more than 2025.
