2026 so far in Appanoose County

  • Wettest month: September, 7.11 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.22 inches on Mar 6.
  • Longest dry stretch: 25 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jan 26 to Feb 19.
  • 2025 by the same date: 32.93 inches at the same station (it missed 9 days). On the 302 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.50 inches more than 2025.
  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • On record: 13th wettest by Nov 12 of 33 years on record here (1974 on); the last wetter year was 2021, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rathbun, 6 miles from the middle of Appanoose County. It reported 311 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Appanoose County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
