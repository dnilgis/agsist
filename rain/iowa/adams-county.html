How much rain has Adams County, Iowa, had in 2026?

47.85 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Corning in Adams County, through Nov 16. That is 15.68 inches more than a normal year to date (149% of normal). Averaged over the whole county, about 48.36 inches, 142% of normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Corning, 4 miles from the middle of Adams County. It reported 302 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 302 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Adams County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
