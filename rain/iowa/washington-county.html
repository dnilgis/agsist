  • Wettest month: September, 8.95 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 3.98 inches on Aug 14.
  • Longest dry stretch: 17 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 3 to Feb 19.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Washington, 4 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Washington County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
