The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sioux Rapids, 11 miles from the middle of Buena Vista County. It reported 315 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
