The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles north-northeast of Hornick, 7 miles from the middle of Woodbury County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
