The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north-northeast of Waukon, 4 miles from the middle of Allamakee County. It reported 318 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
