The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sac City, 7 miles from the middle of Sac County. It reported 318 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
