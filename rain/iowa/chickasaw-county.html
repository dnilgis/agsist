How much rain has Chickasaw County, Iowa, had in 2026?

41.92 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Chickasaw County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 116% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the New Hampton station (NOAA daily record): 37.44 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station in New Hampton. That is 0.33 inches less than a normal year to date (99% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in New Hampton, 1 mile from the middle of Chickasaw County. It reported 320 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
