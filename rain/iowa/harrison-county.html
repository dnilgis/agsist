The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile west-northwest of Logan, 2 miles from the middle of Harrison County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
