The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Northwood, 5 miles from the middle of Worth County. It reported 277 of the 304 days from Jan 1 to Oct 31; the 27 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms
