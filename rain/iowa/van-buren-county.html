The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Keosauqua, 1 mile from the middle of Van Buren County. It reported 291 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 31 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
