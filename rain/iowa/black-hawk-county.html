The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Waterloo Municipal Airport, 7 miles from the middle of Black Hawk County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
