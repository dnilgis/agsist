The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles northeast of Lytton, 10 miles from the middle of Calhoun County. It reported 298 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
