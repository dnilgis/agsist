The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4 miles northeast of Lytton, 10 miles from the middle of Calhoun County. It reported 305 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
