How much rain has Johnson County, Iowa, had in 2026?

42.47 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Iowa City Municipal Airport, Johnson County, through Oct 13. That is 12.08 inches more than a usual year to date (140% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Iowa City Municipal Airport had a lot of its year land in September, with 11.35 inches that month. The biggest single day was Sep 19 at 2.09 inches. On the days both years reported, this year is 17.82 inches more than last year. The longest dry stretch ran 10 days, Jun 22 to Jul 1, without even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Iowa City Municipal Airport (station ID 14937), 3 miles from the middle of Johnson County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Johnson County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
