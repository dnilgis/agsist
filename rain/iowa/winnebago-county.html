How much rain has Winnebago County, Iowa, had in 2026?

31.26 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lake Mills, Winnebago County, through Oct 9. That is 1.52 inches more than a usual year to date (105% of usual).

Through Oct 9, the weather station in Lake Mills had its wettest month in September at 6.14 inches. The biggest single day came early, 2.25 inches on Mar 7, and then the gauge sat empty 16 days in a row from Mar 17 to Apr 1. On the days both years reported, this year is 5.30 inches behind last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lake Mills (station ID 134557), 10 miles from the middle of Winnebago County. It reported 258 of the 282 days from Jan 1 to Oct 9; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 258 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Winnebago County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
