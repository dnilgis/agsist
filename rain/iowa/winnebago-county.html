How much rain has Winnebago County, Iowa, had in 2026?

31.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Winnebago County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 94% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lake Mills, 10 miles from the middle of Winnebago County. It reported 295 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
