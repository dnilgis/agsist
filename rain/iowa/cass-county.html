The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 7.1 miles north-northeast of Atlantic (station ATLANTIC 7.1 NNE), 11 miles from the middle of Cass County. It reported 281 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 281 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Cass County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Cass County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 7.1 miles north-northeast of Atlantic had 28.51 inches in 2025 and 37.64 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 281 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.4 inches more.
