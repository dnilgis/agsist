The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Boone, 2 miles from the middle of Boone County. It reported 319 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 319 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
