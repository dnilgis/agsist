The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Iowa Falls, 9 miles from the middle of Hardin County. It reported 325 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
