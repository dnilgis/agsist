How much rain has Jones County, Iowa, had in 2026?

45.77 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 3 miles south-southwest of Anamosa in Jones County, through Nov 9. That is 9.70 inches more than a usual year to date (127% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 46.73 inches, 133% of usual.

  • Wettest month: September, 8.79 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 4.43 inches on Aug 1.
  • Longest dry stretch: 16 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 4 to Feb 19.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles south-southwest of Anamosa, 9 miles from the middle of Jones County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Jones County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
