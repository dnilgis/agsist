How much rain has Jackson County, Iowa, had in 2026?

40.77 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Bellevue, Jackson County, through Oct 18. That is 9.31 inches more than a usual year to date (130% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Bellevue had its wettest month in September at 11.14 inches, and the biggest single day was Sep 19 with 3.10 inches. Earlier on, it went 16 days with nothing in the gauge, from Feb 4 to Feb 19. On the days both years reported, this year ran 10.79 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bellevue (station ID 130608), 10 miles from the middle of Jackson County. It reported 279 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 279 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Jackson County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
