The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Donnellson, 4 miles from the middle of Lee County. It reported 329 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
