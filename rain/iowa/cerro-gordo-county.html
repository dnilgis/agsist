The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Mason City Municipal Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Cerro Gordo County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
