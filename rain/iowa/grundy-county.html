How much rain has Grundy County, Iowa, had in 2026?

43.46 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Grundy Center, Grundy County, through Oct 13. That is 12.37 inches more than a usual year to date (140% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Grundy Center has had a wet back end of the year. September was the wettest month at 8.53 inches, and the biggest single day came Oct 1 with 4.20 inches. On the days both years reported, that is 11.05 inches more than last year. The longest dry stretch was 16 days, Feb 4 to Feb 19, with nothing in the gauge.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Grundy Center (station ID 133487), 3 miles from the middle of Grundy County. It reported 276 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 276 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Grundy County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
