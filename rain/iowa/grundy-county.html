The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Grundy Center, 3 miles from the middle of Grundy County. It reported 310 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
