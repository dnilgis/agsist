How much rain has Arkansas had in 2026? County by county

63 Arkansas counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 8 (15 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 54 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 72.5% of a usual year to date and half below.
