The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lee Vining, 13 miles from the middle of Mono County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Mono County, California, had in 2026?

Is Mono County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Mono County, about 9.85 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 81% of the normal 12.09 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Mono County in the last week?

0.92 inches from Nov 19 to Nov 25 at the weather station in Lee Vining, and 1.54 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 27 to Nov 25. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Mono County in 2026?

February, with 3.19 inches at the weather station in Lee Vining, counting Jan 1 through Nov 25.

What was the biggest rain in Mono County in 2026?

1.20 inches on Aug 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 25. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Mono County?

How many growing degree days has Mono County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Mono County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
