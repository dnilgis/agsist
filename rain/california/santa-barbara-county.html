The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cachuma Lake, 7 miles from the middle of Santa Barbara County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Santa Barbara County, California, had in 2026?

Is Santa Barbara County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Cachuma Lake had 14.57 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 86% of the usual 16.85 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Santa Barbara County in the last week?

0.02 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Cachuma Lake, and 3.37 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Santa Barbara County in 2026?

February, with 5.34 inches at the weather station in Cachuma Lake, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Santa Barbara County in 2026?

2.03 inches on Oct 22, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How many growing degree days has Santa Barbara County had in 2026?

3,428 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Cachuma Lake, 103% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

Has Santa Barbara County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Cachuma Lake. Usual: Nov 27, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 13 stations inside Santa Barbara County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
