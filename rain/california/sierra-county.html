2026 so far in Sierra County

  • Wettest month: February, 3.22 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 0.68 inches on Apr 1.
  • Longest dry stretch: 43 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, May 31 to Jul 12.
  • 2025 by the same date: 19.62 inches at the same station. On the 291 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.58 inches less than 2025.
  • On 5 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 24.37 inches, 73% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 11, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Sierra County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 15 points above this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Verdi, 26 miles from the middle of Sierra County. It reported 291 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Sierra County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
