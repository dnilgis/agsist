How much rain has Sierra County, California, had in 2026?

26.54 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sierra County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 78% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Verdi station: 11.71 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the weather station 2 miles west of Verdi. That is 7.15 inches less than a normal year to date (62% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Verdi, 26 miles from the middle of Sierra County. It reported 295 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Sierra County in 2026?

1.04 inches on Nov 14, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 15. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Sierra County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 295 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Sierra County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
