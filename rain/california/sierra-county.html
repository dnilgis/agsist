The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Verdi, 26 miles from the middle of Sierra County. It reported 302 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 302 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
