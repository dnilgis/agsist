The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Burbank Glendale Pasadena Airport, 12 miles from the middle of Los Angeles County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Los Angeles County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Los Angeles County?

How many growing degree days has Los Angeles County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 39 stations inside Los Angeles County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
