The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Red Bluff Municipal Airport, 2 miles from the middle of Tehama County. It reported 315 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Tehama County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Tehama County?

How many growing degree days has Tehama County had in 2026?

4,168 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Red Bluff Municipal Airport, 103% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).

Has Tehama County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Red Bluff Municipal Airport. Typical: Nov 28, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Tehama County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
