How much rain has Butte County, California, had in 2026?

15.99 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Oroville Municipal Airport, Butte County, through Oct 18. That is 1.31 inches less than a usual year to date (92% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Oroville Municipal Airport had its wettest month in April, with 6.10 inches. The biggest single day came Apr 21 at 2.11 inches. Then the gauge went 68 days in a row with nothing, not even a trace, from Jun 27 to Sep 2. That leaves the year 1.09 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Oroville Municipal Airport (station ID 93210), 12 miles from the middle of Butte County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Butte County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
