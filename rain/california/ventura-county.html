The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Camarillo Airport, 17 miles from the middle of Ventura County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Ventura County, California, had in 2026?

Is Ventura County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Ventura County, about 13.37 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 23, 80% of the normal 16.63 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Ventura County in the last week?

1.42 inches from Nov 17 to Nov 23 at the weather station at Camarillo Airport, and 2.91 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 25 to Nov 23. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Ventura County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Ventura County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Ventura County?

How many growing degree days has Ventura County had in 2026?

Has Ventura County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Ventura County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
