How much rain has San Bernardino County, California, had in 2026?

4.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over San Bernardino County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 85% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Yucca Mesa station (NOAA daily record): 3.19 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 26, at the weather station in Yucca Mesa. That is 1.85 inches less than a normal year to date (63% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 22 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Yucca Mesa, 45 miles from the middle of San Bernardino County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 330 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 22 stations inside San Bernardino County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
