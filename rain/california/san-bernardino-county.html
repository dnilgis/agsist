How much rain has San Bernardino County, California, had in 2026?

4.46 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over San Bernardino County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 83% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Yucca Mesa station: 3.18 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Yucca Mesa. That is 1.75 inches less than a normal year to date (65% of normal).

This station is 18 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Yucca Mesa, 45 miles from the middle of San Bernardino County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
