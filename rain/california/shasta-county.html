How much rain has Shasta County, California, had in 2026?

29.60 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Shasta County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Redding gauge: 27.41 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 7.9 miles north-northeast of Redding. That is 8.98 inches less than a normal year to date (75% of normal).

This gauge is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.9 miles north-northeast of Redding, 14 miles from the middle of Shasta County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 321 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 14 stations inside Shasta County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
