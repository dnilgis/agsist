2026 so far in San Francisco County

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in San Francisco Downtown, 1 mile from the middle of San Francisco County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
