The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in San Francisco Downtown, 1 mile from the middle of San Francisco County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has San Francisco County, California, had in 2026?

15.13 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over San Francisco County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 82% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The San Francisco Downtown station had 14.15 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17 (85% of normal).

Is San Francisco County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over San Francisco County, about 15.13 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 82% of the normal 18.53 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in San Francisco County in the last week?

1.95 inches from Nov 11 to Nov 17 at the weather station in San Francisco Downtown, and 2.32 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 19 to Nov 17. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in San Francisco County in 2026?

February, with 4.37 inches at the weather station in San Francisco Downtown, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in San Francisco County in 2026?

1.07 inches on Jan 5, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 17.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in San Francisco County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station in San Francisco Downtown had 13.06 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 7 days) and 14.15 inches in 2026. On the 314 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.09 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has San Francisco County had in 2026?

Has San Francisco County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 17: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in San Francisco Downtown.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside San Francisco County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
