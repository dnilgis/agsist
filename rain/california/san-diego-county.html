The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Ramona Airport (station ID 53120), 11 miles from the middle of San Diego County. It reported every one of the 311 days from Jan 1 to Nov 7. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 50 stations inside San Diego County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How much rain has San Diego County, California, had in 2026?

Is San Diego County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Ramona Airport had 11.06 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 7, 95% of the usual 11.69 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in San Diego County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 1 to Nov 7 at the weather station at Ramona Airport, and 3.76 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 9 to Nov 7. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in San Diego County in 2026?

October, with 3.66 inches at the weather station at Ramona Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 7.

What was the biggest rain in San Diego County in 2026?

1.96 inches on Jan 1, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 7.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in San Diego County?

How many growing degree days has San Diego County had in 2026?

Has San Diego County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 7: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Ramona Airport. Usual: Nov 25, the middle of 22 years, 1991-2020.
