How much rain has Inyo County, California, had in 2026?

3.94 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Inyo County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 78% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Death Valley National Park station (NOAA daily record): 1.07 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station in Death Valley National Park. That is 0.82 inches less than a normal year to date (57% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 21 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Death Valley National Park, 30 miles from the middle of Inyo County. It reported 326 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Inyo County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Inyo County, about 3.94 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 78% of the normal 5.03 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Inyo County in 2026?

October, with 0.42 inches at the weather station in Death Valley National Park, counting Jan 1 through Nov 25.

What was the biggest rain in Inyo County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Inyo County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 25, the weather station in Death Valley National Park had 3.34 inches in 2025 and 1.07 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 326 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.93 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Inyo County had in 2026?

6,542 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 25 at the weather station in Death Valley National Park, 107% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (29 years).

Has Inyo County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 25: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Death Valley National Park. Typical: Dec 10, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Inyo County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
