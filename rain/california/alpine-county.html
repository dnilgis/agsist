How much rain has Alpine County, California, had in 2026?

26.67 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Alpine County, Jan 1 to Nov 16, an estimate from a weather grid (100% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. All 8 stations in Alpine County that reported nearly every day are high mountain sites; the one used is the Ebbetts Pass SNOTEL site, 4 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 35.60 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the figures below are that site’s. That is 5.27 inches less than a normal year to date (87% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ebbetts Pass, 4 miles from the middle of Alpine County. It reported every one of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 317 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
