How much rain has Alpine County, California, had in 2026?

27.71 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Alpine County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, an estimate from a weather grid (103% of normal).

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 1.53 in, 7 days 3.35 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 pm Pacific, Nov 18.

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. All 8 stations in Alpine County that reported nearly every day are high mountain sites; the one used is the Ebbetts Pass SNOTEL site, 4 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 36.00 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the figures below are that site’s. That is 5.03 inches less than a normal year to date (88% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ebbetts Pass, 4 miles from the middle of Alpine County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
