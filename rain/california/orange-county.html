How much rain has Orange County, California, had in 2026?

10.76 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Orange County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 101% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Silverado gauge (credited below): 17.12 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile east-southeast of Silverado. That is 3.51 inches more than a normal year to date (126% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This gauge is 25 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile east-southeast of Silverado, 9 miles from the middle of Orange County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 329 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 19 stations inside Orange County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
