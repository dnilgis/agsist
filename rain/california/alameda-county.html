How much rain has Alameda County, California, had in 2026?

6.57 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Livermore Municipal Airport, Alameda County, through Oct 13. That is 3.58 inches less than a usual year to date (65% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Livermore Municipal Airport has had 39 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, from Sep 5 to Oct 13, and it is still going. February was the wettest month at 2.76 inches, and the biggest single day was 0.95 inches on Jan 4. On the days both years reported, this year has 0.11 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Livermore Municipal Airport (station ID 23285), 5 miles from the middle of Alameda County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 15 stations inside Alameda County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Alameda County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Alameda County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Alameda County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at Livermore Municipal Airport had 6.46 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 6.57 inches in 2026. On the 285 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.11 inches more than 2025.
