The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Livermore Municipal Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Alameda County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Alameda County, California, had in 2026?

Is Alameda County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Alameda County, about 12.36 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 84% of the normal 14.70 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Alameda County in the last week?

2.30 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the weather station at Livermore Municipal Airport, and 2.75 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Alameda County in 2026?

February, with 2.76 inches at the weather station at Livermore Municipal Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Alameda County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Alameda County?

How many growing degree days has Alameda County had in 2026?

Has Alameda County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 18: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Livermore Municipal Airport. Typical: Nov 29, the middle of 23 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 17 stations inside Alameda County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
