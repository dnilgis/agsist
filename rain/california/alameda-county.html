The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Livermore Municipal Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Alameda County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Alameda County, California, had in 2026?

Is Alameda County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 17 stations inside Alameda County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
