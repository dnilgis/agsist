The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Sonoma County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Sonoma County, California, had in 2026?

26.26 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sonoma County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 85% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport station had 19.55 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 19 (77% of normal).

Is Sonoma County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Sonoma County, about 26.26 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 85% of the normal 31.04 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Sonoma County in the last week?

5.19 inches from Nov 13 to Nov 19 at the weather station at Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport, and 5.90 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 21 to Nov 19. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Sonoma County in 2026?

November, with 5.57 inches at the weather station at Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 19.

What was the biggest rain in Sonoma County in 2026?

2.91 inches on Nov 18, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 19.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Sonoma County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 19, the weather station at Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport had 22.69 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 19.55 inches in 2026. On the 321 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.62 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Sonoma County had in 2026?

3,041 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 19 at the weather station at Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport, 103% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (21 years).

Has Sonoma County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 19: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Santa Rosa Sonoma County Airport. Typical: Nov 13, the middle of 23 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 323 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 25 stations inside Sonoma County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
