The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Montague Siskiyou Airport, 13 miles from the middle of Siskiyou County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Siskiyou County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Siskiyou County, about 21.87 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 75% of the normal 29.09 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Siskiyou County in the last week?

0.02 inches from Nov 19 to Nov 25 at the weather station at Montague Siskiyou Airport, and 0.87 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 27 to Nov 25. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Siskiyou County in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 14 stations inside Siskiyou County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
