The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Montague Siskiyou Airport (station ID 24259), 13 miles from the middle of Siskiyou County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Siskiyou County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Questions

How much rain has Siskiyou County, California, had in 2026?

5.58 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Montague Siskiyou Airport, Siskiyou County, through Oct 13. That is 2.23 inches less than a usual year to date (71% of usual).

Is Siskiyou County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Montague Siskiyou Airport had 5.58 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 71% of the usual 7.81 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Siskiyou County in 2026?

February, with 1.94 inches at the weather station at Montague Siskiyou Airport, counting Jan 1 through Oct 13.

What was the biggest rain in Siskiyou County in 2026?

0.79 inches on Feb 8, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Oct 13.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Siskiyou County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at Montague Siskiyou Airport had 6.27 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 5.58 inches in 2026. On the 278 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.95 inches less than 2025.
