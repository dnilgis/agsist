The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Marysville Airport, 16 miles from the middle of Yuba County. It reported 312 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
