How much rain has Mendocino County, California, had in 2026?

22.24 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 5 miles north of Fort Bragg, Mendocino County, through Oct 13. That is 5.97 inches less than a usual year to date (79% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 5 miles north of Fort Bragg had most of its year early. February was the wettest month at 9.74 inches, and the biggest one-day reading came Feb 24 with 3.95 inches. From Jul 8 to Aug 4 the gauge went 28 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year sits 2.36 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles north of Fort Bragg (station ID 043161), 20 miles from the middle of Mendocino County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 15 stations inside Mendocino County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Mendocino County, California, had in 2026?

Is Mendocino County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station 5 miles north of Fort Bragg had 22.24 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 79% of the usual 28.21 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Mendocino County in 2026?

February, with 9.74 inches at the weather station 5 miles north of Fort Bragg, counting Jan 1 through Oct 13.

What was the biggest rain in Mendocino County in 2026?

3.95 inches on Feb 24, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 13. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Mendocino County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station 5 miles north of Fort Bragg had 24.44 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 11 days) and 22.24 inches in 2026. On the 275 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.36 inches less than 2025.
