How much rain has Mendocino County, California, had in 2026?

29.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Mendocino County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 73% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.01 in, 7 days 1.31 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 pm Pacific, Nov 25.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Caspar gauge (credited below): 33.21 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles east-southeast of Caspar. That is 1.27 inches less than a normal year to date (96% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This gauge is 23 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles east-southeast of Caspar, 22 miles from the middle of Mendocino County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 329 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 17 stations inside Mendocino County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
