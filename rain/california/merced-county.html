The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Los Banos, 12 miles from the middle of Merced County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Merced County, California, had in 2026?

7.72 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Merced County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 86% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Los Banos station had 6.65 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18 (90% of normal).

Is Merced County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Merced County, about 7.72 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 86% of the normal 8.96 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Merced County in the last week?

0.84 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the weather station in Los Banos, and 1.60 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Merced County in 2026?

February, with 2.06 inches at the weather station in Los Banos, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Merced County in 2026?

0.63 inches on Feb 19, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Merced County?

How many growing degree days has Merced County had in 2026?

Has Merced County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 18: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Los Banos. Typical: Dec 4, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Merced County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
