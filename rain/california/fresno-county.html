The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fresno Yosemite Int'l, 4 miles from the middle of Fresno County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
