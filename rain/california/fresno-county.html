How much rain has Fresno County, California, had in 2026?

13.18 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Fresno County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 84% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Fresno Yosemite Int'l station: 5.50 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Fresno Yosemite Int'l. That is 3.29 inches less than a normal year to date (63% of normal).

This station is 21 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fresno Yosemite Int'l, 4 miles from the middle of Fresno County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
