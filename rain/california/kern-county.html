How much rain has Kern County, California, had in 2026?

5.05 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Kern County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 71% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Bakersfield station: 3.70 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 5 miles northwest of Bakersfield. That is 1.66 inches less than a normal year to date (69% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles northwest of Bakersfield, 19 miles from the middle of Kern County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 15 stations inside Kern County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
