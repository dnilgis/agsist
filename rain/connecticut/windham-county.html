The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hampton, 6 miles from the middle of Windham County. It reported 314 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Windham County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in Windham County in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Windham County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Windham County in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Windham County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
