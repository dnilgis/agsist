The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Tionesta SE Lake, 11 miles from the middle of Forest County. It reported 325 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
