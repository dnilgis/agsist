How much rain has Fayette County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

48.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Fayette County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 113% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Uniontown gauge: 43.86 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles north-northeast of Uniontown. That is 3.89 inches more than a normal year to date (110% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles north-northeast of Uniontown, 4 miles from the middle of Fayette County. It reported 313 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 313 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Fayette County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
