How much rain has Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

39.41 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Susquehanna County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 96% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Forest City gauge: 37.66 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 8, at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile southeast of Forest City. That is 2.11 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile southeast of Forest City, 21 miles from the middle of Susquehanna County. It reported 311 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 311 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Susquehanna County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
