The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.2 miles south-southwest of Hermitage, 12 miles from the middle of Mercer County. It reported 308 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
