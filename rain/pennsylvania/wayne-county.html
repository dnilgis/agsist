2026 so far in Wayne County

  • Wettest month: September, 7.57 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 1.67 inches on Oct 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 11 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Oct 28.
  • 2025 by the same date: 45.36 inches at the same station (it missed 15 days). On the 288 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.11 inches less than 2025.
  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • On record: 36th wettest by Nov 1 of 87 years on record here (1926 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2016. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 39.39 inches, 98% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Wayne County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile west of Pleasant Mount, 10 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported 303 of the 305 days from Jan 1 to Nov 1; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 303 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Wayne County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
