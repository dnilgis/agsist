The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northeast of Beaver Falls, 6 miles from the middle of Beaver County. It reported 309 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
