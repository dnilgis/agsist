The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.7 miles northwest of Canonsburg, 8 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
