The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Norristown, 7 miles from the middle of Montgomery County. It reported 315 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
