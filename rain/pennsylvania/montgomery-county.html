The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Norristown, 7 miles from the middle of Montgomery County. It reported 318 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Montgomery County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Montgomery County in 2026?
