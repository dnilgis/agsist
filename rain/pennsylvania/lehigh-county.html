The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles east-northeast of Schnecksville, 5 miles from the middle of Lehigh County. It reported every one of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Lehigh County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Lehigh County, about 36.75 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 85% of the normal 43.38 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Lehigh County in the last week?

0.91 inches from Nov 7 to Nov 13 at the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles east-northeast of Schnecksville, and 1.54 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 15 to Nov 13. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lehigh County?
