The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles east-northeast of Schnecksville, 5 miles from the middle of Lehigh County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain fell in Lehigh County in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Lehigh County in 2026?

March, with 6.03 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles east-northeast of Schnecksville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Lehigh County in 2026?

2.08 inches on Oct 12, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lehigh County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles east-northeast of Schnecksville had 34.15 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 36.36 inches in 2026. On the 314 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.30 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Lehigh County had in 2026?

3,352 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (29 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Lehigh County in 2026?

Oct 18 (32°F or colder) at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Oct 18, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.
