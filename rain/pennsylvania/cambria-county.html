How much rain has Cambria County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

50.26 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Cambria County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 123% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Ebensburg Sewage Plant station: 47.62 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the Ebensburg Sewage Plant weather station in Ebensburg. That is 4.72 inches more than a normal year to date (111% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ebensburg, 2 miles from the middle of Cambria County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
