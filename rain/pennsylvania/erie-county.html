The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Erie International Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Erie County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Erie County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

Is Erie County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in Erie County in the last week?

0.26 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the weather station at Erie International Airport, and 1.04 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Erie County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Erie County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
