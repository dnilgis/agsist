The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lewistown, 3 miles from the middle of Mifflin County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Mifflin County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

42.07 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lewistown in Mifflin County, through Nov 12. That is 5.06 inches more than a normal year to date (114% of normal).

Is Mifflin County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Lewistown had 42.07 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 114% of the normal 37.01 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Mifflin County in the last week?

0.12 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Lewistown, and 0.34 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Mifflin County in 2026?

August, with 8.56 inches at the weather station in Lewistown, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Mifflin County in 2026?

2.50 inches on Aug 3, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Mifflin County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Lewistown had 36.68 inches in 2025 and 42.07 inches in 2026. 2026 had 5.39 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Mifflin County had in 2026?

3,260 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Lewistown, 104% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Mifflin County in 2026?

Oct 19 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Lewistown. Typical: Oct 23, the middle of 29 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Mifflin County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
