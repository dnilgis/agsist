How much rain has Mifflin County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

42.38 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lewistown, Mifflin County, through Oct 18. That is 8.07 inches more than a usual year to date (124% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Lewistown has caught 7.96 inches more than it had by the same day last year. August did the most of it, with 8.56 inches, the wettest month so far. The biggest single day was Oct 12, with 2.57 inches in the gauge.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lewistown (station ID 364992), 3 miles from the middle of Mifflin County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Mifflin County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
