The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles east-southeast of Waynesboro, 16 miles from the middle of Franklin County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
