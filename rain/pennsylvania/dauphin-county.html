The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Middletown Harrisburg Int'l Airport, 15 miles from the middle of Dauphin County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
