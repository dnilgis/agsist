The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles south of Selinsgrove, 11 miles from the middle of Snyder County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
