How much rain has Tioga County, Pennsylvania, had in 2026?

33.35 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Tioga County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Covington station: 30.26 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 2 miles west-southwest of Covington. That is 4.94 inches less than a normal year to date (86% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west-southwest of Covington, 8 miles from the middle of Tioga County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
