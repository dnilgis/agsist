How much rain has Santa Cruz County, Arizona, had in 2026?

19.61 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Santa Cruz County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 125% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Nogales International Airport station: 20.01 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station at Nogales International Airport. That is 6.73 inches more than a normal year to date (151% of normal).

This station is 26 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Nogales International Airport, 7 miles from the middle of Santa Cruz County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Santa Cruz County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
