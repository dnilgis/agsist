The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jack Rabbit, 30 miles from the middle of Navajo County. It reported 320 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Navajo County, Arizona, had in 2026?

Is Navajo County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Navajo County, about 13.83 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 144% of the normal 9.60 inches for the same days.
