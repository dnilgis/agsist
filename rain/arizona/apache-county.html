2026 so far in Apache County

  • Wettest month: September, 2.49 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.77 inches on Sep 30.
  • Longest dry stretch: 50 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, May 26 to Jul 14.
  • 2025 by the same date: 5.19 inches at the same station (it missed 3 days). On the 299 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.85 inches more than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 14.56 inches, 141% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Apache County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 23 points above this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.
Weather 49 miles from the station: 3,572 corn GDDs May 1 to Nov 10, 103% of the 1991-2020 average (20 yrs)

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sanders, 15 miles from the middle of Apache County. It reported 302 of the 304 days from Jan 1 to Oct 31; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 302 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 17 stations inside Apache County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
