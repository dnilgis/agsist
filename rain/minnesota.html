How much rain has Minnesota had in 2026? County by county

83 Minnesota counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (13 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 64 counties with a NOAA normal are above 92% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Mower County: 3rd wettest by Nov 12 of 126 years on record (1891 on), 127% of normal.
