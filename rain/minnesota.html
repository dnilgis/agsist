How much rain has Minnesota had in 2026? County by county

81 Minnesota counties have a weather station inside the county that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Nov 4 (13 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 62 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 92.5% of a usual year to date and half below.
