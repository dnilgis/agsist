The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles southeast of Mc Intosh, 10 miles from the middle of Corson County. It reported 325 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
