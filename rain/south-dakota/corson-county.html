The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles southeast of Mc Intosh (station ID 395381), 10 miles from the middle of Corson County. It reported 283 of the 284 days from Jan 1 to Oct 11; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Corson County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Corson County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Corson County?
