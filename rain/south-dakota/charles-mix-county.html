The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Platte, 18 miles from the middle of Charles Mix County. It reported 327 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
