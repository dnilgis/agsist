How much rain has Codington County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

15.97 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Watertown Regional Airport, Codington County, through Oct 13. That is 4.38 inches less than a usual year to date (78% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Watertown Regional Airport has 15.97 inches, 11.20 inches less than it had by this day last year. July was the wettest month at 5.04 inches. The biggest single day came Sep 18 with 1.42 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 12 days, Feb 5 to Feb 16, with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Watertown Regional Airport (station ID 14946), 5 miles from the middle of Codington County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Codington County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
