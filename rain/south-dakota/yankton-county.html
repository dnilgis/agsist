How much rain has Yankton County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

21.69 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Yankton County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles east of Yankton, 9 miles from the middle of Yankton County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
