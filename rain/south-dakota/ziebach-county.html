The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 15 miles south-southeast of Dupree, 12 miles from the middle of Ziebach County. It reported 324 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
