The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles east-northeast of Newell, 14 miles from the middle of Butte County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
