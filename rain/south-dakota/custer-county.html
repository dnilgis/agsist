How much rain has Custer County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

14.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Custer County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 77% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Wind Cave, 8 miles from the middle of Custer County. It reported 320 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Custer County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. Is Custer County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Custer County, about 14.44 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 24, 77% of the normal 18.69 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Custer County in 2026?

September, with 3.82 inches at the weather station in Wind Cave, counting Jan 1 through Nov 23.

What was the biggest rain in Custer County in 2026?

1.22 inches on Sep 7, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 23. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Custer County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 23, the weather station in Wind Cave had 15.10 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 10 days) and 14.38 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 7 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.10 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Custer County had in 2026?

2,966 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 23 at the weather station in Wind Cave, 116% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Custer County in 2026?

Oct 16 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Wind Cave. Typical: Sep 23, the middle of 29 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Custer County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
