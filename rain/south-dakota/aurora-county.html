The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in White Lake, 6 miles from the middle of Aurora County. It reported 312 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
