The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles northwest of Onida, 1 mile from the middle of Sully County. It reported 314 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
