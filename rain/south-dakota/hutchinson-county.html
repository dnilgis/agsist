How much rain has Hutchinson County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Menno, 12 miles from the middle of Hutchinson County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
