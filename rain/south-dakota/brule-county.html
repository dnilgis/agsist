The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.5 miles south-southwest of Chamberlain, 15 miles from the middle of Brule County. It reported 300 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 22 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Brule County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

23.63 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Brule County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 105% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Chamberlain gauge had 26.16 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18.

Is Brule County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Brule County, about 23.63 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 105% of the normal 22.46 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Brule County in 2026?

August, with 5.25 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 7.5 miles south-southwest of Chamberlain, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Brule County in 2026?

4.49 inches on Aug 13, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Brule County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the volunteer rain gauge 7.5 miles south-southwest of Chamberlain had 22.38 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 26.16 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 22 days). On the 292 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.80 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Brule County had in 2026?
