The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles northeast of Milesville, 16 miles from the middle of Haakon County. It reported 325 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
