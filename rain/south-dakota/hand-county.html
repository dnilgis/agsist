The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Miller, 2 miles from the middle of Hand County. It reported 300 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
