The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lacreek National Wildlife Refuge, 8 miles from the middle of Bennett County. It reported every one of the 304 days from Jan 1 to Oct 31. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
