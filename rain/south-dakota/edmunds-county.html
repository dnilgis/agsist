How much rain has Edmunds County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

17.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Edmunds County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 88% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Roscoe, 6 miles from the middle of Edmunds County. It reported 317 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
