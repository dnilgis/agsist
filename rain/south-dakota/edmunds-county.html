How much rain has Edmunds County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

12.18 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Bowdle, Edmunds County, through Oct 12. That is 5.71 inches less than a usual year to date (68% of usual).

Through Oct 12, the weather station in Bowdle had its wettest month in June at 2.49 inches, and its biggest single day came Jun 8 with 1.04 inches. It also went 17 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Apr 30 to May 16. On the days both years reported, this year is 11.95 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bowdle (station ID 390834), 21 miles from the middle of Edmunds County. It reported 277 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 277 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Edmunds County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
