How much rain has Edmunds County, South Dakota, had in 2026?

17.69 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Edmunds County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 88% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Roscoe station: 14.20 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 10, at the weather station in Roscoe. That is 5.56 inches less than a normal year to date (72% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Roscoe, 6 miles from the middle of Edmunds County. It reported 304 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
