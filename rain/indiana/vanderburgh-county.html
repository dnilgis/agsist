The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Evansville Regional Airport, 4 miles from the middle of Vanderburgh County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
