How much rain has Madison County, Indiana, had in 2026?

40.85 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Madison County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 103% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4 miles north of Anderson, 2 miles from the middle of Madison County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
