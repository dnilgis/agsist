How much rain has Wabash County, Indiana, had in 2026?

42.27 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wabash County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 116% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Wabash station: 43.58 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 7, at the weather station in Wabash. That is 7.77 inches more than a normal year to date (122% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Wabash, 4 miles from the middle of Wabash County. It reported every one of the 311 days from Jan 1 to Nov 7. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
