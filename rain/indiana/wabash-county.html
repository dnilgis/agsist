The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Wabash, 3 miles from the middle of Wabash County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
