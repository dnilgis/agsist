The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Elliston, 1 mile from the middle of Greene County. It reported 306 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
