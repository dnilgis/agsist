How much rain has Tipton County, Indiana, had in 2026?

38.98 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Tipton County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 103% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Tipton station: 47.45 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 5 miles southwest of Tipton. That is 10.49 inches more than a normal year to date (128% of normal).

This station is 25 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles southwest of Tipton, 7 miles from the middle of Tipton County. It reported 311 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
