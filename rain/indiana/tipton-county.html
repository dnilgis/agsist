The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles southwest of Tipton, 7 miles from the middle of Tipton County. It reported 313 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
