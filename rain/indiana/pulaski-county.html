2026 so far in Pulaski County

  • Wettest month: April, 8.96 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.60 inches on Jun 8.
  • Longest dry stretch: 22 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jan 27 to Feb 17.
  • 2025 by the same date: 27.56 inches at the same station. 2026 had 11.60 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 8th wettest by Nov 10 of 35 years on record here (1991 on); the last wetter year was 2017, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 42.29 inches, 116% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Pulaski County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Francesville, 10 miles from the middle of Pulaski County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Pulaski County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
