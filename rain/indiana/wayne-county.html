How much rain has Wayne County, Indiana, had in 2026?

51.55 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wayne County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 129% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.06 in. NOAA radar county average to 2 am Eastern, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Richmond Water Works station (NOAA daily record): 56.68 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station in Richmond Water Works. That is 17.60 inches more than a normal year to date (145% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 16 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Richmond Water Works, 7 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Wayne County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
