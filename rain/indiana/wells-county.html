How much rain has Wells County, Indiana, had in 2026?

43.79 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wells County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 117% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles west of Ossian, 10 miles from the middle of Wells County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
