2026 so far in Posey County

  • Wettest month: June, 8.50 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 5.02 inches on Jun 28.
  • Longest dry stretch: 14 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Oct 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 40.55 inches at the same gauge (it missed 9 days). On the 293 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.54 inches less than 2025.
  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 36.51 inches, 90% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Posey County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.8 miles northwest of Poseyville, 12 miles from the middle of Posey County. It reported 302 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 302 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Posey County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
