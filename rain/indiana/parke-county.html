The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rockville, 2 miles from the middle of Parke County. It reported 324 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
