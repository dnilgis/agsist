The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bloomington Indiana, 1 mile from the middle of Monroe County. It reported 318 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
