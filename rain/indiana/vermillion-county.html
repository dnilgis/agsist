The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west-northwest of Perrysville, 15 miles from the middle of Vermillion County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
