How much rain has LaPorte County, Indiana, had in 2026?

41.92 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over LaPorte County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 114% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.10 in, 7 days 0.18 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 pm Eastern, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Laporte station (NOAA daily record): 51.56 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station in Laporte. That is 12.36 inches more than a normal year to date (132% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 18 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Laporte, 5 miles from the middle of LaPorte County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
