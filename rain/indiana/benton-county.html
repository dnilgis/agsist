The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5 miles northwest of Earl Park, 13 miles from the middle of Benton County. It reported 313 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
