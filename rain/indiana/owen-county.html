How much rain has Owen County, Indiana, had in 2026?

46.83 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Owen County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 112% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Spencer gauge: 48.29 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 11, at the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles southeast of Spencer. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles southeast of Spencer, 10 miles from the middle of Owen County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
