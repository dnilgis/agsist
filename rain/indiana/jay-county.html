The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.3 miles south of Portland, 3 miles from the middle of Jay County. It reported 316 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
