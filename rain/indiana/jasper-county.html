The longest dry stretch ran 13 days, Aug 18 to Aug 30, with nothing in the gauge. The year sits 5.42 inches less than a usual year, but 0.82 inches more than 2025 had by this day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rensselaer (station ID 127298), 7 miles from the middle of Jasper County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Jasper County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
