The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.5 miles west of Cloverdale, 10 miles from the middle of Putnam County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
