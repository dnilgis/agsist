The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.7 miles west-northwest of Vevay (gauge ID US1INSW0005), 7 miles from the middle of Switzerland County. It reported 272 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Switzerland County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
