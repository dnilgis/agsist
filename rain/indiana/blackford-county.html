The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles east-southeast of Hartford City, 3 miles from the middle of Blackford County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
