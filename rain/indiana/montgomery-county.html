The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles southeast of Crawfordsville, 6 miles from the middle of Montgomery County. It reported 303 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Montgomery County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Montgomery County in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 303 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Montgomery County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
