How much rain has Union County, Indiana, had in 2026?

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Liberty (station ID 125049), 1 mile from the middle of Union County. It reported 282 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Union County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
