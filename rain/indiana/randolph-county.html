The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles north-northwest of Farmland (station ID 122825), 10 miles from the middle of Randolph County. It reported 281 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 281 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Randolph County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
