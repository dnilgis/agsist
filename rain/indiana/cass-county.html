How much rain has Cass County, Indiana, had in 2026?

34.77 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Young America, Cass County, through Oct 18. That is 0.39 inches more than a usual year to date (101% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the station in Young America caught 7.84 inches in August, its wettest month, and 2.78 inches of that came on Aug 14, the biggest single day. It has been dry since, with 11 days in a row of nothing in the gauge from Oct 3 to Oct 13, and that stretch is still going. On the days both years reported, this year is 10.16 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Young America (station ID 129905), 14 miles from the middle of Cass County. It reported 289 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 289 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Cass County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
