Questions and answers

How much rain has Georgia had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 102 Georgia counties has a total in the table above. The most is 62.53 inches at Blue Ridge, Fannin County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Georgia wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 72 counties with a NOAA normal, 6 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 63 below and 3 at it. Half are above 87% and half below. 30 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Georgia rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 57 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
