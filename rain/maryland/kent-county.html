The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.9 miles northwest of Worton, 7 miles from the middle of Kent County. It reported 322 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
