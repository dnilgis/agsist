2026 so far in Kent County

  • Wettest month: August, 6.67 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 3.19 inches on Aug 21.
  • Longest dry stretch: 19 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Nov 5.
  • 2025 by the same date: 34.62 inches at the same gauge (it missed 18 days). On the 288 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.52 inches less than 2025.
  • On 11 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Chestertown, 2 miles from the middle of Kent County. It reported 305 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
