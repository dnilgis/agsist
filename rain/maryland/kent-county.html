How much rain has Kent County, Maryland, had in 2026?

34.39 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Chestertown, Kent County, through Oct 18. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

August was the wettest month at 6.67 inches, and the biggest one-day reading came Aug 21 with 3.19 inches. The gauge went 13 days in a row with nothing in it, from Feb 3 to Feb 15. On the days both years reported, this year is running 1.29 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Chestertown (gauge ID US1MDKN0012), 2 miles from the middle of Kent County. It reported 265 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Kent County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
