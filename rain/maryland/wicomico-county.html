The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Wicomico County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Wicomico County, Maryland, had in 2026?

36.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport in Wicomico County, through Nov 12. That is 5.65 inches less than a normal year to date (87% of normal).

Is Wicomico County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport had 36.44 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 87% of the normal 42.09 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Wicomico County in the last week?

1.04 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport, and 2.20 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Wicomico County in 2026?

August, with 9.25 inches at the weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Wicomico County in 2026?

2.68 inches on Jul 27, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wicomico County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport had 35.76 inches in 2025 and 36.44 inches in 2026. 2026 had 0.68 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Wicomico County had in 2026?

3,942 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport, 107% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has Wicomico County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Salisbury-Wicomico Regional Airport. Typical: Oct 29, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Wicomico County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
