The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Baltimore-Washington International Airport, 12 miles from the middle of Anne Arundel County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Anne Arundel County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How much rain fell in Anne Arundel County in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Anne Arundel County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Anne Arundel County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 14 stations inside Anne Arundel County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
