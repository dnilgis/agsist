The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles northeast of Mechanicsville, 15 miles from the middle of St. Mary's County. It reported 311 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
