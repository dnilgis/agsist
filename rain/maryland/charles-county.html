How much rain has Charles County, Maryland, had in 2026?

34.29 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Charles County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 87% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Nanjemoy gauge: 33.23 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles south of Nanjemoy. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles south of Nanjemoy, 15 miles from the middle of Charles County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Charles County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

0.03 inches from Nov 10 to Nov 16 at the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles south of Nanjemoy, and 0.19 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 18 to Nov 16. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the biggest rain in Charles County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Charles County?
