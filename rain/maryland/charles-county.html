The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles south of Nanjemoy, 15 miles from the middle of Charles County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
