The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles west of Prince Frederick, 3 miles from the middle of Calvert County. It reported 318 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Calvert County, Maryland, had in 2026?

34.66 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Calvert County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 85% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Prince Frederick gauge had 34.10 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17.

Is Calvert County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Calvert County, about 34.66 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 85% of the normal 40.93 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Calvert County in 2026?

August, with 9.06 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles west of Prince Frederick, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Calvert County in 2026?

3.23 inches on Aug 3, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Calvert County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles west of Prince Frederick had 42.39 inches in 2025 and 34.10 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 318 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.29 inches less than 2025.
