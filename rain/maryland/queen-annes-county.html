The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles southwest of Stevensville (station ID 188557), 18 miles from the middle of Queen Anne's County. It reported 273 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 273 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Queen Anne's County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
