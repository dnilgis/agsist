How much rain has Queen Anne's County, Maryland, had in 2026?

34.37 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Queen Anne's County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 85% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Stevensville station (NOAA daily record): 30.66 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station 2 miles southwest of Stevensville. That is 11.92 inches less than a normal year to date (72% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles southwest of Stevensville, 18 miles from the middle of Queen Anne's County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
