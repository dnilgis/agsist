How much rain has Utah had in 2026? County by county

29 Utah counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 27 counties with a NOAA normal are above 105% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Garfield County: the wettest by Nov 12 of 64 years on record (1944 on), 165% of normal.
  • Piute County: 2nd wettest by Nov 2 of 30 years on record (1983 on), 126% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
