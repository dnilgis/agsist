Questions and answers

How much rain has Utah had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 27 Utah counties has a total in the table above. The most is 24.83 inches at Bountiful Bench, Davis County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Utah wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 27 counties with a NOAA normal, 19 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 8 below and 0 at it. Half are above 111% and half below. 0 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Utah rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 26, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 2 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
