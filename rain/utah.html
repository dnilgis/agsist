How much rain has Utah had in 2026? County by county

29 Utah counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 8 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 28 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 106% of a usual year to date and half below.
