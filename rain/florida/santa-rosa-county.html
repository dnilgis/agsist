The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.5 miles east of Gulf Breeze, 21 miles from the middle of Santa Rosa County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
