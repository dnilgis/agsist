How much rain has Manatee County, Florida, had in 2026?

50.35 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Manatee County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 98% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Bradenton station (NOAA daily record): 49.68 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23, at the weather station 5 miles east-southeast of Bradenton. That is 3.97 inches less than a normal year to date (93% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles east-southeast of Bradenton, 11 miles from the middle of Manatee County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Manatee County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
