The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Winter Haven Gilbert Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Polk County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the wettest month in Polk County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Polk County?

Has Polk County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 9: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Winter Haven Gilbert Airport.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Polk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
