The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles south of Fernandina Beach, 21 miles from the middle of Nassau County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
