How much rain has Glades County, Florida, had in 2026?

52.17 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Glades County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 107% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Muse, 21 miles from the middle of Glades County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
