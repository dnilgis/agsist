How much rain has Glades County, Florida, had in 2026?

42.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Muse, Glades County, through Oct 18. That is 8.95 inches less than a usual year to date (83% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Muse had its wettest month in September with 11.54 inches. The biggest single day was Apr 7 at 1.98 inches, and right after it came 22 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Apr 11 to May 2. That leaves the year 4.70 inches less than 2025 had by the same day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Muse (station ID 086048), 21 miles from the middle of Glades County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Glades County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
