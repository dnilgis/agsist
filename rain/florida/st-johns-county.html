The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles northeast of Hastings, 10 miles from the middle of St. Johns County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
