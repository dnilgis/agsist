The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile south of Crawfordville, 2 miles from the middle of Wakulla County. It reported 309 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
