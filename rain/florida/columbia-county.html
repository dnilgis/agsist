How much rain has Columbia County, Florida, had in 2026?

43.51 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Columbia County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 90% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Lake City gauge: 41.16 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 3.1 miles south-southwest of Lake City. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.1 miles south-southwest of Lake City, 6 miles from the middle of Columbia County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
