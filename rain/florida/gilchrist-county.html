The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7 miles east of Trenton, 9 miles from the middle of Gilchrist County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
