How much rain has Orange County, Florida, had in 2026?

50.53 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Orange County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 105% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Orlando Executive Airport station: 42.21 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station at Orlando Executive Airport. That is 6.32 inches less than a normal year to date (87% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Orlando Executive Airport, 3 miles from the middle of Orange County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Orange County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
