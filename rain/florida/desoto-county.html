How much rain has DeSoto County, Florida, had in 2026?

49.29 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over DeSoto County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 98% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Harbour Heights gauge: 43.31 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 3.8 miles north-northeast of Harbour Heights. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.8 miles north-northeast of Harbour Heights, 15 miles from the middle of DeSoto County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
