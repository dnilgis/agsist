The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Punta Gorda Charlotte County Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Charlotte County. It reported 313 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
