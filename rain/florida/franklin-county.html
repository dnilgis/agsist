How much rain has Franklin County, Florida, had in 2026?

54.57 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Apalachicola Airport, Franklin County, through Oct 13. That is 11.37 inches more than a usual year to date (126% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Apalachicola Airport has caught 54.57 inches, 11.37 more than a usual year. October is the wettest month at 15.34 inches, and 7.49 of that came on Oct 5, the biggest single day. The gauge also sat empty 23 days in a row from Apr 9 to May 1. On the days both years reported, this year is 9.20 inches ahead of last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Apalachicola Airport (station ID 12832), 17 miles from the middle of Franklin County. It reported 283 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Franklin County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
