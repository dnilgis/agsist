How much rain has Monroe County, Florida, had in 2026?

26.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Marathon Airport in Monroe County, through Nov 9. That is 10.35 inches less than a usual year to date (72% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 44.45 inches, 90% of usual.

2026 so far in Monroe County

  • Wettest month: September, 4.98 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.99 inches on Nov 2.
  • Longest dry stretch: 13 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 17 to Mar 1.
  • 2025 by the same date: 36.17 inches at the same station. 2026 had 10.11 inches less than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 44.45 inches, 90% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Monroe County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 18 points above this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Marathon Airport, 45 miles from the middle of Monroe County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Monroe County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
