How much rain has Lafayette County, Florida, had in 2026?

44.58 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Mayo, Lafayette County, through Oct 5. That is 2.84 inches less than a usual year to date (94% of usual).

Through Oct 5, the weather station in Mayo had its wettest month in August at 9.64 inches. The biggest single day came Sep 7 with 3.31 inches. Before that, the gauge went 23 days in a row without even a trace, from Apr 10 to May 2. On the days both years reported, this year is 4.05 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mayo (station ID 085539), 5 miles from the middle of Lafayette County. It reported 275 of the 278 days from Jan 1 to Oct 5; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 275 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Lafayette County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
