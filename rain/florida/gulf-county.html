How much rain has Gulf County, Florida, had in 2026?

55.05 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Gulf County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 104% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile east-southeast of Wewahitchka, 11 miles from the middle of Gulf County. It reported 296 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 30 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
