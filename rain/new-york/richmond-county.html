The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles southeast of Staten Island, 1 mile from the middle of Richmond County. It reported 302 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
