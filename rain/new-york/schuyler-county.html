The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles northeast of Hector, 9 miles from the middle of Schuyler County. It reported 304 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 25 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
