The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south-southwest of Spring Valley, 4 miles from the middle of Rockland County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
