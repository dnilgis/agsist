How much rain has Albany County, New York, had in 2026?

35.36 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Alcove, Albany County, through Oct 13. That is 1.71 inches more than a usual year to date (105% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Alcove had a big July. July brought 11.76 inches, and 6.20 of that came on Jul 29 alone, the biggest single day of the year. The longest dry stretch ran 10 days, Jan 28 to Feb 6, with not even a trace in the gauge. On the days both years reported, this year is 6.02 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Alcove (station ID 300063), 9 miles from the middle of Albany County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Albany County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
