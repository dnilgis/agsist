How much rain has Ontario County, New York, had in 2026?

33.98 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Ontario County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 103% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bristol Harbour, 8 miles from the middle of Ontario County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Ontario County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
