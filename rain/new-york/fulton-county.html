The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 7 miles northwest of Gloversville, less than a mile from the middle of Fulton County. It reported 316 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
