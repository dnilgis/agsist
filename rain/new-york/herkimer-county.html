The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Stillwater Reservoir, 33 miles from the middle of Herkimer County. It reported 301 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 27 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Herkimer County, New York, had in 2026?

39.08 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Herkimer County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 88% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Stillwater Reservoir station had 37.75 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24 (92% of normal).

Is Herkimer County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Herkimer County, about 39.08 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 24, 88% of the normal 44.34 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Herkimer County in 2026?

April, with 5.34 inches at the weather station in Stillwater Reservoir, counting Jan 1 through Nov 24.

What was the biggest rain in Herkimer County in 2026?

1.37 inches on Apr 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 24. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Herkimer County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 24, the weather station in Stillwater Reservoir had 40.93 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 37.75 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 27 days). On the 297 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.54 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Herkimer County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 301 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Herkimer County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
