How much rain has Dutchess County, New York, had in 2026?

38.33 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Dutchess County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 95% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Pleasant Valley gauge: 39.01 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south of Pleasant Valley. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south of Pleasant Valley, 5 miles from the middle of Dutchess County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
