How much rain has Clinton County, New York, had in 2026?

25.03 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Plattsburgh International Airport, Clinton County, through Oct 13. That is 0.84 inches more than a usual year to date (103% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Plattsburgh International Airport saved its biggest single day for last, 2.56 inches on Oct 13. June was the wettest month with 4.88 inches. On the days both years reported, this year is running 7.83 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Plattsburgh International Airport (station ID 64776), 13 miles from the middle of Clinton County. It reported 278 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 278 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Clinton County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
