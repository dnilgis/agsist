The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 7 miles east-northeast of Rochelle, 19 miles from the middle of Weston County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
