The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles east-northeast of Laramie, 25 miles from the middle of Albany County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
