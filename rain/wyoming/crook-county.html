How much rain has Crook County, Wyoming, had in 2026?

10.70 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Crook County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 60% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Devils Tower No. 2 station (NOAA daily record): 7.91 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station in Devils Tower. That is 10.19 inches less than a normal year to date (44% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Devils Tower, 7 miles from the middle of Crook County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
