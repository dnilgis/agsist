How much rain has Park County, Wyoming, had in 2026?

May was the wettest month with 1.31 inches, and the biggest single day came May 20 with 0.72 inches. The gauge then went 16 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Jul 27 to Aug 11.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Buffalo Bill (station ID 481175), 21 miles from the middle of Park County. It reported every one of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 31 stations inside Park County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
