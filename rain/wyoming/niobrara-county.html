Redbird station: 12.75 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the weather station in Redbird. That is 1.35 inches less than a normal year to date (90% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Redbird, 16 miles from the middle of Niobrara County. It reported 314 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
