How much rain has Lincoln County, Wyoming, had in 2026?

15.59 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lincoln County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 90% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Fossil Butte station: 15.51 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Fossil Butte. That is 5.04 inches more than a normal year to date (148% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fossil Butte, 30 miles from the middle of Lincoln County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
