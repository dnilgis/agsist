How much rain has Fremont County, Wyoming, had in 2026?

4.95 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Riverton Regional Airport, Fremont County, through Oct 18. That is 2.79 inches less than a usual year to date (64% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Riverton Regional Airport had September as its wettest month, with 1.55 inches. The biggest single day came on Sep 19, at 0.77 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 22 days, Jan 26 to Feb 16, with not even a trace. Last year at this point the gauge had 8.23 inches, so this year sits 3.53 inches less.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Riverton Regional Airport (station ID 24061), 9 miles from the middle of Fremont County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 26 stations inside Fremont County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
