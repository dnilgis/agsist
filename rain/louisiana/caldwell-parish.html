The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Columbia Lock (station COLUMBIA LOCK), 5 miles from the middle of Caldwell Parish. It reported 282 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Caldwell Parish reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Caldwell Parish, Louisiana had in 2026?

Is Caldwell Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The NOAA station in Columbia Lock had 28.77 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 12, 63% of the usual 45.33 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Caldwell Parish in 2026?

May, with 9.10 inches at the NOAA station in Columbia Lock, counting the months through Oct 12.

What was the biggest rain in Caldwell Parish in 2026?

2.21 inches on May 9, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 12. Reports that cover more than one day are not counted as one day.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Caldwell Parish?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the NOAA station in Columbia Lock had 48.94 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 28.77 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 280 days both years reported, 2026 had 21.5 inches less.
