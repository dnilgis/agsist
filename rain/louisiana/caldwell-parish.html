The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Columbia Lock, 5 miles from the middle of Caldwell Parish. It reported 319 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 319 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
