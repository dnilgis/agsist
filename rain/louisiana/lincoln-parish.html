  • Longest dry stretch: 26 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 6 to Oct 31.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.4 miles east-northeast of Ruston, 7 miles from the middle of Lincoln Parish. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
