Ruston gauge: 30.80 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 5.4 miles east-northeast of Ruston. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.4 miles east-northeast of Ruston, 7 miles from the middle of Lincoln Parish. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
