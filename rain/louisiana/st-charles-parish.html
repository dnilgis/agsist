How much rain has St. Charles Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

56.53 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of St Rose, St. Charles Parish, through Oct 18. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of St Rose had June as its wettest month at 12.91 inches. The biggest single day came Jun 18 with 5.77 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 14 days, Mar 17 to Mar 30, with nothing in the gauge. On the days both years reported, this year sits 2.53 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of St Rose (gauge ID US1LASC0006), 4 miles from the middle of St. Charles Parish. It reported 290 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside St. Charles Parish that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
