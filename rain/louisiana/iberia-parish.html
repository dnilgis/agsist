The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles northwest of Jeanerette, 5 miles from the middle of Iberia Parish. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
