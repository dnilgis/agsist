The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Lafayette Regional Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Lafayette Parish. It reported 329 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
