2026 so far in Iberville Parish

  • Wettest month: May, 12.83 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 4.65 inches on Mar 8.
  • Longest dry stretch: 26 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 6 to Oct 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 58.48 inches at the same station (it missed 4 days). On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.70 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 28th wettest by Nov 10 of 64 years on record here (1899 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2024. Years with too many missed days are not counted.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north of Plaquemine, 7 miles from the middle of Iberville Parish. It reported 313 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
