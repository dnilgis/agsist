The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles north-northeast of Addis, 7 miles from the middle of West Baton Rouge Parish. It reported 322 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
