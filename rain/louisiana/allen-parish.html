The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Oberlin Fire Tower, 5 miles from the middle of Allen Parish. It reported 313 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Allen Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How many growing degree days has Allen Parish had in 2026?

Has Allen Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
