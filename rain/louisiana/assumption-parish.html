The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles southwest of Donaldsonville, 12 miles from the middle of Assumption Parish. It reported 317 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 317 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
