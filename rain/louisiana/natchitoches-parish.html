Natchitoches #2 station: 31.80 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Natchitoches. That is 16.39 inches less than a normal year to date (66% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Natchitoches, 6 miles from the middle of Natchitoches Parish. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Natchitoches Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

Is Natchitoches Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in Natchitoches Parish in the last week?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Natchitoches Parish?

Has Natchitoches Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Natchitoches Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
