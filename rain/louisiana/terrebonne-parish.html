The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north of Dulac, 10 miles from the middle of Terrebonne Parish. It reported 308 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Terrebonne Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

42.96 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Terrebonne Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 77% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Dulac station had 46.70 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18 (77% of normal).

Is Terrebonne Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Terrebonne Parish, about 42.96 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 77% of the normal 55.97 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Terrebonne Parish in 2026?

July, with 8.58 inches at the weather station 3 miles north of Dulac, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Terrebonne Parish in 2026?

3.55 inches on Jun 18, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Terrebonne Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station 3 miles north of Dulac had 38.68 inches in 2025 and 46.70 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 14 days). On the 308 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.59 inches more than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 308 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Terrebonne Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
