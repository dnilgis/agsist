How much rain has St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

44.20 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Slidell Airport, St. Tammany Parish, through Oct 13. That is 7.04 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Slidell Airport had 44.20 inches, 7.04 inches less than a usual year. May was the wettest month at 11.94 inches, and the biggest single day was Jun 18 with 5.44 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 12 days, Mar 17 to Mar 28. On the days both years reported, this year is 7.22 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Slidell Airport (station ID 53865), 10 miles from the middle of St. Tammany Parish. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside St. Tammany Parish reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
