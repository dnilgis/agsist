The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport (station ID 53917), 6 miles from the middle of Orleans Parish. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Orleans Parish reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Orleans Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Orleans Parish?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport had 42.46 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 33.31 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 282 days both years reported, 2026 had 9.20 inches less than 2025.
