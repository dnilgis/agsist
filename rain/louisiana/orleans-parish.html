The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Orleans Parish. It reported 315 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Orleans Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

Is Orleans Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport had 35.12 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 66% of the usual 53.15 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Orleans Parish in the last week?

0.06 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport, and 1.81 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Orleans Parish in 2026?

May, with 11.29 inches at the weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Orleans Parish in 2026?

3.72 inches on May 25, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Orleans Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport had 44.30 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 35.12 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 312 days both years reported, 2026 had 9.23 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Orleans Parish had in 2026?

5,748 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport, 102% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Has Orleans Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at New Orleans Lakefront Airport.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Orleans Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
