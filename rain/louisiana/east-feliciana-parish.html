2026 so far in East Feliciana Parish

  • Wettest month: May, 12.15 inches.

  • Longest dry stretch: 22 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 10 to Oct 31.

  • On 3 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 51.99 inches, 94% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 11, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over East Feliciana Parish (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.6 miles north-northeast of Jackson, 9 miles from the middle of East Feliciana Parish. It reported 308 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
