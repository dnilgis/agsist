The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bunkie (station ID 161287), 14 miles from the middle of Avoyelles Parish. It reported 257 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 28 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 257 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Avoyelles Parish reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Avoyelles Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Avoyelles Parish in 2026?

June, with 13.14 inches at the weather station in Bunkie, counting Jan 1 through Oct 12.

What was the biggest rain in Avoyelles Parish in 2026?

3.85 inches on Jun 17, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Avoyelles Parish?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the weather station in Bunkie had 50.47 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 35.67 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 28 days). On the 253 days both years reported, 2026 had 13.29 inches less than 2025.
