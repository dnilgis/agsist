The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.1 miles south-southwest of Atlanta, 18 miles from the middle of Winn Parish. It reported 328 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
