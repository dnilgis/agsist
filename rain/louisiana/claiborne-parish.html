The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile north of Homer, 4 miles from the middle of Claiborne Parish. It reported 323 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
