2026 so far in Evangeline Parish

  • Wettest month: May, 9.76 inches.
  • Biggest single day: not known. 41 reports cover more than one day, and the biggest day could be inside one of them.
  • Longest dry stretch: 5 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 23 to Feb 27.
  • On 41 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 44.95 inches, 87% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Evangeline Parish (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile east-southeast of Fsa-Ville Platte, 9 miles from the middle of Evangeline Parish. It reported 300 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
