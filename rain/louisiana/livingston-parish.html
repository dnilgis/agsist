2026 so far in Livingston Parish

  • Wettest month: May, 14.40 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 4.41 inches on Jun 18.
  • Longest dry stretch: 26 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 6 to Oct 31.
  • On record: 10th wettest by Nov 11 of 46 years on record here (1980 on); the last wetter year was 2024, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 53.22 inches, 93% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 11, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Livingston Parish (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 20 points below this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Livingston, 6 miles from the middle of Livingston Parish. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Livingston Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
