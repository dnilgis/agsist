The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles northeast of New Roads (station ID 166686), 14 miles from the middle of Pointe Coupee Parish. It reported 278 of the 281 days from Jan 1 to Oct 8; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 278 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Pointe Coupee Parish that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
