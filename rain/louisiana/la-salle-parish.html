The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles west-southwest of Trout, 6 miles from the middle of La Salle Parish. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has La Salle Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

35.74 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over La Salle Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 71% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Trout gauge had 38.61 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18.

Is La Salle Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over La Salle Parish, about 35.74 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 71% of the normal 50.25 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in La Salle Parish in 2026?

June, with 8.79 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles west-southwest of Trout, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in La Salle Parish in 2026?

4.81 inches on Jun 16, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in La Salle Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles west-southwest of Trout had 59.02 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 38.61 inches in 2026. On the 320 days both years reported, 2026 had 20.41 inches less than 2025.
