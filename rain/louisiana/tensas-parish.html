The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Fsa-St. Joseph, 8 miles from the middle of Tensas Parish. It reported 313 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
