How much rain has Catahoula Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

44.96 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Red River Lock, Catahoula Parish, through Oct 13. That is 6.98 inches more than a usual year to date (118% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Red River Lock had June as the wettest month at 16.30 inches. The biggest single day was Jun 17 with 5.70 inches. From Feb 16 to Mar 7 it went 20 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year is 4.39 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Red River Lock (station ID 167729), 29 miles from the middle of Catahoula Parish. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Catahoula Parish that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
