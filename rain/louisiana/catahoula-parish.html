How much rain has Catahoula Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

38.42 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Catahoula Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 76% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Fsa-Jonesville gauge: 37.71 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Fsa-Jonesville. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Fsa-Jonesville, 3 miles from the middle of Catahoula Parish. It reported 311 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Catahoula Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

Is Catahoula Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Catahoula Parish, about 38.42 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 76% of the normal 50.28 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Catahoula Parish in 2026?

June, with 7.80 inches at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Fsa-Jonesville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 16.

What was the biggest rain in Catahoula Parish in 2026?

2.37 inches on May 22, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 16. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Catahoula Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 16, the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Fsa-Jonesville had 61.29 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 30 days) and 37.71 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 9 days). On the 281 days both years reported, 2026 had 23.32 inches less than 2025.
