How much rain has Richland Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

39.24 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Richland Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 78% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles west of Fsa-Rayville, 4 miles from the middle of Richland Parish. It reported 319 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
