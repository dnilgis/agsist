The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles west of Fsa-Rayville, 4 miles from the middle of Richland Parish. It reported 290 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
