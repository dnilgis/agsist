The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Plain Dealing, 15 miles from the middle of Bossier Parish. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Bossier Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

30.34 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Plain Dealing in Bossier Parish, through Nov 12. That is 15.12 inches less than a normal year to date (67% of normal).

Is Bossier Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Plain Dealing had 30.34 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 67% of the normal 45.46 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Bossier Parish in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Bossier Parish in 2026?

June, with 10.10 inches at the weather station in Plain Dealing, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Bossier Parish in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bossier Parish?

How many growing degree days has Bossier Parish had in 2026?

5,100 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at a weather station 32 miles from the rain station, 108% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

Has Bossier Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 11: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 32 miles from the rain station. Typical: Nov 15, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Bossier Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
