The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Plain Dealing, 15 miles from the middle of Bossier Parish. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Bossier Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

Is Bossier Parish wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Bossier Parish, about 34.73 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 24, 73% of the normal 47.38 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Bossier Parish in the last week?

0.08 inches from Nov 16 to Nov 22 at the weather station in Plain Dealing, and 1.96 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 24 to Nov 22. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Bossier Parish in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Bossier Parish in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bossier Parish?

How many growing degree days has Bossier Parish had in 2026?

Has Bossier Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 23: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 32 miles from the rain station. Typical: Nov 15, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Bossier Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
