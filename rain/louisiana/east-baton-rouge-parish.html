The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport, 3 miles from the middle of East Baton Rouge Parish. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has East Baton Rouge Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

Is East Baton Rouge Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport had 47.20 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 88% of the usual 53.86 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in East Baton Rouge Parish in the last week?

0.08 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport, and 0.08 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in East Baton Rouge Parish in 2026?

May, with 11.60 inches at the weather station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in East Baton Rouge Parish in 2026?

3.97 inches on Jun 16, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in East Baton Rouge Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport had 52.50 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 47.20 inches in 2026. On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.72 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has East Baton Rouge Parish had in 2026?

5,256 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has East Baton Rouge Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Baton Rouge Metro Airport. Usual: Nov 26, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 13 stations inside East Baton Rouge Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
