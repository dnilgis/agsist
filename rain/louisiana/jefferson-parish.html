How much rain has Jefferson Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

53.95 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at New Orleans Airport, Jefferson Parish, through Oct 18. That is 1.41 inches more than a usual year to date (103% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at New Orleans Airport had June as its wettest month at 13.31 inches, and 7.75 inches of that came on Jun 18 alone. The longest dry stretch ran 10 days, Feb 5 to Feb 14. On the days both years reported, the station caught 10.68 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at New Orleans Airport (station ID 12916), 14 miles from the middle of Jefferson Parish. It reported 288 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Jefferson Parish reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
