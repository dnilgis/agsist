How much rain has Caddo Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

38.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Shreveport in Caddo Parish, through Nov 9. That is 6.38 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual). Averaged over the whole parish, about 34.43 inches, 78% of usual.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Shreveport, 9 miles from the middle of Caddo Parish. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Caddo Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

Is Caddo Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Shreveport had 38.75 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 9, 86% of the usual 45.13 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Caddo Parish in the last week?

1.92 inches from Nov 3 to Nov 9 at the weather station in Shreveport, and 2.38 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 11 to Nov 9. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Caddo Parish in 2026?

June, with 8.60 inches at the weather station in Shreveport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 9.

What was the biggest rain in Caddo Parish in 2026?

2.66 inches on Jun 15, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 9.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Caddo Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 9, the weather station in Shreveport had 51.69 inches in 2025 and 38.75 inches in 2026. 2026 had 12.94 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Caddo Parish had in 2026?

5,138 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 9 at the weather station in Shreveport.

Has Caddo Parish had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 9: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Shreveport.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Caddo Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
