How much rain has Caddo Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

34.68 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Caddo Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 76% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Shreveport station: 39.02 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Shreveport. That is 7.26 inches less than a normal year to date (84% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Shreveport, 9 miles from the middle of Caddo Parish. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
