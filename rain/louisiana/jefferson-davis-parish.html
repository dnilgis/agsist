The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jennings, 10 miles from the middle of Jefferson Davis Parish. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
