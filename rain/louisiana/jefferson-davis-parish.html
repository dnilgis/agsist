How much rain has Jefferson Davis Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

41.37 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Jennings, Jefferson Davis Parish, through Oct 18. That is 10.40 inches less than a usual year to date (80% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Jennings had May as its wettest month at 10.64 inches. The biggest single day came Sep 23 with 2.39 inches. From Mar 17 to Apr 1 the gauge went 16 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. Against last year, the station is 9.83 inches behind where 2025 stood on Oct 13.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jennings (station ID 164700), 10 miles from the middle of Jefferson Davis Parish. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Jefferson Davis Parish that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
