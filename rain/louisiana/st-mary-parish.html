How much rain has St. Mary Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

42.98 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over St. Mary Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 79% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Baldwin gauge: 49.18 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles north of Baldwin. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles north of Baldwin, 12 miles from the middle of St. Mary Parish. It reported 316 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
