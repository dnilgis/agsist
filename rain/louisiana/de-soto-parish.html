2026 so far in De Soto Parish

  • Wettest month: January, 6.98 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 4.37 inches on Jan 25.
  • Longest dry stretch: 23 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Sep 9 to Oct 1.
  • 2025 by the same date: 41.94 inches at the same station (it missed 5 days). On the 306 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.81 inches less than 2025.
  • On 4 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 34.82 inches, 76% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over De Soto Parish (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Logansport, 16 miles from the middle of De Soto Parish. It reported 311 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

Is De Soto Parish wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Logansport had 33.13 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 10, 72% of the usual 46.23 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in De Soto Parish in the last week?

1.17 inches from Nov 4 to Nov 10 at the weather station in Logansport, and 1.61 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 12 to Nov 10. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in De Soto Parish in 2026?

January, with 6.98 inches at the weather station in Logansport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 10.

What was the biggest rain in De Soto Parish in 2026?

4.37 inches on Jan 25, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 10. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in De Soto Parish?

From Jan 1 to Nov 10, the weather station in Logansport had 41.94 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 5 days) and 33.13 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 306 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.81 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside De Soto Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
