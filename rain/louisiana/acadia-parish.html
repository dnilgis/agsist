2026 so far in Acadia Parish

  • Wettest month: May, 13.23 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 3.53 inches on Jan 25.
  • Longest dry stretch: 21 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 11 to Oct 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 53.47 inches at the same station (it missed 2 days). On the 307 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.69 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 73rd wettest by Nov 6 of 98 years on record here (1927 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2023. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 45.32 inches, 85% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Acadia Parish (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Crowley, 5 miles from the middle of Acadia Parish. It reported 309 of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 309 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Acadia Parish reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
