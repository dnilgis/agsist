How much rain has Acadia Parish, Louisiana, had in 2026?

46.42 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Acadia Parish, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 85% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Crowley station: 43.93 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 2 miles northeast of Crowley. That is 12.56 inches less than a normal year to date (78% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Crowley, 5 miles from the middle of Acadia Parish. It reported 320 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
