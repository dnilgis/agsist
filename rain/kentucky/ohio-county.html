The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rochester Ferry, 18 miles from the middle of Ohio County. It reported 316 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
