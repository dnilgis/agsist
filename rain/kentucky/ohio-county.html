The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rochester Ferry, 18 miles from the middle of Ohio County. It reported 320 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
