The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles east-northeast of Owensboro, 3 miles from the middle of Daviess County. It reported 305 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
