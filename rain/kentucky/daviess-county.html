How much rain has Daviess County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

37.51 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles south of Owensboro, Daviess County, through Oct 18. That is 2.24 inches less than a usual year to date (94% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles south of Owensboro caught 11.00 inches in June, its wettest month. Most of that came on Jun 28, when it read 6.20 inches, the biggest one-day reading of the year. The longest dry stretch ran 20 days, Jan 26 to Feb 14, with nothing in the gauge. On the days both years reported, it is 12.19 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles south of Owensboro (gauge ID US1KYDV0003), 4 miles from the middle of Daviess County. It reported 290 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 290 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 8 stations inside Daviess County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Daviess County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles south of Owensboro had 37.51 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 18, 94% of the usual 39.75 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Daviess County in 2026?

June, with 11.00 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles south of Owensboro, counting Jan 1 through Oct 18.

What was the biggest rain in Daviess County in 2026?

6.20 inches on Jun 28, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Daviess County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 18, the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles south of Owensboro had 49.25 inches in 2025 and 37.51 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 290 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.74 inches less than 2025.
