The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.4 miles east-northeast of Sturgis, 7 miles from the middle of Union County. It reported 312 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
