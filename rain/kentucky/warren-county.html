How much rain has Warren County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

34.27 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Bowling Green Warren County Airport, Warren County, through Oct 13. That is 5.66 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual).

July was the wettest month at 8.25 inches, and Jul 11 brought the biggest single day at 2.95 inches. Then the gauge sat empty for 15 days in a row, Aug 21 to Sep 4. Last year at this point it held 50.58 inches, so this year is 16.31 inches behind.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Bowling Green Warren County Airport (station ID 93808), 2 miles from the middle of Warren County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Warren County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
