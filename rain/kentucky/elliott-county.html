The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles south-southeast of Olive Hill, 5 miles from the middle of Elliott County. It reported 308 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
