How much rain has Elliott County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

36.87 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Elliott County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 88% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Olive Hill gauge: 39.76 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles south-southeast of Olive Hill. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles south-southeast of Olive Hill, 5 miles from the middle of Elliott County. It reported 305 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
