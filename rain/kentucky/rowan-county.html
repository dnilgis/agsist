The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cave Run Lake, 7 miles from the middle of Rowan County. It reported 309 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Rowan County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

Is Rowan County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Rowan County, about 37.58 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 86% of the normal 43.76 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Rowan County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Rowan County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Rowan County?
