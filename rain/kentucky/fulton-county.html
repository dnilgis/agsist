2026 so far in Fulton County

  • Wettest month: May, 7.59 inches.
  • Biggest single day: not known. 6 reports cover more than one day, and the biggest day could be inside one of them.
  • Longest dry stretch: 15 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 17 to Oct 31.
  • On 6 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 34.92 inches, 79% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Fulton County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.0 miles west of Fulton, 12 miles from the middle of Fulton County. It reported 287 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 25 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
