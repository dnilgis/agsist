The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.2 miles west-southwest of Greenville, 8 miles from the middle of Muhlenberg County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
