The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Lexington Bluegrass Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Fayette County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Fayette County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Fayette County in 2026?

How many growing degree days has Fayette County had in 2026?

Sources and terms
