The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.3 miles northeast of Fulton, 13 miles from the middle of Hickman County. It reported 309 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
