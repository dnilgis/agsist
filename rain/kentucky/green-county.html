The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Greensburg, 3 miles from the middle of Green County. It reported 302 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 302 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
