How much rain has Lyon County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

35.82 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 5 miles south of Eddyville, Lyon County, through Oct 11. That is 4.35 inches less than a usual year to date (89% of usual).

Through Oct 11, the weather station 5 miles south of Eddyville had 35.82 inches, 4.35 inches less than a usual year. July was the wettest month at 7.23 inches. The gauge stayed empty 13 days in a row from Aug 28 to Sep 9, then got its biggest one-day reading, 1.76 inches, on Oct 2. On the days both years reported, that is 18.69 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles south of Eddyville (station ID 152442), 2 miles from the middle of Lyon County. It reported 276 of the 284 days from Jan 1 to Oct 11; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 276 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Lyon County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
