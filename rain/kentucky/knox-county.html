The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Barbourville, 2 miles from the middle of Knox County. It reported 314 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Knox County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Knox County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Knox County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
