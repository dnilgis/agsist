  • Longest dry stretch: 9 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jan 1 to Jan 9.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Barbourville, 2 miles from the middle of Knox County. It reported 301 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 301 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Knox County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
