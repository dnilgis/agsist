The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.6 miles south-southwest of Simpsonville, 8 miles from the middle of Spencer County. It reported 315 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
