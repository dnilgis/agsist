How much rain has Woodford County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

44.09 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Woodford County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 97% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Midway gauge (credited below): 49.37 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile south-southeast of Midway. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with. It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile south-southeast of Midway, 7 miles from the middle of Woodford County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
