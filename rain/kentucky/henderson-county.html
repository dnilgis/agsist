The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles north-northwest of Robards, 4 miles from the middle of Henderson County. It reported 310 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
