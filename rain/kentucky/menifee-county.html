The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.3 miles east-northeast of Means, 8 miles from the middle of Menifee County. It reported 318 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Menifee County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Menifee County in 2026?
