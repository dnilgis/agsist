The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.6 miles east-northeast of Booneville, 6 miles from the middle of Owsley County. It reported 317 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
