How much rain has Owsley County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

The gauge then went 19 days in a row with nothing, from Sep 14 to Oct 2. On the days both years reported, that is 11.14 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.6 miles east-northeast of Booneville (gauge ID US1KYOY0001), 6 miles from the middle of Owsley County. It reported 267 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Owsley County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
