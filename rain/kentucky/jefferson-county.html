The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.3 miles east-southeast of Louisville, 3 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported 324 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Jefferson County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Jefferson County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Jefferson County?
