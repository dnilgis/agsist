The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.3 miles east-southeast of Louisville, 3 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported 317 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
