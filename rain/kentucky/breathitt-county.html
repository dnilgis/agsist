The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Jackson Julian Carroll Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Breathitt County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
