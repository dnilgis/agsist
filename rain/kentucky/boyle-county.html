How much rain has Boyle County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

42.23 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Boyle County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 94% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Danville, 6 miles from the middle of Boyle County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
