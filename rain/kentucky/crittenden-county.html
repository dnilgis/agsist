The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south-southwest of Marion (station ID 155150), 3 miles from the middle of Crittenden County. It reported 269 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 269 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Crittenden County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Crittenden County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station 1 mile south-southwest of Marion had 44.28 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 118% of the usual 37.63 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Crittenden County in 2026?

July, with 9.76 inches at the weather station 1 mile south-southwest of Marion, counting Jan 1 through Oct 13.

What was the biggest rain in Crittenden County in 2026?

3.03 inches on Jul 27, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 13. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Crittenden County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station 1 mile south-southwest of Marion had 48.69 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 15 days) and 44.28 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 17 days). On the 255 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.01 inches less than 2025.
