The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles north-northwest of Dry Ridge, 7 miles from the middle of Grant County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
