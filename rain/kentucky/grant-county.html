The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles north-northwest of Dry Ridge, 7 miles from the middle of Grant County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Grant County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. The weather station 5 miles north-northwest of Dry Ridge had 37.99 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 98% of the normal 38.87 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Grant County in the last week?

0.25 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station 5 miles north-northwest of Dry Ridge, and 2.06 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Grant County in 2026?

May, with 7.72 inches at the weather station 5 miles north-northwest of Dry Ridge, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Grant County in 2026?

2.11 inches on Jul 9, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Grant County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station 5 miles north-northwest of Dry Ridge had 49.92 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 37.99 inches in 2026. On the 315 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.93 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Grant County had in 2026?

Has Grant County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 4: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 1 miles from the rain station.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Grant County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
