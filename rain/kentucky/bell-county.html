The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles south of Frakes, 16 miles from the middle of Bell County. It reported 311 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
