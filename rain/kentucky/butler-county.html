The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.1 miles southwest of Woodbury, 5 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 314 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
