How much rain has Letcher County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

31.86 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 1 mile southeast of Skyline, Letcher County, through Oct 18. That is 8.80 inches less than a usual year to date (78% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 1 mile southeast of Skyline has 31.16 inches, 9.05 inches less than a usual year. August was the wettest month at 5.82 inches. The longest stretch with nothing in the gauge ran 12 days, May 28 to Jun 8. On the days both years reported, this year is 14.84 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile southeast of Skyline (station ID 157431), 8 miles from the middle of Letcher County. It reported 289 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 289 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Letcher County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Letcher County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station 1 mile southeast of Skyline had 31.86 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 18, 78% of the usual 40.66 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Letcher County in 2026?

August, with 5.82 inches at the weather station 1 mile southeast of Skyline, counting Jan 1 through Oct 18.

What was the biggest rain in Letcher County in 2026?

1.37 inches on Sep 24, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Letcher County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 18, the weather station 1 mile southeast of Skyline had 44.96 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 13 days) and 31.86 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 2 days). On the 276 days both years reported, 2026 had 14.14 inches less than 2025.
