How much rain has Martin County, Kentucky, had in 2026?

33.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 2 miles east of Inez, Martin County, through Oct 18. That is 5.10 inches less than a usual year to date (87% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 2 miles east of Inez has caught 33.82 inches, 4.75 inches less than a usual year. June was the wettest month at 5.98 inches, and the biggest one-day reading was 1.87 inches on Feb 20. The longest dry stretch ran 10 days, Apr 7 to Apr 16. On the days both years reported, this year is 2.79 inches behind last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles east of Inez (station ID 154138), 5 miles from the middle of Martin County. It reported 289 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 289 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Martin County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
