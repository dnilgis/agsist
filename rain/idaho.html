Questions and answers

How much rain has Idaho had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 38 Idaho counties has a total in the table above. The most is 29.61 inches at Avery, Shoshone County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Idaho wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 29 counties with a NOAA normal, 9 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 20 below and 0 at it. Half are above 93% and half below. 9 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Idaho rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 26, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 6 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
