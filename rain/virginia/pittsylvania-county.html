The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Danville Regional Airport (station ID 13728), 17 miles from the middle of Pittsylvania County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Pittsylvania County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Pittsylvania County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Pittsylvania County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at Danville Regional Airport had 33.55 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 22.05 inches in 2026. On the 284 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.74 inches less than 2025.
