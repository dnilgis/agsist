The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north of Victoria, 4 miles from the middle of Lunenburg County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
