How much rain has Grayson County, Virginia, had in 2026?

39.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Grayson County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Independence gauge: 37.93 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.3 miles south of Independence. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.3 miles south of Independence, 6 miles from the middle of Grayson County. It reported 304 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Grayson County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Grayson County, about 39.28 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 91% of the normal 43.19 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Grayson County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Grayson County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Grayson County?
