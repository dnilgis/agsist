Charlottesville station: 33.64 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 2 miles west of Charlottesville. That is 9.53 inches less than a normal year to date (78% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Charlottesville, 2 miles from the middle of Albemarle County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
