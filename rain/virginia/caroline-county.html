The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Corbin, 12 miles from the middle of Caroline County. It reported 307 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Caroline County, Virginia, had in 2026?

33.21 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Caroline County, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 82% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Corbin station had 32.79 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 19 (86% of normal).

Is Caroline County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Caroline County, about 33.21 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 23, 82% of the normal 40.72 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Caroline County in 2026?

July, with 7.86 inches at the weather station in Corbin, counting Jan 1 through Nov 19.

What was the biggest rain in Caroline County in 2026?

1.37 inches on Jul 10, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 19. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Caroline County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 19, the weather station in Corbin had 43.39 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 9 days) and 32.79 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 16 days). On the 298 days both years reported, 2026 had 9.80 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Caroline County had in 2026?

3,963 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 18 at the weather station in Corbin, 107% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (24 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Caroline County in 2026?

Nov 14 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Corbin. Typical: Oct 23, the middle of 21 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 307 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Caroline County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
