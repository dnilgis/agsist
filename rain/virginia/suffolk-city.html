The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Suffolk Lake Kilby, 3 miles from the middle of Suffolk City. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
