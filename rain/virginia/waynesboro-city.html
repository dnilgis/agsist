The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles west-northwest of Waynesboro, 2 miles from the middle of Waynesboro City. It reported 305 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Waynesboro City, Virginia, had in 2026?

30.31 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Waynesboro City, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 79% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Waynesboro gauge had 28.48 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 19.

Is Waynesboro City wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Waynesboro City, about 30.31 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 79% of the normal 38.32 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Waynesboro City in 2026?

October, with 4.99 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles west-northwest of Waynesboro, counting Jan 1 through Nov 19.

What was the biggest rain in Waynesboro City in 2026?

1.80 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 19. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Waynesboro City?

From Jan 1 to Nov 19, the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles west-northwest of Waynesboro had 29.61 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 7 days) and 28.48 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 18 days). On the 300 days both years reported, 2026 had about the same as 2025.
