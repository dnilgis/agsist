How much rain has Washington County, Virginia, had in 2026?

36.45 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 3 miles south of Abingdon, Washington County, through Oct 13. That is 1.54 inches less than a usual year to date (96% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 3 miles south of Abingdon had August as its wettest month at 5.02 inches. Then it went 14 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Aug 23 to Sep 5. The biggest one-day reading was 1.70 inches on Jun 19. On the days both years reported, this year is 3.55 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles south of Abingdon (station ID 440021), 4 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported 284 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Washington County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
