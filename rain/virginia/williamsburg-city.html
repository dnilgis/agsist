The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles east-northeast of Williamsburg, 1 mile from the middle of Williamsburg City. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
