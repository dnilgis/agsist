2026 so far in Warren County

  • Wettest month: September, 4.89 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.10 inches on Oct 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 18 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Nov 4.
  • 2025 by the same date: 43.01 inches at the same station (it missed 19 days). On the 289 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.93 inches less than 2025.
  • On 4 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 37.77 inches, 102% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Warren County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Front Royal, 2 miles from the middle of Warren County. It reported 307 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Warren County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How much rain fell in Warren County in the last week?

0.43 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Front Royal, and 3.65 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Warren County in 2026?

September, with 4.89 inches at the weather station in Front Royal, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Warren County in 2026?

2.10 inches on Oct 12, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 8. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Warren County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station in Front Royal had 43.01 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 19 days) and 34.02 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 5 days). On the 289 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.93 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 307 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Warren County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
