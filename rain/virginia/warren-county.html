The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Front Royal, 2 miles from the middle of Warren County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
