The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.7 miles northeast of Providence Forge, 2 miles from the middle of New Kent County. It reported 319 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
