The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Washington Reagan National Airport, 4 miles from the middle of Arlington County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
