The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles south-southwest of Concord, 9 miles from the middle of Campbell County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
