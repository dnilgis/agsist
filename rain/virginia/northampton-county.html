How much rain has Northampton County, Virginia, had in 2026?

30.99 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Northampton County, Jan 1 to Nov 25. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5 miles north-northwest of Eastville, 8 miles from the middle of Northampton County. It reported 325 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain fell in Northampton County in the last week?

0.20 inches from Nov 18 to Nov 24 at the volunteer rain gauge 5 miles north-northwest of Eastville, and 1.63 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 26 to Nov 24. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Northampton County in 2026?

July, with 6.60 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 5 miles north-northwest of Eastville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 24.

What was the biggest rain in Northampton County in 2026?

2.10 inches on Jul 12, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 24. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Northampton County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 24, the volunteer rain gauge 5 miles north-northwest of Eastville had 32.34 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 10 days) and 28.95 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 315 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.87 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Northampton County had in 2026?

4,166 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 25 at a weather station 9 miles from the rain gauge.

Has Northampton County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 25: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 9 miles from the rain gauge.
