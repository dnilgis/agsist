The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 7 miles southeast of Winchester, 9 miles from the middle of Frederick County. It reported 302 of the 308 days from Jan 1 to Nov 4; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
