The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Richlands, 13 miles from the middle of Tazewell County. It reported 326 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
