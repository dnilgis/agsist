The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Martinsville Filter, 2 miles from the middle of Henry County. It reported 325 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
