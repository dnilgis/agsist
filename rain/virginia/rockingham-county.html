How much rain has Rockingham County, Virginia, had in 2026?

37.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Rockingham County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 96% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Dale Enterprise station: 33.06 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the weather station in Dale Enterprise. That is 1.63 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Dale Enterprise, 5 miles from the middle of Rockingham County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 319 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Rockingham County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
