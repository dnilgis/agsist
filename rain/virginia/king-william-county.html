How much rain has King William County, Virginia, had in 2026?

33.63 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over King William County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 81% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

West Point station: 41.66 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 2 miles northwest of West Point. That is 1.32 inches less than a normal year to date (97% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of West Point, 19 miles from the middle of King William County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
