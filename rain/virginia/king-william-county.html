How much rain has King William County, Virginia, had in 2026?

34.84 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over King William County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 82% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 3.02 in, 7 days 3.38 in. NOAA radar county average to 2 am Eastern, Nov 27.

One weather station’s gauge, the West Point station (NOAA daily record): 41.79 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station 2 miles northwest of West Point. That is 1.97 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of West Point, 19 miles from the middle of King William County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
