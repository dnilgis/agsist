How much rain has King William County, Virginia, had in 2026?

38.64 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 2 miles northwest of West Point, King William County, through Oct 18. That is 1.03 inches less than a usual year to date (97% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 2 miles northwest of West Point had its biggest single day on May 25 with 3.41 inches. Then it went 13 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from May 28 to Jun 9. July was the wettest month at 7.56 inches. The year so far sits 11.16 inches less than last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of West Point (station ID 449025), 19 miles from the middle of King William County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside King William County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
