The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile west-northwest of Emporia, 1 mile from the middle of Emporia City. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
