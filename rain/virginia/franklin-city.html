How much rain has Franklin City, Virginia, had in 2026?

38.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Franklin City, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 84% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles northwest of Franklin, 1 mile from the middle of Franklin City. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
