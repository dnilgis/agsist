The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Staunton Water Treatment, 2 miles from the middle of Staunton City. It reported 320 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
