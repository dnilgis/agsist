How much rain has Latah County, Idaho, had in 2026?

21.68 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Latah County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 81% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.60 in, 7 days 0.70 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 am Mountain, Nov 27.

One weather station’s gauge, the Moscow U of I station (NOAA daily record): 21.75 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Moscow U of I. That is 0.98 inches less than a normal year to date (96% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 15 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Moscow U of I, 13 miles from the middle of Latah County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
