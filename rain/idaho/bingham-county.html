The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Aberdeen, 28 miles from the middle of Bingham County. It reported 322 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
