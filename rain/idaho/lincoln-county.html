  • Wettest month: October, 1.32 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 0.73 inches on Feb 24.
  • Longest dry stretch: 35 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Aug 14 to Sep 17.
  • 2025 by the same date: 5.73 inches at the same station. 2026 had about the same as 2025.
  • On record: 82nd wettest by Nov 9 of 101 years on record here (1909 on); the last wetter year was 2024, the last drier one 2022. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 5.45 inches, 67% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Lincoln County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile west-northwest of Shoshone, 15 miles from the middle of Lincoln County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Lincoln County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
