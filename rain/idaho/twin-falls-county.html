How much rain has Twin Falls County, Idaho, had in 2026?

5.91 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Twin Falls County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 55% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Twin Falls Sun Valley Regional Airport station: 6.16 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station at Twin Falls Sun Valley Regional Airport. That is 2.51 inches less than a normal year to date (71% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Twin Falls Sun Valley Regional Airport, 13 miles from the middle of Twin Falls County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
