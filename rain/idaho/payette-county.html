The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Payette, 10 miles from the middle of Payette County. It reported 311 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Payette County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
