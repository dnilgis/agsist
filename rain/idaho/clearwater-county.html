The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles northeast of Orofino, 27 miles from the middle of Clearwater County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Clearwater County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?
