The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Salmon-Ksra, 16 miles from the middle of Lemhi County. It reported 302 of the 309 days from Jan 1 to Nov 5; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Lemhi County, Idaho, had in 2026?

Is Lemhi County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Salmon-Ksra had 8.57 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 5, 107% of the normal 8.03 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Lemhi County in 2026?

June, with 2.08 inches at the weather station in Salmon-Ksra, counting Jan 1 through Nov 5.

What was the biggest rain in Lemhi County in 2026?

1.00 inch on Sep 2, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 5. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lemhi County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 5, the weather station in Salmon-Ksra had 7.62 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 8.57 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 7 days). On the 301 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.03 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Lemhi County had in 2026?

2,592 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 5 at the weather station in Salmon-Ksra, 112% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Lemhi County in 2026?

Sep 15 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Salmon-Ksra. Typical: Sep 25, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 302 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Lemhi County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
