The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Salmon-Ksra, 16 miles from the middle of Lemhi County. It reported 313 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
