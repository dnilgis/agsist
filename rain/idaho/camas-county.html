How much rain has Camas County, Idaho, had in 2026?

13.32 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Camas County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 78% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Fairfield Ranger Station: 6.05 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the Fairfield Ranger Station weather station in Fairfield. That is 5.44 inches less than a normal year to date (53% of normal).

This station is 25 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fairfield, 8 miles from the middle of Camas County. It reported 311 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
