How much rain has Camas County, Idaho, had in 2026?

13.86 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Camas County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 78% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.11 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 am Mountain, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Fairfield Ranger Station (NOAA daily record): 6.77 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23, at the Fairfield Ranger Station weather station in Fairfield. That is 4.92 inches less than a normal year to date (58% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 20 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fairfield, 8 miles from the middle of Camas County. It reported 315 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
