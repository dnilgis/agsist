How much rain has Washington County, Idaho, had in 2026?

13.87 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Washington County, Jan 1 to Nov 16, an estimate from a weather grid (84% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. All 2 stations in Washington County that reported nearly every day are high mountain sites; the one used is the Van Wyck SNOTEL site, 22 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 15.80 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the figures below are that site’s. That is 4.75 inches less than a normal year to date (77% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Van Wyck, 22 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported every one of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
