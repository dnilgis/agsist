How much rain has Washington County, Idaho, had in 2026?

14.20 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Van Wyck, Washington County, through Oct 15. That is 4.29 inches less than a usual year to date (77% of usual).

Through Oct 10, the weather station in Van Wyck had 3.93 inches less than a usual year. April was the wettest month at 3.90 inches, and the biggest single day came Apr 22 with 0.90 inches. Then the gauge went dry. It caught nothing, not even a trace, for 26 days in a row from Apr 23 to May 18.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Van Wyck (station ID USS0016E14S), 22 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported every one of the 288 days from Jan 1 to Oct 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Washington County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
