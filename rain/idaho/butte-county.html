2026 so far in Butte County

  • Wettest month: September, 1.11 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 0.90 inches on Oct 11.
  • Longest dry stretch: 44 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jun 30 to Aug 12.
  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • On record: 63rd wettest by Nov 10 of 74 years on record here (1933 on); the last wetter year was 2024, the last drier one 2020. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 8.56 inches, 80% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Butte County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Howe, 9 miles from the middle of Butte County. It reported 313 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Butte County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
