How much rain has Butte County, Idaho, had in 2026?

9.45 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Butte County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 85% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Idaho Falls station: 8.12 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23, at the weather station 46 miles west of Idaho Falls. That is 0.77 inches more than a normal year to date (110% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 46 miles west of Idaho Falls, 17 miles from the middle of Butte County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Butte County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
