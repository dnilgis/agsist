The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Grangeville, 32 miles from the middle of Idaho County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Idaho County, Idaho, had in 2026?

Is Idaho County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Grangeville had 18.80 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 93% of the normal 20.32 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Idaho County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Grangeville, and 1.97 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Idaho County in 2026?

March, with 3.68 inches at the weather station in Grangeville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Idaho County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Idaho County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Grangeville had 17.69 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 11 days) and 18.80 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 301 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.97 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Idaho County had in 2026?

2,562 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Grangeville, 128% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (20 years).

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Idaho County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
