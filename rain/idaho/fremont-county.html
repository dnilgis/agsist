How much rain has Fremont County, Idaho, had in 2026?

20.18 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Fremont County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, an estimate from a weather grid (105% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. All 2 stations in Fremont County that reported nearly every day are high mountain sites; the one used is the Island Park SNOTEL site, 14 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 21.00 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the figures below are that site’s. That is 2.35 inches less than a normal year to date (90% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Island Park, 14 miles from the middle of Fremont County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
