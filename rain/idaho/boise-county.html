How much rain has Boise County, Idaho, had in 2026?

11.77 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Centerville Cooper Dr, Boise County, through Oct 5. That is 6.80 inches less than a usual year to date (63% of usual).

Through Oct 5, the weather station in Centerville Cooper Dr had its wettest month in April at 3.85 inches. The biggest single day was Feb 24 with 1.24 inches. Then it went 64 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Jul 1 to Sep 2. On the days both years reported, that is 8.70 inches less than 2025 had.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Centerville Cooper Dr (station ID 101636), 11 miles from the middle of Boise County. It reported every one of the 278 days from Jan 1 to Oct 5. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 278 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Boise County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
