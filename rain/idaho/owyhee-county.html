How much rain has Owyhee County, Idaho, had in 2026?

5.52 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 10 miles west of Murphy, Owyhee County, through Oct 10. That is 2.55 inches less than a usual year to date (68% of usual).

April was the wettest month at 1.82 inches, and the biggest single day was 0.66 inches on Feb 24. The gauge went 42 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Jul 23 to Sep 2. On the days both years reported, that is 2.73 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 10 miles west of Murphy (station ID 04127), 52 miles from the middle of Owyhee County. It reported every one of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Owyhee County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
