The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.4 miles north-northeast of Driggs, 6 miles from the middle of Teton County. It reported 307 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Teton County?
