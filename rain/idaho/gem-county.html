How much rain has Gem County, Idaho, had in 2026?

15.63 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Gem County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 90% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.69 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 am Mountain, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Emmett station (NOAA daily record): 13.46 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23, at the weather station 2 miles east of Emmett. That is 2.66 inches more than a normal year to date (125% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles east of Emmett, 14 miles from the middle of Gem County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Gem County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
