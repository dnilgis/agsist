How much rain has Oneida County, Idaho, had in 2026?

6.59 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles north-northeast of Holbrook, Oneida County, through Oct 17. That is 5.56 inches less than a usual year to date (54% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge near Holbrook got its wettest month in October, with 1.71 inches. Most of that came on Oct 12, the biggest rain of the year at 1.26 inches. From Mar 7 to Mar 31 the gauge went 25 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year is 2.72 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles north-northeast of Holbrook (gauge ID US1IDON0001), 5 miles from the middle of Oneida County. It reported 286 of the 290 days from Jan 1 to Oct 17; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 286 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Oneida County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Oneida County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles north-northeast of Holbrook had 6.59 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 17, 54% of the usual 12.15 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Oneida County in 2026?

October, with 1.83 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles north-northeast of Holbrook, counting Jan 1 through Oct 17.

What was the biggest rain in Oneida County in 2026?

1.26 inches on Oct 12, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Oneida County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 17, the volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles north-northeast of Holbrook had 9.00 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 22 days) and 6.59 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 264 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.05 inches less than 2025.
