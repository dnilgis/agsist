2026 so far in Oneida County

  • Wettest month: October, 3.55 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 1.26 inches on Oct 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 25 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Mar 7 to Mar 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 9.44 inches at the same gauge. 2026 had 0.13 inches less than 2025.
  • On 1 report the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 13.75 inches, 88% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 10, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Oneida County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 18 points above this gauge: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.0 miles north-northeast of Holbrook, 5 miles from the middle of Oneida County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 312 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Oneida County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
