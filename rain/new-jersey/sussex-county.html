The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.6 miles south-southwest of Sparta Twp, 9 miles from the middle of Sussex County. It reported 307 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
