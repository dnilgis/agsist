The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Mount Airy, 6 miles from the middle of Surry County. It reported 323 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
