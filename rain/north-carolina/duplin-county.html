How much rain has Duplin County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

32.62 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Duplin County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 67% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Mount Olive gauge: 39.54 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles southwest of Mount Olive. That is 6.55 inches less than a normal year to date (86% of normal).

This gauge is 19 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles southwest of Mount Olive, 19 miles from the middle of Duplin County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 320 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 5 stations inside Duplin County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
