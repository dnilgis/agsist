The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lenoir, 2 miles from the middle of Caldwell County. It reported 323 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Caldwell County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Caldwell County in 2026?

October, with 7.71 inches at the weather station in Lenoir, counting Jan 1 through Nov 24.

What was the biggest rain in Caldwell County in 2026?

6.75 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 24. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Caldwell County?

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 323 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
