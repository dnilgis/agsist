The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lenoir, 2 miles from the middle of Caldwell County. It reported 322 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
