The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lenoir, 2 miles from the middle of Caldwell County. It reported 308 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Caldwell County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

40.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lenoir in Caldwell County, through Nov 12. That is 2.17 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

Is Caldwell County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Lenoir had 40.28 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 95% of the normal 42.45 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Caldwell County in 2026?

October, with 7.71 inches at the weather station in Lenoir, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Caldwell County in 2026?

6.75 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Caldwell County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Lenoir had 41.75 inches in 2025 and 40.28 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 8 days). On the 308 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.47 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Caldwell County had in 2026?

4,054 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Lenoir, 107% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 308 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Caldwell County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
