How much rain has Burke County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

46.78 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Burke County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 102% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Morganton, 2 miles from the middle of Burke County. It reported 321 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Burke County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

46.78 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Burke County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 102% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. One weather station’s gauge, the Morganton station (NOAA daily record), had 48.52 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 19 (108% of normal). That is not a county-wide figure.

Is Burke County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. Averaged over Burke County, about 46.78 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 102% of the normal 45.90 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Burke County in 2026?

May, with 8.62 inches at the weather station in Morganton, counting Jan 1 through Nov 19.

What was the biggest rain in Burke County in 2026?

6.85 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 19. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Burke County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 19, the weather station in Morganton had 43.86 inches in 2025 and 48.52 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 2 days). On the 321 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.66 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Burke County had in 2026?

4,008 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 19 at the weather station in Morganton, 106% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (25 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Burke County in 2026?

Nov 18 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Morganton. Typical: Oct 27, the middle of 25 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Burke County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
