How much rain has Burke County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

33.24 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Hickory Faa Airport, Burke County, through Oct 13. That is 4.52 inches less than a usual year to date (88% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Hickory Faa Airport, 18 miles from the middle of the county, had its biggest single day on Oct 10 with 2.64 inches. July was the wettest month at 6.28 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 13 days from Apr 6 to Apr 18 with nothing in the gauge. This year sits just 0.05 inches more than last year at this point, and 4.52 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Hickory Faa Airport (station ID 03810), 18 miles from the middle of Burke County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Burke County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
