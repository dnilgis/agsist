How much rain has Caswell County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

26.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Caswell County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 64% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Yanceyville station (NOAA daily record): 26.69 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station 4 miles southeast of Yanceyville. That is 16.22 inches less than a normal year to date (62% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles southeast of Yanceyville, 4 miles from the middle of Caswell County. It reported 323 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
