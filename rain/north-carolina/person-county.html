The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 7 miles east-southeast of Roxboro, 6 miles from the middle of Person County. It reported 310 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 310 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
