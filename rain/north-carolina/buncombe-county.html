The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles north-northwest of Asheville, 2 miles from the middle of Buncombe County. It reported 308 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Buncombe County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles north-northwest of Asheville had 31.60 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 82% of the usual 38.32 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Buncombe County in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Buncombe County in 2026?

July, with 7.38 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles north-northwest of Asheville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Buncombe County in 2026?

2.85 inches on Oct 12, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 8. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Buncombe County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles north-northwest of Asheville had 35.44 inches in 2025 and 31.60 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 308 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.84 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Buncombe County had in 2026?

3,787 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at a weather station 11 miles from the rain gauge, 110% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has Buncombe County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 11 miles from the rain gauge. Usual: Oct 22, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 308 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 23 stations inside Buncombe County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
