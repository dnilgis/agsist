The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.6 miles west-northwest of Durham, 7 miles from the middle of Durham County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
