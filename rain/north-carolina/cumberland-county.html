The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Fayetteville Regional Airport Grannis Field, 5 miles from the middle of Cumberland County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Cumberland County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

0.32 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Fayetteville Regional Airport Grannis Field, and 0.92 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Cumberland County in 2026?

July, with 5.35 inches at the weather station at Fayetteville Regional Airport Grannis Field, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Cumberland County in 2026?

1.98 inches on Aug 27, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Cumberland County?

How many growing degree days has Cumberland County had in 2026?

4,544 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Fayetteville Regional Airport Grannis Field, 102% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (21 years).

Has Cumberland County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Fayetteville Regional Airport Grannis Field. Usual: Nov 10, the middle of 23 years, 1991-2020.
