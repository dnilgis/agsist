The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Stecoah, 8 miles from the middle of Graham County. It reported 317 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Graham County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

50.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Graham County, Jan 1 to Nov 23, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Stecoah station had 46.14 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23.

Is Graham County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Graham County, about 50.44 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 23, 91% of the normal 55.67 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Graham County in 2026?

July, with 9.59 inches at the weather station in Stecoah, counting Jan 1 through Nov 23.

What was the biggest rain in Graham County in 2026?

2.27 inches on Aug 7, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 23. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Graham County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 23, the weather station in Stecoah had 57.72 inches in 2025 and 46.14 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 10 days). On the 317 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.42 inches less than 2025.
