The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.5 miles south of Rutherfordton, 11 miles from the middle of Polk County. It reported 319 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Polk County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

47.50 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Polk County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 96% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Rutherfordton gauge had 47.68 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18 (102% of normal).

Is Polk County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Polk County, about 47.50 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 96% of the normal 49.47 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Polk County in 2026?

July, with 7.82 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 9.5 miles south of Rutherfordton, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Polk County in 2026?

5.75 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Polk County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the volunteer rain gauge 9.5 miles south of Rutherfordton had 51.53 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 47.68 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 316 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.78 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Polk County had in 2026?

4,094 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 18 at a weather station 16 miles from the rain gauge, 103% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

Has Polk County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 18: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 16 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Nov 12, the middle of 29 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 319 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Polk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
