The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.7 miles east-northeast of Gatesville, 1 mile from the middle of Gates County. It reported 286 of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
