How much rain has Hoke County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

34.85 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 3.8 miles northwest of Red Springs in Hoke County, through Nov 11. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with. Averaged over the whole county, about 33.56 inches, 81% of usual.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.8 miles northwest of Red Springs, 12 miles from the middle of Hoke County. It reported 314 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
