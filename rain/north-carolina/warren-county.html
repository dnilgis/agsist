How much rain has Warren County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

18.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 5.0 miles north of Norlina, Warren County, through Oct 18. That is 19.82 inches less than a usual year to date (48% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge 5.0 miles north of Norlina had 17.91 inches, 19.75 inches less than a usual year. July was the wettest month at 2.95 inches, and the biggest single day was 1.33 inches on Aug 22. The gauge went 13 days in a row with nothing in it, May 29 to Jun 10. On the days both years reported, this year is 19.12 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.0 miles north of Norlina (gauge ID US1NCWR0007), 9 miles from the middle of Warren County. It reported 288 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 288 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Warren County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
