2026 so far in Warren County

  • Wettest month: July, 2.95 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.33 inches on Aug 22.
  • Longest dry stretch: 18 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 19 to Nov 5.
  • 2025 by the same date: 39.26 inches at the same gauge. On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 20.46 inches less than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 24.90 inches, 61% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Warren County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 15 points above this gauge: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.0 miles north of Norlina, 9 miles from the middle of Warren County. It reported 310 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 310 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Warren County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
