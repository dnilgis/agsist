How much rain has New Hanover County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

42.74 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Wilmington Int'l Airport, New Hanover County, through Oct 18. That is 8.72 inches less than a usual year to date (83% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Wilmington Int'l Airport has had a lot of its year land in August, with 15.01 inches that month. The biggest single day was Aug 21 at 5.31 inches. Before that, the gauge went 18 days in a row with nothing in it, from Apr 7 to Apr 24. It still sits 8.23 inches less than a usual year, but 2.15 inches more than last year by this date.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wilmington Int'l Airport (station ID 13748), 3 miles from the middle of New Hanover County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside New Hanover County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
