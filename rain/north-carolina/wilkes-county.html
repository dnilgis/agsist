The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in North Wilkesboro, 3 miles from the middle of Wilkes County. It reported 326 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
