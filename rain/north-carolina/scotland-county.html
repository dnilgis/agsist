The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Laurinburg, 6 miles from the middle of Scotland County. It reported 311 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Scotland County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

29.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Laurinburg in Scotland County, through Nov 10. That is 11.29 inches less than a normal year to date (72% of normal).

Is Scotland County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Laurinburg had 29.11 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 10, 72% of the normal 40.40 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Scotland County in 2026?

July, with 7.04 inches at the weather station in Laurinburg, counting Jan 1 through Nov 10.

What was the biggest rain in Scotland County in 2026?

2.31 inches on Jul 24, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 10.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Scotland County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 10, the weather station in Laurinburg had 34.74 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 29.11 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.63 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Scotland County had in 2026?

4,450 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 10 at the weather station in Laurinburg, 102% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Has Scotland County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 10: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Laurinburg. Typical: Nov 6, the middle of 22 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Scotland County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
