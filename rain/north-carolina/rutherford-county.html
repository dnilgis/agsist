The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 8 miles west of Forest City, 7 miles from the middle of Rutherford County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Rutherford County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

45.79 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 8 miles west of Forest City in Rutherford County, through Nov 12. That is 0.96 inches more than a normal year to date (102% of normal).

Is Rutherford County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. The weather station 8 miles west of Forest City had 45.79 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 102% of the normal 44.83 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Rutherford County in the last week?

0.49 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station 8 miles west of Forest City, and 1.90 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Rutherford County in 2026?

August, with 10.77 inches at the weather station 8 miles west of Forest City, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Rutherford County in 2026?

5.10 inches on Oct 11, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Rutherford County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station 8 miles west of Forest City had 53.17 inches in 2025 and 45.79 inches in 2026. 2026 had 7.38 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Rutherford County had in 2026?

4,092 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station 8 miles west of Forest City.

Has Rutherford County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station 8 miles west of Forest City.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Rutherford County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
