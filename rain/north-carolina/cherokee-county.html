How much rain has Cherokee County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

42.23 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 4 miles east-southeast of Murphy, Cherokee County, through Oct 12. That is 4.28 inches less than a usual year to date (91% of usual).

Through Oct 12, the weather station 4 miles east-southeast of Murphy had its wettest month in May at 7.49 inches, and May 7 alone brought 3.37 inches. Before that, the gauge went 18 days in a row with nothing in it, from Mar 18 to Apr 4. On the days both years reported, this year sits 5.64 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles east-southeast of Murphy (station ID 316001), 7 miles from the middle of Cherokee County. It reported 284 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Cherokee County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
