The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.4 miles north-northwest of Pittsboro, 6 miles from the middle of Chatham County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Chatham County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?
