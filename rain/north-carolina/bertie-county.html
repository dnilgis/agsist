How much rain has Bertie County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

31.39 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lewiston, Bertie County, through Oct 15. That is 9.63 inches less than a usual year to date (77% of usual).

Through Oct 11, the weather station in Lewiston had its wettest month in July with 7.67 inches, and its biggest one-day reading came Jul 28 at 1.92 inches. From May 28 to Jun 11 it went 15 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year sits 6.29 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lewiston (station ID 314962), 12 miles from the middle of Bertie County. It reported 286 of the 288 days from Jan 1 to Oct 15; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Bertie County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
