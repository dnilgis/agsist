How much rain has Bertie County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

33.86 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Bertie County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 74% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Lewiston station: 33.55 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 11, at the weather station in Lewiston. That is 10.65 inches less than a normal year to date (76% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lewiston, 12 miles from the middle of Bertie County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Bertie County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

Is Bertie County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Bertie County, about 33.86 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 74% of the normal 45.87 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Bertie County in the last week?

1.50 inches from Nov 5 to Nov 11 at the weather station in Lewiston, and 2.16 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 13 to Nov 11. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Bertie County in 2026?

July, with 7.67 inches at the weather station in Lewiston, counting Jan 1 through Nov 11.

What was the biggest rain in Bertie County in 2026?

1.92 inches on Jul 28, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 11. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bertie County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 11, the weather station in Lewiston had 43.55 inches in 2025 and 33.55 inches in 2026. 2026 had 10.00 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Bertie County had in 2026?

4,498 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at the weather station in Lewiston, 108% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Has Bertie County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 11: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Lewiston. Typical: Nov 4, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
