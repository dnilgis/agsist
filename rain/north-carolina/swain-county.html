The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Oconaluftee, 11 miles from the middle of Swain County. It reported 311 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Swain County, North Carolina, had in 2026?

44.36 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Swain County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Oconaluftee station had 42.11 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 19 (88% of normal).

Is Swain County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Swain County, about 44.36 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 80% of the normal 55.42 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Swain County in 2026?

July, with 8.90 inches at the weather station in Oconaluftee, counting Jan 1 through Nov 19.

What was the biggest rain in Swain County in 2026?

1.46 inches on Jul 23, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 19. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Swain County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 19, the weather station in Oconaluftee had 55.56 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 42.11 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 12 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 13.94 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Swain County had in 2026?

3,604 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 19 at the weather station in Oconaluftee, 107% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (24 years).
