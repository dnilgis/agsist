How much rain has Maine had in 2026? County by county

16 Maine counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 19 (3 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 14 counties with a NOAA normal are above 77% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Androscoggin County: the driest by Nov 19 of 33 years on record (1994 on), 71% of normal.
  • York County: the driest by Nov 19 of 32 years on record (1995 on), 73% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
