How much rain has Colorado had in 2026? County by county

64 Colorado counties have a weather station inside the county that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Nov 3 (11 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 55 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 87% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Grand County: the driest by Nov 3 of 74 years on record (1945 on), 60% of usual.
  • San Juan County: the wettest by Oct 31 of 40 years on record (1987 on), 129% of usual.
  • Bent County: 3rd wettest by Nov 3 of 107 years on record (1894 on), 155% of usual.
