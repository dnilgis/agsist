Questions and answers

How much rain has Colorado had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 64 Colorado counties has a total in the table above. The most is 27.18 inches at Bayfield, La Plata County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Colorado wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 56 counties with a NOAA normal, 21 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 34 below and 1 at it. Half are above 88.5% and half below. 8 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Colorado rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
