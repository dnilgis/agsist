The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles southeast of Decatur, 10 miles from the middle of Morgan County. It reported 313 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
