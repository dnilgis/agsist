How much rain has Conecuh County, Alabama, had in 2026?

41.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Evergreen Middleton Field, Conecuh County, through Oct 18. That is 2.92 inches less than a usual year to date (93% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Evergreen Middleton Field had June as its wettest month at 10.18 inches, and its biggest single day came on Jun 18 with 3.29 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 13 days, from Apr 6 to Apr 18, with not even a trace in the gauge. On the days both years reported, this year has 9.15 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Evergreen Middleton Field (station ID 53820), 4 miles from the middle of Conecuh County. It reported 289 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 289 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Conecuh County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
