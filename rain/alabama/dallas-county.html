The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Marion Junction, 12 miles from the middle of Dallas County. It reported 316 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
