The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Alabaster Shelby County Airport, 9 miles from the middle of Shelby County. It reported 316 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
