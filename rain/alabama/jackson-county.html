How much rain has Jackson County, Alabama, had in 2026?

46.99 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Jackson County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 93% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Scottsboro station: 53.15 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Scottsboro. That is 2.35 inches more than a normal year to date (105% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Scottsboro, 8 miles from the middle of Jackson County. It reported 317 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
