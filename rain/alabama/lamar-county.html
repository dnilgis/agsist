The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles north-northeast of Vernon (gauge ID US1ALLM0004), less than a mile from the middle of Lamar County. It reported 279 of the 295 days from Jan 1 to Oct 22; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Lamar County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
