The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles north-northeast of Vernon, less than a mile from the middle of Lamar County. It reported 305 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Lamar County, Alabama, had in 2026?

42.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lamar County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 84% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Vernon gauge had 40.06 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18.

Is Lamar County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Lamar County, about 42.73 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 84% of the normal 50.57 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Lamar County in 2026?

April, with 6.73 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles north-northeast of Vernon, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Lamar County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lamar County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles north-northeast of Vernon had 53.64 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 30 days) and 40.06 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 17 days). On the 279 days both years reported, 2026 had 12.90 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Lamar County had in 2026?
