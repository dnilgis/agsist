The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile southwest of Red Level, 14 miles from the middle of Covington County. It reported 315 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
