How much rain has Autauga County, Alabama, had in 2026?

44.72 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Autauga County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 92% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 1.04 in, 7 days 1.11 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 am Central, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Billingsley station (NOAA daily record): 39.40 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station in Billingsley. That is 11.29 inches less than a normal year to date (78% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Billingsley, 10 miles from the middle of Autauga County. It reported 326 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Autauga County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
