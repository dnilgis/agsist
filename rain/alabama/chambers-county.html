The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Lafayette, 3 miles from the middle of Chambers County. It reported 315 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
