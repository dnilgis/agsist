How much rain has Walker County, Alabama, had in 2026?

50.64 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Walker County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 99% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.11 in, 7 days 1.76 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 am Central, Nov 27.

One weather station’s gauge, the Oakman station (NOAA daily record): 42.58 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station in Oakman. That is 8.69 inches less than a normal year to date (83% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Oakman, 7 miles from the middle of Walker County. It reported 299 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 30 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
