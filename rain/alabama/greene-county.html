The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Gainesville Lock, 11 miles from the middle of Greene County. It reported 325 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
