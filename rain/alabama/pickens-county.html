The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bevill, 12 miles from the middle of Pickens County. It reported 310 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
