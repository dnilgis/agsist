The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Demopolis, 20 miles from the middle of Marengo County. It reported 292 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 30 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
