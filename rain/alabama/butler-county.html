The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles west-northwest of Greenville, 11 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 312 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
