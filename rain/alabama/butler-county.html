The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles west-northwest of Greenville, 11 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 301 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain fell in Butler County in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Butler County in 2026?

June, with 7.65 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles west-northwest of Greenville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Butler County in 2026?

3.14 inches on Oct 10, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Butler County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the volunteer rain gauge 10.2 miles west-northwest of Greenville had 40.49 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 18 days) and 39.72 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 15 days). On the 284 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.95 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Butler County had in 2026?

4,778 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 6 at a weather station 11 miles from the rain gauge.

Has Butler County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 6: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 11 miles from the rain gauge.
