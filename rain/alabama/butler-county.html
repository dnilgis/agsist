The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles northeast of Greenville, 11 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 309 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Butler County, Alabama, had in 2026?

45.87 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Butler County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Greenville gauge had 36.50 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 12.

Is Butler County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Butler County, about 45.87 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 91% of the normal 50.14 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Butler County in 2026?

June, with 9.36 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles northeast of Greenville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Butler County in 2026?

2.38 inches on Jun 19, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Butler County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles northeast of Greenville had 36.70 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 36.50 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 7 days). On the 305 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.28 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Butler County had in 2026?

4,808 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 9 at a weather station 3 miles from the rain gauge.

Has Butler County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 9: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 3 miles from the rain gauge.
