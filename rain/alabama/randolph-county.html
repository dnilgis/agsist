How much rain has Randolph County, Alabama, had in 2026?

44.32 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Randolph County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 92% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Roanoke gauge: 39.52 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.2 miles north-northeast of Roanoke. That is 9.22 inches less than a normal year to date (81% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.2 miles north-northeast of Roanoke, 9 miles from the middle of Randolph County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 319 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Randolph County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
