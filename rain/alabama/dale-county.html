The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Dothan Regional Airport, 13 miles from the middle of Dale County. It reported 318 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
