The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Troy, 4 miles from the middle of Pike County. It reported 315 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Pike County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
