How much rain has Escambia County, Alabama, had in 2026?

53.63 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Escambia County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 97% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Brewton station: 48.10 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 14, at the weather station 3 miles north-northeast of Brewton. That is 4.24 inches less than a normal year to date (92% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north-northeast of Brewton, 7 miles from the middle of Escambia County. It reported 315 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
