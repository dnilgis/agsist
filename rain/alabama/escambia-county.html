How much rain has Escambia County, Alabama, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Atmore, 17 miles from the middle of Escambia County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the wettest month in Escambia County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Escambia County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Escambia County?

How many growing degree days has Escambia County had in 2026?

Has Escambia County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 24: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Atmore. Typical: Nov 20, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Escambia County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
