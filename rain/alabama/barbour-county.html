How much rain has Barbour County, Alabama, had in 2026?

45.37 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Barbour County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 98% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Clayton station: 50.80 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Clayton. That is 5.95 inches more than a normal year to date (113% of normal).

This station is 15 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Clayton, 4 miles from the middle of Barbour County. It reported 293 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 28 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
