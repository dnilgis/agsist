The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Muscle Shoals Airport, 12 miles from the middle of Colbert County. It reported 320 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
