The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.2 miles east-southeast of Collinsville, 12 miles from the middle of Cherokee County. It reported 319 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
