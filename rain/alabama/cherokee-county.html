Collinsville gauge: 41.85 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.2 miles east-southeast of Collinsville. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.2 miles east-southeast of Collinsville, 12 miles from the middle of Cherokee County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
