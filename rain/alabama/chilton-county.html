The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Clanton, 6 miles from the middle of Chilton County. It reported 313 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
