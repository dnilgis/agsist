How much rain has North Carolina had in 2026? County by county

94 North Carolina counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (26 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 83 counties with a NOAA normal are above 77% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Carteret County: the driest by Nov 12 of 31 years on record (1996 on), 59% of normal.
  • Lenoir County: the driest by Nov 12 of 54 years on record (1967 on), 61% of normal.
  • Rockingham County: the driest by Nov 12 of 62 years on record (1963 on), 52% of normal.
  • Pamlico County: 3rd driest by Nov 11 of 47 years on record (1969 on), 66% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
