The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Waterville Treatment Plt, 10 miles from the middle of Kennebec County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Kennebec County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
