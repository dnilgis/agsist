The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Waterville Treatment Plt, 10 miles from the middle of Kennebec County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Kennebec County, Maine, had in 2026?

32.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Waterville Treatment Plt in Kennebec County, through Nov 12. That is 3.93 inches less than a normal year to date (89% of normal).

Is Kennebec County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Waterville Treatment Plt had 32.75 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 89% of the normal 36.68 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Kennebec County in 2026?

June, with 6.47 inches at the weather station in Waterville Treatment Plt, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Kennebec County in 2026?

1.91 inches on Jun 22, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Kennebec County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Waterville Treatment Plt had 34.17 inches in 2025 and 32.75 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 312 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.57 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Kennebec County had in 2026?

2,668 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Waterville Treatment Plt, 111% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Kennebec County in 2026?

Oct 18 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Waterville Treatment Plt. Typical: Oct 7, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Kennebec County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
