The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hollis, 12 miles from the middle of York County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is York County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in York County in the last week?

What was the biggest rain in York County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in York County?

How many growing degree days has York County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside York County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
