The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Stuttgart Municipal Airport, 16 miles from the middle of Prairie County. It reported 309 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
