The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-southeast of Arkadelphia, 14 miles from the middle of Clark County. It reported 315 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
