2026 so far in Arkansas County

  • Wettest month: March, 5.40 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 1.97 inches on May 22.
  • Longest dry stretch: 27 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jul 31 to Aug 26.
  • 2025 by the same date: 44.76 inches at the same station (it missed 17 days). On the 277 days both years reported, 2026 had 19.28 inches less than 2025.
  • On 2 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 29.85 inches, 70% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Arkansas County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Saint Charles, 15 miles from the middle of Arkansas County. It reported 294 of the 307 days from Jan 1 to Nov 3; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 294 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Arkansas County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
