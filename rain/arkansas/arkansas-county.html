The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Saint Charles (station SAINT CHARLES), 15 miles from the middle of Arkansas County. It reported 263 of the 275 days from Jan 1 to Oct 2; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 263 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Arkansas County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
