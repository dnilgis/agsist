The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles south-southeast of Ashdown, 9 miles from the middle of Little River County. It reported 312 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Little River County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
