The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles south-southeast of Ashdown, 9 miles from the middle of Little River County. It reported 314 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Little River County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Little River County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Little River County?

How many growing degree days has Little River County had in 2026?

Has Little River County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Little River County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
