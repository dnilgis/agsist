How much rain has Crawford County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

33.34 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Crawford County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 71% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Uniontown gauge: 32.05 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 7, at the volunteer rain gauge 2.1 miles east-southeast of Uniontown. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.1 miles east-southeast of Uniontown, 10 miles from the middle of Crawford County. It reported 310 of the 311 days from Jan 1 to Nov 7; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
