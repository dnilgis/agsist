The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 2.1 miles east-southeast of Uniontown (station UNIONTOWN 2.1 ESE), 10 miles from the middle of Crawford County. It reported 259 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Crawford County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
