The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.3 miles north of Berryville, 6 miles from the middle of Carroll County. It reported 317 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
