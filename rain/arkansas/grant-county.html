The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sheridan, 2 miles from the middle of Grant County. It reported 286 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 28 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Grant County in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Grant County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
