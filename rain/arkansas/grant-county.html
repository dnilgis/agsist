How much rain has Grant County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sheridan, 2 miles from the middle of Grant County. It reported 299 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 28 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
