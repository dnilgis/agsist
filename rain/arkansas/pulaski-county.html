The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Little Rock Airport Adams Field, 6 miles from the middle of Pulaski County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
