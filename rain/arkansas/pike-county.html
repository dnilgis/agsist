2026 so far in Pike County

  • Wettest month: April, 7.27 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.61 inches on Jul 16.
  • Longest dry stretch: 27 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 4 to Oct 30.
  • 2025 by the same date: 51.07 inches at the same station (it missed 1 day). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 14.86 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 57th wettest by Nov 8 of 71 years on record here (1944 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2011. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 39.17 inches, 81% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Pike County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile southwest of Antoine, 16 miles from the middle of Pike County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Pike County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
