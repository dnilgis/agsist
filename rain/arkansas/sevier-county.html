The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at De Queen Sevier County Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Sevier County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Sevier County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

27.89 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport, Sevier County, through Nov 8. That is 9.95 inches less than a usual year to date (74% of usual).

Is Sevier County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport had 27.89 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 74% of the usual 37.84 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Sevier County in the last week?

1.18 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport, and 1.54 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Sevier County in 2026?

June, with 7.05 inches at the weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Sevier County in 2026?

2.35 inches on Apr 4, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Sevier County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport had 44.74 inches in 2025 and 27.89 inches in 2026. 2026 had 16.85 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Sevier County had in 2026?

4,758 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport.

Has Sevier County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at De Queen Sevier County Airport.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Sevier County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
