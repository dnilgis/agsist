The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north-northeast of Damascus, 14 miles from the middle of Van Buren County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Van Buren County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

34.78 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 2 miles north-northeast of Damascus in Van Buren County, through Nov 10. That is 10.16 inches less than a normal year to date (77% of normal).

Is Van Buren County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station 2 miles north-northeast of Damascus had 34.78 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 10, 77% of the normal 44.94 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Van Buren County in the last week?

0.47 inches from Nov 4 to Nov 10 at the weather station 2 miles north-northeast of Damascus, and 1.55 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 12 to Nov 10. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Van Buren County in 2026?

May, with 7.17 inches at the weather station 2 miles north-northeast of Damascus, counting Jan 1 through Nov 10.

What was the biggest rain in Van Buren County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Van Buren County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Van Buren County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
