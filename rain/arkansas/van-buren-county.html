The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north-northeast of Damascus, 14 miles from the middle of Van Buren County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
