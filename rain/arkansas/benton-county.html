The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Fayetteville Springdale NW Ar Regl Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Benton County. It reported 311 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Benton County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

Is Benton County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Fayetteville Springdale NW Ar Regl Airport had 30.88 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 78% of the usual 39.78 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Benton County in the last week?

0.74 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Fayetteville Springdale NW Ar Regl Airport, and 2.64 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Benton County in 2026?

June, with 8.19 inches at the weather station at Fayetteville Springdale NW Ar Regl Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Benton County in 2026?

2.66 inches on Mar 4, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Benton County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station at Fayetteville Springdale NW Ar Regl Airport had 49.04 inches in 2025 and 30.88 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 16.59 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Benton County had in 2026?

4,532 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Fayetteville Springdale NW Ar Regl Airport, 118% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (20 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Benton County in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 311 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Benton County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
