The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Magnolia (station MAGNOLIA), 4 miles from the middle of Columbia County. It reported 265 of the 284 days from Jan 1 to Oct 11; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 265 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Columbia County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

Is Columbia County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The NOAA station in Magnolia had 32.20 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 11, 80% of the usual 40.33 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Columbia County in 2026?

June, with 11.44 inches at the NOAA station in Magnolia, counting the months through Oct 11.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Columbia County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 11, the NOAA station in Magnolia had 53.20 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 10 days) and 32.20 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 19 days). On the 256 days both years reported, 2026 had 18.3 inches less.
