The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south of Paragould, 7 miles from the middle of Greene County. It reported 306 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
