The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Fort Smith Regional Airport, 11 miles from the middle of Sebastian County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Sebastian County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
