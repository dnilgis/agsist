The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.2 miles south-southwest of Bigelow, 15 miles from the middle of Perry County. It reported 307 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
