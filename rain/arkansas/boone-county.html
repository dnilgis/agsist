How much rain has Boone County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

34.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Harrison Boone County Airport, Boone County, through Oct 13. That is 0.35 inches less than a usual year to date (99% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Harrison Boone County Airport sits about where a usual year would, 0.35 inches less. May was the wettest month at 8.05 inches, and the biggest single day came Jun 13 with 3.42 inches. The gauge went 12 days in a row with nothing from Aug 25 to Sep 5. Last year by this day it had 1.18 inches more.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Harrison Boone County Airport (station ID 13971), 5 miles from the middle of Boone County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Boone County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
