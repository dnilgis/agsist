2026 so far in Logan County

  • Wettest month: April, 6.43 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.27 inches on Oct 2.
  • Longest dry stretch: 16 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Aug 26 to Sep 10.
  • 2025 by the same date: 51.46 inches at the same station. On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 22.33 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 63rd wettest by Nov 8 of 71 years on record here (1948 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2003. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 30.08 inches, 69% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Logan County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).
Weather 11 miles from the station: 4,722 corn GDDs May 1 to Nov 4

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ratcliff, 11 miles from the middle of Logan County. It reported 309 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Logan County?

How many growing degree days has Logan County had in 2026?

4,722 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 4 at a weather station 11 miles from the rain station.

Has Logan County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 309 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Logan County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
