The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ratcliff, 11 miles from the middle of Logan County. It reported 316 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Logan County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
