How much rain has Mississippi County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

32.13 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Mississippi County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 71% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Blytheville gauge: 31.52 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge 6.1 miles east of Blytheville. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.1 miles east of Blytheville, 18 miles from the middle of Mississippi County. It reported 319 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
