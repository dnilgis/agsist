The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.1 miles east of Blytheville, 18 miles from the middle of Mississippi County. It reported 329 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
