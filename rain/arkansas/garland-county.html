How much rain has Garland County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

33.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Garland County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 68% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Blakely Mountain, 3 miles from the middle of Garland County. It reported 327 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
