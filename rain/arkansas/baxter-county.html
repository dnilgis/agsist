The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile north-northwest of Mountain Home, 5 miles from the middle of Baxter County. It reported 314 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Baxter County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

34.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Baxter County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 81% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Mountain Home station had 39.92 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18 (90% of normal).

Is Baxter County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Baxter County, about 34.16 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 81% of the normal 42.33 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Baxter County in 2026?

June, with 7.10 inches at the weather station 1 mile north-northwest of Mountain Home, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Baxter County in 2026?

3.23 inches on Aug 8, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Baxter County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station 1 mile north-northwest of Mountain Home had 56.40 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 7 days) and 39.92 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 8 days). On the 307 days both years reported, 2026 had 17.06 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Baxter County had in 2026?

4,374 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 18 at the weather station 1 mile north-northwest of Mountain Home, 111% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (23 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Baxter County in 2026?

Nov 13 (32°F or colder) at the weather station 1 mile north-northwest of Mountain Home.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Baxter County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
