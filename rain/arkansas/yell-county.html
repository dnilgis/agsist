30.13 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Yell County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 66% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Dardanelle station: 35.26 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Dardanelle. That is 7.28 inches less than a normal year to date (83% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Dardanelle, 21 miles from the middle of Yell County. It reported 318 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
