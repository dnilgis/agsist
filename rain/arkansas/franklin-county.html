The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 4.6 miles south of Ozark (station OZARK 4.6 S), 6 miles from the middle of Franklin County. It reported 282 of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Franklin County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

Is Franklin County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 4.6 miles south of Ozark had 20.93 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 10, 56% of the usual 37.06 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Franklin County in 2026?

June, with 4.04 inches at the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 4.6 miles south of Ozark, counting the months through Oct 10.

What was the biggest rain in Franklin County in 2026?

1.75 inches on Sep 10, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Oct 10.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Franklin County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 10, the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 4.6 miles south of Ozark had 47.63 inches in 2025 and 20.93 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 282 days both years reported, 2026 had 26.6 inches less.
