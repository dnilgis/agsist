How much rain has Polk County, Arkansas, had in 2026?

38.33 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Polk County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 74% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Vandervoort station (NOAA daily record): 37.17 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 22, at the weather station 1 mile east-northeast of Vandervoort. That is 17.18 inches less than a normal year to date (68% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile east-northeast of Vandervoort, 10 miles from the middle of Polk County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Polk County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Polk County, about 38.33 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 74% of the normal 51.96 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Polk County in the last week?

0.12 inches from Nov 16 to Nov 22 at the weather station 1 mile east-northeast of Vandervoort, and 1.71 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 24 to Nov 22. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Polk County in 2026?

May, with 6.72 inches at the weather station 1 mile east-northeast of Vandervoort, counting Jan 1 through Nov 22.

What was the biggest rain in Polk County in 2026?

3.15 inches on Aug 8, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 22. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Polk County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 22, the weather station 1 mile east-northeast of Vandervoort had 57.50 inches in 2025 and 37.17 inches in 2026. 2026 had 20.33 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Polk County had in 2026?

4,507 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 25 at a weather station 15 miles from the rain station, 110% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Polk County in 2026?

Nov 14 (32°F or colder) at a weather station 15 miles from the rain station. Typical: Nov 1, the middle of 29 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Polk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
