The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles northeast of Hope, 7 miles from the middle of Hempstead County. It reported 311 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
