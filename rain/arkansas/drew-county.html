The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Monticello Municipal Airport, 4 miles from the middle of Drew County. It reported 310 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 310 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
