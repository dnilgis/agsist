How much rain has Nevada had in 2026? County by county

16 Nevada counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 14 counties with a NOAA normal are above 78% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Eureka County: 2nd driest by Nov 9 of 45 years on record (1975 on), 49% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
