Questions and answers

How much rain has Nevada had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 17 Nevada counties has a total in the table above. The most is 12.57 inches at Austin #2, Lander County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Nevada wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 14 counties with a NOAA normal, 6 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 8 below and 0 at it. Half are above 98% and half below. 3 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Nevada rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
