How much rain has Oregon had in 2026? County by county

34 Oregon counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 11 (8 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 29 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 70% of a usual year to date and half below.
