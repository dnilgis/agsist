Questions and answers

How much rain has Oregon had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 34 Oregon counties has a total in the table above. The most is 69.20 inches at Port Orford, Curry County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Oregon wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 30 counties with a NOAA normal, 0 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 30 below and 0 at it. Half are above 71.5% and half below. 4 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Oregon rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 2 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
