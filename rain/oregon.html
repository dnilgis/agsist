Questions and answers

How much rain has Oregon had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 32 Oregon counties has a total in the table above. The most is 70.51 inches at Port Orford, Curry County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Oregon wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 27 counties with a NOAA normal, 0 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 27 below and 0 at it. Half are above 69% and half below. 5 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Oregon rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 27, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 4 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
