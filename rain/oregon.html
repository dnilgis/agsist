How much rain has Oregon had in 2026? County by county

34 Oregon counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 19 (4 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 30 counties with a NOAA normal are above 72% of a normal year to date and half below.
