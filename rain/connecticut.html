Questions and answers

How much rain has Connecticut had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 26, 2026, the station inside each of 8 Connecticut counties has a total in the table above. The most is 51.08 inches at Brookfield, Fairfield County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Connecticut wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 6 counties with a NOAA normal, 2 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 4 below and 0 at it. Half are above 98% and half below. 2 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Connecticut rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 27, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
