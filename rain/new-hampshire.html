Questions and answers

How much rain has New Hampshire had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 10 New Hampshire counties has a total in the table above. The most is 37.60 inches at Jaffrey Silver Ranch Airpark, Cheshire County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is New Hampshire wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 9 counties with a NOAA normal, 0 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 9 below and 0 at it. Half are above 84% and half below. 1 county in the table has no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the New Hampshire rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 26, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
