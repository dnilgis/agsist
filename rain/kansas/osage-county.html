How much rain has Osage County, Kansas, had in 2026?

38.70 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Melvern Lake, Osage County, through Oct 11. That is 7.13 inches more than a usual year to date (123% of usual).

Through Oct 11, the weather station in Melvern Lake went 21 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Jan 25 to Feb 14. Then it caught 3.80 inches on Feb 16, its biggest one-day reading of the year. May was the wettest month at 7.23 inches. On the days both years reported, that is 6.29 inches more than 2025 had.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Melvern Lake (station ID 145210), 10 miles from the middle of Osage County. It reported 274 of the 284 days from Jan 1 to Oct 11; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 274 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Osage County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

What was the biggest rain in Osage County in 2026?

3.80 inches on Feb 16, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 11. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Osage County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 11, the weather station in Melvern Lake had 32.75 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 7 days) and 38.70 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 10 days). On the 268 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.29 inches more than 2025.
