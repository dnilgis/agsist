The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Wakeeney, 7 miles from the middle of Trego County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Trego County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Wakeeney had 25.08 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 9, 111% of the usual 22.51 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Trego County in the last week?

2.06 inches from Nov 3 to Nov 9 at the weather station in Wakeeney, and 3.17 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 11 to Nov 9. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Trego County in 2026?

June, with 5.50 inches at the weather station in Wakeeney, counting Jan 1 through Nov 9.

What was the biggest rain in Trego County in 2026?

2.06 inches on Nov 3, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 9. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Trego County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 9, the weather station in Wakeeney had 20.79 inches in 2025 and 25.08 inches in 2026. 2026 had 4.29 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Trego County had in 2026?

3,891 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 9 at the weather station in Wakeeney, 109% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (29 years).

Sources and terms
