How much rain has Elk County, Kansas, had in 2026?

33.22 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Elk County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 88% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Longton, 10 miles from the middle of Elk County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Elk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
