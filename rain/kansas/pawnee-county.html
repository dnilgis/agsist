How much rain has Pawnee County, Kansas, had in 2026?

19.62 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Larned, Pawnee County, through Oct 13. That is 3.43 inches less than a usual year to date (85% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Larned had a lot of its year come in June, with 9.07 inches that month. The biggest single day was Jun 26 at 2.92 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 12 days, Mar 16 to Mar 27, with nothing in the gauge. On the days both years reported, this year is 9.92 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Larned (station ID 144531), 7 miles from the middle of Pawnee County. It reported 280 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 280 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Pawnee County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
