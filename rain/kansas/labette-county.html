The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles west-southwest of Mound Valley, 8 miles from the middle of Labette County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
