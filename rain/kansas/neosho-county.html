The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Erie, 3 miles from the middle of Neosho County. It reported 315 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Neosho County, Kansas, had in 2026?

Is Neosho County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Erie had 54.33 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 136% of the usual 40.07 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Neosho County in 2026?

June, with 16.79 inches at the weather station in Erie, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Neosho County in 2026?

3.94 inches on Apr 2, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Neosho County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Erie had 39.96 inches in 2025 and 54.33 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 315 days both years reported, 2026 had 14.37 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Neosho County had in 2026?

4,438 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at a weather station 15 miles from the rain station, 114% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

Has Neosho County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 15 miles from the rain station. Usual: Oct 25, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Neosho County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
