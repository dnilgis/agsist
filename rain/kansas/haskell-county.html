The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.7 miles north-northwest of Satanta, 10 miles from the middle of Haskell County. It reported 288 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 27 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
