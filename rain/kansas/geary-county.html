The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.8 miles south-southeast of Milford, 12 miles from the middle of Geary County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
