How much rain has Atchison County, Kansas, had in 2026?

41.56 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Atchison County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 120% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Atchison station: 35.73 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station in Atchison. That is 1.57 inches more than a normal year to date (105% of normal).

This station is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Atchison, 11 miles from the middle of Atchison County. It reported 319 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
