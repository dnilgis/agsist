The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Ransom, 12 miles from the middle of Ness County. It reported 306 of the 307 days from Jan 1 to Nov 3; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 306 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
