How much rain has Brown County, Kansas, had in 2026?

36.77 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Horton in Brown County, through Nov 11. That is 3.07 inches more than a usual year to date (109% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 45.82 inches, 138% of usual.

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 29 points of usual above this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Horton, 11 miles from the middle of Brown County. It reported 298 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Brown County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Brown County?

How many growing degree days has Brown County had in 2026?

3,786 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at the weather station in Horton, 110% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (23 years).

Has Brown County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 11: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Horton. Usual: Oct 15, the middle of 23 years, 1991-2020.
