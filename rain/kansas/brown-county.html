The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Horton, 11 miles from the middle of Brown County. It reported 312 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
