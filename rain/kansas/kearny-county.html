The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lakin, 6 miles from the middle of Kearny County. It reported 314 of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
