How much rain has Barton County, Kansas, had in 2026?

22.13 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Barton County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 87% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Albert station: 22.04 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the weather station 5 miles southeast of Albert. That is 3.18 inches less than a normal year to date (87% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles southeast of Albert, 12 miles from the middle of Barton County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
