The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles northeast of Frankfort, 9 miles from the middle of Marshall County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
