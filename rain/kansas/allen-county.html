The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile west of Iola, 7 miles from the middle of Allen County. It reported 322 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
