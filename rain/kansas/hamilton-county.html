How much rain has Hamilton County, Kansas, had in 2026?

33.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 1 mile northeast of Syracuse in Hamilton County, through Nov 16. That is 16.83 inches more than a normal year to date (203% of normal). Averaged over the whole county, about 24.71 inches, 146% of normal.

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 57 points of normal below this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northeast of Syracuse, 3 miles from the middle of Hamilton County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
