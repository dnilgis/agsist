How much rain has Hamilton County, Kansas, had in 2026?

32.78 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 1 mile northeast of Syracuse in Hamilton County, through Nov 8. That is 16.63 inches more than a usual year to date (203% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 24.39 inches, 145% of usual.

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 58 points of usual below this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northeast of Syracuse, 3 miles from the middle of Hamilton County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Hamilton County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
