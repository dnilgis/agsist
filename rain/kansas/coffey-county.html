How much rain has Coffey County, Kansas, had in 2026?

42.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Coffey County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 116% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.1 miles west-southwest of Waverly, 10 miles from the middle of Coffey County. It reported 323 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 323 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Coffey County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
