The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.2 miles north-northeast of Altoona, 9 miles from the middle of Wilson County. It reported 305 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain fell in Wilson County in the last week?

What was the wettest month in Wilson County in 2026?

June, with 11.50 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 7.2 miles north-northeast of Altoona, counting Jan 1 through Nov 9.

What was the biggest rain in Wilson County in 2026?

3.94 inches on Apr 18, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 9. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wilson County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 9, the volunteer rain gauge 7.2 miles north-northeast of Altoona had 42.63 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 43.22 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 8 days). On the 304 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.24 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Wilson County had in 2026?

4,249 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 10 at a weather station 14 miles from the rain gauge, 113% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (22 years).

Has Wilson County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 10: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 14 miles from the rain gauge.
