The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Marion Reservoir, 2 miles from the middle of Marion County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
