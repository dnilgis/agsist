The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Hill City Municipal Airport, 3 miles from the middle of Graham County. It reported 314 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
