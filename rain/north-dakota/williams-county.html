How much rain has Williams County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

13.56 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Williston Airport, Williams County, through Oct 13. This station has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Williston Airport, 14 miles from the middle of the county, has 13.56 inches for the year. June was the wettest month at 4.06 inches, and the biggest single day was Aug 14 with 2.25 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 13 days, Feb 3 to Feb 15, with not even a trace in the gauge. On the days both years reported, this year is 0.37 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Williston Airport (station ID 94099), 14 miles from the middle of Williams County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Williams County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
