The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-northwest of Cooperstown, 5 miles from the middle of Griggs County. It reported 324 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
