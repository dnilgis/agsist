How much rain has Griggs County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

14.97 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-northwest of Cooperstown, Griggs County, through Oct 16. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-northwest of Cooperstown caught 5.06 inches in September, its wettest month, and 2.73 inches of that came on Sep 29 alone. Before that it sat empty 20 days in a row, Jul 11 to Jul 30. On the days both years reported, this year is 6.04 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-northwest of Cooperstown (gauge ID US1NDGG0003), 5 miles from the middle of Griggs County. It reported 287 of the 289 days from Jan 1 to Oct 16; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Griggs County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
