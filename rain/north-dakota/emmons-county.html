The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles northwest of Hazelton, 17 miles from the middle of Emmons County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
