How much rain has Nelson County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north of Petersburg, 11 miles from the middle of Nelson County. It reported 299 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 27 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
