The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Hettinger Municipal Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Adams County. It reported 304 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
