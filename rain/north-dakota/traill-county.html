The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mayville, 9 miles from the middle of Traill County. It reported 309 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
