How much rain has Traill County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

17.84 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Traill County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 82% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.1 miles east-northeast of Grandin, 19 miles from the middle of Traill County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
