The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Devils Lake Municipal Airport (station ID 94928), 14 miles from the middle of Ramsey County. It reported 286 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Ramsey County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
