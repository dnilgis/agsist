The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Devils Lake Municipal Airport, 14 miles from the middle of Ramsey County. It reported 323 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
