How much rain has Burke County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

18.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 5 miles east-southeast of Northgate, Burke County, through Oct 18. That is 3.77 inches more than a usual year to date (126% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 5 miles east-southeast of Northgate has 18.14 inches, 3.92 more than a usual year. June was the wettest month at 4.23 inches, and the biggest single day was 1.63 inches on Aug 14. It is still 2.32 inches less than last year had by this day. The longest dry stretch ran 15 days, Feb 19 to Mar 5.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles east-southeast of Northgate (station ID 94084), 20 miles from the middle of Burke County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Burke County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
