How much rain has Sheridan County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

13.23 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Mc Clusky, Sheridan County, through Oct 6. That is 2.40 inches less than a usual year to date (85% of usual).

Through Oct 6, the weather station in Mc Clusky had its wettest month in June at 4.08 inches. The biggest single day was 1.50 inches on Sep 29. The gauge went 15 days in a row with nothing in it, Jul 9 to Jul 23. On the days both years reported, that is 4.99 inches less than 2025 had by Oct 6.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mc Clusky (station ID 325710), 8 miles from the middle of Sheridan County. It reported 278 of the 279 days from Jan 1 to Oct 6; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 278 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Sheridan County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
