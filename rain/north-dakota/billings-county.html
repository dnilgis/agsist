How much rain has Billings County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

11.77 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Billings County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 77% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Medora station: 10.21 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 7 miles east of Medora. That is 6.40 inches less than a normal year to date (61% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 7 miles east of Medora, 9 miles from the middle of Billings County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
