The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 38 miles west-southwest of Jamestown (station ID 54937), 20 miles from the middle of Kidder County. It reported every one of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 310 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Kidder County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
