How much rain has Ransom County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

14.88 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Ransom County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 68% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Lisbon station: 15.29 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station in Lisbon. That is 5.92 inches less than a normal year to date (72% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lisbon, 2 miles from the middle of Ransom County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
