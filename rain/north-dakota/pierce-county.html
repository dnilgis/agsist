The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 11.9 miles southeast of Towner, 11 miles from the middle of Pierce County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
