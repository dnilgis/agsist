The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 11.9 miles southeast of Towner (station TOWNER 11.9 SE), 11 miles from the middle of Pierce County. It reported 285 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12, every one. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Pierce County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
