How much rain has Rolette County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

21.18 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Rolette County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 117% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.4 miles north of Dunseith, 14 miles from the middle of Rolette County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
