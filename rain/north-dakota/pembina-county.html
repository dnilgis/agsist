How much rain has Pembina County, North Dakota, had in 2026?

18.30 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Pembina, Pembina County, through Oct 18. That is 1.49 inches less than a usual year to date (92% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the station in Pembina had June as the wettest month by a long way, with 7.67 inches. The biggest single day came Jun 3 at 3.38 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 12 days, Feb 6 to Feb 17, with nothing in the gauge. On the days both years reported, that is 2.13 inches more than last year, even with the total 1.25 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pembina (station ID 14924), 20 miles from the middle of Pembina County. It reported 290 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 290 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Pembina County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
