The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Velva, 18 miles from the middle of McHenry County. It reported 306 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is McHenry County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Velva had 18.29 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 105% of the usual 17.39 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in McHenry County in the last week?

0.73 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station in Velva, and 1.43 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in McHenry County in 2026?

June, with 5.62 inches at the weather station in Velva, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in McHenry County in 2026?

1.61 inches on Jun 23, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 8. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in McHenry County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station in Velva had 21.55 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 18.29 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 6 days). On the 305 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.13 inches less than 2025.
