How much rain has North Dakota had in 2026? County by county

34 North Dakota counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 13 (11 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 27 counties with a NOAA normal are above 83% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • LaMoure County: 2nd driest by Nov 13 of 40 years on record (1949 on), 54% of normal.
