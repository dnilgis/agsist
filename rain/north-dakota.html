How much rain has North Dakota had in 2026? County by county

36 North Dakota counties have a weather station inside the county that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Nov 3 (7 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 30 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 76.5% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record
