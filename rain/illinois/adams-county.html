The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.4 miles east-southeast of Quincy, 8 miles from the middle of Adams County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
