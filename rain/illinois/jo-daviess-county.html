The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north-northeast of Stockton, 12 miles from the middle of Jo Daviess County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
