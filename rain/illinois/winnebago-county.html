The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Rockford Greater Rockford Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Winnebago County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
