The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hillsboro, 5 miles from the middle of Montgomery County. It reported every one of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 324 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
