The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.1 miles west-northwest of Rochelle, 11 miles from the middle of Ogle County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
