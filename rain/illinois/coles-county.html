The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Charleston, 4 miles from the middle of Coles County. It reported 312 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
