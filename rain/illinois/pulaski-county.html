The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Olmsted, 5 miles from the middle of Pulaski County. It reported 301 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Pulaski County, Illinois, had in 2026?

Is Pulaski County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Pulaski County, about 45.81 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 23, 103% of the normal 44.36 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Pulaski County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Pulaski County in 2026?

2.70 inches on Aug 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 23. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Pulaski County?
