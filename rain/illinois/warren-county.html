The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles northwest of Monmouth, 8 miles from the middle of Warren County. It reported 324 of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
