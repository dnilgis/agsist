The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.3 miles east-northeast of Brookport, 12 miles from the middle of Massac County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
