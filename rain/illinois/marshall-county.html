How much rain has Marshall County, Illinois, had in 2026?

35.55 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Marshall County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 103% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Lacon station: 31.96 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Lacon. That is 2.43 inches less than a normal year to date (93% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lacon, 4 miles from the middle of Marshall County. It reported 313 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Marshall County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
