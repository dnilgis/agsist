  • Wettest month: June, 7.04 inches.

  • Longest dry stretch: 14 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 1 to Feb 14.
  • 2025 by the same date: 41.42 inches at the same station. 2026 had 2.46 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 43rd wettest by Nov 8 of 103 years on record here (1910 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2023. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 37.79 inches, 102% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Madison County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Edwardsville, 6 miles from the middle of Madison County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Madison County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
