The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north of Cairo, 14 miles from the middle of Alexander County. It reported 321 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
