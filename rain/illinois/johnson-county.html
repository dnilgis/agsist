The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 5.3 miles north-northwest of Vienna (station VIENNA 5.3 NNW), 4 miles from the middle of Johnson County. It reported 285 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12, every one. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Johnson County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
