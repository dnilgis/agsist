How much rain has Macon County, Illinois, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Decatur, 2 miles from the middle of Macon County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
