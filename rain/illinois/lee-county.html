How much rain has Lee County, Illinois, had in 2026?

43.67 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lee County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 124% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Amboy Water station: 46.19 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station in Amboy Water. That is 11.30 inches more than a normal year to date (132% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Amboy Water, 2 miles from the middle of Lee County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
