The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles southeast of Mazon, 5 miles from the middle of Grundy County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
