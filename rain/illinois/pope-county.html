The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Smithland, 19 miles from the middle of Pope County. It reported 292 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 28 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
