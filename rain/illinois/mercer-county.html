The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Aledo, less than a mile from the middle of Mercer County. It reported 314 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Mercer County, Illinois, had in 2026?

46.61 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Aledo in Mercer County, through Nov 12. That is 11.15 inches more than a normal year to date (131% of normal).

Is Mercer County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Aledo had 46.61 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 131% of the normal 35.46 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Mercer County in the last week?

0.12 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Aledo, and 1.34 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Mercer County in 2026?

August, with 12.91 inches at the weather station in Aledo, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Mercer County in 2026?

2.97 inches on Aug 14, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Mercer County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Aledo had 28.43 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 46.61 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 2 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 16.47 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Mercer County had in 2026?

3,516 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Aledo, 115% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (23 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Mercer County in 2026?

Nov 7 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Aledo.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Mercer County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
