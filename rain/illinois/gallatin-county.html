Shawneetown station: 31.30 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 3 miles west-southwest of Shawneetown. This station has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles west-southwest of Shawneetown, 4 miles from the middle of Gallatin County. It reported 314 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
