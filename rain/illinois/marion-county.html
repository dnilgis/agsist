The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Salem, 2 miles from the middle of Marion County. It reported 322 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
