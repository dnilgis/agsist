The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles north-northwest of St. Elmo, 9 miles from the middle of Fayette County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
