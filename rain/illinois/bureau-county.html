The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles east-northeast of Walnut, 13 miles from the middle of Bureau County. It reported 326 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
