The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles east-southeast of Bridgeville, 10 miles from the middle of Sussex County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
