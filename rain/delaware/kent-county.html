The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.5 miles south-southwest of Smyrna, 11 miles from the middle of Kent County. It reported 312 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
