The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Dover, 5 miles from the middle of Kent County. It reported 287 of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the 30 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Kent County in 2026?

1.45 inches on Aug 21, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 13. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Kent County?

How many growing degree days has Kent County had in 2026?

4,060 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 16 at the weather station in Dover, 107% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Has Kent County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 16: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Dover. Typical: Nov 4, the middle of 29 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 287 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Kent County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
