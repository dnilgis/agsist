How much rain has Pitkin County, Colorado, had in 2026?

7.08 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Aspen Pitkin County Airport Sardy Field, Pitkin County, through Oct 18. That is 5.46 inches less than a usual year to date (56% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Aspen Pitkin County Airport Sardy Field has 6.62 inches, 5.70 inches less than a usual year. April was the wettest month at 1.64 inches, and the biggest single day was Apr 26 with 0.67 inches. The gauge went 13 days in a row with nothing, Jan 10 to Jan 22. On the days both years reported, this year is 1.83 inches behind last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Aspen Pitkin County Airport Sardy Field (station ID 93073), 3 miles from the middle of Pitkin County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Pitkin County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
