The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles east-southeast of Broomfield, 3 miles from the middle of Broomfield County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
