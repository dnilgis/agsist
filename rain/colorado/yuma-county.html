How much rain has Yuma County, Colorado, had in 2026?

16.54 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Yuma County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 97% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Vernon station: 13.53 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 4 miles east-northeast of Vernon. That is 3.87 inches less than a normal year to date (78% of normal).

This station is 19 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles east-northeast of Vernon, 9 miles from the middle of Yuma County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
