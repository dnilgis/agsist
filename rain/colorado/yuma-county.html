The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles east-northeast of Vernon, 9 miles from the middle of Yuma County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
