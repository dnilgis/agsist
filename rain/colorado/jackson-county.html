2026 so far in Jackson County

  • Wettest month: May, 2.59 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.21 inches on May 18.
  • Longest dry stretch: 25 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, May 31 to Jun 24.
  • 2025 by the same date: 7.61 inches at the same station. 2026 had 2.59 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 32nd wettest by Nov 8 of 86 years on record here (1900 on); the last wetter year was 2019, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Walden, 6 miles from the middle of Jackson County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Jackson County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
