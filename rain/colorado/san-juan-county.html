How much rain has San Juan County, Colorado, had in 2026?

42.83 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over San Juan County, Jan 1 to Nov 23, an estimate from a weather grid (125% of normal).

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.05 in, 7 days 0.37 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 am Mountain, Nov 24.

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. All 6 stations in San Juan County that reported nearly every day are high mountain sites; the one used is the Red Mountain Pass SNOTEL site, 9 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 39.70 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the figures below are that site’s. That is 4.70 inches more than a normal year to date (113% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Red Mountain Pass, 9 miles from the middle of San Juan County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 318 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
