The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles west-southwest of Leroy, 16 miles from the middle of Logan County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
