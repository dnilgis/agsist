The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles east-northeast of Littleton, 35 miles from the middle of Arapahoe County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
