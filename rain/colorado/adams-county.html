How much rain has Adams County, Colorado, had in 2026?

11.33 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Adams County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 76% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Brighton station: 6.65 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 3 miles southeast of Brighton. That is 6.83 inches less than a normal year to date (49% of normal).

This station is 27 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southeast of Brighton, 27 miles from the middle of Adams County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
