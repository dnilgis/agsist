How much rain has Costilla County, Colorado, had in 2026?

14.19 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Costilla County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 86% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Fort Garland gauge: 16.30 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 13.3 miles east of Fort Garland. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 13.3 miles east of Fort Garland, 18 miles from the middle of Costilla County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Costilla County, Colorado, had in 2026?

Is Costilla County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Costilla County, about 14.19 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 86% of the normal 16.52 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Costilla County in the last week?

0.24 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the volunteer rain gauge 13.3 miles east of Fort Garland, and 4.40 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Costilla County in 2026?

October, with 5.74 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 13.3 miles east of Fort Garland, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Costilla County in 2026?

1.53 inches on Oct 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Costilla County?
