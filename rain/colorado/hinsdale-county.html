How much rain has Hinsdale County, Colorado, had in 2026?

11.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 1 mile north-northeast of Lake City, Hinsdale County, through Oct 9. That is 2.03 inches less than a usual year to date (85% of usual).

Through Oct 9, the weather station 1 mile north-northeast of Lake City had July as its wettest month at 3.48 inches, and the biggest single day was 1.24 inches on Jul 25. Before that it went 26 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from May 20 to Jun 14. On the days both years reported, this year is 1.30 inches more than last year, even while it runs 2.03 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile north-northeast of Lake City (station ID 054736), 16 miles from the middle of Hinsdale County. It reported every one of the 282 days from Jan 1 to Oct 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Hinsdale County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
