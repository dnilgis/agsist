The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles north-northeast of Woodrow, 18 miles from the middle of Morgan County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
