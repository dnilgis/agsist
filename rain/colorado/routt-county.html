The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles west-northwest of Steamboat Springs, 5 miles from the middle of Routt County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
