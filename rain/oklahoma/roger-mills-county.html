The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile west-southwest of Cheyenne, 6 miles from the middle of Roger Mills County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
