How much rain has Grady County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

26.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Grady County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 82% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Chickasha Experiment Station: 22.34 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the Chickasha Experiment Station weather station in Chickasha. That is 11.53 inches less than a normal year to date (66% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Chickasha, 3 miles from the middle of Grady County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
