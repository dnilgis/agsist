How much rain has Tulsa County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

35.22 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Tulsa Richard L Jones Jr Airport, Tulsa County, through Oct 13. That is 0.44 inches more than a usual year to date (101% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Tulsa Richard L Jones Jr Airport had June as its wettest month at 11.11 inches, and the biggest single day came Jun 6 with 2.72 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 16 days, from Mar 11 to Mar 26, with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. That puts the year 3.55 inches less than last year by the same day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Tulsa Richard L Jones Jr Airport (station ID 53908), 6 miles from the middle of Tulsa County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 20 stations inside Tulsa County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
