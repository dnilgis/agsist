The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Clinton-Sherman Airport, 13 miles from the middle of Washita County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
