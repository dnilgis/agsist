The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south-southwest of Orienta, 4 miles from the middle of Major County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
