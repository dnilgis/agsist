The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Oklahoma City, 15 miles from the middle of Oklahoma County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 330 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
