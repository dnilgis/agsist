The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 0.6 miles southwest of Drumright (station DRUMRIGHT 0.6 SW), 14 miles from the middle of Creek County. It reported 279 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Creek County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
