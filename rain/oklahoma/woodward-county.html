The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles east-northeast of Fargo, 17 miles from the middle of Woodward County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
