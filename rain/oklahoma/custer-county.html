The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Weatherford, 19 miles from the middle of Custer County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
