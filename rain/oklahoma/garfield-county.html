The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Enid, 6 miles from the middle of Garfield County. It reported 322 of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Garfield County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Garfield County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Garfield County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Garfield County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
