How much rain has Dewey County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

13.41 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Leedey, Dewey County, through Oct 13. That is 9.05 inches less than a usual year to date (60% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Leedey had June as its wettest month at 6.74 inches, and the biggest single day came Jun 4 with 2.87 inches. Then the gauge sat empty for 34 days in a row, Jul 25 to Aug 27. On the days both years reported, this year is 15.40 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Leedey (station ID 345090), 21 miles from the middle of Dewey County. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Dewey County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
