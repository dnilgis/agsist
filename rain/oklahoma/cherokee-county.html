The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.9 miles north of Hulbert, 10 miles from the middle of Cherokee County. It reported 319 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
