The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles northwest of Durant, 10 miles from the middle of Bryan County. It reported 324 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
