The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.6 miles east-southeast of Vinita, 17 miles from the middle of Craig County. It reported 306 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
