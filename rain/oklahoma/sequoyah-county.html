How much rain has Sequoyah County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

32.95 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sequoyah County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 74% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Vian gauge: 35.86 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles east-northeast of Vian. That is 9.13 inches less than a normal year to date (80% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.3 miles east-northeast of Vian, 8 miles from the middle of Sequoyah County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 319 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Sequoyah County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
