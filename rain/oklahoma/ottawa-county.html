How much rain has Ottawa County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

39.20 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Ottawa County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 93% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Miami gauge: 41.39 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the volunteer rain gauge 3.7 miles east-northeast of Miami. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.7 miles east-northeast of Miami, 6 miles from the middle of Ottawa County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
