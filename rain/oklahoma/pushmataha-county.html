The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.3 miles southeast of Antlers, 20 miles from the middle of Pushmataha County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the wettest month in Pushmataha County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Pushmataha County in 2026?

6.54 inches on Jun 24, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 11.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Pushmataha County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 11, the volunteer rain gauge 6.3 miles southeast of Antlers had 39.55 inches in 2025 and 39.96 inches in 2026. 2026 had 0.41 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Pushmataha County had in 2026?

4,946 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at a weather station 6 miles from the rain gauge, 113% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (24 years).

Has Pushmataha County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 6 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Nov 2, the middle of 24 years, 1991-2020.
