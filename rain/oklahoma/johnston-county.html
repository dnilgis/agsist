How much rain has Johnston County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

36.03 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Johnston County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 93% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Coleman station: 38.20 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 4 miles west of Coleman. That is 1.33 inches more than a normal year to date (104% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is drier than normal, this station wetter. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west of Coleman, 10 miles from the middle of Johnston County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
