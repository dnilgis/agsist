How much rain has Pontotoc County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

34.86 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Ada, Pontotoc County, through Oct 8. That is 4.11 inches more than a usual year to date (113% of usual).

Through Oct 8, the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Ada has had a lopsided year. It went 21 days in a row with nothing in it, from Mar 12 to Apr 1. Then it caught 3.93 inches on Apr 4, and April finished as the wettest month at 8.68 inches. On the days both years reported, this year is 2.53 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Ada (gauge ID US1OKPN0006), 4 miles from the middle of Pontotoc County. It reported 257 of the 281 days from Jan 1 to Oct 8; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 257 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Pontotoc County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Pontotoc County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

Is Pontotoc County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Ada had 34.86 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 8, 113% of the usual 30.75 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Pontotoc County in 2026?

April, with 8.68 inches at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Ada, counting Jan 1 through Oct 8.

What was the biggest rain in Pontotoc County in 2026?

3.93 inches on Apr 4, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 8. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Pontotoc County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 8, the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Ada had 43.79 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 34.86 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 24 days). On the 252 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.53 inches less than 2025.
