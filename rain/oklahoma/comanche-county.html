The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Lawton Municipal Airport (station ID 03950), 8 miles from the middle of Comanche County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Comanche County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Questions

How much rain has Comanche County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

Is Comanche County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Lawton Municipal Airport had 17.18 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 75% of the usual 22.96 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Comanche County in 2026?

April, with 6.57 inches at the weather station at Lawton Municipal Airport, counting Jan 1 through Oct 13.

What was the biggest rain in Comanche County in 2026?

2.85 inches on Apr 3, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Oct 13.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Comanche County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at Lawton Municipal Airport had 31.06 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 17.18 inches in 2026. On the 283 days both years reported, 2026 had 13.88 inches less than 2025.
