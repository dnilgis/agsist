The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 0.8 miles north of Bunch (station BUNCH 0.8 N), 15 miles from the middle of Adair County. It reported 268 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 17 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 268 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Adair County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
