How much rain has Muskogee County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

25.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Muskogee Davis Field, Muskogee County, through Oct 13. That is 8.65 inches less than a usual year to date (75% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the station at Muskogee Davis Field has 25.75 inches. June was the wettest month at 8.84 inches, and the biggest single day came Jun 6 with 1.98 inches. From Aug 1 to Aug 16 the gauge went 16 days in a row without even a trace. On the days both years reported, that is 15.04 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Muskogee Davis Field (station ID 93953), 3 miles from the middle of Muskogee County. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Muskogee County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
