The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Muskogee Davis Field, 3 miles from the middle of Muskogee County. It reported 313 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Muskogee County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

0.19 inches from Nov 4 to Nov 10 at the weather station at Muskogee Davis Field, and 2.40 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 12 to Nov 10. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Muskogee County in 2026?

June, with 8.84 inches at the weather station at Muskogee Davis Field, counting Jan 1 through Nov 10.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Muskogee County?

How many growing degree days has Muskogee County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Muskogee County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
