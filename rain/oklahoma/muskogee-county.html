The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Muskogee Davis Field, 3 miles from the middle of Muskogee County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Muskogee County, Oklahoma, had in 2026?

Is Muskogee County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Muskogee County, about 30.78 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 74% of the normal 41.73 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Muskogee County in the last week?

0.03 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the weather station at Muskogee Davis Field, and 0.86 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Muskogee County in 2026?

June, with 8.84 inches at the weather station at Muskogee Davis Field, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Muskogee County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Muskogee County?

How many growing degree days has Muskogee County had in 2026?

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
