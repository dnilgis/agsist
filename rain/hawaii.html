How much rain has Hawaii had in 2026? County by county

4 Hawaii counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12. Half the counties are above 184% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Maui County: 2nd wettest by Nov 12 of 79 years on record (1905 on), 264% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
