How much rain has Grant County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

42.18 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 4 miles west-southwest of Lancaster, Grant County, through Oct 13. That is 9.43 inches more than a usual year to date (129% of usual).

April did the most of it with 8.62 inches, and the biggest single day came Sep 19 at 2.86. On the days both years reported, that is 10.23 inches more than 2025 had by now. The longest dry run was 15 days, Feb 3 to Feb 17, with not even a trace in the gauge.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west-southwest of Lancaster (station ID 474546), 5 miles from the middle of Grant County. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Grant County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
