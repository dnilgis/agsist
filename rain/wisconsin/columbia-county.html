How much rain has Columbia County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

34.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Columbia County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 104% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Portage Wwtp station: 34.56 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the Portage Wwtp weather station in Portage. That is 1.18 inches less than a normal year to date (97% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Portage, 7 miles from the middle of Columbia County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
