Fond Du Lac Wwtp station: 31.18 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the Fond Du Lac Wwtp weather station in Fond Du Lac. That is 2.14 inches more than a normal year to date (107% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Fond Du Lac, 3 miles from the middle of Fond du Lac County. It reported 318 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
