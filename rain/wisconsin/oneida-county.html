25.89 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Oneida County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 83% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Rhinelander Oneida County Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Oneida County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
