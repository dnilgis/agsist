How much rain has Oneida County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

24.51 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Oneida County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 79% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Rhinelander Oneida County Airport station: 26.35 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station at Rhinelander Oneida County Airport. That is 1.38 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

This station is 16 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Rhinelander Oneida County Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Oneida County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
