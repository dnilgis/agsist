How much rain has Monroe County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

38.38 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Monroe County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 115% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Cashton station: 45.68 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station 3 miles north-northwest of Cashton. That is 11.21 inches more than a normal year to date (133% of normal).

This station is 18 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north-northwest of Cashton, 14 miles from the middle of Monroe County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
