The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Menomonie, 5 miles from the middle of Dunn County. It reported 320 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
