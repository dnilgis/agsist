The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Trempealeau, 21 miles from the middle of Trempealeau County. It reported 324 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
