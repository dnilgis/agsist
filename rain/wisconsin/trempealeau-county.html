How much rain has Trempealeau County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

40.46 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Trempealeau, Trempealeau County, through Oct 18. That is 10.13 inches more than a usual year to date (133% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Trempealeau had its wettest month in September with 9.32 inches. The biggest single day came Sep 19 at 3.75 inches. On the days both years reported, this year has 6.14 inches more than last year. The longest dry stretch ran 15 days, Feb 3 to Feb 17, with nothing in the gauge.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Trempealeau (station ID 478589), 21 miles from the middle of Trempealeau County. It reported 288 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Trempealeau County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
