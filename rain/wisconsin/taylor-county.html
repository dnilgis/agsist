The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-northeast of Medford, 9 miles from the middle of Taylor County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
