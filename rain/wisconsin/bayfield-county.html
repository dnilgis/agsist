The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west of Clam Lake, 26 miles from the middle of Bayfield County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Bayfield County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
