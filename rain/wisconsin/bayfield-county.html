The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west of Clam Lake, 26 miles from the middle of Bayfield County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
