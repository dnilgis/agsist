How much rain has Waushara County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

24.69 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Wautoma Municipal Airport, Waushara County, through Oct 18. This station has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Wautoma Municipal Airport has caught 23.80 inches this year. September was the wettest month at 6.13 inches. The biggest single day came Jul 1 with 1.83 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 19 days, Jan 18 to Feb 5, with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wautoma Municipal Airport (station ID 00132), 6 miles from the middle of Waushara County. It reported 290 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Waushara County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
