The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wautoma Municipal Airport (station ID 00132), 6 miles from the middle of Waushara County. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Waushara County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
