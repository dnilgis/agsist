How much rain has Barron County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

27.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Cumberland, Barron County, through Oct 13. That is 1.82 inches less than a usual year to date (94% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Cumberland had April as its wettest month at 6.25 inches, and the biggest single day came Apr 28 with 2.02 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 11 days, Feb 7 to Feb 17, with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year is 4.49 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cumberland (station ID 471923), 11 miles from the middle of Barron County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Barron County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
