How much rain has Vilas County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

23.87 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Vilas County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 76% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Eagle River station: 27.64 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Eagle River. That is 2.22 inches less than a normal year to date (93% of normal).

This station is 17 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Eagle River, 16 miles from the middle of Vilas County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
