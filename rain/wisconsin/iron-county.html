The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hurley, 14 miles from the middle of Iron County. It reported 313 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Iron County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How much rain fell in Iron County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Hurley, and 3.56 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Iron County in 2026?

April, with 3.20 inches at the weather station in Hurley, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Iron County in 2026?

2.09 inches on Aug 8, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Iron County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Hurley had 32.98 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 2 days) and 20.91 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.59 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Iron County had in 2026?

2,472 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Hurley, 120% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Iron County in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Iron County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
