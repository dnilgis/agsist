Spooner Ag Research Station: 24.03 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 13, at the Spooner Ag Research Station weather station in Spooner. That is 5.55 inches less than a normal year to date (81% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Spooner, 7 miles from the middle of Washburn County. It reported every one of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
