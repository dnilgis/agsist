The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Friendship, 3 miles from the middle of Adams County. It reported 320 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
