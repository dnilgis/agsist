How much rain has Rusk County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

24.72 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Rusk County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 80% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Big Falls Hydro station: 23.62 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 13, at the weather station in Big Falls Hydro. That is 5.64 inches less than a normal year to date (81% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Big Falls Hydro, 10 miles from the middle of Rusk County. It reported 297 of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the 20 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Rusk County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

24.72 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Rusk County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 80% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Big Falls Hydro station had 23.62 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 13 (81% of normal).

Is Rusk County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Rusk County, about 24.72 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 80% of the normal 30.97 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Rusk County in 2026?

April, with 6.17 inches at the weather station in Big Falls Hydro, counting Jan 1 through Nov 13.

What was the biggest rain in Rusk County in 2026?

2.90 inches on Apr 24, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 13. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Rusk County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 13, the weather station in Big Falls Hydro had 27.35 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 23.62 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 20 days). On the 294 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.13 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Rusk County had in 2026?

2,667 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 16 at the weather station in Big Falls Hydro.

When was the first fall freeze in Rusk County in 2026?

Oct 17 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Big Falls Hydro.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 297 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Rusk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
