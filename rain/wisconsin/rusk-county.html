The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Big Falls Hydro, 10 miles from the middle of Rusk County. It reported 301 of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20; the 23 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Rusk County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Rusk County, about 25.93 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 23, 83% of the normal 31.26 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Rusk County in 2026?

April, with 6.17 inches at the weather station in Big Falls Hydro, counting Jan 1 through Nov 20.

What was the biggest rain in Rusk County in 2026?

2.90 inches on Apr 24, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 20. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Rusk County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 301 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Rusk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
