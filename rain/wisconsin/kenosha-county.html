How much rain has Kenosha County, Wisconsin, had in 2026?

37.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 4 miles northeast of Paddock Lake, Kenosha County, through Oct 13. That is 5.86 inches more than a usual year to date (119% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 4 miles northeast of Paddock Lake had its wettest month in April at 8.68 inches. It went 17 days with nothing in the gauge from Jul 10 to Jul 26, then caught its biggest single day on Jul 27 with 2.90 inches. On the days both years reported, that is 14.77 inches more than 2025 had by this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles northeast of Paddock Lake (station ID 476380), 2 miles from the middle of Kenosha County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Kenosha County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
