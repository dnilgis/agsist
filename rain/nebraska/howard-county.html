The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Saint Paul, 3 miles from the middle of Howard County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
