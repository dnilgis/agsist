How much rain has Colfax County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

31.47 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Colfax County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 114% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Howells station: 29.21 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 8, at the weather station in Howells. That is 2.94 inches more than a normal year to date (111% of normal).

  • Longest dry stretch: 18 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 2 to Feb 19.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Howells, 11 miles from the middle of Colfax County. It reported 306 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
