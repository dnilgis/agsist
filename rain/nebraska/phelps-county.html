How much rain has Phelps County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

23.67 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Holdrege, Phelps County, through Oct 18. That is 1.29 inches less than a usual year to date (95% of usual).

Through Oct 9, the weather station in Holdrege has 23.29 inches, 1.31 inches less than a usual year. September was the wettest month at 4.77 inches, and the biggest single day came Oct 1 with 2.17 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 21 days, Mar 12 to Apr 1, with not even a trace in the gauge. That puts this year 2.10 inches behind last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Holdrege (station ID 253910), 4 miles from the middle of Phelps County. It reported 287 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 287 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Phelps County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
