The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles west-southwest of Naper, 23 miles from the middle of Keya Paha County. It reported 326 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
