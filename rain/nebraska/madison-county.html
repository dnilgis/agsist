How much rain has Madison County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

22.59 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Norfolk Karl Stefan Memorial Airport, Madison County, through Oct 18. That is 1.69 inches less than a usual year to date (93% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Norfolk Karl Stefan Memorial Airport had 21.59 inches, 2.31 inches less than a usual year. August was the wettest month at 5.80 inches, and 3.30 of that came on Aug 13. The gauge went 18 days with nothing from Jul 4 to Jul 21. Last year at this point it had 5.28 inches more.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Norfolk Karl Stefan Memorial Airport (station ID 14941), 10 miles from the middle of Madison County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Madison County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
