The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles northwest of Wakefield, 13 miles from the middle of Dixon County. It reported every one of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
