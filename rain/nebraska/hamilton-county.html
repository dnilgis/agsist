How much rain has Hamilton County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

37.95 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Hamilton County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 140% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles southeast of Marquette, 8 miles from the middle of Hamilton County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
