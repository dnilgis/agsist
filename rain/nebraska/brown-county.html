The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 14.21 miles northeast of Brewster, 24 miles from the middle of Brown County. It reported 321 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
