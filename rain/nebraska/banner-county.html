The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 12 miles west-northwest of Harrisburg, 14 miles from the middle of Banner County. It reported 302 of the 304 days from Jan 1 to Oct 31; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
