How much rain has Banner County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

5.75 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 9.1 miles southwest of Harrisburg, Banner County, through Oct 10. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

On the days both years reported, that is 7.88 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.1 miles southwest of Harrisburg (gauge ID US1NEBA0003), 10 miles from the middle of Banner County. It reported 272 of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Banner County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
