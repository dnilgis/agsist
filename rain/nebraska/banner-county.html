The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.1 miles southwest of Harrisburg, 10 miles from the middle of Banner County. It reported 309 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
