How much rain has Wheeler County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

19.54 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 11.9 miles south of Ewing, Wheeler County, through Oct 18. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 11.9 miles south of Ewing (gauge ID US1NEWH0002), 14 miles from the middle of Wheeler County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Wheeler County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
