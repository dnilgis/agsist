The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 11.9 miles south of Ewing, 14 miles from the middle of Wheeler County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
