The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Tekamah Municipal Airport (station ID 94978), 10 miles from the middle of Burt County. It reported 284 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Burt County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Burt County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Burt County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the NOAA station at Tekamah Municipal Airport had 28.37 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 13 days) and 23.52 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 271 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.90 inches less than 2025.
