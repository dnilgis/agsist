The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southwest of Chadron, 7 miles from the middle of Dawes County. It reported 327 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
