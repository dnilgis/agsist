How much rain has Butler County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

32.60 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Butler County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 114% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles south of Brainard, 8 miles from the middle of Butler County. It reported 317 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 317 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Butler County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
