How much rain has Wayne County, Nebraska, had in 2026?

27.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wayne County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 98% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.35 miles west-southwest of Carroll, 9 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported 320 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
