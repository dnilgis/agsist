2026 so far in Logan County

  • Wettest month: June, 3.56 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.12 inches on Jul 10.
  • Longest dry stretch: 17 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Mar 16 to Apr 1.
  • 2025 by the same date: 23.04 inches at the same station. On the 305 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.77 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 32nd wettest by Nov 3 of 36 years on record here (1990 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2022. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 20.74 inches, 93% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 3, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Logan County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 20 points above this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles west of Stapleton (station ID 258133), 10 miles from the middle of Logan County. It reported 305 of the 307 days from Jan 1 to Nov 3; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 305 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Logan County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
