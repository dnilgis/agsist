Questions and answers

How much rain has New York had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 59 New York counties has a total in the table above. The most is 53.43 inches at Beacon, Putnam County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is New York wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 47 counties with a NOAA normal, 16 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 29 below and 2 at it. Half are above 96% and half below. 12 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the New York rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 3 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
