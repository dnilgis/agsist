How much rain has Dillingham Census Area, Alaska, had in 2026?

42.46 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 1 mile north-northeast of Aleknagik, Dillingham Census Area, through Oct 16. This station has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 10, the weather station 1 mile north-northeast of Aleknagik had 41.50 inches, 11.42 inches less than last year by the same day. April was the wettest month at 7.13 inches. The biggest single day was Aug 3 with 1.87 inches. From Mar 8 to Mar 28 the gauge went 21 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile north-northeast of Aleknagik (station ID 23583), 39 miles from the middle of Dillingham Census Area. It reported every one of the 289 days from Jan 1 to Oct 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Dillingham Census Area that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
