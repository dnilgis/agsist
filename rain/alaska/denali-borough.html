The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mckinley Park, 32 miles from the middle of Denali Borough. It reported every one of the 309 days from Jan 1 to Nov 5. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 309 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Denali Borough reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
