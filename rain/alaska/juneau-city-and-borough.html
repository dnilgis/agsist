The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Juneau Forecast Office, 13 miles from the middle of Juneau City and Borough. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
