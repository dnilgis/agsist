How much rain has Bristol Bay Borough, Alaska, had in 2026?

15.33 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at King Salmon Airport, Bristol Bay Borough, through Oct 18. That is 1.77 inches less than a usual year to date (90% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at King Salmon Airport had its wettest month in August with 3.65 inches. The biggest single day came Aug 3 at 0.69 inches. On the days both years reported, 2026 is 8.24 inches behind 2025. The longest stretch with nothing in the gauge ran 10 days, Mar 19 to Mar 28.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at King Salmon Airport (station ID 25503), 4 miles from the middle of Bristol Bay Borough. It reported 281 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 281 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Bristol Bay Borough that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
