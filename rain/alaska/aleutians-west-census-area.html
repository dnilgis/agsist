The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Saint Paul Island Airport (station ID 25713), 328 miles from the middle of Aleutians West Census Area. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Aleutians West Census Area reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Aleutians West Census Area, Alaska, had in 2026?

Is Aleutians West Census Area wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station at Saint Paul Island Airport had 22.82 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 133% of the usual 17.13 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Aleutians West Census Area in 2026?

August, with 6.34 inches at the weather station at Saint Paul Island Airport, counting Jan 1 through Oct 13.

What was the biggest rain in Aleutians West Census Area in 2026?

1.46 inches on Aug 31, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Oct 13.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Aleutians West Census Area?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at Saint Paul Island Airport had 19.71 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 14 days) and 22.82 inches in 2026. On the 272 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.92 inches less than 2025.
