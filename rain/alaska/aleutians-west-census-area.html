The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Saint Paul Island Airport, 328 miles from the middle of Aleutians West Census Area. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
