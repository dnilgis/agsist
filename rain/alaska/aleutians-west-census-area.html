How much rain has Aleutians West Census Area, Alaska, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Saint Paul Island Airport, 328 miles from the middle of Aleutians West Census Area. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
