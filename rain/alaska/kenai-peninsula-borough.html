The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles southwest of Sterling, 27 miles from the middle of Kenai Peninsula Borough. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 17 stations inside Kenai Peninsula Borough reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
