The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile east-northeast of Sand Point (station ID 25630), 60 miles from the middle of Aleutians East Borough. It reported 282 of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Aleutians East Borough reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Aleutians East Borough wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station 1 mile east-northeast of Sand Point had 30.13 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 10, 89% of the usual 33.74 inches for the same days.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Aleutians East Borough?

From Jan 1 to Oct 10, the weather station 1 mile east-northeast of Sand Point had 48.42 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 30.13 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 279 days both years reported, 2026 had 19.20 inches less than 2025.
