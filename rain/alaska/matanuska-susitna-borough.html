How much rain has Matanuska-Susitna Borough, Alaska, had in 2026?

21.64 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lazy Mountain, Matanuska-Susitna Borough, through Oct 11. That is 7.56 inches more than a usual year to date (154% of usual).

Through Oct 11, the weather station in Lazy Mountain has 21.64 inches, 7.56 more than a usual year. July was the wettest month at 4.50 inches, and the biggest one-day reading came Sep 16 with 1.01. The gauge went 16 days with nothing from Mar 20 to Apr 4. On the days both years reported, this year is 4.62 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lazy Mountain (station ID 505464), 50 miles from the middle of Matanuska-Susitna Borough. It reported 282 of the 284 days from Jan 1 to Oct 11; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Matanuska-Susitna Borough reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
