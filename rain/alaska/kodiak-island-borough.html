How much rain has Kodiak Island Borough, Alaska, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Kodiak Airport, 48 miles from the middle of Kodiak Island Borough. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 323 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Kodiak Island Borough that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
