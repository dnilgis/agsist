How much rain has Kodiak Island Borough, Alaska, had in 2026?

53.30 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Kodiak Airport, Kodiak Island Borough, through Oct 13. That is 3.97 inches less than a usual year to date (93% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Kodiak Airport had 9.98 inches in June, the wettest month of the year. The biggest single day came early, 2.45 inches on Feb 3. The longest run with nothing in the gauge was only 6 days, Aug 29 to Sep 3. The station sits 8.08 inches behind last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Kodiak Airport (station ID 25501), 48 miles from the middle of Kodiak Island Borough. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Kodiak Island Borough that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
