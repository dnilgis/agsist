The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles southeast of Yakutat, 34 miles from the middle of Yakutat City and Borough. It reported 299 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Yakutat City and Borough in 2026?

4.93 inches on Sep 28, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 10. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Yakutat City and Borough?

From Jan 1 to Nov 10, the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles southeast of Yakutat had 140.03 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 7 days) and 114.01 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 15 days). On the 292 days both years reported, 2026 had 13.64 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Yakutat City and Borough had in 2026?

605 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at a weather station 2 miles from the rain gauge, 90% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (29 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Yakutat City and Borough in 2026?

Oct 8 (32°F or colder) at a weather station 2 miles from the rain gauge. Usual: Sep 23, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.
