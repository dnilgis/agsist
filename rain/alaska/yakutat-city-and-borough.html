How much rain has Yakutat City and Borough, Alaska, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles southeast of Yakutat, 34 miles from the middle of Yakutat City and Borough. It reported 323 of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
