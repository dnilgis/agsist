The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mullins (station MULLINS), 10 miles from the middle of Marion County. It reported 282 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Marion County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

July, with 5.42 inches at the NOAA station in Mullins, counting the months through Oct 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Marion County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the NOAA station in Mullins had 51.04 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 30.65 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 281 days both years reported, 2026 had 20.4 inches less.
