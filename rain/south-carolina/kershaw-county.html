The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Lugoff, 8 miles from the middle of Kershaw County. It reported 303 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 303 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
