The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Lugoff, 8 miles from the middle of Kershaw County. It reported 310 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
