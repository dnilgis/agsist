How much rain has Union County, South Carolina, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lockhart, 11 miles from the middle of Union County. It reported 318 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 318 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Union County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
