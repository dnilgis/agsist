How much rain has Orangeburg County, South Carolina, had in 2026?

23.39 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Orangeburg Municipal Airport, Orangeburg County, through Oct 13. That is 10.88 inches less than a usual year to date (68% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Orangeburg Municipal Airport had its wettest month in May with 4.30 inches. The biggest single day was Jul 12 at 2.11 inches. The gauge went 19 days in a row with nothing in it, from Apr 6 to Apr 24. On the days both years reported, this year is 11.61 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Orangeburg Municipal Airport (station ID 53854), 4 miles from the middle of Orangeburg County. It reported 273 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 273 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Orangeburg County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
