The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles south-southeast of Spartanburg, 5 miles from the middle of Spartanburg County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
