The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Columbia, 7 miles from the middle of Richland County. It reported 320 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
