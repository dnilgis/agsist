How much rain has Florence County, South Carolina, had in 2026?

29.50 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Florence Regional Airport, Florence County, through Oct 13. That is 8.02 inches less than a usual year to date (79% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Florence Regional Airport has a short year on its hands, 8.02 inches less than a usual year. August did the heavy lifting at 6.59 inches, and Aug 16 alone brought 2.61 inches, the biggest single day. The gauge went 19 days in a row with nothing at all from Apr 6 to Apr 24. It is 5.33 inches behind last year at this point too.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Florence Regional Airport (station ID 13744), 12 miles from the middle of Florence County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Florence County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
