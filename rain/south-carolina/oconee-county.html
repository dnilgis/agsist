The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Walhalla, less than a mile from the middle of Oconee County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Oconee County, South Carolina, had in 2026?

Is Oconee County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in Oconee County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Walhalla, and 2.61 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Oconee County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Oconee County in 2026?

2.40 inches on Nov 5, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Oconee County?

How many growing degree days has Oconee County had in 2026?

Has Oconee County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 21 stations inside Oconee County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
