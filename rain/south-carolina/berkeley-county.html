How much rain has Berkeley County, South Carolina, had in 2026?

34.57 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Berkeley County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 76% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Summerville gauge: 38.90 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge 9.3 miles north-northeast of Summerville. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.3 miles north-northeast of Summerville, 11 miles from the middle of Berkeley County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
