The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 8 miles northeast of Greenwood, 9 miles from the middle of Greenwood County. It reported 312 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
