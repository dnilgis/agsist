How much rain has Pine County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

20.47 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 6 miles west of Sandstone, Pine County, through Oct 12. That is 7.84 inches less than a usual year to date (72% of usual).

Through Oct 12, the weather station 6 miles west of Sandstone had April as its wettest month at 4.22 inches. The biggest single day came Apr 27 with 1.88 inches. Then the gauge sat empty for 11 days in a row, Apr 28 to May 8, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year is 1.19 inches more than 2025, though still 7.84 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles west of Sandstone (station ID 54932), 12 miles from the middle of Pine County. It reported 284 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Pine County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
