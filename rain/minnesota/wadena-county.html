The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-northeast of Wadena, 12 miles from the middle of Wadena County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
