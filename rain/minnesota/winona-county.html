The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in La Crescent, 25 miles from the middle of Winona County. It reported 315 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
