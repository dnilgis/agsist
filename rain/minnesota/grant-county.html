The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.1 miles east-southeast of Elbow Lake, 11 miles from the middle of Grant County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
