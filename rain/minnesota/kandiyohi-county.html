The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.0 miles east-southeast of Belgrade (gauge ID US1MNKD0001), 19 miles from the middle of Kandiyohi County. It reported 285 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 285 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 5 stations inside Kandiyohi County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Kandiyohi County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The volunteer rain gauge 8.0 miles east-southeast of Belgrade had 22.97 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 88% of the usual 26.13 inches for the same days.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Kandiyohi County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge 8.0 miles east-southeast of Belgrade had 32.74 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 12 days) and 22.97 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 273 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.03 inches less than 2025.
