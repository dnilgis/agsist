The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north-northwest of Woodbury, 9 miles from the middle of Washington County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
