How much rain has Jackson County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

24.38 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 2 miles northeast of Lakefield, Jackson County, through Oct 9. That is 2.68 inches less than a usual year to date (90% of usual).

Through Oct 9, the weather station 2 miles northeast of Lakefield had its wettest month in September at 5.71 inches. The biggest single day came Sep 20 with 2.85 inches. That still leaves the year 2.68 inches less than a usual year, but 1.30 inches more than last year at this point. The longest dry stretch ran 16 days, Feb 4 to Feb 19, with not even a trace in the gauge.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northeast of Lakefield (station ID 214453), 2 miles from the middle of Jackson County. It reported every one of the 282 days from Jan 1 to Oct 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Jackson County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
