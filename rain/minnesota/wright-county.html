The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile south-southwest of Maple Lake, 4 miles from the middle of Wright County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
