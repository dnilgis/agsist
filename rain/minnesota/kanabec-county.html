How much rain has Kanabec County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

19.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Mora, Kanabec County, through Oct 13. That is 6.93 inches less than a usual year to date (74% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Mora had June as its wettest month at 4.62 inches. The biggest single day was Jul 8, with 2.12 inches. On the days both years reported, this year is running 4.52 inches less than 2025, and the gauge sits 6.93 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mora (station ID 215615), 5 miles from the middle of Kanabec County. It reported 273 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 273 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Kanabec County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
