How much rain has Anoka County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

23.40 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Anoka County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 77% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

East Bethel gauge: 22.75 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles east of East Bethel. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles east of East Bethel, 7 miles from the middle of Anoka County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
