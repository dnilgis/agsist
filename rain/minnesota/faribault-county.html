How much rain has Faribault County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

33.94 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Faribault County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 106% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Winnebago station: 39.16 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in Winnebago. That is 7.20 inches more than a normal year to date (123% of normal).

This station is 17 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Winnebago, 12 miles from the middle of Faribault County. It reported 320 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
