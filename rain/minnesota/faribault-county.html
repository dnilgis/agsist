The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Winnebago, 12 miles from the middle of Faribault County. It reported 311 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
