The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile east of Artichoke Lake, 14 miles from the middle of Big Stone County. It reported 314 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
