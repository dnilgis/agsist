The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east of McGregor, 6 miles from the middle of Aitkin County. It reported every one of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
