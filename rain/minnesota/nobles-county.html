The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north-northeast of Worthington, 9 miles from the middle of Nobles County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
