The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.3 miles west-northwest of Rosemount (station ROSEMOUNT 3.3 WNW), 7 miles from the middle of Dakota County. It reported 284 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 27 stations inside Dakota County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.

Questions

How much rain has Dakota County, Minnesota had in 2026?

22.30 inches of rain so far in 2026 at the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.3 miles west-northwest of Rosemount, Dakota County, through Oct 12. That is 7.4 inches less than a usual year to date (75%).

Is Dakota County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.3 miles west-northwest of Rosemount had 22.30 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 12, 75% of the usual 29.70 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Dakota County in 2026?

June, with 4.46 inches at the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.3 miles west-northwest of Rosemount, counting the months through Oct 12.

What was the biggest rain in Dakota County in 2026?

1.74 inches on Aug 1, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Oct 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Dakota County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 12, the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 3.3 miles west-northwest of Rosemount had 33.21 inches in 2025 and 22.30 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 284 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.9 inches less.
