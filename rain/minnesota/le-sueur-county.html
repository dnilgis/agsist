The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles northeast of Montgomery, 11 miles from the middle of Le Sueur County. It reported 306 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
