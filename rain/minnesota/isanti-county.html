How much rain has Isanti County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

25.93 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Isanti County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 85% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cambridge, 3 miles from the middle of Isanti County. It reported 313 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Isanti County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Isanti County, about 25.93 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 85% of the normal 30.41 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Isanti County in 2026?

June, with 4.21 inches at the weather station in Cambridge, counting Jan 1 through Nov 24.

What was the biggest rain in Isanti County in 2026?

1.53 inches on Aug 16, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 24. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Isanti County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 24, the weather station in Cambridge had 25.95 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 21.75 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 15 days). On the 305 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.99 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Isanti County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
