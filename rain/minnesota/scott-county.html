The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south-southwest of Jordan, 5 miles from the middle of Scott County. It reported 318 of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
