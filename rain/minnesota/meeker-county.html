The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Litchfield, less than a mile from the middle of Meeker County. It reported 309 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 309 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Meeker County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
