The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of Park Rapids, 14 miles from the middle of Hubbard County. It reported 307 of the 311 days from Jan 1 to Nov 7; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
