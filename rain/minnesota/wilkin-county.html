The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.1 miles west-northwest of Rothsay, 10 miles from the middle of Wilkin County. It reported 325 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
