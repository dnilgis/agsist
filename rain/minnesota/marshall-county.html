How much rain has Marshall County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

22.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Marshall County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 104% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Goodridge station: 26.27 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 14, at the weather station 12 miles north-northwest of Goodridge. That is 4.23 inches more than a normal year to date (119% of normal).

This station is 15 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 12 miles north-northwest of Goodridge, 23 miles from the middle of Marshall County. It reported 309 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
