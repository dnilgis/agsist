How much rain has Marshall County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

23.45 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 12 miles north-northwest of Goodridge, Marshall County, through Oct 10. That is 3.14 inches more than a usual year to date (115% of usual).

Through Oct 10, the weather station 12 miles north-northwest of Goodridge had September as its wettest month at 6.15 inches. The biggest single day came Jul 24 with 2.77 inches. On the days both years reported, this year is 5.85 inches more than last year. The gauge also went 12 days in a row with nothing in it, from Feb 21 to Mar 4.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 12 miles north-northwest of Goodridge (station ID 04994), 23 miles from the middle of Marshall County. It reported 275 of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 275 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Marshall County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
