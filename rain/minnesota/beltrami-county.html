The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bemidji, 31 miles from the middle of Beltrami County. It reported 319 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
