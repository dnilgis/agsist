The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles north of Detroit Lakes, 9 miles from the middle of Becker County. It reported 313 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
