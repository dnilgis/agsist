How much rain has Becker County, Minnesota, had in 2026?

23.61 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Becker County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 95% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Detroit Lakes gauge: 23.87 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles north of Detroit Lakes. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles north of Detroit Lakes, 9 miles from the middle of Becker County. It reported 320 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Becker County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Becker County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Becker County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Becker County?
