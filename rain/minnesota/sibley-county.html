The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile north of Henderson, 16 miles from the middle of Sibley County. It reported 311 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
