The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Wellston Tippy, 8 miles from the middle of Manistee County. It reported 327 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
