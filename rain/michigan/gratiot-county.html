  • On record: 31st wettest by Nov 20 of 121 years on record here (1892 on); the last wetter year was 2020, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Alma, 7 miles from the middle of Gratiot County. It reported 321 of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
