The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southeast of West Branch, 7 miles from the middle of Ogemaw County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
