The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southeast of West Branch, 7 miles from the middle of Ogemaw County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 327 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
