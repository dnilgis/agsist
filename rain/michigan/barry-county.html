The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles south of Freeport, 10 miles from the middle of Barry County. It reported 319 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
