How much rain has Presque Isle County, Michigan, had in 2026?

30.61 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-southwest of Rogers City, Presque Isle County, through Oct 18. That is 5.84 inches more than a usual year to date (124% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the gauge less than a mile from Rogers City had its wettest month in April, with 5.66 inches. Then it went 13 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Sep 17 to Sep 29. On the days both years reported, this year has 5.79 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-southwest of Rogers City (gauge ID US1MIPI0001), 7 miles from the middle of Presque Isle County. It reported 273 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 273 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Presque Isle County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
