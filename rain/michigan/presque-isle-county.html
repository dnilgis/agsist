How much rain has Presque Isle County, Michigan, had in 2026?

36.99 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Presque Isle County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 128% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Rogers City gauge (credited below): 31.80 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-southwest of Rogers City. That is 2.44 inches more than a normal year to date (108% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This gauge is 20 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-southwest of Rogers City, 7 miles from the middle of Presque Isle County. It reported 320 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 320 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Presque Isle County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
