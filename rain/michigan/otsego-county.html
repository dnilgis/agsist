2026 so far in Otsego County

  • Wettest month: April, 9.31 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 3.56 inches on Apr 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 11 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, May 25 to Jun 4.
  • 2025 by the same date: 31.74 inches at the same station (it missed 2 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 9.88 inches more than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 41.93 inches, 148% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Otsego County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 19 points above this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 9 miles south-southwest of Gaylord, 10 miles from the middle of Otsego County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms
