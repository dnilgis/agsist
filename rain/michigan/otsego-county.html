How much rain has Otsego County, Michigan, had in 2026?

42.33 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Otsego County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 145% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Gaylord station: 42.03 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 9 miles south-southwest of Gaylord. That is 8.57 inches more than a normal year to date (126% of normal).

This station is 19 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 9 miles south-southwest of Gaylord, 10 miles from the middle of Otsego County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
