The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles northwest of Battle Creek, 16 miles from the middle of Calhoun County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
