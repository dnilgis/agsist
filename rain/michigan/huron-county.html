The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south-southeast of Harbor Beach, 19 miles from the middle of Huron County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
