The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Midland, 10 miles from the middle of Midland County. It reported 317 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
