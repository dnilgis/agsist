The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles west of Iron Mountain, 16 miles from the middle of Dickinson County. It reported 313 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the wettest month in Dickinson County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Dickinson County in 2026?

1.95 inches on Mar 16, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Dickinson County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles west of Iron Mountain had 26.49 inches in 2025 and 27.18 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 3 days). On the 313 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.70 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Dickinson County had in 2026?

2,518 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at a weather station 2 miles from the rain gauge, 114% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (22 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Dickinson County in 2026?

Sep 23 (32°F or colder) at a weather station 2 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Sep 22, the middle of 23 years, 1991-2020.
