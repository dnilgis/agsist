The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Iron Mtn-Kingsford, 19 miles from the middle of Dickinson County. It reported every one of the 304 days from Jan 1 to Oct 31. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Dickinson County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How many growing degree days has Dickinson County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 304 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Dickinson County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
