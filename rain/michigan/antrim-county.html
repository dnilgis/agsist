How much rain has Antrim County, Michigan, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-southeast of Mancelona, 8 miles from the middle of Antrim County. It reported 318 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
