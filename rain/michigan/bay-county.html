The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northeast of Bay City, 9 miles from the middle of Bay County. It reported 319 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
