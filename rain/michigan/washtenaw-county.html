How much rain has Washtenaw County, Michigan, had in 2026?

32.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Washtenaw County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 103% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.01 in, 7 days 0.01 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 pm Eastern, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Ann Arbor SE station (NOAA daily record): 31.61 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the Ann Arbor SE weather station in Ann Arbor. That is 3.12 inches less than a normal year to date (91% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ann Arbor, 8 miles from the middle of Washtenaw County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
