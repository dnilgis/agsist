The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Sault Ste Marie Sanderson Field, 16 miles from the middle of Chippewa County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Chippewa County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How much rain fell in Chippewa County in the last week?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Chippewa County?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 8 stations inside Chippewa County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
