The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south-southeast of Stevensville (gauge ID US1MIBN0002), 6 miles from the middle of Berrien County. It reported 285 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 285 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 5 stations inside Berrien County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Berrien County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south-southeast of Stevensville had 36.93 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 18, 113% of the usual 32.78 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Berrien County in 2026?

April, with 8.72 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south-southeast of Stevensville, counting Jan 1 through Oct 18.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Berrien County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 18, the volunteer rain gauge 1.7 miles south-southeast of Stevensville had 24.60 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 10 days) and 36.93 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 6 days). On the 275 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.44 inches more than 2025.
