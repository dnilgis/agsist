How much rain has St. Joseph County, Michigan, had in 2026?

40.14 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Three Rivers, St. Joseph County, through Oct 18. That is 7.41 inches more than a usual year to date (123% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Three Rivers had April as its wettest month at 8.23 inches. The biggest single day came Sep 5 with 2.90 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 11 days, Feb 7 to Feb 17, with nothing in the gauge. That puts this year 11.70 inches ahead of last year at the same point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Three Rivers (station ID 208184), 6 miles from the middle of St. Joseph County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside St. Joseph County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
