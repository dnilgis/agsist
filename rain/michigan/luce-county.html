The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Newberry-Luce County Road Commission, 8 miles from the middle of Luce County. It reported 306 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 23 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Luce County, Michigan, had in 2026?

Is Luce County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Luce County, about 27.74 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 90% of the normal 30.87 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Luce County in 2026?

April, with 3.69 inches at the weather station in Newberry-Luce County Road Commission, counting Jan 1 through Nov 25.

What was the biggest rain in Luce County in 2026?

1.26 inches on Jan 9, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 25. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Luce County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 25, the weather station in Newberry-Luce County Road Commission had 30.22 inches in 2025 and 22.59 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 23 days). On the 306 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.78 inches less than 2025.
