The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north-northeast of Adrian, 3 miles from the middle of Lenawee County. It reported 314 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
