The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.4 miles north-northwest of Gladwin, 13 miles from the middle of Gladwin County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
