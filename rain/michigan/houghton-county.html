The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Calumet-Tamarack, 26 miles from the middle of Houghton County. It reported 312 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Houghton County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Calumet-Tamarack had 36.37 inches in 2025 and 26.33 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 312 days both years reported, 2026 had 9.42 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Houghton County had in 2026?

1,962 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at a weather station 6 miles from the rain station, 110% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).
