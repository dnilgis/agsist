The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Calumet-Tamarack, 26 miles from the middle of Houghton County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How many growing degree days has Houghton County had in 2026?
