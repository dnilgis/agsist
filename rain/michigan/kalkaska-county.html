How much rain has Kalkaska County, Michigan, had in 2026?

32.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Kalkaska, Kalkaska County, through Oct 13. That is 6.84 inches more than a usual year to date (126% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Kalkaska had its year made in April, with 10.01 inches that month. The biggest single day was Apr 13 at 3.54 inches. Then it went 16 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from May 24 to Jun 8. On the days both years reported, that is 7.61 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Kalkaska (station ID 204257), 5 miles from the middle of Kalkaska County. It reported 283 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Kalkaska County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
