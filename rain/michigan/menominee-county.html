The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.1 miles northwest of Menominee, 30 miles from the middle of Menominee County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
