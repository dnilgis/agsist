The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles south-southeast of Stambaugh, 12 miles from the middle of Iron County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
