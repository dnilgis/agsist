The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Engadine Mdot, 24 miles from the middle of Mackinac County. It reported 291 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 30 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Mackinac County, Michigan, had in 2026?

27.32 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Mackinac County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Engadine Mdot station had 27.06 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17.

Is Mackinac County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Mackinac County, about 27.32 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 91% of the normal 29.96 inches for the same days.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Mackinac County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station in Engadine Mdot had 31.05 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 10 days) and 27.06 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 30 days). On the 281 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.76 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Mackinac County had in 2026?

2,237 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 17 at the weather station in Engadine Mdot.
