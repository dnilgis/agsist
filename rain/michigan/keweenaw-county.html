How much rain has Keweenaw County, Michigan, had in 2026?

17.96 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Copper Harbor, Keweenaw County, through Oct 13. That is 2.47 inches less than a usual year to date (88% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Copper Harbor, 29 miles from the middle of the county, had 17.96 inches, 2.47 inches less than a usual year. January was the wettest month at 3.17 inches. The biggest single day came Feb 18 with 1.06 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 9 days, May 26 to Jun 3, with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Copper Harbor (station ID 94899), 29 miles from the middle of Keweenaw County. It reported 279 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 279 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Keweenaw County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
