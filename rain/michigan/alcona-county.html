How much rain has Alcona County, Michigan, had in 2026?

37.99 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Alcona County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 133% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.03 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 pm Eastern, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Glennie station (NOAA daily record): 42.40 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station 2 miles southeast of Glennie. That is 13.94 inches more than a normal year to date (149% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 16 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles southeast of Glennie, 12 miles from the middle of Alcona County. It reported 322 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
