Northport station: 37.93 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 2 miles west of Northport. That is 8.77 inches more than a normal year to date (130% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Northport, 16 miles from the middle of Leelanau County. It reported 319 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
