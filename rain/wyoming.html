How much rain has Wyoming had in 2026? County by county

23 Wyoming counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (5 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 21 counties with a NOAA normal are above 79% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Campbell County: the driest by Nov 12 of 99 years on record (1905 on), 44% of normal.
  • Park County: the driest by Nov 10 of 47 years on record (1950 on), 52% of normal.
  • Lincoln County: 3rd wettest by Nov 12 of 30 years on record (1991 on), 149% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
