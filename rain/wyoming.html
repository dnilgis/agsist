How much rain has Wyoming had in 2026? County by county

23 Wyoming counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 10 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 21 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 76% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Campbell County: the driest by Nov 10 of 99 years on record (1905 on), 45% of usual.
  • Crook County: the driest by Oct 31 of 60 years on record (1959 on), 45% of usual.
  • Big Horn County: 3rd driest by Nov 8 of 42 years on record (1959 on), 62% of usual.
