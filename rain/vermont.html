Questions and answers

How much rain has Vermont had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 13 Vermont counties has a total in the table above. The most is 44.50 inches at Peru, Bennington County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Vermont wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 10 counties with a NOAA normal, 1 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 9 below and 0 at it. Half are above 89% and half below. 3 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Vermont rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 26, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 1 county with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
