How much rain has Alaska had in 2026? Borough by borough

22 Alaska boroughs and census areas have a weather station inside the borough or census area that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Oct 13 (5 stations stop earlier; each borough or census area page gives its own last day). Half the boroughs and census areas are above 103% of a usual year to date and half below.
