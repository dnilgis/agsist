Questions and answers

How much rain has Alaska had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 23 Alaska boroughs and census areas has a total in the table above. The most is 120.59 inches at Yakutat, Yakutat City and Borough. These are station totals, not a state average, and a borough or census area whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Alaska wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 21 boroughs and census areas with a NOAA normal, 13 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 7 below and 1 at it. Half are above 104% and half below. 2 boroughs and census areas in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Alaska rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 6 boroughs and census areas with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
