How much rain has Alaska had in 2026? Borough by borough

22 Alaska boroughs and census areas have a weather station inside the borough or census area that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Oct 25 (4 stations stop earlier; each borough or census area page gives its own last day). Half the 21 boroughs and census areas with a NOAA usual year are above 106% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Matanuska-Susitna Borough: 2nd wettest by Oct 23 of 39 years on record (1985 on), 161% of usual.
