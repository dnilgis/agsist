How much rain has Alaska had in 2026? Borough by borough

23 Alaska boroughs and census areas have a weather station inside the borough or census area that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 11 (6 stations stop earlier; each borough or census area page gives its own last day). Half the 21 boroughs and census areas with a NOAA usual year are above 114% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Aleutians West Census Area: 3rd wettest by Nov 11 of 108 years on record (1916 on), 135% of usual.

Boroughs and census areas without a page

These 6 boroughs and census areas have no station inside them that reported nearly every day in 2026 so far, so they get no page. A missing day is not a dry day, and a total with too many of them would read low. Next to each is the nearest borough or census area that has one.

  • Hoonah-Angoon Census Area nearest: Juneau City and Borough
  • Kusilvak Census Area nearest: Bethel Census Area
  • Petersburg Census Area nearest: Sitka City and Borough
  • Prince of Wales-Hyder Census Area nearest: Ketchikan Gateway Borough
  • Skagway Municipality nearest: Haines Borough
  • Wrangell City and Borough nearest: Ketchikan Gateway Borough

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily.

Rain and usual are in inches. Usual is NOAA’s daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. A station with no NOAA normal shows none. Each borough or census area page has the month by month table.
