How much rain has Alaska had in 2026? Borough by borough

23 Alaska boroughs and census areas have a weather station inside the borough or census area that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (6 stations stop earlier; each borough or census area page gives its own last day). Half the 21 boroughs and census areas with a NOAA normal are above 106% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Boroughs and census areas whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Aleutians West Census Area: 3rd wettest by Nov 12 of 108 years on record (1916 on), 135% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
