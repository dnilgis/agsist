How much rain has Nantucket County, Massachusetts, had in 2026?

17.95 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Nantucket Memorial Airport in Nantucket County, through Nov 11. That is 15.43 inches less than a usual year to date (54% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 36.14 inches, 98% of usual.

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 44 points of usual above this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Nantucket Memorial Airport, 2 miles from the middle of Nantucket County. It reported 305 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 305 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Nantucket County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
