How much rain has Hampden County, Massachusetts, had in 2026?

35.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 3 miles southwest of Westfield, Hampden County, through Oct 16. That is 5.30 inches less than a usual year to date (87% of usual).

Through Oct 11, the weather station 3 miles southwest of Westfield has 32.83 inches, 6.87 inches less than a usual year. July was the wettest month at 7.46 inches, and the biggest single day came Jul 30 with 2.44 inches. The gauge has caught nothing for 11 days in a row, Oct 1 to Oct 11, and that stretch is still going. On the days both years reported, this year is 4.55 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southwest of Westfield (station ID 199193), 9 miles from the middle of Hampden County. It reported 288 of the 289 days from Jan 1 to Oct 16; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Hampden County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
