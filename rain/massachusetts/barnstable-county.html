The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northeast of Barnstable, 1 mile from the middle of Barnstable County. It reported 323 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
