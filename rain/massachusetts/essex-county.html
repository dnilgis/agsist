How much rain has Essex County, Massachusetts, had in 2026?

34.03 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Essex County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 81% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles north-northeast of Danvers, 5 miles from the middle of Essex County. It reported 326 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Essex County?

How many growing degree days has Essex County had in 2026?
