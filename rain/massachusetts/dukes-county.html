The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Vineyard Haven, 4 miles from the middle of Dukes County. It reported every one of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Dukes County, Massachusetts, had in 2026?

Is Dukes County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Dukes County, about 46.02 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 114% of the normal 40.53 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Dukes County in the last week?

4.29 inches from Nov 7 to Nov 13 at the weather station in Vineyard Haven, and 4.77 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 15 to Nov 13. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Dukes County in 2026?

July, with 11.69 inches at the weather station in Vineyard Haven, counting Jan 1 through Nov 13.

What was the biggest rain in Dukes County in 2026?

4.22 inches on Sep 27, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 13. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Dukes County?

How many growing degree days has Dukes County had in 2026?
