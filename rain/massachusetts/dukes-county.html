How much rain has Dukes County, Massachusetts, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Vineyard Haven, 4 miles from the middle of Dukes County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
