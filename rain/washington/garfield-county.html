The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Pomeroy, 4 miles from the middle of Garfield County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
