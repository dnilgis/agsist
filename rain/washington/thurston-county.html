How much rain has Thurston County, Washington, had in 2026?

36.13 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Thurston County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 88% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 1.33 in, 7 days 2.20 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 pm Pacific, Nov 26.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Olympia gauge (credited below): 35.09 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 26, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.3 miles south of Olympia. That is 13.03 inches less than a normal year to date (73% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This gauge is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.3 miles south of Olympia, 7 miles from the middle of Thurston County. It reported 329 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 329 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 13 stations inside Thurston County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
