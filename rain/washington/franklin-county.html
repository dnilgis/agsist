How much rain has Franklin County, Washington, had in 2026?

3.93 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Pasco Tri Cities Airport, Franklin County, through Oct 13. That is 1.37 inches less than a usual year to date (74% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Pasco Tri Cities Airport has caught 3.93 inches, 1.37 inches less than a usual year. February was the wettest month at 1.38 inches, and the biggest single day was 0.50 inches on Apr 22. The gauge went 29 days in a row with nothing in it, from Jul 17 to Aug 14. That leaves this year 1.08 inches behind last year to the same day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Pasco Tri Cities Airport (station ID 24163), 21 miles from the middle of Franklin County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Franklin County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
