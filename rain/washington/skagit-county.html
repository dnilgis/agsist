The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Concrete Ppl Fish Station, 4 miles from the middle of Skagit County. It reported 314 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Skagit County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Skagit County?

Has Skagit County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Concrete Ppl Fish Station. Typical: Nov 10, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 22 stations inside Skagit County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
