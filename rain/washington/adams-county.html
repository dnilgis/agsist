The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles northeast of Lind, 1 mile from the middle of Adams County. It reported 326 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
