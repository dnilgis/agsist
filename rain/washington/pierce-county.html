The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 7.0 miles west-northwest of Gig Harbor (station GIG HARBOR 7.0 WNW), 36 miles from the middle of Pierce County. It reported 285 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12, every one. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 21 stations inside Pierce County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
