How much rain has Chelan County, Washington, had in 2026?

24.65 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Chelan County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 84% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.22 in, 7 days 0.60 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 pm Pacific, Nov 25.

One weather station’s gauge, the Plain station (NOAA daily record): 12.85 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station in Plain. That is 8.46 inches less than a normal year to date (60% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 24 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Plain, 8 miles from the middle of Chelan County. It reported 326 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 326 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Chelan County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
