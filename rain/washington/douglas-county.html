How much rain has Douglas County, Washington, had in 2026?

5.57 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Wenatchee Pangborn Memorial Airport, Douglas County, through Oct 13. That is 0.38 inches more than a usual year to date (107% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Wenatchee Pangborn Memorial Airport put most of its year in March. That month brought 1.39 inches, and 1.19 of that came on Mar 13 alone. From Jul 24 to Aug 14 the gauge went 22 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, 2026 is 2.65 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wenatchee Pangborn Memorial Airport (station ID 94239), 34 miles from the middle of Douglas County. It reported 282 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Douglas County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
