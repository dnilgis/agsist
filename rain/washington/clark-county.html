The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Vancouver Pearson Airport, 14 miles from the middle of Clark County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Clark County, Washington, had in 2026?

Is Clark County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Clark County, about 40.89 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 75% of the normal 54.34 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Clark County in the last week?

0.70 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the weather station at Vancouver Pearson Airport, and 4.14 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Clark County in 2026?

February, with 5.39 inches at the weather station at Vancouver Pearson Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Clark County?

How many growing degree days has Clark County had in 2026?

Has Clark County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 18 stations inside Clark County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
