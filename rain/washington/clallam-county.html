How much rain has Clallam County, Washington, had in 2026?

8.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Port Angeles Fairchild International Airport, Clallam County, through Oct 13. That is 8.13 inches less than a usual year to date (52% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Port Angeles Fairchild International Airport had 8.90 inches, 8.13 inches less than a usual year. March was the wettest month at 2.38 inches. The gauge went 25 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Aug 3 to Aug 27. The biggest single day came after that, 0.54 inches on Sep 25.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Port Angeles Fairchild International Airport (station ID 94266), 20 miles from the middle of Clallam County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 18 stations inside Clallam County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
