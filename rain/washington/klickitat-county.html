The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Dallesport Airport, 25 miles from the middle of Klickitat County. It reported 324 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
