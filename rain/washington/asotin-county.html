The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 14 miles southwest of Asotin, 2 miles from the middle of Asotin County. It reported 309 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
