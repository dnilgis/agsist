The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of St John, 13 miles from the middle of Whitman County. It reported 318 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
