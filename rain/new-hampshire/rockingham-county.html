How much rain has Rockingham County, New Hampshire, had in 2026?

32.31 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in West Hampstead, Rockingham County, through Oct 13. That is 3.50 inches less than a usual year to date (90% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in West Hampstead had its wettest month in July with 4.90 inches. The biggest single day came Aug 4 at 1.72 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 9 days from Jan 29 to Feb 6 with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. That still left this year 1.15 inches more than 2025 on the same days, but 3.50 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in West Hampstead (station ID 279278), 7 miles from the middle of Rockingham County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Rockingham County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
