How much rain has Sullivan County, New Hampshire, had in 2026?

32.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sullivan County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 80% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Newport station: 31.55 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 10, at the weather station in Newport. That is 4.25 inches less than a normal year to date (88% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Newport, 2 miles from the middle of Sullivan County. It reported 310 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 310 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
