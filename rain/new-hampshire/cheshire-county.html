The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jaffrey Silver Ranch Airpark, 15 miles from the middle of Cheshire County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
