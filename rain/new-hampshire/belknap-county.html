How much rain has Belknap County, New Hampshire, had in 2026?

32.24 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lakeport, Belknap County, through Oct 18. That is 3.24 inches less than a usual year to date (91% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Lakeport has caught 31.47 inches, 3.35 inches less than a usual year. May was the wettest month at 6.13 inches, and the biggest one-day reading was 2.17 inches on Aug 3. The gauge went 12 days in a row with nothing from Jul 3 to Jul 14. On the days both years reported, that is 3.49 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lakeport (station ID 274480), 3 miles from the middle of Belknap County. It reported 287 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 287 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Belknap County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Questions

How much rain has Belknap County, New Hampshire, had in 2026?

32.24 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lakeport, Belknap County, through Oct 18. That is 3.24 inches less than a usual year to date (91% of usual).

Is Belknap County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Lakeport had 32.24 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 18, 91% of the usual 35.48 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Belknap County in 2026?

May, with 6.13 inches at the weather station in Lakeport, counting Jan 1 through Oct 18.

What was the biggest rain in Belknap County in 2026?

2.17 inches on Aug 3, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Oct 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Belknap County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 18, the weather station in Lakeport had 28.38 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 32.24 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 286 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.18 inches more than 2025.
