How much rain has Limestone County, Texas, had in 2026?

30.53 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Limestone County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 84% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9.4 miles south-southeast of Mexia, 9 miles from the middle of Limestone County. It reported 313 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 313 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 6 stations inside Limestone County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
