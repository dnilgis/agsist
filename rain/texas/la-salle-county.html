The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Cotulla La Salle County Airport, 11 miles from the middle of La Salle County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
