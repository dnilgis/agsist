How much rain has Baylor County, Texas, had in 2026?

13.55 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Lake Kemp, Baylor County, through Oct 13. That is 7.35 inches less than a usual year to date (65% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Lake Kemp had 65 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Jul 21 to Sep 23. April was the wettest month at 3.67 inches, and the biggest single day was 1.90 inches on Jun 6. That leaves the year 11.13 inches less than last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lake Kemp (station ID 414982), 10 miles from the middle of Baylor County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Baylor County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
