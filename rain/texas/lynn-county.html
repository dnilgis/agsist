How much rain has Lynn County, Texas, had in 2026?

16.61 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Tahoka, Lynn County, through Oct 18. That is 1.51 inches less than a usual year to date (92% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Tahoka had June as its wettest month at 5.13 inches, and 2.20 of that came on Jun 3 alone. Before that, the gauge went 23 days in a row with nothing in it, from Feb 16 to Mar 10. On the days both years reported, this year has 2.46 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Tahoka (station ID 418818), 1 mile from the middle of Lynn County. It reported 290 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 290 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Lynn County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
