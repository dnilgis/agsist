The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles east-northeast of Gainesville, 9 miles from the middle of Cooke County. It reported 314 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Cooke County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Cooke County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. Averaged over Cooke County, about 35.44 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 101% of the normal 35.15 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Cooke County in 2026?

June, with 9.75 inches at the weather station 5 miles east-northeast of Gainesville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Cooke County in 2026?

2.35 inches on Apr 5, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Cooke County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station 5 miles east-northeast of Gainesville had 39.47 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 34.05 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 7 days). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.42 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Cooke County had in 2026?

5,016 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 17 at the weather station 5 miles east-northeast of Gainesville, 109% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).

Has Cooke County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 17: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station 5 miles east-northeast of Gainesville. Typical: Nov 18, the middle of 28 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
