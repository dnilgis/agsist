The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Madisonville, 2 miles from the middle of Madison County. It reported 290 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 29 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
