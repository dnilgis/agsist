How much rain has Coleman County, Texas, had in 2026?

21.27 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the Hords Creek Dam weather station in Hords Creek in Coleman County, through Nov 10. That is 3.45 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 28.18 inches, 112% of usual.

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 26 points of usual above this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hords Creek, 8 miles from the middle of Coleman County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 11, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 314 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
