How much rain has Coleman County, Texas, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Hords Creek, 8 miles from the middle of Coleman County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
