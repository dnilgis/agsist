The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northeast of Medina, 4 miles from the middle of Bandera County. It reported 309 of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
