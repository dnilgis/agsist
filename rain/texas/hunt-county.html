The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.5 miles east-northeast of Royse City, 14 miles from the middle of Hunt County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
