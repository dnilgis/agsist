The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Breckenridge, 4 miles from the middle of Stephens County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Stephens County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

About usual so far. The weather station in Breckenridge had 26.57 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 9, 99% of the usual 26.72 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Stephens County in the last week?

0.53 inches from Nov 3 to Nov 9 at the weather station in Breckenridge, and 4.42 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 11 to Nov 9. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Stephens County in 2026?

October, with 6.39 inches at the weather station in Breckenridge, counting Jan 1 through Nov 9.

What was the biggest rain in Stephens County in 2026?

3.34 inches on Oct 16, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 9. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Stephens County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 9, the weather station in Breckenridge had 27.58 inches in 2025 and 26.57 inches in 2026. 2026 had 1.01 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Stephens County had in 2026?

5,167 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 9 at a weather station 2 miles from the rain station.

Has Stephens County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 9: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 2 miles from the rain station.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Stephens County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
