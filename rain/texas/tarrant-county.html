How much rain has Tarrant County, Texas, had in 2026?

28.88 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Tarrant County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 87% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Richland Hills gauge: 24.89 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Richland Hills. That is 9.76 inches less than a normal year to date (72% of normal).

This gauge is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west of Richland Hills, 4 miles from the middle of Tarrant County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 322 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 31 stations inside Tarrant County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
