The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.4 miles south of Poolville, 12 miles from the middle of Parker County. It reported 319 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
