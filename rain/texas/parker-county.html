How much rain has Parker County, Texas, had in 2026?

28.38 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of Willow Park, Parker County, through Oct 13. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of Willow Park had 28.38 inches. April was the wettest month at 6.41 inches, and the biggest single day was 2.93 inches on Jun 7. The gauge then went 31 days with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Jul 26 to Aug 25. On the days both years reported, this year is about the same as 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of Willow Park (gauge ID US1TXPR0031), 8 miles from the middle of Parker County. It reported 283 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Parker County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
