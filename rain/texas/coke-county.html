How much rain has Coke County, Texas, had in 2026?

22.97 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Coke County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 110% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Bronte station: 20.81 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 14, at the weather station 11 miles north-northeast of Bronte. That is 0.35 inches more than a normal year to date (102% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 11 miles north-northeast of Bronte, 20 miles from the middle of Coke County. It reported 304 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Coke County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in Coke County in the last week?

0.98 inches from Nov 8 to Nov 14 at the weather station 11 miles north-northeast of Bronte, and 5.53 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 16 to Nov 14. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Coke County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Coke County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Coke County?

How many growing degree days has Coke County had in 2026?

Has Coke County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?
