The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-southeast of Crystal City, 13 miles from the middle of Zavala County. It reported 312 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
