How much rain has Zavala County, Texas, had in 2026?

21.51 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-southeast of Crystal City in Zavala County, through Nov 11. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with. Averaged over the whole county, about 27.56 inches, 137% of usual.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile east-southeast of Crystal City, 13 miles from the middle of Zavala County. It reported 308 of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
