The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Crane (station CRANE), 9 miles from the middle of Crane County. It reported 282 of the 282 days from Jan 1 to Oct 9, every one. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Crane County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
