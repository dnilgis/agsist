The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport, 3 miles from the middle of Rockwall County. It reported 319 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Rockwall County, Texas, had in 2026?

31.83 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Rockwall County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 89% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport station had 26.45 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17.

Is Rockwall County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Rockwall County, about 31.83 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 89% of the normal 35.81 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Rockwall County in the last week?

0.36 inches from Nov 11 to Nov 17 at the weather station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport, and 0.37 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 19 to Nov 17. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Rockwall County in 2026?

April, with 5.21 inches at the weather station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Rockwall County in 2026?

2.13 inches on Oct 1, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 17.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Rockwall County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport had 36.59 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 26.45 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 2 days). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 12.08 inches less than 2025.

Has Rockwall County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?
