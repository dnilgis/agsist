How much rain has Rockwall County, Texas, had in 2026?

26.08 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport, Rockwall County, through Oct 13. This station has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport had 26.08 inches. April was the wettest month at 5.21 inches, and the biggest single day came late, 2.13 inches on Oct 1. Before that the gauge went 24 days in a row with nothing, from Aug 10 to Sep 2. On the days both years reported, that is 9.45 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Ralph M Hall Rockwall Municipal Airport (station ID 00448), 3 miles from the middle of Rockwall County. It reported 284 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Rockwall County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
