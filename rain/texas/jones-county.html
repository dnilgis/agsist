The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-northeast of Merkel, 15 miles from the middle of Jones County. It reported 313 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
