The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-northeast of Merkel, 15 miles from the middle of Jones County. It reported 315 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Jones County, Texas, had in 2026?

18.52 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Jones County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 79% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Merkel gauge had 19.90 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18.

Is Jones County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Jones County, about 18.52 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 79% of the normal 23.48 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Jones County in 2026?

October, with 4.55 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-northeast of Merkel, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Jones County in 2026?

2.20 inches on Jun 6, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Jones County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-northeast of Merkel had 26.67 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 8 days) and 19.90 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 7 days). On the 307 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.00 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Jones County had in 2026?

5,051 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 18 at a weather station 29 miles from the rain gauge, 109% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

Has Jones County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 18: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 29 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Nov 15, the middle of 29 years, 1991-2020.
