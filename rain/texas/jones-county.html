How much rain has Jones County, Texas, had in 2026?

15.73 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-northeast of Merkel, Jones County, through Oct 13. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the volunteer rain gauge near Merkel had its wettest month in April at 3.53 inches. The biggest day was 2.20 inches on Jun 6. After that the gauge went 25 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace, from Jul 19 to Aug 12. On the days both years reported, this year is 9.18 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.5 miles east-northeast of Merkel (gauge ID US1TXJO0009), 15 miles from the middle of Jones County. It reported 272 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Jones County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
