How much rain has Kerr County, Texas, had in 2026?

39.25 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Kerr County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 144% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One weather station’s gauge, the Ingram station (NOAA daily record): 35.04 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station 2 miles northwest of Ingram. That is 7.81 inches more than a normal year to date (129% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of Ingram, 7 miles from the middle of Kerr County. It reported 328 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
