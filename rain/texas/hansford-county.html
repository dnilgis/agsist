The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Gruver, 3 miles from the middle of Hansford County. It reported 328 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
