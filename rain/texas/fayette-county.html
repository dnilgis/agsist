The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.6 miles southeast of Smithville (gauge ID US1TXFY0001), 10 miles from the middle of Fayette County. It reported 310 of the 311 days from Jan 1 to Nov 7; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 310 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 9 stations inside Fayette County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Fayette County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Fayette County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The volunteer rain gauge 6.6 miles southeast of Smithville had 30.54 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 7, 91% of the usual 33.69 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Fayette County in 2026?

June, with 5.64 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 6.6 miles southeast of Smithville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 7.

What was the biggest rain in Fayette County in 2026?

2.40 inches on Jul 15, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 7.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Fayette County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 7, the volunteer rain gauge 6.6 miles southeast of Smithville had 24.75 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 30.54 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 307 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.89 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Fayette County had in 2026?

5,321 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 7 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge.

Has Fayette County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 7: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge.
