How much rain has Terry County, Texas, had in 2026?

14.83 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Brownfield, Terry County, through Oct 18. That is 1.03 inches less than a usual year to date (94% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Brownfield had its wettest month in September with 5.56 inches. The biggest single day was Jun 3 at 2.20 inches. From Feb 15 to Mar 30 it went 44 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. That leaves the year 2.94 inches less than last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Brownfield (station ID 411128), 4 miles from the middle of Terry County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Terry County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
