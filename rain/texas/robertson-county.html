The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.5 miles east of Hearne, 11 miles from the middle of Robertson County. It reported 326 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Robertson County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?
