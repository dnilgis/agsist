The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wichita Falls Municipal Airport, 12 miles from the middle of Wichita County. It reported every one of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
