The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wichita Falls Municipal Airport (station ID 13966), 12 miles from the middle of Wichita County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 11 stations inside Wichita County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Questions

How much rain has Wichita County, Texas, had in 2026?

23.41 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Wichita Falls Municipal Airport, Wichita County, through Nov 8. That is 1.80 inches less than a usual year to date (93% of usual).

Is Wichita County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Wichita Falls Municipal Airport had 23.41 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 93% of the usual 25.21 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Wichita County in the last week?

1.28 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Wichita Falls Municipal Airport, and 5.94 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Wichita County in 2026?

October, with 5.54 inches at the weather station at Wichita Falls Municipal Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Wichita County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wichita County?

Has Wichita County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?
