The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles south-southwest of Brackettville, 4 miles from the middle of Kinney County. It reported 300 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 20 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
