How much rain has Gregg County, Texas, had in 2026?

20.91 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Longview E Tx Regional Airport, Gregg County, through Oct 13. That is 13.68 inches less than a usual year to date (60% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Longview E Tx Regional Airport has 15.69 inches less than it had by this day last year. April was the wettest month with 5.15 inches, and the biggest single day was Mar 11 at 2.63 inches. Before that, the gauge went 18 days in a row with nothing in it, from Feb 16 to Mar 5.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Longview E Tx Regional Airport (station ID 03901), 9 miles from the middle of Gregg County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Gregg County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
