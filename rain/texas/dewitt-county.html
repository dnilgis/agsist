2026 so far in DeWitt County

  • Wettest month: May, 7.86 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.51 inches on Oct 2.
  • Longest dry stretch: 16 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jan 26 to Feb 10.
  • 2025 by the same date: 22.95 inches at the same gauge (it missed 12 days). On the 292 days both years reported, 2026 had 6.08 inches more than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 27.97 inches, 89% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over DeWitt County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 10.7 miles north-northwest of Yorktown, 14 miles from the middle of DeWitt County. It reported 303 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 303 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 7 stations inside DeWitt County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
