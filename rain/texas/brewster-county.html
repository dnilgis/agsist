How much rain has Brewster County, Texas, had in 2026?

15.58 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Persimmon Gap, Brewster County, through Nov 8. That is 5.49 inches more than a usual year to date (154% of usual).

One station, not the whole county: averaged over Brewster County, rain is 113% of usual, against 154% at this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

2026 so far in Brewster County

  • Wettest month: July, 6.62 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.96 inches on Apr 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 48 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 12 to Mar 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 6.38 inches at the same station. 2026 had 9.20 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 3rd wettest by Nov 8 of 35 years on record here (1983 on); the last wetter year was 2004, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 13.30 inches, 113% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Brewster County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 41 points below this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Persimmon Gap, 11 miles from the middle of Brewster County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 13 stations inside Brewster County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
