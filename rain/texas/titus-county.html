How much rain has Titus County, Texas, had in 2026?

28.85 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Titus County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 69% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Mount Pleasant station: 22.75 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Mount Pleasant. That is 19.53 inches less than a normal year to date (54% of normal).

This station is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mount Pleasant, 4 miles from the middle of Titus County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
