The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.3 miles southeast of San Ygnacio, 15 miles from the middle of Zapata County. It reported 325 of the 326 days from Jan 1 to Nov 22; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
