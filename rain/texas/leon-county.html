The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.2 miles southeast of Buffalo, 9 miles from the middle of Leon County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Leon County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Leon County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Leon County, about 37.01 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 97% of the normal 38.03 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Leon County in the last week?

2.02 inches from Nov 10 to Nov 16 at the volunteer rain gauge 3.2 miles southeast of Buffalo, and 3.69 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 18 to Nov 16. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Leon County in 2026?

June, with 10.45 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 3.2 miles southeast of Buffalo, counting Jan 1 through Nov 16.

What was the biggest rain in Leon County in 2026?

6.88 inches on Jun 20, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 16. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Leon County?
