The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles south of Beeville, 10 miles from the middle of Bee County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Bee County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Bee County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. Averaged over Bee County, about 29.03 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 23, 101% of the normal 28.87 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Bee County in the last week?

0.11 inches from Nov 13 to Nov 19 at the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles south of Beeville, and 4.37 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 21 to Nov 19. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Bee County in 2026?

June, with 10.79 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 9 miles south of Beeville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 19.

What was the biggest rain in Bee County in 2026?

2.25 inches on Jun 17, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 19. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Bee County?

How many growing degree days has Bee County had in 2026?

5,600 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 23 at a weather station 13 miles from the rain gauge, 104% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Has Bee County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 23: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 13 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Dec 8, the middle of 24 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 24, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 323 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Bee County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
