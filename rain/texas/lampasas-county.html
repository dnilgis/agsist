The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.4 miles north-northwest of Kempner, 10 miles from the middle of Lampasas County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
