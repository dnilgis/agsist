How much rain has Victoria County, Texas, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Victoria Regional Airport, 5 miles from the middle of Victoria County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 330 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 12 stations inside Victoria County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
