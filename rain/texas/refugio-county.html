The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.6 miles south-southeast of Woodsboro, 13 miles from the middle of Refugio County. It reported 296 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 24 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
