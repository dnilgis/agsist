The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of Ballinger, 6 miles from the middle of Runnels County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
