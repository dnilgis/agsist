The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Roscoe, 12 miles from the middle of Nolan County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
