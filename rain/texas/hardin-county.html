The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lumberton, 14 miles from the middle of Hardin County. It reported 310 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 10 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
