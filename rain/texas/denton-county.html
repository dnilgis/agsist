How much rain has Denton County, Texas, had in 2026?

29.40 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Denton County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 85% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Shady Shores gauge: 25.39 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 3.9 miles north of Shady Shores. That is 11.12 inches less than a normal year to date (70% of normal).

This gauge is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.9 miles north of Shady Shores, 4 miles from the middle of Denton County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Denton County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Denton County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Denton County, about 29.40 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 85% of the normal 34.59 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Denton County in the last week?

0.94 inches from Nov 11 to Nov 17 at the volunteer rain gauge 3.9 miles north of Shady Shores, and 1.78 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 19 to Nov 17. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Denton County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Denton County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Denton County?

How many growing degree days has Denton County had in 2026?

5,241 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 17 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge, 109% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (22 years).

Has Denton County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 17: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge. Typical: Nov 10, the middle of 25 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 321 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 24 stations inside Denton County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
