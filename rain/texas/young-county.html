How much rain has Young County, Texas, had in 2026?

14.23 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Graham Municipal Airport, Young County, through Oct 18. This station has no NOAA normal, so there is no usual year to compare it with.

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Graham Municipal Airport has caught 10.78 inches. May was the wettest month at 2.72 inches, and the biggest single day came Sep 30 with 1.70 inches. The gauge stayed dry 35 days in a row from Jul 22 to Aug 25. On the days both years reported, this year is 3.32 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Graham Municipal Airport (station ID 53965), 9 miles from the middle of Young County. It reported 286 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Young County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
