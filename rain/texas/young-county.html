The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Graham Municipal Airport, 9 miles from the middle of Young County. It reported 316 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

Is Young County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Young County, about 29.31 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 104% of the normal 28.20 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Young County in 2026?

October, with 5.18 inches at the weather station at Graham Municipal Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Young County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Young County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station at Graham Municipal Airport had 9.08 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 9 days) and 15.81 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 6 days). On the 308 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.04 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Young County had in 2026?

Has Young County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?
