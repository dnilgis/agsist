The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles southeast of Dalhart, 16 miles from the middle of Hartley County. It reported every one of the 308 days from Jan 1 to Nov 4. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
