The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Freer, 15 miles from the middle of Duval County. It reported 320 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
