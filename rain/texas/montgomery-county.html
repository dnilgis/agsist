The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Conroe, 2 miles from the middle of Montgomery County. It reported every one of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Montgomery County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Montgomery County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Montgomery County, about 42.21 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 94% of the normal 45.11 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Montgomery County in the last week?

1.14 inches from Nov 13 to Nov 19 at the weather station in Conroe, and 1.39 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 21 to Nov 19. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Montgomery County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Montgomery County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Montgomery County?

How many growing degree days has Montgomery County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 323 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 26 stations inside Montgomery County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
