How much rain has Gillespie County, Texas, had in 2026?

37.23 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Gillespie County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 135% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Harper station: 44.14 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 3 miles east-northeast of Harper. That is 18.22 inches more than a normal year to date (170% of normal).

This station is 35 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles east-northeast of Harper, 15 miles from the middle of Gillespie County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
