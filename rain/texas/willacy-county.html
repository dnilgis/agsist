The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Raymondville, 7 miles from the middle of Willacy County. It reported 291 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 25 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Willacy County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is Willacy County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Willacy County, about 29.20 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 124% of the normal 23.50 inches for the same days.

What was the biggest rain in Willacy County in 2026?

3.50 inches on Oct 4, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Willacy County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Raymondville had 36.65 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 30 days) and 29.88 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 25 days). On the 269 days both years reported, 2026 had 10.28 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Willacy County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
