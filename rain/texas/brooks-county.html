How much rain has Brooks County, Texas, had in 2026?

29.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Brooks County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 127% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Falfurrias gauge (credited below): 26.74 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the volunteer rain gauge 8.9 miles south-southwest of Falfurrias. That is 0.90 inches more than a normal year to date (103% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This gauge is 24 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.9 miles south-southwest of Falfurrias, 5 miles from the middle of Brooks County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 327 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Brooks County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
