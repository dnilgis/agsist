How much rain has Matagorda County, Texas, had in 2026?

29.82 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Palacios Municipal Airport, Matagorda County, through Oct 18. That is 4.83 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Palacios Municipal Airport had May as its wettest month at 10.68 inches. The biggest single day came Oct 2 with 3.22 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 19 days from Mar 16 to Apr 3 with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, 2026 is 5.03 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Palacios Municipal Airport (station ID 12935), 19 miles from the middle of Matagorda County. It reported 289 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 289 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Matagorda County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
