How much rain has Hardeman County, Texas, had in 2026?

25.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of Quanah in Hardeman County, through Nov 16. That is 0.87 inches more than a normal year to date (104% of normal). Averaged over the whole county, about 18.86 inches, 78% of normal.

One gauge, not the whole county: the county average is 26 points of normal below this gauge. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northwest of Quanah, less than a mile from the middle of Hardeman County. It reported 313 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 17, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 313 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Hardeman County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
