How much rain has Atascosa County, Texas, had in 2026?

27.18 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Atascosa County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 108% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Poteet gauge: 25.01 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles west of Poteet. That is 1.73 inches less than a normal year to date (94% of normal).

The county average and this gauge disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this gauge drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.9 miles west of Poteet, 13 miles from the middle of Atascosa County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 321 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Atascosa County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
