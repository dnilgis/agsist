The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cope Ranch, 18 miles from the middle of Reagan County. It reported 314 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
