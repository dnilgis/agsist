The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cope Ranch, 18 miles from the middle of Reagan County. It reported 306 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
