The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Cross, 17 miles from the middle of McMullen County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has McMullen County, Texas, had in 2026?

Is McMullen County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in McMullen County in the last week?

3.08 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Cross, and 4.42 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the biggest rain in McMullen County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in McMullen County?

How many growing degree days has McMullen County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside McMullen County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
