How much rain has Scurry County, Texas, had in 2026?

13.82 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Snyder, Scurry County, through Oct 18. That is 4.29 inches less than a usual year to date (76% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Snyder had its wettest month in July at 4.72 inches. The biggest single day came early, 2.10 inches on Apr 2. Then from Jul 31 to Sep 4 the gauge went 36 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, this year is 1.08 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Snyder (station ID 418433), 3 miles from the middle of Scurry County. It reported 279 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 279 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Scurry County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
