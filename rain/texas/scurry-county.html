How much rain has Scurry County, Texas, had in 2026?

15.25 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Snyder in Scurry County, through Nov 10. That is 4.06 inches less than a usual year to date (79% of usual). Averaged over the whole county, about 14.83 inches, 76% of usual.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Snyder, 3 miles from the middle of Scurry County. It reported 302 of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Scurry County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

How much rain fell in Scurry County in the last week?

0.13 inches from Nov 4 to Nov 10 at the weather station in Snyder, and 3.36 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 12 to Nov 10. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Scurry County in 2026?

July, with 4.72 inches at the weather station in Snyder, counting Jan 1 through Nov 10.

What was the biggest rain in Scurry County in 2026?

2.10 inches on Apr 2, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 10.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Scurry County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 10, the weather station in Snyder had 14.44 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 15 days) and 15.25 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 12 days). On the 287 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.53 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Scurry County had in 2026?

4,774 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 10 at the weather station in Snyder, 111% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (23 years).

Has Scurry County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 10: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Snyder. Usual: Nov 16, the middle of 20 years, 1991-2020.
