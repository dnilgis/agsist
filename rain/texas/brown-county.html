2026 so far in Brown County

  • Wettest month: October, 5.73 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.74 inches on Oct 16.
  • Longest dry stretch: 40 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jul 19 to Aug 27.
  • 2025 by the same date: 30.57 inches at the same gauge (it missed 3 days). On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.32 inches less than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 31.28 inches, 117% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Brown County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 20 points above this gauge: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.7 miles east-northeast of Brownwood, 8 miles from the middle of Brown County. It reported 312 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 312 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Brown County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
