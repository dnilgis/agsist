How much rain has Dimmit County, Texas, had in 2026?

16.86 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 3 miles south of Carrizo Springs, Dimmit County, through Oct 13. That is 0.44 inches more than a usual year to date (103% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station 3 miles south of Carrizo Springs had a lot of its year come in May, with 6.35 inches that month and the biggest single day at 1.95 inches on May 1. Then it went 34 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Jul 25 to Aug 27. The year sits at 16.86 inches, 2.82 inches more than 2025 had by this same day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles south of Carrizo Springs (station ID 411486), 8 miles from the middle of Dimmit County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Dimmit County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
