The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in McCook, 12 miles from the middle of Hidalgo County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Hidalgo County, Texas, had in 2026?

23.72 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Hidalgo County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 109% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The McCook station had 20.13 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17 (110% of normal).

Is Hidalgo County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Hidalgo County, about 23.72 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 109% of the normal 21.71 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Hidalgo County in the last week?

0.01 inches from Nov 11 to Nov 17 at the weather station in McCook, and 1.51 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 19 to Nov 17. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Hidalgo County in 2026?

May, with 5.14 inches at the weather station in McCook, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Hidalgo County in 2026?

3.09 inches on Oct 2, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Hidalgo County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station in McCook had 22.34 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 20.13 inches in 2026. On the 317 days both years reported, 2026 had 2.72 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Hidalgo County had in 2026?

5,694 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 17 at the weather station in McCook.

Has Hidalgo County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 17: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in McCook.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Hidalgo County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
