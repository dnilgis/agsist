The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in McCook, 12 miles from the middle of Hidalgo County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Hidalgo County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Hidalgo County, about 24.49 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 25, 111% of the normal 22.02 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Hidalgo County in the last week?

1.12 inches from Nov 19 to Nov 25 at the weather station in McCook, and 2.63 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 27 to Nov 25. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Hidalgo County in 2026?

May, with 5.14 inches at the weather station in McCook, counting Jan 1 through Nov 25.

What was the biggest rain in Hidalgo County in 2026?

3.09 inches on Oct 2, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 25. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Hidalgo County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 25, the weather station in McCook had 22.34 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 21.25 inches in 2026. On the 325 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.60 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Hidalgo County had in 2026?

5,804 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 25 at the weather station in McCook.

Has Hidalgo County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 25: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in McCook.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Hidalgo County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
