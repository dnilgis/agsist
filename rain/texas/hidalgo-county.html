The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Mccook, 12 miles from the middle of Hidalgo County. It reported 315 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Hidalgo County, Texas, had in 2026?

20.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Mccook in Hidalgo County, through Nov 12. That is 2.16 inches more than a usual year to date (112% of usual).

Is Hidalgo County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Mccook had 20.12 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 112% of the usual 17.96 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Hidalgo County in the last week?

1.50 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Mccook, and 1.50 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Hidalgo County in 2026?

May, with 5.14 inches at the weather station in Mccook, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Hidalgo County in 2026?

3.09 inches on Oct 2, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Hidalgo County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Mccook had 22.34 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 4 days) and 20.12 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 1 day). On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.36 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Hidalgo County had in 2026?

5,614 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Mccook.

Has Hidalgo County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Mccook.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 315 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Hidalgo County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
