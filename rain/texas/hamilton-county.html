The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Hamilton Municipal Airport (station ID 00357), 4 miles from the middle of Hamilton County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

This station has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Hamilton County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
