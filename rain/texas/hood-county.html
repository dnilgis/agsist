The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.5 miles north-northwest of Granbury, 4 miles from the middle of Hood County. It reported 316 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Hood County, Texas, had in 2026?

28.69 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Hood County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 89% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Granbury gauge had 27.31 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16 (90% of normal).

Is Hood County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Hood County, about 28.69 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 89% of the normal 32.13 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Hood County in 2026?

October, with 6.56 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 3.5 miles north-northwest of Granbury, counting Jan 1 through Nov 16.

What was the biggest rain in Hood County in 2026?

3.00 inches on Jan 24, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 16. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Hood County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 16, the volunteer rain gauge 3.5 miles north-northwest of Granbury had 34.81 inches in 2025 and 27.31 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 316 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.50 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Hood County had in 2026?

5,240 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 18 at a weather station 3 miles from the rain gauge.

Has Hood County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 18: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 3 miles from the rain gauge.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 316 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Hood County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
