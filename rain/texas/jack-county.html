The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jacksboro, 2 miles from the middle of Jack County. It reported 318 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 318 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
