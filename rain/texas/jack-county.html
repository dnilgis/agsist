How much rain has Jack County, Texas, had in 2026?

29.55 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Jack County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 96% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Jacksboro, 2 miles from the middle of Jack County. It reported 325 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
