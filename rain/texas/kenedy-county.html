The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles southeast of Armstrong, 6 miles from the middle of Kenedy County. It reported 317 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
