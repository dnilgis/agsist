The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Clarendon (station ID 23072), 2 miles from the middle of Donley County. It reported 274 of the 287 days from Jan 1 to Oct 14; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 274 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Donley County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
