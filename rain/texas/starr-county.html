The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rio Grande City, 14 miles from the middle of Starr County. It reported 311 of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26; the 19 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
