How much rain has Guadalupe County, Texas, had in 2026?

31.51 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Guadalupe County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 103% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.03 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 pm Central, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Seguin station (NOAA daily record): 29.76 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station 1 mile south-southwest of Seguin. That is 1.13 inches less than a normal year to date (96% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south-southwest of Seguin, 3 miles from the middle of Guadalupe County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
