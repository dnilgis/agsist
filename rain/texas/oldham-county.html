The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles north of Adrian, 5 miles from the middle of Oldham County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 319 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
