The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 9 miles north-northeast of Stephenville, 10 miles from the middle of Erath County. It reported 318 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
