How much rain has Webb County, Texas, had in 2026?

15.84 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Laredo International Airport, Webb County, through Oct 13. That is 2.48 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Laredo International Airport caught 5.52 inches in June, the wettest month of the year. Most of that came on Jun 15, the biggest single day at 3.53 inches. Before that, the gauge went 22 days in a row with nothing, from Feb 14 to Mar 7. On the days both years reported, that is 0.78 inches more than last year, and still 2.48 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Laredo International Airport (station ID 12907), 18 miles from the middle of Webb County. It reported 283 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Webb County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
