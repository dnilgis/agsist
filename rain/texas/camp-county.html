The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.1 miles west of Pittsburg, 3 miles from the middle of Camp County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
