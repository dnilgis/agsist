The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Welder W'life Foundation, 10 miles from the middle of San Patricio County. It reported 302 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 21 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
