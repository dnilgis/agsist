The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Welder W'life Foundation, 10 miles from the middle of San Patricio County. It reported 298 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has San Patricio County, Texas, had in 2026?

42.15 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Welder W'life Foundation in San Patricio County, through Nov 12. That is 10.87 inches more than a normal year to date (135% of normal).

Is San Patricio County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in Welder W'life Foundation had 42.15 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 135% of the normal 31.28 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in San Patricio County in 2026?

May, with 16.59 inches at the weather station in Welder W'life Foundation, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in San Patricio County in 2026?

6.00 inches on May 27, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 12. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in San Patricio County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 12, the weather station in Welder W'life Foundation had 28.10 inches in 2025 and 42.15 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 18 days). On the 298 days both years reported, 2026 had 14.08 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has San Patricio County had in 2026?

5,746 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station in Welder W'life Foundation, 106% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (25 years).

Has San Patricio County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station in Welder W'life Foundation. Typical: Dec 6, the middle of 20 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 298 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside San Patricio County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
