Questions and answers

How much rain has Rhode Island had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 5 Rhode Island counties has a total in the table above. The most is 52.52 inches at Bristol, Bristol County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Rhode Island wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 3 counties with a NOAA normal, 2 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 1 below and 0 at it. Half are above 110% and half below. 2 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Rhode Island rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 27, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
