How much rain has Rhode Island had in 2026? County by county

5 Rhode Island counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (1 station stops earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 3 counties with a NOAA normal are above 110% of a normal year to date and half below.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
