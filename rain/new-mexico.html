Questions and answers

How much rain has New Mexico had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 33 New Mexico counties has a total in the table above. The most is 24.72 inches at Nogal, Lincoln County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is New Mexico wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 26 counties with a NOAA normal, 23 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 3 below and 0 at it. Half are above 124% and half below. 7 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the New Mexico rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
