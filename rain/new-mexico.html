How much rain has New Mexico had in 2026? County by county

33 New Mexico counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 27 counties with a NOAA normal are above 121% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Doña Ana County: 3rd wettest by Nov 12 of 135 years on record (1892 on), 175% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
