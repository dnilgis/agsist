The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Toledo Express Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Lucas County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Lucas County, Ohio, had in 2026?

Is Lucas County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station at Toledo Express Airport had 32.20 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 104% of the normal 30.94 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Lucas County in the last week?

0.73 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Toledo Express Airport, and 2.05 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Lucas County in 2026?

April, with 6.25 inches at the weather station at Toledo Express Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Lucas County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Lucas County?

How many growing degree days has Lucas County had in 2026?

3,320 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Toledo Express Airport, 108% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has Lucas County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Toledo Express Airport. Typical: Oct 17, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 7 stations inside Lucas County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
