The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Toledo Express Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Lucas County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
