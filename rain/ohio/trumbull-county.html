The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Youngstown-Warren Regional Airport, 6 miles from the middle of Trumbull County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
