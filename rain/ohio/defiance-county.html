The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Defiance, 6 miles from the middle of Defiance County. It reported 311 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
