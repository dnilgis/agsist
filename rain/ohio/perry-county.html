The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of New Lexington, 1 mile from the middle of Perry County. It reported 315 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
