The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of New Lexington, 1 mile from the middle of Perry County. It reported 321 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
