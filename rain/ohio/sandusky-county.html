The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.2 miles east-northeast of Fremont (gauge ID US1OHSD0002), 3 miles from the middle of Sandusky County. It reported 310 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 310 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Sandusky County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

Is Sandusky County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

What was the wettest month in Sandusky County in 2026?

April, with 7.19 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 2.2 miles east-northeast of Fremont, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Sandusky County in 2026?

2.07 inches on Apr 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 8. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Sandusky County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the volunteer rain gauge 2.2 miles east-northeast of Fremont had 37.65 inches in 2025 and 33.91 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 2 days). On the 310 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.36 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Sandusky County had in 2026?

3,189 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 6 at a weather station 3 miles from the rain gauge, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (22 years).

Has Sandusky County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 6: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 3 miles from the rain gauge. Usual: Oct 19, the middle of 24 years, 1991-2020.
