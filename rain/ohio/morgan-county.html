The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles northeast of Glouster, 9 miles from the middle of Morgan County. It reported 322 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the biggest rain in Morgan County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Morgan County?
