The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.7 miles northeast of Glouster, 9 miles from the middle of Morgan County. It reported 313 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
