The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Steubenville, 7 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported 321 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
