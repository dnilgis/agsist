Versailles gauge: 38.95 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles west-southwest of Versailles. That is 2.42 inches more than a normal year to date (107% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.6 miles west-southwest of Versailles, 8 miles from the middle of Darke County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 321 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 8 stations inside Darke County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
