2026 so far in Auglaize County

  • Wettest month: August, 8.65 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.31 inches on Aug 12.
  • Longest dry stretch: 13 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jan 27 to Feb 8.
  • 2025 by the same date: 23.80 inches at the same station. On the 303 days both years reported, 2026 had 14.07 inches more than 2025.
  • On 8 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • On record: 11th wettest by Nov 8 of 76 years on record here (1938 on); the last wetter year was 2020, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 42.25 inches, 121% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Auglaize County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles west of St. Marys, 11 miles from the middle of Auglaize County. It reported 303 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 303 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Auglaize County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
