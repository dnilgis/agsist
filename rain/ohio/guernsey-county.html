The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.7 miles west-southwest of Cambridge, 10 miles from the middle of Guernsey County. It reported 316 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
