How much rain has Fairfield County, Ohio, had in 2026?

38.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Lancaster Fairfield County Airport, Fairfield County, through Oct 13. That is 5.64 inches more than a usual year to date (117% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Lancaster Fairfield County Airport had August as its wettest month at 9.52 inches. The biggest single day came Aug 20 with 2.96 inches. Then the gauge sat empty 11 days in a row, Aug 24 to Sep 3. On the days both years reported, this year has 4.99 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Lancaster Fairfield County Airport (station ID 53844), 2 miles from the middle of Fairfield County. It reported 284 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Fairfield County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
