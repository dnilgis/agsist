How much rain has Hocking County, Ohio, had in 2026?

44.29 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Logan, Hocking County, through Oct 18. That is 8.37 inches more than a usual year to date (123% of usual).

Through Oct 12, the weather station in Logan had August as its wettest month at 10.13 inches. The biggest single day came Aug 12 with 2.20 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 9 days, May 29 to Jun 6, with not even a trace in the gauge. That puts the station 5.25 inches ahead of last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Logan (station ID 334672), 5 miles from the middle of Hocking County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Hocking County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
