How much rain has Allen County, Ohio, had in 2026?

40.89 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Allen County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 114% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lima, 4 miles from the middle of Allen County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
