How much rain has Allen County, Ohio, had in 2026?

40.85 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Allen County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 116% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Lima Wwtp station: 41.30 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the Lima Wwtp weather station in Lima. That is 4.47 inches more than a normal year to date (112% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lima, 4 miles from the middle of Allen County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
