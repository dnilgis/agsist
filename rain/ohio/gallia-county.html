How much rain has Gallia County, Ohio, had in 2026?

39.43 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Gallipolis, Gallia County, through Oct 18. That is 4.93 inches more than a usual year to date (114% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the station in Gallipolis had its wettest month in September, with 8.12 inches. Sep 10 alone brought 4.96 inches, the biggest rain so far. Before that, the gauge caught nothing for 12 days in a row, Aug 24 to Sep 4. On the days both years reported, this year is 0.31 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Gallipolis (station ID 333029), 7 miles from the middle of Gallia County. It reported 270 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 21 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 270 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Gallia County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
