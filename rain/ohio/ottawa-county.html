How much rain has Ottawa County, Ohio, had in 2026?

36.76 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Ottawa County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 117% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Put-In-Bay gauge: 33.08 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 14, at the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles south-southwest of Put-In-Bay. That is 0.13 inches less than a normal year to date (100% of normal).

This gauge is 17 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.1 miles south-southwest of Put-In-Bay, 17 miles from the middle of Ottawa County. It reported 316 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 316 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Ottawa County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
