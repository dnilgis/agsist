The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Cuyahoga County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Cuyahoga County, Ohio, had in 2026?

30.65 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport, Cuyahoga County, through Nov 8. That is 4.96 inches less than a usual year to date (86% of usual).

Is Cuyahoga County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport had 30.65 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 86% of the usual 35.61 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Cuyahoga County in the last week?

0.81 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport, and 2.43 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Cuyahoga County in 2026?

August, with 4.86 inches at the weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Cuyahoga County in 2026?

1.65 inches on Mar 31, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Cuyahoga County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport had 34.78 inches in 2025 and 30.65 inches in 2026. 2026 had 4.13 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Cuyahoga County had in 2026?

3,242 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport, 106% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has Cuyahoga County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Cleveland-Hopkins International Airport. Usual: Oct 31, the middle of 30 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Cuyahoga County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
