The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wooster Wayne County Airport, 3 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported 317 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
