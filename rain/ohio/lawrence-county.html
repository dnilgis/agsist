How much rain has Lawrence County, Ohio, had in 2026?

37.29 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lawrence County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 95% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

South Point station: 32.71 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 12, at the weather station in South Point. That is 10.26 inches less than a normal year to date (76% of normal).

This station is 19 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in South Point, 12 miles from the middle of Lawrence County. It reported 313 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
