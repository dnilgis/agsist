How much rain has Ashland County, Ohio, had in 2026?

38.49 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Ashland County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 107% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Nova gauge: 37.83 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the volunteer rain gauge 3.8 miles west-southwest of Nova. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.8 miles west-southwest of Nova, 13 miles from the middle of Ashland County. It reported 316 of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
