How much rain has Richland County, Ohio, had in 2026?

41.99 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Richland County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 113% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.56 in, 7 days 0.61 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 pm Eastern, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Mansfield Lahm Regional Airport station (NOAA daily record): 36.82 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station at Mansfield Lahm Regional Airport. That is 2.14 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Mansfield Lahm Regional Airport, 3 miles from the middle of Richland County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
