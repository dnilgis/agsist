How much rain has Richland County, Ohio, had in 2026?

42.07 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Richland County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 115% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Mansfield Lahm Regional Airport station: 36.72 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station at Mansfield Lahm Regional Airport. That is 1.39 inches less than a normal year to date (96% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Mansfield Lahm Regional Airport, 3 miles from the middle of Richland County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
