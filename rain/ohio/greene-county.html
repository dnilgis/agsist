The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles south-southeast of Xenia, 6 miles from the middle of Greene County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
