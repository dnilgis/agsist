Questions and answers

How much rain has Iowa had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 88 Iowa counties has a total in the table above. The most is 55.41 inches at Boone, Boone County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Iowa wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 81 counties with a NOAA normal, 63 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 16 below and 2 at it. Half are above 120% and half below. 7 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Iowa rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 26, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 11 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
