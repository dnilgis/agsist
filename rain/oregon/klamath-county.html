Klamath Falls station: 10.33 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 1 mile northwest of Klamath Falls. That is 3.08 inches less than a normal year to date (77% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northwest of Klamath Falls, 34 miles from the middle of Klamath County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 321 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
