How much rain has Polk County, Oregon, had in 2026?

39.88 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Polk County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 80% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Monmouth gauge: 21.45 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 15, at the volunteer rain gauge 6.3 miles south of Monmouth. That is 12.06 inches less than a normal year to date (64% of normal).

This gauge is 16 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6.3 miles south of Monmouth, 13 miles from the middle of Polk County. It reported every one of the 319 days from Jan 1 to Nov 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 319 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 5 stations inside Polk County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
