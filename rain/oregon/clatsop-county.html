The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile southeast of Cannon Beach, 16 miles from the middle of Clatsop County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Clatsop County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Clatsop County in 2026?

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Clatsop County?

How many growing degree days has Clatsop County had in 2026?

Has Clatsop County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?
