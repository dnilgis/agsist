How much rain has Curry County, Oregon, had in 2026?

60.21 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Curry County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 93% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.54 in, 7 days 3.05 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 pm Pacific, Nov 24.

One weather station’s gauge, the Port Orford station (NOAA daily record): 69.20 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station 5 miles east of Port Orford. That is 23.82 inches less than a normal year to date (74% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 19 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles east of Port Orford, 23 miles from the middle of Curry County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
