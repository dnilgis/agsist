The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Haskins, 6 miles from the middle of Yamhill County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Yamhill County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The Haskins Dam weather station in Haskins had 39.75 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 11, 76% of the usual 52.63 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Yamhill County in the last week?

2.43 inches from Nov 5 to Nov 11 at the Haskins Dam weather station in Haskins, and 4.44 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 13 to Nov 11. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Yamhill County in 2026?

February, with 9.47 inches at the Haskins Dam weather station in Haskins, counting Jan 1 through Nov 11.

What was the biggest rain in Yamhill County in 2026?

3.37 inches on Mar 12, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 11.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Yamhill County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 11, the Haskins Dam weather station in Haskins had 44.75 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 39.75 inches in 2026. On the 314 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.00 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Yamhill County had in 2026?

2,506 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at a weather station 13 miles from the rain station, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (21 years).

Oct 11 (32°F or colder) at a weather station 13 miles from the rain station. Usual: Oct 25, the middle of 23 years, 1991-2020.
