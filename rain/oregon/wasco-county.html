How much rain has Wasco County, Oregon, had in 2026?

11.39 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wasco County, Jan 1 to Nov 16, an estimate from a weather grid (77% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. The only station in Wasco County that reported nearly every day is the Clear Lake SNOTEL site, 26 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 19.90 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the figures below are that site’s. That is 11.85 inches less than a normal year to date (63% of normal).

  • Longest dry stretch: 65 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jun 29 to Sep 1.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Clear Lake, 26 miles from the middle of Wasco County. It reported every one of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.
