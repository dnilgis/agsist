How much rain has Wasco County, Oregon, had in 2026?

10.66 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wasco County, Jan 1 to Nov 9, an estimate from a weather grid (75% of usual).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. The only station in Wasco County that reported nearly every day is the Clear Lake SNOTEL site, 26 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 18.90 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 6; the figures below are that site’s.

2026 so far in Wasco County

  • Wettest month: February, 4.20 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 1.30 inches on Feb 23.
  • Longest dry stretch: 65 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jun 29 to Sep 1.
  • 2025 by the same date: 23.50 inches at the same station (it missed 1 day). On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.60 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: the least rain by Nov 6 in 45 years on record here (1981 on). Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 10.66 inches, 75% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Wasco County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).
Weather at this station: 1,504 corn GDDs May 1 to Nov 6, 109% of the 1991-2020 average (27 yrs)

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Clear Lake, 26 miles from the middle of Wasco County. It reported every one of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Wasco County, Oregon, had in 2026?

10.66 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wasco County, Jan 1 to Nov 9, an estimate from a weather grid (75% of usual).

Is Wasco County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Clear Lake had 18.90 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 6, 62% of the usual 30.38 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Wasco County in the last week?

0.40 inches from Oct 31 to Nov 6 at the weather station in Clear Lake, and 1.10 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 8 to Nov 6. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Wasco County in 2026?

February, with 4.20 inches at the weather station in Clear Lake, counting Jan 1 through Nov 6.

What was the biggest rain in Wasco County in 2026?

1.30 inches on Feb 23, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 6.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wasco County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 6, the weather station in Clear Lake had 23.50 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 18.90 inches in 2026. On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 4.60 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Wasco County had in 2026?

1,504 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 6 at the weather station in Clear Lake, 109% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Wasco County in 2026?

Oct 10 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Clear Lake. Usual: Oct 3, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.
