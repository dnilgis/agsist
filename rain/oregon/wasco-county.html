How much rain has Wasco County, Oregon, had in 2026?

11.50 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wasco County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, an estimate from a weather grid (76% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. The only station in Wasco County that reported nearly every day is the Clear Lake SNOTEL site, 26 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 20.20 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the figures below are that site’s. That is 11.75 inches less than a normal year to date (63% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Clear Lake, 26 miles from the middle of Wasco County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Wasco County, Oregon, had in 2026?

Is Wasco County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station in Clear Lake had 20.20 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14, 63% of the normal 31.95 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Wasco County in the last week?

1.30 inches from Nov 8 to Nov 14 at the weather station in Clear Lake, and 2.40 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 16 to Nov 14. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Wasco County in 2026?

February, with 4.20 inches at the weather station in Clear Lake, counting Jan 1 through Nov 14.

What was the biggest rain in Wasco County in 2026?

1.30 inches on Feb 23, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 14.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wasco County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 14, the weather station in Clear Lake had 23.80 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 20.20 inches in 2026. On the 317 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.60 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Wasco County had in 2026?

1,508 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 14 at the weather station in Clear Lake, 109% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Wasco County in 2026?

Oct 10 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in Clear Lake. Typical: Oct 3, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.
