The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Medford International Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Jackson County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Jackson County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Medford International Airport had 8.77 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 68% of the usual 12.85 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Jackson County in the last week?

0.08 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Medford International Airport, and 0.73 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Jackson County in 2026?

February, with 3.02 inches at the weather station at Medford International Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Jackson County in 2026?

1.05 inches on Feb 8, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Jackson County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the weather station at Medford International Airport had 13.98 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 8.77 inches in 2026. On the 311 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.21 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Jackson County had in 2026?

3,160 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Medford International Airport, 103% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (29 years).

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 37 stations inside Jackson County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
