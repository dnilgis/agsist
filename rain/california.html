How much rain has California had in 2026? County by county

55 California counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (5 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 51 counties with a NOAA normal are above 74% of a normal year to date and half below.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
