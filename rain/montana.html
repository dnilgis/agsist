Questions and answers

How much rain has Montana had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 52 Montana counties has a total in the table above. The most is 24.16 inches at Bigfork, Lake County. These are station totals, not a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Montana wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 52 counties with a NOAA normal, 28 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 23 below and 1 at it. Half are above 103% and half below. 0 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Montana rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 4 counties with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
