How much rain has Montana had in 2026? County by county

53 Montana counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 18 (12 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the counties are above 103% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Toole County: 2nd wettest by Nov 18 of 36 years on record (1951 on), 137% of normal.

Rain and normal are in inches, Jan 1 through each station’s last day.
