How much rain has Montana had in 2026? County by county

54 Montana counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (16 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 50 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 99% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Liberty County: the wettest by Nov 11 of 78 years on record (1906 on), 164% of usual.
  • Granite County: 3rd wettest by Nov 12 of 83 years on record (1929 on), 161% of usual.
