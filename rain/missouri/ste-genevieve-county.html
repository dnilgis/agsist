The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Ste. Genevieve, 8 miles from the middle of Ste. Genevieve County. It reported 304 of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
