How much rain has Iron County, Missouri, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Viburnum, 22 miles from the middle of Iron County. It reported 317 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms
