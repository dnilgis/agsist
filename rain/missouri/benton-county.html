The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles northwest of Cole Camp, 13 miles from the middle of Benton County. It reported 314 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
