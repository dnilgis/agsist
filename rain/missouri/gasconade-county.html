How much rain has Gasconade County, Missouri, had in 2026?

41.52 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Gasconade County, Jan 1 to Nov 26, 103% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 1.26 in, 7 days 1.66 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 am Central, Nov 27.

One weather station’s gauge, the Rosebud station (NOAA daily record): 38.90 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 26, at the weather station in Rosebud. That is 3.15 inches less than a normal year to date (93% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Rosebud, 7 miles from the middle of Gasconade County. It reported every one of the 330 days from Jan 1 to Nov 26. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
