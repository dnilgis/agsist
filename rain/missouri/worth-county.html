  • Wettest month: September, 8.64 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 3.08 inches on Nov 3.
  • Longest dry stretch: 31 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Jan 31 to Mar 2.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northeast of Grant City, 1 mile from the middle of Worth County. It reported 302 of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6; the 8 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 12, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 302 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Worth County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
