The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Freedom, 9 miles from the middle of Osage County. It reported 325 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
