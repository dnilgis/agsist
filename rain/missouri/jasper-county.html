The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.5 miles south of Carthage, 4 miles from the middle of Jasper County. It reported 315 of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 315 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 3 stations inside Jasper County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
