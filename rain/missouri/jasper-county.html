The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Joplin Regional Airport (station ID 13987), 9 miles from the middle of Jasper County. It reported 280 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 280 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Jasper County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.

How much rain has Jasper County, Missouri, had in 2026?

Is Jasper County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Drier so far. The weather station at Joplin Regional Airport had 32.16 inches from Jan 1 to Oct 13, 88% of the usual 36.61 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Jasper County in 2026?

April, with 10.29 inches at the weather station at Joplin Regional Airport, counting Jan 1 through Oct 13.

What was the biggest rain in Jasper County in 2026?

2.03 inches on Apr 26, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Oct 13.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Jasper County?

From Jan 1 to Oct 13, the weather station at Joplin Regional Airport had 33.76 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 32.16 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 6 days). On the 279 days both years reported, 2026 had 1.38 inches less than 2025.
