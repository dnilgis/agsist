The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles west of Anderson (station ANDERSON 3W), 8 miles from the middle of McDonald County. It reported 284 of the 284 days from Jan 1 to Oct 11, every one. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 284 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside McDonald County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
