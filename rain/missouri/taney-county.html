The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles north-northeast of Ozark Beach, 5 miles from the middle of Taney County. It reported 322 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
