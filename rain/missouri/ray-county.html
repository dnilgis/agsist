The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.5 miles east-northeast of Excelsior Springs, 12 miles from the middle of Ray County. It reported 311 of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21; the 14 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
