How much rain has New Madrid County, Missouri, had in 2026?

34.06 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over New Madrid County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 76% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile southeast of New Madrid, 6 miles from the middle of New Madrid County. It reported 313 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
