The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Saverton, 16 miles from the middle of Ralls County. It reported 318 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
