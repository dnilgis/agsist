The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-southwest of Advance, 17 miles from the middle of Stoddard County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
