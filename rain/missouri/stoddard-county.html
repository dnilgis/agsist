How much rain has Stoddard County, Missouri, had in 2026?

33.45 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Stoddard County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 75% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

One volunteer rain gauge, the Bloomfield gauge (credited below): 32.93 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 23, at the volunteer rain gauge 2.9 miles south of Bloomfield. That is 9.84 inches less than a normal year to date (77% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.9 miles south of Bloomfield, 1 mile from the middle of Stoddard County. It reported 309 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 309 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 4 stations inside Stoddard County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
