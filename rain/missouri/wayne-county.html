The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Clearwater, 17 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported 302 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 27 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
