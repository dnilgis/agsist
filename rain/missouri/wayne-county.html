The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Wappapello, 16 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported 296 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 27 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
