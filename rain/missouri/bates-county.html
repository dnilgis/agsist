The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west of Butler, 4 miles from the middle of Bates County. It reported 314 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 9 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
