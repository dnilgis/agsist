The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southwest of Winona, 12 miles from the middle of Shannon County. It reported 321 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
