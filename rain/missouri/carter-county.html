The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 4.8 miles west-northwest of Ellsinore, 8 miles from the middle of Carter County. It reported 316 of the 327 days from Jan 1 to Nov 23; the 11 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
