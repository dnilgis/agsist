The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Conception, 13 miles from the middle of Nodaway County. It reported 316 of the 323 days from Jan 1 to Nov 19; the 7 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
