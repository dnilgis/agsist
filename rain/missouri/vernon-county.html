41.32 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Vernon County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 101% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Nevada Water Plant station: 33.66 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the Nevada Water Plant weather station in Nevada Water. That is 9.37 inches less than a normal year to date (78% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Nevada Water, 2 miles from the middle of Vernon County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
