The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge 2.3 miles south of Arnold (gauge ID US1MOJF0032), 13 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported every one of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

This gauge has no NOAA normal, so this page gives no usual year or percent for it.

Why this gauge: 5 stations inside Jefferson County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. The pick goes to one with NOAA normals first, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
