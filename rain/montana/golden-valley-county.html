The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 18 miles north-northwest of Ryegate, 13 miles from the middle of Golden Valley County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
