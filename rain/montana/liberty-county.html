How much rain has Liberty County, Montana, had in 2026?

16.89 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Chester, Liberty County, through Oct 16. That is 6.26 inches more than a usual year to date (159% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Chester had its wettest month in June with 6.39 inches, and its biggest single day was Jun 29 at 1.21 inches. Then it went 25 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Jul 20 to Aug 13. On the days both years reported, this year is 3.53 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Chester (station ID 241692), 4 miles from the middle of Liberty County. It reported 285 of the 289 days from Jan 1 to Oct 16; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 285 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 19 stations inside Liberty County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
