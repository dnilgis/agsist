The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Malta, 7 miles from the middle of Phillips County. It reported every one of the 315 days from Jan 1 to Nov 11. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

What was the wettest month in Phillips County in 2026?

What was the biggest rain in Phillips County in 2026?

1.36 inches on Jun 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 11. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Phillips County?

How many growing degree days has Phillips County had in 2026?

2,766 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 11 at the weather station in Malta.

Sources and terms
