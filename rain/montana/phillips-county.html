The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Malta, 7 miles from the middle of Phillips County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Phillips County, Montana, had in 2026?

Is Phillips County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Phillips County, about 14.36 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 111% of the normal 12.90 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Phillips County in the last week?

0.35 inches from Nov 12 to Nov 18 at the weather station in Malta, and 1.51 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 20 to Nov 18. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Phillips County in 2026?

June, with 4.41 inches at the weather station in Malta, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Phillips County in 2026?

1.36 inches on Jun 1, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Phillips County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station in Malta had 11.00 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 14.59 inches in 2026. On the 319 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.59 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Phillips County had in 2026?

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 322 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Phillips County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
