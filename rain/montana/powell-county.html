How much rain has Powell County, Montana, had in 2026?

27.50 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek in Powell County, through Nov 9. That is 5.95 inches more than a usual year to date (128% of usual). This station is a mountain snow site, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in North Fork Elk Creek, 16 miles from the middle of Powell County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Powell County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in North Fork Elk Creek had 27.50 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 9, 128% of the usual 21.55 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Powell County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 3 to Nov 9 at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, and 3.20 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 11 to Nov 9. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Powell County in 2026?

March, with 4.20 inches at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, counting Jan 1 through Nov 9.

What was the biggest rain in Powell County in 2026?

1.60 inches on Mar 13, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 9.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Powell County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 9, the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek had 17.90 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 20 days) and 27.50 inches in 2026. On the 293 days both years reported, 2026 had 8.90 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Powell County had in 2026?

1,438 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 10 at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, 120% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Powell County in 2026?

Sep 26 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek. Usual: Sep 15, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Powell County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
