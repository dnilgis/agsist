How much rain has Powell County, Montana, had in 2026?

23.79 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Powell County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, an estimate from a weather grid (105% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. The only station in Powell County that reported nearly every day is the North Fork Elk Creek SNOTEL site, 16 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 27.40 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the figures below are that site’s. That is 5.43 inches more than a normal year to date (125% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in North Fork Elk Creek, 16 miles from the middle of Powell County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Powell County, Montana, had in 2026?

Is Powell County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station in North Fork Elk Creek had 27.40 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14, 125% of the normal 21.97 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Powell County in the last week?

0.10 inches from Nov 8 to Nov 14 at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, and 2.40 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 16 to Nov 14. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Powell County in 2026?

March, with 4.20 inches at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, counting Jan 1 through Nov 14.

What was the biggest rain in Powell County in 2026?

1.60 inches on Mar 13, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 14.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Powell County?

How many growing degree days has Powell County had in 2026?

1,442 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 14 at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, 121% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (27 years).

When was the first fall freeze in Powell County in 2026?

Sep 26 (32°F or colder) at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek. Typical: Sep 15, the middle of 27 years, 1991-2020.
