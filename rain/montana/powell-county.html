How much rain has Powell County, Montana, had in 2026?

25.10 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek, Powell County, through Oct 15. That is 5.37 inches more than a usual year to date (127% of usual).

Through Oct 10, the weather station in North Fork Elk Creek had its wettest month in March at 4.20 inches, and its biggest single day came Mar 13 with 1.60 inches. Then it went 17 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, from Apr 27 to May 13. On the days both years reported, it has caught 8.60 inches more than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in North Fork Elk Creek (station ID USS0013C31S), 16 miles from the middle of Powell County. It reported every one of the 288 days from Jan 1 to Oct 15. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 288 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Powell County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
