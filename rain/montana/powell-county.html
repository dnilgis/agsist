The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in North Fork Elk Creek (station ID USS0013C31S), 16 miles from the middle of Powell County. It reported 282 of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 282 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Powell County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
