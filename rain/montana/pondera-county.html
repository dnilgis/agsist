How much rain has Pondera County, Montana, had in 2026?

16.88 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Pondera County, Jan 1 to Nov 16, an estimate from a weather grid (115% of normal).

The gauge here is a mountain snow site. The only station in Pondera County that reported nearly every day is the Pike Creek SNOTEL site, 51 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 38.00 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the figures below are that site’s. That is 0.13 inches less than a normal year to date (100% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pike Creek, 51 miles from the middle of Pondera County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
