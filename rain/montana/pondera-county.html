The gauge here is a mountain snow site. The only station in Pondera County that reported nearly every day is the Pike Creek SNOTEL site, 51 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pike Creek, 51 miles from the middle of Pondera County. It reported 317 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
