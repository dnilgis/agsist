The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 20 miles south of Harlem, 19 miles from the middle of Blaine County. It reported 312 of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
