The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station less than a mile south-southwest of Dummond, 23 miles from the middle of Granite County. It reported 307 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
