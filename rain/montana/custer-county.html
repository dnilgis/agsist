How much rain has Custer County, Montana, had in 2026?

11.90 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Custer County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 85% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Powderville station: 9.51 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the weather station 8 miles north-northeast of Powderville. That is 4.98 inches less than a normal year to date (66% of normal).

This station is 19 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 8 miles north-northeast of Powderville, 38 miles from the middle of Custer County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Custer County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
