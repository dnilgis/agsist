How much rain has Sweet Grass County, Montana, had in 2026?

16.30 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Sweet Grass County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 90% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.08 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 am Mountain, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Melville station (NOAA daily record): 13.93 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station 4 miles west of Melville. That is 5.14 inches less than a normal year to date (73% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 17 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west of Melville, 21 miles from the middle of Sweet Grass County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
