How much rain has Sweet Grass County, Montana, had in 2026?

13.01 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 4 miles west of Melville, Sweet Grass County, through Oct 17. That is 4.76 inches less than a usual year to date (73% of usual).

September was the wettest month at 2.44 inches, and most of that came on Sep 19, the biggest single day at 2.24 inches. Then the gauge sat empty 16 days in a row, Sep 24 to Oct 9. Last year at this point it had 8.01 inches more.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles west of Melville (station ID 245603), 21 miles from the middle of Sweet Grass County. It reported every one of the 290 days from Jan 1 to Oct 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 290 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Sweet Grass County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
