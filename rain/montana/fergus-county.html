How much rain has Fergus County, Montana, had in 2026?

15.22 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Fergus County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 93% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.22 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 am Mountain, Nov 25.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Grass Range, 26 miles from the middle of Fergus County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
