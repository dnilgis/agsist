The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Tizer Basin, 18 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported 308 of the 309 days from Jan 1 to Nov 5; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
