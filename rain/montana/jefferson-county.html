The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Tizer Basin, 18 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported 317 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
