How much rain has Wheatland County, Montana, had in 2026?

13.30 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 11 miles northwest of Harlowton, Wheatland County, through Oct 14. That is 0.87 inches less than a usual year to date (94% of usual).

Through Oct 7, the weather station 11 miles northwest of Harlowton went 15 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, from Jan 25 to Feb 8. Then it caught 2.40 inches on Feb 9, the biggest single day of the year so far. That made February the wettest month at 2.70 inches. On the days both years reported, this year sits 0.34 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 11 miles northwest of Harlowton (station ID 243947), 9 miles from the middle of Wheatland County. It reported every one of the 287 days from Jan 1 to Oct 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 287 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Wheatland County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
