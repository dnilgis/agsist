How much rain has Silver Bow County, Montana, had in 2026?

10.66 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Butte Bert Mooney Airport, Silver Bow County, through Oct 18. That is 0.70 inches less than a usual year to date (94% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Butte Bert Mooney Airport had its wettest month in April at 2.14 inches. The biggest single day was 1.14 inches on Jun 28, and that came right after the longest dry stretch, 14 days in a row with nothing in the gauge from Jun 11 to Jun 24. That leaves it 1.09 inches more than last year by this day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Butte Bert Mooney Airport (station ID 24135), 8 miles from the middle of Silver Bow County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 2 stations inside Silver Bow County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
