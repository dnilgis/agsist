Lima station: 8.98 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 9, at the weather station in Lima. That is 0.82 inches less than a normal year to date (92% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Lima, 38 miles from the middle of Beaverhead County. It reported every one of the 313 days from Jan 1 to Nov 9. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 10 stations inside Beaverhead County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
