How much rain has Lewis and Clark County, Montana, had in 2026?

21.50 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Lewis and Clark County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 109% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.18 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 am Mountain, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Helena Airport station (NOAA daily record): 14.46 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station at Helena Airport. That is 3.61 inches more than a normal year to date (133% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 24 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Helena Airport, 41 miles from the middle of Lewis and Clark County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
