How much rain has Lewis and Clark County, Montana, had in 2026?

12.21 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Helena Airport, Lewis and Clark County, through Oct 18. That is 2.16 inches more than a usual year to date (121% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Helena Airport had its wettest month in June with 2.30 inches. The biggest single day was May 30 at 0.97 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 13 days, Jan 26 to Feb 7, with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace. That still leaves the station 0.28 inches less than last year at this point.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Helena Airport (station ID 24144), 41 miles from the middle of Lewis and Clark County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Lewis and Clark County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
