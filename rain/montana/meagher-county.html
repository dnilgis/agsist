How much rain has Meagher County, Montana, had in 2026?

28.10 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Boulder Mountain, Meagher County, through Oct 10. That is 2.33 inches less than a usual year to date (92% of usual).

Through Oct 10, the weather station in Boulder Mountain had its wettest month in June at 5.70 inches. That same June held the longest dry stretch, 13 days in a row with nothing in the gauge from Jun 14 to Jun 26. Then Jun 28 brought 2.00 inches, the biggest single day so far. The year stands 0.80 inches more than last year to the same day.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Boulder Mountain (station ID USS0011C01S), 19 miles from the middle of Meagher County. It reported every one of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 6 stations inside Meagher County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
