How much rain has Meagher County, Montana, had in 2026?

All 6 stations in Meagher County that reported nearly every day are high mountain sites; the one used is the Pickfoot Creek SNOTEL site, 18 miles from the middle of the county, high in the mountains, where rain and snow are not what the farms below get. It had 27.40 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the figures below are that site’s. That is 1.53 inches more than a normal year to date (106% of normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pickfoot Creek, 18 miles from the middle of Meagher County. It reported every one of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
