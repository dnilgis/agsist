How much rain has Richland County, Montana, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 20 miles south of Brockton, 17 miles from the middle of Richland County. It reported every one of the 324 days from Jan 1 to Nov 20. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 27, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 324 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Richland County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
