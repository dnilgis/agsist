The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Plevna, 7 miles from the middle of Fallon County. It reported 313 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 3 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
