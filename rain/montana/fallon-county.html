The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Plevna, 7 miles from the middle of Fallon County. It reported 318 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Fallon County, Montana, had in 2026?

10.35 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Fallon County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 69% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Plevna station had 9.92 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18 (67% of normal).

Is Fallon County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Fallon County, about 10.35 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 69% of the normal 15.01 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Fallon County in 2026?

June, with 2.43 inches at the weather station in Plevna, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Fallon County in 2026?

0.77 inches on May 31, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Fallon County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station in Plevna had 17.47 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 9.92 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 317 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.55 inches less than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Fallon County had in 2026?

2,809 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 18 at the weather station in Plevna, 112% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (26 years).

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 318 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
