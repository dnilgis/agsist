The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Billings International Airport, 16 miles from the middle of Yellowstone County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Yellowstone County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The weather station at Billings International Airport had 14.04 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 105% of the usual 13.32 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Yellowstone County in the last week?

0.25 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Billings International Airport, and 1.64 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Yellowstone County in 2026?

September, with 4.63 inches at the weather station at Billings International Airport, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Yellowstone County in 2026?

1.65 inches on Sep 18, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Yellowstone County?

How many growing degree days has Yellowstone County had in 2026?

2,866 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at the weather station at Billings International Airport, 111% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 312 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Yellowstone County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
