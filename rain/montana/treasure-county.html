The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 25 miles south-southeast of Hysham, 20 miles from the middle of Treasure County. It reported 318 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
