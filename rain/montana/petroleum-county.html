The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 4 miles east-northeast of Flatwillow, 21 miles from the middle of Petroleum County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
