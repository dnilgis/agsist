How much rain has Florida had in 2026? County by county

58 Florida counties have a weather station inside the county that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026, through Nov 7 (11 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 47 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 98% of a usual year to date and half below.
