How much rain has Florida had in 2026? County by county

58 Florida counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 8 (13 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 47 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 98% of a usual year to date and half below.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily.

Rain and usual are in inches. Usual is NOAA’s daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. A station with no NOAA normal shows none. Each county page has the month by month table.
