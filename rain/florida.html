How much rain has Florida had in 2026? County by county

58 Florida counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 46 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 95.5% of a usual year to date and half below.
