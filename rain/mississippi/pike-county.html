2026 so far in Pike County

  • Biggest single day: not known. 2 reports cover more than one day, and the biggest day could be inside one of them.
  • Longest dry stretch: 16 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Sep 16 to Oct 1.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 5.6 miles southwest of Jayess, 12 miles from the middle of Pike County. It reported 307 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Pike County?

How many growing degree days has Pike County had in 2026?

4,926 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at a weather station 15 miles from the rain gauge, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (28 years).

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 307 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Pike County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
