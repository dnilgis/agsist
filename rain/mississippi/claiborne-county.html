The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile northeast of Port Gibson, 4 miles from the middle of Claiborne County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
