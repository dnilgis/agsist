The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Greenwood Leflore Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Carroll County. It reported 314 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
