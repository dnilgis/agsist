How much rain has Carroll County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

33.94 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Carroll County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 68% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.01 in, 7 days 0.68 in. NOAA radar county average to 1 am Central, Nov 25.

One weather station’s gauge, the Greenwood Leflore Airport station (NOAA daily record): 19.52 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 24, at the weather station at Greenwood Leflore Airport. That is 25.15 inches less than a normal year to date (44% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 24 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Greenwood Leflore Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Carroll County. It reported 322 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
