How much rain has Carroll County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

34.26 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Carroll County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 68% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.11 in, 7 days 1.32 in. NOAA radar county average to 12 pm Central, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Greenwood Leflore Airport station (NOAA daily record): 19.94 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 25, at the weather station at Greenwood Leflore Airport. That is 24.88 inches less than a normal year to date (44% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 24 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Greenwood Leflore Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Carroll County. It reported 323 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
