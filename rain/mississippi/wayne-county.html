The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles west of Waynesboro, 3 miles from the middle of Wayne County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Wayne County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

Is Wayne County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

About normal so far. The weather station 2 miles west of Waynesboro had 50.19 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 12, 98% of the normal 51.47 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Wayne County in the last week?

0.03 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station 2 miles west of Waynesboro, and 0.43 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Wayne County in 2026?

January, with 10.83 inches at the weather station 2 miles west of Waynesboro, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Wayne County in 2026?

7.85 inches on Jan 10, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 12.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wayne County?

How many growing degree days has Wayne County had in 2026?

4,818 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station 2 miles west of Waynesboro, 105% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (25 years).

Has Wayne County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station 2 miles west of Waynesboro. Typical: Nov 13, the middle of 21 years, 1991-2020.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Wayne County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
