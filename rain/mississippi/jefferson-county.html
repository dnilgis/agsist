The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 6 miles southeast of Fayette, 7 miles from the middle of Jefferson County. It reported 290 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 32 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
