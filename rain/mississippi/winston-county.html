How much rain has Winston County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 8.6 miles south-southeast of Louisville, 6 miles from the middle of Winston County. It reported 325 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
