2026 so far in Neshoba County

  • Wettest month: June, 12.70 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.85 inches on Apr 25.
  • Longest dry stretch: 23 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 11 to Nov 2.
  • 2025 by the same date: 47.01 inches at the same gauge (it missed 3 days). On the 304 days both years reported, 2026 had 3.44 inches more than 2025.
  • Across the county: about 41.97 inches, 86% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Neshoba County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.4 miles southeast of Philadelphia, 2 miles from the middle of Neshoba County. It reported 307 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 307 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 2 stations inside Neshoba County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
