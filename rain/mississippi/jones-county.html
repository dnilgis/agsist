The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Laurel, 4 miles from the middle of Jones County. It reported 306 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
