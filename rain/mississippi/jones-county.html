How much rain has Jones County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

55.50 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Jones County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 103% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Laurel, 4 miles from the middle of Jones County. It reported 313 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 313 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
