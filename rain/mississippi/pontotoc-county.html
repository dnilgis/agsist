How much rain has Pontotoc County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

36.94 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Pontotoc County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 74% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Pontotoc Experiment Sta station: 29.65 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the Pontotoc Experiment Sta weather station in Pontotoc. That is 20.32 inches less than a normal year to date (59% of normal).

This station is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pontotoc, 6 miles from the middle of Pontotoc County. It reported 306 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 306 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
