How much rain has Pontotoc County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

35.12 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Pontotoc, Pontotoc County, through Oct 12. That is 7.78 inches less than a usual year to date (82% of usual).

Through Oct 12, the weather station in Pontotoc had its wettest month in April at 6.15 inches. The biggest one-day reading came Apr 29 at 3.30 inches. Then it went 28 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, from Sep 13 to Oct 10. That leaves the station 7.78 inches less than a usual year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pontotoc (station ID 227106), 2 miles from the middle of Pontotoc County. It reported 267 of the 285 days from Jan 1 to Oct 12; the 18 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 267 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Pontotoc County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
