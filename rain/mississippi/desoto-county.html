How much rain has DeSoto County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

32.94 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Pleasant Hill, DeSoto County, through Oct 13. That is 12.38 inches less than a usual year to date (73% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Pleasant Hill has caught 32.94 inches, 12.38 inches less than a usual year. May was the wettest month at 6.81 inches, and the biggest one-day reading was 2.34 inches on Jun 8. From Aug 24 to Sep 11 the gauge went 19 days in a row with nothing in it, not even a trace. On the days both years reported, that is 16.79 inches less than last year.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Pleasant Hill (station ID 227066), 4 miles from the middle of DeSoto County. It reported 280 of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13; the 6 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 280 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside DeSoto County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
