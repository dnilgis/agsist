The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Olive Branch, 10 miles from the middle of DeSoto County. It reported 324 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 324 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
