The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 13.1 miles southeast of Lucedale, 14 miles from the middle of George County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
