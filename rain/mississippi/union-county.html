The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.8 miles south-southwest of Myrtle, 9 miles from the middle of Union County. It reported 321 of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Union County, Mississippi, had in 2026?

Is Union County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Drier so far. Averaged over Union County, about 41.28 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 24, 81% of the normal 50.85 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Union County in 2026?

May, with 8.86 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 7.8 miles south-southwest of Myrtle, counting Jan 1 through Nov 21.

What was the biggest rain in Union County in 2026?

1.32 inches on Jan 9, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 21. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Union County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 21, the volunteer rain gauge 7.8 miles south-southwest of Myrtle had 54.46 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 7 days) and 45.45 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 315 days both years reported, 2026 had 7.33 inches less than 2025.
