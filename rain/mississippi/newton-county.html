The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 5 miles east-northeast of Newton, 5 miles from the middle of Newton County. It reported 315 of the 317 days from Jan 1 to Nov 13; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
