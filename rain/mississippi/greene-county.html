The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 6 miles west-southwest of Leakesville, 5 miles from the middle of Greene County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
