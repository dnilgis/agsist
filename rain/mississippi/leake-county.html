The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Walnut Grove (station WALNUT GROVE), 11 miles from the middle of Leake County. It reported 283 of the 283 days from Jan 1 to Oct 10, every one. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace counts as zero.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 283 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Leake County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read quite differently a few miles away.
