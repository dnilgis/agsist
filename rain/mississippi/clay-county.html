2026 so far in Clay County

  • Wettest month: January, 6.98 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.82 inches on May 26.
  • Longest dry stretch: 21 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 11 to Oct 31.
  • 2025 by the same date: 42.35 inches at the same gauge (it missed 7 days). On the 291 days both years reported, 2026 had 11.78 inches less than 2025.
  • On 4 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 33.61 inches, 71% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 9, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Clay County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1 mile south-southeast of West Point, 9 miles from the middle of Clay County. It reported 297 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
