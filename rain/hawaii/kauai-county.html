How much rain has Kauai County, Hawaii, had in 2026?

106.40 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa in Kauai County, through Nov 24. That is 42.58 inches more than a normal year to date (167% of normal). That is one gauge, not an average over the whole county.

No radar estimate of the last day or week here: NOAA’s radar rain grid does not cover Hawaii.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa, 14 miles from the middle of Kauai County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 328 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 12 stations inside Kauai County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
