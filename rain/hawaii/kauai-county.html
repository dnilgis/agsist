The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa, 14 miles from the middle of Kauai County. It reported every one of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Kauai County, Hawaii, had in 2026?

103.28 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa, Kauai County, through Nov 8. That is 43.69 inches more than a usual year to date (173% of usual).

Is Kauai County wetter or drier than usual in 2026?

Wetter so far. The volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa had 103.28 inches from Jan 1 to Nov 8, 173% of the usual 59.59 inches for the same days.

How much rain fell in Kauai County in the last week?

1.55 inches from Nov 2 to Nov 8 at the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa, and 8.76 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 10 to Nov 8. Those are the latest days the gauge has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

What was the wettest month in Kauai County in 2026?

March, with 15.99 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa, counting Jan 1 through Nov 8.

What was the biggest rain in Kauai County in 2026?

5.24 inches on Mar 13, 2026, the most the gauge caught in one day this year through Nov 8.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Kauai County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 8, the volunteer rain gauge 1.9 miles west-southwest of Kapaa had 59.48 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 25 days) and 103.28 inches in 2026. On the 287 days both years reported, 2026 had 33.97 inches more than 2025.

How many growing degree days has Kauai County had in 2026?

5,461 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 8 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge, 100% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has Kauai County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 8: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at a weather station 8 miles from the rain gauge.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 312 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 13 stations inside Kauai County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
