The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Hilo International Airport 87, 32 miles from the middle of Hawaii County. It reported every one of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Is Hawaii County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

How much rain fell in Hawaii County in the last week?

2.40 inches from Nov 6 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Hilo International Airport 87, and 17.13 inches over the last 30 days, Oct 14 to Nov 12. Those are the latest days the station has reported; NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late.

August, with 20.85 inches at the weather station at Hilo International Airport 87, counting Jan 1 through Nov 12.

What was the biggest rain in Hawaii County in 2026?

How many growing degree days has Hawaii County had in 2026?

5,306 corn GDDs (base 50°F, cap 86°F) May 1 to Nov 12 at the weather station at Hilo International Airport 87, 104% of its 1991-2020 average for the same days (30 years).

Has Hawaii County had a fall freeze yet in 2026?

No, not through Nov 12: no day at 32°F or colder since Jul 1 at the weather station at Hilo International Airport 87.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 13, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 316 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 38 stations inside Hawaii County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
