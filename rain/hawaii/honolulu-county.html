How much rain has Honolulu County, Hawaii, had in 2026?

25.42 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Honolulu International Airport in Honolulu County, through Nov 25. That is 11.54 inches more than a normal year to date (183% of normal). That is one weather station’s gauge (NOAA daily record), not an average over the whole county.

No radar estimate of the last day or week here: NOAA’s radar rain grid does not cover Hawaii.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Honolulu International Airport, 10 miles from the middle of Honolulu County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 329 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 27 stations inside Honolulu County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
