How much rain has Maui County, Hawaii, had in 2026?

30.53 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Kahului Airport, Maui County, through Oct 18. That is 19.39 inches more than a usual year to date (274% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station at Kahului Airport has caught 30.29 inches, 19.26 more than a usual year. Most of it came in March, with 21.11 inches that month. The biggest single day was Mar 13 at 7.40 inches. That came right after the longest dry stretch, 13 days in a row with nothing in the gauge from Feb 26 to Mar 10.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Kahului Airport (station ID 22516), 9 miles from the middle of Maui County. It reported every one of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 291 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 15 stations inside Maui County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes next, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far. Then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
