How much rain has Maui County, Hawaii, had in 2026?

32.44 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station at Kahului Airport in Maui County, through Nov 24. That is 19.46 inches more than a normal year to date (250% of normal). That is one weather station’s gauge (NOAA daily record), not an average over the whole county.

No radar estimate of the last day or week here: NOAA’s radar rain grid does not cover Hawaii.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Kahului Airport, 9 miles from the middle of Maui County. It reported every one of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 328 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 16 stations inside Maui County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
