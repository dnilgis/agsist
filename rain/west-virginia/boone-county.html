How much rain has Boone County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

46.56 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Boone County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 108% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles west-southwest of Danville, 9 miles from the middle of Boone County. It reported 318 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Boone County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

46.56 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Boone County, Jan 1 to Nov 19, 108% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The Danville gauge had 39.21 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18.

Is Boone County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Boone County, about 46.56 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 108% of the normal 43.07 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Boone County in 2026?

July, with 4.79 inches at the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles west-southwest of Danville, counting Jan 1 through Nov 18.

What was the biggest rain in Boone County in 2026?

2.19 inches on Jul 29, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Boone County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles west-southwest of Danville had 55.09 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 1 day) and 39.21 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 4 days). On the 317 days both years reported, 2026 had 15.62 inches less than 2025.
