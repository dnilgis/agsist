2026 so far in Boone County

  • Wettest month: July, 4.79 inches.
  • Biggest one-day reading: 2.19 inches on Jul 29.
  • Longest dry stretch: 15 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 18 to Nov 1.

  • On 2 reports the observer read the gauge after more than one day. That rain counts on the day it was read, so it is in the totals but not split by day.
  • Across the county: about 45.83 inches, 109% of usual, Jan 1 to Nov 8, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Boone County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal).

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.8 miles west-southwest of Danville, 9 miles from the middle of Boone County. It reported 287 of the 310 days from Jan 1 to Nov 6; the 23 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
