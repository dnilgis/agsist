How much rain has Logan County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

47.11 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Logan County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 108% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Logan station: 37.77 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station in Logan. That is 2.07 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Logan, 4 miles from the middle of Logan County. It reported 294 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 28 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

How much rain has Logan County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

Is Logan County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Logan County, about 47.11 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 18, 108% of the normal 43.72 inches for the same days.

What was the biggest rain in Logan County in 2026?

2.05 inches on Aug 12, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 18. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Logan County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 18, the weather station in Logan had 51.32 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 3 days) and 37.77 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 28 days). On the 291 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.95 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 19, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 294 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
