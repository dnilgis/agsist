The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Bluestone Lake, 2 miles from the middle of Summers County. It reported 321 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
