2026 so far in Greenbrier County

  • Wettest month: May, 5.43 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.10 inches on May 22.
  • Longest dry stretch: 14 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Oct 19 to Nov 1.
  • 2025 by the same date: 40.65 inches at the same station (it missed 1 day). On the 309 days both years reported, 2026 had 5.86 inches less than 2025.
  • On record: 42nd wettest by Nov 8 of 90 years on record here (1904 on); the last wetter year was 2025, the last drier one 2024. Years with too many missed days are not counted.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north of Lewisburg, 7 miles from the middle of Greenbrier County. It reported 310 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
