How much rain has Greenbrier County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

42.45 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Greenbrier County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 103% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Lewisburg station: 34.74 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station 3 miles north of Lewisburg. That is 1.91 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles north of Lewisburg, 7 miles from the middle of Greenbrier County. It reported 319 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
