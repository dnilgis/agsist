The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Buckhannon, 6 miles from the middle of Upshur County. It reported 316 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
