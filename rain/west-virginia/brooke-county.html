How much rain has Brooke County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

47.23 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Brooke County, Jan 1 to Nov 18, 126% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Wheeling Ohio County Airport station: 37.50 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station at Wheeling Ohio County Airport. That is 3.81 inches more than a normal year to date (111% of normal).

This station is 15 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Wheeling Ohio County Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Brooke County. It reported 320 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
