How much rain has Lewis County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

56.38 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station in Stonewall Jackson, Lewis County, through Oct 18. That is 14.18 inches more than a usual year to date (134% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the weather station in Stonewall Jackson had July as its wettest month at 10.60 inches. The biggest single day came Jul 22 with 6.20 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 9 days, May 29 to Jun 6, with not even a trace in the gauge. On the days both years reported, this year is 13.14 inches more than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Stonewall Jackson (station ID 468522), 2 miles from the middle of Lewis County. It reported 289 of the 291 days from Jan 1 to Oct 18; the 2 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 289 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Lewis County that reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
