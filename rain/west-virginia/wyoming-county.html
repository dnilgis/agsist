The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in R.D. Bailey Lake, 15 miles from the middle of Wyoming County. It reported 307 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
