How much rain has Wyoming County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

45.21 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wyoming County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 106% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

R.D. Bailey Lake station: 38.96 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the weather station in R.D. Bailey Lake. That is 2.03 inches less than a normal year to date (95% of normal).

The county average and this station disagree: the county is wetter than normal, this station drier. Rain was uneven across the county.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in R.D. Bailey Lake, 15 miles from the middle of Wyoming County. It reported 306 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 15 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Questions

How much rain has Wyoming County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

45.21 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Wyoming County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 106% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal. The R.D. Bailey Lake station had 38.96 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17 (95% of normal).

Is Wyoming County wetter or drier than normal in 2026?

Wetter so far. Averaged over Wyoming County, about 45.21 inches fell from Jan 1 to Nov 17, 106% of the normal 42.65 inches for the same days.

What was the wettest month in Wyoming County in 2026?

June, with 7.50 inches at the weather station in R.D. Bailey Lake, counting Jan 1 through Nov 17.

What was the biggest rain in Wyoming County in 2026?

1.74 inches on Oct 15, 2026, the biggest one-day reading this year through Nov 17. A reading that covers more than one day is not counted as a one-day rain.

How does 2026 compare with 2025 in Wyoming County?

From Jan 1 to Nov 17, the weather station in R.D. Bailey Lake had 40.41 inches in 2025 (2025 missed 5 days) and 38.96 inches in 2026 (2026 missed 15 days). On the 302 days both years reported, 2026 had 0.35 inches less than 2025.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 306 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: it is the only one inside Wyoming County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
