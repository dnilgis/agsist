The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3 miles northeast of Fort Seybert, 12 miles from the middle of Pendleton County. It reported 306 of the 318 days from Jan 1 to Nov 14; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 20, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 306 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: it is the only one inside Pendleton County that reported nearly every day in 2026. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
