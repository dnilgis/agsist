The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 3.9 miles north-northeast of Harrisville, 6 miles from the middle of Ritchie County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
