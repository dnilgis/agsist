How much rain has Pocahontas County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

43.17 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Pocahontas County, Jan 1 to Nov 24, 96% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.01 in. NOAA radar county average to 2 am Eastern, Nov 25.

One weather station’s gauge, the Bartow station (NOAA daily record): 26.61 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 18, at the weather station 1 mile south of Bartow. That is 9.26 inches less than a normal year to date (74% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 22 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile south of Bartow, 19 miles from the middle of Pocahontas County. It reported 297 of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18; the 25 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 25, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 297 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 4 stations inside Pocahontas County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
