The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 1.2 miles east-southeast of Green Bank, 12 miles from the middle of Pocahontas County. It reported every one of the 314 days from Jan 1 to Nov 10. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
