The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 1 mile southwest of Romney, 9 miles from the middle of Hampshire County. It reported 317 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the 4 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
