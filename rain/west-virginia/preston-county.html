The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 7.4 miles north of Thomas, 17 miles from the middle of Preston County. It reported 324 of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25; the 5 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
