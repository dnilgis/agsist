The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Elkins- Randolph County Airport, 8 miles from the middle of Randolph County. It reported every one of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
