The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile west-southwest of Clay, less than a mile from the middle of Clay County. It reported 304 of the 316 days from Jan 1 to Nov 12; the 12 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
