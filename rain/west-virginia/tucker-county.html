How much rain has Tucker County, West Virginia, had in 2026?

54.47 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 at the weather station 3 miles southeast of Davis, Tucker County, through Oct 13. That is 3.39 inches more than a usual year to date (107% of usual).

Through Oct 13, the station 3 miles southeast of Davis had September as its wettest month, at 8.57 inches. The biggest single day was Sep 6, with 2.62 inches. The longest dry stretch ran 6 days, from Jun 30 to Jul 5. On the days both years reported, this year is 0.69 inches less than 2025.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 3 miles southeast of Davis (station ID 462211), 8 miles from the middle of Tucker County. It reported every one of the 286 days from Jan 1 to Oct 13. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day. It is read once a day, usually in the morning, and each reading is filed under the day it was read, so an evening storm can show up on the next day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 286 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 3 stations inside Tucker County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
