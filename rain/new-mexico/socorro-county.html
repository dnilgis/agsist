How much rain has Socorro County, New Mexico, had in 2026?

14.30 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Socorro County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 134% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Socorro gauge: 11.04 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 17, at the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northeast of Socorro. That is 1.10 inches more than a normal year to date (111% of normal).

This gauge is 23 points of normal below the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one gauge can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge less than a mile northeast of Socorro, 4 miles from the middle of Socorro County. It reported 320 of the 321 days from Jan 1 to Nov 17; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this gauge, added up over the same 320 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 17 stations inside Socorro County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
