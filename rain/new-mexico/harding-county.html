How much rain has Harding County, New Mexico, had in 2026?

16.60 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Harding County, Jan 1 to Nov 17, 111% of normal. That is an estimate from the PRISM 4 km weather grid, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

Roy gauge: 20.03 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 16, at the volunteer rain gauge 2.1 miles north-northeast of Roy. This gauge has no NOAA normal, so there is no normal year to compare it with.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.1 miles north-northeast of Roy, 22 miles from the middle of Harding County. It reported 307 of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16; the 13 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
