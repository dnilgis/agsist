The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 16.6 miles southeast of Santa Rosa, 19 miles from the middle of Guadalupe County. It reported 327 of the 328 days from Jan 1 to Nov 24; the one day with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
