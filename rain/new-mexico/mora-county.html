How much rain has Mora County, New Mexico, had in 2026?

18.45 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over Mora County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 104% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

LatelyestimateLast 24 hours 0.00 in, 7 days 0.04 in. NOAA radar county average to 11 am Mountain, Nov 26.

One weather station’s gauge, the Ocate station (NOAA daily record): 20.24 inches, Jan 1 to Nov 21, at the weather station 2 miles northwest of Ocate. That is 3.21 inches more than a normal year to date (119% of normal). It is not a county-wide figure.

This station is 15 points of normal above the county average: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station 2 miles northwest of Ocate, 14 miles from the middle of Mora County. It reported 299 of the 325 days from Jan 1 to Nov 21; the 26 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 26, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 299 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 5 stations inside Mora County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
