How much rain has De Baca County, New Mexico, had in 2026?

One station, not the whole county: the county average is 37 points of normal above this station. Rain fell unevenly here this year. More under Across the county below.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sumner Lake, 18 miles from the middle of De Baca County. It reported every one of the 320 days from Jan 1 to Nov 16. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 18, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected.

Normal is NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily average for this station, added up over the same 320 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.
