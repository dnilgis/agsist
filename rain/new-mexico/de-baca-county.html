How much rain has De Baca County, New Mexico, had in 2026?

17.16 inches of rain have fallen so far in 2026 averaged over De Baca County, Jan 1 to Nov 25, 132% of normal. That is a county-wide estimate (the PRISM 4 km weather grid averaged over the county), not one gauge, against NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal.

The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station in Sumner Lake, 18 miles from the middle of De Baca County. It reported every one of the 329 days from Jan 1 to Nov 25. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
