The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Raton Municipal Crews Field Airport, 12 miles from the middle of Colfax County. It reported every one of the 322 days from Jan 1 to Nov 18. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.
