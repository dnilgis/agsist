2026 so far in Luna County

  • Wettest month: September, 4.14 inches.
  • Biggest single day: 2.15 inches on Sep 29.
  • Longest dry stretch: 23 days in a row with nothing in the gauge, not even a trace, Feb 14 to Mar 8.
  • 2025 by the same date: 6.51 inches at the same station. 2026 had 2.60 inches more than 2025.
  • On record: 12th wettest by Oct 28 of 46 years on record here (1962 on); the last wetter year was 2022, the last drier one 2025. Years with too many missed days are not counted.
  • Across the county: about 11.29 inches, 132% of usual, Jan 1 to Oct 28, from the PRISM 4 km grid averaged over Luna County (usual: NOAA’s 1991-2020 gridded normal). That is 19 points above this station: rain falls unevenly, and one station can catch or miss a storm.

The station, and what usual means

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the station at Deming Municipal Airport (station ID 23078), 6 miles from the middle of Luna County. It reported every one of the 301 days from Jan 1 to Oct 28. Rain here means everything the gauge caught: rain, and snow melted down to water. A trace, too little to measure, adds nothing to the totals, but it ends a dry stretch: something fell that day.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this station, added up over the same 301 days the station reported, so a missing day counts on neither side.

Why this station: 9 stations inside Luna County reported at least 90% of the days in 2026. Mountain snow sites, and stations far above the rest in the county, come last, since their rain is not what the farms below get. Where snow is normal, a station that keeps a full record of both rain and snowfall comes first, over a nearer one whose snowfall record has gaps, if it is no more than 10 miles farther or twice as far, even when only the nearer one has NOAA normals. Then the pick goes to one with NOAA normals, then the most days reported, then the one nearest the middle of the county. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
