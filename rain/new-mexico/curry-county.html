The station

The numbers come from NOAA’s daily record for the volunteer rain gauge 2.3 miles north-northeast of Clovis, 13 miles from the middle of Curry County. It reported 296 of the 312 days from Jan 1 to Nov 8; the 16 days with no report count as unknown, not as dry. Rain here means everything the gauge caught, snow melted down to water.

Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 10, 2026; updated daily. NOAA’s daily numbers come in a few days late and are sometimes corrected. Volunteer gauge data: CoCoRaHS.

Usual means NOAA’s 1991 to 2020 daily normals for this gauge, added up over the same 296 days the gauge reported, so a missing day counts on neither side. The volunteer gauges started in 1998, so none has 30 years of its own; NOAA estimates the rest from long-record stations nearby.

Why this gauge: 6 stations inside Curry County reported nearly every day in 2026, and this is the one used. Your own rain gauge can read differently a few miles away.
