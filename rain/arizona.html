Questions and answers

How much rain has Arizona had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 25, 2026, the station inside each of 14 Arizona counties has a total in the table above. The most is 22.09 inches at Prescott, Yavapai County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Arizona wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 13 counties with a NOAA normal, 10 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 3 below and 0 at it. Half are above 134% and half below. 1 county in the table has no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Arizona rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 26, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 1 county with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
