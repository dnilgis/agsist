Questions and answers

How much rain has Louisiana had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 24, 2026, the station inside each of 57 Louisiana parishes has a total in the table above. The most is 68.12 inches at Livingston, Livingston Parish. These are station totals, not a state average, and a parish whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Louisiana wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 36 parishes with a NOAA normal, 2 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 34 below and 0 at it. Half are above 77% and half below. 21 parishes in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Louisiana rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 25, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. 7 parishes with no station that reported nearly every day so far get no page.
