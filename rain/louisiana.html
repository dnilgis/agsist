Sources and terms

Daily NOAA records through RCC-ACIS, read Nov 9, 2026; updated daily.

Rain and usual are in inches. Usual is NOAA’s daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. A station with no NOAA normal shows none. Each parish page has the month by month table.
