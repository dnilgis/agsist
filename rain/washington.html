How much rain has Washington had in 2026? County by county

39 Washington counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 26 (9 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Each figure below is one weather station’s gauge (NOAA daily record), named in its row, not a county-wide estimate, unless the row says so. Questions and answers

How much rain has Washington had so far in 2026?

Through Nov 26, 2026, the station inside each of 39 Washington counties has a total in the table above. The most is 95.28 inches at Forks, Clallam County. These are station totals (one weather station's gauge per county, NOAA daily record), not county-wide estimates or a state average, and a county whose station missed too many days has no row.

Is Washington wetter or drier than normal so far in 2026?

Of the 32 counties with a NOAA normal, 4 are above 100% of a normal year to date, 28 below and 0 at it. Half are above 77.5% and half below. 7 counties in the table have no NOAA normal and no percent.

Where do the Washington rain numbers come from?

Daily NOAA station records, read Nov 27, 2026 and updated daily; normal is NOAA's daily normal at each station, added up over the days that station reported. Every county in the state has a page.
