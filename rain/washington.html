How much rain has Washington had in 2026? County by county

39 Washington counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 23 (7 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 33 counties with a NOAA normal are above 75% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.
