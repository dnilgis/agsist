How much rain has Washington had in 2026? County by county

39 Washington counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 8 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 33 counties with a NOAA usual year are above 75% of a usual year to date and half below.

Near a record
