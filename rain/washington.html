How much rain has Washington had in 2026? County by county

39 Washington counties have a weather station inside the county that reported nearly every day in 2026, through Nov 12 (6 stations stop earlier; each county page gives its own last day). Half the 34 counties with a NOAA normal are above 78% of a normal year to date and half below.

At or near a record

Counties whose station is having one of its three wettest or driest years to date on record. Only years the station reported fully are counted.

  • Skamania County: the driest by Nov 9 of 57 years on record (1966 on), 73% of normal.
