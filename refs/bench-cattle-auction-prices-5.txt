                                                           1-9-26
Compared to last report: Most of the major fertilizers have decreased in price from late last year. DAP, MAP and Urea
prices have fallen sharply as early estimates suggest corn acreage will be significantly lower this year. Eased tariffs
across many countries have allowed fertilizer imports to climb over the past few months, adding to the overall supply. Of
the seven major fertilizers, all have increased in price compared to this time last year: Anhydrous 11%, DAP 15%, MAP
12%, UAN 32 23%, UAN 28 22%, Urea 18%, and Potash 3% higher than last year. Current dry conditions have softened
fertilizer demand. While some areas picked up rain yesterday, soils are drying quickly as temperatures have been well
above normal for this time of year. Farm diesel prices have fallen sharply. Despite warmer weather, propane demand
remains strong.
