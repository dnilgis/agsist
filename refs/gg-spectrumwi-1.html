Your guide to uniquely Wisconsin holiday gifts

BY Spectrum News Staff Milwaukee
PUBLISHED 6:00 PM ET Nov. 25, 2022 PUBLISHED 6:00 PM EST Nov. 25, 2022
SHARE

MILWAUKEE — Black Friday used to mean the start of the holiday shopping season, but these days, that shopping season started weeks earlier.

From candlemakers to cheese shops, small businesses around Wisconsin are ready to help you find the perfect holiday gift for friends and family. And Taylor Schaefer, the 75th Alice in Dairyland, has some gift suggestions to create magical moments with your family this holiday season.

(Courtesy of Taylor Schaefer/75th Alice in Dairyland)

“The Something Special from Wisconsin program is a great place to go if you are looking for unique Wisconsin gifts for anyone in your life, especially for those people who are hard to shop for,” said Schaefer.

The Something Special from Wisconsin Program began in 1983 to showcase local products and businesses. Shoppers can spot the signature red logo on items from herbal tea to grass fed beef. In order to earn that red logo, a business must certify that at least 50% of its packaging, production or ingredients come from Wisconsin.

“It’s a great way to support our state’s producers and processors and drive local economies too,” said Schaefer.

Here’s your gifting guide to products harvested and created in America’s Dairyland:

Wine and chocolates from Northleaf Winery: https://northleafwinery.com/

(Courtesy of Taylor Schaefer/75th Alice in Dairyland)

Craft beer from Valkyrie Brewing Company: http://www.valkyriebrewery.com/products.html

Organic coffee from Bowers Lake Organic Coffee: https://www.bowerslakecoffee.com/

(Courtesy of Taylor Schaefer/75th Alice in Dairyland)
