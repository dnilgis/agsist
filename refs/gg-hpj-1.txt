A Christmas gift guide created especially for farm and ranch families

By Kylie Reiss

November 20, 2025

Listen Now

0:00

One of my all-time favorite Christmas gifts, wasn’t actually my gift, it was a homemade gift my parents made for my daughter. It’s a play barn that can house all of her farm animals. But what makes it so special is it’s made out of the old barnwood from my grandparents’ farmstead. Christmas gifting should be fun, not a challenge. When it comes to the farmer or rancher in your family, do you struggle? Do you feel like year after year you gift the same thing? I put together this guide, in hopes it helps you think of something different this year. There’s a combination of both fun and functional items. I also included gift ideas for the rest of the family; in case your significant other needs a little help in that department. And don’t forget about Small Business Saturday on November 29. Timeless and unique gifts often come from those little mom-and-pop shops. I will admit shopping big box stores online or using the Amazon app is rather convenient for checking things off the list quickly. But you can’t beat the feeling of shopping small, knowing you’ve supported someone’s livelihood.

For Him:

- Poncho shirt – These shirts are all the hype this season and for good reason.

- Not just any ole Rain Gauge – A Stratus Professional Rain Gauge is NOAA endorsed and meets strict accuracy requirements used by professional weather stations.

- Custom Patch or branded hats – There’s that one hat they continue to wear, the one with the sweat ring stain and dirt all over. And no matter how many times you wash it, it still comes out looking dirty. Here’s your chance to toss it out.

- Another idea that could go with this. Does your farm or ranch have a logo? If not, consider having a graphic designer create one for you.

- Merino Wool – Whether he’s a hunter or just spends a lot of time outdoors this material will keep him warm and dry during the cold winter months. My husband says First Lite has good stuff.

- Warm work vest – A vest can be very useful when you’re trying to stay warm but need more flexibility than wearing a coat and feeling like Randy Parker on ‘A Christmas Story’. Duluth and Schaefer Outfitters have some nice options.

- A ticket to an educational event or membership to a service – This may seem like a boring gift, but an opportunity like this can give back to you, your family, and operation in so many ways. We had a farmwife at this year’s HPJ Live event discuss how she and her husband each meet with a therapist once a week. After going to the first few sessions, the farmer said it’s one of the best hours spent during his week.

- Headlamp – When you need to use both hands for chores or fixing equipment in the dark, this can be more useful than your phone or a regular flashlight.

- Commissioned painting of a favorite cow or aerial photos of your operation

For Her:

- A stylish wildrag – Fringe brand is my favorite.

- Beef Tallow – PureGood offers both

- A custom piece of jewelry with your anniversary date, kids birthdates, or favorite bible verse. Here are a few places to check out: Outlaw Spirit, CH Designs Custom Jewelry

- American Farm Company

- Five Mary’s Pantry Staple

- IdaMae – This TX based company offers a wide variety of quilted goods.

- Artwork or Home Decor – See the For Anyone category for more options.

For the Kids:

- Little Buster toys

- Cowboy/cowgirl hat

- Bored on the Range activity books

- Cowkid Clothing Co.

- American Farm Co.

- IdaMae baby or youth jacket

- Gift experiences and memories – Pair camping accessories such as a sleeping bag with a camping trip. Take a trip to the zoo or museum. A subscription box for STEM activities or a craft kit can be a yearlong gift. Chances are they aren’t going to remember all of the things you bought them, but they will remember the experiences and memories you create with them.

For anyone:

- Sweetwater Pillows – These are one of a kind genuine leather pillows. A unique way to display historic family photos, your brand, or you can choose from array of artist designs.

- Prairie Oak Designs – Sarah is so sweet to work with and can create custom metal home decor, brand/logo signs, etc. Along with the custom designs, she has a variety of home goods in her boutique, and her family also runs a restaurant called, The Brandin’ Iron. Add Florence, KS to your list.

- Meat gift set – Support your local farmer or rancher! There are so many direct-to-consumer options out there. Shop Kansas Farms is a great resource. I might be biased but my sister-in-law, Kinzie has has the best pork breakfast sausage available at 4R Farm.
