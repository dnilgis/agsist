Missouri Weekly Cattle Auction Summary

This report contains information on weekly feeder cattle sales in the state of Missouri, with commentary on prices and changes from the previous week, trade, future markets, and weather conditions potentially impacting purchases. Also included are weight and price statistics for cattle sold.
