What is an Artisan Collective?

WisconsinMade partners with artisans and entrepreneurs throughout Wisconsin to ensure the best of the Badger State is sent to your doorstep. Our artisans are creative and diverse, each with a unique story behind their business. The one thing they have in common is an entrepreneurial spirit, grounded in a strong work ethic and a love for Wisconsin.

Interested in Joining the WisconsinMade Artisan Collective? Apply Here.
