Foreign Holdings of Kansas Ag Land

The following map shows the total amount of acres, by county, reported as holdings through the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978 (AFIDA).  Data was obtained through HERE for the purpose of differentiating energy leases from other types of holdings in the national Farm Service Agency report Foreign Holdings of U.S. Agricultural Land through Dec. 31st, 2024.  A summary report of explaining this information can be found HERE.

Hover your cursor over the county of interest to get detail about that county.  Zoom in using the "+" or go to full screen mode (bottom right corner) for better map viewing.

Questions can be directed to Robin Reid, robinreid@ksu.edu, 785-532-0964
