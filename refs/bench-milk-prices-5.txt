September uniform milk price stumbles

Uniform milk prices stumble again in September with prices falling month over month, a pattern that first began in July. The average uniform milk price across the 11 Federal Milk Marketing Orders (FMMOs) was at $18.98 per hundredweight (cwt) last month, down 78 cents from August, according to the most recent FMMO reports.
