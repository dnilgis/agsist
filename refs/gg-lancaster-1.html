Lancaster Farming Gift Guide: Eight Unique Ag-Themed Gifts

  • Lancaster Farming Staff
  • Nov 25, 2021
  • Nov 25, 2021
  • 0

Struggling to cross people off your holiday shopping list? We get it. Your mother-in law, best friend or farmer can be the hardest to shop for around the holiday season.

For shoppers hard pressed to find that unique and agriculturally themed gift, we’re here to help. Lancaster Farming staffers have curated a holiday gift guide, with recommendations on what we are coveting for the holiday gifts for friends, loved ones and ourselves. 

Have a Book Lover on Your List? 

Winter can be the best time for a farmer to take the time to snuggle up to read a good book or catch up on industry related works. There are numerous books that cover various topics within farming and agriculture. “An Industry Worth Fighting For” by Derrick Josi, a fourth-generation dairy farmer, offers readers a glimpse behind the curtain of a working dairy farm sharing his and many dairy farmers' reality in a balanced view. “Preserving Old Barns: Preventing the Loss of a Valuable Resources,” second edition, by John Porter, a retired University New Hampshire dairy Extension educator, is a resource for barn owners to use in caring for their special structures. The book features over 200 photographs and preservation techniques from timber framer Arron Sturgis.

Fun Livestock or Stock Show Gifts

The California-based small business Blonde Savage creates wooden livestock custom creations such as its popular livestock wreath holder that can be designed to look like a pig, steer, sheep and goat head. The company also can create a pig, steer or sheep wooden sign with built-in LED grooved lights that can be controlled through an app or simply plugged into an outlet. If you're looking for something smaller, but just as creative, Carousel Design of East Aurora, New York, might be the right fit. Graphic designer Taylor Hoelscher has created stickers that embody the love for raising livestock in a fun and eye-popping way. The stickers work great on a laptop, cellphone case, water bottle or anywhere you can imagine having a sticker. Hoelscher also has some of her sticker designs on T-shirts or hoodies so you can give your livestock enthusiast a fashionable outlet to express their livestock passion with others.  

Treat Someone or Yourself with a Charcuterie Box

There is nothing more luxurious, healthy and fun than snacking on various fruits, cheeses and meats. We are loving these personalized quarantine charcuterie boxes, adult lunchable boxes and Charcu-cones from New Jersey small business Board that artistically pair high-quality meats and cheeses, seasonal fruits and crudités into a charcuterie kit that goes your choice of drink — wine or milk. 

Got a Fashionista on Your List?

There are plenty of stylish options to choose from in clothes, boots and accessories like purses and jewelry. Shops like Stockyard Style in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, have a host of Western-themed brands like Tin Haul boots and Hooey. In Lewisburg, Pennsylvania, Showgirl Style has more stock show-themed items. As for accessories, the Mill Hall, Pennsylvania, brand Steel and Stone Jewelry designs affordable stainless steel earrings, bracelets and necklaces, which also can be purchased at the Plow Farms holiday gift shop in Mohnton, Pennsylvania. Some of our top picks are their cow-themed designs. Another popular brand is the California business Classy Trailer, which specializes in handmade sterling silver and copper Western jewelry.  Looking for something a little bigger? Holy Cow Couture’s team of artisans craft handmade cowhide purses in various sizes and styles like totes, wallets and weekender. 

Don’t Forget to Treat your Four-Legged Friends this Christmas

At the AC/EC Farm store in Shoemakersville, Pennsylvania, vendor Emily Hatt creates homemade frosted and unfrosted molasses treats that are perfect to give to livestock or horses.

Give the Gift of Wool

With plenty of sheep farms in the Mid-Atlantic, you can’t go wrong with supplying the crafter or knitter, on your list, with some supplies this Christmas. In Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the Ross Farm sells Leicester Longwool, Romney and Shetland wool in various dye and undyed bundles. 

Lathering Self-Care Gifts

Self-care has come to the forefront during the pandemic, so why not spoil someone with gifts to treat themselves? There are various dairy farmers and soapmakers who also make handcrafted soap from milk. For cow's milk soap, we advise you to check out Sudsy Cow of New Hampshire or Fresh Start Farm in Pennsylvania. For goat milk soap, try Honey Rae in Honey Brook, Pennsylvania, or Lil Swatara Goat Milk Soap in Bethel.

Boozy Gifts that Spice Up a Night In

In most states in the Mid-Atlantic there are plenty of microbreweries, wineries and distilleries. Purchase a favorite spirit, small case of beer or bottle of wine. Looking for something different? Eight Oaks Distillery has created all-in-one craft cocktails to make mixing up a drink easy for the amateur mixologist on your Christmas list. There’s also Root, a Allentown, Pennsylvania, women-owned small business that makes organic cocktail mixers to spice up a drink.
