Illinois Farm Financial Trends in 2025: Summary and Analysis

October 16, 2026
Bradley Zwilling
Farm incomes in 2025 were higher than in 2024, resulting in slightly stronger average financial positions for Illinois farms. However, after three years of lower incomes on grain farms before 2025, working cash has decreased almost 40% from 2022 on the average farm to 2020 levels. With projected continued lower incomes for the rest of 2026 and 2027, farmers will need to find ways to reduce costs while making efficient use of assets.

Sample Cost and Returns for a Migratory Commercial Beekeeping Operation 2025-2026

October 15, 2026
Brittney Goodrich and Allison Altschuler
Honey bees play a critical role in agricultural production, however commercial beekeepers face substantial costs and uncertainty. A representative operation cannot cover total costs and requires government payments to cover cash costs. This highlights the economic impact colony losses and health maintenance have on beekeeping operations. Continuing issues with colony health suggest uncertainty in the economic sustainability of the industry in the long-term.

Wind and Solar Leases in Foreign-Held U.S. Agricultural Land

October 14, 2026
Bruce Sherrick and Juo-Han Tsay
Most long-term leases held by foreign persons are for wind and solar projects, covering 6.3 million acres, or about 0.7 percent of U.S. land in farms. In most counties the share is under 1 percent, and nearly all of these acres are associated with investors from Canada and Western Europe. Because the landowner retains title and AFIDA reports acreage as a whole parcel, lease acres are best viewed separately from ownership when assessing foreign holdings of U.S. agricultural land.

2025 Final ARC/PLC Payments

October 13, 2026
Nick Paulson, Henrique Monaco, Gary Schnitkey, and Carl Zulauf
Final marketing year average (MYA) prices were recently released by the USDA for nearly all eligible commodities, and the Farm Service Agency (FSA) has finalized the payments for most commodities from the Agricultural Risk Coverage (ARC) and Price Loss (PLC) programs for the 2025 crop year. Overall, payment rates were substantial and widespread across most of the US for corn, soybeans and wheat, averaging $62, $29 and $47 per base acre, respectively.

Rural Broadband Infrastructure and Its Implications for Households and Agriculture

October 12, 2026
Nancy Ouedraogo and Mark White
Survey results in this article show that although many rural households now have access to broadband service, the existing infrastructure often falls short of meeting evolving household and business needs. Agriculture has distinct broadband needs that extend beyond residential service requirements, and both must be addressed as rural communities continue to develop. Reliable, high-quality broadband is increasingly important to the competitiveness of production agriculture.

Conservation & Risk, Part 5: Examining a Risk-Payment Gap for Nitrogen Management

October 8, 2026
Natalie Loduca, Jonathan Coppess, Menglin Liu, Emily Judith Diaz-Vallejo, Yue Qin, and Kaiyu Guan
Agricultural conservation policies include assistance for nutrient management. To the extent that this assistance is intended to encourage farmers to reduce application rates for nutrients, such as nitrogen, the policy presents another variation on the conservation-risk challenges examined in this series. This article examines a case study to illustrate the potential costs and risks from reduced nitrogen application alongside the financial assistance available through EQIP.
