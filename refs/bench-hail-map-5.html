    Based on cutting edge science built from the ground up, our HailFACTS reports are derived from a hail verification model shown to be up to four times more accurate than other Hail Detection Algorithm-based (HDA-based) products on the market.

    To objectively determine what hail size affected a given address of interest, the hail verification model combines proprietary hail size algorithms, state-of-the-science artificial intelligence, 3-D storm models, and the best available remote sensing and point-specific weather data.

    The result, an easy to read PDF report that provides you a complete hail history of your location and surrounding areas. A maximum estimated hail size is provided at the location, 1 mile, 3 miles and 10 miles at a tenth of inch accuracy ranging from 0.7” to 4.0”+.
