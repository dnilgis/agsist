7/13/2023

Total Run: 2,898

Market Notes

Compared to two weeks ago: Feeder Steers did not have enough comparable sales to call a trend but generally higher prices were noted. Feeder Heifers traded 5.00 to 10.00 higher. There were not enough comparable sales on steer or heifer calves to call a market trend. Slaughter Cows traded mostly steady. Slaughter Bulls traded 5.00 higher. Trading was active on good buyer demand.

Supply included: 79% Feeder Cattle (41% Steers, 58% Heifers, 1% Bulls); 21% Slaughter Cattle (99% Cows, 1% Bulls). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 83%.
