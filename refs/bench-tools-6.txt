About FasalBachao Farm Calculators

FasalBachao Farm Calculators is a free suite of 15 precision agriculture tools built to help farmers, agronomists, gardeners, and agriculture students make faster, smarter, and more profitable farming decisions — without any guesswork.
Whether you are calculating fertilizer quantities, planning plant spacing, estimating irrigation time, or measuring farm income — FasalBachao has a free calculator for it.

What Makes FasalBachao Different:
Most farming calculators online are built for Western agriculture — they use acres and feet but ignore the reality of South Asian farmers who work in kanals, marlas, and bighas. FasalBachao is built specifically for Pakistani and South Asian farmers — supporting local units while also covering international measurements.
Backed by PhD and MSc Agriculture graduates from the University of Agriculture Faisalabad — every calculator is research-validated and field-tested.

The 15 Free Calculators:
Fertilizer & Soil

Fertilizer Calculator — exact fertilizer quantities per acre for any crop
Phosphorus Cost Saver — compare DAP, SSP, MAP, TSP, NP to find cheapest source

Planting & Population

Plants per Acre Calculator — exact plant population for any row spacing
Plants per Hectare Calculator — metric version for international farmers
Vegetable Garden Size Calculator — plan your perfect kitchen garden

Tree & Environment

Tree Spacing Calculator — trees per acre for any orchard or plantation
WindBreak Calculator — design windbreaks to protect crops from wind
Tree Benefits Calculator — environmental and economic value of trees
Carbon Sequestration Calculator — carbon stored in your trees

Water & Irrigation

Tube Well Discharge Calculator — water output and irrigation time per acre
Tractor Time Calculator — field work duration by equipment size and speed

Farm Management

Agriculture Area Calculator — convert acres, hectares, kanals, marlas
Agriculture Income Calculator — cost of production and income per acre
Wheat Straw Calculator — crop residue estimation from wheat harvest
Spray Tank Mix Calculator — exact pesticide dosage per acre

Who Is This For:

Farmers across Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, and South Asia
Agriculture graduates and extension workers
Home gardeners and kitchen garden enthusiasts
Agronomists and farm consultants
Anyone growing food anywhere in the world

Why Free Forever:
FasalBachao was built on a simple belief — Pakistani farmers deserve access to the same precision agriculture tools that farmers in developed countries use. No paywalls, no subscriptions, no signup required. Just open the calculator and use it.
