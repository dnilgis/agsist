farm bill 2.0

Topline

An updated farm bill is long-overdue. Economic conditions have changed dramatically since the last farm bill became law in 2018, and producers are anxiously awaiting action. The Agricultural Act of 2026 builds upon the investments in farm programs delivered through the Working Families Tax Cuts. A fully modernized farm bill means more certainty for America’s farm families, rural communities and food supply. The legislation also includes authorizing year-round sales of E15.

Farm Bill 2. 0 is designed to strengthen agriculture, support rural communities and ensure our nation’s agricultural legacy remains strong for generations to come. It is built for the people who feed America.

Built for Farmers  

Delivers stronger, more reliable support for farmers by improving risk management tools, modernizing conservation programs, expanding assistance to specialty crop producers, increasing drought flexibility, and creating new market opportunities for American-grown products.

Built for Rural America

Farm Bill 2.0 improves quality of life in rural communities. These policies strengthen rural economies by expanding essential services and investing in critical infrastructure—from local processing and small business development to mental health care, safe drinking water and high-speed internet connectivity.

Built for the Future

Farm Bill 2.0 strengthens the foundation of American agriculture by equipping the next generation of producers with modernized financing tools and expanding investment in research, innovation, and extension services to address emerging agricultural priorities.
