Drought Monitor: Chunk of West Alabama remains in extreme drought

A portion of West Alabama is still stuck in extreme drought territory, according to the latest U.S. Drought Monitor map released today.

The map, which covers through Oct. 28, has extreme drought listed in:

  • Tuscaloosa County's southeast tip
  • Nearly all of Hale County except the northwest
  • The southernmost section of Greene County
  • The easternmost section of Bibb County
  • Nearly all of Marengo County except the northwest tip
  • West Perry County

West Alabama areas in severe drought include:

  • Southern Tuscaloosa County
  • Bibb County, minus the portion in extreme drought
  • Perry County, minus the portion in extreme drought
  • Northwest tip of Marengo County
  • Northwest tip of Hale County
  • Greene County, minus the portion in extreme drought
  • Southernmost portion of Walker County
  • Southernmost portion of Pickens County
  • Westernmost portion of Sumter County

There are currently no burn restrictions in the state, but consider taking precautions if you are lighting a fire outside within the drought-striken areas.
