Tools and Decision Aids

The Agricultural and Food Policy Center (AFPC) has developed educational tools and decision aids to assist in the analysis of agricultural policy issues and assist producers in making informed decisions.

2026 Area-Wide Crop Insurance

Updated: Mar 2026

A stochastic simulation that provides estimated per acre net indemnities based on your expected 2026 harvest prices and actual RMA area yields for:

STAX
- Stacked Income Protection Plan (cotton only)

SCO - Supplemental Coverage Option

ECO - Enhanced Coverage Option

2026 FSA ARC/PLC Expected Payments

Updated: Sep 2026

A stochastic simulation characterizing the probabilities of different levels of expected FSA payments based on 500 possible future realizations of market prices and county yields for:

ARC - Agriculture Risk Coverage

PLC - Price Loss Coverage

2026 ARC-IC Decision Aid

Updated: Oct 2026

Excel workbook

A spreadsheet that combines every FSA farm you have enrolled in ARC-IC in one state into a single ARC-IC farm, for up to fifty farms.
