Direct Hay Weighted Average Report for week ending 10/16/2026

Good to very good demand for all classes of hay. Best demand is coming from western South Dakota where the drought has been severe and
little hay was made this year. Grass hay supplies are limited as the weather wasn't conducive to growing grass as it was dry during April and
May. Drought has gripped the eastern side of the state as well, some areas are very short and made substantially less hay than normal, while
in others spots the alfalfa produced pretty well.
