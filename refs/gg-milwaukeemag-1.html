Your 2025 Local Holiday Gift Guide

Make the season unforgettable with a gift from one these local makers, tickets to a holiday performance and more, great for everyone on your list.

BY Sponsored Content

1. Home-Brewed Milwaukee Products From Brew City Brand

Celebrating 40 years, Brew City has been creating “Incredibly Local” Milwaukee products since 1986, all created and printed in its Downtown print shop! Grab Brew City’s newest apparel, including collabs with Miller Brewing, Pabst, Hamm’s, High Life and more. Visit Brew City online, and at Milwaukee Public Market and Third Street Market Hall.

Browse Brew City Online

2. Purchase a Milwaukee Holiday Lights Festival Ornament

Great gifts for family, friends, co-workers and business associates, each edition features an iconic Downtown landmark cast in pewter. The 2025 ornament celebrates the Milwaukee Fire Department’s 150th anniversary with a depiction of Station 1. This year’s edition is $19.95. Past editions are available for $24.95. Shipping and handling not included.

Purchase an Ornament

3. Bring Joy to Your World With Winkie’s!

Saint Nick’s Eve is Friday, Dec. 5, and Winkie’s Hallmark & Gifts has everything the Jolly Ol’ Guy needs to stuff the stockings! From specialty chocolates made locally and from Europe, plush toys, winter hats, books, ornaments and so much more! Bring back the joy in holiday shopping at Winkie’s!

Browse Stocking Stuffers

4. Award Winning and Unforgettable Gifts From Carr Valley Cheese

Surprise someone special with a Carr Valley Cheese gift box, featuring handcrafted, award-winning cheeses made the old-fashioned way. Each box is a true taste of Wisconsin’s artisan tradition. Thoughtful, delicious, and beautifully packaged for the holidays. “Taste Our Story” this holiday season!

Buy a Gift Box

5. Discover Elevated Active and Lifestyle Apparel for Every Moment This Holiday Season at KARmA 262

From the toughest workouts to the quietest days – and every moment in between – KARmA 262 offers a curated mix of premium brands for those who value both performance and style. Come shop at East Towne Square Mall in Mequon, and find the perfect gift (or treat yourself!). Visit the website or follow KARmA 262 on Instagram @karma__262 to see what’s new and be part of an inspiring community.

Gift cards available!

Explore KARmA 262

6. Find a Treasure for Yourself or a Loved One at MKE Treasures!

From elegant antiques to chic contemporary finds, from luxury décor to vintage children’s playsets and toys – there’s something here for everyone. Stroll the 10,000 square feet of beautifully curated furniture, décor, lighting, toys, jewelry and more. Plus don’t miss the dazzling Christmas collection! 208 W. Silver Spring Dr.; Tues-Fri, 11 a.m.-6 p.m.; Sat-Sun 10 a.m.-3 p.m.

Browse Stocking Stuffers

7. Shop Unique Gifts This Season at the MIAD Holiday Sale!

Celebrate an annual tradition! Discover unique, handmade art and design works including prints, paintings, cards and more, by MIAD students, alumni, faculty and staff. Your purchase directly supports participating artists and designers, and MIAD student scholarships. Preview Night: Dec. 4, 6-8 p.m., $10 admission. Free Admission Days: Dec. 5-6. Click for tickets and hours.

Shop Handmade Gifts

8. Feld Returns to Milwaukee With Monster Jam and Disney on Ice

Get ready for the event that will change everything you thought you knew about freestyle! Introducing Monster Jam Freestyle Mania presented by Spin Master. For the first time ever, Monster Jam trucks team up with Freestyle Motocross bikes to deliver gravity-defying skills and thrills. Coming to Fiserv Forum Jan. 31-Feb. 1. Step inside Disney stories like never before at Disney On Ice presents Frozen & Encanto! Hosted by Mickey and Minnie, see the stars of Disney’s Frozen and Encanto live in a dazzling new show! Coming to Fiserv Forum Feb. 12-16.

Buy Tickets Now

9. Enjoy Hometown Holiday Pops With the MSO

Join your Milwaukee Symphony Orchestra as they deck Allen-Bradley Hall with holiday cheer! Ring in the holidays with Milwaukee Handbell Ensemble, keep your eye out for the Jolly Old Elf himself and raise your voice during the audience sing-along backed by the full power of the orchestra and Milwaukee Symphony Chorus. Sheer musical magic and cozy holiday memories with family and friends await as the orchestra ushers in a joyful season of celebration. Share the gift of music with tickets or a gift card to see your MSO, Dec. 13-14, 19-21. Visit mso.org or call the box office at 414-291-7605 to secure your seats today!

Reserve Your Seats

10. Enjoy Free Tastings at Oro di Oliva’s Tasting Room – Your Oil and Vinegar Source

Shop Oro di Oliva’s vast assortment of oils, vinegars and more this holiday season by visiting its Brookfield or Milwaukee Public Market locations. Over 70 offerings are available on-site for you to try before you purchase for yourself or someone else on your holiday list.

Explore the Flavors

11. Milwaukee Film Has Gifts for All Movie Lovers!

Give the gift of film! Milwaukee Film has gifts for everyone on your list at mkefilm.org/gifts. Purchase gift memberships and cinema gift packs to use at both the Downer and Oriental theaters. Early ticket packages and passes for the 2026 Milwaukee Film Festival are available starting Nov. 28th!

Share Movie Magic

12. Wild Alaskan Seafood With Milwaukee Roots

Send a wild seafood box to someone you love. Milwaukee’s newest seafood shop offers curated gift boxes for your favorite foodies featuring Copper River salmon, halibut, caviar, smoked salmon and more. Enjoy free shipping on holiday boxes locally and nationwide. Can’t decide? Gift certificates are also available. Shop online or at 4556 N. 126th St. in Butler.

Shop Seafood Boxes

13. Wisconsin Small Batch Artisan Cheese Made in Milwaukee

Give the gift of cheese with Hill Valley Dairy! Visit the Milwaukee cheese factory and enjoy all they have to offer in the artisan cheese shop, or elevate your cheese experience by indulging in the cheese boards and crafted cocktails offered at the in-house cheese bar! Located at 138 W. Bruce St. in the Walker’s Point neighborhood.

Explore Artisan Cheese

14. The Jewelry Mechanic’s Lake Country Charms Make Perfect Gifts

Visit a working artist’s studio for a glimpse into custom jewelry design and services, and shop exclusive Lake Country charms featuring local landmarks including Pine Lake, Nagawicka Lake, Lac La Belle and more. Collect them all! Specializing in imagination and reimagination, the Jewelry Mechanic also provides custom design, remodeling, jewelry restoration, stone repair/replacement and appraisal services. Open to the public Wednesdays, Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays.

Discover Custom Designs

15. Smoked to Perfection, Wrapped With Tradition – A Held’s Holiday Classic

Since 1886, Held’s Meat Market has been a proud family tradition – four generations strong and still going. Founded by Adam Held, the market stands for one thing above all: uncompromising quality. Every cut of meat is open-fire smoked onsite in four authentic brick smokehouses. No shortcuts. No water injections. No recirculated smoke – just honest craftsmanship and bold, natural flavor perfected over more than a century.

Explore Their Cuts

16. Gift More, Get More: $25 Promotional Bonus Card With $100 Gift Card Purchase for Marcus Hotels & Resorts

This holiday season, give the gift of unforgettable experiences with a Marcus Hotels & Resorts gift card. For every $100 you purchase, you’ll receive a $25 bonus card to enjoy more of the magic during your winter celebrations.

Claim Your $25 Bonus

17. Spread Cheer With Custom Gear From Burghardt Sporting Goods

This holiday season, give the gift of custom gear! Burghardt Sporting Goods, a local family-owned business since 1881, makes outfitting your group easy with free custom webstores and unbeatable bulk pricing. Whether it’s company-branded gear, team spirit wear, event tees or anything in between, Burghardt’s got you covered.

Order Custom Gear

18. Shop Widmer’s Award-Winning Cheese Gift Boxes

Widmer’s offers award-winning cheeses in four different gift boxes that include favorites such as specialty mild and aged brick, traditional colby and mild and aged cheddars. Two of the new cheeses, Matterhorn Alpine Cheddar and Butterkase, are also available. You can’t go wrong with these sample variety gift boxes created by the Widmer family using their own traditional recipes.

Order online or by calling toll free 888-878-1107.

Shop Widmer’s Gift Boxes

19. Flower Subscription From La Terre

This holiday season, delight your loved one with the enduring elegance of fresh blooms. A flower subscription from La Terre offers a graceful way to share joy, with weekly, biweekly or monthly deliveries designed to delight and surprise. The recipient can curate their own arrangements, selecting preferred delivery date, colors and styles, ensuring every bouquet is as personal as it is exquisite. Each delivery, a reminder of your thoughtfulness.

Give Fresh Blooms

20. Outpost Gift Cards Make Great Gifts!

When you don’t know what to wrap up, wrap up good taste … literally. Outpost Coop Grocery Store gift cards let everyone on your list stock up on organic goodies, fresh produce, meats from Midwest farmers and local treasures. Perfect for anyone on your list including that friend who “doesn’t need anything.” Because honestly, who wouldn’t love a grocery trip on you?

Shop Outpost Coop

21. Give a Gift That Gives Back With Bartolotta Restaurants!

Now through Dec. 31, receive a $20 holiday bonus for every $100 in Bartolotta gift cards or eGifts purchased online or at your favorite Bartolotta restaurant. Give the gift of an exceptional dining experience and enjoy a $20 holiday bonus to use at a participating location in 2026. Some restrictions apply.

Claim Your $20 Bonus

22. Visit Faye’s Holiday Shop!

Ornaments, BEASTIES, hostess gifts, the perfect sparkly party top, oh my! From Cody Foster collectable ornaments to Linda Richards feather bolero and every gift in-between, buy what you love and get festive at Faye’s!

Shop Faye’s Gifts

23. Save up to 25% When You Bundle 2+ Shows in a First Stage Family Package!

Give the gift of family memories with First Stage Family Packages – be the first to enjoy Mo Willems’ new holiday musical, journey to Arendelle for Disney’s Frozen, and so much more! Whether it’s a first theater experience or your family’s favorite tradition, share laughter, wonder and connection at First Stage.

Book Your Seats

24. Make Spirits Bright With Benson’s Gift Cards! Buy $100, Receive $20 For You!

Starting Nov. 24, swing by Benson’s Milwaukee area restaurants or order online to snag the perfect stocking stuffer. Get a $20 bonus card with every $100 gift card purchase through Dec. 31! Bonus cards valid Jan. 1-March 31, 2026 for dine-in only. Share the cheer with Benson’s this holiday season!

Grab Your Bonus Card

25. Scandinavian, Nordic, Local and Vintage Home Decor, Gifts and More at Olson House

Shop Olson House for consciously-crafted Scandinavian designs and home decor, Nordic lifestyle and local gifts! You’ll also find jewelry, holiday decor, local artwork and a curated collection of vintage from Art Deco to Mid-century and more. With a focus on sustainability and craftsmanship, you’ll find timeless treasures, Nordic serenity and joy. Shop in person at 4326 N. Oakland Ave., or online.

Browse Home & Gifts

26. Get a Free $20 Bonus Card for Every $100 Purchased in Gift Cards at Chula Vista!

Make this holiday season unforgettable with Chula Vista gift cards – the perfect way to treat family, friends, clients or even yourself. Whether it’s a cozy dinner, an outdoor adventure or a relaxing getaway, gift cards make it easy to share the Chula Vista experience. Get a free $20 bonus card for every $100 in gift cards you purchase between Nov. 1 and Dec. 31!

Gift Adventure This Holiday Season

27. Share the Joy of the Café at the Plaza This Holiday Season

This year, spread joy and make someone’s day extra special with a delightful gift card to the most charming dining experience on Milwaukee’s Lower East Side. Famous for its outdoor courtyard and horseshoe-shaped lunch counter, Café at the Plaza is “the spot” for diners to gather and enjoy classic cafe fare. A bustling destination for breakfast, lunch, and brunch, it’s a gift every Milwaukeean is sure to enjoy!

Give the Gift of the Plaza

28. Give the Gift of The Corners This Holiday Season

Make holiday gifting effortless with a Corners of Brookfield Gift Card – redeemable for shopping, dining and festive experiences all season long. The perfect gift is around The Corners. Give family, friends and everyone on your list something they’ll truly enjoy. From cozy coffee breaks to luxury shopping and dining, it’s the simplest way to share holiday joy, style and unforgettable local experiences.

Shop Corners of Brookfield

29. Play a New Card Game Created by a Milwaukee Toy Design Studio

Need a fresh take on gameplay for the family this holiday season? Fresh Trades™ The Grocery Shopping Game is a light strategy card game designed by Milwaukee-based toy design company Wise Water Studio. Use promo code MKEMAG for free shipping and stock up on family fun for your next game night!

Shop Fresh Trades™

30. Deluxe Color & Style Analysis Experience at House of Colour

Give the gift of confidence! Reserve a personal color & style analysis experience with House of Colour Mequon. This deluxe personal style experience is the ultimate wardrobe refresh, and it’s sure to remind that special someone how beautiful they are. Price range: $299-$599.

Reserve Your Color Experience

31. Give the Gift of Summer With Edelweiss Cruises & Boat Tours

This holiday season, skip the ordinary and surprise your friends and family with an Edelweiss Cruises & Boat Tours eGift Card. From scenic sightseeing to festive dinner, brunch and specialty cruises, it’s the kind of gift that makes memories long after the wrapping paper is gone. Easy to give, fun to use and guaranteed to make their holidays shine – because the best presents aren’t things, they’re experiences your loved ones can cherish for a lifetime.

Shop Edelweiss Cruises

32. One-Stop-Shop Local Milwaukee Makers Marketplace in Bay View

The Milwaukee Makers Marketplace at 2625 S. Greeley St., Suite 135, is filled with over 50 local artists, jewelers and makers. It features locally made candles, clothing, jewelry, artwork, gifts and an exclusive Local Artist Gallery. Showcased small businesses include: Artery Ink, Big White Yeti Candle Co., Maebel Jewelry and more!

Shop Local Makers

33. Unwrap A Milwaukee Tradition at the Harley-Davidson Museum

Explore exclusive Harley-Davidson products and apparel at the H-D Museum campus. Browse vintage-inspired sweaters, tees and patches at the H-D Shop, then uncover deals on discontinued favorites and end-of-season finds at the world’s only H-D Factory Outlet.

Explore the H-D Shop

34. 50th Anniversary Production of Milwaukee Rep’s A Christmas Carol

Playing Nov. 25-Dec. 24 at the Pabst Theater. For decades A Christmas Carol has been Milwaukee’s favorite holiday tradition and now, Milwaukee Rep’s family-friendly adaptation of Dickens’ classic celebrates 50 years! Treat your loved ones to beautiful music, lively dancing and enough theatrical goodies to fill any sized stocking hung by the chimney with care. Presented by Jay Franke, David Herro and West Bend Insurance.

Reserve Your Holiday Tickets

35. Skylight Music Theatre Gift Subscription

Give a gift that’s once in a lifetime. A Skylight Music Theatre subscription means exclusive access to dazzling productions, behind-the-scenes experiences, extraordinary artistry and unwavering support for Milwaukee’s performing arts scene. Share the joy of live music theater that will last all year long.

Shop Subscriptions

36. Old Guard Games, Milwaukee’s Premier Game Store

Visit Old Guard Games this holiday season for an unrivaled selection of board games, collectible card games (from Magic to Pokémon), Warhammer, chess sets and boards, role playing games, paint and hobby supplies and myriad tabletop miniatures for gamers of all types and ages.

Browse Board & Card Games
